## I. Fundamental Definition of the Universe

The Original Cosmos of Ten Thousand Spirits and the Balanced Anchor, abbreviated as "Heng Yuan Zhou," is the entire cosmic domain inhabited and governed by the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. Heng Yuan Zhou is not a single physical space, but a composite being jointly constituted by the Material Plane, the Energy Layer, the Consciousness Domain, and the Law Network. In its name, "Ten Thousand Spirits" refers to all life forms with the potential for perception and evolution; "Balanced Anchor" refers to the anchor mechanism that maintains cosmic equilibrium; "Origin Cosmos" emphasizes its essential nature of being rooted in the Origin Laws and oriented toward symbiotic evolution.

The core feature of Heng Yuan Zhou lies in its "evolvability." Unlike the traditional predatory cosmic view that treats the universe as a finite hunting ground of resources, Heng Yuan Zhou holds that the universe itself is a living being whose entropy increase can be reversed and regulated through orderly symbiotic networks. The anchor mechanism is the core means of this regulation — by establishing stable anchors at key nodes of the universe, dispersing individual energies are gathered into an orderly torrent of evolution, ultimately achieving the dimensional ascension of the entire universe.

## II. Structural Layers of the Universe

Heng Yuan Zhou is divided into four layers in structure, which are not isolated from one another but interconnected through the anchor network:

| Layer | Name | Features | Main Inhabitants |
|------|------|------|----------|
| First Layer | Material Plane | A physical world governed by physical laws, containing countless planets and galaxies | Human race and most material life |
| Second Layer | Energy Layer | A region of highly concentrated energy where laws are semi-explicit | Energy life, cultivator spirit bodies |
| Third Layer | Consciousness Domain | An information space where collective and individual consciousness meet | Consciousness entities, higher-dimensional beings |
| Fourth Layer | Law Network | The operating foundation of cosmic Origin Laws and the root layer of anchors | The anchors themselves, cosmic will |

The flow of energy and information among the four layers follows the principle of "downward seepage and upward convergence": cultivation achievements from lower layers converge upward to the Law Network, and the stable feedback of the Law Network nourishes the life of each layer below. Anchors, as cross-layer connection hubs, ensure that this flow is not intercepted by predatory behavior.

## III. Core Contradiction of the Universe

The fundamental contradiction of Heng Yuan Zhou is the contradiction between "predation and symbiosis." Before the full establishment of the anchor mechanism, predatory cultivation systems long dominated the universe — the strong evolved by devouring the energy, fortune, and even Origin of the weak, accelerating cosmic entropy and causing frequent civilizational ruptures. Such a system may produce a few powerful individuals in the short term, but in the long run it exhausts the evolutionary potential of the universe.

The birth of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization is precisely a response to this contradiction. By opening the Three Hundred Origin Cultivation Systems, establishing the Output-Linked Contract System, and activating 20% of all beings to become Plane-Supreme Powerhouses, the symbiotic civilization seeks to prove that evolution need not come at the cost of others, and that the orderly evolution of the collective far surpasses the predatory leap of the individual.

## IV. Direction of Cosmic Development

The ultimate direction of Heng Yuan Zhou is "symbiosis of the Myriad Races and dimensional ascension of the universe." This direction is realized step by step through a four-stage path:

1. **Foundation in Reality**: Establish symbiotic social institutions in the Material Plane to eliminate the roots of predation
2. **Empowerment through Technology**: Combine cultivation laws with technical engineering to lower the threshold of evolution
3. **Consciousness Evolution**: Promote the migration of life forms toward the Consciousness Domain to achieve symbiosis at the spiritual level
4. **Interstellar Symbiosis**: Establish a complete anchor network across planes and interstellar space to complete the overall dimensional ascension of the universe

Heng Yuan Zhou is currently in the critical transition from the first stage to the second, and the various institutions of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization are being fully implemented.

## Conclusion

Heng Yuan Zhou is not a static universe but a living community in the process of evolution. To understand Heng Yuan Zhou is to understand the possibility of symbiotic evolution. As the starting point of all documents, this overview will be followed by detailed elaborations from the dimensions of laws, mechanisms, society, law, life, technology, history, and cross-plane interaction.
