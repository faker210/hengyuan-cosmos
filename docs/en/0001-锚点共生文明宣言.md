## I. Historical Background of the Declaration

In the long history of the Original Cosmos of Ten Thousand Spirits and the Balanced Anchor (hereinafter "Heng Yuan Zhou"), predatory cultivation systems once dominated cosmic order for a long time. The strong used the devouring of the Origin, fortune, and life energy of the weak as the ladder of advancement; sects used the monopoly of cultivation methods as the foundation of domination; planes used war and enslavement as the means of expansion. In the short term, this system produced a few supreme powerhouses, but in the long term it exhausted the evolutionary potential of the universe, causing frequent civilizational ruptures, the withering of the Myriad Races, and accelerated cosmic entropy.

The birth of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization is a fundamental response to this historical predicament. This Declaration proclaims: the era of predatory cultivation systems has come to an end, and a new epoch of symbiotic evolution has begun. All life in Heng Yuan Zhou — regardless of race, form, or realm — enjoys equal rights to evolution.

## II. Core Propositions of the Declaration

This Declaration establishes the following unshakable core propositions:

1. **Equality of the Right to Evolution**: Every life possesses the fundamental right to choose its own evolutionary path, and no organization or individual may deprive or restrict this right for any reason.
2. **Anti-predation Principle**: Cultivation through devouring, enslaving, or exploiting the Origin or energy of other lives is prohibited; all evolution must be built upon symbiotic cycles.
3. **Openness of the Systems**: The Three Hundred Origin Cultivation Systems are open to the Myriad Races free of charge; cultivation methods are no longer the private property of the few.
4. **More Work, More Reward**: Evolutionary outcomes match individual effort; the unearned gains of predation are opposed, as is the lazy distribution of egalitarianism.
5. **Self-Growth**: The autonomous evolutionary rhythm of every life is respected — no coercion, no forcing growth by pulling up seedlings, while necessary environmental and resource support is provided.
6. **A Path for Everyone**: Every life is guaranteed to find a cultivation path suited to its own nature; no individual is abandoned by the evolutionary system.
7. **Myriad Races Advancing Together**: Evolution is not a zero-sum game; collaboration and symbiosis among the Myriad Races can produce collective evolutionary efficacy greater than the sum of individuals.

## III. Legal Foundation of the Declaration

This Declaration is not an empty moral appeal but a civilizational program with a solid legal foundation. Its legal basis includes:

| Level of Law | Basis | Explanation |
|----------|------|------|
| Cosmic Law Level | Origin Symbiosis Law | The Origin Laws of the universe themselves incline toward orderly symbiosis rather than disorderly predation; the anchor mechanism is the manifestation of this law |
| Civilizational Constitution Level | Symbiotic Civilization Charter | This Declaration is the spiritual forerunner of the Charter; the Charter concretizes the propositions of the Declaration into enforceable legal provisions |
| Mechanism Guarantee Level | Output-Linked Contract and Anchor Network | Institutional design ensures the propositions of the Declaration are not hollowed out; every life's right to evolution is supported by substantive resources and mechanisms |

## IV. The Declaration's Call to the Myriad Races

This Declaration issues a call to all races and life forms in Heng Yuan Zhou:

- Abandon the old path of predatory cultivation and join the new system of symbiotic evolution
- Open the cultivation knowledge and resources under your control, and participate in the joint construction and optimization of the Three Hundred Systems
- Respect the right of other races to choose their own evolution, and do not impose your own standards upon others
- Actively participate in the construction and maintenance of the anchor network, contributing to the overall dimensional ascension of the universe
- Uphold ethical bottom lines in the process of evolution, and never restore predatory behavior under any pretext

## V. Validity and Amendment of the Declaration

This Declaration took effect on the founding day of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization and is the supreme spiritual program of all civilizational institutions in Heng Yuan Zhou. Any amendment of the Declaration requires the approval of more than two-thirds of the members of the Republic of Myriad Races Assembly, and must not violate the core spirit of "anti-predation and pro-symbiosis." Any law, institution, or cultivation method that conflicts with the spirit of this Declaration is null and void.

## Conclusion

The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization Declaration is the first civilizational program in the history of Heng Yuan Zhou with "symbiosis" at its core and "Myriad Races Advancing Together" as its goal. It proclaims the end of an old era and the beginning of a new one. Under the guidance of the Declaration, Heng Yuan Zhou will move toward the ultimate direction of symbiosis of the Myriad Races and dimensional ascension of the universe.
