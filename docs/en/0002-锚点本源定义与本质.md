## I. Basic Definition of the Anchor Point

The Anchor Point is the core mechanism and existential entity in the Original Cosmos of Ten Thousand Spirits and the Balanced Anchor that maintains cosmic balance, regulates energy flow, and supports the symbiotic network. "An" (anchor) conveys the meaning of stability, fixation, and non-drift; "Dian" (point) conveys the meaning of key node and connection hub. The Anchor Point is not a mere physical coordinate or a point of energy concentration, but a composite existence where the four dimensions of matter, energy, consciousness, and law converge — the manifestation and solidification of the cosmic Origin Laws at specific nodes of space-time.

Structurally, the Anchor Point consists of three parts: the core, the shell, and the tendrils. The core is the access point to the Law Network; the shell is the energy barrier protecting the core; the tendrils are connection conduits extending to the surrounding planes and life forms. The three operate in coordination, enabling the Anchor Point to simultaneously perform three major functions: energy regulation, information transmission, and law manifestation.

## II. Essential Attributes of the Anchor Point

The essence of the Anchor Point can be understood from the following five dimensions:

| Dimension | Essential Attribute | Concrete Manifestation |
|------|----------|----------|
| Law Dimension | Solidified node of the Origin Laws | The Anchor Point is the "anchoring" of the cosmic Origin Laws in space-time, giving the flowing laws a stable operational carrier |
| Energy Dimension | Regulation hub of energy circulation | The Anchor Point regulates the direction and rate of cosmic energy flow, preventing energy from being predatorily intercepted or disorderly dissipated |
| Consciousness Dimension | Convergence interface of collective consciousness | The Anchor Point connects individual consciousness with the cosmic collective consciousness, enabling the wills of the Myriad Races to converge into a common direction of civilization |
| Space Dimension | Stable channel of cross-plane connection | The Anchor Point is the foundation of stable connection between planes; plane travel without anchors is dangerous and uncontrollable |
| Time Dimension | Calibration reference of the timeline | The Anchor Point provides a stable frame of reference for temporal regression and cross-time observation, preventing the emergence of temporal paradoxes |

## III. The Relationship between the Anchor Point and the Origin

"Origin" is the ultimate source and fundamental basis of all laws, energy, and life in the universe. The relationship between the Anchor Point and the Origin can be summarized as "manifestation and bearing":

1. **Origin is the substance; the Anchor Point is the function**: The Origin is the fundamental essence of the universe; the Anchor Point is the concrete mechanism through which the Origin exerts its power. Without the Origin, no Anchor Point can be established; without Anchor Points, the power of the Origin cannot benefit all beings in an orderly manner.
2. **The Origin is one; the Anchor Point is many**: The Origin is unique and unified, while Anchor Points are plural and distributed. Countless Anchor Points jointly constitute the anchor network, distributing the power of the single Origin to every corner of the universe.
3. **The Origin is still; the Anchor Point is dynamic**: The Origin itself is the ultimate existence transcending motion and stillness, while the Anchor Point is the operational carrier of the Origin in the dynamic universe. The establishment, maintenance, calibration, and upgrade of Anchor Points are concrete processes through which the power of the Origin adapts to cosmic changes.

## IV. Classification and Hierarchy of Anchor Points

Anchor Points are divided into the following levels according to function and coverage:

| Level | Name | Coverage | Core Function |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| First Level | Cosmic Origin Anchor | The entire Heng Yuan Zhou | Maintains overall cosmic balance; the root of all Anchor Points |
| Second Level | Plane Core Anchor | A single plane | Maintains the stability of the plane membrane and regulates internal energy circulation |
| Third Level | Star Region Anchor | A galaxy or star region | Connects the planets within a star region and regulates regional energy flow |
| Fourth Level | Planetary Anchor | A single planet | Maintains planetary ecological balance and supports planet-level symbiotic networks |
| Fifth Level | Civilizational Anchor | A single civilizational settlement | Connects civilizational individuals and supports civilization-level symbiotic feedback loops |
| Sixth Level | Individual Anchor | A single life | The Origin anchoring established within a cultivator's body; the foundation of individual evolution |

Anchors at all levels are interconnected through the principle of "downward seepage and upward convergence": cultivation achievements of lower-level anchors converge upward, and the stable feedback of higher-level anchors nourishes downward. The Individual Anchor is the smallest unit of the entire anchor network and the microscopic foundation of the symbiotic civilization's "A Path for Everyone."

## V. Establishment and Maintenance of Anchor Points

Establishing an Anchor Point requires three conditions: a suitable space-time node, sufficient Origin energy, and a clear symbiotic intention. All three are indispensable. The space-time node determines the stability of the Anchor Point; the Origin energy determines its strength; the symbiotic intention determines its nature — an anchor established with predatory intent is not a true Anchor Point but an energy trap.

The maintenance of Anchor Points includes four routine tasks: regular calibration, energy replenishment, tendril repair, and shell reinforcement. Advanced Anchor Points also require law synchronization and cross-plane coordination. The symbiotic civilization has established a dedicated system of anchor maintainers, staffed by advanced cultivators of the Spatial Juridical Dispatch System.

## Conclusion

The Anchor Point is the core mechanism of Heng Yuan Zhou and the material and legal foundation of the symbiotic civilization. Understanding the definition and essence of the Anchor Point is the starting point for understanding the entire Anchor Symbiotic Civilization system. The Anchor Point is not a cold instrument of law, but the response and bearing of the cosmic Origin to the will of the Myriad Races for symbiosis.
