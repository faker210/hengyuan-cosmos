## I. Status and Nature of the Charter

The Symbiotic Civilization Charter is the fundamental law of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization in the Original Cosmos of Ten Thousand Spirits and the Balanced Anchor, and the supreme basis of all laws, institutions, and policies. Taking the Declaration of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization as its spiritual forerunner, the Charter concretizes the core propositions of the Declaration into enforceable legal norms. The Charter's validity covers all races, planes, and individuals that have joined the symbiotic civilization within Heng Yuan Zhou; any local regulations, sect rules, or racial customs that conflict with the Charter are null and void.

The nature of the Charter can be summarized in three points: first, it is a civilizational contract — the basic consensus reached among the Myriad Races on symbiotic evolution; second, it is a bill of rights — clearly stipulating the fundamental rights and freedoms of every life within the symbiotic civilization; third, it is a law limiting power — drawing insurmountable boundaries for the power of civilizational governance institutions.

## II. Basic Principles of the Charter

The Charter establishes the following six basic principles, which run through all of its provisions:

1. **Principle of Symbiosis Supremacy**: All institutional design and exercise of power take the promotion of symbiotic evolution among the Myriad Races as the highest goal. When individual interests conflict with collective symbiotic interests, symbiotic interests take priority, but individuals must be given reasonable compensation and alternative paths.
2. **Anti-predation Principle**: The Charter prohibits all forms of predatory behavior, including but not limited to energy predation, Origin devouring, spiritual enslavement, and resource monopoly. Any cultivation system centered on predation is declared illegal.
3. **Principle of Equality of the Right to Evolution**: All life is equal before the right to evolution, without discrimination based on race, form, origin, or initial realm. States and civilizations have an obligation to provide additional evolutionary support to disadvantaged groups.
4. **Principle of More Work, More Reward**: The distribution of evolutionary resources is based primarily on the individual's contribution to the symbiotic network; the greater the contribution, the more resources and support obtained. At the same time, a basic security line is established to ensure that even individuals with lesser contributions can obtain basic evolutionary conditions.
5. **Principle of Combining Autonomy and Co-governance**: Each race enjoys autonomy over its internal affairs provided it does not violate the Charter; cross-racial and cross-plane public affairs are co-governed by the institutions of the Republic of Myriad Races.
6. **Principle of Reality as Foundation**: All institutional design of the Charter is based on the real world as the only true foundation; no rights and obligations from fictional worlds are recognized as capable of overriding reality.

## III. Basic Structure of the Charter

The Charter consists of six major parts: Preamble, General Provisions, Fundamental Rights and Obligations, Civilizational Institutions, Legal System, and Amendment Procedures:

## IV. Fundamental Rights and Obligations

The core fundamental rights stipulated by the Charter include:

- **Right to Choose Evolution**: Every life has the right to freely choose its cultivation system and evolutionary path; no one may compel it
- **Right to Access Systems**: Every life has the right to obtain, free of charge, the basic methods of the Three Hundred Origin Cultivation Systems
- **Right to Resource Allocation**: Every life has the right to obtain basic cultivation resources through the Output-Linked Contract System
- **Right to Anchor Access**: Every life that reaches a certain realm has the right to access the anchor network at the corresponding level
- **Right to Information and Participation**: Every life has the right to understand the public affairs of the civilization and participate in decision-making
- **Right to Remedy and Appeal**: Every life whose rights are infringed has the right to file an appeal with the Anchor Court

The corresponding core obligations include: abiding by the anti-predation principle, maintaining the stability of the anchor network, participating in symbiotic feedback loops, passing on cultivation knowledge, and protecting the cosmic ecological environment.

## V. Amendment and Guarantee of the Charter

Amendments to the Charter require a joint proposal by more than one-fifth of the members of the Republic of Myriad Races Assembly, approval by a majority of more than two-thirds of the Assembly, and verification by the Cosmic Origin Anchor that they do not violate the symbiotic laws before taking effect. The power to interpret the Charter belongs to the Anchor Court.

To guarantee the implementation of the Charter, the symbiotic civilization has established a constitutional review system: any law, regulation, or policy must undergo constitutional review by the Anchor Court before taking effect; any life that believes a public power act is unconstitutional may file a constitutional lawsuit.

## Conclusion

The Symbiotic Civilization Charter is the institutional cornerstone of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. It transforms the ideal of "anti-predation and pro-symbiosis" into enforceable, remediable, and supervised legal norms, providing solid institutional guarantees for the advancement of the Myriad Races. The Charter is not an immutable dogma but a living fundamental law that continuously improves with the evolution of civilization.
