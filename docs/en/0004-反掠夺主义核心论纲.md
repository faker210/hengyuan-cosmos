## I. Definition and Manifestations of Predationism

Predationism is a cosmic view and cultivation system that takes as its core means the uncompensated appropriation, devouring, and exploitation of the Origin, energy, fortune, and resources of other lives or planes, with the goal of achieving one's own or one's group's evolution. Predationism is not a specific cultivation technique, but a fundamental stance running through cultivation philosophy, social institutions, and civilizational relations.

In the history of Heng Yuan Zhou, predationism has manifested in the following main forms:

| Form | Manifestation | Harm |
|------|------|------|
| Energy Predation | Directly devouring the cultivation energy or Origin of other lives | Causes the death of the preyed-upon or total loss of cultivation; cosmic energy circulation is severed |
| Fortune Predation | Stealing the fortune of others or races through secret arts | Causes the collapse of the preyed-upon's fate and racial decline |
| Method Monopoly | A few sects monopolize cultivation methods, exchanging transmission for loyalty and resources | Causes class solidification; the lower beings lose evolutionary paths |
| Plane Colonization | Higher planes conquer lower planes, plundering their resources and populations | Damages the plane membrane and collapses plane ecosystems |
| Spiritual Enslavement | Turning other lives into cultivation tools through soul control | Deprives the enslaved of free will, violating life's dignity |
| Resource Exhaustion | Unrestrained exploitation of a planet's or plane's resources | Causes ecological extinction and accelerated cosmic entropy |

## II. The Fundamental Fallacies of Predationism

Anti-predationism holds that predationism contains three fundamental fallacies:

### 2.1 Fallacy of Cosmic View: Treating the Universe as a Finite Hunting Ground

The predationist cosmic view regards the universe as a hunting ground with finite resources, believing that evolution must be a zero-sum game and that the strong's advancement must come at the cost of the weak's sacrifice. This cosmic view ignores the universe's own "evolvability" — the universe is not a static resource container, but a living being capable of overall dimensional ascension through orderly symbiosis. When predators devour the weak, they consume not only the weak's energy but the evolutionary potential of the universe as a whole.

### 2.2 Ethical Fallacy: Placing the Strong's Right to Evolution above the Weak's Right to Survival

Predationism takes "the strong devour the weak" as a natural law, believing the strong have the right and even the obligation to devour the weak. This ethical view confuses natural selection with moral choice. In nature, the strong devouring the weak is an unconscious ecological law; but in conscious cultivation civilizations, the strong possess the capacity for choice and therefore bear the responsibility of choice. Predation disguised as "the Way of Heaven" is essentially the strong packaging their own greed as cosmic law.

### 2.3 Efficiency Fallacy: Believing Predation Is the Fastest Evolutionary Path

Predationism claims predation is the fastest and most efficient path of evolution. Yet in the long run, predatory evolution suffers a serious "efficiency ceiling": first, energy obtained through predation often carries the residual will of its original owner, requiring enormous effort to refine and purify; second, predatory acts provoke resistance from the preyed-upon and their affiliated groups, increasing the external costs of cultivation; third, long-term reliance on predation degrades the cultivator's own Origin-based evolutionary capacity, leading to stagnation once prey is unavailable; fourth, predatory cultivators often face the bottleneck of "impure Origin" when breaking through to the supreme realm, making it difficult to truly touch the cosmic Origin.

## III. Core Propositions of Anti-predationism

Anti-predationism does not simply negate all competition and difference, but proposes the following core propositions:

1. **Evolution can be non-zero-sum**: Through symbiotic cycles and the anchor network, the evolution of the Myriad Races can produce synergistic effects, achieving evolutionary efficacy where the whole exceeds the sum of its parts.
2. **Competition should be creative rather than destructive**: Anti-predationism does not oppose healthy competition, but competition should take the form of enhancing one's own creative capacity rather than eliminating the opponent's predatory capacity.
3. **The strong's responsibility is greater than their rights**: The higher a cultivator's realm, the greater their responsibility to the symbiotic network, not the greater their right to predate. Advanced cultivators have the obligation to guide junior cultivators, maintain anchor stability, and protect civilizational security.
4. **Resources should be increased through creation rather than distributed through predation**: The core approach of the symbiotic civilization is to enlarge the pie rather than divide it, continuously creating new cultivation resources through technological progress and law application.
5. **Anti-predation is not egalitarianism**: Anti-predationism opposes unearned predation, not the differences arising from More Work, More Reward. The symbiotic civilization acknowledges differences in evolutionary outcomes, but insists that differences must originate from one's own effort and contribution.

## IV. Institutional Guarantees of Anti-predationism

Anti-predationism is not merely a theoretical proposition; it has a series of institutional guarantees:

- **Legal guarantee**: The Symbiotic Constitution and the Anti-monopoly Law explicitly prohibit predatory behavior; the Anchor Court has jurisdiction over predation cases
- **Mechanism guarantee**: The Output-Linked Contract System ensures resource distribution is based on contribution rather than violence; the anchor network monitors abnormal energy flows
- **System guarantee**: All Three Hundred Origin Cultivation Systems are based on symbiotic circulation principles; no predatory methods exist
- **Educational guarantee**: The Symbiotic Education System makes anti-predation ethics a compulsory subject, eradicating predatory thought at its root
- **Supervision guarantee**: The Republic of Myriad Races Assembly has established an Anti-predation Committee to supervise and investigate predatory behavior

## V. Policy toward Residual Predatory Forces

For the residual predatory forces still existing in the universe, the symbiotic civilization adopts a policy of "isolation, transformation, and sanction": predators who have not yet caused serious harm receive ethical education and systemic transformation, guiding them to join the symbiotic system; predators who refuse transformation and continue to harm symbiotic order are subjected to plane isolation and cultivation restrictions; predators who have caused major disasters are sanctioned according to law. The symbiotic civilization does not advocate fighting predation with predation; all sanctions must pass through the fair trial of the Anchor Court.

## Conclusion

Anti-predationism is one of the theoretical cornerstones of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. It is not merely a critique of the old predatory system, but a positive construction of the possibility of symbiotic evolution. The ultimate goal of anti-predationism is not to eliminate the strong, but to make the power of the strong serve the common evolution of the Myriad Races.
