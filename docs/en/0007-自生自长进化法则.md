## I. Basic Definition of Self-Growth and Self-Development

Self-growth and self-development is one of the core evolutionary laws of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, meaning that the evolution of every life should take its own intrinsic autonomous will and natural rhythm as the fundamental driving force, and that the external environment and institutions should provide support and guarantees for this autonomous evolution, rather than forcibly intervening or over-promoting growth. "Self-growth" emphasizes that the driving force of evolution comes from the life itself; "self-development" emphasizes that the rhythm of evolution follows the life's own laws.

The law of self-growth and self-development stands in sharp contrast to the "forced evolution" of predationism. Predationism often achieves rapid but distorted evolution through devouring the Origin of others, forced energy infusion, soul control, and other means. Although this kind of evolution may raise one's realm in the short term, in the long run it destroys the life's own evolutionary foundation, leading to an impure Origin, unstable realms, and damaged minds. The law of self-growth and self-development insists that truly solid evolution must be grown from within the life itself, from the inside out.

## II. Theoretical Basis of Self-Growth and Self-Development

The establishment of the law of self-growth and self-development is based on a profound understanding of the laws of life evolution:

### 2.1 The Autonomy of Life Is the Fundamental Driving Force of Evolution

Every life possesses an intrinsic evolutionary will and potential, and this autonomy is the most fundamental and enduring driving force of evolution. External incentives and support can ignite this autonomy, but cannot replace it. When a life practices out of its own longing and choice, the focus, creativity, and endurance of its cultivation are far higher than in cases of forced or induced practice.

### 2.2 Every Life Has a Unique Evolutionary Rhythm

Different lives have different evolutionary rhythms due to differences in racial traits, individual endowments, growth environments, and mental maturity. Some lives suit rapid breakthroughs; some need slow accumulation; some can reach high realms at a young age; some bloom late. The law of self-growth and self-development respects this difference and does not require all lives to evolve according to a unified timetable.

### 2.3 Forced Intervention Destroys the Natural Foundation of Evolution

There are numerous cases in the history of cultivation showing that "rapid evolution" achieved through forced energy infusion, Origin transplantation, soul control, and other means is often accompanied by severe aftereffects: unreal floating realms, impure Origins, proliferating inner demons, shortened lifespans, and even cultivation deviation. This is because externally imposed power has not fully merged with the life's own Origin, producing an "alien body rejection" effect. Although self-grown evolution may be slower, every step is solid and steady, with a deep foundation.

## III. Practical Principles of Self-Growth and Self-Development

| Principle | Meaning | Practical Requirements |
|------|------|----------|
| Principle of Autonomous Choice | Cultivators have the right to independently choose their cultivation system, evolutionary path, and cultivation rhythm | Do not force cultivators to choose specific systems; do not set unified breakthrough timetables |
| Principle of Intrinsic Drive | The driving force of cultivation should come from the cultivator's own longing and interest | Education and guidance focus on igniting intrinsic interest, not punishment or bribery |
| Principle of Rhythm Respect | Respect each cultivator's unique evolutionary rhythm | Do not discriminate against or abandon cultivators for slow progress; provide personalized support plans |
| Principle of Foundation Priority | The solidity of evolution takes priority over speed | Encourage cultivators to build solid foundations; do not encourage sacrificing foundations for rapid breakthroughs |
| Principle of Error Tolerance | Allow cultivators to make mistakes and take detours during evolution | Establish fault-tolerant mechanisms; provide cultivators with opportunities for correction and adjustment |
| Principle of Self-Realization | The ultimate goal of evolution is the self-realization of life, not external standards | Do not treat realm height as the sole criterion for measuring evolutionary success |

## IV. The Relationship between Self-Growth and External Support

Self-growth and self-development does not mean "self-ignition and self-extinction" or "laissez-faire". On the contrary, self-growth requires a good external environment and institutional support to be fully realized. The symbiotic civilization provides the following support systems for self-growth:

### 4.1 Information Support

The full opening of the Three Hundred Origin Cultivation Systems provides every cultivator with ample room for choice. Cultivators can find the path most suited to themselves in the rich system library according to their own traits. The symbiotic civilization has also established a "System Compatibility Evaluation Mechanism," providing personalized system selection suggestions to cultivators through the anchor network, but the final choice always remains in the cultivator's hands.

### 4.2 Resource Support

The Output-Linked Contract System and the basic guarantee system ensure that every cultivator can obtain basic cultivation resources. For cultivators with special needs (such as special resource needs caused by racial traits), the symbiotic civilization provides targeted resource allocation. The purpose of resource support is to eliminate cultivators' worries, enabling them to focus on their own evolutionary rhythm.

### 4.3 Mentor Support

The cross-racial mentor network provides guidance and Q&A for cultivators. The mentor's role is that of a "guide" rather than a "controller"; mentors can share experience, point out problems, and provide suggestions, but cannot force cultivators to practice in their own way. The mentor system emphasizes "heuristic teaching," igniting cultivators' autonomous thinking ability.

### 4.4 Environmental Support

The symbiotic civilization is committed to creating a cultivation environment that encourages exploration, tolerates differences, and respects choices. Cultivators can freely try different cultivation methods without fear of discrimination for taking "non-mainstream" paths. The civilization has also established a "Cultivation Innovation Reward Mechanism," encouraging cultivators to innovate and personalize existing systems.

## V. Handling of Special Circumstances

The law of self-growth and self-development generally applies universally, but requires special handling in the following special circumstances:

- **Minor cultivators**: For minor cultivators who do not yet possess full autonomous judgment, their guardians or racial educational institutions provide guiding cultivation arrangements, but their autonomous choice rights should be gradually expanded as their minds mature
- **Those with damaged minds**: For individuals whose minds are damaged for various reasons and who cannot temporarily cultivate autonomously, the symbiotic civilization provides treatment and rehabilitation support, and they follow the principle of self-growth after recovering their autonomous abilities
- **Those who have deviated**: For individuals who have deviated in cultivation or gone mad, the symbiotic civilization provides forced intervention and rescue, but this is medical aid rather than evolutionary intervention; the purpose of rescue is to restore their ability to evolve autonomously
- **Those with dangerous tendencies**: For individuals whose cultivation direction clearly violates the anti-predation principle and may cause serious harm to themselves or others, the symbiotic civilization has the right to restrict and guide them, but should do so as much as possible through education and communication rather than forced means

## VI. The Civilizational Significance of Self-Growth and Self-Development

The law of self-growth and self-development has profound significance for the symbiotic civilization:

First, it protects the diversity of evolution. When every life evolves according to its own rhythm and path, the universe will produce rich and colorful evolutionary forms, and this diversity is an important resource for the overall ascension of the universe.

Second, it improves the quality of evolution. Self-grown evolution has a solid foundation and pure Origin; cultivators' realms are more stable, and their potential to break through higher realms is greater.

Third, it upholds the dignity of life. Every life is the master of its own evolution, not a tool manipulated by others. This autonomy is the core embodiment of the dignity of life.

Fourth, it ignites the creativity of evolution. When cultivators are no longer bound by unified standards and rhythms, they are more likely to explore brand-new evolutionary paths and methods, contributing to the continuous optimization of the Three Hundred Systems.

## Conclusion

Self-growth and self-development is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's profound respect for the laws of life evolution. It tells us that true evolution is not a result imposed by external forces, but the growth of life itself from the inside out. All institutional designs of the symbiotic civilization ultimately exist to create a good environment in which every life can grow on its own, allowing every life to grow into its best self according to its own rhythm.
