## I. Basic Definition of A Path for Everyone

A Path for Everyone is one of the core social goals of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, meaning to ensure that every life in Heng Yuan Zhou — regardless of race, origin, endowment, realm, or wealth — can obtain an evolutionary path suited to itself, and has the opportunity to achieve the elevation of realm and the perfection of life through its own efforts. "Having a path" means not only having cultivation methods to learn, but also having resources to use, mentors to ask, an environment to cultivate in, and institutions to rely on.

A Path for Everyone and More Work, More Reward are the two pillars of the distribution system of the symbiotic civilization. More Work, More Reward solves the "efficiency" problem, motivating the strong to create more value; A Path for Everyone solves the "fairness" problem, ensuring the weak are not abandoned by the evolutionary system. The two complement each other; neither can be dispensed with. Without A Path for Everyone, More Work, More Reward would degenerate into the law of the jungle; without More Work, More Reward, A Path for Everyone would degenerate into egalitarianism.

## II. The Necessity of A Path for Everyone

The establishment of the A Path for Everyone guarantee system is based on the following profound understandings:

### 2.1 The Right to Evolution Is the Most Basic Right of Life

In cultivation civilizations, the right to evolution is not only a development right but also a survival right. When facing various dangers in the universe (such as plane fluctuations, energy storms, alien invasions), lives with low realms have a far lower survival probability than lives with high realms. Therefore, ensuring that every life has an evolutionary path is essentially ensuring every life's basic right to survival.

### 2.2 Differences in Initial Endowments Should Not Determine the Fate of Evolution

Every life's initial endowment (racial talent, family background, birth plane, innate aptitude, etc.) has enormous differences. In the era of predationism, these differences were often magnified into insurmountable chasms — lives from good backgrounds monopolized high-quality resources, while lives from poor backgrounds could not even obtain basic cultivation methods. The symbiotic civilization holds that differences in initial endowments objectively exist but should not become the only factor determining the fate of evolution. The role of institutions is precisely to compensate for these initial differences, giving every life the opportunity to change its fate through acquired effort.

### 2.3 Abandoned Individuals Are the Potential Loss of Civilization

Every life possesses evolutionary potential; many simply lack suitable paths and resources. A life struggling at the bottom may display astonishing talent after obtaining a suitable cultivation system; an individual deemed a "failure" may simply not have found an evolutionary direction suited to itself. The significance of the A Path for Everyone guarantee system lies in not giving up on any potential evolutor, expanding the civilization's talent pool as much as possible.

### 2.4 Universal Evolution Is the Foundation of Cosmic Ascension

The overall ascension of the universe cannot rely only on a few supreme powerful beings, but requires the universal evolution of the myriad races and all beings. The efficiency of the anchor network is positively correlated with the number and quality of lives connected to it. When more lives access cultivation systems and elevate their realms, the energy circulation and law operation of the entire anchor network become more stable and efficient, and the process of cosmic ascension accelerates.

## III. The Hierarchical Structure of the Guarantee System

The A Path for Everyone guarantee system is divided into four levels, forming a complete guarantee chain from basic to advanced:

| Level | Name | Guarantee Content | Coverage |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| First Level | Basic Guarantee | Free cultivation methods, minimum resource allocation, basic medical and educational services | All lives, no threshold |
| Second Level | Targeted Support | Additional resources, mentors, and technical support for disadvantaged groups | Individuals and races with poor initial endowments |
| Third Level | Opportunity Expansion | Advanced cultivation opportunities, cross-plane exchange, participation rights in special projects | Cultivators with potential but insufficient resources |
| Fourth Level | Achievement Incentive | High-level resources, advanced anchor access, civilizational honors | Cultivators who make outstanding contributions |

The first level is "catching the bottom," ensuring no one is abandoned; the second level is "compensating differences," making up for differences in initial endowments; the third level is "cultivating excellence," giving potential individuals more opportunities; the fourth level is "incentive," rewarding outstanding contributors. The four levels progress step by step, forming a complete guarantee and incentive system.

## IV. Specific Contents of Basic Guarantee

Basic guarantee is the core of the A Path for Everyone system, specifically including the following contents:

### 4.1 Guarantee of Cultivation Methods

The basic methods of the Three Hundred Origin Cultivation Systems are open free of charge to all lives, without any threshold. Every life can freely choose a cultivation system suited to itself, without paying fees, joining specific sects, or passing qualification reviews. The anchor network provides system compatibility evaluation, helping cultivators find the path most suited to themselves.

### 4.2 Guarantee of Cultivation Resources

Through the Output-Linked Contract System and public resource allocation, every life is ensured to obtain the resources needed to maintain basic cultivation. Basic resource allocation includes: minimum energy supply, basic cultivation pills, and access permissions to general cultivation equipment. For planes lacking resources, cross-plane resource adjustment is conducted through the anchor network.

### 4.3 Guarantee of Cultivation Environment

The symbiotic civilization has built public cultivation venues on every planet and plane, including public cultivation rooms, energy concentration adjustment zones, law comprehension rooms, and more. These venues are open to all lives free or at low cost, ensuring that even the poorest cultivator has a suitable cultivation environment.

### 4.4 Guarantee of Mentor Guidance

Establish a cross-racial mentor network, where advanced cultivators have the obligation to provide basic guidance to junior cultivators. Every junior cultivator can apply for mentor matching through the anchor network and receive free basic Q&A and cultivation advice. For cultivators with special difficulties, one-on-one targeted guidance is provided.

### 4.5 Guarantee of Medical Rehabilitation

The symbiotic medical system provides cultivators with free basic medical care and rehabilitation services for cultivation deviations. Injuries during cultivation, cultivation deviation madness, energy disorders, and other situations can all receive timely treatment and rehabilitation support, ensuring cultivators do not lose their evolutionary ability due to accidents.

## V. Targets and Methods of Targeted Support

Targeted support addresses the following disadvantaged groups:

- **Low-endowment individuals**: individuals with poor innate aptitude and slow cultivation progress receive additional resource allocation and personalized guidance
- **Impoverished races**: races with overall resource scarcity and low evolutionary levels receive targeted support from the racial development fund
- **Post-disaster groups**: groups that have lost their homes and resources due to plane disasters, wars, etc., receive emergency relief and reconstruction support
- **Cultivators with disabilities**: cultivators with physical or mental disabilities due to various reasons receive special cultivation auxiliary equipment and customized cultivation plans
- **Anomalous lives**: anomalous lives that do not fit conventional cultivation systems receive specialized evaluation and customized evolutionary paths

The methods of targeted support include: additional resource allocation, one-on-one mentor guidance, customized cultivation plans, provision of special equipment, cross-plane exchange opportunities, and more. The goal of support is to help disadvantaged groups reach the basic evolutionary level, after which they gradually transition to the normal More Work, More Reward distribution system.

## VI. Sources of Funds and Resources for the Guarantee System

The operation of the A Path for Everyone guarantee system requires substantial resources and funds, whose sources include:

1. **Public resource income**: output of public spirit veins, energy mineral veins, medicine fields, and other resources directly managed by the symbiotic civilization
2. **Contribution adjustment tax**: a certain proportion of "contribution adjustment tax" levied on high contributors, used for the operation of the guarantee system (progressive tax rate, not exceeding 30%)
3. **Civilizational development fund**: surplus energy produced by the anchor network's operation, partly injected into the civilizational development fund
4. **Technology licensing income**: licensing fees for law engineering technologies owned by the symbiotic civilization
5. **Donations and bequests**: voluntary donations from individuals and races

The income and expenditure of the guarantee system are managed by the Guarantee Committee under the Assembly of the Republic of Myriad Races, with accounts publicly disclosed to all lives periodically, subject to supervision.

## Conclusion

The A Path for Everyone guarantee system is the cornerstone of social fairness in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. It ensures that in a civilization centered on cultivation and evolution, no life is abandoned by the evolutionary system because of birth, endowment, or poverty. A Path for Everyone is not about egalitarianism, but about giving every life a fair starting point, an upward channel, and a promise not to be given up. Under the guarantee of A Path for Everyone, every life can find its own evolutionary path.
