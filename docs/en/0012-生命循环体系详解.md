## I. System Overview

The Life Circulation System is System No. 2 among the Three Hundred Origin Cultivation Systems, ranking second among the "Three Foundations of Origin", and is the foundation of all cultivation systems centered on life energy. This system takes the "benign circulation of life energy" as its core principle, emphasizing that the energy exchange between the cultivator and the environment, and between the cultivator and other lives, must be circular and reciprocal, rather than predatory and one-way. The Life Circulation System not only elevates the individual's life force and realm, but also improves the ecological environment in which the cultivator lives, achieving the symbiotic effect of "cultivating one person, benefiting the whole region".

The Life Circulation System is suitable for lives of all races to practice, especially cultivators with sensitive constitutions, strong affinity with nature, and a focus on life quality and longevity. This system is one of the systems with the widest scope of application and the closest integration with daily life among the Three Hundred Systems, known as "the most down-to-earth Origin system".

## II. Core Principles of the System

The core principle of the Life Circulation System is "the conservation and amplification of life energy circulation", which can be understood from the following three levels:

### 2.1 Individual Internal Circulation

Individual internal circulation refers to the circulation of life energy within the cultivator's own body. Life energy circulates through the cultivator's meridians, organs, dantian, and consciousness along specific paths; each cycle purifies and strengthens the energy. The quality of individual internal circulation determines the cultivator's basic life force, recovery ability, and anti-depletion ability.

Unlike predatory cultivation, the internal circulation of the Life Circulation System does not pursue "the more energy the better", but "the smoother the circulation the better". The accumulation of energy must be built on unobstructed circulation; otherwise, excess energy may cause blockage and backlash.

### 2.2 External Circulation between the Individual and the Environment

External circulation refers to the life energy exchange between the cultivator and the surrounding environment. The cultivator absorbs life energy from the environment (such as heaven-earth spiritual qi, plant essence, mineral energy, etc.), while releasing life energy purified by themselves into the environment (such as life force fields, purifying energy, growth-promoting energy, etc.). This exchange is bidirectional and reciprocal: the cultivator obtains the energy needed for cultivation, and the environment obtains purification and nourishment.

The key to external circulation is "balanced income and expenditure with surplus". The energy absorbed by the cultivator must not exceed the environment's supply capacity, and the energy released must benefit the environment. When external circulation operates well, the environment around the cultivator becomes increasingly livable and ecologically prosperous, forming a benign cycle of "people nourish the land, the land nourishes people".

### 2.3 Symbiotic Circulation between Individuals

Symbiotic circulation refers to the reciprocal life energy exchange between the cultivator and other lives (including other cultivators, ordinary lives, animals and plants, etc.). The forms of symbiotic circulation include: energy transmission and feedback between master and disciple, life energy complementarity between partners, energy sharing between the cultivator and contracted lives, collective energy resonance among community cultivators, etc.

The core principle of symbiotic circulation is "mutual benefit, voluntary equality". Neither party may obtain energy at the cost of harming the other; the exchange must be voluntary and beneficial to both parties. Symbiotic circulation can produce a "1+1>2" synergistic effect, enabling all lives participating in the circulation to progress faster than practicing alone.

## III. Realm Division and Characteristics of Each Stage

The Life Circulation System is divided into five core realms:

| Realm | Name | Core Characteristics | Life Energy Level | Signature Ability |
|------|------|----------|--------------|------------|
| First Realm | Life Perception | Preliminarily perceive the existence and flow of life energy, able to perform basic internal circulation | Life force increased 10%-30% | Life Perception: can perceive the energy state of oneself and surrounding lives |
| Second Realm | Circulation | Internal circulation smooth and stable, begin establishing external circulation with the environment | Life force increased 30%-100% | Energy Breathing: can actively absorb life energy from and release it into the environment |
| Third Realm | Generation | External circulation operates benignly, ecological improvement begins to appear in the surrounding environment | Life force increased 100%-300% | Endless Generation: the life force field continuously nourishes surrounding lives, promoting plant growth and ecological restoration |
| Fourth Realm | Transformation | Life energy highly purified, able to perform fine adjustment and transformation of life forms | Life force increased 300%-1000% | Life Transformation: can accelerate wound healing, repair organ damage, and extend lifespan |
| Fifth Realm | Origin Birth | Touches the Origin laws of life, able to create new life forms and ecosystems | Life force increased 1000% and above | Origin Creation: can create micro-ecosystems in Isolation Space and design new life forms |

The breakthrough of each realm requires passing the "Life Circulation Assessment": assessing the quality of the cultivator's internal circulation, the ecological benefits of external circulation, and the reciprocity of symbiotic circulation; only when all three standards are met can the realm elevation be confirmed.

## IV. Core Cultivation Methods of the System

### 4.1 Internal Circulation Cultivation Methods

Internal circulation cultivation is the foundation of the Life Circulation System, including:

- **Meridian Unblocking Method**: through specific breathing and intention guidance, unblock the meridians throughout the body, ensuring the smoothness of life energy circulation channels
- **Organ Harmonization Method**: regulate the energy balance of the five viscera and six bowels, making the energy exchange among organs coordinated and orderly
- **Dantian Nurturing Method**: nurture the core "life seed" of life energy in the dantian; the stronger the life seed, the greater the circulation power of life energy
- **Heavenly Cycle Method**: guide life energy to circulate along the paths of the small heavenly cycle and great heavenly cycle; each cycle purifies the energy by one degree

### 4.2 External Circulation Cultivation Methods

External circulation cultivation is the distinctive feature of the Life Circulation System, including:

- **Spiritual Qi Breathing Method**: absorb heaven-earth spiritual qi from the environment, purify it in the body, and release it back into the environment, forming a purification cycle
- **Plant Symbiosis Method**: establish energy connections with plants, obtain essence from plants, and deliver growth-promoting energy to them
- **Earth Vein Communication Method**: establish connections with the planet's earth vein energy, participating in planet-level life energy circulation
- **Ecological Restoration Method**: inject one's own life force field into damaged ecological environments, accelerating ecological restoration and species recovery

### 4.3 Symbiotic Circulation Cultivation Methods

Symbiotic circulation cultivation is the advanced content of the Life Circulation System, including:

- **Master-Disciple Transmission Method**: the master transmits purified life energy and cultivation experience to the disciple; the disciple's cultivation progress feeds back to the master, forming a cycle of mutual improvement in teaching and learning
- **Partner Dual Practice Method**: partners, based on deep emotional connections, engage in complementary resonance of life energy, achieving joint elevation
- **Collective Resonance Method**: multiple cultivators practice life circulation simultaneously; individual energy circulations resonate with each other, producing a collective energy field
- **Contract Symbiosis Method**: establish equal symbiotic contracts with other lives (such as spirit beasts, elves, etc.), sharing life energy and cultivation results

## V. Ecological Benefits and Social Value of the System

The unique value of the Life Circulation System lies in that it not only elevates the realm of individual cultivators, but also produces significant ecological benefits and social value:

### 5.1 Ecological Benefits

- **Environmental purification**: the cultivator's life force field can purify harmful substances in the air, water, and soil
- **Ecological restoration**: advanced cultivators can accelerate the recovery of damaged ecosystems, promoting the reproduction of endangered species
- **Climate regulation**: large-scale life circulation cultivation fields can regulate local climate, increasing precipitation and regulating temperature
- **Soil improvement**: the soil around cultivators becomes more fertile, beneficial to agriculture and plant growth

### 5.2 Social Value

- **Medical value**: cultivators of the Life Circulation System are natural healers, providing basic medical services to the community
- **Agricultural value**: the cultivator's life force field can significantly improve crop yield and quality
- **Longevity value**: cultivators of this system generally live longer, accumulating more experience and wisdom for the civilization
- **Harmony value**: the cultivation method of symbiotic circulation promotes harmonious relationships between people, and between people and nature

## VI. Advantages and Limitations of the System

### 6.1 Advantages

- Wide scope of application; almost all races can practice it
- Gentle and safe cultivation process; the risk of cultivation deviation is extremely low
- Significant ecological benefits; highly valued by the civilization and communities
- Obvious lifespan extension effect; the first choice for cultivators pursuing longevity
- Close integration with daily life; no special cultivation environment required

### 6.2 Limitations

- Combat ability improves slowly in the early stage; not suitable for cultivators pursuing rapid combat power
- Certain dependence on the environment; the cultivation effect is limited in areas extremely deficient in life energy
- The breakthrough of high realms requires profound comprehension of the Origin of life, with high requirements for mental disposition
- Symbiotic circulation requires finding suitable partners; the efficiency of solo practice is limited

## VII. Compatibility with Other Systems

The Life Circulation System has good compatibility with other systems, especially:

- Concurrent practice with the Deduction and Iteration System: can deduce the optimal life energy circulation path, accelerating the purification of internal circulation
- Concurrent practice with the Space Law Dispatch System: can construct dedicated life energy circulation spaces, improving cultivation efficiency
- Concurrent practice with consciousness ascension systems: the purification of life energy contributes to the stability and ascension of consciousness
- Concurrent practice with application engineering systems: life circulation technology is the core foundation of ecological engineering and medical engineering

## Conclusion

The Life Circulation System is the most direct embodiment of the "symbiosis" philosophy of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization in the field of cultivation. With the benign circulation of life energy as its core, it closely integrates individual cultivation with ecological improvement and social harmony, proving that evolution need not come at the cost of sacrificing the environment or others. The ultimate goal of the Life Circulation System is to make every cultivator a guardian and nurturer of life — while elevating their own realm, making the surrounding world a better place.
