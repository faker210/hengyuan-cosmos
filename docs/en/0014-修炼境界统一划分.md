## I. The Necessity of Unified Realm Division

Before the establishment of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, there existed thousands of cultivation systems in Heng Yuan Zhou, each with its own independent realm division standards. This chaotic realm system led to a series of problems: the strength of cultivators from different systems was difficult to compare, cross-system communication and collaboration lacked a common language, resource allocation and social status assessment lacked a unified basis, and even the serious injustice of "same realm, different power" appeared.

After the establishment of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, to solve this problem, led by the "Cultivation Standards Committee" under the Republic of Myriad Races Parliament, together with representative cultivators of the Three Hundred Systems, this "Unified Cultivation Realm Division Standard" was formulated after long-term research and demonstration. This standard is not based on the realm names of any single system, but uses four dimensions as core indicators — the cultivator's depth of understanding of the Origin laws of the universe, precision of energy manipulation, degree of consciousness evolution, and contribution to the symbiotic network — establishing a cross-system universal realm division system.

The core principle of unified realm division is "value substance over name": regardless of which system a cultivator uses or what realm name they call it, as long as their substance reaches the corresponding level of the unified standard, they are recognized as being at that realm. This principle ensures the fairness and objectivity of realm assessment.

## II. Overview of the Nine Realms

The unified realm division standard divides the cultivator's evolutionary journey into nine great realms, from low to high:

| Realm No. | Realm Name | Core Characteristics | Rough Corresponding Realm in the Three Foundations of Origin | Social Role Positioning |
|----------|----------|----------|------------------------|--------------|

| Second Realm | Condense Origin Realm | Stably absorb and condense energy, establish basic internal circulation | Deduction / Circulation / Space Control | Junior Cultivator |
| Third Realm | Transform Origin Realm | Energy purification reaches a higher level, able to transform energy into various applications | Iteration / Generation / Space Transformation | Intermediate Cultivator |
| Fourth Realm | Law Origin Realm | Begin to understand and manipulate laws, initial fusion of energy and laws | Integration / Life Transformation / Space Law | Advanced Cultivator |
| Fifth Realm | Dao Origin Realm | Deep understanding of laws in a certain field, forming one's personal "Dao" | Origin / Origin Birth / Space Origin | Master-Level Cultivator |

Among the nine realms, the First to Fifth Realms are the "Five Mortal Cultivation Realms", attainable by most cultivators through effort; the Sixth to Ninth Realms are the "Four Sage Cultivation Realms", requiring extremely high talent, fortune, and effort — the breakthrough of each realm is a cosmic-level major event.

## III. Detailed Standards of Each Realm

### 3.1 First Realm: Sense Origin Realm

The Sense Origin Realm is the starting point of cultivation, with "perception" at its core. The standards for reaching the Sense Origin Realm include:

- Stably perceive the free energy in the universe (spiritual qi, law force, etc.)
- Absorb trace amounts of energy into the body through basic cultivation methods
- Establish preliminary energy perception consciousness, able to distinguish different types of energy
- Physical quality shows initial improvement due to energy infiltration

Cultivators in the Sense Origin Realm are "beginners" in society, with the main task of laying a solid foundation and finding a cultivation system suitable for themselves. The breakthrough of the Sense Origin Realm usually requires several weeks to several months of formal cultivation.

### 3.2 Second Realm: Condense Origin Realm

The core of the Condense Origin Realm is "condensation". The standards for reaching it include:

- Stably absorb cosmic energy and condense it into usable energy reserves in the body
- Establish a basic internal circulation system, energy can circulate along specific paths in the body
- Use condensed energy for basic offense, defense, or auxiliary applications
- Energy purity reaches the basic standard, without excessive impurities

Cultivators in the Condense Origin Realm already possess a basic cultivator identity and can participate in some low-level cultivation tasks and social services. The breakthrough usually requires several months to several years of cultivation.

### 3.3 Third Realm: Transform Origin Realm

The core of the Transform Origin Realm is "transformation". The standards for reaching it include:

- Energy purification reaches a higher level, able to transform one type of energy into another
- Perform preliminary interaction between energy and matter (such as energy attachment to objects, energy shaping, etc.)
- Internal circulation stable and efficient, energy recovery speed greatly improved
- Begin to perceive the existence and operation of laws

Cultivators in the Transform Origin Realm are the backbone of society, able to serve as intermediate mentors, technical personnel, grassroots managers, etc. The breakthrough usually requires several years to over a decade of cultivation.

### 3.4 Fourth Realm: Law Origin Realm

The core of the Law Origin Realm is "law". The standards for reaching it include:

- Understand and preliminarily manipulate the basic laws of a certain field
- Initial fusion of energy and laws, producing "law force" — a higher-order energy form
- Perform spells and skills based on laws
- Consciousness intensity greatly improved, able to engage in deep deduction and meditation for extended periods

Cultivators in the Law Origin Realm are advanced talents in society, able to serve as senior mentors, technical experts, middle managers, etc. The breakthrough usually requires over a decade to several decades of cultivation, and is the first major bottleneck in many cultivators' lives.

### 3.5 Fifth Realm: Dao Origin Realm

The core of the Dao Origin Realm is "forming the Dao". The standards for reaching it include:

- Systematic and profound understanding of laws in a certain field, forming a personal unique "Dao"
- Personal Dao resonates with cosmic laws, able to mobilize law force over a larger range
- Deep fusion of energy and laws, every move contains law force
- Consciousness evolves to a higher level, able to perform cross-plane consciousness perception

Cultivators in the Dao Origin Realm are the "masters" of the civilization, authoritative in their respective fields, able to serve as civilization-level advisors, grand mentors, senior managers, etc. The breakthrough requires extremely high talent and comprehension; many cultivators cannot reach it in their lifetime.

### 3.6 Sixth Realm: Dao Integration Realm

The core of the Dao Integration Realm is "Dao integration". The standards for reaching it include:

- Personal Dao deeply fuses with the Origin laws of the universe, reaching the state of "unity of heaven and man"
- Rewrite the operation of local laws over a large range
- Life form begins to evolve toward higher dimensions, partially free from the limitations of the material body
- Create a stable personal domain (law domain)

Cultivators in the Dao Integration Realm are the "grandmasters" of the civilization, top figures in various fields, with significant influence on the direction of the civilization's development. The breakthrough is a cosmic-level event, requiring the coordination of favorable timing, geography, and harmony among people.

### 3.7 Seventh Realm: Realm Breaking Realm

The core of the Realm Breaking Realm is "breaking the boundaries". The standards for reaching it include:

- Break through the restrictions of plane laws, perform cross-plane traversal without anchor support
- Operate on the plane membrane, such as opening plane channels, repairing plane cracks
- Personal domain can expand into a small independent space
- Life form further high-dimensionalized, able to survive in the law environments of different planes

Experts of the Realm Breaking Realm are "plane-level" existences, able to independently undertake plane-level tasks and responsibilities. The breakthrough is extremely difficult; the number of cultivators reaching this realm in all of Heng Yuan Zhou is limited.

### 3.8 Eighth Realm: World Creation Realm

The core of the World Creation Realm is "world creation". The standards for reaching it include:

- Create complete worlds in Isolation Space, including matter, energy, laws, and life
- Design and formulate the foundational law systems of new worlds
- Personal domain can expand into a complete world
- Life form approaches high-dimensional existence, able to manifest in multiple planes simultaneously

Experts of the World Creation Realm are "world-level" existences, whose created worlds can serve as cultivation resources, experiment sites, civilization refuges, etc. The breakthrough is one of the rarest events in the universe.

### 3.9 Ninth Realm: Origin Realm

The core of the Origin Realm is "Origin". The standards for reaching it include:

- Touch the Origin laws of the universe, able to understand the ultimate operating laws of the universe
- Participate in the fine-tuning and optimization of cosmic laws, influencing the evolutionary direction of the universe
- Life form deeply connects with the Origin of the universe, nearly immortal
- Mobilize the power of the Origin on a cosmic scale

Experts of the Origin Realm are "cosmic-level" existences, the most supreme force in Heng Yuan Zhou. Cultivators reaching this realm are usually called "plane supreme experts", the core target group of the "20% Activation Strategy" of the symbiotic civilization.

## IV. Realm Certification Mechanism

To ensure the fairness and accuracy of realm assessment, the symbiotic civilization has established a strict realm certification mechanism:

1. **Anchor automatic monitoring**: after a cultivator connects to the anchor network, the anchor continuously monitors indicators such as energy level, law understanding, and consciousness intensity; when indicators reach the standard of a new realm, it automatically prompts
2. **Standards Committee assessment**: for realm breakthroughs at the Fourth Realm and above, formal assessment needs to be organized by the Cultivation Standards Committee, covering three aspects: theoretical test, practical combat test, and contribution evaluation
3. **Public announcement and objection period**: after passing the assessment, a thirty-day public announcement period follows; during this period any life may raise objections, and if an objection is upheld, re-assessment is required
4. **Realm certificate issuance**: after the announcement period passes without objections, the anchor network issues a unified realm certificate, and the certificate information is entered into the civilization database

## V. Realms and Corresponding Social Rights and Obligations

Cultivators of different realms enjoy different rights and bear different obligations in the symbiotic civilization:

| Realm | Core Rights | Core Obligations |
|------|----------|----------|
| Sense Origin – Condense Origin Realms | Free access to basic cultivation resources, right to use public cultivation venues | Abide by cultivation ethics, participate in basic community services |
| Transform Origin – Law Origin Realms | Right to advanced resource allocation, right to apply for mentor qualification, right to cross-regional mobility | Guide junior cultivators, participate in technological R&D or social services |
| Dao Origin – Dao Integration Realms | Right to high-level resource allocation, right to high-level anchor access, right to participate in civilization decision-making | Undertake civilization-level research projects, maintain regional anchor stability |
| Realm Breaking – World Creation Realms | Right to plane-level resource allocation, right to create independent spaces, right to participate in plane affairs | Undertake plane-level tasks, maintain plane membrane stability, participate in cross-plane affairs |
| Origin Realm | Right to cosmic-level resource allocation, right to participate in law fine-tuning, right to participate in the civilization's highest decision-making | Maintain the overall stability of the universe, guide the direction of civilization evolution, cultivate reserve talents |

## Conclusion

The unified cultivation realm division standard is one of the infrastructure elements of the cultivation system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. It provides a common "measure" for the Three Hundred Cultivation Systems, making cross-system communication, collaboration, and competition possible, and providing an objective basis for resource allocation, social governance, and talent cultivation. The division of the nine realms is not to create hierarchical differences, but to clearly mark the evolutionary stage of each cultivator, providing them with the most suitable support and guidance. The elevation of realms is not an endpoint, but a new starting point toward higher-level symbiotic evolution.
