## I. Origin and Positioning of the Multiplying Fruit

The Multiplying Fruit originally came from the Devil Fruit setting in the fictional work *One Piece*, whose core ability is to amplify a specific attribute of the user (such as strength, speed, cultivation efficiency, etc.) by multiples. In the worldview of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, the Multiplying Fruit is not a plant fruit that truly exists, but a "conceptual prototype" — researchers of the symbiotic civilization drew inspiration from this fictional concept and, based on the principle of "reality as foundation, laws upgraded", re-expressed its core mechanism in the language of realistic energetics and law studies, ultimately developing the engineering-realizable "Multiplying Fruit Technology".

Multiplying Fruit Technology is the "opening buff" of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization and one of the most important technological advantages in the early stage of the civilization's establishment. Its core function is to provide cultivators with a "hundredfold cultivation deduction amplification", elevating key cultivation indicators such as the cultivator's deduction ability, energy purification efficiency, and law comprehension speed to a hundred times the conventional level. The popularization of this technology made the overall cultivation efficiency of the symbiotic civilization far surpass that of the predationism era, providing key technological support for the implementation of the "20% Activation Strategy".

It must be specially emphasized that Multiplying Fruit Technology is an "efficiency amplification technology" rather than an "energy creation technology". It cannot violate the Law of Conservation of Energy to create energy or power out of nothing, but achieves an equivalent hundredfold amplification by optimizing the energy transformation, law resonance, consciousness computation, and other links in the cultivation process, reducing losses and improving efficiency.

## II. Core Principles of Multiplying Fruit Technology

### 2.1 Principle of Law Resonance Amplification

The core principle of Multiplying Fruit Technology is "law resonance amplification". During operation, the Origin laws of the universe produce "law fluctuations" of specific frequencies; the cultivation activity of a cultivator is essentially a process of resonating with these law fluctuations. In conventional cultivation, the resonance efficiency between the cultivator and law fluctuations is low, and most law energy is lost during the resonance process.

Multiplying Fruit Technology, through a dedicated "law resonance device" (which can be hardware equipment or an energy structure constructed within the cultivator's body), precisely tunes the cultivator's cultivation frequency to exactly match the frequency of the Origin law fluctuations, achieving "perfect resonance". In the state of perfect resonance, the utilization efficiency of law energy rises from the conventional 1%-5% to nearly 100%, equivalent to elevating cultivation efficiency by 20-100 times.

The key to law resonance amplification lies in "precise tuning". Different cultivation systems, different cultivators, and different cultivation stages each have different optimal resonance frequencies. Multiplying Fruit Technology, through real-time monitoring and feedback from the anchor network, automatically tunes each cultivator to the optimal frequency, ensuring the maximization of resonance efficiency.

### 2.2 Principle of Consciousness Acceleration

Besides law resonance amplification, another core principle of Multiplying Fruit Technology is "consciousness acceleration". Activities such as deduction, comprehension, and understanding in cultivation mainly occur in the consciousness domain; the speed of consciousness computation directly determines cultivation efficiency.

Multiplying Fruit Technology, through the "consciousness frequency elevation" technology, elevates the computation frequency of the cultivator's consciousness domain a hundredfold. In the state of consciousness acceleration, the cultivator performs a hundred seconds of deduction in consciousness while only one second passes in reality. This consciousness acceleration does not consume additional life energy, but is achieved by elevating the energy supply efficiency of the consciousness domain a hundredfold — the energy support received by the consciousness domain per unit time increases a hundredfold, so the computation speed correspondingly rises.

The realization of consciousness acceleration requires the cultivator to reach a certain foundation of consciousness intensity. Cultivators below the Sense Origin Realm have insufficient consciousness intensity and cannot bear the load of hundredfold consciousness acceleration; therefore, the amplification of Multiplying Fruit Technology for junior cultivators is appropriately reduced (usually 10-30 times), gradually increasing to a hundredfold as the cultivator's realm rises.

### 2.3 Principle of Energy Purification Enhancement

During cultivation, energy absorbed from the environment needs purification before it can be efficiently utilized by the cultivator. Conventional energy purification efficiency is low, and a large amount of impure energy is wasted. Multiplying Fruit Technology, through the "multi-stage resonance purification" technology, elevates energy purification efficiency a hundredfold.

The principle of multi-stage resonance purification is: the absorbed energy passes sequentially through multiple resonance filters of different frequencies, each filter removing one specific type of impurity, ultimately obtaining high-purity usable energy. Conventional purification usually has only 1-2 stages of filtration, while Multiplying Fruit Technology can achieve fine filtration of over 100 stages, bringing the purity of purified energy close to the level of law force.

## III. Implementation Methods of Multiplying Fruit Technology

Multiplying Fruit Technology has three main implementation methods, suitable for different scenarios and cultivators:

| Implementation Method | Technological Form | Applicable Scenario | Amplification Intensity | Cost |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|------|
| Hardware Amplifier | Law resonance device (wearable equipment or fixed equipment in cultivation venues) | Public cultivation venues, home cultivation, junior cultivators | 10-50 times | Lower, mass-producible |
| Energy Structure Implantation | Constructing permanent multiplying energy structures within the cultivator's body | Intermediate and above cultivators, long-term use | 50-100 times | Medium, requires professional cultivators to assist in construction |
| Law Domain Fusion | Fusing multiplying laws into the cultivator's personal law domain | Advanced cultivators, above the Dao Origin Realm | 100 times and above, customizable amplification direction | Higher, requires the cultivator to reach a high realm |

The three implementation methods can be used in superposition. For example, an advanced cultivator can simultaneously use a hardware amplifier, internal energy structures, and law domain fusion, obtaining a comprehensive amplification far exceeding a hundredfold. However, superposition use requires attention to the "amplification overload" problem — amplification intensity exceeding the cultivator's endurance will lead to adverse reactions such as energy disorder and consciousness overload.

## IV. Application Fields of Multiplying Fruit Technology

### 4.1 Cultivation Amplification

This is the most core application field of Multiplying Fruit Technology, including:

- **Deduction amplification**: elevating the cultivator's deduction ability a hundredfold, the core technological support of the Deduction and Iteration System
- **Purification amplification**: elevating energy purification efficiency a hundredfold, accelerating the cultivator's energy accumulation
- **Comprehension amplification**: elevating the speed of law understanding and comprehension a hundredfold, helping cultivators break through realm bottlenecks faster
- **Recovery amplification**: elevating the energy recovery and physical recovery speed after cultivation a hundredfold, reducing cultivation intervals

### 4.2 Technology R&D Amplification

Multiplying Fruit Technology can be applied to the R&D process of law engineering, elevating R&D efficiency a hundredfold:

- **Experiment acceleration**: using multiplying technology in Isolation Space to accelerate the experiment process, shortening the R&D cycle
- **Simulation amplification**: elevating the precision and speed of technological simulation, reducing the number of physical experiments
- **Design optimization**: rapidly optimizing technical design schemes through hundredfold deduction

### 4.3 Medical Rehabilitation Amplification

In the medical field, Multiplying Fruit Technology can:

- **Treatment acceleration**: elevating the effect and speed of healing techniques a hundredfold, accelerating wound healing and disease recovery
- **Diagnosis amplification**: elevating the precision and speed of medical diagnosis, achieving precise detection of early-stage diseases
- **Rehabilitation training amplification**: accelerating the rehabilitation training process after injury or illness, helping patients recover cultivation ability faster

### 4.4 Education Amplification

In the education field, Multiplying Fruit Technology can:

- **Learning acceleration**: elevating the speed of knowledge learning and skill training a hundredfold, shortening the education cycle
- **Comprehension amplification**: elevating students' comprehension speed and depth of complex knowledge
- **Memory consolidation**: enhancing the memory consolidation effect of learning content, reducing forgetting

## V. Safety Boundaries and Usage Norms of Multiplying Fruit Technology

Although Multiplying Fruit Technology is powerful, it is not without limits. The symbiotic civilization has established strict safety boundaries and usage norms:

### 5.1 Safety Boundaries

1. **Amplification ceiling**: the comprehensive amplification of a single cultivator must not exceed the ceiling of what their realm can endure; exceeding the ceiling will cause "amplification overload"
2. **Usage duration**: the duration of continuous high-multiple amplification must not exceed the cultivator's consciousness and energy endurance limits; regular rest is required
3. **Scope of application**: multiplying technology is mainly applicable to constructive fields such as cultivation, R&D, medicine, and education; its use for purely destructive purposes is prohibited
4. **Age restriction**: underage cultivators using multiplying technology need guardian consent and professional assessment, with appropriately reduced amplification intensity

### 5.2 Usage Norms

1. **Real-name registration**: all users of Multiplying Fruit Technology need to register with their real names in the anchor network, facilitating monitoring and management
2. **Regular examination**: long-term users need regular health examinations of body and consciousness, promptly detecting and handling side effects
3. **Prohibition of abuse**: the use of multiplying technology for exam cheating, unfair competition, illegal profit-making, and other purposes is prohibited
4. **Technology openness**: the basic version of Multiplying Fruit Technology is freely open to all lives; advanced versions are allocated through the More Work, More Reward mechanism

## VI. The Relationship between Multiplying Fruit Technology and the "Reality as Foundation" Principle

Multiplying Fruit Technology is a typical case of the "reality as foundation, laws upgraded" principle, and its development process perfectly embodies this principle:

1. **Inspiration from fiction**: the concept of the Multiplying Fruit comes from the fictional work *One Piece*, embodying "fiction as a source of inspiration"
2. **Mechanism reconstructed with real laws**: researchers did not directly copy the fictional setting, but reconstructed the multiplication mechanism with realistic energetics, law studies, and consciousness studies laws, embodying "fiction's insufficiency supplemented by reality"
3. **Technology verified by practice**: from theory to experiment to engineering, every stage of Multiplying Fruit Technology underwent strict practical verification, embodying "reality as the standard of verification"
4. **Effect conforms to objective laws**: the amplification of multiplying technology is "efficiency amplification" rather than "energy creation", strictly following objective laws such as the conservation of energy, embodying "laws cannot be violated"

## VII. Future Development Directions of Multiplying Fruit Technology

Researchers of the symbiotic civilization are exploring the following development directions of Multiplying Fruit Technology:

- **Personalized amplification**: customizing personalized amplification schemes according to each cultivator's traits and needs, achieving "a thousandfold for a thousand people" precise amplification
- **Targeted amplification**: concentrating amplification on specific cultivation directions (such as only amplifying deduction ability, only amplifying life energy, etc.), improving the pertinence of amplification
- **Group amplification**: developing multiplying technology for groups, enabling an entire cultivation team to receive amplification simultaneously, magnifying the synergistic effect
- **Permanent amplification**: exploring methods to permanently solidify the multiplying effect into the cultivator's Origin, so that cultivators can maintain high-multiple cultivation efficiency even without using amplification devices
- **Cross-plane amplification**: extending multiplying technology to cross-plane scenarios, enabling plane traversal and cross-plane cultivation to also receive amplification support

## Conclusion

The technological principles of the Multiplying Fruit are one of the most representative technological innovations of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. Starting from a fictional concept, through the reconstruction of real laws and the verification of practice, it ultimately became the core technology supporting the leap in cultivation efficiency of the entire civilization. Multiplying Fruit Technology proves the powerful force of the principle that "fiction's insufficiency is supplemented by reality, and what reality lacks is created through the upgrade of laws" — imagination can provide direction for technological innovation, while objective laws and engineering practice can turn imagination into reality. As the "opening buff" of the symbiotic civilization, Multiplying Fruit Technology will continue to provide powerful efficiency support for the symbiotic evolution of the Myriad Races.
