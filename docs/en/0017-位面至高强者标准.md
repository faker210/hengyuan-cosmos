## I. Definition and Positioning of Plane Supreme Experts

"Plane Supreme Expert" is the formal title given by the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization to cultivators who have reached the cosmic-level power tier, referring to the top cultivators whose strength is sufficient to independently support the law stability of a plane and who can exert influence on a cosmic scale. In the unified realm division standard, Plane Supreme Experts correspond to the Ninth Realm "Origin Realm" and above, the most supreme power group in Heng Yuan Zhou.

Being a Plane Supreme Expert is not merely a power-level title, but also a positioning of responsibility and identity. Under the framework of the symbiotic civilization, the greater the power, the greater the responsibility. Plane Supreme Experts are not only successful individuals in cultivation, but also guardians of the civilization, maintainers of laws, and guides for those who come after. The core goal of the "20% Activation Strategy" of the symbiotic civilization is to activate, in the early stage of the civilization's establishment, 20% of all living beings to reach the level of Plane Supreme Expert, laying the power foundation for the overall dimensional ascension of the universe.

It should be specially noted that the "Plane Supreme Expert" of the symbiotic civilization is essentially different from the "Plane Overlord" of the predationism era. The Plane Overlords of the predationism era enslaved planes with power and plundered resources, being the rulers and plunderers of planes; while the Plane Supreme Experts of the symbiotic civilization guard planes with power and serve all living beings, being the guardians and servants of planes. This difference is a direct manifestation of the core anti-predation philosophy of the symbiotic civilization at the level of the strong.

## II. Assessment Standards for Plane Supreme Experts

The assessment of Plane Supreme Experts adopts the "Four-Dimensional Assessment System"; only when all four dimensions meet the standards can one be formally recognized as a Plane Supreme Expert:

| Dimension | Assessment Content | Passing Standard | Weight |
|------|----------|----------|------|
| Power Dimension | Realm, energy level, law manipulation ability | Reach the Ninth Realm of the unified standard (Origin Realm), able to independently mobilize the power of Origin | 40% |
| Character Dimension | Mental maturity, ethical adherence, anti-predation stance | Pass the ethical review of the Anchor Court, confirming adherence to anti-predation principles and symbiotic philosophy | 25% |
| Contribution Dimension | Contribution to the symbiotic network, civilization development, and the common progress of the Myriad Races | Contribution value reaches the top 20% level of the civilization, with at least one civilization-level contribution record | 20% |
| Responsibility Dimension | Willingness and ability to undertake public responsibilities | Willing and able to undertake public responsibilities above the plane level (such as anchor maintenance, civilization guardianship, etc.) | 15% |

The design of the Four-Dimensional Assessment System embodies the expert assessment philosophy of the symbiotic civilization of "having both virtue and talent, with virtue first". Power is only the foundation; character, contribution, and responsibility are equally indispensable standards. A cultivator who is powerful but evil in character and refuses to take responsibility, even if reaching the power level of the Origin Realm, will not be recognized as a "Plane Supreme Expert", but will be classified as a "high-risk individual" and subject to corresponding supervision and restrictions.

## III. Detailed Standards of the Power Dimension

The power dimension is the foundation of Plane Supreme Expert assessment; its detailed standards include:

### 3.1 Realm Standards

- Must reach the Ninth Realm "Origin Realm" of the unified standard, touching the Origin laws of the universe
- Able to stably mobilize the power of Origin, rather than occasional explosive mobilization
- Personal Dao deeply fuses with the Origin laws of the universe, reaching the state of "Dao is Origin, Origin is Dao"

### 3.2 Energy Standards

- The Origin energy stored in the body reaches the "plane level" — that is, the total energy is sufficient to support the basic law operation of a medium-sized plane
- Energy purity reaches over 99%, almost free of impurities
- Energy recovery speed reaches "instant recovery" — even after massive consumption in battle, one can recover to full state in an extremely short time

### 3.3 Law Manipulation Standards

- Able to mobilize and manipulate the power of laws on a cosmic scale
- Able to participate in the fine-tuning and optimization of cosmic laws, exerting substantive influence on the operation of laws
- Able to independently perform cross-plane traversal and operations without anchor support
- Able to create complete worlds and law systems in Isolation Space (World Creation Realm ability)

### 3.4 Life Form Standards

- The life form completes high-dimensional evolution, able to exist and operate simultaneously in the material plane, energy layer, and consciousness domain
- Near immortality — conventional attacks and damage cannot pose a fatal threat; only power of the same or higher level can truly harm it
- Able to anchor one's life imprint in the Origin of the universe, able to be reborn through Origin anchoring even if the physical body is destroyed

## IV. Detailed Standards of the Character Dimension

The character dimension is the most distinctive part of the symbiotic civilization's expert assessment, and the most fundamental difference from the predationism era:

### 4.1 Anti-Predation Stance

- Fundamentally endorse and practice anti-predationism, with no record of predatory cultivation ever
- Even in extreme predicaments, never choose self-rescue at the cost of preying on others
- Actively resist and oppose any form of predatory behavior, daring to say no to predatory forces

### 4.2 Symbiotic Philosophy

- Deeply understand and endorse the core philosophy of the civilization of "Myriad Races symbiosis, cosmic dimensional ascension"
- Always prioritize symbiotic interests in personal cultivation and public affairs
- Actively participate in the construction and maintenance of the symbiotic network, willing to share personal cultivation results and knowledge with all living beings

### 4.3 Mental Maturity

- Emotionally stable, will not harm the civilization and all living beings due to personal emotions
- Possess a grand cosmic view and long-term historical vision, will not sacrifice long-term development for short-term interests
- Able to withstand enormous pressure and setbacks, remaining rational and resolute in predicaments

### 4.4 Ethical Review

The assessment of the character dimension is executed by the "Ethical Review Committee" under the Anchor Court; the review methods include:

- Historical behavior review: review all important behavior records of the cultivator throughout history
- Deep consciousness scan: with the cultivator's voluntary consent, perform a deep scan at the consciousness level, confirming their true inner thoughts
- Situational stress test: set extreme moral dilemmas in simulated environments, observing the cultivator's choices
- Long-term observation: for controversial candidates, conduct observation and evaluation over a period of several years

## V. Detailed Standards of the Contribution Dimension

The contribution dimension measures the cultivator's actual contribution to the symbiotic civilization and the common progress of the Myriad Races:

### 5.1 Contribution Types

- **Cultivation contribution**: exemplary effects of realm breakthroughs, creation of new techniques, innovation of cultivation theories
- **Knowledge contribution**: public sharing of cultivation insights, education and training work, academic research results
- **Technological contribution**: innovation of law engineering technology, optimization of Multiplying Fruit technology, improvement of anchor hardware
- **Service contribution**: anchor maintenance, civilization governance, mentor guidance, cross-race coordination
- **Ecological contribution**: planetary ecological restoration, improvement of plane energy circulation, cosmic environmental protection
- **Guardian contribution**: resisting external threats, protecting civilization security, rescuing lives in disasters

### 5.2 Contribution Quantification

Contribution values are automatically recorded and calculated by the anchor network; different types of contributions are given different weights according to their influence and value. The contribution standard for Plane Supreme Experts is "contribution value reaching the top 20% level of the civilization", and there must be at least one "civilization-level contribution" — a contribution that has produced major and far-reaching influence on the development of the entire civilization.

## VI. Detailed Standards of the Responsibility Dimension

The responsibility dimension measures the cultivator's willingness and ability to undertake public responsibilities:

### 6.1 Responsibility Types

Public responsibilities that Plane Supreme Experts need to undertake include:

- **Anchor guardianship**: responsible for the guardianship and maintenance of one or more advanced anchors
- **Civilization advisor**: serve as the highest-level advisor of the civilization, participating in decisions on the direction of civilization development
- **Mentor inheritance**: cultivate and guide the next generation of cultivators, especially seed candidates with potential to reach the supreme realms
- **Plane patrol**: regularly patrol the planes, monitoring plane stability and civilization development
- **Crisis response**: undertake the most core response tasks when the civilization faces major crises (such as plane collapse, external invasion, etc.)
- **Law participation**: participate in the fine-tuning and optimization of cosmic laws, contributing to the overall dimensional ascension of the universe

### 6.2 Ways of Undertaking Responsibilities

Plane Supreme Experts can choose the responsibility types to focus on according to their interests and strengths, but must not completely refuse to undertake public responsibilities. The symbiotic civilization implements the "obligation system for the strong" — cultivators who reach the Plane Supreme Expert level have the obligation to use part of their power for public affairs. The specific obligation is to invest at least 30% of their time and energy in public responsibilities each year.

## VII. The 20% Activation Strategy

The "20% Activation Strategy" is one of the core strategies of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization in its early stage; its goal is to activate, within the first era after the establishment of the civilization, 20% of all living beings to reach the level of Plane Supreme Expert.

### 7.1 Strategic Goals

- Short-term goal (within 100 years of the civilization's establishment): activate 1% of all living beings to reach the Origin Realm
- Medium-term goal (within 500 years of the civilization's establishment): activate 10% of all living beings to reach the Origin Realm
- Long-term goal (within 1000 years of the civilization's establishment): activate 20% of all living beings to reach the Origin Realm

### 7.2 Strategic Support

The implementation of the 20% Activation Strategy relies on the following supporting conditions:

- **Opening of the Three Hundred Systems**: providing rich cultivation path choices for all living beings
- **Popularization of Multiplying Fruit Technology**: the hundredfold cultivation deduction amplification greatly improves cultivation efficiency
- **Output-Linked Contract System**: providing stable resource support for cultivators
- **A Path for Everyone security system**: ensuring every life has basic cultivation conditions
- **Mentor network**: advanced cultivators guide junior cultivators, accelerating talent cultivation

### 7.3 Strategic Significance

The significance of the 20% Activation Strategy lies not only in cultivating a large number of experts, but also in:

- Providing sufficient power foundation for the overall dimensional ascension of the universe
- Establishing a huge group of experts, preventing the power monopoly of individual experts
- Forming a civilization atmosphere of "experts emerging in succession", inspiring more lives to cultivate diligently
- Providing a broad expert foundation for the Republic of Myriad Races, ensuring that every race has its own supreme expert representatives

## VIII. Rights and Treatment of Plane Supreme Experts

As a return for their responsibilities, Plane Supreme Experts enjoy the following rights and treatment:

- **Resource priority**: enjoy the highest priority in the allocation of cultivation resources
- **Law participation right**: have the right to participate in decisions on the fine-tuning and optimization of cosmic laws
- **Space creation right**: have the right to create their own independent spaces or worlds
- **Civilization decision right**: have seats and voting rights in the highest decision-making body of the civilization
- **Cross-plane passage right**: enjoy free passage rights in all planes controlled by the symbiotic civilization
- **Honor and reverence**: receive the respect and reverence of the entire civilization, with their deeds recorded in the annals of the civilization

## Conclusion

The standards for Plane Supreme Experts are the redefinition by the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization of the concept of "the strong". In the symbiotic civilization, the strong are not plunderers, not overlords, not rulers high above, but guardians, servants, and guides. The power dimension is the foundation, the character dimension is the soul, the contribution dimension is the value, and the responsibility dimension is the commitment — only when the four dimensions are united is one a true "Plane Supreme Expert". The goal of the 20% Activation Strategy is not only to cultivate 20% of experts, but to cultivate 20% of guardians willing to take responsibility for the symbiosis of the Myriad Races and the dimensional ascension of the universe. When 20% of all living beings reach the level of Plane Supreme Expert and practice the symbiotic philosophy, the overall dimensional ascension of the universe will no longer be a distant dream.
