## I. Background and Significance of the Cultivation Resource Open Mechanism

In the age of predationism, cultivation resources were the most central object of contention. Resources such as spirit veins, medicine fields, energy mineral veins, natural treasures, and cultivation secret realms were monopolized by a few experts and sects, and the bottom masses could hardly obtain regular cultivation resources at all. This resource monopoly not only caused severe class solidification, but also led to countless wars and massacres caused by resource contention. Resource monopoly is the economic foundation of the predationist system — as long as resources are monopolized by a few, "A Path for Everyone" can only be an empty phrase.

After the establishment of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, "cultivation resource opening" was made one of the most core institutional reforms. The core goal of the Cultivation Resource Open Mechanism is: break resource monopoly, ensure that every life can obtain basic cultivation resources, and at the same time incentivize the efficient utilization and continuous creation of resources through the More Work, More Reward allocation mechanism. Resource opening is not "equal distribution of resources", but "fair access to resources" — on the premise of guaranteeing basic needs, the allocation of resources is linked to individual contribution, while any form of resource monopoly is strictly prohibited.

The significance of the Cultivation Resource Open Mechanism lies in: it uproots the soil of predationism from the economic foundation, provides material guarantees for the true popularization of the Three Hundred Cultivation Systems, provides resource support for the social goal of "A Path for Everyone", and creates a fair starting point for the common progress of the Myriad Races.

## II. Classification and Grading of Cultivation Resources

### 2.1 Resource Classification

Cultivation resources in Heng Yuan Zhou are divided into the following major categories according to nature and use:

| Resource Category | Specific Content | Main Uses |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Energy Resources | Spirit veins, energy mineral veins, spiritual qi crystals, Origin energy fragments, etc. | Provide energy input needed for cultivation |
| Medicine Resources | Spirit medicines, immortal herbs, alchemy materials, finished pills, etc. | Assist cultivation, treat injuries and illnesses, break through bottlenecks |
| Equipment Resources | Cultivation artifacts, law instruments, multiplication devices, space equipment, etc. | Improve cultivation efficiency, assist law manipulation |
| Space Resources | Cultivation secret realms, spiritual qi concentration zones, law comprehension rooms, Isolation Space, etc. | Provide special cultivation environments |
| Knowledge Resources | Cultivation techniques, cultivation insights, law research materials, technical documents, etc. | Provide theoretical and methodological guidance for cultivation |
| Biological Resources | Spirit beasts, spirit plants, contracted lives, symbionts, etc. | Provide cultivation assistance, symbiotic circulation, special abilities |
| Plane Resources | Undeveloped planes, plane channel usage rights, plane core fragments, etc. | Provide large-scale cultivation and development space |

### 2.2 Resource Grading

All cultivation resources are divided into five grades according to rarity and value:

| Grade | Name | Rarity | Allocation Method |
|------|------|--------|----------|
| First Grade | Ordinary Resources | Widely distributed, abundant reserves | Free opening or low-price allocation |
| Second Grade | Fine Resources | Fairly common, moderate reserves | Basic allocation + contribution exchange |
| Third Grade | Rare Resources | Fairly rare, limited reserves | Contribution exchange + application approval |
| Fourth Grade | Epic Resources | Very rare, extremely few reserves | Competitive allocation + priority for civilization-level projects |
| Fifth Grade | Legendary Resources | Extremely rare, nearly extinct | Special allocation by the civilization's highest decision-making body |

## III. Core Principles of Resource Opening

### 3.1 Basic Guarantee Principle

Every life has the right to obtain the First and Second Grade resources needed to maintain basic cultivation. Basic guarantee resources are allocated free of charge or at low prices through the "A Path for Everyone security system", without thresholds or discrimination. The standard of basic guarantee is periodically adjusted by the Republic of Myriad Races Parliament according to the civilization's resource conditions and development stage, ensuring that the guarantee level matches the civilization's carrying capacity.

### 3.2 More Work, More Reward Principle

Above the basic guarantee, the allocation of Third Grade and above resources is mainly based on the individual's contribution value to the symbiotic network. The greater the contribution, the more resources can be exchanged and the higher the grade. The More Work, More Reward principle incentivizes cultivators to diligently elevate their own realms and make greater contributions to the civilization, while also ensuring that resources flow to the individuals and groups who can utilize them most effectively.

### 3.3 Anti-Monopoly Principle

Any individual, organization, or race is strictly prohibited from monopolizing cultivation resources. Specific anti-monopoly measures include:

- The Third Grade and above resources held by a single individual or organization must not exceed 1% of the civilization's total reserves
- Hoarding is prohibited — resource holders must not maliciously hoard resources or inflate prices when resources are scarce
- Exclusive possession is prohibited — public resources (such as public spirit veins, public secret realms, etc.) must not be exclusively possessed by private parties
- Violations of the anti-monopoly law will be severely sanctioned by the Anchor Court, including resource confiscation, contribution value zeroing, restriction of cultivation permissions, etc.

### 3.4 Sustainable Utilization Principle

The development and utilization of all resources must follow the sustainability principle, and predatory exploitation like draining the pond to catch fish is prohibited. Specific requirements include:

- The resource development speed must not exceed the natural regeneration speed of the resource (for non-renewable resources, alternative plans must be formulated)
- Resource development must undergo environmental impact assessment to prevent irreversible damage to the ecological environment
- For endangered resources, mining bans or restrictions are implemented to protect the regeneration capacity of the resources
- Encouragement of artificially cultivating and synthesizing rare resources through technological means (such as law engineering, multiplication technology, etc.), reducing dependence on natural resources

### 3.5 Open and Transparent Principle

The resource allocation process must be open and transparent, subject to the supervision of all lives:

- The reserves, distribution, and development status of all public resources are regularly disclosed to society
- The standards, processes, and results of resource allocation are open and verifiable
- A resource allocation appeal mechanism is established; individuals who object to allocation results can apply for reconsideration
- The "Resource Fairness Committee" under the Anchor Court is established to specifically supervise the fairness and legality of resource allocation

## IV. Specific Mechanisms of Resource Opening

### 4.1 Output-Linked Contract Mechanism

The output-linked contract is the core institutional design of cultivation resource opening; its basic operation has been detailed in the "More Work, More Reward Distribution Principles" document. In the field of resource opening, specific applications of the output-linked contract include:

- **Spirit vein contract**: public spirit veins are contracted to cultivator groups or race communities; the contractors are responsible for the maintenance and sustainable development of the spirit veins, obtaining a certain proportion of the spirit vein output
- **Medicine field contract**: public medicine fields are contracted to cultivators with planting ability; the contractors are responsible for the planting and care of spirit medicines, with the harvest distributed proportionally
- **Secret realm management**: public cultivation secret realms are entrusted to professional teams for management; the managers are responsible for the maintenance and safety of the secret realms, and users pay reasonable usage fees
- **Resource exploration**: cultivators are encouraged to participate in resource exploration of undeveloped areas; explorers have priority contracting rights for newly discovered resources

### 4.2 Contribution Value Exchange Mechanism

The symbiotic civilization has established a unified "Contribution Value Exchange System"; cultivators can accumulate contribution values by contributing to the symbiotic network, and then exchange the contribution values for various cultivation resources:

- Ways to obtain contribution values include: cultivation progress, knowledge sharing, technological innovation, public service, ecological restoration, civilization guardianship, etc.
- The exchange system implements "clearly marked prices"; every resource has a clear contribution value price, and the price is dynamically adjusted according to the resource's scarcity and demand
- The exchange system supports the "advance" function — promising cultivators can advance a certain amount of contribution values for resource exchange, repaying in the future by making contributions
- The exchange system is connected to the anchor network in real time, ensuring the safety and reliability of contribution value recording and exchange

### 4.3 Public Resource Free Opening Mechanism

The following types of resources are freely open to all lives:

- **Knowledge resources**: basic techniques of the Three Hundred Cultivation Systems, public cultivation insights, basic law research materials, etc.
- **First Grade energy resources**: basic spiritual qi, ordinary energy crystals, and other widely distributed low-order energy resources
- **Public cultivation spaces**: public cultivation rooms, basic law comprehension rooms, ordinary Isolation Space, etc.
- **Basic medical resources**: basic healing techniques, ordinary wound-healing pills, basic rehabilitation services, etc.
- **Basic education resources**: basic cultivation courses, introductory mentor guidance, use of general cultivation equipment, etc.

### 4.4 Special Resource Targeted Allocation Mechanism

For Fourth and Fifth Grade rare resources, a "targeted allocation" mechanism is implemented:

- Rare resources are prioritized for civilization-level key projects (such as anchor construction, plane development, technological breakthroughs, etc.)
- For individuals with special needs (such as needing specific resources to break through key realms, needing rare medicines to treat special injuries and illnesses, etc.), targeted allocation can be obtained through application and approval
- Allocation plans for rare resources are formulated by the "Resource Allocation Committee" under the Republic of Myriad Races Parliament, and executed after approval by the Parliament
- The results of targeted allocation are disclosed to society and subject to supervision

## V. Resource Creation and Sustainable Development

Cultivation resource opening is not only "distributing the existing stock", but also "creating incremental volume". The symbiotic civilization vigorously encourages the artificial creation and sustainable development of resources:

### 5.1 Artificial Cultivation of Resources

- **Artificial planting of spirit medicines**: through law engineering technology, cultivate various spirit medicines in artificial environments, reducing dependence on wild spirit medicines
- **Artificial production of energy**: through technologies such as material energy transformation devices and law resonance generators, artificially produce spiritual qi and energy crystals
- **Artificial breeding of spirit beasts**: establish spirit beast breeding bases, artificially breed various spirit beasts, protecting wild spirit beast populations
- **Artificial construction of secret realms**: through space law technology, artificially construct cultivation secret realms, increasing the supply of cultivation spaces

### 5.2 Resource Recycling

- **Energy recovery**: establish energy recovery systems to recover and reuse energy lost during cultivation, energy dispersed in battle, etc.
- **Medicine residue treatment**: conduct secondary extraction and treatment of alchemy residue, improving the utilization rate of medicine resources
- **Equipment recycling and remanufacturing**: recycle and remanufacture scrapped cultivation equipment, reducing material waste
- **Space cyclic use**: clean and reuse used Isolation Space, temporary cultivation spaces, etc.

### 5.3 Resource Substitution Technology

- Encourage the development of substitutes for rare resources, achieving "replacing rare materials with common materials" through technological means
- For non-renewable legendary resources, formulate strict protection and use plans, while increasing R&D investment in substitution technology
- Establish a "Resource Substitution Technology R&D Fund" to fund related research projects

## VI. Supervision and Relief of Resource Opening

### 6.1 Supervision Mechanisms

- **Real-time monitoring by the anchor network**: the anchor network monitors the development, allocation, and use of all public resources in real time, providing timely warnings when anomalies are found
- **Resource Fairness Committee**: specifically supervises the fairness and legality of resource allocation, accepting reports and complaints
- **Social supervision**: all lives are encouraged to supervise and report improper conduct in resource development and allocation
- **Regular audit**: independent audits of resource management institutions are conducted annually, with audit results disclosed to society

### 6.2 Relief Mechanisms

- **Allocation appeal**: individuals who object to resource allocation results can apply to the Resource Fairness Committee for reconsideration
- **Resource dispute arbitration**: resource disputes are arbitrated by the Anchor Court, and the arbitration results have legal effect
- **Compensation for infringement**: individuals harmed by resource monopoly, resource plunder, and other conduct can file lawsuits with the Anchor Court to demand compensation
- **Emergency relief**: in special situations (such as disasters, wars, etc.), activate the emergency resource allocation mechanism to ensure the basic resource needs of the affected groups

## Conclusion

The Cultivation Resource Open Mechanism is the core of the economic system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. Through the five principles of "basic guarantee + More Work, More Reward + anti-monopoly + sustainability + openness and transparency", it constructs a fair, efficient, and sustainable resource allocation system. The ultimate goal of the resource open mechanism is not to let all lives obtain the same amount of resources, but to let every life have a fair opportunity to obtain resources, let resources flow to where they can create the most value, and let the civilization's total resources grow continuously under sustainable premises. When cultivation resources are no longer private property monopolized by a few, but public wealth shared by the Myriad Races, circularly amplified, the ideal of "A Path for Everyone" can truly take root, and the goal of the common progress of the Myriad Races can truly be realized.
