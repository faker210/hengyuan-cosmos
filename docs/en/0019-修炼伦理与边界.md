## I. Definition and Status of Cultivation Ethics

Cultivation ethics is the sum of moral norms and legal norms in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization that regulate the conduct of cultivators, delimit cultivation boundaries, and maintain cultivation order. In the age of predationism, cultivation was regarded as the "privilege of the strong", and cultivators could resort to any means to enhance their power — devouring others' Origins, enslaving others' souls, plundering others' resources, conducting inhumane cultivation experiments, and so on. This cultivation culture of "power above all, ethics as nothing" is the deepest spiritual root of the predationist system.

After the establishment of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, cultivation ethics construction was made one of the most core spiritual civilization constructions. The core proposition of cultivation ethics is: "The acquisition and use of power must be constrained by ethics." The symbiotic civilization holds that cultivation is not purely a personal matter, but a public matter involving others, involving civilization, involving the cosmos as a whole. While pursuing the enhancement of their own power, cultivators must respect the rights of others, maintain the order of civilization, and protect the ecology of the cosmos. Cultivation ethics is not a shackle on cultivation, but a guardian of cultivation — it ensures that cultivation always walks on the correct path of "symbiotic evolution" and never slides into the abyss of "predatory destruction".

Cultivation ethics holds a lofty status in the legal system of the symbiotic civilization. The Symbiotic Constitution explicitly stipulates the basic principles of cultivation ethics; the Anchor Court has a special "Ethics Tribunal" to hear cultivation ethics cases; the realm certification of all advanced cultivators must pass ethical review. Cultivation ethics is not an optional "soft constraint", but a mandatory "hard norm".

## II. Basic Principles of Cultivation Ethics

### 2.1 Principle of Life Dignity

Every life possesses inviolable dignity and value, and no cultivation conduct may come at the cost of damaging life dignity. Specifically:

- Prohibiting the use of other lives as "materials" or "tools" of cultivation
- Prohibiting inhumane cultivation experiments (such as vivisection, soul torture, etc.)
- Prohibiting the enslavement or control of the free will of other lives
- Respecting the right of every life to choose its own evolutionary path, and not forcing others to cultivate according to one's own way

### 2.2 Anti-Predation Principle

Cultivation must be built on the foundation of symbiotic circulation, and must not use the plunder of others as a means. Specifically:

- Prohibiting the devouring or plundering of the Origin, energy, or fortune of other lives
- Prohibiting the enhancement of one's own cultivation speed or realm by harming others
- Prohibiting the monopolization of cultivation resources for improper gains
- Prohibiting the use of "the strong eat the weak" as an excuse to justify predatory conduct

### 2.3 Informed Consent Principle

Cultivation conduct involving others (such as master-disciple inheritance, dual cultivation of partners, energy sharing, cultivation experiments, etc.) must be conducted on the premise that the other party is fully informed and voluntarily consents. Specifically:

- Fully explaining to the other party the content, risks, and benefits of the cultivation conduct
- Ensuring that the other party has complete independent judgment ability, free from coercion or inducement
- The other party has the right to withdraw at any time, and must not be forced to continue
- For minors or individuals with mental disabilities, their guardians exercise the consent right on their behalf on the basis of professional assessment

### 2.4 Principle of Minimal Harm

Cultivation conduct should avoid harming others and the environment as much as possible. When some impact is inevitable, the least harmful method should be chosen, and reasonable compensation should be made for the harm caused. Specifically:

- Cultivation experiments should be prioritized in Isolation Space to prevent risk diffusion
- Combat-type cultivation should be conducted in dedicated cultivation grounds as much as possible, avoiding damage to the public environment
- Energy pollution, spatial damage, etc. generated during cultivation should be cleaned up and repaired in a timely manner
- High-risk cultivation activities are prohibited in densely populated areas

### 2.5 Principle of Honesty and Non-Deception

Cultivators should remain honest in cultivation-related matters and must not engage in fraud. Specifically:

- Prohibiting the forgery of cultivation realms, contribution values, examination results, etc.
- Prohibiting fraud or plagiarism in cultivation insights and research results
- Prohibiting the provision of false information in resource applications and project declarations
- Prohibiting the use of false promises to defraud others of cultivation resources or trust

### 2.6 Principle of Responsibility

The greater the power, the greater the responsibility. Advanced cultivators should assume public responsibilities commensurate with their power. Specifically:

- Cultivators who reach a certain realm have the obligation to guide junior cultivators
- Advanced cultivators have the obligation to participate in public affairs such as anchor maintenance and civilization guardianship
- Cultivators bear responsibility for the consequences of their own cultivation conduct, and must not shirk it
- When civilization faces crisis, advanced cultivators have the obligation to step forward

## III. Absolute Prohibitions of Cultivation

The following conduct constitutes the "absolute prohibitions" of cultivation; no cultivator may touch them under any circumstances, and violators will face the severest sanctions:

| Prohibition Type | Specific Conduct | Sanctions |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Origin Plunder | Devouring or plundering the Origin or life core of other lives | Abolition of cultivation, permanent imprisonment, Origin annihilation for serious cases |
| Soul Enslavement | Turning other lives into tools through soul control, spiritual enslavement, etc. | Abolition of cultivation, mandatory ethical reformation, permanent deprivation of cultivation rights |
| Race Genocide | Systematic slaughter or extermination of a race for cultivation purposes | Highest trial of the Anchor Court, capital punishment |
| Plane Destruction | Destroying an entire plane to obtain resources or power | Capital punishment, and bearing full responsibility for plane restoration |
| Inhumane Experiments | Conducting inhumane cultivation experiments on intelligent lives | Abolition of cultivation, permanent imprisonment, destruction of all research results |
| Time Crime | Using time regression ability for large-scale historical tampering or murder | Capital punishment, and permanent deprivation of time-related abilities |
| Law Pollution | Deliberately polluting the cosmic Origin laws, causing irreversible damage to the cosmos as a whole | Capital punishment, and law purification executed by the civilization's highest body |

The setting of absolute prohibitions embodies the symbiotic civilization's firm stance on the bottom line of cultivation ethics. These prohibitions have no "gray zone", no "excusable circumstances"; any conduct touching the absolute prohibitions will face the severest sanctions.

## IV. Gray Zones of Cultivation Ethics and Handling Principles

Besides the clear prohibitions, there are some "gray zones" in cultivation ethics — these conducts are not inherently absolutely good or evil; their ethical nature depends on the specific situation, motive, method, and consequences. For gray zones, the symbiotic civilization adopts the following handling principles:

### 4.1 Common Gray Zones

- **Harm in combat cultivation**: cultivators may harm each other in sparring training; whether such harm is ethical depends on whether both parties gave informed consent and whether the harm is within a reasonable range
- **Resource competition**: whether competition among cultivators for rare resources is ethical depends on whether the competition method is fair and whether anti-monopoly rules are observed
- **Power asymmetry in master-disciple relationships**: there is naturally a power asymmetry between master and disciple; whether the master abuses power needs specific judgment
- **Risk-taking in cultivation**: some cultivation methods carry high risks themselves; whether a cultivator's voluntary assumption of risk is ethical requires evaluating the informed awareness of risk and the availability of alternatives
- **Cross-race cultivation cooperation**: cooperation between different races may involve cultural differences and power asymmetry, requiring specific assessment

### 4.2 Handling Principles

For ethical issues in gray zones, the symbiotic civilization adopts the handling method of "specific situation analysis + ethics committee assessment + precedent guidance":

1. **Specific situation analysis**: no "one-size-fits-all" approach; comprehensive judgment based on the specific situation, motive, method, and consequences
2. **Ethics committee assessment**: for complex ethical issues, submit to the "Ethics Committee" under the Anchor Court for professional assessment
3. **Precedent guidance**: the assessment results of the Ethics Committee form precedents, providing guidance for subsequent similar cases
4. **Education first**: for improper conduct in gray zones, education and correction are prioritized over direct severe sanctions
5. **Dynamic adjustment**: as civilization develops and ethical understanding deepens, the judgment standards for gray zones can be dynamically adjusted

## V. Education and Internalization of Cultivation Ethics

The maintenance of cultivation ethics cannot rely solely on external sanctions and supervision; it more requires cultivators to identify with and internalize it from the depths of their hearts. The symbiotic civilization has established a systematic cultivation ethics education system:

### 5.1 Basic Education

- Cultivation ethics is a compulsory course for all cultivators; systematic ethical education must begin from the Sense Origin Realm
- Education content includes ethical principles, prohibition lists, case analysis, situation simulation, etc.
- Education methods include classroom teaching, case discussion, role-playing, mentors' teaching by precept and example, etc.

### 5.2 Advanced Education

- As cultivators' realms elevate, the depth and breadth of ethical education correspondingly elevate
- Advanced cultivators need to learn more complex ethical issues (such as cross-plane ethics, time ethics, world-creation ethics, etc.)
- Cultivators above the Dao Origin Realm need to pass the "Advanced Ethical Assessment" before continuing to elevate their realms

### 5.3 Practice and Internalization

- Encouraging cultivators to practice ethical principles in daily cultivation, transforming ethical requirements into conscious behavioral habits
- Establishing the "Ethical Model" selection system, commending cultivators with outstanding performance in cultivation ethics
- Mentors have the obligation to impart ethical spirit while teaching cultivation skills, achieving "transmitting both the Dao and the art"

## VI. Supervision and Enforcement of Cultivation Ethics

### 6.1 Supervision Mechanisms

- **Anchor network monitoring**: the anchor network continuously monitors the cultivation conduct of cultivators, providing timely warnings for suspected ethical violations
- **Ethical reporting system**: any life can report violations of cultivation ethics to the Ethics Tribunal of the Anchor Court, and reporters are protected
- **Periodic ethical assessment**: cultivators who reach the Law Origin Realm and above need to undergo an ethical assessment each year, with the results recorded in their personal cultivation files
- **Social organization supervision**: cultivation organizations, race communities, etc. are encouraged to conduct internal supervision of members' cultivation ethics

### 6.2 Enforcement Mechanisms

- **Ethics Tribunal**: the Anchor Court has a special Ethics Tribunal responsible for hearing cultivation ethics cases
- **Graded sanctions**: according to the severity of ethical violations, graded sanctions are applied, from warnings, fines, contribution value deduction, restriction of cultivation permissions, abolition of cultivation, to permanent imprisonment and capital punishment
- **Ethical reformation**: for violators with willingness to repent, ethical reformation can be conducted alongside sanctions; upon successful reformation, some rights can be partially restored
- **Criminal record**: records of cultivation ethics violations are permanently recorded in personal files, affecting subsequent realm certification, resource allocation, public position applications, etc.

## VII. Dynamic Expansion of Cultivation Boundaries

Cultivation ethics and boundaries are not immutable dogmas, but living norms that are dynamically adjusted with the development of civilization, technological progress, and deepening understanding. The symbiotic civilization has established a dynamic expansion mechanism for cultivation boundaries:

1. **Ethical assessment of new technologies**: whenever a new cultivation technology or law application emerges, ethical assessment is conducted first; promotion is allowed only after passing the assessment
2. **Ethical discussion of new scenarios**: when civilization enters new development stages (such as the cross-plane era, interstellar era, etc.), special ethical discussions are organized to formulate ethical norms adapted to the new scenarios
3. **Periodic revision of ethical norms**: the specific norms of cultivation ethics are revised every fifty years, with the revision process extensively soliciting opinions from all lives
4. **Continuous accumulation of ethical precedents**: the judgments of the Ethics Tribunal form precedents, continuously enriching and refining the specific connotations of cultivation ethics

## Conclusion

Cultivation ethics and boundaries are the core pillar of the spiritual civilization of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. They answer a fundamental question: "What kind of person should a cultivator become?" The answer of the symbiotic civilization is: a cultivator should become a person with power and, even more, with morality; a person in whom personal evolution and the symbiosis of the Myriad Races are unified; a person who pursues power but is absolutely never enslaved by power. Cultivation ethics is not a shackle on power, but a sublimation of power — it ensures that power always serves the dignity of life and the progress of civilization, and never becomes a tool of destruction and plunder. In a cosmos where power can move mountains and seas, can create worlds and destroy worlds, cultivation ethics is the last and most important line of defense guarding the bottom line of civilization.
