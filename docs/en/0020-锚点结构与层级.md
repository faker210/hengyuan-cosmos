## I. Essential Definition of Anchors

An anchor is the core mechanism in Heng Yuan Zhou that maintains cosmic balance, regulates energy flow, and supports the symbiotic network. Essentially, an anchor is not a mere physical node or energy convergence point, but a "solidified projection" of the cosmic Origin laws at specific space-time coordinates — it transforms abstract laws into tangible structures that can be perceived, dispatched, and maintained, enabling the scattered energy of individuals to converge and be shared through an orderly network.

The core functions of an anchor can be summarized in three aspects:

1. **Law Anchoring**: fixing the operating parameters of the cosmic Origin laws at specific nodes, preventing cosmic imbalance caused by law drift
2. **Energy Hub**: serving as a transfer node for energy convergence, transformation, and distribution, realizing cross-level and cross-plane energy dispatch
3. **Symbiotic Interface**: providing a standard interface for individual lives to access the symbiotic network, enabling every cultivator's achievements to flow into the collective evolutionary current

Unlike resource nodes such as "spirit veins" and "cave heavens" in the predatory system, anchors do not belong to any individual or organization as private property, but are public infrastructure belonging to all symbiotic lives of Heng Yuan Zhou. Any act of privatizing and monopolizing anchors violates the basic principles of the Symbiotic Constitution.

## II. The Five-Level Hierarchy of Anchors

The anchor system of Heng Yuan Zhou is divided into five levels according to coverage scope, law depth, and energy capacity. The levels form a tree-like topological structure: higher-level anchors govern lower-level anchors, while lower-level anchors converge energy and information upward, forming a complete closed loop of "downward permeation and upward convergence".

| Level | Name | Order of Magnitude | Coverage | Core Function | Law Depth |
|------|------|--------|----------|----------|----------|
| Level 1 | Cosmic Origin Anchor | 1 | Entire Heng Yuan Zhou | Maintains the stability of the fundamental laws of the universe, governs the entire anchor network | Origin Layer |
| Level 2 | Plane Core Anchor | 1-3 per plane | Entire single plane | Maintains plane law stability, dispatches plane-level energy flow | Law Layer |
| Level 3 | Star Domain Hub Anchor | Dozens per star domain | Star domain scope | Connects galaxy-level anchors, undertakes regional energy transfer | Semi-Law Layer |
| Level 4 | Galaxy Node Anchor | Hundreds per galaxy | Galaxy scope | Connects planet-level anchors, provides regional symbiotic network access | Energy Layer |
| Level 5 | Planet Access Anchor | Thousands per planet | Planet surface and near-Earth | Provides direct access interfaces for individual lives | Material Layer |

The connections among the five levels of anchors are not a simple superior-subordinate relationship, but follow the principle of "redundant backup and dynamic routing". Each lower-level anchor connects to at least two higher-level anchors simultaneously; when a certain path fails, energy and information can automatically switch to backup paths, ensuring the high availability of the entire network.

## III. Internal Structure of Anchors

Regardless of level, every anchor's internal structure follows the unified "Three Cores, Two Rings" architecture, which is the foundation for the stable operation of anchors:

### 3.1 The Three Core Structure

1. **Law Core**: the core of the anchor, storing the Origin law parameters anchored by the anchor. The stability of the Law Core directly determines the operational quality of the anchor; once the Law Core is impacted, the entire anchor will experience law drift or even collapse
2. **Energy Core**: responsible for the reception, storage, transformation, and output of energy. The Energy Core has multi-type energy compatibility, capable of simultaneously handling multiple forms such as material energy, spiritual energy, and consciousness energy
3. **Information Core**: responsible for the information processing of the symbiotic network, including cultivation feedback collection, system optimization instruction distribution, and individual status monitoring. The Information Core is the key to the anchor's "intelligent dispatch"

### 3.2 The Two Ring Structure

1. **Protection Ring**: the outer defense structure enveloping the Three Cores, possessing triple defense capabilities of physical defense, energy defense, and consciousness defense, preventing anchors from external attacks or accidental damage
2. **Calibration Ring**: located between the Protection Ring and the Three Cores, responsible for continuously calibrating the operating parameters of the Three Cores, ensuring that the Law Core does not drift, the Energy Core does not overflow, and the Information Core does not malfunction

## IV. The Establishment and Activation Process of Anchors

The establishment of an anchor is a systematic project, requiring five stages: site selection, foundation laying, activation, calibration, and network access:

1. **Site Selection Stage**: through the Space Law Dispatch System, detect indicators of candidate coordinates such as law concentration, energy stability, and geological activity, screening out nodes that meet the establishment conditions
2. **Foundation Laying Stage**: implant the anchor core seed at the selected coordinates, inject initial energy to begin the solidification of the Law Core. This stage usually requires energy input at the level of Plane Supreme Experts
3. **Activation Stage**: when the solidification of the Law Core is complete, activate the Energy Core and Information Core through specific rituals or technical means, giving the anchor basic operational capability
4. **Calibration Stage**: after activation, the anchor needs continuous calibration for at least one standard cycle, ensuring all parameters remain stable within the allowable range
5. **Network Access Stage**: the calibrated anchor officially accesses the symbiotic network, beginning to undertake energy transfer and information processing tasks

## Conclusion

The structure and hierarchy of anchors are the infrastructure skeleton of the symbiotic civilization of Heng Yuan Zhou. Understanding the five-level hierarchy and the "Three Cores, Two Rings" internal structure of anchors is the prerequisite for understanding the operating principles of the entire symbiotic network. As cross-plane exploration advances, the anchor system will continue to expand, eventually covering every corner of Heng Yuan Zhou.
