## I. Definition and Positioning of the Symbiotic Network

The symbiotic network is the concrete implementation of the anchor mechanism at the application layer, and is the distributed information and energy exchange system connecting all symbiotic lives of Heng Yuan Zhou. If anchors are the "infrastructure skeleton" of the symbiotic civilization, then the symbiotic network is the "nervous system and circulatory system" running on this skeleton — it enables individual lives scattered across the cosmos to exchange information in real time, share energy, and evolve in coordination, ultimately forming an organic community of life.

The core positioning of the symbiotic network can be understood from three dimensions:

1. **Information dimension**: the symbiotic network is a distributed information processing system on a scale of ten thousand, undertaking functions such as cultivation feedback collection, system optimization distribution, individual status monitoring, and cross-plane communication
2. **Energy dimension**: the symbiotic network is a cosmic-level energy dispatch system, realizing the convergence, transformation, distribution, and sharing of energy through anchor nodes, ensuring that energy flows to where it is most needed
3. **Evolution dimension**: the symbiotic network is an accelerator of collective evolution. Through the network resonance effect, the cultivation achievements of individuals can benefit all, realizing the symbiotic evolution of "one person comprehends the Dao, the Myriad Races benefit"

The design philosophy of the symbiotic network is "decentralized but hierarchical". It does not rely on any single central node — even if the Cosmic Origin Anchor is temporarily offline, the subnetworks of each region can still operate independently; but at the same time it retains the five-level anchor hierarchy, ensuring the efficiency of large-scale energy dispatch and law calibration.

## II. Topological Structure of the Symbiotic Network

The symbiotic network adopts a "hybrid topology" architecture, combining the advantages of tree-like hierarchical structure and mesh-like peer connections:

| Topology Type | Application Level | Advantage | Limitation |
|----------|----------|------|------|
| Tree-like Hierarchy | Among the five-level anchor system | High dispatch efficiency, clear management | Single point of failure risk |
| Mesh Peer-to-Peer | Among same-level anchors | High redundancy, strong fault tolerance | High dispatch complexity |
| Star Access | Between planet access anchors and individuals | Simple access, convenient management | Concentrated load on access anchors |

In this hybrid topology, each anchor node possesses three kinds of connections simultaneously:

1. **Uplink connection**: connecting to at least two higher-level anchors, for energy upward convergence and information reporting
2. **Downlink connection**: connecting to the lower-level anchors under its jurisdiction, for energy downward permeation and instruction distribution
3. **Horizontal connection**: connecting to adjacent anchors of the same level, for load balancing and redundant backup

This "three-way connection" design ensures that the symbiotic network maintains overall connectivity under any local failure. When an uplink connection is interrupted, the node can forward data to adjacent nodes through horizontal connections, and then reach the higher level through the adjacent nodes' uplink connections.

## III. Information Flow Mechanism of the Symbiotic Network

The information flow of the symbiotic network follows the five-stage closed loop of "collection — convergence — processing — distribution — feedback":

### 3.1 Information Collection

For every individual cultivator accessing the symbiotic network, key data during cultivation (energy fluctuations, realm breakthroughs, system fit, abnormal states, etc.) is automatically collected through Planet Access Anchors. The collection process follows the principle of minimum necessity, collecting only evolution-related data and not involving personal privacy information.

### 3.2 Information Convergence

The collected raw data converges upward along the anchor hierarchy, undergoing preliminary aggregation and deduplication at each level. Planet-level data converges to Galaxy Node Anchors, forming galaxy-level cultivation statistics; continues upward to Star Domain Hub Anchors, forming star domain-level trend analysis; and finally converges to Plane Core Anchors, forming a plane-level panoramic view of evolution.

### 3.3 Information Processing

Plane Core Anchors and the Cosmic Origin Anchor possess powerful information processing capabilities, capable of deep analysis of the massive data converged upward. Processing content includes:

- Cultivation system fit analysis, identifying which systems work best for which ethnic groups
- Bottleneck pattern recognition, discovering common cultivation bottlenecks and triggering system optimization
- Abnormal state early warning, detecting risks such as energy deviation and law erosion and intervening in time
- Evolution trend prediction, predicting future evolution directions and resource needs based on historical data

### 3.4 Information Distribution

Processing results are distributed downward through the anchor network to nodes at all levels, ultimately reaching individual cultivators. Distribution content includes: optimized cultivation techniques, personalized cultivation suggestions, risk warnings, resource allocation notices, etc. The distribution mechanism supports two modes: broadcast (notifying all) and unicast (personalized push).

### 3.5 Feedback Closed Loop

After individual cultivators receive the distributed information, their subsequent cultivation effects are collected again, forming a complete feedback closed loop. This closed loop enables the symbiotic network to continuously optimize itself and constantly improve the efficiency of collective evolution.

## IV. Energy Sharing Mechanism of the Symbiotic Network

The energy sharing of the symbiotic network follows the three principles of "allocation according to need, more work more reward, surplus sharing":

1. **Allocation according to need**: each cultivator receives the basic energy allocation needed to maintain normal cultivation according to their cultivation system, current realm, and physical condition. This allocation is guaranteed through the Output-Linked Contract System
2. **More work more reward**: the greater a cultivator's contribution to the symbiotic network (such as providing high-quality cultivation feedback, participating in anchor maintenance, guiding other cultivators), the more additional energy quota they receive
3. **Surplus sharing**: the surplus energy produced by cultivators (such as overflow energy released when breaking through realms, secondary energy generated during cultivation) automatically flows into the public energy pool of the symbiotic network for cultivators in need

The concrete implementation of energy sharing is completed through the Energy Cores of anchors. Each anchor maintains a local energy pool, with energy coming from the surplus contributions of the individuals under its jurisdiction and the downward-distributed allocation from higher levels. When the local energy pool is insufficient, the anchor borrows from adjacent anchors through horizontal connections; when the local energy pool overflows, surplus energy converges upward to higher-level energy pools.

## Conclusion

The symbiotic network is the core operating system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, connecting countless scattered individuals in the cosmos into an organic whole. Understanding the topological structure, information flow, and energy sharing mechanism of the symbiotic network is the key to understanding how the symbiotic civilization achieves "the common progress of the Myriad Races". With technological progress and the expansion of the anchor network, the symbiotic network will continue to evolve, ultimately becoming the underlying infrastructure supporting the overall dimensional ascension of the cosmos.
