## I. Origin and Core Philosophy of the Output-Linked Contract Mechanism

The output-linked contract mechanism is the core institution of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization in the field of resource allocation. Its design inspiration comes from the household output-linked contract responsibility system in agricultural production in the real world, upgraded and created based on the cultivation laws and cosmic rules of Heng Yuan Zhou. The core goal of this mechanism is to solve the fundamental problem of "how to allocate cultivation resources fairly and efficiently to all symbiotic lives".

In the era of the predatory cultivation system, the allocation of cultivation resources followed the principle of "the strong take all" — whoever was stronger or had the bigger sect could occupy more spirit veins, pills, techniques, and other resources, while the bottom masses had almost no formal channel to obtain resources. This allocation method not only caused severe class solidification, but also led to enormous waste of resources: a few predators occupied resources far exceeding their needs, while large numbers of promising bottom lives could not evolve for lack of resources.

The output-linked contract mechanism is precisely the fundamental correction of this defect. Its core philosophy can be summarized in four phrases:

1. **Everyone has a field**: every symbiotic life has the right to obtain a basic cultivation resource allocation, ensuring the minimum evolution guarantee
2. **Payment linked to output**: resource allocation is linked to cultivation results; the more output and contribution, the more resource quota obtained
3. **Autonomous operation**: after obtaining resource allocation, cultivators have the right to independently choose their cultivation system, cultivation method, and evolution direction
4. **Surplus to the public**: the surplus energy and resources produced by cultivators automatically flow into the public pool for all symbiotic lives to share

## II. The Three-Level Responsibility Field System of the Output-Linked Contract Mechanism

The output-linked contract mechanism divides cultivation resources into three levels of "responsibility fields", corresponding to different levels of resource allocation and output requirements:

| Responsibility Field Level | Name | Resource Content | Output Requirement | Applicable Objects |
|------------|------|----------|----------|----------|
| Level 1 | Basic Guarantee Field | Basic energy allocation, introductory cultivation techniques, basic cultivation guidance | Maintain basic cultivation state, periodically submit cultivation feedback | All symbiotic lives |
| Level 2 | Advanced Contract Field | Advanced energy quota, advanced cultivation resources, dedicated mentor guidance | Reach designated cultivation realm, produce quantifiable cultivation results | Cultivators with some cultivation foundation |
| Level 3 | Core Co-creation Field | Top-grade energy supply, rare cultivation resources, plane-level support | Undertake core tasks such as anchor maintenance, system R&D, cross-plane exploration | Plane Supreme Experts and core contributors |

### 2.1 Basic Guarantee Field

The Basic Guarantee Field is the "natural right" of every symbiotic life, obtainable without any prerequisite conditions. Its resource allocation standard is guaranteed by the Symbiotic Constitution, and no organization or individual may deduct it. The purpose of the Basic Guarantee Field is to ensure "A Path for Everyone" — even the lowest life can obtain the minimum resources needed to begin cultivation.

The output requirement of the Basic Guarantee Field is extremely low, requiring only that the cultivator maintains a basic cultivation state and periodically (once per standard cycle) submits cultivation feedback data. These feedback data will be used for the optimization of the symbiotic network's systems, which is itself a valuable contribution.

### 2.2 Advanced Contract Field

The Advanced Contract Field is oriented to cultivators who have completed basic cultivation and possess certain evolution potential. Cultivators can apply to the anchor management institution of their region for the Advanced Contract Field through the "contract application" process. The core indicators of application evaluation include: current cultivation realm, feedback quality during the basic cultivation period, system fit assessment, personal evolution willingness, etc.

The resource allocation of the Advanced Contract Field is significantly higher than that of the Basic Guarantee Field, including more sufficient energy quotas, higher-level cultivation techniques, dedicated mentor guidance, etc. Correspondingly, contractors need to reach the agreed cultivation realm within the designated period and produce quantifiable cultivation results (such as breakthrough records, optimization suggestions, technological innovations, etc.).

### 2.3 Core Co-creation Field

The Core Co-creation Field is the highest level of the output-linked contract mechanism, oriented to individuals who have reached the Plane Supreme Expert realm or made core contributions to the construction of the symbiotic civilization. The resource allocation of the Core Co-creation Field has almost no upper limit — as long as the task requires it, cultivators can draw on plane-level or even cosmic-level energy and resources.

Correspondingly, contractors of the Core Co-creation Field need to undertake the core tasks of the symbiotic civilization, including but not limited to: the establishment and maintenance of anchors, the R&D and optimization of cultivation systems, cross-plane exploration and archaeology, the handling of strange lives and strange energies, and the ethical adjudication of dark replacement bodies, etc.

## III. The Compensation and Allocation Algorithm of the Output-Linked Contract

The core of the output-linked contract mechanism is "More Work, More Reward", and the realization of "More Work, More Reward" depends on a scientific, transparent, and auditable compensation algorithm. This algorithm is automatically run by the Information Core of the symbiotic network, ensuring the fairness and efficiency of the allocation process.

The core formula of the compensation algorithm can be simplified as:

**Final Quota = Base Quota × (1 + Contribution Coefficient) × System Fit Coefficient × Difficulty Coefficient**

The definitions of each coefficient are as follows:

1. **Base Quota**: the fixed quota determined according to the responsibility field level, serving as the baseline of allocation
2. **Contribution Coefficient**: calculated based on the cultivator's actual output during the assessment period; the more and higher-quality the output, the larger the coefficient. The value range of the contribution coefficient is 0 to 10, with the vast majority of cultivators having a contribution coefficient between 0.5 and 3
3. **System Fit Coefficient**: calculated based on the match between the cultivator's chosen system and their personal traits; the higher the fit, the larger the coefficient. This coefficient encourages cultivators to choose the system most suitable for themselves, rather than blindly pursuing popular systems
4. **Difficulty Coefficient**: calculated based on the difficulty of the task undertaken by the cultivator; the more arduous and risky the task, the larger the coefficient. This coefficient encourages cultivators to challenge high-difficulty tasks, avoiding "picking easy ones and avoiding hard ones"

The operation of the compensation algorithm is completely transparent; every cultivator can query the details of their quota calculation through the symbiotic network, including the specific values and calculation bases of each coefficient. If a cultivator objects to the calculation result, they can file an appeal with the anchor management institution for manual review.

## IV. Supervision and Guarantee of the Output-Linked Contract Mechanism

To ensure the fair operation of the output-linked contract mechanism, the symbiotic civilization has established a triple supervision and guarantee system:

1. **Technical supervision**: the Information Core of the symbiotic network monitors all quota calculation processes in real time; once an anomaly is found (such as abnormally high coefficients, quota calculation errors), it automatically triggers the audit process
2. **Legal supervision**: the Symbiotic Constitution and the Anti-Monopoly Law explicitly prohibit any organization or individual from manipulating quota calculations or encroaching on others' resource quotas. Illegal acts will be severely sanctioned by law
3. **Democratic supervision**: the anchor management institution of each region has a supervision committee composed of cultivator representatives, which periodically reviews the quota allocation situation and accepts cultivators' complaints and suggestions

## Conclusion

The output-linked contract mechanism is a great institutional innovation of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization in the field of resource allocation. It not only guarantees the basic evolution rights of every symbiotic life, but also fully mobilizes the initiative and creativity of individuals through the "More Work, More Reward" incentive mechanism. The successful operation of this mechanism is an important institutional guarantee for the symbiotic civilization to realize the "common progress of the Myriad Races".
