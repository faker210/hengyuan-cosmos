## I. Basic Concepts and Principles of Energy Conduction

Anchor energy conduction refers to the process of energy being transmitted from one node to another in the anchor network, and is the physical foundation for the symbiotic network to realize energy sharing and dispatch. In Heng Yuan Zhou, energy is not a static existence, but a dynamic entity that constantly flows, transforms, and circulates. One of the core functions of the anchor network is to provide stable, efficient, and low-loss conduction paths for this dynamic flow.

Anchor energy conduction follows the following basic principles:

1. **Shortest path priority**: on the premise of satisfying other constraints, energy transmission prioritizes the path with the shortest physical distance and fewest hops, to reduce transmission loss
2. **Load balancing**: when multiple paths are available, energy transmission automatically selects the less loaded path, avoiding performance degradation of certain anchor nodes due to overload
3. **Loss minimization**: through path optimization and energy transformation technology, keep the energy loss during transmission at the lowest level
4. **Safe isolation**: different types and grades of energy remain isolated during transmission, preventing energy conflicts and pollution
5. **Dynamic routing**: when a path fails or is congested, energy transmission automatically switches to backup paths, ensuring uninterrupted transmission

## II. The Five-Level Path System of Energy Conduction

Corresponding to the five-level hierarchy of anchors, energy conduction paths are also divided into five levels, each undertaking energy transmission tasks of different scopes and scales:

| Path Level | Name | Transmission Scope | Transmission Scale | Typical Application |
|----------|------|----------|----------|----------|
| Level 1 | Cosmic Trunk Path | Cross-plane, cross-star domain | Cosmic-level energy torrent | Energy dispatch between the Cosmic Origin Anchor and Plane Core Anchors |
| Level 2 | Plane Trunk Line Path | Cross-star domain within a plane | Plane-level energy torrent | Energy dispatch between Plane Core Anchors and Star Domain Hub Anchors |
| Level 3 | Star Domain Branch Path | Cross-galaxy within a star domain | Star domain-level energy flow | Energy dispatch between Star Domain Hub Anchors and Galaxy Node Anchors |
| Level 4 | Galaxy Access Path | Cross-planet within a galaxy | Galaxy-level energy flow | Energy dispatch between Galaxy Node Anchors and Planet Access Anchors |
| Level 5 | Planet Terminal Path | Planet surface and near-Earth | Individual-level energy supply | Energy supply between Planet Access Anchors and individual cultivators |

### 2.1 Cosmic Trunk Path

The cosmic trunk path connects the Cosmic Origin Anchor with each Plane Core Anchor, and is the "energy aorta" of the entire anchor network. This level of path has the most massive transmission scale, capable of carrying cosmic-level energy torrents, mainly used for energy surplus-deficit adjustment among planes and energy supply for law calibration.

The establishment of the cosmic trunk path is extremely difficult, requiring the opening of cross-plane energy channels between Plane Core Anchors and the Cosmic Origin Anchor. This process usually requires multiple Plane Supreme Experts to cast in coordination and consumes large amounts of rare resources. Therefore, the number of cosmic trunk paths is extremely limited; each plane usually has only 1-2 trunk paths connecting to the Cosmic Origin Anchor.

### 2.2 Plane Trunk Line Path

The plane trunk line path connects the Plane Core Anchor with each Star Domain Hub Anchor, and is the "energy main road" within a plane. This level of path has a large transmission scale, capable of carrying plane-level energy torrents, mainly used for energy surplus-deficit adjustment among star domains and energy supply for plane-level public services.

The establishment of plane trunk line paths is relatively easy, usually planned uniformly by the Plane Core Anchor, constructing a mesh trunk line network among the star domains within the plane. Each star domain usually has 2-3 trunk line paths connecting to the Plane Core Anchor, ensuring redundant backup.

### 2.3 Star Domain Branch Path

The star domain branch path connects the Star Domain Hub Anchor with each Galaxy Node Anchor, and is the "energy branch road" within a star domain. This level of path has a medium transmission scale, mainly used for energy surplus-deficit adjustment among galaxies and energy supply for star domain-level public services.

### 2.4 Galaxy Access Path

The galaxy access path connects the Galaxy Node Anchor with each Planet Access Anchor, and is the "energy access network" within a galaxy. This level of path has a small transmission scale, mainly used for energy surplus-deficit adjustment among planets and energy supply for galaxy-level public services.

### 2.5 Planet Terminal Path

The planet terminal path connects the Planet Access Anchor with individual cultivators, and is the "last mile" of the entire energy conduction network. This level of path has the smallest transmission scale, but the largest number, directly serving the cultivation needs of every symbiotic life.

The planet terminal path usually adopts wireless energy transmission technology; cultivators can obtain energy allocations from the nearest Planet Access Anchor through the access interface of the symbiotic network. The transmission distance of the terminal path is usually on the order of hundreds of kilometers, ensuring that any location on the planet surface can obtain stable energy supply.

## III. Loss and Control of Energy Conduction

Energy inevitably produces loss during transmission; the magnitude of loss depends on various factors such as transmission distance, path quality, and energy type. The anchor network controls loss within an acceptable range through various technical means:

| Loss Type | Cause | Control Means | Typical Loss Rate |
|----------|----------|----------|------------|
| Distance Loss | Natural attenuation of energy propagating in space | Relay amplification (energy amplification at each anchor node passed) | About 0.1% per light-year |
| Transformation Loss | Loss when energy transforms between different forms | Optimize transformation algorithms, improve Law Core precision | About 1-3% per transformation |
| Impedance Loss | "Energy impedance" encountered during path transmission | Path calibration, impedance matching technology | About 0.5% per hop |
| Leakage Loss | Energy leaking to surrounding space during transmission | Protection Ring encapsulation, energy confinement field | About 0.2% per hop |

Through the above control means, the end-to-end energy transmission efficiency of the anchor network can usually reach over 85% — that is, of the energy transmitted from the Cosmic Origin Anchor to individual cultivators, over 85% can ultimately be effectively utilized. This efficiency was unimaginable in the predatory system era, when energy transmission efficiency was usually below 30%.

## IV. Safety and Protection of Energy Conduction

As a key infrastructure of the symbiotic civilization, the safety protection of energy conduction paths is crucial. The anchor network has established a triple safety protection system:

1. **Path encryption**: all energy transmission paths adopt law-level encryption technology, preventing energy from being stolen or tampered with. Encryption keys are dynamically generated by the Law Core of anchors and changed once per standard cycle
2. **Anomaly detection**: the Information Core of the symbiotic network monitors the energy flow state of all paths in real time; once an anomaly is found (such as sudden energy surge, abnormal traffic patterns, path parameter drift), it immediately triggers alarms and takes isolation measures
3. **Emergency cutoff**: when a path suffers a severe attack or an uncontrollable fault, the anchor management institution can remotely cut off the energy transmission of that path, preventing fault diffusion to the entire network. After cutoff, energy automatically switches to backup paths

## Conclusion

Anchor energy conduction paths are the "circulatory system" of the symbiotic network, enabling energy to flow efficiently, safely, and stably across the cosmos. The construction of the five-level path system, the application of loss control technology, and the establishment of safety protection mechanisms jointly ensure the reliable operation of this circulatory system. With the advancement of cross-plane exploration, energy conduction paths will continue to extend, eventually covering every corner of Heng Yuan Zhou.
