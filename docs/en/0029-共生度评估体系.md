## I. The Meaning and Purpose of the Symbiosis Index

The symbiosis index is the core indicator measuring the operating health of the anchor symbiotic network, reflecting the degree of symbiosis among all lives in the network. It is not a single number but a comprehensive indicator system covering multiple dimensions such as individual growth, collective cohesion, resource allocation, and network operation.

The purposes of establishing the symbiosis index evaluation system are:

1. **Status monitoring**: to understand in real time the operating state of the symbiotic network and discover problems in time
2. **Effect evaluation**: to evaluate the implementation effects of major symbiotic policies, providing a basis for policy adjustment
3. **Development guidance**: to provide direction for the development of the symbiotic civilization through index analysis
4. **Early warning**: to issue early warnings when the symbiosis index declines or anomalies appear, preventing the deterioration of the symbiotic order

The symbiosis index evaluation follows the principles of objectivity, comprehensiveness, dynamicity, and transparency: the data must be objective and authentic; the indicators must cover all important dimensions; the evaluation must be dynamically adjusted according to the actual situation; and the evaluation process and results must be transparent and open.

## II. The Index Model of the Symbiosis Index

The symbiosis index adopts a multi-level index model, divided into three levels: first-level indicators (4), second-level indicators (12), and third-level indicators (specific measurement items). The following is the first and second-level index structure:

| First-Level Indicator | Second-Level Indicator | Measurement Content |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Individual Development Index | Cultivation Progress Index | Average cultivation progress and realm distribution of all lives |
| | Growth Equity Index | Growth differences among lives in different regions, races, and strata |
| | Individual Satisfaction Index | Individual satisfaction with the symbiotic network and policies |
| Collective Cohesion Index | Network Participation Index | Participation of all lives in symbiotic network construction and maintenance |
| | Responsibility Fulfillment Index | Completion of public responsibility fields by all lives |
| | Cultural Identity Index | Degree of identification with the symbiotic culture and philosophy |
| Resource Allocation Index | Resource Equity Index | Fairness of resource distribution among different regions and strata |
| | Resource Efficiency Index | Efficiency of resource utilization |
| | Resource Sustainability Index | Sustainability of the energy pool and resource reserves |
| Network Operation Index | Network Stability Index | Stability of anchor network operation and fault rates |
| | Energy Conduction Index | Efficiency and loss of energy conduction |
| | Security Index | Safety condition of the network against external threats |

### 2.1 Individual Development Index

The individual development index measures the development level of each life in the symbiotic network. It includes three second-level indicators: cultivation progress, growth equity, and individual satisfaction.

The cultivation progress index reflects the average cultivation level of all lives, calculated based on the realm distribution of all lives. The growth equity index reflects the differences in growth opportunities and growth rates among lives; a smaller difference indicates higher equity. The individual satisfaction index is obtained through regular satisfaction surveys, reflecting individuals' subjective evaluation of the symbiotic network.

### 2.2 Collective Cohesion Index

The collective cohesion index measures the degree of unity and collaboration of the symbiotic civilization as a whole. It includes three second-level indicators: network participation, responsibility fulfillment, and cultural identity.

The network participation index reflects the participation of all lives in symbiotic network construction, maintenance, and governance. The responsibility fulfillment index reflects the completion of public responsibility fields by all lives. The cultural identity index reflects the identification of all lives with the symbiotic culture and philosophy, obtained through cultural surveys and behavioral analysis.

### 2.3 Resource Allocation Index

The resource allocation index measures the fairness and efficiency of resource distribution. It includes three second-level indicators: resource equity, resource efficiency, and resource sustainability.

The resource equity index reflects the difference in per-capita resource acquisition among different regions and strata; a smaller difference indicates higher equity. The resource efficiency index reflects the conversion rate of resources into cultivation results; higher efficiency indicates a healthier allocation mechanism. The resource sustainability index reflects the replenishment speed of the energy pool and resource reserves; a slower replenishment than consumption indicates unsustainability.

### 2.4 Network Operation Index

The network operation index measures the technical operation level of the anchor network. It includes three second-level indicators: network stability, energy conduction, and security.

The network stability index reflects the stability of anchor network operation, including fault rates, calibration frequency, and downtime. The energy conduction index reflects the efficiency and loss of energy conduction, directly related to the economic benefits of the network. The security index reflects the safety condition of the network against external threats, including the frequency of attacks and defense success rates.

## III. Data Collection and Processing

### 3.1 Data Collection

The data of the symbiosis index mainly comes from the following channels:

1. **Automatic collection**: the anchor network automatically collects data such as cultivation status, energy flow, and network operation in real time
2. **Regular surveys**: conduct regular satisfaction surveys and cultural identity surveys to obtain subjective evaluation data
3. **Special surveys**: for specific indicators (such as growth equity), conduct special surveys to obtain in-depth data
4. **External information**: collect information on external threats and cosmic environment changes

Data collection strictly follows the principles of "minimum necessity" and "privacy protection": only collect data necessary for index calculation, and all personal data is anonymized.

### 3.2 Data Processing

The collected raw data must be processed before calculating the index, including:

1. **Data cleaning**: remove noise data, outliers, and duplicate records
2. **Standardization**: standardize data of different dimensions and units to a comparable range
3. **Weight setting**: set weights for each indicator according to its importance
4. **Index calculation**: weighted aggregation of all levels of indicators to obtain the overall symbiosis index

The weight setting is not fixed but adjusted according to the development stage of the civilization. For example, in the early stage, network construction indicators may have higher weights; in the mature stage, individual development indicators may have higher weights.

## IV. Application and Early Warning of the Evaluation Results

### 4.1 Application of the Evaluation Results

The symbiosis index evaluation results are mainly used in the following aspects:

1. **Policy formulation**: formulate and adjust symbiotic policies based on the evaluation results
2. **Resource allocation**: adjust the resource allocation plan according to the deficiencies reflected by the indicators
3. **Regional improvement**: provide specific improvement directions for regions with lower indices
4. **Feedback optimization**: feed the evaluation results into the feedback loop mechanism, driving continuous optimization of the network

### 4.2 Early Warning Mechanism

When the symbiosis index or its sub-indicators reach warning thresholds, the early warning mechanism is activated:

| Warning Level | Trigger Condition | Response Measures |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Blue Warning | A single second-level indicator is below the warning line for 3 consecutive cycles | Focus on tracking the relevant indicator, analyze the cause |
| Yellow Warning | A single first-level indicator is below the warning line for 3 consecutive cycles | Convene a special evaluation meeting, formulate improvement plans |
| Orange Warning | The overall symbiosis index is below the warning line, or two first-level indicators are below it simultaneously | Mobilize the symbiotic civilization-wide improvement actions |
| Red Warning | The symbiosis index continues to decline for 6 cycles or drops sharply | Activate the emergency plan, comprehensively investigate and rectify |

### 4.3 Transparency and Supervision

The evaluation process and results of the symbiosis index are open to all lives, subject to the supervision of the whole civilization. Every life can inquire about the evaluation data and calculation methods of the relevant indicators. If a life doubts the evaluation results, it can apply for re-evaluation to the evaluation institution.

## Conclusion

The symbiosis index evaluation system is the "health examination system" of the anchor symbiotic network, providing the symbiotic civilization with objective, comprehensive, and dynamic monitoring and evaluation capabilities. Through the continuous operation of the index system, the symbiotic civilization can discover problems in time, optimize policies promptly, and adjust the direction of development, ultimately driving the symbiotic network toward ever healthier and more prosperous development. The symbiosis index is not only a technical indicator but also a concrete manifestation of the values of the symbiotic civilization — the degree of symbiosis is the measure of the civilization's progress.
