## I. Constitutional Status and Background of Enactment

The *Symbiotic Constitution* is the fundamental law of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization within the Wanling Hengmao Origin Universe (Hengyuan Universe), possessing the highest legal effect. All laws, administrative regulations, local regulations, and autonomous rules must not contravene the Constitution; all organizations and individuals must take the Constitution as the fundamental guideline for their activities and bear the duty of upholding the dignity of the Constitution and ensuring its implementation.

The Symbiotic Constitution was enacted at the formal establishment of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. Its core purpose is to establish, at the level of fundamental law, the civilizational foundation of "opposing predation and promoting symbiosis," fixing in the highest legal form the core philosophy of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization — returning the right to choose destiny and evolution to all beings, more work more reward, self-growth and self-development, a path for everyone, and the joint advancement of all races — so as to prevent the restoration of predatory institutions.

## II. Basic Principles of the Constitution

The Symbiotic Constitution establishes seven basic principles, constituting the value cornerstone of the entire legal system:

| Principle | Connotation |
|------|------|
| Equality of All Races | All forms of life, regardless of race, cultivation, or birth, are equal before the law |
| Symbiotic Evolution | Individual evolution must not come at the expense of the evolutionary rights of other individuals or the collective |
| More Work, More Reward | Resource allocation takes contribution as the core basis; opposes gain without labor and predatory appropriation |
| Self-Growth and Self-Development | Respects every life's right to independently choose its evolutionary path; opposes forced assimilation and thought control |
| A Path for Everyone | The state and civilization have the obligation to provide every life with a basic evolutionary path and resource guarantee |
| Reality as Foundation | Law takes the real world as the sole true basis; fictional settings have no legal effect |
| Sustainability | All development must take into account the sustainability of the Origin of the Universe; opposes draining the pond to catch all the fish |

## III. Basic Rights of Citizens (All Beings)

The Symbiotic Constitution confers the following basic rights upon all life forms with perceptive capacity within the Hengyuan Universe:

1. **Right to Evolutionary Choice**: Every life has the right to freely choose its cultivation system, evolutionary path, and way of life; no organization or individual may force interference.
2. **Right to Access Cultivation Resources**: Every life has the right to obtain, free of charge, the basic methods of the Three Hundred Sets of Origin Cultivation Systems and to receive necessary cultivation resource support through the Joint-Output Contracting Mechanism.
3. **Right to Equal Protection**: Every life enjoys equal treatment in the application of law, resource allocation, education, and medical care; discrimination based on race, cultivation, or birth is prohibited.
4. **Right to Symbiotic Participation**: Every life has the right to participate in the decision-making and supervision of public affairs, expressing opinions and exercising rights through the symbiotic network.
5. **Right to Cultural Preservation**: Every race and civilization has the right to preserve its own cultural traditions, language, script, and customs; the symbiotic civilization respects cultural diversity.
6. **Right to Remedy and Appeal**: Every life, when its rights are infringed, has the right to obtain remedy through the judicial system and to appeal and file accusations against illegal acts.

## IV. Basic Obligations

Rights and obligations are reciprocal; the Symbiotic Constitution simultaneously stipulates the basic obligations of all beings:

1. **Obligation to abide by the Constitution and laws**
2. **Obligation to maintain the symbiotic order and the public interest**
3. **Obligation to participate in labor and contribution in accordance with the law** (the counterpart of more work, more reward)
4. **Obligation to protect the Origin of the Universe and the ecological environment**
5. **Obligation to respect the lawful rights of other lives**
6. **Obligation to guide junior cultivators within one's capacity** (the legal basis of the mentor network)

## V. Institutional Setup of the State (Civilization)

The Symbiotic Constitution establishes the governance structure of the symbiotic civilization, implementing the "three powers separate and balanced" mechanism of the Anchor Assembly, the Symbiotic Executive Committee, and the Symbiotic Court:

- **Anchor Assembly**: the supreme authority, composed of representatives of all races in proportion, exercising legislative power and the power to decide major matters
- **Symbiotic Executive Committee**: the supreme administrative organ, responsible for the execution of the Constitution and laws and daily governance
- **Symbiotic Court**: the supreme judicial organ, independently exercising judicial power, responsible for constitutional review and adjudication of major cases

In addition, an "Anchor Oversight Committee" is established as an independent supervisory organ, responsible for supervising all power organs, preventing the abuse of power and the resurgence of predatory behavior.

## VI. Constitutional Amendment and Guarantees

Amendment of the Symbiotic Constitution shall take effect only after passage by a two-thirds or greater majority of the Anchor Assembly and approval by a simple majority in a universal referendum. This rigorous procedure ensures the stability of the Constitution as the fundamental law, preventing short-term interest groups from easily tampering with the foundation of civilization.

Guarantees for the implementation of the Constitution include: the constitutional review system (the Symbiotic Court has the power to declare laws violating the Constitution invalid), the constitutional litigation system (all beings may file lawsuits directly on the basis of the Constitution), and the constitutional oath system (all public officials must swear allegiance to the Constitution upon taking office).

## Conclusion

The *Symbiotic Constitution* is the legal cornerstone of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization and the supreme declaration of anti-predation. It transforms "symbiosis" from an idea into an executable, remediable, and guaranteed legal institution, providing the fundamental rule-of-law framework for the joint advancement of all races. All subsequent sectoral laws are enacted on the basis of this Constitution.
