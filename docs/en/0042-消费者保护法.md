## I. Legislative Purpose and Scope of Application

The *Consumer Protection Law* is one of the core laws of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's legal system. Together with the *Symbiotic Labor Law*, it constitutes the two pillars of economic life. This Law aims to protect the lawful rights and interests of all consumers within the Hengyuan Universe, regulate market transaction behavior, maintain a fair, honest, and symbiotic market order, and prevent predatory commercial practices from infringing upon the rights and interests of all beings.

This Law applies to all consumers within the Hengyuan Universe who purchase or use goods or receive services to meet personal or family living needs, as well as to business operators who provide goods or services to consumers. Cross-plane transactions are likewise governed by this Law, but implementing rules may be formulated according to plane differences.

## II. Basic Rights of Consumers

This Law establishes that consumers enjoy the following nine basic rights:

| Right | Connotation |
|------|------|
| Right to Safety Guarantee | Consumers enjoy the right to have their personal and property safety undamaged when purchasing or using goods and receiving services |
| Right to Know the Truth | Consumers have the right to know the true situation of the goods purchased or used or the services received, including composition, efficacy, risks, etc. |
| Right to Independent Choice | Consumers have the right to independently choose goods or services, to compare, identify, and select, and to refuse forced transactions |
| Right to Fair Transaction | Consumers have the right to obtain fair transaction conditions such as quality guarantee, reasonable prices, and accurate measurement |
| Right to Claim Compensation by Law | Consumers who suffer personal or property damage from purchasing or using goods or receiving services enjoy the right to obtain compensation in accordance with the law |
| Right to Association for Rights Protection | Consumers have the right to establish or join consumer organizations in accordance with the law to protect their lawful rights and interests |
| Right to Obtain Knowledge | Consumers have the right to obtain knowledge and information concerning consumption and consumer rights protection |
| Right to Be Respected | When purchasing or using goods and receiving services, consumers enjoy the right to have their personal dignity and ethnic customs and habits respected |
| Right to Supervision | Consumers have the right to supervise goods and services as well as consumer rights protection work, and to offer criticisms and suggestions |

## III. Obligations of Business Operators

When providing goods or services to consumers, business operators shall fulfill the following obligations:

1. **Obligation of Lawful Operation**: Business operators must abide by the Constitution and laws and engage in business activities within their approved scope.
2. **Obligation of Safety Guarantee**: Business operators shall ensure that the goods or services they provide meet the requirements for safeguarding personal and property safety, and shall provide truthful explanations and clear warnings for goods and services that may endanger safety.
3. **Obligation of Truthful Information**: Business operators shall provide consumers with truthful information about goods or services and shall not make false or misleading publicity. Cultivation-type goods and services must truthfully indicate their efficacy boundaries and potential risks.
4. **Obligation of Quality Guarantee**: Business operators shall guarantee that, under normal use of goods or receipt of services, the goods or services provided have the quality, performance, purpose, and validity period they ought to have.
5. **Obligation of Fair Transaction**: Business operators shall not, through standard clauses, notices, declarations, in-store notices, or other means, make unfair or unreasonable provisions that exclude or restrict consumer rights, reduce or exempt operator liability, or increase consumer responsibility.
6. **Obligation of After-sales Service**: Business operators shall, in accordance with state regulations or agreements with consumers, bear responsibility for repair, replacement, return, and other after-sales services.
7. **Obligation of Privacy Protection**: Business operators shall protect consumers' personal information and consciousness data; without consumer consent, they shall not collect, use, or disclose them.

## IV. Dispute Resolution and Legal Liability

When a consumer and a business operator have a consumer rights dispute, it may be resolved through the following channels:

- Negotiating a settlement with the business operator
- Requesting mediation by a consumer organization
- Filing a complaint with the relevant administrative department
- Requesting an arbitration institution to arbitrate in accordance with the arbitration agreement reached with the business operator
- Filing a lawsuit with the Symbiotic Court

Business operators who violate the provisions of this Law and infringe upon consumers' lawful rights and interests shall bear the following liabilities:

| Illegal Act | Legal Liability |
|----------|---------|
| Providing defective goods or services causing damage | Bear compensation liability, including medical expenses, lost work compensation, disability compensation, etc. |
| Fraudulent conduct | Increase compensation at the consumer's request, in an amount three times the price of the goods purchased or the cost of the services received |
| False publicity | Ordered to cease the illegal act, eliminate the impact, and pay a fine |
| Forced transactions | Ordered to rectify, refund unreasonable fees, and pay a fine |
| Infringing consumer privacy | Bear compensation for mental damage; if circumstances are serious, criminal liability shall be pursued |

## V. Consumer Organizations and Social Supervision

The state encourages and supports all organizations and individuals in conducting social supervision over acts that damage consumers' lawful rights and interests. The Consumers' Association is a social organization lawfully established to conduct social supervision over goods and services and to protect consumers' lawful rights and interests, fulfilling the following functions:

1. Providing consumers with consumption information and consulting services
2. Participating in the supervision and inspection of goods and services by relevant administrative departments
3. Reflecting, inquiring, and making suggestions to relevant administrative departments on issues concerning consumers' lawful rights and interests
4. Accepting consumer complaints, and investigating and mediating the matters complained about
5. Supporting damaged consumers in filing lawsuits against acts that damage consumers' lawful rights and interests

## Conclusion

The *Consumer Protection Law* is the guardian of the market order of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. By granting consumers full rights, restraining business operator behavior, and establishing a pluralistic dispute resolution mechanism, it ensures that market transactions operate on the track of fairness, honesty, and symbiosis, preventing the commercial sphere from becoming a new hotbed of predatory behavior.
