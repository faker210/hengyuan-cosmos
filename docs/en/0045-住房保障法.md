## I. Legislative Purpose and Basic Principles

The *Housing Security Law* is one of the five supporting laws of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. It aims to guarantee the right of residence of all lives within the Hengyuan Universe, establish a fair and sustainable housing security system, use spatial legal principle dispatch technology to provide suitable living spaces for all races, and prevent speculation and predation in the housing sector.

This Law establishes the following basic principles:

| Principle | Connotation |
|------|------|
| Residence Guarantee | Every life has the right to obtain suitable living space; the state and civilization have the obligation to guarantee basic residence needs |
| Public Welfare Priority | Housing is first a necessity of life rather than a speculative commodity; guaranteed housing has a public welfare attribute |
| Spatial Efficiency | Use spatial legal principle dispatch technology to improve spatial utilization efficiency and expand livable space |
| Non-Human Adaptation | Provide adapted living spaces for the physiological characteristics and living habits of different races |
| Livable Environment | Living spaces should possess a good ecological environment, infrastructure, and public services |
| Anti-Speculation | Prohibit speculative profiteering with housing as the target, preventing housing from becoming a tool for plundering wealth |

## II. Legal Guarantees of the Right of Residence

This Law explicitly stipulates that the right of residence is a basic right conferred upon every life by the Symbiotic Constitution, specifically including:

1. **Basic Right of Residence**: Every life has the right to obtain living space not lower than the minimum standard, with the minimum standard determined according to racial body size and living needs.
2. **Right to Apply for Guaranteed Housing**: Eligible lives have the right to apply for guaranteed housing, with application conditions and allocation standards open and transparent.
3. **Right to Residence Safety**: Living spaces should meet safety standards; dangerous buildings and unsuitable living spaces shall not be rented or allocated.
4. **Right to Residence Stability**: Residents of guaranteed housing enjoy stable residence rights when meeting conditions and shall not be arbitrarily evicted.
5. **Right to Living Environment**: Residents have the right to obtain clean air, water sources, and a good surrounding environment, and have the right to file complaints about environmental pollution.
6. **Right to Housing Renovation**: Residents have the right to reasonably renovate their living spaces on the premise of not affecting building safety and the rights of others.

## III. Housing Security System

The housing security system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization is divided into three types of housing, covering different levels of demand:

| Type | Name | Guaranteed Group | Allocation Method | Rent/Price |
|------|------|----------|----------|-----------|
| First type | Public Rental Housing | Low-income groups, junior cultivators, new immigrants | Application and review system, queued allocation | Symbolic low rent |
| Second type | Shared Ownership Housing | Middle-income groups, intermediate cultivators | Application and review system, lottery allocation | Below market price, shared ownership between individual and state |
| Third type | Commercial Housing | High-income groups, advanced cultivators | Market transaction | Market price, subject to regulation |

In addition, spatial legal principle dispatch technology is used to build "folded space residential zones": expanding the residential area in limited physical space through space-folding technology, providing more living space for high-density areas. Folded space residential zones must undergo safety assessment to ensure spatial stability.

## IV. Housing Adaptation for Non-Human Races

In response to the special residence needs of different races within the Hengyuan Universe, this Law establishes a housing adaptation institution for non-human races:

- **Aquatic races**: Provided with underwater living spaces or amphibious residences, equipped with water circulation and water quality maintenance systems.
- **Flying races**: Provided with high-altitude living spaces or residences with takeoff and landing platforms, equipped with air traffic management.
- **Energy lives**: Provided with living spaces of suitable energy concentration, equipped with energy stabilization devices.
- **Giant races**: Provided with large-scale living spaces, with building structures specially reinforced.
- **Microscopic races**: Provided with microscopic residential zones, equipped with scaling passages and dedicated facilities.

Non-human housing adaptation standards are jointly formulated by the housing competent authority together with representatives of all races and updated regularly.

## V. Housing Construction and Management

- **Housing Construction Planning**: The state formulates housing construction plans based on population growth and immigration, ensuring a balance between housing supply and demand.
- **Construction Standards**: The construction of guaranteed housing must meet safety, environmental protection, and livability standards; lowering construction quality is prohibited.
- **Property Management**: Residential areas implement a model combining autonomous management and professional property services; residents have the right to participate in property management decisions.
- **Housing Maintenance**: The maintenance of guaranteed housing is the responsibility of the housing management institution, with maintenance funds drawn from public accumulation.
- **Exit Mechanism**: Residents of guaranteed housing whose income or cultivation exceeds the guarantee standard should exit the guaranteed housing or convert to commercial housing, with a reasonable transition period granted for exit.

## VI. Anti-Speculation and Supervision

To prevent speculation and predation in the housing sector, this Law establishes the following supervision measures:

1. Limit the number of commercial housing units held by individuals; progressive property taxes are levied on amounts exceeding the limit.
2. Prohibit the subletting and resale of guaranteed housing; violators have their guarantee eligibility revoked and housing recovered.
3. Establish a public information system for housing transactions, preventing behind-the-scenes operations and price manipulation.
4. Implement qualification management and fund supervision for real estate developers, preventing unfinished projects and fraud.
5. Establish housing complaint and dispute resolution mechanisms, protecting the lawful rights and interests of residents.

## VII. Legal Liability

Acts violating the provisions of this Law shall bear the following liabilities depending on the severity of the circumstances:

- Those who allocate guaranteed housing in violation of regulations shall be ordered to correct and the responsible persons disciplined.
- Those who sublet or resell guaranteed housing shall have their guarantee eligibility revoked, housing recovered, and be fined.
- Developers who lower construction quality shall be ordered to rework and fined; in serious circumstances, their qualifications shall be revoked.
- Those engaged in housing speculation shall be subject to high speculation taxes and fines.
- Those who rent dangerous buildings causing harm to residents shall bear compensation liability; in serious circumstances, criminal liability shall be pursued.

## Conclusion

The *Housing Security Law* is the implementation of the "a path for everyone" philosophy of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization in the residence domain. By establishing a classified housing security system, using spatial legal principle technology to expand living space, adapting to the residence needs of non-human races, and combating housing speculation, it ensures that every life can have a safe, suitable, and stable home, providing secure residence guarantees for the joint advancement of all races.
