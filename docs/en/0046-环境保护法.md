## I. Legislative Purpose and Basic Principles

The *Environmental Protection Law* is one of the five supporting laws of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. It aims to protect the ecological environment and the Origin of the Universe within the Hengyuan Universe, establish the environmental governance philosophy of "symbiotic evolution, sustainable development," prevent the destruction of the cosmic ecosystem by predatory development, and safeguard the foundation of survival for the joint advancement of all races.

This Law establishes the following basic principles:

| Principle | Connotation |
|------|------|
| Origin Protection | The Origin of the Universe is the foundation of all life and evolution; it must be protected as a priority; destructive exploitation is prohibited |
| Ecological Symbiosis | Humanity and nature, all races and ecosystems are in a symbiotic relationship; destroying ecology is destroying one's own foundation of survival |
| Sustainability | All development activities must take into account the interests of both the present and future generations; opposes draining the pond to catch all the fish |
| Prevention First | Environmental protection is prevention-oriented; establishes an environmental impact assessment institution to prevent pollution from occurring |
| Polluter Responsibility | Entities that cause environmental pollution and ecological destruction must bear the responsibility for treatment and compensation |
| Public Participation | Encourages public participation in environmental protection; guarantees the public's right to know and participate in the environment |
| Cross-Plane Coordination | Environmental protection advances in coordination across planes; establishes a cross-plane environmental governance cooperation mechanism |

## II. Scope of Environmental Protection

The environmental scope protected by this Law includes all ecosystems and environmental elements within the Hengyuan Universe:

1. **Atmospheric Environment**: Including the energy concentration and compositional balance of each planet's atmosphere and energy layers.
2. **Water Environment**: Including the water quality and quantity of surface water, groundwater, oceans, and energy waters between planes.
3. **Soil Environment**: Including the soil quality and fertility of land soils and cultivation resource ore veins.
4. **Biological Diversity**: Including species protection and habitat protection of all flora, fauna, microorganisms, and strange lives.
5. **Energy Environment**: Including the concentration and distribution balance of heaven-earth spiritual energy, origin energy, and law fragments.
6. **Spatial Environment**: Including the spatial stability of planes, spatial fissures, and the environmental safety of folded space.
7. **Consciousness Domain Environment**: Including the information cleanliness of collective consciousness space and the prevention of spiritual pollution.

## III. Main Institutions

### (I) Environmental Impact Assessment Institution

All development and construction projects that may have a significant impact on the environment must undergo environmental impact assessment before commencement. Assessment content includes: the project's impact on atmospheric, water, soil, biological, energy, spatial, and other environmental elements, prevention and mitigation measures, environmental risk assessment, etc. Projects that do not pass environmental impact assessment shall not commence construction.

### (II) Pollutant Discharge Permit and Total Amount Control Institution

The state implements permit management and total amount control for pollutant discharge. Pollutant discharge units must obtain pollutant discharge permits and discharge pollutants in accordance with the types, concentrations, and total amounts specified in the permits. The state determines the total amount of pollutant discharge for each region and plane according to environmental carrying capacity, decomposing and implementing them level by level.

### (III) Ecological Protection Red Line Institution

The state delimits ecological protection red lines in areas with special ecological value, including:

| Red Line Type | Protected Content |
|----------|---------|
| Origin Protection Zone | Core production areas of the Origin of the Universe's energy; all development activities are prohibited |
| Species Habitat | Core habitats of rare and endangered species; destruction and disturbance are prohibited |
| Ecological Barrier Zone | Key ecosystems maintaining regional ecological balance; development is restricted |
| Cultivation Sacred Grounds | Energy-rich areas with special cultivation value; protective use is implemented |
| Plane Boundary Belt | Ecological transition belts between planes; spatially destructive development is prohibited |

### (IV) Environmental Monitoring and Early Warning Institution

The state establishes an environmental monitoring network covering the entire universe, monitoring changes in environmental elements in real time. For major environmental pollution and ecological destruction events that may occur, an early warning mechanism is established, issuing early warning information promptly and initiating emergency response.

### (V) Ecological Compensation Institution

Regions and groups whose development is restricted due to environmental protection have the right to receive ecological compensation. Ecological compensation funds are drawn from public accumulation and resource development returns, used for the economic development of the compensated regions and the improvement of residents' lives.

## IV. Environmental Regulation of Cultivation Activities

The impact of cultivation activities on the environment is a special domain of environmental protection in the Hengyuan Universe; this Law establishes dedicated regulatory provisions:

1. **Energy Extraction Regulation**: The extraction of cultivation resources must conform to the principle of sustainability; destructive extraction leading to energy depletion is prohibited. Extraction volume shall not exceed the natural recovery rate of regional energy.
2. **Cultivation Waste Disposal**: Energy residues, discarded pills, damaged artifacts, and other waste generated during cultivation must be disposed of in accordance with regulations; random discarding causing energy pollution is prohibited.
3. **Advanced Cultivation Environmental Assessment**: The breakthroughs and seclusion of advanced cultivators may have significant impacts on the surrounding environment; environmental assessment must be conducted in advance and protective measures taken.
4. **Plane Development Permit**: The development of new planes must undergo environmental assessment and ecological survey; development may proceed only after formulating an ecological protection plan.
5. **Strange Life Habitat Protection**: The habitats of strange lives are included in the ecological protection red lines; exploration and development without permission are prohibited.

## V. Legal Liability

Acts violating the provisions of this Law shall bear the following liabilities depending on the severity of the circumstances:

- Those who commence construction without passing environmental impact assessment shall be ordered to stop construction, restore the original state, and fined.
- Those who discharge pollutants exceeding standards shall be ordered to treat within a time limit and fined; in serious circumstances, ordered to suspend operations and close.
- Those who destroy ecological protection red lines shall be ordered to restore the original state and fined; in serious circumstances, criminal liability shall be pursued.
- Those who destructively extract cultivation resources shall have their illegal gains confiscated and be fined; their extraction permits shall be revoked.
- Those who cause major environmental pollution accidents shall bear compensation liability; if constituting a crime, criminal liability shall be pursued.
- Environmental supervision personnel who neglect their duties or abuse their power shall be disciplined; if constituting a crime, criminal liability shall be pursued.

## VI. Public Participation and Environmental Public Interest Litigation

The public has the right to participate in environmental protection, with specific channels including:

1. Submitting opinions and suggestions on environmental impact assessment projects.
2. Reporting and complaining about acts of polluting the environment and destroying ecology.
3. Establishing environmental protection organizations in accordance with the law, participating in environmental supervision and public welfare activities.
4. For acts that pollute the environment, destroy ecology, and harm public interests, environmental protection organizations may file environmental public interest litigation with the Symbiotic Court.

## Conclusion

The *Environmental Protection Law* is the legal guarantee of the sustainable development philosophy of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. By establishing institutions such as environmental impact assessment, ecological protection red lines, and total amount control, it integrates environmental protection into all development and cultivation activities, ensuring the sustainability of the Origin of the Universe and the ecosystem, and guarding the eternal foundation of survival for the joint advancement of all races.
