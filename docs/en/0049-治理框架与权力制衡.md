## I. Overview of the Governance Framework

The governance framework of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization is built on the principle of "separation and balance of the three powers," forming a governance system in which the Anchor Assembly (legislative power), the Symbiotic Executive Committee (executive power), and the Symbiotic Court (judicial power) check and balance each other, with the Anchor Oversight Committee exercising supervisory power throughout the process. This framework aims to prevent the abuse of power, ensure that governance serves the joint advancement of all races, and guarantee the rule of law and fairness within the Hengyuan Universe.

The governance framework follows the following basic principles:

| Principle | Connotation |
|------|------|
| Separation and Balance of the Three Powers | Legislative, executive, and judicial powers are independent and mutually check and balance; no power may be dominant |
| Whole-Process Supervision | The Anchor Oversight Committee conducts supervision over the entire governance process |
| Rule of Law Governance | All power operates within the legal framework; governance behavior must be legal and lawful |
| Multi-Race Participation | All races participate in governance through due channels, ensuring that governance represents the will of all races |
| Open and Transparent | Governance activities are open and transparent, ensuring the public's right to know and participate |
| Serving the Symbiotic Public Interest | Governance exists to serve the joint advancement of all races; the use of public power for private gain is prohibited |

## II. Structure of the Governance Framework

The governance framework of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization includes the following organs:

| Organ | Nature | Core Functions |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Anchor Assembly | Legislative organ | Enacts and amends laws, examines and approves major plans and budgets, supervises the executive and judicial organs |
| Symbiotic Executive Committee | Executive organ | Implements laws, administers public affairs, manages economic, cultural, social, and cultivation affairs |
| Symbiotic Court | Judicial organ | Exercises adjudicative power independently, resolves disputes, safeguards the authority of the rule of law |
| Anchor Oversight Committee | Supervisory organ | Supervises all governance organs, investigates corruption and dereliction of duty |

In addition, advisory institutions, expert committees, and racial representative councils are established to provide advisory support for governance.

## III. The Anchor Assembly (Legislative Power)

### (I) Composition and Term

The Anchor Assembly is the supreme legislative organ of the Hengyuan Universe, with members composed of representatives elected from all races and all planes. The term of office is ten standard years, with regular re-elections.

### (II) Legislative Power

The Anchor Assembly exercises the following legislative powers:

1. Enacting and amending the Symbiotic Constitution.
2. Enacting and amending basic laws (criminal law, civil law, cultivation law, etc.).
3. Enacting and amending administrative regulations and special laws.
4. Interpreting laws and supervising the implementation of laws.

### (III) Decision-Making Power

The Anchor Assembly exercises the following decision-making powers:

1. Examining and approving the civilizational development plan and annual budget.
2. Examining and approving major construction projects and important international (inter-universe) affairs.
3. Deciding on war, peace, and other major state affairs.
4. Electing, appointing, or dismissing major leading personnel.

### (IV) Supervisory Power

The Anchor Assembly supervises the executive and judicial organs, including:

1. Hearing and deliberating work reports.
2. Inquiry and questioning procedures.
3. Special investigation committees.
4. Vote of no confidence in the executive committee (in accordance with the law).

## IV. The Symbiotic Executive Committee (Executive Power)

### (I) Organizational Structure

The Symbiotic Executive Committee is the supreme executive organ of the Hengyuan Universe, composed of the Executive Chief and department heads. The Executive Chief is elected by the Anchor Assembly and appointed after confirmation.

### (II) Administrative Functions

The Symbiotic Executive Committee exercises the following functions:

1. Implementing laws and resolutions of the Anchor Assembly.
2. Managing economic, social, cultural, educational, medical, and cultivation affairs.
3. Formulating administrative regulations and policies.
4. Managing public resources and the public accumulation fund.
5. Handling diplomatic affairs with other universes.

### (III) Power Restraints

The power of the Symbiotic Executive Committee is subject to the following restraints:

1. Major decisions must be reported to the Anchor Assembly for review.
2. Administrative regulations shall not contravene laws.
3. Administrative acts are subject to judicial review by the Symbiotic Court.
4. The implementation of administrative power is supervised by the Anchor Oversight Committee.

## V. The Symbiotic Court (Judicial Power)

The Symbiotic Court is the supreme judicial organ of the Hengyuan Universe, exercising adjudicative power independently. Its core functions include:

1. Adjudicating civil, criminal, administrative, and cultivation cases.
2. Adjudicating constitutional cases and exercising constitutional review.
3. Exercising the power of unified legal interpretation.
4. Executing judgments and safeguarding the authority of the rule of law.

The independence of the Symbiotic Court is guaranteed by the constitution and laws; judges enjoy lifelong tenure and are not subject to replacement at will during their tenure. Judicial power is subject to the supervision of the Anchor Assembly and the Anchor Oversight Committee, but no organ may interfere with the independent adjudication of individual cases.

## VI. The Anchor Oversight Committee (Supervisory Power)

### (I) Establishment and Term

The Anchor Oversight Committee is the supreme supervisory organ of the Hengyuan Universe, established in accordance with the constitution, with the power to supervise all governance organs and public officials.

### (II) Supervisory Functions

The Anchor Oversight Committee exercises the following functions:

1. Supervising the law enforcement and law-abiding status of all governance organs.
2. Investigating and handling corruption, dereliction of duty, and abuse of power.
3. Supervising the use of public resources and the public accumulation fund.
4. Accepting complaints and reports from all lives and filing them for investigation.
5. Submitting supervision reports to the Anchor Assembly.

### (III) Procedural Guarantees

The Anchor Oversight Committee's supervision is governed by strict procedures: investigations follow statutory procedures; the rights and interests of the investigated persons are protected; and supervision results are open and transparent.

## VII. Checks and Balances Between the Powers

The checks and balances between the governance powers are embodied in:

| Power | Checked By | Balance Mechanism |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Legislative power (Anchor Assembly) | Executive veto, judicial review | The executive may return bills with objections; the court may review laws |
| Executive power (Executive Committee) | Legislative review, judicial review, oversight supervision | Laws and budgets are reviewed by the legislature; administrative acts are reviewed by the courts; oversight by the oversight organ |
| Judicial power (Symbiotic Court) | Legislative review, oversight supervision | Judges are appointed and dismissed by the legislature; judicial discipline is supervised by the oversight organ |
| Supervisory power (Oversight Committee) | Legislative review, procedural constraints | The scope and procedures of supervision are prescribed by law; the legislature supervises its operations |

## VIII. Multi-Race Governance Participation

The governance framework guarantees the participation of all races through the following channels:

1. **Racial Representative Councils**: Each race establishes a racial representative council, electing representatives to participate in the Anchor Assembly.
2. **Proportional Representation**: The allocation of seats in the Anchor Assembly guarantees the representation of major races and the basic representation of minority races.
3. **Racial Veto Mechanism**: For legislation involving the vital interests of a particular race, the racial representative council may exercise a racial veto; a special voting procedure is required to override it.
4. **Consultative Governance**: Special race affairs are governed through consultation with the races concerned.

## Conclusion

The *Governance Framework and Balance of Powers* is the institutional core of the rule of law governance of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. By establishing the "separation and balance of the three powers" governance structure, the whole-process supervision institution, and the multi-race participation mechanism, it ensures the lawful, fair, and transparent operation of public power, prevents the abuse and corruption of power, and provides institutional guarantees for the joint advancement of all races.
