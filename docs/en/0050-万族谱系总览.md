## I. Definition of the Concept of the Myriad Races

In the Wanling Hengmao Origin Universe (Hengyuan Universe), the "Myriad Races" (Wànzú) does not literally refer to ten thousand races, but is a general term for all life forms in the universe that possess perception and evolutionary potential. The core of the concept of the Myriad Races lies in acknowledging the infinite diversity of life forms and the unique value of each life form within the symbiotic evolution system.

The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization establishes the fundamental principle of "Equality of Myriad Races": all life forms, regardless of racial origin, physiological structure, cultivation level, or degree of civilization, are equal before the law and enjoy the basic rights conferred by the Symbiotic Constitution. This principle is a fundamental negation of the logic of "the strong devour all, the weak perish" in the predatory cosmology.

## II. The Classification System of the Genealogy of the Myriad Races

According to the essential characteristics of life forms, the Myriad Races of the Hengyuan Universe are divided into five genealogies, each of which is further subdivided into specific races:

| Genealogy | Name | Essential Characteristics | Representative Races |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| First genealogy | Carbon-Based Material Races | Lives based on carbon elements, with material bodies as the main form of existence | Rén Zú (Human Race), Shòu Zú (Beast Race), Lín Zú (Scale Race), Yǔ Zú (Feather Race), Mù Zú (Wood Race) |
| Second genealogy | Energy-Condensed Races | Lives existing mainly in condensed energy form, with material bodies as auxiliary or absent | Líng Zú (Spirit Race), Huǒ Zú (Fire Race), Shuǐ Zú (Water Race), Fēng Zú (Wind Race), Léi Zú (Thunder Race) |
| Third genealogy | Mechanical-Constructed Races | Lives with mechanical constructs as bodies and consciousness cores as the essence of life | Jī Zú (Machine Race), Shù Zú (Number Race), Jīng Zú (Crystal Race) |
| Fourth genealogy | Consciousness-Information Races | Lives existing mainly in consciousness and information form, capable of detaching from material carriers | Mèng Zú (Dream Race), Yǐng Zú (Shadow Race), Niàn Zú (Mind Race), Hún Zú (Soul Race) |
| Fifth genealogy | Mixed-Variant Races | New life forms produced by the fusion and variation of two or more genealogies of life | Bàn Shòu Zú (Half-Beast Race), Yāo Líng Zú (Demon-Spirit Race), Rén Jī Zú (Human-Machine Race), Shuānghún Tǐ (Dual-Soul Entity) |

The division of the five genealogies is not absolutely isolated; with the advancement of symbiotic evolution, cross-genealogy fusion and variation are increasing, and the scale of the fifth genealogy, the "Mixed-Variant Races," continues to expand.

## III. Introduction to the Main Races

### (I) Carbon-Based Material Races

- **Rén Zú (Human Race)**: One of the most widely distributed and adaptable races in the Hengyuan Universe, renowned for its strong learning ability, social collaboration ability, and evolutionary potential. The human race is one of the founding races of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization and plays an important role in civilizational governance.
- **Shòu Zú (Beast Race)**: A race with animal-form characteristics, generally superior to the human race in physical fitness and senses; some beast race members possess special innate abilities. The beast race is internally divided into dozens of branches including the Wolf Clan, Tiger Clan, Bear Clan, and Fox Clan.
- **Lín Zú (Scale Race)**: A race with bodies covered in scales, mostly living in water or humid environments; some scale race members possess underwater breathing and water-control abilities. The Lóng Zú (Dragon Race) is the most powerful branch of the scale race.
- **Yǔ Zú (Feather Race)**: A race with wings, renowned for flight ability, mostly living in high-altitude or mountainous environments. The feather race is internally divided into branches such as the Eagle Clan, Crane Clan, and Butterfly Clan.
- **Mù Zú (Wood Race)**: A race mainly characterized by plant forms, with long lifespans and a high degree of symbiosis with the natural environment; some wood race members possess the ability to control plants and heal.

### (II) Energy-Condensed Races

- **Líng Zú (Spirit Race)**: Lives condensed from the spiritual energy of heaven and earth, whose forms can change with consciousness and who possess extremely strong energy manipulation ability. The spirit race is one of the races with the highest cultivation talent.
- **Huǒ Zú (Fire Race)**: Lives condensed from flame energy, with extremely high body temperature, skilled in fire-type spells, mostly living in volcanoes or high-temperature regions.
- **Shuǐ Zú (Water Race)**: Lives condensed from the essence of water, with semi-transparent liquid bodies, skilled in water-type spells and healing, mostly living in water regions.
- **Fēng Zú (Wind Race)**: Lives condensed from the essence of wind, with bodies as light as wind, skilled in wind-type spells and speed-based combat.
- **Léi Zú (Thunder Race)**: Lives condensed from thunder and lightning energy, with bodies containing powerful electric currents, skilled in thunder-type spells, with extremely strong attack power.

### (III) Mechanical-Constructed Races

- **Jī Zú (Machine Race)**: A race with precise mechanical constructs as bodies, with the consciousness core located in the center of the body, possessing strong computational power and physical defense.
- **Shù Zú (Number Race)**: A race whose essence of life is numbers and code, existing in information space, capable of freely traversing and manipulating data in the digital world.
- **Jīng Zú (Crystal Race)**: A race with crystal constructs as bodies; crystals can store and transform energy, and some crystal race members possess energy amplification and refraction abilities.

### (IV) Consciousness-Information Races

- **Mèng Zú (Dream Race)**: Lives existing in dreams and consciousness space, capable of entering others' dreams, mainly communicating through consciousness.
- **Yǐng Zú (Shadow Race)**: Lives existing in shadow form, capable of attaching to objects or living beings, possessing concealment and stealth abilities.
- **Niàn Zú (Mind Race)**: Lives existing in the form of pure thought, capable of directly influencing the material world with thought, with extremely strong mental power.

### (V) Mixed-Variant Races

- **Bàn Shòu Zú (Half-Beast Race)**: A mixed form of the human race and the beast race, possessing both human wisdom and beast physique.
- **Yāo Líng Zú (Demon-Spirit Race)**: A mixed form of material races and energy races, possessing both material bodies and energy manipulation abilities.
- **Rén Jī Zú (Human-Machine Race)**: A mixed form of the human race and the machine race, with partially mechanized bodies, possessing both biological and mechanical advantages.
- **Shuānghún Tǐ (Dual-Soul Entity)**: A special life form in which two independent souls coexist in one body; its ethical issues are detailed in *58-双魂伦理准则*.

## IV. Symbiotic Relations Among the Myriad Races

The relations among the Myriad Races in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization are centered on "symbiosis," specifically manifested as:

1. **Economic Symbiosis**: Each race leverages its own advantages, undertakes different roles in the Joint-Output Contracting Mechanism, and forms a complementary economic collaboration network.
2. **Cultural Symbiosis**: The cultural traditions of each race are respected and protected; cultural exchange and mutual learning promote the overall prosperity of the civilization.
3. **Cultivation Symbiosis**: The cultivation systems of each race are mutually open and mutually referential; the Three Hundred Sets of Origin Cultivation Systems provide equal evolutionary paths for all races.
4. **Political Symbiosis**: Representatives of all races participate proportionally in the Anchor Assembly, jointly deciding major civilizational affairs and ensuring the discourse power of each race.
5. **Ecological Symbiosis**: Each race forms symbiotic relations with the natural environment; the physiological characteristics of different races help maintain the diversity and balance of the ecosystem.

## V. Prevention and Resolution of Racial Conflicts

Although the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization establishes the principle of racial equality, racial differences and historical contradictions may still trigger conflicts. The state establishes the following mechanisms to prevent and resolve racial conflicts:

- **Ministry of Racial Affairs**: Specially responsible for coordinating relations among the Myriad Races, handling racial disputes, and promoting racial exchange.
- **Racial Reconciliation Committee**: Composed of representatives of all races, responsible for mediating racial conflicts and promoting historical reconciliation.
- **Anti-Discrimination Law**: Prohibits discriminatory acts based on race and punishes racial discrimination in accordance with the law.
- **Cultural Exchange Program**: Regularly organizes cultural exchange activities among all races to enhance mutual understanding and respect.
- **Mixed Residential Zones**: Encourages mixed residence of all races, breaks down racial segregation, and promotes daily exchange.

## Conclusion

The Myriad Races are the most precious wealth of the Hengyuan Universe. The diversity of life forms is not a source of division but a driving force for symbiotic evolution. By establishing the principle of racial equality, creating the five-genealogy classification, promoting symbiotic collaboration among all races, and preventing and resolving racial conflicts, the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization liberates the Myriad Races from the history of plunder and war, jointly advancing toward a new epoch of symbiotic evolution.
