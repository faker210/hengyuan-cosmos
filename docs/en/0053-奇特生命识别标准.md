## I. Definition and Scope of Anomalous Life

In the Wanling Hengmao Origin Universe (Hengyuan Universe), "anomalous life" refers to unknown life forms that cannot be classified into the five categories of the existing Myriad Races genealogy (Carbon-Based Material Races, Energy-Condensed Races, Mechanical-Constructed Races, Consciousness-Information Races, Mixed-Variant Races), or life forms that, although classifiable into a category, possess anomalous characteristics that may significantly affect the symbiotic order.

The causes of anomalous life are diverse, including but not limited to: natural gestation in plane fissures, accidental products of high-energy experiments, alien life invading across planes, prehistoric life awakening from ancient ruins, and new life forms produced by law variation. Due to the unknown nature and potential risks of anomalous life, establishing scientific and rigorous identification standards is a prerequisite for ensuring symbiotic security.

## II. Basic Principles of Identification

The identification of anomalous life follows the following basic principles:

| Principle | Connotation |
|------|------|
| Principle of cautious presumption | Before comprehensive identification is completed, presume that anomalous life has potential risks and take necessary isolation and observation measures |
| Principle of multi-dimensional assessment | Conduct comprehensive assessment from multiple dimensions including life form, energy characteristics, behavioral patterns, consciousness level, and risk rating |
| Principle of non-discrimination | The purpose of identification is to assess risks and determine symbiotic modes, not to make value judgments about or discriminate against anomalous life |
| Principle of dynamic updating | Identification standards are dynamically updated as understanding of anomalous life deepens, avoiding rigid classification constraints |
| Principle of symbiosis priority | Under the premise of controllable risk, prioritize incorporating anomalous life into the symbiotic system rather than simply eliminating it |

## III. Identification Dimensions and Standards

The identification of anomalous life is conducted from the following seven dimensions, each with specific assessment standards:

### (I) Life Form Dimension

Assess the material composition and mode of existence of anomalous life:

1. **Material composition**: What elements or energy constitute it, and whether it follows known physical laws.
2. **Mode of existence**: Whether it is a physical form, energy form, information form, or mixed form.
3. **Form stability**: Whether the form is stable, and whether it changes spontaneously or under external influence.
4. **Body size range**: The range of body size, and whether it can be scaled or changed.
5. **Life cycle**: Whether it has life stages such as birth, growth, reproduction, and death, and the length of its lifespan.

### (II) Energy Characteristics Dimension

Assess the energy attributes and energy level of anomalous life:

| Assessment Item | Standard |
|--------|------|
| Energy type | Whether it belongs to known or unknown energy types, and whether the energy attribute is affinitive or repulsive |
| Energy level | Energy concentration and power level, from level one (faint) to level ten (cosmic) |
| Energy stability | Whether the energy is stable, and whether there is a risk of loss of control or self-detonation |
| Energy affinity | The degree of affinity with the spiritual energy of heaven and earth and Origin energy, and whether it can absorb and transform them |
| Energy pollution | Whether it will pollute or destructively affect the surrounding energy environment |
| Energy infectivity | Whether the energy is infectious, and whether it will infect or transform other life |

### (III) Consciousness Level Dimension

Assess the consciousness and intelligence level of anomalous life:

1. **Perception ability**: Whether it can perceive the outside world, and the range and manner of perception.
2. **Self-awareness**: Whether it has self-cognition and self-awareness, and can distinguish itself from the outside world.
3. **Intelligence level**: The level of learning ability, logical reasoning ability, and problem-solving ability.
4. **Emotional ability**: Whether it has emotional experiences, and the types and intensity of emotions.
5. **Communication ability**: Whether it can communicate with other life, and the manner and efficiency of communication.
6. **Moral cognition**: Whether it has moral judgment ability, and can understand the symbiotic concept and legal norms.

### (IV) Behavioral Pattern Dimension

Assess the behavioral characteristics and tendencies of anomalous life:

- **Aggressiveness**: Whether it has a tendency to actively attack other life, and the trigger conditions and manner of attack.
- **Predation**: Whether it has behavioral patterns of plundering other life's energy or resources.
- **Sociality**: Whether it has social behavior, whether solitary or gregarious, and what its social structure is.
- **Reproduction method**: The manner and speed of reproduction, and whether there is a risk of over-reproduction.
- **Migration habits**: Whether it has migration or dispersal habits, and the range and speed of dispersal.
- **Stress response**: Its response pattern when threatened or stimulated, and whether it will produce uncontrollable destruction.

### (V) Environmental Impact Dimension

Assess the impact of anomalous life on the surrounding environment:

1. **Ecological impact**: Its impact on the local ecosystem, and whether it will destroy ecological balance.
2. **Energy impact**: Its impact on the surrounding energy environment, and whether it will cause energy disorder or depletion.
3. **Spatial impact**: Its impact on spatial structure, and whether it will cause spatial fissures or spatial instability.
4. **Law impact**: Its impact on cosmic laws, and whether it will interfere with or distort local law operation.
5. **Pollution impact**: Whether it will produce material, energy, or information pollution, and the scope and duration of pollution.

### (VI) Symbiotic Potential Dimension

Assess the potential of anomalous life to be incorporated into the symbiotic system:

| Assessment Item | Standard |
|--------|------|
| Communication feasibility | Whether an effective communication channel can be established |
| Concept acceptance | Whether it can understand and accept the symbiotic concept |
| Collaboration possibility | Whether it can collaborate and help with other life |
| Value contribution | The contribution and value it may make after being incorporated into the symbiotic system |
| Integration difficulty | The difficulty and time required to integrate into the existing symbiotic system |
| Long-term stability | The stability and sustainability of the long-term symbiotic relationship |

### (VII) Risk Rating Dimension

Comprehensively considering the assessment results of the above dimensions, determine the risk rating of anomalous life:

| Risk Rating | Name | Criteria | Disposition Direction |
|----------|------|----------|----------|
| First level | Low risk | Relatively high consciousness level, no aggressiveness, controllable environmental impact, high symbiotic potential | Friendly symbiotic disposition |
| Second level | Medium risk | Average consciousness level, some aggressiveness or environmental impact, requires guidance and monitoring | Observation and isolation disposition |
| Third level | High risk | Low or malicious consciousness level, strong aggressiveness, severe environmental damage, low symbiotic potential | Dissolution and transformation disposition |
| Fourth level | Extremely high risk | Possesses cosmic-level destructive power, cannot communicate, uncontrollable, threatens civilizational survival | Emergency dissolution + reporting |

## IV. Identification Process

The identification of anomalous life follows the following standardized process:

1. **Discovery and reporting**: Any organization or individual that discovers anomalous life should immediately report to the local life administration agency.
2. **Preliminary isolation**: After receiving the report, the life administration agency should immediately conduct preliminary isolation of the anomalous life to prevent its spread or harm.
3. **Identification team formation**: Form an identification team composed of life scientists, cultivators, alien race representatives, legal experts, and others.
4. **Multi-dimensional assessment**: The identification team conducts a comprehensive assessment of the anomalous life according to the seven dimensions, forming an assessment report.
5. **Risk rating**: Determine the risk rating based on the assessment results and propose disposition recommendations.
6. **Review and approval**: Submit the assessment report and disposition recommendations to the higher-level life administration agency for review and approval; those at high risk or above must be filed with the Anomalous Life Tribunal of the Supreme Symbiotic Court.
7. **Disposition implementation**: Implement the disposition according to the approved plan, with the entire process recorded.
8. **Follow-up monitoring**: Conduct follow-up monitoring after the disposition is completed, evaluate the disposition effect, and adjust the disposition plan when necessary.

## V. Identification Institutions and Personnel

- **Life Administration Bureau**: A specialized national institution for the management of anomalous life, responsible for the identification, registration, disposition, and monitoring of anomalous life.
- **Identification expert group**: An identification team composed of experts from various fields, responsible for specific identification and assessment work.
- **On-site disposition team**: Responsible for on-site isolation, control, and disposition implementation of anomalous life, equipped with professional equipment and protective measures.
- **Anomalous Life Tribunal**: A specialized tribunal established by the Symbiotic Court, responsible for legal disputes involving anomalous life and judicial review of high-risk dispositions.

## Conclusion

The identification standards for anomalous life are the scientific defense line of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization in responding to unknown life forms. By establishing a multi-dimensional assessment system, a risk rating system, and a standardized identification process, it ensures comprehensive understanding, accurate assessment, and appropriate disposition of anomalous life. While safeguarding symbiotic security, it retains channels for incorporating possible new life forms into the symbiotic system, embodying the governance wisdom of "cautious yet not exclusive, safe yet not closed."
