## I. Definition and Scope of Anomalous Energy

In the Wanling Hengmao Origin Universe (Hengyuan Universe), "anomalous energy" refers to energy forms that do not belong to the conventional energy types covered by the known Three Hundred Sets of Origin Cultivation Systems, and that possess anomalous characteristics or unknown origins. The difference between anomalous energy and anomalous life is: anomalous life is an existence with life characteristics, while anomalous energy is an energy form that does not possess complete life characteristics but may have anomalous activity.

The sources of anomalous energy include but are not limited to: alien universe energy leaking from plane fissures, prehistoric energy sealed in ancient ruins, variant energy produced by high-energy experiments, unknown energy naturally formed in the depths of the universe, and special energy condensed from law fragments. The unknown nature and potential danger of anomalous energy make it a key target of energy governance in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization.

## II. Classification System of Anomalous Energy

According to its essential characteristics and behavioral patterns, anomalous energy is divided into five major categories, each further subdivided into specific types:

### (I) Classification by Energy Essence

| Category | Name | Essential Characteristics | Representative Types |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| First category | Elemental variant type | Varied from known elemental energy, retaining some elemental characteristics but with anomalous manifestations | Darkfire, Netherwater, Astral Wind, Violet Thunder |
| Second category | Law-derived type | Directly condensed from fragments of cosmic laws, possessing law-level power | Temporal Energy, Spatial Energy, Causal Energy, Destiny Energy |
| Third category | Consciousness-projection type | Energy formed by the projection of powerful consciousness or collective thought, possessing consciousness attributes | Telekinesis, Faith Power, Vow Power, Curse Power |
| Fourth category | Alien-intrusion type | Alien energy from other universes or planes, not following the conventional laws of this universe | Void Energy, Chaotic Energy, Abyssal Energy, Holy Light Energy |
| Fifth category | Mixed-variant type | New energy produced by the mixed variation of two or more types of energy, with complex and changeable characteristics | Chaotic Thunderfire, Fissure Spacetime Energy, Life-Death Mixed Energy |

### (II) Classification by Energy Behavioral Pattern

- **Stable type**: Energy with stable properties, not spontaneously changing or spreading, relatively easy to control.
- **Active type**: Energy with active properties, spontaneously fluctuating or spreading, requiring continuous monitoring.
- **Erosive type**: Possessing the characteristic of eroding other energy or matter, continuously assimilating the surrounding environment.
- **Devouring type**: Possessing the characteristic of devouring other energy to strengthen itself, with a risk of uncontrolled growth.
- **Infectious type**: Possessing infectivity, capable of infecting other energy or life forms and converting them into the same type of energy.
- **Self-destructive type**: Energy that is unstable, with a risk of spontaneous explosion or annihilation.
- **Intelligent type**: Energy possessing preliminary consciousness or intelligence, capable of autonomous action and decision-making.

### (III) Classification by Energy Risk Rating

Referring to the risk rating system of anomalous life, anomalous energy is also divided into four risk ratings:

| Risk Rating | Name | Criteria | Disposition Direction |
|----------|------|----------|----------|
| First level | Low risk | Stable properties, no erosiveness, usable, controllable environmental impact | Development and utilization disposition |
| Second level | Medium risk | Relatively active properties, some erosiveness or diffusivity, requires control | Sealing and monitoring disposition |
| Third level | High risk | Strong erosiveness/devouring/infectivity, uncontrollable, severe environmental damage | Purification and dissolution disposition |
| Fourth level | Extremely high risk | Possesses cosmic-level destructive power, law-level threat, uncontrollable, threatens civilizational survival | Emergency dissolution + reporting |

## III. Detailed Explanation of Major Anomalous Energy Types

### (I) Elemental Variant Type

- **Darkfire**: Varied from fire elements, dark black in color, extremely high temperature but non-luminous, possessing the characteristic of devouring light. Darkfire can burn substances that conventional flames cannot burn, including energy and space.
- **Netherwater**: Varied from water elements, dark green in color, possessing corrosive and death attributes, capable of eroding the vitality of life forms. Netherwater is commonly used in necromantic spells and corrosive cultivation.
- **Astral Wind**: Varied from wind elements, extremely strong with cutting attributes, capable of cutting matter and energy. Astral Wind mostly originates at high altitudes and near spatial fissures.
- **Violet Thunder**: Varied from thunder elements, purple in color, with power far exceeding conventional thunder and lightning, possessing the characteristic of disrupting laws. Violet Thunder is a cultivation resource coveted by advanced cultivators, but the risk of obtaining it is extremely high.

### (II) Law-Derived Type

- **Temporal Energy**: Condensed from fragments of the law of time, capable of accelerating or slowing local time flow, or even performing short-distance time jumps. Temporal Energy is extremely rare and unstable; improper use may cause time paradoxes.
- **Spatial Energy**: Condensed from fragments of the law of space, capable of performing spatial folding, spatial teleportation, spatial cutting, and other operations. Spatial Energy is the core resource of the spatial legal principle dispatch system.
- **Causal Energy**: Condensed from fragments of the law of causality, capable of intervening in causal chains and changing the causal relationships of events. The use of Causal Energy is strictly restricted to prevent causal disorder.
- **Destiny Energy**: Condensed from fragments of the law of destiny, capable of influencing the fate trajectories of life forms and altering fortune and luck. The use of Destiny Energy involves profound ethical issues and is subject to the strictest control.

### (III) Consciousness-Projection Type

- **Telekinesis**: Energy formed by the projection of powerful thought, capable of directly manipulating matter and energy with the mind. Telekinesis is the innate ability of the Niàn Zú (Mind Race); other races can also master it through cultivation.
- **Faith Power**: Energy condensed from the faith and worship of a large number of believers, possessing sacred attributes and healing abilities. Faith Power is the core resource of divine cultivation systems, but excessive reliance on Faith Power may cause one's consciousness to be assimilated by believers.
- **Vow Power**: Energy condensed from the beautiful wishes of all beings, possessing creative and blessing attributes. Vow Power is one of the important energy sources of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization; spreading the symbiotic concept can accumulate Vow Power.
- **Curse Power**: Energy condensed from intense resentment and malice, possessing destructive and cursing attributes. Curse Power is dangerous negative energy; improper use will rebound upon the user and is subject to strict control.

### (IV) Alien-Intrusion Type

- **Void Energy**: Alien energy from the void of the universe, possessing the characteristics of nothingness and annihilation, capable of dissolving matter and energy. Void Energy is a cosmic-level dangerous energy and must be researched and utilized under strictly isolated conditions.
- **Chaotic Energy**: Primordial energy from the chaotic state before the birth of the universe, possessing infinite possibilities and uncontrollability. Chaotic Energy can be transformed into any type of energy, but the transformation process is extremely dangerous.
- **Abyssal Energy**: Dark energy from the abyss of the universe, possessing corrosive and corrupting characteristics, capable of eroding the consciousness and body of life forms. Abyssal Energy is closely related to the origin of Dark Replacement Entities (see *57-黑暗置换体起源与本质*).
- **Holy Light Energy**: Sacred energy from higher planes, possessing purifying and healing characteristics, capable of purifying dark energy and healing severe wounds. Holy Light Energy is an effective weapon against Abyssal Energy, but excessive use may cause life ascension to lose control.

## IV. Analysis of the Characteristics of Anomalous Energy

Compared with conventional energy, anomalous energy possesses the following common characteristics:

| Characteristic | Description |
|------|------|
| Unpredictability | The behavioral patterns of anomalous energy often do not conform to conventional energy laws and are difficult to predict precisely |
| High activity | Anomalous energy usually possesses high activity and easily reacts with other energy or matter |
| Exclusivity | Some anomalous energy possesses exclusivity, repelling or dissolving other types of energy |
| Infectivity | Some anomalous energy possesses infectivity, capable of infecting or converting other energy and life forms |
| Evolvability | Anomalous energy may evolve or vary over time, producing new characteristics |
| High value | Anomalous energy often possesses power and uses incomparable to conventional energy, with extremely high research and utilization value |
| High risk | The unknown nature and anomalous characteristics of anomalous energy make it highly risky; improper control may cause serious consequences |

## V. Detection and Monitoring of Anomalous Energy

To promptly discover and control anomalous energy, the state establishes an anomalous energy detection and monitoring system:

- **Energy monitoring network**: Establish energy monitoring stations throughout the universe, monitoring energy fluctuations in various regions in real time and promptly discovering anomalous energy signals.
- **Plane fissure monitoring**: Conduct focused monitoring of plane fissures to prevent alien energy from invading through fissures.
- **Ruins energy monitoring**: Conduct energy monitoring of ancient ruins and archaeological sites to prevent the leakage of sealed anomalous energy.
- **Experiment energy monitoring**: Conduct energy monitoring of high-energy experiments and cultivation breakthroughs to prevent accidentally produced anomalous energy from losing control.
- **Energy early warning system**: Establish an anomalous energy early warning system, issuing different levels of early warning information according to the risk rating of the energy.

## Conclusion

Anomalous energy is one of the most research-worthy and most dangerous existences in the Hengyuan Universe. By establishing a systematic classification system, deeply studying the characteristics of various types of anomalous energy, and establishing detection and monitoring networks, the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization lays a scientific foundation for the safe control and rational utilization of anomalous energy. In *56-奇异能量三类处置流程*, standardized disposition plans for anomalous energy at different risk ratings will be elaborated in detail.
