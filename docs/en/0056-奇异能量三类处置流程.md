## I. Overall Principles of the Disposition Process

The disposition of anomalous energy is the core link of energy governance in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. According to the risk rating results in *55-奇异能量分类与特性*, the disposition of anomalous energy is divided into three types of standardized processes: development and utilization type, sealing and monitoring type, and purification and dissolution type. The three types of processes correspond to the three ratings of low risk, medium risk, and high risk, forming a complete disposition system from utilization to control and then to elimination.

The disposition process follows the following overall principles:

| Principle | Connotation |
|------|------|
| Safety first principle | The primary goal of energy disposition is to ensure safety and prevent harm caused by energy losing control |
| Value maximization principle | Under the premise of safety and controllability, maximize the value utilization of anomalous energy as much as possible |
| Proportionality principle | The intensity of disposition measures should correspond to the risk rating of the anomalous energy |
| Minimal environmental impact principle | The disposition process should minimize its impact on the environment as much as possible, preventing energy pollution |
| Whole-process monitoring principle | Monitor the disposition process throughout, grasp the energy state in real time, and respond to anomalies promptly |

## II. First Type: Development and Utilization Disposition Process

The development and utilization disposition applies to anomalous energy identified and assessed as First level (low risk), namely energy forms with stable properties, no erosiveness, usability, and controllable environmental impact.

### (I) Disposition Goal

Incorporate anomalous energy into the civilization's energy resource system, conduct safe and efficient development and utilization, and transform it into beneficial resources that promote symbiotic evolution.

### (II) Disposition Steps

| Step | Name | Specific Content | Responsible Institution |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| Step one | Energy appraisal | Conduct a comprehensive appraisal of the anomalous energy, determining parameters such as its type, characteristics, purity, and usable value | Energy Administration Bureau + appraisal expert group |
| Step two | Safety assessment | Assess the safety of developing and utilizing the anomalous energy, including stability, reactivity, environmental impact, etc. | Safety assessment expert group |
| Step three | Utilization plan | Formulate a detailed development and utilization plan, including utilization methods, technical paths, safety measures, economic benefit analysis, etc. | Energy Administration Bureau + technology R&D institution |
| Step four | Pilot utilization | Conduct small-scale pilot utilization in a controlled environment to verify the feasibility and safety of the plan | Technology R&D institution + cultivators |
| Step five | Scale promotion | After the pilot succeeds, gradually expand the scale of utilization and incorporate the anomalous energy into the conventional energy resource system | Energy Administration Bureau + Resource Management Department |
| Step six | Standardized management | Formulate standards for the extraction, storage, transportation, and use of anomalous energy, incorporating them into the standardized management system | Standardization management institution |
| Step seven | Continuous monitoring | Conduct continuous monitoring of the entire development and utilization process, regularly assessing safety and utilization efficiency | Energy monitoring network |

### (III) Main Utilization Directions

| Utilization Direction | Applicable Energy Types | Specific Uses |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| Cultivation resource | Elemental variant type, law-derived type | As cultivation resources for advanced cultivators, improving cultivation efficiency and realms |
| Energy supply | Stable anomalous energy | Transformed into conventional energy, providing energy for cities, infrastructure, and plane development |
| Technological application | Spatial Energy, Temporal Energy | Used for spatial legal principle technology, cross-plane communication, time backtracking, and other high-end technologies |
| Medical application | Holy Light Energy, Vow Power | Used in medical fields such as healing severe wounds, purifying negative energy, and mental treatment |
| Weapon equipment | Violet Thunder, Astral Wind | Used to forge advanced weapons and protective equipment, enhancing the civilization's defense capability |
| Scientific research | All types of anomalous energy | Used in academic fields such as basic science research, law research, and cosmology research |

### (IV) Utilization Safety Regulations

- The extraction of anomalous energy must be conducted under the guidance of professionals, equipped with necessary protective equipment and emergency devices.
- The storage of anomalous energy must use specialized energy storage devices, and storage facilities must be equipped with energy shielding and safety protection systems.
- The transportation of anomalous energy must use specialized energy transport containers, and transport routes must avoid densely populated areas and ecologically sensitive areas.
- The use of anomalous energy must comply with operating procedures; excessive use or non-compliant operations are prohibited.
- Establish emergency response plans for anomalous energy utilization accidents and conduct regular emergency drills.

## III. Second Type: Sealing and Monitoring Disposition Process

The sealing and monitoring disposition applies to anomalous energy identified and assessed as Second level (medium risk), namely energy forms with relatively active properties, some erosiveness or diffusivity, requiring control.

### (I) Disposition Goal

Under the premise of ensuring safety, seal and continuously monitor the anomalous energy to prevent its spread or harm; meanwhile conduct continuous research to explore the possibility of safe utilization or purification.

### (II) Disposition Steps

| Step | Name | Specific Content | Responsible Institution |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| Step one | Energy containment | Use specialized energy containment devices to seal the anomalous energy and prevent its spread | On-site disposal team + technical expert group |
| Step two | Transfer and transportation | Transfer the sealed anomalous energy to a specialized energy sealing facility, with whole-process monitoring during transportation | Energy Administration Bureau + Safety Security Department |
| Step three | Facility sealing | Store the anomalous energy in a specialized sealing facility equipped with multiple layers of energy shielding, spatial stabilization, and safety protection systems | Energy sealing facility management institution |
| Step four | Continuous monitoring | Conduct 24-hour continuous monitoring of the sealed anomalous energy, monitoring parameters such as energy fluctuations, temperature, and pressure in real time | Monitoring team |
| Step five | Periodic inspection | Conduct periodic inspections of the anomalous energy to assess changes in its stability and risk rating | Appraisal expert group |
| Step six | Research and exploration | Conduct research on the anomalous energy under controlled conditions, exploring technical paths for safe utilization or purification and dissolution | Scientific research institution |
| Step seven | Type conversion | According to research and monitoring results, those whose risk drops to First level are converted to the development and utilization type, and those whose risk rises to Third level are converted to the purification and dissolution type | Energy Administration Bureau + Anomalous Life Tribunal |

### (III) Sealing Facility Standards

- **Site selection standards**: Sealing facilities are sited in areas with sparse population, stable geology, and stable energy environment, away from ecological protection zones and important infrastructure.
- **Construction standards**: Sealing facilities adopt multiple protective structures, including three layers of protection: physical barriers, energy barriers, and spatial locking, ensuring energy cannot leak.
- **Equipment standards**: Equipped with professional equipment such as high-precision energy monitoring devices, automatic containment systems, emergency purification systems, and backup energy systems.
- **Management standards**: Implement strict access management and operating regulations; only authorized professionals may enter the sealing area.
- **Emergency standards**: Equipped with complete emergency plans and emergency equipment, including energy leakage emergency handling, explosion emergency response, and personnel evacuation plans.

### (IV) Long-Term Sealing Management

For anomalous energy that still cannot be safely utilized or purified and dissolved after long-term research, long-term sealing management is implemented:

- Long-term sealed anomalous energy undergoes a comprehensive inspection and risk assessment once a year.
- Sealing facilities undergo a comprehensive overhaul and upgrade every five years to ensure the safety and reliability of the facilities.
- Information on long-term sealed anomalous energy is incorporated into the civilization's energy resource archives, with regular academic exchange and research cooperation.
- If new technical means emerge that can safely utilize or purify and dissolve long-term sealed anomalous energy, the re-assessment and disposition procedure should be initiated promptly.

## IV. Third Type: Purification and Dissolution Disposition Process

The purification and dissolution disposition applies to anomalous energy identified and assessed as Third level (high risk), namely energy forms with strong erosiveness/devouring/infectivity, uncontrollability, and severe environmental damage. Fourth level (extremely high risk) anomalous energy is subject to the emergency dissolution procedure.

### (I) Disposition Goal

Through purification or dissolution, eliminate the danger of the anomalous energy, transform it into harmless basic energy or completely dissolve it, ensuring civilizational and ecological safety.

### (II) Disposition Methods

The purification and dissolution disposition is divided into two methods, selected according to the specific situation of the anomalous energy:

| Method | Name | Applicable Situation | Specific Method |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| Method one | Energy purification | Anomalous energy that can be purified and is usable after purification | Use purification energies such as Holy Light Energy and Vow Power, or specialized purification devices and spells, to remove the dangerous attributes from the anomalous energy, transforming it into harmless usable conventional energy |
| Method two | Energy dissolution | Anomalous energy that cannot be purified or whose purification risk is too high | Use specialized dissolution devices or spells to decompose the anomalous energy into basic particles or nothingness, completely eliminating its existence and danger |

### (III) Disposition Steps

| Step | Name | Specific Content | Responsible Institution |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| Step one | Disposition approval | The purification and dissolution of high-risk anomalous energy must be jointly approved by the Energy Administration Bureau and the Anomalous Life Tribunal; extremely high risk may be disposed first and approved retroactively | Energy Administration Bureau + Anomalous Life Tribunal |
| Step two | Plan formulation | Formulate a detailed purification and dissolution plan, including disposition methods, technical means, safety measures, emergency plans, environmental impact assessment, etc. | Technical expert group + safety assessment expert group |
| Step three | Safety isolation | Transfer the anomalous energy to a specialized purification and dissolution facility equipped with the highest level of safety protection and energy containment devices | On-site disposal team |
| Step four | Purification and dissolution | Implement purification and dissolution according to the plan, with whole-process monitoring; upon any anomaly, immediately activate the emergency plan | Technical expert group + advanced cultivators |
| Step five | Product handling | Inspect the energy and matter produced by purification and dissolution; usable ones are incorporated into resource reserves, harmless ones are naturally discharged, and harmful ones undergo safe disposal | Resource management institution + Department of Ecological Environment |
| Step six | Environmental restoration | Restore the environment affected during the disposition process, restoring ecological and energy balance | Department of Ecological Environment |
| Step seven | Archive filing | Organize and archive the records, data, and reports of the entire disposition process, forming a complete disposition archive | Energy Administration Bureau |

### (IV) Emergency Dissolution Procedure

For Fourth level (extremely high risk) anomalous energy, namely anomalous energy with cosmic-level destructive power, law-level threat, uncontrollability, and direct threat to civilizational survival, the emergency dissolution procedure applies:

1. The highest-ranking person in charge on site may directly order the initiation of emergency dissolution without prior approval, but must report to the Energy Administration Bureau and the Anomalous Life Tribunal for filing within 24 hours after the disposition.
2. Emergency dissolution may mobilize the civilization's highest level of force and energy resources, including the joint action of the Supreme Powers of the Plane and the centralized invocation of Anchor energy.
3. The priority goal of emergency dissolution is to eliminate the threat and protect life and civilizational safety, not to pursue energy recovery and utilization.
4. After emergency dissolution, a comprehensive environmental assessment and restoration should be conducted to prevent secondary disasters and energy residue.
5. Emergency dissolution cases should undergo post-hoc review and analysis, summarizing lessons learned and improving the early warning and disposition mechanisms.

### (V) Main Purification and Dissolution Technologies

| Technology Name | Principle | Applicable Energy Types |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Holy Light Purification Art | Use the purifying attribute of Holy Light Energy to neutralize and dissolve dark and negative energy | Abyssal Energy, Curse Power, Netherwater |
| Vow Power Purification Array | Use collective vow power to construct a purification array, gently purifying anomalous energy | Consciousness-projection type, mildly erosive type |
| Energy Counterbalance Method | Use energy with opposite attributes to the anomalous energy for counterbalancing, causing mutual annihilation | Elemental variant type, opposite-attribute energy |
| Law Dissolution Art | Invoke spatial or temporal laws to banish the anomalous energy to another space or accelerate its decay | Law-derived type, high-concentration energy |
| Anchor Dissolution Array | Use the origin attributes of Anchor energy to decompose the anomalous energy and return it to the origin of the universe | Various types of high-risk anomalous energy |
| Mechanical Dissolution Device | Use high-tech energy dissolution devices to decompose energy structures through physical means | Stable type, energy amenable to physical processing |

## Conclusion

The three-type disposition process for anomalous energy is the standardized operational system of energy governance in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. From the value transformation of development and utilization, to the cautious control of sealing and monitoring, to the safe elimination of purification and dissolution, the three types of processes form a complete energy risk response chain, fully tapping the potential value of anomalous energy while firmly holding the bottom line of civilizational safety, achieving the energy governance goal of "equal emphasis on utilization and control, and simultaneous consideration of development and safety".
