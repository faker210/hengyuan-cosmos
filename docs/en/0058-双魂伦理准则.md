## I. The Raising of the Dual-Soul Ethics Problem

The dual-soul ethics problem is the fundamental ethical problem that the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization must answer when facing Dark Replacement Entities (see *57-黑暗置换体起源与本质*). When a single body contains two independent souls — the original host soul and the dark replacement soul — a series of profound ethical problems arise:

- Do both souls possess independent personality rights?
- Do the rights of the original host soul take precedence over those of the dark replacement soul?
- Is it moral to annihilate the dark replacement soul in order to protect the original host soul?
- When the interests of the two souls conflict, how should they be weighed?
- Does society have an obligation to invest resources in rescuing original host souls that have been darkly replaced?
- Do dark replacement souls have a "right to exist"?

These problems have no simple answers; systematic ethical principles must be established to guide practice. The Dual-Soul Ethics Code is precisely the normative document formulated in response to these problems, and is the ethical foundation for the identification, treatment, and disposition of Dark Replacement Entities.

## II. Basic Principles of Dual-Soul Ethics

The Dual-Soul Ethics Code establishes the following six basic principles as the value cornerstone for handling dual-soul problems:

| Principle | Connotation |
|------|------|
| Soul dignity principle | Every soul with independent consciousness enjoys inherent dignity and shall not be arbitrarily annihilated or enslaved |
| Original-soul priority principle | In dual-soul conflicts, the legitimate rights of the original host soul take precedence over those of the dark replacement soul, because the original host is the legitimate owner of the body |
| Least harm principle | When handling dual-soul problems, the option causing the least harm to all relevant parties should be chosen, avoiding the annihilation of souls as much as possible |
| Proportionality principle | The measures taken should correspond to the degree of risk of the dual-soul entity; excessive disposition is prohibited |
| Informed consent principle | When the original host soul can express its will, major decisions involving the original host's body and soul should obtain the original host's consent |
| Best-effort rescue principle | Society has the obligation to make every reasonable effort to rescue original host souls that have been darkly replaced, and shall not lightly give up |

## III. Determination of Personality Rights of the Two Souls

The primary problem of dual-soul ethics is the determination of the personality rights of the two souls. This Code establishes the following determination rules:

### (I) Personality Rights of the Original Host Soul

The original host soul is the legitimate owner of the body, and its personality rights are fully recognized and protected:

1. The original host soul enjoys all the basic rights conferred by the Symbiotic Constitution, including the right to life, the right to physical integrity, the right to health, the right to personal dignity, etc.
2. The original host soul has the final decision-making power over its own body; when it can express its will, any major disposition involving the body must obtain the original host's consent.
3. The original host soul's legitimate rights and interests, such as property rights, identity rights, and social relations, are protected by law and are not lost due to dark replacement.
4. The original host soul has the right to receive social rescue and support, including purification treatment, legal aid, psychological counseling, etc.
5. Even if deeply suppressed, the personality rights of the original host soul still exist; it shall not be declared dead or deprived of rights.

### (II) Personality Rights of the Dark Replacement Soul

The determination of the personality rights of the dark replacement soul is relatively complex; this Code adopts the position of "limited recognition":

| Development Stage | Personality Rights Determination | Scope of Rights |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| Energy erosion period (First stage) | No independent personality | An independent soul has not yet formed; no independent personality rights |
| Soul contamination period (Second stage) | No independent personality | Still a contaminated state of the original host soul; no independent personality rights |
| Dual-soul formation period (Third stage) | Limited personality rights | Has preliminary independent consciousness, enjoys basic soul dignity rights, but does not enjoy body control rights or full citizenship rights |
| Dark dominance period (Fourth stage) | Limited personality rights | Has fully independent consciousness, enjoys soul dignity rights and the right to be treated with respect, but due to its danger, its rights are subject to necessary restrictions |
| Complete replacement period (Fifth stage) | Full personality rights (but as a hostile existence) | The original host has perished, the dark soul becomes the only soul of the body, with full personality rights, but due to its hostile behavior, compulsory measures including annihilation may be taken in accordance with the law |

### (III) Specific Content of Limited Personality Rights

The limited personality rights enjoyed by the dark replacement soul in the third and fourth stages include:

1. **Right to soul dignity**: It shall not be treated with insult, abuse, or torture; basic respect should be maintained even in control and disposition.
2. **Right to painless annihilation**: If it is finally decided to annihilate the dark replacement soul, a painless, rapid method should be adopted to reduce its suffering.
3. **Right of expression**: Under safe and controllable conditions, the dark replacement soul has the right to express its will and position, but its expression has no legal binding force.
4. **Right to be heard**: When making major decisions involving the dark replacement soul, its opinion should be heard within a reasonable scope.

The dark replacement soul does not enjoy the following rights: body control rights, freedom of movement, citizen political rights, property ownership (the original host's property belongs to the original host), and reproductive rights (to prevent the spread of dark bloodlines).

## IV. Weighing Rules for Dual-Soul Interest Conflicts

When the interests of the original host soul and the dark replacement soul conflict, they are weighed according to the following rules:

### (I) Conflict Types and Weighing Rules

| Conflict Type | Weighing Rule |
|----------|---------|
| Survival rights conflict (one party's survival is conditional on the other party's demise) | The survival right of the original host soul takes precedence; when coexistence is impossible, the survival of the original host is protected first |
| Body control rights conflict | The original host soul has the final control right over the body; the dark soul's body control right is strictly restricted |
| Expression rights conflict | The expression of the original host soul has legal binding force; the expression of the dark soul serves only as reference |
| Property rights conflict | The body and property belong to the original host; the dark soul has no property rights |
| Treatment choice rights conflict | The original host soul has the right to choose whether to accept purification treatment; the dark soul has no right to prevent it |
| Disposition method conflict | For dispositions involving the annihilation of the dark soul, the will of the original host soul has a decisive role (but must undergo ethical review) |

### (II) Respect for and Limitation of the Original Host's Will

When the original host soul can express its will, the original host's will should be fully respected, with the following exceptions:

1. Wills of the original host that clearly harm public safety or the legitimate rights and interests of others are not protected.
2. Expressions of will made by the original host under the influence of the dark soul that are not genuine are not legally valid.
3. If the original host voluntarily chooses to fuse with the dark soul, strict ethical review and risk assessment should be conducted to confirm that the existence after fusion will not pose a threat to society.
4. If the original host voluntarily chooses to give up treatment and accept complete replacement by the dark soul, society should intervene and counsel, but if the original host is fully lucid and repeatedly confirms, the original host's autonomous decision-making right should ultimately be respected (this is an ethical gray area requiring case-by-case review).

## V. Dual-Soul Ethical Review Mechanism

To ensure that the handling of dual-soul problems complies with ethical principles, the state establishes a dual-soul ethical review mechanism:

### (I) Ethics Review Committee

- **Composition**: Composed of ethicists, jurists, life scientists, cultivators, representatives of alien races, and representatives of the general public; experts with experience in Dark Replacement Entity research or treatment shall account for no less than one third.
- **Duties**: Review and adjudicate major ethical problems involving dual souls, including decisions to annihilate dark souls, approval of dual-soul fusion, and determination of the validity of the original host's will.
- **Independence**: The Ethics Review Committee exercises its authority independently and is free from interference by administrative organs and individuals.
- **Procedure**: Review adopts a hearing system, making adjudications after hearing opinions from the original host (if able to express), the treatment team, family members, social representatives, and all parties.

### (II) Matters That Must Undergo Ethical Review

The following matters must be reviewed and approved by the Dual-Soul Ethics Review Committee before implementation:

1. Implementing disposition that annihilates the dark soul for Dark Replacement Entities in the third and fourth stages.
2. Implementing dual-soul fusion surgery on Dark Replacement Entities.
3. Implementing soul separation surgery on Dark Replacement Entities.
4. Making final disposition decisions for Dark Replacement Entities whose original host's will is to give up treatment.
5. Implementing treatment plans that may cause damage to the original host soul.
6. Conducting scientific research involving souls on Dark Replacement Entities.
7. Other matters involving major dual-soul ethical problems.

## VI. Social Rights and Obligations of Dual-Soul Entities

### (I) Social Rights

While the original host soul still exists, the dual-soul entity (Dark Replacement Entity) enjoys the following social rights:

1. The right to receive basic livelihood security and medical treatment.
2. The right to receive legal aid and ethical consultation.
3. The right to contact relatives and friends under safe and controllable conditions.
4. The right to receive education and participate in cultural activities (within isolation facilities).
5. The right to appeal and request review of disposition decisions.

### (II) Social Obligations and Restrictions

The dual-soul entity simultaneously bears the following obligations and restrictions:

1. The obligation to accept isolation control and treatment.
2. The obligation not to attack or infect other life forms (violators will be subject to stricter control).
3. The obligation to cooperate with identification assessment and monitoring.
4. Freedom of movement is strictly restricted; it shall not leave the isolation facility before being cured.
5. It shall not engage in cultivation, the spread of dark energy, or other activities that may aggravate replacement.

## VII. Frontier Controversies in Dual-Soul Ethics

There remain some frontier controversies in the field of dual-soul ethics that have not yet reached consensus and need continuous exploration in practice:

1. **The "original sin" problem of the dark soul**: The dark replacement soul is destructive from birth. Does this mean it is inherently "evil"? Is there a possibility of a "benevolent" dark soul?
2. **The ethical legitimacy of soul fusion**: The new soul produced by dual-soul fusion is neither the original host nor the dark soul. How should the ethical status of this "third existence" be determined?
3. **The limit of resource investment**: How many resources should society invest to rescue the original host soul within a Dark Replacement Entity? When the rescue cost is extremely high and the success rate extremely low, is there still an obligation to make best efforts to rescue?
4. **The possibility of reforming the dark soul**: Can the dark soul be reformed into a non-destructive existence through education, reformation, energy transformation, and other means? If so, should the reformed dark soul obtain full personality rights?
5. **The balance between prevention and privacy**: To prevent the generation of Dark Replacement Entities, is it permissible to monitor living beings for Abyssal Energy exposure? Does such monitoring violate soul privacy?

These controversial problems are continuously studied by dual-soul ethics research institutions; the Ethics Review Committee makes adjudications in individual cases according to specific circumstances, and the relevant conclusions will continuously update this Code.

## Conclusion

The Dual-Soul Ethics Code is a profound manifestation of the life ethics of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. By establishing basic principles such as soul dignity, original-soul priority, and least harm, determining the boundaries of the personality rights of the two souls, and establishing interest conflict weighing rules and ethical review mechanisms, it provides a solid ethical foundation for the governance of Dark Replacement Entities. The core spirit of dual-soul ethics is: even when facing the most dangerous enemy, do not give up the steadfast defense of life dignity; even under the most urgent security threat, do not cross the ethical bottom line. This is precisely the fundamental mark that distinguishes the symbiotic civilization from the predatory civilization.
