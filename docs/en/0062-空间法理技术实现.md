## I. The Theoretical Foundation of Spatial Legal Principle Technology

The spatial legal principle dispatch system is the third of the Three Hundred Sets of Origin Cultivation Systems, listed as one of the "Three Foundations of Origin". This system takes the understanding and dispatch of spatial laws as its core, emphasizing precise manipulation of spatial legal principles rather than simple spatial tearing. Spatial legal principle technology is precisely the product of engineering the law mechanism of this cultivation system, and is one of the core pillars of the Law Engineering Layer in the technological system of the Hengyuan Universe.

The theoretical foundation of spatial legal principle technology is built upon a threefold understanding of the essence of space:

- **Space is a container of laws**: space is not nothingness, but the bearing structure of origin laws; the attributes of space are determined by the laws operating within it.
- **Space is a dispatchable resource**: through precise manipulation of spatial legal principles, the folding, extension, isolation, and interconnection of space can be achieved.
- **Space is an ethical existence**: spatial manipulation must follow symbiotic ethics, and must not come at the cost of destroying the spatial structures of other planes.

## II. The Core Modules of Spatial Legal Principle Technology

In engineering, spatial legal principle technology is decomposed into five core modules, each corresponding to one realm level of the spatial legal principle dispatch system:

| Module | Corresponding Realm | Technical Function | Engineering Implementation |
|------|----------|----------|--------------|
| Space Perception Module | Space Perception (Shíkōng) | High-precision scanning and modeling of the spatial structure of a target area | Law resonance sensor array, radar-like principle |
| Space Manipulation Module | Space Mastery (Yùkōng) | Limited deformation and dispatch of space, such as spatial folding | Miniature spatial legal principle engine, energy-driven spatial curvature adjustment |
| Space Transformation Module | Space Transformation (Huàkōng) | Transformation and reconstruction of matter and energy in the spatial dimension | Space-matter conversion array, requiring high-concentration energy support |
| Spatial Law Module | Law Space (Fǎkōng) | Directly rewriting the operating laws of local space, constructing custom law spaces | Law programming interface, the core technology of isolated spaces |
| Spatial Origin Module | Source Space (Yuánkōng) | Touching the origin level of spatial laws, achieving cross-plane spatial interconnection | Anchor-level spatial legal principle core, deployed only at the main Anchor |

The five modules progress according to realm levels; lower-level modules have high technical maturity and can be mass-produced and popularized, while higher-level modules are technically difficult and limited in deployment. Currently, the spatial legal principle technology of the Hengyuan Universe has fully mastered the first three modules; the fourth module is in the laboratory verification stage, and the fifth module has only completed deduction at the theoretical level.

## III. The Engineering Parameter System of Spatial Manipulation

Spatial legal principle technology has established a complete engineering parameter system for quantifying the precision and scope of spatial manipulation:

1. **Spatial curvature precision**: measures the fineness of spatial deformation; the unit is the "law unit" (fǎfēn); civilian-grade precision is 1 law unit, and research-grade can reach 0.001 law units.
2. **Spatial folding ratio**: the ratio of the distance after folding to the original distance; the current engineering upper limit is 1:1000, meaning one thousand kilometers can be folded into one kilometer.
3. **Spatial isolation degree**: the degree of law penetration between an isolated space and the main plane; the higher the isolation degree, the lower the pollution risk; the standard archaeological isolated space has an isolation degree of 99.97%.
4. **Spatial stability duration**: the sustained stable duration of artificially manipulated spatial structures, constrained by both energy supply and law precision.
5. **Spatial connectivity bandwidth**: the information and energy transmission rate of cross-space channels, a fundamental parameter of cross-plane communication technology.

These parameters are all incorporated into the energy standardization and metering system for unified management, ensuring interoperability of spatial legal principle equipment from different manufacturers and different planes.

## IV. Main Application Fields

The application of spatial legal principle technology covers multiple key fields:

- **Construction of isolated spaces**: providing safe, independent spatial environments for archaeological experiments and dangerous law research.
- **Replication Mirror system**: achieving high-precision mirror replication of target areas through spatial legal principles, the core of archaeological programs.
- **Cross-plane communication**: constructing cross-plane information channels using spatial folding and interconnection technology.
- **Transportation**: spatial folding channels greatly shorten interstellar travel time and reduce energy consumption.
- **Resource storage**: high-isolation spaces are used to store dangerous energy entities and anomalous matter.
- **Expansion of living space**: expanding living area on limited land through spatial extension technology.

## Conclusion

The technological implementation of spatial legal principle dispatch is a model case of engineering the origin cultivation system. It proves that cultivation laws can not only enhance individual capabilities, but can also be transformed into technological infrastructure shared by the entire civilization. As spatial legal principle technology advances from the Space Mastery layer to the Law Space layer, the Hengyuan Universe is gaining the ability to reshape spatial structures, laying the technological foundation for the construction of the cross-plane network in the Interstellar Symbiosis Stage.
