## I. The Definition and Status of Anchor Hardware

Anchor hardware is the material carrier of the Anchor mechanism, a physical interface that conducts the Origin Energy and information of the law network to the material plane. If the Anchor itself is a concept at the level of laws, then Anchor hardware is the engineering realization of this concept — it is a set of precise law engineering devices capable of establishing a stable Anchor connection at a designated spatial location, and distributing, regulating, and providing feedback on the energy and information conducted by the Anchor.

Anchor hardware and infrastructure are the most important material foundation of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. Without Anchor hardware, the opening of the Three Hundred Sets of Cultivation Systems would lack energy supply, the Joint-Output Contracting Mechanism would lack the means of resource dispatch, and cross-plane communication and the symbiotic network would lack physical connections. The coverage and operational quality of Anchor hardware directly determine the evolutionary efficiency of the symbiotic civilization.

## II. The Tiered System of Anchor Hardware

Anchor hardware is divided into four tiers according to function and scale:

| Tier | Name | Number Deployed | Core Functions | Energy Scale |
|------|------|----------|----------|----------|
| First Tier | Cosmic Main Anchor | 1 | The core hub of the cosmic Anchor network, the general entrance of Origin Energy | Giga Anchor Energy level |
| Second Tier | Plane Main Anchor | 1 per major plane | Plane-level energy dispatch and communication backbone nodes | Mega Anchor Energy level |
| Third Tier | Regional Anchor | Several per continent/star region | Regional-level energy distribution and communication access | Kilo Anchor Energy level |
| Fourth Tier | Community Anchor | Town/community level | End-stage energy supply and personal cultivation support | Anchor Energy level |

The four tiers of Anchor hardware constitute a complete coverage network from the cosmic core to every community. Higher-level Anchors distribute energy and information to lower-level Anchors, while lower-level Anchors report cultivation feedback and operational status back to higher-level Anchors, forming a "downward distribution and upward aggregation" flow structure of energy and information.

## III. The Core Components of Anchor Hardware

Each Anchor hardware installation (regardless of tier) contains the following core components:

1. **Law resonance core**: the heart of Anchor hardware, made of high-purity law crystals, responsible for establishing a stable resonance connection with the law network. The manufacturing of the law resonance core requires the collaboration of spatial legal principle technology and energy standardization technology, and is the component with the highest technical content in Anchor hardware.
2. **Energy distribution array**: responsible for distributing the Origin Energy conducted by the Anchor to different output channels according to preset proportions. The precision of the energy distribution array directly affects the fairness of resource allocation in the Joint-Output Contracting Mechanism.
3. **Information processing module**: contains a simplified version of the Multiplication Fruit Deduction Engine, responsible for processing the information flow in the Anchor network, including cultivation feedback, communication routing, and status monitoring.
4. **Symbiotic feedback interface**: a dedicated interface connected to the symbiotic feedback circulation system, responsible for collecting the evolution data of all cultivators and life forms in the local area and reporting it back to the Anchor network.
5. **Safety protection system**: includes multiple safety mechanisms such as law barriers, intrusion detection, and emergency cutoff, ensuring that Anchor hardware cannot be exploited by predators or subjected to law pollution.

## IV. The Supporting Infrastructure System

The operation of Anchor hardware requires the support of a complete set of supporting infrastructure:

- **Energy supply facilities**: provide continuous basic energy supply for Anchor hardware, including energy collection stations, energy storage arrays, and energy transmission pipeline networks.
- **Spatial legal principle facilities**: provide spatial stability and isolation support for Anchor hardware, including spatial anchoring devices, isolated space maintenance systems, and spatial anomaly monitoring stations.
- **Communication infrastructure**: includes cross-plane communication base stations, intra-plane communication networks, and data storage and processing centers.
- **Maintenance and support facilities**: include Anchor hardware maintenance stations, spare parts warehouses, technical personnel training centers, and emergency repair teams.
- **Standardized interface facilities**: interface specifications and testing facilities that ensure interoperability between Anchor hardware and supporting equipment of different tiers and different manufacturers.

## V. The Construction and Operation of Anchor Hardware

The construction of Anchor hardware follows the principle of "unified planning, tiered construction, open sharing":

1. **Unified planning**: the layout of Anchor hardware throughout the universe is planned in a unified manner by symbiotic governance institutions, ensuring even coverage and avoiding duplicate construction and coverage blind spots.
2. **Tiered construction**: first-tier and second-tier Anchors are directly constructed and operated by civilization-level institutions; third-tier and fourth-tier Anchors may be constructed with the participation of plane governments, community organizations, or compliant enterprises, but must comply with unified technical standards and operational norms.
3. **Open sharing**: the basic services of Anchor hardware (energy supply, communication access, cultivation support) are open free of charge to all life forms; advanced services charge reasonable fees according to energy costs.
4. **Anti-monopoly regulation**: the Anti-Monopoly Law explicitly prohibits any organization or individual from monopolizing the construction and operation rights of Anchor hardware, ensuring the public nature of Anchor infrastructure.

## Conclusion

Anchor hardware and infrastructure are the "skeleton and blood vessels" of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. They transform the abstract Anchor mechanism into tangible, constructible, and maintainable material engineering, giving the concept of symbiotic evolution a solid material carrier. With the comprehensive coverage of the four-tier Anchor network, the Hengyuan Universe is forming a complete energy and information circulation system from the cosmic core to every individual life form — this is the fundamental material guarantee for the ascension of civilization.
