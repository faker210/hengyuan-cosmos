## I. The Fundamental Principles of Technological Ethics

The technological ethics of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization are established on the fundamental value of "symbiotic evolution". Any research, development, and application of technology must answer a core question: does this technology promote the symbiotic evolution of all life, or does it provide new means for predation and harm? If the answer is the latter, then no matter how great the individual efficacy of the technology, it must be strictly restricted or prohibited.

The fundamental principles of technological ethics include three:

- **The principle of non-harm**: the application of technology must not cause unnecessary harm to any life form, and must not provide tools for predatory cultivation.
- **The principle of symbiotic gain**: the development of technology should aim to enhance the Symbiosis Rating of the entire civilization, rather than merely strengthening the abilities of a few individuals.
- **The principle of transparency and traceability**: the research and development processes and application records of all law-engineering-level technologies must be traceable and subject to ethics review and public oversight.

## II. The Technical Risk Grading System

The symbiotic civilization has established a strict technical risk grading system, conducting risk assessment and classified management for all law-engineering-level technologies:

| Risk Grade | Name | Characteristics | Management Requirements |
|----------|------|------|----------|
| R0 | No Risk | Reality technology layer technologies, not involving law manipulation | Routine management, free research and application |
| R1 | Low Risk | Law empowerment layer technologies, performance improvements to existing technologies | Filing management, must pass basic safety tests |
| R2 | Medium Risk | Law engineering layer technologies, involving limited law manipulation | Licensing management, must pass ethics review and isolated space verification |
| R3 | High Risk | Involving high-risk operations such as spatial rewriting, consciousness intervention, and energy fusion | Strict control, research only by authorized institutions in S-Level isolated spaces |
| R4 | Forbidden Grade | Technologies that may cause plane collapse, consciousness extinction, or law pollution | Full prohibition of research and application, related knowledge classified as forbidden |

Risk grades are not immutable. As technologies mature and safety measures improve, some R3-grade technologies may be downgraded to R2 grade; conversely, if new safety hazards are discovered, technologies may also be subject to upgraded control.

## III. Key Safety Boundaries

Technological ethics and safety boundaries clearly delineate the following unbreakable red lines:

### 3.1 Energy Safety Boundary

- Experiments releasing energy exceeding the carrying capacity of the main plane are prohibited.
- The research and development of energy extraction technologies aimed at plundering the energy of other life forms are prohibited.
- Research on Anomalous Energy must be conducted in isolated spaces and must strictly follow the Disposition Process for the Three Types.

### 3.2 Spatial Safety Boundary

- Spatial law rewriting experiments are prohibited in an unisolated state.
- The opening of cross-plane channels must undergo safety assessment to prevent permanent damage to plane barriers.
- The construction and destruction of isolated spaces must follow standard protocols, leaving no spatial residue.

### 3.3 Consciousness Safety Boundary

- The research and development of unauthorized consciousness manipulation and memory tampering technologies are prohibited.
- Research on Dark Replacement Entities must comply with the Dual-Soul Ethics Code and the Disposition Rules for the Four Branches.
- Consciousness domain interface technology must be equipped with an irreversible safety circuit breaker mechanism.

### 3.4 Temporal Safety Boundary

- Temporal regression operations must strictly follow the principle of "observe first, interfere in a limited manner".
- Any experiment or operation that may cause time paradoxes is prohibited.
- Records of temporal regression must be subject to continuous monitoring by the time paradox prevention and control mechanism.

## IV. The Ethics Review Mechanism

To ensure the implementation of technological ethics, the symbiotic civilization has established a multi-layered ethics review mechanism:

1. **Pre-research review**: all research and development projects of R2-grade and above technologies must submit an ethics review application before commencement, with an independent Technology Ethics Committee assessing their risks and benefits.
2. **In-experiment monitoring**: the experimental process of R3-grade technologies must have an ethics observer present throughout; experimental data are uploaded in real time to the ethics monitoring system of the Anchor network.
3. **Pre-application assessment**: before new technologies move from the laboratory to mass production, they must undergo independent safety assessment and social impact assessment.
4. **Post-application tracking**: applied technologies continue to receive safety tracking; when new ethical issues are discovered, re-assessment or even recall procedures can be initiated at any time.
5. **Public participation**: major technological decisions must go through public hearing procedures, ensuring that the direction of technological development conforms to the common interests of the entire civilization.

## V. The List of Forbidden Technologies

The following technologies are explicitly listed as R4-grade forbidden technologies, with research and development fully prohibited:

- Plane-destruction-level weapons: weapon technologies capable of destroying the spatial structure of an entire plane.
- Consciousness extinction devices: technologies capable of erasing or enslaving the consciousness of life forms in bulk.
- Predatory law engines: technologies operating on the mechanism of systematically plundering the origin of other life forms.
- Time paradox generators: technologies that deliberately create time paradoxes to obtain improper benefits.
- Law pollution disseminators: technologies that deliberately spread law pollution to destroy other civilizations.

The knowledge related to forbidden technologies is encrypted and stored in the forbidden archive of the main Anchor, and can only be accessed upon authorization at the highest authority of the symbiotic governance institutions in a civilization-level security crisis.

## Conclusion

Technological ethics and safety boundaries are the "braking system" of the technological system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. They ensure that the development of technology always serves the fundamental goal of symbiotic evolution and does not become alienated into new tools of predation. In an era where laws can be engineered and manipulated, ethical boundaries are more important than technical capability — only by holding the boundaries can technology truly become an aid rather than a threat to the joint advancement of all races.
