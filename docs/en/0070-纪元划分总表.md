## I. The Basic Principles of Era Division

The division of historical eras in the Hengyuan Universe takes the four-stage ascension path as its fundamental framework, with the development state of the Anchor mechanism as the core basis of periodization. Each era corresponds to a fundamental transformation in the relationship between civilization and the universe; the boundaries between eras are not arbitrary points in time, but are marked by major events of universe-level significance.

Era division follows three principles:

- **The principle of ascension correspondence**: each great era corresponds to one stage of the four-stage ascension path, while the sub-periods within an era correspond to key progress within that stage.
- **The principle of event marking**: the starting and ending points of an era are determined by iconic major events, rather than simple equal divisions of time.
- **The principle of civilization state**: each era is named after the core characteristics of the civilization of that period, reflecting the principal contradictions and development direction of the time.

## IV. The Inner Logic of Era Evolution

The evolution of eras in the Hengyuan Universe is not linear passage of time, but a spiral-upward evolutionary process. Each era resolves the core contradictions left by the previous era while nurturing the development conditions of the next:

- The fundamental contradiction of the Pre-Anchor Era was the contradiction between predation and symbiosis, whose extreme development led to the inevitability of Anchor awakening.
- The Anchor Awakening Era resolved the question of "whether Anchors exist", but faced the institutional challenge of "whom the Anchors serve".
- The Symbiotic System Establishment Period completed institutional construction, but the monopoly of cultivation resources still constrained the evolution of the masses.
- The System Opening and Activation Era broke the monopoly of cultivation, but the leap of individual capabilities brought new ethical and security challenges.
- The First Ascension Era verified the feasibility of ascension, but the ascension of a single plane was insufficient to drive the overall evolution of the universe.
- The Myriad Races Republic Era unified the institutions of civilization, but the universe still contained a vast number of uncon- tacted planes and races.
- The Cross-Plane Exploration Era expanded the boundaries of civilization, but dispersed planes needed closer interstellar connections.
- The Interstellar Symbiosis Era ultimately points to the ultimate goal of the overall ascension of the universe.

## V. Positioning of the Current Era

According to the current development state of the Hengyuan Universe, civilization is at the critical juncture of transitioning from the Third Era (Symbiotic System Establishment Period) to the Fourth Era (System Opening and Activation Era). Symbiotic institutions have been implemented in the main planes, the opening work of the Three Hundred Sets of Systems is being comprehensively advanced, and the 20% Activation Strategy has entered its implementation phase. Multiple core technologies of the law engineering layer (the Multiplication Fruit Deduction Engine, spatial legal principle devices, and the Replication Mirror System) have moved from the laboratory to mass production.

The core task of this transitional period is: to ensure that the opening of the Three Hundred Sets of Systems truly benefits all life forms, while cultivating the first batch of Supreme Powers of the Plane through the 20% Activation Strategy, accumulating sufficient energy and experience for the first ascension.

## Conclusion

The Chronology of Eras — Master Table is the macro-level framework for understanding the historical evolution of the Hengyuan Universe. It condenses the long history of the universe into eight eras with clear inner logic, each era being a necessary step toward the overall ascension of the universe. Subsequent documents will expand in detail on each era and the major events within them.
