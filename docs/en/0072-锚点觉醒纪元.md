## I. The Historical Position of the Anchor Awakening Era

The Anchor Awakening Era is the turning-point era in which the Hengyuan Universe moved from darkness to light. It begins with the awakening of the first-generation Anchor and ends with the signing of the Symbiotic Charter, marking a new historical stage in which the universe moved from "predation dominance" to "symbiotic construction". This era corresponds to the initiation period of the "reality foundation" stage on the four-stage ascension path, and its core task is to establish the Anchor mechanism, disseminate anti-predation thought, and prepare the conditions for the comprehensive implementation of the symbiotic institutions.

The time span of the Anchor Awakening Era is approximately several thousand years (main plane standard time). In this long period, the Anchor mechanism grew from nothing to something, from weak to strong; anti-predation thought developed from the awakening of a few into a broad social movement, ultimately converging into an irreversible historical current.

## II. The Awakening of the First-Generation Anchor

The beginning of the Anchor Awakening Era is the awakening of the first-generation Anchor. At a certain critical node of the universe, a special life form (called the "first-generation Anchor" or "Anchor Sovereign" by later generations) completed the first stable connection with the law network, becoming the first being in the history of the Hengyuan Universe to consciously operate the Anchor mechanism.

The awakening of the first-generation Anchor has the following historical significance:

| Dimension | Before Awakening | After Awakening |
|------|--------|--------|
| Law Connection | No one could stably connect to the law network | The first-generation Anchor established the first stable law anchor |
| Energy Conduction | Energy naturally dispersed, flowing disorderly | The Anchor could directionally conduct and distribute origin energy |
| Information Sharing | Knowledge monopolized, transmission broken | The Anchor network could transmit information and knowledge across planes |
| Evolutionary Path | The only path was predation | The possibility of symbiotic evolution was verified for the first time |
| Cosmic Cognition | The universe was a hunting ground of finite resources | The universe is an evolvable community of life |

After awakening, the first-generation Anchor did not choose to become a new predator. Instead, based on deep reflection on the dark history of the Pre-Anchor Era, it established the fundamental mission of "returning the choice of destiny and evolution to all life forms". This decision is the most important historical turning point of the Anchor Awakening Era.

## III. The Multiplication Fruit and the Opening Buff

Shortly after awakening, the first-generation Anchor obtained a crucial "opening buff" — the Multiplication Fruit of One Piece. This special item from a fictional world, after the first-generation Anchor's law analysis and technological processing, became the source of hundredfold cultivation deduction amplification.

The engineered application of the Multiplication Fruit played a decisive role in the Anchor Awakening Era:

- It enabled the first-generation Anchor and its followers to deduce cultivation paths at hundredfold speed, rapidly establishing the theoretical framework of the Three Origin Foundations (deduction iteration, life cycle, spatial legal principle dispatch).
- It accelerated the organization and verification of the knowledge left over from the Pre-Anchor Era, laying the foundation for the refinement of the Three Hundred Sets of Origin Cultivation Systems.
- It made the research and development speed of Anchor mechanism technology far exceed the norm, completing in several thousand years the technological accumulation that would have originally required tens of thousands of years.

The application of the Multiplication Fruit strictly follows the principle of "reality as foundation, law-driven upgrading"; its ability is locked to the cultivation deduction dimension and must not be used for direct combat or predation.

## IV. The Rise of the Anti-Predation Movement

With the establishment of the Anchor mechanism and the dissemination of symbiotic thought, a broad anti-predation movement rose in the Hengyuan Universe. This movement was not a violent revolution, but a civilization transformation centered on "institutional replacement":

1. **The stage of thought dissemination**: the first-generation Anchor and its followers disseminated anti-predation thought through the Anchor network, exposed the fundamental harms of the predatory system, and proposed alternative plans of symbiotic evolution.
2. **The stage of demonstrative construction**: symbiotic demonstration zones were established in Anchor-covered regions, implementing institutions of resource sharing, open cultivation, and fair distribution, proving the feasibility of the symbiotic path with tangible results.
3. **The stage of institutional competition**: the symbiotic demonstration zones engaged in institutional competition with predatory forces; more and more life forms and regions chose to join the symbiotic system, and the territory of predatory forces kept shrinking.
4. **The stage of peaceful integration**: for the remaining predatory forces, a strategy of "education and transformation as the main means, coercion and restriction as auxiliary" was adopted, promoting their transition to the symbiotic system while ensuring safety.

The core concept of the anti-predation movement is: do not eliminate the lives of predators, but eliminate the institutional soil on which predators depend for survival. When the symbiotic system can provide better evolutionary paths for all life forms, predatory behavior naturally loses the basis of its existence.

## V. The Signing of the Symbiotic Charter

The end point of the Anchor Awakening Era is the signing of the Symbiotic Charter. After the decisive victory of the anti-predation movement, representatives of the main planes gathered together and jointly signed the Symbiotic Civilization Charter, formally establishing the fundamental institutions of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization.

The core contents of the Symbiotic Charter include:

- Establishing "more work, more reward; self-growth and self-development; a path for everyone; joint advancement of all races" as the basic program of the civilization.
- Announcing that the Three Hundred Sets of Origin Cultivation Systems would be opened free of charge to all life forms.
- Establishing the Joint-Output Contracting Mechanism as the basic institution of resource allocation.
- Planning the 20% Activation Strategy as the near-term evolutionary goal.
- Establishing the four-stage ascension path as the long-term development direction of the civilization.

The signing of the Symbiotic Charter marks the end of the Anchor Awakening Era and the beginning of the Symbiotic System Establishment Period.

## Conclusion

The Anchor Awakening Era is the "hour of dawn" in the history of the Hengyuan Universe. The awakening of the first-generation Anchor brought the first ray of symbiotic light to the universe; the rise of the anti-predation movement expanded this ray into a rising sun illuminating the earth; and the signing of the Symbiotic Charter laid an eternal institutional foundation for this sun. This era proves: the turning point of a civilization often begins with the awakening and choice of one being.
