## I. The Historical Tasks of the Symbiotic System Establishment Period

The Symbiotic System Establishment Period is the institutional construction period of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, corresponding to the "reality foundation" stage on the four-stage ascension path. This era begins with the signing of the Symbiotic Charter and ends with the comprehensive opening of the Three Hundred Sets of Origin Cultivation Systems; its core historical task is to transform the principled provisions of the Charter into operational social institutions, completing the landing from "concept" to "reality".

The time span of the Symbiotic System Establishment Period is approximately several hundred years. In this period, the symbiotic civilization completed comprehensive institutional construction covering the legal system, social institutions, education, medical care, housing safeguards, and economic distribution mechanisms, laying a solid social foundation for the subsequent popularization of cultivation and technological breakthroughs.

## II. The Establishment of the Legal System

The establishment of the legal system was the primary task of the Symbiotic System Establishment Period. With the Symbiotic Constitution as its fundamental law, the symbiotic civilization gradually established a complete legal framework:

| Legal Tier | Core Law | Main Contents |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Fundamental law | Symbiotic Constitution | Establishes the fundamental institutions of the civilization, the basic rights and obligations of citizens, and the governance framework |
| Foundational law | Symbiotic Labor Law | Protects labor rights and establishes the distribution principle of more work, more reward |
| Foundational law | Consumer Protection Law | Protects the legitimate rights and interests of life in consumption and services |
| Guarantee law | Education Guarantee Law | Establishes free basic education and an open higher education system |
| Guarantee law | Medical Care Guarantee Law | Establishes a universal free medical care and cultivation health safeguard system |
| Guarantee law | Housing Guarantee Law | Ensures every life form enjoys the basic right to housing |
| Guarantee law | Environmental Protection Law | Establishes an ecological protection and energy pollution prevention and control system |
| Regulatory law | Anti-Monopoly Law | Prohibits cultivation monopoly, technology monopoly, and resource monopoly |

This legal system of "one constitution, two foundational laws, five guarantee laws, and one regulatory law" constitutes the institutional skeleton of the symbiotic civilization. The law-making process fully embodied the principle of "joint advancement of all races": all major races had representatives participating in legislation, ensuring that laws could accommodate the needs of different life forms.

## III. The Reconstruction of Social Institutions

Within the legal framework, the symbiotic civilization carried out a comprehensive reconstruction of social institutions:

### 3.1 Reconstruction of the Education System

- A free education system from enlightenment to higher education was established, with basic law engineering knowledge listed as general education content.
- The master-apprentice monopoly system of cultivation methods was abolished, replaced by an open mentor network.
- A personalized education assessment mechanism was established, recommending suitable cultivation systems based on the traits of each life form.

### 3.2 Reconstruction of the Medical System

- A universal free medical care system was established, applying the cultivation achievements of the Life Cycle System to disease treatment.
- A cultivation health monitoring system was established, monitoring the physical and mental states of cultivators in real time to prevent Deviation.
- Dedicated medical and ethical disposition processes were established for Anomalous Life and Dark Replacement Entities.

### 3.3 Reconstruction of Housing and the Environment

- A basic housing guarantee system was established, ensuring that every life form enjoys safe and comfortable living space.
- Spatial extension technology was used to expand living space on limited land, alleviating population pressure.
- An ecological restoration mechanism was established, using the Life Cycle System to restore the environment damaged in the Pre-Anchor Era.

### 3.4 Reconstruction of Economic Distribution

- The Joint-Output Contracting Mechanism was established, fairly distributing cultivation resources and public resources according to contribution and need.
- The hereditary resource system was abolished, and any organization or individual was prohibited from monopolizing key resources.
- An energy standardization and measurement system was established, providing a technical foundation for fair distribution.

## IV. The Practice of the Five Typologies of the Symbiotic Civilization

In the process of institutional construction, different planes and regions developed five typologies of the symbiotic civilization according to their own history, culture, and racial characteristics:

| Typology | Core Characteristics | Typical Regions |
|------|----------|----------|
| East Asian intensive type | High population density, efficient resource utilization, collectivist tradition | Eastern settlement zone of the Rén Zú |
| Nordic welfare type | High welfare guarantees, individual freedom, harmony between humans and nature | Jīnglíng Zú (Elf Race) regions in the high-cold zones |
| North American pluralistic type | Multi-racial cohabitation, cultural inclusiveness, innovation-driven | Pluralistic multi-racial zone of the New Continent |
| Continental European social type | Social market economy, industry self-governance, cultural heritage | Revival zone of the ancient civilizations |
| Developing catch-up type | Rapid industrialization, infrastructure priority, learning-oriented society | Late-developing regions of the former predation zones |

The five typologies are not mutually exclusive; they are institutional practices with different emphases under the unified framework of the Symbiotic Constitution. They maintain close exchange and competition among themselves, jointly enriching the institutional connotation of the symbiotic civilization.

## V. The Preliminary Construction of Anchor Infrastructure

The Symbiotic System Establishment Period was also a period of large-scale construction of Anchor infrastructure:

- The First-Level Universal Main Anchor was completed in the early period of the era, becoming the core of the universe-wide Anchor network.
- Second-Level Plane Main Anchors were successively deployed in the main planes, with plane-level energy dispatch capability initially formed.
- Third-Level Regional Anchors covered densely populated regions, providing energy support for the popularization of cultivation.
- Fourth-Level Community Anchors began pilot deployment, accumulating experience for subsequent comprehensive coverage.

The construction of Anchor infrastructure follows the principle of "unified planning, tiered construction", ensuring efficient utilization of resources and fairness of coverage.

## Conclusion

The Symbiotic System Establishment Period is the "foundation-building era" of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. In several hundred years, it completed the fundamental institutional transformation from the predatory order of the Pre-Anchor Era to the symbiotic order. The establishment of the legal system provided a rule framework for the civilization; the reconstruction of social institutions provided practical safeguards for all life forms; the practice of the five typologies provided pluralistic paths for institutional innovation; and the construction of Anchor infrastructure laid the material foundation for subsequent development. When the moment of the comprehensive opening of the Three Hundred Sets of Origin Cultivation Systems arrived, the symbiotic civilization was fully prepared institutionally.
