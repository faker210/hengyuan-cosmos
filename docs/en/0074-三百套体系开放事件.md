## I. The Historical Background of the Event

The Opening of the Three Hundred Sets of Systems is one of the most revolutionary events in the history of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. During the several hundred years of the Symbiotic System Establishment Period, the symbiotic civilization had completed the construction of the legal system, social institutions, and Anchor infrastructure; however, the opening of cultivation methods remained gradual — basic cultivation methods had been opened free of charge, but the advanced systems and the complete Three Hundred Sets of Systems were still in the process of step-by-step release.

The reason for this gradual opening is that the Three Hundred Sets of Origin Cultivation Systems are orthodox cultivation paths directly refined from the origin laws of the universe, and their comprehensive opening required the maturation of three preconditions:

- **Institutional precondition**: the Anti-Monopoly Law and cultivation ethics norms must be perfected, preventing the systems from being abused or re-monopolized.
- **Technological precondition**: the Anchor infrastructure must possess sufficient energy supply capability to support large-scale cultivation demand.
- **Social precondition**: the education system must be able to provide the masses with sufficient cultivation guidance, preventing safety accidents caused by blind cultivation.

By the end of the Symbiotic System Establishment Period, all three preconditions had matured, and the comprehensive opening of the Three Hundred Sets of Systems was put on the agenda.

## II. The Course of the Opening Event

The Opening of the Three Hundred Sets of Systems was implemented in three stages:

### 2.1 The Preparation Stage (the decade before the opening)

- A "Three Hundred Sets of Systems Opening Committee" was established, presided over personally by the first-generation Anchor, with members including representatives of all major races, cultivation masters, and legal experts.
- A final safety review and ethical assessment of the Three Hundred Sets of Systems was conducted, adding usage restrictions and guidance notes to systems with risks.
- An "Origin Cultivation System Knowledge Base" was built, digitizing the complete methods, cultivation guides, and risk warnings of the Three Hundred Sets of Systems, connected to the Anchor network.
- The first batch of "system mentors" was trained, ensuring sufficient guidance strength in every region.

### 2.2 The Opening Stage (the opening day and the following year)

- On "Symbiotic Opening Day", the comprehensive opening of the Three Hundred Sets of Systems was announced simultaneously to the entire universe through the Anchor network.
- All life forms could freely access the Origin Cultivation System Knowledge Base through community Anchors or personal terminals, obtaining the complete methods of any system.
- In the first month of the opening, the Anchor network's traffic reached an all-time peak, and the system operated stably after stress testing.
- Within the first year of the opening, over 10% of life forms formally began cultivation, and the system compatibility assessment mechanism provided personalized recommendations for every cultivator.

### 2.3 The Consolidation Stage (the decade after the opening)

- Cultivation feedback was continuously collected, and the cultivation paths of each system were optimized through the Multiplication Fruit Deduction Engine.
- A rapid response mechanism for cultivation problems was established, promptly issuing guidance on common problems arising in cultivation.
- Pre-assessment requirements were added to certain high-risk systems, ensuring cultivators possess sufficient foundation and cognition.
- Cultivation white papers were regularly published, summarizing the opening experience and guiding subsequent cultivation popularization work.

## III. The Social Impact of the Opening Event

The comprehensive opening of the Three Hundred Sets of Systems had a profound impact on the society of the Hengyuan Universe:

| Impact Dimension | Before the Opening | After the Opening |
|----------|--------|--------|
| Cultivation threshold | Methods monopolized, hard for the lower strata to obtain | All free and open, everyone can cultivate |
| Cultivator ratio | About 10% | Rose above 30% within ten years |
| Class mobility | Extremely rigid, birth determined destiny | Cultivation ability became the main channel of mobility |
| Innovation vitality | Suppressed by monopoly, slow innovation | Diverse cultivation practices gave rise to much innovation |
| Predatory remnants | Gray zones still existed | The predatory path thoroughly lost its institutional basis |

The most profound impact of the opening event is that it truly fulfilled the promise of "a path for everyone". Before this, "a path for everyone" was more of an institutional ideal; after this, it became a reality that every life form could touch.

## IV. Challenges and Responses During the Opening

The comprehensive opening of the Three Hundred Sets of Systems was not smooth sailing; the process faced many challenges:

1. **Cultivation safety challenge**: a large number of beginners began cultivating simultaneously, and the risk of Deviation and cultivation accidents rose sharply. Countermeasures included: establishing a cultivation health monitoring system, a mandatory basic assessment system, full coverage of the mentor network, and an emergency medical response mechanism.
2. **System abuse challenge**: some cultivators attempted to use the open systems for predation or harm. Countermeasures included: cultivation ethics education, technical monitoring of high-risk cultivation behavior, and a legal accountability mechanism.
3. **Resource pressure challenge**: the surge in the number of cultivators caused a sharp rise in energy demand, putting pressure on the Anchor infrastructure. Countermeasures included: accelerating the deployment of Fourth-Level Community Anchors, optimizing energy distribution algorithms, and promoting energy cycle cultivation methods.
4. **Cultural conflict challenge**: different races had differences in their understanding and practice of the cultivation systems, and some traditional forces resisted the new systems. Countermeasures included: respecting cultural traditions, encouraging localized innovation of the systems, and promoting step-by-step through demonstration effects.

## V. The Historical Significance of the Opening Event

The historical significance of the Opening of the Three Hundred Sets of Systems can be understood at three levels:

- **Institutional level**: it marked the symbiotic civilization's entry from "institutional construction" into a new stage of "capability release", with institutional advantages beginning to transform into tangible evolutionary momentum.
- **Individual level**: it truly returned the choice of evolution to every life form, turning "more work, more reward; self-growth and self-development" from an ideal into a practicable path.
- **Universal level**: it provided the precondition for the implementation of the 20% Activation Strategy, accumulated a broad cultivator foundation for the first ascension, and is a key step toward the overall ascension of the universe.

## Conclusion

The Opening of the Three Hundred Sets of Systems is the "liberation moment" of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. What it liberated was not a certain race or a certain class, but the evolutionary potential of all life forms. At the moment when the Three Hundred Sets of Origin Cultivation Systems were opened to the entire universe through the Anchor network, every life form in the Hengyuan Universe obtained the same starting point — the only differences being their own efforts and choices. This is precisely the most profound realization of "returning the choice of destiny and evolution to all life forms".
