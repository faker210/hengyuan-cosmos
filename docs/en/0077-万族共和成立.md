## I. The Historical Background of the Founding of the Myriad Races Republic

The founding of the Myriad Races Republic was the inevitable political result of the success of the first ascension. Before the first ascension, although all the planes and races of the Hengyuan Universe operated within the framework of the Symbiotic Constitution, they still maintained considerable institutional differences and administrative independence from one another. The five types of symbiotic civilization each had their own emphasis, and coordination between planes was mainly conducted through bilateral consultation and temporary mechanisms, lacking a unified supreme governing body.

The success of the first ascension exposed the limitations of this decentralized governance model:

- The ascension process required large-scale cross-plane, cross-race resource coordination, and decentralized governance was inefficient.
- Unified interface standards and exchange mechanisms were needed between ascended planes and non-ascended planes.
- The collective consciousness symbiosis of the consciousness evolution stage required a higher degree of institutional unity.
- The imminent cross-plane exploration required unified foreign policies and security safeguards.

Against this background, establishing a unified Myriad Races Republic polity became a consensus of the entire civilization.

## II. The Preparatory Process of the Myriad Races Republic

The preparation of the Myriad Races Republic lasted about twenty years and was divided into three stages:

### 2.1 The Constitutional Stage (the first decade)

- A "Myriad Races Republic Constitutional Convention" was established, composed of representatives of all major races and planes.
- The "Myriad Races Republic Charter" was formulated on the basis of the Symbiotic Constitution, clarifying the organizational principles and power structure of the republican polity.
- The three major political principles of "racial equality, plane autonomy, and republican unity" were established.
- The draft charter was passed through a civilization-wide referendum.

### 2.2 The Institution Establishment Stage (the middle five years)

- The first Myriad Races Republic Parliament was elected, with representation quotas comprehensively distributed according to racial population and the number of planes.
- A Republic Executive Committee was formed as the daily administrative body.
- A Republic Supreme Court was established, responsible for legal rulings across races and planes.
- A Republic Anchor Administration was established to manage the Anchor infrastructure of the entire universe in a unified manner.

### 2.3 The Founding Ceremony Stage (the final year)

- The Myriad Races Republic Founding Ceremony was held at the ascension demonstration plane.
- The first-generation Anchor announced the formal founding of the Myriad Races Republic.
- Representatives of all member races and planes signed the "Myriad Races Republic Founding Declaration".
- The Republic Era officially began.

## III. The Political Architecture of the Myriad Races Republic

The Myriad Races Republic adopts a republican architecture of "Parliament–Executive–Judiciary" with the separation of three powers, while preserving the special status of the Anchor mechanism:

| Institution | Function | Composition |
|------|------|----------|
| Myriad Races Parliament | Supreme legislative body, enacting universe-wide laws | Representatives of all races and planes elected proportionally |
| Executive Committee | Supreme administrative body, executing laws and policies | Elected by the Parliament, headed by a Chief Executive |
| Republic Supreme Court | Supreme judicial body, ruling on cross-race and cross-plane disputes | Judges appointed by the Parliament, serving for life |
| Anchor Directorate | Supreme governing body of Anchor infrastructure | Composed of high-level cultivators and technical experts |
| First-Generation Anchor | Spiritual leader and ultimate guarantor of the civilization | Not elected, above parties and races |

The political design of the Myriad Races Republic fully embodies the principle of "joint advancement of all races": any race, regardless of size, holds at least one seat in the Parliament; any law involving the special rights of a particular race must obtain the consent of that race's representatives.

## IV. The Core Policies of the Myriad Races Republic

After its founding, the Myriad Races Republic launched a series of core policies with far-reaching impact:

1. **Racial Equality Law**: establishes by law the equal status of all intelligent life, prohibiting any form of racial discrimination and racial segregation.
2. **Cultural Protection Law**: protects the cultural traditions, languages, and scripts of all races, encouraging cultural exchange rather than cultural assimilation.
3. **Unified Technical Standards**: promotes unified technical standards and interface specifications throughout the universe, facilitating cross-plane technical exchange.
4. **Ascension Coordination Policy**: establishes a universe-wide unified ascension queuing and coordination mechanism, ensuring fair distribution of ascension opportunities.
5. **Cross-Plane Exploration Planning**: formulates unified cross-plane exploration policies, regulating exploration conduct and protecting the indigenous civilizations of uncontacted planes.
6. **Consciousness Symbiosis Promotion Policy**: in the consciousness evolution stage, promotes consciousness exchange and understanding among all races, eliminating historical estrangement.

## V. The Challenges Facing the Myriad Races Republic

The founding of the Myriad Races Republic was not an endpoint but a new starting point. It faced many challenges:

- **Coordination of racial differences**: different races have enormous differences in physiological structure, cultural traditions, and values; the enforcement of unified laws must fully consider racial particularities.
- **Imbalance in plane development**: the development gap between ascended and non-ascended planes, and between developed and developing regions, needs to be continuously narrowed.
- **Ethical boundaries of consciousness fusion**: the collective consciousness symbiosis of the consciousness evolution stage requires careful handling of the relationship between individual consciousness independence and collective consciousness fusion.
- **Response to external unknowns**: cross-plane exploration is about to begin; the universe may contain undiscovered races and civilizations, and the republican polity must possess the resilience to respond to external changes.

## Conclusion

The founding of the Myriad Races Republic is a milestone in the political development of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. It transformed "joint advancement of all races" from an ideal into institutions, encompassing scattered races and planes within a unified political community. The Myriad Races Republic does not seek to eliminate racial differences, but to seek symbiosis within difference; it does not seek to build a homogenized empire, but to construct a pluralistic republic. Under the banner of the Myriad Races Republic, all life forms of the Hengyuan Universe for the first time possess a shared political identity — "citizens of the Myriad Races Republic".
