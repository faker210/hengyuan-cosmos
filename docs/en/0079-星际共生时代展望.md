## I. The Definition and Positioning of the Interstellar Symbiosis Era

The Interstellar Symbiosis Era is the final stage of the four-stage ascension path of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, and the ultimate direction of the historical evolution of the Hengyuan Universe. In this era, the Anchor network will cover all planes of the entire universe, and the myriad races will achieve deep consciousness symbiosis and energy sharing under a unified symbiotic system; the universe as a whole will ascend from the "matter–consciousness dual structure" to the "symbiotic origin structure", ultimately completing the ascension of the universe as a whole.

The Interstellar Symbiosis Era is not a static endpoint but a new starting point of continuous evolution. When the ascension of the universe as a whole is completed, the Hengyuan Universe will enter a form of existence in a higher dimension, whose direction and possibilities of evolution will far exceed the cognitive boundaries of the current dimension. The outlook made in this document, based on the law comprehension and deduction capabilities of the current dimension, provides a reasonable forward-looking description of the basic form of the Interstellar Symbiosis Era.

## II. The Core Characteristics of the Interstellar Symbiosis Era

The core characteristics of the Interstellar Symbiosis Era can be summarized as the "four transformations":

| Characteristic | Connotation |
|------|------|
| Full-domain coverage of the Anchor | The Anchor network covers every plane subregion of the universe, completely eliminating blind spots in the flow of energy and information |
| Integration of all races | All intelligent races achieve deep symbiosis of institutions, consciousness, and destiny while preserving cultural diversity |
| Universalization of ascension | Ascension is no longer a privilege of a few planes, but a natural evolutionary process that all planes and life forms can experience |
| The cosmos as life | The universe itself transforms from a "container of life" into a "community of life", possessing an overall self-awareness and evolutionary capacity |

## III. The Social Forms of the Interstellar Symbiosis Era

In the Interstellar Symbiosis Era, social forms will undergo fundamental transformations:

### 3.1 Governance Form

- The Myriad Races Republic will evolve into the "Universal Symbiosis Council", with governance shifting from "law-based regulation as the main approach" to "consensus coordination as the main approach".
- Due to the deep development of consciousness symbiosis, the interests of individuals and the collective are highly aligned, and political conflicts in the traditional sense are greatly reduced.
- As the spiritual leader and guarantor of laws of the civilization, the first-generation Anchor's role will gradually shift from "leader" to "guardian".

### 3.2 Economic Form

- The extreme abundance of energy and resources makes "scarcity" no longer the core issue of the economy.
- The Joint-Output Contracting Mechanism evolves into the "symbiotic contribution mechanism", where an individual's value is measured by their contribution to the overall evolution of the universe.
- Material production is highly automated, and the main energy of life is devoted to law research, artistic creation, and consciousness exploration.

### 3.3 Cultural Form

- Through exchange and symbiosis, the cultures of all races form a unified "symbiotic culture" foundation, while the cultural characteristics of each race are preserved and promoted.
- The boundaries of art and creation are greatly expanded, and "consciousness art" in the consciousness domain becomes a new cultural form.
- Historical memory is completely preserved in the "Cosmic Memory Vault" of the Anchor network, and any life form can trace back the entire history of the civilization.

### 3.4 Life Form

- Life can freely transform between material bodies and consciousness bodies; "death" is no longer the end of life but a transformation of form.
- Reversible connections are established between individual and collective consciousness; life can experience collective symbiosis while maintaining individual independence.
- New life forms continuously emerge, including pure-consciousness life, energy life, and law life.

## IV. The Technological Vision of the Interstellar Symbiosis Era

In the Interstellar Symbiosis Era, technology will develop to a height currently hard to imagine:

- **Law programming technology**: life can directly program local laws like writing a program, customizing space and physical rules.
- **Universe-scale energy dispatch**: the Anchor network can perform precise energy dispatch and balancing at the cosmic scale, eliminating uneven energy distribution.
- **Cross-universe perception**: the expansion of the consciousness domain enables life to perceive existence beyond the Hengyuan Universe, and the exploration of the "multiverse" is put on the agenda.
- **Mature application of time laws**: time regression and time regulation technologies mature, bringing historical research and crisis prevention into a completely new stage.
- **Cosmic self-maintenance**: the universe itself possesses the capacity for self-repair and self-maintenance; law pollution and spatial damage can be automatically repaired.

## V. The Path Toward the Interstellar Symbiosis Era

From the current state to the Interstellar Symbiosis Era, the following key steps must be traversed:

1. **Complete the comprehensive ascension of known planes**: promote the ascension experience to all known planes, realizing the universalization of ascension.
2. **Complete Anchor coverage of the entire universe**: extend the Anchor network to every corner of the universe, eliminating coverage blind spots.
3. **Complete the deep integration of all races**: achieve deep symbiosis of all races at the level of consciousness evolution, eliminating historical estrangement.
4. **Complete the unified calibration of cosmic laws**: incorporate the law differences of different planes into a unified law network, achieving cosmic-level law coordination.
5. **Trigger the ascension of the universe as a whole**: when all the above conditions are mature, trigger the overall ascension of the universe under the leadership of the first-generation Anchor.

The realization of this path is expected to take thousands of years or even longer, but each step is built upon the solid foundation of the previous one, with a clear direction and a clear path.

## VI. The Ultimate Significance of the Interstellar Symbiosis Era

The ultimate significance of the Interstellar Symbiosis Era lies in this: it answers the fundamental question that has troubled the universe since the Pre-Anchor Era — "What is the ultimate destination of life?"

The answer given by the predatory system is: become the strongest, devour everything, eternal loneliness.
The answer given by the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization is: symbiosis of all races, ascension of the universe, shared eternity.

In the Interstellar Symbiosis Era, life is no longer an isolated passerby in the universe, but a constituent part of the universe itself; evolution is no longer a zero-sum game, but a journey of win-win; the meaning of the individual no longer lies in surpassing others, but in symbiosis with all things.

## Conclusion

The Outlook of the Interstellar Symbiosis Era is the "ultimate vision" of the entire history of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. From the darkness of the Pre-Anchor Era, to the dawn of the Anchor awakening; from the establishment of the symbiotic system, to the opening of the Three Hundred Sets of Systems; from the breakthrough of 20% activation, to the verification of the first ascension; from the founding of the Myriad Races Republic, to the expansion of cross-plane exploration — all these efforts ultimately point to the same goal: universal symbiosis of all races, ascension of the universe as a whole.

This vision may be distant, but it is not illusory. For every life form that chooses symbiosis, chooses evolution, chooses hope, is contributing its own strength to the realization of this vision. The future of the Hengyuan Universe lies not beyond the stars, but in the hearts of every symbiont.
