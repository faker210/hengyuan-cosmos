## I. The Definition and Essence of Time Regression

Time regression refers to the behavior of sending consciousness, information, or matter back to a past time node through the time-dimension operation capability of the Anchor law network. Within the law framework of the Hengyuan Universe, time is not a one-way irreversible linear flow, but an operable dimension in the law network. However, operations in the time dimension carry extremely high risks and strict restrictions; any time regression behavior may trigger causal chain disturbance, timeline splitting, and even universe-level law oscillations.

The essence of time regression is "creating a regression channel on the time dimension of the law network, allowing specific information or energy to propagate in reverse along the channel". This process consumes a huge amount of Anchor energy, and the farther back the regression goes and the more complex the regressed content, the exponentially greater the energy consumption. Currently, the technical capability of the Hengyuan Universe only supports regression at the level of consciousness and information; complete material-level regression remains at the stage of theoretical research.

It must be particularly emphasized that time regression is not equivalent to "time travel". The regressor does not enter the past in a complete material form, but arrives at the target time node in the form of a consciousness projection or information packet. The regressor's existence in the past period is "semi-transparent" — observable, but with strictly limited physical interaction capability. This design is itself part of the safety mechanism of time regression.

## II. The Seven Basic Principles of Time Regression

Time regression operations in the Hengyuan Universe follow the following seven basic principles, established by the Anchor Assembly in the form of special resolutions, possessing legal effect second only to the Constitution:

| Principle No. | Principle Name | Core Meaning |
|----------|----------|----------|
| First Principle | Observe First, Then Intervene Within Limits | Any regression must first complete the pure observation stage; only after confirming no major risks may limited intervention be conducted |
| Second Principle | Conservation of Causality | Regression behavior must not alter the core causal chains that have already occurred; causal deviations arising from regression must be corrected after the regression ends |
| Third Principle | Anchor Anchoring | All regression operations must use a stable Anchor as the origin of time coordinates; blind regression without an Anchor is prohibited |
| Fourth Principle | Minimal Impact | All behavior in the regression process must follow the principle of minimal impact, reducing disturbance to the past timeline as much as possible |
| Fifth Principle | Traceability | Every regression must completely record operation logs, including the regressor's identity, target time, observed content, intervention behavior, and causal deviation assessment |
| Sixth Principle | Prohibition of Paradoxes | Any operation that may lead to time paradoxes is strictly prohibited, including but not limited to the grandparent paradox, the information paradox, and the ontological paradox |
| Seventh Principle | Post-Hoc Audit | After the regression ends, an independent Time Law Audit Committee must conduct a post-hoc audit to confirm that no uncontrollable timeline disturbance was caused |

Among the seven principles, "Observe First, Then Intervene Within Limits" is the most core operational principle, and the other six principles all unfold around this core. The detailed operational specifications of this principle are found in Document 83, "The Guidelines for Observing First and Intervening Within Limits".

## III. The Hierarchical Management of Time Regression

According to the time span and degree of intervention of the regression, time regression operations are divided into three management levels:

1. **First-Level Regression (Recent Observation)**: the regression time span does not exceed 100 years of the standard time of one's own plane, and only pure observation is conducted without any intervention. Approved by the Plane Administration, with no fewer than two time law supervisors present to supervise the operation process.

2. **Second-Level Regression (Distant Observation)**: the regression time span exceeds 100 years but does not exceed 10,000 years, or extremely light intervention is required during the regression (such as transmitting a single piece of information). Must be approved by the Time Law Committee under the Anchor Assembly, with a detailed emergency plan for causal deviations formulated.

3. **Third-Level Regression (Deep Intervention)**: the regression time span exceeds 10,000 years, or substantive intervention is required during the regression. Must be approved by more than two-thirds of the plenary session of the Anchor Assembly, and subject to a constitutional review by the Symbiotic Court, confirming that the intervention behavior conforms to the fundamental principles of the Symbiotic Constitution.

Time regression at any level is prohibited from being used for the following purposes: seeking private gain for individuals or small groups, altering the destinies of historical figures to satisfy personal wishes, tampering with the natural process of civilizational evolution, and any form of "historical revisionism" operation.

## IV. Energy and Technical Constraints of Time Regression

The energy consumption of time regression is extremely enormous. Taking consciousness projection regression as an example, the energy required to regress 1 year is about 10,000 standard energy units; to regress 100 years, about 1 million standard energy units; and to regress 10,000 years, about 10 billion standard energy units. The relationship between energy consumption and the time span of regression is approximately logarithmic-linear, but energy spikes occur when crossing major historical nodes — this is because the causal chain density at major historical nodes is extremely high, and regression operations must overcome greater causal inertia.

At the technical level, time regression requires the coordinated operation of the "time law anchoring device" and the "consciousness projection transmission array". The former is responsible for establishing a stable time coordinate at the target time node; the latter is responsible for safely delivering the regressor's consciousness projection and maintaining its existence in the past period. All regression equipment must pass the safety certification of the Anchor Oversight Committee; the use of uncertified self-made equipment for regression operations is prohibited.

## Conclusion

Time regression is one of the most powerful and most dangerous law operation capabilities of the Hengyuan Universe. The seven basic principles and the hierarchical management system together constitute the safety framework of time regression, whose core spirit is "revere causality, operate with prudence". The legitimate uses of time regression are limited to archaeological research, the restoration of historical truth, and the early warning of extinction-level crises; any use beyond this scope will be regarded as a desecration of the laws of the universe.
