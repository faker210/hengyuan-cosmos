## I. The Core Concept of the Guidelines

"Observe first, then intervene within limits" is the primary guideline for time regression and cross-plane archaeology operations in the Hengyuan Universe, whose core concept can be summarized in sixteen characters: "Observe without touching, measure without disturbing, deliberate before acting, act with measure." The establishment of this guideline is based on a profound reverence for the causal laws of the universe — any direct intervention in the past or in another plane may trigger unpredictable causal chain reactions, and sufficient advance observation is the only reliable means of minimizing risk.

The philosophical foundation of the guideline is the principle of "cognitive humility". The Hengyuan Universe acknowledges that even a Supreme Power of the Plane cannot fully grasp all the causal connections of a time node or a plane civilization. Therefore, before taking any intervention action, comprehensive observation must be conducted with the most prudent attitude, understanding the causal structure and evolution trends of the target object as completely as possible. Only on the premise of sufficient observation data and clear risk assessment is limited intervention permitted.

"Observe first, then intervene within limits" is not two independent steps, but an organic operational closed loop: observation provides the basis for intervention, and the results of intervention feed back as new observation data, the two cycling and advancing progressively. This closed loop ensures that every intervention is built upon the latest and most complete cognitive foundation.

## II. Operational Specifications of the Observation Stage

The observation stage is the foundation of the entire operational process, and its quality directly determines the safety and effectiveness of subsequent intervention. The observation stage must follow the following operational specifications:

### 2.1 Observation Duration Requirements

The observation duration must not be less than 1% of the total length of the target period, and the absolute duration must not be less than 72 hours of the standard time of one's own plane. For example, for regression observation of a historical period spanning 100 years, the pure observation stage must not be less than 1 year; for observation of a period spanning 10 years, the pure observation stage must not be less than 72 hours. This minimum duration requirement ensures that observers can capture the periodic changes and long-term trends within the target period, avoiding one-sided judgments caused by an overly short observation window.

### 2.2 Observation Dimension Requirements

Observation must cover the following five dimensions, none of which may be omitted:

| Observation Dimension | Specific Content | Observation Method |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Material Dimension | Geographical environment, ecosystems, infrastructure, distribution of material resources | Spatial scanning, material analysis |
| Energy Dimension | Energy density distribution, law parameters, energy flow patterns | Energy spectrum analysis, law detection |
| Life Dimension | Species distribution, population numbers, health status, evolutionary level | Vital sign scanning, genetic sampling |
| Consciousness Dimension | Civilizational form, social structure, ideas and concepts, collective consciousness tendencies | Passive monitoring of the consciousness domain, cultural analysis |
| Causal Dimension | Key causal chains, historical nodes, evolution trends, potential risks | Causal chain tracing, trend deduction |

### 2.3 Observation Neutrality Requirements

The observation stage must strictly maintain neutrality; observers must not influence the observation target in any way. Specifically prohibited behaviors include: any form of communication with the observation target, leaving any material or energy traces in the observation area, guiding or hinting at the behavior of the observation target, and releasing any thought fluctuations in the consciousness domain that may be perceived.

The observer's state of existence during the observation stage should be "pure passive perception", that is, only receiving information without outputting any information. The Anchor anchoring device will generate a layer of "observation shielding field" for the observer, ensuring that the observer's existence cannot be perceived by the observation target, while also preventing the observer from unintentionally releasing interference signals.

## III. The Definition and Threshold of Limited Intervention

After completing the observation stage, the operation team must submit the "Observation Report and Intervention Feasibility Assessment", and may only enter the limited intervention stage after approval by the Time Law Committee or the Plane Administration. The "limited" in "limited intervention" is reflected in the following three aspects:

### 3.1 Limited Scope

Intervention behavior must be strictly limited to the "intervention permit area" designated in the approval document, which usually does not exceed 1% of the total area of the observation area. Any intervention behavior beyond the permit area is regarded as a violation, and the operator will bear legal responsibility.

### 3.2 Limited Degree

The energy output and information injection of the intervention must not exceed the "intervention threshold". The intervention threshold is dynamically determined according to the causal sensitivity of the target object, with the calculation formula: intervention threshold = total energy of the target area × causal sensitivity coefficient × 0.0001. The causal sensitivity coefficient ranges from 0.1 to 10; the higher the degree of civilization and the more critical the historical node, the larger the coefficient. This means that the intervention threshold for critical historical nodes is extremely low, and almost no substantive energy injection is permitted.

### 3.3 Limited Duration

The duration of intervention behavior must not exceed 10% of the total duration of the observation stage, and the absolute duration must not exceed 24 hours of the standard time of one's own plane. After the intervention ends, the operator must immediately withdraw from the target period or target plane, and must not linger.

## IV. Post-Intervention Causal Assessment and Correction

After the limited intervention ends, the operation team must immediately conduct a "post-intervention causal assessment", monitoring the causal chain changes caused by the intervention behavior through the Anchor network. The assessment content includes: the magnitude of causal deviation, the propagation range of the deviation, the long-term evolution trend of the deviation, and whether there is a paradox risk.

If the assessment finds that the causal deviation exceeds the acceptable range (the deviation magnitude exceeds 0.01% of the total causality of the target area), the operation team must immediately initiate the "causal correction procedure". The methods of causal correction include: injecting compensatory information at subsequent time nodes, conducting fine-tuning of causal chains through the Anchor network, and, in extreme cases, conducting a second regression to offset the impact of the previous intervention.

All intervention behaviors and their causal assessment results must be entered into the "Time Operations Archive" for permanent preservation, for use in subsequent audits and research.

## Conclusion

The guidelines for observing first and intervening within limits are the crystallization of the Hengyuan Universe's wisdom in the field of time and plane operations. With sufficient observation as the premise, strict thresholds as the boundary, and post-hoc assessment as the guarantee, they construct an operational paradigm of "know before acting, act with measure". The fundamental purpose of this guideline is not to fetter the hands of exploration, but to ensure that every exploration does not come at the cost of destroying the causal order of the universe.
