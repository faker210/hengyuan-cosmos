## I. Scheme Overview

The off-site archaeology scheme of the Replication Mirror is the standard technical scheme for cross-plane and time-regression archaeology in the Hengyuan Universe, whose core idea is "no entry, no contact, only replication". Traditional archaeology requires archaeologists to personally enter the archaeological site for excavation, which inevitably causes physical interference and environmental pollution to the site. The Replication Mirror scheme, through spatial legal principle technology, completely replicates the archaeological target into an off-site isolated space without touching the original object at all, and then conducts all archaeological operations within the isolated space.

"Off-site" is the key feature of this scheme. The replication operation is completed in a safe area adjacent to the target plane or target period, but the generation of the copy and all subsequent archaeological research are conducted entirely in an independent space completely isolated from the target. This means that archaeological personnel never need to enter the target area, or even the target plane, from beginning to end — the entire archaeological process can be completed remotely in an isolated space in one's own plane.

The technical foundation of the off-site archaeology scheme of the Replication Mirror is the high-level application of the spatial legal principle dispatch system. Only cultivators who have reached the fourth level (the Fǎkōng Realm) or above of the spatial legal principle dispatch system, or those riding a Replication Mirror vehicle of equivalent technical level, can perform high-precision replication operations. Currently, the Hengyuan Universe has achieved engineered mass production of Replication Mirrors; standard-type Replication Mirror equipment can be operated by cultivators at the third level (the Huàkōng Realm) to complete routine archaeological tasks.

## II. The Technical Principles of the Replication Mirror

The core components of the Replication Mirror are the "spatial law scanning array" and the "particle state reconstructor". Its working process is divided into three stages:

### 2.1 The Spatial Scanning Stage

After the Replication Mirror is deployed, it first conducts an omnidirectional spatial law scan of the target area. The scanning array emits high-frequency spatial waves that penetrate all material and energy structures within the target area, recording all quantum parameters of each particle, including position, momentum, spin, and energy state. At the same time, the scanning array also detects the energy field distribution, law parameter fluctuations, and residual consciousness imprints of the target area.

The scanning process is completely passive — the spatial waves do not change any state of the matter they penetrate, and therefore cause no interference to the archaeological site. The scanning precision depends on the technical level of the Replication Mirror; the scanning precision of standard-type equipment is sub-atomic, while high-end research-type equipment can reach the quark level.

### 2.2 The Data Transmission Stage

The massive data obtained by scanning is transmitted to the isolated space through the encrypted channel of the Anchor network. Data transmission adopts the "quantum state encryption protocol", ensuring that data is neither stolen nor tampered with during transmission. The data volume is extremely enormous — for an area the size of an ordinary room, the complete scan data is approximately 10 to the 30th power bytes, requiring the high-dimensional data channel of the Anchor network to complete transmission within a reasonable time.

### 2.3 The Copy Reconstruction Stage

After receiving the data, the particle state reconstructor in the isolated space reconstructs the complete copy of the target area particle by particle in the isolated space, according to the precise parameters recorded in the scan. The reconstruction process starts from the bottom-level particles, building up layer by layer: atoms, molecules, material structures, energy fields, and consciousness imprints. After reconstruction is complete, the copy is completely identical to the original at the physical level — including the material, structure, surface traces, internal damage of the artifacts, and even the residual energy fields and consciousness imprints.

It should be particularly noted that the "consciousness imprints" in the copy are the residual environmental consciousness absorbed by the original during its long existence, and are not real consciousness entities. The copy possesses no autonomous consciousness and generates no new consciousness activity. This point has been confirmed by the judicial interpretation of the Symbiotic Court: the copy does not fall within the category of "life", and research on the copy does not involve ethical issues of life.

## IV. Key Points of Off-Site Archaeology Operations

The off-site archaeology scheme of the Replication Mirror requires attention to the following key points in actual operations:

1. **Deployment position selection**: The Replication Mirror should be deployed beyond the law safety distance of the target area, both ensuring that the scanning range covers the target and avoiding law disturbance to the target area caused by the device itself. The safety distance is usually more than 3 times the maximum dimension of the target area.

2. **Replication timing selection**: For time-regression archaeology, the deployment and scanning of the Replication Mirror should be conducted during the "law calm period" of the target period, avoiding replication operations at the moment of major historical events — at such times the causal chain density is extremely high, the energy consumption of replication operations increases substantially, and slight disturbance to the causal line may occur.

3. **Copy integrity verification**: After copy reconstruction is completed, a "copy-original consistency verification" must be conducted. The verification method is to scan the copy a second time and compare the scan data with the original scan data particle by particle; the error rate must be lower than 10 to the negative 12th power. If the error rate exceeds the standard, the copy must be destroyed and replication must be redone.

4. **Multi-round replication strategy**: For ultra-large relics, a single replication may not cover the entire area. In this case, the "zoned multi-round replication" strategy is adopted, dividing the relic into multiple areas to be replicated separately, and then splicing them together in the isolated space. During splicing, it must be ensured that the particle states at the boundaries of adjacent areas are seamlessly connected, without law faults.

## Conclusion

The off-site archaeology scheme of the Replication Mirror is a model of the Hengyuan Universe's engineering application of the laws of spatial legal principles. Through the technical path of "zero-contact replication and off-site research", it fundamentally resolves the contradiction between "knowledge acquisition" and "site protection" in traditional archaeology. This scheme not only greatly improves archaeological efficiency, but more importantly, it ensures that every archaeological operation leaves no trace on the target civilization and historical site — truly realizing "quietly I leave, just as quietly I came".
