## I. The Legislative Background and Core Concept of the Principle

The "knowledge-only retrieval" principle is one of the fundamental principles of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's cross-plane and time-regression archaeology, and its legal origin can be traced back to Article 48 of the Symbiotic Constitution: "No material, energy, or living being obtained from other planes or other time periods may be brought into one's own plane without special permission; the legitimate output of archaeological activities is limited to abstract knowledge and information." The establishment of this principle is an institutional choice made by the Hengyuan Universe after summarizing the painful lessons of early cross-plane exploration.

In the early period of the establishment of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, cross-plane exploration was still in a state of disorder. Some explorers brought material samples, energy crystals, and even living beings from other planes back to their own plane for research, triggering multiple serious "cross-plane pollution incidents". The most famous case is the "Alien Crystal Incident" — an energy crystal brought back from an Energy-Manifest Plane, due to the incompatibility of its law parameters with one's own plane, triggered law disorder in the law network of one's own plane lasting three years, causing anomalies in the cultivation systems of three star domains, and harming tens of thousands of cultivators to varying degrees. Subsequent investigations showed that if the "knowledge-only retrieval" principle had been followed at the time, this disaster could have been completely avoided.

The core concept of the "knowledge-only retrieval" principle is: knowledge is the only safe medium in cross-civilizational exchange. Matter and energy carry the law information of their source plane, and may trigger unpredictable reactions after entering a different law environment; whereas knowledge, as abstract information, can, after appropriate encoding and purification, shed the law imprints of its source plane and become a safe resource shared across planes.

## II. The Definition and Classification of "Knowledge"

Within the legal framework of the "knowledge-only retrieval" principle, "knowledge" has a strict definition and classification. Not all information can be regarded as "knowledge" and freely retrieved — only abstract information that has undergone "information purification" treatment and been confirmed to carry no law pollution meets the retrieval standard.

Knowledge is divided into three categories according to its degree of abstraction and safety level:

| Knowledge Category | Definition | Safety Level | Retrieval Requirement |
|----------|------|----------|----------|
| Level 1 Knowledge (Pure Abstract Knowledge) | Abstract concepts, mathematical theorems, logical deductions, philosophical ideas, etc., completely detached from material carriers | Highest (Green) | May be directly retrieved, no special approval required |
| Level 2 Knowledge (Descriptive Knowledge) | Descriptive information about objective things, including historical records, geographical data, biological characteristics, cultural descriptions, etc. | Medium (Yellow) | Must undergo information purification treatment, retrieved after approval by the Plane Administration |
| Level 3 Knowledge (Technical Knowledge) | Practical knowledge containing specific technical parameters, engineering schemes, cultivation methods | Lower (Orange) | Must undergo strict information purification and safety assessment, retrieved after special approval by the Anchor Assembly |

The following contents do not fall within the category of "knowledge" and are strictly prohibited from retrieval: material samples of any form (including microorganisms, cells, gene fragments), energy crystals or energy storage devices of any form, living beings or consciousness entities possessing autonomous consciousness, raw data of any form that has not undergone information purification, and any technical details that may be used for predatory cultivation.

## III. Information Purification Technology

Information purification is a key technical link in ensuring the safety of retrieved knowledge. Its core goal is to thoroughly strip the source plane law imprints carried in the raw information, retaining only the pure semantic content. The information purification process is divided into four steps:

### 3.1 Information Deconstruction

First, the raw information to be retrieved is structurally deconstructed, dismantled into two parts: "semantic content" and "carrier imprint". Semantic content is the abstract meaning that the information expresses; carrier imprint is the source plane law parameters absorbed by the information during storage and transmission. Information deconstruction is achieved through the "semantic-carrier separation algorithm", which can identify and separate the semantic components and law components in the information.

### 3.2 Law Stripping

Thorough law stripping is conducted on the separated carrier imprint. The stripping method is to pass the information through a "law-neutralization channel" — a special transmission channel whose law parameters are set to be completely neutral; when information passes through, all non-neutral law parameters it carries are absorbed by the channel walls. After law stripping, the information no longer carries any law imprints from the source plane.

### 3.3 Semantic Reconstruction

On the basis of law stripping, the semantic content is re-encoded, reconstructing the information using the standard information encoding system of one's own plane. Semantic reconstruction ensures that the retrieved knowledge can be correctly read and understood in the law environment of one's own plane, while also further eliminating any source plane information structures that may remain.

### 3.4 Safety Detection

The purified information must undergo strict safety detection, including: law residue detection (confirming that the information carries no foreign law parameters), semantic integrity detection (confirming that the purification process has not altered the semantic content of the information), and potential risk assessment (assessing the social and safety impacts that the knowledge may bring when disseminated in one's own plane). Only information that passes all three detections is permitted for retrieval.

## IV. The Retrieval Procedure and Audit System

Knowledge retrieval follows strict procedural management. A complete retrieval operation includes the following links:

1. **Knowledge organization**: the archaeological team organizes the research results into standardized knowledge documents, clearly marking the knowledge category and content summary.
2. **Purification application**: submit a knowledge purification and retrieval application to the Plane Administration or the Time Law Committee, attaching the knowledge documents and the archaeological mission permit documents.
3. **Information purification**: a professional information purification technical team conducts the four-step information purification treatment on the knowledge documents.
4. **Safety detection**: the purified knowledge undergoes three detections by an independent safety detection institution.
5. **Approved retrieval**: after the detections pass, the approval institution signs the retrieval permit, and the knowledge is retrieved to the knowledge database of one's own plane through the encrypted channel of the Anchor network.
6. **Archival entry**: the retrieved knowledge is officially entered into the database after verification by the knowledge database of one's own plane, and opened to researchers with appropriate permissions.

All retrieval operations must undergo a "retrieval audit". The audit content includes: whether the source of the retrieved knowledge is legitimate, whether the purification process is standard, whether the safety detection is complete, and whether the approval procedure is compliant. The audit is responsible by the Knowledge Retrieval Audit Office under the Anchor Oversight Committee, which conducts spot checks on all retrieval operations of the current quarter every quarter and a comprehensive audit every year. The audit results are reported to the Anchor Assembly and the summary is disclosed to the public.

## V. Violations and Legal Liability

Behaviors violating the "knowledge-only retrieval" principle include: retrieving unpurified information without approval, disguising material samples as knowledge for retrieval, deliberately hiding source plane law imprints in retrieved knowledge, and assisting others in illegal retrieval.

According to the Symbiotic Constitution and the Cross-Plane Interaction Management Law, the legal liability for illegal retrieval behaviors includes: confiscation of the items and information illegally retrieved, suspension or revocation of the party's archaeological and travel qualifications, fines of more than 10 times the value of the illegally retrieved items, imprisonment for serious cases, and for cases causing law pollution or ecological damage, bearing all restoration costs and compensation liability.

## Conclusion

The "knowledge-only retrieval" principle is the wisdom bottom line of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization in the field of cross-plane interaction. With strict definitions, mature purification technology, standard retrieval procedures, and severe legal liability, it constructs a solid "knowledge safety firewall". The significance of this principle lies not only in preventing pollution at the physical level, but more importantly, in establishing an ethical view of civilizational exchange — interaction between civilizations should center on the collision of ideas, not on the plunder of material.
