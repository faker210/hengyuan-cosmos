## I. Terminology Notes

This dictionary collects the core terms in the setting system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, aiming to unify the use of terms across all documents and ensure the consistency and accuracy of conceptual expression. All terms are sorted by the first letter of their Chinese pinyin; each entry contains the term name, the English reference name (if any), the definition, and a brief explanation. This dictionary is a dynamically updated document, and new terms will be continuously added as the setting system expands.

## III. Terminology Usage Standards

1. The terms included in this dictionary are standard terms, and all documents should give priority to the standard expressions in this dictionary.
2. The same concept must use the same term in different documents, and synonyms must not be arbitrarily substituted.
3. New terms generated in new settings should be promptly submitted to the Terminology Committee for review, and added to this dictionary after passing the review.
4. If the definition of a term is revised, the latest version of this dictionary prevails, and the revision content must also be recorded in the document version notes.
5. When citing a term, the full name may be used at its first appearance, and the abbreviation may be used subsequently, but the clarity of the meaning in context must be ensured.

## Conclusion

The glossary is the linguistic cornerstone of the entire setting system. The unified terminology system ensures conceptual consistency among the hundred documents, enabling readers to switch seamlessly between different modules without comprehension ambiguity. As the setting system continues to enrich and improve, this dictionary will be continuously updated, always serving as the authoritative source of terminology for all documents.
