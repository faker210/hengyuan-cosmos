## I. Index Notes

This index lists all three hundred cosmic origin cultivation systems opened by the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, arranged in six major categories, with fifty systems in each category, numbered continuously from 001 to 300. The first three systems (Deduction Iteration, Life Cycle, Spatial Legal Principle Dispatch) are the "Three Origin Foundations", the cultivation foundation of all systems. Each system lists its number, name, and a brief description of its core direction. This index is a reference tool; for the detailed cultivation methods and realm divisions of each system, refer to the dedicated document of the corresponding system.
