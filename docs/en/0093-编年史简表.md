## I. Notes on the Chronology

This compendium records, on the basis of Hengyuan Universe standard time, the major historical events and key milestones in the development of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, arranged in chronological order of eras. The time span extends from the ancient period of the Pre-Anchor Era to the future outlook of the Interstellar Symbiosis Era. Each era lists its start and end times, its characteristics, and its major events. This compendium is a highly condensed historical index; for the detailed history of each era, see the dedicated accounts in documents 70–79.

## III. The Pre-Anchor Era (approx. −10 billion to −10,000 years)

The Pre-Anchor Era was the longest era in the history of the Hengyuan Universe, lasting approximately ten billion years. Its core characteristic was that predatory cultivation systems dominated the universe, with civilizations repeatedly cycling through "rise—zenith—predation—collapse."

| Time node | Major event |
|----------|----------|
| approx. −10 billion years | The law network of the Hengyuan Universe stabilized; the first material planes formed; the universe entered a habitable state |
| approx. −8 billion years | The first intelligent life emerged on multiple material planes; primitive cultivation systems began to sprout |
| approx. −5 billion years | Predatory cultivation systems gradually became mainstream; the strong achieved rapid evolution by devouring the weak |
| approx. −3 billion years | The first universe-scale predation war erupted; civilizations in multiple star regions suffered devastating blows |
| approx. −1 billion years | The drawbacks of the predatory system began to manifest; the overall entropy increase of the universe accelerated; cultivation resources gradually dried up |
| approx. −500 million years | The first thinkers reflecting on the predatory system appeared; the sprouts of symbiotic thought began to take shape |
| approx. −100 million years | Small-scale experimental symbiotic civilizations emerged in multiple star regions, but all were destroyed by predatory civilizations |
| approx. −1 million years | The universe as a whole entered the "Great Decline"; both the number of civilizations and the level of cultivation fell to historical lows |
| approx. −10,000 years | A cultivator on the First Plane, during long-term cultivation, accidentally perceived a stable node in the law network; the anchor mechanism was discovered for the first time |

## IV. The Anchor Awakening Era (−10,000 to −1,000 years)

The Anchor Awakening Era was the transitional period of the anchor mechanism from discovery to initial application. Its core characteristic was the gradual maturation of anchor technology and the spread of symbiotic thought.

| Time node | Major event |
|----------|----------|
| −10,000 years | The anchor mechanism was discovered for the first time; the discoverer named it the "Anchor" and began systematic research |
| −9,000 years | The first artificial anchor was successfully established, proving that anchors could be artificially constructed and maintained |
| −8,000 years | Anchor technology began to spread within a small scope; the first anchor cultivators appeared |
| −7,000 years | It was discovered that anchors could serve as benchmarks for cross-plane connections; the first recorded anchor-assisted plane traversal succeeded |
| −6,000 years | Symbiotic thought began to combine with anchor technology; the first theoretical texts on "Anchor Symbiosis" were published |
| −5,000 years | The first symbiotic city-state based on anchors was established, proving the feasibility of the symbiotic system |
| −4,000 years | Predatory civilizations launched attacks on symbiotic city-states; the symbiotic city-states successfully defended themselves by virtue of anchor technology |
| −3,000 years | Symbiotic thought spread across multiple star regions; more and more civilizations began to attempt the symbiotic transition |
| −2,000 years | The first "Great Symbiosis Assembly of the Ten Thousand Races" convened; representatives from dozens of races jointly discussed the future of symbiotic civilization |
| −1,000 years | Anchor technology and symbiotic thought had acquired the conditions for establishing a unified civilization; the Establishment Period of the Symbiotic System began |

| −800 years | The Output-Linked Contract Mechanism began to be promoted across the entire domain |

| −600 years | The five supporting laws on education, medical care, housing, environment, and antitrust were all promulgated and implemented |
| −500 years | The excavation and compilation of the Three Hundred Origin Cultivation Systems was officially launched |
| −400 years | The first fifty Origin Cultivation Systems completed compilation and verification |
| −300 years | Breakthroughs were made in the technological application of the Multiplying Fruit; the hundredfold deduction amplification technology matured |
| −200 years | All Three Hundred Systems completed compilation and entered the final safety verification stage |
| −100 years | The conditions for the full opening of the Three Hundred Origin Cultivation Systems matured; the opening period began |

## Conclusion

The Chronology Compendium is the epic of struggle of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization — from ignorance to awakening, from weakness to strength, from its own plane to the entire universe. The succession of each era bears witness to symbiotic ideals triumphing over predatory ideals, and civilized progress triumphing over barbaric decline. History is not the dust of the past but the seed of the future — to understand this history is to understand whence the symbiotic civilization came and whither it goes.
