## I. Notes

This document records the key figures and important organizations in the development of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, arranged by historical period and organizational type. The key figures section lists each figure's name, period, core contributions, and historical standing; the important organizations section lists each organization's name, founding time, core functions, and historical role. This document is a dynamically updated document; as history advances and organizations evolve, it will be continuously supplemented and revised.

## Conclusion

Key figures and organizations are the living carriers of the history of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. From the pioneering thinkers of antiquity to the civilization leaders of the present, from the supreme power institutions to civil social organizations, every figure and organization plays an irreplaceable role in the grand narrative of the symbiotic civilization.
