## I. Notes

This document compiles common questions and answers about the setting system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, arranged by theme. The questions cover all aspects, including the core concepts of the worldview, the cultivation system, the social system, the legal system, cross-plane interaction, and the rules of time. The goal of this document is to provide a quick getting-started guide for readers encountering the setting of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization for the first time, while also providing authoritative answers to common questions for in-depth researchers. This document is a dynamically updated document; new Q&As will be continuously added based on reader feedback.

## II. Worldview and Core Concepts

**Q1: What is the core proposition of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization?**
A: The core proposition of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization is "against predation, for symbiosis." Specifically, it opposes predatory cultivation systems that come at the cost of devouring the energy, fortune, or even origins of the weak, and advocates achieving common evolution through symbiotic cooperation. The core concept of the civilization is "returning the right to choose destiny and evolution to all living beings," and its basic policies are "more work, more gain; self-directed growth; a path for everyone; joint advancement of the Ten Thousand Races."

**Q2: What does the principle of "Reality as the Foundation" mean?**
A: "Reality as the Foundation" is one of the fundamental stances of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, meaning that the real world is the only real world, and the world of fiction is a hypothetical thought experiment. In setting construction, what is lacking in fiction is supplemented with reality, and what does not exist in reality is upgraded and created based on objective laws. This principle ensures the logical self-consistency and real-world perceptibility of the setting system, avoiding the collapse of settings caused by pure fantasy.

**Q3: What is the relationship between the Hengyuan Universe and the universe?**
A: The full name of the Hengyuan Universe is the "Wanling Hengmao Origin Universe." It is the entirety of the universe inhabited and governed by the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. It is not an ordinary single physical universe, but a composite entity formed by four levels — material planes, energy layers, the consciousness domain, and the law network. It can be understood that the Hengyuan Universe is a super-universe system containing countless planes, with the anchor mechanism as its core regulatory means.

## III. The Cultivation System

**Q4: How did the Three Hundred Origin Cultivation Systems come about?**
A: The Three Hundred Systems are not man-made fabricated techniques but orthodox cultivation paths directly distilled from the origin laws of the universe and verified through anchors. During the Establishment Period of the Symbiotic System, the team led by "Sānbǎi Shī" conducted a systematic excavation and compilation of the laws of the universe, ultimately summarizing Three Hundred cultivation systems covering all evolutionary dimensions. These systems are divided into six major categories — Origin Deduction, Life Cycle, Spatial Legal Principle, Consciousness Ascension, Symbiotic Network, and Applied Engineering — with fifty systems in each category.

**Q5: Why are the first three systems called the "Three Origin Foundations"?**
A: The first three systems — Deduction Iteration (System No. 1), Life Cycle (System No. 2), and Spatial Legal Principle Dispatch (System No. 3) — are the cultivation foundations of all other systems. The Deduction Iteration System provides the foundation of wisdom and methodology; the Life Cycle System provides the foundation of life energy; the Spatial Legal Principle Dispatch System provides the foundation of spatial and law operations. Cultivators of any other system are advised to first practice at least one of the Three Origin Foundations to establish a stable evolutionary foundation.

**Q6: What is the Multiplying Fruit? What is its relationship with One Piece?**
A: The Multiplying Fruit is one of the "opening buffs" of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, originating from the Multiplying Fruit setting in the One Piece world. In the Hengyuan Universe, the Multiplying Fruit has been technologically applied and can enhance a cultivator's deductive ability a hundredfold; it is the core auxiliary tool of the Deduction Iteration System. This setting embodies the principle of "supplementing what fiction lacks with reality, and upgrading and creating what reality does not have based on laws" — interesting settings from fictional works are incorporated into the technological system of the symbiotic civilization after being transformed through law-based adaptation.

**Q7: What is the 20% Activation Strategy? Why 20%?**
A: The 20% Activation Strategy is a staged strategic goal of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, aiming to activate 20% of all living beings in the universe as Supreme Powers of the Plane in the early stage. The choice of 20% is based on the dynamic analysis of the symbiosis network — when 20% of the nodes in a network reach a high-energy state, the overall resonance effect of the network triggers a critical point, driving the accelerated evolution of the remaining 80% of nodes. These 20% Supreme Powers of the Plane will serve as the backbone force, playing a core role in anchor maintenance, mentor guidance, and civilization construction.

## IV. Social System and Law

**Q8: What are the Five Symbiotic Civilization Typologies?**
A: The Five Symbiotic Civilization Typologies are five types of symbiotic societies defined on the basis of the characteristics of real-world civilizations, including: the East Asian Intensive Type (characterized by highly efficient organization and collectivism), the Nordic Welfare Type (characterized by high welfare and social equality), the North American Pluralistic Type (characterized by diverse cultures and innovative vitality), the Continental European Social Type (characterized by the social market economy and labor participation), and the Developing Catch-up Type (characterized by rapid development and latecomer advantages). These five typologies are not mutually exclusive classifications but reference models that can learn from one another and develop through integration.

**Q9: How does the Output-Linked Contract Mechanism work?**
A: The Output-Linked Contract Mechanism is the core resource allocation system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, inspired by the household output-linked contract responsibility system in reality. Its mode of operation is: the cultivation resources of the universe are allocated to individuals or groups through "contracting"; contractors obtain the right to use and benefit from resources through labor and contributions. More work, more gain — the greater the contribution, the more resources obtained. This mechanism both ensures the fair distribution of resources and stimulates the labor initiative of individuals, achieving the unity of fairness and efficiency.

**Q10: What is the relationship between the Symbiotic Constitution and ordinary laws?**
A: The Symbiotic Constitution is the fundamental law of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization and has the highest legal effect. All laws, administrative regulations, and local regulations must not contravene the Constitution. The Symbiotic Labor Law, the Consumer Protection Law, and the five supporting laws on education, medical care, housing, environment, and antitrust are all departmental laws enacted on the legislative basis of the Symbiotic Constitution, responsible for making the principled provisions of the Constitution concrete and operable. The Symbiotic Court has the power to conduct constitutional review of laws that violate the Constitution and declare them void.

## V. Cross-Plane Interaction

**Q11: What is the archaeological scheme of "Replication Mirror + isolated space + knowledge-only retrieval"?**
A: This is the standard scheme for cross-plane and time-regression archaeology of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, consisting of three core elements:
1. **Replication Mirror**: Performs a complete 1:1 replication of the archaeological target without touching the original; the replication process does not alter any state of the original.
2. **Isolated space**: The replica is generated in an independent space completely isolated from the target; archaeologists conduct all research operations within the isolated space.
3. **Knowledge-only retrieval**: After archaeology ends, only abstract knowledge and information may be returned; the replica's matter and energy must be destroyed within the isolated space and must not be brought back to the home plane.
This scheme ensures zero interference of the archaeological process with the target plane or target period, while preventing cross-plane pollution.

**Q12: Why must the principle of "observe first, then intervene within limits" be followed?**
A: The core concept of this principle is "cognitive humility" — even a Supreme Power of the Plane cannot fully grasp all causal relationships of a time node or a plane civilization. Any direct intervention in the past or in another plane may trigger unpredictable causal chain reactions. Therefore, one must first conduct comprehensive observation with the most prudent attitude, and only when observational data are sufficient and risk assessment is clear may limited intervention strictly bounded in scope and degree be permitted. This principle is the fundamental guarantee of the safety of time regression and cross-plane operations.

**Q13: What conditions are required for cross-plane traversal?**
A: Plane traversal requires the following conditions: first, the traveler must possess cultivation at the third rank of the Spatial Legal Principle Dispatch System (the Huàkōng Realm) or above, or be carried by a traversal vehicle that meets the standards; second, a traversal permit of the corresponding level issued by the Plane Administration must be obtained (Level A archaeological observation, Level B academic exchange, Level C resource cooperation, Level D emergency intervention); third, both the starting point and the destination must have anchors for coordinate calibration; fourth, the three-stage purification procedures (biological purification, energy purification, consciousness purification) must be completed; fifth, traversal energy must be carried by oneself, and drawing energy from the target plane is prohibited.

## VI. Rules of Time

**Q14: What is the difference between time regression and time travel?**
A: In the setting of the Hengyuan Universe, time regression is not equal to time travel. The time regressor does not enter the past in a complete material form, but arrives at the target time node in the form of a consciousness projection or information packet. The regressor's existence in the past period is "semi-transparent" — observable, but physical interaction ability is strictly limited. This design itself is part of the time regression safety mechanism, reducing the possibility of the regressor causing physical interference to the past.

**Q15: What types of time paradoxes are there? How are they prevented and controlled?**
A: Time paradoxes are divided into four major categories: the parricide paradox (the regressor's actions eliminate the causal conditions of their own existence), the information paradox (information transmitted from the future to the past forms an information cycle without a source), the ontological paradox (the regressor's interaction with their past self causes ontological consciousness confusion), and the event paradox (the regressor alters key historical events, causing the reconstruction of the causal chain). The prevention and control mechanisms include: pre-regression causal assessment and causal isolation of the regressor before the operation, a whitelist system for intervention behaviors during the operation, real-time causal monitoring and three-level emergency response during the operation, and post-operation causal repair and audit. The goal of the entire prevention and control system is to eliminate paradoxes in their infancy.

## VII. Life and Races

**Q16: What are Anomalous Life and Anomalous Energy?**
A: Anomalous Life refers to life forms that do not conform to the conventional definition of life and possess special forms of existence, such as pure-energy life, consciousness-form life, and mechanical life. Anomalous Energy refers to energy forms that do not conform to conventional energy classification and possess special attributes and behavioral patterns, such as law energy, consciousness energy, and causal energy. For these two types of special existences, the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization has established three types of standardized handling procedures (identification and classification, assessment and control, integration and symbiosis), ensuring that they are properly managed and rationally utilized within the framework of the symbiotic civilization.

**Q17: What is a Dark Replacement Entity? What is the Dual-Soul Ethics all about?**
A: A Dark Replacement Entity is a special life form in whose body two souls exist simultaneously — usually a native soul and an external "replacement" soul. This state raises complex ethical issues: both souls have their own rights and personalities, and neither can be simply eliminated. The core of the Dual-Soul Ethics guidelines is to respect the respective rights of the two souls and prohibit forced fusion or unilateral elimination. According to the specific situation, Dark Replacement Entities are divided into four branches, each adopting a different handling approach: the coexistence type (the two souls coexist harmoniously), the alternation type (the two souls take turns controlling the body), the separation type (the two souls are separated into different carriers through technical means), and the fusion type (deep fusion into a new soul on the basis of mutual voluntariness).

## VIII. Setting Methodology

**Q18: How is this setting system different from ordinary web-novel settings?**
A: The greatest difference of the setting system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization lies in its core stance of "anti-predation." Ordinary web-novel settings often center on the protagonist's personal growth and power enhancement, and the cultivation system is essentially predatory — the protagonist grows stronger through devouring, combat, and seizing resources. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization fundamentally opposes this model, advocating the common evolution of the Ten Thousand Races through symbiotic cooperation. In addition, this setting system emphasizes "Reality as the Foundation," striving for logical self-consistency and conformity with objective laws in all settings, avoiding pure fantasy and the collapse of settings.

**Q19: What exactly is the Four-Stage Ascension Path?**
A: The Four-Stage Ascension Path is the strategic route for the overall ascension of the Hengyuan Universe:
1. **Founding Reality**: Establish a symbiotic social system on the material planes, eliminate the root of predation, and complete the institutional foundation construction of the civilization.
2. **Technological Empowerment**: Combine cultivation laws with technological engineering, lower the threshold of evolution, and achieve a leap in productive forces.
3. **Consciousness Evolution**: Promote the migration of life forms toward the consciousness domain, achieving symbiosis and sublimation at the spiritual level.
4. **Interstellar Symbiosis**: Establish a complete anchor network across planes and across the stars, completing the overall ascension of the universe.
The Hengyuan Universe is currently in the critical period of transition from the first stage to the second stage.

**Q20: How can one participate in the expansion of this setting system?**
A: The setting system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization is an open and continuously expanding system. Anyone can conduct expansion creation based on the existing setting framework, including but not limited to: detailing the specific techniques of a certain cultivation system, creating the culture and history of a certain race, designing the civilization form of a certain plane, writing the detailed events of a certain historical period, and developing new technologies and application scenarios. Expansion settings should follow the principle of "Reality as the Foundation, upgrading through laws," ensuring logical self-consistency with existing settings. Excellent expansion settings can be incorporated into the formal setting system after review.
