## I. Notes

The Setting Contradiction Self-Check List is the quality control tool of the setting system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, used to systematically identify possible setting contradictions and logical inconsistencies when creating new settings, revising old settings, and updating documents. This self-check list is compiled on the basis of the 14 core settings and the consistency check list established in SPEC.md, covering all modules including the worldview, the cultivation system, the social system, the legal system, life forms, the technological system, historical eras, and cross-plane interaction. Any new setting or revised content must pass all check items of this self-check list before being formally incorporated into the setting system.

## V. Self-Check Process and Usage

### 5.1 Self-Check Process for New Setting Creation

1. **Preliminary self-check**: after the creation is completed, the creator checks item by item against this self-check list and marks the items with problems.
2. **Revision and improvement**: revise the marked problem items to ensure all pass.
3. **Cross-review**: submit to another setting creator for cross-review, focusing on cross-module consistency.
4. **Committee approval**: major setting changes must be submitted to the Setting Maintenance Committee for approval.
5. **Archival update**: after passing all checks, formally incorporate into the setting system and update the relevant index documents.

### 5.2 Self-Check Process for Revising Old Settings

1. **Impact assessment**: assess the other documents and setting modules that the revised content may affect.
2. **Related-document checks**: conduct consistency checks on all affected related documents.
3. **Synchronized revision**: revise the affected content in the related documents in synchronization.
4. **Version recording**: record the revision content and version number in Document 99 "Document Version and Maintenance Notes."
5. **Announcement notice**: major revisions must publish a change announcement to all setting users.

### 5.3 Regular Comprehensive Self-Check

The Setting Maintenance Committee conducts a comprehensive setting consistency self-check every quarter, systematically checking all 100 documents to identify potential setting contradictions and logical inconsistencies. The self-check results form the "Quarterly Setting Consistency Report," which is made public to all setting users.

## Conclusion

The Setting Contradiction Self-Check List is the "quality constitution" of the setting system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. Through systematized, checklist-style check items, it ensures that the setting system always maintains logical self-consistency and terminology unification in the process of continuous expansion and revision. What a vast setting system fears most is not insufficient content richness, but rampant internal contradictions and inconsistency between earlier and later parts. The existence of this self-check list is precisely to ensure that the setting system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, while maintaining openness and growth, always possesses a solid foundation of consistency.
