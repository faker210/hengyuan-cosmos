# Chapter 103 | Sense-Origin Realm: Differentiated Cultivation Systems of the Myriad-Race Groups

> Module: Module One · Deep Realm Refinement | Grounding documents: Docs 14, 50, 52, 10

## Introduction: The Myriad Races Are Not Ten Thousand Kinds of Humans

The Axiom of Myriad Races Republic of Heng Yuan Zhou (The Original Cosmos of Ten Thousand Spirits and the Balanced Anchor) is the most easily misunderstood in concept: the Myriad Races are not "ten thousand kinds of humans," but billions of life forms across five lineages—carbon-based, energy, mechanical, consciousness, and hybrid. The Sense-Origin Realm, as the starting point of the Nine Realms, must carry this diversity in the posture of "one realm, myriad methods": called by the same name, the beast-race's "perception" is smell and intuition, the spirit-race's "perception" is energy resonance, the machine-race's "perception" is data scan.

To measure the Myriad Races by the standard of a single species is itself an arrogant waste of energy-tier. Based on *Overview of the Myriad-Race Lineages* (Doc 50) and *Classification and Symbiosis of Alien Races* (Doc 52), this article unfolds the differentiated cultivation systems of the Myriad Races in the Sense-Origin Realm.

The Six Top Axioms are the legislative foundation of Myriad-Race differentiation: the Axiom of Anti-Predation—each race draws energy only from the cosmic circulation, never devouring another's origin; the Axiom of Myriad Races Republic—equal evolutionary entitlement regardless of lineage; the Axiom of Collective Dimensional Ascension—every race's talent feeds back into civilization, stamping out big-material-small-use; the Axiom of Causal Self-Discipline—cross-race co-cultivation respects the causal boundary of other races; the Axiom of Law Priority—the law framework of the Myriad-Race differentiated cultivation system is stable and self-consistent, never retconned for plot convenience, and can expand infinitely without collapsing. The Law of Universal Infinite Equality for All Beings establishes that every being inherently carries Fundamental Infinite Power; the laid-back retain a guaranteed baseline infinity and will not be eliminated by the times, while grinders and peerless youths can stack higher tiers of infinity. Infinite origin is carried by all; predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Sense-Origin Differences Across the Five Lineages

According to the five-part Myriad-Race classification of Doc 53 (carbon-based material races, energy-condensed races, mechanical-constructed races, consciousness-information races, hybrid-variant races), the starting points of each race in the Sense-Origin Realm are naturally different:

| Lineage | Representative groups | Sense-Origin features | Inclined systems |
|---------|----------------------|------------------------|------------------|
| Carbon-based material | Human, beast, wood races | Perception starts from the flesh and five senses; needs time to establish energy-perception consciousness | Life Circulation System, Deductive Iteration System |
| Energy-condensed | Spirit races, elemental life | Innately an energy body; "perception" is near instinct, easily skipping basics to condense directly | Spatial Juridical Dispatch System, Origin Deduction Category |
| Mechanical-constructed | Machine race, mechanical life | "Perceives" energy in a data-ized way; high precision but lacks intuition | Applied Engineering Category, Deductive Iteration System |
| Consciousness-information | Consciousness entities, high-dimensional existences | Perception is consciousness activity itself; the Sense-Origin Realm highly overlaps its origin form | Consciousness Ascension Category |
| Hybrid-variant | Cross-plane mixed-bloods, anomalous life | Starting points differ greatly; case-by-case assessment required | System-compatibility assessment first |

Differences must be respected, but must not be used to manufacture a "superior race": the spirit race perceives fast but its flesh bearing is weak; the machine race is precise but its spiritual evolution is hard; the consciousness race starts high but has more remedial lessons once it leaves the material plane. The unified standard of the Sense-Origin Realm (Doc 14) assesses the substantive ability of "perceive-absorb-discriminate," not the concrete form of perception—this is the racial version of the "value substance over name" principle.

## II. Differentiated Paths: Each Race Has Its Road, Every Road Reaches the Origin

- Beast-race path: anchored in bodily intuition, train body-feel first then resonance; the Sense-Origin Realm is "awakening the energy-language in the bloodline."
- Wood-race path: anchored in the rhythm of growth, slow and stable absorption; the Sense-Origin Realm syncs with tree rings, one realm may be cultivated for a hundred years without hurry.
- Spirit-race path: anchored in energy resonance, the Sense-Origin Realm stresses "convergence" over "opening," learning to control the overload of innate perception.
- Machine-race path: anchored in data calibration, turning energy perception into a recomputable model; the Sense-Origin Realm is "calibrating the sensors."
- Consciousness-race path: anchored in lucidity of consciousness, the Sense-Origin Realm is "consciousness landing," learning to express perception in the language of the material plane.

Every path leads to the same set of realm certification, but behind it lies each one's native logic. This is precisely the grassroots form of the Myriad-Race origin's two-way circulation: each race draws from the cosmos in its own way and feeds back to civilization in its own way; the circulation is never one-directional.

## III. Cross-Race Co-Cultivation: The True Meaning of the Mentor Network

Myriad-Race differentiation does not mean each cultivates alone; cross-race co-cultivation is precisely the most valuable scene of the Sense-Origin Realm:

- Cross-race mentors: a spirit-race mentor calibrates energy perception for a beast-race student; a human mentor helps a spirit-race student understand flesh bearing—the essence of the mentor network (Doc 10) is civilization-level flow of experience.
- Compatibility assessment: the system-compatibility assessment mechanism provides case plans for mixed-blood individuals and anomalous life, stamping out "labeling by race."
- Equal certification: realm certification and resource allocation recognize only data and contribution, not birth; the Myriad-Race peerless youths (Chapter 102) share the dual-path mechanism.

## IV. Comparison: Racial Narrow-Mindedness Is the Ceiling of Inclusive Systems

This is precisely the direct manifestation of the "Tang-Luo ceiling" on the Myriad-Race dimension: even the top-tier inclusive system of the old era (Tang Luo's Peerless Academy) opened only to the human race—beasts, plant spirits, and planet wills were all excluded from "inclusiveness." That is no mere kind omission, but the structural inevitability of the predatory worldview: in the "hunting-ground worldview," whoever is not of my kind is either prey or a tool, never a fellow cultivator.

The answer given by Heng Yuan Zhou's full Myriad-Race coverage baseline is: the Sense-Origin Realm is not the human starting point, but the Myriad-Race starting point; 99% coverage is not a slogan, but every race has a door wide open.

## V. Five Core Landing Points

- Anti-energy-tier waste: every race's talent is a scarce resource; measuring the Myriad Races by a single standard is the greatest big-material-small-use.
- Anti-predation: all differentiated paths rest on circulation and symbiosis, none on devouring another race's origin.
- Tang-Luo ceiling comparison: inclusiveness of racial narrow-mindedness is not inclusiveness but grace; the object of grace is always "one of us."
- Full Myriad-Race coverage: five lineages plus anomalous life plus cross-plane mixed-bloods; the Sense-Origin Realm faces 99% full coverage of the Myriad Races.
- Hyper-pluralistic full openness: each race's Sense-Origin methods are openly shared; the first link of the fully open chain does not distinguish race.

## Conclusion

The Myriad-Race panorama of the Sense-Origin Realm proves one thing: cosmic diversity is not an obstacle to evolution, but the fuel of evolution. When every race perceives the world and feeds back to civilization in its own best way, the Myriad-Race symbiotic cosmos moves from "allowing difference" to "needing difference"—and this is precisely the life-science basis on which the Hyper-Pluralistic Balanced-Anchor Realm stands.
