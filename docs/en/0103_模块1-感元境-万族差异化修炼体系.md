# Entry 103 | Sense Origin Realm: Differentiated Cultivation System for Myriad Races

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 50, 52, 10

## Introduction: Myriad Races are not ten thousand kinds of humans

The republican axiom of Myriad Races in Heng Yuan Zhou (The Original Cosmos of Ten Thousand Spirits and the Balanced Anchor) is most easily misunderstood conceptually: Myriad Races are not "ten thousand kinds of humans," but billions of life forms across five genealogy lineages — carbon-based, energy, mechanical, consciousness, and hybrid. As the starting point of the nine realms, the Sense Origin Realm must embrace this diversity with the posture of "one realm, myriad methods": the same name Sense Origin Realm, but "perception" for the beast tribe is smell and intuition, for the spirit tribe is energy resonance, and for the machine tribe is data scanning.

Using a single species' standard to measure all Myriad Races is itself arrogant energy waste. This document follows the Myriad Races Genealogy Overview (Document 50) and Alien Classification and Symbiosis (Document 52), and unfolds the differentiated cultivation system of Myriad Races in the Sense Origin Realm.

The six top-level axioms are the legislative foundation of Myriad Race differentiation: the anti-predation axiom — each race draws energy only from cosmic circulation, never devouring others' origin; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — equal evolutionary qualification regardless of genealogy; the collective ascension axiom — each race's talent must feed back into civilization, eliminating talent underuse; the causal self-discipline axiom — cross-race co-cultivation respects others' causal boundaries; the law-first axiom — the legal framework of the Myriad Race differentiated cultivation system is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse. The universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose higher levels of infinity; infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Sense Origin Realm Differences Across Five Genealogies

According to Document 53's five major classifications (carbon-based material race, energy condensation race, mechanical construction race, consciousness information race, hybrid variation race), each race's Sense Origin Realm starting point is naturally different:

| Genealogy | Representative groups | Sense Origin Realm features | Preferred system |
|-----------|----------------------|-----------------------------|------------------|
| Carbon-based material | Humans, beasts, wood tribe | Perception starts with body and five senses; needs time to build energy-perception consciousness | Life Cycle System, Deduction Iteration System |
| Energy condensation | Spirits, elemental life | Naturally energy bodies; "perception" is near instinct; tends to skip basics and condense directly | Spatial Law Dispatching System, origin deduction type |
| Mechanical construction | Machine tribe, mechanical life | "Perceives" energy in data form; high precision but lacks intuition | Application engineering, Deduction Iteration System |
| Consciousness information | Consciousness entities, higher-dimensional beings | Perception is itself consciousness activity; Sense Origin Realm highly overlaps with their origin form | Consciousness ascension type |
| Hybrid variation | Cross-plane half-bloods, exotic life | Starting points vary wildly; must be case-assessed | System compatibility assessment first |

Differences must be respected, but cannot be used to create "superior races": spirit races perceive fast but have weak physical bearing; machine races have high precision but struggle with spiritual evolution; consciousness races start high but have more catching-up to do after leaving the material plane. The unified standard of the Sense Origin Realm (Document 14) assesses the substantive ability of "perceive-absorb-identify," not the specific form of perception — this is the racial version of the "substance over name" principle.

## II. Differentiated Paths: Each Race Has Its Own Way, All Lead to Origin

- Beast tribe path: anchored by bodily intuition, first train somatic sensation then induction; Sense Origin Realm is "awakening the energy language in the bloodline"
- Wood tribe path: anchored by growth rhythm, slow and stable absorption; Sense Origin Realm syncs with annual rings; one realm can be cultivated for a hundred years without haste
- Spirit tribe path: anchored by energy resonance; Sense Origin Realm emphasizes "contraction" not "opening," learning to control innate perception overload
- Machine tribe path: anchored by data calibration, converting energy perception into recomputable models; Sense Origin Realm is "calibrating sensors"
- Consciousness tribe path: anchored by consciousness clarity; Sense Origin Realm is "consciousness landing," learning to express perception in the language of the material plane

Each path leads to the same realm certification, but behind the certification lies its own native logic. This is precisely the grassroots form of the Myriad Race origin two-way circulation: each race draws from the cosmos in its own way and gives back to civilization in its own way; circulation is never one-directional.

## III. Cross-Race Co-Cultivation: The True Meaning of the Mentor Network

Myriad Race differentiation does not mean each cultivates alone; cross-race co-cultivation is precisely the most valuable scene of the Sense Origin Realm:

- Cross-race mentors: spirit mentors help beast students calibrate energy perception; human mentors help spirit students understand physical bearing — the essence of the mentor network (Document 10) is civilization-level experiential flow
- Compatibility assessment: the system compatibility assessment mechanism provides individualized solutions for half-bloods and exotic life, eliminating "race labeling"
- Equal certification: realm certification and resource allocation recognize only data and contribution, not birth; Myriad Race Youth Supremes (Entry 102) share the dual-path mechanism

## IV. Comparison: Racial Narrow-Mindedness is the Ceiling of Universal Welfare

This is precisely the direct manifestation of the "Tang-Luo upper limit" in the Myriad Race dimension: the old era's top-tier universal welfare system (Tang-Luo's Peerless Academy) also only opened its doors to humans — beasts, plant spirits, and planetary wills were all excluded from "universal welfare." That was not merely a well-intentioned omission, but a structural necessity of the predatory worldview: in the "hunting ground cosmos view," those not of your kind are either prey or tools, never fellow cultivators.

Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad Race full-coverage baseline gives the answer: the Sense Origin Realm is not humanity's starting point, but the Myriad Races'; 99% coverage is not a slogan, but an open door for every race.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- Anti-energy waste: each race's talent is a scarce resource; using a single standard to measure Myriad Races is the greatest misuse of talent
- Anti-predation: all differentiated paths are based on circulation and symbiosis, none on devouring other races' origin
- Tang-Luo upper limit comparison: racially narrow universal welfare is not welfare, but patronage; the object of patronage is always only "our own kind"
- Myriad Races full coverage: five genealogies + exotic life + cross-plane half-bloods, Sense Origin Realm is 99% open to all Myriad Races
- Hyper-multiverse full openness: each race's Sense Origin methods are openly shared, the first link of the fully open chain is race-blind

## Conclusion

The Myriad Race panorama of the Sense Origin Realm proves one thing: cosmic diversity is not an obstacle to evolution, but its fuel. When every race perceives the world and gives back to civilization in the way it is best at, the symbiotic cosmos of Myriad Races moves from "allowing difference" to "needing difference" — and this is precisely the life-science foundation on which the hyper-multiverse Balanced Anchor Realm stands.

The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's Myriad Race differentiated cultivation system ensures every race can find a perception path suited to its own life structure in the Sense Origin Realm. All perceptual data and breakthrough trajectories are filed in the Alaya Network, achieving cross-race experiential sharing and supervision.
