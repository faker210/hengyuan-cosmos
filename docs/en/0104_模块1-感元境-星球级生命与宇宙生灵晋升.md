# Entry 104 | Sense Origin Realm: Promotion Logic for Planetary Life and Cosmic Beings

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 00, 14, 53, 98

## Introduction: A planet is not a hunting ground, but a cultivator

The cosmic structure of Heng Yuan Zhou (The Original Cosmos of Ten Thousand Spirits and the Balanced Anchor) has four layers: Material Plane, Energy Layer, Consciousness Domain, and Law Network (Document 00). In this picture, "cultivators" are far more than humans, beasts, and spirits — planets themselves can possess consciousness, the Energy Layer can give birth to life, and projections of cosmic will dwell in the Law Network. The Sense Origin Realm exists for them too: a star core's "perception," an energy sea's "awareness," cosmic will's "self-clarity" are all planetary-scale versions of the Sense Origin Realm.

The old system treated planets as hunting grounds and the cosmos as a resource warehouse (Document 04's "draining the pond," "plane colonization"); the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization treats planets as residents and the cosmos as a community of life. This document unfolds the promotion logic of planetary life and cosmic beings.

The six top-level axioms hold at the macro scale as well: the anti-predation axiom — planets draw energy from the cosmos and give back to their ecosystems, never draining the pond dry; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — planets, energy seas, consciousness entities, and material life share equal evolutionary qualification; the collective ascension axiom — every self-clarification of cosmic beings is for the whole's ascension; the causal self-discipline axiom — star-core anchoring and ecological transformation strictly follow observe-first, limited-interfer-second; the law-first axiom — the legal framework of planetary life and cosmic being promotion logic is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse. The universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose higher levels of infinity; infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Sense Origin Realm of Planetary Life: Star Core Awakening

For a planet to become a cultivator, the first thing is "perceiving itself" — this is the planetary-scale Sense Origin Realm:

- Star-core perception: the planetary core's energy field begins to autonomously perceive external energy and law fluctuations, equivalent to establishing a star-core-level energy perception consciousness
- Ecological resonance: the planet's perception resonates with the life activities of all Myriad Races on its surface; the more prosperous the ecology, the clearer the planet's perception
- Consciousness sprout: after perception accumulates to a critical point, a non-personified "planetary consciousness sprout" forms — note, per "reality-based" principles and extended setting suggestions (Document 98 E02), it is closer to a self-organizing law phenomenon than any specific deity

Planetary life's promotion follows the trinity logic of "ecology-energy-consciousness": it cannot "perceive" by devouring surface life — that path is draining the pond, the planetary version of predatory self-destruction. What the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization provides planets is star-core anchor access: letting the star-core perception connect to the Alaya Network, obtaining channels to converse with other planes and other planets.

## II. Promotion Logic of Cosmic Beings: From Individual to Collective

The promotion logic of cosmic beings (energy life, consciousness entities, existences in the Law Network) differs from material life:

- Energy layer life: Sense Origin Realm is "stabilizing self-boundaries," learning to remain an independent perceptual subject amid the torrent of energy
- Consciousness domain life: Sense Origin Realm is "consciousness landing," learning to express perception in communicable form rather than chaos
- Law network existences: Sense Origin Realm is "self-clarity," realizing "I am I" from the operation of laws

Their commonality: promotion is not "eating more," but "connecting more clearly." The closer cosmic beings approach origin, the more they understand the true meaning of the Myriad Race origin two-way circulation — individual perception connects to the network, the network feeds back individual growth; this is the cosmic-scale Sense Origin Realm.

## III. Sense Origin Realm Interface for Exotic Life and Multiverse Residual Civilizations

According to Document 53's exotic life recognition standard, life forms that cannot be classified into the five genealogies also possess evolutionary qualification:

- Exotic life: such as void drifters, time-fragment life; the Sense Origin Realm for them is "finding an anchor" — finding a stable perceptual coordinate amid drift
- Multiverse residual civilizations: remnants from old multiverse eras; their Sense Origin Realm is "relearning literacy" — rebuilding energy perception with the language of the new civilization

These two categories are the corners most easily missed by the 99% full-coverage baseline, and also the touchstone of Heng Yuan Zhou's "not one less."

## IV. Comparison: Hunting-Ground Cosmos View vs. Symbiotic Cosmos View

- Predatory planetary logic: planets are resources, drain and discard; planetary will is an obstacle, erase it
- Symbiotic planetary logic: planets are fellow cultivators; star cores connect to anchors, ecology feeds back into civilization
- Predatory cosmic logic: the cosmos is a hunting ground; the strong harvest everything until nothing remains
- Symbiotic cosmic logic: the cosmos is a living being; Myriad Races and planets evolve together, ultimately ascending as a whole

The predatory faction enslaves even planets, so its ceiling is "overlord of one star region"; the symbiotic faction treats even planets as companions, so its ceiling is the Balanced Anchor hyper-multiverse realm — lifting the entire cosmos and ascending together.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- Anti-energy waste: the evolutionary potential of planetary life is the universe's largest sleeping asset; treating planets as hunting grounds is hyper-scale talent underuse
- Anti-predation: star-core access, ecological resonance — all promotion logic is insulated from devouring and draining
- Tang-Luo upper limit comparison: the old-era universal welfare system excluded even intelligent life beyond humans, let alone planetary consciousness; the welfare ceiling simply does not hold at the cosmic scale
- Myriad Races full coverage: planets, energy life, consciousness entities, exotic life, multiverse residual civilizations — all within 99% coverage
- Hyper-multiverse full openness: planetary-scale Sense Origin methods (star-core anchor access protocol) are openly shared, belonging to the macro-scale first link of the fully open chain

## Conclusion

When a planet begins to "perceive itself" in the anchor network, when an energy sea learns to "know itself," the symbiotic cosmos of Myriad Rays is truly complete. The Sense Origin Realm is not only the starting point of the individual, but of civilization — from the tiniest perception to the grandest star core, Heng Yuan Zhou writes "everyone has a path" as "every being has a path."
