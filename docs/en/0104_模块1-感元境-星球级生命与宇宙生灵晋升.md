# Chapter 104 | Sense-Origin Realm: The Promotion Logic of Planetary Life and Cosmic Beings

> Module: Module One · Deep Realm Refinement | Grounding documents: Docs 00, 14, 53, 98

## Introduction: A Planet Is Not a Hunting Ground, but a Cultivator

The cosmic structure of Heng Yuan Zhou (The Original Cosmos of Ten Thousand Spirits and the Balanced Anchor) has four layers: the Material Plane, the Energy Layer, the Consciousness Domain, and the Law Network (Doc 00). In this picture, "cultivator" is far more than the human, beast, and spirit races—a planet itself can hold consciousness, the energy layer can give birth to life, and projections of cosmic will dwell in the Law Network. The Sense-Origin Realm exists for them too: the star core's "perception," the energy sea's "awareness," the cosmic will's "self-luminosity," all are planetary-scale versions of the Sense-Origin Realm.

The old system treated planets as hunting grounds and the cosmos as a resource warehouse (Doc 04's "resource drain," "plane colonization"); the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization treats planets as residents and the cosmos as a life-community. This article unfolds the promotion logic of planetary life and cosmic beings.

The Six Top Axioms hold on the macro scale as well: the Axiom of Anti-Predation—a planet draws energy from the cosmos and feeds back to its ecology, never draining the pond dry; the Axiom of Myriad Races Republic—planets, energy seas, consciousness entities, and material life share equal evolutionary entitlement; the Axiom of Collective Dimensional Ascension—every self-luminosity of a cosmic being serves the whole's ascension; the Axiom of Causal Self-Discipline—star-core docking and ecological transformation strictly observe observe-first-then-limited-interference; the Axiom of Law Priority—the law framework of the promotion logic for planetary life and cosmic beings is stable and self-consistent, never retconned for plot convenience, and can expand infinitely without collapsing. The Law of Universal Infinite Equality for All Beings establishes that every being inherently carries Fundamental Infinite Power; the laid-back retain a guaranteed baseline infinity and will not be eliminated by the times, while grinders and peerless youths can stack higher tiers of infinity. Infinite origin is carried by all; predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Sense-Origin Realm of Planetary Life: Star-Core Awakening

For a planet to become a cultivator, the first thing is "perceiving itself"—this is the planetary-scale Sense-Origin Realm:

- Star-core perception: the energy field of the planetary core begins to autonomously sense external energy and law fluctuations, equivalent to establishing a star-core-grade energy-perception consciousness.
- Ecological resonance: the planet's perception resonates with the life activities of the Myriad Races on its surface; the more prosperous the ecology, the clearer the planet's perception.
- Consciousness sprout: after perception accumulates to the critical point, a non-personified "planet-consciousness sprout" forms—note that, per "Reality as Foundation" and the extended-setting suggestion (Doc 98 E02), it is closer to a law self-organization phenomenon than to any concrete deity.

The promotion of planetary life follows the trinity logic of "ecology-energy-consciousness": it cannot "perceive" by devouring surface beings—that road is called draining the pond dry, the planetary version of predatory self-destruction. What the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization provides the planet is star-core anchor docking: letting the star-core perception dock into the Alaya Network, gaining a channel of dialogue with other planes and other planets.

## II. The Promotion Logic of Cosmic Beings: From Individual to Collective

The promotion logic of cosmic beings (energy life, consciousness entities, existences in the Law Network) differs from that of material life:

- Energy-layer life: the Sense-Origin Realm is "stabilizing one's own boundary," learning to remain an independent perceiving subject amid the torrent of energy.
- Consciousness-domain life: the Sense-Origin Realm is "consciousness landing," learning to express perception in communicable form rather than a chaos.
- Law-network existences: the Sense-Origin Realm is "self-luminosity," realizing "I am I" from the running of the laws.

Their common point: promotion is not "eating more," but "connecting more clearly." The closer a cosmic being approaches the origin, the more it understands the true meaning of the two-way circulation of the Myriad-Race origin—individual perception docks into the network, the network feeds back into individual growth; this is the cosmic-scale Sense-Origin Realm.

## III. The Sense-Origin Interface of Anomalous Life and Residual Multiverse Civilizations

According to the anomalous-life identification standard of Doc 53, life forms that cannot fall into the five lineages also hold evolutionary entitlement:

- Anomalous life: such as void drifters and time-fragment life; the Sense-Origin Realm for them is "finding an anchor"—finding a stably perceivable coordinate amid drifting.
- Residual multiverse civilizations: civilizational legacies from the old multiverse era; their Sense-Origin Realm is "learning to read again"—rebuilding perception of energy in the new civilization's language.

These two kinds of existences are the corners most easily missed by the Myriad-Race 99% coverage baseline, and also the touchstone of Heng Yuan Zhou's "not one less."

## IV. Comparison: Hunting-Ground Cosmology vs. Symbiotic Cosmology

- Predatory planetary logic: the planet is a resource, drained then discarded; the planet will is an obstacle, to be obliterated.
- Symbiotic planetary logic: the planet is a co-cultivator, star-core docks to the anchor, ecology feeds back into civilization.
- Predatory cosmic logic: the cosmos is a hunting ground, the strong reap everything, until there is nothing left to reap.
- Symbiotic cosmic logic: the cosmos is a living body, the Myriad Races and planets evolve together, ultimately ascending as a whole.

The predatory faction even enslaves planets, so its ceiling is "the overlord of one star region"; the symbiotic faction treats even planets as companions, so its ceiling is the Balanced-Anchor Hyper-Pluralistic Realm—lifting the entire cosmos up and ascending together.

## V. Five Core Landing Points

- Anti-energy-tier waste: the evolutionary potential of planetary life is the cosmos's largest dormant asset; treating a planet as a hunting ground is super-massive big-material-small-use.
- Anti-predation: star-core docking, ecological resonance—every promotion logic is insulated from devouring and draining the pond.
- Tang-Luo ceiling comparison: the old-era inclusive system excluded even intelligent life beyond humans, let alone planet consciousness; the inclusive ceiling does not hold at cosmic scale at all.
- Full Myriad-Race coverage: planets, energy life, consciousness entities, anomalous life, residual multiverse civilizations—all folded into the 99% coverage.
- Hyper-pluralistic full openness: planetary Sense-Origin methods (the star-core anchor docking protocol) are openly shared, the first link of the fully open chain at the macro scale.

## Conclusion

When a planet begins to "perceive itself" in the anchor network, when an energy sea learns to "know itself," the Myriad-Race symbiotic cosmos is truly complete. The Sense-Origin Realm is not only the individual's starting point, but also civilization's starting point—from the tiniest perception to the grandest star core, Heng Yuan Zhou has written "A Path for Everyone" into "A Path for All Things."
