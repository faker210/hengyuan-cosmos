# Entry 105 | Sense Origin Realm: Risks, Breakthrough Costs, and Alaya Supervision Mechanism

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 19, 04, 97

## Introduction: The more universal power becomes, the more supervision is required

The hyper-multiverse fully open baseline of Heng Yuan Zhou (The Original Cosmos of Ten Thousand Spirits and the Balanced Anchor) makes Sense Origin Realm methods freely available to all Myriad Races — but this by no means implies "laissez-faire." On the contrary: when cultivation shifts from a privilege of the few to a basic right of all Myriad Races, supervision shifts from "controlling the strong" to "protecting everyone." The Alaya Network and multi-layer supervision mechanism is precisely the infrastructure built for this goal.

The Sense Origin Realm seems low-risk, yet it is the source of all cultivation risks: every hidden danger left here amplifies along the nine-realm path. This document systematically reviews the risks, breakthrough costs, and supervision mechanisms of the Sense Origin Realm.

The six top-level axioms are the value origin of the supervision mechanism: the anti-predation axiom — the first mission of supervision is to make predation nowhere to hide; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — supervision treats all Myriad Races equally; the collective ascension axiom — protecting cultivators is protecting civilizational assets, eliminating talent underuse; the causal self-discipline axiom — no cultivation behavior may disturb the causality of others or the environment; the law-first axiom — the legal framework of the Alaya supervision mechanism is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse. The universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose higher levels of infinity; infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Four Core Risks of the Sense Origin Realm

1. Qi deviation / demonic backlash: perceptual confusion — cultivators eager for results force open perceptual channels, causing energy backflow and chaotic consciousness. Sense Origin Realm qi deviation is not fatal, but may permanently damage perceptual precision
2. Energy pollution: absorbing energy with impurities or residual will traces contaminates the personal energy map, laying groundwork for future "impure origin"
3. Shortcut inner demon: the sentient-origin greed-shortcut inner demon's typical form in the Sense Origin Realm is the temptation of "devouring others' energy is faster" — remnants of the old system especially like spreading such heretical fast-track doctrines among junior cultivators
4. Predation risk: junior cultivators have weak energy and low vigilance, making them exactly the "prey" in the eyes of predatory remnants; spiritual enslavement and energy plunder often start from this realm

## II. Breakthrough Costs: Controllable, But Must Be Real

Breaking through from Sense Origin Realm to Condensed Origin Realm requires real costs:

- Time cost: normal path takes weeks to months (Document 14); rushing to compress time accepts higher risk
- Resource cost: basic resources are allocated by the contracted responsibility system (Document 90), more work more gain, no work no gain — eliminating both "resource freeloading" and "resource monopoly"
- Foundation cost: forcing a breakthrough before energy identification is complete leaves hidden perceptual wounds; the breakthrough cost is "pressing on wounded" vs. "resetting to start"
- Failure cost: breakthrough failure may trigger energy backlash, but the anchor network's monitoring and repair mechanisms ensure failure is reversible and repeatable

The core principle: breakthrough costs must be real (otherwise growth has no weight), but the predatory-style "one false step, eternal regret" irreversible destruction is never allowed.

## III. Alaya Network and Multi-Layer Supervision

Sense Origin Realm supervision forms a closed loop via "Alaya Network and multi-layer supervision":

Layer 1 · Anchor automatic monitoring: the anchor network continuously monitors energy levels, perceptual stability, energy purity, and other metrics (Document 14's certification mechanism); anomalies trigger instant warnings
Layer 2 · Ethical review: advanced breakthroughs require review by the Ethical Review Committee (Document 17), confirming anti-predation stance and symbiotic philosophy
Layer 3 · Anti-Predation Committee: the Anti-Predation Committee under the Myriad Races Republican Council (Document 04) is specifically responsible for supervising and investigating predation; spread of "fast-track heresies" in the Sense Origin Realm is a key target
Layer 4 · Anchor Court and judicial backstop: major crimes involving spiritual enslavement and energy plunder are tried by the Anchor Court according to law
Layer 5 · Civilizational supervision: publicity and objection periods (Document 14) allow every being to object to breakthrough certification; supervisory power belongs to the Myriad Races

## IV. Cultivation Ethics Landing in the Sense Origin Realm

The six cultivation ethics principles of Document 19 concretized in the Sense Origin Realm:

- Dignity of life: Sense Origin Realm cultivation never treats others as "material" or "tools"
- Anti-predation: the first mouthful of energy comes from circulation, not devouring
- Informed consent: dual cultivation, energy sharing, and collective cultivation are always premised on voluntariness
- Minimum harm: cultivation occurs in regulated venues; energy pollution is cleared on detection
- Honesty without deception: realm certification and contribution reporting prohibit fabrication
- Accountability: even in the Sense Origin Realm, basic community service obligations apply — however small the power, responsibility exists

## V. Comparison: Unregulated Jungle vs. Full-Stack Guardianship

- Predatory Sense Origin Realm: no supervision, only a hunting ground; no responsibility, only strength and weakness. Geniuses are hunted, kindness is devoured
- Symbiotic Sense Origin Realm: full-stack guardianship, risks can be warned, costs can be repaired, predation can be pursued

This supervision is not a restraint on freedom, but the fulfillment of "everyone has a path": only when the weakest Sense Origin Realm cultivator can cultivate in peace is the hyper-multiverse fully open truly open.

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- Anti-energy waste: the supervision mechanism ensures no talent is wasted by risk, no effort is intercepted by predation
- Anti-predation: striking "fast-track heresies" at the source, nailing the shortcut inner demon in the first realm
- Tang-Luo upper limit comparison: the old-era universal welfare system (Tang-Luo's Peerless Academy) had no supervision network; under patronage, undercurrents flowed; Heng Yuan Zhou's openness comes with full-stack guardianship
- Myriad Races full coverage: the supervision mechanism treats 99% of Myriad Races equally; exotic life and planetary life are equally protected by anchor monitoring, ensuring no talent is wasted
- Hyper-multiverse full openness: every link of openness is paired with guardianship; openness and safety are never a single-choice question

## Conclusion

The supervisory philosophy of the Sense Origin Realm can be condensed to one sentence: control risks, keep costs real, keep the road open. The Alaya Network and multi-layer supervision mechanism give Myriad Race cultivators a third choice beyond "absolute safety" and "false safety" for the first time — steady growth amid real costs and full-stack guardianship. This is precisely where the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization outranks all predatory systems: it does not rule by fear, nor promise by deception, but lets people grow stronger with peace of mind through institutions.
