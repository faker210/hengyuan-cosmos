# Chapter 106 | Sense-Origin Realm: System Comparison Between the Predatory Faction and the Symbiotic Faction

> Module: Module One · Deep Realm Refinement | Grounding documents: Docs 04, 01, 14

## Introduction: Two Cultivation Logics, Two Kinds of Universe

The Sense-Origin Realm is not only the starting point of cultivation, but also the very first crossroads of worldview: from the very first energy absorption, a cultivator is already standing at the fork—either "I take from the world," or "I grow with the world." These two sentences sound close, but they lead to two entirely different universes: the one is a hunting ground where the strong eat the weak; the other is a life-community where all beings rise together.

This article gives the system-level comparison between the Predatory Cultivation System and the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization.

The Six Top Axioms are the dividing line itself: the Axiom of Anti-Predation separates "take" from "grow"; the Axiom of Myriad Races Republic separates "my kind alone" from "all races"; the Axiom of Collective Dimensional Ascension separates "I alone ascend" from "we ascend together"; the Axiom of Causal Self-Discipline separates "debt elsewhere" from "I bear my own fruit"; the Axiom of Law Priority separates "rules for my convenience" from "stable and self-consistent law." The Law of Universal Infinite Equality for All Beings establishes that every being inherently carries Fundamental Infinite Power; the laid-back retain a guaranteed baseline infinity and will not be eliminated by the times, while grinders and peerless youths can stack higher tiers of infinity. Infinite origin is carried by all—the predatory logic's "only the strong may survive" is already invalid on its face.

## II. The Predatory Faction's Three Fallacies

1. Fallacy of the zero-sum: it believes power is fixed, so to gain it one must take from another—blind to the fact that the world cycle's energy is renewable and expansive.
2. Fallacy of the natural elimination: it believes the weak deserve to be eliminated—blind to the fact that a laid-back baseline infinity lets the slow keep up without being crushed.
3. Fallacy of the "enlightened predator": it believes a kind predator is the best predator—blind to the fact that the structure of predation itself is the disease, not the predator's temper.

## III. The "Enlightened Predator" and Historical Regression

The so-called enlightened predator—that is, the strong who does not kill wantonly, who "shares some" with the weak, who calls himself benevolent—is the most dangerous self-deception of the predatory faction. A kind hunter is still a hunter; his kindness is only the refinement of his prey-management. History repeatedly shows that the "enlightened" era of a predatory system is always the eve of regression: once the hunting grounds are exhausted, the "benevolent" strong are the first to turn on their own people.

## IV. The Righteous Rogue: A Warning to the "Chosen"

The Symbiotic faction does not pretend that its own side is perfect. The most insidious variant is the "righteous rogue": one who uses the mantle of justice to still plunder, who says "I do this for the symbiosis" while draining others for his own breakthrough.

Take Lin Hao, the protagonist of this universe, as the touchstone: his path is not to become the strongest in a hunting ground, but to prove that the strong can rise while still leaving the weak a way home. Whenever he is tempted to say "I am special, so the rules don't apply to me," that is the precise moment the Shortcut Inner Demon knocks—this is the inner line every cultivator of the symbiotic faction must hold.

## V. Five Core Landing Points

- Anti-energy-tier waste: the predatory faction's "strong eat weak" wastes more evolutionary potential than any war; the symbiotic faction's two-way circulation is the true high-efficiency.
- Anti-predation: every row of the master table is a red line the symbiotic faction will not cross.
- Tang-Luo ceiling comparison: the predatory faction's ceiling is one overlord over a graveyard; the symbiotic faction's ceiling lifts the whole cosmos up.
- Full Myriad-Race coverage: the master table covers every dimension from energy source to final destination, leaving no blind spot.
- Hyper-pluralistic full openness: the comparison itself is open evidence—the predatory faction dares not publish it, because every dimension exposes its own smallness.

## Conclusion

A table does not need rhetoric; the two columns already speak. In the Sense-Origin Realm, the first absorption a cultivator chooses decides which column he will stand in for the rest of his life—this is why this comparison is placed at the very beginning of the whole library: let every reader see, before choosing the path, which universe he is about to enter.
