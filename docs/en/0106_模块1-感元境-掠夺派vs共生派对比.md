# Entry 106 | Sense Origin Realm: Predatory Faction vs. Symbiotic Faction Detailed Comparison

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 04, 01, 14

## Introduction: The same starting point, two opposite roads

The Sense Origin Realm is the starting point of the nine realms, and also the fork where Myriad Race life first faces a path choice. The same talent, the same cosmos, the same four characters "perceive energy" — the Predatory and Symbiotic factions give diametrically opposite answers. Taking the Sense Origin Realm as a slice, this document systematically compares the fundamental differences between the two paths in route, cost, and destination, and answers a core question: why is the Predatory faction's ceiling always lower than the Symbiotic faction's floor?

The six top-level axioms are the watershed of the two paths: the anti-predation axiom — the source of the first mouthful of energy defines the path; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — in the Symbiotic faction's eyes, Myriad Races are fellow cultivators, in the Predatory faction's eyes, all beings are prey; the collective ascension axiom — the Symbiotic faction grows strong for ascension, the Predatory faction grows strong for monopoly, eliminating talent underuse; the causal self-discipline axiom — all Symbiotic operations are reportable, traceable, and correctable; the law-first axiom — the legal framework of the Predatory vs. Symbiotic comparison is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse. The universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose higher levels of infinity; infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. The Three Predatory Fallacies Projected in the Sense Origin Realm

The three fallacies of predatism argued in Document 04, Anti-Predation Core Treatise, find their complete projection already in the Sense Origin Realm:

1. Cosmological fallacy: treating the cosmos as a finite hunting ground — learning to "grab" in the Sense Origin Realm means deciding from day one that evolution is a zero-sum game
2. Ethical fallacy: putting the strong's evolutionary rights above the weak's survival rights — once "devouring" is uncorked in the Sense Origin Realm, there is no way back
3. Efficiency fallacy: believing predation is fastest — perception stolen in the Sense Origin Realm carries the original owner's residual will traces; late-stage refining costs far exceed cultivating it yourself

The Predatory Sense Origin cultivator overdrafts the most expensive foundation at the fastest speed; the Symbiotic Sense Origin cultivator banks the thickest capital at the steadiest pace.

## III. Enlightened Predators: Even Benevolence Cannot Save the Predatory Path

A complex subject must be faced: the "enlightened predator" — those relatively benevolent, even genuinely well-intentioned powerhouses who want to protect all beings. Their traits: not actively evil, willing to dispense shelter, sincere to those around them, yet the entire system still rests on a predatory base — monopolizing high-tier Dao, hoarding origin access, practicing "patronage" under the name of "protection."

In the Sense Origin Realm context, "enlightened predator" cultivation is: feeding genius the best resources, but never truly yielding the path to them — because the "path" itself is a monopoly. This explains why even benevolent powerhouses cannot reach hyper-multiverse civilization heights: he stands atop the predatory system, and the predatory system's ceiling is his ceiling.

## IV. Why the Predatory Faction Must Historically Regress

The structural fate of predatory civilization is: when the strong falls, civilization regresses. The system's entire operation hinges on the top individual's benevolence and might — while he lives, order barely holds; when he is gone, the hunting ground restarts. This "single-point dependence" is not a moral defect, but a structural defect: the predatory system never writes order into institutions, only bets order on the strong individual.

This is the primitive form of "protagonist uniqueness": civilization's survival rests on one person. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's answer is to write peace into institutions and the Law Network (anchors, law, councils, contracted responsibility), so that even when the mightiest Plane Transcendent falls, the symbiotic network keeps running.

## V. The Righteous Rogue: Ascension Threshold and the Newborn Path

Leaping from the Predatory faction to the Symbiotic requires crossing an ascension threshold called "the righteous rogue": daring to tear up accepted old law, overturning the old system's bottom logic, fearing no pressure from vested interests, and re-negotiating a fair symbiotic contract.

The protagonist Lin Hao is precisely the representative of this path: his congenital origin was deficient; walking the beast-soul path would only keep damaging his body — he was a victim of the old rules, not a beneficiary — forced to reconstruct the rules, he deduced Path D · Humanoid Artificial Dao-Rule Soul Rings, replacing hunting-and-stripping rings with artificial Dao rules, transforming from a victim of the predatory system into a reconstructor of the symbiotic civilization. The Sense Origin Realm is the earliest entrance to this threshold: one who chooses the symbiotic path at the starting point is already closer to hyper-multiverse civilization than the "enlightened predator" trapped atop the predatory system.

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- Anti-energy waste: the Predatory faction wastes talent on self-destructive involution; the Symbiotic faction invests talent in incremental co-building
- Anti-predation: the essential divide between the two paths lies in the first mouthful of energy in the Sense Origin Realm
- Tang-Luo upper limit comparison: Tang-Luo-style "universal welfare" is still a patronage system — all beings depend on the strong, not co-building; the Symbiotic faction gives everyone a path, no dependence
- Myriad Races full coverage: in the Predatory faction's eyes, beings are prey and tools; in the Symbiotic faction's eyes, Myriad Races are fellow cultivators — 99% coverage has no exceptions
- Hyper-multiverse full openness: the Predatory faction monopolizes everything; the Symbiotic faction opens all tiers from the Sense Origin Realm onward

## Conclusion

The Predatory vs. Symbiotic comparison in the Sense Origin Realm condenses the core contradiction of all cosmic history: predation brings short-lived strongmen and eternal regression; symbiosis brings lasting prosperity and whole ascension. The Predatory Sense Origin cultivator pours every effort to win at the starting line, only to find the finish line bears his own ceiling; the Symbiotic Sense Origin cultivator takes the first steady step, only to find the road's end is the Balanced Anchor hyper-multiverse realm. The choice is decided at the starting point — this is not fate, this is axiom.

Under Heng Yuan Zhou's legal framework, the Symbiotic Sense Origin path root-prevents the Predatory faction's energy waste and civilizational regression risks.
