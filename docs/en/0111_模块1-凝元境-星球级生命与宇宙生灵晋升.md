# Entry 111 | Condensed Origin Realm: Promotion Logic for Planetary Life and Cosmic Beings

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 00, 14, 24, 53

## Introduction: Star cores must also learn to "bank energy and turn circulation"

The Sense Origin Realm (Entry 104) was about planets and cosmic beings first "perceiving themselves." Entering the Condensed Origin Realm, macro-scale life faces homework completely isomorphic to the individual: condensing perceived cosmic energy into usable reserves and building its own "internal circulation." A planet's Condensed Realm is the process by which star-core energy moves from "cosmic handout" to "autonomous revenue-expenditure closure" — no longer passively receiving stellar radiation, but learning to build stable energy circulation among ecology, atmosphere, and star core.

The six top-level axioms hold at the macro scale as well: the anti-predation axiom — planets draw increments from the cosmos and give back to ecology, never draining the pond dry; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — planets, energy seas, consciousness entities, and material life share condensation qualification; the collective ascension axiom — every step of star-core closure serves the whole's ascension; the causal self-discipline axiom — star-core access and ecological transformation observe-first, then limited interference; the law-first axiom — the legal framework of planetary Condensed promotion logic is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Condensed Origin Realm of Planetary Life: Star-Core Revenue-Expenditure Closure

A planet with awakened star-core perception must complete three things entering the Condensed Realm:

- Energy condensation: condensing scattered energy (stellar radiation, cosmic rays, core heat) at the star core into autonomously callable reserves — this is the planetary version of "banking a nest egg"
- Star-core internal circulation: building an energy flow circuit of "star core-mantle-atmosphere-ecology," letting energy self-rotate in rhythm within the planet rather than dissipating one-directionally
- Revenue-expenditure self-check: confirming planetary energy purity (no foreign predatory will contamination) and circulation stability, avoiding "black-hole star absorption"

The predatory old path is clear at a glance: "condensing" by mining the planet and draining the core is equivalent to force-extracting the planet's internal circulation into one-way outflow — the planet is either ruined or dead. Heng Yuan Zhou's star-core anchor access protocol (Document 24) is precisely the opposite: connecting star-core circulation to the Alaya Network, balancing surplus and deficit with other planets' circulations.

## II. The Condensed Origin Realm of Cosmic Beings: Energy Boundaries and Self-Rotation

- Energy layer life: Condensed Realm is "condensing self-boundaries," learning to hold one's reserves undiluted amid the energy torrent
- Consciousness domain life: Condensed Realm is "condensing consciousness density," converging diffuse consciousness into a self-rotating information closure
- Law network existences: Condensed Realm is "condensing law projection," converging diffuse law self-organization into stable operational rhythm

The commonality remains: condensation is not "eating more," but "turning oneself more steadily."

## III. Condensed Realm Interface for Exotic Life and Multiverse Residual Civilizations

Per Document 53:

- Exotic life (void drifters, time-fragment life): Condensed Realm is "establishing an energy anchor amid drift," letting circulation self-rotate without fixed coordinates
- Multiverse residual civilization remnants: Condensed Realm is "rebuilding revenue-expenditure with the new civilization's circulation language," bringing ruin-era residual energy into a healthy closure

## IV. Comparison: Hunting-Ground Cosmos View vs. Symbiotic Cosmos View

- Predatory: planets are ore, absorb stars and cores, drain and discard
- Symbiotic: planets are fellow cultivators, star-core closure connects to the network, surplus and deficit balance each other

The predatory faction drains even the planet's internal circulation; its ceiling is "star-region overlord." The symbiotic faction treats even planets as co-builders; its ceiling is the Balanced Anchor hyper-multiverse realm.

## V. Cross-Realm Handover: How Star-Core Closure Upgrades to Terrain Engineering

A planet that has built revenue-expenditure closure in the Condensed Realm must upgrade "circulation" to "engineering" entering the Transform Realm. Planetary-scale handover is subtler than the individual: the individual's internal circulation serves itself, but the planet's star-core closure serves the surface Myriad Races — once entering terrain engineering, every energy-matter conversion of the star core directly affects surface ecology, and must be co-decided with surface Myriad Races, not unilaterally by the star core. This is precisely the biggest difference between planetary and individual cultivation: the individual's circulation is private; the planet's circulation is public affairs. Cosmic beings at this handover must learn "converting energy into transmissible form" — energy layer life must do cross-element conversion; consciousness domain life must learn energy-information encoding. Exotic life and multiverse residual civilization remnants gain, at this handover, the new skill of "converting drift/ruin energy into usable resources."

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning

When a planet learns to condense stellar gifts into its star core and let energy self-rotate among ecologies, the symbiotic cosmos of Myriad Races gains a resident that banks its own nest egg and turns its own circulation. The macro-scale version of the Condensed Realm tells us: "everyone has a path" has long been written as "every being has a path."
