# Entry 113 | Condensed Origin Realm: Predatory Faction vs. Symbiotic Faction Detailed Comparison

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 04, 01, 14

## Introduction: The way you bank your nest egg reveals the path's true color

The Sense Origin Realm (Entry 106) divide was "where does the first mouthful of energy come from"; the Condensed Realm divide escalates to "how do you bank your nest egg and build circulation." Both condensing energy, the Predatory faction thinks "swallow it in and press it into myself," the Symbiotic faction thinks "absorb it, clean it, turn it into a healthy circulation." Starting from this realm, path superiority is no longer just a moral question, but an efficiency question — can stolen energy truly flow into your own circulation?

The six top-level axioms are the watershed of the two paths: the anti-predation axiom — the source of energy defines the path; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — the Symbiotic faction sees all beings as fellow cultivators, the Predatory faction sees them as prey; the collective ascension axiom — the Symbiotic grows strong for ascension, the Predatory grows strong for monopoly, eliminating talent underuse; the causal self-discipline axiom — all Symbiotic operations are reportable and traceable; the law-first axiom — the Condensed Realm comparison framework is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. The Three Fallacies Projected in the Condensed Realm

The three predatism fallacies argued in Document 04 are especially clear in the Condensed Realm:

1. Cosmological fallacy: treating evolution as zero-sum, the Condensed Realm "enriches oneself" with stolen energy
2. Ethical fallacy: devouring beings in the name of "material" and "soul rings," the internal circulation stained with blood
3. Efficiency fallacy: believing forcing is fastest — stolen energy carries the original owner's will traces; late-stage refining costs far exceed self-cultivation, and embrittled pipelines break at the first shock

## III. Enlightened Predators: Even Benevolence Cannot Save a Forced Circulation

"Enlightened predators," even if relatively benevolent, still cultivate students in the Condensed Realm by "feeding the best pills, using the strongest rings," but never teaching students to build their own healthy circulation. Students seem fast-tracked, but in fact their circulation rests on a predatory base — when the base collapses, all pipelines shatter. He stands atop the predatory system, and the predatory system's ceiling is his ceiling.

## IV. Why the Predatory Faction Must Historically Regress

Predatory Condensed Realm powerhouses tie civilization to one person: while the strong lives, resources are distributed by him; when he falls, internal circulation experience is buried with him, successors start robbing from zero again. This single-point dependence is not a moral defect, but a structural defect — the symbiotic faction writes circulation experience into the anchor network and the 300 systems, learnable by all, passed down through generations.

## V. The Righteous Rogue: The Condensed Realm Meaning of Path D

Lin Hao's congenital origin was deficient; walking the old path of beast-soul hunting-and-stripping rings only made him more injured. Forced to reconstruct the rules, he deduced Path D · Humanoid Artificial Dao-Rule Soul Rings, replacing hunting-and-stripping with artificial Dao rules — this is precisely the Condensed Realm-level "righteous rogue": tearing up old law, re-negotiating a non-predatory condensation contract. One who chooses the symbiotic path in the Condensed Realm is already closer to hyper-multiverse civilization than the enlightened predator trapped atop the predatory system.

## VI. Cross-Realm Handover: Brittle Circulation vs. Healthy Circulation Divide in the Transform Realm

The brittle circulation the Predatory faction forces in the Condensed Realm and the healthy circulation the Symbiotic faction builds in sequence draw their first true efficiency gap at Transform Realm breakthrough. Brittle-circulation cultivators enter the transformation phase, and at the slightest pressure their pipelines shatter, forcing them back to rebuild circulation; healthy-circulation cultivators can pour all their energy into conversion rate and shaping craft. This divide is repeatedly verified in the ascension pattern of the five mortal-cultivation realms: Condensed Realm time saved by predatory cultivation is doubled back as pipeline repair in the Transform Realm. The deeper impact is: even if a brittle-circulation cultivator barely reaches the Transform Realm, their shaping products always carry original owner's will traces and cannot enter the Myriad Races' shared craft system — their "achievements" must be hoarded privately, contradicting the axiom of open Dao at all tiers.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- Anti-energy waste: the Predatory faction wastes talent on forced, embrittled circulations; the Symbiotic faction invests talent in healthy circulation construction
- Anti-predation: cutting off "swallow pills strip rings" from the second realm, the path divide is real and effective
- Tang-Luo upper limit comparison: Tang-Luo-style "universal welfare" is still a patronage system, all beings depend on the strong; the Symbiotic faction builds evidence-based circulations on their own
- Myriad Races full coverage: the Predatory faction sees beings as material, the Symbiotic faction sees Myriad Races as fellow cultivators; 99% coverage has no exceptions
- Hyper-multiverse full openness: the Predatory faction monopolizes everything; the Symbiotic faction opens all tiers from the Condensed Realm onward

This divide once again confirms the cold fairness of the five mortal-cultivation realms' ascension pattern: it asks not your birth, not your talent, but whether every step you took was clean. The Predatory faction's brittle circulation saves a little time at every realm, but repays double at every handover; the Symbiotic faction's healthy circulation starts slowly, but crosses every realm cleanly. Over time, the gap between the two paths is no longer "who is faster or slower," but "who can keep walking, who can't."

## Conclusion

The two paths in the Condensed Realm compete not over who banks more, but whose circulation lasts longer. The Predatory faction pours every effort to rob a vault, only to find the vault built on quicksand; the Symbiotic faction steadily turns out a healthy pipeline, and the water flows endlessly. The way you bank your nest egg has already written your destination.

In the final analysis, the Condensed Realm Predatory vs. Symbiotic comparison described in this document is no isolated case — it operates within Heng Yuan Zhou's overall coordinates; its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
