# Entry 117 | Transform Origin Realm: Differentiated Cultivation System for Myriad Races

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 50, 55, 15

## Introduction: Each race's transformation "dialect" is different

The Condensed Realm (Entry 110) covered how each race banks and turns internal circulation; the Transform Realm's differentiation deepens into "transformation craft" itself. Humans transform energy into shields and weapons; spirits transform one element into another; machine tribes transform energy into compute output; consciousness tribes transform energy into information density — the same "energy transformation," but each race's craft language is a completely different dialect. Using one transformation standard to measure all Myriad Races is pressing billions of kinds of talent onto one assembly line.

The six top-level axioms are the legislative foundation of Myriad Race differentiation: the anti-predation axiom — each race's transformation circulates increments only, does not refine others' stock; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — equal evolutionary qualification regardless of genealogy; the collective ascension axiom — each race's transformation craft must feed back into civilization, eliminating talent underuse; the causal self-discipline axiom — cross-race co-cultivation respects others' causal boundaries; the law-first axiom — the legal framework of the Myriad Race Transform differentiation system is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. Transformation Craft of Each Race: Every Race Has Its Unique Skill

- Beast tribe: using bloodline as furnace, converting wild instinct into short bursts or defense; difficulty is "holding it in"
- Wood tribe: using growth as transformation, converting energy into sustained growth power; products are most "alive"
- Spirit tribe: using elemental resonance as transformation, interconverting wind-fire-water-earth; difficulty is cross-domain loss
- Machine tribe: using topology as transformation, energy to compute to kinetic energy, precisely recomputable
- Consciousness tribe: using encoding as transformation, energy to information; products are most "ethereal" yet most precise

## III. Cross-Race Co-Cultivation: Civilizational Flow of Craft

- Cross-race mentors: machine mentors help human students model transformation loss; spirit mentors help wood students do cross-domain conversion — the craft-layer form of Myriad Race origin two-way circulation
- Half-blood compatibility: half-bloods go through system compatibility assessment, eliminating race labeling
- Equal certification: realm certification recognizes only conversion rate and stability, not birth

## V. Cross-Realm Handover: How Each Race's Transformation Craft Connects to the Law Domain

Transformation craft practiced by each race in the Transform Realm must connect to their respective law-affinity domains entering the Law Realm. The beast tribe's wild transformation connects to "power and instinct laws"; the wood tribe's growth transformation connects to "birth-death laws"; the spirit tribe's elemental interconversion connects to "elemental laws"; the machine tribe's compute transformation connects to "structure and space laws"; the consciousness tribe's encoding transformation connects to "logic laws." The Myriad Race origin two-way circulation manifests at this handover as: each race's law understanding is not closed; spirit students can audit human systematic deduction of birth-death laws; machine students can help consciousness students ground laws. Half-bloods need system compatibility assessment most at this handover — if two law affinities conflict, a fusion plan must be designed, not forcing majoring in one.

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning

This cross-race experiential flow is precisely the law-layer form of Myriad Race origin two-way circulation — each race invests its best law understanding into the public pool, and takes back other races' strengths; knowledge thickens with use, civilization widens with steps.
