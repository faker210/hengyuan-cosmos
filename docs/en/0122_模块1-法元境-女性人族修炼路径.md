Female humans in the Transform Realm (Entry 115) transformed energy into healing; the Law Realm must understand the "why it works" behind healing — per Document 14, the Law Realm's core is understanding and manipulating basic laws, producing Dharma Power. For female humans, laws need not only go the male "reasoning modeling" route, but can also take the entry of "emotional resonance, rhythm perception." This is not talent weakness, but another law-touching route long neglected by the old system.

The six top-level axioms are the foundation of this article: the anti-predation axiom — understanding laws does not mean monopolizing them; the Dao is open at all tiers; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — there is more than one entry to touching laws; female resonant law-touching is equally legitimate; the collective ascension axiom — female law understanding serves Myriad Races, eliminating talent underuse; the causal self-discipline axiom — manipulating laws is high-risk, most requiring observe-first, interfere-second; the law-first axiom — the legal framework of the female human Law path is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.
