# Entry 124 | Law Origin Realm: Differentiated Cultivation System for Myriad Races

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 50, 52, 10

## Introduction: Each race has a different "kinship" with laws

The Transform Realm (Entry 117) covered each race's transformation craft dialect; the Law Realm's differentiation deepens into "kinship with laws." Humans must learn laws from scratch; spirits are innately close to elemental laws; machine tribes treat laws as recomputable models; consciousness tribes are nearly neighbors to laws themselves — the same "understanding laws," but each race's distance to laws is completely different. Using one law-touching standard to measure all Myriad Races is pressing billions of ways of conversing with the cosmos into one exam.

The six top-level axioms are the legislative foundation of Myriad Race differentiation: the anti-predation axiom — each race touches laws without monopolizing or body-snatching; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — equal evolutionary qualification regardless of genealogy; the collective ascension axiom — each race's law understanding must feed back into civilization, eliminating talent underuse; the causal self-discipline axiom — cross-race law co-cultivation respects others' causal boundaries; the law-first axiom — the legal framework of the Myriad Race Law differentiation system is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. Law-Touching Paths of Each Race: Every Race Has Its Kinship

- Beast tribe: touching laws through bloodline intuition, law understanding carries wildness; difficulty is "taming wildness to stabilize spells"
- Wood tribe: touching laws through growth rhythm, deepest understanding of birth-death laws; spells are slowest yet most resilient
- Spirit tribe: touching laws through elemental resonance, elemental laws near instinct; difficulty is cross-domain breadth
- Machine tribe: touching laws through model recomputation, most precise at structure and space laws; difficulty is the "liveness" of laws
- Consciousness tribe: touching laws through consciousness clarity, closest to logic and consciousness laws; difficulty is material-plane landing

## III. Cross-Race Co-Cultivation: Civilizational Flow of Law Experience

- Half-blood compatibility: half-bloods go through system compatibility assessment, eliminating race labeling

## V. Cross-Realm Handover: How Each Race's Law Affinity Coagulates Into Each Race's Dao

Laws understood by each race in the Law Realm must coagulate into each race's own Dao in the Dao Realm. The beast tribe coagulates understanding of power-instinct laws into "Dao of Blood"; the wood tribe coagulates understanding of birth-death laws into "Dao of Life-Nurturing"; the spirit tribe coagulates understanding of elemental laws into "Dao of Reincarnation"; the machine tribe coagulates understanding of structure laws into "Dao of System"; the consciousness tribe coagulates understanding of logic laws into "Dao of Clarity." Half-bloods need system compatibility assessment at this handover — if two law understandings do not fuse, the Dao cannot be established.
