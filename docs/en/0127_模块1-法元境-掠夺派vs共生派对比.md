# Entry 127 | Law Origin Realm: Predatory Faction vs. Symbiotic Faction Detailed Comparison

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 04, 01, 14

## Introduction: Are laws public tools or hoarded privileges

The Transform Realm (Entry 120) competed over "what you transform"; the Law Realm competes over something more fundamental — whose laws are they after all. The Predatory faction understands laws as hoarded privileges: whoever comprehends them owns them, monopolized, sealed, body-snatched, Dao-seized; the Symbiotic faction understands laws as public tools open at all tiers: the Dao has no hoarding, no monopoly, anyone can learn. The same set of laws, different paths, and the civilizational knowledge ecosystem is worlds apart.

The six top-level axioms are the watershed of the two paths: the anti-predation axiom — the Dao is open at all tiers, no hoarding, no monopoly; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — the Symbiotic sees Myriad Races as fellow cultivators, the Predatory sees all beings as prey; the collective ascension axiom — the Symbiotic comprehends for ascension, the Predatory comprehends for monopoly, eliminating talent underuse; the causal self-discipline axiom — all Symbiotic law operations are reportable and traceable; the law-first axiom — the Law Realm comparison framework is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. The Three Fallacies Projected in the Law Realm

Document 04's three fallacies are especially clear in this realm:

1. Cosmological fallacy: treating cosmic laws as finite private property, monopolizing once comprehended
2. Ethical fallacy: body-snatching and Dao-seizing, appropriating others' Dao-comprehension achievements as one's own
3. Efficiency fallacy: believing Dao-seizing is fastest — seized comprehension carries original owner will traces; late-stage digestion costs runaway

## III. Enlightened Predators: Even Benevolence Cannot Save Knowledge Monopoly

"Enlightened predators," even if relatively benevolent, still serve their law transmission the patronage logic of "the strong protect all beings" — he is willing to teach a few apprentices, but never truly yields the path to everyone. He stands atop the predatory system, and the ceiling of knowledge monopoly is his ceiling.

## IV. Why the Predatory Faction Must Historically Regress

Predatory law knowledge hinges on the top individual: while the strong lives, knowledge is monopolized by him; when he falls, knowledge is buried with him, and civilization starts comprehending from zero again. This is precisely the knowledge-science root of historical regression. The symbiotic faction writes law knowledge into the anchor network and the 300 systems, shared across the Alaya Network — this is precisely the knowledge-structure answer to eliminating historical regression.

## V. The Righteous Rogue: The Law Realm Meaning of the Open Contract

Lin Hao's deduction of Path D · Humanoid Artificial Dao-Rule Soul Rings is essentially tearing up old law at the law layer: replacing hoarded hunting-and-stripping with open artificial Dao rules. One who chooses knowledge openness in the Law Realm is already closer to hyper-multiverse civilization than the enlightened predator trapped atop the predatory system.

## VI. Cross-Realm Handover: Monopolized Knowledge vs. Open Knowledge Divide in the Dao Realm

The predatory law knowledge monopolized in the Law Realm and the symbiotic open law system draw their first true efficiency gap at Dao Realm breakthrough. Monopolized-knowledge cultivators establishing Dao have only their own hoarded law fragments in hand; Dao material is sparse. Open-knowledge cultivators stand on the shoulders of the 300 systems and Alaya Network sharing; Dao material is infinite. The deeper divide: the Predatory Dao established is hegemonic; when the Dao-establisher collapses, monopolized knowledge is buried with him. The Symbiotic Dao established is fair; when the Dao-establisher falls, fairness is still written in institutions and networks. This is the ultimate cost of knowledge monopoly at Dao Realm ascension — it makes the Dao-establisher a single-point dependence of civilization, which is precisely the structure the republican axiom of Myriad Races aims to eradicate.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- Anti-energy waste: the Predatory faction wastes comprehension on monopoly and body-snatching; the Symbiotic faction invests comprehension in open co-building
- Anti-predation: cutting off "body-snatching and Dao-seizing" from the fourth realm, the path divide is real and effective
- Tang-Luo upper limit comparison: Tang-Luo-style "universal welfare" is still patronage, knowledge depends on the strong; symbiotic knowledge is learnable by all
- Myriad Races full coverage: the Predatory sees all beings as prey, the Symbiotic sees Myriad Races as fellow cultivators; 99% coverage has no exceptions
- Hyper-multiverse full openness: the Predatory monopolizes laws; the Symbiotic opens all tiers from the Law Realm onward

The cost of knowledge monopoly in the Dao Realm also manifests at a more covert level: monopolizers themselves cannot be truly free. They live in the anxiety of "fear of knowledge being lost, fear of being Dao-seized"; their Dao, lacking dialogue and falsification by others, tends to be fragile and paranoid. Open-knowledge cultivators are the opposite; their Dao is repeatedly polished in Myriad Races dialogue, standing firmer as it is established. This is the deep efficiency gap between monopoly and openness — monopoly seems to protect the strongman, but actually traps him on an island.

## Conclusion

The two paths in the Law Realm compete not over who comprehends faster, but whether after comprehending, they are willing to let others learn. The Predatory faction pours every effort to comprehend into private property, only to find the private property decays with them; the Symbiotic faction steadily writes knowledge into the network, and wisdom grows in Myriad Races' hearts through generations. Whose laws are they after all — the answer to this question has already written civilization's destination.

In the final analysis, the Law Realm Predatory vs. Symbiotic comparison described in this document is no isolated case — it operates within Heng Yuan Zhou's overall coordinates; its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
