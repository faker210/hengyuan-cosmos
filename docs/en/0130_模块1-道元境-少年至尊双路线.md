> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 23, 16, 17

## Introduction: Can fast-track attain the Dao? Yes, but the Dao must grow on its own

The Youth Supreme dual path in the Law Realm (Entry 123) solved "understanding laws and revering laws"; entering the Dao Realm, the question elevates to the most profound gate of mortal cultivation — per Document 14, the Dao Realm requires extremely high talent and comprehension, many never reach it in a lifetime. Youth Supreme talent faces its greatest temptation here: since hundredfold deduction can accelerate everything, why not accelerate even "Dao establishment"? The answer: Dao structure can be deduction-accelerated, but the Dao's soul must grow on its own in the real world.

The six top-level axioms are the master outline of the dual path in the Dao Realm: the anti-predation axiom — fast-track relies on technology not plagiarism; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — the dual path faces all Myriad Race youth; the collective ascension axiom — youth Dao serves civilization, eliminating talent underuse; the causal self-discipline axiom — cross-plane perception holds observe-only; the law-first axiom — the Youth Supreme Dao dual path framework is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Fast-Track Path: Hundredfold Deduction, Pressing Dao Structure into the Acceleration Domain

- Hundredfold deduction: relying on Multiplication Fruit technology (Document 16), compressing "which Dao structure is self-consistent, which resonance is most stable" trial-and-error into the Origin Acceleration Domain
- Dao archive fully open: the 300 systems' Dao-establishment exemplars are fully open to Youth Supremes
- Resource tilt: the 20% Activation Strategy (Document 23) prioritizes allocating consciousness resources needed for Dao establishment
- Mentor escort: Plane Transcendent mandatory mentors (Document 17) guide him in calibrating the Dao-heart throughout

## II. Quest Path: Quest Sediment Domain, Letting the Dao's Soul Grow

The acceleration domain can give Dao structure, but cannot give "the Dao's soul":

- Slow tempering: personally experiencing epic-level adversity in the Quest Sediment Domain, letting the Dao's soul be hammered out in real life-and-death
- Epic birth mechanism: letting the Youth Supreme personally establish Dao, not inherit a written script
- Conservation of experiential quality: the acceleration domain handles Dao structure, the sediment domain handles Dao soul — ability and Dao-heart grow together

## III. Two-Way Tension: Dynamic Tuning of Dao-Heart Metrics

- Dao-heart stability: if the acceleration-established Dao cracks at first contact with reality, sediment domain share is increased
- Anti-hollow review: if "Dao structure is beautiful, Dao-heart shatters at a touch," tempering is automatically added
- Anti-waste review: long-term sediment without Dao advancement automatically adds deduction resources
- Informed consent: minor path adjustments require guardian consent and ethical review

## IV. Risks and Supervision: Special Guardianship of Genius Dao Establishment

- Dao-heart shattering: Youth Supreme Dao establishment is unsteady; one major setback collapses the Dao-heart; anchor instant warning
- Plagiarism risk: predatory remnants most want to "borrow Youth Supreme Dao establishment then Dao-seize"; dual guardianship by mentor and anchor
- Overstep risk: cross-plane perception spying on others without filing; Alaya supervision instantly stops
- Shortcut inner demon: the sentient-origin greed-shortcut inner demon rises amid the arrogance of "I have established the Dao"

## V. Cross-Realm Handover: How the Dao-Establishing Youth Grows Grandmaster Bearing

The Youth Supreme has established his own Dao in the Dao Realm; before the cosmic-level breakthrough of Dao Integration, what he must grow is "bearing weight." The acceleration domain can give Dao integration structure, but cannot give shoulders that uphold Myriad Races. The Quest Sediment Domain in this critical period must let the Youth Supreme personally bear once a civilization-level responsibility, experiencing the weight of a grandmaster. The Anchor Council does a dual assessment before breakthrough: first whether the Dao integration structure is stable, second whether the Youth Supreme's bearing weight is worthy of the civilizational influence after Dao integration. If bearing weight is insufficient, even if the Dao-heart is formed, Dao integration breakthrough is delayed and tempering supplemented first. This rule is the last buffer for Youth Supremes moving to sacred cultivation — the ascension pattern of the five mortal-cultivation realms manifests as: Dao integration structure can be accelerated, bearing weight cannot.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- Anti-energy waste: Youth Supreme Dao-establishment talent is a universal scarce resource; the old system wastes it on single-plank bridges; Heng Yuan Zhou deploys it to civilization
- Anti-predation: fast-track relies on technology not plagiarism, quest relies on tempering not harvesting
- Tang-Luo upper limit comparison: old-era geniuses were patronizingly Dao-established with upper limits locked; Heng Yuan Zhou youth have the Dao archive fully open, directly approaching Dao integration
- Myriad Races full coverage: Myriad Race Youth Supremes share the Dao dual path
- Hyper-multiverse full openness: Dao Realm dual-path methods are publicly transparent

The process of Youth Supremes growing bearing weight is essentially transforming from "a genius upheld by civilization" to "a grandmaster who upholds civilization." This identity switch cannot be completed by deduction, only by being pressed, upheld, and then confirmed in real responsibility. The Quest Sediment Domain acts one last time here precisely to let the Youth Supreme taste "cannot bear" before entering Dao integration — the cosmic-level breakthrough — then learn "can bear." Only thus, after Dao integration, will he not be backlashed by his own power, but become shoulders Myriad Races can rely on.

## Conclusion

Dao Realm Youth Supremes prove: Dao structure can be accelerated, the Dao's soul cannot. The acceleration domain gives them Dao-establishment speed worthy of their talent; the sediment domain gives them Dao-heart depth worthy of that speed. When geniuses are no longer material for "Dao-seizing protagonists," the myth of protagonist uniqueness loses its soil.

The Youth Supreme moving from being upheld to upholding, this turn is closer to the original meaning of "attaining the Dao" than any breakthrough he makes. The Dao is not how high one stands alone, but whether he is willing to bend down and let the Myriad Races behind him step on his shoulders to go one layer higher.
