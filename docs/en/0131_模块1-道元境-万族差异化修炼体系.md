# Entry 131 | Dao Origin Realm: Differentiated Cultivation System for Myriad Races

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 50, 52, 10

## Introduction: Each race's "Dao" is billions of ways of living

The Law Realm (Entry 124) covered each race's kinship with laws; the Dao Realm's differentiation deepens into "Dao forms." A human's Dao is a road, a beast tribe's Dao is a kind of blood, the wood tribe's Dao is a stretch of growth, a spirit tribe's Dao is an elemental reincarnation, a machine tribe's Dao is a self-consistent system — the same "attaining the Dao," but the Daos each race establishes are fundamentally not the same thing. Using one Dao standard to measure all Myriad Races is pressing billions of ways of living into one success manual.

The six top-level axioms are the legislative foundation of Myriad Race differentiation: the anti-predation axiom — each race establishes Dao without plagiarism or monopoly; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — equal evolutionary qualification regardless of genealogy; the collective ascension axiom — each race's Dao must feed back into civilization, eliminating talent underuse; the causal self-discipline axiom — cross-race Dao dialogue respects others' causality; the law-first axiom — the legal framework of the Myriad Race Dao differentiation system is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## IV. Comparison: Racial Narrow-Mindedness is the Welfare Ceiling

The Tang-Luo-style old-era universal welfare system only recognizes human-style "Dao," dismissing beast blood and wood growth as "heretical branches." ## V. Cross-Realm Handover: How Each Race's Dao Embraces Origin

Daos established by each race in the Dao Realm must, entering Dao Integration, each embrace cosmic origin in billions of poses. The beast tribe's Dao of Blood must let "wildness return to heaven and earth"; the wood tribe's Dao of Life-Nurturing must let "growth breathe with origin"; the spirit tribe's Dao of Reincarnation must let "elements return to the torrent"; the machine tribe's Dao of System must "access the origin substrate"; the consciousness tribe's Dao of Clarity must "merge into the law network." The Myriad Race origin two-way circulation manifests at this handover as: each race's Dao integration pose differs, yet all embrace origin in the same direction. Half-bloods need system compatibility assessment at this handover — if two Daos are uncoordinated, they pull against each other during Dao integration.
