# Entry 132 | Dao Origin Realm: Promotion Logic for Planetary Life and Cosmic Beings

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 00, 14, 24, 53

## Introduction: A planet can also have its own "Dao"

The Law Realm (Entry 125) planet learned to fine-tune local laws; the Dao Realm must coagulate these fine-tunings into a "planetary Dao" — per Document 14, the Dao Realm's core is attaining the Dao: forming a personal Dao, resonating with cosmic laws. A planet's Dao Realm is when the star core no longer just scatters law fine-tunings, but distills a self-consistent "ecological Dao" belonging to this planet: gravity, climate, and birth-death operate self-consistently under this Dao, and surface Myriad Races' life activities resonate with the planetary Dao. The planet for the first time upgrades from "local fine-tuner" to "master of a self-consistent ecological Dao."

The six top-level axioms hold at the macro scale as well: the anti-predation axiom — the planetary Dao is built on ecological increments, never using the Dao as a weapon; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — planets, energy seas, and consciousness entities share Dao-attainment qualification; the collective ascension axiom — the planetary Dao serves Myriad Races, eliminating talent underuse; the causal self-discipline axiom — cross-plane perception holds observe-only; the law-first axiom — the legal framework of planetary Dao promotion logic is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Dao Realm of Planetary Life: Star-Core Ecological Dao

A planet that has learned law fine-tuning must complete three things entering the Dao Realm:

- Distilling ecological Dao: integrating scattered fine-tunings into a self-consistent "ecological Dao"
- Dao-law resonance: the planetary Dao resonates with cosmic laws, mobilizing law power over a larger range
- Myriad Race resonance: surface Myriad Races' life activities resonate with the planetary Dao, ecology self-consistent

The predatory old path is clear here: forcibly turning the "planetary Dao" into hegemony ruling other planets. Heng Yuan Zhou's star-core Dao (Document 24 transmission path) is precisely the opposite: the planetary Dao must be confirmed by the Anchor Council, with ecological self-consistency as the sole purpose.

## II. The Dao Realm of Cosmic Beings: Energy/Consciousness Dao

- Energy layer life: Dao Realm is "distilling an energy Dao," coagulating one's own energy form into a Dao resonating with the cosmos
- Consciousness domain life: Dao Realm is "distilling a consciousness Dao," coagulating clear consciousness into a self-consistent Dao
- Law network existences: Dao Realm is "distilling a Dao from law self-organization"

The commonality remains: attaining the Dao is not "dominating the cosmos," but "resonating deeper with the cosmos."

## III. Dao Realm Interface for Exotic Life and Multiverse Residual Civilizations

Per Document 53:

- Exotic life (void drifters, etc.): Dao Realm is "distilling one's own wandering Dao amid drift"
- Multiverse residual civilization remnants: Dao Realm is "distilling a rebuilding Dao from ruins and history"

## IV. Comparison: Hunting-Ground Cosmos View vs. Symbiotic Cosmos View

- Predatory: the planetary Dao is hegemony, used to conquer other planets
- Symbiotic: the planetary Dao is ecological self-consistency, used to nourish Myriad Races

The predatory faction turns the Dao into hegemony; the symbiotic faction nurtures the Dao into a home.

## V. Cross-Realm Handover: How the Planetary Dao Embraces Origin

A planet that has distilled an ecological Dao in the Dao Realm must let this Dao embrace cosmic origin entering Dao Integration. The key reminder at handover: planetary Dao integration is not turning the whole planet into a springboard to swallow the cosmos, but letting the star core's ecological Dao harmoniously resonate with origin laws. At this stage, the star core must be dual-confirmed by the Anchor Council and the Symbiosis Court that its Dao direction is symbiotic not hegemonic. Cosmic beings at this handover must upgrade from "distilling a Dao" to "merging with origin." Whether planet or cosmic being, after Dao integration, they become Dao-Integration grandmasters who uphold their race and ecology — the five mortal-cultivation realms are thus complete.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- Anti-energy waste: star-core Dao attainment is the universe's greatest sleeping wisdom; using it as hegemony is hyper-scale talent underuse
- Anti-predation: star-core Dao attainment, cosmic beings establishing Dao — all insulated from weaponization and predation
- Tang-Luo upper limit comparison: the old-era universal welfare system never recognized planets as qualified to attain the Dao
- Myriad Races full coverage: planets, energy life, consciousness entities, exotic life, multiverse residual civilizations all within 99% coverage
- Hyper-multiverse full openness: star-core Dao-attainment protocol is openly shared, the macro-scale fifth link of the fully open chain

The dual confirmation before planetary Dao integration (Anchor Council + Symbiosis Court) reflects the special ethics of planetary cultivation: a planet's Dao integration affects not one cultivator, but the fate of the entire surface ecology. Therefore its Dao direction must be scrutinized more carefully than an individual's. This "the more macro the more careful" principle is fully consistent with individual cultivators' "the higher the tier the stronger the causal constraint" — the greater the power, the stricter the supervision of its Dao direction. This is not distrusting the planet, but being responsible to the entire ecology.

## Conclusion

When a planet distills its own ecological Dao and resonates with surface Myriad Races, the cosmos gains a resident that can establish its own Dao. The macro-scale version of the Dao Realm once again confirms: the Dao is not the strongman's private property, but a way of living co-built by Myriad Races and planets.

The more macro the more careful, this principle is isomorphic with the individual's "the higher the tier the heavier the causality." A planet's Dao integration affects the entire ecology, so its Dao direction deserves one more scrutiny than an individual's — this is not harshness, but the most basic responsibility to countless lives.

One more scrutiny, one less disaster; caution toward macro life ultimately turns into gentleness toward all small lives.

This caution toward the macro ultimately lands on gentleness toward the micro, letting every small life dwelling on the planet grow freely within safe laws.

In the final analysis, the Dao Realm planetary life and cosmic being promotion described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
