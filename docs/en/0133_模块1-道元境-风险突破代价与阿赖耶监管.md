# Entry 133 | Dao Origin Realm: Risks, Breakthrough Costs, and Alaya Supervision Mechanism

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 19, 28, 49

## Introduction: Dao-establishers are the ones who most need supervision

Law Realm (Entry 126) risks were in "law turbulence"; Dao Realm risks escalate to "Dao inflation." When a cultivator distills a personal Dao that resonates with the cosmos and mobilizes large-range law power, he for the first time gains the ability to "influence civilization's direction with a personal Dao" — the stronger the Dao-establisher, the more he needs supervision. The Alaya Network and multi-layer supervision's task in this realm escalates from "watching law operations" to "watching the Dao's direction."

The six top-level axioms are the value origin of the supervision mechanism: the anti-predation axiom — the first mission of supervision is to make plagiarism and Dao-seizing nowhere to hide; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — supervision treats all Myriad Races equally; the collective ascension axiom — guarding Dao-establishers is guarding civilization's direction; the causal self-discipline axiom — cross-plane perception holds observe-only; the law-first axiom — the Dao Realm supervision framework is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Four Core Risks of the Dao Realm

1. Dao-heart shattering: the personal Dao cannot withstand falsification; the Dao-establisher's mind collapses, the realm regresses
2. Hegemony inflation: the Dao-establisher tries to force "my Dao" above cosmic laws and others' Daos, turning hegemonic
3. Overstep spying: cross-plane consciousness perception spying on others without filing, disturbing causality
4. Shortcut inner demon: the sentient-origin greed-shortcut inner demon in this realm upgrades to "plagiarizing others' Dao as one's own"

## II. Breakthrough Costs: Real, But Controllable and Reversible

Breaking through from the Dao Realm to Dao Integration requires real costs:

- Comprehension cost: the comprehension needed to attain the Dao cannot be compressed; many never reach it in a lifetime
- Dao-heart cost: Dao establishment must be repeatedly hammered by reality; time and setbacks cannot be skipped
- Cross-plane cost: the training consumption of consciousness cross-plane perception is real
- Failure cost: Dao-heart shattering is reversibly repairable, but requires dual backstop by mentor and anchor

## III. Alaya Network and Multi-Layer Supervision Closed Loop

- Anchor automatic monitoring: real-time monitoring of Dao self-consistency and resonance stability; any Dao-heart shake triggers instant warning
- Dao-direction filing: the Dao-establisher's Dao direction must be filed; hegemonic tendencies are assessed by the Anchor Supervision Committee
- Cross-plane ethics: cross-plane consciousness perception must go through anchor connection filing (Document 28), observe only, do not interfere
- Governance checks and balances: Dao-establishers participating in civilizational decision-making must accept governance framework checks and balances (Document 49)
- Publicity objection period: thirty days of publicity, all Myriad Races may raise objections to "hegemonic tendencies"

## IV. Cultivation Ethics Landing in the Dao Realm

- Informed consent: influencing others with a personal Dao must obtain consent
- Minimum harm: Dao-Dao conflicts must go through the Symbiosis Court, not be suppressed by force
- Accountability: Dao Realm cultivators must undertake civilization-level research and grand-mentor obligations

## V. Cross-Realm Handover: How Dao-Heart Hazards Amplify in Dao Integration

The supervision system's meaning in the Dao Realm is clearest at cross-realm handover: a hegemonic tendency uncorrected in the Dao Realm is amplified, entering Dao Integration, into an attempt to swallow origin. Similarly, one undisposed Dao-heart crack, under the heaven-human unity impact of Dao Integration, will completely shatter. The Alaya Network and multi-layer supervision therefore list "Dao-direction assessment and Dao-heart purity" as a mandatory item before Dao Integration breakthrough: the Anchor Supervision Committee assesses whether the Dao direction is hegemonic, the Symbiosis Court confirms no swallowing tendency; any hazard must be corrected before passage. This rule guards the cultivator's Dao integration direction — whether Dao integration is embrace or swallowing must be fixed before breakthrough.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- Anti-energy waste: supervision keeps Dao-establishers on the right path, not wasting comprehension on hegemony and shattering
- Anti-predation: striking "plagiarism and Dao-seizing" at the source, Dao always belongs to the Dao-establisher
- Tang-Luo upper limit comparison: the old-era universal welfare system had no Dao-establisher supervision; hegemony pressured others at will; Heng Yuan Zhou has full-stack guardianship
- Myriad Races full coverage: supervision treats 99% of Myriad Races equally; planetary star-core Dao is equally filed
- Hyper-multiverse full openness: every link of openness is paired with guardianship

Including "Dao direction" in supervision is the Alaya Network's key step from external operations deep into internal mind-consciousness. Previously supervision watched "visible" risks like energy, products, and law operations; by the Dao Realm, supervision must watch the "invisible" Dao direction — because once the Dao direction is hegemonic, the harm it produces is far deeper than one qi deviation. The Anchor Supervision Committee's Dao-direction assessment seems to be "controlling thought," but actually guards civilization from being kidnapped by any hegemony — this is the same coin as eradicating the single-point dependence of "protagonist uniqueness."

## Conclusion

The Dao Realm's supervisory philosophy remains: control risks, keep costs real, keep the road open. When every Dao-establisher's direction is gently yet firmly supervised, the symbiotic cosmos will not be kidnapped by any hegemony — this is precisely the structural answer to eradicating the single-point dependence of "protagonist uniqueness."

Supervision reaching Dao direction is not to "control thought," but to guard civilization from being kidnapped by any hegemony. A civilization that dares to inspect the strongman's mind is one that truly has "republicanism of Myriad Races" written into its bones — it does not rely on worshipping the strongman, but on checks and balances to guard symbiosis.

Guarding symbiosis with checks and balances, resisting hegemony with openness, Dao Realm supervision writes the republicanism of Myriad Races from a slogan into daily life.
