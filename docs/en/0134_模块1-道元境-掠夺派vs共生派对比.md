# Entry 134 | Dao Origin Realm: Predatory Faction vs. Symbiotic Faction Detailed Comparison

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 04, 01, 14

## Introduction: When establishing Dao, do you establish hegemony or fairness

The Law Realm (Entry 127) competed over "whose laws are they"; the Dao Realm competes over "what kind of Dao is established." The Predatory faction establishes hegemony: my Dao overrides all Myriad Races, those who submit prosper; the Symbiotic faction establishes fairness: my Dao resonates with Myriad Races, benefiting me and others alike. The same "establishing Dao" action, different paths, and the civilizational meaning of the Dao is worlds apart.

The six top-level axioms are the watershed of the two paths: the anti-predation axiom — hegemony is built on predation, fairness on symbiosis; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — the Symbiotic sees Myriad Races as fellow cultivators, the Predatory sees all beings as subjects; the collective ascension axiom — fairness is established for ascension, hegemony for monopoly, eliminating talent underuse; the causal self-discipline axiom — all fairness operations are reportable and traceable; the law-first axiom — the Dao Realm comparison framework is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. The Three Fallacies Projected in the Dao Realm

Document 04's three fallacies are especially clear in this realm:

1. Cosmological fallacy: treating the cosmos as private property, establishing Dao means declaring kingship
2. Ethical fallacy: pressing down Myriad Races with hegemony, submit-prosper oppose-perish
3. Efficiency fallacy: believing suppression is fastest — hegemony's accumulated resentment erupts entirely when the Dao-establisher falls, civilization must regress

## III. Enlightened Predators: Benevolent Hegemony Is Still Hegemony

"Enlightened predators," even if benevolent, their Dao is still hegemony — he is willing to protect Myriad Races, but never resonates with them; instead he condescendingly "decides for them." He stands atop the predatory system, and the ceiling of hegemony is his ceiling.

## IV. Why the Predatory Faction Must Historically Regress

The Predatory hegemony hinges on one Dao-establisher: while he lives, hegemony barely holds; when he falls, accumulated resentment erupts entirely, civilization regresses. This is precisely the Dao Realm form of "protagonist uniqueness" single-point dependence — civilization's survival hinges on one "Dao." The symbiotic faction writes fairness into institutions and law networks; when the Dao-establisher falls, fairness still operates.

## V. The Righteous Rogue: The Dao Realm Meaning of Fairness

Lin Hao's deduction of Path D · Humanoid Artificial Dao-Rule Soul Rings is essentially tearing up old law at the Dao layer: replacing hegemonic hunting-and-stripping with fair artificial Dao rules. One who chooses fairness in the Dao Realm is already closer to hyper-multiverse civilization than the enlightened predator trapped atop hegemony.

## VI. Cross-Realm Handover: Hegemony vs. Fairness Divide in Dao Integration

The Predatory hegemony established in the Dao Realm and the Symbiotic fairness draw their first true efficiency gap at Dao Integration breakthrough. Hegemonic cultivators try to swallow origin during Dao integration, are bounced back by origin laws, Dao-heart and realm both collapse; fair cultivators embrace origin during Dao integration, heaven-human unity is stably achieved. The deeper divide: hegemonic Dao-integrators ascend alone, behind them is a civilizational wasteland no one upholds; fair Dao-integrators lift Myriad Races up together, behind them is Myriad Races advancing as a whole. This is the ultimate cost of hegemony at Dao Integration ascension — it makes the cultivator a single-point dependence of civilization, which is precisely the structure the collective ascension axiom aims to eradicate.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- Anti-energy waste: the Predatory faction wastes Dao-establishment talent on hegemony; the Symbiotic faction invests talent in fairness
- Anti-predation: cutting off "plagiarism and suppression" from the fifth realm, the path divide is real and effective
- Tang-Luo upper limit comparison: Tang-Luo-style "universal welfare" is still hegemonic patronage, beings depend on it; symbiotic fairness is establishable by all
- Myriad Races full coverage: the Predatory sees Myriad Races as subjects, the Symbiotic sees Myriad Races as fellow cultivators; 99% coverage has no exceptions
- Hyper-multiverse full openness: the Predatory monopolizes the Dao; the Symbiotic opens all tiers from the Dao Realm onward

The divide between hegemony and fairness in Dao Integration can also be quantified by "protagonist uniqueness dependence": hegemonic Dao-integrators have extremely high protagonist uniqueness dependence, civilization's survival hinges on him alone, his collapse loses everything; fair Dao-integrators have extremely low dependence, his Dao is already written into institutions and networks, civilization operates whether he is present or not. What Heng Yuan Zhou pursues is precisely the latter — writing peace and ascension into law networks, not betting on any single strongman. This is also why the symbiotic faction's ceiling is the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, while the predatory faction's ceiling is forever "a living overlord."

## Conclusion

The two paths in the Dao Realm compete not over whose Dao is higher, but over whose interests the Dao is built on. The Predatory faction pours every effort into establishing hegemony, only to find hegemony cracks with them; the Symbiotic faction steadily builds the Dao on Myriad Races' interests, and fairness continues in Myriad Races' hearts through generations. What kind of Dao to establish has already written civilization's destination.

The level of protagonist uniqueness dependence ultimately converts to civilization's lifespan. A hegemonic civilization with extremely high dependence has a lifespan equal to its overlord; a fair civilization with extremely low dependence has an infinite lifespan. The Symbiotic faction dares to set its sights on the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm precisely because its civilization depends on no single individual.

In the final analysis, the Dao Realm Predatory vs. Symbiotic comparison described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
