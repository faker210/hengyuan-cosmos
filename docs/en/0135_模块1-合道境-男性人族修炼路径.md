# Entry 135 | Dao Integration Realm: Male Human Cultivation Path, Pain Points, and Breakthrough Features

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 51, 17, 49

## Introduction: The apex of the five mortal-cultivation realms, the heaven-human leap from "attaining the Dao" to "Dao integration"

Male humans in the Dao Realm (Entry 128) established their own Dao; Dao Integration answers a question that reaches the apex of the five mortal-cultivation realms: can the personal Dao "merge into the cosmos." Per the Unified Cultivation Realm Classification (Document 14), Dao Integration's core is "Dao integration": the personal Dao deeply fuses with cosmic origin laws, reaching "heaven-human unity"; can rewrite local law operation over a large range; life form begins high-dimensional evolution, partially freeing itself from the physical body; can create a stable personal domain (law domain). This realm's breakthrough is **a cosmic-level event requiring right timing, right place, right people**; those who complete it are civilization's "grandmasters," top in every field, with important influence on civilization's direction — after Dao Integration, one enters the four sacred-cultivation realms.

The six top-level axioms enter the "Dao integration stage" in Dao Integration: the anti-predation axiom — Dao integration is not swallowing the cosmos, but symbiosis with the cosmos; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — Dao Integration's door remains open to all Myriad Races; the collective ascension axiom — grandmaster-tier beings' job is precisely to "lift Myriad Ranks up together"; the causal self-discipline axiom — rewriting local laws is the highest-risk operation, most requiring observe-first, interfere-second; the law-first axiom — Dao Integration's "heaven-human unity" legal definition is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Portrait of Male Humans in Dao Integration

Male humans who cross the Dao Realm enter a "grandmaster's perspective":

- Strong sense of responsibility, understanding Dao integration as "upholding civilization's direction"; suited for grandmaster positions
- Strong domain desire, initial law domain creation makes him think "my domain my rules"; prone to conflict with others' domains
- Initial high-dimensional sensation: life form partially frees itself from the physical body, beginning to understand "I am not just this body"
- Reverence for cosmic-level breakthrough: knowing this leap is a cosmic event, instead generates the deepest humility

## II. Cultivation Path: From Attaining the Dao to Heaven-Human Unity

Under the unified classification, Dao Integration has four milestones; male humans must actively self-correct:

1. Dao-origin fusion: deeply fusing the personal Dao with cosmic origin laws, approaching "heaven-human unity"
2. Local law rewriting: fine-tuning local law operation over a large range
3. Life high-dimensionalization: life form evolves high-dimensionally, partially freeing itself from the physical body
4. Law domain creation: creating a stable personal domain

Male humans' natural biases: over "domain sovereignty" under "domain co-building"; over "rewriting laws" under "revering origin"; over "high-dimensional transcendence" under "mortal-cultivation responsibility." Anchor certification directly lists the purity of heaven-human unity, domain symbiosis, and law rewriting filing as assessment items.

## III. Pain Points and Costs: Dao Integration Backlash and Domain Conflicts

Dao Integration pain points are the deepest of the five mortal-cultivation realms:

- Dao integration backlash: the personal Dao fails to truly merge with origin, is "bounced back" by origin laws; Dao-heart and realm both collapse
- Domain conflict: personal domain conflicts with others' domains and public laws, triggering regional law turbulence
- High-dimensional loss: after partially freeing from the physical body, existential confusion about "who am I"
- Shortcut inner demon: the sentient-origin greed-shortcut inner demon in this realm upgrades to "swallowing cosmic origin for instant origin-level ascension" — the predatory faction's ultimate temptation

Breakthrough costs are real: cosmic-level breakthrough requires right timing, right place, right people, cannot be forced; but the Anchor Council and mentor system escort the breakthrough, costs are controllable.

## IV. Breakthrough Features: From "Dao-attainer" to "Dao Integration Grandmaster"

Markers for male humans entering the Boundary-Breaking Realm (sacred cultivation) from Dao Integration:

- Heaven-human unity: the personal Dao truly fuses with origin laws, no more "my Dao vs. cosmic laws" crack
- Domain symbiosis: personal domain harmoniously coexists with public laws and others' domains, no mutual swallowing
- High-dimensional ease: freely switching between material and high-dimensional, not lost
- Grandmaster self-awareness: knowing Dao integration is only the start of sacred cultivation, bearing Myriad Races' ascension on shoulders

## V. Cross-Realm Handover: How Mortal Completeness Moves to Sacred Boundary-Breaking

Dao Integration is the apex of the five mortal-cultivation realms; male human grandmasters must complete the identity switch from "mortal cultivation" to "sacred cultivation" before breaking through to the Boundary-Breaking Realm. The key reminder at handover: Dao integration is not the endpoint, but the start of sacred cultivation — he used to cultivate within the material plane; the Boundary-Breaking Realm requires learning stable cross-plane operation, needing to expand the personal domain into a small independent space. Male humans who treat Dao integration as "stopping here" get stuck at the threshold between mortal and sacred. The Anchor Council assesses before breakthrough whether he is psychologically and capably ready to "lift Myriad Races up together" — the responsibility of the four sacred realms officially presses on his shoulders from the Boundary-Breaking Realm onward.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- Anti-energy waste: male sense of responsibility is civilization's scarce grandmaster resource; channeled toward "lifting Myriad Races up" is co-building
- Anti-predation: institutionally cutting off the ultimate temptation of "swallowing origin," Dao integration is symbiosis not swallowing
- Tang-Luo upper limit comparison: in the old-era universal welfare system, "Dao integration" was monopolized by the strong as being-above-others; in Heng Yuan Zhou, Dao integration is for lifting Myriad Races
- Myriad Races full coverage: the male human path shares 99% coverage with female humans (Entry 136) and all Myriad Races (Entry 138)
- Hyper-multiverse full openness: Dao Integration methods are free and public; the five mortal realms are complete here, the sixth link of the fully open chain

## Conclusion

Dao Integration is the highest point of the male human's mortal-cultivation path. Predation teaches him to swallow origin and claim supremacy; symbiosis teaches him to merge into origin and integrate the Dao. Making heaven-human unity real, making the domain symbiotic, he completes all homework of the five mortal-cultivation realms, carries Myriad Races' expectations, and moves toward the broad sky of the four sacred-cultivation realms.

In the final analysis, the Dao Integration male human cultivation path described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
