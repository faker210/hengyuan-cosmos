## Introduction: A cosmic-level breakthrough, what qualifies the Youth Supreme to hold it

The Youth Supreme dual path in the Dao Realm (Entry 130) solved "Dao structure and soul"; entering Dao Integration, the question reaches the apex of mortal cultivation — per Document 14, Dao Integration breakthrough is a cosmic-level event requiring right timing, right place, right people. Youth Supreme talent faces the ultimate temptation here: since hundredfold deduction can accelerate everything, why not fast-track even "Dao integration"? The answer: Dao integration structure can be deduction-accelerated, but the "bearing weight" Dao integration requires must grow on its own in the real world.

The six top-level axioms are the master outline of the dual path in Dao Integration: the anti-predation axiom — fast-track relies on technology not swallowing; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — the dual path faces all Myriad Race youth; the collective ascension axiom — youth grandmasters' job is lifting Myriad Ranks up, eliminating talent underuse; the causal self-discipline axiom — rewriting local laws most requires observe-first, interfere-second; the law-first axiom — the Youth Supreme Dao integration dual path framework is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. Quest Path: Quest Sediment Domain, Letting Bearing Weight Grow

The acceleration domain can give Dao integration structure, but cannot give "bearing weight":

- Slow tempering: personally bearing epic-level responsibility in the Quest Sediment Domain, training shoulders to hold Myriad Races
- Adversity growth subplot: letting the Youth Supreme personally solve once a civilization-level problem, understanding a grandmaster's weight
- Conservation of experiential quality: the acceleration domain handles Dao integration structure, the sediment domain handles grandmaster bearing weight — ability and responsibility grow together

## III. Two-Way Tension: Dynamic Tuning of Bearing Weight Metrics

## V. Cross-Realm Handover: How the Youth Grandmaster Bears Sacred-Cultivation Responsibility

The Youth Supreme completes mortal completeness in Dao Integration; before breaking through to the Boundary-Breaking Realm, he must confirm he can bear sacred-cultivation responsibility. The key reminder at handover: although the Youth Supreme meets the realm standard, his age and experience may not be worthy of the causal weight of cross-plane operation — one cross-plane misjudgment's impact far exceeds mortal-stage. The Quest Sediment Domain intervenes one last time here, letting the Youth Supreme experience "the causal weight of sacred-cultivation operation" through slow tempering. The Anchor Council does the ultimate assessment before breakthrough: whether ability, Dao-heart, and bearing weight are all level. Only when level may he enter sacred cultivation; missing one item, he supplements — this is the closing of the Youth Supreme dual path mechanism running from the first realm all the way here.
