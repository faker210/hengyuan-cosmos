# Entry 138 | Dao Integration Realm: Differentiated Cultivation System for Myriad Races

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 50, 52, 10

## Introduction: Each race's way of "heaven-human unity" is billions of poses embracing the cosmos

The Dao Realm (Entry 131) covered each race's Dao forms; Dao Integration's differentiation deepens into "the pose of heaven-human unity." A human's Dao integration is the cultivator embracing the cosmos; a beast tribe's is wildness resonating with heaven and earth; the wood tribe's is growth breathing with origin; the spirit tribe's is elements returning to the cosmic torrent; the machine tribe's is a system accessing the origin substrate — the same "Dao integration," but each race's pose merging into the cosmos is completely different. Using one Dao integration pose to measure all Myriad Races is pressing billions of ways of embracing the cosmos into one standard pose.

The six top-level axioms are the legislative foundation of Myriad Race differentiation: the anti-predation axiom — each race's Dao integration is symbiosis not swallowing; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — equal evolutionary qualification regardless of genealogy; the collective ascension axiom — each race's grandmaster must lift its race and Myriad Ranks up, eliminating talent underuse; the causal self-discipline axiom — cross-race Dao integration dialogue respects others' causality; the law-first axiom — the legal framework of the Myriad Race Dao integration differentiation system is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

- Wood tribe: Dao integration is "growth breathing with origin," a century of growth coagulates into unity breathing with origin
- Spirit tribe: Dao integration is "elements returning to the torrent," wind-fire-water-earth melt back into the cosmic energy sea
- Machine tribe: Dao integration is "system accessing the substrate," connecting the precision Dao to origin law substrate
- Consciousness tribe: Dao integration is "consciousness merging into laws," merging the Dao of Clarity into the law network

## III. Cross-Race Co-Cultivation: The Civilizational Dialogue of Domains

- Cross-race mentors: beast grandmasters help human students understand wildness unity; machine grandmasters help consciousness students do substrate access — the Dao-integration-layer form of Myriad Race origin two-way circulation
- Half-blood compatibility: half-bloods go through system compatibility assessment, eliminating race labeling
- Equal certification: realm certification recognizes only heaven-human unity purity, not birth

## IV. Comparison: Racial Narrow-Mindedness is the Welfare Ceiling

The Tang-Luo-style old-era universal welfare system only recognizes human-style "Dao integration," dismissing beast and wood tribe unity as "heretical branches." Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: Dao Integration is not humanity's coronation, but a pantheon of Myriad Races embracing the cosmos — 99% coverage, not a single race missing.

## V. Cross-Realm Handover: How Each Race's Dao Integration Moves to Sacred Cross-Plane

Each race in Dao Integration embraces origin in billions of poses; breaking through to the Boundary-Breaking Realm, each must learn stable cross-plane operation. Beast grandmasters must let the wild domain extend cross-plane; wood grandmasters must let growth rhythm cross different planes' law environments; spirit grandmasters must let the elemental domain adapt to different planes; machine grandmasters must let the system domain be cross-plane compatible; consciousness grandmasters must let the consciousness domain manifest on different planes. The Myriad Race origin two-way circulation manifests at this handover as: each race's cross-plane experience is openly shared, so later generations need not grope from scratch. Half-bloods need system compatibility assessment at this handover — cross-plane environments test half-blood domains most severely.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- Anti-energy waste: each race's Dao integration pose is a scarce way of embracing the cosmos; a single standard is the greatest waste
- Anti-predation: all races' Dao integration is based on symbiosis, none on swallowing
- Tang-Luo upper limit comparison: racially narrow universal welfare is not welfare, but patronage
- Myriad Races full coverage: five genealogies + exotic life + multiverse residual civilizations all within Dao Integration's 99% coverage
- Hyper-multiverse full openness: each race's Dao integration methods are openly shared, the sixth link of the fully open chain is race-blind

The open sharing of each race's cross-plane experience is the Myriad Race origin two-way circulation's last concentrated manifestation at the door of sacred cultivation. Cross-plane environments differ wildly in laws; any race groping alone pays a heavy price; while converging each race's cross-plane experience into the Alaya Network, later generations stand on the shoulders of the entire race's experience. This sharing is not charity, but the most efficient self-preservation — any race's stumble across a plane becomes a road sign for all. Myriad Races dare to move to sacred cultivation precisely because behind them is such a mutually upholding experience network.

## Conclusion

Dao Integration's Myriad Races embrace panorama proves: poses of embracing the cosmos are billions, but the direction of embracing is one — merge into origin, uphold Myriad Races. When beast wildness and machine system converse in the same Dao integration hall, Myriad Race symbiosis moves from "allowing difference" to "needing difference," and the five mortal-cultivation realms are complete here.
