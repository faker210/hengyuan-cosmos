# Entry 140 | Dao Integration Realm: Risks, Breakthrough Costs, and Alaya Supervision Mechanism

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 19, 49, 48

## Introduction: A cosmic-level breakthrough deserves cosmic-level guardianship

Dao Realm (Entry 133) risks were in "Dao inflation"; Dao Integration risks escalate to "the cost of a cosmic-level breakthrough." Per Document 14, Dao Integration breakthrough is a cosmic-level event requiring right timing, right place, right people. When a cultivator can rewrite local laws and create a personal domain, his influence on civilization's direction is already grandmaster-tier — such a breakthrough must be paired with cosmic-level guardianship. The Alaya Network and multi-layer supervision's task in this realm escalates from "watching the Dao's direction" to "escorting the cosmic-level breakthrough."

The six top-level axioms are the value origin of the supervision mechanism: the anti-predation axiom — the first mission of supervision is to make origin-swallowing nowhere to hide; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — supervision treats all Myriad Races equally; the collective ascension axiom — guarding Dao integration grandmasters is guarding civilization's direction; the causal self-discipline axiom — rewriting local laws most requires observe-first, interfere-second; the law-first axiom — the Dao Integration supervision framework is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Four Core Risks of Dao Integration

1. Dao integration backlash: the personal Dao fails to truly merge with origin, is bounced back by origin laws; Dao-heart and realm both collapse
2. Domain conflict: personal domain conflicts with public laws and others' domains, triggering regional law turbulence
3. Swallowing temptation: predatory remnants incite "swallowing origin for instant origin-level ascension," the ultimate form of predatory cultivation
4. Shortcut inner demon: the sentient-origin greed-shortcut inner demon peaks in this realm — cosmic-level breakthrough temptation is greatest

## II. Breakthrough Costs: Real, But Controllable and Reversible

Breaking through from Dao Integration to the Boundary-Breaking Realm (sacred cultivation) requires real costs:

- Timing cost: cosmic-level breakthrough requires right timing, right place, right people, cannot be forced
- Dao-heart cost: must be repeatedly hammered by reality; bearing weight cannot be skipped
- High-dimensional cost: the adaptation process of life high-dimensionalization is real
- Failure cost: Dao integration backlash is reversibly repairable, but requires dual backstop by Anchor Council and Symbiosis Court

## III. Alaya Network and Multi-Layer Supervision Closed Loop

- Anchor automatic monitoring: real-time monitoring of heaven-human unity purity; any Dao integration shake triggers instant warning
- Breakthrough escort: before cosmic-level breakthrough, the Anchor Council assesses right timing, right place, right people
- Domain filing: personal domains must be filed; domain conflicts are adjudicated by the Symbiosis Court (Document 48)
- Governance checks and balances: Dao integration grandmasters participating in civilizational decision-making must accept governance framework checks and balances (Document 49)
- Publicity objection period: thirty days of publicity, all Myriad Races may raise objections to "swallowing tendencies"

## IV. Cultivation Ethics Landing in Dao Integration

- Informed consent: rewriting local laws affecting others must obtain consent
- Minimum harm: domain expansion must not swallow others' domains
- Accountability: Dao integration grandmasters must undertake the grandmaster obligation of lifting Myriad Ranks up

## V. Cross-Realm Handover: How Dao Integration Hazards Amplify at the Sacred Stage

The supervision system's meaning in Dao Integration is clearest at cross-realm handover: a swallowing tendency uncorrected in Dao Integration becomes, entering the Boundary-Breaking Realm's cross-plane operation, cross-plane predation, with harm amplified to cosmic scale. Similarly, an undisposed domain conflict hazard triggers plane-level law turbulence during cross-plane extension. The Alaya Network and multi-layer supervision therefore list "Dao integration direction purity and domain symbiosis" as the ultimate mandatory item before Boundary-Breaking Realm breakthrough: any swallowing tendency or unresolved domain conflict must be corrected before passage. This rule guards the cultivator's sacred cultivation threshold — entering sacred cultivation, causal weight rises geometrically; half a step wrong is a cosmic-level accident.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- Anti-energy waste: supervision keeps Dao integration grandmasters on the symbiotic path, not wasting cosmic-level talent on swallowing
- Anti-predation: striking "swallowing origin" at the source, Dao integration is symbiosis not swallowing
- Tang-Luo upper limit comparison: the old-era universal welfare system had no Dao integration supervision, grandmasters claimed supremacy at will; Heng Yuan Zhou has full-stack guardianship
- Myriad Races full coverage: supervision treats 99% of Myriad Races equally; planetary star-core Dao integration is equally filed
- Hyper-multiverse full openness: every link of openness is paired with guardianship

The reason sacred-stage supervision is an order of magnitude stricter than mortal stage is the geometric rise in causal weight. One misoperation in mortal stage affects one cultivator; one cross-plane misjudgment in sacred stage affects Myriad Races across an entire plane region. The "ultimate safety check" the Alaya Network sets at the Dao Integration door is not distrust of the strongman, but reverence for all things in the cosmos. The perfection of the five mortal-cultivation realms must carry this reverence to enter sacred cultivation — otherwise the higher the realm, the more the cosmic disaster.

## Conclusion

Dao Integration's supervisory philosophy remains: control risks, keep costs real, keep the road open. When every Dao integration grandmaster's cosmic-level breakthrough is gently yet firmly escorted, the symbiotic cosmos will not be led astray by any swallowing temptation — this is precisely the confidence to dare move to sacred cultivation after the five mortal realms are complete.

Reverence for causality is the only pass mortal completeness hands to sacred cultivation. Without this reverence, the higher the realm the greater the cosmic hazard; with it, the higher the realm the greater the cosmic blessing. Every check at the Dao Integration door is selecting travelers worthy of higher freedom for the cosmos.

With reverence in place, the road dares to be opened; every pause at the Dao Integration door is for walking farther.
