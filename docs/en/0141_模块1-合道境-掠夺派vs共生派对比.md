## Introduction: Is Dao integration swallowing the cosmos or embracing it

The Dao Realm (Entry 134) competed over "establishing hegemony or fairness"; Dao Integration competes over the mortal apex's most fundamental thing — is Dao integration ultimately "I swallow the cosmos" or "I embrace the cosmos"? The Predatory faction's Dao integration is swallowing: swallowing cosmic origin into one's own body, claiming supremacy; the Symbiotic faction's Dao integration is embracing: merging one's own Dao into origin, lifting Myriad Ranks up together. The same "heaven-human unity," different paths, and the endpoint of the five mortal-cultivation realms is worlds apart.

The six top-level axioms are the watershed of the two paths: the anti-predation axiom — swallowing is predatory increment, embracing is symbiotic increment; the republican axiom of Myriad Races — the Symbiotic sees Myriad Races as fellow cultivators, the Predatory sees all beings as nourishment; the collective ascension axiom — embracing integrates for ascension, swallowing integrates for monopoly, eliminating talent underuse; the causal self-discipline axiom — all embracing operations are reportable and traceable; the law-first axiom — the Dao Integration comparison framework is stable and self-consistent, never rewritten for plot convenience, and infinitely expandable without collapse; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## III. Enlightened Predators: Benevolent Swallowing Is Still Swallowing

"Enlightened predators," even if benevolent, their Dao integration is still swallowing — he is willing to ascend with those around him, but never lifts Myriad Ranks together; instead he treats Myriad Races as "portable nourishment." He stands atop the predatory system, and the ceiling of ascending alone is his ceiling.

## IV. Why the Predatory Faction Must Historically Regress

The Predatory Dao integrator ascends alone: while he lives, he suppresses Myriad Races by himself; when he ascends/falls, no one upholds Myriad Races, civilization regresses. This is precisely what the collective ascension axiom aims to eradicate: "the strongman ascending alone." The Symbiotic Dao integrator lifts Myriad Ranks up together: when the grandmaster falls, the symbiotic network still upholds Myriad Ranks — this is the ultimate structural answer to eradicating historical regression.
