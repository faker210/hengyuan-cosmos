## II. Three Energy-Level Ascension Patterns

Pattern 1: from "energy problem" to "comprehension problem." Condensed and Transform solve the engineering problem of "how to accumulate and transform energy"; Law and Dao begin requiring "comprehension" — resources cannot pile it up, only deduction and tempering. This explains why the Law Realm is the "first major bottleneck": it is the switch point from engineering logic to comprehension logic.

Pattern 2: ascension thresholds move step by step from "trainable" to "cannot be forced." Condensed relies on patience, Transform on craft, Law on deduction, Dao on comprehension, Dao Integration on right timing, right place, right people — the higher up, the marginal returns of individual effort diminish, and the importance of civilizational backstop increases. This is precisely why the anchor network, mentor system, and dual-track time domains become more critical in higher realms.

Pattern 3: "reverse compensation" of ascension. Predatory cultivation can seize speed in low realms, but pays reverse costs of "impure origin," "cracked Dao-heart," and "swallowed resentment" at every cross-realm ascension — time saved in low realms is repaid double in high realms. Symbiotic ascension starts slow, but crosses every threshold cleanly.
