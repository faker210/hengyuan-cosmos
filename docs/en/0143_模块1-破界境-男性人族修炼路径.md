# Entry 143 | Boundary-Breaking Realm: Male Human Cultivation Path, Pain Points, and Breakthrough Features

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 17, 28

## Introduction: The first threshold of sacred cultivation, from "attaining Dao within the boundary" to "standing across boundaries"

In Heng Yuan Zhou's nine-realm system, the Boundary-Breaking Realm is the seventh realm and the first gate of the "four sacred-cultivation realms." Per the Unified Cultivation Realm Classification (Document 14), the first five realms (Sense through Dao) are mortal cultivation; the cultivator always polishes themselves within this cosmos' legal framework; from the sixth realm Dao Integration onward enters sacred cultivation, while the Boundary-Breaking Realm truly requires the cultivator to **break through plane-law limits and travel cross-plane without anchor support**. This is a qualitative watershed: no matter how strong a mortal cultivator is, he remains a person within the boundary; only a boundary-breaker gets the first pass toward the plane-level stage.

At this moment the six top-level axioms running through the whole text must be restated — the anti-predation axiom, the republican axiom of Myriad Races, the collective ascension axiom, the causal self-discipline axiom, and the law-first axiom. The Boundary-Breaking Realm is dangerous precisely because it, for the first time, hands "cross-plane operation rights" to the individual: without the five axioms as backstop, boundary-breaking power immediately degenerates into the predatory faction's "cross-plane harvesting"; with the five axioms, boundary-breaking becomes a transportation network shared by all Myriad Races. This entry focuses on the complete portrait of male humans in the Boundary-Breaking Realm: path, pain points, breakthrough features, and why males most easily take the fork in this realm; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Portrait of Male Humans in the Boundary-Breaking Realm

The physical stamina, willpower, and deduction ability male humans built in the five mortal realms become a double-edged sword in the Boundary-Breaking Realm. Per Human Positioning and Traits (Document 51), humans are adaptable and good at collaboration; on this foundation, male individuals commonly show:

- Naturally sensitive to "boundaries, limits, breakthrough"; likes challenging rules; has an impulse to "rip the plane membrane open and see"
- Clear authoritarian tendency; in cross-plane missions, prone to deciding on one's own, treating filing as a burden
- Strong in breakthrough engineering, suited for hard work like opening plane channels and repairing rifts
- Strong competitive desire; after boundary-breaking, prone to treating "I can go where others cannot" as identity capital

The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's handling of this endowment is not suppression, but **targeted guidance**: male humans in the Boundary-Breaking Realm are best suited for plane-channel engineering positions in the Spatial Law Dispatch System (Origin Three-Basic Set #3), converting the "impulse to tear boundaries" into "road-building, road-mending, road-management" public service, anti-energy-waste at the root.

## II. Cultivation Path: Complete Chain from Dao Integration to Boundary-Breaking

The Boundary-Breaking Realm is not achieved in one leap; its standard chain is already specified in Document 14, with four milestones:

1. **Law decoupling training**: on the basis of Dao Integration's heaven-human unity, practice letting the personal Dao temporarily "partially detach" from this cosmos' legal framework, understanding the elasticity of law boundaries
2. **Plane-membrane perception**: able to perceive the structure, thickness, and weak points of the plane membrane, distinguishing the interfaces between material planes, energy layers, and consciousness domains
3. **Anchor-free travel drill**: first under anchor guardianship, then without anchor support, complete short-distance cross-plane travel and safely return
4. **Channel operation certification**: obtain formal authority to open temporary plane channels, identify and repair plane rifts

For male humans, the path design must actively hedge three tendencies: first, "greedy for distance" — having just learned short-distance travel, wanting to cross large plane clusters; second, "greedy for speed" — skipping plane-membrane perception and forcefully ripping, leaving law trauma; third, "greedy for solitude" — refusing to travel with anchor supervision. The Boundary-Breaking Realm certification (Document 14 anchor automatic monitoring + Standard Committee assessment + thirty-day publicity) examines these three item by item, eliminating "crossing boundaries with illness."

## III. Pain Points and Costs: The Cross-Plane Shortcut Inner Demon

The Boundary-Breaking Realm is the first major eruption of the sentient-origin greed-shortcut inner demon at the sacred stage. Male humans' typical pain points:

- **Resource temptation**: discovering rich plane resources after crossing boundaries, getting the idea of "quietly taking without filing" — this is precisely the "disturbing causality" the causal self-discipline axiom explicitly forbids
- **Loneliness deification**: walking alone across planes for a long time produces a "I am above all beings" mindset, sliding into the enlightened predator's psychological soil
- **Law rejection**: different planes' law environments differ wildly; forceful adaptation causes rejection reactions between the personal domain's laws and outside laws
- **Disconnection risk**: one calculation error in anchor-free travel may cause loss between plane rifts; precedents exist in history

Breakthrough costs are equally real: forceful boundary-breaking tears the personal domain; at minimum the domain collapses and must be rebuilt, at worst the soul is flung into plane gaps. The Alaya Network and multi-layer supervision for the first time comprehensively intervene in this realm — cross-plane operations strictly filed, observe-first then limited interference, only relaying knowledge not disturbing causality. This is not binding the strongman, but protecting the strongman himself from being backlashed by his own ambition.

## IV. Breakthrough Features: From "Boundary Grandmaster" to "Plane-Level Power"

Markers for male humans who successfully break the boundary and formally enter the ranks of plane-level power:

- **Stable anchor-free travel**: able to anchor-free travel and safely return in any known plane cluster
- **Operable plane membrane**: able to precisely open channels and mend rifts, not crudely tear
- **High-dimensionalized life form**: able to survive simultaneously in material plane, energy layer, and consciousness domain law environments
- **Domain outward expansion**: personal domain can expand into a small independent space, possessing the rudiment of the World-Creation Realm

Breakthrough is not the endpoint. Above the Boundary-Breaking Realm, the World-Creation Realm requires the cultivator to evolve from "can walk" to "can create worlds." And a boundary-breaker with pure foundation and full filing has already become the backbone of the 20% Activation Strategy at the cross-plane level.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: male engineering breakthrough force and breakthrough desire are channeled into plane transportation network construction, not for monopolizing a single plane
- **Anti-predation**: boundary-breaking authority is not tied to predatory resource extraction; crossing boundaries only builds roads, does not harvest
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: in the old system, strong ones "able to cross boundaries" were often overlords swallowing plane resources alone; Heng Yuan Zhou hands cross-plane rights to regulated public positions, eliminating intra-boundary monopoly
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: male humans are only one kind of boundary-breaker; wood tribe, spirit tribe, planetary consciousness also walk this cross-boundary road (see Entry 146)
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Boundary-Breaking Realm methods and channel maps are publicly networked, a link in the "basic methods → Dao sacrifice → multiverse level → Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm" fully open chain

## Conclusion

The Boundary-Breaking Realm is the gate where male humans for the first time extend their "personal Dao" into cosmic public space. In the predatory system, this step means becoming a plane overlord; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, this step means becoming a builder and guardian of plane transportation. Once the cross-boundary step carries filing and responsibility, boundary-breaking is no longer destroying boundaries, but opening the blood vessels of Myriad Race symbiosis.
