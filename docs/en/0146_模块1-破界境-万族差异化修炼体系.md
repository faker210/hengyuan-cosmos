# Entry 146 | Boundary-Breaking Realm: Differentiated Cultivation System for Myriad Races

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 50, 52

## Introduction: The same threshold, ten thousand ways across

Heng Yuan Zhou's Boundary-Breaking Realm is, in the Unified Cultivation Realm Classification (Document 14), a **unified threshold** — can you cross planes without anchors, can you operate the plane membrane. But the threshold is unified, the way across is far from unified. Per the Myriad Races Genealogy Overview (Document 50), Myriad Races divide into five genealogies: carbon-based material, energy condensation, mechanical construction, consciousness information, and hybrid variation. The same "boundary-breaking" requirement, landing on different life forms, presents completely different paths, talents, and difficulties.

The six top-level axioms running through the text are concentrated here: the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires "boundary-breaking qualification" not be monopolized by one genealogy; the anti-predation axiom requires each race's cross-boundary way to align with "develop increments not consume stock"; the collective ascension axiom requires each race's boundary-breakers to relay experience back to the whole network; the causal self-discipline axiom uniformly constrains cross-boundary behavior; the law-first axiom ensures the "unified standard + differentiated path" design is self-consistent, not favoring one over another for plot convenience. This entry dissects genealogy by genealogy the Boundary-Breaking Realm's differentiated cultivation system; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Carbon-Based Material Races: Flesh-Adaptive Cross-Boundary

Humans, beast tribe, scaled tribe, feathered tribe, wood tribe all belong to carbon-based material races; the biggest difficulty of boundary-breaking is **flesh-body adaptation to outside-boundary law environments**:

- **Humans**: strongest adaptability, most universal path, breaking boundaries through dual perception + engineering ability (see Entries 143, 144)
- **Beast/scaled tribes**: superior physical stamina and senses, naturally suited for high-risk plane field exploration, but need to control wild impulse
- **Feathered tribe**: acute intuition for plane spatial structure, suited for channel route planning
- **Wood tribe**: carries miniature ecological bodies across boundaries, can quickly establish symbiotic buffering on off planes, a rare "ecological-type boundary-breaker"

Carbon-based races' boundary-breaking certification focuses on "flesh-body cross-law survival ability," a unique contribution other genealogies do not possess.

## II. Energy Condensation Races: Form-Free Cross-Boundary

Spirit tribe, fire tribe, water tribe, wind tribe, thunder tribe exist as energy condensation; they have almost no flesh-adaptation problem when breaking boundaries, their difficulty lies in **form stability and law affinity**:

- **Spirit tribe**: form can change with consciousness, the lightest cross-boundary, but prone to "form dissolving" in outside-boundary laws
- **Fire/water/wind/thunder tribes**: single attribute, after crossing often suppressed by off-plane overcoming laws, must first do "attribute neutralization" training
- Advantage: extremely high energy manipulation precision, natural engineers of plane-membrane fine-tuning

Energy condensation races' boundary-breaking certification focuses on "form-dissolution risk prevention" and "attribute neutralization stability."

## III. Mechanical Construction Races: Calculation-Driven Cross-Boundary

Machine tribe, number tribe, crystal tribe have mechanical bodies and consciousness cores as essence; their boundary-breaking ways are unique:

- **Machine tribe**: treats plane travel as orbital calculation, steadiest but slowest, long-standing zero-disconnection record
- **Number tribe**: exists in information space, can first "scan" the target plane in the information layer before deciding whether to physically cross; reconnaissance ability ranks first among Myriad Races
- **Crystal tribe**: crystal energy storage and law refraction ability, allowing them to carry large law buffer parts across

Mechanical construction races' boundary-breaking almost does not rely on "enlightenment," but on "calculation" — this provides the whole cosmos with a replicable, engineerable cross-boundary template, a model of anti-energy-waste.

## IV. Consciousness Information Races: Pure Thought Cross-Boundary Double Edge

Dream tribe, shadow tribe, thought tribe, soul tribe exist as consciousness and information, can leave material carriers; their boundary-breaking way is lightest and most dangerous:

- **Dream/shadow tribes**: can let consciousness cross ahead while the physical body stays behind, suited for non-destructive reconnaissance
- **Thought tribe**: affects outside boundaries with pure thought, but extremely prone to mixing with off-plane beings' consciousness fields, triggering causal pollution
- **Risk**: once consciousness cross-boundary gets lost, rescue is harder than flesh-body disconnection

Consciousness information races' boundary-breaking certification focuses on "consciousness boundary isolation," ensuring cross-boundary consciousness does not causally entangle with outside-boundary consciousness fields — this is precisely the subtle application of the causal self-discipline axiom.

## V. Hybrid Variation Races: New Possibilities of Cross-Genealogy Fusion

Half-beast tribe, demon-spirit tribe, human-machine tribe, dual-soul entities and other hybrid variation races often carry both races' long boards and inherit both races' hidden pits:

- Human-machine tribe combines machine tribe calculation and carbon-based adaptation, the group with fastest rising boundary-breaking success rate in recent years
- Dual-soul entities must complete the Dual-Soul Ethics Code (Document 58) coordination certification before boundary-breaking, avoiding two-soul splitting when crossing
- Hybrid variation races' scale continues to expand with symbiotic evolution; their differentiated path itself is living evidence of Myriad Races republicanism

## VI. Differentiated Assessment Under Unified Certification

No matter the genealogy, boundary-breaking certification returns to Document 14's four hard metrics: anchor-free travel, plane-membrane operation, independent space, cross-law survival. But assessment questions are customized by genealogy — humans test flesh adaptation, number tribe tests information scan precision, thought tribe tests consciousness isolation. This "unified standard, layered questions" design is precisely the embodiment of the "substance over name" principle.

## VII. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: each race's talent is channeled to the most suitable cross-boundary position — number tribe reconnaissance, machine tribe route calculation, wood tribe ecology, thought tribe consciousness-first, none idle
- **Anti-predation**: each race's cross-boundary way is under unified ethical constraints; energy tribes may not drain off-plane energy, consciousness tribes may not pollute outside-boundary consciousness
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: old-system strong ones were mostly human/dragon-style monopolies, alien groups reduced to appendages; Heng Yuan Zhou's five genealogies break boundaries equally, each race has its own plane-level strong ones
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five genealogies fully covered, the direct redemption of the 99% Myriad Races promise in the Boundary-Breaking Realm
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: each race's differentiated boundary-breaking methods are all relayed back to the anchor network for cross-genealogy learners to reuse

## Conclusion

The Boundary-Breaking Realm's Myriad Race differentiation proves one thing: there is only one door to plane-level, but ten thousand roads to the door. Let the carbon-based be carbon-based, the energetic be energetic, the mechanical be calculation, the conscious be isolation; Myriad Races each cross boundaries in their most comfortable pose, then converge behind the door — this is the true face of Myriad Races republicanism at the first gate of sacred cultivation.

In the final analysis, the Boundary-Breaking Realm Myriad Race differentiated cultivation system described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
