# Entry 149 | Boundary-Breaking Realm: Predatory Faction vs. Symbiotic Faction Detailed Comparison

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 04, 05

## Introduction: The same cross-boundary door, two worldviews

The Boundary-Breaking Realm puts the two civilizational paths' fundamental divergence on the same stage for the first time. Per the Unified Cultivation Realm Classification (Document 14), boundary-breaking = anchor-free cross-plane travel, operating the plane membrane. When this "cross-boundary right" falls into the **Predatory** hand, it is a license to open territory and plunder; when it falls into the **Symbiotic** (Anchor Symbiotic Civilization) hand, it is a work pass for building roads and networks. This entry puts the two paths' complete differences in the Boundary-Breaking Realm on the table, making the divergence tangible, comparable, and judgeable.

The six top-level axioms running through the text are precisely the symbiotic faction's point-by-point counter-propositions to the predatory: the anti-predation axiom says no to "cross-boundary harvesting"; the republican axiom of Myriad Races says no to "plane lords"; the collective ascension axiom says no to "solitary ascension"; the causal self-discipline axiom says no to "overstepping interference"; the law-first axiom says no to "plot-style cheats." Per Anti-Predation Core Treatise (Document 04) and Myriad Races Progress Basic Program (Document 05), this entry does a systematic comparison; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. Crossing Motivation: Territory Desire vs. Transportation Network

The Predatory's first motivation for boundary-breaking is "this plane is mine now." Crossing = prospecting resources, drawing boundaries to be king; the plane membrane is for him a gash to "tear open and snatch a handful." Its psychological root is the sentient-origin greed-shortcut inner demon — treating the cosmos as a cake to be divided.

The Symbiotic's first motivation for boundary-breaking is "does this road connect." Crossing = prospecting law compatibility, recording plane information, assessing whether a channel is worth building. The plane membrane is for him an interface "needing protection and maintenance." He cares about roads, not territory.

This divergence seems abstract, but in practice is: every Predatory crossing, cosmic stock decreases by a fraction; every Symbiotic crossing, Myriad Races' passages increase by one.

## III. Resource Logic: Consuming Stock vs. Making Increments

Predatory: after crossing, directly mine off-plane veins, drain plane origin energy, essentially **zero-sum predation**. Document 04 already argues its efficiency ceiling — the more it preys, the drier the preyed plane, eventually nothing to prey, strong ones fight each other to death.

Symbiotic: after crossing only does "knowledge relay" and "law compatibility"; resources come from the home plane's incremental circulation (Origin Three-Basic). Off-plane beings and ecology are preserved as-is, even benefit from the channel. Essentially **positive-sum symbiosis**.

One judgment criterion suffices: a boundary-breaker comes back from crossing, did he bring back a pile of others' ore, or a map and a set of techniques? The former is predatory, the latter is symbiotic.

## IV. Relation to Beings: Lord vs. Guardian

The Predatory treats off-plane beings as chives — raise them, harvest regularly. Plane lords and beings are master-and-slave; history has proven countless times this relationship inevitably erupts in rebellion.

The Symbiotic treats off-plane beings as neighbors — first assesses the impact of crossing on them, confirms "no causal disturbance" before acting. Guardians and beings are service-and-trust. The Plane Transcendent's (Origin Realm, see Entry 157) "the greater the power the greater the responsibility" is precisely the apex of this logic.

## V. Supervision and Ceiling: Unruled vs. Networked

Predatory boundary-breakers have no filing, no supervision, no accountability — seemingly free, actually fragile: once a stronger one appears, his "territory" immediately changes hands — this is the predatory system's "extremely high protagonist uniqueness dependence" dead spot, always relying on new overlords overthrowing old ones, civilization spinning in place.

Symbiotic boundary-breakers are constrained by the Alaya five gates — seemingly not free, actually stable: ability recorded by the network, experience reused network-wide, status accumulated by contribution. Strong ones emerge in numbers rather than one family monopolizing, precisely the 20% Activation Strategy's institutional goal.

## VI. Typical Ending Comparison

- Predatory boundary-breaker's ending: either backlashed by laws in cross-boundary predation, or overthrown in endless internal strife; the "territory" he guarded collapses with his death, beings return to the sea of suffering
- Symbiotic boundary-breaker's ending: step by step promoting to World-Creation, Origin, Multiverse, becoming a permanent node of the Myriad Races network; the road he built continues to benefit beings after him

## VII. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: the Predatory burns cross-boundary genius into "short-lived overlords"; the Symbiotic nurtures it into "perpetual builders"; the former is the greatest waste
- **Anti-predation**: this entry's thesis — cross-boundary harvesting and cross-boundary road-building, one character difference, two civilizational paths
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style cross-boundary strong ones run amok for a while by protagonist uniqueness; Heng Yuan Zhou boundary-breakers perpetuate by network and contribution, extremely low protagonist uniqueness dependence
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the symbiotic path defaults all Myriad Races can break boundaries; the predatory path only tolerates one lord
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the symbiotic faction's roads, maps, and techniques are fully open; the predatory's "territory" is never open

## Conclusion

The Boundary-Breaking Realm is a touchstone: the door is the same door, but the direction across determines whether one is an overlord or a road-builder. The Predatory faction walks the cosmos smaller and smaller; the Symbiotic faction walks the cosmos bigger and bigger. Myriad Races' choice is never "whether to cross boundaries," but "after crossing, do you build a road or dig a grave."

In the final analysis, the Boundary-Breaking Realm Predatory vs. Symbiotic comparison described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
