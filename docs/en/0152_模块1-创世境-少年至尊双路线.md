# Entry 152 | World-Creation Realm: Youth Supreme Exclusive "Fast-Track + Quest" Dual Path

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 16, 37

## Introduction: Let the youngest hands learn the heaviest pen

Heng Yuan Zhou's Youth Supremes walked the "fast-track + quest" dual path once in the Boundary-Breaking Realm (Entry 145); by the World-Creation Realm, the weight of these two lines suddenly multiplies. Per the Unified Cultivation Realm Classification (Document 14), the World-Creation Realm requires creating a complete world containing life — this is holding the fates of all beings. Can a young person who has not yet seen the world pick up this pen? This entry gives Heng Yuan Zhou's answer.

The six top-level axioms running through the text are even more indispensable here: the collective ascension axiom requires the world the Youth Supreme creates to ultimately benefit Myriad Races; the anti-predation axiom requires him not to write the world as his own "private game"; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires new-world beings to be not his puppets; causal self-discipline and law-first require his design to be self-consistent and not disturb existing causality. Per Multiplication Fruit Technical Principle (Document 16) and Symbiotic Education System (Document 37), unfolding the Youth Supreme's dual path in the World-Creation Realm. Among them, Article 6 the universal equal infinite law establishes the origin logic that all beings inherently possess basic infinite power, and predation loses its meaning.

## I. Why the World-Creation Realm Is Especially Dangerous for Youth Supremes

A Youth Supreme going wrong in the Boundary-Breaking Realm loses a road; going wrong in the World-Creation Realm loses **the fates of all beings in an entire world**. The Youth Supreme's special risks in this realm:

- Mind not yet mature yet holding the power to "create beings," most prone to designing a "self-centered" world
- The fast-track path lets him quickly learn world-creation technology, but not learn reverence for the life within the world
- The "stars holding the moon" environment constantly reinforces the "I am omnipotent" illusion

Therefore the World-Creation Realm's dual path assessment is far stricter than boundary-breaking — it is no longer just "power and mind both achieved," but proving this young person **is qualified to be responsible for an entire world's beings**.

## II. Fast-Track Path: Technology First, World Field as Training Ground

The Youth Supreme's fast-track path in the World-Creation Realm chain:

1. **Design acceleration**: using Multiplication Fruit hundredfold deduction amplification, quickly mastering the systematic knowledge of law design
2. **Virtual world sandbox**: in the quest domain simulation review, repeatedly "create and destroy, destroy and recreate," practicing the technology
3. **Small-world pilot**: first create a low-complexity, low-life-density small world as a training field
4. **Network review**: world blueprint and operation data are publicly networked, accepting Myriad Races mentor review

The fast-track path solves "whether you can create," but it **never issues world-creation certification alone** — no matter how skilled the technology, without passing the quest line's mind assessment, he cannot be allowed to create worlds containing beings.

## III. Quest Path: Reverence First, Understand All Beings

The quest path Youth Supreme actively slows down to supplement "reverence for all beings":

1. **Be an ordinary person in a world**: live as an ordinary being in existing mature public world fields, experiencing what it feels like to be designed and influenced
2. **Review old world history**: observe in quest domain simulation review how authoritarian creator gods turned their own world into a prison
3. **Deep talks with all-beings mentors**: long-term exchange with beings of different races and circumstances, understanding the "created" ones' demands
4. **Responsibility commitment**: publicly write down one's original intention for creating a world — to reclaim another good field for Myriad Races, not build a kingdom for oneself

The quest path trades for a solid foundation in the mind dimension (25% weight in Plane Transcendent assessment), and also guarantees no overturn when pushing the Origin Realm later.

## IV. Pain Points and Risks: Three Collapses of the Youth Creator

- **Toying with beings**: treating life in the world as NPCs, designing game-like cruel trials for his own viewing
- **Self-appointed protagonist**: arranging a "sole protagonist" in the world, stripping other beings' equal evolutionary qualification — this is precisely the predatory worldview in replica
- **Escaping responsibility**: creating the world then washing hands, ignoring collapse within it

The Alaya Network and multi-layer supervision implement a "dual mentor system" for Youth Supreme world creation: one technical mentor teaches design, one mind mentor watches the original intention; beings' happiness within the world is included in real-time monitoring; once beings are found instrumentalized, world-creation authority is immediately suspended.

## V. Breakthrough Features: A Youth Creator God of Dual-Path Unity

Markers for the Youth Supreme who successfully creates a world:

- Technical line: complete world built, laws self-consistent, autonomously running
- Mind line: passes ethics review, confirming new-world beings possess equal evolutionary qualification
- The world opens as a public world field, not private property
- Publicly acknowledges his creation ability derives from Myriad Races' co-built education and technology infrastructure

Such a "light pen, heavy mind" youth creator god is precisely the next generation the collective ascension axiom most wants to cultivate.

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: a Youth Supreme's world-creation talent locked into a private world is the greatest waste; the dual path channels it into Myriad Races' public world fields
- **Anti-predation**: explicitly forbidden to write the world as a predatory script with "youth sole protagonist"
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style youth protagonist is himself the world's chosen sole one; Heng Yuan Zhou youth creator gods design worlds where beings are equal
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the dual path is race-blind, all races' Youth Supremes are equally forged by technology and mind
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the youth creator's sandbox and review process is publicly networked, for Myriad Races youth to observe and learn

## Conclusion

The only way to let the youngest hand hold the heaviest pen is to first make him revere every life on paper. Fast-track gives him efficiency, quest gives him a bottom line; technology lets him build a world, original intention makes him worthy of it. The Youth Supreme's world creation is therefore not a genius's coronation, but a coming-of-age upheld by Myriad Races.

In the final analysis, the World-Creation Realm Youth Supreme dual path described in this document is no isolated case — always pointing toward the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's co-building direction; law self-consistency is backstopped by the law-first axiom, not drifting with narrative convenience.
