# Entry 153 | World-Creation Realm: Differentiated Cultivation System for Myriad Races

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 50, 12

## Introduction: Ten thousand races, ten thousand worlds

Heng Yuan Zhou's World-Creation Realm is, in the Unified Cultivation Realm Classification (Document 14), "creating a complete world in an isolated space and designing its foundational laws." When this task falls into different life forms' hands, the worlds created will be completely different. Per the five-genealogy division of the Myriad Races Genealogy Overview (Document 50), the same "world creation" gives birth to carbon-based, energy, mechanical, consciousness, and fused myriad world forms. This entry dissects genealogy by genealogy the World-Creation Realm's differentiated cultivation system.

The six top-level axioms running through the text show astonishing inclusiveness here: the republican axiom of Myriad Races recognizes that "worlds" are not only human imagination forms; the anti-predation axiom requires each race's creation to be incremental development; the collective ascension axiom requires each race's world experience to be relayed back network-wide; causal self-discipline and law-first require no matter which kind of world, it is self-consistent and does not overstep. Per the Life Cycle System (Document 12), unfolding the five genealogies' creation differences. Among them, Article 6 the universal equal infinite law establishes the origin logic that all beings inherently possess basic infinite power, and predation loses its meaning.

## I. Carbon-Based Material Races: Ecological Worlds

Humans, wood tribe, beast tribe, scaled tribe, feathered tribe creating worlds naturally lean toward **matter-ecology worlds**:

- **Humans**: designing social-type worlds containing civilizational collaboration, emphasizing beings' autonomy
- **Wood tribe**: world creation is creating a "living forest world," the entire world itself is a super-life; ecological self-cycle is its substrate law
- **Beast/feathered/scaled tribes**: creation often includes free migration and wild-balance laws, avoiding designing the world as a greenhouse
- Advantage: life cycle within the world is most natural, highest habitability

The carbon-based races' unique contribution is providing Myriad Races with "habitable, reproducible" physical world fields.

## II. Energy Condensation Races: Law Worlds

Spirit tribe, fire tribe, water tribe, wind tribe, thunder tribe creating worlds lean toward **energy-law worlds**:

- **Spirit tribe**: can create "pure law worlds," minimal matter layer, extremely exquisite law layer, suited for advanced cultivator deduction
- **Fire/water/wind/thunder tribes**: creating distinctly attributed elemental worlds, becoming dedicated dojos for corresponding cultivators
- Advantage: extremely high law self-consistency, natural laboratory for law engineering research
- Difficulty: if the world must hold carbon-based life, an extra energy buffer layer is needed

Energy tribes' worlds are mostly "cultivation resources" — precisely one of the three uses of created worlds described in Document 14.

## III. Mechanical Construction Races: Precision Worlds

Machine tribe, number tribe, crystal tribe creating worlds lean toward **calculation-construction worlds**:

- **Machine tribe**: creating "mechanical civilization worlds," laws run by engineering logic, the steadiest, least collapsing worlds among Myriad Races
- **Number tribe**: can directly create "digital worlds/information worlds," the world itself is a runnable huge program, suited for simulation review and consciousness habitation
- **Crystal tribe**: creating "crystal energy-storage worlds," energy circulates and amplifies infinitely within, a rare high-dimensional energy field
- Advantage: engineerable, replicable, verifiable, zero design defects
- Difficulty: somewhat "hard" for life needing emotion and chaotic creativity

Mechanical tribes' precision worlds provide infrastructure for the Multiverse Realm's (from Entry 164) cross-cosmos information interconnection.

## IV. Consciousness Information Races: Dream Worlds

Dream tribe, shadow tribe, thought tribe, soul tribe creating worlds lean toward **consciousness-information worlds**:

- **Dream tribe**: can create "dream worlds," carrying Myriad Races' consciousness cultivation and collective subconscious exploration
- **Shadow/thought tribes**: creating "hidden/thought worlds," suited for non-destructive consciousness research under causal self-discipline requirements
- **Soul tribe**: creating "consciousness resting worlds," providing safe habitation for life needing consciousness transition
- Difficulty: the boundary between consciousness worlds and real consciousness fields must be strictly isolated, otherwise causal pollution
- Value: the Quest Sediment Domain, Youth Supreme subplots, and soul-spirit resting all depend on such worlds

Consciousness tribes' worlds are the "invisible yet most precision" kind, deeply bound to the causal self-discipline axiom.

## V. Hybrid Variation Races: Fusion Worlds

Half-beast tribe, demon-spirit tribe, human-machine tribe, dual-soul entities creating worlds often build **cross-genealogy fusion worlds**:

- Human-machine tribe creates "physical + digital" dual-layer worlds, the most popular composite world field in recent years
- Dual-soul entities creating worlds must first complete dual-soul coordination certification (Document 58), avoiding world substrate logic splitting
- Hybrid world scale continues to expand with Myriad Races fusion, itself a living specimen of Myriad Races republicanism

## VI. Differentiated Creation Assessment Under Unified Standards

No matter the genealogy, world-creation certification returns to Document 14's hard metrics: complete world, self-consistent laws, life seeding, autonomously runnable. But the connotation of "complete world" is customized by genealogy — wood tribe's completeness is ecological self-cycle, number tribe's completeness is program self-run, dream tribe's completeness is consciousness self-consistency. This "unified standard, ten thousand world forms" design is precisely the redemption of the 99% Myriad Races coverage in the World-Creation Realm.

## VII. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: each race's creation talent is channeled to the world type it is best at, none forced to create "a world good in others' eyes"
- **Anti-predation**: each race's world is newly built increment, not swallowing and remodeling existing planes
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: old-system strong ones' worlds were mostly "human/dragon centered"; Heng Yuan Zhou's five genealogies each create their own worlds, coexisting equally
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five genealogies fully covered, world forms vary by race, absolutely no "standard-answer world"
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: each race's creation experience is all relayed back to the anchor network, cross-genealogy learners can borrow wood tribe ecology methods, number tribe program methods

## Conclusion

The World-Creation Realm's Myriad Races differences show there is no single template for a "good world." Wood tribe's forests, number tribe's programs, dream tribe's dreams, human tribe's societies — as long as the life inside can live equally and freely, it is a good world. Myriad Races each create their own worlds, then relay experience back to the same network, and the cosmos truly becomes rich.

In the final analysis, the World-Creation Realm Myriad Race differentiated cultivation system described in this document is no isolated case — always pointing toward the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's co-building direction; its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable; law self-consistency is backstopped by the law-first axiom, not drifting with narrative convenience.
