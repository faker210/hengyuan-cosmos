# Entry 155 | World-Creation Realm: Risks, Breakthrough Costs, and Alaya Supervision Mechanism

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 21, 27

## Introduction: Creating a world needs more supervision than destroying one

Boundary-Breaking Realm supervision prevents "tearing down existing worlds"; World-Creation Realm supervision prevents "creating a bad world." Per the Unified Cultivation Realm Classification (Document 14), the World-Creation Realm requires creating a complete world containing matter, energy, laws, and life in an isolated space, and designing its foundational laws — this is being responsible for an entire world's fate. One crooked stroke of the pen is the sea of suffering of myriad beings. This entry dissects the World-Creation Realm's risk map, cost structure, and Alaya supervisory mechanism.

The six top-level axioms running through the text enter the engineering implementation of "creation responsibility" here: the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires new beings not be the designer's private property; the causal self-discipline axiom constrains creation from polluting existing causality; the anti-predation axiom prevents "in the name of creation, swallowing in fact"; the collective ascension axiom requires the created world to give back to Myriad Races; the law-first axiom requires the blueprint be self-consistent, never rewriting. Per Symbiotic Network Operation Principle (Document 21) and Anchor Calibration and Maintenance (Document 27), unfolding supervision; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Risk Map of the World-Creation Realm

| Risk Type | Manifestation | Consequence |
|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| Design risk | Law conflict, substrate logic self-contradiction | The world collapses on its own |
| Beings risk | Designing beings as tools, slaves, toys | Stripping equal evolutionary qualification |
| Causal risk | Isolated space loses control and leaks out | Disturbing home plane's existing causality |
| Property risk | Locking the world as private property, never opening | Violating Myriad Races republicanism |
| Deification risk | The creator god is authoritarian within the world | Sliding into the predatory worldview |

Among these, "beings risk" and "property risk" are the core restricted zones Heng Yuan Zhou extracted from the predatory era — old-system creator gods treated worlds as private property and beings as chives; this is precisely what supervision must eradicate.

## II. Breakthrough Costs: Backlash from Creating a Bad World

Costs of forceful or hasty world creation:

- **Consciousness drawn in**: when the world collapses, the creator god's consciousness is drawn into the collapsing system, trapped and hard to escape
- **Origin depletion**: repeated law design failures consume large amounts of the creator's origin energy
- **Law pollution**: failed world residue pollutes the isolated space, must be cleaned before second creation
- **Qualification suspension**: if the world is deemed "penning beings," world-creation authority is suspended and reassessed

In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, these costs are resolved upfront: before creation there are hundreds of virtual sandbox trial-and-error runs, during creation there is real-time Anchor Council calibration, after failure there are residue-cleaning channels. Costs are compressed from "catastrophic" to "learning cost" — this is the direct embodiment of anti-energy-waste.

## III. Alaya Supervisory Mechanism: Six Gates

Per Symbiotic Network Operation Principle (Document 21), World-Creation Realm supervision is deeper than boundary-breaking, with six gates:

1. **Blueprint gate**: the world blueprint (matter/energy/law/life four-layer design) must be reviewed by the Anchor Council, confirming laws self-consistent and beings have paths
2. **Sandbox gate**: before creation, run through in quest domain simulation review, no collapse record
3. **Beings review gate**: the ethics committee confirms new-world beings are designed as "ends" not "tools," possessing equal evolutionary qualification
4. **Isolation gate**: strict causal isolation layer between the isolated space and the home plane, preventing leakage
5. **Publicity gate**: world blueprint and operation data are network-publicized for thirty days, Myriad Races may raise objections
6. **Openness gate**: the completed world must open as a public world field, registered into joint contracting, private locking forbidden

## IV. Balance of Supervision and Creative Freedom

The philosophy of creation supervision is still "guard their creation, prevent their authoritarianism":

- Blueprint review only checks three red lines — "self-consistency, beings' qualification, openness" — and does not dictate what the world specifically looks like; you can create forests, digital worlds, dream worlds, all fine
- Once a creator proves long-term output of healthy open worlds, their review process is automatically simplified (trust accumulation)
- Myriad Races are not only supervised subjects, but reviewers — whether the world is good is voted on by the beings inside and those who have used it

This "rigid bottom line, aesthetic freedom" supervision both eliminates authoritarian god-kingdoms and does not strangle the creator god's imagination.

## V. Disposal of Bad Worlds

If a world is judged to violate red lines (such as penning beings, private locking not opening, laws anti-beings):

- First suspend the creator god's authority, isolate the world
- Assess the situation of beings inside the world, transfer beings to other public world fields if necessary
- The world itself, per the Symbiosis Court's ruling, is rectified or destroyed; before destruction, beings are settled first
- Never sacrifice beings inside the world to "punish" the creator god — this is the bottom line of the causal self-discipline axiom

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: sandbox trial-and-error absorbs the cost of "creating bad worlds" upfront, not letting repeated real collapses burn money
- **Anti-predation**: the six gates specifically cut the predatory path of "in the name of creation, swallowing and penning"
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: old-system creator gods had final say and no one could control them; Heng Yuan Zhou creators are constrained by Myriad Races review and public openness
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: supervision treats five genealogies and planetary consciousness equally, Youth Supremes are not exempt either
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the supervisory framework and "good world" standard are network-public, Myriad Races jointly participate in defining what a good world is

## Conclusion

Creating a world is the heaviest thing civilization allows an individual to do. Precisely because it is heavy, it needs institutional backstop all the more. Creation supervision on the surface draws red lines for the creator god, but essentially speaks for the not-yet-born beings inside the world — ensuring every created world, from the moment of birth, stands on the side of the five axioms.
