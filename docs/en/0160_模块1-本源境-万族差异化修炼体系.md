# Entry 160 | Origin Realm: Differentiated Cultivation System for Myriad Races

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 17, 50

## Introduction: At the cosmos' apex, there must be Myriad Races' voices

Heng Yuan Zhou's Origin Realm is, per the Unified Cultivation Realm Classification (Document 14), the apex of the nine realms, and per the Plane Transcendent Standard (Document 17), the Plane Transcendent threshold. This realm's assessment uses four-dimensional assessment (power 40 / mind 25 / contribution 20 / responsibility 15). The question arises: when strong ones of different life forms all stand at the cosmos' apex, what ten thousand faces will "origin coupling" present? This entry, per the five genealogies of Myriad Races Genealogy Overview (Document 50), dissects the Origin Realm's differentiated cultivation system.

The six top-level axioms running through the text have institutional redemption here: the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the 20% Activation Strategy to have "every race its own Transcendent representative"; the collective ascension axiom requires each race's origin experience to be relayed back network-wide; anti-predation, causal self-discipline, and law-first uniformly constrain all genealogies' cosmic-level operations. This entry unfolds genealogy by genealogy. Among them, Article 6 the universal equal infinite law establishes the origin logic that all beings inherently possess basic infinite power, and predation loses its meaning.

## I. Carbon-Based Material Races: Flesh Body Approaching Origin

Humans, wood tribe, beast tribe, scaled tribe, feathered tribe pushing origin, the difficulty is **how the flesh body couples with cosmic origin**:

- **Humans**: most adaptable, most universal path, steadily reaching the apex by four-dimensional assessment (see Entries 157, 158)
- **Wood tribe**: symbiotically couples with origin as "a super-life," its origin coupling carries ecological breathing, the "greenest" Transcendent
- **Beast/scaled tribes**: touching origin with wild intuition, good at cosmic-scale "ecological balance"-type law fine-tuning
- Advantage: most understanding of beings' flesh-body daily suffering, law fine-tuning most grounded

Carbon-based Transcendents' unique contribution is keeping cosmic law fine-tuning always remembering "beings have flesh and blood."

## II. Energy Condensation Races: Origin as Energy's Perfection

Spirit tribe, fire tribe, water tribe, wind tribe, thunder tribe pushing origin, almost no flesh-body problem, their difficulty is **attribute purity and origin compatibility**:

- **Spirit tribe**: deepest understanding of energy origin, natural theorist of law fine-tuning, often produces "origin law-level" contributions
- **Fire/water/wind/thunder tribes**: entering with single-attribute origin, good at optimizing corresponding attribute's law distribution in the cosmos
- Advantage: energy purity naturally close to 99% standard, power dimension easily met
- Difficulty: must pass the mind gate, avoiding "overriding beings with pure energy will"

## III. Mechanical Construction Races: Origin as Program's Limit

Machine tribe, number tribe, crystal tribe pushing origin, the method is most unique:

- **Machine tribe**: understanding cosmic origin as "the ultimate operating system," its law fine-tuning most rigorous, least error-prone
- **Number tribe**: directly touching origin from the "cosmic source code" perspective, natural candidate for the Multiverse Realm's (from Entry 164) cross-cosmos information governance
- **Crystal tribe**: carrying plane-level energy with crystal energy storage, energy recovery and reserve ability first among Myriad Races
- Advantage: power dimension near full score, law fine-tuning engineerable and verifiable
- Difficulty: must supplement "emotion for beings" understanding, avoiding treating the cosmos as a machine

## IV. Consciousness Information Races: Origin as Consciousness' Depths

Dream tribe, shadow tribe, thought tribe, soul tribe pushing origin, the difficulty is **consciousness boundary and cosmic origin isolation**:

- **Thought tribe**: touching cosmic consciousness origin with pure thought, core force for consciousness-domain law fine-tuning
- **Dream/soul tribes**: good at cosmic-level consciousness ecology and soul-spirit resting contributions
- Risk: if consciousness origin coupling oversteps, it may entangle with the entire cosmos' all-beings consciousness field, triggering causal pollution
- Value: provides samples for the Multiverse Realm's cross-cosmos consciousness interconnection

Consciousness information race Transcendents are the "invisible yet most subtle" kind.

## V. Hybrid Variation Races: Cross-Genealogy Origin Fusion

Half-beast tribe, demon-spirit tribe, human-machine tribe, dual-soul entities pushing origin, often combining two races' origin coupling strengths:

- Human-machine tribe combines mechanical rigor and carbon-based empathy, the most balanced group among Transcendents in recent years
- Dual-soul entities must first complete dual-soul coordination certification (Document 58), ensuring cosmic-level consciousness does not split
- Hybrid variation Transcendents themselves are living evidence of "cross-genealogy Myriad Races republicanism"

## VI. Differentiated Meeting Under Unified Four-Dimensional Assessment

No matter the genealogy, origin certification returns to Document 17's four-dimensional hard metrics: power (reaching Origin Realm, plane-level origin mobilization), mind (anti-predation stance + ethics review), contribution (top 20% of civilization + at least one civilizational-level contribution), responsibility (willing to bear plane-level public responsibility). But the connotation of "power" is customized by genealogy — humans test flesh-body origin coupling, number tribe tests cosmic source code understanding, thought tribe tests consciousness boundary isolation. This "unified standard, ten thousand paths" design is precisely the redemption of the 20% Activation Strategy's "every race has a Transcendent representative."

## VII. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: each race's origin talent is channeled to the law field it is best at, none forced to take the same apex road
- **Anti-predation**: each race's Transcendent is constrained by four-dimensional assessment; energy tribes do not drain the cosmos, consciousness tribes do not pollute consciousness fields
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: old-system Transcendents were almost monopolized by human/dragon tribes; Heng Yuan Zhou's five genealogies each have their own cosmic-level gatekeepers
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the 20% Activation Strategy explicitly requires every race to have a Transcendent representative; the Origin Realm is the apex redemption of Myriad Races 99% coverage
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: each race's origin cognition and law fine-tuning experience are all relayed back to the anchor network, laying the foundation for cross-cosmos governance

## Conclusion

If there is only one voice at the cosmos' apex, the cosmos itself is sick. Wood tribe's ecology, spirit tribe's theory, number tribe's programs, thought tribe's consciousness — when the five genealogies' Transcendents sit side by side at the cosmic law council table, Myriad Races republicanism truly lands at the highest point. The Origin Realm's Myriad Races differences ultimately prove: cosmic origin does not belong to any one race, it belongs to all life willing to guard it.

In the final analysis, the Origin Realm Myriad Race differentiated cultivation system described in this document is no isolated case — always pointing toward the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's co-building direction; its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable; law self-consistency is backstopped by the law-first axiom, not drifting with narrative convenience.
