# Entry 162 | Origin Realm: Risks, Breakthrough Costs, and Alaya Supervisory Mechanism

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 17, 48

## Introduction: The hand that can fine-tune the cosmos must be the cleanest hand

The Origin Realm is the apex of the nine realms, and also the realm with the deepest, heaviest supervisory mechanism. Per the Plane Transcendent Standard (Document 17), Origin Realm strong ones have the right to participate in cosmic law fine-tuning and mobilize cosmic origin power. Once this hand loses control, the consequence is not a plane or a world, but **the entire cosmos' law direction**. How to make the cosmos' apex power both function and not harm beings is precisely this entry's topic.

The six top-level axioms running through the text become the soul of supervision here: the causal self-discipline axiom is the legal source for cosmic-level operation filing; the anti-predation axiom distinguishes "gatekeeper" from "overlord"; the collective ascension axiom requires supervision to prevent strong ones from achieving immortality alone; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the four-dimensional assessment to be impartial; the law-first axiom requires this supervision itself to be stable and self-consistent, not relaxed for plot convenience. Per Document 17's four-dimensional assessment and Judicial System and Adjudication Mechanism (Document 48), unfolding the Origin Realm's risk map, costs, and supervision; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Risk Map of the Origin Realm

| Risk Type | Manifestation | Consequence |
|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| Law risk | Cosmic-level fine-tuning error | Entire-cosmos law disorder, beings' disaster |
| Authoritarian risk | Strong ones treating fine-tuning rights as private rights | The cosmos becomes one person's private property |
| Immortal detachment | Looking down on beings after near-immortality | Sliding into predatory cosmic mastery |
| Responsibility evasion | Rejecting 30% public obligation | Strong ones enjoy privilege without bearing responsibility |
| Overlord restoration | Old era's "plane overlord" mindset recurs | Enslaving the cosmos, beings regress |

Among these, "authoritarian risk" and "overlord restoration" are the core extracted by Heng Yuan Zhou from the predatory era's bloody lessons — cosmic-level power if unrestrained is more terrifying than any disaster.

## II. Breakthrough Costs: Cosmic-Level Backlash from Coupling Failure

Costs of forcefully pushing origin:

- **Origin rejection**: failed coupling with cosmic origin, origin power backlashes; at best realm drops, at worst body-and-spirit annihilation
- **Law oscillation**: fine-tuning error triggers cosmic-level law oscillation, affecting billions of beings
- **High-risk classification**: those whose power is met but mind/responsibility not met are not recognized as Transcendents, instead classified as "high-risk individuals" under long-term supervision
- **Historical blemish**: if carrying predatory records, permanently stuck at the mind dimension, insulated from the Origin Realm

In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, these costs are resolved upfront: before pushing origin there are hundreds of sandbox cosmos rehearsals, during operations there are senior Transcendents full-time guardianship, after errors there are law stability repair channels. Costs are compressed from "cosmic-level disaster" to "controllable learning cost."

## III. Alaya Supervisory Mechanism: Four-Dimensional Assessment as the Guideline

Per Document 17, Origin Realm supervision takes the **four-dimensional assessment system** as the guideline, supplemented by the Alaya Network and multi-layer supervision:

1. **Power dimension (40%)**: reaching Origin Realm, energy plane-level purity 99%, can independently mobilize origin — this is the basic threshold
2. **Mind dimension (25%)**: executed by the Anchor Court ethics review committee — historical behavior review, consciousness deep scan, situational stress test, long-term observation four-layer gatekeeping
3. **Contribution dimension (20%)**: contribution value reaching civilization top 20%, at least one civilizational-level contribution record, automatically measured by the anchor network
4. **Responsibility dimension (15%)**: willing and able to bear plane-level public responsibility, accepting annual 30% time-energy strongman mandatory service

All four dimensions **met** before recognition as a Plane Transcendent; no matter how strong the power, if mind or responsibility is not met, rather than issue certification, the cosmos is not handed over to a "high-risk individual."

## IV. Balance of Supervision and Cosmic-Level Freedom

Origin supervision's philosophy is still "guard their great use, prevent their loss of control":

- After power is met, fine-tuning laws must go through cosmic-level filing, observe first, then limited operation, only relay knowledge, do not disturb causality
- Routine law maintenance can be done jointly by Transcendents, major law changes must be co-discussed by the Anchor Council and Symbiosis Court
- Supervision data is network-transparent; Transcendents themselves are also supervisors — this is two-way co-governance, not one-way monitoring
- The 20% Activation Strategy deliberately cultivates **a large group of Transcendents**, preventing individual strong ones from monopolizing cosmic laws — this is precisely the institutional design preventing "extremely high protagonist uniqueness dependence"

## V. Cosmic-Level Disposal of High-Risk Individuals

If an Origin Realm strong one breaks red lines (such as authoritarian laws, enslaving beings):

- Immediately freeze their law fine-tuning rights and origin mobilization rights
- Adjudicated by the Symbiosis Court per the Judicial System and Adjudication Mechanism
- At best re-education and power reduction, at worst complete origin coupling deprivation, never reused
- Throughout, never sacrifice the beings they guard — stabilize beings first, then dispose of the strong one

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: pre-rehearsing cosmic-level power in sandboxes, avoiding repeated cosmic-level disasters burning money
- **Anti-predation**: the four-dimensional assessment specifically identifies "overlords wearing symbology's cloak," cutting cosmic-level predation at the source
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: old-system Transcendents authoritatively rule the cosmos with no one to control them; Heng Yuan Zhou Origin Realm strong ones are constrained by four-dimensional assessment and co-governance
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the four-dimensional assessment treats five genealogies and planetary consciousness equally, Youth Supremes are not exempt either
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the supervisory framework and "cosmic-level gatekeeper" standard are network-public, Myriad Races jointly participate in defining what Transcendence is

## Conclusion

The Origin Realm's supervision is Heng Yuan Zhou's ultimate answer to the proposition of "absolute power." The hand that can fine-tune the cosmos must be the cleanest hand — this relies not on moral conscience, but on four-dimensional assessment and co-governance networks. Caging the cosmos' apex power in an institutional cage is the only way beings dare hand over the entire cosmos' future. The Origin Realm's supervision is therefore not distrust of the strong, but the deepest responsibility for all beings.
