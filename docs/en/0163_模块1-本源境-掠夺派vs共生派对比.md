# Entry 163 | Origin Realm: Predatory Faction vs. Symbiotic Faction Detailed Comparison

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 17, 04

## Introduction: Cosmos master and cosmos gatekeeper, one character difference

The Origin Realm is the apex of the nine realms, and the ultimate examination hall for the two civilizational paths' divergence. Per the Plane Transcendent Standard (Document 17), Origin Realm strong ones can mobilize cosmic origin and fine-tune cosmic laws. When this power falls into the **Predatory** hand, it is the coronation of "cosmos master"; when it falls into the **Symbiotic** hand, it is the responsibility of "cosmos gatekeeper." This entry puts the two paths' complete differences in the Origin Realm on the table.

The six top-level axioms running through the text are precisely the symbiotic faction's fundamental negation of the predatory worldview: the anti-predation axiom opposes "cosmos masters enslaving beings"; the republican axiom of Myriad Races opposes "one race one person monopolizing the cosmos"; the collective ascension axiom opposes "alone achieving immortality"; the causal self-discipline axiom opposes "casually changing beings' fates"; the law-first axiom opposes "plot-style heaven-emperors." Per Anti-Predation Core Treatise (Document 04) and Document 17, unfolding the systematic comparison; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. Identity: Cosmos Master vs. Cosmos Gatekeeper

The Predatory reaching the Origin Realm self-styles "cosmos master." He believes cosmic origin is his war booty, beings are his subjects (actually slaves), and laws are his private implements. Its psychological root is the sentient-origin greed-shortcut inner demon's cosmic-level form — treating the entire cosmos as a cake that can be privately owned.

The Symbiotic reaching the Origin Realm self-assumes "cosmos gatekeeper." He believes cosmic origin belongs to all things, beings are equal life he must guard, and laws are public assets he must optimize. His power comes from four-dimensional assessment and Myriad Races mandate, not personal martial force.

The root of this divergence is put most transparently by Document 17: the predatory era's "plane overlord" enslaves the plane by force, the symbiotic civilization's "Plane Transcendent" guards the plane by force — one character difference, two civilizational paths.

## III. Law Fine-Tuning: Authoritarian vs. Co-Governance

The Predatory fine-tunes cosmic laws by own will, for private gain — wherever there are resources, he changes laws to shapes favorable to himself; wherever there is resistance, he changes laws into suppression tools.

The Symbiotic fine-tunes cosmic laws through filing, for beings' welfare — observe first, then limited operation, only relay knowledge, do not disturb causality; major changes must be co-discussed by the Anchor Council and Symbiosis Court. He changes laws to make beings live better, not to make himself stronger.

One judgment criterion: this law fine-tuning ultimately benefits one person, or makes billions of beings stretch more? The former is master, the latter is gatekeeper.

## IV. Immortality and Responsibility: Monopoly vs. Obligation

The Predatory pursues immortality to forever monopolize supremacy — he wants to forever ride on beings' heads.

The Symbiotic accepts near-immortality, but bears annual 30% time-energy public responsibility (Document 17 strongman mandatory service) — he wants to forever stand beside beings. Immortality is not a reward to him, but a longer service term.

## V. Power Structure: Monopoly vs. Co-Governance

The Predatory Origin strong one maintains rule by "protagonist uniqueness" — the cosmos can have only one strongest, and he is that one. This structure inevitably heads to internal strife: once a stronger one appears, the old master is immediately overthrown, the cosmos spins in place; this is precisely the predatory system's "extremely high protagonist uniqueness dependence" dead spot.

The Symbiotic Origin strong one relies on "group co-governance" — the 20% Activation Strategy deliberately cultivates a large group of Transcendents, every race has a representative, no one can authoritatively rule the cosmos. This structure is perpetual: strong ones emerge in numbers rather than one family monopolizing, protagonist uniqueness dependence drops to extremely low.

## VI. Typical Ending Comparison

- Predatory Origin strong one: enjoying momentary supremacy on the god-throne, ultimately falling in internal strife, rebellion, or his own causal backlash; the cosmos he enslaved collapses with him
- Symbiotic Origin strong one: becoming a permanent node of the anchor network, his law optimization experience benefiting later generations; the cosmos continuously ascends in co-governance

## VII. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: the Predatory wastes the cosmos' scarcest origin power on "maintaining one-person supremacy"; the Symbiotic converts it into public momentum for Myriad Races ascension
- **Anti-predation**: this entry's thesis — master and gatekeeper, one private one public, two cosmic paths
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style Transcendents rely on protagonist uniqueness to authoritatively rule the cosmos; Heng Yuan Zhou Transcendents are constrained by four-dimensional assessment and co-governance, perpetually serving
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the symbiotic Transcendent group defaults five-genealogy every race has a representative; the predatory cosmos only holds one master
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the symbiotic law optimization and cosmic cognition are network-shared; the predatory "god-throne" is never open

## Conclusion

The Origin Realm is the answer examination hall to the ultimate question "whose servant is the strongman." The power of cosmic origin, used to master beings or to guard beings, determines whether one is predatory or symbiotic. A master, however strong, is only one person's god-throne; a gatekeeper, however humble, guards the home of ten thousand generations of beings. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is written in the four-dimensional assessment: only the hand willing to bear responsibility for beings is worthy of touching cosmic origin.
