# Entry 164 | Multiverse Realm: Male Human Cultivation Path, Pain Points, and Breakthrough Features

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 17, 28

## Introduction: From "gatekeeper of one cosmos" to "connector of many cosmoses"

**[Expansion setting, pending terminology committee calibration]** The Multiverse Realm is the newly added tenth realm outside the nine-realm official names (Sense/Condensed/Transform/Law/Dao/Dao Integration/Boundary-Breaking/World-Creation/Origin); its definition is established at the normative stage, and official terminology remains subject to the terminology committee's final calibration. The working definition this entry relies on is: **Multiverse Realm — crossing a single cosmos' boundary, governing and connecting multiple cosmoses' origins, law authority upgraded to multiverse scale, promotion requiring Alaya Network-supervised multi-dimensional trials**.

Male humans at the Origin Realm (Entry 157) are already Plane Transcendents of "one cosmos"; the Multiverse Realm requires them to take a grander step — **no longer only guarding one cosmos, but connecting and governing multiple independently existing cosmoses' origins**. This is yet another leap of the four sacred-cultivation realms (Boundary-Breaking/World-Creation/Origin/Multiverse) at cosmic scale.

The six top-level axioms running through the text extend to "multiple cosmoses" here: the anti-predation axiom requires that connecting multiple cosmoses is not swallowing multiple cosmoses; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires every cosmos' beings to have equal qualification; the collective ascension axiom requires him to uplift multiple cosmoses together; the causal self-discipline axiom requires cross-cosmos operations to be strictly filed; the law-first axiom ensures the Multiverse Realm expansion setting itself is self-consistent and does not conflict with the nine-realm foundation. This entry focuses on the portrait of male humans in the Multiverse Realm; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Portrait of Male Humans in the Multiverse Realm

From the Origin Realm's "single-cosmos gatekeeper" to the Multiverse Realm's "multi-cosmos connector," male humans' long boards and risks are simultaneously amplified:

- **Multi-cosmos coordination ability**: male strong ones used to engineering and systems thinking are suited to designing connectivity architecture between multiple cosmoses' origins
- **Monopoly tendency escalation**: able to govern multiple cosmoses' origins, most prone to misreading "connection" as "rule" — escalating from cosmos master to "multi-cosmos master"
- **Architecture design talent**: good at designing cross-cosmos law interfaces and origin channels
- **Scale imbalance**: when scheduling between multiple cosmoses, prone to neglecting individual beings' micro welfare within a single cosmos

The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's handling of this endowment is strictly defining "governing multiple cosmoses" as "connecting and serving" — Multiverse Realm strong ones are not emperors of multiple cosmoses, but **transportation hubs and coordinators** between multiple cosmoses' origins.

## II. Cultivation Path: Complete Chain from Origin to Multiverse

The Multiverse Realm's working definition path has four milestones:

1. **Single-cosmos completion**: first doing "one cosmos'" law fine-tuning and guarding well at the Origin Realm; this is the prerequisite for entering the Multiverse Realm
2. **Multi-cosmos perception**: perceiving other cosmoses' origins beyond one's own, understanding the fundamental differences among cosmoses' laws
3. **Origin connection**: under Alaya Network supervision, establishing stable, non-swallowing connection interfaces between multiple cosmoses' origins
4. **Multi-dimensional trial certification**: passing Alaya Network-supervised multi-dimensional trials, confirming possession of multiverse-scale law authority and mind

For male humans, the path must actively hedge three tendencies: first, **swallowing-style connection**, sucking other cosmoses' origins into oneself; second, **scale drift**, forgetting micro beings while coordinating multiple cosmoses; third, **authoritarian cross-cosmos affairs**. The multi-dimensional trial examines these tendencies item by item.

## III. Pain Points and Costs: The Temptation of Multiple Cosmoses

The Multiverse Realm is the sentient-origin greed-shortcut inner demon's eruption at the "cosmos cluster" scale:

- **Swallowing temptation**: when connecting multiple cosmoses, directly devouring other cosmoses' origins to grow oneself — this is the predatory cosmic hegemony's ultimate form
- **Master illusion**: mistaking governing multiple cosmoses for overriding all cosmoses' beings
- **Scale disorientation**: lost in multi-cosmos information floods, forgetting one's guarding original intention
- **Cross-cosmos causal pollution**: cross-cosmos operations disturb multiple cosmoses' causal chains, consequences far exceeding single-cosmos

Breakthrough costs: multiverse coupling failure causes simultaneous rejection by multiple cosmoses' origins, at worst consciousness torn apart between the cosmos cluster. The Alaya Network and multi-layer supervision's intervention in the Multiverse Realm is "multi-dimensional trials" — in multiple cosmoses' simulated scenarios, simultaneously examining power, mind, and causal self-discipline; any one cosmos' assessment failure means overall failure.

## IV. Breakthrough Features: From "Cosmic-Level Power" to "Multi-Cosmos Connector"

Markers for male humans who pass multi-dimensional trials and enter the Multiverse Realm:

- Crossing a single cosmos' boundary, consciousness and authority covering multiple cosmoses' origins
- Establishing stable connections with multiple cosmoses' origins, not swallowing
- Law authority upgraded to multiverse scale, can coordinate law interfaces across cosmoses
- Passing Alaya Network-supervised multi-dimensional trials, mind and causal self-discipline met

Breakthrough is not the end — the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm (from Entry 171) awaits, upgrading multiverse connection to "comprehensively balanced symbiosis of the Myriad Races origin balanced-anchor network."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: channeling male multi-cosmos architecture ability into connection and coordination, not used to monopolize multiple cosmoses
- **Anti-predation**: the Multiverse Realm explicitly forbids swallowing-style connection; connecting multiple cosmoses only does incremental interconnection
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: old-system "multi-cosmos-level strong ones" often devour various cosmoses; Heng Yuan Zhou Multiverse Realm strong ones only act as connection hubs
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: male humans are only one Multiverse Realm candidate; all races and planetary consciousnesses also walk this cross-cosmos road
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the Multiverse Realm's connection architecture and cross-cosmos channel design are network-public, laying the foundation for the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm

## Conclusion

The Multiverse Realm raises male humans' horizon from "one cosmos" to "multiple cosmoses." In predatory hands, this horizon inflates into hegemony devouring various cosmoses; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, it settles into a hub connecting various cosmoses. Once the cross-cosmos step carries causal filing and service original intention, multiverse is no longer swallowing, but Myriad Races shaking hands at a larger scale.
