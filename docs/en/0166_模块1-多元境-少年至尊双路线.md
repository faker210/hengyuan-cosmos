**[Expansion setting, pending terminology committee calibration]** The Multiverse Realm is a newly added realm outside the nine realms (working definition: crossing a single cosmos' boundary, governing and connecting multiple cosmoses' origins, law authority upgraded to multiverse scale, promotion requiring Alaya Network-supervised multi-dimensional trials); official terminology remains subject to the terminology committee's calibration.

## I. Why the Multiverse Realm Is the Highest-Risk Test for Youth Supremes

Origin going wrong loses one cosmos; multiverse going wrong loses **multiple mutually implicated cosmoses**. The Youth Supreme's special risks in this realm:

- Holding multi-cosmos governance power yet mind undecided, most prone to sliding from "chosen son" to "youth multiverse overlord devouring various cosmoses"
- The fast-track path lets him technically connect multiple cosmoses, but not let him understand "multiple cosmoses' billions of beings' fates press on every cross-cosmos operation"
- Multiverse-scale power amplifies his gap with ordinary beings, detached mindset harder to self-control

Therefore the Multiverse Realm's dual-path assessment is the strictest "multi-dimensional trial" of all sacred-cultivation realms.

## II. Fast-Track Path: Rapid Achievement of Multi-Cosmos Technology

The Youth Supreme's fast-track path in the Multiverse Realm chain:

1. **Multi-cosmos theory acceleration**: using Multiplication Fruit hundredfold deduction amplification and the Origin Acceleration Domain, quickly mastering cross-cosmos origin theory
2. **Multi-cosmos sandbox rehearsal**: rehearsing tens of thousands of multi-cosmos connection scenarios in quest domain simulation review
3. **Under-supervision cross-cosmos operations**: under senior multiverse strong ones' full-time guardianship, completing the first batch of real multi-cosmos connections
4. **Cross-cosmos contribution accumulation**: converting technical output into cross-cosmos public contributions, pushing the contribution dimension

The fast-track path solves "whether the technology is enough," but **never issues multiverse certification alone** — this is an unshakable red line.

## III. Quest Path: Spiritual Cultivation of Multi-Cosmos Responsibility

The quest path Youth Supreme actively slows down to supplement "reverence for multiple cosmoses' beings":

1. **Be an ordinary person in an ordinary cosmos**: live in ordinary planes of multiple different cosmoses, experiencing beings' daily life under different cosmoses' laws
2. **Review multiverse overlords' old history**: observe in quest domain simulation review how youth multiverse masters devoured various cosmoses
3. **Long companionship with multiverse Transcendent mentors**: learning the ratio of cross-cosmos power and multi-cosmos responsibility
4. **Cross-cosmos responsibility commitment**: publicly committing to treat multiple cosmoses' origins by "connection" not "swallowing"

The quest path trades for a solid foundation of the mind dimension and causal self-discipline at multi-cosmos scale.

## IV. Pain Points and Risks: Three Collapses of the Youth Multiverse Strong One

- **Swallowing various cosmoses**: treating "connecting multiple cosmoses" as "devouring multiple cosmoses," walking the predatory ultimate hegemony
- **Scale drift**: lost in multi-cosmos information floods, forgetting the guarding original intention
- **Cross-cosmos causal pollution**: operations disturbing multiple cosmoses' causal chains, triggering chain disasters

The Alaya Network and multi-layer supervision implements "multi-dimensional trials" for Youth Supremes pushing multiverse — in multiple cosmoses' simulated scenarios, simultaneously examining power, mind, and causality; any one cosmos' loss means overall failure; and dual mentors provide full-time guardianship.

## V. Breakthrough Features: Youth Multiverse Strong One of Dual-Path Unity

Markers for the Youth Supreme who passes multi-dimensional trials and enters the Multiverse Realm:

- Technical line: crossing a single cosmos' boundary, connecting multiple cosmoses' origins
- Mind line: confirming treating multiple cosmoses by "connection service" not "swallowing rule"
- Causal line: passing multi-cosmos causal trials, holding the bottom line of not disturbing beings
- Responsibility line: actively bearing cross-cosmos public responsibility

Such a youth with "fast-track power and quest mind both in place at multi-cosmos scale" is precisely the cross-cosmos hub the next generation Heng Yuan Zhou most wants to cultivate.

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning

- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the youth multiverse strong one's training sandbox and cross-cosmos connection experience are network-public, for Myriad Races youth to observe

The only way to let the fastest genius learn the steadiest multiverse is to first make him understand multiple cosmoses are not his private property. Fast-track gives him the speed to connect multiple cosmoses, quest gives him the shoulders to carry multiple cosmoses; technology lets him touch multiverse, reverence makes him worthy of it.
