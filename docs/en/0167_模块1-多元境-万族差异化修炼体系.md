# Entry 167 | Multiverse Realm: Differentiated Cultivation System for Myriad Races

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 17, 50

## Introduction: Between multiple cosmoses, ten thousand ways to connect

**[Expansion setting, pending terminology committee calibration]** The Multiverse Realm is a newly added realm outside the nine realms (working definition: crossing a single cosmos' boundary, governing and connecting multiple cosmoses' origins, law authority upgraded to multiverse scale, promotion requiring Alaya Network-supervised multi-dimensional trials); official terminology remains subject to the terminology committee's calibration.

The Multiverse Realm requires strong ones to cross a single cosmos and govern and connect multiple cosmoses' origins. When this task falls into different life forms' hands, what ten thousand faces will it present? This entry, per the five genealogies of Myriad Races Genealogy Overview (Document 50), dissects the Multiverse Realm's differentiated cultivation system.

The six top-level axioms running through the text are redeemed at multi-cosmos scale here: the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires Myriad Races beings among multiple cosmoses to interconnect equally; the anti-predation axiom requires cross-cosmos connection to absolutely not swallow; the collective ascension axiom requires every race's cross-cosmos experience to be relayed back network-wide; causal self-discipline and law-first uniformly constrain all genealogies' multi-cosmos operations. This entry unfolds genealogy by genealogy. Among them, Article 6 the universal equal infinite law establishes the origin logic that all beings inherently possess basic infinite power, and predation loses its meaning.

## I. Carbon-Based Material Races: Flesh-Body-Walking Multi-Cosmos Hubs

Humans, wood tribe, beast tribe, scaled tribe, feathered tribe walking the Multiverse Realm, the difficulty is **how the flesh body adapts to multiple cosmoses' fundamentally different laws**:

- **Humans**: strongest cross-cosmos adaptability, most universal path, steadily reaching the apex by multi-dimensional trials
- **Wood tribe**: using "cross-cosmos ecological transplantation" as its unique contribution, bringing one cosmos' ecological wisdom to another cosmos
- **Beast/feathered tribes**: good at multi-cosmos field exploration and cross-cosmos migration channel planning
- Advantage: most understanding of different cosmoses' flesh-body beings' daily life, cross-cosmos connection most grounded

## II. Energy Condensation Races: Energy Translators of Multi-Cosmos Origin

Spirit tribe, fire tribe, water tribe, wind tribe, thunder tribe walking the Multiverse Realm, the difficulty is **mutual translation of different cosmoses' energy systems**:

- **Spirit tribe**: deepest understanding of multi-cosmos energy origin, natural designer of cross-cosmos law interfaces
- **Fire/water/wind/thunder tribes**: good at "translating" one cosmos' attribute energy into another cosmos' compatible form
- Advantage: cross-cosmos energy purity and compatibility processing ability first among Myriad Races
- Difficulty: must guard against the predatory temptation of "draining one cosmos' energy to infuse another"

## III. Mechanical Construction Races: Multi-Cosmos Source Code Interfacing

Machine tribe, number tribe, crystal tribe walking the Multiverse Realm, the method is most unique:

- **Number tribe**: directly interfacing cross-cosmos from the "cosmic source code" perspective, the top seed for Multiverse Realm cross-cosmos information governance
- **Machine tribe**: treating cross-cosmos origin connection as "multi-system interface engineering," most rigorous, least error-prone
- **Crystal tribe**: carrying plane-level energy cross-cosmos with crystal energy storage, energy scheduling ability first among Myriad Races
- Advantage: multi-cosmos interface engineerable and verifiable
- Difficulty: must supplement "cross-cosmos beings' emotion," avoiding treating the multiverse as a big machine

## IV. Consciousness Information Races: Multi-Cosmos Consciousness Web

Dream tribe, shadow tribe, thought tribe, soul tribe walking the Multiverse Realm, the difficulty is **multi-cosmos consciousness field boundary isolation**:

- **Thought tribe**: touching multiple cosmoses' consciousness origins cross-cosmos with pure thought, the core of multi-cosmos consciousness interconnection
- **Dream/soul tribes**: good at cross-cosmos consciousness resting and multi-cosmos soul-spirit transition
- Risk: once multi-cosmos consciousness fields are confused, causal pollution is cross-cosmos-scale, consequences extremely heavy
- Value: providing consciousness-layer samples for the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's "Myriad Races origin balanced-anchor network"

## V. Hybrid Variation Races: Pioneers of Cross-Cosmos Fusion

Half-beast tribe, demon-spirit tribe, human-machine tribe, dual-soul entities walking the Multiverse Realm, often combining two races' cross-cosmos strengths:

- Human-machine tribe combines mechanical interfaces and carbon-based adaptability, the most balanced candidate for cross-cosmos hubs
- Dual-soul entities must first complete dual-soul coordination certification (Document 58), ensuring multi-cosmos consciousness does not split
- Hybrid variation tribes themselves are living evidence of "cross-genealogy, cross-cosmos fusion"

## VI. Differentiated Meeting Under Unified Multi-Dimensional Trials

No matter the genealogy, multiverse certification returns to the working definition's hard metrics: crossing a single cosmos' boundary, connecting multiple cosmoses' origins, law authority reaching multiverse scale, passing Alaya Network-supervised multi-dimensional trials. But the connotation of "connection" is customized by genealogy — humans test flesh-body cross-cosmos adaptation, number tribe tests multi-cosmos source code interfacing, thought tribe tests multi-cosmos consciousness isolation. This "unified standard, ten thousand paths" design is precisely the redemption of Myriad Races 99% coverage in the Multiverse Realm.

## VII. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: each race's cross-cosmos talent is channeled to the connection step it is best at, none forced to take the same cross-cosmos road
- **Anti-predation**: each race's cross-cosmos operations are constrained by multi-dimensional trials; energy tribes do not drain cosmoses, consciousness tribes do not pollute consciousness fields
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: old-system "cross-cosmos strong ones" mostly devoured various cosmoses; Heng Yuan Zhou's five genealogies each make their own connection hubs
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five genealogies fully covered, multi-cosmos interconnection defaults every race's beings have paths
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: each race's cross-cosmos experience is all relayed back to the anchor network, laying the foundation for the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm

## Conclusion

Interconnection between multiple cosmoses should not have only one way. Wood tribe's ecological transplantation, spirit tribe's energy translation, number tribe's source code interfacing, thought tribe's consciousness web — when the five genealogies' multiverse strong ones stand side by side to connect multiple cosmoses' origins into one web, cross-cosmos is both rich and safe. The Multiverse Realm's Myriad Races differences ultimately prove: cross-cosmos does not belong to any one race, it belongs to all life willing to connect rather than swallow.

In the final analysis, the Multiverse Realm Myriad Race differentiated cultivation system described in this document is no isolated case — always pointing toward the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's co-building direction; law self-consistency is backstopped by the law-first axiom, not drifting with narrative convenience.
