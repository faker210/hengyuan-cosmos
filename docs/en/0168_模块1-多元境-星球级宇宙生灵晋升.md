## I. Promotion Logic: From "a mother of one cosmos" to "a hub of multiple cosmoses"

Planetary-level beings' chain for walking the Multiverse Realm:

1. **Single-cosmos completion**: first making its own cosmic region self-consistent and stable at the Origin Realm
2. **Perceiving neighboring cosmoses**: consciousness extending beyond its own cosmos' boundary, perceiving other independent cosmoses' origins
3. **Cosmos-level connection**: under Alaya supervision, using itself as a hub, establishing non-swallowing connections with multiple cosmoses' origins
4. **Multi-dimensional trials**: passing ethics and causality assessment in multi-cosmos scenarios, obtaining multiverse-scale law authority

For planetary consciousness, "crossing a single cosmos' boundary" is not it moving away, but making its own cosmic region's law interfaces compatible and interoperable with multiple neighboring cosmoses — it still guards its own home, yet makes home-to-home into a network.

## II. Unique Pain Points and Costs

Giant life walking the Multiverse Realm costs are astonishing in scale:

- **Cosmos-cluster-level self-consistency**: it must simultaneously maintain law compatibility between its own cosmos and multiple neighboring cosmoses, self-consistency pressure rises exponentially
- **Multi-cosmos beings bound**: it shares fates with multiple cosmoses' beings, any one cosmos' oscillation affects it
- **Cosmos-cluster causal pollution**: cross-cosmos operations disturb multiple cosmoses' causal chains, consequences far exceeding single cosmos
- **Behemoth temptation ultimate version**: giant life with eons of accumulation is most prone to sliding into the "swallow neighboring cosmoses to grow" cosmic behemoth mindset

The Alaya Network and multi-layer supervision implements "multi-dimensional trials" for giant multiverse candidates — in multiple cosmoses' simulated scenarios, simultaneously assessing their self-consistency, mind, and causal self-discipline; any one cosmos' loss means overall failure.

## III. Nested Coordination with Individual Multiverse Strong Ones

Planetary-level beings walking the Multiverse Realm are never isolated:

- Individual multiverse strong ones (Entries 164, 165) act as "cross-cosmos probes," first prospecting neighboring cosmoses' laws
- Giant life uses its own cosmic region as a "hub node," connecting multiple cosmoses into a network
- The billions of beings it carries access the multi-cosmos origin two-way circulation through it

This "giant hub–individual probe" nested structure is precisely the three-dimensional implementation of the collective ascension axiom at multi-cosmos scale.

## IV. Breakthrough Features: From "Single-Cosmos Mother" to "Multi-Cosmos Hub"

Markers for planetary-level beings who pass multi-dimensional trials and enter the Multiverse Realm:

- Its own cosmic region establishes stable, non-swallowing connections with multiple independent cosmoses' origins
- Its own law interfaces are compatible and interoperable with multiple neighboring cosmoses
- Passing multi-cosmos causal trials, not causing disturbance to any one cosmos' beings
- Actively bearing multi-cosmos hub responsibility

Giant life reaching this step already stands at the Multiverse Realm apex, gazing toward the higher Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm (from Entry 171) — comprehensively balanced symbiosis of the Myriad Races origin balanced-anchor network.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: converting giant life's eons of accumulation into multi-cosmos interconnection hub power, not letting it sleep or run amok
- **Anti-predation**: cosmos-cluster-level interconnection relies on compatibility not swallowing, this is the fundamental negation of the predatory cosmic behemoth
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: old-system cosmic behemoths grew by devouring various cosmoses; Heng Yuan Zhou planetary consciousness acts as a cross-cosmos hub
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: planetary consciousness and cosmic origin bodies are included in the Multiverse Realm sequence; "paths for all cosmoses" extends to "paths for the multiverse"
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: giant cross-cosmos experience is relayed back network-wide, directly providing cosmos-cluster-level samples for the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm

Here a scale difficulty unique to giant life must be added: individual cultivators cross-cosmos only care about themselves; planetary consciousness crossing cosmoses equals affecting the billions of beings it carries. Therefore every time it "shakes hands with a neighboring cosmos," it must first complete a beings-impact assessment within its own cosmos — confirming that this hand reaching out will not make any life at home suffer. This "settle home first, then drop by" order is an ethics required course individual cultivators need not face but giant life cannot avoid.

When a conscious planet learns to connect its home to more homes, the multiverse is no longer isolated islands, but a network of mutual upholding. Planetary-level beings' multiverse path ultimately proves: true strength is not swallowing more cosmoses, but letting more cosmoses connect and grow together.

In the final analysis, the Multiverse Realm planetary and cosmic being promotion described in this document is no isolated case — always pointing toward the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's co-building direction; law self-consistency is backstopped by the law-first axiom, not drifting with narrative convenience.
