# Entry 169 | Multiverse Realm: Risks, Breakthrough Costs, and Alaya Supervisory Mechanism

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 17, 21

## Introduction: Multi-cosmos matters must be backstopped by multi-cosmos institutions

**[Expansion setting, pending terminology committee calibration]** The Multiverse Realm is a newly added realm outside the nine realms (working definition: crossing a single cosmos' boundary, governing and connecting multiple cosmoses' origins, law authority upgraded to multiverse scale, promotion requiring Alaya Network-supervised multi-dimensional trials); official terminology remains subject to the terminology committee's calibration.

The Multiverse Realm strong one's hand already reaches into multiple cosmoses' origins. Once this hand loses control, the consequence is not one cosmos, but **a mutually implicated cosmos cluster**. How to make multi-cosmos-level power both function in interconnection and not harm beings is precisely this entry's topic — the core mechanism is the "Alaya Network-supervised multi-dimensional trials" emphasized in the working definition.

The six top-level axioms running through the text become the soul of multi-cosmos supervision here: the causal self-discipline axiom is the legal source for cross-cosmos filing; the anti-predation axiom distinguishes "multi-cosmos hub" from "multi-cosmos overlord"; the collective ascension axiom requires supervision to prevent strong ones from swallowing various cosmoses; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires multi-dimensional trials to be impartial; the law-first axiom requires this supervision itself to be self-consistent, not relaxed for plot convenience. Per Symbiotic Network Operation Principle (Document 21) and Document 17, unfolding; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Risk Map of the Multiverse Realm

| Risk Type | Manifestation | Consequence |
|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| Swallowing risk | Making "connection" into "devouring neighboring cosmoses' origins" | Various cosmoses merged into one person |
| Cross-cosmos causal pollution | Cross-cosmos operations disturb multiple cosmoses' causality | Chain civilizational disasters |
| Scale drift | Losing original intention in multi-cosmos information floods | Forgetting guardianship, sliding into monopoly |
| Master-slaving | Making multi-cosmos interconnection into master-slave enslavement structure | Violating Myriad Races republicanism |
| Interface conflict | Multi-cosmos law interface design conflict | Interconnection network collapses |

Among these, "swallowing risk" and "master-slaving" are the Multiverse Realm's core restricted zones — this is precisely the predatory "multi-cosmos master" ultimate temptation.

## II. Breakthrough Costs: Backlash from Multi-Cosmos Rejection

Costs of forceful cross-cosmos connection:

- **Multi-cosmos rejection**: multiple cosmoses' origins simultaneously reject the outsider, consciousness torn apart between the cosmos cluster
- **Causal chain injury**: disturbing one cosmos' causality implicates multiple adjacent cosmoses
- **Interface collapse**: connection interface design error, already established interconnection network backlashes
- **Qualification freezing**: if judged to have swallowing tendency, multiverse authority permanently frozen

In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, these costs are resolved upfront: before cross-cosmos operations there are thousands of multi-cosmos sandbox rehearsals, during operations there are senior multiverse strong ones' guardianship, after errors there are interface repair channels. Costs are compressed from "cosmos-cluster disaster" to "controllable learning cost."

## III. Alaya Supervisory Mechanism: Multi-Dimensional Trials

Per the working definition and Symbiotic Network Operation Principle (Document 21), the core of Multiverse Realm supervision is **multi-dimensional trials** — it is not a single exam, but simultaneous examination in multiple cosmoses' simulated scenarios:

1. **Power dimension**: can stably connect multiple cosmoses' origins, law authority reaching multiverse scale
2. **Mind dimension**: facing multi-cosmos temptation, does one hold the original intention of "connection not swallowing"
3. **Causal dimension**: in multi-cosmos operations, does one hold "only relay knowledge, do not disturb beings' causality"
4. **Responsibility dimension**: is one willing to bear cross-cosmos public hub responsibility

The multi-dimensional trials' key design is **any one cosmos' loss means overall failure** — strong ones are not allowed to "obey rules in one cosmos, run wild in another."

On top of this, cross-cosmos operation filing gate is layered: each multi-cosmos operation submits a plan in advance, observe first then limited interference, only relay knowledge, do not disturb causality.

## IV. Balance of Supervision and Cross-Cosmos Freedom

Multiverse supervision's philosophy is still "guard their interconnection, prevent their swallowing":

- Routine cross-cosmos connection only needs free filing, no layer-by-layer approval
- When involving multi-cosmos law changes and large-scale origin scheduling, escalate to the cross-cosmos Anchor Council
- Supervision data is cross-cosmos transparent; multiverse strong ones themselves are also supervisors
- Once a multiverse strong one proves long-term zero-swallowing, zero-pollution, their process is automatically simplified (trust accumulation)

## V. Cross-Cosmos Disposal of High-Risk Individuals

If a multiverse strong one breaks red lines (swallowing neighboring cosmoses, master-slaving interconnection):

- Immediately freeze their cross-cosmos connection rights and multiverse-scale law authority
- Adjudicated by the cross-cosmos Symbiosis Court
- At best re-education and power reduction, at worst severing their connection to various cosmoses' origins
- Throughout, first stabilize various cosmoses' implicated beings, then dispose of the strong one

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: pre-rehearsing multi-cosmos power in sandboxes, avoiding repeated cosmos-cluster-level disasters burning money
- **Anti-predation**: multi-dimensional trials specifically identify "swallowers wearing interconnection's cloak"
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: old-system cross-cosmos strong ones devour various cosmoses with no one to control them; Heng Yuan Zhou multiverse strong ones are constrained by multi-dimensional trials and co-governance
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: multi-dimensional trials treat five genealogies and planetary consciousness equally, Youth Supremes are not exempt either
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the cross-cosmos supervisory framework and "multi-cosmos hub" standard are network-public, Myriad Races jointly participate in defining

## Conclusion

Multi-cosmos matters must be backstopped by multi-cosmos institutions. The Multiverse Realm's supervision, on the surface constraining cross-cosmos strong ones, essentially speaks for beings in countless cosmoses not yet affected — ensuring every cross-cosmos handshake stands on the side of the five axioms. Caging multi-cosmos power in the multi-dimensional trial cage, various cosmoses dare connect to each other.
