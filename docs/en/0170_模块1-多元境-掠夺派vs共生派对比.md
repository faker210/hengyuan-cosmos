# Entry 170 | Multiverse Realm: Predatory Faction vs. Symbiotic Faction Detailed Comparison

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 04, 17

## Introduction: Swallowing various cosmoses and connecting various cosmoses, one character difference

**[Expansion setting, pending terminology committee calibration]** The Multiverse Realm is a newly added realm outside the nine realms (working definition: crossing a single cosmos' boundary, governing and connecting multiple cosmoses' origins, law authority upgraded to multiverse scale, promotion requiring Alaya Network-supervised multi-dimensional trials); official terminology remains subject to the terminology committee's calibration.

The Multiverse Realm pushes the two civilizational paths' divergence to the grandest stage of "multiple cosmoses." This power to connect multiple cosmoses' origins, when it falls into the **Predatory** hand, is the hegemony of "devouring various cosmoses"; when it falls into the **Symbiotic** hand, it is the hub of "connecting various cosmoses." This entry puts the two paths' complete differences in the Multiverse Realm on the table.

The six top-level axioms running through the text are the symbiotic faction's fundamental negation of the predatory multiverse worldview: the anti-predation axiom opposes "swallowing neighboring cosmoses"; the republican axiom of Myriad Races opposes "multi-cosmos master-slaving"; the collective ascension axiom opposes "alone spanning various cosmoses"; the causal self-discipline axiom opposes "cross-cosmos disturbing beings"; the law-first axiom opposes "plot-style multiverse masters." Per Anti-Predation Core Treatise (Document 04) and Document 17, unfolding; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. Cross-Cosmos Essence: Swallowing vs. Connecting

The Predatory reaching the Multiverse Realm understands "crossing a single cosmos, connecting multiple cosmoses' origins" as "swallowing multiple cosmoses' origins to take private possession." Every step he takes, one independent cosmos is merged into his body — various cosmoses become fewer and fewer, he alone becomes bigger and bigger. This is the cosmos-cluster-scale form of zero-sum predation.

The Symbiotic reaching the Multiverse Realm understands "connecting multiple cosmoses" as "establishing compatible interfaces between multiple independent cosmoses' origins." Every step he takes, various cosmoses remain independent and autonomous, only gaining an interoperable road between them — various cosmoses become more and more connected, everyone together becomes bigger and bigger. This is the cosmos-cluster-scale form of positive-sum symbiosis.

One judgment criterion: after a multiverse strong one takes a step, has the total number of various cosmoses decreased or increased? Decreased is swallowing, increased is connecting.

## III. Various Cosmoses' Beings: Merged vs. Autonomous Interconnection

The Predatory's various cosmoses' beings, after being merged into one body, lose autonomy — their cosmic laws are remodeled to serve that one multiverse master, their fates are governed by one consciousness.

The Symbiotic's various cosmoses' beings, each living autonomously in their own cosmos, interoperate and share knowledge via interfaces, but are not governed by any external strong one.

## IV. Law Authority and Supervision: Authoritarian vs. Multi-Dimensional Trials

The Predatory remodels various cosmoses' laws by own will, no one can control him, maintaining rule by protagonist uniqueness.

The Symbiotic's multiverse-scale law authority is only used to coordinate interfaces, constrained by Alaya Network-supervised multi-dimensional trials — any one cosmos' loss means overall failure. This is precisely the multi-cosmos version of protagonist uniqueness dependence dropping from "extremely high" to "extremely low."

## V. Ending: Dying in Internal Strife vs. Perpetual Interconnection

The Predatory multiverse strong one swallows to the end, various cosmoses all merged, no increments left to swallow, ultimately collapsing in his own causal backlash or residual rebellion; the various cosmoses he swallowed are damaged along with him.

After the Symbiotic multiverse strong one builds the cross-cosmos interconnection network, various cosmoses collectively ascend via the network; he himself continues to promote toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm (from Entry 171), and the network runs perpetually after him.

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: the Predatory wastes multi-cosmos power on "maintaining one person swallowing various cosmoses"; the Symbiotic converts it into network momentum for various cosmoses' collective ascension
- **Anti-predation**: this entry's thesis — swallowing and connecting, one minus one plus, two multiverse paths
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style multiverse strong ones devour various cosmoses; Heng Yuan Zhou multiverse strong ones only act as connection hubs
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: symbiotic interconnection defaults various cosmoses' Myriad Races beings autonomous; the predatory only holds one multiverse master
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: symbiotic cross-cosmos interfaces and interconnection experience are network-shared; the predatory's "swallowing list" is never open

A deeper divergence must be pointed out: the predatory's multiverse gets colder the more it swallows — merged cosmoses lose autonomy, between them only remains the relationship of tribute to one master; the symbiotic's multiverse gets warmer the more it connects — various cosmoses each autonomous, via interoperable roads exchange knowledge, ecology, and goodwill. A cold network only falls apart when the master weakens; a warm network becomes more stable overall when every node strengthens. The lifespan difference between the two networks is precisely the civilizational sustainability difference.

## Conclusion

The Multiverse Realm is the examination hall to the ultimate question "what is cross-cosmos power used for." Swallowing various cosmoses seemingly wins the entire cosmos cluster, actually loses Myriad Races; connecting various cosmoses seemingly only built a few roads, actually wins ten thousand generations of symbiosis. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is written in the multi-dimensional trials: only the hand willing to connect rather than swallow is worthy of holding multiple cosmoses' origins.
