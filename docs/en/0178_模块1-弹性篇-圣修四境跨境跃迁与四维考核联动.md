## II. How the Four-Dimensional Assessment Accumulates with Cross-Boundary Ascension

Per the Plane Transcendent Standard (Document 17), the four-dimensional assessment is not calculated once at the Origin Realm, but accumulates at every leap of the sacred-cultivation four realms:

- **Power dimension (40%)**: Boundary-Breaking accumulates cross-plane operation power, World-Creation accumulates world design power, Origin accumulates cosmic origin mobilization power, Multiverse accumulates multi-cosmos governance power — every leap adds a brick to "power dimension meeting standard"
- **Mind dimension (25%)**: Boundary-Breaking tests "crossing boundaries not harvesting," World-Creation tests "creating not authoritarian," Origin tests "holding power not authoritatively deciding," Multiverse tests "connecting not swallowing" — every leap is an additional mind exam
- **Contribution dimension (20%)**: Boundary-Breaking builds roads, World-Creation builds public world fields, Origin does law optimization, Multiverse builds cross-cosmos networks — every leap accumulates civilizational-level contribution records
- **Responsibility dimension (15%)**: Boundary-Breaking bears plane transportation responsibility, World-Creation bears world-guarding responsibility, Origin bears 30% public obligation, Multiverse bears cross-cosmos hub responsibility — every leap increases public responsibility

In other words: **the Plane Transcendent four-dimensional assessment is essentially the final settlement of accumulated values from every leap of the sacred-cultivation four realms**. A person discovering at the Origin Realm that mind or responsibility is not met has root causes already planted in the Boundary-Breaking and World-Creation leaps.
