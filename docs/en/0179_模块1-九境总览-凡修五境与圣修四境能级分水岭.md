# Entry 179 | Nine-Realms Overview: The Energy-Level Watershed Between Five Mortal-Cultivation Realms and Four Sacred-Cultivation Realms

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 00, 10

## Introduction: One watershed separates two evolutionary worldviews

Heng Yuan Zhou's unified realm classification has nine realms: Sense Origin, Condensed Origin, Transform Origin, Law Origin, Dao Origin, Dao Integration, Boundary-Breaking, World-Creation, Origin. The first five are the "five mortal-cultivation realms," the last four are the "four sacred-cultivation realms." This dividing line is not an artificial rank label, but a watershed where energy-level essence, responsibility volume, and karmic cost all simultaneously leap. Understanding this watershed is understanding why the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization both makes "paths for all" reach the five mortal-cultivation realms, and guards the sacred-cultivation four realms' entrance with four-dimensional assessment.

The six top-level axioms take layered effect here: the anti-predation axiom requires whether mortal or sacred, energy always comes from creation and circulation not devouring; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the five-realm path to be open to 99% of Myriad Races; the collective ascension axiom requires sacred-cultivation strong ones not to ascend alone, but uplift mortal-cultivation beings forward together; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the closer to origin, the stricter the filing and restraint of cross-plane operations; the law-first axiom ensures the nine-realm definitions never rewrite, never inflate with plot; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry overviews the watershed's energy-level structure, providing a unified coordinate for subsequent detailed entries.

## I. Five Mortal-Cultivation Realms: "Evolutionary Basic Education" Accessible to All

The five mortal-cultivation realms (Sense Origin, Condensed Origin, Transform Origin, Law Origin, Dao Origin) share the feature: cultivators still mainly operate in material planes and energy layers, energy comes from environmental circulation and anchor network supply, the individual's manipulation of laws is "understanding and using" not "rewriting and creating."

| Realm | Core Lesson | Energy Scale | Typical Social Role |
|-------|-------------|--------------|---------------------|
| Sense Origin Realm | Perceive energy and law aura | Trace infiltration | Beginner |
| Condensed Origin Realm | Condense energy, establish internal circulation | Basic reserve | Junior cultivator |
| Transform Origin Realm | Energy purification and transformation | Intermediate usable | Social backbone, grassroots mentor |
| Law Origin Realm | Energy and law initially merge, birth law power | Advanced combat power | Technical expert, mid-level management |
| Dao Origin Realm | Attain personal Dao, cross-plane consciousness perception | Grandmaster level | Civilization consultant, senior mentor |

The five mortal-cultivation realms' design goal is "compulsory education full coverage": any human male/female, Youth Supreme, Myriad Races being, relying on the 300 Origin Cultivation Systems and joint contracting resource allocation, should be able to reach Dao Origin Realm. This stage's bottleneck is mainly method and time, not birth and monopoly.

## II. Four Sacred-Cultivation Realms: Energy Level and Responsibility Simultaneously Leap

The four sacred-cultivation realms (Dao Integration, Boundary-Breaking, World-Creation, Origin) share the feature: the cultivator's way of existence itself begins to change the cosmos — Dao Integration ones rewrite local laws, Boundary-Breaking ones operate the plane membrane, World-Creation ones create complete worlds in isolated space, Origin ones participate in cosmic law fine-tuning.

| Realm | Core Leap | Energy Nature | Responsibility Volume |
|-------|-----------|---------------|------------------------|
| Dao Integration Realm | Personal Dao deeply merges with origin laws | Law domain forms | Grandmaster level, influencing civilization direction |
| Boundary-Breaking Realm | Anchor-free cross-plane, operating plane membrane | Plane level | Independently bearing plane-level tasks |
| World-Creation Realm | Creating complete worlds and law systems | World level | Creating cultivation resources and refuges |
| Origin Realm | Touching origin, participating in law fine-tuning | Cosmic level | Plane Transcendent threshold |

Why the watershed is an "energy-level watershed": errors in the five mortal-cultivation realms at most hurt oneself and the local area; one misjudgment in the four sacred-cultivation realms can affect an entire plane or even cross-plane causality. Therefore the four sacred-cultivation realms' entrance must be double-gated by anchor certification and Standard Committee assessment.

## III. The Watershed's Triple Criteria

The watershed is not a single threshold of some realm, but three criteria simultaneously crossed:

1. **Energy criterion**: three indicators — energy purity, law manipulation precision, consciousness evolution — simultaneously cross the Dao Origin→Dao Integration threshold, none dispensable.
2. **Karma criterion**: has the cultivator passed ethics review, confirming cross-plane operations observe first then limited interference, only relay knowledge not disturb causality.
3. **Contribution criterion**: does one have actual contribution records of giving back to Myriad Races, not merely personal combat power met.

These three criteria directly correspond to the Plane Transcendent four-dimensional assessment system (power 40 / mind 25 / contribution 20 / responsibility 15)'s front projection at the watershed.

## IV. Two Realm Extensions Beyond the Watershed

Above the nine realms, the setting also marks two extension realms pending terminology committee calibration: Multiverse Realm and Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm (alias "Hyper-Multiverse Balanced Anchor Realm"). They are the direction of continued ascension after the Origin Realm; this entry only gives coordinate hints: the Multiverse Realm points to law network nodes across multiverse scale, the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm points to the ultimate ascension form of Myriad Races co-building and whole-network sharing. Detailed deduction see Entry 214.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: layering the "accessible to all" five mortal-cultivation realms and "strictly assessed" four sacred-cultivation realms, avoiding low-level ones forced to bear high-level costs, and avoiding high-level ones being underused
- **Anti-predation**: at the watershed there is no "devour to break through" shortcut; predatory energy hits a "impure origin" hard bottleneck at Dao Origin Realm
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: in Tang-Luo-style gift systems, commoners' ceiling is locked at low ranks; Heng Yuan Zhou's five mortal-cultivation realms are fully free and open, the watershed only screens mind and contribution, not birth
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the five mortal-cultivation realms cover 99% of Myriad Races races, the four sacred-cultivation realms similarly reserve channels for plant spirits, planetary consciousness, mechanical tribes, etc., not human-exclusive
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: nine-realm definitions, certification standards, and methods are all public, no hoarding monopoly, infinitely expandable without collapse

## Conclusion

The five mortal-cultivation realms are Heng Yuan Zhou's "entry ticket" for all beings; the four sacred-cultivation realms are Heng Yuan Zhou's "responsibility bond" for strong ones. What this energy-level watershed separates is never high/low/noble/base, but "how much karmic cause you can bear." With the watershed explained clearly, the nine-realm system has the skeleton of infinitely expanding without collapse.

In the final analysis, the nine-realms overview · five mortal-cultivation and four sacred-cultivation energy-level watershed described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
