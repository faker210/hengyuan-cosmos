# Entry 180 | General Realm Promotion Law: Anchor Certification and Standard Committee Assessment Mechanism

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 49, 29

## Introduction: Promotion is not self-proclaimed, but network-verifiable

In predatory old systems, realm titles are strongmen self-proclaimed — smashing a mountain with one palm, calling oneself "some emperor." Heng Yuan Zhou abolishes this tradition, replacing it with "anchor certification + Standard Committee assessment" as the sole legitimate channel for all realm promotion. This mechanism's core conviction is: a realm is substantive mastery of cosmic laws, not an oppressive declaration over others; since it is substantive mastery, it must be measurable, assessable, publicizable, open to objection.

The six top-level axioms run throughout: the anti-predation axiom requires the promotion channel to have no "pay homage to enter the door" monopoly threshold; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires assessment standards to treat Myriad Races equally; the collective ascension axiom requires promoted strong ones to immediately enter the mentor and give-back chain; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the assessment itself not to disturb the candidate's karma; the law-first axiom requires assessment standards to be stable and reproducible, not temporarily changed for any individual case; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry dissects this realm-wide promotion mechanism.

## I. Four-Level Promotion Process

Per the Unified Cultivation Realm Classification (Document 14), a legitimate realm promotion must complete four steps:

1. **Anchor automatic monitoring**: after a cultivator accesses the anchor network, anchors continuously monitor four indicators — energy level, law understanding, consciousness intensity, symbiosis degree; meeting standard automatically prompts.
2. **Standard Committee assessment**: breakthroughs at the fourth realm (Law Origin Realm) and above require the Cultivation Standard Committee to organize formal assessment, including theoretical test, practical test, contribution evaluation three parts.
3. **Publicity and objection period**: after passing assessment, publicize for thirty days; any being may raise objections; if objections hold, reassess.
4. **Realm certificate issuance**: no objection after publicity, the anchor network issues a unified certificate, entered into the civilization database.

The first four mortal-cultivation realms (Sense Origin to Transform Origin) mainly use anchor automatic monitoring, assessment lighter; from Law Origin Realm officially enter Standard Committee assessment; Dao Origin Realm and above add ethics review; from Dao Integration Realm add plane-level responsibility interview; the Origin Realm enters Plane Transcendent four-dimensional assessment.

## II. Realm-Wide Structure of Assessment Content

| Realm Segment | Theoretical Test | Practical Test | Contribution Evaluation | Special Step |
|---------------|------------------|----------------|--------------------------|--------------|
| Sense–Condensed | Basic method identification | Perception stability | Community service hours | None |
| Transform–Law | Law principle deduction | Controlled sparring | Number of juniors mentored | Path compliance review |
| Dao–Dao Integration | Personal Dao systematized defense | Law domain actual test | Civilization-level contribution item | Ethics deep scan |
| Boundary-Breaking–World-Creation | Plane jurisprudence and causal filing | Isolated space operation | Plane task records | Causal self-discipline oath |
| Origin Realm | Cosmic law fine-tuning proposal | Cross-plane anchor-free operation | 20% Activation training results | All four-dimensional assessment passed |

The key of assessment is not "how strong in combat," but "how deep understanding, how solid contribution, how heavy responsibility." This forms a fundamental divergence from the predatory system's "he with the biggest fist levels up."

## III. Anti-Cheating and Anti-Monopoly Designs

The promotion mechanism fears two things most: cheating and monopoly. Symbiotic civilization's countermeasures include:

- **Whole-network traces**: the entire assessment process leaves traces in the Alaya Network, traceable and auditable, eliminating under-the-table operations.
- **Multi-person blind review**: Standard Committee committee members review blind from different locations, private contact between candidates and committee members is forbidden, avoiding door-paying bad habits.
- **Contribution verifiable**: contribution evaluation only recognizes output automatically recorded by the anchor network, not "verbally claiming to have helped someone."
- **Anti-monopoly clause**: no one may monopolize assessment slots under the guise of master-disciple, sect, or resource binding; methods are fully public at all levels.

## IV. Failure and Repair

Failing assessment does not mean permanent failure. The anchor network gives short-board diagnosis: insufficient perception precision, or empty contribution record, or mind test overstepping. Cultivators may re-apply after remedial study. The only irreparable hard injury is predatory records — any with evidence of devouring others' origin or disturbing others' causality, promotion channel is frozen by law; this is the causal self-discipline axiom's rigid projection in promotion institutions.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: replacing "famous teacher nods" with measurable standards, letting truly promising cultivators not be buried, and letting seniority-only drifters have nowhere to hide
- **Anti-predation**: promotion does not reward predatory battle records; predatory records directly freeze the channel, institutionally cutting "proving Dao by killing"
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style promotion relies on elders and holy grace endorsements, slots held by sects; Heng Yuan Zhou promotion relies on network-verifiable hard indicators, poor households and Myriad Races take the same exam
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: assessment language, somatosensory standards, and contribution criteria are adapted to the five-genealogy life forms, not forcing human bodies as the sole ruler
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: assessment syllabus, question bank, and past cases are all public, no hoarding; the law-first axiom guarantees standards are versioned, iterable, and never rewritten

## Conclusion

The promotion mechanism is symbiotic civilization's key link in institutionalizing "power returns to power, responsibility returns to responsibility." Anchor certification is responsible for "measuring accurately," the Standard Committee for "assessing fairly," publicity objection for "letting supervision in"; the three combined, realm titles return from oppressive names to verifiable growth milestones.
