# Entry 181 | Path D · Humanoid Artificial Dao-Rule Soul Rings: Realm-Wide Adaptation from Sense Origin to Origin

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 10, 51, 04

## Introduction: Remaking predatory soul rings into symbiotic Dao-rule interfaces

"Path D · Humanoid Artificial Dao-Rule Soul Rings" (hereinafter Path D) is a signature cultivation path custom-designed for the human race by Heng Yuan Zhou. Its prototype is the familiar old-system paradigm of "hunting soul beasts, obtaining soul rings," but symbiotic civilization flips this paradigm root and branch: soul rings no longer come from devouring soul beasts' origin, but are "artificially made" by the anchor network in an engineering manner — packaging a reusable fragment of cosmic origin law as a "Dao-rule interface" implantable into human energy circuits.

In other words, the old paradigm asks "which soul beast did you kill," Path D asks "which validated origin law fragment did you access, and what did you relay back to the symbiotic network with it." This flip itself is the embodiment of the six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom requires developing increments not consuming stock; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires soul beasts no longer be hunted, humans no longer locked by birth; the collective ascension axiom requires artificial Dao-rule soul rings' output to give back to the whole network; the causal self-discipline axiom requires every artificial law fragment to leave auditable traces; the law-first axiom requires Path D's interface specifications never rewritten by plot; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry sorts out Path D's realm-wide adaptation from Sense Origin Realm to Origin Realm.

## I. Path D's Engineering Essence

Path D is not a cultivation method made out of thin air, but the "interface packaging" of the 300 Origin Cultivation Systems on humans:

- **Legal source**: Dao-rule fragments are all extracted from cosmic origin laws and validated by the anchor network, not taken from any living being.
- **Manufacturing controllable**: each artificial Dao-rule soul ring is produced by the anchor workshop per standard parameters; purity, stability, compatibility all calibratable.
- **Detachable and replaceable**: unlike devouring-style soul rings "welded" to the host origin, artificial Dao-rule soul rings can be replaced losslessly with realm upgrades, avoiding one mistake for life.
- **Whole-network shared**: Path D's interface specifications are free and open, any race may borrow its engineering approach, not human hoarding.

Its true value lies in: letting a human youth of bottom birth under old rules, without any talent-myth blessing, also steadily rise along an engineering-validated path.

## III. Why "Human-Race" Exclusive Interface Design

The human race's traits (Document 51) are strong adaptability, good social collaboration, large evolutionary potential, but no single talent outstanding. Path D is precisely designed for this "all-around but not specialized" constitution:

- It uses artificial interfaces to supplement humans' dullness in law perception;
- Uses engineering stability to compensate humans' short boards in lifespan and energy accumulation;
- Uses network sharing to amplify humans' "good collaboration" trait into a systemic advantage.

In other words, Path D is not giving humans special treatment, but calibrating humans' average constitution to the fairest starting line in the entire cosmos.

## IV. Coordination with Multiplication Fruit Technology

Path D and Multiplication Fruit technology (hundredfold cultivation deduction amplification) are naturally complementary: artificial Dao-rule soul rings provide "the right law interfaces," the Multiplication Fruit provides "fast deduction efficiency." Superimposed, they give human cultivators, without predation or monopoly, the possibility of competing on the same stage as top-talent races. This is also one of the technical foundations for Path D representatives like Lin Hao to grow rapidly (see Entries 183, 184).

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: using engineering interfaces to realize humans' average potential, avoiding the waste of "ordinary talent means elimination"
- **Anti-predation**: soul ring source changed from "hunting soul beasts" to "artificial Dao rules," completely cutting violence as evolutionary path
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style soul rings must hunt soul beasts, slots monopolized by birth and sects; Path D soul rings are artificial, free, replaceable, commoner humans directly obtain realm-wide adaptation
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: although Path D is named after human interfaces, its engineering specifications are open to Myriad Races; other genealogies may replicate and adapt, no racial patents
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Dao-rule fragments, manufacturing processes, replacement standards are public at all levels, infinitely stackable with new realm interfaces without collapse

## Conclusion

Path D's deep significance is rewriting a cultivation paradigm once bloodstained into a clean, manufacturable, shareable engineering technology. It proves one thing: the cruelest link in the old system can precisely be transformed by symbiotic civilization into the most inclusive link.

In the final analysis, Path D · humanoid artificial Dao-rule soul rings realm-wide adaptation described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
