## Introduction: Two roads, short-term and long-term give opposite answers

The old system's predatory soul ring path advocates "hunting soul beasts, devouring origin, proving Dao by killing"; Path D · humanoid artificial Dao-rule soul rings advocates "engineering manufacturing, whole-network sharing, proving Dao by co-building." On the surface, the predatory path surges early, Path D climbs steadily, as if predatory is faster. But Anti-Predation Core Treatise (Document 04) already pointed out predatory evolution has an "efficiency ceiling." This entry compares the two roads segment by segment across the nine realms, seeing clearly the short-term dividend and long-term cost.

The six top-level axioms are the judging bench of this comparison table: the anti-predation axiom judges the predatory path inherently noncompliant; the republican axiom of Myriad Races judges the predatory path blocks the vast majority of beings' roads; the collective ascension axiom judges the predatory path's strong ones ultimately isolated; the causal self-discipline axiom judges the predatory path's late-stage karma entanglement; the law-first axiom judges the predatory path's combat power inflation must retcon and collapse; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. The Predatory Path's Four Efficiency Losses

The predatory evolution efficiency ceiling listed in Document 04 materializes on the soul ring path as four losses:

1. **Refining loss**: predated soul rings carry original-owner will residual traces; cultivators must spend massive effort purifying, over 70% of energy spent on "washing clean."
2. **Resistance loss**: hunting triggers soul beast tribes and victim associates' resistance, external security costs rise exponentially with realm.
3. **Degeneration loss**: long-term dependence on devouring degrades one's own origin evolution ability; once nothing to devour, immediately stagnates.
4. **Capping loss**: the closer to origin, the harder the "impure origin" bottleneck, over 90% of predatory strong ones stop lifetime around Dao Integration.

Path D pre-zeros all four losses: Dao-rule rings are inherently pure, no resistance needed, not dependent on external things, no bloodline ceiling.

## III. Social-Dimension Efficiency Difference

Beyond energy-level efficiency, there is also a "social account":

- **Trainable headcount**: predatory path one soul ring raises one person, at the cost of killing one soul beast; Path D one Dao-rule ring can be replicated and mass-produced, Myriad Races walk together.
- **Failure cost**: predatory path killing wrong soul beast or devouring wrong attribute is lifelong hidden danger; Path D Dao-rule rings can be replaced losslessly, trial-and-error cost extremely low.
- **Civilizational output**: predatory path strong ones ultimately head to authoritarianism and closure; Path D strong ones give back to the whole network per joint contracting, energy level grows the more used.

Adding these three accounts, the predatory path is "zero-sum pumping," Path D is "positive-sum expansion."
