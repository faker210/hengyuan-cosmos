# Entry 183 | Lin Hao Cultivation Path Full-Mirror Tracking: From Old-Rules Victim to Rules Reconstructor

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 181, 11, 04

## Introduction: A person crushed by old rules, how he personally rewrites the rules

Lin Hao is the most representative practitioner in the Path D · humanoid artificial Dao-rule soul ring system. His specialness is not extraordinary talent, but that he started from old rules — in that predatory system of hunting soul beasts, devouring resources, and judging by birth, he was the one crushed: no sect background, no ring-bestowment grace, no bloodline myth; under old rules he should have stopped lifetime at low mortal-cultivation ranks, reduced to a stepping stone beneath civilization's ladder.

Precisely because of this, when he accessed the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's Path D, his growth was not "genius cheating," but "an old-rules victim using new rules to walk back the lost road step by step." This trajectory has specimen significance for Myriad Races. The six top-level axioms take effect one by one in him: the anti-predation axiom made him refuse to retread the devouring old road; the republican axiom of Myriad Races made him certain birth should not decide ceilings; the collective ascension axiom made him ultimately relay experience back to the whole network; the causal self-discipline axiom made him observe first then interfere in cross-plane operations; the law-first axiom made his path always deduce self-consistently within the law network, not plot-style reversal; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry uses the nine realms as a mirror to track Lin Hao's realm-wide growth.

## I. First Half: Victim Stage Under Old Rules

Before contacting Path D, Lin Hao experienced the old system's standard predicament:

- **Perception start neglected**: no guidance, long-term misjudgment of energy attributes, taking many detours.
- **Resources monopolized**: low-rank resources tilted toward sect and aristocratic children, he was forced to grope at lowest cost.
- **Shortcut temptation moment**: a predatory remnant once sold him a "fast devour-ring" method, he nearly slipped.

This stage his true realm long lingered around Condensed Origin Realm, seemingly mediocre, actually accumulating firsthand cognition of "the rules' evil" — this cognition later became his first-hand experience understanding symbiotic laws.

## II. Middle Section: Realm-Wide Climb After Accessing Path D

His climb speed is not a miracle, but the inevitable result of "correct path × Multiplication Fruit × first-half lessons" superimposed.

## III. Second Half: From Beneficiary to Rules Reconstructor

Lin Hao's divergence from ordinary geniuses is that after crossing the watershed he did not stop at personal ascension:

- **Writing the path into textbooks**: organizing his own detours into Path D standard lesson plans, for latecomers to avoid mines.
- **Giving back to the bottom**: actively taking mentor duties, specifically receiving bottom human youth of similar birth to him.
- **Pushing interface expansion**: participating in Path D's adaptation engineering for replication by other genealogies, making the path from "human interface" into "Myriad Races-borrowable paradigm."

He completed the identity transformation from "old-rules victim" to "new-rules co-builder" — this is precisely the strong-one posture the collective ascension axiom requires.

## IV. Specimen Significance

Lin Hao's trajectory proves the thing Heng Yuan Zhou most wants to prove: an ordinary person with no opportunity under old rules, within the symbiotic system, can walk the entire nine realms, ultimately becoming a rules reconstructor. His existence itself is the strongest refutation of "the strong are fated," "bloodline theory," and "gift-style civilization."
