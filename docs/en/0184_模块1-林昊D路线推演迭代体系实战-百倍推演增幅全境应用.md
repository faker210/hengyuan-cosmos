## I. The Deduction Iteration System's Underlying Actions

Per Deduction Iteration System (Document 11), its closed loop is:

1. **Modeling**: abstracting the current cultivation problem into an operable law model.
2. **Hundredfold deduction**: using the Multiplication Fruit to speed consciousness computation a hundredfold, in the consciousness domain parallel-testing many paths.
3. **Validation**: each path is cheaply trialed in isolated space or simulated environment.
4. **Iteration**: correcting the model per feedback, converging to the optimal solution.

Lin Hao's characteristic is using this closed loop extremely "hard" — others deduce ten times, he deduces a thousand, until all hidden costs are exposed.

## III. Why It Is "Realm-Wide" Application, Not Low-Rank Toy

Hundredfold deduction amplification is often misunderstood as a "low-rank accelerator." Lin Hao's practice proves it is precisely the high-rank core:

- The lower the rank, deduction solves "taking fewer detours";
- The higher the rank, deduction solves "not making disasters" — above Dao Integration, one law misjudgment can destroy a plane; only after acting out consequences a hundred times in the consciousness domain can one dare act.

This also explains why Multiplication Fruit technology is "efficiency amplification" not "energy creation" (Document 16): it does not create power for cultivators, but saves them all invalid trial-and-error, using the same energy on the blade.
