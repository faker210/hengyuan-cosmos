# Entry 189 | Myriad Races Per-Group Realm Adaptation Overview: Carbon/Energy/Mechanical/Consciousness/Hybrid Five Genealogies

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 50, 14, 103

## Introduction: The same nine-realm ruler, measuring ten thousand lives across five genealogies

The Unified Cultivation Realm Classification (Document 14) uses the same ruler for all cultivators, but the same ruler does not mean the same usage. Myriad Races are divided by life essence into five genealogies — carbon-based material races, energy condensation races, mechanical construction races, consciousness information races, hybrid variation races — each genealogy's nine-realm adaptation method, bottleneck location, and breakthrough features are entirely different. This entry gives a realm-wide overview, answering a fundamental question: how does the same nine-realm system let a stone-made life, a flame-made life, and an information-made life all reach Origin?

The six top-level axioms are this overview's legitimacy source: the anti-predation axiom requires genealogy adaptation to be "strong ones teaching weak ones how to be human" no more, but equal mutual reference; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the five genealogies 99% full coverage, no genealogy excluded; the collective ascension axiom requires each genealogy's strong ones to uplift each other not go alone; the causal self-discipline axiom requires cross-genealogy interference also observe first then limited interference; the law-first axiom requires adaptation plans versioned, self-consistent, never retconned; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. Core Logic of Genealogy Adaptation: Not Cutting Feet to Fit Shoes

The mistake old systems often made was imposing human-body cultivation paradigms on other races — requiring stone-made mechanical races to "sit in meditation and circulate breath," requiring information-made consciousness races to "refine essence into qi," resulting either in adaptation failure or being forced to mutate into half-human monsters. Heng Yuan Zhou's principle is **"emphasize essence over names"**: no matter what a life is composed of, as long as its essence meets that realm's four-dimensional standards (law understanding depth, energy manipulation precision, consciousness evolution degree, symbiotic network contribution), it is recognized as that realm.

Thus the mechanical race's "perception" is data sampling, the consciousness race's "condensation" is information aggregation, the plant spirit's "breakthrough" is root network expansion — different names, equivalent essence.

## III. Each Genealogy's Origin Realm Channel

The most critical question: can all five genealogies reach Origin Realm and beyond? The answer is yes, paths differ:

- **Carbon-based**: body high-dimensionalization, gradually shedding material shackles;
- **Energy condensation**: from free condensation to law-level stable condensation;
- **Mechanical construction**: from data iteration to origin coding (see Entry 194);
- **Consciousness information**: from information aggregation to law consciousness (see Entry 195);
- **Hybrid variation**: multi-genealogy fusion, both risks and advantages (see Entry 196).

## IV. Cross-Genealogy Resonance Bonus

Genealogies are not isolated islands. Cross-genealogy supplementary cultivation of homologous Three-Basic can produce perspectives carbon-based bodies alone find hard to obtain: mechanical race's precise computation + human race's social collaboration + consciousness race's cross-layer perception, combined realm stability often higher than single genealogy. This is precisely the cultivation-science basis for Myriad Races republicanism's "1+1>2."
