# Entry 191 | Alien Beast Cultivation Path Realm-Wide: From Instinct to Law Resonance

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 50, 104, 19

## Introduction: From "hunted soul beast" to "respected fellow cultivator"

Alien beasts are the most tragic group in old systems: they cultivated for thousands of years, yet were defined as "walking soul rings"; humans hunted them, stripped their origin to trade for their own realms. Heng Yuan Zhou wipes out this thousand-year grievance with the anti-predation axiom — alien beasts (beast tribe, scale tribe, feather tribe, and all walking-beast-form sentient life) are no longer materials, but cultivators explicitly protected by the republican axiom of Myriad Races. Their realm-wide path's core is **from primitive instinct toward conscious law resonance**.

The six top-level axioms weigh extra heavily on alien beasts: the anti-predation axiom directly ends the old habit of hunting alien beasts for rings; the republican axiom of Myriad Races lists alien beasts as fellow cultivators with humans and plant spirits; the collective ascension axiom requires powerful alien beasts to become plane guardians not mountain overlords; the causal self-discipline axiom requires humanity's historical hunting of alien beasts to be systematically settled and reconciled; the law-first axiom requires alien beasts' awakening and Dao-attainment process to be self-consistent, not via plot jumps of "suddenly speaking"; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Alien Beast Life Traits

Alien beasts differ from both humans and plant spirits:

- **Strong instinct**: bodily instinct and sensory intuition far exceed ordinary people; before Transform Origin, progress relies on "comprehension" not "training."
- **Wide sentience divergence**: from ignorant beast to awakened being spans a huge range, cultivation starting points vary.
- **Strong territorial sense**: naturally inclined to occupy a territory, needs guidance toward guarding not predation.

Thus the first gate of alien beast cultivation is not becoming strong, but **awakening wisdom** — sublimating chaotic instinct step by step into clear consciousness.

## III. From Instinct to Law Resonance: The Key Turning Point

Alien beasts' Dao attainment path is opposite to humans': humans "understand laws → manipulate laws," alien beasts "extreme use of instinct → instinct resonates with laws." They do not need to decompose laws into concepts as humans do, but polish millions of years of hunting, nesting, migration instinct to the same frequency as cosmic laws; every move is in accord with the Dao. This "wildness Dao" after Dao Origin Realm becomes specialization in certain law domains — for example, wind-tribe alien beasts' understanding of speed law is often purer than human spells.

## IV. Reconciliation Mechanism for Old Hunting Karma

For historical hunting old accounts, symbiotic civilization does not do nihilistic "let bygones be bygones," but sets reconciliation procedures:

- **Karmic settlement**: residual will of hunted races is identified and recorded in the Alaya Network;
- **Ecological compensation**: high-hunting-frequency planes are sentenced by the Symbiosis Court to ecological restoration;
- **New contract**: alien beasts sign new territorial contracts with humans and plant spirits; mountains and forests are guarded by alien beasts, cities managed by humans, non-aggression.

This is the causal self-discipline axiom's practice at the ethnic-group level.

## V. High-Rank Roles of Alien Beast Strong Ones

Alien beast strong ones above Dao Integration often serve as plane ecological guardians and disaster rescuers — their intuition of natural imbalance is a living early warning beyond mechanical prediction. This role is not "being worshipped," but a natural extension of their instinct-law resonance.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: re-evaluating alien beasts' thousand-year instinct accumulation from "materials to be stripped" into high-rank law resonance resources
- **Anti-predation**: hunting alien beasts for rings is designated historical original sin and current felony, completely ended
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style system soul beasts are the protagonist's nourishment, no matter how strong alien beasts are they are experience packs; in Heng Yuan Zhou alien beasts are fellow cultivators, reaching Origin
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: beast tribe, scale tribe (including dragon branches), feather tribe, insect tribe, and all walking-beast/flying-insect sentient forms have realm-wide channels
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: alien beast wisdom-awakening textbooks, instinct resonance methods, and ethnic reconciliation contracts are all public

## Supplementary Analysis: Alien Beast Instinct-to-Law-Resonance Transformation

Alien beasts' promotion from instinct-driven to law-resonant centers on instinct energy being recoded into law language. The Heng Yuan Zhou system does not tame alien beasts' wildness, but provides an "instinct translator" via the Alaya Network, letting fierce strength find its proper place at the law level. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization rejects old systems' predatory logic of treating alien beasts as soul ring resources; the universal equal infinite law ensures alien beasts and humans share evolutionary qualification. The law-first axiom requires alien beasts' resonance path to match their instinct endowment, not forcedly reformed. Once this path is walked through, the alien beast genealogy becomes the most combat-capable guardians in Myriad Races republicanism.

## Conclusion

Alien beasts' realm-wide path is Heng Yuan Zhou's loudest rehabilitation to the old world: you are not soul rings, you are fellow cultivators. The road from instinct to law resonance they walked for millions of years, now finally reaches the sunlight.
