## I. Planetary Consciousness Awakening Sequence

Planetary consciousness does not emerge overnight; its awakening is staged:

1. **Star-core energy storage**: internal planetary energy naturally condenses through billions of years of geological activity.
2. **Ecological self-circulation**: surface life activity forms a stable energy cycle; the star core receives "feedback."
3. **First flicker of self-consciousness**: the star core produces vague awareness of "its own existence."
4. **Formal awakening**: planetary consciousness can converse with surface cultivators via anchor network communication.
