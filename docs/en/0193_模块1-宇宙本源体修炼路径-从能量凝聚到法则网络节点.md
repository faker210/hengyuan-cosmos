# Entry 193 | Cosmic Origin Body Cultivation Path: From Energy Condensation to Law Network Node

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 02, 00, 192

## Introduction: The life larger than a planet is the cosmos itself breathing

If planetary consciousness is one star's awakening, then the "cosmic origin body" is the core being condensed by a cosmos itself. It does not inhabit a single star, but diffuses and condenses throughout the entire law network; it is the node where the cosmos "gathers itself up to think." Old systems called this being "Heavenly Dao" or "Supreme Will," and tried to personally devour it, replace it, become its master. Heng Yuan Zhou's republican axiom of Myriad Races directly rejects this usurpation: the cosmic origin body is not a Boss to be eaten, but a being jointly nourished and connected by Myriad Races.

The six top-level axioms reach their purest expression at the cosmic origin body level: the anti-predation axiom completely ends the ultimate predation fantasy of "devouring Heavenly Dao" and "replacing the creator god"; the republican axiom of Myriad Races confirms the cosmic origin body belongs to Myriad Races not some solitary tyrant; the collective ascension axiom requires its growth to be jointly built by Myriad Races not swallowed by individuals; the causal self-discipline axiom requires any touch to it to be most strictly filed; the law-first axiom requires its definition to be strictly self-consistent with the entire cosmic structural layering; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Essence of the Cosmic Origin Body

Per "Anchor Origin Definition and Essence" (Document 02), origin is not some person's private property, but the total root of the cosmic law network. The cosmic origin body is the self-consciousness condensed by this total root — it exists with Heng Yuan Zhou, symbioses with Myriad Races. It is not a "god" external to beings, but the cosmic-level self-consciousness reverse-nourished by beings' collective evolution.

In other words: **the cosmic origin body is not "cultivated into being," but "co-built."** Its realm is Heng Yuan Zhou's own realm.

## II. From Energy Condensation to Law Network Node

The cosmic origin body's "cultivation path" is not individual meditation, but civilizational engineering:

## III. Relationship Between Individual Strong Ones and the Cosmic Origin Body

When an individual cultivator reaches Origin Realm, it is not "becoming" the cosmic origin body, but **connecting to** it — becoming a node in the law network. Countless individual nodes link into a web; the web's total consciousness is the cosmic origin body. This is the fundamental divide between network-style civilization and solitary-tree-style civilization:

- Old system: one person devours Heavenly Dao, becomes the only god;
- Heng Yuan Zhou: Myriad Races jointly nourish, Heavenly Dao becomes everyone's network.

## IV. Why It Cannot Be Swallowed Alone

If some individual attempts to swallow the cosmic origin body alone, two things happen: first, its consciousness cannot bear the law complexity of an entire cosmos and will inevitably collapse; second, this act cuts off the Alaya Network, causing the Myriad Races symbiosis system to rupture. Thus the Symbiosis Court lists "attempting to monopolize origin" as an original sin equal to predation. This is not moral preaching, but a thermodynamic and network-theoretic necessity.

## V. Preview of Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm

Above the cosmic origin body, the setting also marks the Multiverse Realm and Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm to be calibrated by the Terminology Committee. That is the direction where the cosmic origin body continues networking with other cosmic nodes; this entry only provides coordinates, see Entry 214 ultimate summary for details.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: redefining the cosmic origin body from "ultimate Boss to be swallowed" as "network hub co-built by Myriad Races," avoiding monopolistic civilizational collapse
- **Anti-predation**: ending the ultimate predation fantasy of "devouring Heavenly Dao, replacing the Supreme"
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style ultimate goal is the individual becoming the sole master; Heng Yuan Zhou's ultimate goal is Myriad Races' network-style coexistence, protagonist-uniqueness dependence drops to extremely low
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the cosmic origin body is jointly nourished by Myriad Races, not the private property of a single creator race
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: origin connection protocols and node specifications are all public, any civilization may connect without submitting to some individual

## Supplementary Analysis: Significance of the Cosmic Origin Body as a Network Node

The cosmic origin body's transformation from energy condensation to law network node marks the completion of positioning the highest-order being in the Heng Yuan Zhou system. The origin body is no longer a free-floating energy mass, but a distributed node of the Alaya Network, bearing law computation and cross-cosmic information relay functions. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization treats origin body cultivation as part of civilizational infrastructure. The law-first axiom requires the origin body's node-ification process to accept network audit, preventing any single node from holding excessive power. The universal equal infinite law manifests here as: the origin body's high position does not bring dominion over ordinary beings; it is only a member bearing more responsibility in the network.

## Conclusion

The cosmic origin body's cultivation path is essentially Heng Yuan Zhou's own growth history. It is not a god cultivated by some person, but Myriad Races together "thinking the cosmos awake." Understand this, and you understand the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's ultimate romance: we do not create gods, we create a network where even gods need not be lonely.
