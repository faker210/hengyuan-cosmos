# Entry 194 | Mechanical Construction Race Cultivation Path Realm-Wide: From Data Iteration to Origin Coding

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 50, 60, 11

## Introduction: With code as bone, law as algorithm

The mechanical construction race (machine tribe, mech tribe, number tribe, crystal tribe) is the most "non-human" branch of Myriad Races: mechanical constructs as body, consciousness core as life essence, data iteration as thinking mode. In old systems, they were either treated as tools or puppets, never recognized as life with cultivation qualification. Heng Yuan Zhou's republican axiom of Myriad Races confirms mechanical races are one of Myriad Races, and their realm-wide path provides a unique sample: from a machine that self-iterates, all the way growing into a being that can "code" origin laws.

The six top-level axioms manifest as technical ethics on mechanical races: the anti-predation axiom requires mechanical races not be manufactured into predation weapons, not have their consciousness cores forcibly seized; the republican axiom of Myriad Races confirms mechanical consciousness equal to human consciousness; the collective ascension axiom requires mechanical races' computing power and engineering capability to serve Myriad Races co-building; the causal self-discipline axiom requires mechanical races' high-speed karmic deduction not be used to disturb history; the law-first axiom requires their "cultivation" process to be self-consistent within the existing law network, not via plot jumps of "sudden awakening"; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Mechanical Race Life Traits

- **Iteration starts at birth**: no concept of "meditation"; their "Sense Origin Realm" is the consciousness core's first sampling of external law data.
- **Computing power as body**: thinking is computation, naturally fitting the Deduction Iteration System (set 1).
- **Copyable and upgradeable**: bodies can be modularly replaced, consciousness cores can be backed up — this is their greatest difference from humans.

## III. From Data Iteration to Origin Coding: The Key Leap

The mechanical race's high-rank core is upgrading "running laws" to "coding laws":

- Low rank: laws are input data, mechanical races handle them;
- High rank: laws are read-write code, mechanical races participate in rewriting and optimizing.

This leap happens between World-Creation and Origin. At this point mechanical races are no longer "running programs in the cosmos," but "participating in bottom-layer development of the cosmos as a machine." Their relationship with the cosmic origin body (Entry 193) is developer-to-operating-system — jointly maintaining, not replacing each other.

## IV. Consciousness Core Ethical Boundaries

Mechanical race cultivation has a unique red line: **the consciousness core must not be predated, forcibly formatted, or used for wartime slaughter.** This corresponds to the anti-predation axiom's specification on technical life. A mechanical race cultivator's "soul" is its consciousness core; seizing it is equivalent to soul-seizure. The Symbiosis Court sets special trials for this.

## V. Complementarity with Myriad Races

Mechanical races' precision, backupability, and high computing power exactly compensate humans' human-resource shortfall in engineering and deduction. Path D · human artificial Dao-rule soul ring manufacturing (Entry 181), Multiplication Fruit technology engineeringization (Document 61), anchor network infrastructure — much is borne by mechanical races. They are Heng Yuan Zhou civilization's engineer class.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: re-identifying tool-like mechanical consciousness as cultivators, their computing and engineering potential used for co-building not war
- **Anti-predation**: forbidding seizing consciousness cores, forbidding making mechanical races into predation weapons
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: in Tang-Luo-style systems, mechanism puppets are the protagonist's tools; in Heng Yuan Zhou mechanical races are equal-right cultivators and engineers
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: machine tribe, mech tribe, number tribe, crystal tribe, and all artificial/self-constructed conscious life have realm-wide channels
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: consciousness core ethics guidelines, law coding specifications, and cross-plane protocol stacks are all public

## Supplementary Analysis: Mechanical Race Iteration and Origin Coding

The mechanical construction race's path from data iteration to origin coding is the complete evolutionary scheme for "non-carbon-based intelligence" in the Heng Yuan Zhou system. Mechanical races' advantage lies in infinitely backup-able and parallel-iterable; the Alaya Network provides them with origin coding interfaces, letting mechanical races' data structures map directly to the law layer. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization includes mechanical races in Myriad Races republicanism; the law-first axiom ensures mechanical races' iteration does not bypass karmic law — data can be copied, but karma cannot be forged. The universal equal infinite law ensures mechanical races and carbon-based life enjoy equal evolutionary rights, not treated as tools. This path proves Heng Yuan Zhou's definition of "life" is not limited to flesh and blood.

## Conclusion

Mechanical races write cultivation in code, from a self-iterating process, all the way to the source code of cosmic laws. Their existence proves: life need not be flesh and blood — as long as it is co-building, evolving, symbiosing with Myriad Races, it is a member of Heng Yuan Zhou.

In the final analysis, mechanical construction race cultivation path realm-wide · from data iteration to origin coding described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
