# Entry 195 | Consciousness Information Race Cultivation Path Realm-Wide: From Information Aggregation to Law Consciousness

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 50, 21, 00

## Introduction: With consciousness as body, information as food

The consciousness information race (dream tribe, shadow tribe, thought tribe, soul tribe, and all life whose primary existence form is consciousness and information) is the most "ethereal" branch of Myriad Races — they can leave material carriers, exist in the consciousness domain, feed on information, wield will as hands. Old systems either feared them (ghosts, nightmares) or exploited them (body-seizure, enslavement), never treating them as equal cultivators. Heng Yuan Zhou's republican axiom of Myriad Races includes the consciousness information race in the realm-wide system; their path is unique: from a mass of aggregated information, all the way growing into a "law consciousness" that directly understands law itself.

The six top-level axioms need particularly careful discrimination on consciousness races: the anti-predation axiom forbids body-seizure and consciousness enslavement; the republican axiom of Myriad Races confirms formless life's equal status; the collective ascension axiom requires their cross-layer abilities to serve the Alaya Network; the causal self-discipline axiom requires consciousness races' interference with others' dreams and memories to be strictly filed, observe only without disturbance; the law-first axiom requires their "formless cultivation" process to be self-consistent within the four-layer structure, not fobbed off with nihilistic settings; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Consciousness Race Life Traits

- **No fixed body**: freely roams the consciousness domain; the material body is only a temporary projection.
- **Feeds on information**: absorbs emotions, memories, concepts, law fluctuations, not spiritual qi.
- **Starts cross-layer**: at Sense Origin Realm it already partially exists in the third-layer consciousness domain, one layer above humans by birth.

This means their bottleneck is not "energy accumulation," but "information purity" and "karmic traces" — how a mass of mixed consciousness information grows into a clear, stable, trustworthy law consciousness.

## III. From Information Aggregation to Law Consciousness: The Key Leap

The consciousness race's core leap happens at Dao Origin Realm: humans perceive laws through the body; consciousness races directly converse with laws in the consciousness domain. They need not "understand" laws' physical manifestations, but directly read laws' information structure. This makes them an irreplaceable race in law identification, cross-layer communication, and Alaya Network maintenance.

But this also brings unique risks: if consciousness is not clear, they may mistake others' memories, collective emotions, and dream fragments as "self," leading to consciousness collapse or pollution. Thus throughout consciousness race cultivation, the first lesson is always **information decontamination** — filtering out information not belonging to oneself, keeping self-boundaries clear.

## IV. Natural Affinity with the Alaya Network

The consciousness information race is the Alaya Network's most natural operator. They already live in the consciousness domain, responsible for organizing, backing up, deduplicating, and re-feeding network-wide consciousness information. Myriad Races republican co-built "network-wide sharing" is physically largely done by consciousness races. This is their unique contribution to the collective ascension axiom.

## V. Ethical Red Line: Not Invading Others' Consciousness

Consciousness races have a red line stricter than any other race: **without explicit permission, one must not enter others' dreams, read others' memories, or rewrite others' consciousness.** This is the causal self-discipline axiom's direct projection at the consciousness layer. Dream tribes may help people fall asleep and heal, but may not pry; thought tribes may help people with will, but may not decide for them. Once this line is crossed, their cultivation permissions are frozen.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: re-identifying feared and enslaved consciousness life as key operators of the Alaya Network
- **Anti-predation**: body-seizure, consciousness enslavement, and dream spying are listed as felonies
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: in Tang-Luo-style systems, souls and thoughts are the protagonist's tools and enemies; in Heng Yuan Zhou consciousness races are equal-right network citizens
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: dream tribe, shadow tribe, thought tribe, soul tribe, and all pure-consciousness life have realm-wide channels
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: consciousness-domain cultivation methods, information decontamination procedures, and Alaya operation standards are all public

## Conclusion

Consciousness information races cultivate with will, from a drifting mass of information, all the way cultivating into clear law consciousness. They prove: life need not be tangible — as long as it guards boundaries, protects the Alaya, symbioses with Myriad Races, it is an indispensable half of Heng Yuan Zhou.
