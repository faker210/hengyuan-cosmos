# Entry 197 | Predatory Faction Realm-Wide Corruption Trajectory: From Sense-Origin Shortcut to Origin Backlash

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 04, 19, 199

## Introduction: The end of shortcuts is backlash

The Predatory Faction were not villains from the start. The vast majority of predatory cultivators slid into the abyss step by step, starting from one "harmless shortcut" at Sense Origin Realm. This entry does not caricature the Predatory Faction as "born bad," but traces their realm-wide corruption trajectory — what was the temptation at each step, what was the cost, and finally how they are backlashed by their own origin at Origin Realm. This is a negative textbook, and also the best early-warning system.

The six top-level axioms are this trajectory diagram's demon-revealing mirror: the anti-predation axiom deems shortcuts noncompliant from the first step; the republican axiom of Myriad Races reveals every step of predation steps on some being's path; the collective ascension axiom foretells the Predatory Faction will eventually be isolated; the causal self-discipline axiom records every karmic account and settles it at high ranks; the law-first axiom reveals the predatory route's combat power inflation inevitably retcons and collapses; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. Three Psychological Nodes of Corruption

Corruption is not uniform, but turns at three psychological nodes:

1. **"Just this once"**: at Sense–Condensed, one small predation brings wondrous effect, self-persuading "never again."
2. **"Might makes right is simply the natural order"**: around Dao Origin Realm, packaging predation as cosmic law, completing ethical self-anesthesia.
3. **"I am above all beings"**: after Boundary-Breaking, treating Myriad Races as resources, completely cutting off the symbiotic circuit.

By the third node, the Predatory Faction are no longer "cultivators who took a wrong road," but "high-risk individuals" as defined by the Symbiosis Court.

## III. Origin Backlash: The Final Settlement

Document 04 long ago argued: predatory cultivators often face the "impure origin" bottleneck when breaking through to the supreme realm. The end of the corruption trajectory is exactly here — the thousands of residual traces devoured simultaneously betray at the moment of touching the cosmic origin. Because the cosmic origin only accepts pure energy built from increments; any being carrying predatory residual traces is instinctively rejected by origin.

Thus the most ironic ending appears: those who climbed to the top through predation are precisely those who cannot touch origin. The tower they spent a lifetime building collapses on its own at the last floor.

## IV. Policy on Remnant Predatory Forces

Symbiotic civilization does not advocate counter-predation. For cultivators still on the corruption trajectory, per three policies of "isolate, convert, sanction": those who have not caused major harm receive ethical education and system conversion; those who refuse conversion and continue to cause harm are plane-isolated and cultivation-restricted; those who caused major disasters are legally sanctioned after fair trial by the Anchor Court. Sanctions leave traces throughout, and themselves must not become new predation.

## V. Early-Warning Value

This corruption trajectory diagram's greatest value is letting every cultivator currently at Sense Origin who has entertained "taking a shortcut" see: the bite you swallow today is precisely the bite that will bite you to death at Origin Realm.
