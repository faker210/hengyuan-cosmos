## Introduction: Every step brings others along

In contrast to the Predatory Faction's corruption trajectory (Entry 197), the Symbiotic Faction's realm-wide trajectory is an ascension curve that "grows wider as it walks." Its feature is not individual surge, but **every step brings more people along**: at Sense Origin they teach each other, at Dao Origin they open lineages and teach, at Dao Integration they build domains guarding a region, at Boundary-Breaking they cross planes to aid the weak, at World-Creation they create worlds to nourish beings, at Origin Realm they connect to the whole network, ultimately leading to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm (alias "Hyper-Multiverse Balanced Anchor Realm").

The six top-level axioms are not constraints but engines on the Symbiotic Faction: the anti-predation axiom lets them walk only the increment; the republican axiom of Myriad Races makes their path naturally grow wider; the collective ascension axiom is the direction marker for the entire trajectory; the causal self-discipline axiom makes their ascension leave no dirty accounts; the law-first axiom lets this curve extend self-consistently, not via plot cheats; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## III. Social Mechanism Guarantees for Ascension

The Symbiotic Faction trajectory is feasible not because of individual moral loftiness, but because of institutions:

- **Joint contracting**: more work more gain, co-builders' energy and contribution positively feedback;
- **Mentor obligation system**: high ranks must bring low ranks, the strong are not lonely;
- **Alaya network-wide sharing**: knowledge not hoarded, one person attaining Dao benefits ten thousand;
- **Four-dimensional assessment**: mind-state and responsibility together weigh 40%, forcing the strong onto the co-building road.

Thus "growing wider" is not an ideal, but the inevitable output of institutional design.

## VI. Compound-Interest Essence of the Ascension Curve

The Symbiotic Faction trajectory seems slow, but is actually a compound-interest curve: the contributions, consensus, and network relationships sedimented at every realm become the principal for the next realm. Fellow disciples bonded at Sense Origin become comrades opening lineages together at Dao Origin; disciples taught at Dao Origin become colleagues on cross-plane work at Boundary-Breaking; low-rank planes co-built at Boundary-Breaking become the consensus basis for law fine-tuning at Origin. The Predatory Faction's curve is linear or even decaying, every step must make new enemies; the Symbiotic Faction's curve is exponential, every step stores allies for the next. This is why steady-starting symbionts far exceed early-surge predators at final energy level — not higher talent, but every step he took was not wasted, all turned into upward steps.
