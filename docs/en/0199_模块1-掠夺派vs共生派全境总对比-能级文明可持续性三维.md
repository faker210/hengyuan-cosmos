## Introduction: Putting two trajectories on one general ledger

Entry 197 drew the Predatory Faction's corruption curve; Entry 198 drew the Symbiotic Faction's ascension curve. This entry puts the two curves on one general ledger, doing a realm-wide final comparison from three dimensions: **energy-level efficiency, civilization output, and sustainability.** This table is not for moral preaching, but to answer one most practical question — in the long run, which road's general ledger is more cost-effective.

The six top-level axioms are this general table's verdict framework: the anti-predation axiom deems the predatory route noncompliant from birth; the republican axiom of Myriad Races deems the predatory route blocks the majority's road; the collective ascension axiom deems the predatory route's civilization output negative; the causal self-discipline axiom deems the predatory route's karmic debt must eventually be settled; the law-first axiom deems the predatory route's combat power inflation inevitably retcons. The three-dimensional comparison only quantifies these five verdicts; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. Dimension One: Energy-Level Efficiency

Short-term look at the Predatory Faction, long-term look at the Symbiotic Faction. The Predatory Faction's "fast" is borrowing money from the future: energy brought by devouring in the first half is repaid doubly in the second half via refining, confrontation, and degradation, ultimately 90%+ capped around Dao–Dao Integration. The Symbiotic Faction seems slow, but actually every step's energy is clean, usable, accumulable, and compoundable; late-stage net efficiency overtakes. This fully agrees with Document 04's "efficiency ceiling" argument.

## III. Dimension Two: Civilization Output

The Predatory Faction's strong one reaching the top produces a dictator and a conquered ruin; the Symbiotic Faction's strong one reaching the top produces Myriad Races republicanism, the Alaya Network, and three hundred open systems. Plug in "civilization weight" — individual combat power = destructive power × civilizational consensus degree: Predatory Faction's destructive power is high but consensus degree is negative, civilization weight extremely low; Symbiotic Faction's destructive power is ethically constrained but consensus degree extremely high, civilization weight rises exponentially with realm.

## IV. Dimension Three: Sustainability

This is the most fundamental dimension. The Predatory Faction consumes the cosmos's stock; each strong one born costs the destruction of other lives and planes; the cosmos's overall entropy increase accelerates, doomed to depletion. The Symbiotic Faction develops the cosmos's increment; Myriad Races' ordered symbiosis reverses entropy increase, and the cosmos as a whole sustainably ascends. The former is finite hunting-ground logic; the latter is evolvable-cosmos logic. **On the dimension of sustainability, the Predatory Faction cannot win in principle.**

## V. Responses to Common Counterarguments

- **"Didn't the Predatory Faction also produce strong ones in history?"** — Yes, but all short-lived, all capped, civilizations regressed behind them. Individual cases do not overturn the general ledger.
- **"Symbiosis is too slow, what if we can't wait?"** — The Multiplication Fruit's hundredfold deduction and Origin Acceleration Domain have pulled symbiosis speed to an acceptable range, fast without collapse.
- **"Can the two factions compromise?"** — Anti-predation is not egalitarianism, but the "rob while repairing" compromise also cannot pass the purity test at Origin Realm; there is no middle road.
