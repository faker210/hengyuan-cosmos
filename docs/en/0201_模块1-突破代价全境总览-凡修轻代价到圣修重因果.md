# Entry 201 | Breakthrough Cost Realm-Wide Overview: From Mortal-Cultivation Light Cost to Sacred-Cultivation Heavy Karma

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 17, 179

## Introduction: The higher the step, the more it weighs on not just one's own life

Every realm breakthrough has a cost. Heng Yuan Zhou does not pretend "painless ascension," but grades the cost, clarifies it, makes it controllable: the five mortal-cultivation realms' cost falls mainly on the cultivator themselves, light and repairable; the four sacred-cultivation realms' cost rises to karmic responsibility to all beings, heavy and not to be taken lightly. This entry gives a realm-wide overview, answering "what each realm must pay, and why this magnitude."

The six top-level axioms are the legal basis for cost grading: the anti-predation axiom stipulates cost must not be passed onto others; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires cost to be jointly backed by institutions not borne alone by individuals; the collective ascension axiom stipulates high-rank costs are paid in the form of "give-back"; the causal self-discipline axiom stipulates cross-plane breakthrough karmic accounts are strictly filed; the law-first axiom stipulates the cost curve is stable and self-consistent, not arbitrarily increased or lessened with plot; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. Why Cost Grows Heavier Across the Watershed

The cost nature changes fundamentally on both sides of the watershed (Entry 179):

- Mortal-cultivation costs are mainly "inward" — if cultivation goes wrong, it hurts oneself;
- Sacred-cultivation costs are "outward" — Dao Integration rewrites local laws, Boundary-Breaking operates plane membranes, World-Creation creates worlds, Origin fine-tunes the cosmos; every step affects all beings.

Thus cost magnitude rises synchronously with energy level, not because "high ranks are harder to cultivate," but because "one mistake at high rank can kill many people." This directly corresponds to the Plane Transcendent four-dimensional assessment's mind 25% and responsibility 15% weighting.

## III. Cost Must Not Be Passed to Others

The anti-predation axiom has a hard constraint on cost: **a cultivator's own breakthrough cost must not be passed to the weak through predation.** In old systems, strong ones broke through by sucking the weak, slaughtering sects, colonizing planes — this is passing the cost out. Heng Yuan Zhou explicitly forbids: if you want to ascend, you pay yourself; if you cannot afford it, institutions backstop (joint contracting, mentor network, Multiplication Fruit burden reduction), but you may not pay with others' lives.

## IV. How Institutions Backstop the Cost

Heng Yuan Zhou does not press cost entirely on the individual:

- **Joint contracting**: basic cultivation resources allocated by contribution, not ruined by "breakthrough fees";
- **Multiplication Fruit**: hundredfold deduction greatly compresses the time and energy cost of invalid trial-and-error;
- **Isolation space**: high-risk breakthroughs proceed in isolation space, mistakes do not affect beings;
- **Alaya supervision**: before breakthrough, karmic simulation is done first, foreseeable cost informed in advance.

Thus cost still exists, but it is controlled within "bearable, repairable, not harming others."

## V. The Highest Cost: Responsibility Itself

At Origin Realm, the heaviest cost is no longer energy or lifespan, but **responsibility**. Plane Transcendents must invest at least 30% of their time and energy in public responsibility each year — this is a continuing, lifelong cost. But this is not punishment, but the collective ascension axiom's natural requirement: you stand at a position that can affect the cosmos, and your cost is continuing to be responsible for the cosmos.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: using isolation space and multiplication deduction to compress invalid cost in advance, not letting cultivators pay heavy prices for meaningless trial-and-error
- **Anti-predation**: cost strictly forbidden to be passed to the weak; predatory breakthroughs are double-blocked by institution and karma
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style strong ones break through via dead people and sect sacrifices; Heng Yuan Zhou breaks through by self-payment and institutional backstop, no one buried with them
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: cost backstop mechanisms face all Myriad Races groups, not differentiated by life form
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cost grading tables, isolation-space breakthrough procedures, and karmic simulation methods are all public

## Supplementary Analysis: Law Logic of the Cost Gradient

Breakthrough cost's realm-wide gradient from mortal-cultivation light cost to sacred-cultivation heavy karma has an internal logic: the law-first axiom's automatic charging mechanism for energy-level jumps. Heng Yuan Zhou sets no extra punishment; cost is the law's own inertia — the higher the energy level, the denser the karmic chains touched, and the cost of adjustment naturally larger. The Alaya Network issues a cost prediction report before every breakthrough, letting cultivators make informed choices in advance. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, through joint contracting and knowledge sharing, disperses individually borne cost into civilizational common experience. The universal equal infinite law ensures low-realm cultivators are not belittled for "light cost," and high-realm cultivators gain no privilege from "heavy cost."

## Conclusion

Breakthrough cost is not the ticket price of ascension, but the ruler of how wide a scope you can be responsible for. Mortal cultivators pay their own cost; sacred cultivators pay beings' cost — see this cost table clearly, and you understand why the high-rank threshold must be guarded by mind-state and responsibility, not by fists alone.

In the final analysis, breakthrough cost realm-wide overview · from mortal-cultivation light cost to sacred-cultivation heavy karma described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
