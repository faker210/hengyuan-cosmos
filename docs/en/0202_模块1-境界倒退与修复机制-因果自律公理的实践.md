# Entry 202 | Realm Backward-Slide and Repair Mechanism: Practice of the Causal Self-Discipline Axiom

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 82, 83, 19

## Introduction: You can be repaired after falling, but you cannot cheat and redo

Cultivation is not a straight line that only rises. Breakthrough failure, mind-state loss, karmic backlash, energy collapse — all may cause realm backward-slide. Old systems have almost only two attitudes toward backward-slide: either treating one as a permanent cripple and kicking them away; or forcibly pulling them back via "devouring pills, seizing others' foundations," essentially manufacturing new predation. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is **repairable, but cheating forbidden** — backward-slide can be healed, but cannot be one-click redone by disturbing karma and devouring others. This is precisely the causal self-discipline axiom's practice at the individual level.

The six top-level axioms fully take position on backward-slide repair: the anti-predation axiom forbids repairing backward-slide via predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races acknowledges everyone's right to stand up again; the collective ascension axiom requires the repair process to deepen one's understanding of beings; the causal self-discipline axiom is this entry's protagonist — repair must respect karmic continuity, no historical reverse-driving; the law-first axiom requires the repair mechanism to be self-consistent, not via plot cheats of "turning back time"; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. Causal Self-Discipline: Why You Cannot "Turn Back Time"

Old web novels commonly solve backward-slide via "turning back time, doing it over"; Heng Yuan Zhou's causal self-discipline axiom explicitly vetoes this. Per "Time Retrospection Basic Principles" (Document 82) and "Observe-First-Then-Limited-Interference Guideline" (Document 83):

- Time retrospection is only for observation and archaeology, not for individual fate-changing;
- Return knowledge only, do not disturb karma;
- Thoroughly eradicate "reincarnation harvesting" and "historical reverse-driving."

Thus if you have slid back, you have slid back; you cannot wipe away the mistake by rewinding time. What you can do is cultivate again with the lesson. This seems harsh, but is actually fair — otherwise strong ones with time retrospection ability could infinitely trial-and-error, and ordinary beings would never have fairness.

## III. Three-Tier Repair Mechanism Plan

| Tier | Applicability | Repair Means | Ethical Boundary |
|------|---------------|--------------|------------------|
| Tier 1 repair | Energy backlash, foundation hidden injuries | Symbiotic medicine, Life Cycle System recuperation | Free, transparent |
| Tier 2 repair | Mind-state loss, path deviation | Mentor companionship, re-walking basics, community service | No forced brainwashing |
| Tier 3 repair | Karmic backlash, overstepping fault | Karmic settlement, compensation to victims, long-term observation | Per Anchor Court ruling |

All three tiers **do not use predatory means**. Repair energy comes from joint contracting and ecological cycles, not devouring others.

## IV. Re-Growth of Backward-Sliders

Heng Yuan Zhou does not treat backward-sliders as cripples. One real backward-slide and repair is often a key lesson in mind-state maturation — Entry 186's Youth Supreme ordeal mechanism's "bare-cultivation period without golden finger" is precisely a deliberately designed mini backward-slide. Cultivators who have experienced falling and standing up again often have more stable foundations than those who walked a smooth road all the way. This is also why the Standards Committee does not discriminate against candidates "with a backward-slide history but repaired," looking only at current substance.

## V. The Only Irreparable Hard Injury

The only irreparable one is confirmed predatory original sin — those who intentionally devour others' origin or massively disturb karma; their backward-slide is not given a "re-cultivation opportunity," but enters isolation and sanction procedures per law. This is the causal self-discipline axiom's rigid boundary: you can make a mistake, and institutions give you repair; but if you use harming others as a means to re-cultivate, institutions no longer give you another chance.

## Supplementary Analysis: Network Backstop for Backward-Slide Repair

The realm backward-slide repair mechanism is the causal self-discipline axiom's practical manifestation in the Heng Yuan Zhou system. Backward-slide is not shameful; what is shameful is refusing to admit fault after backward-slide. The Alaya Network does not make punitive records when a cultivator backward-slides, but generates a backward-slide attribution report pointing out which karmic chain went wrong. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization provides backward-sliders with repair paths: returning to the previous realm's safe track, re-walking the broken law cognition. The law-first axiom requires repair to follow law not rely on resource stacking. The universal equal infinite law ensures backward-sliders are not permanently marked as "losers"; after repair, their historical records automatically clear.

## Conclusion

The causal self-discipline axiom's requirement on individuals is: after falling, you can be helped up, but you may not push others over to use as a stepping stone, nor may you turn back time to pretend you never fell. This "no cheating" is precisely Heng Yuan Zhou's greatest respect for every person who has fallen.

In the final analysis, realm backward-slide and repair mechanism · practice of the causal self-discipline axiom described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
