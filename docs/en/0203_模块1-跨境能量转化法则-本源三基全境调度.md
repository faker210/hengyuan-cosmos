# Entry 203 | Cross-Boundary Energy Transformation Law: Realm-Wide Dispatch of Origin Three-Basic

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 15, 11, 12, 13

## Introduction: Energy does not disappear, it only gets wrongly stuck

Every cultivation bottleneck is ultimately an energy-transformation bottleneck: Sense Origin is "cannot suck in," Transform Origin is "cannot convert out," Law Origin is "cannot merge with laws," Boundary-Breaking is "cannot cross planes." Heng Yuan Zhou summarizes cross-category, cross-plane, cross-layer energy transformation laws as the "cross-boundary energy transformation law," and dispatches it realm-wide by the Origin Three-Basic (deduction iteration, life cycle, space jurisprudence dispatch). This entry explains how this law runs through the nine realms.

The six top-level axioms are the ethical base of cross-boundary transformation: the anti-predation axiom requires transformation to develop increments not extract stock; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires energy dispatch to benefit Myriad Races not some race's private occupation; the collective ascension axiom requires high-position energy to nourish downward, low-position achievements to feed back upward; the causal self-discipline axiom requires cross-plane energy flow to leave traces and file; the law-first axiom requires transformation formulas to be stable and self-consistent, not change numbers with plot; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Three Directions of "Cross-Boundary"

"Cross-boundary" refers to three types of crossing:

1. **Cross-type**: transformation among spiritual qi ↔ magical power ↔ law power (Transform–Law Origin core).
2. **Cross-layer**: flow among material plane ↔ energy layer ↔ consciousness domain ↔ law network (Boundary-Breaking–Origin core).
3. **Cross-plane**: energy dispatch between different material planes (Boundary-Breaking Realm and above).

## II. Realm-Wide Division of Labor of Origin Three-Basic

The Origin Three-Basic happen to manage the three cross-boundary directions:

| Origin Three-Basic | Primary Cross-Boundary | Core Realm Projection |
|--------------------|------------------------|------------------------|
| Deduction Iteration (set 1) | Cross-type: computing optimal transformation path | Initial glimpse → Origin |
| Life Cycle (set 2) | Cross-layer: benign circulation of energy and life | Sense-birth → Source-birth |
| Space Jurisprudence Dispatch (set 3) | Cross-plane: precise dispatch of space and plane membranes | Space-awareness → Source-space |

The three synergize to constitute complete cross-boundary dispatch capability. Cultivating only one will hit a ceiling in some direction.

## IV. Transformation Efficiency and Loss

Per "Energy Cycle and Transformation Law" (Document 15), every transformation has loss. Symbiotic civilization's goal is not to eliminate loss (that violates energy conservation), but **to reduce loss to the engineering limit**:

- The Multiplication Fruit's multi-stage resonance purification greatly reduces absorption–purification loss;
- Deduction iteration pre-computes the optimal path for every transformation, avoiding trial-and-error waste;
- Space jurisprudence dispatch lets cross-plane flow avoid the high-loss "tearing" old road.

The predatory route's transformation efficiency is low precisely because it additionally pays loss that should not exist on "refining residual traces."

## V. Public Nature of Energy Dispatch

Cross-boundary energy dispatch at high ranks is not a private skill but a public function. Strong ones above Boundary-Breaking dispatching plane energy must leave Alaya traces and joint-contracting bookkeeping — every increment you dispatch must be returned per contribution, not taken as private. This is the anti-predation axiom's landing in energetics: energy may be dispatched by strong ones, but dispatch power does not equal ownership.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: three-basic synergy compresses transformation loss to the engineering limit, the same energy travels farther
- **Anti-predation**: dispatch power ≠ ownership, cross-boundary energy may not extract other races' stock
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style energy transformation relies on attribute countering and devouring residual traces, huge loss; Heng Yuan Zhou relies on three-basic synergy, clean and efficient
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: three-basic and transformation formulas are open to the five genealogies, each race combines per its traits
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: transformation formulas, dispatch protocols, and loss-control engineering are all public

## Supplementary Analysis: Network Settlement of Energy Dispatch

The cross-boundary energy transformation law and Origin Three-Basic's realm-wide dispatch have their execution platform in the Alaya Network's distributed computation layer. Heng Yuan Zhou takes the Origin Three-Basic — energy, information, karma — as three basic parameters for cross-realm dispatch; the network balances their proportions in real time, preventing any parameter from overloading. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's joint contracting system manifests here: whoever dispatches energy producing civilizational increments receives the corresponding weight allocation. The law-first axiom requires dispatch to proceed within the law framework, not allowing cross-realm forced seizure. The universal equal infinite law ensures dispatch does not favor high-realm individuals; resources always flow to where civilizational increments are greatest.

## Conclusion

The cross-boundary energy transformation law turns "how energy flows" from metaphysics into engineering. The Origin Three-Basic each manage one direction and synergize realm-wide; this is Heng Yuan Zhou's technical answer in energetics to "how to become strong without predation."

In the final analysis, cross-boundary energy transformation law · realm-wide dispatch of Origin Three-Basic described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
