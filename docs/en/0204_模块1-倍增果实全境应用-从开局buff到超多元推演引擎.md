# Entry 204 | Multiplication Fruit Realm-Wide Application: From Opening Buff to Hyper-Multiverse Deduction Engine

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 16, 61, 11

## Introduction: Hundredfold amplification is an efficiency tool, not an energy cheat

The Multiplication Fruit technology is Heng Yuan Zhou's most iconic "opening buff": raising a cultivator's deduction, purification, comprehension, and recovery efficiency by a hundredfold (Document 16). But it is often misread as "cheating." This entry gives a realm-wide application overview, emphasizing a bottom line — the Multiplication Fruit is **efficiency amplification technology, not energy creation technology**. It does not violate energy conservation, but only minimizes invalid loss. From Sense Origin to hyper-multiverse scale, its form changes, essence does not.

The six top-level axioms run through realm-wide application: the anti-predation axiom explicitly forbids multiplication technology from being used for destruction; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the basic version to be free and open to Myriad Races; the collective ascension axiom requires saved time to be used for give-back not monopolization; the causal self-discipline axiom requires its deduction output still be subject to cross-plane filing; the law-first axiom requires amplification formulas to be stable and versionable iterated without retconning; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. Why Low Ranks Actually Amplify Less

Below Sense Origin, consciousness strength is insufficient, so the Multiplication Fruit only gives 10–30x not a hundredfold. This is not "stinginess," but protection: a hundredfold consciousness acceleration would overwhelm immature consciousness structures. Amplification increases with realm, same origin as the Youth Supreme dual-track growth (Entry 185)'s "force-feeding prevention" design.

## III. From Personal Buff to Hyper-Multiverse Engine

The Multiplication Fruit's high-rank application is no longer saving time for some individual, but becoming the entire civilization's deduction infrastructure:

- **R&D**: hundredfold experimental acceleration of law engineering in isolation space;
- **Medicine**: diagnosis and rehabilitation acceleration;
- **Education**: learning and understanding hundredfold faster;
- **Hyper-multiverse**: pulling cross-multiverse karmic simulation computation speed to usable magnitude.

Lin Hao's Path D actual combat (Entry 184) is precisely this engine applied to a personal path; above Origin Realm, it is elevated to a whole-network public deduction engine.

## IV. Safety Boundaries

The Multiplication Fruit has strict usage norms (Document 16):

- **Amplification cap**: may not exceed the cultivator's endurance, preventing "amplification overload";
- **Usage duration**: continuous high-multiple use must have intervals, preventing consciousness overload;
- **Purpose restriction**: only for construction, forbidden for destruction, cheating, illegal profit;
- **Real-name registration**: users leave traces, periodic health checks.

These boundaries themselves are the causal self-discipline axiom's reins on "super-efficiency."

## V. Personalized and Grouped Directions

Future directions (Documents 16, 61) include thousand-person-thousand-multiple personalized amplification, targeted amplification, group amplification, permanent solidification, cross-plane amplification. Among them group amplification is most critical — it turns the Multiplication Fruit from a "personal opening buff" into a "Myriad Races' shared deduction engine," consistent with the collective ascension axiom's direction.

## VI. The Fairness Boundary of an Efficiency Tool

It must be emphasized: the Multiplication Fruit's "universal benefit" attribute — its basic version is free and open to all life — is fundamentally different from old systems' "genius by bloodline, commoner by grace" cheat logic. The Multiplication Fruit does not create privileges; it erases information gaps and tool gaps — letting a human youth from the bottom, without any lineage, also use top-tier deduction efficiency. True fairness is not everyone having the same talent, but everyone being able to push their own talent to the limit. The Multiplication Fruit is precisely the technical embodiment of this. Meanwhile it remains efficiency amplification not energy creation; no one should expect to become a god out of thin air with it — it only makes effort more efficient, it does not pay for effort itself. This restraint is precisely why it can safely grow from an opening buff into a hyper-multiverse deduction engine.

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: compressing 95%+ invalid loss in cultivation to the minimum, the same energy produces a hundredfold value
- **Anti-predation**: amplification only raises efficiency not creates energy, and is forbidden for destruction, not rewarding predation
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style "talent bloodline" is a private cheat, the more it is used the more dependent; Heng Yuan Zhou's Multiplication Fruit is open technology, the more it is used the more autonomous
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: basic version free and open to Myriad Races, each race gets adapted amplification by consciousness strength
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: basic technology public, advanced version allocated by more work more gain, hyper-multiverse deduction engine shared network-wide

## Conclusion

The Multiplication Fruit grows from an "opening buff" into a hyper-multiverse-scale deduction engine, relying not on magic, but on doing efficiency to the engineering limit. It proves: in symbiotic civilization, the best cheat is studying the laws thoroughly, not robbing others clean.
