# Entry 205 | Three-Hundred Systems Realm-Wide Adaptation: Realm Distribution of Six Categories × Fifty Each

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 10, 91, 189

## Introduction: Three hundred roads leading to the same origin

The three hundred origin cultivation systems are divided into six categories by evolutionary dimension, fifty per category (Document 10). This is not three hundred unrelated methods, but a path network covering Myriad Races, the nine realms, and all evolutionary directions. This entry gives a realm-wide distribution overview: what direction each of the six categories excels in, how they spread across the nine realms, and how they adapt to Myriad Races' five genealogies.

The six top-level axioms are this path network's design drawing: the anti-predation axiom guarantees all three hundred are based on symbiotic cycles, not one predatory method; the republican axiom of Myriad Races guarantees the three hundred provide enough choices for Myriad Races' 99% coverage; the collective ascension axiom requires the three hundred's achievements to feed back to the whole network; the causal self-discipline axiom requires path choice and cross-boundary movement to leave traces auditable; the law-first axiom requires the three hundred to be compatible and superimposable, not mutually contradictory, not redesigned with plot; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. Foundation Status of Origin Three-Basic

The first three — deduction iteration, life cycle, space jurisprudence dispatch — respectively represent the first three categories, collectively called the Origin Three-Basic. Any cultivator is advised to first cultivate at least one of the Three-Basic as foundation, then supplementary-cultivate other major categories. This is because the Three-Basic respectively correspond to the three most basic things: "compute correctly, live well, walk far"; missing any makes high ranks prone to collapse.

## III. Supplementary Cultivation Matrix

The three hundred are not mutually exclusive. The Anchor Network's system compatibility assessment issues combination recommendations for each cultivator:

- **Among the Three-Basic** compatibility highest, recommended supplementary cultivation;
- **Within the same category** compatibility good, may deepen;
- **Cross-major-category** supplementary cultivation needs higher foundation and anchor support;
- **Main cultivation no more than three**, avoiding energy dispersion.

This matrix guarantees "everyone has a road" is not empty talk — a plant spirit may mainly cultivate the Life Cycle class and supplementary-cultivate the Symbiosis Network class; a mechanical race may mainly cultivate the Deduction class and supplementary-cultivate the Applied Engineering class.

## IV. Why "Three Hundred" Not "The Only One"

Old systems only allow one main road (e.g., predatory martial soul), forcing everyone through the same narrow path. Heng Yuan Zhou sets three hundred because Myriad Races' traits differ vastly: no single system can simultaneously adapt to stone, flame, information, and mixed blood. The meaning of three hundred is letting every life form find the road "custom-made for itself," not cutting feet to fit shoes.

## V. Continuous Openness of Systems

The three hundred are not final. The Anchor Network continuously collects cultivation feedback, iterates and optimizes, and when conditions mature adds new directions outside existing systems. But all newly added systems must pass compatibility and anti-predation review — this is the law-first axiom's constraint on "infinite expansion": you may add roads, but you may not add a road leading to predation.

## VI. A Real Sample of Path-Choice

Take a mechanical-race youth from the bottom: he initially intuitively mainly cultivated the Applied Engineering class, transforming his body into a precision workshop, but hit the consciousness-domain ascension ceiling at Law Origin Realm. The Anchor Network's compatibility assessment recommended he supplementary-cultivate the Consciousness Ascension class, then add one Origin Three-Basic. After adjustment, his cross-category ability instead became a unique advantage — he could both build it and "think it through." This sample shows: the three-hundred-systems' value is not how strong any single one is, but that it lets cultivators combine like building blocks until they piece together the road best suited to their own life form. In old systems with only one main road, this youth would have been forced to stop at the consciousness-domain ceiling long ago.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: the three hundred let every talent have a matched path, eliminating "talent misplaced"
- **Anti-predation**: all three hundred are based on symbiotic cycles, not one providing predatory methods
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems have only one martial soul main road, limited by bloodline; Heng Yuan Zhou's three hundred are free and open, everyone may choose
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the path network of six categories × fifty covers the five genealogies of carbon, energy, mechanical, consciousness, and hybrid
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: three-hundred basic methods are open at all levels, supplementary matrices and iteration mechanisms are all transparent

## Conclusion

The three hundred systems are not three hundred arts, but an evolutionary road network custom-woven for Myriad Races. Roads are numerous enough to contain every life form, yet uniformly point to the same clean origin — this is "everyone has a road" delivered in engineering.

In the final analysis, three-hundred systems realm-wide adaptation · six categories × fifty realm distribution described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
