# Entry 206 | Origin Three-Basic In-Depth Analysis: Realm-Wide Synergy of Deduction Iteration / Life Cycle / Space Jurisprudence Dispatch

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 11, 12, 13, 10

## Introduction: Get three things right, the other two hundred ninety are just extensions

The Origin Three-Basic are the foundation of the three hundred systems: Deduction Iteration (set 1), Life Cycle (set 2), Space Jurisprudence Dispatch (set 3). They respectively answer the three most fundamental cultivation questions — **how to think, how to live, how to walk**. This entry deeply analyzes each of the Three-Basic's logic, and why they must synergize realm-wide, not be cultivated alone.

The six top-level axioms are the Three-Basic's common base: the anti-predation axiom requires all Three-Basic be based on increment and circulation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the Three-Basic be free and open to Myriad Races; the collective ascension axiom requires the Three-Basic's achievements feed back to the whole network; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the Three-Basic's cross-plane operations leave traces and file; the law-first axiom requires the Three-Basic's realm projections be stable, non-conflicting, infinitely superimposable; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Deduction Iteration System: How to Think

The core is "hundredfold cultivation deduction amplification," using the Multiplication Fruit to speed consciousness computation hundredfold, parallel-deducing many paths in the consciousness domain, then verifying, iterating, converging. Its five stages are: initial glimpse, deduction, iteration, fusion, origin. It solves "choosing the right road" — not wasting one wrong move. Lin Hao's Path D actual combat (Entry 184) is this system's extreme use.

## II. Life Cycle System: How to Live

The core is the benign circulation of life energy, emphasizing that between the cultivator and environment is circulation not predation. Its five stages are: sense-birth, circulation, ceaseless, transformation, source-birth. It solves "being well-nourished" — your energy is not drawn once and gone, but forms a self-circulation with ecology and yourself. Plant spirits (Entry 190) are this system's natural masters.

## III. Space Jurisprudence Dispatch System: How to Walk

The core is precise understanding and dispatch of spatial laws, the cultivation foundation for cross-plane interaction, isolation-space construction, and mirror-copy technology. Its five stages are: space-awareness, space-guidance, space-transformation, space-law, source-space. It solves "being able to go out and come back" — not by tearing planes, but by jurisprudence dispatch. Boundary-Bearing Realm's cross-plane anchoring-less operations rely precisely on this.

## IV. Realm-Wide Synergy: Why None Is Dispensable

| Missing Item | Consequence |
|--------------|-------------|
| Missing deduction iteration | Road chosen wrong, the harder you try the more off |
| Missing life cycle | Energy exhausted, insufficient staying power |
| Missing space jurisprudence | Unable to cross layers and planes, capped at mortal cultivation |

The Three-Basic are exactly like three legs of a tripod:

- **Deduction** designs the optimal circulation formula for **Life Cycle**;
- **Life Cycle** provides sustainable energy supply for **Space Jurisprudence**;
- **Space Jurisprudence** provides cross-layer, cross-plane exercise space for **Deduction** and **Life Cycle**.

Cultivating deduction alone easily becomes "empty calculation without practice"; cultivating life cycle alone easily becomes "can nourish but cannot go out"; cultivating space jurisprudence alone easily becomes "walks far but has no root." The Three-Basic unified is the stable evolutionary chassis.

## V. Correspondence of Three-Basic with Nine Realms

Per Document 14, the Origin Three-Basic's five stages exactly cover the five mortal-cultivation realms:

| Nine Realms | Deduction Iteration | Life Cycle | Space Jurisprudence Dispatch |
|-------------|---------------------|------------|-------------------------------|
| Sense Origin | Initial glimpse | Sense-birth | Space-awareness |
| Condensed Origin | Deduction | Circulation | Space-guidance |
| Transform Origin | Iteration | Ceaseless | Space-transformation |
| Law Origin | Fusion | Transformation | Space-law |
| Dao Origin | Origin | Source-birth | Source-space |

After Dao Origin, the Three-Basic simultaneously enter the post-Dao-Integration stage "beyond single-system five stages," synergize with the other 297 systems, leading to Boundary-Breaking, World-Creation, Origin.

## VI. Common Misconceptions in Supplementary-Cultivating the Three-Basic

Although the Three-Basic are recommended for supplementary cultivation, three misconceptions are common in practice: first, greed for quantity, forcibly cultivating three or more systems at once, energy dispersion making none solid; second, one-sidedness, only picking one liked Basic, e.g., only cultivating deduction while belittling life cycle, resulting in the most precise calculation still lacking staying power; third, wrong order, forcibly cultivating space jurisprudence dispatch before the life cycle's internal circulation chassis is solid, resulting in energy dissipation when crossing planes. The Anchor Network's compatibility assessment is set precisely to correct deviations: it gives clear recommendations on main cultivation, supplementary cultivation, and postponement based on the cultivator's current realm, constitution, and goals. Three-Basic synergy is not a slogan, but a prescription dynamically adjusted per individual.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: Three-Basic synergy simultaneously blocks the three major waste sources of "wrong road chosen, exhaustion, inability to go far"
- **Anti-predation**: all Three-Basic are based on incremental deduction, circulatory nourishment, and jurisprudence dispatch; nowhere needs predation
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style cultivation only weighs "how to fight"; Heng Yuan Zhou's Three-Basic weigh "how to think, how to live, how to walk," higher dimensionality
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the Three-Basic provide a common chassis for the five genealogies, each race overlays characteristic systems
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Three-Basic methods, five-stage standards, and synergy matrices are all public, infinitely iterable

## Conclusion

Deduction iteration teaches you to think right, life cycle teaches you to live well, space jurisprudence dispatch teaches you to walk steadily. Get these three things right, and the other 297 systems are just icing on the cake. The Origin Three-Basic are Heng Yuan Zhou's cultivation science's "first grade of elementary school."

In the final analysis, Origin Three-Basic in-depth analysis · realm-wide synergy described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
