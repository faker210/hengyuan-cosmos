# Entry 207 | Plane Transcendent Realm-Wide Responsibilities: Job-Post System from Boundary-Breaking to Origin

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 17, 49, 179

## Introduction: Strong ones are not titles, but job posts

Plane Transcendent is not an enviable title, but an entire set of public job posts. Document 17 is clear: those reaching Origin Realm and above must invest at least 30% of their time and energy in public responsibility each year. This entry unfolds this "job description" — starting from Boundary-Breaking Realm, through World-Creation Realm, to Origin Realm, how strong ones' responsibilities progressively weigh, and how they find their workstation in the governance structure.

The six top-level axioms are the job-post system's warrant: the anti-predation axiom stipulates strong ones may not turn posts into private power; the republican axiom of Myriad Races stipulates posts are open to Myriad Races' strong ones, not exclusive to one race; the collective ascension axiom is the entire reason posts exist — lifting Myriad Races forward; the causal self-discipline axiom stipulates post behavior leaves traces throughout and is audited; the law-first axiom stipulates post responsibilities are stable, not idling when individuals leave; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. Six Major Post Responsibilities

Per Document 17, Plane Transcendents may choose focus per specialty, but may not completely refuse:

1. **Anchor guarding**: responsible for stable operation of one or more high-level anchors;
2. **Civilization advising**: participating in civilizational development direction decisions;
3. **Mentoring lineage**: cultivating the next generation, especially Origin Realm seeds;
4. **Plane inspection**: periodically monitoring plane stability and civilizational conditions;
5. **Crisis response**: bearing core tasks when planes collapse or external invasion occurs;
6. **Law participation**: participating in cosmic law fine-tuning and optimization.

Posts are not honorary nameplates, but real watch-duty.

## III. Posts Meshing with Governance Structure

Strong-one posts are embedded in four-layer governance: Anchor Council legislates, Symbiosis Executive Committee administers, Symbiosis Court judicially adjudicates, Anchor Oversight Committee supervises. Origin Realm strong ones may hold seats on the Council, but have no veto-style autocratic power; their post behavior is audited by the Oversight Committee. This structurally eliminates "strong one is dictator."

## IV. Returns and Constraints of Posts

As return for 30% public time, strong ones enjoy resource priority, law participation rights, space-creation rights, civilizational decision rights, etc. (Document 17). But these returns are "post benefits" not "private privileges": reclaimed upon retirement or transfer. Posts are tied to benefits, not individuals.

## V. Turn from "Overlord" to "Guardian"

This is the job-post system's deepest meaning: old systems' "plane overlord" treats planes as private property; Heng Yuan Zhou's "Plane Transcendent" treats planes as a post. The same power magnitude — the former rules, the latter serves. This turn is the anti-predation axiom's final landing at the strong-one level.

## VI. Post Rotation and Burnout Prevention

Origin Realm strong ones on year-round watch duty also face burnout and responsibility overload. Symbiotic civilization sets up a post rotation system for this: guardians need not guard the same anchor for life, but may rotate among the six responsibilities per specialty and energy; consciousness races, mechanical races and other monitoring-strong genealogies may bear watch-duty, while flesh-body-type strong ones may bear crisis response. Rotation both prevents single-person responsibility overload and lets strong ones accumulate a pan-domain view across posts. Meanwhile, post benefits follow responsibility not the person; once a strong one exits public service due to seniority or will, corresponding privileges are reclaimed, and new passing examinees take over — this guarantees the job-post system is a continuously running public machine, not a lifelong title for the few.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: compiling top-tier power into public posts, eliminating "talent underuse" and "strong ones idle"
- **Anti-predation**: post power split by four-layer governance, strong ones may not privatize public
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style overlords rule planes and prey on beings; Heng Yuan Zhou's Transcendents stand watch and guard, 30% time in service
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: posts are open to Myriad Races' per-group Origin Realm strong ones, not human-exclusive
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: job-post descriptions, watch-duty systems, and audit standards are all public

## Supplementary Analysis: Network Establishment of the Job-Post System

The Plane Transcendents' job-post system from Boundary-Breaking to Origin is not honorary titles in Heng Yuan Zhou but established posts. Each post has a clear responsibility list, four-dimensional assessment metrics, and Alaya Network real-time performance records. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's post rotation system ensures no Transcendent holds the same post long-term, preventing power ossification. The law-first axiom requires post setup to obey the law network's operational needs, not creating posts for people. The universal equal infinite law ensures post-promotion channels are open to all races, selection standards unified, birth not looked at.

## Conclusion

The Plane Transcendents' job-post system translates "the greater the power" into "the heavier the post." When strong ones understand they are not kings but watch-duty officers, Heng Yuan Zhou's ascension is truly stable.

In the final analysis, plane transcendent realm-wide responsibilities · job-post system from Boundary-Breaking to Origin described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
