# Entry 208 | Four-Dimensional Assessment System Realm-Wide Implementation: Strength 40 / Mind 25 / Contribution 20 / Responsibility 15

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 17, 29, 180

## Introduction: Assessing only fists cannot test guardians

The four-dimensional assessment system is Heng Yuan Zhou's core ruler for rating strong ones: strength 40%, mind-state 25%, contribution 20%, responsibility 15%. The meaning of this weight set is — it openly declares: **strength is only the foundation, not the whole; no matter how hard one's fists, a mind-deficient person is not a Plane Transcendent.** This entry explains how these four dimensions are implemented realm-wide, and why these weights.

The six top-level axioms are the legal source of four-dimensional weights: the anti-predation axiom requires the mind-state dimension to rigidly veto predators; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the contribution dimension to recognize whole-network records not personal boasting; the collective ascension axiom directly corresponds to the responsibility dimension; the causal self-discipline axiom is embedded in mind-state and responsibility review; the law-first axiom requires weights to be stable, not temporarily adjusted for any strong one; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Four-Dimensional Weight Table

| Dimension | Weight | Assessment Content | Nature |
|-----------|--------|--------------------|--------|
| Strength | 40% | Realm, energy, law manipulation | Baseline threshold |
| Mind-state | 25% | Anti-predation stance, ethical坚守, mental maturity | Rigid veto item |
| Contribution | 20% | Cultivation/knowledge/technology/service/ecology/guard contribution | Evidence item |
| Responsibility | 15% | Willingness and ability to bear public responsibility | Commitment item |

Strength takes four-tenths, seemingly heaviest, but it is a "passing line" not a "bonus item" — without meeting strength, nothing else counts; while mind-state, contribution, and responsibility together take six-tenths, which is the key to picking out "strong ones" from "thugs."

## II. Why These Weights

- **Strength 40%**: without strength, guarding cannot be discussed, but strength itself is not worthy of worship.
- **Mind-state 25%**: a strong one with lost mind-state is civilization's greatest risk source; the weight must be high enough to be a veto item.
- **Contribution 20%**: contribution is "what you have done for Myriad Races," only recognizing anchor-network empirical records.
- **Responsibility 15%**: responsibility is "whether you are willing to continue doing it," corresponding to the annual 30% public-time obligation.

This weight table itself is the collective ascension axiom quantified — six-tenths given to "what you have done for others."

## III. Realm-Wide Implementation Path

The four-dimensional assessment is not a one-time big exam only used at Origin Realm, but a growth scale penetrating from low ranks:

| Realm Segment | Strength Focus | Mind/Contribution/Responsibility Penetration |
|---------------|----------------|------------------------------------------------|
| Sense–Condensed | Perception and basic energy | Community service (contribution sprout) |
| Transform–Law | Energy transformation and magical power | Guiding beginners, path compliance (responsibility sprout) |
| Dao Origin Realm | Personal Dao | Ethical deep scan (mind-state定型) |
| Dao Integration–Boundary-Breaking | Domain and cross-plane | Plane task records, karmic filing |
| Origin Realm | Cosmic-level power | Full four-dimensional assessment, no one dispensable |

Thus cultivators know from day one: just practicing fists can never make a true strong one.

## IV. Rigid Veto

The mind-state dimension has "one-vote veto" power: anyone confirmed by ethical review to have predatory records or having chosen harm-others in extreme moral dilemmas, no matter how strong, is directly classified as a high-risk individual, with promotion and public posts frozen. This is the anti-predation axiom's "red ink" mark on the assessment form.

## V. Four Dimensions and Civilization Weight

The four-dimensional assessment and "civilization weight = individual combat power × civilizational consensus degree" are mutually表里: strength determines combat power ceiling; mind-state/contribution/responsibility determine civilizational consensus degree. The more balanced the four dimensions, the higher the civilization weight — this is precisely the relationship Entry 213 will expand.

## VI. Assessment Is Not a One-Time Ticket

The four-dimensional assessment is not tested once at Origin Realm and valid for life. It is dynamic re-evaluation: Plane Transcendents already recognized have their mind-state, contribution, and responsibility continuously tracked by the Alaya Network. If a strong one in old age becomes prideful over past merit and stops giving back, or slips in mind-state tests, re-evaluation issues warnings; for serious cases, permissions and post benefits are reduced per procedure, not "one exam decided for life." This design plugs the old-system loophole of "lying flat after reaching the peak," and makes the four-dimensional assessment a growth scale running through a strong one's whole life, not just an admission ticket.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: using four-dimensional scales to screen out lopsided "only fighting" types from the strong-one sequence, avoiding giving top-tier power to high-risk people
- **Anti-predation**: mind-state dimension one-vote vetoes predators, institutionally sealing off "attaining Dao through killing"
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style strong ones only compare strength and bloodline, mind-state endorsed by protagonist halo; in Heng Yuan Zhou mind-state takes 25% and can be vetoed
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: four-dimensional standards adapt to the five genealogies' life forms, contribution metrics recognize each race's characteristic contributions
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: assessment outlines, ethical review procedures, and contribution measurement rules are all public

## Supplementary Analysis: Network Execution of Four-Dimensional Assessment

The four-dimensional assessment system of strength 40, mind 25, contribution 20, responsibility 15 is entirely executed by the Alaya Network's automatic scoring system. Heng Yuan Zhou dares to quantify assessment because the network can collect objective data on three dimensions in real time: strength dimension's energy-level output, mind-state dimension's karmic self-discipline score, contribution dimension's civilizational increment records. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's assessment is not for elimination but for precise assistance — whichever dimension falls below threshold, the network automatically pushes corresponding training resources. The law-first axiom ensures assessment standards themselves are subject to charter review and cannot be arbitrarily modified. The universal equal infinite law ensures assessment uses the same ruler for all races.

## Conclusion

The four-dimensional assessment system's essence is redefining "what is a strong one" as "what is someone worthy of being entrusted with the cosmos." Strength is only the admission ticket; mind-state, contribution, and responsibility are the seats. This weight table is Heng Yuan Zhou's new definition of the words "strong one."
