# Entry 209 | 20% Activation Strategy Realm-Wide Advancement: Activation Ratios and Rhythm by Realm

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 23, 17, 75

## Introduction: Activation is not a slogan, but a per-realm timetable

The "20% Activation Strategy" is symbiotic civilization's core strategy in its early period: within the first epoch after civilization's establishment, activate 20% of all beings to Plane Transcendent (Origin Realm) level (Documents 17, 23). This entry lands this strategy across the nine realms, explaining how many should be activated at each realm segment, at what rhythm, and why this ratio is not a guess.

The six top-level axioms are the strategy's overall goal: the anti-predation axiom requires activation to rely on openness and technology not predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the 20% to be distributed among Myriad Races not concentrated in one race; the collective ascension axiom is the reason the strategy exists — activate enough strong ones to lift Myriad Races in ascension; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the activation process not disturb existing karma; the law-first axiom requires the timetable to be versioned, traceable, and not arbitrarily postponed; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## III. Why 20% Not 100%

A commonly asked question: why not 100% all become Origin strong ones? The answer lies in matching energy level and responsibility: Origin Realm's power is enough to affect the cosmos; if everyone instantly reached Origin, mind-state and responsibility education cannot keep up, it would instead be a disaster. 20% is the balance point where "strength foundation is enough to support ascension, yet not overwhelming the rhythm of mind-state cultivation." This is also why activation must advance synchronously with the four-dimensional assessment and ordeal mechanism.

## IV. Balanced Distribution Requirement for Myriad Races

20% is not 20% human. The strategy explicitly requires each of Myriad Races to have its own Transcendent representative: the five genealogies of carbon, energy, mechanical, consciousness, hybrid, and even plant spirits and planetary consciousnesses, should each have proportional seats in the 20%. Otherwise "activation" would degenerate into a single race's rise, violating the republican axiom of Myriad Races.

## V. Long-Term Significance of the Strategy

Document 17 points out that the deep meaning of 20% activation is more than cultivating strong ones, but also: providing a power foundation for the cosmos's overall ascension, preventing individual strong ones' power monopoly, forming an atmosphere of strong ones emerging in succession, and providing a broad strong-one foundation for Myriad Races republicanism. When 20% of beings all reach Origin and practice symbiotic ideals, the cosmos's overall ascension is no longer a dream.

## VI. Buffer Design Behind the Rhythm

The reason 20% activation advances in three hundred, five hundred, one thousand years, not instant universal Origin, is that mind-state education rhythm cannot be compressed by the Multiplication Fruit. Strength can be hundredfold accelerated, but mind-state and responsibility require real ordeals, real co-working, real mistakes and repair to temper. Thus the strategy deliberately binds activation speed to the maturity of the four-dimensional assessment and ordeal mechanism (Entry 186): every activation ratio increase, first confirm supporting ethical review, mentor network, and karmic supervision have kept up. Better to go slower than let a mind-state-unstable strong one rush into Origin Realm — that is not accelerating ascension, but planting a失控火种 in the cosmos.
