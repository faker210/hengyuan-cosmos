# Entry 210 | Joint Contracting Mechanism Realm-Wide Adaptation: Realm Differences in Resource Allocation

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 22, 06, 18

## Introduction: More work more gain, but first guarantee everyone has food

The joint contracting mechanism is Heng Yuan Zhou's resource allocation base: allocation of cultivation resources by contribution, more work more gain, no work no gain. But "more work more gain" is not a rerun of the jungle law — it has a hard prerequisite: the everyone-has-a-road guarantee system first guarantees every life's basic cultivation conditions, then on top of this opens gaps by contribution. This entry explains how this mechanism adapts across the nine realms: at different realm segments, how the proportion of basic guarantee vs more-work-more-gain changes.

The six top-level axioms are the allocation mechanism's ethical base: the anti-predation axiom stipulates resources come from creating increments not predating stock; the republican axiom of Myriad Races stipulates basic guarantee treats Myriad Races equally; the collective ascension axiom requires high-rank resources to be prioritized for lifting the whole; the causal self-discipline axiom requires allocation throughout leave traces auditable; the law-first axiom stipulates allocation formulas be stable, not changed by personal favor; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Realm-Wide Allocation Structure

| Realm Segment | Basic Guarantee (everyone gets a share) | More Work More Gain (floats by contribution) | Resource Nature |
|---------------|------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|-----------------|
| Sense–Condensed | Vast majority | Very small | Free basic methods, public cultivation grounds |
| Transform–Law | Basic + advanced ration | Larger float | Advanced resources, mentor qualification |
| Dao–Dao Integration | Basic guarantee | Large-proportion float | High-grade resources, anchor access |
| Boundary-Breaking–World-Creation | Basic guarantee | By post contribution | Plane-level resource dispatch |
| Origin Realm | Post benefits | By public responsibility | Cosmic-level resources, law participation rights |

The pattern is clear: **the lower the rank, the higher the basic guarantee proportion; the higher the rank, the higher the more-work-more-gain and post-benefits proportion.** Low ranks guarantee no one starves; high ranks reward real contribution.

## II. Why Low Ranks Get Universal Benefit First

At Sense–Condensed, cultivators are still laying foundations; if "more work more gain" were cruelly applied then, it would repeat old systems' tragedy of "bottom ranks cannot afford cultivation." Thus symbiotic civilization sets basic resources — methods, cultivation grounds, initial multiplication devices — as free public goods, letting anyone start the road first. This is the republican axiom of Myriad Races' landing in allocation.

## III. Why High Ranks Are by Contribution and Post

Above Dao Origin, cultivators have already proven their potential; at this point resource allocation should tilt toward those "contributing more to the whole network": only contribution values in the top 20% qualify to compete for high-grade resources; Origin Realm's resource benefits are tied to their public post (Entry 207). This both rewards contribution and directs resources to where value can be amplified most.

## IV. Anti-Monopoly and Anti-Egalitarianism

Joint contracting must simultaneously guard against two extremes:

- **Anti-monopoly**: the law forbids anyone monopolizing resource allocation rights; high-grade resources although allocated by contribution, their total expands with civilizational increments, no zero-sum cake-cutting;
- **Anti-egalitarianism**: anti-predation is not communal pot luck, contribution differences really exist, more work more gain retains reasonable gaps.

Document 06 is clear: symbiotic civilization recognizes differences in evolutionary results, but differences must come from one's own effort and contribution, not violence and birth.

## V. Relationship with Anti-Predation

Joint contracting is anti-predation's economic alternative: old systems allocate resources by "robbing"; symbiotic civilization allocates resources by "making" — the larger the increment you create, the more you receive. Thus the strong one's rise is no longer at the weak one's predated expense, and the entire resource pool grows larger.

## VI. Transitional Protection During Transition

Transitioning from predatory allocation to joint contracting, the hardest part is not designing formulas, but bearing the bottom cultivators exploited by old systems for a millennium. Symbiotic civilization sets transition protection for this: in early transition, Sense–Condensed stages have no contribution threshold, purely universal rationing; for cultivators converted from the Predatory Faction, their conversion-period contribution is counted as "correction is contribution," not requiring them to match native symbionts' output from day one. This transitional design avoids secondary harm of "the new system eliminates old victims from day one," letting joint contracting both open reasonable gaps yet not replay the jungle during transition.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: resources directed to those contributing most, avoiding waste of "lying on resources idling"
- **Anti-predation**: allocation by contribution not robbery, economically eliminating predation motive
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style resources rely on sects and holy grace, commoners get none; in Heng Yuan Zhou basics are free and high ranks by contribution, a poor family can rise through hard work
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: basic guarantee treats all Myriad Races groups equally, contribution measurement recognizes each race's characteristic contributions
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: allocation formulas, contribution measurement, and anti-monopoly clauses are all public

## Conclusion

The essence of joint contracting realm-wide adaptation is one sentence: low ranks let everyone have a road, high ranks let more work more gain. Land this sentence on every segment of the nine realms, and Heng Yuan Zhou is neither communal-pot egalitarianism nor weak-eats-strong hunting ground — it is an allocation machine that grows the cake bigger.

In the final analysis, joint contracting mechanism realm-wide adaptation · realm differences in resource allocation described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
