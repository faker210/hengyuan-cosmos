The republican axiom of Myriad Races is the second of the six top-level axioms, but it is not a slogan hung on the wall, but daily practice running through every realm: Sense Origin's mutual-aid sparring, Transform Origin's technology sharing, Dao Origin's lineage opening and teaching, Dao Integration's domain co-governance, Boundary-Breaking's cross-plane poverty alleviation, World-Creation's jointly-built worlds, Origin Realm's whole-network consensus. This entry lands "co-building" from abstract idea onto concrete actions across the nine realms. Among them the sixth universal equal infinite law establishes the origin logic that everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power and predation loses its meaning.

The six top-level axioms are mutually表里 in this entry: the republican axiom of Myriad Races is the protagonist, the anti-predation axiom guarantees co-building is not forced, the collective ascension axiom is co-building's direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guarantees cross-plane co-building does not disturb karma, the law-first axiom guarantees co-building mechanisms are stable and replicable.

## II. Upgrade from "Mutual Aid" to "Consensus"

Co-building has an internal upgrade line:

- Low ranks are **mutual aid** — you help me spar, I help you diagnose;
- Mid ranks are **sharing** — I put methods, technologies, insights onto the Alaya Network;
- High ranks are **co-governance** — your and my domains and planes coordinate by common rules;
- Top ranks are **consensus** — cosmic law fine-tuning is executed after Myriad Races form consensus through the Anchor Council.

This upgrade line is the whole process of Myriad Races republicanism from "everyone cultivating together" to "everyone governing the cosmos together."

## III. Alaya Network as Co-Building Infrastructure

Co-building does not automatically happen by moral consciousness; it relies on infrastructure: the Alaya Network lets knowledge be shared network-wide, contributions automatically recorded, consensus reached cross-plane. Without this network, "Myriad Races co-building" is only a wish; with this network, co-building becomes runnable daily life. The consciousness information race (Entry 195) is precisely this network's main operator.

## IV. Unity of Co-Building and More Work More Gain

Some worry "co-building" becomes "communal pot luck." Heng Yuan Zhou's solution: every contribution to co-building is recorded by Alaya, and converted to resource returns via joint contracting (Entry 210). Thus you helping others both achieves symbiosis and does not shortchange yourself — co-building is not sacrifice, but the most efficient more-work-more-gain.

## V. Prospect of Hyper-Multiverse Consensus

The Multiverse Realm and Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm above the nine require co-building scale to extend to other cosmoses. At this point "consensus" is no longer a cosmos's internal Myriad Races consensus, but a cross-multiverse node consensus. This is the republican axiom of Myriad Races' extension at maximum scale (see Entry 214).

## VI. Daily Forms of Co-Building

Myriad Races republican co-building is not always grand world-creation and legislation; it largely happens in unremarkable daily life: two alien youths at Sense Origin sharing one cultivation ground; a Transform Origin engineer transmitting a new formula to the Alaya Network for ten thousand to replicate; a Dao Origin grandmaster writing their personal Dao as public lecture notes; a Dao Integration guardian proactively regulating climate for a neighboring tribe. It is these day-after-day small moves that sediment into trust in the Alaya Network. Without these daily acts, "Myriad Races republicanism" is only a charter on paper; with these daily acts, it becomes the real road under every cultivator's feet. Co-building's ultimate form — hyper-multiverse consensus — is nothing but extending this daily trust to places farther beyond the cosmos.
