# Entry 212 | Law Anchoring Permission Realm-Wide Progression: From Local Plane to Multiverse Scale

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 20, 24, 28

## Introduction: How wide a law you can anchor depends on how much responsibility you bear

"Law anchoring permission" refers to a cultivator's qualification to participate in anchoring, stabilizing, or fine-tuning cosmic laws at what scale. It is not a privilege where more is better, but an authorization strictly matched to realm, mind-state, and responsibility: Sense Origin can only perceive, Law Origin initially touches, Dao Integration can anchor locally, Boundary-Breaking cross-planes, Origin participates in cosmic-level fine-tuning, the Multiverse direction further extends cross-multiverse. This entry explains how this permission progresses realm-wide.

The six top-level axioms are the authorization logic for permission progression: the anti-predation axiom stipulates permissions must not be used to monopolize laws; the republican axiom of Myriad Races stipulates permissions are open to Myriad Races' strong ones by assessment; the collective ascension axiom stipulates the larger the permission the heavier the public responsibility borne; the causal self-discipline axiom stipulates every anchoring operation leaves traces auditable; the law-first axiom stipulates permission levels are stable, no over-reach allowed; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. Why Permission Progresses by Realm Not by Desire

Law anchoring is a high-risk operation: one over-reach anchoring may distort a plane's law operation, even cause ecological collapse. Thus permission must strictly match "how large a consequence you can bear." At low realm, giving you large permission would instead harm you and harm beings. The essence of permission progression is the progression of responsibility capability.

## III. Public Nature of Permission

Law anchoring permission is not private property. Even an Origin Realm strong one must have their fine-tuning proposal form consensus through the Anchor Council, and execute only after Alaya audit. Anchors (Documents 20, 24) are public hubs connecting across layers; any strong one is only an authorized node on this public network, not a private owner of laws. This fundamentally distinguishes from old systems' pattern of "some emperor single-handedly covering the sky and fixing laws."

## IV. Karmic Constraints on Cross-Plane Anchoring

Cross-plane anchoring above Boundary-Breaking is subject to the strictest constraints of the causal self-discipline axiom: observe first then limited interference, return knowledge only not disturb karma. Every step of cross-plane anchoring must prove its disturbance to the target plane is minimized, and pass review by cross-plane ethical guidelines (Document 88). This is the rein preventing strong ones from treating other planes as experimental fields.

## V. Consequences of Over-Reach

Unauthorized law anchoring — i.e., "privately changing the heavens" — is treated as a direct attack on the symbiotic network. Light cases freeze permissions and backward-slide repair (Entry 202); serious cases after Anchor Court trial enter sanction procedures. Permission can be granted, and can be reclaimed; this is precisely what distinguishes it from old systems' "power is right."

## VI. A Lesson from a Permission Reclamation

Historically there was a Boundary-Breaking Realm strong one who, after obtaining cross-plane anchoring rights, tried to bias the patrolled plane's laws toward their own tribe, covertly establishing hidden colonization. The Alaya Network monitored anomalous shifts in law operation; the Anchor Oversight Committee launched an investigation; the Anchor Court ruled: their cross-plane anchoring permission was immediately reclaimed, their realm backed off to Dao Integration and accepted long-term observation. This case became a model — permission is granted, so it can be reclaimed; no matter how strong you are, once you turn public law anchoring permission into a private tool, institutions will not hesitate to yank out that key. It is these repeated impartial reclamations that keep law anchoring permission always a public tool, not strong ones' private domain.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: giving high-risk law operation rights to those whose ability and responsibility match, avoiding large-scale waste and disaster from over-reach
- **Anti-predation**: laws must not be privately monopolized, any strong one is only an authorized public node
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style strong ones privately fix laws by power, single-handedly covering the sky; in Heng Yuan Zhou law anchoring permissions progress by assessment and are network-wide audited
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: permissions open to Myriad Races' per-group strong ones by four-dimensional assessment, not monopolized by one race
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: permission hierarchy tables, approval procedures, and over-reach handling are all public and transparent

## Conclusion

Law anchoring permission's realm-wide progression strictly separates "power" and "authority": how much power you have is one thing; at what scale you are allowed to touch laws is another. This separation is Heng Yuan Zhou's institutional firewall against being kidnapped by any single strong one.
