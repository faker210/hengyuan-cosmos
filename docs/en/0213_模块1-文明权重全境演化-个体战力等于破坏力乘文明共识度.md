# Entry 213 | Civilization Weight Realm-Wide Evolution: Individual Combat Power = Destructive Power × Civilizational Consensus Degree

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 29, 199, 208

## Introduction: How much you can destroy counts less than how many people recognize you

"Civilization weight" is Heng Yuan Zhou's formula for measuring a strong one's true weight: **individual combat power = destructive power × civilizational consensus degree**. This formula's revolutionary nature is: it turns "power" from a one-dimensional number into a product. A solitary tyrant with enormous destructive power but zero consensus degree has a civilization weight approaching zero or even negative; a guardian whose destructive power is ethically constrained but whose consensus degree is extremely high has a civilization weight rising exponentially with realm. This entry explains how this formula evolves realm-wide.

The six top-level axioms are the formula's legal base: the anti-predation axiom lowers predators' consensus degree; the republican axiom of Myriad Races defines consensus degree as Myriad Races' recognition not one person's supremacy; the collective ascension axiom is the source of high consensus degree; the causal self-discipline axiom lets high-destructive-power ones not act recklessly; the law-first axiom makes this formula stable and computable, not tampered with by plot; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Formula Breakdown

| Factor | Meaning | How to Gain | How to Lose |
|--------|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Destructive power | Objective power magnitude | Realm, energy, law manipulation | Injury, backward-slide, sealing |
| Civilizational consensus degree | Myriad Races and civilization's recognition of their legitimacy | Anti-predation stance, contribution, responsibility | Taking predatory road, bearing karmic debts |

The key is: **destructive power may be high, but when consensus degree is negative, civilization weight is negative.** This is why a "peak strong one" who slaughters beings is actually worthless on Heng Yuan Zhou's ledger — their destructive power multiplied by negative consensus equals civilization's negative asset.

Around Dao Origin Realm is the key divergence point: choosing the co-building road, consensus degree rises all the way, destructive power is amplified by high consensus; choosing the predatory road, consensus degree turns negative, no matter how high destructive power is it is canceled by the negative sign. This fully agrees with Entry 199's predatory-vs-symbiotic three-dimensional conclusion.

## III. Why It Is Multiplication Not Addition

If it were addition (combat power = destructive power + consensus degree), an evildoer with extremely high destructive power could still get by on "adding a bit of good reputation." Multiplication eliminates this possibility: consensus degree zero, no matter how high destructive power it multiplies out to zero; consensus degree negative, the larger the destructive power the greater the harm. The multiplicative structure forces strong ones to truly win recognition, not rely on power to subdue or good reputation to whitewash.

## IV. Relationship with Four-Dimensional Assessment

The civilization weight formula and four-dimensional assessment system (Entry 208) are mutually表里: strength 40% determines destructive power; mind-state 25% + contribution 20% + responsibility 15% determine civilizational consensus degree. The more balanced the four dimensions, the larger the product. This is why "lopsided strong ones" do not go far in Heng Yuan Zhou.

## V. Ultimate Direction of Civilization Weight

Civilization weight's ultimate direction is Heng Yuan Zhou's redefinition of "powerful": true power is not how much you can destroy, but how many lives can safely entrust themselves to you. A guardian who lets Myriad Races sleep soundly has far higher civilization weight than an overlord who keeps Myriad Races awake.

## VI. How Consensus Degree Is Measured

Civilizational consensus degree is not vague "popularity," but measurable: the Alaya Network comprehensively scores a strong one's contribution records, number of times sought for help and followed, trust votes in Myriad Races councils, and choices in extreme moral dilemmas, giving a dynamic consensus score. This score is not decided by any one person or one tribe, but is the statistical result of whole-network long-term behavior. Thus the most reliable way for a strong one to raise civilization weight is not show, but continuously doing real contributions and bearing real responsibilities — because the whole network is silently keeping accounts. This turns "winning recognition" from power-play into day-after-day steadiness.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: using the multiplicative formula to delist "high destructive power but low consensus" high-risk force from the "powerful" ledger
- **Anti-predation**: predators' consensus degree is negative, the larger destructive power the more negative civilization weight, mathematically negating predation
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style overlords have extremely high destructive power but negative consensus; by this formula their civilization weight is negative, the so-called "strongest" is only a one-dimensional destructive-power illusion
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: consensus degree is given jointly by Myriad Races, not endorsed by some faction; the wider the coverage the more real the consensus
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: weight formula and symbiosis assessment methods (Document 29) are all public, computable cross-civilization

## Conclusion

The civilization weight formula in one sentence: how much you can destroy does not count; how many people truly trust you does. It rewrites "strong" from a synonym of violence into a product of responsibility and recognition. This is Heng Yuan Zhou's final pricing of "power."
