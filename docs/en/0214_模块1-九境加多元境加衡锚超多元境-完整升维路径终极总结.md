## II. Three-Stage Logic

The entire path can be summarized in three stages:

1. **Five mortal-cultivation realms: train yourself well.** From perception to Dao attainment, the core is making personal energy, laws, and mind-state solid. This stage everyone has a road, 99% coverage.
2. **Four sacred-cultivation realms: take up responsibility.** From Dao Integration to Origin, the core shifts from "personal cultivation" to "public post" — guardian, creator, law participant. This stage has the four-dimensional assessment guarding the entrance.
3. **Two extension realms: bring Myriad Races along.** The Multiverse Realm and Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, the core is extending ascension from one cosmos to a cross-multiverse Myriad Races co-building network.

The three stages' progression is precisely the complete arc of "first perfect oneself, then benefit a region, ultimately Myriad Races jointly ascend."

## III. The Path's Sole Legitimacy

This path is legitimate and infinitely extendable, relying not on plot halo but on five premises holding simultaneously:

It must be emphasized again: the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is not some individual's endpoint of climbing alone.
