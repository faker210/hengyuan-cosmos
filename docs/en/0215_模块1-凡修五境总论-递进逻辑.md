# Entry 215 | Five Mortal-Cultivation Realms General Discussion: Progressive Logic of Sense / Condensed / Transform / Law / Dao Origin

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 04, 08

## Introduction: The five mortal-cultivation realms are Myriad Races' main stage

In Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s nine-realm system, the first five realms — Sense Origin, Condensed Origin, Transform Origin, Law Origin, Dao Origin — are collectively called the "five mortal-cultivation realms." These five realms cover 99%+ of cultivators, and are the evolutionary main stage Myriad Races actually walk with their own feet. Compared with the four later sacred-cultivation realms, the five mortal-cultivation realms' features are: open thresholds, diverse paths, reachable by all, but every realm's progression has strict law logic, no skipping levels, no shortcutting effort.

The six top-level axioms run through these five realms throughout: the anti-predation axiom requires every realm's energy to come from symbiotic circulation not predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the five realms' doors open to all Myriad Races beings; the collective ascension axiom requires every realm's results to feed back to the anchor network; the causal self-discipline axiom constrains cultivators from disturbing others' karma; the law-first axiom ensures the five realms' progression laws are stable and self-consistent, never retconning; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry discusses the five mortal-cultivation realms' progressive logic in general: why this order? What core problem does each realm solve? What are the transition laws between the five realms?

## I. The Five-Realms Progression Core Logic: Perceive → Condense → Transform → Law → Attain Dao

The five mortal-cultivation realms' progression is not arbitrary arrangement, but follows a strict law-cognition chain:

| Realm | Core Action | Fundamental Problem Solved | Transition Marker |
|-------|-------------|------------------------------|-------------------|
| Sense Origin Realm | Perceive | "What is in the cosmos?" | From no perception to perception |
| Condensed Origin Realm | Condense | "How do I keep energy?" | From dissipation to internal circulation |
| Transform Origin Realm | Transform | "What can energy become?" | From single-form to multi-form |
| Law Origin Realm | Law | "What are the rules behind energy?" | From energy to law |
| Dao Origin Realm | Attain Dao | "Which road will I take?" | From law to personal Dao |

This chain's internal logic is: **first perceive existence, then condense resources, then transform and apply, then understand rules, finally form one's own road.** Skipping any link causes foundation defects — e.g., skipping Sense Origin to directly Condense Origin causes condensed impurities due to unclear perception; skipping Transform Origin to directly Law Origin cannot fuse laws due to crude energy manipulation.

## II. Transition Laws Between the Five Realms

### 2.1 Sense → Condensed: From "seeing" to "keeping"

Sense Origin solves "can you perceive"; Condensed Origin solves "can you stably use." The transition key is not energy accumulation amount, but **perception stability** — only when you can stably perceive energy in any environment can you build reliable internal circulation. The anchor network automatically detects the perception channel's signal-to-noise ratio at transition points; below threshold Condensed Origin Realm is not allowed, preventing "blind cultivation."

### 2.2 Condensed → Transform: From "storage" to "use"

After Condensed Origin builds internal circulation, energy is "stored"; Transform Origin requires energy to be "used" out — transformed into offense-defense, shaping, interaction. The transition key is **energy purity** and **transformation efficiency**. Purity insufficient, transformation produces impurity backlash; efficiency insufficient, transformation loses practical meaning. This stage is the first exam of "anti-energy waste": many cultivators hoard large amounts of low-purity energy at Condensed Origin, only to find at Transform Origin that 90%+ cannot be effectively transformed, equal to waste.

### 2.3 Transform → Law: From "energy" to "law"

Transform Origin cultivates energy itself; Law Origin cultivates the laws behind energy. This is the **most critical qualitative leap** in the five mortal-cultivation realms — from "using energy" to "understanding rules." The transition marker is initial fusion of energy and law, producing "magical power," a higher-order form. This transition has the highest elimination rate, because it requires cultivators to leap from "operation level" to "understanding level"; thinking mode must fundamentally shift. The anchor network provides a "law perception guidance" function for this, helping cultivators naturally transition from energy operation to law observation.

### 2.4 Law → Dao: From "understanding laws" to "forming personal Dao"

Law Origin understands "what are the cosmos's laws"; Dao Origin requires answering "what is my Dao." The transition key is **the prototype of personal Dao** — not copying cosmic laws, but on the basis of deeply understanding laws, forming one's own unique expression and practice path. Dao Origin cultivators are civilization's "masters"; their personal Dao will become the reference path for the next generation of cultivators.

## III. Common Features of the Five Mortal-Cultivation Realms

- **Open thresholds**: all five realms are based on the three-hundred origin cultivation systems, basic methods free and open to Myriad Races, no race/gender/birth threshold
- **Diverse paths**: every realm has six categories × 50 systems to choose from; cultivators may choose the optimal path per their own endowment
- **Transparent monitoring**: anchor network monitors throughout, progress transparent, cultivators always know where they are and what is missing
- **Shared results**: every realm's cultivation insights and breakthrough experiences automatically upload to the Alaya Network for Myriad Races sharing
- **Controllable risk**: breakthrough costs tracked throughout, Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision ensures qi deviation is repairable

## IV. The Boundary Between Five Mortal-Cultivation and Four Sacred-Cultivation Realms

The dividing line between the five mortal-cultivation and four sacred-cultivation realms is between Dao Origin and Dao Integration. In the mortal-cultivation stage, cultivators "understand and use laws"; in the sacred-cultivation stage, cultivators begin "participating in law creation and rewriting." This distinction is not artificial hierarchy division, but law itself's qualitative leap — when personal Dao deeply fuses with cosmic laws, the cultivator turns from "law user" into "law co-builder."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: the five-realm progression strictly follows the perceive→condense→transform→law→attain-Dao law chain, eliminating foundation waste from skipping levels; every bit of cultivation effort converts to effective evolution
- **Anti-predation**: all five realms are based on symbiotic circulation principles, energy comes from creation and circulation, not swallowing one bit of others; shortcut inner demons are identified and guided from Sense Origin
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems lock the vast majority of cultivators at the fierce-realm ceiling; the five mortal-cultivation realms in Heng Yuan Zhou are 99% of beings' main stage, the road is open and walkable to the end
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the five mortal-cultivation realms' progressive logic applies equally to Myriad Races' five genealogies (carbon/energy/mechanical/consciousness/hybrid), each race has adapted paths
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: five-realm methods all open, insights all shared, cultivation results connected to the Alaya Network, laying the foundation for Dao Integration → Boundary-Breaking → World-Creation → Origin → Multiverse → Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse

## Conclusion

The five mortal-cultivation realms are not simple "leveling and fighting monsters," but a complete cognitive-upgrade chain from perceiving the cosmos to forming personal Dao. Every realm has clear law logic, cannot be crossed, cannot be shortcut. In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, this chain is not the privilege of the few geniuses, but the evolutionary main road every Myriad Races being can walk. Walk the five realms solidly, and the four sacred-cultivation realms' doors will naturally open.
