# Entry 216 | Four Sacred-Cultivation Realms General Discussion: Qualitative-Lever Leaps of Dao Integration / Boundary-Breaking / World-Creation / Origin

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 17, 03

## Introduction: From law users to law co-builders

In Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s nine-realm system, the last four realms — Dao Integration, Boundary-Breaking, World-Creation, Origin — are collectively called the "four sacred-cultivation realms." Unlike the five mortal-cultivation realms' gradual cognitive upgrade, every one of the four sacred-cultivation realms is a **cosmic-level qualitative leap**: cultivators rise from "understanding and using laws" to "participating in law creation, rewriting, and guarding." These four realms are not quantitative accumulation, but fundamental identity shift — from the cosmos's "tenant" to the cosmos's "co-builder."

The six top-level axioms manifest the most concentrated constraint in the four sacred-cultivation realms: the anti-predation axiom requires sacred strong ones not use law permissions to prey on beings; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires sacred strong ones' power to serve Myriad Races not be privately hoarded; the collective ascension axiom requires sacred strong ones to lift Myriad Races along, not ascend alone; the causal self-discipline axiom's constraint on cross-plane operations is strictest at the sacred-cultivation stage; the law-first axiom ensures the four sacred-cultivation realms' law definitions are stable, not inflated by plot needs; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry discusses the four sacred-cultivation realms' qualitative-leap logic in general: what is the qualitative-leap core of each realm? Why are these four realms "sacred" not "mortal"?

## I. Qualitative-Leap Map of the Four Sacred-Cultivation Realms

| Realm | Qualitative-Leap Core | Identity Shift | Power Tier | Social Role |
|-------|----------------------|----------------|------------|-------------|
| Dao Integration Realm | Personal Dao merges into the cosmic law network | From "using laws" to "integrating with laws" | Law-level | Grandmaster |
| Boundary-Breaking Realm | Breaking through plane barriers' law constraints | From "intra-plane" to "cross-plane" | Plane-level | Plane strong one |
| World-Creation Realm | Weaving laws in isolated space, creating worlds | From "user" to "creator" | World-level | World-creator |
| Origin Realm | Touching cosmic origin, participating in law fine-tuning | From "intra-world" to "origin layer" | Cosmic-level | Plane Transcendent |

These four realms' qualitative leap is not quantitative like "more energy, stronger magic power," but **existence-dimensional leap** — every realm means the cultivator's "existence" expands to a new dimensional layer.

## II. Dao Integration Realm: First Qualitative Leap of Heaven-Human Unity

Dao Integration Realm is the watershed between mortal and sacred cultivation. A Dao Origin cultivator has already formed personal Dao, but this Dao is still "personal"; Dao Integration requires personal Dao to deeply fuse with cosmic origin laws, reaching the state where "every move is in accord with Dao."

The core marker of this qualitative leap is: the cultivator no longer needs to "deliberately" use laws; laws themselves are his natural extension. He can rewrite local laws' operation within a wide range; life form begins evolving to higher dimensions, partially shedding material body constraints. Dao Integration strong ones can create stable personal law domains, the first expansion from "personal power" to "law space."

In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, breaking through to Dao Integration must pass Standards Committee assessment, in which the mind-state dimension takes 25% — it is not enough that power reaches Dao Integration; **the Dao heart must be in sync with symbiotic ideals**. A cultivator harboring predation, even if power reaches Dao Integration standards, will have their "Dao" reject cosmic symbiotic laws and cannot truly integrate.

## III. Boundary-Breaking Realm: Breaking Through Plane Barriers

Boundary-Breaking Realm's qualitative-leap core is **cross-plane operation capability**. Before Dao Integration, no matter how strong a cultivator is, they are constrained by their plane's laws; Boundary-Breaking cultivators can break through plane membrane limits and travel cross-plane without anchor support.

This leap's meaning far exceeds "travel capability" — it means the cultivator's existence is no longer confined to a single law system. Boundary-Breaking strong ones can operate plane membranes: opening channels, repairing cracks, monitoring plane fluctuations. They are bridges and guardians between planes.

The causal self-discipline axiom reaches its strictest in this realm: cross-plane operations must be strictly filed, observe first then limited interference, return knowledge only not disturb karma. Boundary-Breaking strong ones violating this principle will be immediately intervened by the Alaya Network, restricting cross-plane permissions — because cross-plane disturbance consequences may affect the entire plane chain's karmic stability.

## IV. World-Creation Realm: Law Weaving and Small-World Creation

World-Creation Realm is the most creativity-concentrated of the four sacred-cultivation realms. Cultivators can create complete worlds in isolated space — including matter, energy, laws, life. This is not "creating from nothing," but **weaving a self-consistent law system per cosmic laws' bottom logic**, turning isolated space from "blank" into "world."

World-Creation's qualitative leap lies in: cultivators turn from "law users" into "law designers." The world they design must satisfy the law-first axiom — self-consistent, stable, expandable. A world with non-self-consistent laws collapses on its own during creation; this is why World-Creation breakthroughs are extremely rare: not insufficient power, but insufficient design ability.

In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, worlds created by World-Creation strong ones are not private property but civilization's public resources — usable as cultivation resource zones, experimental sites, civilizational sanctuaries, new-race cultivation zones. Creators have guardianship responsibility for their created worlds, but no monopoly rights.

## V. Origin Realm: Cosmic-Level Being and Plane Transcendent Threshold

Origin Realm is the nine-realm system's apex. Touching cosmic origin laws, understanding the cosmos's ultimate operating rules, able to participate in cosmic law fine-tuning and optimization. Life form deeply connects with cosmic origin, near-immortal.

Origin Realm strong ones are "Plane Transcendents," rated by the four-dimensional assessment system: strength 40%, mind 25%, contribution 20%, responsibility 15%. This is not a power ranking, but **responsibility certification** — only cultivators meeting all four-dimensional standards qualify to participate in cosmic law fine-tuning.

Origin Realm is not the endpoint. In the extended setting (pending Terminology Committee calibration), above Origin Realm are also the Multiverse Realm (multi-cosmos governance and law permission upgrade) and Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm (Myriad Races' origin-balanced symbiotic ultimate form), reserving space for the realm system's infinite expansion.

## VI. Common Iron Rules of the Four Sacred-Cultivation Realms

- **Qualitative leaps cannot be skipped**: every realm is an existence-dimensional leap, cannot be skipped by energy stacking
- **Mind-state synchronized assessment**: every realm breakthrough must pass ethical review, power and Dao heart must be in sync
- **Responsibility increases with realm**: the higher the realm, the heavier the public responsibility; Origin Realm strong ones spend at least 30% of time annually on public affairs
- **Results shared network-wide**: sacred strong ones' cultivation insights, law understanding, and world-creation designs all connect to the Alaya Network
- **Permanently monitorable**: Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision monitor sacred strong ones throughout, preventing power abuse

## VII. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: the four sacred-cultivation realms' qualitative leap relies on law understanding and Dao-heart sublimation, not energy stacking; every realm breakthrough's energy consumption is far less than predatory cultivation's energy waste
- **Anti-predation**: sacred strong ones' law permissions are tools for serving Myriad Races, not weapons for predation; Predatory Faction cultivators even if power meets standards cannot integrate Dao due to "Dao-heart rejection"
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems lock the ceiling at the fierce realm; the four sacred-cultivation realms in Heng Yuan Zhou are Myriad Races civilization's advanced goal, the 20% Activation Strategy is batch-cultivating Origin Realm strong ones
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the four sacred-cultivation realms set no race limits; every Myriad Races group has its own grandmasters, plane strong ones, world-creators, and Transcendents
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the four sacred-cultivation realms' law understanding and world-creation designs are all openly shared, laying the foundation for the Multiverse Realm and Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's infinite expansion

## Conclusion

The four sacred-cultivation realms are not "stronger power," but "heavier responsibility." From Dao Integration to Origin, every realm's qualitative leap accompanies expansion of freedom and deepening of constraint. In predatory-faction systems, high realm means greater predation right; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, high realm means greater service obligation. This is the true meaning of "sacred" — not above beings, but bearing beings' weight.
