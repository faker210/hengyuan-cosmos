# Entry 217 | Sense Origin Realm Deep Supplement: Seven Levels of Perception Precision

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 15, 21

## Introduction: Perception precision is the first realm's true watershed

Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s Sense Origin Realm superficially is the "perceiving energy" entry stage, but internal perception precision differences actually determine how far a cultivator can go in the future. Many cultivators think "Sense Origin is just about being able to perceive," not knowing perception precision gaps will be exponentially amplified in every subsequent realm — a coarse perceiver condenses more energy impurities at Condensed Origin, has lower transformation efficiency at Transform Origin, understands laws more shallowly at Law Origin. Perception precision is not a metaphysical "feels good" question, but **the bottom parameter determining the entire cultivation career's quality**.

The six top-level axioms take effect on the Sense Origin perception-precision dimension equally: the anti-predation axiom requires perception training not damage the environment; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires perception-precision training methods open to Myriad Races; the collective ascension axiom requires perception data shared back to the network; the causal self-discipline axiom requires perception behavior not overstep to pry into others' privacy; the law-first axiom ensures the seven-level division's law definitions are stable and self-consistent; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry subdivides Sense Origin's perception precision into seven levels, explaining their features, training methods, and transition conditions one by one.

## II. L1-L3: Establishing Basic Perception

**L1 hazy sense** is Sense Origin's starting point. The cultivator first perceives free energy in the cosmos, this feeling is like seeing a glimmer of light in darkness — vague, unstable, fleeting. Training method is guiding consciousness into "perception mode" via basic methods, lasting weeks to months. The anchor network provides a "perception guidance wave" at this level, lowering the entry threshold.

**L2 outline sense** requires the cultivator to upgrade from "knowing energy exists" to "knowing where energy is and what it looks like." Training focus is direction sense and form sense — close eyes, which direction is energy? Thick or thin? Flowing or still? This level's transition marker is: being able to stably locate at least three independent energy sources within five meters.

**L3 classification sense** is Sense Origin's most critical basic gate. The cultivator must learn to distinguish different energy types: pure spiritual qi, law power, impurity energy, others' residual energy, etc. Lack of classification ability directly causes Condensed Origin to condense wrong energy. This level's training is provided by the anchor network's "energy sample library"; cultivators repeatedly identify various energy samples in a safe environment until accuracy meets standard.

## III. L4-L5: Deepening Perception Precision

**L4 stability sense** solves "can you maintain perception in complex environments." In actual cultivation, energy environments are often noisy — multiple energies interweaving, external interference nonstop, own emotional fluctuation. L4 cultivators must maintain the perception channel's signal-to-noise ratio under these complex conditions. Training methods include: maintaining perception in simulated energy storm environments, keeping perception stable during emotional fluctuation, not fatiguing after long perception.

**L5 precision sense** is Sense Origin's deep-cultivation core. Cultivators reaching this level can distinguish different purity, frequency, source of the same energy. This is critical for subsequent Condensed Origin's "choosing what to condense" — cultivators with insufficient precision sense will also condense impurity energy into internal circulation, endless trouble. L5 training methods include: energy spectrum analysis, purity comparison exercises, source tracking training.

## IV. L6-L7: High-Order Forms of Perception

**L6 field sense** requires the cultivator to upgrade from "perceiving one point" to "perceiving one surface." Their consciousness can simultaneously cover an area, mapping that area's energy distribution. This ability directly applies to Transform Origin's environmental utilization and Law Origin's law observation. L6 training methods are expanding perception range, training parallel perception, learning field modeling.

**L7 resonance sense** is Sense Origin's apex. Cultivators reaching this level have their perception itself already resonate with the energy field — perception behavior is no longer "seeing with eyes," but "being in sync with energy." In this state, deep perception itself is a kind of cultivation: energy in resonance auto-optimizes, auto-purifies, auto-calibrates. L7 cultivators after entering Condensed Origin have condensation efficiency far exceeding ordinary cultivators — because from the perception stage they have already completed energy's initial screening and calibration.

## V. Relationship of Seven Levels with Subsequent Realms

The seven levels of perception precision are not Sense Origin's internal play, but directly affect basic parameters of every subsequent realm:

- **For Condensed Origin**: cultivators with L5+ precision sense reduce impurity rate by 60%+ when condensing
- **For Transform Origin**: L6 field sense directly improves energy transformation's environmental adaptation
- **For Law Origin**: L7 resonance sense is the prerequisite for law perception — no resonance then cannot "hear" laws' voice
- **For Dao Origin**: intuition cultivated by long-term high-precision perception is important material for forming personal Dao
