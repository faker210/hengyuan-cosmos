# Entry 218 | Condensed Origin Realm Deep Supplement: Three Modes of Internal Circulation Construction

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 15, 12

## Introduction: Internal circulation is the cultivator's "energy heart"

Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s Condensed Origin Realm's core task is building the body's internal energy circulation. If Sense Origin is "learning to see," Condensed Origin is "learning to use" — absorbing, condensing, circulating perceived energy, forming an energy system that runs continuously without external supply. Internal circulation is to a cultivator what the heart is to a living body: unstable circulation, and all subsequent cultivation is a castle in the air.

The six top-level axioms run through Condensed Origin's internal circulation construction equally: the anti-predation axiom requires internal circulation energy all come from symbiotic circulation, no predatory residual traces; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the three internal circulation modes to be adapted openly to Myriad Races; the collective ascension axiom requires internal circulation efficiency improvements ultimately feed back to the anchor network; the causal self-discipline axiom constrains internal circulation from extracting environmental or others' karmic energy; the law-first axiom ensures the three modes' law definitions are stable, not retconned by faction disputes; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry subdivides Condensed Origin's internal circulation construction into three modes, analyzing their respective principles, pros/cons, and adapted populations.

## I. Three Internal Circulation Modes Overview

| Mode | Core Principle | Circulation Feature | Adapted System | Typical Pros/Cons |
|------|----------------|---------------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Mode A: Steady circulation | Energy flows at constant speed along fixed path | Stable, gentle, high fault tolerance | Life Cycle System | Steady but slow, for beginners |
| Mode B: Pulse circulation | Energy periodically accelerates-decelerates | Strong burst, fast recovery | Deduction Iteration System | Flexible but needs control |
| Mode C: Multi-direction interwoven circulation | Multiple circulation paths run simultaneously | Large capacity, wide adaptability | Space Jurisprudence Dispatch System | Complex but highest efficiency |

## II. Mode A: Steady Circulation

Steady circulation is the most basic and safest internal circulation mode. After introducing energy into the body, the cultivator flows it at constant speed along one fixed main path — from core to limbs, from surface to deep, from absorption to release, forming an uninterrupted stable loop.

**Core principle**: steady circulation's law base is "energy conservation and uniform distribution." Energy in circulation does not accelerate, does not stagnate, does not favor one side, like a gentle river. This mode's requirement on energy purity is relatively lenient, and control requirements are also low, beginners' first choice.

**Advantages**:
- Extremely high stability, almost no energy disorder
- Low spiritual-power requirement, cultivators can maintain it synchronously in daily activities
- High fault tolerance, runs even if energy purity is slightly insufficient

**Disadvantages**:
- Circulation efficiency relatively low, energy utilization rate about 60%-70%
- Insufficient burst, cannot quickly mobilize large energy in emergencies
- Limited expansion space, late-stage improvement slow

**Adapted population**: cultivators newly entering Condensed Origin, individuals with weak spiritual power, weaker-constitution groups among carbon-based life, plant-type intelligent life.

## III. Mode B: Pulse Circulation

Pulse circulation on the basis of steady circulation introduces a "periodic speed variation" mechanism. Energy does not flow at constant speed, but runs in a pulse pattern of accelerate-maintain-decelerate per a certain rhythm. During acceleration, energy passes at high speed through specific paths, producing concentrated output; during deceleration, energy returns to a gentle state, completing absorption and repair.

**Core principle**: pulse circulation's law base is "energy's wave nature and resonance amplification." Energy itself is wavelike; pulse circulation uses periodic waves to amplify energy's instantaneous output several-fold, while completing recovery during intervals. This mode resembles the heart's contraction-diastole — not continuous output, but rhythmic output.

**Advantages**:
- Strong instantaneous burst, high practical value
- Energy utilization rate about 80%-90%, far higher than steady circulation
- Fast recovery, energy naturally recharges during pulse intervals
- Wide adaptability, can adapt to different scenarios by adjusting pulse frequency

**Disadvantages**:
- High control requirement, pulse rhythm disorder causes energy backlash
- Large spiritual-power consumption, long pulse circulation maintenance causes fatigue
- Steep initial learning curve, requires repeated rhythm-control practice

**Adapted population**: cultivators with some spiritual-power foundation, Deduction Iteration System cultivators, combat-type cultivators needing practical ability, energy-condensation races (naturally adapted to wave modes).

## IV. Mode C: Multi-Direction Interwoven Circulation

Multi-direction interwoven circulation is Condensed Origin's highest-tier internal circulation mode. The cultivator does not just build one main circulation path, but simultaneously runs multiple mutually interwoven energy loops — main circulation responsible for daily operation, branch loops responsible for specialized functions, intersections responsible for energy exchange and purification.

**Core principle**: multi-direction interwoven circulation's law base is "energy's multi-dimensional distribution and cross purification." Multiple circulation paths interweave like a neural network; energy at intersections auto-exchanges, auto-purifies, auto-adapts. This mode has the highest complexity, but also the highest efficiency — energy utilization rate can reach 95%+.

**Advantages**:
- Highest energy utilization, minimal waste
- Multiple loops can parallel-process different energy types
- Strongest expansion ability, late-stage seamless upgrade to Transform Origin's multi-form transformation
- Auto-purification function significantly reduces impurity accumulation

**Disadvantages**:
- Construction complexity extremely high, requires precise path-network planning
- Requires perception precision at least L5+ (see Entry 217)
- Initial construction cycle long, usually months of planning and debugging
- Once a branch has problems, requires precise locating and repair

**Adapted population**: cultivators with perception precision L5+, Space Jurisprudence Dispatch System cultivators, consciousness information races (naturally adapted to multi-line parallel), senior cultivators with clear long-term planning.

## V. Choice and Evolution of Three Modes

The three modes are not mutually exclusive, but an **evolution spectrum**: most cultivators start with Mode A, consolidate then upgrade to Mode B, ultimately per endowment choose whether to enter Mode C. The anchor network gives personalized advice when cultivators enter Condensed Origin — based on their perception precision, spiritual power, system choice, recommending the optimal starting mode and upgrade path.

Key principle: **no skipping, no forcing**. If perception precision is insufficient, do not force Mode C; if spiritual power is insufficient, do not force maintaining Mode B. The anchor network monitors circulation efficiency and energy disorder metrics in real time; when risk exceeds threshold it automatically warns, guiding cultivators to fall back to a safer mode.

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: the three modes' energy utilization rate progresses from 60% to 95%, choosing the adapted mode is avoiding energy waste; Mode C's auto-purification function eliminates impurity waste at the source
- **Anti-predation**: all three internal circulations are based on symbiotic circulation principles, energy from environmental free and anchor supply, not swallowing others' origin; circulation contains no predatory residual traces
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' cultivators' internal circulation is crude, energy utilization under 40% with impurity accumulation; Heng Yuan Zhou's three-mode system raises utilization to 95%, opening the efficiency gap at the root
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the three modes are adapted to all five Myriad Races genealogies — carbon-based use A/B, energy races use B, consciousness races use C, mechanical races can digitally simulate, hybrid races can combine
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the three modes' construction methods, path maps, and debugging tools are all free and open; the Alaya Network shares tens of thousands of path design schemes

## Conclusion

The three modes of internal circulation construction reveal Condensed Origin's true lesson: not "how much energy you sucked in," but "how good a circulation system you built." In predatory-faction systems, cultivators wildly devour energy but do not understand circulation management; the result is chaotic accumulation of energy in the body, looks huge but collapses at a touch. In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, the three modes progress layer by layer, pulling energy utilization efficiency to the extreme — this is "anti-energy waste" in Condensed Origin's concrete realization.
