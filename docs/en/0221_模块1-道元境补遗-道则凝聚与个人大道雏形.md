# Entry 221 | Dao Origin Realm Deep Supplement: Dao-Rule Condensation and Personal Great Dao Prototype

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 02, 11

## Introduction: From "understanding laws" to "my Dao"

Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s Dao Origin Realm is the five mortal-cultivation realms' apex. Law Origin cultivators "heard" laws; Dao Origin cultivators must answer a more fundamental question: **which road will I take using these laws?** This is "attaining Dao" — from understanding the cosmos's laws to forming one's own personal great Dao prototype. Dao Origin Realm is not "understanding more laws," but "integrating understood laws into a system of one's own."

The six top-level axioms run through Dao Origin equally: the anti-predation axiom requires personal Dao to be based on creation not predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires every Myriad Race to have "Dao-attainment" qualification and path; the collective ascension axiom requires personal Dao ultimately serve Myriad Races; the causal self-discipline axiom constrains personal Dao not interfere with others' karma; the law-first axiom ensures Dao-rule condensation's law definitions are stable, not retconned by faction disputes; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry deep-dives Dao Origin's core lesson: what is "Dao-rule condensation"? How does the personal great Dao prototype form? What is its fundamental difference from Dao Integration's "integrating with Dao"?

## I. Three Meanings of Dao-Rule Condensation

### 1.1 What Is "Dao-Rule"

Dao Origin's "Dao-rules" are not cosmic laws themselves, but **core principles the cultivator distills from laws, guiding their own cultivation and practice**. If laws are "how the cosmos runs," Dao-rules are "how I act accordingly."

Dao-rule condensation's process is essentially taking law information perceived since Law Origin, through one's own understanding, filtering, integration, distilling into a few core principles. These principles are not taught by others, but "grown" by the cultivator's own cultivation experience.

### 1.2 Three Stages of Dao-Rule Condensation

| Stage | Name | Feature | Output |
|-------|------|---------|--------|
| Stage 1 | Law accumulation | Broadly perceiving laws in various fields | Law knowledge library |
| Stage 2 | Law filtering | Filtering core laws per own endowment | Core law set (3-7 items) |
| Stage 3 | Dao-rule distillation | Sublimating core laws into personal Dao-rules | Personal great Dao prototype |

**Stage 1 law accumulation** is Dao Origin's foundation. Cultivators do not rush to "attain Dao," but broadly perceive laws across multiple fields — combat laws, life laws, space laws, time laws, karma laws (observation level), etc. The broader the accumulation, the more choices.

**Stage 2 law filtering** requires the cultivator to filter 3-7 core laws most fitting them from accumulated laws, per own endowment, interest, social responsibility. The filtering standard is not "which is strongest," but "which fits me best" — this filtering usually requires mentor and anchor network assistance.

**Stage 3 Dao-rule distillation** is Dao Origin's core. The cultivator distills filtered core laws into their own Dao-rules — not copying laws verbatim, but expressing them in own language, own practice way. Once Dao-rules are distilled, they become the guiding principles for all subsequent cultivation and practice.

## II. Features of the Personal Great Dao Prototype

Dao Origin's "personal great Dao" is still a prototype, not a complete "Dao." It has the following features:

- **Core clear**: 3-7 core Dao-rules, clearly expressed
- **Practically verifiable**: Dao-rules are not empty talk, but repeatedly verified in cultivation practice
- **Open evolution**: the prototype allows evolution and correction as cultivation deepens
- **Non-exclusivity**: personal great Dao does not exclude others' Dao, Myriad Races each have their own
- **Symbiosis-oriented**: in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, personal great Dao must be compatible with symbiotic ideals

### Example Dao-rules (a space-law-direction cultivator's Dao-rule prototype):
1. Space is not a container, but laws' fabric — can be understood, can be woven
2. Every inch of space has its texture; following the texture yields twice the result with half the effort
3. Opening space is not destruction, but "growing" new parts on the existing fabric
4. The space Dao's ultimate goal is to let Myriad Races flow freely in space

## III. The Divide Between Dao Origin and Dao Integration

Dao Origin's personal great Dao is a "prototype"; Dao Integration's great Dao is "fused with the cosmos." The two differ in:

| Dimension | Dao Origin Realm | Dao Integration Realm |
|-----------|------------------|------------------------|
| Dao state | Personal prototype | Deep fusion with cosmic laws |
| Law relationship | Using laws | Being laws |
| Influence range | Personal domain | Larger-scope rewriting of local laws |
| Life form | Material-dominant | Beginning high-dimensionalization |
| Assessment requirement | Dao-rule prototype clear | Dao heart in sync with cosmic symbiotic laws |

The key difference is: Dao Origin's Dao is "my Dao"; Dao Integration's Dao is "my Dao is the cosmic Dao." From "my Dao" to "my Dao is the cosmic Dao," a thorough Dao-heart sublimation is needed in between — this is precisely why breaking through to Dao Integration is a cosmic-level event.

## IV. Common Obstacles to Dao Origin Breakthrough

- **Vague Dao-rules**: cannot distill clear Dao-rules, cultivation practice lacks guidance
- **Narrow Dao-rules**: only choosing combat laws, neglecting life, karma and other dimensions, causing late-stage Dao-heart narrowness
- **Exclusive Dao-rules**: believing own Dao is "uniquely correct," excluding others' Dao — directly conflicts with the republican axiom of Myriad Races
- **Rigid Dao-rules**: treating Dao-rules as uncorrectable dogma, refusing to evolve as cultivation deepens
- **Predatory Dao-rules**: Dao-rules built on predatory experience (e.g., "the strong must devour the weak"), reject symbiotic laws

The anchor network has specialized identification and guidance mechanisms for these obstacles, ensuring Dao Origin cultivators' Dao-rules take the right path.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: Dao-rule condensation avoids the waste of "cultivating everything, excelling at nothing" — with core Dao-rules, cultivation resources concentrate on core directions
- **Anti-predation**: Dao-rules must be based on symbiotic ideals; the Predatory Faction's "Dao" will reveal its rejection at Dao Origin, cannot truly distill
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' cultivators have no concept of "attaining Dao," all abilities come from external soul rings; Heng Yuan Zhou's Dao Origin cultivators have their own Dao-rules, fundamentally transcending the "being equipped" mode
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: every Myriad Races group has its own Dao-rule condensation path, plant spirits' Dao, planetary consciousness's Dao, mechanical life's Dao each have features
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Dao-rule distillation methods and ten thousand personal great Dao prototype cases are all openly shared, paving the way for Dao Integration → Boundary-Breaking → higher realms

## Conclusion

Dao Origin Realm is the five mortal-cultivation realms' closing piece. In this realm, the cultivator finally turns from "learning laws" to "having my own Dao." In predatory-faction systems, cultivators spend their whole lives "collecting equipment" (soul rings, bloodline, magical treasures), never forming their own Dao; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, Dao Origin cultivators have their own Dao-rule prototype — this is not only power's maturity, but also personality and thought's maturity. Attaining Dao is not becoming stronger, but becoming more complete.

In the final analysis, Dao Origin Realm supplement · Dao-rule condensation and personal great Dao prototype described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
