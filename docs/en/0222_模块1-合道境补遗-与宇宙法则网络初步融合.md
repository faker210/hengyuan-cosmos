# Entry 222 | Dao Integration Realm Deep Supplement: Initial Fusion with the Cosmic Law Network

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 20, 21

## Introduction: From "my Dao" to "Dao is me"

Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s Dao Integration Realm is the four sacred-cultivation realms' first realm, and also the true watershed between mortal and sacred cultivation. Dao Origin cultivators have a personal great Dao prototype, but that is still "my Dao"; Dao Integration requires personal Dao to deeply fuse with the cosmic law network, reaching the state where "every move is in accord with Dao." This is not simple power enhancement, but **fundamental transformation of existence mode** — from "cultivating in the cosmos" to "cultivating together with the cosmos."

The six top-level axioms manifest the most concentrated constraint in Dao Integration: the anti-predation axiom requires an integrated Daoist's Dao heart to be in sync with symbiotic laws, predatory Dao will inevitably reject at this realm; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires integrated Daoists' power to serve Myriad Races; the collective ascension axiom requires integrated Daoists to lift Myriad Races along; the causal self-discipline axiom imposes the strictest constraint on integrated Daoists' law operations; the law-first axiom ensures the fusion definition is stable and self-consistent; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry deep-dives Dao Integration's core mechanism: what exactly is "initial fusion" with the cosmic law network? What are the fusion's markers, process, and risks?

## I. What Is the "Cosmic Law Network"

Heng Yuan Zhou's cosmos is not chaos, but a precise law network. This network is interwoven by countless laws, each law associated with and constraining every other law. Among the four-layer structure — material plane, energy layer, consciousness domain, law network — the law network is the bottommost operating-rules layer, governing all operations of the three upper layers.

Dao Integration's "fusion" is the cultivator's personal Dao producing **structural docking** with this law network — not "using" laws in the network, but "becoming" a living node in the network.

## II. Three Stages of Fusion

| Stage | Name | Fusion State | Marker |
|-------|------|--------------|--------|
| Stage 1 | Dao-heart resonance | Personal Dao-rules resonate with cosmic laws | During cultivation can "feel" the law network's existence |
| Stage 2 | Node docking | Personal Dao connects to the law network as a living node | Can mobilize law network resources in a larger range |
| Stage 3 | Domain unfold | Personal domain stably takes shape, bidirectional interaction with the law network | Personal domain is the law network's extension |

### 2.1 Dao-Heart Resonance Stage

Fusion's first step is not technical operation, but **Dao-heart sync**. Dao Origin's personal great Dao prototype must fundamentally align with cosmic symbiotic laws — if personal Dao is predatory, it will produce "frequency rejection" with the symbiotic law network, no matter how strong the power, Dao Integration cannot be achieved.

This is why the mind-state dimension takes 25% in the four-dimensional assessment — Dao Integration is not "power enough suffices," but "Dao heart must be right." A cultivator harboring predation, even if power reaches Dao Integration standards, their Dao heart's rejection from cosmic symbiotic laws will prevent fusion; forced fusion only causes Dao-heart collapse.

### 2.2 Node Docking Stage

After Dao-heart sync, personal Dao begins structural docking with the law network. The cultivator no longer needs to "deliberately call" laws — their intent naturally connects to the network, law power responds like arms and fingers. Cultivators at this stage can rewrite local laws' operation in a larger range, and their personal domain begins to stably take shape.

The node docking process is assisted throughout by the anchor network: anchor structure (Document 20) provides docking interfaces, the symbiosis network (Document 21) provides channel monitoring, ensuring the docking process does not damage the law network's overall stability.

### 2.3 Domain Unfold Stage

Fusion completion's marker is the stable unfold of the personal law domain. This domain is not a "personal small world," but **the law network's local extension** — law operation within the domain is fully synchronized with the cosmic law network; cultivators in the domain can directly call network resources.

After Dao Integration's domain unfolds, the cultivator's life form begins evolving to higher dimensions, partially shedding material body constraints. Their consciousness can simultaneously operate on the material plane and the law network layer; perception range and response speed both undergo a qualitative leap.

## III. Risks and Supervision in Fusion

Dao Integration's fusion process accompanies major risks, with the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision intervening throughout:

- **Dao-heart rejection risk**: when personal Dao rejects symbiotic laws, the system automatically halts fusion, guiding the cultivator to correct the Dao heart
- **Network overload risk**: personal domain unfolding too large may affect law network stability; the anchor network monitors in real time and limits domain expansion speed
- **Law misoperation risk**: integrated Daoists' rewriting of laws must be filed in the anchor network; unfiled law rewriting auto-rolls back
- **Karma disturbance risk**: integrated Daoists' law operations have large influence range; the causal self-discipline axiom requires all operations to be filed in advance

## IV. Social Role of Dao Integration Realm

Dao Integration cultivators are civilization's "grandmasters," with important influence on civilization's development direction. Their social roles include:

- **Grand mentors**: guiding Dao Origin and below cultivators' Dao-rule distillation
- **Law consultants**: providing high-level theoretical support for civilization's law engineering
- **Regional anchor guardians**: responsible for maintaining one or more regional anchors' stability
- **Cross-system coordinators**: coordinating law understanding differences among the three-hundred systems

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: Dao Integration fusion raises the cultivator's law invocation efficiency from Law Origin's about 40% to 80%+, eliminating intermediate loss
- **Anti-predation**: Dao Integration's Dao-heart sync mechanism fundamentally excludes predators — predatory Dao and symbiotic law network naturally reject; forced Dao Integration inevitably fails
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' highest is only the "fierce realm" ceiling; Dao Integration in Heng Yuan Zhou is civilization grandmasters' standard stage, the two are not on one dimension
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Dao Integration fusion is open to every Myriad Race group, each has its own integration way — energy life directly fuses with the energy law network, consciousness life fuses with consciousness-domain laws
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Dao Integration fusion methods, Dao-heart calibration tools, and docking protocols are all open, paving the way for Boundary-Breaking → World-Creation → Origin → higher realms

## Conclusion

Dao Integration's essence is not "stronger," but "larger" — the cultivator's existence expands from the individual to the law network layer. In predatory-faction systems, "Dao Integration" often means "my own Dao overrides everything," ultimately ending up alone; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, "Dao Integration" means "my Dao merges into the cosmic symbiotic Dao," ultimately leading to Myriad Races' common prosperity. One does subtraction, one does multiplication; this is the fundamental difference of the first sacred-cultivation realm.
