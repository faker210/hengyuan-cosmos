# Entry 223 | Boundary-Breaking Realm Deep Supplement: Plane Barrier Breakthrough and Cross-Plane Capability

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 28, 24

## Introduction: First breaking free of the plane's cage

Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s Boundary-Breaking Realm is the four sacred-cultivation realms' second realm. Dao Integration cultivators, though fused with the cosmic law network, are still constrained by their home plane's law framework — like a fish swimming freely in the ocean, still in the ocean. Boundary-Breaking's core qualitative leap is **first breaking free of plane laws' limits, achieving free cross-plane operation**.

The six top-level axioms manifest the strictest constraint in Boundary-Breaking's cross-plane capability: the anti-predation axiom forbids cross-plane predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires cross-plane channels be fairly open to Myriad Races; the collective ascension axiom requires cross-plane exploration to serve Myriad Races' common progress; the causal self-discipline axiom is strictest in cross-plane operations — observe first then limited interference, return knowledge only not disturb karma; the law-first axiom ensures boundary-breaking laws' definitions are stable and self-consistent; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry deep-dives Boundary-Breaking's core ability: how are plane barriers broken through? What are the rules and boundaries of cross-plane operation?

## I. The Essence of Plane Barriers

Every plane has a "plane membrane" — a law-level boundary barrier. Its function is:
- Maintaining the internal law system's independence and stability
- Preventing different planes' laws from interfering with each other
- Protecting plane ecology from external energy impact

Before Dao Integration, no matter how strong a cultivator is, they cannot penetrate this membrane — not insufficient power, but **insufficient law-level permission**. Boundary-Breaking's breakthrough essentially is obtaining law permission to operate the plane membrane.

## II. Three Ability Tiers of Boundary-Breaking

| Tier | Ability | Description |
|------|---------|-------------|
| Tier 1 | Perception penetration | Perceiving the plane membrane's existence and structure |
| Tier 2 | Stable traversal | Safely cross-plane traversal with anchor assistance |
| Tier 3 | Free operation | Independently traversing, opening channels, repairing cracks without anchor assistance |

### 2.1 Perception Penetration Tier

Boundary-Breaking entry's first ability. The cultivator for the first time "sees" the law membrane wrapping their plane — it is not a physical barrier, but a law-level boundary. After perceiving the plane membrane, the cultivator begins to understand its structure: where is thick, where is thin, where are natural cracks.

### 2.2 Stable Traversal Tier

With the anchor network's assistance, the cultivator can safely traverse the plane membrane into adjacent planes. This tier's traversal has several features:
- Must operate near anchor nodes, using the anchor's cross-plane channel
- Must file before traversal, the Alaya Network records traversal trajectory
- After traversal must obey the target plane's law environment, not carry home-plane law interference
- Stay duration limited, preventing karma disturbance accumulation

### 2.3 Free Operation Tier

Boundary-Breaking apex ability. The cultivator no longer relies on anchor assistance, able to independently perform cross-plane operations:
- **Independent traversal**: penetrating the plane membrane at any position
- **Channel opening**: establishing a stable cross-plane channel between two planes
- **Crack repair**: repairing naturally occurring or artificially damaged plane membrane cracks
- **Environmental adaptation**: freely surviving and operating in different planes' law environments

## III. Iron Rules of Cross-Plane Operation

Boundary-Breaking cultivators' cross-plane capability is not unlimited; the causal self-discipline axiom is strictest in this realm:

1. **Filing system**: all cross-plane operations must be filed in advance, stating purpose, duration, possible impact
2. **Observation priority**: entering a new plane observe first, do not immediately interfere
3. **Limited interference**: only perform low-impact operations like knowledge return, technical support; do not change the target plane's historical trajectory
4. **Karma isolation**: cross-plane operation's karmic impact is strictly limited to the operator themselves, not spreading to the target plane
5. **Emergency evacuation**: any abnormal situation immediately evacuates, no risk-taking

These iron rules exist because cross-plane disturbance consequences may affect the entire plane chain's karmic stability. One unfiled cross-plane interference may trigger unpredictable chain reactions in the target plane — this is why Boundary-Breaking strong ones' cross-plane permissions are strictly controlled by the anchor network.

## IV. Social Role of Boundary-Breaking Realm

Boundary-Breaking strong ones are "plane-level" beings; their social roles include:

- **Plane explorers**: exploring new planes, assessing their ecology and law features
- **Plane bridges**: maintaining cross-plane channels, promoting Myriad Races' cross-plane exchange
- **Crack repairers**: monitoring and repairing plane membrane cracks, preventing plane collapse
- **Cross-plane resource coordinators**: allocating resources among different planes, supporting weak planes
- **Crisis responders**: when a plane encounters disaster, performing cross-plane rescue

## V. Boundary-Breaking's Deep Binding with Causal Self-Discipline

Boundary-Breaking is the realm where the causal self-discipline axiom is executed most strictly. The reason is simple: cross-plane operation's influence range far exceeds single-plane operation. A law change controllable on the home plane may, after cross-planing, amplify into disaster due to different law environments.

The Alaya Network's monitoring of Boundary-Breaking strong ones includes:
- Traversal trajectory recorded throughout
- Karmic impact simulation prediction for every cross-plane operation
- Abnormal interference auto-warning and permission freezing
- Karma disturbance retrospection and repair

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: Boundary-Breaking's cross-plane exploration is not for predating new plane resources, but for knowledge sharing and ecological mutual rescue; exploration outputs all feed back to Myriad Races, not privatized
- **Anti-predation**: cross-plane channels are fairly open to Myriad Races, not monopolized; cross-plane predatory cultivation is forbidden, violators have boundary-breaking permissions frozen
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' cultivators cannot even leave their own plane, let alone cross-plane; Heng Yuan Zhou's Boundary-Breaking are plane-level strong ones, the dimensions differ
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Boundary-Breaking does not restrict race, each has its own cross-plane explorers; Myriad Races' five genealogies have different adaptation advantages in cross-plane
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cross-plane technology, channel maintenance methods, and plane ecology knowledge are all open, paving the way for World-Creation → Origin → Multiverse Realm

## Conclusion

Boundary-Breaking's core is not "fly farther," but "walk with more rules." In predatory-faction systems, cross-plane often means cross-plane predation — going to a new plane means looting resources then leaving; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, cross-plane means observe first, then limited interference, return knowledge only not disturb karma. The greater the ability, the stricter the rules — this is Boundary-Breaking's true meaning.
