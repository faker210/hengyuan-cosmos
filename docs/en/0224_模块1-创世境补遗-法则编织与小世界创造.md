# Entry 224 | World-Creation Realm Deep Supplement: Law Weaving and Small-World Creation

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 64, 60

## Introduction: From law users to law designers

Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s World-Creation Realm is the four sacred-cultivation realms' third realm. Boundary-Breaking cultivators have learned to traverse between planes; World-Creation cultivators obtain a more fundamental ability — **creating a complete world from scratch in isolated space**. This is not "magic trick" creation from nothing, but per cosmic laws' bottom logic, weaving a self-consistent law system, turning a blank space from "non-existent" into "existent."

The six top-level axioms are concentrated in World-Creation: the anti-predation axiom requires created worlds serve Myriad Races not be privately hoarded; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires world-creation ability be open to qualified individuals among Myriad Races; the collective ascension axiom requires created worlds feed back to civilization; the causal self-discipline axiom constrains the creation process not to affect existing worlds' karma; the law-first axiom is World-Creation's direct law — created worlds must be self-consistent, stable, expandable, otherwise they collapse on their own; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry deep-dives World-Creation's core mechanism: what is the law weaving process? What is a small world's complete from-nothing-to-something process?

## I. The Essence of Law Weaving

World-creation is not "making things," but "weaving laws." A world is a world not because it has mountains, waters, and people, but because it has a **self-consistent law system** — gravity law, energy law, time law, life law... these laws jointly determine the world's operation. A world with non-self-consistent laws collapses on its own during creation; this is why World-Creation breakthroughs are extremely rare.

Law weaving's essence is the cultivator using their deep fusion with the cosmic law network to "grow" a new law system in isolated space. This system does not copy existing planes, but is independently woven per design goals — yet must follow cosmic laws' bottom logic, otherwise it cannot hold.

## II. Five Stages of World-Creation

| Stage | Name | Core Task | Output |
|-------|------|-----------|--------|
| Stage 1 | Space opening | Opening blank space with isolation technology support | Stable isolated space |
| Stage 2 | Law base weaving | Weaving the most basic law skeleton | World law framework |
| Stage 3 | Matter-energy injection | Injecting matter and energy per law framework | World physical layer |
| Stage 4 | Life design | Designing the life system under the law framework | Life prototype |
| Stage 5 | Self-consistency verification | Whole-network verification of whether the world is self-consistent and stable | A runnable small world |

### 2.1 Space Opening Stage

World-creation's first step is opening a stable isolated space. This requires isolated space construction technology (Document 64) support — World-Creation cultivators can independently complete space opening, their personal domain expanding into isolated space. This space must have clear boundaries with the external cosmos, but the boundary itself does not affect the external cosmos's operation.

### 2.2 Law Base Weaving Stage

This is World-Creation's most core work. The cultivator needs to weave the world's basic law skeleton:
- **Space-time laws**: time flow rate, spatial dimensions
- **Energy laws**: energy forms, transformation rules
- **Matter laws**: matter composition, physical constants
- **Life laws**: life forms, evolution rules

The law base is not "more is better," but "more self-consistent is better." A concise self-consistent law system is easier to stably run than a complex contradictory one. World-creators must find balance between "design freedom" and "law self-consistency."

### 2.3 Matter-Energy Injection Stage

After the law skeleton is built, begin injecting matter and energy. This step is not "randomly putting things in," but strictly per the law framework — different law systems require different matter compositions. A world with high-spiritual-energy laws has matter composition completely different from a world with low-spiritual-energy laws.

### 2.4 Life Design Stage

If the world-creation goal includes life, the cultivator needs to design the life system under the law framework. This step is extremely cautious — life law design directly affects the world's long-term stability. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization requires all created worlds' life design to pass ethical review, ensuring no predatory life forms are designed.

### 2.5 Self-Consistency Verification Stage

After the world initially takes shape, it must pass the Alaya Network's comprehensive self-consistency verification:
- Are laws contradictory among themselves
- Does matter-energy match laws
- Can the life system stably evolve
- Can the world run long-term
- Is there expansion room

After verification passes, this small world officially becomes the World-Creation cultivator's "work."

## III. Functional Positioning of Small Worlds

In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, small worlds created by World-Creation strong ones are not private property but civilization's public resources. Their functional positioning includes:

- **Cultivation resource zones**: cultivation assistance in specific law environments
- **Experimental fields**: safe experimental environments for law engineering
- **Civilization sanctuaries**: life refuge for disaster-stricken planes
- **New-race cultivation zones**: assisting new races' safe evolution
- **Myriad Races exchange spaces**: neutral platforms for cross-race cultural exchange

Creators have guardianship responsibility for their created worlds, but no monopoly rights — any Myriad Races member may use these worlds through legitimate application.

## IV. The Rarity of World-Creation Realm

World-Creation breakthrough is one of the cosmos's rarest events. The reason is not insufficient power, but **insufficient design ability** — the vast majority of cultivators can use laws, but cannot design a self-consistent law system from scratch. This requires the cultivator not only to deeply understand laws, but also to have systems thinking, creativity, and rigorous self-consistency verification ability.

In the 20% Activation Strategy, World-Creation strong ones' proportion is far lower than Origin Realm — this is normal, because world-creation itself is a higher-order ability.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: world-creation is not energy waste from nothing, but "growing" new worlds within existing law frameworks; well-designed small worlds' long-term output far exceeds initial investment
- **Anti-predation**: created worlds serve Myriad Races, not privately hoarded or monopolized; using created worlds as predation bases is forbidden
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems cannot even leave their planes, let alone create worlds; Heng Yuan Zhou's World-Creation are world-level strong ones, civilization's creators
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: world-creation ability is open to qualified individuals among Myriad Races, each race's creators design worlds with distinctive features
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: world-creation methodology, law weaving technology, and self-consistency verification tools are all open, paving the way for Origin → Multiverse → Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm

## Conclusion

World-Creation's essence is "from builder to designer." In predatory-faction systems, strong ones' ultimate dream is "occupying existing worlds as boss"; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, World-Creation strong ones' ultimate ability is "designing a new world from scratch." The former is grabbing shares of the old cake; the latter is creating a new cake — this is the anti-predation axiom's manifestation at the highest realm: develop increments, do not consume stock.
