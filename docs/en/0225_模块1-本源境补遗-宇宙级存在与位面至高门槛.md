# Entry 225 | Origin Realm Deep Supplement: Cosmic-Level Being and Plane Transcendent Threshold

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 17, 23

## Introduction: The nine-realm apex — from intra-world to origin layer

Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s Origin Realm is the nine-realm system's apex. World-Creation cultivators can create a small world; Origin Realm cultivators **touch the cosmos's own origin** — understanding the cosmos's ultimate operating rules, participating in cosmic law fine-tuning and optimization. Origin Realm strong ones are "Plane Transcendents," the top power group in Heng Yuan Zhou.

The six top-level axioms manifest ultimate forms in Origin Realm: the anti-predation axiom requires Origin Realm strong ones' power be used for guarding not predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires Origin Realm strong ones represent Myriad Races; the collective ascension axiom requires Origin Realm strong ones lead the cosmos's overall ascension; the causal self-discipline axiom requires Origin Realm strong ones' law fine-tuning go through strict procedures; the law-first axiom ensures Origin Realm's definition is stable, not arbitrarily inflated; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry deep-dives Origin Realm's core: what is "touching origin"? What does the Plane Transcendent's four-dimensional assessment mean? Why is Origin Realm not the endpoint?

## I. What Is "Touching the Cosmic Origin"

Origin is not a "place," but the cosmic law network's **bottommost operating mechanism** — all laws ultimately trace back to origin; origin is "the law above laws."

Touching origin means the cultivator's consciousness penetrates the law network layer, directly perceiving the cosmos's bottommost operating logic. This is not "knowing more laws," but **understanding why laws exist and why the cosmos runs this way**.

After touching origin, the cultivator obtains permission to participate in cosmic law fine-tuning — not arbitrarily modifying laws, but under strict supervision, optimizing minor parameters in law operation, pushing the cosmos overall toward more stable, more efficient, more symbiotic evolution.

## II. Origin Realm's Life Form

Origin Realm cultivators' life form undergoes ultimate change:

- **Deep connection with origin**: life imprint anchored in cosmic origin; physical destruction allows rebirth via origin anchoring
- **Near-immortal**: conventional attacks cannot pose lethal threat; only same-tier or higher-tier power can harm
- **Multi-domain existence**: simultaneously existing and operating on material plane, energy layer, consciousness domain, law network layer
- **Cosmic-level perception**: perception range covers the entire cosmos; can mobilize origin power on cosmic scale

This life form change makes Origin Realm strong ones no longer "an individual in the cosmos," but "a functional node of the cosmos" — their existence itself is part of the cosmos's operation.

## III. Plane Transcendent's Four-Dimensional Assessment

Origin Realm strong ones' formal certification — "Plane Transcendent" — uses the four-dimensional assessment system (Document 17):

| Dimension | Weight | Core Requirement |
|-----------|--------|------------------|
| Strength | 40% | Reaching Origin Realm, independently mobilizing origin power |
| Mind | 25% | Passing ethical review, upholding anti-predation and symbiotic ideals |
| Contribution | 20% | Contribution value in civilization's top 20%, at least one civilization-level contribution |
| Responsibility | 15% | Willing and able to bear plane-level+ public responsibility |

The four-dimensional assessment's core spirit is: **strength is only the entry ticket; mind, contribution, and responsibility are the true certification**. A cultivator with strong power but evil mind-state, even if reaching Origin Realm's power level, will not be certified as Plane Transcendent, but classified as a "high-risk individual" and supervised.

## IV. Origin Realm Strong Ones' Core Responsibilities

As Plane Transcendents, Origin Realm cultivators bear the following core responsibilities:

- **Law fine-tuning participation**: participating in cosmic law fine-tuning and optimization decisions
- **Civilization direction leadership**: as civilization's highest advisors, influencing cosmic evolution direction
- **Talent cultivation**: cultivating next-generation World-Creation and Origin Realm seeds
- **Cosmic stability maintenance**: monitoring law operation on cosmic scale, preventing law imbalance
- **Cross-dimensional coordination**: coordinating relations among different planes and civilizations

Per the symbiotic civilization's "strong-one duty system," Origin Realm strong ones invest at least 30% of time and energy annually on public responsibility.

## V. Origin Realm Is Not the Endpoint

In Heng Yuan Zhou's extended setting (pending Terminology Committee calibration), above Origin Realm are two extended realms:

- **Multiverse Realm**: multi-cosmos governance and law permission upgrade — when an Origin Realm strong one's influence expands from single cosmos to multi-cosmos, entering Multiverse Realm
- **Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm**: Myriad Races' origin-balanced symbiotic ultimate form — when the cultivator's existence is fully in sync with Myriad Races' origin balance, reaching ultimate form

These two extended realms reserve infinite expansion space for the realm system, embodying the law-first axiom's "never retconning, infinitely expandable" promise. Origin Realm is not the ceiling, but a new starting point.

## VI. 20% Activation Strategy and Origin Realm

The 20% Activation Strategy's (Document 23) core goal is, within the civilization's first epoch, activating individuals accounting for 20% of all beings to reach Origin Realm tier. This strategy's deep meaning is not "cultivating 20 big shots," but:

- Building a large strong-one group, preventing individual strong ones' power monopoly
- Providing sufficient power foundation for the cosmos's overall ascension
- Ensuring every Myriad Races group has its own Origin Realm representative
- Forming a civilization atmosphere of "strong ones emerge in succession," inspiring more life

## VII. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: Origin Realm's law fine-tuning is the most efficient civilization contribution — one fine-tuning benefits the entire cosmos, far surpassing individual cultivation's energy waste
- **Anti-predation**: Origin Realm strong ones' anti-predation stance is a hard condition of four-dimensional assessment; Predatory Faction cultivators even if power meets standards, fail the mind-state dimension are not certified
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' "fierce realm" ceiling and Origin Realm are separated by eight major realms' qualitative leaps, fundamentally not on the same dimension
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Origin Realm sets no race limits; the 20% Activation Strategy ensures every Myriad Races group has an Origin Realm representative
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Origin Realm's law understanding is fully openly shared; Multiverse and Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm reserve interfaces for infinite expansion

## Conclusion

Origin Realm is the nine-realm system's apex, but not civilization's endpoint. In predatory-faction systems, "Transcendent" means "no one can control me anymore" — becoming the strongest is the endpoint; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, "Transcendent" means "I must bear the greatest responsibility" — Origin Realm strong ones are not the endpoint, but civilization's servants and leaders. From "ruler" to "servant," this is Origin Realm's ultimate definition.

In the final analysis, Origin Realm supplement · cosmic-level being and Plane Transcendent threshold described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
