# Entry 226 | Multiverse Realm Deep Supplement: Multi-Cosmos Governance and Law Permission Upgrade

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 14, 28, 02

> Note: Multiverse Realm is an extended setting; terminology "pending Terminology Committee calibration."

## Introduction: The existence leap from single-cosmos to multi-cosmos

Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s Origin Realm strong ones touch the single-cosmos origin; Multiverse Realm is the extended realm above Origin Realm — when the cultivator's influence expands from single-cosmos to **multi-cosmos**, law permission undergoes qualitative upgrade, entering Multiverse Realm. This realm's core is no longer "fine-tuning laws within one cosmos," but "coordinating law relations among multiple cosmoses."

The six top-level axioms manifest cross-cosmos-scale constraints in Multiverse Realm: the anti-predation axiom forbids cross-cosmos predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires multi-cosmos Myriad Races equality; the collective ascension axiom requires multi-cosmos coordinated ascension; the causal self-discipline axiom reaches its extreme in cross-cosmos operations; the law-first axiom ensures Multiverse Realm's law definitions are stable, infinitely expandable without collapse; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry deep-dives Multiverse Realm's core definition: what is "multi-cosmos governance"? What are the specific contents of law permission upgrade?

## I. Why Multiverse Realm Is Needed

Origin Realm strong ones' power covers a single cosmos. But Heng Yuan Zhou's cosmology is not "only one cosmos" — multiple cosmoses exist, each with its own law system and Myriad Races civilization. When Origin Realm strong ones' exploration and responsibility expand to other cosmoses, single-cosmos law permission is no longer enough:

- Cross-cosmos law coordination requires higher-tier permission
- Different cosmoses' law differences require a unified coordination framework
- Multi-cosmos karmic isolation and communication require new mechanisms

Multiverse Realm is the extended realm set to solve these problems. It is not "hit stronger," but **expansion of existence scope and responsibility scope**.

## II. Multiverse Realm's Core Capabilities

| Capability Dimension | Single-Cosmos Origin Realm | Multiverse Realm |
|----------------------|------------------------------|-------------------|
| Perception range | Single cosmos | Multi-cosmos network |
| Law permission | Fine-tuning within single cosmos | Cross-cosmos law coordination |
| Existence form | Single-cosmos origin anchoring | Multi-cosmos nodes simultaneously anchored |
| Karma influence | Controllable within single cosmos | Cross-cosmos karma isolation and coordination |
| Life form | Cosmic-level | Hyper-cosmic-level |

### 2.1 Multi-Cosmos Perception Network

Multiverse cultivators' consciousness is no longer confined to single cosmos, but has built a perception network covering multiple cosmoses. They can simultaneously perceive the law operation status of multiple cosmoses, monitoring each cosmos's stability and development. This is not "watching many screens simultaneously," but **expansion of existence itself** — their consciousness naturally distributes across multiple cosmoses.

### 2.2 Cross-Cosmos Law Coordination

Different cosmoses' law systems each have features. Multiverse cultivators' core work is not "unifying all cosmoses into one law system," but **coordinating relations among different law systems** — preventing law conflicts, promoting law exchange, and when necessary building cross-cosmos law bridges.

This coordination work's complexity far exceeds single-cosmos law fine-tuning; it requires the cultivator to simultaneously understand multiple different law systems and find ways for them to symbiotically coexist.

### 2.3 Multi-Cosmos Anchoring and Existence

Multiverse cultivators' life imprint is no longer anchored to a single cosmos's origin, but **simultaneously anchored to multiple cosmoses' origin nodes**. This means they exist and operate in multiple cosmoses simultaneously; any single cosmos's disaster cannot completely annihilate them — this is an existence form one step further than Origin Realm's "near-immortal."

## III. Multiverse Realm's Law Permission Upgrade

From Origin Realm to Multiverse Realm, law permission undergoes the following key upgrades:

- **Permission scope**: from single-cosmos law fine-tuning right upgraded to multi-cosmos law coordination right
- **Permission tier**: from "participating in fine-tuning" upgraded to "cross-cosmos law architecture design"
- **Permission constraint**: constrained by multi-cosmos symbiosis protocols, stricter than single cosmos
- **Permission sharing**: Multiverse strong ones' cross-cosmos knowledge shared network-wide

This permission upgrade is not "do whatever one wants," but "can do good on a larger scale." The larger the permission, the stricter the constraint — this is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's consistent iron rule.

## IV. Social Role of Multiverse Realm

Multiverse strong ones are "hyper-cosmic-level" beings; their social roles include:

- **Cross-cosmos envoys**: representing Heng Yuan Zhou in contacting and exchanging with other cosmoses' civilizations
- **Law architects**: designing cross-cosmos law bridges and coordination mechanisms
- **Multi-cosmos crisis response**: when multiple cosmoses face common crises, performing coordinated response
- **Myriad Races cross-cosmos coordinators**: coordinating relations among different cosmoses' Myriad Races civilizations
- **Hyper-cosmic knowledge inheritance**: returning cross-cosmos law understanding to the Alaya Network

## V. Multiverse Realm and Causal Self-Discipline's Ultimate Practice

Cross-cosmos operation's karmic impact far exceeds cross-plane operation. Multiverse strong ones' causal self-discipline practices include:

- **Multi-cosmos karma isolation**: any operation's karmic impact is strictly limited within the target cosmos
- **Cross-cosmos observation priority**: entering a new cosmos observe for a long time first, do not rush to interfere
- **Knowledge return principle**: only return knowledge and technology, do not change the target cosmos's historical trajectory
- **Multi-cosmos arbitration mechanism**: matters involving cross-cosmos interests are negotiated by an arbitration committee composed of Multiverse strong ones

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: cross-cosmos law coordination avoids repeated waste of each cosmos independently groping; one cosmos's experience can be shared to other cosmoses via the Alaya Network
- **Anti-predation**: Multiverse Realm strictly forbids cross-cosmos predation — any cross-cosmos interference for predatory purposes immediately freezes permission
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems cannot even leave their own plane; Multiverse Realm is a cross-cosmos-level being, dimensional gap unquantifiable
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Multiverse Realm sets no race threshold; every Myriad Races group's Origin Realm strong ones have the opportunity to grow into Multiverse Realm
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cross-cosmos law coordination methods and multi-cosmos anchoring technology are all open, reserving interfaces for Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm

## Conclusion

Multiverse Realm is the first proof of Heng Yuan Zhou's realm system's infinite expandability. When Origin Realm is no longer seen as the "endpoint," but as the starting point toward higher tiers, the realm system gains self-renewal ability — this is precisely the law-first axiom's "never retconning, infinitely expandable" promise in practice. Multiverse Realm is not for "enjoying fighting more," but for letting Myriad Races' symbiotic civilization cross cosmic boundaries and continue growing.
