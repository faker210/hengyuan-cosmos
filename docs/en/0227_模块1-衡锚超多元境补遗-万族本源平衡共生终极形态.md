## I. What Is "Myriad Races Origin-Balanced Symbiosis"

Myriad Races origin-balanced symbiosis is not egalitarianism of "all races equally strong," but **each Myriad Races group's origin development in dynamic balance**:

- No race's origin over-inflates
- No race's origin is suppressed and atrophies
- Origin energy circulation between races is unobstructed
- Every race's origin characteristics are fully developed
- The overall origin ecology is rich and stable

A Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm cultivator, their existence itself is the **living anchor point** of this balance — their consciousness deeply connects with Myriad Races' origin network, able to perceive balance's drift and calibrate when drift is too large.

A Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm cultivator's consciousness no longer belongs to a single cosmos or single race, but is fully in sync with Myriad Races' origin network. They can simultaneously perceive carbon-based material races, energy condensation races, mechanical construction races, consciousness information races, and hybrid variation races' origin state — not "listening to reports," but **directly perceiving**.

When some group's origin development is too fast (may cause inflation) or too slow (may cause atrophy), the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm cultivator perceives this imbalance and performs fine-tuning calibration. The calibration method is not "command," but **guidance** — through the Alaya Network conveying information, adjusting resource allocation, supporting weak groups.

A Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm cultivator is not "Myriad Races' king," but "Myriad Races' balance anchor." They do not rule any race, do not command any individual; their existence only ensures Myriad Races' origin ecology's balance and stability. This fundamentally opposes the predatory-faction system's "strongest rules all" mode.

Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is called the ultimate form, not because it is "strongest," but because it **achieves complete closure of the five axioms**:

Although Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is called the "ultimate form," it is not the endpoint. The law-first axiom promises "never retconning, infinitely expandable" — this means when new cosmic structures, new life forms, new law dimensions appear in the future, the realm system can naturally expand, and above Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm there can be new tiers.

Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's "ultimate" refers to **the ultimate within the currently known law framework**, not a metaphysical absolute endpoint. The cosmos is open, laws are expandable, and the realm system is also infinite.
