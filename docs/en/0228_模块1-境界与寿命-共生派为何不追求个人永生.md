# Entry 228 | Realm and Lifespan: Why the Symbiotic Faction Does Not Pursue Personal Immortality

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 04, 01, 07

## Introduction: Immortality is predationism's ultimate illusion

In Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s cultivation system, realm advancement indeed accompanies lifespan extension — this is natural law, no need to evade. But the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's attitude toward "lifespan" fundamentally differs from predatory-faction systems: the Predatory Faction pursues personal immortality, believing the longer you live the stronger; the Symbiotic Faction believes **personal immortality is not the goal, civilization's perpetual continuation is the goal**. One person living ten thousand years is nothing; Myriad Races civilization's perpetual evolution is the true achievement.

The six top-level axioms run through the relationship between realm and lifespan equally: the anti-predation axiom forbids extending lifespan by predatory means; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires lifespan extension benefit Myriad Races not be monopolized by individuals; the collective ascension axiom requires personal lifespan serve civilization's continuation; the causal self-discipline axiom constrains that life-death cycle's natural law not be artificially broken; the law-first axiom ensures the correspondence between lifespan and realm is stable and self-consistent; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry answers a core question: why do Symbiotic Faction cultivators not pursue personal immortality? How should lifespan extension brought by realm advancement be used?

## I. The Natural Correspondence Between Realm and Lifespan

In Heng Yuan Zhou's law system, realm advancement indeed accompanies lifespan extension; this is natural law:

| Realm | Approximate Lifespan | Principle of Lifespan Extension |
|-------|----------------------|----------------------------------|
| Sense Origin Realm | Slightly above mortals | Energy infiltration delays aging |
| Condensed Origin Realm | 2-3x mortals | Internal circulation strengthens life mechanisms |
| Transform Origin Realm | 5-10x mortals | Energy qualitative leap repairs cells |
| Law Origin Realm | 20-50x mortals | Laws participate in life regulation |
| Dao Origin Realm | 100x+ mortals | Personal Dao maintains life form |
| Dao Integration Realm | Several thousand years | Life form high-dimensionalizes |
| Boundary-Breaking Realm | Cross-plane immortal | Existence does not depend on a single flesh body |
| World-Creation Realm | Near-immortal | Can persist in self-created worlds |
| Origin Realm | Anchored to origin | Physical destruction allows origin rebirth |

This is the result of laws' natural operation, not what cultivators "pursue." Realm reached, lifespan naturally extends; not reached, forcing it is useless.

## II. Why the Symbiotic Faction Does Not Pursue "Personal Immortality"

### 2.1 Immortality's Essence Is Interrupting Karmic Cycle

In predatory-faction systems, cultivators pursue "eternal youth" and "living as long as heaven and earth." But from the law level, life and death are part of the cosmos's energy cycle — old life's dissipation frees energy and positions for new life. Forcibly pursuing personal immortality is essentially **interrupting the natural rhythm of karmic cycle**, letting one life long occupy energy and resources that could flow.

This directly conflicts with the anti-predation axiom: personal immortality's essence is permanently robbing other lives' evolution resource space. You live ten thousand years, the spiritual veins, cultivation resources, and social positions you occupy never free up — this is not "strong," this is "squatting."

### 2.2 True Immortality Is Civilization's Perpetual Continuation

What the Symbiotic Faction pursues is not "I never die," but "Myriad Races civilization always moves forward." Even if a cultivator's flesh body dissipates, their Dao-rules, their knowledge, their contributions have already merged into civilization's whole through the Alaya Network — this is true "immortality": not personal consciousness's eternal continuation, but **personal contributions' perpetual inheritance**.

Origin Realm strong ones' "origin-anchored rebirth" ability is also not for "living forever," but for continuing to bear responsibility when civilization needs it. When they believe their mission is complete, they may choose natural dissipation, yielding the origin-anchored position to the next generation of strong ones.

### 2.3 Generational Turnover Is Civilization Evolution's Necessary Mechanism

A healthy civilization needs generational turnover. If the older generation of cultivators forever occupies the highest positions, the new generation has no growth space — this is the root of class solidification. The Symbiotic Faction encourages cultivators to take the initiative to "pass the baton" after bearing current responsibility: gradually yielding law fine-tuning participation rights and civilization decision seats to younger cultivators.

This active baton-passing is not "giving up power," but **keeping civilization's metabolic vitality**. A civilization without generational turnover, no matter how strong, will eventually stagnate due to rigidity.

## III. The Correct Way to Use Lifespan Extension

Symbiotic Faction cultivators do not pursue immortality, but also do not waste the time brought by lifespan extension. Extended lifespan should be used for:

- **Knowledge inheritance**: passing cultivation insights and law understanding to the next generation
- **Civilization contribution**: using more time to do more public service
- **Law research**: using longer lifecycles to research deeper laws
- **Myriad Races coordination**: using long-term perspective to handle cross-race, cross-plane affairs

Rather than:
- Secluding oneself, not communicating with the outside
- Monopolizing resources, not letting latecomers have development space
- Repeatedly modifying one's Dao, pursuing "perfection" without practicing
- Predating others' origin to extend lifespan

## IV. Attitudes Toward Lifespan at Each Realm

- **Five mortal-cultivation realms**: lifespan extension is a byproduct, main energy on cultivation and contribution, not deliberately pursued
- **Dao Integration - Boundary-Breaking**: lifespan already reaches thousand-year tier, beginning to bear civilization inheritance responsibility
- **World-Creation - Origin**: near-immortal existence, core mission is guarding and inheritance, not "being alive" itself
- **Multiverse Realm and above**: existence form already transcends the "lifespan" concept, core is symbiosis with Myriad Races origin

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: not pursuing personal immortality avoids the enormous energy waste of "predating to extend life"; the time of lifespan extension is all used for civilization contribution
- **Anti-predation**: immortality's essence is robbing resource space; the Symbiotic Faction fundamentally negates this pursuit
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' ultimate pursuit is "one person ruling immortal"; Heng Yuan Zhou's ultimate pursuit is "Myriad Races civilization perpetual evolution"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: lifespan extension favors no race, each race's cultivators have natural lifespan extension mechanisms
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the law relationship between lifespan and realm is fully open; Myriad Races all know how to naturally extend life without taking predatory shortcuts

## Conclusion

In predatory-faction narratives, "eternal youth" is cultivation's ultimate goal; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, "eternal youth" is only a byproduct of realm advancement, the true goal is "Myriad Races perpetual evolution." One person living ten thousand years is nothing; Myriad Races civilization evolving perpetually for ten thousand epochs is true greatness. This is the Symbiotic Faction's attitude toward lifespan: not refusing natural extension, not pursuing artificial immortality, using extended time to serve Myriad Races.
