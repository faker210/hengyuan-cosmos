# Entry 232 | Realm and Resources: How Joint Contracting Prevents Resource Monopoly

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 22, 18, 47

## Introduction: Strong ones earn more for more work, but strong ones do not monopolize

Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s resource allocation principle is Joint Contracting, more work more reward. The higher a cultivator's realm, the greater their contribution to civilization, the more resource allotment they receive — this is the efficiency principle. But simultaneously, the Anti-Monopoly Law and resource open mechanism ensure **strong ones get more but do not monopolize** — resources are for cultivation and contribution, not for hoarding and exclusive possession.

The six top-level axioms are directly embodied in the relationship between realm and resources: the anti-predation axiom forbids acquiring resources by predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires resources be fairly open to Myriad Races; the collective ascension axiom requires resource investment drive collective ascension; the causal self-discipline axiom constrains resource acquisition not to damage environmental karma; the law-first axiom ensures resource allocation laws are stable and self-consistent; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry analyzes how the Joint Contracting mechanism motivates strong ones while preventing resource monopoly.

## II. Joint Contracting's Core Logic

The Joint Contracting mechanism (Document 22)'s core is "more work more reward, no work no reward":
- **Work**: the cultivator's contribution — including cultivation breakthroughs, knowledge sharing, tech R&D, public service, mentor guidance
- **Reward**: resource allotment — cultivation materials, energy supply, space use rights, technology use rights
- **Linkage**: the higher the contribution value, the more resource allotment; contribution stagnates, resource allotment returns to basic level

This mechanism ensures resources flow to "those most able to create value," not "those best at snatching."

## III. Five Lines of Defense Against Monopoly

### 3.1 First Defense: Resource Publicness Principle

Heng Yuan Zhou's cultivation resources are legally public resources, not private property. Use rights obtained through Joint Contracting do not represent ownership. Cultivators have only "use right" and "benefit right" over resources, no "hoarding right" and "monopoly right."

### 3.2 Second Defense: Resource Audit

The anchor network audits all cultivators' resource use throughout:
- Personal resource stock ceiling monitoring
- Abnormal hoarding auto-warning
- Long-unused resources auto-recovered and reallocated
- Resource use efficiency evaluation

If a Dao Origin cultivator hoards large resources but does not invest in cultivation or research, the system auto-recovers idle resources.

### 3.3 Third Defense: Anti-Monopoly Law

The Anti-Monopoly Law (Document 47) explicitly forbids:
- Monopolizing core resources like spiritual veins, mines
- Monopolizing cultivation methods' dissemination
- Using resources as leverage to coerce other cultivators
- Organizational resource monopoly (sects, families' multi-generational control over resources)

Violators are tried by the anchor court, serious ones stripped of cultivation permissions.

### 3.4 Fourth Defense: Everyone-Has-a-Road Guarantee System

The Everyone-Has-a-Road Guarantee System (Document 08) ensures that even cultivators without any contribution can obtain basic cultivation resources. This fundamentally prevents "resource monopoly causes the bottom to have no road" — you may monopolize high-tier resources, but the bottom's basic resources are always guaranteed.

### 3.5 Fifth Defense: 20% Activation Strategy

The 20% Activation Strategy's goal is to let 20% of beings reach Origin Realm. When the number of strong ones is large enough, any single strong one's monopolization attempt is checked by the strong-one group itself — this is the most fundamental anti-monopoly: **not relying on law to forbid monopoly, but on cultivating enough strong ones to make monopoly impossible**.

## IV. Why Strong Ones Do Not Monopolize

Besides institutional defenses, there are deeper reasons:

- **Monopoly harms own interests**: in the Alaya Network, knowledge sharing earns the highest contribution value; monopolizing knowledge instead lowers contribution rating
- **Monopoly causes Dao-heart rejection**: at Dao Integration breakthrough, Dao heart rejects symbiotic laws — monopolistic mentality is incompatible with symbiotic laws
- **Monopoly raises cultivation cost**: regions with monopolized resources have worsening cultivation environments, ultimately backlashing against the monopolist
- **Monopoly is inefficient**: in the era of Multiplication Fruit technicization, gaining resources through tech innovation is far more efficient than relying on monopoly

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: resource audit and idle recovery mechanisms ensure every bit of resource is effectively used, no hoarding waste
- **Anti-predation**: Joint Contracting + Anti-Monopoly Law institutionally eliminates predatory resource acquisition space
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' resources are monopolized by sects and top strong ones, entry cultivators have no resources to use; Heng Yuan Zhou's Joint Contracting ensures resources are allocated by contribution
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: resource allocation is fair to every Myriad Races group, each race has resource acquisition rights
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the resource open mechanism expands with cosmos expansion, cross-cosmos resource allocation is open to Myriad Races

## Conclusion

The relationship between realm and resources is not "the stronger occupies more," but "the stronger contributes more, uses more transparently." The Joint Contracting mechanism ensures efficiency — more work more reward; the five anti-monopoly defenses ensure fairness — get more but do not monopolize. In predatory-faction systems, resource allocation is "robbed"; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, resource allocation is "earned" — one character's difference, worlds apart.
