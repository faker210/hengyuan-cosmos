# Entry 235 | Realm and Ethics: Whole-Realm Practice of Cultivation Ethics and Boundaries

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 19, 04, 58

## Introduction: The stronger, the more boundaries needed

Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s cultivation ethics' core is "the stronger, the more boundaries needed." In predatory-faction systems, strong ones' ethical bottom line lowers as power grows — the stronger the more wanton; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, strong ones' ethical bottom line rises as power grows — the stronger the less able to overstep. This is cultivation ethics' whole-realm practice's core principle.

The six top-level axioms in the relationship between realm and ethics — the anti-predation axiom directly is the ethical bottom line; the republican axiom of Myriad Races is ethics' value foundation; the collective ascension axiom is ethics' goal orientation; the causal self-discipline axiom is ethics' operation norm; the law-first axiom ensures the ethics system is stable and self-consistent; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry maps cultivation ethics and boundaries' concrete practice at each realm.

## I. Ethics Boundaries' Hierarchical Map

| Realm | Core Ethical Requirement | Boundary-Crossing Consequence |
|-------|---------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Sense - Condensed Origin | No predation, no harm, no prying | Basic ethics review, warning education |
| Transform - Law Origin | No monopoly, no misguidance, no abuse | Suspending some cultivation permissions |
| Dao - Dao Integration | No rejection, no unilateralism, no overstepping | Dao-heart calibration, restricting law operations |
| Boundary-Breaking - World-Creation | No disturbance, no colonization, no plane predation | Freezing cross-plane permissions |
| Origin Realm | No private law fine-tuning, no unilateral cosmic direction | Suspending law fine-tuning participation right |

## II. Five Mortal-Cultivation Realms' Ethical Practice

### 2.1 Sense - Condensed Origin's Ethical Bottom Line

This stage cultivators' ethics boundaries are mainly at personal behavior level:
- **No predation**: not devouring other lives' energy or origin
- **No harm**: not using cultivation ability to harm the innocent
- **No prying**: not using perception ability to pry into others' privacy
- **No misguidance**: not spreading erroneous cultivation methods to mislead others

These bottom lines seem simple, but are precisely where sentient beings' origin-attachment · shortcut inner demon most easily breaks — "just suck a little, it's fine." The anchor network focuses on monitoring ethical bottom lines at this stage, immediately warning upon boundary-crossing.

### 2.2 Transform - Law Origin's Ethical Deepening

This stage cultivators' ethics boundaries expand to knowledge and technology level:
- **No knowledge monopoly**: cultivation insights must be shared, not privately hoarded
- **No technology abuse**: technology tools for cultivation and contribution, not for harm
- **No misleading beginners**: as mentors must be responsible to students, not teaching wrong methods
- **No creating danger**: law experiments conducted in safe environments, not endangering others

## III. Four Sacred-Cultivation Realms' Ethical Practice

### 3.1 Dao - Dao Integration's Dao-Heart Ethics

This stage's ethics core is Dao heart:
- **Dao heart not rejecting**: personal Dao cannot exclude Myriad Races' Dao
- **No unilateralism**: law operations not personal unilateral, seek collective opinion
- **No overstepping**: law rewriting conducted within authorized scope
- **No discrimination**: not discriminating against other cultivators by race or birth

The Dao-heart sync mechanism at Dao Integration breakthrough (see Entry 222) is essentially ethics review — impure Dao heart cannot integrate.

### 3.2 Boundary-Breaking - World-Creation's Cross-Plane Ethics

This stage's ethics core is cross-plane operation norms:
- **No karma disturbance**: cross-plane operations strictly filed, observe first then interfere
- **No colonization**: not treating low-level planes as colonies
- **No plane resource predation**: cross-plane resource allocation based on protocols, not robbery
- **No taking natives**: not relocating any life without natives' consent

### 3.3 Origin Realm's Cosmic-Level Ethics

Origin Realm strong ones' ethics reach cosmic level:
- **No private law fine-tuning**: law fine-tuning must be collective decision
- **No unilateral cosmic direction**: cosmic evolution direction decided by Myriad Races republicanism
- **No origin anchoring abuse**: origin rebirth ability only for fulfilling mission, not for evading responsibility
- **No personality cult**: strong ones are servants, not gods

## IV. Ethics Review's Enforcement Mechanism

Cultivation ethics do not rely on self-awareness, but have institutional guarantees:
- **Alaya Network monitoring**: auto-identifying potentially ethics-violating behavior
- **Ethics review committee**: human review of suspicious behavior
- **Twin-soul ethics code**: specialized ethics norms for twin-soul, peculiar life and other special cases (Document 58)
- **Tiered penalties**: from warning to permission suspension to cultivation right deprivation, tiered by severity
- **Repair mechanism**: after boundary-crossing, repair through ethics education and practice, no one-strike-kill

## V. The Relationship Between Ethics and Freedom

Ethics boundaries do not restrict freedom, but guarantee freedom. A high-realm cultivator without ethics boundaries, seemingly free, actually at any time may be backlashed by karma or sanctioned by institutions due to own boundary-crossing. A cultivator under strict ethics boundaries, though operations must be filed and reviewed, every step is safe, sustainable, not backlashed.

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: ethics boundaries avoid the enormous waste of "crossing line → sanctioned → re-cultivating"
- **Anti-predation**: ethical bottom line forbids predation from the first realm, is the anti-predation axiom's daily practice
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' strong ones have zero ethics, the stronger the more lawless; Heng Yuan Zhou's ethics escalate whole-realm
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: ethics norms treat every Myriad Races group equally, special groups have specialized ethics codes
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the ethics system expands with realm expansion, cross-cosmos operations have corresponding ethics norms

## Conclusion

The higher the realm, the stricter the ethics boundaries — this is not restraint on strong ones, but protection of Myriad Races. In predatory-faction systems, ethics is a tool the weak use to restrain strong ones; strong ones never abide. In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, ethics are boundaries strong ones voluntarily bear; the stronger the more consciously abided. Whether a cultivator is willing to accept ethical constraints is itself the touchstone of whether the Dao heart is pure.
