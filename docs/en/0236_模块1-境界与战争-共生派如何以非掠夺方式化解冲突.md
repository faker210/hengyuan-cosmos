# Entry 236 | Realm and War: How the Symbiotic Faction Resolves Conflict in Non-Predatory Ways

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 04, 03, 48

## Introduction: Not using war to solve war

In Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s cultivation civilization, war is not the preferred way to resolve conflict — in fact, after the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization was established, large-scale war has basically disappeared. But this does not mean no conflict: inter-race resource disputes, inter-plane law friction, residual predatory forces' harassment — these conflicts still exist. The difference is: **how does the Symbiotic Faction resolve these conflicts in non-predatory ways?**

The six top-level axioms in the relationship between realm and war — the anti-predation axiom directly negates war predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races provides peaceful resolution frameworks; the collective ascension axiom guides conflict parties toward co-construction; the causal self-discipline axiom constrains conflict operations not to expand karma; the law-first axiom ensures conflict resolution mechanisms' law definitions are stable; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry analyzes the Symbiotic Faction's complete conflict resolution system.

## II. Why the Symbiotic Faction Does Not Fight

### 2.1 War Is a Zero-Sum Game

In predatory-faction systems, war is a means to acquire resources — win and you rob the loser's resources. But in the Symbiotic Faction's positive-sum system, war's cost far exceeds benefit: the cultivation environment war destroys takes centuries to recover; cultivators war kills are civilization's talent loss; war-triggered karma disturbance takes long-term repair.

**War's cost is always higher than negotiation's cost** — this is the Symbiotic Faction's fundamental economic reason for not fighting.

### 2.2 War Cultivates Predatory Thinking

War not only destroys matter, but also destroys people's hearts. A cultivator accustomed to "win and grab resources" finds it hard to accept symbiotic circulation ideals. From an education perspective, war is predatory thinking's breeding ground — the Symbiotic Faction rooting out war from the root is rooting out predatory thinking's revival from the root.

### 2.3 Strong Ones Do Not Need War to Prove Power

In predatory-faction systems, strong ones display power and gain prestige through war. In the Symbiotic Faction system, strong ones' prestige comes from contribution and responsibility — how much you do for Myriad Races determines how much prestige you have. No need to prove oneself through fighting.

## III. The Conflict Resolution Toolbox

Although the Symbiotic Faction does not fight, this does not mean no "strength" — it's just that strength is used to resolve conflict not create it:

### 3.1 Mediation and Arbitration
The community mediation committee is composed of Transform - Law Origin cultivators, responsible for personal and small-group conflicts. Mediation is based on the symbiotic constitution and cultivation ethics; a solution both sides can accept is the final solution.

### 3.2 Resource Reallocation
When conflict stems from resource struggle, the Joint Contracting mechanism starts resource reallocation — not "loser gives resources to winner," but reallocation based on both sides' actual contribution and needs.

### 3.3 Knowledge Sharing Plans
When conflict stems from knowledge monopoly, the Alaya Network mandates sharing — cultivation knowledge you hoard must be made public, letting both conflict parties cultivate with the same knowledge system.

### 3.4 Cross-Race Cooperation Projects
When inter-racial conflict arises, the Myriad Races Republican Assembly starts cross-race cooperation projects — letting two races find common interest in cooperation, turning opposition into collaboration.

### 3.5 Isolation and Sanctions
For residual predatory forces that refuse mediation and continuously harm symbiotic order, plane isolation and cultivation restrictions are applied. This "don't fight but lock up" method both protects symbiotic order and avoids war's karmic cost.

## IV. High-Realm Strong Ones' Role in Conflict Resolution

- **Transform - Law Origin**: community mediators, handling personal and small-group conflicts
- **Dao - Dao Integration**: racial conflict coordinators, participating in Myriad Races Republican Assembly negotiation
- **Boundary-Breaking - World-Creation**: plane conflict arbiters, cross-plane coordination
- **Origin Realm**: cosmic-level conflict's final adjudicator, but adjudication based on whole consensus not personal will

High-realm strong ones' role in conflict resolution is not "beat the other side," but "find a solution both sides can accept." This is far harder than fighting — fighting only needs power, reconciliation needs wisdom.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: not fighting avoids war's enormous energy waste — the energy and talent war consumes, used for construction can create tenfold value
- **Anti-predation**: negating war predation from the root, conflict resolution all based on positive-sum game
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' conflict resolution method is fighting — the winner robs the loser; Heng Yuan Zhou's conflict resolution all based on negotiation and mediation
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: conflict resolution mechanisms are equally open to every Myriad Races group, no favoritism by race identity
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the conflict resolution toolbox expands with cosmos expansion, cross-cosmos conflict has corresponding coordination mechanisms

## Conclusion

The relationship between realm and war is not "the stronger the better at fighting," but "the stronger the less need to fight." In predatory-faction systems, war is part of cultivation — kill monsters to level up, rob resources, fight for hegemon; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, war is civilization's failure — if negotiation works, no fighting; if mediation works, no confrontation. The higher a cultivator reaches, the more they should become a peace promoter, not a war tool. This is the Symbiotic Faction's definition of "strong one": not the strongest warrior, but the best mediator.
