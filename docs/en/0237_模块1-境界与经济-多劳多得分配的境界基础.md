# Entry 237 | Realm and Economy: The Realm Foundation of More-Work-More-Reward Distribution

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 06, 22, 41

## Introduction: Cultivation is labor, contribution is reward

Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s economic system's core principle is more work more reward. And "work" measurement is closely tied to realm — not "the higher the realm the more the reward," but **the higher the realm, the greater the contribution one can make, the more reward for contribution**. Realm is "labor ability" foundation, contribution is "labor" content, reward is "labor" result. The three are tightly linked through the Joint Contracting mechanism.

The six top-level axioms are directly embodied in the relationship between realm and economy: the anti-predation axiom negates getting something for nothing; the republican axiom of Myriad Races ensures fair distribution; the collective ascension axiom guides economy to serve civilization ascension; the causal self-discipline axiom constrains economic behavior not to damage ecology; the law-first axiom ensures distribution laws are stable and self-consistent; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry analyzes how the more-work-more-reward distribution system operates based on realm.

## II. Why Realm Is the Foundation of Distribution

### 2.1 Realm Determines Contribution Ability

It is not "high realm gets more," but "high realm can make greater contributions." One Origin Realm strong one's one law fine-tuning benefits the entire cosmos; a Sense Origin cultivator's contribution can only benefit a few around them. Differences in contribution magnitude objectively determine differences in distribution tier.

This is not "distribute by realm," but "distribute by contribution" — it's just that high-realm cultivators can objectively make greater contributions.

### 2.2 Realm Advancement Requires Continuous Investment

The cultivation process from Sense Origin to Origin Realm requires continuous investment of time, energy, and resources. The more-work-more-reward principle is also embodied here — the more you invest in cultivation (the higher the realm), the greater your contribution potential to civilization, and the more returns you receive. This is reasonable return on cultivation investment.

### 2.3 But Realm Is Not the Only Distribution Criterion

More-work-more-reward is not realm-only. A Transform Origin cultivator who undertakes a large amount of mentor work and public service may have a contribution value exceeding a Dao Origin cultivator who cultivates in seclusion. The core of distribution is "contribution value"; realm is only one dimension of contribution ability.

## III. Distribution's Concrete Mechanism

### 3.1 Contribution Value Auto-Measurement

The anchor network auto-records all cultivator contributions:
- Cultivation breakthrough contributions: realm advancement, new method creation
- Knowledge sharing contributions: insight upload, teaching coaching
- Tech R&D contributions: law engineering innovation
- Public service contributions: anchor maintenance, community service
- Guardianship contributions: threat defense, rescue

Each contribution has a quantitative score, aggregated into personal contribution value.

### 3.2 Joint Contracting Linkage

Contribution value directly links to resource allotment:
- Higher contribution value, higher resource allotment priority
- Contribution value reaching different thresholds unlocks different tiers of resource permissions
- Contribution value persistently low, resource allotment returns to basic level

### 3.3 Progressive Adjustment Tax

High contributors pay progressive adjustment tax (up to 30%), used for the Everyone-Has-a-Road Guarantee System. This ensures distribution differences do not lead to polarization — strong ones get more, but also give back to society through tax.

## IV. The Dialectic Between Realm and Economy

Realm advancement promotes economic contribution; economic resources feed back realm advancement — this is a positive feedback loop:

- Cultivator realm advancement → contribution ability strengthens → contribution value rises → obtains more resources → resources feed back cultivation → realm further advances

This loop's key is: **contribution is the intermediate step** — you cannot directly buy realm with resources; you must first make contributions to obtain resources, then use resources to cultivate and advance realm. This ensures economic resources always flow to those "truly creating value."

## V. Why Not Distribute Directly by Realm

If distributing directly by realm ("you are Dao Origin so you automatically get Dao Origin's resources"), it would cause:
- Some lying flat: reaching a certain realm then no longer contributing, eating from stored grain
- Resource waste: people not working occupy resources without working
- Incentive failure: since realm equals reward, why work hard to contribute?

So distribution's core is always "contribution value"; realm is only a reference dimension of contribution ability. An Origin Realm strong one who does nothing may have resource allotment lower than a Transform Origin cultivator actively contributing — this is true more work more reward.

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: more work more reward ensures resources flow to those most able to create value, no "occupying resources without working" waste
- **Anti-predation**: distribution based on contribution not violence, predators cannot obtain legitimate resources through predation
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' economy is "strong rob weak"; Heng Yuan Zhou's economy is "more work more reward, progressive adjustment"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the distribution system is equal for every Myriad Races group, each race's contributions are measured
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: contribution measurement and distribution mechanisms expand with cosmos expansion, cross-cosmos contributions also measured

## Conclusion

The relationship between realm and economy is not "the stronger the richer," but "the stronger the more able to make greater contributions, the greater the contribution the more the reward." The more-work-more-reward distribution system takes realm as ability foundation, contribution as measurement standard, Joint Contracting as implementation mechanism, ensuring unity of efficiency and fairness. In predatory-faction systems, economy is robbed; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, economy is earned — this is the fundamental difference between realm and economy relationship.

In the final analysis, realm and economy · the realm foundation of more-work-more-reward distribution described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
