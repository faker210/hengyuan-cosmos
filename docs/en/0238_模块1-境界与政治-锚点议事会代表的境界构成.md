# Entry 238 | Realm and Politics: Realm Composition of Anchor Council Representatives

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 49, 03, 77

## Introduction: Governance is not strong ones' privilege, but Myriad Races' common affair

In Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s political governance framework, the Anchor Council is the legislative body, the Symbiotic Executive Committee is the administrative body, the Symbiotic Court is the judicial body, and the Anchor Supervision Committee is the oversight body. This system's design principle is "power checks and balances" — no institution or individual can unilaterally decide. And in representative composition, realm is not a "qualification threshold," but a "capability reference."

The six top-level axioms are directly embodied in the relationship between realm and politics: the anti-predation axiom negates strong-one dictatorship; the republican axiom of Myriad Races is the political system's legitimacy foundation; the collective ascension axiom guides politics to serve civilization ascension; the causal self-discipline axiom constrains political decisions' karmic impact; the law-first axiom ensures the governance system's law definitions are stable; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry analyzes the Anchor Council and each governance body's realm composition principles.

## I. Governance Bodies' Realm Composition Map

| Body | Main Duty | Representative Realm Composition | Decision Weight Basis |
|------|-----------|---------------------------------|------------------------|
| Anchor Council | Legislation | Representatives of every Myriad Races group, no realm limit | Racial population + contribution |
| Symbiotic Executive Committee | Administration | Mainly mid-high-realm administrators | Professional ability + contribution record |
| Symbiotic Court | Judiciary | Law experts Dao Origin and above | Legal literacy + ethics review |
| Anchor Supervision Committee | Oversight | Independent supervisors Dao Integration and above | Independence + moral prestige |
| Plane Transcendents Meeting | Cosmic-level decisions | Origin Realm strong ones | Four-dimensional assessment system |

## II. Anchor Council: Realm Is Not a Threshold

As the legislative body, the Anchor Council's representatives are elected by every Myriad Races group. **Realm is not a threshold for serving as representative** — a Sense Origin cultivator, if elected by their own race as representative, can also enter the Council.

Council seats are allocated by racial population and contribution comprehensively, ensuring small races also have a voice channel. Decisions use a combination of consensus and majority vote; matters involving a race's fundamental interests require the relevant race's consent.

This design's core is: **legislation is Myriad Races' right, not strong ones' privilege**. Low-realm cultivators' interests also need representation; they cannot have no voice just because realm is low.

## III. Executive Committee: Professional Ability First

The Symbiotic Executive Committee is responsible for daily administration; its members are mainly mid-high-realm cultivators — not because "high realm should be officials," but because:
- Transform - Law Origin cultivators have enough law knowledge to execute technical administrative work
- Dao - Dao Integration cultivators have enough vision to formulate regional-level policies
- Executive work requires corresponding knowledge and capability reserves; realm is a reference indicator of ability

But Executive Committee members' selection is not "rank by realm," but by professional ability and contribution record. An excellent Law Origin administrator may be more suited for an administrative post than a Dao Origin cultivator who cultivates in seclusion.

## IV. Symbiotic Court: Law Experts' Threshold

Symbiotic Court judges are mainly law experts Dao Origin and above. This is not realm discrimination, but because:
- Judicial adjudication requires deep understanding of laws and ethics' relationship
- Cross-race, cross-plane cases require sufficient law knowledge
- Judges' ethics review ability directly affects adjudication fairness

But judge selection also requires passing specialized legal exams and ethics review — reaching realm does not make one a judge.

## V. Anchor Supervision Committee: Independent Supervisors

The Anchor Supervision Committee's members are mainly independent cultivators Dao Integration and above. The Supervision Committee's core requirement is **independence** — not subordinate to any other body, responsible only to Myriad Races. High-realm cultivators serve as supervisors because they have enough prestige and ability to supervise any body.

## VI. Plane Transcendents Meeting: Cosmic-Level Decisions

The Plane Transcendents Meeting composed of Origin Realm strong ones is responsible for cosmic-level major decisions — law fine-tuning, cross-cosmos coordination, civilization direction planning. But this meeting's decision method is not "Origin Realm strong ones vote," but four-dimensional-assessment-based collective discussion, and resolutions must be submitted to the Myriad Races Republican Assembly for filing.

Even Origin Realm strong ones cannot decide unilaterally — cosmic-level decisions must go through collective negotiation.

## VII. Why Realm Does Not Directly Determine Political Power

If realm directly determined political power, it would become "strong-one dictatorship" — the strongest person is boss, everyone else only obeys. This directly conflicts with the republican axiom of Myriad Races.

The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's design is: **realm determines capability reference, election determines political rights, contribution determines social prestige**. The three are independent, not substitutes. A high-realm cultivator does not necessarily have political power; a representative with political power does not necessarily have the highest realm.

## VIII. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: governance bodies' professional configuration ensures the most suitable person does the most suitable work, no "high realm must be official" waste
- **Anti-predation**: political power comes from election not force, fundamentally negating the "strong rule" logic
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' politics is "strongest becomes emperor"; Heng Yuan Zhou's politics is Myriad Races republicanism, power checks and balances
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the Council represents every Myriad Races group, no realm limit
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the governance system expands with cosmos expansion, cross-cosmos governance has corresponding coordination bodies

## Conclusion

The relationship between realm and politics is not "the stronger the more power," but "the stronger the more responsibility to participate in public affairs, but political rights come from election not realm." The Anchor Council's realm composition principle embodies the republican axiom of Myriad Races' core: politics is Myriad Races' common affair, not strong ones' private domain.
