# Entry 239 | Realm and Law: The Symbiotic Constitution's Constraints on Cultivators at Each Realm

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 40, 48, 47

## Introduction: Before the law Myriad Races are equal; strong ones are no exception

Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s legal system's core is the Symbiotic Constitution Outline. This constitution applies to all Myriad Races beings — regardless of race, realm, or contribution. Strong ones have no extralegal privilege; the weak are not discriminated by law. Law's binding force strengthens with realm advancement, because high-realm cultivators' actions have larger influence range, legal violation consequences more severe.

The six top-level axioms are directly embodied in the relationship between realm and law: the anti-predation axiom is the Anti-Monopoly Law and Cultivation Ethics Law's legislative foundation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races is the constitution's legitimacy source; the collective ascension axiom guides law to serve civilization ascension; the causal self-discipline axiom is cross-plane law's legislative principle; the law-first axiom ensures the legal system is stable and self-consistent; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry maps the Symbiotic Constitution and specialized laws' concrete constraints on cultivators of different realms.

## I. Legal Constraints' Hierarchical Map

| Realm | Applicable Core Laws | Special Constraints | Enforcement Body |
|-------|----------------------|---------------------|------------------|
| Sense - Condensed Origin | Cultivation Ethics Law, Anti-Predation Law | Basic ethics norms | Community mediation + anchor monitoring |
| Transform - Law Origin | Anti-Monopoly Law, Tech Safety Law | Knowledge sharing obligation | Regional enforcement teams |
| Dao - Dao Integration | Law Operation Law, Anchor Protection Law | Law operation filing system | Anchor Supervision Committee |

## II. Five Mortal-Cultivation Realms' Legal Constraints

### 2.1 Sense - Condensed Origin: Basic Ethics Law

This stage cultivators are mainly constrained by the Cultivation Ethics Law and Anti-Predation Law:
- Forbidden to predate other lives' energy or origin
- Forbidden to use cultivation ability to harm the innocent
- Forbidden to pry into others' privacy
- Obeying public cultivation venue rules

Violation consequences: from warning education to suspending basic cultivation resource allotment.

### 2.2 Transform - Law Origin: Anti-Monopoly and Tech Safety

This stage cultivators begin using law technology; legal constraints expand to:
- **Anti-Monopoly Law**: forbidding monopolizing cultivation resources, knowledge, technology
- **Tech Safety Law**: law experiments must be conducted in safe environments
- **Knowledge sharing obligation**: cultivation insights must be uploaded to the Alaya Network
- **Mentor Responsibility Law**: as mentors have responsibility for students' safety

Violation consequences: suspending some cultivation permissions; serious violators stripped of mentor qualification.

## III. Four Sacred-Cultivation Realms' Legal Constraints

### 3.1 Dao - Dao Integration: Law Operation Law

This stage cultivators begin manipulating laws; legal constraints escalate:
- **Law operation filing system**: all law operations must be filed in advance
- **Anchor Protection Law**: forbidding damaging the anchor network
- **Dao-heart Review Law**: Dao Integration breakthrough must pass Dao-heart ethics review
- **Regional Responsibility Law**: regional anchor guardians bear legal responsibility for their jurisdiction

Violation consequences: freezing law operation permissions; serious violators forcibly Dao-heart calibrated.

### 3.2 Boundary-Breaking - World-Creation: Cross-Plane Law

This stage cultivators begin cross-plane operations; legal constraints further escalate:
- **Cross-plane operation approval system**: cross-plane traversal must apply for approval
- **Plane Protection Law**: forbidding damaging plane membranes, forbidding plane colonization
- **Karma Isolation Law**: cross-plane operations' karmic impact must be isolated
- **World-Creation Registration Law**: created small worlds must be registered and filed

Violation consequences: freezing cross-plane permissions; serious violators plane-isolated.

### 3.3 Origin Realm: Cosmic Law Fine-Tuning Law

Origin Realm strong ones are constrained by the highest-tier law:
- **Collective decision system**: law fine-tuning must be collectively discussed by Origin Realm
- **Whole-cosmos public notice**: major law fine-tunings publicly announced cosmos-wide
- **Regular audit**: law fine-tuning audit every hundred years
- **Responsibility traceability system**: decision-makers of erroneous fine-tunings bear legal responsibility

Violation consequences: suspending law fine-tuning participation right; serious violators stripped of Plane Transcendent certification.

## IV. Realm Equality Before the Law

One of the Symbiotic Constitution's core principles is "before the law Myriad Races are equal" — realm is not an exemption. An Origin Realm strong one violating the Anti-Monopoly Law, and a Sense Origin cultivator violating the same law, are equal before the law.

But law's penalty severity is indeed related to realm — because high-realm cultivators' legal violations have greater impact, so penalties are also heavier. This is not "discriminating against strong ones," but "responsibility matches impact."

## V. The Judicial System's Realm Composition

Symbiotic Court judges are mainly Dao Origin and above (see Entry 238), but during judicial trial:
- Judges do not sentence based on the defendant's realm
- Jury system ensures trial fairness
- Cross-race cases tried by cross-race judges
- The highest appellate body is open to cases of all realms

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: legal constraints ensure strong ones do not abuse power causing waste — violating law costs far more than abiding
- **Anti-predation**: the Anti-Predation Law takes effect from the first realm, is the legal system's core
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' strong ones are above the law; Heng Yuan Zhou's strong ones are under the law's strictest constraints
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: law applies equally to every Myriad Races group, no exemption by race identity
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the legal system expands with cosmos expansion, cross-cosmos has corresponding legal frameworks

## Conclusion

The relationship between realm and law is not "the stronger the more able to be above the law," but "the stronger the more strictly constrained by law." The Symbiotic Constitution's constraints on cultivators of each realm escalate whole-realm — this is because strong ones' actions have larger influence range, violation consequences more severe. In predatory-faction systems, "strong ones are above the law" is the ultimate fantasy; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, "strong ones are under the strictest legal constraints" is the basic reality. This is the true meaning of rule of law in cultivation civilization.
