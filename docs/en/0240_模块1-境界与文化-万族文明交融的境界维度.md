# Entry 240 | Realm and Culture: The Realm Dimension of Myriad Races Cultural Fusion

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 05, 50, 52

## Introduction: The higher the cultivation, the broader the culture

In Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s civilization ecology, Myriad Races each have their own cultural traditions — carbon-based material races' civilization, energy condensation races' civilization, mechanical construction races' civilization, consciousness information races' civilization, hybrid variation races' civilization. Realm is not only a yardstick of power, but also a yardstick of cultural vision — **the higher a cultivator's realm, the stronger their cultural inclusiveness, the deeper the cultural fusion**.

The six top-level axioms are directly embodied in the relationship between realm and culture: the anti-predation axiom negates cultural predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races is cultural equality's foundation; the collective ascension axiom guides cultural fusion to serve civilization ascension; the causal self-discipline axiom constrains cultural influence not to be crudely implanted; the law-first axiom ensures cultural fusion's law framework is stable; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry analyzes how realm advancement drives Myriad Races cultural fusion.

## I. Cultural Fusion's Realm Tiers

| Realm | Cultural Vision | Fusion Mode | Typical Manifestation |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|------------------------|
| Sense - Condensed Origin | Home race culture dominant | Contact and understanding | Knowing other races' cultural knowledge |
| Transform - Law Origin | Cross-cultural learning | Skill exchange | Learning other races' cultivation methods |
| Dao - Dao Integration | Cultural fusion | Dao-rules mutual reference | Personal Dao fusing multi-race elements |
| Boundary-Breaking - World-Creation | Cross-plane culture | Civilization bridge | Inter-plane cultural exchange |
| Origin Realm | Cosmic culture | Civilization design | Guiding Myriad Races civilization's common evolution |

## II. Five Mortal-Cultivation Realms' Cultural Fusion

### 2.1 Sense - Condensed Origin: Cultural Enlightenment

This stage cultivators have just begun contacting the cultivation world; cultural vision is mainly within home-race culture. But basic education already includes Myriad Races cultural common sense — knowing the cosmos has not only one's own race, but also five genealogy Myriad Races life.

This stage's cultural fusion is "knowing it exists" — knowing other races' existence, understanding basic cultural differences, cultivating basic cultural respect.

### 2.2 Transform - Law Origin: Cross-Cultural Learning

This stage cultivators begin hitting bottlenecks in cultivation, needing to learn from other races' cultivation methods. Cultural fusion upgrades from "knowing" to "learning":
- Energy condensation races learn carbon-based life's life cycle theory
- Consciousness information races learn mechanical construction races' precise operation
- Each race exchanges cultivation insights on the Alaya Network

This cross-cultural learning is bidirectional — you learn mine, I learn yours, mutually enriching.

### 2.3 Dao Origin: Dao-Rules Mutual Reference

Dao Origin cultivators when distilling personal Dao-rules begin consciously drawing inspiration from multi-race cultures:
- Human cultivators from plant spirits' growth rhythm attain "Dao follows nature"
- Energy life from mechanical races' precise logic attain "the order of laws"
- Each race's Dao-rules mutually inspire and enrich

This stage's cultural fusion has already deepened to the "Dao" level — not surface cultural exchange, but worldview and philosophy-level mutual reference.

## III. Four Sacred-Cultivation Realms' Cultural Fusion

### 3.1 Dao Integration: The Depth of Cultural Fusion

Dao Integration cultivators' personal Dao fuses with cosmic laws; their cultural vision expands to the entire cultivation region. This stage's cultural fusion manifests as:
- Personal Dao-rules fused with multi-race cultural elements
- As grandmasters guiding disciples of other races, cultural understanding deepens
- Participating in cross-race law seminars, promoting cultural-level consensus

### 3.2 Boundary-Breaking - World-Creation: Cross-Plane Cultural Bridges

Boundary-Breaking and World-Creation cultivators traverse different planes, becoming cultural bridges between planes:
- Bringing one plane's culture back to another plane
- Designing multicultural coexistence environments in self-created small worlds
- Promoting peaceful exchange between different plane civilizations

This stage's cultural fusion has already transcended the racial level, entering the civilization level.

### 3.3 Origin Realm: Cosmic-Level Cultural Design

Origin Realm strong ones participate in cosmic civilization direction planning; their cultural vision is cosmic-level:
- Designing a framework for Myriad Races cultures' common development
- Coordinating cultural relations among different cosmic civilizations
- Guiding Myriad Races cultures toward symbiosis while preserving diversity

## IV. Cultural Fusion Principles

Myriad Races civilization fusion follows these principles:
- **Cultural equality**: no "advanced culture" vs. "inferior culture" distinction
- **Tradition respect**: no forced cultural assimilation, each race's cultural traditions respected
- **Bidirectional exchange**: cultural fusion is bidirectional, not one-way cultural output
- **Autonomous choice**: each race autonomously chooses which foreign cultural elements to absorb
- **Diversity protection**: cultural fusion does not equal cultural unification; diversity itself is wealth

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: cultural fusion avoids the repeated waste of each culture independently groping — one race's cultural breakthrough can inspire Myriad Races
- **Anti-predation**: cultural fusion opposes cultural predation — no forced assimilation, no cultural colonialism
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' culture is a single imperial culture; Heng Yuan Zhou's culture is Myriad Races multi-race fusion
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: cultural fusion fully covers all five Myriad Races genealogies
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cultural fusion expands with cosmos expansion, cross-cosmic cultural exchange has corresponding mechanisms

## Conclusion

The relationship between realm and culture is not "the stronger the more only home-race culture," but "the stronger the more understanding of Myriad Races culture." A cultivator's cultural inclusiveness grows synchronously with realm — starting from home-race culture, through cross-cultural learning, ultimately reaching the vision of Myriad Races cultural symbiosis. This is the republican axiom of Myriad Races in culture's concrete realization.
