# Entry 246 | Realm Comparison · Cover the Sky: Realm Inflation and Setting Collapse

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 04, 244, 17

## Introduction: Infinite inflation from "Great Emperor" to "Mortal-World Immortal"

Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s realm system is rooted in law self-consistency, while Cover the Sky's realm system is characterized by "continuous inflation" — from the early "Great Emperor" to the late "Mortal-World Immortal," realm names continuously increase, power numbers continuously inflate, early settings are continuously overturned. This inflation is not "natural growth," but typical manifestation of **plot kidnapping setting**.

The six top-level axioms run through the comparison throughout: the anti-predation axiom points out Cover the Sky's predatory essence; the republican axiom of Myriad Races contrasts Cover the Sky's oligarchic structure; the collective ascension axiom contrasts Cover the Sky's solitary immortal ascension; the causal self-discipline axiom contrasts Cover the Sky's history falsification; the law-first axiom directly points to Cover the Sky's setting self-consistency collapse; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Cover the Sky's Realm Inflation Trajectory

Cover the Sky trilogy (Cover the Sky, Perfect World, Sacred Ruins)'s realm system has undergone multiple inflations:

- **Cover the Sky arc**: Great Emperor as ceiling, "Mortal-World Immortal" as hidden tier
- **Perfect World**: from immortal to Immortal Emperor, realm count doubles
- **Sacred Ruins**: stacking new realms further above, above Immortal Emperor there are even higher tiers
- **Cross-book linkage**: different books' realms cross-reference, continuously breaking old ceilings

This inflation's result is: readers cannot remember how many realms there are; early characters' strength positioning repeatedly changes; settings contradict themselves.

## II. Three Manifestations of Realm Inflation

### 2.1 Name Inflation
Every segment of plot needs new realm names, otherwise the protagonist's growth "is not worth watching." From "Quasi-Emperor" to "Great Emperor" to "Mortal-World Immortal" to "Immortal King" to "Immortal Emperor"... names get bigger, but actual law connotation gets emptier.

### 2.2 Number Inflation
Early setting "Great Emperor" is the cosmos's strongest, one Great Emperor can suppress an era. Late "Great Emperor" becomes cannon fodder, below Immortal Emperor all are ants. Number inflation's result is early "strongest" becomes late "weakest," readers feel deceived.

### 2.3 Setting Contradiction
Early-set "ultimate law" late becomes basic law; early "cosmos's unique" becomes "one of parallel cosmoses." To let the protagonist continue getting stronger, early settings are continuously overturned — this is retcon.

## III. Cover the Sky's Energy-Level Waste Analysis

- **Protagonist waste**: Ye Fan from mortal to Heavenly Emperor, all the way predatory leveling, massive defeated opponents' energy wasted
- **Supporting-character waste**: early vivid characters late become background boards, their settings and storylines wasted
- **Setting waste**: early carefully designed law system overturned in late inflation, front-loaded setting resources wasted
- **Civilization waste**: Cover the Sky's cosmos civilizations are continuously "harvested," one era's civilization results reduced to ashes after strong ones predate them

## IV. Cover the Sky's Protagonist Uniqueness Dependency

By the five-level standard, Cover the Sky's protagonist uniqueness dependency is **medium (Ye Fan / oligarch type)** — Ye Fan as protagonist is indeed civilization's apex, but Cover the Sky also has other Great Emperor-tier figures, not completely solitary-tree type. But overall, civilization's core progress revolves around the protagonist; supporting characters are more "running mates."

## V. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

| Assessment Dimension | Cover the Sky Score | Explanation |
|----------------------|---------------------|-------------|
| Realm self-consistency | Low | Realm definitions continuously change with plot needs, front-back contradiction |
| Retcon risk | Extreme | The trilogy continuously breaks previous works' settings, retcon is normal |
| Ending risk | Medium-high | After cross-book linkage settings are overly complex, hard to self-consistently conclude |
| Plot kidnapping setting | Extremely severe | Protagonist's need to get stronger is the sole driver of realm inflation |

**Core problem**: Cover the Sky's realm inflation is not "laws naturally expanding," but the vicious cycle of "plot needs stronger enemies → needs stronger protagonist → needs higher realm." Law definitions completely obey plot needs, this is typical plot kidnapping setting.

## VI. Heng Yuan Zhou's Alternative Solution

How does Heng Yuan Zhou avoid realm inflation?
- **Law-first**: realm definitions precede plot, not modified due to plot needs
- **Dimensional leap**: every realm corresponds to an existence dimension, not number inflation
- **Infinite expandability but self-consistent**: new realm additions based on new law dimension discoveries, not to make the protagonist stronger
- **Myriad Races group**: strong ones are a group not individuals, no "protagonist must be strongest" plot pressure

## VII. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: Cover the Sky's realm inflation causes massive front-loaded setting waste; Heng Yuan Zhou's every realm is effective accumulation, not overturned and restarted
- **Anti-predation**: Cover the Sky's leveling is essentially predatory — fight strong enemies, seize energy; Heng Yuan Zhou replaces predation with symbiotic circulation
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo is ceiling lock, Cover the Sky is infinite inflation — both are problems, Heng Yuan Zhou takes the middle road
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Cover the Sky's strong ones are concentrated in the protagonist and few supporting characters; Heng Yuan Zhou's 20% Activation Strategy lets all Myriad Races have opportunity
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Heng Yuan Zhou's expansion is based on law self-consistency, Cover the Sky's inflation is based on plot needs

## Conclusion

Cover the Sky's realm inflation is essentially "continuously breaking self-set ceilings to let the protagonist keep winning." Short-term very satisfying — protagonist gets stronger, battles get bigger; long-term is disaster — settings continuously collapse, reader trust consumed. Heng Yuan Zhou starting from the law-first axiom lets the realm system be based on law structure not plot needs, rooting out inflation and collapse from the source.
