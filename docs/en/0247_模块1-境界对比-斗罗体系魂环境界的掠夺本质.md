# Entry 247 | Realm Comparison · Douluo: The Predatory Essence of Soul-Ring Realms

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Document 04, Path D · Human-Made Dao-Rule Soul Rings, Document 14

## Introduction: Every soul ring is a life

Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s realm system is rooted in symbiotic circulation, while Douluo's realm system centers on soul rings — every realm advancement requires hunting soul beasts and seizing soul rings. This "level up = hunt" setting is predationism's typical manifestation in cultivation systems. The higher Douluo's realm, the deeper the predation accumulation; behind its "strength" lie countless soul beast lives' extinction.

The six top-level axioms run through the comparison throughout: the anti-predation axiom directly negates the soul ring mechanism; the republican axiom of Myriad Races points out the soul ring system's exploitation of the soul beast race; the collective ascension axiom contrasts Douluo's solo ascension; the causal self-discipline axiom points out the soul ring mechanism's karmic debt; the law-first axiom directly points to the soul ring system's setting self-consistency problem; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Soul Ring Mechanism's Predatory Essence

Douluo's cultivation path:
- Every major realm advancement requires hunting a soul beast
- Seizing the soul beast's soul ring and soul bone as abilities
- The stronger the soul beast, the better the soul ring, the stronger the cultivator
- Ultimately reaching Title Douluo and even higher tiers

This mechanism's essence is: **the cultivator's evolution is built on the soul beast race's extinction**. Behind every high-tier cultivator, hundreds and thousands of hunted soul beasts. The soul beast race is continuously slaughtered for cultivators' advancement, ultimately trending endangered or extinct.

## II. The Soul Ring Mechanism's Threefold Predation

### 2.1 Life Predation
Hunting soul beasts to seize soul rings directly deprives soul beasts of life. This is not "food chain"-style natural predation, but conscious predation for one's own cultivation.

### 2.2 Ability Predation
Abilities soul beasts cultivated for hundreds or even thousands of years are directly seized by cultivators through soul rings. Cultivators need not cultivate these abilities themselves — "kill and take" suffices — this is direct predation of soul beasts' cultivation results.

### 2.3 Racial Predation
Soul beasts as a race, their living space is continuously compressed by human cultivators. To obtain stronger soul rings, cultivators go deep into soul beast habitats to hunt, causing soul beast population to plummet. This is racial-level predation.

## III. Douluo's Energy-Level Waste

- **Soul beast waste**: energy soul beasts cultivated for thousands of years is instantly seized, most wasted in soul ring fusion
- **Cultivator waste**: cultivators' abilities come from external soul rings, they themselves do not understand laws, actual energy efficiency extremely low
- **Social waste**: soul master society revolves around soul rings, massive social resources used for hunting soul beasts rather than tech innovation
- **Ecological waste**: soul beast endangering causes ecological chain breakage, the entire planet's energy circulation damaged

## IV. Douluo's Protagonist Uniqueness Dependency

By the five-level standard, Douluo's protagonist uniqueness dependency is **high (Tang San / bestowed type)** — Tang San's strength comes from innate full spirit power + twin spirits + various adventures, essentially a "bestowed" strong one. Other characters' growth space is compressed by Tang San's protagonist aura.

## V. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

| Assessment Dimension | Douluo Score | Explanation |
|----------------------|--------------|-------------|
| Realm self-consistency | Low | The soul ring mechanism contradicts the "soul beasts have spirit" setting — if soul beasts have wisdom, hunting is murder |
| Retcon risk | High | Late soul beasts "becoming gods" contradicts early "soul beasts = materials" setting |
| Ending risk | Medium | The soul-beast-endangered problem is never truly solved |
| Plot kidnapping setting | Severe | To make protagonist leveling convenient, soul beasts are set as "huntable materials" |

**Core problem**: Douluo's soul ring mechanism is self-contradictory in setting — on one hand acknowledging soul beasts have wisdom and emotion, on the other treating them as huntable materials. This contradiction becomes more obvious late, ultimately causing setting collapse.

## VI. Heng Yuan Zhou's Alternative: Path D · Human-Made Dao-Rule Soul Rings

Heng Yuan Zhou's response to Douluo is **Path D · human-made Dao-rule soul ring** technology. This technology does not hunt any soul beast, but through law engineering technology artificially manufactures Dao-rule soul rings — simulating soul rings' ability effects from the law level, but entirely based on symbiotic circulation principles.

Path D's core difference:
- Does not hunt soul beasts, does not predate life
- Human-made Dao-rules come from law understanding, not seizing soul beasts' abilities
- Technology fully open, everyone can use
- Efficiency far higher than predatory soul rings (because no refining loss)

## VII. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: the soul ring mechanism's refining loss rate is extremely high; Path D human-made Dao-rule soul rings approach 100% efficiency
- **Anti-predation**: the soul ring mechanism is predationism's typical; Path D fundamentally negates hunting-based leveling
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo locks the ceiling, Douluo relies on hunting — both are inferior to Heng Yuan Zhou
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: in Douluo soul beasts are hunted objects; in Heng Yuan Zhou Myriad Races are all equal
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Path D human-made Dao-rule soul ring technology is fully open, providing Myriad Races with non-predatory leveling paths

## Conclusion

Douluo's soul-ring realms are essentially "proving the Dao through killing" predatory cultivation. Behind every realm advancement lies one or more lives' extinction. This system looks effective short-term — cultivators indeed get stronger — but long-term it consumes the entire planet's life foundation. Heng Yuan Zhou's Path D · human-made Dao-rule soul ring technology fundamentally provides a non-predatory alternative: no hunting needed, no predation needed, technicized human-made Dao-rules can equally achieve realm advancement. This is the anti-predation axiom's victory at the technology level.
