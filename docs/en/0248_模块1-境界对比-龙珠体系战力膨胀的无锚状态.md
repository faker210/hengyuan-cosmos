# Entry 248 | Realm Comparison · Dragon Ball: The Unanchored State of Power Inflation

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 04, 244, 17

## Introduction: Infinite inflation from power level 5 to Super Saiyan God

Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s realm system is rooted in law self-consistency, while Dragon Ball's power system is characterized by "unanchored inflation" — from the early "power level 5" to the late "Super Saiyan God," power numbers and forms continuously upgrade, early settings repeatedly overturned. Dragon Ball's realm inflation is not based on law logic, but on the vicious cycle of "enemies get stronger → protagonist must get stronger."

The six top-level axioms run through the comparison throughout: the anti-predation axiom points out Dragon Ball's combat essence; the republican axiom of Myriad Races contrasts Dragon Ball's personal heroism; the collective ascension axiom contrasts Dragon Ball's single-person invincibility; the causal self-discipline axiom contrasts Dragon Ball's casual resurrection; the law-first axiom directly points to Dragon Ball's setting self-consistency problem; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Dragon Ball's Power Inflation Trajectory

Dragon Ball's power system has undergone multiple stages of inflation:

- **Early arc**: measured by "power level" numbers, Raditz's power level around 1000
- **Namek arc**: Super Saiyan, power level over 100 million
- **Cell Games arc**: beyond Super Saiyan
- **Buu arc**: fusion, Gotenks and other new forms
- **Battle of Gods arc**: Super Saiyan God
- **Dragon Ball Super arc**: Super Blue, Ultra Instinct, Perfected Ultra Instinct...

Every stage needs new forms to face new enemies; power inflation has no end.

## II. The Core Problem of "Unanchored State"

Dragon Ball's fundamental problem is **no law anchor** — power advancement is not based on understanding laws, but on "transformations" and "training out new modes." This causes:

- **Vague realm definitions**: Super Saiyan 1, 2, 3, God, Blue, Ultra Instinct... are these realms or forms?
- **Numbers meaningless**: power level numbers completely fail late, because numbers are too large to compare
- **Logic break**: early "training ten years" and late "transform once" intensity completely unequal
- **Power inversion**: new form when first appears crushes everything, a few chapters later becomes "base form"

## III. Dragon Ball's Energy-Level Waste

- **Combat waste**: Dragon Ball's battles often destroy planets and cities, massive energy wasted in destruction
- **Resurrection waste**: Dragon Balls can casually resurrect the dead, life-death's karmic meaning dissolved
- **Training waste**: protagonist's training results often "crushed on debut" by new enemies, front-loaded effort wasted
- **Supporting-character waste**: Z Fighters except the protagonist group all reduced to backgrounds, no longer participating in battles

## IV. Dragon Ball's Protagonist Uniqueness Dependency

By the five-level standard, Dragon Ball's protagonist uniqueness dependency is **extreme (Goku / solitary-tree type)** — the entire cosmos's fate ultimately must be solved by Goku (or Vegeta) transforming. Other characters' growth space is completely compressed by protagonist aura.

## V. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

| Assessment Dimension | Dragon Ball Score | Explanation |
|----------------------|-------------------|-------------|
| Realm self-consistency | Extremely low | Form naming arbitrary, realms and forms confused, no unified standard |
| Retcon risk | Extreme | Every stage overturns the previous stage's "strongest" setting |
| Ending risk | Medium | As long as sequels keep coming it never "ends," but settings are already unmanageable |
| Plot kidnapping setting | Extremely severe | Power inflation's sole cause is "needing stronger enemies" |

**Core problem**: Dragon Ball from the start never established a law-level realm framework — what it has is only "transformation forms." When forms are insufficient, new forms are invented. This is "unanchored state" — no law anchor, power inflation has no end and no direction.

## VI. Heng Yuan Zhou's Alternative Solution

How does Heng Yuan Zhou avoid unanchored inflation?
- **Law anchoring**: realm definitions based on law dimensions, not transformation forms
- **Dimensional leap**: every realm corresponds to true existence-dimension advancement, not number getting bigger
- **Myriad Races group**: strong ones are a group, no pressure of "only the protagonist carries"
- **Self-consistency verification**: every realm's definition goes through law self-consistency verification, not changed due to plot needs

## VII. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: Dragon Ball's combat destruction and wasted training are enormous waste; Heng Yuan Zhou's every realm advancement has actual civilization contribution
- **Anti-predation**: Dragon Ball's "strong protect weak" is essentially still "strongest calls the shots"; Heng Yuan Zhou's strong ones serve Myriad Races
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo locks the ceiling, Dragon Ball is infinite inflation — both are unanchored; Heng Yuan Zhou has law anchors
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Dragon Ball's protagonist group has only a few people; Heng Yuan Zhou's 20% Activation Strategy lets all Myriad Races have opportunity
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Heng Yuan Zhou's expansion is based on law self-consistency; Dragon Ball's inflation is based on transformation needs

## VIII. Dragon Ball Model's Lessons for Cultivation Civilization

Although Dragon Ball's power inflation model has many problems at the setting level, its lessons for cultivation civilization are not without value. Dragon Ball's "train—break through—train again" cycle rhythm, and the protagonist's spirit of constantly breaking through self through hard training, are themselves positive. The problem is not "cultivation," but "cultivation without a law framework" — only training power not understanding laws, only transforming not knowing rules.

Heng Yuan Zhou absorbs Dragon Ball's spiritual core of "hard training, never giving up," but puts it in a law-first framework: cultivation is not to transform to show enemies, but to understand cosmic laws, raise existence dimensions, serve Myriad Races symbiosis. Similarly "protagonist keeps getting stronger," one is unanchored inflation, the other is anchored growth — this is the difference.

## Conclusion

Dragon Ball's power inflation is essentially the consequence of "no law anchor" — no law framework constrains realm definitions, the protagonist invents whatever strength is needed. Short-term, new forms and new transformations are indeed very hype; long-term, settings get messier and messier, readers already cannot remember how many forms there are. Heng Yuan Zhou starting from the law-first axiom gives every realm a clear law definition and existence dimension — not "transform when stronger is needed," but "entering new realm after understanding new law dimensions." This is the fundamental difference between "anchored" and "unanchored."
