# Entry 249 | Realm Comparison · Perfect World: The Solitary-Dao Essence of Immortal Emperor

> Module: Module 1 · In-Depth Realm Refinement | Baseline references: Documents 04, 227, 05

## Introduction: The strongest's loneliness — Immortal Emperor is one person's Dao

Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos)'s realm system has Myriad Races symbiosis as ultimate direction, while Perfect World's Immortal Emperor realm takes "solitary Dao" as its essence — Shi Hao, cultivating to the highest realm, ultimately stands at the apex alone, companions, kin, and civilization behind him all having become passersby. Immortal Emperor is not "king of Myriad Races," but "the last person." This solitary-Dao essence is the inevitable result of predationism systems taken to the extreme.

The six top-level axioms run through the comparison throughout: the anti-predation axiom points out solitary Dao's predatory root; the republican axiom of Myriad Races directly negates solitary Dao; the collective ascension axiom contrasts Immortal Emperor's solo apex; the causal self-discipline axiom contrasts Immortal Emperor's solo karma-bearing; the law-first axiom directly points to Immortal Emperor's setting self-consistency problem; the universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Immortal Emperor Realm's Solitary-Dao Characteristics

Perfect World's Immortal Emperor realm has these solitary-Dao characteristics:

- **Unique**: at most one Immortal Emperor in the same era
- **Lonely**: Immortal Emperor stands at the cosmos's apex, no peers
- **Bears everything**: the cosmos's fate is borne by the Immortal Emperor alone
- **Forgets old companions**: old-era companions dissipate in the long river of time, Immortal Emperor walks alone

This "one person carries the cosmos" setting on the surface is epic, but actually is the **collective ascension axiom's opposite** — not Myriad Races ascending together, but one person ascending, everyone else staying behind.

## II. Solitary Dao's Root: The Ultimate Result of Predatory Cultivation

Immortal Emperor's solitary Dao is not accidental, but the inevitable result of predatory cultivation taken to the extreme:

### 2.1 The Path of Solo Strengthening
Predatory cultivation's core is "I myself get stronger" — swallowing resources, seizing adventures, grabbing legacies. Cultivators walk faster and faster, companions fall further and further behind. Ultimately when reaching the apex, looking back, no one can keep up.

### 2.2 Knowledge Monopoly's Inevitability
Predatory cultivation does not share knowledge — my method is mine, cannot teach others. The result is only the protagonist knows how to get stronger, others can never catch up.

### 2.3 Solo Karma-Bearing's Cost
Predated power carries original-owner will residual traces, requiring solo refining. Immortal Emperor's power accumulates countless karmic residuals, ultimately can only be borne alone — because no one can help share.

## III. Perfect World's Energy-Level Waste

- **Companion waste**: Shi Hao's companions each have talent, but ultimately none can keep up with the protagonist, their talents wasted
- **Civilization waste**: Nine Heavens Ten Earths' civilization repeatedly destroyed and rebuilt in continuous great wars, accumulation wasted
- **Time waste**: Immortal Emperor lives countless epochs, but most time spent fighting alone, not co-building civilization with Myriad Races
- **Knowledge waste**: Immortal Emperor's cultivation experience is not systematically shared, Myriad Races cannot benefit

## IV. Perfect World's Protagonist Uniqueness Dependency

By the five-level standard, Perfect World's protagonist uniqueness dependency is **extreme (Shi Hao / solitary-tree type)** — Shi Hao as Wild Heaven Emperor is the entire cosmos's unique ultimate guardian. Without Shi Hao, the cosmos is finished. This "one person carries the whole cosmos" setting is solitary Dao's typical manifestation.

**Core problem**: Immortal Emperor's "unique" setting is to make the protagonist a "unique hero," but this contradicts civilization development's objective law — a healthy civilization cannot have only one strong one. For the protagonist's lonely epic feel, civilization's collective growth is sacrificed.

## VI. Heng Yuan Zhou's Alternative: Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm

Heng Yuan Zhou's response to Immortal Emperor's solitary Dao is the **Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm** — the ultimate form of Myriad Races' origin balanced symbiosis.

Fundamental differences from Immortal Emperor's solitary Dao:
- Immortal Emperor is "one person stands above Myriad Races"; Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is "fully in frequency with Myriad Races' origin"
- Immortal Emperor's strength is lonely; Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's strength is symbiotic with Myriad Races
- Immortal Emperor alone bears everything; Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm strong ones are anchors of Myriad Races' balance
- Immortal Emperor is the end (nothing above); Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is current framework's ultimate but still expandable

## VII. Five Core Takeaways

- **Universal infinite backstop**: the universal equal infinite law establishes that every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing have a guarantee, strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose, predation naturally loses meaning
- **Anti-energy waste**: Immortal Emperor's solitary Dao causes companion talent waste and civilization accumulation waste; Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm lets Myriad Races evolve together, not wasting any talent
- **Anti-predation**: Immortal Emperor's power accumulation process is essentially predatory; Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is entirely based on symbiotic circulation
- **Tang-Luo upper limit comparison**: Tang-Luo locks the ceiling, Perfect World is the solitary-Dao apex — both are inferior to Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad Races symbiosis
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Immortal Emperor is only one; Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's direction is that all Myriad Races can walk it
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's methodology is fully open, not an endpoint only the protagonist can reach

## Conclusion

Immortal Emperor realm's solitary-Dao essence is the ultimate form of predatory cultivation taken to the extreme — one person gets stronger beyond reach, then stands lonely at the apex. This setting is literary epic, but at the civilization level is tragedy: Myriad Races' fate hinges on one person; this person falls and civilization is finished. Heng Yuan Zhou's Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm takes a completely different road — not one person standing above Myriad Races, but fully in frequency with Myriad Races' origin. One is solitary Dao, one is symbiosis; one is the end, one is the starting point. This is the ultimate divergence of two realm philosophies.
