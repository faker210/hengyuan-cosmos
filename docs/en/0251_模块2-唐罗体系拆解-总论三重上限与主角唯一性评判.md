# Chapter 251 | Tang-Luo System Teardown (General Theory): The Old Era's Inclusive Ceiling and the Criterion for Protagonist Uniqueness

> Module: Module Two · Cross-Heaven Canon Civilization Benchmarking and Critique Library | Series: Full Teardown of the Tang-Luo System (first of a multi-part series)

## Introduction: Inclusivity Is the Limit of Kindness, Not the End of Civilization

Tang Luo, from *I Can See Combat Power*, is the most top-tier inclusive-type protagonist in traditional web novels: he breaks the aristocratic monopoly, opens the mid-to-low tier cultivation system to the human race, rebuilds Peerless Academy, and popularizes martial knowledge. Taken individually, these three deeds alone are enough to place him among the cross-heaven protagonist lineage as "the one who looks most like a good person."

But Heng Yuan Zhou (The Original Cosmos of Ten Thousand Spirits and the Balanced Anchor) judges Tang Luo not by how much good he did, but by how far the system he built can go. As the general theory of the Tang-Luo teardown series, this article establishes three main lines of judgment: energy-tier waste, historical regression, and predatory thinking; it also introduces a brand-new criterion—"protagonist-uniqueness dependence" under comparison with the Gu Chensha system—as the unified yardstick for this series and for all cross-heaven benchmarking pieces (251–500).

The five-dimensional judgment bedrock of this article is jointly constituted by the Six Top Axioms and the Five Mandatory Cores: the Axiom of Anti-Predation (develop increments, do not consume stock), the Axiom of Myriad Races Republic (all beings' equal evolutionary entitlement), the Axiom of Collective Dimensional Ascension (the strong feed back to the Myriad Races, no big-material-small-use), the Axiom of Causal Self-Discipline (observe first, then limited interference, feed back knowledge only), the Axiom of Law Priority (law comes first, never retcon, setting self-consistency above plot convenience); the Five Mandatory Cores—anti-energy-tier-waste, anti-predation, Tang-Luo ceiling comparison, full Myriad-Race coverage, and hyper-pluralistic full openness. All subsequent cross-heaven benchmarking pieces (251–500) uniformly adopt this bedrock.
6. **Law of Universal Infinite Equality for All Beings**: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently carry Fundamental Infinite Power; the laid-back and the slacker retain a guaranteed baseline infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; grinders and peerless youths can stack more and higher tiers of infinity. Infinite origin is carried by everyone inherently, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Analysis of "Energy-Tier Waste" in This System's Strong

Tang Luo's energy tier is not low: breaking the monopoly, rebuilding the academy, popularizing knowledge—these are "civilization-reconstruction-grade" feats requiring top-tier vision and execution. But his power deployment suffers from systematic energy-tier waste:

- Waste on the racial dimension: inclusivity covers only the human race. Alien beasts, plant spirits, planetary will, and cosmic beings are all excluded from "inclusivity"—not out of insufficient ability, but because the worldview simply reserved no place for them.
- Waste on the tier dimension: opening extends only to the Xiong Realm. The high-level great Dao and the ultimate path are entirely private—the most precious things are precisely what he did not bring out.
- Waste on the model dimension: a gift-giving system. Light is bestowed by the strong, and all beings depend on the strong—no matter how large "inclusivity" grows, it is only the projection of the strong's will, not a product co-built by all beings.

Conclusion: Tang Luo used "civilization-reconstruction-grade" energy tier for "patching the old framework." It is like wielding cosmic-level power yet using it only to repair one's own eaves—superb material, super small use.

## II. Why This Civilization Must Inevidently Regress Historically

The historical regression of the Tang-Luo system is not "possible" but "inevitable," and the reason lies in the structure:

1. Single-point dependence: Peerless Academy and martial popularization all rest on Tang Luo alone. While he lives, the system runs; once he enters seclusion, falls, or changes his mind, the system trembles with him—this is "protagonist uniqueness" made manifest at the institutional level.
2. Gift-style transmission: knowledge popularization is "the strong give," not "all beings have." Those who are gifted have no sense of ownership; once the benefactor is absent, transmission breaks immediately.
3. Ceiling-driven involution: the Xiong Realm is a locked ceiling. The better the mid-to-low tiers are opened, the fiercer the involution—inclusivity built a larger arena, but did not open a higher door.

The fate of predatory-faction civilizations (including the "enlightened predatory" Tang-Luo civilization) is exactly the same: **to bet order on one person is to bet civilization on a single log bridge.** This is not a moral problem, but a structural one.

## III. Deep Teardown of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Tang Luo's personal conduct is far above that of most cross-heaven protagonists, but "predatory thinking" is defined not by malice, but by structure:

- Knowledge monopoly is predation: not opening the high-level great Dao is knowledge-level predation—clutching the upward channel in one's own hand.
- Racial exclusion is predation: benefiting only the human race amounts to nationalizing the Myriad Races' evolutionary right into one race—a civilization-level resource monopoly.
- Gift-dependence is predation: letting all beings survive by clinging to the strong deprives them of the subject qualification to "co-build"—the gentlest predation is making people accustomed to being given.

In Heng Yuan Zhou terms: Tang Luo is a typical "enlightened predator." He is relatively merciful and truly well-intentioned, but he stands on the ceiling of a predatory system and has never overthrown and rewritten the underlying law. What he lacks is not kindness, but the "righteous rogue" step—daring to tear apart the conventionally accepted old law and truly return the monopolized great Dao to the Myriad Races.

## V. Comparison with the Gu Chensha System: Shattering Protagonist Uniqueness

Introducing Gu Chensha from *Dragon Symbol* as a comparison anchor establishes this series' most important criterion.

Factual features of the Gu Chensha system: the first in all history to cultivate the Heavenly-Dao Law; his sage way is the sky-mending way and the heaven-reforming way; he is willing to bear all karmic sin on behalf of all beings; in his later days he casually creates universes; he is widely recognized in web fiction as one of the power ceilings; his worldview even raises the grand proposition that "heaven, earth, and all beings can all become dragons."

Criterion for judging protagonist-uniqueness dependence (enabled for this series and all cross-heaven benchmarking pieces):

| Tier | Uniqueness Dependence | Civilizational Form | Representative |
|------|----------------------|---------------------|----------------|
| Extreme | Civilization's survival rests on one protagonist; protagonist's absence means collapse | Single-log-bridge civilization | Gu Chensha: beings are "made into dragons," the opportunity rests on the "first in all history" alone |
| High | Order exists while the protagonist exists; protagonist's absence means regression | Gift-style civilization | Tang Luo: inclusivity rests on one person's kindness; institutions are absent |
| Medium | Depends on a small group of strong; the strong group's absence means turmoil | Oligarchic civilization | Ye Fan (Shrouding the Heavens): ten thousand years of peace bears the surname "Ye"; no institution established |
| Very low | Order is written into institutions and networks; any individual's absence does not affect operation | Network-style civilization | Heng Yuan Zhou: anchor + law + council + Myriad-Race Republic |

The lesson of the Gu Chensha system is precisely the opposite of its surface: the greater his "bearing sin on behalf of beings" magnanimity, the more it proves that beings outsourced karma to a single person; the grander his promise that "heaven and earth and all beings can become dragons," the more it exposes that its realization depends entirely on the "first in all history"'s mercy. Shattering protagonist uniqueness is to turn "being made into dragons" into "becoming dragons together"—no longer waiting for a sage, but letting the Myriad Races themselves cultivate the ability to no longer need someone else to bear it on their behalf. This is the ultimate divide between Heng Yuan Zhou and every cross-heaven system.

## VI. Assessment of Setting Self-Consistency and Retconning/Rushed-Ending Risk

The number-one hidden cause of death in long web novels is not hiatus, but retconning—setting kidnapped by plot convenience: early iron rules are casually broken in later days, power inflates to the point of inconsistency, and the realm system collapses overall in mid-to-late stages. This section, per the Axiom of Law Priority (law comes first, never retcon, setting self-consistency above plot convenience), establishes "setting self-consistency / retconning-and-rushed-ending risk" as a fixed dimension by which Heng Yuan Zhou judges every benchmarked IP.

Consider two counter-examples. *Douluo Continent* is founded on the spirit-ring system: the early hard constraint that "a hundred-year spirit ring is already scarce, and a thousand-year spirit ring marks an elite" is a reader-acknowledged hard rule, yet in mid-to-late stages it is broken by "hundred-thousand-year spirit beasts," "spirit souls," and "极限Douluo" one after another; the spirit ring devalues from a scarce resource to a nearly mass-producible standard equipment, and the numerical ladder painstakingly built early is later watered down wholesale, leaving readers to explain it away with "protagonist halo." This is typical plot-kidnapping-setting: to let the protagonist keep leveling up, early iron rules are torn up one by one, and power inflates out of control. *Shrouding the Heavens*, by contrast, collapses in late-stage realms: the carefully divided early realms of Wheel-Sea, Dao-Palace, Four-Poles, Dragon-Transformation, and so on lose their binding force at the Great Emperor and Red-Dust Immortal stages; who is stronger or weaker serves entirely the emotional needs of hot-blooded scenes; "whoever has the final say" gives way to "whoever the plot needs to win." The direct consequence of setting yielding to plot is that late-stage readers no longer believe in these rules.

The root of retconning and rushed endings in traditional long-form IP lies not in the author's penmanship, but in structure: law does not come first, so setting serves plot; once the plot needs a leap in rank, the rules give way, and thus power inflation, realm collapse, and foreshadowing left unfinished become inevitable. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is precisely the Axiom of Law Priority: law comes first, never retcon, never rush the ending; setting self-consistency above plot convenience; the worldview may expand infinitely without collapsing. The 300 origin systems, the nine-realm promotion, and Alaya supervision all legislate first and expand later; newly added dimensions (the Multi-Realm, the Balanced-Anchor Hyper-Pluralistic Realm) are always incrementally connected via appendix specifications, never overturning old settings—this is the institutional guarantee of "expanding infinitely without collapsing."

Score for the Tang-Luo system: setting self-consistency is **medium-high**. Tang Luo's realm system (capped at the Xiong Realm) and Peerless Academy's operating rules are largely self-consistent within his worldview, with no obvious self-contradiction in the early stages; but its self-consistency highly depends on the premise that "Tang Luo himself remains in place." Once the plot requires him to enter seclusion, fall, or change his mind, the system's rules hang in the air, and self-consistency begins to leak. Retconning/rushed-ending risk rating is **medium**: its internal rules have not yet shown Douluo-style late-stage watering down, nor Shrouding-style realm collapse; the main risk point is "single-point dependence"—the system's rules rest on one person rather than on law, and once the plot requires the protagonist to leave, the rules cannot continue.

From this section onward, **setting self-consistency / retconning-and-rushed-ending risk assessment** is enabled as a fixed judgment dimension for all 251–500 benchmarking pieces, standing alongside energy-tier waste, historical regression, predatory thinking, protagonist-uniqueness dependence, and the symbiotic alternative; every piece must judge, score, and give a risk rating.

## Five Core Landing Points

- **Anti-energy-tier waste**: the Tang-Luo system uses Xiong-Realm-grade talent for personal martial breakthrough, with energy-tier utilization under thirty percent; Heng Yuan Zhou folds the same-grade talent into Myriad-Race Republic co-building, with energy-tier utilization approaching full.
- **Anti-predation**: Tang Luo's Peerless Academy is essentially gift-style predation, with knowledge monopolized by the protagonist and his circle; Heng Yuan Zhou's 300 origin cultivation systems are fully disclosed at all tiers, with no private hoarding and no monopoly.
- **Tang-Luo ceiling comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's Xiong-Realm ceiling locks the civilization's ceiling, and the protagonist's absence means regression; Heng Yuan Zhou replaces protagonist dependence with a law network, with order written into institutions.
- **Full Myriad-Race coverage**: the Tang-Luo system covers only human martial artists, excluding alien beasts and foreign races; Heng Yuan Zhou achieves 99% full Myriad-Race coverage, with all five lineages—carbon-based, energy, mechanical, consciousness, and hybrid—having a path.
- **Hyper-pluralistic full openness**: the Tang-Luo system is trapped in single-planet martial art, with no cross-plane capability; Heng Yuan Zhou already crosses planes at the Origin Realm, and the Balanced-Anchor Hyper-Pluralistic Realm achieves multiverse dominion and Myriad-Race symbiosis.
