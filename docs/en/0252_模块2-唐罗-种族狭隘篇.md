# Entry 252 | Tang-Luo Decomposition · Racial Narrowness: The Civilization Ceiling Where Universal Benefit Covers Only Humanity

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 2, continuing Entry 251 general treatise)

## Introduction: Good will stops at the racial boundary

Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos) evaluates all Myriad Realms systems on the base of six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom, the republican axiom of Myriad Races, the collective ascension axiom, the causal self-discipline axiom, the law-first axiom, and the universal equal infinite law. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Among them, the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings to have equal evolution qualification, with 99% full coverage of Myriad Races — human men/women, Youth Supremes, strange beasts, plant spirits, planet consciousness, cosmic origin bodies, multiverse residual civilizations all have roads. Tang Luo's universal benefit precisely draws a hard ceiling on the "humanity" racial line.

This entry takes the law-first axiom as core: laws should precede racial stance. If a civilization's laws only recognize one race's evolution rights, no matter how well-intentioned, it only contracts predation's boundary from "aristocrat vs commoner" to "humanity vs Myriad Races," the structure unchanged. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Tang Luo breaking aristocratic monopoly, popularizing martial arts, rebuilding Wushuang Academy is civilization-reconstruction-tier energy. But this energy's投放 range is rigidly limited within humanity:

- **Object waste**: humanity occupies only a tiny fraction of this world's sentient beings. Strange beasts becoming wise, plants opening spirits, mountain-river wills awakening are not non-existent in this world-view, but all are not on the "universal benefit" list — over 90% of potential cultivators are default-excluded
- **Structural waste**: meridians, methods, alchemy systems opened within humanity could originally be adapted cross-racially, but were never transformed due to human-centered perspective. The same energy, invested within humanity is "universal benefit," stepping outside racial boundary it is zero
- **Ecological waste**: human and demon races long opposed, Tang Luo's universal benefit did not convert into institutional ecological reconciliation, only divided the cake evenly within humanity, external contradictions unchanged

Energy-level waste's essence: holding civilization-reconstruction-tier transformative power, only doing allocation reform within one race. **Super-large talent, super-small use** — using cosmos-scale power to repair one race's roof.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Once racial boundaries are legislatively solidified, civilizational regression is structurally inevitable:

1. **External enemy-making**: strange beasts and foreign races excluded from universal benefit cannot rise through cultivation, can only adopt preying on human resources as survival strategy — the more successful universal benefit is, the more external threats are institutionally manufactured
2. **Internal conservatism**: within humanity, "exclusive" dividends form racial chauvinism consensus; any voice advocating opening Myriad Races is seen as "betraying one's own race." After Tang Luo's death, this chauvinism automatically takes over the system
3. **Sample exhaustion**: the cultivation system iterates only within one race's physiological upper limit, losing the possibility of colliding and evolving with foreign-race methods; after a hundred years the system enters technological stagnation

The historical regression trajectory is not collapse, but "solidification": prosperity within humanity continues, but the entire civilizational ecosystem spins in place, ultimately being reduced-dimensioned by a more open external civilization.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Tang Luo's good will toward humanity is real, but predatory thinking is defined by structure not ill will:

- **Exclusive possession is predation**: limiting cultivation resources and high-tier knowledge to "humanity's property" is equivalent to declaring Myriad Races' evolution rights zero. This is the same logic as aristocratic families limiting knowledge to "family property," only with a larger scope
- **Othering is predation**: non-human sentient life is default-classified as "demon," "monster," "food," not entering moral consideration circles. This othering is the psychological precondition for predation's rationalization
- **Bestowal does not cross races**: Tang Luo's bestowal list has no place for foreign races — not that he bullies foreign races, but he never thought foreign races should also be included in "all beings." The gentlest predation is when even those being predated are not seen

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard, Tang Luo is the **enlightened predator**: relatively merciful, truly well-intentioned, extremely just within humanity, but he stands on the predatory system's ceiling and has never expanded the definition of "all beings" from one race to Myriad Races. What he lacks is not good will, but institutional vision under the republican axiom of Myriad Races.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

Scored by the law-first axiom:

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-high**. "Humanity first" is self-consistent within its world-view; human-demon opposition is the early-laid world-view底色, no self-contradictions seen
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Risk point lies in: if late plot needs "demon beasts suddenly get stronger" to create climaxes, the early "humanity uniquely strong" setting will be torn up for plot convenience — this is racial-narrowness settings' common flaw; once plot needs external enemies, it can only force-raise foreign-race combat power, creating number water-inflation
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: high (bestowal-style civilization). Racial boundary jurisprudence is backed only by Tang Luo alone; once he retires, human chauvinism automatically resurfaces, no one can correct it

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

- **Anti-energy waste**: civilization-reconstruction-tier energy locked within one race, 90% of sentient beings excluded

- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's good stops at humanity, exactly the old era's universal-benefit boundary
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: strange beasts / plant spirits / planet consciousness must be included in the evolution-qualification circle

## Conclusion

Tang Luo's universal benefit is real universal benefit, but also small-scale universal benefit. It proves one thing: if good will is not written into the definition of "all beings," it will sooner or later be locked by racial boundaries. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is not to criticize Tang Luo for being insufficiently good, but to legislate — the republican axiom of Myriad Races written into the constitution, so that "who counts as all beings" no longer depends on a strong one's radius of good will.
