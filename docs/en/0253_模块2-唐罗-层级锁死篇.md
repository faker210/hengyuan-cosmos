# Entry 253 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | Tier Lock: The Essence of Private Great Dao Above Fierce Realm

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 3)

## Introduction: Locking the best door

Among the six top-level axioms, the anti-predation axiom's first article is "Great Dao public at all tiers, no private hoarding, no monopoly." The Tang-Luo system's crux is precisely here: he opened the mid-low tier doors, but locked the highest door — the Great Dao above fierce realm — firmly in his own hand. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core: if laws legislate the highest truth as private, then all lower-tier opening is only "profit-sharing," not "liberation." Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Tang Luo himself stands at this world's apex, mastering the ultimate path above fierce realm. This energy's waste is the most glaring in the whole series:

- **Top-tier knowledge idle**: the highest-tier Great Dao insights, law understanding, breakthrough paths are all held by Tang Luo alone. He lives, this knowledge is only his personal combat power; he goes into seclusion, this knowledge equals non-existent for the entire civilization
- **Downward adaptation missing**: mid-low tier methods are opened, but top-tier methods have no "translated version," "adapted version," "ladder version" — the opened system broke the last rung of the ladder, all beings climb to fierce realm and hit the wall
- **Solo-cultivation waste**: Tang Luo alone deduces, alone breaks through, did not open the deduction process to an Alaya-style shared network; work that billions of intellects could participate in is pressed onto one brain

Energy-level waste formula: civilization-reconstruction-tier top-tier cognition is compressed into one person's private combat power. **This is not that the strong one is too strong, but that civilization is too poor** — poor in the place that should be richest.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Tier-lock's historical regression logic chain:

1. **Ceiling involution**: the more thoroughly mid-low tiers are opened, the more geniuses flood to fierce realm, but there is no road above. Geniuses kill each other, internally fight, go into demonic deviation under the ceiling — universal benefit creates more wall-hitters
2. **Transmission fault**: Tang Luo dies, top-tier Great Dao goes to the grave with him. Even if an unparalleled genius appears in later generations, there is no road to walk, can only grope from scratch — civilization's top height is one-time zeroed
3. **Faith attachment**: all beings place all hope of "breaking the upper limit" on Tang Luo's "someday in a good mood opening another tier," losing independent deduction will. After a hundred years, civilization at the top completely loses hematopoiesis capacity

The regression form: not collapse, but **capping**. Civilization looks unprecedentedly prosperous under Tang Luo's rule, but actually top-tier knowledge only flows out not in, decreasing generation by generation.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Tang Luo's personal conduct is irreproachable, but structurally:

- **Knowledge monopoly is predation**: highest-tier Great Dao is cultivation civilization's most core means of production. Making it private is equivalent to locking the nation's top laboratory in one's own secret chamber — the same thing as aristocratic families monopolizing methods back then, only the person locking the door changed to Tang Luo
- **Ladder truncation is predation**: opening mid-low tiers but truncating the top is a carefully designed "controllable opening" — letting all beings have hope and something to strive for, but always missing the last step. This is the bestowal system's most precise component
- **Autocratic breakthrough is predation**: Tang Luo alone decides "which tier to open, when to open, for whom to open"; all beings have no say in their own evolution path — nominally universal benefit, essentially approval-system

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo is the **enlightened predator**. He may even sincerely believe "the timing for top-tier Great Dao is not ripe, giving it would harm all beings" — but this "for your own good" monopoly is still monopoly. What he lacks is the "righteous rogue": daring to hand over his most precious Dao, all at once, indiscriminately, irreversibly.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-high**. "Top-tier private" is logically self-consistent within its world-view: top strong ones keeping hole cards is consistent with web-novel convention
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium-high**. This is this system's most dangerous minefield — if late plot needs new enemies or new climaxes, the author's most natural move is "Tang Luo broke through again," "there is yet another tier above the top." Each time the ceiling is temporarily raised is one retcon; Douluo's soul-ring watering, Cover the Sky's realm collapse are all on this road
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: high. Top-tier knowledge has no second backup, Tang Luo is all

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the most precious top-tier cognition is compressed into one person's combat power
- **Anti-predation**: private Great Dao is the highest form of knowledge predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo opened the lower half of the ladder, but clutches the upper half in his hand
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the ladder should be open to all Myriad Races beings, not one race
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: top tier not opened, civilization can never reach the multiverse layer
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

The Tang-Luo system's truth: he tore down aristocratic families' walls, but built a door higher up for which only he has the key. Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment is — doors can exist, but keys must hang in public places. Under the law-first axiom, opening top-tier Great Dao is not the strong one's bestowal, but civilization's legislation.
