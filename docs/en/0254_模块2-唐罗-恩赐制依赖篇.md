# Entry 254 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | Bestowal Dependency: The Structure of Strong Ones Almsgiving and All Beings Attachment

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 4)

## Introduction: The gentlest chain

Among the six top-level axioms, the collective ascension axiom requires "strong ones not ascending alone, lifting Myriad Races forward to evolve together." Tang Luo achieved "lifting," but did not achieve "together" — he lifts all beings as they walk, but all beings' feet never learn to walk. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core: order must be written into laws, not into strong ones' good will; the latter can be withdrawn at any time. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization** — this entry is precisely the anatomy of the words "high dependency." The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The bestowal system's energy-level waste is a kind of "relational waste":

- **Recipients enfeebled**: every alms weakens the recipient's self-reliance. The more Tang Luo gives, the less all beings dare to venture on their own — ability atrophies under repeated acceptance of alms
- **Supervision vacant**: without Alaya-style multi-layer supervision, Tang Luo's bestowal depends entirely on his personal judgment of "who deserves it, how much." Judgment bias has no check; energy mismatch has no correction
- **Network single-pointed**: billions of beings' evolution resources are all distributed through this one node of Tang Luo. His node exists, the network exists; his node breaks, the whole network paralyzes — this is the most expensive network architecture

Energy-level waste quantification: civilization energy that could support ten thousand independent evolution nodes is compressed into one "super dealer." **Civilization's muscle has grown into one person's arm.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

The bestowal system's historical regression is written into the structure:

1. **Benefactor dependency chain**: all beings take "being almsgiven" as a survival mode, no longer investing in their own hematopoiesis. Tang Luo is here, all is well; Tang Luo goes into seclusion for a year, society immediately shows "cutoff panic"
2. **Selective disability**: recipients have no ownership consciousness. Knowledge is "what Tang Luo gave," so they will not maintain, iterate, or defend it — transmission fluctuates with the benefactor's mood
3. **Flattery politicized**: under the bestowal system, being close to the center of power is more cost-effective than improving oneself. Civilization's clever minds begin studying "how to please Tang Luo" rather than "how to break through realms" — this is all attachment-type civilizations' internal-friction law

The historical regression form is "euthanasia": no war, no collapse, just day by day less able to walk on its own.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Depriving subjectivity is predation**: the bestowal system's most hidden predation is depriving all beings of the belief "I can create myself." Tang Luo gave you everything, except the legal confirmation of "you were already qualified."
- **Attachment is a chain**: all beings attach to strong ones for survival; on the surface the benefactor is giving, but actually all beings have mortgaged the sovereignty of evolution. This is not Tang Luo's conspiracy, but the bestowal structure's automatic product
- **Good will's closed loop**: the kinder Tang Luo is, the deeper the attachment; the deeper the attachment, the more all beings cannot leave him; cannot leave, the less they dare question any of his decisions — good will ultimately closes into unchecked power

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo is the **enlightened predator**, and a specimen of bestowal-style predation. His good will is 100% sincere, but a sincere attachment structure is still an attachment structure. He lacks the "righteous rogue," and even more lacks a set of institutions not depending on him personally.

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Tang-Luo Bestowal System | Anchor Symbiosis Alternative |
|--------------------------|------------------------------|
| Strong ones almsgive, all beings receive | Myriad Races republican co-building, order legislated by all beings, strong ones are only one of the executing bodies |
| Benefactor decides distribution | Joint Contracting, more work more pay, 20% Activation Strategy; distribution by institution not individual adjudication |
| Single-point distribution | Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision, distributed nodes, no single-point failure |
| Recipients attach | Everyone-Has-a-Road Guarantee System: evolution qualification is a natural right, not someone's gift |

Alternative's crux: rewrite "Tang Luo gives" into "the system has." The same good will, once put into institutions, no longer depends on the well-wisher being in office.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-high**. "Strong one shows grace, all beings are grateful" fits this type of web novel's emotional logic, internally self-consistent
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. The risk is not setting contradiction, but the author's narrative fatigue — long-form writing late, it is hard to maintain "Tang Luo is forever merciful" characterization; once Tang Luo turns dark, changes heart, or makes mistakes, all front-loaded "grace narrative" must be rewritten; the author can only force a save with "had his reasons," which is bestowal-type long-form's most common ending path
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: extreme risk point. The bestowal system pushes dependency to the ceiling — this entry judges it at the upper edge of the high-dependency band, close to Gu Chensha-style solitary-tree type

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: billions of nodes compressed into one distribution node, network architecture's enormous waste
- **Anti-predation**: depriving all beings' subjectivity is the gentlest yet firmest predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the thicker Tang Luo's alms, the heavier the chain of attachment
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: bestowal only reaches those who are seen; institutions cover 99%
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: single-node networks cannot expand across planes; distributed networks can
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

The bestowal system's fundamental problem is not that the almsgiver is not good enough, but that the act of almsgiving itself treats all beings as recipients, not as contracting parties. Heng Yuan Zhou's choice: no sages, but a constitution; no alms, but rights confirmation.
