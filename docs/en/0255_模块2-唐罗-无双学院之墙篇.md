# Entry 255 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | The Wall of Wushuang Academy: Class Solidification Inside and Outside the Academy

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 5)

## Introduction: Tearing down one wall, building another

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings to have equal evolution qualification; the law-first axiom requires law first. Tang Luo rebuilt Wushuang Academy with the original intention of tearing down aristocratic families' monopoly on education — but once a new academy becomes the sole cultivation entry, it itself becomes a new wall. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core: the education entry cannot rest on one school, one principal. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- **Single-campus waste**: Wushuang Academy concentrates the best teachers, methods, resources in one place. World talents must travel a thousand li to attend — travel cost, quota screening, the talent waste of those who fail admission, all borne by civilization
- **Admission as stratification**: the academy's selection mechanism itself manufactures new classes. Admitted enter the fast track, failures return to their original tracks — under universal benefit's halo, the silent majority who "cannot get in" are ignored
- **Curriculum tier waste**: courses inside the academy are still allocated high-tier resources by "whom Tang Luo has taken a fancy to." Under the same roof, class revives in the form of another class schedule

Energy-level waste's essence: Tang Luo used one academy to concentrate old aristocratic families' dispersed monopoly into one school's centralized monopoly. **Smashed the aristocrats, fattened the top students.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Entry narrowing**: Wushuang Academy quotas are limited, while the cultivation population explodes due to popularization. Thousands of troops cross a single-log bridge; the elite on this side and the "failures" on the other form a new binary opposition
2. **Principal leaves, tea cools**: the academy was personally founded by Tang Luo; school rules, ethos, teachers all are his personality projection. He retires, who succeeds? Can the successor maintain his standards? — this is all "principal-star system" schools' destiny
3. **Knowledge vacuum outside school**: outside the academy, private self-learners who cannot get orthodox courses sprout wild methods. Demonic deviation, pseudo-methods rampant, evil cultivators proliferating are precisely the byproducts of "orthodoxy in one place only"

Regression form: the academy runs better and better, outside the academy grows wilder and wilder; civilization as a whole is torn into two worlds — "inside school" and "outside school."

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Entry approval is predation**: who can enroll, who can take high-tier courses, approval power is in the academy, ultimately in Tang Luo. Turning "can one cultivate" into "can one be selected" is downgrading a natural right to an approval qualification
- **Curriculum layering is predation**: high-tier courses only offered to "whom Tang Luo fancies" has exactly the same structure as aristocratic families back then only transmitting to "family descendants," only the selection standard changed from bloodline to "principal's eye favor"
- **Knowledge single-point is predation**: cultivation orthodoxy exists only here, equivalent to declaring all other learning paths "non-orthodox" — this is knowledge monopoly's school form

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo is the **enlightened predator**. His original intention in founding the academy was supremely good, but the "single orthodox academy" form itself is just old monopoly with a new sign. What he lacks is breaking the academy into a network — a cross-plane mentor network, letting knowledge be everywhere rather than elites gathered in one place.

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Tang-Luo Wushuang Academy | Anchor Symbiosis Alternative |
|---------------------------|------------------------------|
| Sole orthodoxy, centralized campus | Cross-plane mentor network, distributed teaching, any corner can enroll |
| Selection system, limited quotas | Everyone-Has-a-Road Guarantee System, Education Guarantee Law backstop, no "can't get in so no road" |
| Courses layered by principal's eye | Symbiotic Education System layered by talent and progress, but every tier fully open, queryable, audit-able |
| Teachers concentrated on famous masters | All courses uploaded to Alaya Network, billions of students and billions of mentors paired in real time |
| Principal leaves, tea cools | Education written into constitution, curriculum standards adjudicated by Symbiotic Court, not depending on any famous master |

Alternative's crux: turn "one academy" into "one network." The wall is torn down, do not build it back.

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

1. **Modeling**: replicate Wushuang Academy's admission model and off-campus resource vacuum belt
2. **Variables**: Group A single-academy system; Group B injects cross-plane mentor network
3. **Observation**: number of off-campus evil cultivators within a hundred years, success rate of failures, overall cultivation population growth
4. **Review expectation**: Group B overall success rate several times Group A, evil-cultivator incidents approach zero — proving "concentrating elites" is inferior to "distributed universal benefit"

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-high**. "Protagonist founds a badass academy" is this type of world-view's standard, logically self-consistent
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Typical risk: late academy scale inflates to the point of self-inconsistency — from "one academy" to "countless academies under heaven," the early "sole orthodoxy" setting is quietly diluted; or to create conflict, temporarily "a traitor appears in the academy / a teacher turns dark," plot needs override school rules
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: high. The academy's soul is Tang Luo himself, institutions are only his extension

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: centralized schooling causes triple waste of travel, screening, and failure
- **Anti-predation**: entry approval power downgrades a natural right to an approval qualification
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo tore down aristocratic families' walls, but built a new building on the original site
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the academy should be cross-racial, cross-plane, covering 99% of beings
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: distributed networks match multiverse scale
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Wushuang Academy is a monument to Tang Luo's good will, and also a monument to Tang Luo's limits. The monument tells later generations what he broke; the memorial tells later generations what he did not break — the word "sole" itself is a wall.
