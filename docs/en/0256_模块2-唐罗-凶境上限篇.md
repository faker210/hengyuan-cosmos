## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The fierce-realm ceiling causes "endogenous" energy-level waste:

- **Genius attrition**: generation after generation of top geniuses rush to fierce realm, hit the wall, then consume each other under the wall. They could have continued breaking through and feeding back to civilization, but are forced into internal friction by the ceiling
- **Resource idling**: to compete for limited "fierce-realm seats" under the ceiling, civilization invests massive resources into zero-sum competition — whoever takes one more seat grabs it from someone else. These resources could have been invested in incremental frontier expansion
- **Hope inflation**: universal education manufactures millions of people who "want to break through," but only promises a tiny few "can reach the top." Hope is watered down by inflation; anxiety is magnified by deflation

Energy-level waste formula: the more successful opening is, the more wall-hitters; the more wall-hitters, the more violent civilization's internal friction. **Universal benefit's good will is folded by the ceiling into involution's fuel.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Zero-sumization**: under the ceiling, evolution shifts from "baking a bigger cake" to "dividing the cake." Civilization's geniuses no longer cooperate in frontier expansion, but calculate each other — because there are no increments above
2. **Solidification**: those who reached fierce realm first become new vested-interest groups; they guard positions under the ceiling and obstruct latecomers. Old aristocratic families fell, a new "fierce-realm club" was established
3. **Despair transmission**: the top's hopeless emotion transmits downward. Mid-low disciples watch seniors under the ceiling involute — what is the meaning of their own effort? — civilization's striving will is systematically eroded within a hundred years

Regression form: civilization has no external enemy, but slowly suffocates under the internal ceiling. This is the quietest, yet most thorough regression.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Capping is predation**: nailing the entire civilization's height to one number is equivalent to sentencing billions of beings "you've reached the end in this life." This is total-amount predation — not robbing your things, but robbing your future
- **Scarcity manufacturing**: fierce-realm seats become scarce resources; the competition for seats is naturally predatory. Tang Luo's universal benefit inadvertently manufactured a larger predatory battlefield
- **Vested-interest reproduction**: those who reached the apex first become new powerholders; they in turn maintain the ceiling — because the ceiling protects their positions. If Tang Luo does not personally smash the ceiling, the universal benefit he established will eventually be hollowed out by new powerholders under the ceiling

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo is the **enlightened predator**, but he himself is also the ceiling's prisoner — he himself does not know how to go higher, can only console himself with "fierce realm is the ultimate." What he lacks is not the courage to smash the wall, but a set of expansion laws of "beyond the wall there is another wall, beyond that infinite walls."
