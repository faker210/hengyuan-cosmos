# Entry 257 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | Critique of Pseudo-Universal-Benefit: The Limit of Good Will and the Absence of Institutions

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 7)

## Introduction: Why a good person's good deeds do not count

The order of the six top-level axioms is: the anti-predation axiom, the republican axiom of Myriad Races, the collective ascension axiom, the causal self-discipline axiom, the law-first axiom, and the universal equal infinite law. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Note the last one — the law-first axiom ranks at the highest execution position of the axiom system. Tang Luo's problem is not that he is not beneficent, but that his "beneficence" is not written into law. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core: universal benefit not written into law is only good will; good will is laudable, but institutions are absent. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Pseudo-universal-benefit's energy-level waste is "maintainability" waste:

- **No-backup good will**: each of Tang Luo's good policies has no backup, no successor, no operations manual. Runs well when he is alive; once he retires, all good policies become "legacy favors" — legacy favors are naturally weathered by time
- **No quantified standards**: how much universal benefit is enough? Who benefits, how much, how to monitor? No data at all. Tang Luo almsgives by feel, civilization benefits by luck
- **No correction mechanism**: when universal-benefit policy deviates, there is no Alaya Network feedback, no Symbiotic Court adjudication, no supervision committee questioning. If wrong, can only wait for Tang Luo himself to discover it

Energy-level waste's essence: good policies built at civilization-reconstruction-tier cost have, due to lack of institutionalization, **extremely short half-life**. Good policies are not overthrown, they are forgotten.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Legacy-favor decay law**: every generation, the understanding of "how Tang Luo thought back then" decays one layer. After three generations, good policies only have names left, the spirit is dead
2. **Inertial restoration**: old aristocratic families' remnants, new powerholders' ambitions, all wait for the day Tang Luo is gone. They need not positively overthrow universal benefit, only slowly hollow it out — change school rules, change quotas, change curricula
3. **Legitimacy transfer**: when Tang Luo is in office, legitimacy comes from him personally; once he dies, a legitimacy vacuum appears. Who fills it? There have always been only two answers: a new strongman, or restoration of old institutions. Tang Luo left no third answer — a constitution

Regression form: within one or two generations after Tang Luo's death, civilization slowly slides back onto the pre-reform track. Not dramatic collapse, but slow-boiled frog restoration.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Not legislating is predation**: Tang Luo gave all beings benefits, but did not write these benefits into "all beings' inalienable rights." Benefits not written into law can be withdrawn at any time — this is equivalent to disguisedly telling all beings: these were never yours in the first place
- **Good-will substituting institutions is predation**: replacing "institutions guarantee you well" with "I am good to you" deprives all beings of the ability to hold their own fate in hand
- **Rule of man substituting rule of law is predation**: civilization's operation rests on one person's virtue, and virtue is not transmissible. Taking the non-transmissible as the foundation of state is itself irresponsible to civilization

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo is the **enlightened predator** — "enlightened" reaches its maximum in this entry, and "predation" appears in its most hidden form. He is not a bad person, he is a good person who has not yet learned to write a constitution. The "righteous rogue" he lacks now concretizes as: does he dare rewrite his own good from "I am willing" to "you must have."

Alternative's crux: Tang Luo did everything right, except he missed the step of "writing it down." Heng Yuan Zhou legislates this step — good policies not in the constitution equal not done.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-high**. "Sage lord in office, all under heaven well-governed" fits traditional narrative logic
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Typical ending path: the author late finds "as soon as Tang Luo dies it restores" is hard to write, so forcibly makes Tang Luo immortal, reincarnates, forever present — this uses setting cheats to solve institutional absence, extremely retcon-tasting
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: high (bestowal-style). Pseudo-universal-benefit and bestowal-style are two sides of the same coin; this entry is their theoretical summary

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: good policies have extremely short half-life due to no institutions; civilization investment is depreciated
- **Anti-predation**: not writing good policies into rights is equivalent to allowing withdrawal at any time
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's good is real, but real good also needs a constitution to guard
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: institutions cover 99%; good will can only cover those seen
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cross-plane order can only rely on laws, not on sages
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Tang Luo's universal benefit is called "pseudo" not because he did too little, but because what he did did not become "law." Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment: good will is worthy of respect, but good will cannot substitute for legislation. If a civilization pins hope on the next Tang Luo, it will always be waiting for a savior — and saviors never come the second time.
