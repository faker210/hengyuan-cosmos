# Entry 258 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | In-Depth Analysis of Protagonist Uniqueness: When Tang Luo Is Absent, Civilization Regresses

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 8)

## Introduction: The risk of betting civilization on one person

Among the six top-level axioms, the law-first axiom's subtext is: law first, the individual is replaceable. The Tang-Luo system's reverse operation is — individual first, laws are the individual's footnotes. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core, deeply dissecting "protagonist uniqueness," the Tang-Luo system's fatal weakness. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**; this entry gives the complete judgment argument. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Protagonist uniqueness itself is the greatest energy-level waste:

- **Backup missing**: civilization's every key post — adjudicator, method source, ceiling gatekeeper, academy principal, resource distributor — all held by Tang Luo alone or the tiny handful he personally appointed. These posts could have been shared by a set of institutions
- **Zero substitutability**: Tang Luo's ability, vision, charisma are unreplicable. Civilization actually has no "spare tire," only "waiting for the next Tang Luo"
- **Growth suppression**: because Tang Luo is too strong, too complete, too correct, those around him lose the opportunity to grow into "the second adjudicator." Under a giant tree not a blade of grass grows

Energy-level waste's essence: one person's light shines so brightly that it eclipses ten thousand stars that could have glowed. **This is the greatest waste of civilization's talent reserve.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Protagonist uniqueness's historical regression is in three acts:

1. **Tang Luo in office**: civilization prospers, all problems are suppressed by him alone. But beneath prosperity, the institutional vacuum quietly expands
2. **Tang Luo declining**: he ages, sickens, goes into seclusion. No one can replace him; various posts start idling, old forces stir
3. **Tang Luo absent**: single-point collapse. All institutions attached to him fail simultaneously — the academy is leaderless, method transmission breaks, resource distribution goes chaotic, new powerholders under the ceiling openly seize power

Three acts together are a parabola: the apex is Tang Luo; the curve rises and falls with his life cycle. Civilization's fate is strictly synchronized with a mortal's lifespan — this is the mathematical destiny of all single-point-dependency civilizations.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Exclusive decision-making is predation**: Tang Luo decides everything for everyone, equivalent to depriving all beings of the right to "make their own mistakes, grow on their own, bear the consequences." On the surface merciful, actually letting all beings never grow up
- **Irreplaceability is predation**: forging oneself into irreplaceable is an exquisite power-maintenance technique — make all beings unable to leave you, and you are forever safe. Tang Luo may not have intended it, but the structure's product is this
- **Deification is predation**: when Tang Luo is deified by all beings, every word he speaks becomes "sacred edict." Rational discussion space is closed by divinity, civilization loses its self-correction ability

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo is the **enlightened predator**, and his predation form is precisely "irreplaceability itself." He lacks the "righteous rogue," and even more lacks actively making himself "detachable" — consciously cultivating a successor group, dispersing adjudication authority, fading personal aura.

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Tang-Luo Single Point | Anchor Symbiosis Network Style |
|-----------------------|--------------------------------|
| One person adjudicates | Anchor Council collectively legislates, judiciary and administration separated |
| One person transmits | Alaya Network saves all knowledge, any individual's absence does not affect transmission |
| One person guards the gate | Law-anchored authority benefits all beings, rules by law not by person |
| One person's light | 20% Activation Strategy, ten thousand light points light up simultaneously |
| Irreplaceable | Anchor structure multi-layer redundant, any layer damaged the network self-heals |

Alternative's crux: Heng Yuan Zhou does not want "a better Tang Luo," but a network that "runs even without Tang Luo." Network-style civilization's protagonist uniqueness dependency is **extremely low** — any individual exits, civilization runs as usual.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-high**. "Chosen one" is self-consistent within its world-view
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium-high**. Long-form web novels handling "protagonist uniqueness" have almost only three roads: let the protagonist never exit (immortality cheat), let the protagonist reincarnate (restart retcon), let the protagonist's disciples take over (quality crashes). All three roads are retcon
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: **high (bestowal-style)**. This entry's final judgment: Tang Luo in the five-tier standard (extreme/high/medium/extremely low) clearly falls in the "high" tier, between Gu Chensha (extreme/solitary-tree) and Ye Fan (medium/oligarchic)

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: single-point light eclipses ten thousand stars, talent reserve wasted
- **Anti-predation**: exclusive decision-making, irreplaceability, deification — triple hidden predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's greatness is precisely his system's soft spot
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: network-style order covers 99%; single-point order covers only one person's attention
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cross-plane governance cannot possibly depend on a single point, must be distributed
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Tang Luo absent, civilization regresses — this is not doubt of Tang Luo's ability, but the mathematical verdict on single-point structure. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: rather than looking for the next Tang Luo, build a civilization that no longer needs Tang Luo.
