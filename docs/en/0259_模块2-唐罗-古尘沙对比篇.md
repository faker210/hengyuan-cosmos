# Entry 259 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | Comparison With Gu Chensha: Differences Between Solitary-Tree and Bestowal Styles

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 9)

## Introduction: Two people who "bear things on behalf of all beings"

Among the six top-level axioms, the collective ascension axiom opposes "strong ones ascending alone." Gu Chensha (Dragon Seal) and Tang Luo, on the surface both top strong ones who "bear things on behalf of all beings," but Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment places them in two tiers: Gu Chensha = extreme dependency / solitary-tree type, Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal style. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core, dissecting the structural roots of these two tiers' difference. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

First compare Gu Chensha system's energy-level waste:

- **Gu Chensha's waste**: the first in history to cultivate Heavenly Dao Fa, his Sacred Dao is sky-mending and sky-reforming Dao, willing to bear all sentient beings' sins and karma on their behalf; late casually creates cosmoses. His energy is cosmos-creation-tier, but he spends it on "atoning for all beings" — karma could have been cultivated by all beings themselves; once he takes it all, all beings permanently lose the chance to cultivate it themselves
- **Tang Luo's waste**: energy is civilization-reconstruction-tier, spent on "universal benefit within humanity," the radius one notch smaller than Gu Chensha's, but still super-large talent super-small use

The two's waste forms differ: Gu Chensha is **creation-tier energy used to atone for others**, Tang Luo is **reconstruction-tier energy used for intra-racial allocation**. The former wastes in radius's infinite expansion (one person carries the whole cosmos), the latter wastes in radius's over-contraction (carrying only one race).

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

- **Gu Chensha system's regression**: all beings outsource karma to "history's first," their own causal self-discipline ability completely atrophies. Gu Chensha is here, the cosmos is at peace; once Gu Chensha's Dao dissipates, billions of beings are instantly drowned by the karma they themselves accumulated — this is solitary-tree civilization's typical collapse
- **Tang Luo system's regression**: bestowal-style dependency, Tang Luo retires, institutional vacuum, old forces restore. Regression speed is slower than Gu Chensha's, because Tang Luo at least left the skeleton of academies, methods, organizations

Regression rhythm difference: solitary-tree is **sudden collapse** (one log breaks, the whole house falls), bestowal is **slow regression** (the skeleton remains, but the soul is gone). Tang Luo's system has one more layer of organizational redundancy than Gu Chensha's, so it regresses slower — but is still regressing.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Gu Chensha's predation**: he "is willing to bear everything on behalf of all beings," on the surface most selfless, actually deprives all beings of the fundamental right to "bear karma themselves, grow on their own." The greater he is, the more pygmies all beings become. This is "extreme dependency" tier's predation form — repaying all with virtue, all beings forever owe him, forever cannot be his equal
- **Tang Luo's predation**: he gives all beings favors, making all beings attach. The predation form is "attachment," not "compensation." Attachers can at least cultivate on their own, only cultivation direction is set by the benefactor

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table:

| Dimension | Gu Chensha | Tang Luo |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| Uniqueness dependency | Extreme | High |
| Civilization form | Solitary-tree | Bestowal style |
| Predation form | Compensation (living for you) | Attachment (living on you) |
| All beings' subject | Those made into dragons | Recipients |
| Collapse speed | Sudden | Slow regression |

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Solitary-Tree / Bestowal | Anchor Symbiosis Network Style |
|---------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Gu Chensha bears sins for all beings | All beings' causal self-discipline, causal self-discipline axiom written into constitution, cross-plane operations observe first then limitedly interfere |
| Tang Luo bestows on all beings | Myriad Races republican co-building, evolution qualification naturalized |
| One person bears / one person gives | Alaya whole-network sharing, multi-layer supervision, distributed sharing |
| Made into dragons / almsgiven | Co-becoming dragons / co-legislating, everyone is a contracting party |

Alternative's crux: whether "bearing for you" or "giving you candy," neither is as good as "building with you."

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Gu Chensha system**: setting self-consistency medium-high, combat ceiling extremely high but world-view still self-consistent; retcon-ending risk medium — late power inflates to "casually creating cosmoses," plot has no more room to level up, can only prop up with "stronger cosmoses"
- **Tang Luo system**: setting self-consistency medium-high; retcon-ending risk medium
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency comparison**: Gu Chensha = extreme, Tang Luo = high. The essence of the two-tier gap: Gu Chensha did not even leave an organizational skeleton, Tang Luo at least left the academy and institutional prototype — although the prototype decays, it is better than nothing

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: creation-tier / reconstruction-tier energy both compressed into one person's undertaking
- **Anti-predation**: compensation and attachment are predation's two ultimate forms
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo took half a step further than Gu Chensha — left the organization, but not the institutions
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: solitary-tree / bestowal both cannot cover 99%; networks can
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cross-plane governance must be distributed; solitary-tree / bestowal both cannot step across planes
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Gu Chensha and Tang Luo, one is "I live for you," the other is "I give you life." Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: what all beings want is neither of these, but "we live for ourselves." Between solitary-tree and bestowal, and networks, the last step is merely: step oneself down from civilization's center.
