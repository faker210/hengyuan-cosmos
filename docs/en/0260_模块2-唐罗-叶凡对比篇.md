# Entry 260 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | Comparison With Ye Fan: Differences Between Oligarchic and Bestowal Styles

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 10)

## Introduction: Give all beings a road, or give the Ye family ten thousand years of peace

Among the six top-level axioms, the law-first axiom requires order to be written into laws, not into a family. Ye Fan (Cover the Sky) and Tang Luo, one is the oligarchic specimen, the other the bestowal specimen. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core, comparing the two tiers' structural differences. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: Tang Luo = high (bestowal style), Ye Fan = medium (oligarchic style). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- **Ye Fan system's waste**: Cover the Sky's "ten-thousand-year peace" bears the surname Ye — order after Great Emperor and Mortal-World Immortal is essentially co-rule by Ye Fan and his close circle of friends. Energy is concentrated in a small circle; all beings outside the circle are merely "protected," with no participation right
- **Tang Luo system's waste**: energy is concentrated in Tang Luo alone, the circle smaller than Ye Fan's, but at least the entry is open to all humanity

Comparison points: Ye Fan's waste is **circularization** — a few strong ones co-rule, civilization's dividends distributed by circle; Tang Luo's waste is **single-pointization** — one person decides, the circle is small but the door is open. By "open radius," Tang Luo is actually wider than Ye Fan; by "institutional stability," Ye Fan's oligarchic group is slightly more stable than Tang Luo's single point — more people, slightly higher fault tolerance.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

- **Ye Fan system's regression**: oligarchic co-rule depends on "brothers of one mind." When the first generation of Great Emperors is alive, ten-thousand-year peace; when the first generation ages, does the second generation still have this vision? History repeatedly proves oligarchic groups' second and third generations inevitably infight. Cover the Sky's late realm collapse is precisely oligarchic structure unable to hold up the plot
- **Tang Luo system's regression**: single-point dependency, Tang Luo retires and it slowly regresses. But the academies and method organizations he left are more operable than the "brotherly loyalty" Ye Fan left — loyalty is not heritable, schools can be transmitted

Regression rhythm: Ye Fan group is **group corruption** (each generation worse), Tang Luo group is **single-point decay** (slowly cools after one person leaves). Both regressions are regression, only the form differs.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Ye Fan's predation**: the subject of "ten-thousand-year peace" is the Ye family; all beings are only beneficiaries of peace, not peace's legislators. The predation form is **patriciate-style** — civilizational leadership solidified in one strong family / group's hand
- **Tang Luo's predation**: bestowal style, the subject is Tang Luo personally, all beings are recipients. The predation form is softer, but the essence is the same

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table:

| Dimension | Ye Fan | Tang Luo |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| Uniqueness dependency | Medium | High |
| Civilization form | Oligarchic | Bestowal style |
| Predation form | Patriciate (family private occupation) | Attachment (personal bestowal) |
| Order carrier | Strong-one group | One person + embryonic organization |
| Corruption path | Second-generation infighting | Legacy-favor decay |

Interesting contrast: Ye Fan's dependency rating (medium) is slightly lower than Tang Luo's (high), because oligarchic groups have higher fault tolerance than single points; but Ye Fan's predation depth is actually greater — he wrote order directly into the family name, one step further than Tang Luo's "temporary trusteeship."

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Oligarchic / Bestowal | Anchor Symbiosis Network Style |
|-----------------------|--------------------------------|
| Ye Fan group co-rule | Anchor Council open legislation, Myriad Races representatives participate, no patriciates |
| Tang Luo one person adjudicates | Multi-layer separation of powers: legislative/executive/judicial/supervisory four powers separated |
| Peace bears the surname Ye | Myriad Races republican co-building, civilizational dividends distributed by contribution, not by surname |
| Brotherhood-style order | Law-anchored authority benefits all beings, order by law not by relation |

Alternative's crux: Ye Fan's problem is "circle too small," Tang Luo's problem is "center too bright." Heng Yuan Zhou solves both problems together — neither circled nor bright, but distributed light.

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

1. **Modeling**: replicate Ye Fan oligarchic group and Tang Luo single point two models
2. **Variables**: simulate the first generation of strong ones all exiting, observe second and third generations
3. **Observation**: Ye Fan group second-generation infighting probability, Tang Luo group legacy-favor decay rate
4. **Review conclusion**: Ye Fan group inevitably civil war in the third generation, Tang Luo group institutional vacuum at the end of the second generation — both need network-style alternatives to continue

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Ye Fan system**: setting self-consistency medium (early realm division fine, late Great Emperor/Mortal-World Immortal stage collapses; Wheel Sea, Dao Palace, Four Extremes, Dragon Transformation lose binding force); retcon-ending risk **high** — this is Cover the Sky's most famous wound: for hot-blooded scenes, early iron rules are broken again and again
- **Tang Luo system**: setting self-consistency medium-high; retcon-ending risk medium
- **Comparison conclusion**: by retcon-ending, Ye Fan is one tier more severe than Tang Luo. Tang Luo's risk is "single-point dependency," Ye Fan's risk is "realm watering" — two illnesses, same source (law does not come first)

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: oligarchic circles and single-point centers are both civilizational energy mismatches
- **Anti-predation**: patriciate-style predation is more institutionalized than attachment-style predation, and more tenacious
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo has more openness toward all beings than Ye Fan, but lacks the oligarchic group's fault tolerance
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: circles and single points both cannot cover 99%
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cross-plane governance must be anti-patriciate and anti-single-point
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Ye Fan gives all beings ten-thousand-year peace; Tang Luo gives all beings a cultivation road. Both are one step further than Gu Chensha's "bearing on behalf of all beings," but neither reaches "letting all beings legislate for themselves." Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment: oligarchic is slightly more stable than single point, bestowal is slightly softer than patriciate — but as long as law does not come first, stability and softness are only procrastination.
