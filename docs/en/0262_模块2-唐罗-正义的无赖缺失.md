# Entry 262 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | Missing Righteous Rogue: Why Tang Luo Dares Not Overthrow Old Jurisprudence

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 12)

## Introduction: Breaking the old world requires a bit of "rogue"

Among the six top-level axioms, the anti-predation axiom's thoroughness requires: if old jurisprudence is built on predation, it must be overthrown, not patched. Tang Luo broke aristocratic monopoly, but he did not overthrow old jurisprudence — he reformed within the old jurisprudence's framework. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core, dissecting "righteous rogue," the quality Tang Luo lacks. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- **Reformist consumption**: Tang Luo spent civilization-reconstruction-tier energy on "patching within the old jurisprudence framework." Break one aristocratic family, three more pop up; open one method, old jurisprudence still defends "knowledge belongs to the strong." Energy is diluted in endless reform
- **Legitimacy debt**: because he did not overthrow old jurisprudence, his reforms always carry the legitimacy debt of "you were originally a beneficiary of this system." Old jurisprudence can counterattack at any time, he must continuously spend energy defending
- **Revolution incomplete**: a truly thorough revolution burns even the characters of old jurisprudence. Tang Luo only burned the aristocrats in the book, leaving the book itself. The next batch of aristocrats will grow out of this book again

Energy-level waste: **using revolutionary strength to do reform's work.** Big talent small use, this entry gives the sharpest version.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Old jurisprudence still there**: as long as "knowledge can be exclusively occupied," "the strong can decide the weak's fate," "humanity is superior to Myriad Races" these jurisprudences are not overthrown, old forces can at any time rely on old jurisprudence to retake everything
2. **Reform reversible**: every reform Tang Luo makes is "interpretable" under old jurisprudence. Newly risen powerholders only need to change one interpretation, and reform is hollowed out
3. **Revolution fruits stolen**: because jurisprudence was not burned clean, old forces learned to change skins. They no longer call themselves "aristocratic families," renamed "academy high-level," "Tang Luo's personal disciples" — the jurisprudence is still that jurisprudence, only the name changed

Regression form: Tang Luo thought he changed civilization, actually only changed a few housekeepers for civilization; the house's blueprint is still the old one.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

"Righteous rogue" definition: a revolutionary quality that dares to tear apart all conventionally accepted old jurisprudence, undeterred by "doing great things does not stick to minor details." It is not hooliganism, but after seeing through "old jurisprudence itself is predation's product," daring not to play by old jurisprudence's rules.

Why Tang Luo dares not be the "righteous rogue":

1. **He is an old jurisprudence beneficiary**: he himself climbed up the old jurisprudence ladder. Letting him overthrow old jurisprudence is equivalent to negating his own origin
2. **He cherishes his reputation**: he wants to be "sage lord," not "rogue." Overthrowing old jurisprudence inevitably offends a large number of people, including his friends, comrades-in-arms, benefactors — he cannot bear it
3. **He believes in reformism**: he truly believes "within the old framework it can slowly get better." This belief stops him at reform

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo = **enlightened predator**, and a "reformist enlightened predator." The gap between him and "revolutionaries" (those who truly overthrow old jurisprudence) is exactly this one step of "righteous rogue." What he lacks is not strength, not wisdom, not good will, but a bit of necessary "rogue spirit" — daring to flip the table and rearrange.

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-high**. Reform narrative is self-consistent within its world-view
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. If long-form writing continues to level up, it eventually cannot avoid the problem of "old jurisprudence blocking the road." The author has two choices: let Tang Luo suddenly turn dark and revolutionize (character collapses), or let old jurisprudence collapse on its own (retcon). Both roads are hard
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: high. Without new jurisprudence, reforms all rely on Tang Luo's personal prestige to maintain

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

- **Anti-energy waste**: revolutionary strength doing reform's work, energy diluted
- **Anti-predation**: not overthrowing old jurisprudence is equivalent to acknowledging predation's legitimacy
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is a good reformist, but not a thorough revolutionary
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: as long as old jurisprudence is not voided, Myriad Races cannot have true equality
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cross-plane order must establish new law, cannot rely on old framework patching

Either don't change, or change to the root.
