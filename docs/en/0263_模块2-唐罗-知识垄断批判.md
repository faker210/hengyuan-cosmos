# Entry 263 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | Critique of Knowledge Monopoly: Not Opening the High-Tier Great Dao Is Predation

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 13)

## Introduction: The highest wall is the wall of knowledge

Among the six top-level axioms, the anti-predation axiom expressly states: "Great Dao public at all tiers, no private hoarding, no monopoly." Tang Luo opened mid-low tier methods, but privately hoarded the high-tier Great Dao. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core, specifically arguing: not opening the high-tier Great Dao is predation at the knowledge level, no different from robbing money. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Knowledge monopoly's energy-level waste is "stock-type":

- **Stock frozen**: the high-tier Great Dao in Tang Luo's brain is civilization's crystallization ground out by billions of beings over generations. These crystallizations could have been infinitely copied and distributed, but are frozen in one person's skull
- **Iteration stagnant**: knowledge only evolves through exchange. Tang Luo deducing alone means his high-tier Great Dao can only evolve along his one line of thought. Billions of possible directions are limited by his one brain's capacity
- **Reinventing the wheel**: later geniuses, to break through fierce realm, must grope from scratch for answers Tang Luo already knew. Civilization walks the same road thousands of times, every generation repeating the dead end "Tang Luo already knew"

Energy-level waste: **civilization's most precious knowledge asset is depreciated at "one-person portion."** Knowledge's marginal cost is zero, but monopoly turns it into the most expensive scarce commodity.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Knowledge generations broken**: Tang Luo dies, high-tier knowledge goes to zero. Even if later generations produce the most genius person, they must rediscover what Tang Luo knew hundreds of years ago — civilization's top-tier knowledge curve permanently drops
2. **Pseudo-knowledge floods**: orthodox high-tier knowledge not public,民间 will derive large numbers of pseudo-methods, evil cultivators, crooked paths. True knowledge because scarce, fake knowledge because flooded — this is monopoly's inevitable companion
3. **Apprenticeship = attachment**: to learn real things, one can only take Tang Luo or his personal disciples as masters. Master-disciple relations become personal-attachment relations. Knowledge transmission is kidnapped into personal control

Regression form: civilization's total knowledge starts net loss after Tang Luo's death. It looks like it's still advancing, actually it's being buried with Tang Luo.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Private hoarding is predation**: high-tier Great Dao is cultivation civilization's most core means of production. Privately hoarding it is equivalent to locking a nation's top laboratory in one's own warehouse. This is the same as aristocratic families hiding methods in family genealogies, only the person locking the door differs
- **Slow release is predation**: Tang Luo does not refuse to give, but "gives by mood, by closeness, by timing." This selective, rhythmic opening is essentially treating knowledge as chips to maintain his own authority
- **Fragmented chapters are predation**: the methods he opens are always "incomplete versions" — true secrets transmitted only after apprenticeship. Incomplete methods themselves are a design: making you unable to leave him

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo = **enlightened predator**, his predation form is precisely "knowledge private hoarding." He may truly believe " unsealing high-tier Great Dao too early would harm young people" — but this judgment itself is the monopolist's most classic self-defense. The "righteous rogue" he lacks now concretizes as: dares he hand over his most precious Dao, indiscriminately, irreversibly, including both "true secrets" and "fragmentary chapters," all at once.

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Tang-Luo Knowledge Monopoly | Anchor Symbiosis Full Knowledge Opening |
|----------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| High-tier Great Dao privately hoarded | 300 Origin systems fully public, Origin Three-Basic (deduction iteration / life circulation / space law jurisdiction scheduling) all tiers no private Dao |
| Fragmentary chapters to fish disciples | Complete methods + all commentaries + all deduction process, uploaded all at once |

Alternative's crux: knowledge is not Tang Luo's property, it is civilization's heritage. The only lawful way to inherit heritage is returning all of it to Myriad Races.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-high**. "Strong ones keeping hole cards" fits web-novel convention
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium-high**. Long-form writing's most common retcon for "knowledge hoarding": to give new characters something to do, temporarily invent "some secret technique Tang Luo did not transmit"; or to let the protagonist continue leveling, temporarily let Tang Luo "remember" he still has new ultimate moves — knowledge stock stretches and shrinks with plot needs, no principle at all
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: high. High-tier knowledge has no second backup

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: civilization's crystallization depreciated at one-person portion, reinventing-the-wheel cost enormous
- **Anti-predation**: privately hoarding high-tier Great Dao = knowledge predation, isomorphic with robbing money
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo opened mid-low tiers, but locked the most precious
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: knowledge monopoly first harms the most vulnerable 99%
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cross-plane knowledge sharing is civilization ascension's precondition
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

What Tang Luo most should open is not his resources, not his kindness, but his Dao. Resources can be divided slowly, kindness can be repaid slowly, but the Dao — if the Dao is not open, civilization forever grows on his one brain. Heng Yuan Zhou's iron law: Great Dao has no private hoarding. This is not moral preaching, it is survival law.
