# Entry 264 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | Critique of Myriad-Races Exclusion: Benefiting Humanity Alone Is Civilizational Resource Monopoly

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 14)

## Introduction: One race's benefit, Myriad Races' sorrow

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings to have equal evolution qualification, with 99% full coverage. Tang Luo's universal-benefit radius stops at humanity's boundary, equivalent to declaring over 90% of the world's sentient beings are not within "all beings." This entry takes the law-first axiom as core, arguing that "benefiting humanity alone" is essentially civilizational resource monopoly. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Myriad-Races exclusion causes "ecological" energy-level waste:

- **Other races' talents idled**: among strange beasts there are many with extraordinary talent; plant spirits, mountain-river wills, planet consciousness each have their unique perception and cultivation paths. These talents are obliterated by the "non-human" label, unable to convert into civilization energy
- **Ecological conflict consumption**: under the human-demon opposition norm, humanity spends massive resources on defense, expedition, and extermination. This consumption could have been used for joint frontier expansion — excluding Myriad Races is equivalent to spending a large part of civilization's budget on one's own neighbors
- **Knowledge monocropping**: human methods only iterate within humanity's physiological upper limit, losing the opportunity to collide with foreign-race methods. Civilization's knowledge ecosystem is artificially monocropped; monocropped ecosystems have extremely poor risk resistance

Energy-level waste: **expelling 90% of sentient beings from "all beings" is equivalent to cutting civilization's talent pool by 90%.** This is the deepest energy-level waste — not wasting power, but wasting "possibility."

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Enemy externalization**: excluded Myriad Races cannot participate in human civilization, can only exist in hostile mode. Civilization forever has external enemies, forever war-preparing, forever unable to fully develop
2. **Internal conservatism**: humanity forms a superiority complex from "exclusive enjoyment," any voice advocating opening Myriad Races is seen as betrayal. Civilization's thought boundaries close increasingly
3. **Sample exhaustion**: without foreign-race reference, humanity does not know where its own methods have problems. A closed-door cultivation system will inevitably lag behind open-type civilizations after a hundred years

Regression form: the more prosperous humanity is internally, the tenser the whole world's ecology. Ultimately either defeated externally, or trapped to death by internal conservatism.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Racial exclusive occupation is predation**: defaulting the world's cultivation resources, knowledge, territory as humanity's property is equivalent to declaring Myriad Races "resources" or "enemies." This is civilizational-level resource monopoly
- **Othering is predation**: non-human sentient life is deprived of qualification to enter moral circles. Tang Luo is extremely good to humanity, but this good does not extend beyond the racial boundary — the radius of good is the radius of predation
- **Ecological predation**: under human-demon opposition, humanity exterminates demon beasts, destroys spirit veins, pollutes ecology for cultivation resources. This predation is not Tang Luo personally doing, but systematic behavior under the "humanity first" jurisprudence

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo = **enlightened predator**, his predation radius abruptly stops at the racial boundary. He is not maliciously xenophobic, but never included Myriad Races in the definition of "all beings." What he lacks is not good will toward Myriad Races, but the legislative courage to expand the definition of "all beings" from one race to Myriad Races.
