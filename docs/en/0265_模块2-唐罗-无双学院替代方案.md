# Entry 265 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | Wushuang Academy Alternative: Cross-Plane Mentor Network

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 15)

## Introduction: From one academy, to one network

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings to have equal evolution qualification; the collective ascension axiom requires strong ones to feed back to Myriad Races. Tang Luo's Wushuang Academy is the old "concentrating elites" model; Heng Yuan Zhou's alternative is the new "distributed network" model. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core, concretely giving Wushuang Academy's institutional alternative. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Wushuang Academy's energy-level waste (already described in Entry 255) is re-quantified from the alternative perspective:

- **Campus cost**: centralized schooling, students travel a thousand li to attend, travel time, quota screening, failure attrition — civilization annually pays hidden costs for "centralization" far exceeding the academy itself
- **Teacher idling**: famous teachers' time is limited; centralized teaching means a famous teacher teaches only a hundred at a time. If switched to recorded broadcast + Q&A, the same teacher can teach a million — teacher utilization rate differs by three orders of magnitude
- **Geographic privilege**: the academy's location becomes the cultivation center, surrounding land prices soar, population clusters, other regions decline. Geographic monopoly itself is new unfairness

Energy-level waste: **under the centralized model, education resource utilization rate is under one-tenth of the distributed model.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Centralized academy model's regression path:

1. **Geographic solidification**: development gap between the academy's location and other regions widens generation by generation; civilization's talent geographic map is locked
2. **Famous-teacher dependency**: course quality rests on famous teachers; famous teachers retire or die, course quality immediately drops. No-backup teaching system
3. **Knowledge walls**: inside the academy walls is orthodoxy, outside the walls is crooked paths. The higher the walls are built, the more barren off-campus knowledge ecology

Regression form: the academy runs better and better, but civilization's education map gets more and more deformed.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

The predatory thinking behind Wushuang Academy:

- **Entry approval power**: who can enroll is decided by the academy. Turning "can one cultivate" into "can one be selected" downgrades a natural right to an approval qualification
- **Orthodoxy discourse power**: the academy declares itself "sole orthodoxy," other learning paths automatically demoted to "crooked paths." This is knowledge discourse monopoly
- **Famous-teacher personal attachment**: to follow famous teachers, students must migrate, must apprentice, must obey master-disciple ethics. Personal relations are kidnapped by knowledge relations

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo = **enlightened predator**, the academy is the concrete carrier of "knowledge entry" in his predatory structure.

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative: Cross-Plane Mentor Network

Alternative's complete design:

| Module | Design |
|--------|--------|
| Course library | All courses recorded and uploaded to Alaya Network, learnable, rewatchable, speed-adjustable anytime |
| Mentor matching | By students' talent and progress, automatically match cross-plane mentors, no racial or geographic restriction |
| Practice nodes | Practice dojos set locally, cultivate nearby, no need to travel a thousand li |
| Q&A mechanism | Difficult questions uploaded to network, billions of mentors and students collaboratively answer |
| Certification system | Cultivation progress certified by Alaya Network, not depending on any academy's diploma |
| Myriad-Races coverage | Courses adapted to the five physiological genealogies, strange beasts, plant spirits, planet consciousness each have dedicated paths |

Alternative's key: turn "centralized teaching" into "distributed learning," turn "famous-teacher aura" into "network consensus." Education is everywhere, orthodoxy is everywhere — so there is no such thing as "walls."

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-high**. The alternative is self-consistent with Alaya Network, cross-plane communication technology and other foundational settings
- **Retcon-ending risk: low**. Distributed network model is naturally anti-retcon — it does not need any single-point setting to support
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: after the alternative, dependency drops from "high" to "extremely low"

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: raise education resource utilization from one-tenth to ten-tenths
- **Anti-predation**: abolish entry approval power, cultivation qualification naturalized
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: from one academy to one network is a quantitative-to-qualitative leap
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: cross-racial, cross-plane, covering 99%
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: distributed networks naturally support multiverse-tier expansion
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Wushuang Academy is a monument to Tang Luo's good will; the cross-plane mentor network is Heng Yuan Zhou's institutional answer. The crux of the comparison: Tang Luo asks "how to send more people into the academy," Heng Yuan Zhou asks "why is an academy needed." When knowledge is everywhere, the academy has completed its historical mission — take a bow.
