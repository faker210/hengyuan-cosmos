## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The hundred-year seclusion simulation's essence is removing "Tang Luo in-office-period energy" from civilization, watching the naked-running civilization. Waste's form becomes visible here:

- **No one backfills**: Tang Luo leaves, adjudication, transmission, distribution, supervision all idling. These posts should have been shared by institutions, now can only be held by him alone — the price of combined holding is, once he leaves, all posts simultaneously go unemployed
- **Transmission vacuum**: high-tier Great Dao has no one to explain it, new disciples receiving fragmentary chapters have no one to Q&A. Knowledge stock starts naturally decaying
- **Credit contraction**: Tang Luo is gone, all "graces," "privileges," "appointments" he issued start depreciating. Social credit system goes into seclusion chamber with him

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression: Hundred-Year Timeline

By Quest Sediment Domain simulation, Tang Luo's hundred-year seclusion civilization trajectory has four segments:

**Years 0–10: Inertia period**
- Old institutions still running, academy as usual, resources as usual
- Surface prosperity, but every key decision is delayed by "waiting for Tang Luo to leave seclusion"
- Old aristocratic family remnants begin tentative activity

**Years 10–30: Crack period**
- Academy has personnel strife, who will succeed as principal?
- High-tier methods have no one to explain, demonic-deviation rate rises
- Border foreign races start probing, border generals dare not decide, retreating step by step
- The first batch of ballads "missing Tang Luo" appear — missing itself is a crisis signal

**Years 30–70: Restoration period**
- Old aristocratic families change skins and return, new powerholders self-claim "Tang Luo's old subordinates" and re-monopolize
- Academy changes from "universal benefit" back to "aristocratic prep school"
- Mid-low tier methods nominally still there, actually new powerholders set various hidden thresholds
- Human-demon border war breaks out, because no one leads reconciliation

**Years 70–100: Regression complete**
- Civilization on the surface still "misses Tang Luo," actually has returned to his pre-reform state
- If Tang Luo leaves seclusion at this point, he will find his hundred years of effort have left only a few place names

Conclusion: **hundred-year seclusion = hundred-year civilizational regression.** Not dramatic collapse, but slow-boiled frog restoration.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Predatory thinking exposed by the hundred-year simulation:

- **Single-point dependency is predation**: betting civilization's fate on one person deprives civilization of the ability to bear things itself. The hundred-year simulation proves this deprivation's price is an entire era

- **Good will undefended**: he never prepared for "when I am not here." This is not negligence, but the bestowal system's intrinsic feature — bestowers do not intend for all beings to stand on their own

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo = **enlightened predator**, the hundred-year simulation gives his "predation" temporal dimension: every day he is not there, predation automatically executes.
