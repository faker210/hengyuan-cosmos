# Entry 272 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | Female Cultivators' Situation: The Gender Blind Spot in Universal Benefit

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 22)

## Introduction: Universal benefit covered humanity, then what

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races expressly states: human men and women, all beings, equal evolution qualification. Tang Luo's universal benefit wrote "humanity," but left a blind spot at the "human men and women" tier. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core, dissecting female cultivators' situation in the Tang-Luo system. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Gender blind spot's energy-level waste:

- **Half the population idled**: human women are half the population; their talent, comprehension, creativity are partially idled by traditional gender roles in the Tang-Luo system
- **Mentor structure single**: Wushuang Academy's high-tier mentors, core courses are male-dominated. Female disciples even if enrolled still lack same-sex role models and gender-adapted cultivation paths
- **Prejudice hidden**: on the surface "both men and women may enroll," actually high-tier resources, inner-circle positions are still male-led. This hidden prejudice is harder to break than explicit bans

Energy-level waste: **half of humanity's cultivation talent is discounted.** Tang Luo's universal benefit looks like it covers humanity, but effective coverage is only half of humanity.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Talent attrition**: due to gender prejudice, the most talented batch of women is left in family, marriage, auxiliary roles. Civilization's talent pool is artificially cut in half
2. **Prejudice solidified**: when Tang Luo is in office, society defaults to "strong ones are mostly men." This default is sanctified by his prestige. Once he retires, prejudice rebounds, female cultivators' situation is actually harder than pre-reform
3. **Knowledge lopsided**: because women are long marginalized, the cultivation knowledge system lacks female-perspective iteration. Methods are optimized by male physiological and psychological traits; female cultivators get half the result with twice the effort

Regression form: civilization looks gender-equal, actually quietly losing half its geniuses.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Blind spot is predation**: Tang Luo is not deliberately oppressing women, but he never treated "female cultivators" as a group needing specialized design. This "default neglect" itself is structural predation
- **Bestowal incomplete**: he bestowed "enrollment qualification," but did not bestow "gender-adapted curricula," "female mentor network," "childbearing-period cultivation guarantee." Qualification is nominal, actual thresholds still there
- **Good will's blind spot**: Tang Luo's good will radius drew to "humanity," did not draw to "women among human men and women." The blind spot is not ill will, but good will itself's shape is not round

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo = **enlightened predator**, the gender blind spot is one of his "enlightenment" boundaries — his good toward humanity still carries the gender default of his era.
