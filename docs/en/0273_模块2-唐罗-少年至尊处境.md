# Entry 273 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | Youth Supremes' Situation: The Bestowal Attribute of the Genius Mechanism

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 23)

## Introduction: Genius is not a bestowal toy

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings to have equal evolution qualification; the collective ascension axiom requires no wasting big talent on small tasks. Youth Supremes in the Tang-Luo system — those child-prodigy geniuses — on the surface are darlings, actually are bestowal system specimens. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Youth Supremes mechanism's energy-level waste:

- **Sample bias**: Tang Luo's genius selection concentrates on youths he can see and reach. Geniuses in remote areas, from bottom families, among foreign races are overlooked because they are not "seen"
- **Fast-track dependency**: selected Youth Supremes enjoy Tang Luo's personal coaching and top-tier resource tilt. They grow fast, but their growth path is locked by Tang Luo's eye — whichever direction Tang Luo favors, they go that way; multiple possibilities are compressed
- **Forced growth**: concentrating resources too early, Youth Supremes reach at twenty what others reach at fifty. But foundation, character, experience cannot keep up with realm, late stage prone to collapse

Energy-level waste: **geniuses are cultivated as "sage lord's works," not as "masters of their own fate."** This is the greatest waste of genius.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Genius generations broken**: Tang Luo's Youth Supreme generation was personally selected and taught by him. He retires, how does the next generation of genius get selected, who teaches them? Selection mechanism not institutionalized
2. **Looking only to the sage lord**: Youth Supremes are used to "being selected, being arranged," losing the courage to independently frontier. Tang Luo is here, they are sharp blades; Tang Luo is gone, they are scattered soldiers
3. **Lateral comparison missing**: because resources concentrate on a few Youth Supremes, ordinary disciples have no chance to spar with them as equals. Genius lacks opponents, growth is actually constrained

Regression form: Youth Supreme generation brilliant, second generation mediocre.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Selecting is possessing**: Tang Luo selecting a youth is equivalent to declaring this youth "belongs to me." Youth Supreme's identity, resources, path are all defined by Tang Luo. This is possession of genius subjectivity
- **Fast-track is attachment**: the faster Youth Supremes grow, the more dependent on Tang Luo. The higher their realm, the more they cannot leave Tang Luo's coaching. The flip side of fast-track is attachment
- **Specimenization**: Youth Supremes are displayed as "Tang Luo's good-policy results," used to prove universal benefit works. They are civilization's showrooms, not independent individuals

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo = **enlightened predator**, the Youth Supremes mechanism is his predation structure's concrete form of "genius possession."

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Tang-Luo Youth Supremes | Anchor Symbiosis Genius Mechanism |
|-------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Tang Luo personally selects | Alaya Network monitors whole-population talent, auto-identifies |
| Fast-track + attachment | "Fast-track + Quest" dual routes, Youth Supremes choose fast or steady |
| One-person coaching | Cross-plane mentor network, multiple mentors collaboratively |
| Path locked | 300 systems fully open, geniuses choose direction themselves |

Alternative's crux: turn "sage lord's work" into "network node." Geniuses do not need to be selected, they need to be seen — after being seen, they walk the road themselves.

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-high**. Youth Supremes are web-novel standard
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Long-form writing if it needs stronger villains, the cheapest move is temporarily letting some Youth Supreme "suddenly erupt" — talent numbers stretch with plot
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: high

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: geniuses cultivated as works, subjectivity deprived
- **Anti-predation**: selecting is possessing, fast-track is attachment
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo selects talents by eye; Heng Yuan Zhou selects talents by network
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: genius selection must cover 99%, not only reach youths who are seen
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: batch genius output can support cross-plane civilization
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Supplementary Discussion: De-Bestowal of the Genius Mechanism

Youth Supremes' situation in the Tang-Luo system is essentially the bestowal mechanism's product — geniuses are selected, cultivated, entrusted with missions, but choice is not in their own hands. Heng Yuan Zhou's alternative is Youth Supreme dual-track: fast-track and Quest-track run in parallel, choice lies with the cultivator. The Alaya Network provides path evaluation, does not make decisions for people. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's law-first axiom ensures genius growth is not plot-kidnapped, growth rhythm decided by law-cognition maturity. The universal equal infinite law ensures unselected ordinary geniuses also have complete paths.

## Conclusion

Youth Supremes are not Tang Luo's private property, they are civilization's kindling. Tang Luo holds the kindling in his palm, very warm; but the kindling should be scattered, letting the whole prairie burn. Heng Yuan Zhou's 20% Activation Strategy is the way to scatter kindling.
