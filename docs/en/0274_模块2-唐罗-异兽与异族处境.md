## Introduction: Universal benefit is named "all beings," but the list has only humanity

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires 99% full coverage of Myriad Races. On Tang Luo's universal-benefit list, strange beasts, foreign races, plant spirits, planet consciousness are all absent. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core, specifically making a situation report for "the forgotten 99%." Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The forgotten ones' energy-level waste:

- **Strange-beast talent idled**: demon beasts that can open sentience, rare insects that can breathe spiritual qi, their cultivation talent is not recognized in the Tang-Luo system. Human cultivators meeting them, first reaction is "hunt demon for elixir"
- **Foreign-race civilizations suppressed**: mountain foreign races, sea-floor clans, starry sky remnants originally had their own cultivation traditions. Tang Luo's universal benefit did not dialogue with them, but default-classified them as "to be civilized / to be suppressed"
- **Planet consciousness voiceless**: mountain-river wills, continental spirit consciousness are the world's own life. In the Tang-Luo system they are not even "characters," only background boards

Energy-level waste: **expelling 99% of sentient beings from "all beings" is equivalent to cutting civilization's talent pool, knowledge base, ecological foundation all by 90%.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Human-demon war normalized**: because strange beasts have no upward channel, they can only take preying on human resources as survival strategy. War is structurally inevitable
2. **Ecological destruction**: humanity for resources suppresses demon beasts, destroys spirit veins, long-term damages world ecology. After a hundred years spirit veins deplete, humanity itself declines with it
3. **Knowledge isolation**: human methods lose collision with foreign-race methods, stagnate after a hundred years

Regression form: the stronger humanity is, the more barren the world; the more barren the world, the more humanity cannot get stronger.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Hunting demons is predation**: treating strange beasts as "elixir sources" is treating sentient life as resources. This is the most straightforward predation
- **Othering is predation**: strange beasts, foreign races in the Tang-Luo system are not "we," but "they." After othering, preying on them is no longer a moral issue
- **Silence is predation**: Tang Luo never legislated for them, never spoke for them. Silence itself is acquiescence — acquiescing to humanity continuing to prey

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo = **enlightened predator**, his predation radius extends to the entire non-human world. His good toward humanity is real, but this good does not cross species.
