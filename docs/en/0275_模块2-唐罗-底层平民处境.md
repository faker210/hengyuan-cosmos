# Entry 275 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | Bottom Commoners' Situation: The Involution Fate After Bestowed Entry

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 25)

## Introduction: The door opens, behind it is an arena

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings to have equal evolution qualification. Tang Luo broke aristocratic monopolies; bottom commoners for the first time "may" cultivate. But "may" is not "have a road" — the door opens, behind it is a more crowded arena. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core, dissecting bottom commoners' situation. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Bottom commoners' energy-level waste:

- **Starting-line disadvantage**: commoners' children may enroll, but their families, neighborhoods, early education are far inferior to aristocratic remnants. Tang Luo opened the door, but did not smooth the gap between inside and outside
- **Competition amplified**: originally only aristocratic children competed in cultivation, now plus tens of millions of commoners' children. Ten times the competitors, unchanged slots — tenfold involution
- **Success illusion**: very few commoners' children succeeded, held up as "Tang Luo good policy" showpieces. The vast majority exhausted themselves in the arena, finally quietly exiting. Successful stories obscure failures' silence

Energy-level waste: **Tang Luo built a bigger arena, but did not add more exits.** This is the truth of "bestowed entry."

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Involution exhaustion**: tens of millions of commoners' children consume each other under the fierce-realm ceiling. Within ten years most become involution fuel
2. **Class-solidification reskinned**: the few successful commoners' children become new powerholders after decades. They merge with old aristocratic remnants, forming new monopolistic classes. Universal benefit only changed class threshold from "bloodline" to "talent + luck"
3. **Hope depreciation**: because success probability is too low, commoners' children's striving spirit exhausts in one or two generations. Civilization's bottom loses vitality

Regression form: the door opens, the people behind it are more desperate than those in front.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Entry ticket is predation**: Tang Luo gives commoners an entry ticket, but arena rules are his. In his rules, talent, resources, birth are still key variables. The entry ticket looks equal, actually only gives "qualification to be eliminated"
- **Showcase effect is predation**: holding up a few successful commoners as showcases makes tens of millions of failures think "I can too." This uses the few's story to rationalize the many's failure
- **Involution under the ceiling is predation**: commoners' children's youth, resources, life consumed in involution ultimately become new powerholders' stepping stones. Tang Luo's universal benefit inadvertently fuels new powerholders

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo = **enlightened predator**, his predation form is "arena style" — gives you contest rights, but the rules long predetermined you cannot win.

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Tang-Luo Bestowed Entry | Anchor Symbiosis Equal Roads |
|-------------------------|------------------------------|
| Enrollment = entry ticket | Everyone-Has-a-Road Guarantee System: not "contest rights," but "victory-guaranteed road" |
| Arena involution | 20% Activation Strategy: batch-produce strong ones, no zero-sum competition |
| Starting-line gap | Education Guarantee Law + Medical Guarantee Law: smooth early gaps |
| Few showcases | Alaya Network: whole-population monitoring, no genius buried |
| Involution under ceiling | Origin Realm fully open: the arena has no top |

Alternative's crux: not giving commoners an entry ticket, but tearing down the entire arena — cultivation is not a contest, it is co-building.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: tens of millions of commoners' children consumed in involution
- **Anti-predation**: entry ticket = contest rights ≠ qualification to win
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo opened the door, but did not build roads
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: bottom commoners are the 99%'s most silent part
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: involution-style civilizations cannot support cross-plane expansion
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Supplementary Discussion: Path Reconstruction for Bottom Commoners

Bottom commoners in the Tang-Luo system even if they enter the bestowal arena still cannot escape involution fate, because total resources are fixed, slots finite. Heng Yuan Zhou through the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization changes the resource pool from "zero-sum cake" to "infinite increment" — the universal equal infinite law ensures everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power; competition is no longer dividing cake but baking cake. The Alaya Network provides bottom commoners a complete path starting from Sense Origin; the law-first axiom ensures the path is not monopolized by aristocratic families. Bottom commoners are no longer objects of bestowal, but subjects co-building civilization.

## Conclusion

Tang Luo's door is a real door, but behind it is not an exit, it is an arena. Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment: giving someone an entry ticket is not as good as tearing down the arena. What commoners want is not "you may try," but "there must be a road."
