# Entry 276 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | Analysis of Aristocratic Remnants: The Monopoly Was Not Thoroughly Broken

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 26)

## Introduction: Overthrew the aristocrats, left the aristocrats' shadow

Among the six top-level axioms, the anti-predation axiom requires Great Dao all-tier openness, anti-monopoly constitutionalized. Tang Luo overthrew old aristocratic families, but the old families' remnants — wealth, connections, knowledge, habits — were not liquidated. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core, dissecting how aristocratic remnants reskin and return. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Aristocratic remnants' energy-level waste:

- **Old capital not liquidated**: wealth, industries, connections accumulated by old families, Tang Luo did not touch. They change names, still are social top
- **Old knowledge not forcibly opened**: families' inherited methods, Tang Luo only "no longer forbids private transmission," did not force upload. They still privately hoard, only from "legal monopoly" to "gray monopoly"
- **Old habits continue**: family children receive elite education from childhood, commoners' children start from zero. Ten years later, family children still occupy academy high tiers

Energy-level waste: **overthrew the family signboard, left the family's foundation.** Signboard changed, inside is still old people.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Reskinned return**: old families rename as "Tang Luo direct disciples," "academy high tier," "chamber-of-commerce leaders." Monopoly form changed, monopoly essence unchanged
2. **New-old merge**: the few rising commoner geniuses decades later intermarry, ally with old family remnants, forming new mixed powerholding class
3. **Institutional absence**: because no anti-monopoly constitution, the new powerholding class can legally accumulate wealth, knowledge, connections, until monopoly forms again

Regression form: Tang Luo overthrew old aristocrats, but new aristocrats began growing under his rule. After he dies, new aristocrats become open.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Compromise is predation**: Tang Luo took "overthrow but not liquidate" compromise toward old families. The compromise price is — old family remnants retained most strength, waiting to counterattack
- **Reskinning is predation**: new powerholders self-claim "beneficiaries of Tang Luo good policy," monopoly no longer called "aristocratic family," called "elite." Name changed, predation structure unchanged
- **No legislation is predation**: because no anti-monopoly law, new powerholders' accumulation is legal. Legal predation is harder to break than illegal predation

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo = **enlightened predator**, his compromise toward old families is essentially reaching an unspoken agreement with old predators: "I won't exterminate you, you change names and continue."
