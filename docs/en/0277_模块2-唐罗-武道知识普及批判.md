# Entry 277 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | Critique of Martial-Way Knowledge Popularization: The Boundary and Ceiling of Popularization

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 27)

## Introduction: Popularization is real popularization, but popularization has a boundary

Among the six top-level axioms, the anti-predation axiom requires Great Dao all-tier openness. Tang Luo popularized martial-way knowledge; this is one of his most praiseworthy good policies. But popularization has a boundary — inside the boundary is good policy, outside the boundary is another kind of monopoly. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core, dissecting popularization's boundary. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Boundary-caused energy-level waste of popularization:

- **Incomplete-version popularization**: methods Tang Luo popularizes are mostly "basic version," "streamlined version." True formulas, mind-methods, incantations still circulate only within Tang Luo's direct-disciples circle. Students learn versions "that can enter the door," cannot learn versions "that can reach the top"
- **Q&A right monopolized**: students have problems in practice, can only seek Q&A from Tang Luo or his direct disciples. Q&A right itself is a resource, monopolized
- **Advancement channel monopolized**: from basic version to advanced version, requires "taking a master" to unlock. Popularization's end is a new threshold

Energy-level waste: **popularization lets ten thousand enter the door, but the road from entry to summit is paved as a tolled secondary highway.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Knowledge dualization**: the popularized "public version" and privately hoarded "true-formula version" form two knowledge tiers. The public version trains mid-tier, the true-formula version trains top tier
2. **Master-disciple revival**: because advancement requires taking a master, the master-disciple relationship again becomes cultivation circles' core. Tang Luo overthrew aristocratic master-disciple monopoly, then personally rebuilt his own master-disciple monopoly
3. **Popularization illusion**: the world sees "everyone may learn," mistakenly thinking knowledge is fully open. Actually the most critical part is still locked — popularization became a smoke screen obscuring monopoly

Regression form: on the surface knowledge popularized, actually knowledge stratified.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Fragmentary chapters hook disciples**: popularizing incomplete versions is to hook talented students to come take disciples. This is knowledge popularization's most hidden commercialization design
- **Q&A right is power**: whoever can answer questions holds interpretation right. Tang Luo clutching interpretation right is clutching knowledge's meaning
- **Popularization is control**: popularization is not to let all beings stand on their own, but to guide all beings toward directions Tang Luo wishes. Popularization is the gentlest guidance technique

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo = **enlightened predator**, popularization is his predation structure's concrete form of "knowledge stratification."

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Tang-Luo Popularization Boundary | Anchor Symbiosis All-Tier Openness |
|----------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Incomplete-version popularization | Complete methods + all commentaries + all deduction processes, uploaded at once |
| Q&A right monopolized | Alaya Network collaborative Q&A, billion-plus mentors and students participating |
| Taking a master unlocks advancement | Advancement no threshold, auto-opened by progress |
| Knowledge dualization | Single knowledge layer, distinction between public and hoarded disappears |
| Popularization = guidance | Knowledge neutrally public, learners choose direction themselves |

Alternative's crux: popularization is not "giving part," but "giving all" — including true formulas, commentaries, deduction processes.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-high**. Fragmentary chapters hooking disciples is a classic web-novel design
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Long-form late if it needs new unique moves, the cheapest move is "Tang Luo comprehended a new true formula again" — knowledge stock stretches with plot
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: high

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: incomplete popularization makes students reinvent true formulas
- **Anti-predation**: fragmentary chapters hooking disciples = knowledge commercialization
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo popularized 90%, locks the 10% true formula
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: true formulas not open, 99% can never reach the top
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: all-tier openness is the precondition for cross-plane cooperation
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Supplementary Discussion: Boundary Design of Knowledge Popularization

The Tang-Luo system's martial-way knowledge popularization boundary and ceiling are essentially knowledge monopoly remnants. Heng Yuan Zhou through the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization fully opens the 300 cultivation systems; the Alaya Network provides full-chain teaching resources from entry to hyper-multiverse. The law-first axiom ensures knowledge openness has a ladder: low-tier knowledge fully open, high-tier knowledge requires corresponding realm's law understanding as entry threshold — not hoarding, but preventing low-realm beings from misusing high-tier laws and causing disasters. The universal equal infinite law ensures openness is not alms, but everyone's inherent evolution right.

## Conclusion

Popularization is Tang Luo's best deed, but popularization's boundary is precisely his most incomplete place. Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment: popularization is not giving part, but giving all. Giving part's popularization is advertising; giving all's popularization is liberation.
