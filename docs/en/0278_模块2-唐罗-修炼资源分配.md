# Entry 278 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | Cultivation Resource Distribution: Resource Attachment Under the Bestowal System

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 28)

## Introduction: Whoever holds resources holds life

Among the six top-level axioms, the anti-predation axiom requires developing increments, not consuming stock; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings to have equal evolution qualification. The Tang-Luo system's cultivation resource distribution is the bestowal system's core component — resources are given by Tang Luo, all beings live off Tang Luo. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Bestowal-system resource distribution's energy-level waste:

- **Rent-seeking cost**: to get Tang Luo's resource allocation, all beings study "how to please Tang Luo," not "how to improve cultivation." Civilization's bright minds are massively consumed in rent-seeking
- **Mismatch uncorrectable**: resource distribution judged by Tang Luo alone, judgment bias unchecked. Truly talented but not social persons cannot get resources; social but mediocre-talented persons get them
- **Stock thinking**: Tang Luo distributes existing resources (stock), not developing increments. However fairly he divides the cake, the cake does not grow

Energy-level waste: **the entropy of resource distribution far exceeds the resources' own value.** Of ten thousand portions, maybe under 30% actually hits the blade.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Rent-seeking culture**: because resources are bestowed by Tang Luo, cultivation circles form a "stay near the power center" culture. Geniuses do not frontier, they schmooze
2. **Increment stagnation**: because no one is incentivized to develop new resources (once developed, must hand over to Tang Luo for distribution), civilization's total resource amount stagnates
3. **Attachment network**: around Tang Luo forms a complex human-relations network, master-disciple network, faction network. These networks weave denser, finally trapping Tang Luo himself

Regression form: resource distribution grows more complex, total resources shrink.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Distribution is sovereignty**: whoever distributes resources is civilization's master. Tang Luo holding distribution right is holding all beings' lives
- **Attachment is chains**: all beings, for resources, must attach to Tang Luo. Once attachment forms, the attached's personality, judgment, courage are all bound by chains
- **Bestowal is taming**: Tang Luo bestowing resources is essentially taming — making all beings used to "waiting for reward," not "going to earn"

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo = **enlightened predator**, resource distribution right is his predation structure's hardest component.

## Supplementary Discussion: Deconstructing Resource Attachment

The Tang-Luo system's cultivation resource distribution attachment logic's core is resources held by the bestower, recipients must attach. Heng Yuan Zhou's alternative is joint contracting and increment development: resources are no longer fixed stock, but increments that cultivators continuously create through civilization engineering. The Alaya Network real-time settles each cultivator's resource output and consumption; the law-first axiom ensures distribution is by contribution, not by personal connection. Under the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, the universal equal infinite law makes resource attachment fundamentally meaningless — since origin is infinite, why attach?
