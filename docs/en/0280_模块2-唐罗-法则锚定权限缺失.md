# Entry 280 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | Absence of Law-Anchored Authority: Strong Ones Above the Law

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 30)

## Introduction: Who will manage the strongest person

Among the six top-level axioms, the law-first axiom requires laws first. Heng Yuan Zhou has a core term — **law-anchored authority**: any strong one's actions must be anchored on public law, not on his personal judgment. The Tang-Luo system's greatest blank is precisely this: Tang Luo himself is above the law. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Above-the-law energy-level waste:

- **Strong-one self-consumption**: Tang Luo as the strongest, no law constrains, no law protects. Every day he faces countless requests, temptations, conspiracies, all judged by himself. This mental consumption is enormous
- **Decisions without anchor**: no law as decision anchor, Tang Luo's judgment depends on intuition, experience, mood. Decision quality fluctuates widely
- **Subordinates without basis**: Tang Luo's subordinates execute his orders, but the orders themselves have no legal basis. Subordinates do not know "why this is done," only follow. Once circumstances change, subordinates cannot adapt

Energy-level waste: **the strongest one's energy is consumed on problems that should have been solved by law.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Strong-one willfulness**: because Tang Luo is above the law, every decision may be willful. Willful decisions repeat, civilization's expectations become unstable
2. **Subordinates mechanized**: because orders have no legal basis, subordinates only follow, do not think. Tang Luo retires, subordinates all become puppets waiting for orders
3. **Rules change with person**: rules Tang Luo sets today may change tomorrow by his mood. All beings cannot form stable expectations, social trust low

Regression form: civilization under Tang Luo's rule looks ordered, actually order is his personal will's projection. The projection sways with his mood.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Above-the-law = lawless**: strong ones above the law is essentially strong ones possessing "arbitrary power." Arbitrary power's predation on weak ones is more dangerous than law-based predation
- **Not anchoring is predation**: Tang Luo's actions not anchored on public law is equivalent to every decision being "free discretion." Free discretion = disguised predation
- **No supervision is predation**: no one can supervise him, because no law exists to supervise him by. Absolute freedom = absolute predation potential

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo = **enlightened predator**, his predation form is "arbitrary discretion." His good will limits his abuse of power, but good will itself cannot be transmitted.

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Tang-Luo Above-the-Law | Anchor Symbiosis Law-Anchored |
|------------------------|------------------------------|
| Strong-one arbitrary discretion | Law-anchored authority benefits all beings, all actions anchored on public law |
| Decisions by intuition | Decisions have legal basis, reviewable, traceable |
| Subordinates only follow | Subordinates know why, can adapt |
| Rules change with person | Laws stable, law revision requires legislative procedure |
| No supervision | Anchor Supervision Committee + Symbiotic Court, strong ones also judged |

Alternative's crux: replace "Tang Luo's judgment" with "law's judgment." Strong ones are not above the law, but under the law.

- **Setting self-consistency: medium**. Protagonist above-the-law is "strong-one privilege" in his world-view, self-consistent but fragile
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium-high**. Long-form late if it needs "Tang Luo errs" to create conflict, the early "Tang Luo always right" is torn; if it needs "Tang Luo judged" to create drama, the early "Tang Luo above the law" is torn
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: high

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the strongest one's energy consumed on problems that could have been legalized
- **Anti-predation**: arbitrary discretion = disguised predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is a good arbitrary-discretionist, but arbitrary discretion itself is a bad institution
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: law-anchored authority benefits all beings, strong ones no exception
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cross-plane governance must have portable laws, cannot rely on arbitrary discretion
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Heng Yuan Zhou's law-anchored authority is not to manage weak ones, but to manage strong ones. What the Tang-Luo system most lacks is not protection for weak ones, but constraint on the strongest. A civilization above the law, even if its ruler is a sage, is only temporary good governance — once the sage retires, good governance ends.
