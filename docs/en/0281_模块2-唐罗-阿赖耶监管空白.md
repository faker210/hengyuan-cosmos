## Introduction: Where Tang Luo can see has universal benefit; what about where he cannot see

Among the six top-level axioms, the causal self-discipline axiom requires Alaya whole-network sharing; the collective ascension axiom requires no wasting big talent on small tasks. Tang Luo's universal benefit covers only places he can see — academy, cities, core disciples. Corners he cannot see: remote mountain villages, frontier tribes, underground dark markets, remain supervision blanks. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Supervision-blank energy-level waste:

- **Monitoring blind spots**: Tang Luo has no Alaya Network, his attention is finite. The world is vast, places he can see are under 10%. What happens in 90% of places, he does not know
- **Feedback lag**: problems in remote areas must report layer by layer to reach Tang Luo. During reporting, information is filtered, embellished, delayed. What Tang Luo sees is no longer the truth
- **Calibration missing**: because he cannot see grassroots, Tang Luo's good policies distort at grassroots. He thinks policies are effective, actually grassroots long ago changed policies beyond recognition

Energy-level waste: **Tang Luo's good will is discounted by his own attention boundary.** What he can benefit is only what his sight reaches.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Grassroots distortion**: good policies reach grassroots, twisted by local strongman interest groups. Tang Luo thinks universal benefit is delivered, actually grassroots commoners are still exploited
2. **Feedback failure**: because information is filtered layer by layer, Tang Luo's perception of grassroots is severely distorted. He thinks the world is peaceful, actually undercurrents surge
3. **Blind spots become thieves**: in supervision blanks, gray industries, underground forces, smuggling networks naturally grow. These forces after decades grow large, in turn erode universal-benefit results

Regression form: the myth of "peace under heaven" under Tang Luo's rule is built on the suffering of the grassroots he cannot see.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Blind spot is predation**: places Tang Luo cannot see, predators run wild. His "not managing" is equivalent to acquiescing predation in those places
- **Information monopoly**: middle layers to protect themselves monopolize upward information flow. Tang Luo is deceived by his own subordinates without knowing
- **Universal benefit's performativity**: Tang Luo's universal benefit mainly occurs in "places he can see" — academy openings, disciples awarded, city tours. These performative universal benefits obscure grassroots real suffering

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo = **enlightened predator**, his predation form is "supervision blind spots" — he cannot see, so he is not responsible; but his not being responsible does not mean he has no responsibility.
