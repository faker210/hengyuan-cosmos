# Entry 282 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | Absence of Causal Self-Discipline: No Reporting Mechanism for Cross-Plane Operations

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 32)

## Introduction: If Tang Luo reaches cross-plane, what will he do

Among the six top-level axioms, the causal self-discipline axiom expressly states: cross-plane operations strictly reported, observe first then limitedly interfere, only transmit knowledge back without disturbing karma. The Tang-Luo system is currently confined to a single plane, but if his ability continues growing, he will inevitably reach cross-plane. The problem: his system has prepared no reporting mechanism for cross-plane operations. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Causal self-discipline absence's energy-level waste:

- **No observation habit**: Tang Luo does anything as long as "I think it right, I do it." He has no "observe then interfere" habit. Cross-plane operations by intuition easily disturb innocent karma
- **No reporting concept**: Tang Luo's actions need not be reported to anyone. In his own plane, this is "sage lord's freedom"; stepping out of the plane, this is "interference without permission"
- **No return mechanism**: if Tang Luo learns new knowledge cross-plane, there is no Alaya Network to return it. Knowledge flows with his person, cannot benefit his own race

Energy-level waste: cross-plane energy without reporting, observation, return is one-time consumption — done is done, no trace.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Karmic pollution**: if Tang Luo by intuition interferes cross-plane, he may inadvertently alter other planes' history. These changes will flow back in unpredictable ways
2. **Karmic enemies**: cross-plane interference without reporting may conflict with other planes' anchor civilizations. His own race makes enemies because of Tang Luo's personal behavior
3. **Knowledge waste**: cross-plane learned knowledge not returned, his own race cannot benefit. Tang Luo personally gets stronger, his own race does not

Regression form: the more Tang Luo personally cross-planes, the stronger he gets; his own race's civilization instead grows more fragile due to karmic pollution.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Not reporting is predation**: cross-plane operations without reporting is equivalent to freely cutting on others' karmic tree. This is cross-plane-scale predation
- **Not observing is predation**: interfering without observing is equivalent to operating without looking at the patient. This is disrespect to other planes' life
- **Taking without giving is predation**: cross-plane only taking knowledge without returning is inter-civilizational one-way predation

By the Gu Chensha / Tang Luo judgment table standard: Tang Luo = **enlightened predator**, when his predation form extends cross-plane, it becomes "unreported interference."

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Tang-Luo No Causal Self-Discipline | Anchor Symbiosis Causal Self-Discipline |
|------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| By intuition cross-plane | Observe-First-Then-Limited-Interference Principle constitutionalized |
| No reporting | Cross-plane operations strictly reported, Cross-Plane Ethics Code backstop |
| Take without giving | Only-Return-Knowledge Principle: take knowledge only, not karma |

Alternative's crux: cross-plane is not free exploration, but rule-bound exploration. Rules are not bonds, but protection — protecting others, also protecting oneself.

- **Anti-predation**: unreported cross-plane interference = cross-plane predation

## Supplementary Discussion: Reporting System for Cross-Plane Operations

The Tang-Luo system's absence of causal self-discipline, no reporting mechanism for cross-plane operations, means strong ones can freely disturb other planes' karmic chains. Heng Yuan Zhou's alternative is cross-plane operation reporting system: any cross-plane act must first be registered on the Alaya Network, stating purpose, scope, and expected karmic impact. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's law-first axiom requires cross-plane operations to follow observe-first-then-limited-interference. The universal equal infinite law ensures the rights of beings on interfered planes are not trampled by outsiders.

## Conclusion

If Tang Luo stays only on his own plane, he is a good sage lord; but if he reaches cross-plane, he without causal self-discipline is a cross-plane hazard. Heng Yuan Zhou's causal self-discipline axiom does not bind strong ones, but lets them go farther — the precondition of going far is going by the rules.
