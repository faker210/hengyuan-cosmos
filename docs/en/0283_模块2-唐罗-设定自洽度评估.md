# Entry 283 | Tang-Luo Decomposition | Setting Self-Consistency Assessment: The Tang-Luo System's Internal Logic

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Tang-Luo System (Entry 33)

## Introduction: Within the Tang-Luo system, can the logic be self-consistent

Among the six top-level axioms, the law-first axiom requires setting self-consistency over plot convenience. The sixth segment in the first six-segment structure — setting self-consistency / retcon-ending risk assessment — receives special expansion in this entry. This entry takes the law-first axiom as core, systematically assessing the Tang-Luo system's internal logical self-consistency. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Tang Luo = high dependency / bestowal-style civilization**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Insufficient self-consistency's energy-level waste:

- **Patch cost**: because internal logic occasionally has holes, the author must continuously patch. Patching itself consumes creative energy
- **Reader confusion**: settings not self-consistent, readers will be confused, question, abandon the book. Confusion itself is civilization-level narrative waste
- **Expansion constrained**: because underlying logic has holes, world-view expansion must be cautious, otherwise old holes are amplified by new content

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression (Self-Consistency Perspective)

Regression caused by insufficient setting self-consistency:

1. **Rule drift**: because underlying logic is not hard, rules drift with plot. Readers gradually stop believing these rules
2. **Explanation cost**: every rule drift requires the author to spend large space explaining. Explanation replaces narrative, story becomes hard to read
3. **Trust collapse**: once readers discover rules can be arbitrarily modified, they stop immersing. Narrative's foundation — "believing in this world" — collapses

Regression form: not civilization regression, but narrative regression — readers no longer believe the world the author tells.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Predatory thinking behind insufficient self-consistency:

- **Convenience first**: for plot convenience, the author may sacrifice setting self-consistency. This is predation on reader trust
- **Protagonist halo**: if settings cannot be self-consistent, use "protagonist halo" to brush off. The halo is predation on laws
- **Number padding**: to let the protagonist continue leveling, early iron laws are torn. This is predation on the numerical ladder early-built with effort

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Tang-Luo Self-Consistency Medium-High | Heng Yuan Zhou Self-Consistency High |
|---------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Rules occasionally drift | Laws first, rules do not drift |
| Patch-style maintenance | Law-first, no patch needed |
| Protagonist halo backstop | Law-anchored, no halo concept |
| Expansion constrained | Can expand infinitely without collapse |

Tang-Luo system's self-consistency score: **medium-high**. Its realm system, academy rules are largely self-consistent within the world-view; no Douluo-style late padding or Shrouding-the-Heavens-style realm collapse is seen. But self-consistency highly depends on the premise that "Tang Luo himself is always in office"; once plot requires him to leave, rules hang in the air.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: patch cost and reader-confusion cost
- **Anti-predation**: convenience first = predation on reader trust
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo self-consistency medium-high, main risk in single-point dependency
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: self-consistency must cover 99% beings' situations, not only the protagonist

## Supplementary Discussion: Network Verification of Internal Logic

The Tang-Luo system's setting self-consistency is judged medium self-consistent after Alaya Network's formal verification — it can explain itself but many places rely on protagonist halo to fill cracks. Heng Yuan Zhou's law-first axiom requires any setting to pass logical self-consistency verification in the law network, not rely on plot patch-up. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's self-consistency standard is higher: not only internally contradiction-free, but also compatible with Myriad Races republicanism, the universal equal infinite law, and other underlying axioms. The Tang-Luo system's logical holes have nowhere to hide in network verification.

## Conclusion

The Tang-Luo system's self-consistency is above-average among traditional web novels — it at least has no Douluo-style number padding, nor Shrouding-the-Heavens-style realm collapse. But its self-consistency is "person present = self-consistent"; once the person retires, it leaks. Heng Yuan Zhou's requirement is "law present = self-consistent"; any individual exits, self-consistency does not drop.
