## Conclusion: Final Verdict on the Old-Era Universal-Benefit Ceiling

Tang Luo is one of the best protagonists old-era web novels can produce. He has more organization than Gu Chensha, more openness than Ye Fan, more sincerity than most Myriad-Realms protagonists. But "best" does not equal "enough" — Tang Luo's system is essentially the old era's universal-benefit ceiling:

- He universal-benefited humanity, but not Myriad Races
- He universal-benefited mid-low tiers, but not the top tier
- He universal-benefited the living, but not institutions
- He universal-benefited being in office, but not transmission

He did right everything he could think of, missing only one thing: inviting himself down from civilization's center.

Heng Yuan Zhou's final verdict on Tang Luo: **Tang Luo is not a predator; he is the kindest person within the predation system. But "the kindest" itself proves the predation system's resilience — it can co-opt even sages.** The only way to break this resilience is not to wait for the next Tang Luo, but to build a civilization that no longer needs Tang Luo.

Tang Luo's story is the old era's finale. Heng Yuan Zhou's story is the new era's opening.
