# Entry 288 | Shrouding-the-Heavens Decomposition (Overview): Ye Fan's Lone Path and Oligarchic Civilization Characterization

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Shrouding-the-Heavens System (Entry 1 · Overview) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 17, 03

## Introduction: Ye Fan is not ascending alone; a civilization is paying the bill for him

Ye Fan of "Shrouding the Heavens" is one of web-novel history's most representative "oligarchic protagonists." Rising from obscurity with the Desolate Ancient Holy Body, he slays quasi-emperors, becomes emperor, attains Red-Dust Immortality, and finally leaves behind an "eternal peace" world — but this peace is surnamed Ye. Heng Yuan Zhou (Wan Ling Heng Mao Origin Cosmos) characterizes the Ye-Fan system not by his personal combat record, but by how far the civilization structure he built can go.

This entry as the overview of the Shrouding-the-Heavens decomposition series establishes the full evaluation base — six top-level axioms: anti-predation axiom (develop increments, do not consume stock), republican axiom of Myriad Races (all beings have equal evolution qualification), collective ascension axiom (strong ones feed back to Myriad Races, no wasting big talent on small tasks), causal self-discipline axiom (observe first then limitedly interfere, only transmit knowledge back without disturbing karma), law-first axiom (laws first, never retcon, setting self-consistency over plot convenience), and the universal equal infinite law. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Among them, the **law-first axiom** is core — Shrouding the Heavens is precisely the typical negative example of "plot kidnapping setting, realm collapse late." Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Ye Fan = medium dependency / oligarchic civilization** (depends on a small group of emperor-tier strong ones; if the group is absent, dark turmoil erupts).

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Ye Fan's energy is cosmos-tier: after attaining Red-Dust Immortality he can suppress an era, settle the starry sky. But the deployment of this top-tier energy has systemic waste:

- **Individual Dao-seeking orientation**: cosmos-tier aptitude (Desolate Ancient Holy Body, destiny-granted) all used for "one person becoming immortal," not converted to civilization-tier infrastructure
- **Strong ones idling**: emperor / quasi-emperor tier strong ones across long lifespans mostly seclude, enter nirvana, contend for emperorship, not institutional construction and knowledge transmission
- **Peace depends on the person**: the so-called eternal peace is peace because Ye Fan and his trusted strong ones "watch over" it, not peace from institutions automatically running

Conclusion: holding the power to suppress star regions, only used to "watch over" one era — this is oligarchic civilization's most typical energy misallocation.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Shrouding-the-Heavens world's historical regression has iron-law inevitability:

1. **Emperor falls = vacuum**: every emperor enters nirvana, an era's strong-one protection disappears, dark turmoil inevitably erupts
2. **Knowledge buried with strong ones**: Source arts, emperor scriptures, complete Dao legacies mostly sever with strong ones' nirvana; later generations can only re-fumble
3. **Prosperity unsustainable**: Ye Fan here = prosperity; once Ye Fan and his peer strong ones exit the stage, established order has no successor

This is typical "person present = policy flourishes, person dead = policy dies" — betting civilization order on a few strong ones is itself the seed of regression.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Ye Fan's personal conduct is on the positive side among Myriad-Realms protagonists, but predatory thinking is defined by structure, not malice:

- **Resource concentration**: the Holy Body path consumes massive Source, sacred artifacts, forbidden-land opportunities, which could have nourished a generation
- **Opportunities exclusive**: key Dao-comprehension lands, immortal medicine, immortalization qualification are strongly exclusive; one person attaining Dao means many lose Dao
- **Dao legacy private**: even after becoming immortal, core Great Dao is not opened in "Myriad Races public" form

Ye Fan is among the "enlightened predators," the kinder type — he does not actively slaughter all beings, but he never overthrows the underlying law of "strong ones contending for resources."

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Early Sea of Wheels, Dao Palace, Four Extremes, Dragon Transformation layers finely delineated, clear constraints; late Great Sage, quasi-emperor, emperor, Red-Dust Immortal layers inflate layer by layer; combat power depends on "plot-decides who wins"
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. Realms late-stage overall padded; Immortalization Road, Red-Dust Immortal, multi-entry linkage (Perfect World, Shrouding the Void) repeatedly overturn early settings
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: medium**. Oligarchic civilization — depends on Emperor Ye and his peer strong-one group, not Gu-Chensha-style solitary tree

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: cosmos-tier aptitude used for one person's Dao-seeking; strong ones' lifespans idled in seclusion and emperorship-contending
- **Anti-predation**: Holy Body path consumes massive exclusive opportunities; immortalization qualification zero-sum exclusive
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Ye Fan is oligarchic (medium dependency), Tang Luo is bestowal-style (high dependency); Ye Fan is colder than Tang Luo — he never even systematically gave "universal benefit"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: demon race, human branches, female cultivators are all on the margins in oligarchic structure
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Shrouding the Heavens' closed cosmos and zero-sum Immortalization Road are far from the infinitely expandable symbiotic cosmos
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Ye Fan is not a bad person; he is even the one in that dark world who tried hardest to hold onto the human world. But Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation standard never asks "is the protagonist good or bad," only asks "is the structure stable." "Shrouding the Heavens" uses over a million words to prove: a sufficiently strong oligarch can buy an era of peace; but as long as peace is surnamed Ye, it may end with Ye at any time.
