# Entry 289 | Shrouding-the-Heavens Decomposition | Energy-Level Waste Analysis: Cosmos-Tier Aptitude Used for Personal Dao-Seeking

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Shrouding-the-Heavens System (Entry 2) | Benchmark base: Documents 15, 18, 04

## Introduction: When a civilization bets everything on one person's dantian

Among the six top-level axioms, the collective ascension axiom requires strong ones not to ascend alone but to lift Myriad Ranks forward; anti-energy-waste is this module's mandatory core. This entry specially discusses Shrouding-the-Heavens' most glaring energy misallocation: **cosmos-tier aptitude systematically invested in personal Dao-seeking**. The law-first axiom reminds us: evaluating energy deployment must not be kidnapped by "protagonist invincible" thrill, but must see whether resources are converted to civilization-tier stock. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Shrouding-the-Heavens world's energy waste has at least three layers:

- **Aptitude layer**: the Desolate Ancient Holy Body is rarer than rare across eons, should have been civilization's shared "hardware sample," but is compressed into Ye Fan alone's leveling engine. A physique sample that could be studied, popularized, replicated, only serves one person's immortality
- **Resource layer**: Source, immortal medicine, massive Life Spring, Divine Bridge, Dao Platform resources the Holy Body needs, under the "one person Dao-seeking" logic are drawn without upper limit. If these resources nourished a generation, they could batch-produce Law-Yuan and Dao-Yuan tier backbone
- **Lifespan layer**: emperor-tier strong ones often have thousands to tens of thousands of years of lifespan, but mostly used for seclusion, waiting out time, contending for emperorship, not precipitating experience into transmissible civilization assets

In Heng Yuan Zhou's words: this is stuffing a power plant into one person's flashlight.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How energy waste directly produces regression:

1. **Resource drain = barrenness**: after the Holy Body path drains a region's Source veins, later generations' cultivation threshold in that region skyrockets; civilization's overall energy drops rather than rises
2. **Experience generational break**: emperor nirvanas, lifetime Dao comprehension buried with him; later generations start from zero re-walking the old road — same detours repeated billions of times
3. **Backbone discontinuity**: resources all stacked at the top; mid-low tiers (Sea of Wheels through Dao Palace stage) lack nourishment; the civilization pyramid's base shrinks

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Ye Fan's predatory thinking concentrates in the "Holy Body specialness" narrative:

- **The dark side of "my fate is mine"**: emphasizing Holy Body supremacy is equivalent to declaring this physique's resources should prioritize him
- **Zero-sum narrative of opportunities**: immortal medicine, Immortalization Road slots, top Dao-comprehension lands are all zero-sum goods you "win by grabbing"; never conceived "expand increments, everyone has a share"
- **Knowledge private**: even late after becoming emperor, systematic feedback to bottom cultivators (textbookization, universal benefit) is almost absent

He is not an evil person, but he is the embodiment of the predatory structure that "cosmos-tier aptitude is naturally spent on oneself."

It needs further pointing out: energy waste is not static, but compound-interest-amplified. Ye Fan eats an extra mouthful of Source in Sea of Wheels / Dao Palace stage,同期 competitors have one less mouthful; by quasi-emperor / emperor stage, this one mouthful's gap has accumulated into a chasm. Shrouding the Heavens has no resource-reproduction concept; Source used a bit, less a bit; immortal medicine plucked one plant, extinct one plant — this is the closed zero-sum cosmos's essence. Heng Yuan Zhou dares to say "develop increments" because it engineers Origin circulation, letting energy be manufactured, replicated, standardized, not dug out bit by bit from veins by the protagonist. However hard Ye Fan digs, he digs stock; Heng Yuan Zhou builds factories. This is not a gap in realm, but a gap in mode of production.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: cosmos-tier aptitude, Source veins, lifespan triple waste
- **Anti-predation**: physique resources should prioritize the Holy Body is a structural predatory narrative
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least universally benefited human mid-low tiers; Ye Fan did not even give systematic universal benefit; energy more concentrated at the top
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: resources all flow to human top Holy Body; Myriad Races do not even qualify for a share of the soup
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: total resources closed, zero-sum, no increment-development consciousness
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

Energy waste is not Ye Fan's personal sin, but the inevitable product of this logic that "personal Dao-seeking is civilization's highest goal." Heng Yuan Zhou's proposition: aptitude is civilization's public asset, not some one person's dantian fuel.
