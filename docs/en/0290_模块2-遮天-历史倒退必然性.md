# Entry 290 | Shrouding-the-Heavens Decomposition | Inevitable Historical Regression: Emperor Falls = Dark Turmoil

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Shrouding-the-Heavens System (Entry 3) | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 07, 04

## Introduction: Between prosperity and chaos lies only one person's nirvana

Among the six top-level axioms, the causal self-discipline axiom requires rooting out civilization regression and historical backsliding; the collective ascension axiom requires strong ones to feed back to Myriad Ranks. This entry dissects the Shrouding-the-Heavens world's cruelest law — **the day an emperor enters nirvana is the start of civilization regression**. The law-first axiom requires us to treat this "law" as a setting-level hard flaw, not tragic aesthetics. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Energy waste behind historical regression is "protection vacuum" waste:

- Emperor present, cosmos-tier suppression energy covers the starry sky; emperor dies, this energy instantly zeroes out, rather than precipitating into institutions
- Quasi-emperor, Great Sage tier strong ones slaughter each other over legacy before and after the emperor falls; protection energy consumed in civil war
- Energy that could maintain order all consumed in the long chaos of "waiting for the next emperor to be born"

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

This is this entry's core; Shrouding-the-Heavens' regression is a structural iron law:

1. **Suppression-vacuum law**: what the emperor suppresses is not specific enemies, but "the space for strong ones to do evil." The person leaves, the space opens
2. **Dark-turmoil cycle law**: whenever ancient supremes, forbidden-land dark beings feel lifespans ending, they harvest lives to extend; civilization population cliff-drops
3. **Knowledge extinction law**: in turmoils aristocratic families, Dao legacies are blood-washed; cross-eon accumulated cultivation knowledge batch-buried
4. **Population and civilization double drop**: every dark turmoil is a population-zero, civilization-restart regression

Shrouding-the-Heavens' "history" is not an upward line, but an oscillating loop of "rise — turmoil — zero — re-rise" — every oscillation does not guarantee returning to the previous height.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Ye Fan himself ended his era's dark turmoils, but his predatory thinking manifests in:

- **"I decide" path dependency**: solving turmoils is "I am stronger, I suppress," not "build institutions that do not depend on my suppressing"
- **Avoiding turmoil roots**: forbidden lands, ancient supremes, lifespan anxiety — these structural lesions are not rooted out, only temporarily held down by Ye Fan's personal force
- **Treating chaos as backdrop**: dark turmoils in narrative are the protagonist's sharpening stone, not civilization problems needing institutional solutions

It is worth emphasizing: Shrouding-the-Heavens' regression is not that chaos is worse than prosperity, but that every prosperity is shorter than the previous. This is the oscillating loop's true horror: after turmoil, civilization energy may not return to pre-turmoil height, because knowledge generational break, population loss, Dao-legacy severance superimpose, making rebuilding lose its starting point. The causal self-discipline axiom writes rooting out civilization regression and historical backsliding into the top level precisely because it sees through oscillating civilization's essence — it seems to cycle, actually declines. Ye Fan ended his era's turmoils, but did not institutionally cut this downward spiral; the world after him only pushed the spiral back a few generations.
