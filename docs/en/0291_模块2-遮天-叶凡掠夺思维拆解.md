# Entry 291 | Shrouding-the-Heavens Decomposition | Ye Fan's Predatory Thinking Deconstructed: Resource Consumption of the Holy Body Path

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Shrouding-the-Heavens System (Entry 4) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 18, 22

## Introduction: One person's immortalization road, how many people's possibilities must it eat

Among the six top-level axioms, the anti-predation axiom is the origin of all critique — not taking slaughter and predation as the evolution path, Great Dao all-tier public with no private hoarding. This entry takes "predatory thinking defined by structure, not by malice" as methodology, dissecting the resource-consumption logic of Ye Fan's Holy Body path. The law-first axiom reminds: structural critique cannot be waived because the protagonist is a good person. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Energy waste under Ye Fan's predatory thinking is "crowding-out waste":

- Every Holy Body breakthrough requires far-above-ordinary resources; these resources are absorbed by Ye Fan at high price / by force, raising同期 other cultivators' thresholds
- Massive battles erupting over key resources consume energy that could have been used for production
- Opportunities Ye Fan harvests (Dao-comprehension lands, emperor-scripture fragments) if dispersed and taught, could light up a batch of peer strong ones, but only light up one

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How predatory thinking produces regression:

1. **Peers' ceiling lowered**: the share the Holy Body alone eats directly means fewer peer competitors
2. **Dao legacies destroyed by contention**: clan-extermination wars over resources sever several complete lineages
3. **"Winner takes all" becomes model**: later generations emulate Ye Fan's path; every genius tries to replicate the "grab resources → become emperor" path, internal friction intensifies

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

This is this entry's core. Ye Fan's predatory thinking has three layers:

- **Resource-exclusion layer**: the Holy Body physique determines his "rigid demand" for Source, Life Spring, Divine Bridge resources; narrative defaults that he should prioritize getting them — this is physique privilege written into the world-view
- **Opportunity zero-sum layer**: immortal medicine, immortal materials, ancient-imperial-mountain opportunities, whatever Ye Fan gets is whatever others lose; the world-view never has the increment idea of "make new medicine"
- **Dao-legacy private layer**: even if kind to others, Ye Fan did not textbookize or publicly release the Dao he realized; his heirs are personally "selected" trusted followers, not open education "toward Myriad Ranks"

It needs emphasizing: Ye Fan did not actively slaughter innocents; he is the kinder tier of "enlightened predators"; but he never challenged the underlying axiom that "cultivation is grabbing resources," so the further he walks, the more he solidifies this predatory logic.

Here we must say a fair word for Ye Fan: under Shrouding-the-Heavens' Heavenly Dao rules, a Holy Body if not grabbing resources simply cannot survive to become emperor — the rules themselves are predatory; Ye Fan only played optimally within the rules. But Heng Yuan Zhou's critique precisely points here: the problem is not that Ye Fan is not kind enough, but that he never asked whether this rule itself should exist. He took "cultivation is grabbing resources" as heaven's law, so the stronger he gets, the more this rule is consolidated. The true righteous rogue — the kind of strong one Heng Yuan Zhou expects — should begin dismantling this rule during Dao-seeking, not after Dao-seeking continue using this rule to distribute resources. What Ye Fan lacks is not good will, but questioning the underlying law.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Holy Body resource demand inflates with realm; the reason "must eat this much" grows more far-fetched late
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Immortal-medicine and divine-material quantities rise and fall with plot needs
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: medium**. The predatory path's reasonableness entirely relies on "protagonist halo" endorsement

From another angle, Ye Fan's predatory thinking also has an easily-forgiven cloak: he does it to protect people close to him. This motive of grabbing for loved ones makes readers emotionally stand with him. But Heng Yuan Zhou asks: is grabbing for loved ones structurally different from grabbing for strangers? No — as long as resources are zero-sum, what is grabbed for loved ones is taken from strangers. The true solution is not "grab more selectively," but to grow the cake, so Ye Fan need not reach for others' bowls to protect anyone.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: crowding-out peer resources, battle internal friction, opportunities concentrated on one person
- **Anti-predation**: physique privilege, zero-sum opportunities, private Dao legacy three-layer predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's predation is "not opening up," Ye Fan's predation is "actively grabbing resources"; Ye Fan is closer to classic predatory narrative
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: resources highly concentrated on human top Holy Body; Myriad Ranks and branches almost have no share
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: zero-sum resource view cannot support multiverse collaborative development
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Ye Fan's predatory thinking is not evil, but the old Heavenly Dao's script written for him. What Heng Yuan Zhou must do is not judge Ye Fan, but burn the script itself — cultivation should not be grabbing, evolution should not be gambling.
