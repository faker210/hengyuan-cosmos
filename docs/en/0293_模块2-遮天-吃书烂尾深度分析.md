# Entry 293 | Shrouding-the-Heavens Decomposition | In-Depth Analysis of Retcon / Bad Ending: Late Settings Overturn Early Ones

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Shrouding-the-Heavens System (Entry 6) | Benchmark base: Documents 97, 99, 14

## Introduction: Holes dug early, filled with bulldozers late

Among the six top-level axioms, the law-first axiom requires "never retcon, never end badly." This entry specifically discusses how Shrouding-the-Heavens as a long-serialized IP late-stage constantly overturns early iron laws — this is not an author-ability problem, but the inevitable ending of the "setting serves plot" structure. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Retconning itself is the biggest narrative energy waste:

- Early chapters spent establishing some iron law late overturned, equivalent to those chapters' narrative investment zeroing out
- The author repeatedly "explains" why early settings do not count; the text is full of self-defense
- Old readers' nostalgia is repeatedly consumed, emotional trust overdrawn in repeated reversals

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

"Regression" retconning creates inside the world-view:

1. **History rewritten**: some early emperor's glorious combat record late shrinks due to new settings appearing
2. **Karma chain broken**: early foreshadowing cannot be paid off, becomes hanging "setting ghosts"
3. **Era sense confused**: after Shrouding the Heavens, Perfect World, Shrouding the Void trilogy linkage, character identities, era order repeatedly adjusted; readers cannot build a clear timeline

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Behind retconning is the author's predation on setting assets:

- **"The protagonist needs" above all**: as long as Ye Fan needs to cross tiers, early iron laws step aside — this is open predation on early world settings
- **Foreshadowing becomes debt**: early carefully buried suspense late is written off with "actually it's not what you think," equivalent to reneging
- **Cross-work repeated retconning**: trilogy linkage, same character's settings drift across works, repeatedly squeezing readers' cross-work nostalgia

Retconning also has an often-overlooked cost: it punishes careful readers. Those who chase chapter by chapter, carefully record foreshadowing, try to rebuild the world-view in their heads, are the most invested, most emotionally attached audience. Each retcon is a betrayal of these most loyal readers. Shrouding-the-Heavens' late retconning does not harm casual readers, but those old book fans who followed Ye Fan from Sea of Wheels all the way to Red-Dust Immortal. Heng Yuan Zhou writing setting self-consistency into the top-level axiom is essentially a solemn promise to reader trust — you read carefully, I write carefully, never disown the account.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: low**. After trilogy linkage, settings fight each other; timeline and character relationships in many places irreconcilable
- **Retcon-ending risk: extremely high**. Shrouding the Heavens itself is already a risk case; overlaid with Perfect, Shrouding-the-Void linkage, risk spills over
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: medium**. Retconning mainly serves Ye Fan (and series protagonists) continued narration

Retconning also has a chain reaction: it makes the author late-stage increasingly afraid to write new settings, because new settings will sooner or later be overturned. So late narration increasingly relies on rearranging known elements rather than true world-view expansion. Shrouding-the-Heavens' late "freshness" drop is largely not the author running out of talent, but retconning binding his own hands and feet — he dare not establish new settings, fearing new settings will also be overturned by future plot. The law-first axiom's freedom to the author is precisely the opposite: because rules are first set and cannot be overturned, the author dares to expand boldly, knowing newly written things will not be slapped by his own future.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: early narrative investment zeroed out, explanation cost skyrockets
- **Anti-predation**: predating early setting assets and reader trust
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo shows no obvious retconning; Shrouding-the-Heavens' retconning is a disaster area among Myriad-Realms IPs
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: retconned settings often involve supporting characters, foreign races, ancient history — the marginalized's history is sacrificed first
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cross-work linkage itself requires high self-consistency; Shrouding-the-Heavens' linkage is precisely the negative example
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Retconning is not Shrouding-the-Heavens alone's disease, but the "setting serves plot" writing style's genetic disease. Heng Yuan Zhou writing laws before plot is precisely rooting out retconning from the base.

The opposite of retconning is not "never make a mistake," but "when a mistake occurs it can be fixed." Heng Yuan Zhou has version maintenance notes and a setting-contradiction self-check table; when wrong, register, revise, inform readers; Shrouding-the-Heavens' approach is to pretend it never erred. The former is honest, the latter is lucky.
