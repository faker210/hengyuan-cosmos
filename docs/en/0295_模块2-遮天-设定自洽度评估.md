## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Behind low self-consistency is predation on readers' "credence resources":

- World rules repeatedly modified is equivalent to repeatedly collecting reader trust deposits
- Once readers discover rules are not credible, they no longer invest emotion in any new setting
- Protagonist halo becomes universal explanation, "why can he" — "because he is the protagonist" — this is fundamental predation on setting seriousness

Quantifying self-consistency must also distinguish two kinds: one is "internal self-consistency," i.e., the world-view internally does not fight itself; the other is "reader-predictability self-consistency," i.e., readers can infer what happens next from existing rules. Shrouding-the-Heavens early has both self-consistency acceptable; late both collapse — internal settings contradict each other, readers completely give up prediction. Heng Yuan Zhou's law-first axiom simultaneously guarantees both: law anchoring keeps internal non-fighting; clear thresholds let readers calculate. These two self-consistencies combined is truly high-self-consistency world-view.
