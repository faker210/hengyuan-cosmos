## Introduction: Outsource civilization safety to strong ones, and accept that strong ones may leave post at any time

Among the six top-level axioms, the collective ascension axiom requires strong ones to feed back rather than dominate; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings to enjoy equal protection. This entry specifically discusses Shrouding-the-Heavens world's unique "dark turmoil" — it is not natural disaster, but the periodic eruption of the "strong ones absent" institutional flaw. The law-first axiom reminds: having turmoil cycle set as world-view iron law is itself structural bankruptcy. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Energy waste in dark turmoils is shocking:

- Every turmoil harvests billions of beings; these lives could have grown into civilization builders, but are used as "lifespan nourishment"
- Forbidden-land ancient supremes sleep for eons, wake only to harvest; eons of cultivation energy used for evil rather than construction
- Post-turmoil civilization reconstruction cost is extremely high, starting from zero again — construction energy repeatedly zeroed

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

This entry's core: dark turmoil = institutionalized regression engine:

1. **Harvesting mechanism**: ancient supremes, dark beings near end of lifespan feed on billions of beings to extend — this is world-view-recognized "legal predation"
2. **Protection outsourced**: humanity and Myriad Ranks' safety fully outsourced to emperor-tier strong ones; strong ones absent, harvesting runs unimpeded
3. **Fear taming**: Myriad Ranks long-term survive in "waiting for turmoil"; civilization character becomes fawning, short-sighted, afraid to accumulate

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Ye Fan ended his era's turmoils, but his response is still predatory:

- **Stopping violence with violence**: use stronger personal force to suppress ancient supremes, not root out the "extend life by harvesting" path
- **Strong-one logic**: the solution to turmoils is "produce someone who can fight better," never conceived "let no one need fear turmoil"
- **Treat symptoms not roots**: lifespan anxiety, harvesting-lives-to-extend two root diseases remain; Ye Fan only pressed down the lid

Dark turmoil also has a narrative-ignored dimension: it is a "fear civilization." In long history of waiting for turmoil, Myriad Ranks formed a fawning, short-sighted, afraid-to-accumulate civilization character — anyway hard-built Dao legacies will sooner or later be burned down by ancient supremes, so why build? This character is scarier than turmoil itself, because it continues to act in peacetime between turmoils, grinding Myriad Ranks' drive bit by bit. Heng Yuan Zhou's Alaya supervision must not only prevent turmoil, but restore Myriad Ranks' "construction courage" — let people dare accumulate, dare transmit, dare place hope in tomorrow. This is the deep goal of rooting out turmoil.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Turmoil causes (harvest lives to extend) early self-consistent, but turmoil scale, forbidden-land count late fluctuate with plot
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium-high**. Turmoil grimness serves as Ye Fan's entrance ignition point
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: medium**. Whether turmoil calms depends on whether Ye Fan and peer strong ones are present

Dark turmoil also has an institutional consequence: it makes "strong one" and "guardian" identities coincide, thereby depriving civilization of the ability to independently judge strong ones. Myriad Ranks cannot tell whether an emperor is guarding or harvesting, because both use the same kind of power. Heng Yuan Zhou through Alaya supervision separates "guarding" from "harvesting" — guarding behavior recorded, reviewed; harvesting behavior early-warned, interfered. Strong ones no longer need to prove they are good people by "character," because institutions watch them for Myriad Ranks.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.
