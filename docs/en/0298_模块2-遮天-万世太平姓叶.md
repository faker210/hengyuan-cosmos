# Entry 298 | Shrouding-the-Heavens Decomposition | Eternal Peace Surnamed Ye: The Essence of Oligarchic Civilization

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Shrouding-the-Heavens System (Entry 11) | Benchmark base: Documents 03, 26, 30

## Introduction: Peace is good, but on what grounds does peace bear the surname Ye

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires governance power to belong to Myriad-Ranks consensus, not any family; the collective ascension axiom requires strong ones to feed back, not hereditarily. This entry directly dissects the core proposition of Shrouding-the-Heavens' ending — "eternal peace surnamed Ye." This phrase is not praise, but Heng Yuan Zhou's most precise pathological slice of oligarchic civilization. The law-first axiom reminds: even peace's surname is designated, showing order has never truly been institutionalized. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

"Peace surnamed Ye" energy waste:

- Ye Fan and his peer strong-one group use cosmos-tier force to "watch over" the world; time that could have been used for institutional construction is consumed "keeping watch"
- Later elites mistakenly think peace comes from the Ye family's blessing, losing the will and ability to build their own order
- Cultivators outside the Ye family's trusted line lack channels to rise to the "co-governance layer"; civilization governance energy idles

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Oligarchic peace's regression logic:

1. **Peace dependent**: peace is not an institutional product, but the Ye family and their circle's "bestowed peace"; circle present = peace present
2. **Succession no procedure**: who comes to "watch" after the Ye family? Settings do not answer — this is leaving civilization to suspense
3. **Myriad-Ranks subjectivity lost**: because there is always someone covering, Myriad Ranks never learn to manage themselves; once coverage fails, they do not even know how to end chaos

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Behind "eternal peace surnamed Ye" is ownership-level predation:

- **Treating civilization as private property**: peace is "given" by the Ye family, equivalent to defaulting that the world is the Ye family's industry
- **Co-governance rights confiscated**: Myriad Ranks are excluded from governance, only enjoying the "protected" position
- **Inter-generational monopoly**: Ye Fan's line and his trusted allies form a de facto oligarchic group; civilization's top is de facto hereditary

Ye Fan may never thought "heredity," but once the structure forms, it will grow hereditary teeth itself.

Eternal peace surnamed Ye also has a hidden layer: it semantically binds "peace" and "Ye family." Under this binding, any questioning of the Ye family is automatically read as a threat to peace — so Myriad Ranks even automatically give up the right to criticize the Ye family. This is oligarchy's most sophisticated self-protection: it needs no force to suppress dissent, because dissent has already automatically disappeared in Myriad Ranks' hearts. Heng Yuan Zhou writing peace into the charter and dispersing governance power to the council precisely severs this semantic binding — peace does not belong to any surname; criticizing any family is not opposing peace.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. "Eternal peace" mechanism is vague; how Ye Fan "watches for eternity" lacks rule support
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Eternal peace is more like a slogan, not a calculable institution
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: medium**. Peace directly hinges on Ye Fan and his family — this is precisely the definition of "medium dependency / oligarchic"

Eternal peace surnamed Ye also has a generational problem: Ye Fan's descendants may not have Ye Fan's ability. The first-generation Ye family may still hold; the second, third generation? Shrouding-the-Heavens is vague. Heng Yuan Zhou taking governance power from families precisely because it knows "a wise ruler's descendants remain wise" is an assumption never historically confirmed. Betting civilization on family genes is more dangerous than betting on one person — a person eventually dies, but a family may continue mediocre for a thousand years.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: cosmos-tier force used to "keep watch," governance energy idled
- **Anti-predation**: declaring civilization peace as family private property
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's universal benefit at least had an academy as public-institution prototype; Ye Fan's peace has no public institution at all
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: in "Ye-surnamed peace," Myriad Ranks are beneficiaries, not participants
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: family-style peace cannot export a stable paradigm to other multiverse civilizations
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

"Eternal peace" four characters, Shrouding-the-Heavens only delivered the first two — "eternal" is vision, "peace" is what Ye Fan fought out. The peace Heng Yuan Zhou wants is not bestowed by anyone, but one no one can take away.

Changing peace's surname from "Ye" to "Myriad Ranks" requires only one constitutional clause; but making Myriad Ranks truly believe this clause requires generations of institutional practice. What Shrouding-the-Heavens lacks is not the clause, but practice.

In the final analysis, Shrouding-the-Heavens · eternal peace surnamed Ye described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
