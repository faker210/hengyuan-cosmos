# Entry 299 | Shrouding-the-Heavens Decomposition | Source Masters and Source Arts: Shrouding-the-Heavens Version of Predatory Cultivation

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Shrouding-the-Heavens System (Entry 12) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 18, 12

## Introduction: Those who can "dig Source" hold civilization's blood-drawing needle

Among the six top-level axioms, the anti-predation axiom requires developing increments, not consuming stock. This entry specifically discusses Shrouding-the-Heavens world's "Source Masters" and "Source Arts" — this is a professional system that systematizes and esoterically transmits "finding, mining, claiming mines"; essentially Shrouding-the-Heavens version's predatory-cultivation industrial assembly line. The law-first axiom reminds: Source Arts' monopolistic design is precisely the technical incarnation of Shrouding's zero-sum resource view. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Source Arts system's energy waste:

- Source-seeking, claiming, locking process consumes massive cultivators' wisdom and time, which could have been used to develop incremental energy
- Once Source veins are found, they are usually circled by great clans and great sects, becoming private mines; public resources privatized
- Source-Arts knowledge is family-esoterically transmitted, outsiders cannot learn; all society wastes repeating groping on "not knowing where Source is"

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Source-Arts monopoly's regression consequences:

1. **Resources rent-seeking**: whoever knows Source Arts claims mines first; ordinary cultivators without Source Arts always lag one step
2. **Knowledge generational break**: once a Source-Arts family declines, its vein-finding maps are lost; later generations re-grope
3. **Circle-land wars**: Source-vein ownership triggers continuous contention; cosmos-tier energy consumed on "grabbing mines" rather than "alchemy / cultivation / construction"

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Ye Fan's relationship with the Source-Arts system is typical of predatory cultivation:

- **Holds Source-seeking technology**: Ye Fan's side holds high-tier Source Arts, meaning permanent first move in resource competition
- **Source veins privatized**: found Source veins go to their own faction, not opened to the whole cultivation world
- **Technical barriers**: Source Arts blockaded externally, forming a technical class where "those who know take all, those who do not starve"

This is not Ye Fan's original evil, but the system he is in — Source-Arts families — has always been thus; Ye Fan only sat in the driver's seat of this predatory machine.

The Source-Arts system also has a deep consequence: it produces a kind of "technological fatalism." Ordinary cultivators without Source Arts gradually believe "I naturally cannot find Source, so this is my fate" — technical monopoly not only monopolizes resources, but monopolizes people's self-perception. This fatalism is harder to break than resource scarcity itself, because it makes people stop trying to resist. Heng Yuan Zhou publicly publishing resource-exploration technology network-wide is not only to raise utilization, but to break this fatalism — let every ordinary cultivator know, not finding Source is not your fate, it is technology being hidden. Knowledge openness itself is liberation.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium**. Source-Arts system is finely designed early Shrouding, self-consistency acceptable; late Source-vein count and Source-Arts effect fluctuate with plot
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Source-Arts' wondrousness late can be adjusted by plot needs
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: medium**. Ye Fan's side holds Source-Arts first move; oligarchic resource advantage thereby consolidated

Source-Arts monopoly also produces a special contempt chain: those who know Source Arts look down on those who do not, even if the latter's combat power is higher. This contempt chain is harder to break than strength rank, because it is packaged as "technical barriers." Heng Yuan Zhou making technology public is not only for resource utilization, but also to tear down this technical contempt chain — you can find mines, I can find mines too; between us no one is higher. Knowledge's democratization ultimately points to personality's equalization.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: mine-finding technology secret, circle-land wars, repeated groping
- **Anti-predation**: Source-seeking technology esoteric = resource rent-seeking legalized
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least wanted to open martial-way knowledge; Shrouding even keeps mine-finding technology esoteric, knowledge monopoly deeper
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: ordinary cultivators and Myriad-Rank branches without Source Arts always at the bottom in resource competition
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: zero-sum mine-grabbing logic cannot support cross-multiverse resource collaboration
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Source Arts is not Shrouding-the-Heavens world's "signature profession," but proof that it institutionalized predation — even "where to loot from" has become esoteric; this world's fairness never existed from the womb.

Source Arts' secret is essentially "making ordinary people feel they are not worthy." Heng Yuan Zhou making Source Arts public is not to make everyone a Source Master, but to let everyone no longer feel unworthy.

In the final analysis, Shrouding-the-Heavens · Source Masters and Source Arts described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
