# Entry 300 | Shrouding-the-Heavens Decomposition | Desolate Ancient Aristocratic Families: Knowledge Monopoly and Class Solidification

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Shrouding-the-Heavens System (Entry 13) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 47, 36

## Introduction: When cultivation itself becomes a bloodline question

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires Great Dao all-tier public, no private Dao and no monopoly; the anti-predation axiom opposes strong ones monopolizing knowledge and Great Dao. This entry dissects Shrouding-the-Heavens world's "Desolate Ancient Aristocratic Families" — those bloodline families transmitting from antiquity to today, holding top methods and resources. They are not villains, but the legal skeleton of Shrouding's class solidification. The law-first axiom reminds: the family institution is world-view-default "legitimate order"; critique must point to institutions, not individuals. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Aristocratic-family system's energy waste:

- Top methods, emperor scriptures circulate only inside families; all-society geniuses cannot access them, massive potential strong ones wasted at mid-low tiers
- Family children get "trial-and-error rights" by birth; ordinary geniuses without resources repeatedly struggle at low tiers, talent mismatch
- Families to preserve monopoly consume massive energy on guarding out, guarding theft, guarding splitting; internal friction severe

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Aristocratic-family solidification's regression consequences:

1. **Upward channels locked**: birth determines ceiling; poor geniuses even with peerless aptitude can barely access top legacies
2. **Close-relative degeneration**: family internal transmission narrows narrower, methods rigidify in repeated compilations
3. **Revolution cycles**: monopoly accumulates to critical point, inevitably erupts "poor family flips the table" wars; civilization energy zeroes out again in revolution

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Ye Fan though from a poor family, ultimately is "co-opted" by the aristocratic-family system as a new oligarch:

- **Overthrow old families, become a new family**: Ye Fan breaks several old-line families; he and his trusted allies become the new top
- **Methods still transmitted selectively**: even after Ye Fan realizes Dao, his core legacy is still "select heirs," not "open to Myriad Ranks"
- **New bottle old wine**: old families' monopoly structure is not overturned, only a new batch of surnames

Ye Fan's predatory thinking manifests: his way of resisting families is to become a stronger family, not to abolish the family institution.

Desolate Ancient Aristocratic Families also have an often-romanticized dimension: they are narratively depicted as positive images of "long transmission, deep heritage." But by Heng Yuan Zhou's eyes, long heritage is precisely the problem — a family that has transmitted a hundred thousand years means this world has not produced new upward channels for a hundred thousand years; the most excellent talents in those hundred thousand years still can only attach to old families. A truly healthy civilization should be every generation new forces rise, old forces naturally turnover, not a few surnames sitting on the throne for a hundred thousand years. Ye Fan breaks old families but becomes a new family, showing he only completed blood-transfusion, not institution-transfusion — blood-transfusion will re-solidify; institution-transfusion roots out.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium**. Family settings early self-consistent; late to make way for Ye Fan, old-line families "passing by = destroyed," count and strength fluctuate
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Family strength varies strong and weak by protagonist need
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: medium**. Ye Fan ultimately becomes new oligarch, family structure under his hand continues rather than overturns

Desolate Ancient Aristocratic Families also have an often-overlooked economic base: they have existed a hundred thousand years because they monopolize resource-distribution channels. Poor geniuses even with peerless aptitude must go through families to get cultivation resources — families need not directly forbid you to cultivate; they only need to control resource channels to make you naturally eliminated. Heng Yuan Zhou's resource distribution by joint contracting precisely takes resource channels from families back to public, so poor geniuses need not attach to any family to get what they need.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: talent mismatch, guard-out internal friction, method rigidification
- **Anti-predation**: distributing Great Dao and resources by bloodline is the most thorough knowledge predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo explicitly makes breaking aristocratic monopoly his mission; Ye Fan though breaks old families does not abolish the institution
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: under the family system poor families and Myriad-Rank branches are always the excluded
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: bloodline-solidified cosmos cannot open upward channels to Myriad Ranks
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Desolate Ancient Aristocratic Families are not Shrouding-the-Heavens' "setting flavor," but evidence it cannot design fairness. A world that distributes the highest methods by bloodline — what "Great Dao" can it talk about?

Breaking old families is easy, Ye Fan did it; abolishing the family institution is hard, Shrouding did not. The difference between blood-transfusion and institution-transfusion is the difference between oligarchic and symbiotic civilization.

In the final analysis, Shrouding-the-Heavens · Desolate Ancient Aristocratic Families described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
