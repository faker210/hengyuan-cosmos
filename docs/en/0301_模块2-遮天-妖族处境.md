## Introduction: In Shrouding-the-Heavens world, the demon race is not even "equal citizen"

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings to have equal evolution qualification. This entry dissects the demon race's situation in Shrouding-the-Heavens world — as mounts, vassals, or crusade targets, never recognized as a co-governance subject equal to "human cultivation circles." The law-first axiom reminds: racial inequality is not supporting-character setting, but structure written into the world-view. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Demon race marginalized energy waste:

- The demon race is innately physically strong, could have been an important civilization-building force, but is positioned as "demon beast / mount / enemy"
- Demon geniuses excluded from mainstream lineages, massive talent wasted
- Long-term human-demon enmity, cosmos-tier energy consumed in racial war rather than common development

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Racial inequality's regression consequences:

1. **Civilization civil war**: human-demon conflict periodically erupts, consuming civilization total
2. **Knowledge fragmentation**: demon cultivation system runs parallel to human, no exchange, repeated trial-and-error
3. **Symbiosis potential stifled**: Myriad Ranks could complement each other (e.g., demon physique + human Dao path), but wasted by prejudice

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Ye Fan's relationship with the demon race is a typical sample of Myriad-Race inequality:

- **Demon race as supporting cast**: demon strong ones mostly appear as opponents, mounts, or tool characters, rarely with independent civilization lines
- **Human perspective default**: narrative from human standpoint, demon race's "legitimate demands" almost never taken seriously
- **Might-is-right racial version**: demon strong ones are either subjugated or destroyed, no "co-govern with demon race" option

Ye Fan's predatory thinking here manifests: he defaults that humanity is the cosmos' protagonist race, the demon race is only a functional presence in plot.

The demon race's situation also has a structural reason: Shrouding-the-Heavens' demon race is designed to "human-shape-shift," which on the surface gives demons equal opportunity, actually rewards "becoming human." The more demon-like a human, the higher status; the demon retaining its own form is treated as a beast. This is hidden racial assimilation logic — it does not directly eliminate the demon race, but induces the demon race to self-erase its nature. Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad Races republicanism is precisely the opposite: it requires each race to retain its traits, develop its own path; humans need not become demons, demons need not become humans. True equality is not assimilation, but each appreciating its own beauty.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Demon-race strength and human relations adjust by plot need, sometimes strong, sometimes vulnerable
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Demon race's positioning in the trilogy is inconsistent early and late
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: medium**. Ye Fan as human strong one, his stance is the narrative stance

The demon race's situation also has a narratological consequence: because the demon race is marginalized, Shrouding-the-Heavens cosmos' "diversity" is actually fake. On the surface countless races, actually narrative only cares about human strong ones' contention; the demon race is just a backdrop. A cosmos truly inclusive of Myriad Ranks should let the demon race have its own epic, its own philosophy, its own civilization peak — not only "some demon strong one as villain" functional character. Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad Races five genealogies, each with independent cultivation path and civilization history, this is true diversity.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.
