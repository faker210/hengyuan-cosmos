# Entry 302 | Shrouding-the-Heavens Decomposition | Critique of Human Centeredness: Racial Limitation on Evolution Qualification

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Shrouding-the-Heavens System (Entry 15) | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 51, 53

## Introduction: On what grounds is "humanity" defaulted as protagonist

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races expressly states "all beings have equal evolution qualification." This entry further builds on the demon-race entry (301): Shrouding-the-Heavens world setting "humanity" as default protagonist race is itself an unexamined privilege. The law-first axiom requires: the world-view cannot write "who deserves to cultivate" as a racial question. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Human-centeredness energy waste:

- Human cultivation system gets all narrative and resource tilt; other races' possibilities systematically omitted
- Non-human geniuses even with outstanding aptitude are narratively downgraded to "foreign prodigies," screen time and resources disproportionate
- Different races' evolution paths that could have complemented each other cannot unfold due to "human supremacy"

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Racial-centeredness regression consequences:

1. **Qualification discrimination**: world-view defaults "humanity is the orthodox cultivator," other races' cultivation seen as "heterodox"
2. **Boundary closure**: lack of knowledge flow between humanity and foreign races, each internal-frictions in closed systems
3. **Diversity lost**: single perspective dominates narrative; civilization loses diverse wisdom Myriad Ranks could have contributed

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Ye Fan's human-centeredness is the racial-upgraded version of predatory thinking:

- **Human-centered narrative**: Ye Fan's growth = humanity's growth; other races are only backdrop in this process
- **"For our humanity" moral packaging**: guarding humanity is endowed with moral legitimacy, but never asks whether foreign races also have equal right to "guard their own race"
- **Qualification monopoly**: humanity explains the highest Great Dao as "humanity's legacy," equivalent to distributing cosmos-tier evolution rights by race

Human centeredness also has a narrative convenience: it lets readers naturally identify with the protagonist, lowering creative cost. The author need not bother explaining "why this race deserves sympathy," because readers default the human protagonist is just. But this convenience has a price — it deprives the author of the chance to practice "understanding the world from the other's perspective." Shrouding-the-Heavens' cosmology expands larger and larger, but never walks out of the human perspective; this is precisely the convenience's price. Heng Yuan Zhou requiring each race to have a complete path, each perspective an independent narrative, is not for political correctness, but to make the world-view truly cosmos-scale — a cosmos with only human perspective, however large, is still one village.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Why humanity is "naturally" protagonist, the world-view gives no strong explanation, entirely by narrative inertia
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Foreign-race forces' strength fluctuates with plot need
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: medium**. Ye Fan's protagonist status rests on the premise of "humanity protagonist"

Human centeredness also has a logical hole: in Shrouding-the-Heavens cosmos, on what grounds is humanity protagonist? The world-view's answer is "humanity adapts well" or "humanity produced Ye Fan." But this is precisely circular reasoning — because humanity produced Ye Fan, humanity is important; because humanity is important, Ye Fan's story is worth telling. Heng Yuan Zhou does not accept this circularity. It requires each race's importance not to depend on "whether it produced a protagonist," but on that race's inherent qualification as a civilization member. Qualification precedes achievement.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: foreign-race talent wasted, knowledge flow closed
- **Anti-predation**: distributing evolution qualification by race is cosmos-tier discrimination
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo world internally only humanity, but does not explicitly discriminate against outer races; Shrouding writes human centeredness into narrative skeleton
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: this entry directly targets the Myriad-Races axiom — humanity enjoys no evolution privilege
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: race-centered cosmos cannot face true plural civilizations

Human centeredness also produces a moral superiority: humanity naturally stands on the just side, the demon race naturally on the dangerous side. This binary division makes Shrouding-the-Heavens world's moral judgment extremely simple — whoever attacks humanity is villain; the demon attacking humans is evil. But in a truly complex moral world, good and evil do not depend on race, but on action. Heng Yuan Zhou judges good and evil by action, not by race; this is civilization progress from "racial morality" to "behavioral morality."
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Human centeredness is not Shrouding-the-Heavens' "perspective choice," but proof it cannot imagine a "non-human protagonist." Heng Yuan Zhou's proposition: Great Dao does not divide races, qualification does not ask birth.

Human centeredness is not Shrouding-the-Heavens' perspective choice, but the boundary of its imagination. Heng Yuan Zhou requires the author to walk out this boundary, not for political correctness, but to make the world-view truly deserve the word "cosmos."
