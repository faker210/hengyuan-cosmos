## Introduction: Women may cultivate, but can women decide their own Dao

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races expressly states "human men and women" all have a road. This entry examines female cultivators' situation in Shrouding-the-Heavens world: they are many in number, many geniuses, but long-term occupy positions of "contested, guarded, sacrificed," lacking independent Dao legacies, independent political status, independent narrative arcs. The law-first axiom reminds: gender injustice is not detail, but structure. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Female geniuses marginalized energy waste:

- Female cultivators even with outstanding aptitude narratively often arranged as male lead's beauties, Dao companions, sacrifices
- Female strong ones' independent Dao legacies are rare; their wisdom and experience mostly attach to male strong ones' lineages
- "Women hindered by emotion" stereotyping attributes massive female geniuses' bottlenecks to emotion, not resources

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Gender injustice's regression consequences:

1. **Talent waste**: half the population's potential not institutionally developed, civilization energy naturally halved
2. **Narration monotone**: female characters instrumentalized, the world becomes flat
3. **Generational replication**: female strong ones also raise next-generation women by old rules; injustice self-replicates in structure

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Ye Fan's relationship with female characters is the gender version of predatory thinking:

- **Female serves plot**: female characters' joys and sorrows mostly revolve around Ye Fan or his peers
- **"Guarding" subtext**: protecting women behind one's back defaults that they cannot stand shoulder to shoulder with men to govern
- **Dao legacies transmitted by men**: core lineages still take male trusted line as axis; women are mostly "attached inheritors"

Ye Fan personally may not intend it, but the structure he is embedded in arranges women's Dao positions beside the male lead.

Female cultivators' situation also has a trap often packaged as "romantic": strong women sacrificing for the male lead is rendered as deep love, not tragedy. In this narrative, the stronger the woman, the more moving her sacrifice — but this precisely takes her strength as the sacrifice's weight, not as independent value. Several female strong ones' exits in Shrouding-the-Heavens are essentially all this "trading strength for emotion" structure. Heng Yuan Zhou requires female cultivators to have their own Dao, their own goals, their own endpoints — she may walk with the male lead, but her existence is not to set off the male lead, let alone to block a blade for the male lead at critical moments.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Female strong ones' strength varies strong and weak, entirely by plot need whether she "helps the male lead" or "holds the male lead back"
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Female characters' fate adjusts by author mood
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: medium**. Female characters mostly revolve around Ye Fan as center

Female cultivators' situation is also deeply tied to "emotional plot." In Shrouding-the-Heavens world female characters' ability is often tied to their emotional relationship with the protagonist — women who like the protagonist are stronger, get more screen time; women unrelated to the protagonist are marginalized. This conflates female cultivators' value with their love life, demeaning their cultivator identity. Heng Yuan Zhou requires female cultivators' value to be determined by their cultivation achievement and civilization contribution, not by which male lead they relate to.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: half the population's potential not institutionally developed
- **Anti-predation**: arranging women's Dao position as attached is evolution-rights predation at gender level
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo world female characters equally marginalized; both fail on gender fairness
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the Myriad-Races axiom expressly includes "human men and women"; this entry implements this clause
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: gender-unjust cosmos cannot claim to face all beings

Female cultivators' situation is also tied to "Dao-companion economy": a female strong one's value in narrative is often amplified through her emotional relationship with the male lead. This structure absorbs female cultivators' independent achievement into the emotional plot. Heng Yuan Zhou requires female cultivators to have independent narrative arcs — her growth revolves around no one's emotion, her endpoint does not depend on who she walks with.
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Shrouding-the-Heavens world's women can cultivate to very high realms, but "very high" defined by whom, "how far" arranged by whom, is never decided by themselves. True feminism is not letting women also be able to beat the male lead, but letting women not have to fight around the male lead at all.

Female cultivators' situation ultimately tests whether this world truly believes "all are equal before cultivation." Shrouding-the-Heavens verbally believes, structurally does not — this is precisely what it most needs reforming.
