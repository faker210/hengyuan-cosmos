# Entry 304 | Shrouding-the-Heavens Decomposition | Youth Supreme Mechanism: Genius's Solitary-Dao Fate

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Shrouding-the-Heavens System (Entry 17) | Benchmark base: Documents 08, 23, 17

## Introduction: Genius is not blessed, but pushed onto a single-log bridge

Among the six top-level axioms, the collective ascension axiom requires strong ones to feed back to Myriad Ranks; the republican axiom of Myriad Races opposes geniuses being institutionally isolated. This entry dissects Shrouding-the-Heavens world's "Youth Supreme" mechanism — each generation's top geniuses are endowed with cross-tier-growth resources and pressure, but most end up lonely, dying young, or making wedding clothes for others. The law-first axiom reminds: genius solitary Dao is not tragic aesthetics, but a product of institutional failure. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Youth Supreme mechanism's energy waste:

- Each generation only nourishes a few "seed" geniuses; the rest of the same generation become backdrop
- Seed geniuses die young en masse in brutal competition; early investment zeroes out with death
- Surviving geniuses are pushed into a zero-sum "only one can immortalize" situation, mutually regarding each other as enemy

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Youth Supreme mechanism's regression consequences:

1. **Single-generation cultivation**: civilization bets on a few geniuses of one generation; losing the bet = generational gap
2. **Genius mutual slaughter**: to contend for the sole quota, same-generation geniuses slaughter each other; civilization internal energy zeroes
3. **Solitary-Dao cycle**: each generation repeats "select seed — mutual slaughter — survivor — nirvana" cycle, no institutional accumulation

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Ye Fan himself is a survivor of the Youth Supreme mechanism; his predatory thinking manifests:

- **Default "Supreme only one"**: under world-view setting, Ye Fan accepts the premise that "same-generation supremes must contend"
- **Stepping on same-generation peers to rise**: Ye Fan's growth path is essentially winning out over same-generation supremes
- **Survivorship bias**: Ye Fan's success is narratively rendered as "deserved," masking other same-generation geniuses' needless sacrifice

Ye Fan is not a villain, but his "luck" rests on a whole generation of geniuses being systematically wasted.

The Youth Supreme mechanism also has a hidden psychological cost: it makes geniuses unable to be true friends. Because everyone knows only one of the same generation can reach the end, all interactions secretly carry competition; all kindness may be read as exploitation. Ye Fan's relationship with same-generation prodigies is contaminated by zero-sum presupposition from the start — he cannot have pure peer camaraderie, because anyone close may become an opponent on the future immortalization road. Heng Yuan Zhou's 20% Activation Strategy broadly cultivates the backbone, essentially to let geniuses be friends — no need to worry same-generation peers will become stumbling blocks on one's immortalization road, because the road is wide enough to hold everyone.

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium**. Youth Supreme mechanism early self-consistent; late to give Ye Fan more opponents, "same-generation supremes" count inflated
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Same-generation geniuses' strength adjusts by plot need
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: medium**. Ye Fan as survivor, his status rests on same-generation peers being eliminated

The Youth Supreme mechanism also has an intergenerational transmission problem: each generation's supremes grow up in brutal competition, so their way of treating next-generation geniuses is necessarily brutal — because this is how they were cultivated. Brutality self-replicates across generations. Heng Yuan Zhou's 20% Activation Strategy matters not only to cultivate a few more strong ones, but more to change the generational culture that "geniuses must slaughter each other to grow." The new generation grows up cooperating; when they raise the next generation they will no longer use slaughter.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: seeds dying young, same-generation mutual slaughter, backdrop-ification
- **Anti-predation**: betting civilization resources on one seed is predation on others' opportunity
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least wanted the poor universally to have roads; Shrouding only raises even one genius
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: under the Youth Supreme mechanism, Myriad-Rank geniuses are scarce even in "being a seed" opportunity
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: civilization betting on a single hero cannot support cross-multiverse stability
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

The Youth Supreme's tragedy precisely exposes this world's incompetence — it cannot raise a group of strong ones, only bet on one. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: do not want one genius, want generation after generation of capable fighters.

The Youth Supreme's solitary-Dao fate is the inevitable result of the "only raise one genius" logic. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is simple and crude: raise a hundred, let them cooperate with each other, not slaughter each other.

In the final analysis, Shrouding-the-Heavens · Youth Supreme mechanism described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
