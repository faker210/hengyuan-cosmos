# Entry 306 | Shrouding-the-Heavens Decomposition | Ye Fan vs. Tang Luo: Oligarchic vs. Bestowed Comparison

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Shrouding-the-Heavens System (Entry 19) | Benchmark base: Documents 30, 04, 08

## Introduction: One does not universal-benefit, one only universal-benefits — neither reaches Myriad Ranks

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the universal-benefit object to be Myriad Ranks, not one race; on the five-tier protagonist-uniqueness-dependence scale, Tang Luo = high / bestowed type, Ye Fan = medium / oligarchic type. This entry contrasts Tang Luo and Ye Fan to locate the respective limitations of "two good-intention protagonists." The law-first axiom reminds: neither is a villain, but neither touches the institutional bedrock. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Two civilizations' energy-waste forms:

- **Tang Luo (bestowed)**: energy goes to "universal-benefiting humanity's mid-low tiers," well-intentioned but capped at Fierce Realm; high-tier Great Dao still private
- **Ye Fan (oligarchic)**: energy goes to "suppressing the cosmos + cultivating trusted line," not even systematic universal-benefit done

Comparison conclusion: Tang Luo's goodwill coverage > Ye Fan's, but Ye Fan's martial energy > Tang Luo's — one warmer, one fiercer, but neither builds institutions.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Two regression mechanisms:

1. **Tang Luo**: present = academy opens, gone = academy collapses; single-point dependence
2. **Ye Fan**: emperor present = world at peace, emperor gone = turmoil erupts; oligarchic dependence
3. Both share: both tie order to person / small circle, neither legislates

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

| Dimension | Tang Luo (bestowed) | Ye Fan (oligarchic) |
|-----------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Goodwill | Sincerely universal-benefits humanity | Sincerely guards the mortal realm |
| Knowledge openness | Mid-low open, high private | Almost no systematic opening |
| Racial scope | Humanity only | Human-centered, demon race marginal |
| Institutional construction | Academy prototype built | No institution built |
| Predation nature | Gentle bestowal | Cold oligarchy |

Tang Luo is "warmer" than Ye Fan, but his warmth is also top-down bestowal; Ye Fan is "colder" than Tang Luo, but he at least bears cosmos-tier suppression.

Tang Luo and Ye Fan's comparison can go one layer deeper: Tang Luo's goodwill is "free," Ye Fan's order is "expensive." Tang Luo universal-benefits humanity, cost borne mainly by Tang Luo personally; Ye Fan suppresses the cosmos, cost borne by the entire cosmos' resources and Myriad Ranks' freedom. In other words, Tang Luo's goodwill is downward-distributed welfare; Ye Fan's order is upward-collected protection fee — Myriad Ranks hand over freedom in exchange for Ye Fan covering them. Heng Yuan Zhou wants neither welfare-style bestowal nor protection-fee-style order; it wants Myriad Ranks to govern themselves, costs and gains shared by Myriad Ranks.

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Dimension | Tang Luo Bestowed | Ye Fan Oligarchic | Heng Yuan Zhou Network |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|------------------------|
| Dependence | High | Medium | Extremely low |
| Universal benefit | Humanity's mid-low | Almost none | Myriad Ranks 99% |
| Institution | Academy prototype | None | Charter + Council + Judiciary |
| Great Dao | High private | Hidden | All tiers public |
| Exit | Immediate regression | Immediate turmoil | Unaffected |

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency**: Tang Luo medium-high; Ye Fan medium-low
- **Retcon-ending risk**: Tang Luo medium; Ye Fan high
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: Tang Luo = high; Ye Fan = medium

Tang Luo and Ye Fan's comparison can also land on "reader expectation." Tang Luo's readers expect "who he universal-benefited this time"; Ye Fan's readers expect "who he beat this time." Behind these two expectations are two civilizational-imagination differences: one imagines strong ones distributing downward, one imagines strong ones campaigning upward. Heng Yuan Zhou's reader expectation should be "what Myriad Ranks co-built this time" — neither bestowing downward nor campaigning upward, but co-building forward. This is one dimension higher than the former two.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Tang Luo wastes on high-tier private, Ye Fan wastes on suppression idle-running
- **Anti-predation**: Tang Luo is gentle predation, Ye Fan is oligarchic predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: this entry is the comparison — Tang Luo warm but narrow, Ye Fan fierce but cold
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: neither covers Myriad Ranks
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: neither can support cross-multiverse stability

Tang Luo and Ye Fan's comparison should also land on "knowledge openness." Tang Luo at least opens mid-low martial way to humanity's poor; Ye Fan almost does no systematic opening. This means Ye Fan's oligarchic civilization is at the knowledge level more closed than Tang Luo's bestowed civilization. Heng Yuan Zhou making 300 systems fully public doubly transcends both closures — walks farther than Tang Luo, and farther than Ye Fan.
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Tang Luo and Ye Fan, one says "I give you," the other says "I watch over you." What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is "you do it yourself." Goodwill cannot replace institutions, and neither can martial force.

Tang Luo vs. Ye Fan is the difference between "I give" and "I watch." What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is neither giving nor watching, but "we together." From "give" to "watch" to "together" is civilization imagination's triple jump.

Tang Luo vs. Ye Fan's comparison finally lands on one sentence: Tang Luo wants the mortal realm to be a bit better, Ye Fan wants the mortal realm to be a bit steadier. But "better" and "steadier" are not what Myriad Ranks themselves want — what Myriad Ranks want is to decide themselves how good the mortal realm should be, how steady it should be. This is the fundamental divide between bestowed, oligarchic, and networked types.
