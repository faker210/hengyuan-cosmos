# Entry 308 | Shrouding-the-Heavens Decomposition | Anchor Symbiosis Alternative: Republicanizing the Emperor Institution

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Shrouding-the-Heavens System (Entry 21) | Benchmark base: Documents 03, 49, 40

## Introduction: Turn "emperor" from a throne into a post

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires power institutionalized, not personalized; the collective ascension axiom requires strong ones to feed back, not dominate. This entry directly gives the alternative: how to republicanize Shrouding-the-Heavens world's "emperor dictatorship" into the Anchor Symbiosis institution. The law-first axiom requires: the alternative must be operable, calculable, not stay at slogans. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Pre-republicanization energy waste, as repeatedly pointed out in the previous 11 entries: emperor lifespan used for closed-doors emperor-contention rather than construction; knowledge buried with strong ones; suppression energy zeroed with personal death. The first step of the alternative is to stop this waste.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

The emperor dictatorship's regression has been argued (see Entry 297). The alternative targets three regression mechanisms:

1. Suppression vacuum → Alaya whole-network supervision
2. Knowledge burial with the dead → cultivation experience enters the Alaya
3. Succession no procedure → Anchor Council election + assessment

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

The predatory structures republicanization must dismantle:

- Change "emperor = rules" to "the emperor is also under law"
- Change "trusted line selects heirs" to "Myriad Ranks public examination + assessment"
- Change "speaking for Heaven" to "Anchor Council collective resolution"

Ye Fan personally may still exist as one of the "first-generation anchors," but his power must accept Symbiotic Court and Anchor Supervision Committee review.

Republicanization also has an easily overlooked political detail: Ye Fan as first-generation anchor, his "retirement" must be institutionalized. If Ye Fan sits indefinitely in the anchor position, republicanization exists in name only. Heng Yuan Zhou's practice: first-generation anchors contribute most, but still have tenure, assessment, handover procedure. Ye Fan may be the first anchor, but the second must be elected by the Council under four-dimension assessment, not designated by Ye Fan. This clause looks cold, actually is true respect for Ye Fan — asking him down from the altar, making him a flesh-and-blood builder, not an idol forever looked up to.

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

This is the core of this entry, giving the concrete reform path:

| Shrouding Original | Republicanization Reform |
|---------------------|--------------------------|
| Emperor alone dictates | Anchor Council legislation (Myriad-Rank representatives) |
| Emperor no executive separation | Symbiotic Executive Committee executive |
| Emperor = supreme judge | Symbiotic Court judiciary |
| Emperor unmonitored | Anchor Supervision Committee |
| Succession by fighting | Plane Transcendent four-dimension assessment (Force 40 / Heart 25 / Contribution 20 / Responsibility 15) |
| Emperor scriptures privately transmitted | 300 Origin systems fully public |
| Turmoil no early warning | Alaya whole-network supervision + anomaly early warning |

Ye Fan from "cosmos' sole master" becomes "one of the first-generation anchors + the first strong one to pass four-dimension assessment." He is still respected, but no longer deified.

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

1. **Modeling**: replicate pre-reform emperor dictatorship
2. **Variables**: inject the seven reforms above
3. **Observation**: dark-turmoil incidence, Myriad-Rank participation, civilization energy curve
4. **Review**: conclusion — after reform turmoil incidence drops to near zero; Myriad-Rank governance participation rises significantly; civilization energy shifts from oscillation to continuous rise

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Post-reform setting self-consistency**: high — institutions clear, rules precede
- **Retcon-ending risk**: zero — any strong one's rise follows assessment procedure; no "protagonist changes rules anytime"
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: drops from "medium" to "extremely low" — Ye Fan is only one of first-generation anchors; civilization does not depend on him

Republicanization must also handle the "force monopoly" problem. In Shrouding-the-Heavens world, Ye Fan's personal force far exceeds all competitors, meaning any institution Ye Fan does not recognize he can overturn with force. The real difficulty of republicanization is not designing the Council, but making the strongest voluntarily put his own force in the institution's cage. Heng Yuan Zhou's four-dimension assessment's "Heart 25 points, Responsibility 15 points" is designed precisely for this — no matter how strong your force, if Heart and Responsibility do not meet standard, you do not deserve anchor qualification. Institutions must make the strongest willing to be constrained, not coerced by external force.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: emperor lifespan institutionally used for construction; knowledge no longer buried with the dead
- **Anti-predation**: dismantling dictatorial power into four-powers separation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's academy prototype is only the first step; this plan walks complete republicanization
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Council includes Myriad-Rank representatives; demon race, women, poor families all have seats
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the reformed institution can replicate to cross-multiverse civilizations

Republicanization also must handle the "Ye Fan trusted line" placement problem. Followers Ye Fan personally promoted cannot automatically become new oligarchs after republicanization. Heng Yuan Zhou's practice: they may still enter the Council through ability-based assessment, but cannot automatically get a seat by virtue of "having followed Ye Fan." This clause ensures republicanization does not become "oligarchy with a new name."
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

The emperor need not be eliminated, but the emperor must be put in the institution's cage. Ye Fan may be the first anchor, but his grandson cannot automatically be the second — this is the divide between republicanization and hereditary rule.

The first step of republicanization is not abolishing the emperor, but making the emperor accept assessment. Ye Fan need not lose his strength, but he must accept: his strength serves Myriad Ranks, not Myriad Ranks serving his strength.

In the final analysis, Shrouding-the-Heavens · Anchor Symbiosis alternative described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
