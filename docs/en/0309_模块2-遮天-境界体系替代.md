# Entry 309 | Shrouding-the-Heavens Decomposition | Cultivation-Realm Alternative: Stable Nine-Realms-plus-Two Framework

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Shrouding-the-Heavens System (Entry 22) | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 10, 17

## Introduction: Rather than add realms, make every realm count

Among the six top-level axioms, the **law-first axiom** is this entry's core — law precedes, never retcons. The alternative to Shrouding-the-Heavens' dozen-layer realm inflation (Entry 292) is not to design another more complex system, but to replace it with Heng Yuan Zhou's "nine realms + two realms" stable framework. The law-first axiom requires: realm count must be capped, every layer must be calculable. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Realm inflation's narrative energy waste (see Entry 292): readers' predictive power zeroed, explanation cost surged, supporting characters devalued. The nine-plus-two framework's design goal is to eliminate all this waste.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Realm inflation dilutes civilization's historical sense: the count of "emperors" in each era is unclear; ancient history degenerates into vague legend. A stable realm framework requires each layer to have explicit population caps, explicit promotion thresholds; history thereby becomes researchable.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Realm inflation is essentially "protagonist privilege" institutionalized: when the protagonist needs to cross tiers, rules step aside. The key to the nine-plus-two framework is changing "who can break through" from plot preference back to law thresholds — Ye Fan too must walk every realm's statutory steps.

The nine-plus-two framework also has a philosophical advantage: it makes "apex" an intelligible endpoint, not a carrot always dangled ahead. Shrouding's Red-Dust Immortal is vague partly because the author needs to keep writing, so cannot nail down the endpoint. Heng Yuan Zhou's practice: write the Origin Realm clearly, write the Multiverse Realm and the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm clearly as appendices, then open new exploration directions at higher dimensions — not vagueness, but nailing down the known and stating the unknown. Readers know where the protagonist is now, and know what lies ahead, but are not suffocated by "maybe there is still higher."

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Post-reform setting self-consistency**: high — realms capped, law-anchored
- **Retcon-ending risk**: zero — new dimensions only via appendices, do not overturn old settings
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: drops from medium to low — Ye Fan within the framework is still a strong one, but no longer enjoys rule exemption

The nine-plus-two framework also has an engineering advantage: it makes "expansion" addition rather than multiplication. Each added realm in Shrouding must re-explain its relation to all previous realms — multiplicative complexity. After Heng Yuan Zhou's nine realms are capped, new dimensions only join as appendices — additive complexity; appendices do not change any rule of the previous nine, only stack at higher layers. This is why Heng Yuan Zhou can expand infinitely without collapsing, while Shrouding collapses by the tenth layer.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: eliminate explanation cost and readers' predictive-power loss
- **Anti-predation**: cancel the protagonist's rule exemption
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least caps at Fierce Realm without inflation; this plan gives Shrouding an equally stable cap
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: each of the nine realms includes Myriad-Ranks differentiated paths, no racial limits
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: stable framework is the precondition for cross-multiverse expansion

The nine-plus-two framework should also be paired with explicit "per-realm population cap." Shrouding late-stage Sages and Sage Kings appearing in batches is precisely because no population constraint. Heng Yuan Zhou through the Anchor Network and resource-distribution mechanism makes each realm's strong-one count match civilization resources, avoiding inflation. Clear realms + population caps; both are indispensable.
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Cultivation realms are not more grand by being more numerous. Nine layers capped, every layer counts, beats a dozen rubber stamps. Shrouding's problem was never too few layers, but too many layers with none of them counting.

Capping at nine realms is not small-mindedness, but daring to let rules decide. Shrouding's lesson proves: many layers ≠ big vision; clear layers = stable vision.

The realm-system alternative is not to tear down Shrouding's realms and start over, but on the basis of preserving its good layering (Sea of Wheels, Dao Palace subdivisions), cut the late-stage inflation and replace it with Heng Yuan Zhou's nine-realm cap. Take the essence, remove the inflation; this is the rational alternative under the law-first axiom.
