What Myriad Ranks want is not a lifetime pulse, but ten thousand years of compound interest.

## Conclusion

Institutional-alternative benefit is not "about the same," but an order-of-magnitude gap. Ye Fan alone can protect one lifetime of peace; one set of institutions can protect ten thousand generations of Myriad Ranks — this is solitary Dao vs. symbiosis's settlement.

Institutional-alternative benefit calculation should also subtract "cultural transition cost" — Myriad Ranks from "wait for Ye Fan to arrange" to "arrange themselves" requires generations to learn self-governance. But Quest Sediment Domain simulation shows this cost is far below the cost of maintaining dark turmoil. Short-term labor pain for long-term peace is civilization history's most cost-effective trade.

Ultimately, institutional alternative is not a negation of Ye Fan, but responsibility to Myriad Ranks. Ye Fan has done well enough; Myriad Ranks deserve a future that does not depend on Ye Fan. This is the ultimate lesson Heng Yuan Zhou extracts from the Shrouding-the-Heavens system.
