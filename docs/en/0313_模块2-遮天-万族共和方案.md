# Entry 313 | Shrouding-the-Heavens Decomposition | Myriad Races Republican Plan: Breaking Human Centeredness

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Shrouding-the-Heavens System (Entry 26) | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 50, 77

## Introduction: Return the cosmos to Myriad Ranks

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races is this entry's core — all beings have equal evolution qualification, Myriad Ranks 99% full coverage. This entry targets Shrouding-the-Heavens' human centeredness (Entry 302) and the demon race's marginalized situation (Entry 301), giving the concrete Myriad-Races republicanization plan. The law-first axiom requires: republicanization is not a slogan, but redistribution of seats, paths, resources. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Wastes caused by human centeredness (demon-race talent wasted, knowledge flow closed, racial-war internal friction) all released after Myriad-Races republicanization: demon-race physique talents integrated into civilization construction, Myriad-Rank knowledge complements each other, racial war disappears.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Racial inequality's three regression mechanisms (qualification discrimination, boundary closure, diversity lost) are respectively handled after republicanization:

1. Qualification discrimination → Myriad Ranks equal constitutionalized
2. Boundary closure → Myriad-Rank knowledge whole-network shared
3. Diversity lost → Myriad Ranks five genealogies each with complete path

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Ye Fan's "for our humanity" justice narrative is re-examined from the Myriad-Races republican perspective: guarding humanity is legitimate, but placing humanity's interests above Myriad Ranks is predation at the racial level. Republicanization requires Ye Fan to shift from "humanity's guardian" to "one member of the Myriad Ranks Council."

Myriad Races republicanism also has a practical difficulty: different races' evolution paces differ enormously. The demon race may take a millennium to human-shape-shift; humanity builds foundation in decades; plant spirits perceive the world on century scales. If deliberated at the same pace, young races never get a word in. Heng Yuan Zhou's practice is to design different deliberation cycles and expression channels for different races — not all vote simultaneously, but each race participates at its own pace, ultimately reaching cross-pace consensus in the Anchor Council. This is far more complex than Shrouding-the-Heavens' "humanity decides, demon race listens" structure, but it truly realizes Myriad Races republicanism.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Post-reform self-consistency**: high
- **Retcon-ending risk**: zero
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: drops from medium to low — Ye Fan no longer monopolizes cosmic narrative as "humanity's representative"

Myriad Races republicanism also has a cultural-fusion problem: different races' values differ enormously; how does the Council reach consensus? Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is not to force unified values, but on the "six top-level axioms" minimum consensus, allow each race to retain its cultural details. Myriad Races republicanism ≠ Myriad Ranks homogenization, but plural coexistence on a minimum consensus. Shrouding-the-Heavens world has not even reached the minimum consensus (humanity-demon equality), let alone fusion at higher levels.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: release demon-race talent, eliminate racial war
- **Anti-predation**: extend evolution qualification from humanity alone to Myriad Ranks
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo universal-benefits only within humanity; this plan directly breaks racial boundaries
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: this entry is the Myriad-Races axiom's engineering realization
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Myriad Races republicanism is the precondition for cross-multiverse civilization intercourse

Myriad Races republicanism also needs to handle "demon-race shape-shift right." In Shrouding-the-Heavens, a demon is recognized as an intelligent race only after human-shape-shifting; Heng Yuan Zhou requires the demon race to retain its own form and still enjoy full citizenship; shape-shifting is the demon race's free choice, not the threshold to enter civilization. This is the shift from "assimilation-style equality" to "ontological equality."
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Shrouding-the-Heavens world's "cosmos" is actually only "humanity's cosmos." A true cosmos must invite the demon race, foreign races, all sentient beings back to the table. Ye Fan may sit, but he cannot monopolize the seat.

Myriad Races republicanism is not pulling the demon race up to humanity's position, but canceling "position high/low" itself. The demon race's problem in Shrouding-the-Heavens is not that its position is not high enough, but that there is no such thing as equal position at all.

Myriad Races republicanism also requires "demon-race cultivation systems" and "humanity cultivation systems" to exist in parallel, not humanity's system transforming the demon race. In Shrouding-the-Heavens world, demon-race cultivation is mostly a variant of humanity's system; Heng Yuan Zhou retains for the demon race an independent path based on its physique traits, so the demon race need not human-shape-shift to reach the endpoint.

Myriad Races republicanism does not want to eliminate racial differences, but to make differences no longer a reason for privilege or discrimination. Every race has its own beauty; civilization is a plural ensemble, not a single unison.

In the final analysis, Shrouding-the-Heavens · Myriad Races republican plan described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
