## Introduction: Why Heng Yuan Zhou dares to stop after writing nine realms

Among the six top-level axioms, the **law-first axiom** is this entry's core — law precedes, never retcons, never ends in dissolution; setting self-consistency overrides plot convenience; plot may not kidnap setting. This entry directly answers: what institutional design does Heng Yuan Zhou use to root out Shrouding-style realm inflation. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Realm inflation's essence is "law giving way." Heng Yuan Zhou's comparative design converts every impulse to "add a realm" into process cost, suppressing inflation at the source.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Shrouding-the-Heavens' regression mechanism includes "rules drift with plot." Heng Yuan Zhou precludes drift through three institutional designs:

1. **Law anchoring**: each realm's breakthrough has statutory thresholds, no reliance on sudden enlightenment or lucky picks
2. **Appendix increments**: new dimensions (Multiverse Realm, Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm) may only join as appendices, not overturn old settings
3. **Version maintenance**: document versions and maintenance notes unified, any modification traceable

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Behind realm inflation is the predatory thinking of "when the protagonist needs to cross tiers, change the rules." Heng Yuan Zhou cancels this predation through "law-anchored authority universal to all beings" — even if Ye Fan arrives, he must walk the nine realms' statutory steps.

Law-first also has a benefit: it lets the author dare to end. Shrouding-the-Heavens' realm inflation and trilogy retconning partly stem from the author not daring to stop — stopping means cannot keep selling. The law-first axiom makes ending a designable node: when nine realms are filled, they are filled; when Origin Realm is reached, it is reached; no need to add layers to extend life. Heng Yuan Zhou's document system (0–99 foundation, 100–250 realms, 251–500 benchmarking...) is itself a product of law-first — each numbered segment has clear boundaries; when written, it is written, no layer-adding to extend life.
