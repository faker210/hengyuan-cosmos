## Introduction: By the trilogy's third book, settings are no longer settings, but playdough

Among the six top-level axioms, the **law-first axiom** requires never to retcon. This entry takes *Sacred Ruins* (protagonist Chu Feng) as the vantage to examine the Shrouding trilogy's late-stage retcon and dissolution — when the third book must "continue writing" the first two books' characters, realms, timelines, the speed of overturning early settings has run out of control. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Late-trilogy energy waste:

- To make way for Chu Feng, Ye Fan and Shi Hao's combat power is repeatedly "adjusted"; the epic feel accumulated in early stages depreciates
- The trilogy's shared world-view gets vaguer the more it is written; readers give up understanding, only watch fights
- Large numbers of early foreshadowing cannot be recovered in the third book, becoming floating "setting ghosts"

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Late-trilogy regression:

1. **Combat-power inflation out of control**: Ye Fan, Shi Hao, Chu Feng who is stronger depends entirely on momentary emotional need
2. **History thoroughly relativized**: the trilogy's "ancient history" is repeatedly rewritten; readers can no longer know which version counts
3. **Ending open to the point of no ending**: to leave sequel space, key questions left undecided

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Late-trilogy predatory thinking upgrades to "cross-work predation":

- Used Ye Fan and Shi Hao as Chu Feng's "senior NPCs"; their entire epic value compressed into carrying Chu Feng's sedan chair
- Readers' emotional investment in the first two books repeatedly consumed in the third
- Setting modifiability pushed to the extreme — as long as Chu Feng needs it, any iron law of the first two books can be changed

Late-trilogy retcon also has a structural reason: the third book must simultaneously serve two batches of readers — old readers following the first two, and new readers of the third. To let new readers in, understanding threshold must be lowered; to move old readers, frequent tributes to the earlier works are required. The result of pleasing both ends: the world-view is simplified to "everyone is friends, together beat monsters," complex settings sacrificed. This is serialized fiction's structural dilemma. Heng Yuan Zhou writes from the start as an independent document system; each piece self-consistent, each piece independently readable, while maintaining holistic consistency through numbering and relations — no need to please readers through vagueness.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency**: extremely low
- **Retcon-ending risk**: extreme
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: Ye Fan = medium; trilogy as a whole depends on three protagonists taking turns carrying the show, structure more fragile

Late trilogy also has a "protagonist inflation" problem. Ye Fan, Shi Hao, Chu Feng three protagonists must all be shaped as ceiling-tier strong ones, so three's combat power is forced to the same level. But three protagonists grew up in different periods, different works; forcibly flattening them necessarily means some work's (often the earlier one's) protagonist is weakened. This is commercial serialized trilogies' structural tragedy. Heng Yuan Zhou does not set a single protagonist, but a group of anchors, each with division of labor and assessment; no need to pull everyone to the same combat-power line.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: early epic feel depreciates, foreshadowing floating, readers give up understanding
- **Anti-predation**: cross-work predation on earlier works' settings and readers' emotion
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo single book self-consistent, no such problem; trilogy crossover drags Shrouding's self-consistency to the lowest
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: late-trilogy supporting characters thoroughly instrumentalized
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: out-of-control trilogy cannot serve as a cross-multiverse baseline

Late trilogy also has a "protagonist retirement" problem. Ye Fan and Shi Hao as earlier works' protagonists, how to place them in Sacred Ruins? Retire them, readers are reluctant; let them remain ceiling, new protagonist Chu Feng cannot establish authority. This is the trilogy structure's death knot. Heng Yuan Zhou does not set a single protagonist, fundamentally avoiding this retirement dilemma — anchors can hand over, new people can take the baton; no need to keep old protagonists perched on the ceiling.
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

The trilogy is not Shrouding's sublimation, but Shrouding's self-consistency funeral. An author willing to let his own early settings make way for a new protagonist publicly admits: his world's rules were never rules.

Late trilogy also has a "power inflation" problem: when Ye Fan, Shi Hao, Chu Feng are all pulled to the ceiling, the ceiling itself loses meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou does not set a single ceiling, but tiered assessment; each anchor takes responsibility at his own tier, no need to pull everyone to the same height.
