## Introduction: An unspeakable highest realm equals no highest realm

Among the six top-level axioms, the **law-first axiom** requires setting self-consistency over plot convenience. This entry dissects Shrouding-the-Heavens' ultimate realm "Red-Dust Immortal" — as Ye Fan's final landing point, it should have been the book's clearest setting, but is precisely the vaguest: how strong is a Red-Dust Immortal, can it rise further, what distinguishes it from an emperor — all by plot need. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Red-Dust Immortal's vagueness is essentially predation on the "highest realm" setting asset:

- The author made the ultimate realm a rubber stamp, as big as needed
- Ye Fan as a Red-Dust Immortal, his combat power stretches by plot need, no statutory upper limit
- This vagueness itself is a "protagonist privilege" — the highest need not obey rules

Red-Dust Immortal vagueness also has a narratological consequence: it turns "immortalization" from a goal with a threshold into an adjective. Ye Fan becomes immortal in the Red Dust, but what does a Red-Dust Immortal actually mean? Immortality? Invincibility? Transcending worldly dust? Definition unclear, so "immortalize" becomes another way of saying "very strong." Heng Yuan Zhou's Origin Realm and Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm are clear because every realm has statutory assessment indicators and duty requirements — reaching Origin Realm, you are not only strong, you must bear cosmos-tier responsibility. A realm is an identity, even more a duty; a vague realm has only power, no duty.
