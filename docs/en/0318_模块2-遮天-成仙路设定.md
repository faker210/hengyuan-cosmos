# Entry 318 | Shrouding-the-Heavens Decomposition | Immortalization-Road Setting: Shrouding's Version of Plundering Other Realms' Origin

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Shrouding-the-Heavens System (Entry 31) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 18, 15

## Introduction: The so-called immortalization road is dividing the whole cosmos as resources

Among the six top-level axioms, the **anti-predation axiom** is this entry's core — develop increments, do not consume stock; do not use slaughter-predation as the evolution path. This entry dissects Shrouding-the-Heavens' "immortalization road" setting: the quotas for immortalization are limited; strong ones slaughter across the cosmos for them; essentially "immortalization" is designed as a cross-star-region zero-sum resource predation. The law-first axiom requires: zero-sum cultivation is old Heavenly Dao, not an axiom. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Immortalization road's energy waste:

- Strong ones erupt in cosmos-tier war to fight limited quotas; energy consumed in mutual slaughter
- Immortalization quotas monopolized by a few strong ones; ordinary cultivators excluded from the start
- To fight for quotas, resources across the cosmos are prematurely consumed in war preparation

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Immortalization road's zero-sum design necessarily creates regression:

1. **Quota scarcity law**: immortalization quotas set as limited, equivalent to announcing at cosmic scale "immortalization is a few people's privilege"
2. **Strong ones' melee period**: around immortalization road's opening, strong ones slaughter, civilization energy zeroes
3. **Winner takes all**: the immortalizer takes away all opportunities; non-immortalizers have nothing

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Ye Fan's process through the immortalization road is typical of plundering other realms' Origin:

- **Quota exclusive**: the immortalization quota Ye Fan gets means other same-generation strong ones lose the chance
- **Other-realm resources**: the immortalization road involves cross-star-region, cross-plane resource contest; what Ye Fan's side "takes" is what other planes lose
- **Zero-sum world-view**: the world-view has no "expand immortalization quotas" increment thinking, defaults the cake is that big

Immortalization-road zero-sum also has a deep psychological mechanism: it unifies Myriad Ranks' hope on "grabbing quotas," so Myriad Ranks stop thinking "why are quotas limited." When everyone is slaughtering over limited quotas, no one has time to ask "can quotas become more" — this is precisely zero-sum design's greatest success: it makes the oppressed slaughter each other, thereby not questioning the oppression itself. Heng Yuan Zhou developing increment Origin essentially breaks this psychological mechanism: let Myriad Ranks see the cake can grow, so they stop fighting each other. This is civilization awakening from "grabbing the cake" to "baking the cake."

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency**: medium-low — quota count fluctuates with plot
- **Retcon-ending risk**: medium-high — quota count serves whether Ye Fan can smoothly immortalize
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: medium — Ye Fan's immortalization depends on him winning the zero-sum contest

Immortalization-road zero-sum also creates a "fate narrative": strong ones fight for immortality as if it is Heavenly Dao's destined cycle. But Heng Yuan Zhou's causal self-discipline axiom requires asking: who designed this cycle? Why are quotas limited? When everyone takes zero-sum as Heaven's will, no one asks whether Heaven's will can be modified. Ye Fan accepted the immortalization road's zero-sum rules, so all his effort is within the "grabbing" frame. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is to step out of the "grabbing" frame and ask: why can't everyone immortalize?

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: strong-one wars, war-preparation consumption, winner takes all
- **Anti-predation**: zero-sum quotas + plundering other realms' Origin, is the anti-predation axiom's direct opposite
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least does not do cross-star-region plunder; Shrouding pushes plunder to cosmic scale
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: quotas monopolized by human top oligarchs, Myriad Ranks excluded
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: zero-sum predation logic cannot support cross-multiverse symbiosis

Immortalization-road zero-sum is also deeply tied to "lifespan anxiety." In Shrouding-the-Heavens world strong ones have limited lifespan, so the immortalization road becomes the only lifespan-extending hope — this gives zero-sum contest survival anxiety. Heng Yuan Zhou through life-circulation-system public fundamentally releases lifespan anxiety; when lifespan is no longer a problem, immortalization road's zero-sum contest loses its motive.
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

The immortalization road is not Shrouding-the-Heavens' "grand epic," but proof it cannot imagine "immortalization need not be grabbed." Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is simple: make the cake bigger, not grab the cake.

Immortalization-road zero-sum's cleverest part is keeping Myriad Ranks busy fighting each other, so they do not ask why quotas are limited. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: make quotas more, make the cake bigger.

Immortalization-road zero-sum also turns "immortalization" from a natural evolution into a political transaction — who is qualified to be on the road is distributed by strong ones at the negotiating table. Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad Races republicanism requires: whether there is a road, who can be on it, is decided by Myriad-Rank consensus, not strong ones splitting the spoils.
