# Entry 319 | Shrouding-the-Heavens Decomposition | Cosmic Battlefield: Zero-Sum Game of Civilizational Resources

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Shrouding-the-Heavens System (Entry 32) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 15, 18

## Introduction: When the cosmos itself is designed as one big battlefield

Among the six top-level axioms, the anti-predation axiom opposes zero-sum predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires Myriad Ranks to symbiose, not mutually annihilate. This entry dissects Shrouding-the-Heavens world's "cosmic battlefield" setting — star regions are designed as mutually resources, mutually battlefields in a zero-sum pattern; between civilizations only life-and-death. The law-first axiom requires: treating the cosmos as a casino is not an axiom, but old Heavenly Dao. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Cosmic battlefield's energy waste:

- Wars between star regions consume cosmos-tier energy; energy civilization could have used for construction all goes to mutual destruction
- Defeated star regions' cultivators, knowledge, resources taken in by winners; the defeated side's civilization accumulation zeroed
- Long-term war makes all civilizations place strongest ones in military rather than construction posts

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Zero-sum battlefield's regression:

1. **Civilization = enemy state**: star regions default hostile; cooperation impossible
2. **Spoils logic**: one civilization's strength necessarily at another's decline
3. **Arms race**: all civilizations forced to prioritize military development; livelihood and knowledge investment compressed

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Ye Fan's role in the cosmic battlefield is the amplified version of predatory thinking:

- **Cross-star-region plunder**: resources Ye Fan's side "takes" on the cosmic battlefield all come from defeated star regions
- **Inter-civilizational zero-sum**: Ye Fan faction's strength rests on other star regions' failure
- **War = justice**: guarding humanity's star region is endowed with moral legitimacy, but never reflects on "why must we fight"

The cosmic battlefield also has an ecological consequence: it makes long-term trust between star regions impossible. Today's ally may be tomorrow's enemy, because resources are limited and war can restart anytime. Under this structure, any cross-star-region knowledge cooperation is impossible — you dare not teach cultivation methods to a star region that may come attack you tomorrow. So cosmic civilization as a whole is in a state of "each groping, no exchange"; wisdom that could have been shared is locked in closed star regions. Heng Yuan Zhou's cross-plane anchor connection precisely breaks this distrust — when star regions are not battlefields but partners, knowledge can flow at cosmic scale.

- **Setting self-consistency**: medium-low — star-region strength adjusts by plot need
- **Retcon-ending risk**: medium — battlefield scale serves Ye Fan's fight scenes
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: medium — Ye Fan faction wins the zero-sum battlefield

The cosmic battlefield also makes "diplomacy" impossible. Under zero-sum pattern, between star regions there are only two relationships: enemy state and conquered enemy state. No neutrality, no cooperation, no trade. Under this structure, cosmic civilization's total wisdom is forever dispersed across hostile star regions, unable to converge. Heng Yuan Zhou's cross-plane anchor connection precisely changes the cosmos from battlefield to network — star regions exchange knowledge, coordinate resources through anchor connections, not through war to fight over resources.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

- **Anti-energy waste**: war internal friction, civilization zeroing, arms race
- **Anti-predation**: cross-star-region plunder is cosmos-tier predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo war limited to internal humanity; Shrouding upgrades war to cosmic scale

- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: zero-sum battlefield cannot symbiose with cross-multiverse civilizations

The cosmic battlefield also makes "pacifism" impossible. Under zero-sum pattern, the side advocating peace is eliminated by the war side, so pacifists cannot survive. Heng Yuan Zhou's cross-plane anchor connection precisely gives pacifists living space — not by personal goodwill, but by institutional protection.
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Designing the cosmos as a battlefield is equivalent to announcing at cosmic scale "cooperation is impossible." Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: the cosmos is not a battlefield, but a work site — all civilizations co-build on this site, not mutually demolish.

The cosmic battlefield also makes "civilizational exchange" taboo. Star regions dare not exchange goods and knowledge, because knowledge may be used by the enemy. This closedness makes each star region reinvents the wheel; civilization as a whole advances slowly. Heng Yuan Zhou's cross-plane connection precisely makes knowledge flow replace resource contest.
