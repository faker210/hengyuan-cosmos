# Entry 320 | Shrouding-the-Heavens Decomposition | Critique of Individual Immortality: The Essence of What Ye Fan Pursues

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Shrouding-the-Heavens System (Entry 33) | Benchmark base: Documents 26, 02, 05

## Introduction: One person living forever, can Myriad Ranks stop progressing

Among the six top-level axioms, the collective ascension axiom requires strong ones not to ascend alone; the republican axiom of Myriad Races opposes individuals dominating Myriad Ranks. This entry directly critiques Ye Fan's ultimate pursuit — immortalizing in the Red Dust, individual immortality. This is not condemning Ye Fan for craving life, but asking: taking "one person living forever" as civilization's ultimate goal is itself the endpoint of solitary-Dao narrative. The law-first axiom requires: individual immortality cannot replace collective ascension. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Individual immortality's energy waste:

- One person living eons, his personal wisdom if not institutionalized, zeroes after eons
- If the immortal does not actively feed back, his existence itself is implicit suppression of "Myriad Ranks ascending on their own"
- To maintain immortality, the individual must continuously consume resources, forming an eternal resource black hole

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How individual immortality creates regression:

1. **Established interests solidified**: the immortal does not exit, latecomers have no seat — top solidification
2. **Myriad-Rank subjectivity atrophied**: anyway someone is forever at the top, Myriad Ranks lose self-advocacy
3. **History loses metabolism**: no individual exit, institutions lose update pressure

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Ye Fan pursuing individual immortality is predatory thinking's ultimate form:

- **Setting the evolution endpoint as "I do not die"**: rather than "Myriad Ranks evolve together"
- **Eternally occupying the top position**: even if Ye Fan does not intend to monopolize, "he is forever at the top" itself squeezes latecomers
- **Taking personal eternity as civilization goal**: civilization's hope is simplified to "Ye Fan keeps living"

Individual immortality also has an overlooked paradox: the immortal increasingly does not want to change. Because any change may bring risk, and the immortal already has everything, his most rational choice is to maintain the status quo. So individual immortality's endpoint is not progress, but stagnation — a strong one who lives forever ultimately becomes the old order's staunchest defender, because the old order is his order. Heng Yuan Zhou's strong-one post-duty system requires strong ones to hand over the baton, requires new people to rise; essentially institutionally countering this "immortal conservatization" natural trend. Civilization needs metabolism, even if this means even the strongest must exit.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Individual immortality setting self-consistency**: medium-low — how the immortal continues not bored, not monopolistic, the world-view does not answer
- **Retcon-ending risk**: medium — the immortal's behavior follows plot need
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: medium — Ye Fan's immortality = civilization endpoint

Individual immortality also conflicts with the causal self-discipline axiom: one person living too long, his behavior accumulates into a huge karma debt, yet no one can settle with him. If Ye Fan is immortal, countless decisions he makes over ten thousand years, who reviews their consequences? Heng Yuan Zhou's strong-one post-duty system requires not only handing over the baton, but also every decision the strong one makes in office accepting Alaya recording and Symbiotic Court review. The immortal cannot be exempt from karma — this is Heng Yuan Zhou's most fundamental limit on "individual immortality."

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: personal wisdom not institutionalized, resource black hole
- **Anti-predation**: setting the evolution endpoint as personal non-death
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo does not pursue individual immortality; Ye Fan's immortality pushes solitary Dao to its endpoint
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: individual immortality and Myriad-Rank ascension are opposing goals
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: collective ascension can cross multiverse, individual immortality cannot

Individual immortality also conflicts with the republican axiom of Myriad Races: the immortal occupies resources but does not hand over, equivalent to forever locking some among Myriad Ranks at the top. Heng Yuan Zhou's strong-one handover system essentially realizes republicanism in the time dimension — not only distributing power spatially, but also distributing power temporally, letting every generation have a chance to rise.
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

One person living forever is not civilization's glory, but civilization's stagnation. True immortality is not Ye Fan not dying, but Myriad Ranks' civilization perpetuating. Heng Yuan Zhou does not want a hero who never dies, but a civilization that never stops.

Individual immortality also makes "death" lose meaning. When strong ones need not die, their awe for life, their cherishing of finiteness, are both diluted. Heng Yuan Zhou does not oppose long life, but it requires the long-lived to bear duties matching their longevity — the longer they live, the more they must leave roads for later generations, not occupy roads.

In the final analysis, Shrouding-the-Heavens · individual immortality critique described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
