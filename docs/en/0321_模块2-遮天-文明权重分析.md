# Entry 321 | Shrouding-the-Heavens Decomposition | Civilization-Weight Analysis: Ye Fan's Personal Weight Crushes Civilizational Consensus

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Full Decomposition of the Shrouding-the-Heavens System (Entry 34) | Benchmark base: Documents 26, 49, 03

## Introduction: In Shrouding-the-Heavens cosmos, Ye Fan alone equals the whole civilization

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires governance weight to belong to Myriad-Rank consensus; the collective ascension axiom requires individual weight not to override civilization. This entry uses a quantitative lens to analyze "civilization weight" — in Shrouding-the-Heavens world's major decisions, how much share does Ye Fan's personal will vs. Myriad-Rank consensus each take. The law-first axiom requires: civilization weight must be dispersed, otherwise it is dictatorship. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Weight over-concentration's waste:

- Ye Fan alone bears all civilization-tier decisions; his misjudgment = civilization misjudgment
- Myriad Ranks' wisdom idles due to no decision power; civilization loses plural input
- Ye Fan's personal energy exhausted by infinite decisions, cannot focus on what he should really do

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Weight imbalance's regression:

1. **Decision risk concentrated**: Ye Fan one wrong move, civilization loses the whole board
2. **Myriad Ranks no participation sense**: anyway Ye Fan decides, Myriad Ranks do not care, do not take responsibility
3. **Error-correction mechanism absent**: no one has the right to veto Ye Fan; civilization loses self-correction ability

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Ye Fan's predatory thinking ultimately manifests as weight's absolute concentration:

- **Deciding for Myriad Ranks**: Ye Fan defaults he has the right to decide "how the whole cosmos should be"
- **Myriad Ranks no veto**: Ye Fan's decisions, Myriad Ranks can only accept
- **Consensus does not exist**: Shrouding-the-Heavens world has no council, no vote, no review — only Ye Fan and his circle's will

Civilization weight also has a quantitative reference: in Heng Yuan Zhou, one anchor's weight is allocated through four-dimension assessment (Force 40 / Heart 25 / Contribution 20 / Responsibility 15), and any anchor's weight is checked by the Council and Supervision Committee. This means even the strongest anchor, his will only takes a part of civilization decisions; the rest is jointly decided by Myriad-Rank representatives, professional committees, and legal clauses. Ye Fan's weight in Shrouding approaches 100%; this is not because he is not great enough, but because that world did not design institutions that "let others also have a voice." Weight dispersion does not weaken strong ones, but protects them — so he need not alone bear all decision consequences.

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Shrouding Weight Structure | Heng Yuan Zhou Weight Structure |
|----------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Ye Fan personal weight ≈ 100% | Anchor Council Myriad-Rank representatives share weight |
| No veto design | Symbiotic Court + Anchor Supervision Committee |
| Decisions concentrated in one person | Legislative / executive / judicial / supervisory four powers separated |
| Myriad Ranks no participation | Myriad Ranks 99% full coverage co-governance |
| Error correction by Ye Fan's self-reflection | Institutional error-correction mechanism |

Heng Yuan Zhou requires: any individual's civilization weight must not exceed a certain ratio; the rest must be dispersed across Myriad-Rank consensus.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Self-consistency under weight concentration**: medium-low — why Ye Fan has the right to decide everything, the world-view gives no legal basis
- **Retcon-ending risk**: medium
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: medium — weight concentration is precisely the quantitative definition of "medium dependency / oligarchic"

Civilization weight should also include "narrative-discourse weight," not only "decision weight." In Shrouding-the-Heavens world, Ye Fan not only decides policy, but also decides narrative — what is right, what is just, who is a hero, all defined from Ye Fan's side's perspective. Heng Yuan Zhou requires discourse dispersion: every race, every class has its own narrative channel; the Anchor Council only coordinates, not monopolizes the right to define justice. If Ye Fan enters Heng Yuan Zhou, what he must first hand over is not force, but the right to interpret "what is justice."

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: decision risk concentrated, Myriad-Rank wisdom idle, Ye Fan's energy exhausted
- **Anti-predation**: taking Myriad-Rank consensus rights back to one person
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least disperses power to students through an academy; Ye Fan's weight is more concentrated than Tang Luo's
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: weight concentration = Myriad Ranks have no share
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: weight-dispersed civilization can cross-multiverse co-govern

Civilization weight should also include "historical narration rights." In Shrouding, Ye Fan's side decides how history is written — who is a hero, who is a villain, which war was just. Heng Yuan Zhou requires historical narration rights dispersed to Myriad Ranks; every race has its own historiographers and narratives. If Ye Fan enters Heng Yuan Zhou, the "Annals of Ye Fan" he writes is only one scroll in Myriad-Rank history, not all history.
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

One person's weight equaling the whole civilization is not "greatness," but "imbalance." Heng Yuan Zhou's proposition: civilization's decisions must be made by civilization itself, not done on its behalf by the strongest.

Civilization weight should also include "veto" allocation. In Shrouding, Ye Fan is the sole holder of veto; Heng Yuan Zhou disperses veto to the Symbiotic Court and Anchor Supervision Committee. Any civilization-tier decision cannot pass by a single will — this is weight dispersion's concrete institutional form.
