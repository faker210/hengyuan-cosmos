## Introduction: When strong ones can return to the past at will, history becomes playdough

Among the six top-level axioms, the **causal self-discipline axiom** requires cross-plane operations to be strictly filed, observe first then limitedly intervene, only transmit knowledge back without disturbing causality. This entry dissects Shrouding-the-Heavens world's causal self-discipline absence in time travel and cross-plane operations — strong ones can backtrack, intervene, rewrite the past at will; the law of cause and effect is kidnapped by plot convenience. The law-first axiom requires: time travel must have rules, otherwise it is predation on history. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

- Readers' trust in history collapses — since the past can be changed, does history still mean anything?

Causal self-discipline absence's regression:

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Ye Fan's side's cross-time, cross-plane operations are the causal version of predatory thinking:

- **Willful backtracking**: to save someone, to change a situation, willfully return to the past
- **Take without giving**: take resources and knowledge from the past / other worlds, but bear no causal cost
- **Karma debt unrecorded**: disturbances Ye Fan makes have no bookkeeper; civilization's causal ledger is a mess

Causal self-discipline also has a real-world analogy: it is like civilization's accounting system. Every cross-plane operation is an entry; must be registered, must be traceable, must have someone responsible. Shrouding-the-Heavens world has no such accounting, so strong ones willfully withdraw resources from the past and other worlds without ever keeping books — when karma debt accumulates beyond repayment, it erupts as a "great calamity." Heng Yuan Zhou's Alaya Network + Symbiotic Court is essentially this causal accounting system: observe first (reconcile), then limitedly intervene (small payment), only transmit knowledge back (transfer only, no cash withdrawal). A civilization without accounting will sooner or later go causally bankrupt.

## Conclusion

Time travel is not unusable, but must not be misused. Heng Yuan Zhou's causal self-discipline axiom is clear: you may observe, you may transmit knowledge back, but you may not treat others' history as playdough. Shrouding's lesson: once strong ones can willfully change the past, the two words "history" of civilization are dead.

Causal self-discipline also requires "time travelers" to be responsible for their actions. In Shrouding, Ye Fan returns to the past to save someone, no one is held accountable; Heng Yuan Zhou requires every cross-time operation to be filed, consequences evaluated, karma debt borne. Time is not a private prop, but a civilization asset shared by Myriad Ranks.
