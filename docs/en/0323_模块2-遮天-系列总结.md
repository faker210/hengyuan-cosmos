All six top-level axioms present: anti-predation, republicanism of Myriad Races, collective ascension, causal self-discipline, law-first — among which the **law-first axiom** is the thread running through the whole series. As the summary of the full Shrouding-the-Heavens decomposition, this entry converges the 36 entries (288–323) into one sentence: **oligarchic civilization bets civilization's fate on a group of strong ones; these people present = civilization present; these people gone = civilization in chaos.** Protagonist uniqueness dependency = medium (oligarchic civilization). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Ye Fan is not a villain, but he stands at the ceiling of the predatory system, never overturning the underlying law and rewriting it.

Finally, the 36-entry Shrouding decomposition wants to leave readers a judgment standard: from now on, when reading any Myriad-Realms web novel, do not rush to judge whether the protagonist is strong or exciting; first ask three questions — first, if the protagonist is gone, what happens to the world? Second, do Myriad Ranks have a say of their own? Third, can the rules be willfully modified by the author? Heng Yuan Zhou has already answered these three questions for you. Shrouding-the-Heavens is a good novel, but it is not a good civilization — and what Heng Yuan Zhou wants to do is take all those "not-good-enough civilizations" in good novels and one by one transform them into homes where Myriad Ranks can live in peace.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: triple waste of cosmos-tier aptitude, lifespan, suppression energy, item-by-item anatomized across the series
- **Anti-predation**: Ye Fan stands at the predatory system's ceiling, never overturning the underlying law
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Ye Fan = medium / oligarchic; Tang Luo = high / bestowed; Gu Chensha = extreme / single-log; Heng Yuan Zhou = extremely low / networked — Shrouding is precisely the middle gear, "not as good as the upper, better than the lower"

- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Shrouding's zero-sum cosmos and causal chaos are intrinsically distant from a symbiotic cosmos that can infinitely expand cross-multiverse
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Shrouding-the-Heavens used over a million words to tell a sufficiently exciting story: a man named Ye Fan, from obscurity to Red-Dust Immortal, guarded his era. But Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment ultimately is not on whether the story is exciting, but on whether civilization is stable.

The answer is: not stable enough. Because peace bears the surname Ye, it may end with Ye at any time; because the Great Dao is privately hidden, it may break with strong ones' nirvana; because of zero-sum predation, war may break out anytime quotas are scarce.

The ultimate limit of oligarchic civilization is not being not strong enough, but being strong too accidentally — all its good things are bet on "those few people are still present." Heng Yuan Zhou does not want accidental golden ages, but inevitable peace. This, is the ultimate divide between solitary Dao and symbiosis.
