# Entry 324 | Perfect-World Decomposition (Overview): Shi Hao's Solitary Dao and Single-Log Civilization Typology

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Perfect-World Full Decomposition (Entry 1 · Overview) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 17, 03

## Introduction: Shi Hao did not save an era, but bet a civilization on his own shoulders

Perfect World's Shi Hao, known to later generations as the Desolate Heaven Emperor, is web literature history's most tragic and most lonely protagonist. Born with Supreme Bone dug out, youth exiled to Stone Village, taking himself as seed, single-handedly deciding eternity, alone pacifying dark turmoil, holding up for later Shrouding era a sky with no darkness. Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Universe)'s respect for Shi Hao goes without saying — he was the only one who did not kneel in that dark age. But judging civilization structure, respect must yield to structural analysis: Shi Hao used a lifetime to prove "one person can carry one cosmos," and precisely proved "one cosmos should not be carried by one person."

This entry as the overview of the Perfect-World decomposition series establishes the full judging base — six top-level axioms: anti-predation axiom (develop increments, do not consume stock), republican axiom of Myriad Races (all beings equal evolution qualification), collective ascension axiom (strong ones feed back to Myriad Ranks, no great-material-small-use), causal self-discipline axiom (observe first then limitedly intervene, only transmit knowledge back without disturbing causality), law-first axiom (law precedes, never retcons, setting self-consistency over plot convenience), universal equal infinite law. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Among them the **law-first axiom** is core — Perfect World's eighteen-layer realm inflation and multi-part crossover retconning are precisely typical lesions of law not preceding. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency** — slightly lower than Gu Chensha's pure single-log, because he once had Stone Village, Liu Shen, Myriad-Realms comrades, but civilization ultimately still rests on his single-handed autocracy; single-log features overwhelm oligarchic remnants.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Shi Hao's energy is Emperor Immortal tier — one of Myriad-Realms ceilings. But this energy's direction has extreme waste:

- **One-person civilizationization**: pacifying dark turmoil, opening Shrouding era, blocking samsara and weird-source — all done by Shi Hao alone. The civilization engineering Myriad Ranks could have co-built is compressed into one person's epic
- **Comrades idling**: Liu Shen, Cao Yusheng, Myriad-Realms strong ones in long campaigns mostly as foils and sacrifices, not converted into institutionalized civilization-conservation force
- **No successor**: after single-handedly deciding eternity, later (Shrouding era) still needs emperors "to suppress darkness one by one," showing Shi Hao did not precipitate the solution into law and institution

Holding Emperor Immortal-tier power, the final use is "carrying darkness for Myriad Ranks for a lifetime" — over-great material, over-tragic, also over-wasteful.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Perfect World's historical regression is structurally destined:

1. **Generational break = crisis**: after Shi Hao, Nine Heavens and Ten Earths and Immortal Realm have no equal night-watcher; darkness returns in more covert forms (i.e., Shrouding's dark turmoil, Sacred Ruins' weirdness)
2. **Knowledge dies with the person**: the complete self-seed method, the concrete path of single-handedly deciding eternity, not precipitated as "Myriad-Rank public"; later generations can only guess across generations
3. **Tragedy depends on the individual**: every time darkness comes, it waits for "the next Shi Hao," rather than starting civilization's own immune mechanism

This is more extreme "person present = policy flourishes" than Shrouding — Shrouding at least has a group of emperors on rotation; Perfect World has no "rotation" at all, only the one Desolate.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Shi Hao's conduct is noble, never bloodthirsty, but predatory thinking is defined by structure, not malice:

- **Resource-exclusive Dao realization**: taking himself as seed though claiming "no need for external seeking," actually devours massive cave-heavens, divine materials, era fortune; one person's seed drains one realm's nutrients
- **Opportunity zero-sum exclusion**: Supreme Bone, Crown-All Body, various innate supreme treasures' contention strongly zero-sum
- **Dao legacies hidden**: the Emperor Immortal path not opened to Myriad Ranks; later generations cannot even imitate

Shi Hao is the most tragic "enlightened predator" — he predates not for private enjoyment, but to hold back darkness; but "predating for justice" still bets civilization's fate on personal morality.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Early Blood-Drawing, Cave-Heaven, Spirit-Transforming, Inscription Formation, Array-Position layering fine; later Supreme, True Immortal, Immortal King, Emperor Immortal layers inflate; realm count reaches eighteen, combat-power constraints loosen
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. Perfect World and Shrouding, Sacred Ruins multi-part crossover; timeline, samsara line, weird source repeatedly revised; early iron laws overturned late
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Emperor Immortal-tier aptitude used for one person's autocracy over eternity; Myriad-Rank co-building engineering compressed into personal epic
- **Anti-predation**: self-seed devours one realm's nutrients, opportunity zero-sum exclusion, Dao legacies hidden not public
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Shi Hao single-log (extreme-leaning-medium) is more extreme than Tang Luo bestowed (high) — he did not even have time to systematically establish "universal benefit" before alone shouldering darkness
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Foreign-Realm Myriad Ranks demonized as "dark"; foreign races, female cultivators mostly foils
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Perfect World's closed era and zero-sum dark game are far from the infinitely expandable symbiotic cosmos
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Shi Hao was the only one who did not kneel in that dark age; Heng Yuan Zhou salutes him. But saluting does not mean endorsing the structure. A cosmos that needs the Desolate Heaven Emperor alone to carry eternity is itself a failed cosmos — true Myriad Ranks republicanism needs no one to "autocratically decide," because night watch is everyone's business.
