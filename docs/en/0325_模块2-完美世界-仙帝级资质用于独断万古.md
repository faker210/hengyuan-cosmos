# Entry 325 | Perfect-World Energy-Waste Analysis: Emperor-Immortal-Tier Aptitude Used for Single-Handed Autocracy Over Eternity

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Perfect-World Full Decomposition (Entry 2) | Benchmark base: Documents 17, 15, 04

## Introduction: Highest-tier power, loneliest use

Heng Yuan Zhou judges a strong one not by how high he can fight, but by how much civilization stock his energy converts into. Shi Hao is a Myriad-Realms ceiling-tier Emperor Immortal, yet converts nearly all this cosmos-tier energy into "one person's battle." This entry takes the six top-level axioms as base — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), universal equal infinite law — item by item anatomizing Perfect World's energy waste. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Shi Hao's energy waste is not a capability problem, but a direction problem, concentrated in four dimensions:

- **Civilization-engineering dimension**: Emperor Immortal-tier power could have been used to re-cosmic laws, open increment Origin, establish cross-era night-watch institutions; actually used to "personally stepping out again and again." Equivalent to holding the law-amendment right, yet spending a lifetime as a firefighter
- **Time dimension**: Shi Hao's long campaigning lifespan is heavily consumed in one-on-one dark-source battles, alone patching samsara gaps. The same energy invested in institutional construction could have freed Myriad Ranks from night-watcher dependence within a century
- **Talent dimension**: Liu Shen, Myriad-Realms comrades, Stone Village juniors could have been cultivated into second-tier, echelon "rotating night watch"; actually mostly foils, sacrifices, or exits; strong-one capacity one-time consumed
- **Knowledge dimension**: the complete self-seed path, key cognition of single-handedly deciding eternity, not precipitated in a copyable, teachable, Alaya-archivable form

In Heng Yuan Zhou terminology: this is typical "great-material-small-use" and "energy mismatch" — Emperor Immortal-tier aptitude compressed into a blade only drawn at critical moments.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Energy mismatch necessarily leads to regression:

1. **Night-watcher dependence solidified**: Myriad Ranks accustomed to "wait for Shi Hao when darkness comes"; their own evolution stagnates
2. **Institutional vacuum**: Shi Hao's military exploits not converted into laws; he exits and darkness immediately returns
3. **Era break**: Shrouding era, Sacred Ruins era still must re-face the same kind of darkness, equivalent to making later generations redo the same hard problem

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Shi Hao's "self-seed" seems to need no external seeking, actually is the most efficient stock devouring:

- One person's seed drains cave-heavens, divine materials, era fortune
- Key battlefield opportunities are strongly exclusionary
- He pours all predated energy into personal combat power, not back into Myriad Ranks

This is the most tragic sample of "predating for justice."

- **Anti-energy waste**: Emperor Immortal-tier aptitude compressed into personal combat power, civilization-engineering dimension nearly idle
- **Anti-predation**: self-seed devours one realm's stock without feedback
