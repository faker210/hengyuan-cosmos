# Entry 326 | Perfect-World Inevitable Historical Regression: Civilization Breaks Generations After Shi Hao

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Perfect-World Full Decomposition (Entry 3) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 26, 21

## Introduction: The price of single-handedly deciding eternity is no successor after eternity

The four characters "single-handedly decide eternity" read heroically; what Heng Yuan Zhou reads is a sigh. Shi Hao blocked darkness for the later Shrouding era, but did not leave later generations a force that can night-watch on its own. This entry takes the six top-level axioms as base — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), universal equal infinite law — to argue: Perfect World's historical regression is not accident, but the inevitable product of single-log structure. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The generational-break problem is essentially failure of energy precipitation:

- Shi Hao poured all energy into "holding darkness now," leaving no energy to build "tomorrow's night watchmen"
- Self-seed cannot be replicated, equivalent to taking the most precious evolution path away with the person
- Shrouding era's emperors must grope from zero for the same kind of darkness, energy paid repeatedly

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

This entry's core, argued in three layers:

1. **Inheritance layer broken**: single-log civilization's highest achievement rests on one person. Shi Hao exits; highest combat power, highest cognition, highest night-watch responsibility simultaneously hang. Shrouding era still needs "one emperor suppresses one era," proving the solution was never institutionalized
2. **Knowledge layer broken**: the path of single-handedly deciding eternity, the final cognition of the dark source, not Alaya-archived, not opened via teaching systems. Later generations can only guess from legends; knowledge compound interest zeroed
3. **Confidence layer broken**: Myriad Ranks long-term protected, losing the training to "solve darkness themselves." Darkness arrives, first reaction is still "wait for the next Desolate," not starting its own immunity

Conclusion: the more successful the single-handed autocracy over eternity, the more thorough the generational break — because Myriad Ranks were even deprived by Shi Hao's strength of the chance to "learn to carry it themselves."

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Shi Hao's "solitude" structurally takes civilization's sovereignty as well:

- He made all major decisions and fights for Myriad Ranks
- Myriad Ranks thereby lost civilization-subject qualification, degenerated into the protected
- This "gentle takeover" makes Myriad Ranks immediately regress to savagery in his absence

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

|-----------------------------------------|------------------------------|

| Path non-replicable | 300 systems all-tier public, teachable and learnable |

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Multi-era handoffs rely on timeline patches; generational-break logic stitched with vague concepts like "samsara," "weirdness"
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. Shrouding, Sacred Ruins repeatedly revise Perfect World's ending
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**

Add one layer: the generational-break cost can also be converted into repeated tuition. Shi Hao paid it in ancient times, Ye Fan paid it again in later generations, the Sacred Ruins era paid it a third time — the same hard problem of how to respond to darkness is repeatedly answered by civilization with three person-level epics, yet no one ever wrote the standard answer into a textbook. Heng Yuan Zhou's Alaya Network is precisely the opposite: one generation's experience is immediately archived; later generations only retrieve, need not re-examine. Generational break is not literary tragedy, but the most expensive kind of repetition in civilization economics.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: energy not precipitated into inheritable institutions
- **Anti-predation**: single-handed autocracy essentially takes over Myriad Ranks' sovereignty
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least left the Peerless Academy; Shi Hao did not even leave a replicable night-watch institution
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Myriad Ranks protected-ized, losing evolution-subject qualification
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: generational-break civilization cannot export stable order to the multiverse
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Supplementary Analysis: Network Deduction of Generational-Break Crisis

The inevitability that civilization breaks generations after Shi Hao has been repeatedly verified through Alaya Network's Quest Sediment Domain simulation. Why Heng Yuan Zhou can avoid this outcome is that the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization ties civilization's survival to the network, not individuals — the law-first axiom ensures institutions do not stop rotating due to any individual's absence. The universal equal infinite law means civilization's foundation is 99% of Myriad-Rank beings, not some Emperor Immortal. In Heng Yuan Zhou's view, Shi Hao's single-handed autocracy over eternity is tragedy, not hero narrative: a true strong one should let civilization continue forward behind him, not tie civilization to himself.

## Conclusion

The other side of single-handedly deciding eternity is that no one can succeed for eternity. Heng Yuan Zhou does not celebrate "one person holding everything," but pursues "no one needs to hold everything alone."

In the final analysis, Perfect World · civilization breaks generations after Shi Hao described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
