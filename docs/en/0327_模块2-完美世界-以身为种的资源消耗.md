# Entry 327 | Perfect-Work Energy-Waste Analysis: Shi Hao's Predatory Thinking — The Resource Cost of Taking Oneself as Seed

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Perfect-Work Full Decomposition (Entry 4) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 18, 15

## Introduction: The cleanest cultivation hides the most thorough stock devouring

"Taking oneself as seed" is Perfect World's most fascinating setting — no external seeking, self-opening cave-heaven, one's own body as the seed of the cosmos. It sounds fully anti-predatory, actually takes predation to the extreme: not grabbing readymade, but converting one realm's nutrients into one's own "seed." This entry takes the six top-level axioms as base — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), universal equal infinite law — to dissect self-seed's resource-consumption logic. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Self-seed's waste:

- It condenses a cave-heaven law that could have been "co-farmed by Myriad Ranks" into "one person's exclusive inner cosmos"
- The bigger the seed, the more realm nutrients it draws; the fewer external resources distributable
- Ultimately strong one and environment form a "this消彼长" zero-sum, not "symbiotic mutual growth"

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

When the strongest one's growth comes at the cost of realm stock:

1. Realm ecosystem overdrawn by strong one's growth
2. Other beings' distributable resources decrease, general stagnation
3. Era ends, strong one exits, leaving an hollowed-out realm

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking (This Entry's Core)

Heng Yuan Zhou judges predation not by malice but by structure. Self-seed's predation manifests in three layers:

- **Hidden stock devouring**: on the surface "no external seeking," actually cave-heaven opening, divine-material absorption, era-fortune convergence, all are transferring public stock into the individual body
- **Growth zero-sum**: every tier Shi Hao advances, same-realm competitors' relative situation worsens by a notch. Supreme Bone, innate supreme treasures' contention is explicit zero-sum; self-seed is implicit zero-sum
- **No feedback**: after the inner cosmos matures, its output does not feed back to the realm, but follows the person in single-handedly deciding eternity

Key distinction: Heng Yuan Zhou's "self-origin self-growth evolution law" develops **increment** Origin — the stronger the strong ones, the larger Myriad Ranks' Origin pool; while self-seed develops **stock** — the bigger the strong one's seed, the poorer the realm. This is precisely the dividing line between the anti-predation axiom and predatory cultivation.

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Self-Seed (Stock Devouring) | Anchor Symbiosis (Increment Development) |
|------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Inner cosmos within one person | Myriad Ranks co-farm the Origin pool |
| Growth consumes the realm | Growth feeds back to the realm |
| Opportunity zero-sum exclusion | 300 systems fully open |
| Nutrients private | Joint contracting, more work more gain |

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium**. Self-seed is self-consistent within the "individual inner cosmos" concept, but its relation to Myriad-Rank resource conservation is never strictly quantified
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium-high**. The "seed" boundary expands with combat-power inflation
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**

Add one layer: self-seed's zero-sum nature can be tested with realm-nutrient conservation. If the maturation of Shi Hao's inner cosmos is accompanied by continuous net outflow of external cave-heavens, divine materials, era fortune, then the stronger he is, the poorer the realm; this on the ledger is negative-sum. Heng Yuan Zhou's self-origin self-growth law requires strong one and realm to be positive-sum — the stronger the strong one, the larger the Origin pool. The dividing line between the two cultivation views ultimately is: does my growth need to cut a piece off you?

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: seed grows, realm impoverishes; energy conversion efficiency is negative
- **Anti-predation**: self-seed is hidden stock devouring, no feedback to Myriad Ranks
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's predation still stays at "resource concentration"; self-seed writes predation into the body of cultivation
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: one person's seed drains nutrients; Myriad Ranks' evolution space is squeezed
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: stock logic cannot support multiverse's incremental expansion
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Supplementary Analysis: Resource-Consumption Contrast

Self-seed's resource-consumption pattern, under Heng Yuan Zhou's Alaya Network audit, presents as extremely high single-person resource occupancy. If the resources Shi Hao alone consumes are dispersed across the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's 300 systems, they could support several thousand cultivators' simultaneous promotion. The law-first axiom opposes this centralized consumption, because the law network's optimal solution is distributed resource scheduling. The universal equal infinite law negates at the Origin level the reasonableness of "one person devours Myriad Ranks" — since everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, no one needs to "seed" for everyone.

## Conclusion

Self-seed is web literature's most romantic cultivation symbol, but on Heng Yuan Zhou's energy ledger, it is a bad account of "personal surplus, realm deficit." True symbiotic evolution should be the stronger the strong one, the richer Myriad Ranks, not the stronger the lonelier.

In the final analysis, Perfect World · self-seed's resource consumption described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
