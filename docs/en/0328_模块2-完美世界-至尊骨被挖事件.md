# Entry 328 | Perfect-Work Supreme-Bone Dig-Out Incident: The Predatory System's Original-Sin Exhibit

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Perfect-Work Full Decomposition (Entry 5) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 47, 08

## Introduction: One bone dug out illuminates the entire system's lesion

Shi Hao was born with the Supreme Bone; in childhood it was dug out by elders of his clan and transplanted to another clansman. This is Perfect World's most painful opening, and its most valuable civilization specimen — it uses the most extreme means to place the "predatory system's original sin" in front of readers. This entry takes the six top-level axioms as base — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), universal equal infinite law — to dissect this event's institutional meaning. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The Supreme-Bone incident's waste:

- A Supreme who could have grown naturally is artificially interrupted, talent taken
- The transplant recipient gets a "predated aptitude," destined to walk unsteadily on this road
- The whole event wastes two aptitudes, only manufactures one hatred

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

The Supreme-Bone incident is no isolated case, but the institution's inevitability:

1. **Aptitude private**: the Supreme Bone is defaulted as private property that a clan can "distribute," showing the highest aptitude is monopolized by strong-one families from the start
2. **Bloodline = resource**: talent is treated as transplantable, contestable stock, not a road everyone can walk
3. **Weak unprotected**: child Shi Hao unprotected, proving the system has no "everyone-has-a-road" baseline

Such a system: golden ages are accident, predation is the norm.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Tellingly, Shi Hao later taking back the Supreme Bone and re-cultivating the Supreme path is essentially "responding to predation with predation":

- He used stronger force to take back what was taken
- But he did not overturn the "aptitude can be contested" law, only changed from victim to stronger contender
- Heng Yuan Zhou's stance: sympathize with Shi Hao's plight, but do not endorse him using the predation path to solve predation — this only perpetuates the system unchanged

The true solution is not "grab the bone back," but "make bone-style aptitude no longer diggable."

Add one layer: the Supreme-Bone dig-out's institutional meaning goes deeper than one family tragedy. It shows that in this world, the highest-tier aptitude is defaulted as distributable private property, not a public qualification everyone can strive for. Heng Yuan Zhou's anti-monopoly constitutionalization and Everyone-Has-a-Road Guarantee System precisely want to root out from the legal source the possibility of digging out aptitude — not punishing the bone-digger after the fact, but making bone-digging have no legal basis from the start.

## Supplementary Analysis: Law Interpretation of the Original-Sin Incident

The Supreme-Bone dig-out incident is the predatory system's original-sin exhibit; its tragedy is repeatedly replayed in Heng Yuan Zhou's Alaya Network records as a negative textbook. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization extracts from this event the anti-predation axiom: a cultivator's Origin talent may not be seized, may not be transplanted, may not be traded. The law-first axiom ensures "talent belongs to the person" is written into the charter. The universal equal infinite law means Supreme-Bone-tier talent should have been attainable by everyone through cultivation, not only by digging out others' — Lin Hao's Path D artificial Dao-rule rings are precisely the institutional response to this original sin.

## Conclusion

The Supreme-Bone dig-out is Perfect World's first blunt blow to readers, and Heng Yuan Zhou's first diagnosis of this system: when aptitude can be dug out, this civilization from the first word stands on predation's side.
