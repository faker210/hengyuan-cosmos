# Entry 331 | Perfect-Work Plot-Hijacks-Setting Case: Forced Sacrifice for Pathos

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Perfect-Work Full Decomposition (Entry 8) | Benchmark base: Documents 19, 04, 17

## Introduction: The most moving tragedy often happens because the setting "needs" it

Perfect World's sacrifice scenes (comrades fall, beloved women die, myriad saints offer themselves) are tear-jerkers, and also what Heng Yuan Zhou anatomizes in depth. The question is not "is it tragic," but "is this tragedy manufactured for emotion." This entry takes the six top-level axioms as base — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), universal equal infinite law — to argue: when setting gives way to pathos, plot has hijacked setting. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Forced sacrifice's waste:

- Casualties that could have been avoided by institutions, early warning, contingency plans are "arranged" as sacrifices for pathos
- A strong one's death in battle is permanent loss of energy stock
- Readers swept by emotion ignore "this should not have happened"

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Structural consequences of sacrifice narrative:

1. Packaging "deaths caused by institutional deficiency" as "great sacrifice" numbs readers
2. Since sacrifice seems tragic, no one asks "why was there no contingency"
3. Next darkness arrives, still relies on "sacrificing another batch" to pass

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

To make Shi Hao's single-handed autocracy over eternity more tragic, those around him are "arranged" to sacrifice:

- This uses others' fates to pave the way for the male lead's epic
- Liu Shen, beloved women, comrades' existence partially functions to "die at the right time" to catalyze the protagonist
- Heng Yuan Zhou's critique: tragedy cannot become a fig leaf for individual instrumentalization

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Key sacrifices often rely on conveniences like "villain suddenly becomes smart" or "protagonist slips at the critical moment"
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. Deaths arranged for tears are later反复 for resurrection
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**

Add one layer: forced sacrifice for pathos, in setting terms, breaks the causal chain. When a comrade who could have been saved by an institutional contingency happens to die at that moment to make the protagonist lonelier, what readers read is not fate, but the author's favoritism. Heng Yuan Zhou's causal self-discipline axiom requires every loss to have traceable causation — this is not opposing tragedy, but opposing treating tragedy as an emotion button needing no explanation.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: avoidable casualties consumed by emotion

- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's tragedies mostly come from external oppression; Shi Hao's tragedies often come from "plot needs him lonelier"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: supporting roles, beloved women, foreign races all as pathos tools
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the multiverse need not manufacture depth through sacrifice
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Supplementary Analysis: Institutional Critique of Pathos Narrative

The forced-sacrifice-for-pathos typical case, in Heng Yuan Zhou's view, is not "artistic appeal," but literary packaging of institutional failure. A true civilization need not manufacture depth by sacrificing characters — the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, through the law-first axiom, gives Myriad Ranks dignity at the institutional level, and pathos is no longer a necessary means to drive plot. The Alaya Network records the conflict between such "sacrifice-for-sacrifice" settings and the universal equal infinite law: any character's life or death should not be hijacked by plot need; every being's evolution right is charter-protected.

## Supplementary Analysis: Alternative Aesthetics to Pathos Narrative

Heng Yuan Zhou is not opposing all tragic aesthetics, but opposing institutional tragedy — that kind of tragedy where institutional design flaws force characters to sacrifice. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization encourages "overcoming difficulty," not "being overcome by difficulty." The Alaya Network records Myriad Ranks' feedback on various tragic narratives, finding what truly moves Myriad Ranks is persistence in adversity, not meaningless death. The law-first axiom ensures narrative serves Myriad-Rank spiritual growth; law does not require anyone to die to prove conviction.

## Conclusion

Pathos is literature's legitimate means, but "tragedy for tragedy, forced sacrifice for tears" is betrayal of law. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is not a fairytale without tragedy, but that when tragedy occurs, readers can be certain it is fate, not the author's emotional hijack.
