# Entry 332 | Perfect-Work Self-Consistency Assessment: Internal Contradictions of the Self-Seed System

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Perfect-Work Full Decomposition (Entry 9) | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 26, 04

## Introduction: A system claiming "self-consistent" — where exactly is it leaking?

"Taking oneself as seed" is Perfect World's core theoretical signboard — no reliance on external things, self-opening cave-heaven, one's own body as the seed of the cosmos. It sounds closed-loop and self-consistent. But when Heng Yuan Zhou shines the six top-level axioms into it, it finds this "self-seed" leaks in several places internally. This entry takes the anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), and universal equal infinite law as base, item by item assessing its internal contradictions. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Internal contradictions first manifest as energy-caliber inconsistency:

- It claims "no external seeking," yet massively absorbs cave-heavens, divine materials, era fortune
- The same system externally claims self-sufficiency, internally relies everywhere on stock input
- Readers cannot tell whether Shi Hao is "self-seed" or "devouring the realm"

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Cost of insufficient self-consistency:

1. Law boundaries vague, later generations cannot accurately reproduce
2. Every explanation falls back on "the protagonist is special"
3. The system fails with the protagonist's death, degenerating into legend

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

The core of internal contradiction is the caliber conflict between "self-seed" and "predation":

- On one hand it emphasizes "inner cosmos self-sufficiency," on the other it devours one realm's resources
- This self-contradiction is covered by "Shi Hao is a special case"
- Once you substitute him as an ordinary person, the system immediately fails

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment (This Entry's Core)

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. The fundamental contradiction between "self-sufficiency" and "realm-devouring" is unresolved
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. Contradictions covered by "protagonist special" / "late enlightenment"
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**

Add one layer: the self-seed internal contradiction can be exposed by one arithmetic problem: if "no external seeking" is true, then Shi Hao's growth should not change realm stock; if the plot's massive absorption of divine materials and fortune is true, then realm stock must be consumed. Both cannot be true simultaneously. Heng Yuan Zhou requires any cultivation system first pass the energy-conservation-calculable gate, not shut mouths with unfalsifiable rhetoric like "the protagonist is a special case."

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: caliber inconsistency makes energy unmeasurable
- **Anti-predation**: stock devouring under the name "self-sufficiency"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo system internal self-consistency is medium-high; self-seed's internal contradiction is more hidden and more fatal
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: self-consistency only holds for the "special case," Myriad Ranks cannot apply it
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: only a strictly self-consistent law framework can support multiverse expansion

Add one more layer: the simplest way to test whether a system is truly self-consistent is to swap the protagonist for another person and run it again. If it immediately fails when swapped, it is not system self-consistency, but protagonist self-consistency. Heng Yuan Zhou's 300 Origin systems are each designed to be reproducible by any qualifying being; that is what is called system-level self-consistency.

- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Supplementary Analysis: Dissecting the System's Internal Contradiction

The self-seed system's internal contradiction — emphasizing individual self-sufficiency yet relying on external resources — is fully exposed after the Alaya Network's logical dissection. Heng Yuan Zhou's Anchor Symbiotic Civilization does not require cultivators to be isolated-self-sufficient "like self-seed," but realizes two-way empowerment of individual and civilization through the law network. The law-first axiom ensures individual evolution does not contradict collective ascension; the Alaya Network is precisely the interface between the two. The universal equal infinite law dissolves the seed's exclusivity: Origin is infinite, everyone can seed, no need for one person to seed for all under heaven.

## Supplementary Analysis: Unity of Self-Sufficiency and Interconnection

Self-seed's contradiction lies in the opposition between "self-sufficiency" and "interconnection." Heng Yuan Zhou realizes the unity of the two through the Alaya Network: the individual in the law network is both an independent node and an organic part of the network. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization neither requires cultivators to cultivate alone away from civilization, nor requires the individual to fully attach to civilization. The law-first axiom ensures individual and collective each have boundaries, not invading each other. The universal equal infinite law is the rights basis of this unity — the individual's infinite power is not isolated, but part of the infinite cosmos shared with Myriad Ranks.

## Conclusion

Beneath self-seed's romance hides an unhealed wound of "self-sufficiency or predation." Heng Yuan Zhou's law-first axiom requires: a system must be self-consistent for all people, all conditions, not just for the protagonist alone.

In the final analysis, Perfect World · self-seed system's internal contradictions described in this document are no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
