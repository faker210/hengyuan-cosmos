# Entry 333 | Perfect-Work Critique of Single-Handed Autocracy: The Ultimate Fragility of One-Person Civilization

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Perfect-Work Full Decomposition (Entry 10) | Benchmark base: Documents 49, 17, 04

## Introduction: The loneliest greatness is also the most fragile order

"Single-handed autocracy over eternity" is Shi Hao's title and Perfect World's ultimate image. One person, blocking the dark source, holding up an eternal blue sky for later generations. Heng Yuan Zhou salutes him, but cannot help pointing out: tying eternity's safety to one person's decision is civilization's ultimate fragility. This entry takes the six top-level axioms as base — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), universal equal infinite law — to anatomize the two words "single-handed autocracy." The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Single-handed autocracy's waste:

- All eternity's major decisions press on one person; decision bandwidth maxed out
- Myriad Ranks lose the chance to participate in decisions, and lose decision-making ability
- The cost of one misjudgment is borne by all beings of eternity

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Single-handed autocracy necessarily leads to:

1. Person present = policy flourishes, person gone = policy dies
2. Myriad Ranks excluded from governance, governance ability degenerates
3. Eternity's "peace" is actually "supervised" peace, not self-rule

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

"Single-handed autocracy" itself is monopoly over governance power:

- Shi Hao decided eternity's direction for Myriad Ranks
- This takeover came from goodwill, but deprives Myriad Ranks of political-subject qualification
- Well-intentioned dictatorship is still dictatorship — Heng Yuan Zhou looks at structure, not motive

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Single-Handed Autocracy | Anchor Symbiosis Governance |
|-------------------------|------------------------------|
| One person decides eternity | Anchor Council collective legislation |
| Decision bandwidth single point | Symbiotic Executive Committee division of labor |
| Misjudgment unbalanced | Symbiotic Court + Anchor Supervisory Committee checks |
| Myriad Ranks supervised | Myriad Ranks republican co-build |

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

1. Replicate the autocratic model's decision bandwidth
2. Compare under same conditions the collective-decision model
3. Count decision-error rate and civilization resilience
4. Output "Single-Handed-Autocracy Fragility Report"

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. "One person can autocratically decide eternity" lacks verifiable bandwidth in governance logic
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. The autocracy's boundary repeatedly adjusts with sequel needs
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**

Add one layer: the governance-bandwidth problem of single-handed autocracy is often masked by combat narrative. Even if a person is as strong as an Emperor Immortal, his decision bandwidth is still finite; pressing all eternity's major choices on this one nerve equals pressing civilization's fault tolerance to zero. Heng Yuan Zhou uses the Anchor Council to distribute decisions to the collective not because the collective is smarter, but because the collective's fault-tolerance bandwidth is far greater than any single individual.

One closing line: handing eternity to one person saves the work of governance design, but pays for it with the entire civilization's stability. Heng Yuan Zhou would rather spend a bit more institutional cost than bet this stability on any one person's lifespan.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: decision-bandwidth single-point overload
- **Anti-predation**: single-handed autocracy = monopolizing governance power
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least built an academy; Shi Hao has no governance division of labor, autocracy more thorough
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Myriad Ranks excluded from eternity's governance
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: one-person decision cannot support the multiverse's complexity

Add one more layer: the opposite of single-handed autocracy is not chaos, but co-governance. Many mistakenly think dispersing power will throw the world into chaos; actually Heng Yuan Zhou's three-powers separation plus supervisory network proves that when eternity's responsibilities are split, each only guards one own grid, and civilization is more stable than when one person alone carries it.

- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Supplementary Analysis: One-Person Civilization's Fragility

One-person civilization's ultimate fragility, in the Alaya Network's stress test, manifests as SPDCI approaching 0.95. Why Heng Yuan Zhou's Anchor Symbiotic Civilization can press SPDCI to 0.05 is that the law-first axiom ties civilization's operation to the institutional network, not individuals. Shi Hao's stirring single-handed autocracy over eternity, in Heng Yuan Zhou's evaluation framework, is precisely civilization's most unsafe state — civilization's survival should not depend on one person's willpower. The universal equal infinite law ensures civilization's subject is Myriad Ranks, not one person.

## Supplementary Analysis: From Fragility to Resilience

The opposite of one-person civilization's ultimate fragility is not another stronger one-person, but a resilient network. Heng Yuan Zhou's Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, through distributed decision-making, multi-layer redundancy, Myriad Ranks republicanism — the three-piece set — raises civilization resilience to unprecedented heights. The Alaya Network automatically switches redundant paths when any node fails; the law-first axiom ensures system degradation does not affect core rights. The universal equal infinite law means resilience comes from 99% of Myriad-Rank participants, not from some irreplaceable lone peak.

## Conclusion

Single-handed autocracy over eternity is web literature's most tragic romance, but the eternity Heng Yuan Zhou wants is the eternity Myriad Ranks guard together, not the eternity some one person carries alone. Lonely greatness is too fragile to survive one sequel.

In the final analysis, Perfect World · one-person civilization's ultimate fragility described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
