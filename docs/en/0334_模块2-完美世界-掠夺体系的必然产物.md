Perfect World's dark turmoil is often narrated as "foreign invasion" — Foreign Realm, dark beings, weird source. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**.
