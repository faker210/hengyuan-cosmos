# Entry 335 | Perfect-Work Emperor-Immortal Institution Critique: Personal Dictatorship and Civilization Bet

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Perfect-Work Full Decomposition (Entry 12) | Benchmark base: Documents 49, 17, 04

## Introduction: When "strongest" is defaulted as "most deserving to rule"

Perfect World's Emperor Immortal is both combat apex and the cosmos order's actual master. Heng Yuan Zhou does not object to strong ones being strong, but firmly objects to the default setting "strongest = most deserving to dictate." This entry takes the six top-level axioms as base — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), universal equal infinite law — to critique the Emperor Immortal institution. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Emperor Immortal institution's waste:

- Tying governance power to combat power makes the strongest person do the most trivial governance
- The strong one's decision bandwidth filled by governance affairs, unable to continue evolution and construction
- Myriad Ranks' governance potential left idle

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Emperor Immortal institution's regression logic:

1. Emperor Immortal present = dictatorship, absent = vacuum
2. The next Emperor Immortal is "fought out," not "elected"
3. Every power handover is a bloody battle

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

The Emperor Immortal institution's essence is "taking power by force":

- Whoever is strongest rules, no checks
- Power does not come from Myriad-Rank authorization, but from combat power
- Even if Shi Hao's conduct is flawless, the institution itself is still dictatorship — a good man's dictatorship is still dictatorship

| Handover by bloody battle | Myriad-Rank election and assessment |

Add one layer: the Emperor Immortal institution directly converts "strongest" into "most deserving to rule," conflating two entirely different capability dimensions. Heng Yuan Zhou's Plane Transcendent four-dimension assessment (Power 40, Mind 25, Contribution 20, Responsibility 15) precisely separates these two dimensions — you can be very strong, but to sit on the governance seat you must meet contribution and responsibility standards.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: strong-one bandwidth crowded out by governance
- **Anti-predation**: taking power by force = institutionalized predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo has no dictator's name but dictator's reality (bestowed); Shi Hao has dictator's reality; neither established checks
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Myriad Ranks have no political participation right
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: dictatorial institutions cannot replicate to the multiverse

Add one more layer: good-man dictatorship and bad-man dictatorship, structurally no difference. Because dictatorship's danger is not whether the dictator is good, but that once he exits, no mechanism catches the power. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is not a better Emperor Immortal, but to make the Emperor Immortal position itself redundant.

- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Supplementary Analysis: Institutional Alternative to Emperor-Immortal Dictatorship

The Emperor Immortal institution's personal dictatorship and civilization bet, in Heng Yuan Zhou's assessment, is one of the worst models of civilization governance. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's alternative is a Myriad-Ranks Republican Council plus the Alaya Network's distributed decision system; the law-first axiom ensures any "Emperor-Immortal-tier" decision must undergo charter review. The universal equal infinite law negates the legitimacy of "one person represents all under heaven" — people under heaven do not need a dictator to decide for them. The Emperor Immortal institution's republicanization essentially disperses power from one person's hands into the network.

## Supplementary Analysis: Plan to Abolish the Emperor-Immortal Position

The Emperor Immortal institution's republicanization is not swapping another person onto the Emperor-Immortal seat, but abolishing the position itself. Heng Yuan Zhou decomposes the Emperor Immortal's power into three functions: law maintenance to the Alaya Network, civilization decision-making to the Myriad-Ranks Republican Council, security to the Transcendent post system. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's law-first axiom ensures no single position can hold all power. The universal equal infinite law means the very concept of "supreme power" should not exist — every being inherently possesses infinite power, no heaven-acting dictator is needed.

## Conclusion

The Emperor Immortal institution directly converts "strongest" into "most deserving to rule"; this is predatory thinking's political endgame. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: being strong is not the same as ruling; order must come from Myriad Ranks republicanism, not from fists.
