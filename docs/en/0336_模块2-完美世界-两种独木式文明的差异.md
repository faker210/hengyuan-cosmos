# Entry 336 | Perfect-Work Shi Hao vs. Gu Chensha: Differences Between Two Single-Log Civilizations

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Perfect-Work Full Decomposition (Entry 13 · Comparison) | Benchmark base: Documents 17, 04, 03

## Introduction: Both single logs — one bears the sins of all beings, one makes all beings dragons

Dragon Symbol's Gu Chensha and Perfect World's Shi Hao are the two thickest single logs in the web-literature protagonist lineage. The first, the first across ages to cultivate the Dao of Heaven, willing to bear all beings' sins and karmic retribution; the second single-handedly decides eternity, alone blocking darkness for later generations. Heng Yuan Zhou takes the six top-level axioms as base — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), universal equal infinite law — to compare these two single logs' differences. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency; Gu Chensha = extreme / pure single-log**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The two single logs' wastes are same-with-differences:

- Gu Chensha: energy used to "make all beings dragons"; all beings are pitied into leveling up, without evolution-subject qualification
- Shi Hao: energy used to "bear darkness for all beings"; Myriad Ranks are protected into survival, without night-watch-subject qualification
- Difference: Gu Chensha is a "giving-type single log," Shi Hao a "guarding-type single log" — the former takes back the leveling right, the latter takes back the survival right

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

The two's regression mechanisms differ:

- Gu Chensha: all beings "made dragons"; once he stops pitying, all beings lack even the ability to crawl themselves
- Shi Hao: Myriad Ranks "guarded"; once he stops autocratically deciding, Myriad Ranks lack even the ability to night-watch themselves
- Same destination by different paths: both outsource civilization's lifeline to one person

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- Gu Chensha's predation lies in "giving": the more he gives, the more all beings cannot leave him
- Shi Hao's predation lies in "guarding": the longer he guards, the more Myriad Ranks regress into infants
- One gently takes over the right to grow, one tragically takes over the right to survive

Add one layer: the two single logs' difference can also be divided by what was given. Gu Chensha makes all beings dragons; what all beings lose is the ability to crawl by themselves. Shi Hao makes Myriad Ranks guarded; what Myriad Ranks lose is the ability to night-watch by themselves. The former takes away the growth right, the latter takes away the survival right. Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad Ranks republicanism returns both to Myriad Ranks — letting them crawl themselves and night-watch themselves.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: both press cosmos-tier energy on one shoulder
- **Anti-predation**: giving and guarding are both "gentle takeover"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo bestowed (high) sits between them; single-log (extreme) is more extreme
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: both degrade Myriad Ranks into the passive side
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: a single log cannot support the multiverse's governance complexity
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Supplementary Analysis: Comparison of the Two Single-Log Types

Shi Hao and Gu Chensha's two single-log civilizations differ but, after the Alaya Network's typological analysis, fall into the same major category: both tied to one person, both highly fragile. Heng Yuan Zhou uses the two as positive and negative cases of "single-log" for comparative analysis — Gu Chensha-style bearing sins for all beings is milder, Shi Hao-style single-handed autocracy over eternity is more stirring, but both have extremely high SPDCI. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's Myriad Ranks republicanism is the overall transcendence of single-log; the law-first axiom ensures civilization does not bet on anyone.

## Supplementary Analysis: Typological Summary of Single-Log Civilization

Though Shi Hao and Gu Chensha differ, in Heng Yuan Zhou's typology they belong to the same "single-log" major category — both tie civilization's survival to one person. The Alaya Network's stress test shows single-log civilizations, however stirring, all have SPDCI above 0.9. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's attitude toward single-log is not mockery but regret: these strong ones could have built civilization more solidly, yet used all their energy to alone carry the sky. The law-first axiom's answer is simple — the sky should not be carried by one person, but should be held up by a law network co-built by Myriad Ranks.

## Conclusion

Gu Chensha makes all beings dragons, Shi Hao bears darkness for all beings. Heng Yuan Zhou salutes their greatness, but insists: true salvation is that all beings no longer need anyone to make them dragons, to carry the night for them.
