# Entry 337 | Perfect-Work Shi Hao vs. Ye Fan: Solitary-Dao Inheritance Between Perfect World and Shrouding

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Perfect-Work Full Decomposition (Entry 14 · Comparison) | Benchmark base: Documents 17, 04, 21

## Introduction: The darkness your predecessor carried for you, you still have to carry again

Perfect World and Shrouding the Heavens share the same "trilogy cosmos": Shi Hao in remote antiquity single-handedly decides eternity; Ye Fan in far later times suppresses dark turmoil. The two are the parent and child generations of the same solitary Dao. Heng Yuan Zhou takes the six top-level axioms as base — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), universal equal infinite law — to compare this pair of "close-heeled solitary Daoists." The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency; Ye Fan = medium / oligarchic**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- Shi Hao in antiquity expends enormous effort to pacify darkness; Ye Fan in later ages still must face the same kind of darkness from scratch
- Two generations repeatedly pay the same civilization bill
- Shi Hao's solution is not transmitted to Ye Fan, equivalent to the previous generation's energy output zeroing

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

The parent-child solitary Daoists' tragedy lies precisely here:

1. Shi Hao pacifies darkness, institutions not established
2. Later Ye-Fan era darkness returns, emperors on rotation suppress it
3. Ye Fan leaves "eternal peace surnamed Ye"; further later (Sacred Ruins) must start over again

Each "era"'s golden age relies on the strongest of that era, yet never precipitates into cross-generation institutions.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- Shi Hao autocratically decides, taking governance power into his own hands
- Ye Fan is oligarchic, taking peace into his own clan
- One takes it more absolutely (single log), the other takes it slightly wider (oligarchic, at least a group of emperors)
- Essence the same: neither writes "how to respond to darkness" into Myriad Ranks' own law

## Supplementary Analysis: The Genealogy of Solitary-Dao Inheritance

Shi Hao and Ye Fan's solitary-Dao inheritance, in Heng Yuan Zhou's genealogy analysis, belongs to the same "solitary-Dao" tradition: the strong one's personal power is extremely strong, but civilization's foundation is extremely weak. The Alaya Network traces the similarity of the two systems' civilization-survival indicators — both rapidly degenerate after the protagonist's absence. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's law-first axiom fundamentally opposes the solitary-Dao tradition: civilization's power should be distributed in the network, not concentrated on a lone peak. The universal equal infinite law robs the solitary Dao of its tragic narrative basis of "someone must step forward."

## Supplementary Analysis: The End of the Solitary-Dao Tradition

Shi Hao and Ye Fan's solitary-Dao inheritance, in Heng Yuan Zhou's view, is a road that has come to its end. The solitary-Dao tradition's core assumption is "the strong must be lonely," but the Alaya Network's data shows lonely cultivators' long-term civilization contribution is far lower than network collaborators. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's Myriad Ranks republicanism encourages the strong to walk with Myriad Ranks; the law-first axiom ensures walking with others does not weaken the strong but amplifies their contribution. The universal equal infinite law means the strong need not prove greatness through loneliness — true greatness is letting Myriad Ranks all keep up.

## Conclusion

The darkness Shi Hao carried in antiquity, Ye Fan carried again in later ages. This is not inheritance, this is repetition. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is one generation writing the solution into law, so that ten thousand generations of descendants need not pay the same tuition.

In the final analysis, Perfect World · solitary-Dao inheritance between Perfect World and Shrouding described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
