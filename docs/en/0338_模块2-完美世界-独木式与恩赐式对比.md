# Entry 338 | Perfect-Work Shi Hao vs. Tang Luo: Single-Log vs. Bestowed Comparison

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Perfect-Work Full Decomposition (Entry 15 · Comparison) | Benchmark base: Documents 17, 04, 251

## Introduction: One carries darkness for you, one opens the door for you — neither gave you the key

Tang Luo (I Can See Combat Power) breaks aristocratic-family monopoly and rebuilds the Peerless Academy, representing the bestowed-type protagonist; Shi Hao single-handedly decides eternity, representing the single-log-type protagonist. Heng Yuan Zhou takes the six top-level axioms as base — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), universal equal infinite law — to compare these two "for-your-own-good" models. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency; Tang Luo = high / bestowed**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- Tang Luo: energy used to "open the door and run a school," but the door only opens to human mid-lower ranks
- Shi Hao: energy used to "alone carry darkness," the door simply not opened
- Tang Luo at least made the gesture of "universal benefit"; Shi Hao had no time even for the gesture before going to the front line

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

- Tang Luo: person present = policy flourishes; Tang Luo exits and the academy model hangs
- Shi Hao: person present = policy flourishes even more; Shi Hao exits and eternity has no night watchman
- Both tied to one person; only Tang Luo tied to "goodwill," Shi Hao tied to "pathos"

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- Tang Luo is an "enlightened predator": breaks monopoly but does not open the highest Great Dao
- Shi Hao is a "tragic predator": devours one realm's stock to carry darkness
- Tang Luo's predation still has room (could open a bit more); Shi Hao's predation has no room (he bet everything)

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Tang Luo Bestowed (High) | Shi Hao Single-Log (Extreme-Leaning-Medium) | Anchor Symbiosis |
|---------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|-------------------|
| Strong one gives a door | Strong one guards the door | Myriad Ranks build their own doors |
| Universal benefit tied to goodwill | Night watch tied to pathos | Order tied to law |
| Institution absent | Institution even more absent | Charter runs automatically |

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-high (Tang Luo) / medium-low (Shi Hao)**
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium (Tang Luo) / high (Shi Hao)**
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: Tang Luo = high; Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium**

Add one layer: the dividing line between bestowed and single-log is whether the door is opened. Tang Luo at least opened one door (to human mid-lower ranks); Shi Hao had no time even to open the door before going to the front. But the two reach the same destination by different paths: the key is not in Myriad Ranks' hands. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is not to swap a door-opener, but to return the rights to build doors, guard doors, and open doors all to Myriad Ranks.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Tang Luo opens the door but not the top; Shi Hao has no time even to open
- **Anti-predation**: bestowal and autocracy are both takeover
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: this entry is the Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison — Shi Hao is more extreme than Tang Luo, having not even time for "universal benefit"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Tang Luo only benefitted humans; Shi Hao did not even systematically benefit humans
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: neither touched multiverse expansion

Add one more layer: the shared illusion of bestowal and autocracy is making all beings think they have no choice. Tang Luo giving a door, Shi Hao guarding the door, both hint that Myriad Ranks can only wait for the strong. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants to break is precisely this waiting — Myriad Ranks need not wait for anyone to open a door, because the door was built by everyone together.

- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Supplementary Analysis: Comparison of Single-Log and Bestowed

Shi Hao single-log vs. Tang Luo bestowed, after Alaya Network quantification, shows different failure modes: single-log rapidly collapses (SPDCI ≈ 0.95), bestowed slowly retreats (SPDCI ≈ 0.82). Heng Yuan Zhou's Anchor Symbiotic Civilization takes neither — the law-first axiom requires civilization to be tied neither to one person nor to a bestower, but to the network institution. The universal equal infinite law means whether single-log or bestowed, both are illegitimate at the rights level.

## Supplementary Analysis: Taxonomy of Failure Modes

Single-log rapid collapse and bestowed slow retreat represent two different civilization failure modes. Heng Yuan Zhou's Alaya Network models the two separately: single-log degrades 80–95% within a century of the protagonist's absence; bestowed degrades 60–85%. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's goal is to press the degradation rate below 5%. The law-first axiom is the meta-rule for achieving this goal — institutions do not depend on individuals, so degradation has no trigger. The universal equal infinite law ensures degradation does not occur at the rights level, because everyone's basic infinite power does not disappear with institutional changes.

## Conclusion

Tang Luo opened a door for Myriad Ranks; Shi Hao blocked a patch of sky for Myriad Ranks. But the door's key is in Tang Luo's hand, the sky's weight on Shi Hao's shoulders — Myriad Ranks never have their own key, nor need to learn to carry the sky themselves. This is the shared lesion of bestowed and single-log.
