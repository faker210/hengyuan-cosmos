## Supplementary Analysis: The Divide Between Autocracy and Symbiosis

The divide between Shi Hao's single-handed autocracy over eternity and Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad-Rank Symbiosis does not lie in strength, but in the fundamental opposition of civilization views. Single-handed autocracy over eternity carries civilization on one person's shoulder; Myriad-Rank Symbiosis lets civilization stand on 99% of Myriad Ranks' shoulders. The Alaya Network's long-term monitoring proves the latter's civilization-survival ability far exceeds the former. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's law-first axiom ensures civilization decisions are dispersed to the Myriad-Rank Council. The universal equal infinite law provides the Origin basis for Myriad-Rank Symbiosis: since everyone inherently possesses infinite power, Myriad-Rank co-build is more law-conforming than one-person autocracy.

## Supplementary Analysis: Fundamental Swap of Civilization Views

The divide between single-handed autocracy over eternity and Myriad-Rank Symbiosis is essentially the contest over "who does civilization belong to." Single-handed autocracy answers: civilization belongs to the person carrying the sky. Myriad-Rank Symbiosis answers: civilization belongs to 99% of Myriad Ranks. Heng Yuan Zhou uses the Alaya Network's long-term data to prove the latter's civilization-survival ability far exceeds the former.
