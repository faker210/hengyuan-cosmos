# Entry 341 | Perfect-Work Liu Shen Setting Analysis: The Bestowed Nature of the Mentorship

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Perfect-Work Full Decomposition (Entry 18) | Benchmark base: Documents 37, 08, 05

## Introduction: Why a kind divine tree still could not escape the frame of bestowal

Liu Shen is Perfect World's most beloved character — a sentient divine tree, guarding Stone Village, enlightening Shi Hao, gentle yet powerful. Heng Yuan Zhou has full respect for Liu Shen, yet must still anatomize her structural role: the "mentorship" Liu Shen represents is essentially still bestowal. This entry takes the six top-level axioms as base — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), universal equal infinite law — to analyze Liu Shen. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Liu Shen's energy is used to "guard and enlighten" Stone Village:

- Her strength benefits only Stone Village, one small community
- Her knowledge and power not diffused as whole-civilization infrastructure
- The longer she exists, the more Stone Village depends on her, rather than growing itself

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

The mentorship's bestowed nature leads to:

1. Liu Shen present = Stone Village safe; Liu Shen falls = Stone Village endangered
2. Stone Village's juniors accustomed to being sheltered by the divine tree, lacking self-reliance
3. Guardianship becomes a gentle cage

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Liu Shen herself does not predate, but the mentorship structure is:

- Knowledge "enlightened" by the strong to the weak, not "self-cultivated" by the weak
- The enlightened have no ownership, only usage rights
- The divine tree falls, knowledge withers with it — isomorphic to Tang Luo's bestowal system

Worth noting: Liu Shen herself is a plant spirit, precisely proving that plant spirits in the Myriad-Rank genealogy can also become strong ones; unfortunately this setting only serves the protagonist, not promoted as a Myriad-Rank-wide rule.

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Liu-Shen Mentorship (Bestowed) | Anchor Symbiosis Education |
|---------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Divine tree enlightens Stone Village | Symbiotic Education System universalized |
| Knowledge lives and dies with the divine tree | Cross-plane mentor network + Alaya archive |
| Small community benefits | Myriad Ranks 99% full coverage |
| Enlightened have no ownership | Everyone-Has-a-Road Guarantee System |

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium**. Liu Shen's strength boundary adjusts with plot
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium-high**. Liu Shen repeatedly dies and resurrects
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**

Add one layer: Liu Shen as a plant-spirit strong one was inherently excellent evidence that Myriad Ranks have roads — it proves non-carbon, non-human life can also reach the extreme. Unfortunately this evidence is only used to serve the protagonist, not promoted to a general rule. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants to do is precisely turn Liu-Shen-style exceptions into rules generally attainable by plant spirits, energy spirits, planet consciousness.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: divine tree energy benefits only one village
- **Anti-predation**: mentorship = knowledge bestowal
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Liu-Shen mentorship and Tang-Luo academy system are both bestowal, smaller scale
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Liu Shen as a plant-spirit strong one could have proven Myriad Ranks have roads, yet not promoted
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: mentorship cannot expand to the multiverse

Add one more layer: the mentorship's greatest hidden danger is making disciples outsource their insight to the teacher. The more thoroughly Liu Shen enlightens, the more easily Shi Hao entrusts key judgments to her. Heng Yuan Zhou's Symbiotic Education System aims to cultivate people who can judge themselves, not excellent students who cannot leave their teacher.

- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Supplementary Analysis: The Bestowed Essence of the Mentorship

Liu-Shen-style mentorship's bestowed nature lies in the one-way giving relation between mentor and disciple; the disciple's growth entirely depends on the mentor's bestowal. Heng Yuan Zhou's alternative is the Alaya Network's distributed mentor system: cultivators no longer bind to a single mentor, but obtain teaching resources in corresponding fields from the network as needed. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's law-first axiom ensures knowledge dissemination does not depend on personal relationships; the universal equal infinite law ensures every cultivator has equal knowledge-access rights.

## Supplementary Analysis: Distributed Mentor System

The alternative to Liu-Shen-style mentorship is the Alaya Network's distributed mentor system. Cultivators no longer bind to a single mentor, but obtain corresponding teaching resources from the network according to their current cultivation stage, including four modules: theoretical explanation, combat simulation, peer sparring, and mind-guidance. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's law-first axiom ensures teaching quality does not depend on personal charisma, but is guaranteed by the network's standardized curriculum system. The universal equal infinite law means every cultivator has equal knowledge-access rights, no need to seek connections or swear allegiance.

## Conclusion

Liu Shen is Perfect World's gentlest being, but a gentle mentorship is still bestowal. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is not one divine tree enlightening one village, but an education system that lets Myriad Ranks learn to grow by themselves — the divine tree can be one member of it, yet should not be the only sun.

In the final analysis, Perfect World · bestowed nature of the mentorship described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
