# Entry 342 | Perfect-Work Stone Village Setting: Civilizational Limitations of the Closed Community

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Perfect-Work Full Decomposition (Entry 19) | Benchmark base: Documents 36, 50, 05

## Introduction: Why warm Stone Village cannot grow into a true civilization

Stone Village is Perfect World's spiritual homeland — a closed, warm, blood-tied small village, Shi Hao's haven in his youth. Heng Yuan Zhou cherishes this warmth, but must point out: the closed community's warmth is precisely the hotbed of civilizational limitation. This entry takes the six top-level axioms as base — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), universal equal infinite law — to analyze Stone Village. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Stone Village's energy concentrates "within the bloodline":

- Stone Village's resources, legacies, protection all circulate inside the village
- The vast Myriad Ranks outside have nothing to do with Stone Village
- The warm small community fosters a pattern of intimate-within, closed-without

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

The closed community's limitations:

1. Resources and talents neither flow out nor come in; evolution stagnates
2. Intra-village status by bloodline, not contribution
3. Once external enemies break the closure, the village is completely unprepared

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Stone Village is what Shi Hao "guards," but the guard's boundary is closed:

- He fights for Stone Village, yet builds no institutions for the Myriad Ranks outside
- The more Stone Village is guarded, the more isolated from the outside
- The closed community's "we" precisely excludes the vast "they"

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Stone Village (Closed Community) | Anchor Symbiosis (Open Civilization) |
|----------------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Bloodline as tie | Myriad Ranks republicanism, all beings equal |
| Intra-village circulation | Joint contracting, more work more gain |
| Closed to the outside | Cross-plane connection, networked interflow |
| Guarded by the divine tree | Everyone has a road, institutions bottom out |

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-high**. Stone Village's setting is relatively stable early to late
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Stone Village's scale inflates with the protagonist's strength
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**

Add one layer: the closed community's warmth is real, but the warmth's boundary is civilization's boundary. The more intimate Stone Village is, the more the Myriad Ranks outside are seen as "them." Heng Yuan Zhou does not deny community warmth, only requires that when village walls grow higher, do not forget that outside the walls are also beings — the first step of Myriad Ranks republicanism is often drawing our radius a little wider.

One closing line: a community can be small, but civilization cannot. Stone Village's warmth is worth preserving, but Stone Village's walls must come down one day — this is not betraying the homeland, but letting the homeland grow a world.

In the final analysis, Stone Village is not the answer, but the starting point. It taught Shi Hao what mutual guardianship is, yet failed to teach him how to hand this guardianship over to Myriad Ranks.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: energy circulates intra-village without overflow
- **Anti-predation**: closure = external exclusion
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Stone Village is smaller and more closed than Tang Luo's human-race scope
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Stone Village only recognizes within the bloodline
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: a closed community cannot extend to the multiverse

Add one more layer: a closed community's resilience is often fake — its safety comes from the divine tree and the protagonist, not itself. Once external variables break the closure, this resilience instantly evaporates. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is a community that can stand on its own even if any one individual leaves.

- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Supplementary Analysis: Closed Community's Limitation

Stone-Village-style closed community's civilizational limitation lies in its scale effect being insufficient to support cross-plane civilization's complexity. Heng Yuan Zhou, through the Alaya Network, connects closed communities into an open network; the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's Myriad Ranks republicanism encourages law-interface interflow among communities. The law-first axiom ensures a community's boundary does not constitute exclusion from Myriad-Rank rights. The universal equal infinite law means beings outside the closed community also have evolution rights; Stone Village should not be a "chosen corner," but integrate into the larger Myriad-Rank network.

## Supplementary Analysis: Communities' Network Connection

Stone-Village-style closed community's way out is not to expand scale, but to connect to the network. Heng Yuan Zhou, through the Alaya Network, connects closed communities into open nodes; the community's internal mutual-aid tradition is preserved, while external resources and law interfaces can also be connected. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's law-first axiom ensures community boundaries do not constitute exclusion from Myriad-Rank rights. The universal equal infinite law means beings outside Stone Village also have the right to interact with Stone Village; closure is not protection, but isolation.

## Conclusion

Stone Village is warm, but a warm small village cannot replace civilization. Heng Yuan Zhou does not want to deny Stone Village's warmth, but to let this warmth walk out of the village walls and cover Myriad Ranks — because a true homeland should have no wall of "outside the village = outsider."

In the final analysis, Perfect World · closed community's civilizational limitations described in this document are no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
