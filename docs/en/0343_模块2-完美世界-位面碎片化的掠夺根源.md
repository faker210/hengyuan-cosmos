## Introduction: Fragmented realms are destined to breed predators

Perfect World's world is sliced into fragmented planes like "Nine Heavens and Ten Earths," mutually isolated, each struggling on its own. Heng Yuan Zhou holds: plane fragmentation is not neutral geography, but predation's structural root. This entry takes the six top-level axioms as base — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), universal equal infinite law — to dissect fragmentation. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Fragmentation's waste:

- Each plane's resources limited; strong ones can only "grab," not "open up"
- Planes mutually isolated; knowledge, resources, talents cannot flow
- Repeated construction, repeated evolution; global efficiency extremely low

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Fragmentation necessarily leads to predation:

1. Intra-plane resources peak; strong ones can only cross-plane predation
2. Isolation manufactures "we/they" opposition
3. No unified spatial jurisdiction; conflicts have no adjudication

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Shi Hao's campaigns are essentially contending back and forth among fragmented realms:

- He is not co-building in one unified cosmos, but punching through battlefields one by one among fragments
- Each plane is an object he draws nutrients from
- A fragmented world naturally grows fragmented predators
