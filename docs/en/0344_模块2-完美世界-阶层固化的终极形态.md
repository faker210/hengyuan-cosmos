## Introduction: Ascension is not liberation, but welding class onto the sky

The "Immortal Realm" is Perfect World's higher-tier world — mortal cultivators pass tribulations and ascend, entering the nation of immortals. It sounds like a reward; what Heng Yuan Zhou sees is: class solidification pushed to the cosmos's ultimate form. This entry takes the six top-level axioms as base — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), universal equal infinite law — to anatomize the Immortal Realm. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**.

The Immortal Realm's waste:

- Ascension qualification monopolized by a tiny few
- Higher-tier resources inside the Immortal Realm further concentrated at the top
- The gulf between mortal realm and Immortal Realm insurmountable; bottom loses hope

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Class solidification's regression logic:

2. Immortal Realm strong ones need not descend to build; mortal realm left to fend for itself
3. "Becoming an immortal" becomes the only reward; after becoming an immortal? Nothing designed

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

The Immortal Realm is predation's terminal:

- The Immortal Realm does not feed back to the mortal realm, only extracts
- The ascension institution is essentially the mortal realm's one-way tribute to the Immortal Realm
