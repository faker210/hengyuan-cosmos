# Entry 345 | Perfect-Work Foreign Realm Setting: Civilizational Confrontation as Zero-Sum Game

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Perfect-Work Full Decomposition (Entry 22) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 52, 05

## Introduction: Setting another civilization as "an enemy that must be destroyed" is the laziest and most failed setting

The "Foreign Realm" is Perfect World's hostile civilization in the heavens — generations of enemies of Nine Heavens and Ten Earths, never-ending. Heng Yuan Zhou respects war narrative, but must point out: setting civilizational relations as zero-sum is the laziest and most failed worldview. This entry takes the six top-level axioms as base — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), universal equal infinite law — to anatomize the Foreign Realm. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Zero-sum confrontation's waste:

- Two civilizations' energy all poured into mutual consumption
- Increments that could have been co-developed became mutually destructive stock
- Long wars make two generations pay a heavy price

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Zero-sum confrontation's regression:

1. Civilizational relations set as "not our kind = enemy"
2. No dialogue, trade, symbiosis option
3. War becomes the only state; development yields to survival

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Shi Hao's expeditions against the Foreign Realm are "righteous predation":

- Under the pretext of civilization's survival, he systematically campaigns against the Foreign Realm
- This campaign is righteous in the plot, but structurally still predation
- The problem is not Shi Hao, but the setting: from the start it left the two races no symbiosis road

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Foreign Realm (Zero-Sum Confrontation) | Anchor Symbiosis (Myriad Races Republicanism) |
|----------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| Civilizational relation = mortal enemy | Myriad Ranks, all beings equal evolution qualification |
| Mutual stock consumption | Develop increments, co-evolve |
| No dialogue channel | Symbiotic Court cross-civilization adjudication |
| Victory or death | Myriad Ranks republican co-build |

Heng Yuan Zhou Document 52 "Foreign-Race Classification and Symbiosis" emphasizes: foreign races are not enemies that must be destroyed, but civilizational partners needing identification, classification, symbiosis.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Why the Foreign Realm is hostile, motivation simplifies with plot to "innately evil"
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. The Foreign Realm's essence and ending are revised multiple times
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**

Add one layer: setting civilizational relations as victory-or-death saves the work of designing dialogue mechanisms, but traps Myriad Ranks forever in a war budget. Heng Yuan Zhou's Symbiotic Court and Myriad Ranks republicanism precisely want to give mortal enemies an exit without bloodshed — why enemies are enemies often is only because institutions gave them no second road.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: civilizational energy mutually consumed
- **Anti-predation**: expedition is righteousness-dressed predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo world's conflict is intra-clan; Foreign Realm is civilization-level zero-sum, larger scale and more rigid
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Foreign-Realm Myriad Ranks stripped of evolution qualification
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: zero-sum logic cannot support multiverse coexistence

Add one more layer: zero-sum narrative is laziest and most expensive. Lazy because no dialogue needs designing; expensive because Myriad Ranks forever pay war tax. Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad Ranks republicanism precisely wants this war tax redirected to common Origin construction.

- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Supplementary Analysis: Civilizational Confrontation as Zero-Sum Game

The Foreign Realm and Nine Heavens and Ten Earths' zero-sum game is the inevitable product of the limited-resource hypothesis under the predatory system. Heng Yuan Zhou, through the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, proves resources need not be zero-sum — the universal equal infinite law ensures every being inherently possesses basic infinite power, and confrontation loses meaning. The Alaya Network provides a "observe first then limitedly intervene" safe framework for contact between different civilizations. The law-first axiom ensures civilizational conflicts are resolved through law negotiation, not war.

## Supplementary Analysis: From Confrontation to Dialogue

The Foreign Realm and Nine Heavens and Ten Earths' zero-sum confrontation has as its alternative the "observe first then limitedly intervene" diplomatic principle. Heng Yuan Zhou, through the Alaya Network, establishes cross-civilization communication channels; the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization does not try to destroy either side, but pulls both sides into the Myriad-Ranks Republican dialogue framework. The law-first axiom ensures inter-civilizational interest distribution is decided by law negotiation, not war. The universal equal infinite law means beings on both sides of the confrontation have equal survival and evolution rights; war cannot be a means to resolve civilizational differences.

## Conclusion

Setting another civilization as "an enemy that must be destroyed" saves the author's design effort, but traps Myriad Ranks forever in war. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is not to destroy the Foreign Realm, but to make the Foreign Realm one race within Myriad Ranks republicanism — enemies could have been partners.

In the final analysis, Perfect World · civilizational confrontation as zero-sum game described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
