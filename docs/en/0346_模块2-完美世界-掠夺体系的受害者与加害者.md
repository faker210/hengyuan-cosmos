# Entry 346 | Perfect-Work Dark Beings Setting: Victims and Perpetrators of the Predatory System

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Perfect-Work Full Decomposition (Entry 23) | Benchmark base: Documents 57, 59, 04

## Introduction: Are dark beings born evil, or forced into evil by the system?

Perfect World's dark beings are often set as pure evil — devouring for a living, beyond redemption. But Heng Yuan Zhou, using the causal self-discipline axiom, asks: are they born this way, or did the predatory system force them into perpetrators? This entry takes the six top-level axioms as base — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), universal equal infinite law — to view dark beings dialectically. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: killing does not solve generation
- **Anti-predation**: defining the predated as evil is second predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo world's enemies are mostly aristocratic families; dark beings are system-alienated victims, more complex in nature
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: dark beings also have evolution qualification, yet are stripped
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: only transformation mechanisms can support the multiverse

Add one more layer: treating dark beings as pure evil equals forfeiting the question why they became this way. And every root-cure solution starts from this forfeited question. Heng Yuan Zhou's classified disposal of Dark Replacement Bodies precisely picks this question back up.

- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Supplementary Analysis: Dialectics of Victim and Perpetrator

Dark beings are both victims and perpetrators of the predatory system; this dual identity is fully recorded in the Alaya Network's archives. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization does not make a simple good-evil dichotomy of dark beings, but through the anti-predation axiom institutionally eliminates the soil that produces dark beings. The law-first axiom ensures the degenerate path is reversible — the Alaya Network provides repair channels for predators willing to turn back. The universal equal infinite law means dark beings are not permanently excluded from evolution rights.

## Supplementary Analysis: Redemption and Transformation

Dark beings' dual identity in Heng Yuan Zhou leads to a key institutional design: redemption channels. The Alaya Network provides a complete repair path for predators willing to turn back — stop predatory behavior, accept mind-repair, re-connect to the network after passing the law-cognition exam. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization sets no permanent banishment order; the law-first axiom ensures causal self-discipline is self-repair, not external punishment. The universal equal infinite law means dark beings are not stripped of evolution rights either — they merely took a detour; once the detour ends they can still return.

## Conclusion

Dark beings are both perpetrators and victims. Heng Yuan Zhou does not advocate being soft on evil, but insists: without understanding where evil comes from, you can only kill one batch, and another batch emerges. True root-cure is to cut the predatory rules that force people into darkness.

In the final analysis, Perfect World · victims and perpetrators of the predatory system described in this document are no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
