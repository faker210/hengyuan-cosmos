Perfect World unfolds from a human-race perspective; Myriad Ranks are either allied foils or demonized enemies. Heng Yuan Zhou, with the Myriad Ranks republican axiom as core yardstick, requires all beings to have equal evolution qualification. This entry takes the six top-level axioms as base — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), universal equal infinite law — to examine Myriad-Rank situation. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**.
