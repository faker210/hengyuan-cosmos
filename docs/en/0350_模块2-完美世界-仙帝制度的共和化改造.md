## Introduction: How many steps to transform "Emperor-Immortal dictatorship" into "Myriad Ranks republicanism"

The previous 26 entries critiqued Perfect World's single-log civilization. From this entry the turn to construction begins: what Heng Yuan Zhou asks is not "where Perfect World went wrong," but "if Anchor Symbiosis is injected, how should the Emperor-Immortal institution be republicanized." This entry takes the six top-level axioms as base — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), universal equal infinite law — to give the reform blueprint. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **before reform = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency; post-reform target = extremely low / networked**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones (Pre-Reform Diagnosis)

Before reform, the Emperor-Immortal institution's energy waste: the Emperor Immortal alone carries eternity, Myriad Ranks excluded from governance. The first step of reform is to divert the governance power pressed on the Emperor Immortal's shoulder to the institutional network.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression (Pre-Reform Diagnosis)

Dictatorial institutions regress because the person gone, policy dies. The core of republican reform is to make order not tie to any one Emperor Immortal.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking (Pre-Reform Diagnosis)

The Emperor Immortal takes power by force. The key to reform is changing "who is strongest who rules" to "who passes assessment who takes the post."

## IV. Anchor Symbiosis Alternative (This Entry's Core)

The Emperor-Immortal institution's republican reform takes four steps:

| Reform Step | Old Emperor-Immortal System | Republicanized Reform |
|-------------|-----------------------------|------------------------|
| Legislation | Emperor Immortal's single word decides | Anchor Council collective legislation |
| Administration | Emperor Immortal personally handles | Symbiotic Executive Committee division-of-labor administration |
| Judiciary | Strong one = adjudicator | Symbiotic Court independent judiciary |
| Supervision | None | Anchor Supervisory Committee multi-layer supervision |

An Emperor-Immortal-tier strong one like Shi Hao is not deposed, but enters posts through the "Plane Transcendent four-dimension assessment" (Power 40 / Mind 25 / Contribution 20 / Responsibility 15) — his strength used for institutional construction, not alone autocratic. After reform, Myriad Ranks may still look up to Shi Hao, yet need no longer bet eternity on his one shoulder.

## Supplementary Analysis: Republicanizing the Emperor-Immortal Institution

The Emperor-Immortal institution's republican reform is Heng Yuan Zhou's core alternative to the Perfect-Work system. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization sets no one-person-rule Emperor-Immortal seat, but a Myriad-Ranks Republican Council plus a Transcendent post system. The law-first axiom ensures every post is charter-bound; the Alaya Network does compliance review on all decisions. The universal equal infinite law means the power position of "Emperor Immortal" itself should be abolished, not have another person sit on it.

## Supplementary Analysis: Three Stages of Republican Reform

The Emperor-Immortal institution's republican reform takes three stages: stage one, decompose Emperor-Immortal power into three independent functions — network supervision, deliberative decision, security; stage two, each functional module taken over by post-holders elected by Myriad-Rank representatives; stage three, the Alaya Network takes over routine operation, humans (and Myriad-Rank beings) only participate in deliberation at major decisions. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's law-first axiom ensures every stage is charter-reviewed. The universal equal infinite law is the rights foundation throughout the reform.

Republican reform is not negating Shi Hao, but letting his greatness need not come at the price of "alone carrying eternity." After reform, Perfect World still has the legend of the Desolate Heaven Emperor, but the night of eternity is guarded together by Myriad Ranks.
