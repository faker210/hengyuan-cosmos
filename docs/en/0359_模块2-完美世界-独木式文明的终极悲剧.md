# Entry 359 | Perfect-Work Series Conclusion: The Ultimate Tragedy of Single-Log Civilization

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Perfect-Work Full Decomposition (Entry 36 · Series Conclusion) | Benchmark base: Documents 01, 04, 17

## Introduction: Thirty-six diagnoses point to the same disease

From Entry 324 to this entry, the Perfect-Work decomposition series has 36 entries, from overview to energy-level waste, historical regression, predatory thinking, setting critique, then to alternatives and institutional design. All diagnoses point to the same disease: the ultimate tragedy of single-log civilization. This entry takes the six top-level axioms as base — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom (core), universal equal infinite law — to close the whole series. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Shi Hao = extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones (Series Closing)

The whole series repeatedly proves one thing: Shi Hao presses almost all Emperor-Immortal-tier energy onto "one person single-handed over eternity." The Myriad-Rank co-build project is compressed into a personal epic; comrades spin empty, knowledge does not sediment, no institutions for successors. This is not an ability problem, but a direction problem — over-qualified, over-tragic, also over-wasteful.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression (Series Closing)

Single-log civilization's regression is iron law: the person lives, policy rises; the person dies, policy dies. After Shi Hao, the Shrouding era still needs Emperors one by one to suppress darkness; the Sacred Ruins era starts over again. The same civilization bill paid by three generations. The more successful the single-handed autocracy over eternity, the more thorough the discontinuity — because Myriad Ranks are even deprived of the chance to learn to bear the night themselves.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking (Series Closing)

Shi Hao is the most tragic "enlightened predator." He predates not for private enjoyment, but to bear darkness; he uses himself as a seed, but actually devours a realm's stock; he single-handedly rules eternity, but actually takes over Myriad Ranks' governance right and survival right. Heng Yuan Zhou sympathizes with his fate, but insists: predating for justice is still betting civilization on personal morality.

## IV. Anchor Symbiosis's Alternative Picture (Series Closing)

The alternatives given by the whole series can be summarized as one master map:

| Single-Log Tragedy | Anchor Symbiosis Way Out |
|---------------------|---------------------------|
| One person bears eternity | Myriad Ranks jointly night-watch |
| Self-seed devours stock | Self-generation self-growth opens increment |
| Eighteen-layer inflation retcons | Nine-plus-two never retcons |
| Emperor Immortal dictatorship | Council + executive + court + supervision |
| Fight after darkness gains strength | Alaya early intervention |
| Human-centeredness + Foreign-Realm hostility | Myriad Ranks republicanism, foreign races back in fold |
| Personal weight crushes consensus | Individual-collective weight balance |

## V. Final Conclusion of the Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review (Series Closing)

The simulation box's final answer: Shi Hao absent = civilization collapse; after injecting Anchor Symbiosis, the same initial conditions steadily run. Single-log civilization's resilience equals that lone log's resilience — once it breaks, civilization breaks with it.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment (Series Overall)

- **Setting self-consistency: low**. Eighteen-layer inflation, trilogy crossover contradictions, self-seed caliber conflicts
- **Retcon-ending risk: extreme**. Leaving gaps for sequels, forced sacrifice for pathos, late-realm padding
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: extreme-leaning-medium / single-log tendency** — slightly lower than Gu-Chensha's pure single-log (he had Stone Village, Liu Shen, comrades), but civilization's endgame still ties to him alone, single-log characteristics outweigh oligarchic remnants

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Emperor-Immortal energy compressed into personal epic, civilization-engineering dimension nearly idle
- **Anti-predation**: self-seed devours stock, single-handed takeover of governance, predation for justice
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's gift-style (high) is at least self-consistent within one generation; Shi Hao's single-log (extreme-leaning-medium) cannot even pass down one generation's experience, more extreme than Tang Luo
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: in Perfect World, Myriad Ranks are guardians-protected, demonized, instrumentalized; the one thing they are not is co-builders
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Perfect World, single-log, zero-sum, retcon-style, is still a civilization-level distance from an infinitely expandable symbiotic cosmos
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Supplementary Analysis: Heng Yuan Zhou's Answer to Single-Log Tragedy

To single-log civilization's ultimate tragedy, Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is not "switch to a stronger person," but "tear down the single log, build the network." The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's Myriad Ranks republicanism + law-first axiom + Alaya Network supervision — three-piece set, none dispensable. The universal equal infinite law is all of this's rights foundation — when civilization no longer needs some one person to bear it, the soil of tragedy no longer exists. Shi Hao's story deserves sympathy, but what Heng Yuan Zhou wants is to make such tragedies never repeat.

## Conclusion

Shi Hao is the only person in that dark era who did not kneel; Heng Yuan Zhou pays him its deepest respect. But respect does not equal approving the structure. The ultimate conclusion of Perfect World's 36-entry decomposition is only one sentence: **single-log civilization's tragedy is not that the log is not strong enough, but that it bets the entire civilization's life on a piece of wood that will age, will break, will die alone.**

True Myriad Ranks republicanism does not need anyone to single-handedly rule eternity. Because night-watching is everyone's business.

In the final analysis, Perfect World · ultimate tragedy of single-log civilization described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
