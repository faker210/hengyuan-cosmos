# Entry 363 | Dou Continent · Decomposing Tang San's Predatory Thinking: Institutional Predation of Soul-Ring Hunting

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dou Continent System Decomposition (Entry 4) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 19, 58

## Introduction: When predation is written into the two words "level up"

The anti-predation axiom's core is: do not use killing-and-plundering as the evolution path. The most special thing about Dou Continent is that its predation is not some villain's evil deed, but the legal procedure written into every leveling up — a Soul Master who wants to advance must go to the forest to hunt a soul beast, strip off its Origin soul ring cultivated over hundreds or thousands of years. Tang San is merely this system's most excellent executor. This entry, taking the law-first axiom as core, decomposes how this "institutional predation" is internalized into geniuses' instinct. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: **high (gift-style leaning oligarchic)**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- **Hunting = loss**: every breakthrough consumes a soul beast's century-spanning Origin, and the soul beast's cultivation also consumed the plane's Origin — this is double loss, not transformation
- **Efficiency mismatch**: a soul beast's thousand-year bitter cultivation is "transformed" by a human hunt into a one-tier soul ring; energy utilization extremely low
- **Endowments spinning empty**: Tang San's past-life Tang Sect hidden weapons and profound skills could have developed into a "non-killing" technical path, but the soul-ring system forces him back to the old hunting road

Conclusion: treating "killing" as leveling fuel is the greatest waste in the energy loop.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

- **Predation internalized**: when predation is a legal procedure, generations of cultivators all believe "becoming strong = killing"; no one questions the underlying law
- **Ecological backlash**: high-tier soul beasts are hunted extinct first; latecomers can only hunt low-tier ones; the overall cultivation ceiling moves down with declining stock
- **Moral regression**: humans are taught from childhood that soul beasts are "soul rings"; the view of life is systematically reshaped; Myriad-Rank reconciliation forever impossible

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking (This Entry's Core)

Tang San's predatory thinking has a three-layer structure:

1. **Instrumentalizing the other**: in his eyes a soul beast is first "which ring, what years, what attribute," and only secondarily a living being — this is the predator's most fundamental cognitive distortion
2. **Rationalizing killing**: he does not feel he is killing, but feels he is "doing a quest, gathering rings"; moral sense is entirely absorbed by procedure
3. **Institutional inheritance**: after becoming a god, he does not reform this hunting system, but maintains it — because this system is precisely the source of his legitimacy for reaching the top

Key insight: Tang San is not "a good person forced to kill," but "a genius trained by institutions to no longer treat killing as a problem." This is precisely where institutional predation is most horrifying — it does not require you to be evil, only requires you to look on with indifference.

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Dou Institutional Predation | Anchor Symbiosis Alternative |
|------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Hunt soul beasts for Origin | Path D · human-engineered Dao-Law soul rings: generate Origin rings via man-made Dao laws, kill no beast throughout |
| Soul beast = soul ring | Soul beast as one of Myriad Ranks, equally evolves via self-generation self-growth evolution law |
| Killing rationalized | Cultivation ethics and boundaries written into charter; develop increment, do not consume stock |
| Strong ones maintain old system | Lin Hao path: a victim of old rules awakens then overturns old rules, reconstructs rules |

The alternative's crux: rewrite "kill one beast to level one" as "cultivate one Dao-Law to nurture Myriad Ranks."

The bottom-extinguishing of this institutional predation is precisely Path D · human-engineered Dao-Law soul rings: when soul rings can be man-made, the thinking of "treating soul beasts as consumables" loses its institutional soil. Lin Hao, as a victim of old rules, demonstrates another kind of genius — he does not treat the other as a material library to inventory attributes, but condenses the Origin needed for breakthrough from his own Dao Origin; each ring need not be taken, killing is no longer a legal procedure. This is precisely the way to root predation out of geniuses' instinct.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: low**. Late patches like "spirit souls" and "spirit-soul ring fusion" are essentially partial retcons of early "must hunt"
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. To ease hunting controversy, the author keeps patching, but never touches the underlying "killing to level" law
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high**

The most insidious part of institutional predation is that it makes geniuses executors without realizing it. Every time Tang San murmurs "which ring, what attribute" before hunting, he has already reduced another being to data. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants to dismantle is precisely this cognitive distortion: when the other is first a life, and only secondarily a resource, predation loses soil at the human-heart level.

Look at institutional predation along the causal chain: what Tang San's generation hunts is the soul beast's Origin cultivated over a hundred or thousand years; and this soul beast could have continued to evolve and feed back to the plane's ecology. One hunt simultaneously erases a future high-tier life and a stretch of plane Origin that could have accumulated. The predator thinks he has earned a ring, but actually loses an entire future energy flow — this is the deep cost of the negative-sum loop.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: killing to level is double energy loss
- **Anti-predation**: predation written into leveling legal procedure, internalized into instinct
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's predation is "privately hoarded knowledge"; Tang San's predation is "killing to seize Origin" — the latter is harder, more irreversible
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: institutional predation first erases soul beasts' life subjectivity
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: a system that treats killing as fuel cannot symbiose with any external civilization
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Tang San's hand is steady, his blade fast, his road smooth — the problem lies in the road itself. Heng Yuan Zhou does not judge every soul beast he killed, only points out one thing: when leveling must step on other races' bones, no matter how glorious this road is, it is a dead end.
