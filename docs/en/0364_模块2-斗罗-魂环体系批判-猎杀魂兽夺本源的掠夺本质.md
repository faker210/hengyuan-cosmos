# Entry 364 | Dou Continent · Soul-Ring System Critique: The Predatory Essence of Hunting Soul Beasts for Origin

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dou Continent System Decomposition (Entry 5) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 15, 19

## Introduction: A cultivation model that writes the word "seize" into its mechanism

The soul ring is Dou Continent's cornerstone mechanism: every time a Soul Master passes a ten-tier bottleneck, a soul ring must be obtained to break through, and a soul ring can only come from a soul beast's death. In other words, every leap of this cultivation model takes "seizing the Origin another being cultivated for a lifetime" as its sufficient and necessary condition. It is not incidentally predatory; it is itself predation. This entry, taking the law-first axiom as core, anatomizes the predatory essence of the soul-ring mechanism head-on. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: **high (gift-style leaning oligarchic)**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- **Origin mismatch**: a soul beast's century-bitterly-cultivated Origin is compressed into a ring that only helps one person level one tier; energy loss over 90%
- **Stock dependence**: soul rings have no "artificial synthesis" path; every advance consumes finite soul-beast stock
- **Structural lock**: because soul rings are a hard constraint, the human cultivation route is forced to deeply bind with hunting; technically there is no second road

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Negative-sum loop**: hunt more → fewer soul beasts → higher rings harder → cultivation more involuted; civilization energy net declines
2. **Intergenerational overdraft**: the current generation uses up soul-beast stock that could have been shared by ten thousand generations; what is left to descendants is desert
3. **Deformed knowledge**: all cultivation knowledge accumulates around "how to hunt more efficiently," not "how to develop increment"

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

The soul-ring mechanism takes predation to the "atomic" level:

- **Every ring must be seized**: Tang San, from first ring to ninth, every ring is a killing; predation is evenly spread across the whole growth, hence harder to detect
- **Year-worship**: pursuing "thousand-year," "ten-thousand-year," "hundred-thousand-year" rings is essentially pursuing "richer plunder" — predation scale is aestheticized, glorified
- **Attribute calculation**: choosing a ring first checks attribute match; the other's Origin is treated as a "material library" inventoried item by item

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Dou Soul-Ring Mechanism | Anchor Symbiosis Alternative |
|-------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Hunt soul beasts for Origin | Path D · human-engineered Dao-Law soul rings: condense Origin via man-made Dao laws, zero killing |
| Stock predation | Develop increment Origin; energy loop net-grows |
| Other = material | Other = republican member, self-generation self-growth evolution law guarantees |
| Every ring must be seized | Dao laws grow by themselves; breakthrough comes from one's own Dao Origin accumulation |

The alternative's crux: a soul ring should not be "a robbed ring," but "a Dao-Law grown by oneself." Lin Hao is precisely the model of "breaking through without killing one beast" on Path D.

The soul-ring mechanism's alternative is not "switch to a kinder kind of hunting," but Path D · human-engineered Dao-Law soul rings — condensing a functionally equivalent Origin ring via man-made Dao laws, moving the ring's source from the other's corpse to one's own Dao. Lin Hao is precisely the model of zero-killing breakthrough: he does not rely on thousand-year ten-thousand-year soul beasts for show, but on Dao laws growing by themselves; attribute matching relies not on inventorying soul beasts, but on his own Dao-Origin deduction. The ring remains, only it changes from loot back to something grown by oneself.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: low**. Soul-ring year rules are repeatedly broken by "adventure rings," "innate rings"
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. Late "spirit souls" are almost a direct negation of "must hunt"; early iron laws exist in name only
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high**

The reason the soul-ring mechanism is a textbook of predation is that it takes predation to the atomic level — every level must seize once, evenly spread across growth, hence harder to detect. Readers follow Tang San hunting all the way up and actually get used to "kill beast = level up." This narration-internalized predation is harder to reflect on than any villain.

The soul-ring mechanism's predation also shows in "year-worship." Pursuing higher-year rings is essentially pursuing richer plunder. When "thousand-year ten-thousand-year hundred-thousand-year" becomes cultivators' value yardstick, predation scale is aestheticized and glorified; hunting a hundred-thousand-year soul beast even becomes a feat to show off. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants to critique is precisely this narrative that packages predation as glory — it lets predators feel no moral burden.

Look at the soul-ring mechanism in the whole of civilizational history: it is an institution that monetizes "the other's life." A soul beast cultivates for a century; the Origin condensed is compressed into a ring that can be carried, traded, rank-compared; its life process is completely reduced to "which ring, what attribute" data. This reduction is predation's epistemological root: once life is digitized, killing changes from a moral problem to an arithmetic problem. The reason Heng Yuan Zhou wants to pull the bottom out is that what it wants to dismantle is not some hunting rule, but this thinking habit of converting life into numbers. Path D · human-engineered Dao-Law soul rings let cultivators condense Origin from their own Dao Origin; step one requires no conversion of any life into numbers — this is liberation at the epistemological level.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: 90% of Origin lost in hunting
- **Anti-predation**: soul-ring mechanism itself = predation mechanism
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo predates knowledge; Tang San predates life Origin — weight order of magnitude heavier
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the entire soul-beast species is filed as "consumables"
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: zero-killing man-made Dao-Law rings are the universal solution that can be exported
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root
