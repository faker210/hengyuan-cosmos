# Entry 365 | Dou Continent · Soul-Beast Extinction Crisis: The Ecological Disaster of Predatory Cultivation

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dou Continent System Decomposition (Entry 6) | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 46, 50

## Introduction: When an entire species is treated as "renewable resource"

The Myriad Ranks republican axiom requires all beings to have equal evolution qualification. Dou Continent's soul beasts are the most thoroughly defined race among Myriad Ranks — they are not neighbors, not kin, but "walking soul rings." As cultivator numbers grow and soul-ring demand inflates, the most direct consequence is soul-beast population collapse. This entry, taking the law-first axiom as core, argues this ecological disaster is no accident, but the inevitable product of predatory cultivation. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: **high (gift-style leaning oligarchic)**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- **High-tier populations collapse first**: rarer ten-thousand-year and hundred-thousand-year soul beasts are geniuses' prime hunt targets; the most precious gene banks disappear first
- **Food-chain rupture**: soul beasts have ecological functions in the forest food chain; their sharp decline desertifies entire hunting grounds
- **Irreversible loss**: a hundred-thousand-year soul-beast species going extinct loses the Origin diversity the plane accumulated over millennia

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Resource curve inversion**: more cultivators → fewer soul beasts → harder for latecomers to break through; civilization ceiling declines with population growth
2. **Hunting ground desertification**: hunting grounds like the Star Dou Great Forest degrade from "rich" to "exhausted"; the young generation has no rings to hunt
3. **Late patches to extend life**: relying on "spirit souls" and "farmed soul beasts" to extend life is essentially admitting the hunting road cannot continue

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Selective sympathy**: Tang San has warmth toward human-form soul beasts like Xiao Wu, but continues hunting the uncountable non-human-form soul beasts — sympathy only for "human-like," not for "merely beasts"
- **Scale-insensitivity**: at the individual level he kills only one each time, but accumulated, genius groups are precisely the main force of soul-beast extinction
- **No ecological rescue**: after becoming a god, he holds divine power enough to reverse the ecology, yet pushes no soul-beast protection institution

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Dou Hunting Ecology | Anchor Symbiosis Alternative |
|---------------------|-------------------------------|
| Soul beast = consumable | Soul beasts filed into Myriad-Ranks genealogy, protected by equal evolution qualification |
| High-tier extinct first | Alaya whole-network population monitoring; red-line species trigger intervention |
| Hunting ground desertified | Environmental Protection Law + life-circulation system; hunting grounds perpetual |
| Human-form exception | Myriad Ranks republicanism does not distribute rights by "human-like" |

The alternative's crux: Path D · human-engineered Dao-Law soul rings eliminate the "must kill soul beasts" need at the source; the ecological disaster is institutionally dismantled in advance.

What cuts off this ecological disaster at the source is the combination of Path D · human-engineered Dao-Law soul rings and Alaya population monitoring: the former makes humans no longer need to hunt soul beasts; the latter gives remnant soul-beast populations institutional protection. Lin Hao-style reconstructors will not continue selective blindness toward non-human-form soul beasts, but bring the whole soul-beast race into Myriad Ranks republicanism — it need not human-form itself to be treated as a life, not "a ring a few years short of being stripped."

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: low**. The Star Dou Great Forest goes from vast hunting ground to late "almost gone"; order of magnitude contradicts early
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. To supply rings for the protagonist, soul-beast resources fluctuate, entirely serving plot
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high**

Soul-beast extinction is not an apocalyptic episode, but a settlement table written into the setting. High-tier soul beasts extinct first; latecomers can only hunt low-tier; the overall ceiling moves down with stock. The Star Dou Great Forest from rich to exhausted is precisely the negative-sum loop's直观 curve — the more successful the hunt, the more barren the hunting ground.

Soul-beast extinction also has an overlooked cascading consequence: ecological-niche vacuum. High-tier soul beasts play key roles in the forest food chain and plane energy loop; after their sharp decline, low-tier soul beasts and plant spirits lose evolutionary checks; the whole hunting ground degenerates from ecological prosperity to desert. This secondary disaster is often harder to recover from than direct hunting — hunting can stop, but once ecology loses balance, it takes a long time to self-heal, and the Dou world never gave that time.

The ecological disaster also has an intergenerational-justice dimension. The current generation of human cultivators enjoys the dividends of hunting soul beasts, yet leaves the "no beasts to hunt" desert to descendants. This is not a fair intergenerational contract, but an overdraft. Heng Yuan Zhou's life-circulation system precisely ensures cultivation does not crowd out descendants' living space. The reason Path D is repeatedly emphasized is not only its zero killing, but that it makes each generation's cultivation incremental accumulation rather than stock overdraft — descendants inherit not a desert, but a world thicker than their parents'.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: high-tier soul-beast gene bank irreversibly lost
- **Anti-predation**: an entire race defined as consumables
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo excludes Myriad Ranks but at least does not hunt them; Tang San's system directly consumes one of Myriad Ranks
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: soul beasts are the race most deserving of rescue under the 99% Myriad-Ranks full-coverage claim
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: a system that actively exterminates neighbor races cannot coexist with multiversal civilization
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Soul-beast extinction is the bill written into the Dou world's setting. Heng Yuan Zhou's verdict: as long as the law that "leveling = killing" is not removed, extinction is not a crisis, but a settlement. What Path D aims to do is precisely cancel this account at the root.

In the final analysis, Dou Continent · soul-beast extinction crisis · ecological disaster of predatory cultivation described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
