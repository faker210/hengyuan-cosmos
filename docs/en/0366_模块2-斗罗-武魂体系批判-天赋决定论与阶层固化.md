# Entry 366 | Dou Continent · Martial-Soul System Critique: Talent-Determinism and Class Hardening

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dou Continent System Decomposition (Entry 7) | Benchmark base: Documents 08, 36, 51

## Introduction: That awakening at age six decides a lifetime

The Everyone-Road-Guarantee System's premise is "roads are not closed by birth." Dou Continent is precisely the opposite: every child awakens a martial soul at six; that moment basically decides a lifetime — whether it is a trash soul, an ordinary soul, or innate full soul power is already written in the bloodline. Martial-soul strength is hereditary, marriage-selectable, clan-monopolizable; cultivation qualification thus degenerates into birth qualification. This entry, taking the law-first axiom as core, anatomizes how the martial-soul system turns talent-determinism into institutionalized class hardening. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: **high (gift-style leaning oligarchic)**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- **Trash-soul people idle**: many who awaken trash souls are judged "this lifetime has nothing to do with cultivation"; their latent talent is directly voided by institutions
- **Bloodline monopoly**: top souls pass down only within a few families; talent resources are distributed by bloodline, not effort
- **Infant selection**: age six decides a lifetime; the vast majority don't even get one "try again"

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Upward channel narrowing**: cultivation qualification locked by clans and bloodlines; commoner genius upward mobility almost impossible
2. **Elite involution**: limited quality souls concentrated in a few families; mutual marriage, inter-marry alliances, forming closed circles
3. **Talent waste**: hundreds of millions of trash-soul people not included in civilization construction; the plane's largest human-resource idling

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Birth dividend**: Tang San is born of Tang Sect and Clear Sky Clan, two top cultivation aristocracies; his "genius" is first a product of birth
- **Monopoly as honor**: top families treat souls as private preserves; marriage outside must guard against "soul outflow"; double monopoly of knowledge and talent
- **No threshold breaking**: after Tang San becomes a god, he does not push "everyone can cultivate" reform; the soul threshold remains guarded by bloodlines

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Dou Martial-Soul System | Anchor Symbiosis Alternative |
|-------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Age-six awakening decides lifetime | Everyone-Road-Guarantee System; multiple tests, multiple chances |
| Soul bloodline monopoly | 300 Origin cultivation systems fully open; methods do not tie to bloodline |
| Trash soul = trash person | Myriad Ranks 99% full coverage; no "cannot cultivate" fate |
| Clan marriage-selection monopoly | Anti-monopoly written into charter; knowledge and talent publicized |

The alternative's crux: change "talent decides lifetime" to "effort + institutions give roads." Lin Hao's path is valuable precisely because he self-proves via Dao laws, not bloodline.

The key to breaking talent-determinism is letting "testing" not happen only once at six, and "roads" not only martial souls. Path D · human-engineered Dao-Law soul rings precisely provide this new road: people without martial souls can equally cultivate via man-made Dao laws; Lin Hao is the typical example of self-proving via Dao laws rather than bloodline. When the clan-monopolized soul is no longer the only pass, trash-soul people can be re-seen within the Everyone-Road-Guarantee System, and class hardening can possibly loosen.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Soul rules are largely self-consistent, but "mutated souls" and "innate full soul power" frequently break for plot
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium-high**. Late new souls and god-tier souls emerge endlessly; early soul genealogy is diluted
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high**

The martial-soul system's cruelty is that it treats one age-six test as a lifelong verdict. But talent is never single-dimensional: some mature late, some cross-connect, some have endowments beyond martial souls. Writing these people off is the plane's largest talent tax. What Path D wants to abolish is precisely this tax.

Talent-determinism's other harm is that it manufactures a "fate-acceptance culture." Soul-less people are taught from childhood "you are like this for life," so they voluntarily abandon self-improvement; those with souls see talent as everything and neglect postnatal effort. The whole society's talent view is distorted into "bloodline decides winner"; individual struggle's meaning is hollowed out. What Heng Yuan Zhou's Everyone-Road System wants to break is precisely this fate-acceptance instilled from age six.

Talent-determinism also manufactures a self-fulfilling prophecy: those judged trash-soul are dissuaded from childhood, so they really stay mediocre; those judged geniuses are supported from childhood, so they really rise. Society then looks back and marvels "indeed bloodline decides everything." But it does not see that those mediocre ones were institutionally sentenced and abandoned in advance. What Path D · human-engineered Dao-Law soul rings breaks is precisely this self-fulfilling curse — giving those judged "trash" the chance to prove they need not have been.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: hundreds of millions of trash-soul people institutionally idled
- **Anti-predation**: talent and knowledge bloodline-monopolized
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo anti-aristocratic monopoly, opens middle-low roads; Tang San himself is the aristocratic system's greatest beneficiary and maintainer
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: trash-soul people and collateral bloodlines are the first sacrificed by the martial-soul system
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: a system that decides talent by bloodline cannot open equally to Myriad Ranks
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

The martial-soul system is a fate drawn at birth. Heng Yuan Zhou does not deny talent differences, only opposes "writing talent differences into a final verdict." True fairness is not that everyone has the same talent, but that everyone has a road to walk.
