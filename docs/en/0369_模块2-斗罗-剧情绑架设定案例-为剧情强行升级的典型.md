# Entry 369 | Dou Continent · Plot-Kidnaps-Setting Cases: A Typical Example of Forced Upgrading for Plot

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dou Continent System Decomposition (Entry 10) | Benchmark base: Documents 09, 97, 17

## Introduction: When "plot needs" outweighs "setting is so"

The law-first axiom explicitly stipulates: plot must not kidnap setting. Dou Continent, however, offers a whole set of classic "plot-kidnaps-setting" cases — whenever the protagonist needs to leap ranks and counter-kill, the strength rules set early temporarily yield. This entry, taking the law-first axiom as core, inventories case by case this "forced upgrading for plot" operation and its harms. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: **high (gift-style leaning oligarchic)**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- **Cost of rank-leaping counter-kills**: the protagonist time and again defeats opponents he could not possibly beat, equivalent to declaring "the realm gap from hard accumulation is meaningless"
- **Numbers elasticized**: strength is no longer a hard metric, but a rubber band that stretches with plot
- **Supporting roles spinning empty**: supporting roles train hard by rules, yet always become the protagonist's stepping stone when plot needs

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Rule authority collapses**: all beings discover "rules can be rewritten by plot," so no longer respect rules
2. **Effort devalued**: since rank-leaping counter-kills become the norm, step-by-step cultivation looks foolish
3. **Expectation chaos**: no one can predict results by rules; civilization game degenerates to watching the size of the protagonist's halo

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Halo = predation**: the protagonist halo is essentially "awarding to the protagonist, for plot needs, victory that belonged to the opponent"
- **Exception addiction**: every "this once is an exception" overdraws rule authority
- **Others' experience useless**: supporting roles' rule-based cultivation is worthless before the protagonist's halo

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Dou Plot-Kidnaps-Setting | Anchor Symbiosis Alternative |
|---------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Rank-leaping counter-kill by halo | Breakthrough standards clear; realm gap = strength gap |
| Rules stretch with plot | Law-anchored permissions; rule changes must go through legislative procedure |
| Supporting roles as stepping stones | Plane Transcendent four-dimensional exam; victory and defeat have basis |
| Exception addiction | Setting-contradiction self-check table; exceptions recorded and corrected |

The alternative's crux: let results be decided by rules, not by "whomever plot needs to win."

To deal with plot-kidnaps-setting, Heng Yuan Zhou relies on rule rigidity. After Path D · human-engineered Dao-Law soul rings decouple breakthrough from killing, even if the protagonist needs rank-leaping counter-kills, he can no longer pad it with "temporarily kill a hundred-thousand-year soul beast." Every Lin Hao leap is on the open account of Dao-Law accumulation; others' hard training by rules is no longer wasted effort — results are decided by rules, not by "whomever plot needs to win."

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: low**. The more rank-leaping counter-kill cases, the less credible the strength system
- **Retcon-ending risk: extreme**. Plot kidnapping is precisely retcons' direct cause
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high**

Rank-leaping counter-kills are relished by readers, yet no one notices the cost behind them: every halo exception is an insult to those who train hard by rules. A supporting role's ten-chapters-trained realm, the protagonist crosses in one page. Rule authority is eaten by exceptions, one by one.

Plot-kidnaps-setting also has a side effect: supporting roles instrumentalized. To let the protagonist rank-leap counter-kill, supporting roles' strength must stretch with plot — sometimes a roadblock, sometimes a paper tiger. Supporting roles are no longer characters with independent logic, but knobs adjusting the protagonist's difficulty. This instrumentalization reduces the whole world to backdrops setting off the protagonist; the worldview's realism collapses with it.

Plot-kidnaps-setting also has a paradox worth pointing out: it claims to be "hot-blood," but is actually the least hot-blood writing. True hot-blood is the protagonist pushing within the limits rules allow; readers know he cannot cross the line, so their heart aches for his persistence. Rank-leaping counter-kills pull away this line; the protagonist wins casually, and readers' heartache loses its object. Late Dou is "satisfying" to be sure, but the tension of "can he hold on after all" is pulled away together with the halo.

Supplementary verdict: the essence of plot-kidnaps-setting is treating readers' trust as a credit card to overdraw. Its costs are three: first, rule authority decreases with exception count; second, supporting roles are instrumentalized as difficulty knobs; third, the tension of "can he hold on after all" is pulled away by the halo. Heng Yuan Zhou blocks all three at once via law rigidity — results decided by rules, supporting roles have independent logic, breakthroughs have open accounts.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: realm gap zeroed by halo; effort value damaged
- **Anti-predation**: halo awards opponent's victory to the protagonist
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's victories are largely within rules; Dou makes exceptions the norm
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: supporting roles and Myriad Ranks are reduced to tools setting off the protagonist
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: a system extended by halo cannot establish credit with external civilizations
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Plot-kidnaps-setting is long-form writing's most hidden suicide. Heng Yuan Zhou's verdict: let plot yield to law, and the story gets more stable the more it is written; let law yield to plot, and the story gets more virtual the more it is written.
