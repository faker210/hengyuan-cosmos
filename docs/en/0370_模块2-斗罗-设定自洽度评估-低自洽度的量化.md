## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- **Self-consistency cost spillover**: to maintain surface self-consistency, the author needs many explanatory patches, which themselves create new contradictions
- **Reader compensatory work**: readers are forced to make up "why this time is okay" themselves; cognitive burden shifted to the audience
- **Setting entropy growth**: setting written more and more; contradictions grow exponentially; self-consistency maintenance cost out of control

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Quantified score (out of 100):

| Dimension | Score | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Realm numerical consistency | 25/100 | Late twenty-layer overall inflation |

A self-consistency of 25 does not say Dou is worthless, but says its setting credit has been overdrawn to a dangerous line. Readers no longer believe "next time it will follow rules," and instead self-explain via "protagonist halo." When a worldview needs readers to lie on its behalf, it has already lost its qualification as a world.

Low self-consistency also backfires on the worldview's solemnity. When readers discover rules can be overturned by plot, they no longer take any setting of the world seriously — divine positions no longer sacred, realms no longer serious, everything is only "the picture the author wants you to see." Once a worldview loses seriousness, the values and thought it carries lose weight with it. Self-consistency is the bottom line of a worldview's dignity.

Setting self-consistency also concerns a world's "livability." If readers are to seriously step into a world, they must believe its rules are stable and predictable. Dou's rules are sometimes loose, sometimes tight; readers cannot truly move in — they know "rules loosen when the protagonist is in trouble," so they always stand outside the play as spectators. A low-self-consistency worldview is a world that lets readers only ever be tourists, never residents. Heng Yuan Zhou pursues high self-consistency precisely so its world can be truly lived in.

Supplementary verdict: self-consistency is not showing off, but a worldview's load-bearing wall. Low self-consistency means three things: readers cannot truly move in, setting cannot seriously carry values, new readers cannot easily trust. Heng Yuan Zhou, via the setting-contradiction self-check table, periodically audits and leaves every change on a traceable account. For ~25 self-consistency to recover, start by stopping "this once is an exception."
