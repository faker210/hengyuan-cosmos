# Entry 371 | Dou Continent · Divine-Realm Setting Critique: Closed Theocracy and Class Hardening

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dou Continent System Decomposition (Entry 12) | Benchmark base: Documents 49, 03, 30

## Introduction: A god is not the endpoint, but another kind of wall

In most web novels, "becoming a god" is the answer to every problem. Dou Continent's Divine Realm reveals a colder truth: becoming a god is not the end, but entering a more imposing hierarchy — priest, third-tier god, second-tier god, first-tier god, God-King; divine positions are not only closed, but layer upon layer oppressive. This entry, taking the law-first axiom as core, critiques the Divine Realm's closed theocracy and class hardening. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: **high (gift-style leaning oligarchic)**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- **Divine-position ceiling infighting**: deities fight internally for rank; energy consumed in infighting rather than construction
- **Theocracy monopolizes knowledge**: the Divine Realm's highest laws are not publicly revealed; the mortal world and low-tier gods have no way to learn
- **God-King autocracy**: the God-King has near-absolute control over Divine-Realm resources; power unchecked

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Class reproduction**: once Divine-Realm ranks are fixed, promotion channels close; classes replicate generation by generation
2. **No institutional descent**: gods' intervention in the mortal world is entirely by personal will; no institutional feedback
3. **God-King dependence**: Divine-Realm order ties to the God-King alone; God-King succession is turmoil

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **From oppressed to oppressor**: Tang San in the mortal world resisted the Spirit Hall hegemony; after becoming a god he himself became the new hegemony's peak
- **Divine positions hereditary**: distributing the most precious divine positions to blood kin, completing the turn from "revolution" to "holding court"
- **No institutions built**: holding theocracy yet building no power checks; divine rule replaces rule of law

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Dou Closed Theocracy | Anchor Symbiosis Alternative |
|----------------------|-------------------------------|
| God-King autocracy | Anchor Council legislation + Symbiotic Court judiciary + Supervisory Committee oversight |
| Divine positions hereditary | God-tier qualification open-examined; law-anchored permissions universally benefited all beings |
| Class hardening | Social structure and class mobility written into charter; promotion and demotion have basis |
| Intervention by personal will | Cross-plane intervention observe first then limitedly intervene; only transmit knowledge back |

The alternative's crux: turn the "Divine Realm" from another feudal dynasty into a republican polity with checks and balances.

The way out of closed theocracy is to turn the "Divine Realm" into a republican polity with checks and balances; Path D · human-engineered Dao-Law soul rings here provides open-exam standards for god-tier qualification — divine positions no longer family-privately granted by Tang San's family. Lin Hao-style reconstructors hold Dao laws, yet do not privately set up a divine dynasty, but make law-anchored permissions universally benefited all beings. Thus the turn from oppressed to oppressor will not repeat.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Divine-rank count and power relations repeatedly adjusted between sequels
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. Divine-Realm setting is a multi-part crossover disaster area
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high**

The Divine Realm's most ironic point is that it proclaims "becoming a god is liberation," yet internally is more imposing than the mortal world. Priests pressure second-tier gods, second-tier pressure first-tier, first-tier pressure God-King — the door mortals risk life to enter leads to another denser cage behind. Building a high dimension into a feudal dynasty is Dou's deepest misunderstanding of "transcendence."

Closed theocracy's other crisis is that it manufactures an endless staircase of "above the ceiling there is another ceiling." Above priests there are priests; above God-King there may be higher — strong ones are endless, but also forever without a true "top." This infinite internal friction makes deities spend endless lifespan in rank fights rather than construction. A theocracy with no true endpoint is a construction site that never stops work yet never delivers.

The Divine Realm's closed theocracy also masks a fact: its so-called "immortality" is actually "freezing." Deities no longer progress, no longer evolve, only repeat rank fights in eternity. This immortality is one step from death — it has no future. Heng Yuan Zhou's high-dimensional strong ones, by contrast, continuously build and iterate in eternity; they are immortal because they are always growing. Packaging "stagnation" as "eternity" is closed theocracy's greatest concept-swapping of transcendence.

Supplementary verdict: closed theocracy's paradox is that the more it wants eternity, the more it tends to freeze. Deities no longer build, only fight rank; no longer evolve, only guard achievement. This immortality is one step from death — it has no future. Heng Yuan Zhou's high-dimensional strong ones continuously iterate in eternity; immortal because always growing. Packaging stagnation as eternity is the deepest concept-swapping of transcendence.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: god-tier energy consumed in internal rank fights
- **Anti-predation**: divine positions hereditary = turning evolution qualification into family property
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo stops in the mortal world, at least does not privately set up a divine dynasty; Tang San directly moves the divine dynasty beyond the sky
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: within the Divine Realm, low-tier gods and mortal Myriad Ranks are equally pressed one layer down
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: unchecked theocracy cannot coexist equally with multiversal civilization
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Becoming a god is not freedom; in Dou, it is only changing to a more imposing cage. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: true high dimension should have no "God-King"; what it should have is Myriad Ranks' own council.
