# Entry 373 | Tang San vs. Tang Luo: Predatory vs. Gift-Style Comparison

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dou Continent System Decomposition (Entry 14 · Comparison) | Benchmark base: Documents 251, 04, 261

## Introduction: One treats "giving" as good, one treats "killing" as routine

Tang Luo (I Can See Combat Power) is the representative of gift-style civilization — he breaks aristocratic monopoly, popularizes human martial arts; though he did not overturn old law, at least he is "giving." Tang San is the representative of predatory civilization — every step of his climb uses killing to seize Origin as fuel. Both belong to "enlightened predators," but their texture is entirely different. This entry, taking the law-first axiom as core, compares the two civilizations: predatory and gift-style. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: **Tang San = high (gift-style leaning oligarchic)**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- **Tang-Luo-style**: clutching high-tier Great Dao and not releasing; waste in "hidden non-release"
- **Tang-San-style**: pouring energy directly into the killing loop; waste in "non-renewable consumption"
- **Difference**: Tang Luo's waste is "not spent"; Tang San's waste is "spent in the wrong place and irreversible"

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Tang-Luo path**: gift-style — person present, policy present; person dies, policy dies
2. **Tang-San path**: predatory — stock hunting; the more cultivation, the thinner the plane
3. **Conclusion**: Tang Luo's regression is "supply cut"; Tang San's regression is "hollowing out"; the latter's harm is deeper and more irreversible

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Tang Luo**: predation in privately hoarded knowledge, does not harm life
- **Tang San**: predation in life Origin, and institutionalized
- **Moral weight**: Tang Luo's "not releasing" can still be debated as timing not yet ripe; Tang San's "must kill" has no excuse to hide behind

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Dimension | Tang Luo Gift-Style | Tang San Predatory | Anchor Symbiosis |
|-----------|---------------------|--------------------|------------------|
| Predation object | Knowledge | Life Origin | No predation |
| Renewability | Can be opened | Irreversible | Develop increment |
| Degree of goodwill | Higher | Low | Institutional goodwill |
| Way out | Open high-tier Great Dao | Path D man-made Dao-Law rings | Myriad Ranks republicanism |

The alternative's crux: Tang San needs "changing institutions" more than Tang Luo does, not "changing to a kinder protagonist."

Tang San's killing predation is more irreversible than Tang Luo's privately hoarded knowledge, so the alternative must be more thorough: Path D · human-engineered Dao-Law soul rings directly abolishes hunting, rather than Tang-Luo-style reform of "open part of knowledge." Lin Hao's zero-killing breakthrough demonstration shows that for Tang-San-style systems, making the protagonist kind alone is not enough; the law that "one must kill" must be pulled out from the root.

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

1. **Modeling**: juxtapose the gift-style and predatory worlds
2. **Variables**: respectively inject "open knowledge" and "abolish hunting"
3. **Observation**: plane Origin net-value curve
4. **Review**: output "Gift-Style vs. Predatory Civilization Damage Comparison"

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency**: Tang Luo medium-high, Tang San low
- **Retcon-ending risk**: Tang Luo medium, Tang San extreme
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: Tang San = high; Tang Luo = high (gift-style)

Tang San is harder than Tang Luo — hard in that Tang Luo's predation can still be debated as "timing not yet ripe," while Tang San's predation is "must kill." Tang Luo only clutches knowledge and does not release; Tang San takes a life with every level. Between reversible and irreversible lies the survival of a species.

Tang-Luo gift-style and Tang-San predatory actually correspond to two imaginations of "goodness": Tang Luo believes strong ones give alms; Tang San believes strong ones seize. The former at least admits all beings qualify to be given alms; the latter does not even need that layer of warmth. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is to transcend both: neither alms-giving gift, nor seizing predation, but letting Myriad Ranks themselves grow into a form that no longer needs alms and need not be seized.

Tang-Luo gift-style and Tang-San predatory also correspond to two different exit difficulties. Once Tang Luo's giving stops, all beings merely lose a supply; once Tang San's predation stops, the whole cultivation system paralyzes from "no rings to hunt." This shows Tang San's system depends more on predation and is harder to reform. The reason Path D is key is precisely that it is not "pause hunting," but "replace hunting" — eliminating paralysis risk from the root, letting the Dou world complete transition without collapsing.

Supplementary verdict: Tang San is harder than Tang Luo, hard in that his predation is irreversible. Tang Luo only clutches knowledge and does not release; Tang San takes a life with every level. Between reversible and irreversible lies a species' survival. Path D must be more thorough toward Tang San — not open part of knowledge, but directly abolish hunting itself. This is the dividing line between negative-sum and recoverable.

One final line: Tang Luo's road may still be repaired; Tang San's road must be redrawn. Because every step of his treads on irreversible killing, no local patch can save this negative-sum line.

This is also the fundamental reason Heng Yuan Zhou tiers Tang San and Tang Luo separately.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Tang San's waste irreversible, Tang Luo's still recoverable
- **Anti-predation**: Tang San kills to seize Origin, weight order of magnitude heavier than Tang Luo's hoarded knowledge
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: this entry's core — Tang San is predatory, Tang Luo is gift-style; Tang San harder
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Tang San directly consumes one of Myriad Ranks; Tang Luo at least does not kill
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: predatory civilization is harder to export externally than gift-style
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Tang Luo, however limited, at least thought about "giving"; Tang San, however glorious, never thought about "not killing." Heng Yuan Zhou's ruler is simple: civilization's high or low does not depend on how strong the protagonist is, but on his attitude toward other races' lives.
