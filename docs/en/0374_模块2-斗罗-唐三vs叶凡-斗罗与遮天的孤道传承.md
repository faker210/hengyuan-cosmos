## Introduction: Two protagonists who "decide the cosmos alone"

Ye Fan (Shrouding the Heavens) is the representative of oligarchic civilization; Tang San is the representative of gift-style leaning oligarchic. The two share the same lesion — "lone-path inheritance": civilization's highest path ties to the protagonist alone; he present so the path is present, he retires so the path dies. This entry, taking the law-first axiom as core, compares Dou and Shrouding's two lone-path inheritances. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: **Tang San = high (gift-style leaning oligarchic); Ye Fan = medium (oligarchic)**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.
