## Introduction: Two people climbing up in a cruel world

Shi Hao (Perfect World) "becomes emperor amid mountains of corpses and a sea of blood"; his predatory color is equally heavy — war nourishes war, devouring Origin, suppressing Myriad Realms. Tang San climbs via systematic soul-ring hunting. One is iron-blood conquest in a chaotic age; the other is refined predation within institutions. This entry, taking the law-first axiom as core, compares the texture of these two predations: Dou and Perfect. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: **Tang San = high (gift-style leaning oligarchic)**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- **Shi-Hao-style**: energy expands with conquest, much consumed in war and devouring; civilization-tier sediment thin
- **Tang-San-style**: energy poured into family divine positions and stock hunting
- **Difference**: Shi Hao's predation is "chaotic-age unavoidable"; Tang San's predation is "institutional righteous indignation" — the latter lacks excused context

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Shi-Hao path**: chaotic-age conquest then establishes new order, but order still relies on combat power
2. **Tang-San path**: institutional predation in a peaceful world; regression is chronic hollowing
3. **Comparison**: Shi-Hao-style regression accompanies violent conflict; Tang-San-style regression hidden and lasting

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Shi Hao**: predation in "war nourishing war"; strong enemies are resources
- **Tang San**: predation in "beasts as rings"; beings are consumables
- **Key**: Shi Hao's predation mostly happens on friend-or-foe battlefields; Tang San's predation generalizes "the other as resource" to the entire soul-beast species
