# Entry 376 | Tang San vs. Heng Yuan Zhou: The Divide Between Soul-Ring Predation and Man-Made Dao Laws

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dou Continent System Decomposition (Entry 17 · Comparison) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 02, 10

## Introduction: One road kills to its end, one road is created

This is the Dou series' most critical head-on divide: Tang San's road is "kill soul beasts for soul rings" — every level consumes a stretch of non-renewable other-Origin; Heng Yuan Zhou's road is "Path D · human-engineered Dao-Law soul rings" — condense Origin by itself via man-made Dao laws, zero killing, net growth. The two are not a difference of degree, but opposite in direction. This entry, taking the law-first axiom as core, draws this divide clearly. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: **Tang San = high (gift-style leaning oligarchic); Heng Yuan Zhou = extremely low (network-style)**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- **Tang San**: energy loop is negative — hunt stock; 90% of Origin lost
- **Heng Yuan Zhou**: energy loop is positive — develop increment; Myriad-Rank Origin two-way circulation
- **Divide**: the former the more used the less, the latter the more used the more; the former is a consumption account, the latter is a growth account

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Tang-San-style**: stock exhausted = endpoint
2. **Heng-Yuan-Zhou-style**: law precedes, institutions chartered; civilization keeps growing
3. **Divide**: regression is not Dou's accident, but the mathematical inevitability of the "consume stock" road

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Tang San**: the other = resource
- **Heng Yuan Zhou / Lin Hao**: the other = republican member, Origin symbiotic loop
- **Divide**: predatory thinking's core is "eat the other as stock"; symbiotic thinking's core is "build increment with the other"

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative (Divide Master Table)

| Dimension | Tang-San Soul-Ring Predation | Heng-Yuan-Zhou Man-Made Dao Laws |
|-----------|-------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Origin source | Hunt soul beasts (stock) | Man-made Dao laws (increment) |
| Killing | Required | Zero killing |
| Loop direction | Negative-sum | Positive-sum |
| Dependency | High (gift-style leaning oligarchic) | Extremely low (network-style) |
| Protagonist | Tang San (rule beneficiary) | Lin Hao (rule reconstructor) |
| Ending | Stock depletion | Infinite expansion |

The alternative's crux: this is not "improving Dou," but "switching to a road with the opposite thermodynamic direction."

This divide lands on people as the Tang San vs. Lin Hao contrast: Tang San pushes old rules to the extreme, using soul rings to walk the world thinner and thinner; Lin Hao starts from zero on Path D, using man-made Dao laws to walk the world thicker and thicker. The former's endpoint is the God-King's lone peak; the latter's endpoint is Myriad-Rank symbiosis's plain. Heng Yuan Zhou does not deny the God-King's strength, only points out: a lone peak walked thinner and thinner cannot, after all, support a cosmos that can infinitely expand — the divide is not in talent high or low, but in thermodynamic direction choice.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency**: Dou low; Heng Yuan Zhou law-preceded, contradiction self-check, high
- **Retcon-ending risk**: Dou extreme; Heng Yuan Zhou near zero
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: Tang San high; Heng Yuan Zhou extremely low

This nine-fold output and zero-killing divide is essentially a thermodynamic-direction contest: the negative-sum road walks the plane thinner and thinner, the positive-sum road walks it thicker and thicker. Tang San chose the former, so his endpoint is a lone peak; Heng Yuan Zhou chose the latter, so its endpoint is a plain. Direction wrong, the stronger the more dangerous.

This divide's real-world significance is: it proves there is no natural link between leveling and killing. Dou welds the two together because the author needs a simple brute leveling engine; Path D pries them apart, proving Dao laws can be man-made, Origin can be incremental. Once readers accept "leveling need not kill," the legitimacy of Dou's negative-sum machine is shaken at the root — the divide is not only a setting contest, but an imagination contest.

This divide can also extend to readers' reading ethics. Dou lets readers thrill to hunting, equal to repeatedly rehearsing "the other as resource" in imagination; Path D lets readers thrill to self-creating Dao laws, equal to rehearsing "develop increment" in imagination. Imagination shapes habit, habit shapes reality. The reason Heng Yuan Zhou cares about this divide is not only at the setting level, but also that it affects how readers see the real world's others — the divide may be on paper, but its impact is in human hearts.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: negative-sum consumption vs. positive-sum growth
- **Anti-predation**: the other as resource vs. the other as member
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang San vs. Heng Yuan Zhou's gap is the total gap between predation and symbiosis
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: man-made Dao laws, at the source, let Myriad Ranks no longer eat each other
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: only the positive-sum road can be infinitely exported externally
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root
