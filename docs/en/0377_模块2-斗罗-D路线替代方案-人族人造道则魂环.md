## Introduction: Soul rings need not come from killing

Critique's endpoint is alternative. All of Dou Continent's lesions — soul-beast extinction, ecological disaster, institutional predation — tie to the stake that "soul rings must be obtained by hunting soul beasts." Path D · human-engineered Dao-Law soul rings is precisely born to pull out this stake: no longer seize Origin from the other, but condense functionally equivalent "soul rings" via human-self-deduced man-made Dao laws. This entry, taking the law-first axiom as core, presents this symbiotic alternative head-on. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: **Dou = high; what Path D inheritor Lin Hao walks is a new network-style road**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Path D squares the waste account entry by entry:

- **Abolish killing loss**: no longer consume a soul beast's century Origin for one ring; the 90% loss goes to zero
- **Origin net growth**: man-made Dao laws deduce increment from plane laws; the energy loop turns positive
- **Technology universal benefit**: the deduction-iteration system makes "ring-making" technological and replicable; no longer relying on adventures

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression (How Path D Blocks It)

1. **Cut stock dependence**: advancement no longer consumes soul beasts; the population-collapse timepoint is abolished
2. **Knowledge can sediment**: ring-making technology is public; descendants iterate on predecessors' basis
3. **Institutions replace persons**: Dao laws can be made by all; not tied to any one genius

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking (How Path D Pulls the Bottom Out)

- **No longer need "the other = resource"**: when soul rings can be man-made, the thinking of "treating soul beasts as consumables" loses institutional soil
- **Abolish "every ring must be seized"**: breakthrough comes from one's own Dao-Origin accumulation, not a hunt
- **Lin Hao's demonstration**: he awakens as a victim of old rules, yet uses Path D's zero killing to break through — proving the road can be switched

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative (Path D Detail)

Path D is not "kinder hunting," but "no longer hunting" — it moves the ring's source from the other's corpse to one's own Dao laws.

Path D's engineering points are three: first, Dao laws deduce increment from plane laws, rather than seize stock from soul beasts; second, ring-making technology is standardized via the deduction-iteration system, replicable and universal-benefited; third, Lin Hao makes it public, rather than turning it into a new private monopoly. The three combined, the "ring" changes from "a robbed ring" to "a Dao-Law grown by oneself"; leveling and killing are institutionally completely unbound.

- **Path-D self-consistency: high**. Man-made Dao laws are law-precedently defined, not stretching with plot
- **Retcon-ending risk: near zero**. New Dao laws join incrementally via the deduction-iteration system's caliber, not overturning old setting

The significance of man-made Dao-Law soul rings is not only "not killing," but more turning "ring-making" from adventure into technology. When it can be deduced, replicated, universally benefited, soul rings change from a genius's patent to all beings' public good. The democratization of leveling starts with this one man-made ring.

Lin Hao's contribution is standardizing and teachable-izing this "increment" extraction, turning it from one genius's epiphany into a technology learnable by all.
