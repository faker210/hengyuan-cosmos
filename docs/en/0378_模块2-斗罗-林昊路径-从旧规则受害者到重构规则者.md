Tang San is the old rules' greatest beneficiary — he was born above the rules. Lin Hao is the opposite: he was originally a victim under Dou-style old rules, yet did not walk the road of "becoming the next Tang San," but instead overturned the old rules themselves. This identity reversal is the most essential difference between Heng Yuan Zhou and all predatory systems. This entry, taking the law-first axiom as core, decomposes Lin Hao's "victim → reconstructor" path. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: **Dou = high; the new network-style road Lin Hao walks = extremely low**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.
