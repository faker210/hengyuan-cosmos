The Dou world's bottom-level prejudice is "human-centeredness": soul beasts are resources, human-form soul beasts are "exceptions," martial-soulless people are "trash." The republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings to possess equal qualification for evolution. This entry, taking the law-first axiom as core, gives the republican plan to break human-centeredness and martial-soul discrimination. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: **Dou = high; after republic = network-style**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.
