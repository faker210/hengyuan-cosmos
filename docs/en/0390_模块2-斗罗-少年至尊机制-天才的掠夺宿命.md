## Introduction: Young geniuses — prizes or cannon fodder

The Dou world abounds in "young geniuses": innate full soul power, top martial souls, early fame. It looks like an award for talent, but Heng Yuan Zhou sees the other side — these Youth Supremes are often fuel for the predatory system: the earlier a genius rises to the top, the earlier he is drawn into the cycle of hunting soul beasts, fighting over resources, and stepping-stoning the plot. This entry, taking the law-first axiom as core, dissects geniuses' predatory fate under the Youth-Supreme mechanism. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: **high (gift-style leaning oligarchic)**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.
