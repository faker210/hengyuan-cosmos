# Entry 391 | Dou Continent | Commoners' Situation: The Bottom Fate of Martial-Soulless People

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dou Continent System Decomposition (Entry 32 · Situation Analysis) | Benchmark base: Documents 08, 36, 51

## Introduction: The moment of awakening at six, most people are sentenced

The bottom line of the everyone-has-a-road guarantee is "not shut out by birth." The Dou Continent's silent majority are those commoners who awaken a "trash martial soul" or have no usable martial soul at all — they are sentenced by the system at age six: this life has nothing to do with cultivation; they can only be farmers, craftsmen, soldiers. All of Dou-world's stories belong to martial-souled geniuses; the hundreds of millions of martial-soulless people are only background. This entry, taking the law-first axiom as core, sees these silenced people. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: **high (gift-style leaning oligarchic)**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- **Trash-martial-soul people idle**: hundreds of millions of martial-soulless people's brains and muscles confined to bottom occupations
- **Talent misjudged**: one awakening at age six determines a lifetime; large numbers of "late-blooming" talents missed
- **Social burden**: lack of mobility between bottom population and cultivator class; social costs run high

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

1. **Class locked**: martial-soulless people have no upward road; society hardens
2. **Talent fault line**: civilization relies on a few martial-soul geniuses; basic creativity weak
3. **Contradiction accumulation**: the gulf between bottom and cultivators is a potential social explosion point

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Protagonist born of aristocracy**: Tang San belongs to a martial-soul clan; he cannot see, nor ever cares about, martial-soulless people's fate
- **Story blind spot**: almost no protagonist line for a martial-soulless person in the whole book
- **Structural disregard**: not taking martial-soulless people as "people" into view is itself a predation

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Dou Martial-Soulless | Anchor-Symbiosis Alternative |
|----------------------|-------------------------------|
| Six years decides a lifetime | Everyone has a road; multiple tests, multiple chances |
| Trash martial soul = trash person | Path D man-made Dao-Law rings; no martial soul can also cultivate |
| No upward road | Social structure and class mobility constitutionally written |
| Background props | Education / medical / housing guarantee full coverage |

The alternative's crux: no martial soul is not a trash person, but old rules do not recognize him. What Path D must do is give him a road.

The martial-soulless people's way out is precisely Path D · human-engineered Dao-Law soul rings' original intent: it makes that age-six awakening verdict no longer a lifelong sentence; martial-soulless people via man-made Dao laws can likewise cultivate. Lin Hao was himself a victim under old rules; his making this road public is precisely to open a door that did not exist before for those hundreds of millions of silenced people — they too deserve a road.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Self-consistency: medium**. Under martial-soul rules, martial-soulless situation largely self-consistent
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high**

Martial-soulless people are the Dou world's silent majority. That age-six awakening sentences them for life; thereafter no one asks. But a civilization's bottom color is precisely written by how it treats the most silent — Path D opening a door for them is precisely the response to this silence.

The martial-soulless situation also concerns civilization's troop source and creativity. A plane of hundreds of millions, if only a few martial-souled participate in cultivation, equals excluding 90% of the population from the highest-level civilizational creation. This manpower waste is more fatal than any resource waste. Path D opening a door for martial-soulless people releases not only individual fate, but the entire plane's sleeping creativity.

The martial-soulless situation should also be viewed in civilization's total population. If 90% of the population is excluded from cultivation, this plane's highest civilizational achievement is created by only 10%; its imaginative space, technical paths, philosophical exploration are all severely constrained. Path D opening a door for martial-soulless people equals reconnecting 90% of creativity to civilization's main trunk — the plane's upper limit is for the first time co-decided by all, not by a few.
