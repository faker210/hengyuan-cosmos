## Introduction: They are not rings; they are living lives

Soul beasts are the Dou world's greatest tragic group. They have their own cultivation, clans, wisdom (human-form ones prove this directly), yet in human jurisprudence they are wholly defined as "soul rings" — a walking cultivation resource. This entry, taking the law-first axiom as core, speaks specifically for this ethnic group institutionally objectified. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: **high (gift-style leaning oligarchic)**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Self-consistency: low**. Soul-beast magnitude and wisdom level contradict early-late
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high**

The soul-beast tragedy begins with a taxonomic demotion. Once written into the "resource" catalog, all their resistance becomes "prey struggling." What Heng Yuan Zhou must do is rewrite this catalog: they are called one of Myriad Ranks, not walking rings.

Soul beasts being defined as resources also brings a philosophical consequence: humankind loses the possibility of equal dialogue with neighboring races. When soul beasts are only rings, humans need not understand them, respect them, much less co-build with them. This cognitive closure traps the Dou world forever in the unipolar narrative of "humans eat beasts." Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad-Ranks republic is first of all a cognitive revolution — moving soul beasts from the "resource column" back to the "life column."

Soul beasts being defined as resources also makes "human-forming" a pitiful survival strategy. A soul beast must human-form to earn qualification to be regarded as human; those choosing to keep beast form, even with wisdom, cannot escape being hunted. This is a discrimination forcing neighboring races to "become like us to deserve to live." Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad-Ranks republic rejects this standard — soul beasts keeping beast form are still equal members, need not first become human to deserve breath.

Supplementary verdict: soul beasts forced to "human-form" to deserve living is a discrimination forcing neighboring races to become like humans to deserve breath. Wise soul beasts keeping beast form are still hunted. Heng Yuan Zhou rejects this standard — beast form or human form, they were already equal members, need not first become someone else to deserve existence.

Life's value never depends on whether it looks human. This sentence is the most direct refusal to "human-form to deserve living."

Equality needs no approval; life itself is the qualification.

Moving soul beasts from the resource column back to the life column is not charity, but correcting a classification error lasting a millennium. They belonged there all along — Heng Yuan Zhou only re-sticks the misplaced label to the right position.

This is Heng Yuan Zhou's thousand-years-late recognition of the soul-beast race.

This is precisely where its true weight lies.
