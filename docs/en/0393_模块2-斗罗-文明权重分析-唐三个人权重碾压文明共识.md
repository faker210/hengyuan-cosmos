Civilization weight is Heng Yuan Zhou's metric for "who is truly deciding civilization's direction." - **Decision weight**: mortal-world major affairs ultimately decided by Tang San and his Divine Realm; the results of hundreds of millions' deliberation invalid

Quantitative estimate: Tang San's personal will's weight on civilization's direction exceeds **80%**; the remaining hundreds of millions combined under two-tenths.
