# Entry 395 | Dou Continent | Series Summary: The Ultimate Catastrophe of Predatory Cultivation

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dou Continent System Decomposition (Entry 36 · Summary) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 01, 30

## Introduction: One person becomes a god, one world runs dry

Thirty-six entries of decomposition later, Dou Continent's full picture is finally clear: with the most gorgeous leveling thrill, it demonstrates predatory cultivation's ultimate catastrophe to the fullest — Tang San ascends the God-King's throne, soul beasts head for extinction, the Divine Realm closes and stagnates, martial-soulless people fall silent, twenty realms collapse, the setting retcons to ending. This entry, taking the law-first axiom as core, closes the entire Dou decomposition series. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: **high (gift-style leaning oligarchic)**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones (Series Grand Ledger)

- God-King-tier energy utilization only 15-20%; 80% of power used to deify the whole family
- Hunting soul rings causes ~90% Origin loss; plane energy net-consumed
- Divine Realm closed; the mortal world loses divine-tier feedback; thousands of years of standing still

In one sentence: the glory of one person becoming a god is bought with the whole world's depletion.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression (Series Grand Ledger)

1. Soul-beast stock depleted; evolution built on non-renewable consumption
2. Divine Realm closed and self-defending; the mortal world loses feedback
3. Divine positions family-hereditary; person present, policy rises; person dies, policy dies
4. Martial-soulless people structurally idled; civilization's basic creativity weak

Regression is no accident, but the mathematical inevitability of the two roads "consume stock + family monopoly."

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking (Series Grand Ledger)

- Tang San is old rules' greatest beneficiary, and also their firmest maintainer
- He internalizes the institutional predation of "kill soul beast for ring" into genius instinct
- He overthrew Spirit Hall's monopoly, but pocketed monopoly into his own family
- He drops in as a transmigrator, disturbing this world's causality without filing

Predatory thinking in him is not evil, but system — this is what most deserves vigilance.

One person becoming a god is not ascension; letting Myriad Ranks move forward together via man-made Dao laws and the Alaya Network is Heng Yuan Zhou's recognized true way out.

- **Comprehensive self-consistency: ~25/100 (low)**
- **Retcon-ending risk: extreme (this series' #1 negative textbook)**
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high (gift-style leaning oligarchic)**
- **Series positioning**: Dou Continent is the dual typical case of "predatory cultivation + plot kidnapping setting," Heng Yuan Zhou's five axioms' best negative footnote

Thirty-six entries' final landing point can condense to Heng Yuan Zhou's one verdict: predatory cultivation can send one person to God-King, but takes the whole world toward depletion. Tang San is not a villain; he only walked a road that walks thinner and thinner. What Heng Yuan Zhou gives is another road that walks thicker and thicker.

Geniuses still shine, but their shine is built on making Myriad Ranks thicker, not making the world thinner. This is Heng Yuan Zhou's complete answer to Dou.
