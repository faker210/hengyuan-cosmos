# Entry 396 | Manghuang System Decomposition (Overview): Ji Ning and the Lone-Path Cultivation System Verdict

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Manghuang Ji Full Decomposition (Entry 1 · Overview) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 07, 17

## Introduction: When a transmigrator treats the whole cosmos as his own dantian

Ji Ning of *Manghuang Ji* is I Eat Tomatoes' culmination of "dual-system flow": body-refining and qi-refining cultivated in parallel, from a youth at Black-White Academy all the way to Controller, finally rescuing his beloved and ascending the cosmos's peak. It is dense with thrills, leveling crisp, yet it is also one of the web-literature history's most extreme realm inflation and most thorough late-setting collapse samples. Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Universe) on the Ji-Ning system's verdict does not ask how many people he saved, how many he loved, but only whether the road he walked took Myriad Ranks to the heavens, or let Myriad Ranks continue paying for one person's transcendence.

As the Manghuang series' master outline, this entry establishes the full judging base — the **six top-level axioms**: anti-predation axiom (develop increment, do not consume stock), republican axiom of Myriad Races (all beings equal qualification for evolution), collective ascension axiom (strong ones do not ascend alone), causal self-discipline axiom (cross-plane observe-first then limited intervention), law-first axiom (law precedes, never retcon, setting self-consistency above plot convenience), and the universal equal infinite law. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Among them the **law-first axiom is core** — Manghuang is precisely the #1 negative textbook of "realm inflation, multi-book linkage retcon." Protagonist-uniqueness-dependency verdict: **Ji Ning = high-leaning-medium dependency / lone-path-cultivation-style civilization**.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Ji Ning's energy level is Controller-tier — cosmos birth, spacetime reversal, worlds born and die in one thought. But this energy's direction is lone-path style:

- **Dual-system resource black hole**: body-refining must devour fierce beasts, refine magic treasures, refine divine-demonic bodies; qi-refining must devour heaven-earth qi, seize cave-heaven blessed lands; the same person eats two shares of resources, consumption several times that of a single-system cultivator
- **Ultimate energy used for private purpose**: Controller-tier spacetime manipulation is first used for the private obsession of "resurrecting Yu Wei," not for transforming cosmic laws or universal-benefiting Myriad Ranks
- **Companions spinning empty**: Northern-Road Immortals, Bodhi, Three Purities and other strong ones have long lifespans, mostly used for taking sides, seclusion, gambling lives, not for sedimenting institutions

Conclusion: holding power to open heaven and earth, the first thought is to save his own Dao-companion — this is not evil, but the most typical lone-path cultivation energy misallocation.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Manghuang-world regression is structural, and more violent than Cover the Sky:

1. **Major power falls = era broken**: Three Realms war, Wujian Gate war, large batches of Return-to-Void and True Immortals fall in one battle, lineages buried with them; later generations re-cultivate
2. **War = resource zeroing**: every great calamity, realm domains shatter, cave-heavens collapse, accumulated cultivation resources physically evaporate
3. **Realm infinitely raised**: cultivation targets set higher and higher, lower-realm civilizational achievements treated as "clods," unmaintained, layer by layer abandoned

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Ji Ning is upright and loyal, but predatory thinking is defined by structure:

- **Double resources taken for granted**: dual body-qi cultivation equals openly declaring "the best two shares of resources should be mine"
- **Seizing treasure = justice**: secret realms, inheritances, magic treasures allocated by "adventure + strength," might-makes-right packaged as destiny
- **Transmigrator arbitrage**: crosses realms with past-life memory and mind advantage, yet never filed for causal self-discipline; civilization bears an external variable for free

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Sixteen realms layer upon layer; early "True Immortal = Three Realms supreme," late True Immortals reduced to gatekeeping servants; numerical steps overall watered
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. Late linkage with *Swallowing Starry Sky*; cosmos model, spacetime rules repeatedly overturned; early iron rules voided late
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high-leaning-medium**. Lone-path cultivation style — civilization survival depends on Ji Ning personally peaking, but he still has Bodhi, Nüwa and other pillars beside him, so judged "high-leaning-medium" rather than "extremely high"

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Controller-tier energy used to save one person; dual-system resource black hole devours generations of support
- **Anti-predation**: double resources taken for granted, seize-treasure-as-justice zero-sum narrative
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is gift-style (high dependency), Ji Ning is lone-path-cultivation-style (high-leaning-medium); Tang Luo at least systematically universal-benefited humankind, Ji Ning has not even built universal-benefit consciousness
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: demon races, plant spirits, mortal humans with no spiritual root are all fuel or background props in the lone-path structure
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: realms can infinitely inflate yet have no law constraint; the more expanded the more collapsed, the opposite of "infinitely expandable without collapse"
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Ji Ning is not a selfish man; he is the kind who would die ten thousand times for his beloved. But Heng Yuan Zhou does not ask about affection, only structure — when the cosmos-strongest's first grand wish is private, the whole cosmos is doomed to be only his background prop. Lone-path cultivation's ultimate limitation is not that the protagonist is not strong enough, but that only he alone is strong.
