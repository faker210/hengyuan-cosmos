## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Manghuang's energy waste is the most exaggerated among Myriad-Realms benchmark samples, at least four layers:

- **Aptitude layer**: Ji Ning is born with divine-demonic bloodline, simultaneously cultivating body and qi; this "dual-system compatibility sample" should have been a cosmos-level research topic, replicable and generalizable, yet compressed into one person's leveling engine
- **Resource layer**: body-refining must devour fierce beasts, refine divine-demonic bodies; qi-refining must seize cave-heavens blessed lands, devour heaven-earth qi. The same person eats two resource lines, consumption two to three times an ordinary cultivator's, and demand expands exponentially the higher the tier
- **Lifespan layer**: from Return-to-Void to Eternal Sovereign often counted in hundred-thousand epochs; Ji Ning's long lifespan is almost all used for "next instance, next treasure, next realm," not writing books, establishing institutions, teaching disciples
- **Ultimate layer**: after ascending Controller, spacetime reversal, cosmos rebirth power's first use is resurrecting Yu Wei — this private grand wish exhausts Controller-tier energy's first large-scale release

In Heng Yuan Zhou's words: this is connecting an entire galaxy's electricity to one person's flashlight.
