Among the six top-level axioms, the causal self-discipline axiom requires thoroughly rooting out civilizational regression and historical reversing; the collective ascension axiom requires strong ones to feed back to Myriad Ranks. This entry specializes in the Manghuang world's coldest structural law — **the day a major power falls is the hour civilization breaks generations.** The law-first axiom here is not rhetoric: Manghuang's regression is no accident, but the inevitable output of its "lineage follows the person, resources perish with war" law. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = high-leaning-medium. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

On the surface regression, but first point out the energy spinning behind it: Manghuang's strong ones pour massive energy into "mutual-destruction-style" great calamities — Three Realms war, Wujian Gate war; strong ones clashing's aftershocks flatten realm domains, evaporate spirit veins. This energy could have stabilized laws and reinforced worlds, yet in battle after battle between camps it turns to radioactive dust. **The energy waste of major-power war is the first driving force of civilizational regression.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Manghuang's regression is the overlay of three iron laws:

1. **Fall = break generations**: once the Three Purities, Bodhi, Northern-Road Immortal level fall in battle, their lifetime comprehension of laws is buried with them; later cultivators grope the same road from scratch
2. **War = zeroing**: after a great calamity, cave-heavens shatter, spirit veins sever, mortals die by the hundred million; civilization stock accumulated over ten million years physically returns to zero
3. **Raise = abandon**: realm targets raised all the way; old realm domains, old arts are seen by new generations as "low-tier," unmaintained, layer by layer abandoned — Three Realms in Chaos Immortal Realm's eyes are no more than a newbie village

Conclusion: Manghuang's civilization curve is not rising, but "spiral zeroing" — every tier ascended, the tier underfoot rots.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Behind regression is the lone-path logic Ji Ning represents's disregard for "civilization stock":

- **Values affection not institutions**: Ji Ning bleeds for Yu Wei, for his tribe, yet never thinks "how to make the next great war not zero things"
- **Strong ones as chess pieces**: major powers in narrative are "combat-power units"; their Dao insights are not civilizational assets, but experience packs or cannon fodder on the protagonist's road
- **Ascend = abandon**: after cultivating to higher realms, duty to maintain lower realm domains is near zero — gone and no longer cares

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Great calamity's "destructive power" and "recovery power" both swing with plot; early heaven-rending and earth-destroying, late casually rebuilt
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium-high**. Three Realms, outside Three Realms, Chaos Immortal Realm's geographic boundaries redrawn several times; old realm domains' "destruction" often quietly resurrected by new setting
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high-leaning-medium**. Whether civilization recovers after a great calamity depends on whether Ji Ning has spare strength to clean up

Worth emphasizing: Manghuang's regression is not slow, but negative-sum; Cover the Sky at least has a recovery period between two dark upheavals, while Manghuang even the recovery period is drained by late inflation — old realm domains just catch their breath, a new higher tier defines them as low-tier again, even the meaning of rebuilding is denied. This is the most insidious regression: civilization is not only broken, but persuaded that being broken means no need to rebuild. The Alaya Network is the antidote precisely because it permanently stores the experience of rebuilding once, so the second, third great calamity no longer starts from zero.
