# Entry 399 | Manghuang System Decomposition · Ji-Ning Predatory-Thinking Decomposition: Resource Consumption on the Cultivation Road

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Manghuang Ji Full Decomposition (Entry 4) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 18, 22

## Introduction: a kind person can also stand at the top of a predatory structure

The six top-level axioms open with the anti-predation axiom — develop increment, do not consume stock, the Great Dao fully open at all tiers. This entry specializes in Ji Ning's predatory thinking. Must first explain: Ji Ning is not a villain; he values affection, keeps his word, protects his own; in the Manghuang world he already counts as a rare good person. But in Heng Yuan Zhou's definition, **predatory thinking is judged not by malice but by structure** — whether you stand at the top of this jurisprudence of "strong ones seize stock, might makes right" and take it as self-evident. The law-first axiom requires us to pull this jurisprudence itself into the sunlight for inspection. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = high-leaning-medium. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The first consequence of predatory thinking is the energy waste of resource allocation. Every step of Ji Ning's road is "consuming stock": devouring fierce beasts for body-refining, seizing spirit veins for qi-refining, entering secret realms to grab inheritances. This stock could have supported a generation via "develop increment," but under predatory jurisprudence it is defined as "ownerless, the able take" — so the best grabber takes the most, yet resource utilization is pitifully low.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How predatory jurisprudence manufactures regression:

1. **Stock gets scarcer the more grabbed**: cave-heavens, spirit veins, heaven-earth treasures are all closed stock; under zero-sum games, one person becomes Dao means many lose Dao
2. **Latecomers have no road**: predecessors grab all easy-to-mine resources; later generations can only gnaw hard bones; overall cultivation threshold rises generation by generation
3. **Strong ones define ownership**: whoever is strong interprets "what is ownerless"; the weak do not even qualify to say "this is mine"

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

This is this entry's core, decomposing Ji Ning's predatory structure layer by layer:

- **Resource privatization narrative**: every spirit treasure, every beast pill, Ji Ning's first reaction is "can I refine it," not "whose was this originally, is it public or not." Seizing treasure is in narrative beautified as "adventure"
- **Dual-system double predation**: others grab one share, Ji Ning grabs two — body-refining needs fierce beasts, qi-refining needs spirit qi; his predation coverage naturally doubles
- **Causation not settled**: the transmigrator arrives with past-life advantage; what he plunders is not only the Manghuang world's material stock, but also the "time-difference dividend," yet never settles this cross-plane account
- **The "righteous rogue" absent**: Ji Ning never reaches that step — tearing up the old jurisprudence of "the able take" and rewriting resource-allocation rules as Myriad-Ranks public. He is an orderly strong one, not a revolutionary rogue

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. "Ownerless" boundaries move with plot; early rare materials later casually obtainable
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium-high**. Total resource volume continuously watered to serve protagonist leveling; early scarcity constraints become nominal late
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high-leaning-medium**. If predatory jurisprudence had no Ji-Ning-this-"strongest-predator" maintaining order, it might collapse even faster

Add another layer: predatory thinking in Manghuang is even packaged as the protagonist's virtue. When Ji Ning grabs treasures, narrative emphasizes he protects his own, keeps his word, does not indiscriminately kill; thus predation becomes righteous predation. Heng Yuan Zhou does not deny his personal conduct, but precisely therefore it is more dangerous — a predator with no moral flaw makes readers mistakenly think this jurisprudence itself has no problem. What truly needs changing is not Ji Ning's character, but the rules that force him to grab; as long as the rules remain, swap in a good person, and Myriad Ranks are still eaten clean.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: predatory mining utilization low; large stock lost in fighting
- **Anti-predation**: "the able take" zero-sum narrative is Ji-Ning-road's bottom jurisprudence
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least broke aristocratic monopoly; Ji Ning never challenged the jurisprudence itself of "strong ones seize resources"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: predation's endpoint is Myriad Ranks supporting one clan one person; demon races, mortals are all stock
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: a closed stock pool cannot support infinite expansion; predatory jurisprudence is fundamentally opposed to hyper-multiverse full openness
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

## Conclusion

Ji Ning's kindness is real, but kindness cannot stop structure. A good person standing at the top of the predatory pyramid only makes this pyramid look less evil — yet its efficiency at squeezing Myriad Ranks has not dropped one bit. What Heng Yuan Zhou must ask is not "is Ji Ning bad," but "should this jurisprudence that forces even good people to grab be swapped out wholesale."
