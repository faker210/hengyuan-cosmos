# Entry 400 | Manghuang System Decomposition · Qi-Refining-System Critique: The Zero-Sum Game of Devouring Heaven-Earth Qi

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Manghuang Ji Full Decomposition (Entry 5) | Benchmark base: Documents 15, 18, 04

## Introduction: cultivation's first principle is treating heaven and earth as an ATM

Among the six top-level axioms, the anti-predation axiom requires developing increment and not consuming stock; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings equal evolution. This entry specializes in decomposing Manghuang's **qi-refining system** — this system is almost the textbook definition of "predating stock": cultivators breathe in heaven-earth qi; qi is finite stock; you take one more breath, others take one less. The law-first axiom reminds us: to judge a cultivation system's good or evil, look at whether its energy loop is "blood-drawing" or "symbiotic." Protagonist uniqueness dependency = high-leaning-medium. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The qi-refining system's energy waste is systemic: after qi is drawn into a cultivator's dantian, most dissipates as heat in "breakthrough–battle–lifespan burning"; very little sediments into inheritable law knowledge. Top cultivators like Ji Ning are even qi black holes — wherever he goes, the local spirit vein thins a few degrees. **High absorption, low sedimentation, high dissipation** is precisely the energy-waste triangle.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

The qi-refining zero-sum game's regression logic:

1. **Spirit veins finite**: a domain's total qi is constant; the more and stronger cultivators, the less each person can draw, so "spirit-vein fighting" never stops
2. **Blessed lands monopolized**: qi-rich places are circled by great sects, great clans; those without backing can only hard-cultivate in thin lands — this is cultivation-version "land enclosure"
3. **Generation deterioration**: generations of cultivators extract make spirit veins non-renewable; later generations face an increasingly impoverished heaven and earth

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

The predatory core of Ji Ning's qi-refining road:

- **Breathing in is predation**: qi-refining's essence is to take heaven-earth qi originally shared by all beings into one's personal dantian. This system is zero-sum from day one
- **Cave-heaven blessed-land seizure**: Ji Ning all the way occupies caves, seizes spirit veins, turning "qi-rich zones" into private cultivation grounds
- **Qi before all beings**: when draining a domain's qi to break a realm, narrative never cares whether mortals in the same domain suffer reduced harvests, emaciation

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Manghuang Qi-Refining System | Anchor-Symbiosis Energy System |
|-------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Draw heaven-earth qi into dantian (zero-sum stock) | Develop incremental Origin energy; Myriad-Rank Origin two-way circulation |
| Blessed lands circled by sects monopolized | Resource allocation jointly contracted; qi universally accessible |
| After absorption mostly heat-dissipated | Energy circulation and transformation law; energy recovered and reused |
| Spirit veins extracted poorer and poorer | Energy standardization metering; develop increment without consuming stock |
| Strong ones alone occupy rich qi | 20% activation strategy; qi allocated by contribution not by fist |

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

1. **Modeling**: replicate Manghuang's "total qi — cultivator count — per-capita drawable" triangle
2. **Variables**: compare "zero-sum qi-pumping," "symbiotic circulation," "Anchor energy infrastructure injected" three models
3. **Observation**: spirit-vein half-life, per-capita cultivator energy, mortal-domain agricultural yield-loss rate
4. **Review**: quantified conclusion — under the zero-sum qi-pumping model, spirit-vein half-life sharply shortens as cultivator count rises; the symbiotic-circulation model lets civilization energy grow in step with heaven-earth energy

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Qi "concentration" rises and falls with plot needs; early as thin as a poor mine, late casually grabbing a spirit spring
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Total qi volume never given a hard constraint; as soon as the protagonist levels, "the world widens, qi grows richer"
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high-leaning-medium**. Qi allocation order maintained by top strong ones; once Ji Ning exits, fighting reignites

Also settle a fine account: qi-refining system's zero-sum is not only "you breathe, I have less," but that it does not even produce qi increment. Spirit veins are innate; cultivators are only porters; no one thinks about how to regenerate spirit veins or circulate qi. Heng Yuan Zhou's energy-circulation law is the alternative precisely because it turns cultivation from porters into developers — energy is not stock snatched from heaven and earth, but increment jointly nurtured by Myriad Ranks. Whoever first works through this layer jumps out of Manghuang-style zero-sum trap.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: high absorption, low sedimentation, high dissipation; qi largely turns to waste heat
- **Anti-predation**: breathing in heaven-earth qi is essentially privatizing shared stock
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least opened human arts; Manghuang cannot even achieve "qi accessible to all"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: demon races, mortals even the "right to breathe" is stripped by blessed-land monopolies
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: a zero-sum qi pool cannot support an infinite cosmos; must turn to increment development

The qi-refining system also has an often-overlooked time dimension: it makes cultivators treat "grabbing qi" as a lifelong career. A cultivator from entry to ascension, decades, centuries all fighting others for a breath of qi, simply has no spare to think "where does qi come from." Heng Yuan Zhou's energy-circulation law answers this head-on — energy can be nurtured, circulated, standardized. When cultivators discover qi no longer needs grabbing, only then do they first have spare to do other things. Cultivation's first thread of freedom is not being stronger, but no longer having to grab.

## Conclusion

The qi-refining system's original sin is not "breathing qi," but that it defaults qi as stock that can be personally enclosed. Heng Yuan Zhou's energy view is the opposite: energy should circulate, should increment, should make Myriad Ranks richer the more they use it, not poorer the more they draw. A civilization treating heaven and earth as an ATM will sooner or later discover — the day the ATM has no money either.

In the final analysis, Manghuang Ji · qi-refining-system critique · zero-sum game of devouring heaven-earth qi described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
