# Entry 401 | Manghuang System Decomposition · Body-Refining-System Critique: Predation Internalized as the Body Is the Battlefield

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Manghuang Ji Full Decomposition (Entry 6) | Benchmark base: Documents 12, 19, 04

## Introduction: when cultivation becomes refining other beings into your own bones

Among the six top-level axioms, the anti-predation axiopmost detests "treating the other as fuel"; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings equal evolution. This entry specializes in decomposing Manghuang's **body-refining system** — it is colder than qi-refining: qi-refining only breathes qi, but body-refining must devour fierce beasts, refine divine-demonic blood essence, refine alien creatures' bones and tendons into one's own body. Predation no longer happens externally, but is **internalized** into part of the body. The law-first axiom requires us to face squarely: does a "body-is-battlefield" cultivation method itself violate life ethics? Protagonist uniqueness dependency = high-leaning-medium. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Body-refining's energy waste lies in "irreversible devouring loss": when a fierce beast or divine-demonic blood essence is refined, most of its life information and law comprehension is lost in the refining process; only bone-muscle strength is retained. The stronger Ji Ning's body-refining, the more beings' complete lives are dimension-reduced to "a few muscles' density." **Compressing an entire life into one segment of hardness is enormous energy-level waste.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Body-refining internalized predation's regression logic:

1. **Hunting = ecological collapse**: top body-refining cultivators must hunt high-tier fierce beasts; entire ecologies are hunted empty; demon-kind civilization has not even qualification to exist
2. **Bloodline pollution risk**: refining alien blood essence makes the cultivator's own bloodline mixed; later inheritance difficult
3. **Kill-to-prove-Dao loop**: the stronger body-refining, the more must kill; the more kill, the more ecology collapses; civilization's available resources fewer and fewer

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

The predatory core of Ji Ning's body-refining road:

- **The other as material**: fierce beasts, divine demons, alien beasts in narrative are not "living beings," but "body-refining materials" — this is the nakedest species discrimination
- **Devouring = strength**: body strength = devour amount; strength and killing are equated
- **Dual-system double slaughter**: Ji Ning both qi-refines and body-refines, equal to pumping qi while killing fierce beasts; predation coverage doubled

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Manghuang Body-Refining System | Anchor-Symbiosis Life System |
|--------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Hunt fierce beasts to refine body | Life-circulation system: symbiotic cultivation, energy conversion; not kill-to-prove-Dao |
| Refine divine-demonic blood essence | Myriad-Ranks differentiated cultivation paths; demon races as equal races not materials |
| Body strength = devour amount | Cultivation ethics and boundaries: must not devour other lives as evolution means |
| Ecology hunted to collapse | Environmental Protection Law: cultivation must not destroy biosphere |
| Mixed bloodline hard to inherit | Alaya records life information; study not devour |

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

1. **Modeling**: replicate Manghuang's "fierce-beast population — body-refining cultivator hunt volume — ecological carrying capacity" model
2. **Variables**: compare "hunting body-refining," "symbiotic cultivation," "Anchor life-circulation injected" three paths
3. **Observation**: high-tier fierce-beast population survival rate, demon-kind civilization development index, cultivator physique improvement curve
4. **Review**: quantified conclusion — under the hunting body-refining model, demon-kind population endangered within a century; under the symbiotic model cultivator physique also improves and ecology is perpetual

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Body-refining materials' "tier" rises with plot; early refining a bear is already impressive, late casually refining a chaos behemoth
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Refining ratio, blood-compatibility never given hard rules; the protagonist always "happens to be compatible"
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high-leaning-medium**. Body-refining resources monopolized by top cultivators; ecological order suppressed by the strongest

The body-refining system also has an often-overlooked cost: it ties a cultivator's growth to killing dead-tight. Body strength equals devour amount, meaning the stronger a strong one, the more blood on his hands. This system's subtext is: kind people cannot cultivate the supreme physique — because they cannot bear to kill. What Heng Yuan Zhou's life-circulation system overturns is precisely this premise: strength need not be at the cost of blood; a physique raised by symbiotic cultivation is no worse than devouring fierce beasts. One who moves the battlefield into the body will eventually discover his own body is full of others' lives.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: complete lives dimension-reduced to muscle density; most information lost
- **Anti-predation**: "the other as material" is species-level internalized predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least did not treat alien beasts as pills; Manghuang body-refining directly writes foreign races into the menu
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: in the body-refining system demon races are not even a "race," only a mine vein
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: a system based on hunting, the wider it expands the wider the slaughter

The body-refining system also at the social level creates a strange loop: the stronger a cultivator, the more high-tier fierce beasts he needs to hunt; high-tier fierce beasts fewer; cultivators to keep getting stronger kill into more dangerous regions; the more they kill the more ecology collapses; finally even low-tier fierce beasts cannot be found. Heng Yuan Zhou's life-circulation system uses symbiotic cultivation to replace hunting, equal to installing an exit for this vicious loop — the cultivator gets stronger, but the prey remains. Sustainable strength is truly strength; strength bought by hollowing out the world around is only early overdraft.

## Conclusion

What the body-refining system most deserves criticism is not that it is strong, but that it defaults "the more other beings die, the stronger I become." Heng Yuan Zhou's life-circulation system gives another road: strength need not step on corpses; symbiosis can also buy physique. One who moves the battlefield into the body will eventually discover — his own body is only a material block in a larger food chain.

In the final analysis, Manghuang Ji · body-refining-system critique · predation internalized as body-is-battlefield described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
