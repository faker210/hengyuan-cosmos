## Introduction: when "True Immortal" goes from Three-Realms supreme to gatekeeping servant

Among the six top-level axioms, the **law-first axiom is this entry's core** — law precedes, never retcon, setting self-consistency above plot convenience. Manghuang's sixteen realms (Post-Heaven → Pre-Heaven → Purple-Fu → Myriad-Forms → Yuanshen → Return-to-Void → True Immortal → Immortal → Pure-Yang True Immortal → Da Luo Dao Ancestor → Chaos Immortal → World Realm → Life-Death Dao Lord → Eternal Sovereign → Supreme → Controller) is the extreme sample of web-literature realm inflation. Early "True Immortal = Three-Realms legend," late True Immortal reduced to Chaos Immortal Realm's gatekeeping servant; early "Return-to-Void can open a sect," late Return-to-Void does not even count as cannon fodder. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = high-leaning-medium. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Realm inflation's energy waste lies in **old realms' sedimentation wholly voided**: the "True Immortal" a cultivator in early story hard-cultivates a lifetime to reach has no value in new setting, equal to their lifetime effort devalued by history. Every realm Ji Ning breaks is not adding to the original civilization, but hollowing out the meaning of the tier underfoot — energy not wasted in battle, wasted in "the arbitrary switching of interpretive right."

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How realm inflation manufactures regression:

1. **Numerical watering**: the early carefully built numerical ladder (century Foundation, millennium Golden Core) is wholly diluted by late "casual breakthroughs"
2. **Authority devalued**: once-strong ones are redefined in the new realm system as "weak"; civilization's consensus on "what is strong" collapses again and again
3. **Target drift**: cultivation target always in the next tier; no one settles down to build the current tier; civilization forever in "preparation phase"

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Behind realm inflation is the protagonist's predation of "interpretive right":

- **Numbers rise with the protagonist**: the realm ladder's expansion is not the result of law evolution, but to give Ji Ning something to level — rules tailor-made for the protagonist
- **Old strong ones' sacrifice**: to make new realms sound more impressive, old strong ones are quietly downgraded; their lifetime fame is stripped by plot
- **Endless greed**: sixteen realms not enough, still adding upward — this is the author-level projection of "all-beings' Origin grasping · shortcut mind-demon"

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: low**. Sixteen realms layer upon layer watered; numerical ladder overall collapsed; early constraints late nominal
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. Realms no upper limit, no hard rules; author adds as he wants; readers can no longer predict late from early rules
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high-leaning-medium**. Realm inflation entirely serves Ji Ning's leveling path; meaningless without the protagonist

Realm inflation's other consequence is reader numbness to numbers. Early a Purple-Fu cultivator founds a sect; late a Purple-Fu cultivator sweeps the courtyard on the street. Readers are not persuaded by plot, but numbed by numbers repeatedly washed away. The reason Heng Yuan Zhou's nine-realm framework dares to be fixed is that from the start it accepts each tier's ceiling — Sense Origin is Sense Origin, will not become Boundary-Breaking because the protagonist needs it. Realms with no ceiling sound free, but actually make everyone lose the ruler measuring strength.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: old realms' sedimentation wholly voided; lifetime hard-cultivation devalued by history
- **Anti-predation**: protagonist's monopoly of "numerical interpretive right"; old strong ones' fame stripped by plot
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo system realms (capped at Fierce Realm) at least have a top; Manghuang has no top at all, inflation out of control
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the higher the realm, the fewer care about lower-realm beings; inflation is only the top's self-inflation
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: looks like infinite expansion, but actually every tier expanded collapses one tier; the opposite of "infinitely expandable without collapse"
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power is inherent in all; the easygoing retain a guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin erases predatory motivation, predatory involution loses meaning from the root

Realm inflation is not "grand vision," but "rules not kept." Heng Yuan Zhou with "nine realms fixed + two realms appendix" proves: true infinite expansion is the framework first nailed dead, then increment filled in, not the framework changing with the protagonist's appetite. Sixteen layers sound grand, but taken apart every layer slaps early in the face — this is the cost of law not preceding.
