## Introduction: when two novels share one cosmos, setting becomes mutual sabotage

Among the six top-level axioms, the **law-first axiom** here is almost a "truth-revealing mirror" — never retcon, never end in collapse, setting self-consistency above plot convenience. Both Manghuang Ji and *Swallowing Starry Sky* are I-Eat-Tomatoes works; late they are forcibly compiled into one cosmos model, so two separately valid early settings crash into each other in linkage: cosmos model, spacetime rules, strong-one tiers rewritten repeatedly. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = high-leaning-medium. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Retcon itself is the greatest energy waste: to make a new setting cohere, the author must tear down the early carefully laid worldview and retell it; readers to follow the new setting must repeatedly overturn old understandings. Ji Ning's Controller position, after two-book linkage, its "Controller" weight is also redefined — the early-established "cosmos peak" cognition late proves to be only a local corner in some larger cosmos. **All early worldview trust is depreciated in one go during linkage.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How linkage retcon manufactures regression feeling:

1. **Cosmos boundary redrawn**: Manghuang's "cosmos" after linkage shrinks to a corner in a larger model; all early "cosmos-tier" descriptions devalued
2. **Rules mutually exclusive**: Manghuang's law system and Swallowing Starry Sky's cultivation system (Martial-Artist — Thought-Form — Starry-Sky tier) originally incompatible; forced merger makes rules fuzzy
3. **Foreshadowing abandoned**: many foreshadowings Manghuang early laid are shelved or rewritten for linkage needs; readers' expectations systematically dashed

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Behind retcon is the author-level monopoly of "setting interpretive right":

- **Setting serves business**: multi-book linkage is essentially IP operation; to continue popularity does not hesitate to overturn early setting

- **Readers treated as tools**: readers' emotional investment in old setting is treated by the author as consumable stock — this is another kind of "stock predation"

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: low**. The two works' law systems are mutually exclusive; after linkage cannot both hold
- **Retcon-ending risk: extreme**. Multi-book linkage is web-literature's retcon disaster area; Manghuang and Swallowing Starry Sky's cosmos model to this day has no recognized self-consistent explanation
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high-leaning-medium**. After linkage Ji Ning's "peak" status relies on the author's redefinition, not law guarantee

Linkage retcon also has a hidden cost: it puts single-work old readers in an awkward position. Manghuang readers spent over a million characters building a cosmology, only to be notified after Swallowing Starry Sky linkage that the cosmos they thought was just a bubble. This kind of disrespect for old readers is essentially treating existing readers as consumable business assets. Heng Yuan Zhou's version management goes the opposite direction — every bit of old readers' trust is non-depreciable asset; new setting can only join incrementally, must never go back to deny every word they have read.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

- **Anti-energy waste**: early worldview trust depreciated in one linkage

Retcon-ending also has a scoring dimension: do readers still believe. This equals the author personally scrapping his own rule system. The reason Heng Yuan Zhou places the law-first axiom last among five axioms yet makes it this entry's core is precisely that once the rule system loses credit, the whole work's worldview collapses by half.

Multi-book linkage is not the original sin; the original sin is doing linkage without version management or self-consistency check. Heng Yuan Zhou's approach is plain: new dimensions all via appendix, old setting not one character changed. Manghuang and Swallowing Starry Sky's crash tells us — the bigger the worldview wants to be, the more must first nail "rules must not be casually overturned," otherwise the bigger the messier.
