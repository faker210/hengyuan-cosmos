# Entry 404 | Manghuang System Decomposition · Plot-Kidnapping-Setting Cases: The Typical Forced-Breakthrough-for-Plot

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Manghuang Ji Full Decomposition (Entry 9) | Benchmark base: Documents 09, 97, 14

## Introduction: when "time to break through" matters more than "time to cultivate"

Among the six top-level axioms, the **law-first axiom** states clearly: plot must not kidnap setting; setting self-consistency above plot convenience. Manghuang Ji is a dense sample of "plot kidnapping setting" — whenever Ji Ning falls into a plot desperate situation, the early hard-won iron rules like "requires millennia of hard-cultivation," "requires heaven-earth treasures," "requires comprehension epiphany," are one-click broken by "life-death moment epiphany," "natal divine ability awakening," "cave-heaven adventure" tropes. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = high-leaning-medium. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Plot-kidnapping-setting's direct cost is early hard-cultivation buildup wasted: the realms Ji Ning methodically accumulates early become dispensable after a few "epiphany breakthroughs" — since at a desperate moment he can surge, what meaning does ordinary hard-cultivation have? **Cultivation's seriousness is overdrafted by plot; readers' trust in "effort" is repeatedly consumed.** This is narrative-tier energy waste.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How plot-kidnapping-setting makes civilizational cognition regress:

1. **Rules unpredictable**: readers cannot judge "what situations allow breakthrough," because breakthrough conditions swing with plot emotion
2. **Effort devalued**: since epiphany exceeds hard-cultivation, other cultivators' millennia of hard-cultivation in the civilization becomes a joke
3. **Chance overwhelms necessity**: civilization evolution relies on "protagonist adventure" not "law regularity"; knowledge cannot be inherited

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Behind plot-kidnapping-setting is the protagonist's monopoly of "rule-exception right":

- **Others hard-cultivate, he epiphanizes**: the same realm, others need a millennium; Ji Ning breaks through on the line of life and death — rules work for others, not for him
- **Natal divine ability's plot summons**: every time a comeback is needed, a new divine ability, new transformation happens to awaken
- **Fortune as privilege**: narrative packages "protagonist halo" as fortune; essentially a privilege above law

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: low**. Breakthrough conditions swing with plot emotion; no stable recomputable rules
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. Plot-kidnapping is retcon's pre-symptom; once accustomed to changing rules for plot, late inevitably comprehensively collapses
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high-leaning-medium**. All rule-exception rights concentrate on Ji Ning alone; others cannot enjoy

What plot-kidnapping-setting hurts most is actually other characters. When Ji Ning can always epiphany-comeback at life-death moments, those supporting characters who methodically hard-cultivated a millennium become a joke — their effort is worthless before the protagonist's plot needs. Readers thrill on the surface, but inside know: this set of rules only works for the protagonist. Heng Yuan Zhou legislating breakthrough conditions is precisely to make supporting characters' effort also count, to make millennia of hard-cultivation not the protagonist's footnote, but a path anyone can repeat. Once rules only protect the protagonist, the blood of others in the civilization was shed in vain.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: early hard-cultivation buildup overdrafted by epiphany tropes
- **Anti-predation**: protagonist monopolizes "rule-exception right"; others hard-cultivate while he epiphanizes
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo system at least breakthrough has clear thresholds; Manghuang's threshold moves with plot
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: rule-exception right belongs to the protagonist alone; Myriad Ranks forever hard-cultivate by old rules
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: a worldview with unstable rules, the more expanded the more loopholes

Plot-kidnapping-setting also has a side effect: it makes the word "effort" cheap in Manghuang. Readers seeing Ji Ning epiphany-comeback, think not "this is the result of his effort," but "anyway the protagonist will win." Once effort decouples from result, other hard-cultivators' images in the civilization all blur. Heng Yuan Zhou legislating breakthrough conditions is to make effort hard currency again — meet conditions and you can break through, not meet and you cannot, regardless of who you are. Equality before rules is the respect for effort.

## Conclusion

"Breakthrough for the thrill" is web-literature's old ailment; Manghuang just takes it to the extreme. Heng Yuan Zhou's stance is hard: breakthrough can be exciting, but must be exciting within the law framework. Once rules start yielding to plot, readers will no longer believe in this world — and a world no one believes, however lively, is a castle in the air.
