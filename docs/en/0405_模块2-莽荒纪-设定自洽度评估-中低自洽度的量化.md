## Introduction: giving Manghuang's worldview a self-consistency checkup sheet

Among the six top-level axioms, the **law-first axiom** requires "setting self-consistency above plot convenience." The previous nine entries have critiqued Manghuang from angles of energy, regression, predation, realms, retcon; this entry does a systematic quantification — using Heng Yuan Zhou's setting-contradiction self-check table to give Manghuang's worldview a self-consistency checkup sheet. Conclusion first: **setting self-consistency = medium-low**, retcon-ending risk = high. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = high-leaning-medium. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Low self-consistency is itself the greatest energy waste: when rules can change anytime, strong ones' understanding of rules cannot sediment into reliable knowledge — the law comprehended today may be overturned by new setting tomorrow. The knowledge value of Ji Ning's every Dao-comprehension is discounted by unstable rules. **Rules unreliable, Dao-comprehension cannot be inherited; this is civilization energy's hidden drain.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How low self-consistency manufactures regression:

1. **Knowledge cannot accumulate**: rules change anytime; last generation's experience may be invalid for the next
2. **Consensus repeatedly collapses**: what is strong, what is right, how big the world, redefined every so often
3. **Education cannot systematize**: no stable rules, cannot compile stable textbooks; civilization can only "oral-heart-transmission" style drain with people

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Behind low self-consistency is the author-level monopoly of "rule-defining right":

- **Rules change as needed**: whenever logic doesn't work, add a new setting to bypass
- **Protagonist is exception**: all rules leave a back door before Ji Ning; his success does not prove the rules, only proves he is the protagonist
- **Myriad Ranks die by rules, protagonist lives by need**: this double standard is itself predation on Myriad Ranks — they are bound by rules, the protagonist unbound

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low (~3.5/10)**. Early has method; mid-late comprehensively yields to plot and linkage
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. Realm inflation, multi-book linkage, plot kidnapping triple overlay
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high-leaning-medium**. Self-consistency highly relies on Ji Ning as "rule exception" existing

Medium-low self-consistency also has a quantitative angle: early-iron-rule survival rate. We list Manghuang's early-established rules one by one, count how many still stand late. Result is, numerical iron rules — realm thresholds, resource scarcity, lifespan limits — have the lowest survival; emotional settings — Dao-companion love, tribe grace — have the highest survival. This shows Manghuang's self-consistency was not worn away by time, but selectively sacrificed by the author's emotional preference. Law-precedence means even the author's emotional preference cannot stand above rules.

This checkup sheet can also cross-compare horizontally: Tang-Luo self-consistency medium-high, Cover the Sky medium-low, Manghuang medium-low-leaning-low. Manghuang bottoms out in self-consistency among the three, precisely because it simultaneously carries two mountains: realm inflation and multi-book linkage. Self-consistency is not a minor flaw; it determines whether a worldview deserves to be taken seriously — what readers want is not flashier setting, but steadier rules.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: unstable rules cause Dao-comprehension knowledge unable to sediment and inherit
- **Anti-predation**: protagonist monopolizes rule-exception right; Myriad Ranks punished by rules
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo system self-consistency medium-high, Manghuang medium-low; Tang-Luo's problem is single-point dependence, Manghuang is the rules themselves collapsing
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: when rules unstable, bottom Myriad Ranks are first run over by new rules
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: a low-self-consistency worldview does not qualify for infinite expansion

Medium-low self-consistency can also be tested by "can readers replay." A self-consistent worldview lets readers extrapolate late from early rules; Manghuang cannot, because rules change anytime. This un-replayability downgrades readers from "participants" to "bystanders" — they no longer try to understand this world, only watch how the author makes it up. Heng Yuan Zhou writing the setting-contradiction self-check table into the foundation is precisely to make the worldview always replayable, deducible, trustworthy.

Medium-low self-consistency is not Manghuang's unique disease, but it dragged this disease into late and still worsening.
