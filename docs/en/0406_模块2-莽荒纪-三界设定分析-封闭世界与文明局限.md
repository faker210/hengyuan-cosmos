# Entry 406 | Manghuang System Decomposition · Three-Realms-Setting Analysis: Closed World and Civilizational Limitation

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Manghuang Ji Full Decomposition (Entry 11) | Benchmark base: Documents 80, 78, 05

## Introduction: Three Realms are not a cosmos, but a yard with very high walls

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires Myriad Ranks 99% full coverage; hyper-multiverse full openness is the mandatory core. Manghuang's "Three Realms" (Heaven, Earth, Human) is the early main stage — it looks complete, but is actually a closed yard with extremely high walls: mortal, immortal, divine three layers separated; mortals hard to ascend, immortals never look back. This entry uses Heng Yuan Zhou's plane theory to examine this "closed world"'s civilizational limitation. The law-first axiom reminds: geographic setting boundaries must legislate first, otherwise late will be redrawn by plot. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = high-leaning-medium. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Closed Three Realms' energy waste lies in "vertical blockade": Heaven Realm's rich qi open only to immortals; Human Realm's thin qi supports mortals; no energy circulation between the two layers. Heaven's surplus energy cannot feed back to Human; Human's vitality cannot rise upward — the entire Three Realms' energy flow is cut. **A closed system with a broken loop inevitably exhausts from within.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Closed Three Realms' regression logic:

1. **Upward channel locked**: mortal cultivation relies on spiritual roots, adventures, immortal fortune; channel extremely narrow; Human Realm civilization forever stuck at low energy
2. **Heaven complacent**: immortals long reside in Heaven, know not mortal hardship, no duty to feed back; Heaven and Human drift apart
3. **Boundary = ceiling**: outside Three Realms at first "unknowable," later opened by plot; early everyone thought Three Realms is all; this cognitive limitation itself is civilization's ceiling

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Behind Three-Realms setting is the monopoly of "channel right":

- **Immortal-mortal partition**: immortals have a duty not to interfere in the human world; essentially keeping the human world as a "lower quarter" penned
- **Ascend = abandon**: after Ji Ning ascends, Human Realm to him is only memory, no construction duty
- **Channel decided by strong ones**: who can go to heaven, who must stay; rules interpreted by Heaven's strong ones

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Three-Realms boundary early "impassable," late casually opened; outside world continuously appended by new setting
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium-high**. Three-Realms geography redrawn several times; "outside Three Realms" definition expands with inflation
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high-leaning-medium**. Three-Realms order maintained by top strong ones; boundary opening decided by Ji Ning's plot needs

Three-Realms closure also has a psychological consequence: it makes Three-Realms residents truly believe they are the cosmos's all. This cognitive limitation is scarier than physical walls — when a civilization believes it has reached the top, it loses the drive to explore outward. Heng Yuan Zhou's Cross-Plane Exploration Era is the opposite: it tells you from day one the cosmos is open, layers must flow. The closed world's greatest loss is not a few fewer cultivation quotas, but the entire civilization's imagination nailed dead by an invisible wall.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: vertical blockade causes Heaven's surplus energy unable to feed back to Human
- **Anti-predation**: immortal-mortal partition is structural stripping of mortals' upward right
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least opened class within humankind; Manghuang did not even open immortal-mortal two layers
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: outside Three Realms Myriad Ranks early not recognized as "world residents" at all
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: a closed yard is fundamentally opposed to a hyper-multiverse full-open cosmology

Three-Realms closure also manufactures a false sense of security. Heaven residents think outside the wall is unknown, so rest content; until the wall is opened by plot, they discover outside already strong ones like clouds. This "suddenly seeing the world" shock is itself a fuse of civilizational regression — because it was unprepared. Heng Yuan Zhou's open-plane view is the opposite: it from the start acknowledges outside the wall is a world, encourages residents to explore with a plan. Closed is not safety, but hoarding accidents until they explode all at once.

## Conclusion

Three-Realms setting's problem is not "small," but "closed." A yard that pens mortals at low energy and locks immortals in complacency, however beautiful, is still only a yard. Heng Yuan Zhou's plane view is open — layers must flow, strong and weak must feed back, civilization will not exhaust from within.
