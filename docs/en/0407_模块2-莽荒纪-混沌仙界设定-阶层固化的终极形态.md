# Entry 407 | Manghuang System Decomposition · Chaos Immortal Realm Setting: The Ultimate Form of Class Hardening

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Manghuang Ji Full Decomposition (Entry 12) | Benchmark base: Documents 36, 49, 05

## Introduction: when cultivation's end is a bigger class pyramid

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires breaking strong ones' monopoly of knowledge and the Great Dao; the collective ascension axiom requires strong ones to feed back to Myriad Ranks. Manghuang late "Chaos Immortal Realm" should have been a higher civilization stage, but is actually a **pyramid of class hardened to the extreme**: Eternal Sovereigns, Life-Death Dao Lords, World Realms layer upon layer clear; birth, lineage, bloodline decide which layer you stop on; new faces extremely hard to squeeze into the top. This entry uses Heng Yuan Zhou's social-structure theory to examine this hardening. The law-first axiom reminds: if class rules are not legislated, hardening will be taken as self-evident. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = high-leaning-medium. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Class hardening's energy waste lies in "top spinning empty": Eternal-Sovereign-tier strong ones have endless lifespans, but large portions used to maintain vested status, suppress newcomers, not explore new knowledge. They occupy the best resources, most complete inheritances, yet produce very little new law knowledge — **top energy not flowing, not sedimenting, equals civilization raising the most resources on a group of "maintainers."**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Chaos Immortal Realm's hardening regression logic:

1. **Upward channel closed**: new faces to squeeze into the Sovereign tier rely not on replicable effort, but adventure, backer, taking sides
2. **Knowledge monopoly**: highest laws circulate only inside the Sovereign circle; outsiders cannot even touch the door
3. **Maintainership overwhelms pioneering**: the top fears most "rules rewritten by new forces," so tends to maintain the status quo; civilization's vitality asphyxiated

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Behind Chaos Immortal Realm's hardening is predatory occupation of "top seats":

- **Seats private-property-ified**: Sovereign positions treated as family, school private property; outsiders may not covet
- **Birth = fate**: born in a Sovereign clan vs. born in Human Realm obscurity, starting point differs by more than a dozen major realms
- **Ji Ning's breakthrough is also predatory**: he relies on himself alone to kill into the top, breaking old hardening, but his "breakthrough" did not establish new mobility institutions, only swapped one person sitting at the top

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Class boundaries blur with plot; early "Sovereign unshakable," late Ji Ning alone overturns a stretch
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium-high**. Chaos Immortal Realm's "old Sovereigns" combat strength swings with plot needs
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high-leaning-medium**. Hope of breaking hardening all on Ji Ning alone, not institutions

Chaos Immortal Realm's class hardening's most poisonous point is it makes the top believe this is fair. Old Sovereigns will say: we also killed up from obscurity, why cannot latecomers? But they conveniently forgot the road by which they killed up back then was long sat dead by themselves. The reason Heng Yuan Zhou's exam system replaces birth heredity is precisely to let "could come up back then" not equal "now let you monopolize." Class hardening fears most not bottom discontent, but the top packaging vested interest as a struggle myth.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: top energy spins empty on maintainership; produces very little new knowledge
- **Anti-predation**: seat private-property-ification is systematic predation of Myriad Ranks' upward right
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least broke human aristocratic monopoly; Manghuang in Immortal Realm upgrades monopoly to cosmos tier
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Myriad Ranks in Chaos Immortal Realm's top almost no seats; even human offshoots hard
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: a hardened pyramid cannot support an open cosmos; expansion only changes place to keep stratifying

Chaos Immortal Realm's hardening also has a technical detail: it treats "old" as a synonym of strength. Old Sovereigns are strong not because they understand laws better, but because they occupied the best resources longest. This "seniority = strength" illusion makes the whole Immortal Realm mistakenly think itself at peak. Heng Yuan Zhou's four-dimensional exam (strength, mind, contribution, responsibility) takes strength apart; seniority is only one variable. When contribution and responsibility enter the exam, old Sovereigns living off past laurels are no longer naturally strong ones.

## Conclusion

Chaos Immortal Realm is not civilization's endpoint, but hardening's ultimate form — the higher, the more monolithic. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is not "produce another Ji Ning to overturn it," but institutionally make the top forever open to newcomers. Swapping the top sitter is not progress; making the top no longer hereditary is progress.
