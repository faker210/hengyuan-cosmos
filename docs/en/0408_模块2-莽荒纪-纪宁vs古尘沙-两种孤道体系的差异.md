## Introduction: two people atop one log, neither left a ladder for all beings

The six top-level axioms take the law-first axiom as core and the republican axiom of Myriad Races as bottom line. This entry introduces *Dragon Talisman*'s Gu Chensha as a comparison anchor — he is web-literature's recognized combat ceiling (the first ever to cultivate Heavenly-Dao Law, casually creates cosmoses), protagonist uniqueness dependency = extremely high (single-log style civilization). Ji Ning (high-leaning-medium) and Gu Chensha (extremely high) both belong to the lone-path lineage, but lone in different ways. This entry distinguishes the two's differences, then explains why **both lone paths lead to the same ending.** Protagonist uniqueness dependency = high-leaning-medium (Ji Ning side). The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The two's energy-waste directions differ: Gu Chensha is "energy so vast he casually creates cosmoses," wasted on the grand gesture of "bearing all beings' karmic retribution"; Ji Ning is "energy climbing all the way to Controller," wasted on "using spacetime to save one person." Gu Chensha's waste is divine — outsourcing all beings' sin to one person; Ji Ning's waste is human — pressing cosmos-tier energy into private affection. **One too divine, one too emotional, neither returned energy to institutions.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

The two's regression mechanisms are isomorphic:

1. **Single-point dependence**: Gu Chensha = single-log style (extremely high), all beings "are made into dragons"; Ji Ning = lone-path style (high-leaning-medium), Myriad Ranks make way for him alone to level
2. **Promise tied to the person**: the grander Gu Chensha's promise "I'll bear sin for all beings," the more it proves all beings outsourced their fate; the more moving Ji Ning's promise "save Yu Wei, protect my tribe," the more it proves order relies on him personally
3. **No institution to take over**: both left personal legends strong enough, but left no institution that can turn without them

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

The two's predation differences:

- **Gu Chensha = divine predation**: in the posture of "I do this for your own good," takes all beings' subjectivity into himself — the gentlest predation makes people feel "with him it's enough"
- **Ji Ning = mortal predation**: in the posture of "I want to get strong to protect those I love," takes Myriad Ranks' resources into one self — the most honest predation never pretends it is for all beings
- **Common blind spot**: neither reached the step of the "righteous rogue" — tearing up old jurisprudence, truly returning the Great Dao to Myriad Ranks

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: Ji Ning medium-low vs. Gu Chensha medium**. Gu Chensha system though single-log but self-consistent within its "sky-mending, sky-reforming" logic; Ji Ning dragged by realm inflation and multi-book linkage, self-consistency lower
- **Retcon-ending risk: Ji Ning high vs. Gu Chensha medium**
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: Ji Ning high-leaning-medium, Gu Chensha extremely high** — both belong to the lone-path lineage, only degree differs

Gu Chensha and Ji Ning's comparison also has an easy-to-overlook layer: Gu Chensha's strength is divine, Ji Ning's is human. Divine strength makes all beings dare not question; human strength makes all beings feel yearning — but neither kind of strength ever asked whether all beings themselves want it. Heng Yuan Zhou's stance is: whether divine or human, taking all beings' subjectivity into one person is the same predation. Gu Chensha bears sin for all beings, Ji Ning kills monsters for all beings; it sounds one grander than the next, but all beings' own hands are empty throughout.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Gu Chensha wasted on divine posture, Ji Ning wasted on private affection
- **Anti-predation**: divine predation and mortal predation both take Myriad Ranks' subjectivity into one person
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo = gift-style (high), Ji Ning = lone-path style (high-leaning-medium), Gu Chensha = single-log style (extremely high); three tiers progressing
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Gu Chensha's promise "all can become dragons" is grandest, yet its realization path depends most on him alone
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the lone-path lineage's ultimate limitation — the stronger, the more dependent on one person; the less able to network-open

Gu Chensha and Ji Ning also have a quantifiable difference: the world after they are gone. After Gu Chensha leaves, all beings lose their only sky-mender; after Ji Ning leaves, Manghuang loses its only peaker. Both losses make the world colder, but the reason for cold differs — Gu Chensha's cold is "no one will carry for us again," Ji Ning's cold is "no one will grab for us again." What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is not to make the world not cold, but to let Myriad Ranks make their own fire.

## Conclusion

Ji Ning and Gu Chensha are the two ends of the lone-path lineage: one emotional, one near-divine. But Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment does not ask emotional or divine, only one thing — after you are gone, can all beings still walk on their own? Neither of the two could answer. Shattering the lone path is not denying their greatness, but letting no one's greatness become all beings' crutch.
