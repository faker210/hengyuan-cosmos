# Entry 409 | Manghuang System Decomposition · Ji Ning vs. Tang Luo: Predatory vs. Gift-Style Comparison

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Manghuang Ji Full Decomposition (Entry 14) | Benchmark base: Documents 251, 261, 04

## Introduction: one directly grabs, one gives out pieces — actually two ways of sitting at the same table

The six top-level axioms open with the anti-predation axiom and take the law-first axiom as core. This entry compares two key benchmark protagonists: *I Can See Combat Power*'s Tang Luo (gift-style / high dependency) and Manghuang's Ji Ning (predatory lone-path / high-leaning-medium dependency). Tang Luo is the typical "enlightened predator" — he breaks aristocratic monopoly, universalizes martial path, but high-tier Great Dao is private, grace tied to one person; Ji Ning cannot even be bothered to "universal benefit," directly takes resources by strength. This entry distinguishes the two's similarities and differences. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: Ji Ning = high-leaning-medium, Tang Luo = high. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The two's waste directions differ: Tang Luo's energy is wasted on "gift-style administration" — he spends large effort rebuilding Peerless Academy, personally universalizing martial path; energy direction is civilization infrastructure but limited in scale; Ji Ning's energy is wasted on "personal leveling" — all resources piled on himself, infrastructure almost zero. **Tang Luo at least built a school; Ji Ning did not even build one.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

The two's regression mechanisms compared:

1. **Tang Luo = gift-style regression**: universal benefit is given by the strong; the patron absent and it breaks
2. **Ji Ning = predatory regression**: resources are grabbed; the strong stops grabbing and fighting reignites
3. **Common lesion**: both bet order on one person; neither built "tea not cooling when person leaves" institutions

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

The two's predation key differences:

- **Tang Luo = implicit predation**: he universal-benefits humankind's lower-middle tier, but privately hoards high-tier Great Dao — the gentlest predation makes people accustomed to being alms-given
- **Ji Ning = explicit predation**: he directly devours resources, seizes secret realms, never pretends universal benefit — the most honest predation never pretends it is for your own good
- **Who is worse?**: on Myriad Ranks' coverage, Tang Luo only benefits humankind, Ji Ning does not even benefit humankind; on institutional consciousness, Tang Luo at least has the "academy" prototype, Ji Ning has none. **Both are people atop the predatory pyramid, only one wears a hat and one does not.**

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Dimension | Tang Luo (Gift-Style / High) | Ji Ning (Predatory / High-Leaning-Medium) | Anchor Symbiosis |
|-----------|------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|-------------------|
| Resource allocation | Strong one alms-gives | Strong one grabs | Jointly contracted, more work more pay |
| Knowledge open | Lower-middle open, high-tier private | All private | All-tier public, no private Dao |
| Institutional prototype | Peerless Academy | None | Anchor Council + Alaya Network |
| Myriad Ranks coverage | Humankind only | Humankind top only | Myriad Ranks 99% full coverage |
| Protagonist absent | Universal-benefit breaks | Fighting reignites | Network-style civilization runs as usual |

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: Tang Luo medium-high vs. Ji Ning medium-low**. Tang Luo realms capped, rules internally self-consistent; Ji Ning dragged by inflation and linkage
- **Retcon-ending risk: Tang Luo medium vs. Ji Ning high**
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: Tang Luo high vs. Ji Ning high-leaning-medium** — Tang Luo more dependent on personal good will, Ji Ning more dependent on personal combat strength

Tang Luo and Ji Ning also have a key difference: Tang Luo at least admits he is giving; Ji Ning does not even have the concept of giving. Tang Luo's problem is grace; Ji Ning's problem is even grace is saved — he defaults resources should be his. This means reforming Manghuang is harder than reforming Tang Luo: Tang Luo only needs to learn to let go; Ji Ning needs to learn that this thing was not his to begin with. From predation to grace is still progress; from grace to co-build is the endpoint. Both are still halfway, just stopped at different positions.

Putting Tang Luo and Ji Ning on one table also reveals the "kindness" gradient. Tang Luo's kindness has a boundary, he only benefits humankind; Ji Ning's kindness cannot even be bothered to think about a boundary. But Heng Yuan Zhou does not reward the kindness gradient; it rewards structural root reform. No matter how far kindness goes, as long as the bottom jurisprudence is still zero-sum, it is still within the predation lineage.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Tang Luo wasted on gift administration, Ji Ning wasted on personal leveling
- **Anti-predation**: implicit predation and explicit predation both stand atop the pyramid
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: this entry is the comparison — Tang Luo at least universal-benefits humankind, Ji Ning does not even universal-benefit
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: both only cover humankind; Myriad Ranks share none
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: both grace and predation have not touched open-cosmos institutional design

Tang Luo and Ji Ning's comparison can also extend to "institutional imagination." Tang Luo at least thought of building an academy, though the academy is also gift-style; Ji Ning did not even have the thought of "set up some institution"; in his world there are only enemies and himself. This shows predatory protagonists not only plunder resources, but also plunder imagination — he cannot imagine an order not relying on personal strong-arm rule. Heng Yuan Zhou's four bodies — Anchor Council, Symbiotic Executive Committee, Symbiotic Court, Inspection Committee — are precisely the reverse proof of this imagination.

## Conclusion

Tang Luo wears a hat, Ji Ning does not, but they sit at the same predatory table. Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment is not "who is worse," but "should this table be flipped" — grace and predation both do not solve the problem; the true alternative is changing the table to Myriad Ranks' own dining table.
