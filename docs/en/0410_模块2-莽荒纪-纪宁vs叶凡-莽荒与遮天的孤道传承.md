# Entry 410 | Manghuang System Decomposition · Ji Ning vs. Ye Fan: The Lone-Path Lineage Between Manghuang and Cover the Sky

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Manghuang Ji Full Decomposition (Entry 15) | Benchmark base: Documents 288, 289, 17

## Introduction: two Tomato-style thrillers, the same lesion of "one man becomes immortal, civilization pays the bill"

The six top-level axioms take the law-first axiom as core. This entry compares *Manghuang Ji*'s Ji Ning (lone-path / high-leaning-medium) with *Cover the Sky*'s Ye Fan (oligarchic / medium dependency). Both are top-tier protagonists of the "I-Eat-Tomatoes lineage," both walk the lone path "from obscurity to the cosmos peak," but belong to different civilizational forms: Ye Fan relies on a peer-Emperor group to maintain "ten thousand generations of peace"; Ji Ning relies on himself alone to kill through sixteen realms. This entry distinguishes this lineage and difference. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: Ji Ning = high-leaning-medium, Ye Fan = medium. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The two's waste is isomorphic: cosmos-tier aptitude all used for personal Dao-confirmation. Ye Fan wastes on "Holy-Body path devouring massive source"; Ji Ning wastes on "dual-system double devouring." The difference lies in lifespans' direction — Ye Fan uses long lifespans to "watch an era"; Ji Ning uses long lifespans to "run instances to break realms." **Both used civilization-tier energy as personal flashlights.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

The two's regression mechanisms compared:

1. **Ye Fan = oligarchic regression**: Emperors pass away and dark turmoil returns; order tied to the strong-one group
2. **Ji Ning = lone-path regression**: a Great Expert falls and the lineage breaks; order tied to one person
3. **Common lesion**: both "person lives and policy rises, person dies and policy ends"; neither wrote peace into institutions

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

The two's predation key differences:

- **Ye Fan = oligarchic predation**: relies on peer-Emperor group to jointly plunder Myriad Ranks to feed the top; peace bears the surname "Ye"
- **Ji Ning = lone-path predation**: no group; one person devours double resources; even the oligarchs' "spoils-sharing pact" is skipped
- **Who is more isolated?**: Ye Fan at least has peers like Ruthless One, Wushi; Ji Ning is more thorough — on his road there are few who walk side by side. **On the lone-path lineage, Ji Ning is more "lone" than Ye Fan.**

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: Ye Fan medium-low vs. Ji Ning medium-low**. Both dragged by late realm watering
- **Retcon-ending risk: Ye Fan medium-high vs. Ji Ning high**. Ji Ning additionally has multi-book linkage, risk higher
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: Ye Fan medium vs. Ji Ning high-leaning-medium** — Ji Ning more isolated than Ye Fan

The difference between Ye Fan and Ji Ning is actually a microcosm of two generations of web-novel protagonists' mindsets. Ye Fan's generation still spoke of companions, of the Emperor group; though oligarchic, at least acknowledged war cannot be fought by one person. Ji Ning's generation went further, even skipping side-by-side companions; one person, one road, killing to the end. This more-lone trend is not progress, but a regression in civilizational cooperation consciousness — pressing institutional construction that should be a group's task entirely onto one person's talent. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is not a lone hero who can fight better, but institutions that let a group cooperate.

Ye Fan and Ji Ning's other difference lies in attitude toward "long life." Ye Fan seeks long life to protect; Ji Ning seeks Controller to resurrect. Different purposes, but means both are monopolizing resources. Heng Yuan Zhou asks not "for whom do you seek long life," but "on what grounds is the long-life ticket issued only to you alone."

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: cosmos-tier aptitude equally pressed into personal Dao-confirmation
- **Anti-predation**: oligarchic predation and lone-path predation both use Myriad Ranks to feed the top
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo = gift-style (high), Ye Fan = oligarchic (medium), Ji Ning = lone-path style (high-leaning-medium); each branch has its own lesion
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: both treat Myriad Ranks only as backdrop
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: both works are closed-cosmos zero-sum immortal roads

Ye Fan and Ji Ning also have an easy-to-overlook common point: neither left institutions. Ye Fan left the reputation of "ten-thousand-generation peace"; Ji Ning left the legend of "Controller." Reputations and legends both fade with time; only institutions can cross time. Heng Yuan Zhou writing order into the charter, into Alaya, into the Council is precisely to not bet civilization on reputation or legend. Legends expire; institutions do not.

This "more lone" trend is precisely the direction Heng Yuan Zhou wants to reverse. The further civilization goes, the more it needs cooperation not lone heroism. The romance of one person punching through everything, maybe thrilling on low planes, is a disaster on a hyper-multiverse requiring Myriad Ranks' joint governance.

## Conclusion

Ji Ning and Ye Fan are like two generations of the lone-path lineage's heirs: Ye Fan still knew to pull a group to "share" peace; Ji Ning simply carries it alone to the end. But Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment is — pulling a group to carry and carrying alone are both inferior to letting Myriad Ranks carry themselves. Peace should not be surnamed Ye, nor surnamed Ji; it should be surnamed "Law."

In the final analysis, Manghuang Ji · Ji Ning vs. Ye Fan · lone-path lineage between Manghuang and Cover the Sky described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
