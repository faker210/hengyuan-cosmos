# Entry 411 | Manghuang System Decomposition · Ji Ning vs. Shi Hao: Predation Comparison Between Manghuang and Perfect World

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Manghuang Ji Full Decomposition (Entry 16) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 17, 261

## Introduction: two youths walking out of the great wilderness, whose road "eats" Myriad Ranks more

The six top-level axioms take the anti-predation axiom as bottom line. This entry compares *Manghuang Ji*'s Ji Ning (lone-path / high-leaning-medium) with *Perfect World*'s Shi Hao (Heaven Emperor Huang, the other extreme of predatory growth). Shi Hao is known for "devouring" — swallowing sun and moon, swallowing all things, taking the body as seed; his predation intensity ranks among the top among protagonists across the realms. Ji Ning dual-system devours; Shi Hao takes "devouring" itself and cultivates it into a Great Dao. This entry distinguishes the two predations' intensity and structural differences. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: Ji Ning = high-leaning-medium, Shi Hao = high. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Both are devouring types, but Shi Hao's devouring is more "internalized": he directly swallows sun, moon, stars, and all laws into himself, taking the body as seed. Ji Ning at least still splits body-refining and qi-refining two lines; Shi Hao treats the entire cosmos as his dantian. **Shi Hao's energy waste lies in — the more he swallows, the flatter the cosmos as external ecology becomes; the fuller one person alone, the emptier Myriad Ranks.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

The two's regression mechanisms compared:

1. **Shi Hao = devouring-style regression**: taking the body as seed, after devouring all things, the external world becomes nourishment for his "inner cosmos"
2. **Ji Ning = resource-style regression**: devours resources not the world, but also zero-sum
3. **Common lesion**: both define "getting strong" as "possessing more," never as "getting strong together with Myriad Ranks"

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

The two's predation key differences:

- **Ji Ning = grabbing resources**: grabs secret realms, spiritual veins, cave-heavens; plunders "stock resources"
- **Shi Hao = swallowing the world**: swallows sun and moon, swallows laws; plunders "the cosmos itself's existence"
- **Who is more extreme?**: Shi Hao's "taking the body as seed" is philosophically more radical — he wants to turn the entire cosmos into a part of himself. Ji Ning still only "uses" resources; Shi Hao "eats" the world. **Neither has walked out of the old road of "plundering stock," only differing in magnitude.**

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: Ji Ning medium-low vs. Shi Hao medium-low**. Both dragged by realm inflation
- **Retcon-ending risk: Ji Ning high vs. Shi Hao high**. Shi Hao late multi-book linkage (*Sacred Void*) also retcons
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: Ji Ning high-leaning-medium vs. Shi Hao high** — Shi Hao as "Heaven Emperor Huang" has stronger single-log flavor

Shi Hao's swallowing and Ji Ning's grabbing also have a philosophical divide: Ji Ning grabs usable resources; Shi Hao swallows everything. Ji Ning at least still treats the cosmos as external world to use; Shi Hao wants to eat even the external world. This means Shi Hao's road walked to the end leaves in the cosmos only him and his inner small cosmos — that is not strength, but self-seclusion at ultimate loneliness. Heng Yuan Zhou's symbiotic system is the opposite: it wants the strong to flourish together with the external world, not to eat the external world empty and then individually perfect.

Shi Hao and Ji Ning's predation-intensity difference also shows in attitude toward ecology. Ji Ning at least still goes around; Shi Hao swallows whatever he sees. This difference seems subtle, but actually decides the relation between civilization and the external world: use the external, or eliminate the external. Heng Yuan Zhou chooses symbiosis with the external.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: devouring-type protagonist treats external world as fuel; all energy enters one person
- **Anti-predation**: grabbing resources and swallowing world are both the old road of "possessing more = getting strong"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least does not "devour"; Ji Ning and Shi Hao both push predation to the extreme
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: under the devouring model Myriad Ranks are nourishment, not even fuel
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: swallowing the world is fundamentally opposed to an open cosmos

Shi Hao and Ji Ning's predation comparison also has a time dimension: Shi Hao's devouring is irreversible; once swallowed it becomes part of his body; Ji Ning's plunder though zero-sum, at least resources still circulate in the external world. This means the Shi Hao model consumes the cosmos faster — every point stronger, the cosmos thinner. Heng Yuan Zhou's symbiotic model wants reversible, recyclable, sustainable, not eating the cosmos dry in one go.

## Conclusion

Ji Ning grabs resources, Shi Hao swallows the world — one grabs within stock, one even eats the cosmos itself. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: getting strong need not presuppose possession; symbiosis also makes people strong. "Taking the body as seed" sounds domineering, but what is seeded inside one's own body is, after all, another's world.
