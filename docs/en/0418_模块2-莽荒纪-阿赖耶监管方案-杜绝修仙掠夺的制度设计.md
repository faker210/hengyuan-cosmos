# Entry 418 | Manghuang System Decomposition · Alaya Supervision Plan: Institutional Design to Eradicate Cultivation Predation

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Manghuang Ji Full Decomposition (Entry 23) | Benchmark base: Documents 21, 27, 49

## Introduction: the cultivation world's biggest loophole is the absence of a "recorder"

The six top-level axioms take the anti-predation axiom as bottom line and the causal self-discipline axiom as supplement. One root cause why predation runs rampant in Manghuang's cultivation world is **the absence of whole-network recording and supervision** — who devoured how many resources, who killed how many beings, who occupied how many spiritual veins, all decided by fists; no one records, no one holds accountable. This entry prescribes medicine: using Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision, install on Manghuang's cultivation world a "recorder + monitoring net." The law-first axiom is core. Protagonist uniqueness dependency: before installation = high-leaning-medium, after = extremely low. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

In a cultivation world without records, energy waste goes unaccounted: how much resource Ji Ning devoured, how much energy Great-Expert battles wasted, all are muddled accounts. After installing Alaya, every flow of energy is recorded, metered, recovered — **waste is waste first of all because no one keeps the books.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

In a cultivation world without supervision, regression goes uncorrected:

1. **Predation undiscovered**: strong ones seize resources, kill beings; no network record
2. **Errors uncorrected**: whoever took a wrong road; no whole-network comparison
3. **Experience unsedimented**: Dao-comprehension goes with the person, because no one records

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

How Alaya supervision dismantles predation's "anonymity":

- **Predation traceable**: who occupied spiritual veins, who refined fierce beasts; whole-network searchable; cannot whitewash with "ownerless goods"
- **Strong ones pre-warned**: top cultivators' abnormal devouring, Alaya pre-warns; Anchor Inspection Committee intervenes
- **Breakthrough auditable**: any "plot-tier breakthrough" must pass self-consistency check; no one enjoys rule exception

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Manghuang No-Supervision Loophole | Alaya Supervision Design |
|------------------------------------|---------------------------|
| Predation unrecorded | Alaya whole-network record; every resource flow traceable |
| Strong-one anomaly un-warned | Anchor Inspection Committee: abnormal devouring = warning + intervention |
| Breakthrough conditions vague | Breakthrough audit: any leap must pass self-consistency check |
| Dao-comprehension goes with the person | Lifetime Dao-comprehension real-time recorded; passing does not bury |
| Strong one defines resources | Symbiotic Court adjudicates; resource ownership not by fists |

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

1. **Modeling**: install Alaya Network on Manghuang's world; cover resources, cultivation, breakthrough three domains
2. **Variables**: compare "no supervision," "record only," "record + warning + audit" three supervision intensities
3. **Observation**: predation incident rate, resource waste rate, lineage-inheritance rate
4. **Review**: quantified conclusion — after three-layer supervision fully on, predation incident rate drops to under one-tenth of the no-supervision model; lineage-inheritance rate rises by an order of magnitude

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Before installation setting self-consistency: medium-low**. No records means rules cannot be externally verified
- **After installation setting self-consistency: high**. All rules auditable, traceable
- **After installation retcon-ending risk: extremely low**. Recording makes retconning impossible to hide
- **After installation protagonist uniqueness dependency: extremely low**. Supervision network does not depend on any individual

Alaya supervision is not to monitor to the point of suffocation, but to turn invisible predation into visible accounts. Manghuang's world's biggest problem is muddled accounts — who devoured how much, who occupied how much, all by conscience. Once every item is on the network, however strong a strong one dares not drain a spiritual vein, because the whole network is watching. Supervision's purpose is not punishment, but to turn predation from anonymous to real-name. When predators cannot pretend it is ownerless goods, rules' teeth truly grow in.

Alaya supervision also has a benefit: it makes rules learnable. Manghuang's rules are only in strong ones' brains; outsiders cannot learn; Alaya makes rules public; anyone can search, learn, compare themselves. Public rules are rules; rules hidden in strong ones' brains are only unwritten rules.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: first keep the books, then recover; waste has nowhere to hide
- **Anti-predation**: predation traceable, strong ones pre-warned, breakthrough auditable
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's Peerless Academy is an offline school; Alaya is a whole-network supervision net
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Myriad Ranks equal in whole-network record; predators nowhere to hide
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: whole-network naturally cross-plane; expansion does not break supervision

Alaya supervision also has an ethical premise: it supervises strong ones, not mortals. In Manghuang's world mortals have little to devour or seize anyway; who truly needs supervision are top cultivators. Heng Yuan Zhou's supervision net specifically watches energy-anomalous beings; the stronger the more watched — this is the complete opposite of the old world where "strong = privilege." Supervision is not distrust, but strong ones' energy is inherently large; large energy must be matched with large supervision.

The highest state of supervision is making the supervised self-disciplined. Manghuang maintains order by fists; Heng Yuan Zhou uses the network to internalize rules. When every cultivator knows his every move is whole-network searchable, self-discipline replaces heteronomy.

## Conclusion

The cultivation world's biggest loophole is not strong ones too strong, but no one keeps the books. Alaya supervision's meaning is to make every predation leave traces, make every breakthrough auditable. When predation is no longer anonymous, however strong a strong one can only walk the straight road — this is institutions' true domestication of power.

In the final analysis, Manghuang Ji · Alaya supervision plan · institutional design to eradicate cultivation predation described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
