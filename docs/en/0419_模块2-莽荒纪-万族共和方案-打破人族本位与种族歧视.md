Myriad-Races republicanism also has an often-overlooked dimension: ecological wisdom. Demon tribes are not just another kind of human; they represent a survival wisdom completely different from humankind — symbiosis with nature, inheritance by bloodline, cultivation by instinct. Manghuang wiped out this wisdom as barbarism, equal to cutting off an arm. Myriad-Races republicanism is not almsgiving to demon tribes, but humankind itself needs demon tribes' wisdom to complete the cultivation system. Treating demon tribes as friends, humankind itself can avoid several hundred years of detours.

Myriad-Races republicanism's gains are also in innovation. When humankind alone dominates, the cultivation system has only one road; after Myriad-Races republicanism, demon tribes' bloodlines, plant spirits' circulation, consciousness races' deduction all become new routes. Civilization's innovation rate is proportional to how many races it contains.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

Myriad-Races republicanism is not a slogan, but the civilizational optimum proven by practice.
