# Entry 421 | Manghuang System Decomposition · Heavens-Devouring Great Law: Manghuang-Version Predatory Cultivation

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Manghuang Ji Full Decomposition (Entry 26) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 19, 12

## Introduction: a martial art that writes "devouring" as the Great Dao

The six top-level axioms take the anti-predation axiom as bottom line. In Manghuang's world, "devouring" is pushed to the Great-Dao level — settings like the Heavens-Devouring Great Law elevate predation from "means" to "cultivation principle": devour others' blood essence, devour heaven-earth qi, devour law fragments; the more devoured the stronger. This is predatory cultivation's extreme expression in the Manghuang version. This entry dissects this martial art's ethics and structure. The law-first axiom is core. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = high-leaning-medium. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The Heavens-Devouring Great Law's energy waste lies in "devour without transforming": the blood essence, qi, law fragments devoured are mostly lost in refinement; only combat power retained. A martial art whose principle is "devouring" has inherently low energy-conversion efficiency — **because by design it is not for circulation, but for hoarding.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How "devouring" as the Great Dao manufactures regression:

1. **Encourages predation**: the martial art's principle is devouring, equal to legislating encouragement for cultivators to grab and kill
2. **Ecology depleted**: the more devoured, the fewer surrounding fierce beasts, spiritual veins, heaven-earth treasures
3. **Karma accumulates**: power devoured carries karma; the more devoured the heavier the karmic debt

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Behind the Heavens-Devouring Great Law is predation legitimized:

- **"Devouring is the right path"**: the martial art writes predation as cultivation principle, equal to providing theoretical endorsement for strong ones to grab
- **Unbounded devouring**: from devouring fierce beasts to devouring laws, "devouring" target has no upper limit; ethical boundaries disappear as combat strength inflates
- **Ji Ning's dual-system superposition**: he both refines qi and refines body, equal to adding "devour flesh" on top of "devour qi" — double practitioner of the Heavens-Devouring Great Law

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Manghuang Heavens-Devouring Great Law | Anchor-Symbiosis Martial-Art View |
|----------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Devour others' blood, flesh | Life-cycle system: symbiotic cultivation; do not Dao-confirm by killing |
| Devour heaven-earth qi | Myriad Ranks' origin dual circulation; develop increment |
| Devour law fragments | Deduction-iteration system: research laws, not devour |
| Devouring = right path | Cultivation ethics and boundaries: must not devour other lives |
| Unbounded devouring | Alaya supervision: devouring = predation; traceable and intervenable |

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Before installation self-consistency: medium-low**. "Devouring" boundary inflates with combat strength; no hard rules
- **After installation self-consistency: high**. Cultivation ethics legislated; devouring must pass boundary check
- **After installation retcon-ending risk: low**
- **After installation protagonist uniqueness dependency: extremely low**

The Heavens-Devouring Great Law also has a hidden cost: it makes cultivators lose feel for boundaries. Today devour fierce beasts, tomorrow devour spiritual veins, the day after devour laws; the devouring target steps up, ethical boundary steps back. In the end, devouring a cosmos is just cultivation need. Heng Yuan Zhou's cultivation ethics precedes martial arts precisely to nail the boundary dead at the first step — some things cannot be devoured, not because they cannot be devoured, but because after devouring one is no longer human. Boundaries, once started to yield, can never be taken back.

The Heavens-Devouring Great Law also has an ethical test: if the devouring target were someone you love, would you still cultivate this martial art? Manghuang cannot answer, because it never treats devouring targets as lives. Heng Yuan Zhou's cultivation ethics answers this question first — precisely because the person you love is also a life, no life may be devoured.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: devour without transforming; energy-conversion efficiency inherently low
- **Anti-predation**: writing predation as cultivation principle is ultimate endorsement of predatory jurisprudence
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least does not devour; Manghuang cultivates "devouring" into the Great Dao
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: under Heavens-Devouring Myriad Ranks are all "devourable things"
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: unbounded devouring is fundamentally opposed to an open cosmos

The Heavens-Devouring Great Law also manufactures a distorted cultivation view: cultivators treat "how much can be devoured" as proof of their own value. Thus cultivation is no longer to understand laws, but to prove one can devour more. This goal misalignment makes strong ones more and more paranoid the more they cultivate. Heng Yuan Zhou's cultivation goal is to understand laws and feed back to Myriad Ranks, not devour everything. Cultivation's endpoint should be a better world, not an emptied world.

Cultivation ethics' boundary must be nailed dead from day one. Once cultivators grow accustomed to "for strength anything can be devoured," ethics can never be taken back. Boundaries, late established is worse than early.

## Conclusion

The Heavens-Devouring Great Law writes predation as the Great Dao, equal to legislating at the martial-art level encouragement for strong ones to grab. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: cultivation ethics must precede cultivation martial arts — you may be powerful, but not powerful by devouring others. A martial art that empties the more it devours sounds domineering, but is actually treating the entire cosmos as its own buffet.

In the final analysis, Manghuang Ji · Heavens-Devouring Great Law setting · Manghuang-version predatory cultivation described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
