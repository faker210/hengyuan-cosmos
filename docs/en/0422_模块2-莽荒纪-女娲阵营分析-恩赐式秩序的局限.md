# Entry 422 | Manghuang System Decomposition · Nüwa Faction Analysis: Limitations of Gift-Style Order

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Manghuang Ji Full Decomposition (Entry 27) | Benchmark base: Documents 03, 261, 05

## Introduction: the god who made humans still only raises them as children

The six top-level axioms take the republican axiom of Myriad Races as bottom line. Manghuang's world's "Nüwa faction" represents an order — creating humans, protecting humans, gifting humans, but essentially still **gift-style order**: all beings are children Nüwa made; order is bestowed by Nüwa; beings live dependent on Nüwa. This is isomorphic to Tang Luo's gift-style civilization. This entry analyzes this order's goodwill and limitations. The law-first axiom is core. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = high-leaning-medium. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Nüwa faction's energy waste lies in "over-doing": Nüwa and faction Great Experts take disasters for all beings, legislate for them, decide for them; beings' growth subjectivity long deprived. **The stronger the god, the weaker the people — this is gift-style order's deepest energy mismatch.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Gift-style order's regression logic:

1. **Dependency cultivated**: beings accustomed to god-protection; once god withdraws, beings lack self-rescue ability
2. **Subjectivity atrophied**: long arranged; beings lose the ability to legislate themselves, decide themselves
3. **God dies = vacuum**: once Nüwa faction falls in battle, beings cannot even maintain order themselves

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Behind gift-style order is "doing-for-you"-style implicit predation:

- **"I do this for your own good"**: Nüwa makes and protects humans; the goodwill is real, but takes beings' subjectivity into herself
- **Order bestowed by god**: beings do not participate in legislation; they are only beneficiaries
- **The gentlest predation**: making people accustomed to being arranged is harder to resist than direct grabbing — because it is wrapped in grace

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Nüwa Gift-Style Order | Anchor-Symbiosis Order |
|------------------------|-------------------------|
| God legislates for beings | Myriad-Ranks republican co-build; beings legislate themselves |
| God wards off disasters for beings | Alaya supervision + institutional construction; beings have self-rescue ability |
| Beings depend on god | Law anchors rights universal-benefited to all |
| God dies = vacuum | Network-style civilization; god absent runs as usual |
| Order hangs on god's goodwill | Order hangs on institutions and law |

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

1. **Modeling**: replicate Nüwa gift-style order vs. symbiotic order two models
2. **Variables**: uniformly inject "Nüwa faction withdraws" shock
3. **Observation**: beings' self-rescue rate, order-maintenance duration, Myriad Ranks' subjectivity development
4. **Review**: quantified conclusion — under the gift model beings' self-rescue rate extremely low; withdrawal = chaos; under the symbiotic model beings have their own order; withdrawal does not affect operation

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Nüwa faction self-consistency: medium**. Its "make-and-protect" logic internally self-consistent, but conflicts with late inflated cosmos model
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Nüwa faction's position redrawn in multi-book linkage
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high at faction level**. Order hangs on Nüwa faction's goodwill

Nüwa faction's grace also has a deeper problem: it makes beings treat god's existence as taken-for-granted infrastructure. Beings no longer think about what to do if god is gone, because Nüwa is always there. This psychological dependency is harder to treat than any institutional flaw — it is not giving beings a suboptimal order, but making beings lose the ability to imagine their own order. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is not a stronger Nüwa, but Myriad Ranks who, when Nüwa is gone, can overnight build their own council.

Nüwa faction's grace also has a paradox: the stronger the god, the weaker the beings; the weaker the beings, the more afraid the god is to let go. This is a self-reinforcing loop. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants to break is precisely this loop — let beings get strong, so god can truly retire.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: god over-does; beings' subjectivity atrophies
- **Anti-predation**: "doing-for-you" is the gentle predation of taking beings' subjectivity into god
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Nüwa faction = gift-style (high), isomorphic to Tang Luo; god does people's work, people never grow up
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: grace only reaches "those god made"; Myriad-Ranks offshoots may not be included
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: gift order lacks cross-plane institutional resilience

Nüwa faction's grace also has an overlooked cost: it makes Nüwa herself tired. A god who bears all beings' karmic retribution essentially transfers Myriad Ranks' growth cost entirely onto herself. The stronger the god, the weaker the beings; the weaker the beings, the more the god cannot let go. This is a lose-lose loop. Heng Yuan Zhou's approach is to let Myriad Ranks grow their own abilities; god can instead retire. True compassion is making beings no longer need compassion.

The day god retires is the day beings truly grow up. Heng Yuan Zhou's ultimate goal is raising a Myriad Races that no longer needs god — this sounds paradoxical, but is gift-style order's only exit.

God can be great, but great to the point of walking for beings is beings' misfortune.

Letting Myriad Ranks stand on their own is true compassion.

Letting beings no longer need god is god's purpose of existence.

## Conclusion

Nüwa's goodwill is real, but goodwill cannot solve "children raised never grow up." What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is not a stronger Nüwa, but letting Myriad Ranks themselves learn not to depend on Nüwa. Gift-style order's ceiling is clear — god present = peace, god gone = chaos; symbiotic order's goal is making Myriad Ranks themselves become gods.
