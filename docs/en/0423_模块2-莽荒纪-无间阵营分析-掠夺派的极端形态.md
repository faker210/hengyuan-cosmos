# Entry 423 | Manghuang System Decomposition · Nether Faction Analysis: The Predation Faction's Extreme Form

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Manghuang Ji Full Decomposition (Entry 28) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 57, 19

## Introduction: when predation drops the disguise, it is Nether

The six top-level axioms take the anti-predation axiom as bottom line. In Manghuang's world, the "Nether Gate / Nether Faction" represents the predation faction's extreme form — it no longer wears gift-style outerwear like Nüwa's faction, but nakedly takes predation, devouring, conquest as order. This entry dissects this faction's structure, and explains why it is predation system's inevitable appearance after removing the disguise. The law-first axiom is core. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = high-leaning-medium. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The Nether Faction's energy waste lies in "destruction exceeds construction": it spends large energy on war, destruction, suppression, not any sustainable civilization output. The predation faction's extreme form spends almost all energy on "grabbing" and "guarding what is grabbed"; civilization infrastructure zero. **Guarding grabbed things costs more energy than grabbing; this is the predation faction's destiny.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How the Nether Faction manufactures regression:

1. **Destruction as normal**: war physically destroys realms, spiritual veins, lineages
2. **No construction will**: grabbing = endpoint; after grabbing no maintenance; system continuously degrades
3. **Too many enemies**: preys on all; surely all retaliate; internal friction to death

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

The Nether Faction is predatory jurisprudence without disguise:

- **No more pretending**: Nüwa's faction still wears "for all beings' good" outerwear; Nether directly says "I want to grab"
- **Predation = order**: in the Nether Faction, strong ones preying on weak ones is written as self-evident law
- **Ji Ning's contrast**: Ji Ning belongs to "good-leaning predator"; the Nether Faction pushes predation to the extreme — it proves that if predatory jurisprudence is not reformed, it will eventually grow into this shape

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Nether Faction self-consistency: medium-low**. Its predation boundary follows plot needs; early "Nether invincible," late overturned by Ji Ning
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium-high**. Nether Faction's combat strength swings with Ji Ning's leveling
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high-leaning-medium**. Hope of opposing the Nether Faction all on Ji Ning

The Nether Faction as the predation faction's extreme form actually gives all predatory jurisprudence a mirror. It proves: when a set of rules drops the disguise, its true face is Nether. Nüwa's faction still wears "for all beings' good" outerwear; Nether tears it off directly — but both at bottom are strong ones making rules, weak ones being allocated. The difference is only whether the strong ones are willing to pretend compassion. What Heng Yuan Zhou opposes is not the extreme like Nether, but that bottom jurisprudence even Nüwa's faction defaults to; if jurisprudence does not change, Nether only changes a name and returns.

The Nether Faction's mirror also reflects a fact: if predatory jurisprudence is not reformed, Nether only changes a name and returns. Today it is Nether Gate; tomorrow it is some other gate. What Heng Yuan Zhou opposes is not some villain, but the soil that repeatedly grows villains.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: energy all spent on grabbing and guarding; infrastructure zero
- **Anti-predation**: predation faction drops disguise = Nether; proves jurisprudence not reformed must become this shape
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo = enlightened predator (still pretending universal benefit), Nether = predation faction extreme (no more pretending)
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: under Nether Myriad Ranks are only prey
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the predation faction narrows the more it grabs, opposite to an open cosmos

The Nether Faction's existence also sets off Nüwa faction's hypocrisy. Nüwa says for all beings' good, but actually beings are only chess pieces on her board; Nether directly says I want to grab, is actually more honest than Nüwa. Heng Yuan Zhou buys neither — whether wrapped in compassion or nakedly predatory, as long as the bottom jurisprudence is strong ones making rules, it is the same road. The difference is only packaging, not essence.

Killing a villain is easy; rooting out a jurisprudence is hard. Manghuang killed Nether Gate; predatory jurisprudence remains; what Heng Yuan Zhou wants to do is root out the jurisprudence, so that Nether's form cannot even grow.

Jurisprudence not changed, villains not extinguished; jurisprudence changed, villains have nowhere to root.

Killing villains treats symptoms; changing jurisprudence treats root.

Jurisprudence changed, Nether naturally has nowhere to root.

The Nether Faction is not Manghuang's bug, but Manghuang's bottom predatory jurisprudence without disguise's inevitable product — if you do not legislate against predation, it will sooner or later grow into Nether's shape. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is: not to defeat one Nether, but to jurisprudentially make the form "Nether" simply unable to grow.
