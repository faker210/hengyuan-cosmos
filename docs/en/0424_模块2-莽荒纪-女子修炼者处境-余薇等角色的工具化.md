# Entry 424 | Manghuang System Decomposition · Female Cultivators' Situation: Instrumentalization of Yu Wei and Other Characters

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Manghuang Ji Full Decomposition (Entry 29) | Benchmark base: Documents 08, 37, 51

## Introduction: she is a Dao companion, a tribulation, a reason for resurrection — alone not herself

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races makes clear "human men and women" equally have roads; the collective ascension axiom requires strong ones carrying Myriad Ranks upward together. In Manghuang's world, female cultivators (represented by Yu Wei) are typically instrumentalized: she can be the protagonist's Dao companion, his love tribulation, the reason he later masters spacetime to resurrect, but rarely a subject with independent cultivation goals, independent will, independent civilizational contribution. This entry dissects this instrumentalization. The law-first axiom is core. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = high-leaning-medium. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Female cultivators' energy waste lies in "aptitude appropriated by narrative": Yu Wei and other characters have cultivation aptitude, but are set as consumables for the protagonist's emotional line — her death is to give Ji Ning the motive to "save his wife"; her resurrection is to complete the protagonist's completeness. **An independent strong one's aptitude is compressed into a prop in the protagonist's arc.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How female cultivators being instrumentalized manufactures regression:

1. **Half the potential buried**: half the population's cultivation aptitude not seriously cultivated
2. **Women no institutional position**: Chaos Immortal Realm, Three Realms top almost no independent seats for female strong ones
3. **Love tribulation as tool**: women's fate decided by "emotional relation with protagonist," not own cultivation

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Behind instrumentalization is the protagonist's predation of female characters' subjectivity:

- **"She died for me"**: Yu Wei's death narratively used as emotional fuel for Ji Ning's leveling
- **"I live for her"**: Ji Ning's later insistence is essentially pulling her back into his own narrative — her existence's meaning still serves the protagonist
- **Independent personality absent**: Yu Wei rarely has her own cultivation goals, her own enemies, her own Dao

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Manghuang Women's Situation | Anchor-Symbiosis Female Cultivation |

| Dao companion = emotional consumable | Myriad-Ranks republicanism: female cultivators have independent paths and independent goals |
| Death = protagonist-leveling fuel | Symbiotic education system: men and women taught and examined equally |

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Manghuang women's setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Yu Wei's life-death line swings with protagonist plot; early "certain death," late "resurrection"
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Resurrection-line retcon traces obvious
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high-leaning-medium**. Women's fate hangs on protagonist emotional line

Female-cultivator instrumentalization also has a structural consequence: it makes the position of Dao companion the highest-risk role. In Manghuang, once a woman is defined as the protagonist's Dao companion, she loses her own story line, only remaining three functional slots: loved, suffering, rescued. Yu Wei's tragedy is not that she died, but that when alive she also had no own goal. Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad-Ranks republicanism wants to restore Dao companion from functional slot to independent player — she can be a Dao companion, but first she is herself, with her own Dao, her own enemies, her own completeness.

Female-cultivator instrumentalization is also contagious. When women are instrumentalized, men also flatten — they only retain two dimensions: "strong" and "loving." Heng Yuan Zhou making all characters complete people makes the whole civilization's narrative layers thicken.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: half the population's cultivation potential appropriated by narrative
- **Anti-predation**: taking female characters' subjectivity into the protagonist's arc
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo system women's situation equally marginal, but Manghuang writing "resurrecting Dao companion" as the main reason for protagonist's peak; instrumentalization heavier
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: human men and women equally have roads; Manghuang only seriously takes "his woman"
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: a civilization with half the potential buried lacks openness resilience

Female-cultivator instrumentalization also drags down the whole civilization's emotional narrative layers. When female characters only have "loved—suffering—rescued," male characters' relations also flatten — Ji Ning's deep love for Yu Wei, because Yu Wei has no independent personality, instead appears thin. Heng Yuan Zhou making women independent players instead makes love scenes more moving, because both are complete people. Respecting the other half makes love not a fairy tale.

## Conclusion

Yu Wei is not without charm, but all her charm is borrowed to illuminate Ji Ning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment is simple: a female cultivator is first herself, then someone else's Dao companion. Restoring her from "resurrection reason" to "independent strong one" lets civilization gain another half a card table.
