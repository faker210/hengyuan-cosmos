# Entry 426 | Manghuang System Decomposition · Commoners' Situation: The Bottom-Level Destiny of the Spiritual-Root-Less

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Manghuang Ji Full Decomposition (Entry 31) | Benchmark base: Documents 08, 36, 51

## Introduction: without a spiritual root, you do not even qualify to be exploited

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires Myriad Ranks 99% full coverage and all beings' equal evolution qualification. Manghuang's world's bottom-level tragedy is the spiritual-root-less commoners: they do not even have cultivation's admission ticket; in strong ones' struggles, Great-Expert battles, they are not even "the exploited," only numbers in the backdrop. This entry dissects this bottom destiny. The law-first axiom is core. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = high-leaning-medium. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The spiritual-root-less commoners' energy waste is human resources completely ignored: the vast majority of mortals' potential is directly zeroed after the "no spiritual root" verdict. A civilization that pronounces 99% of its population "unrelated to cultivation" equals locking 99% of human energy. **This is not wasting one resource, but wasting an entire civilization's population base.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How the no-spiritual-root system manufactures regression:

1. **Talent funnel**: 99% population not cultivated; civilization can only select geniuses from 10%
2. **Great-calamity cannon fodder**: mortals die by the hundred million in war, yet have no name
3. **Class locked**: those with spiritual roots cultivate; those without are mortals for generations; class never moves

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Behind the no-spiritual-root system is monopoly of "evolution qualification":

- **Spiritual root = admission ticket**: cultivation qualification monopolized by this natural threshold; root-less excluded from day one
- **Mortals = backdrop**: in narrative mortals appear only when "how many died in great war," not as individuals
- **Ji Ning's blind spot**: he cultivates, levels, saves his wife, yet never thought about "how to give root-less people a road"

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Manghuang no-root setting self-consistency: medium**. Spiritual-root verdict rules internally self-consistent, but its "root-less = waste" ethics unexamined
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Late after protagonist levels, spiritual-root importance diluted
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high-leaning-medium**. Mortals' fate hangs on whether strong ones "pass by"

The root-less commoners also have an overlooked value: they are civilization's base. Strong cultivators no matter how strong, still rely on mortals to farm, work, bear children. Manghuang treating mortals as backdrop equals stepping on the base. A civilization where 99% have no road — these 99%, even if they do not rebel, are only silently consuming civilization — because they see no hope. Heng Yuan Zhou's Everyone-Has-a-Road guarantee is not almsgiving, but making the base a base again. A top without a base, however high, is a castle in the air.

The root-less commoners also have a hidden value: they are civilization's memory keepers. Cultivators busy cultivating; only mortals farm, tell stories, pass generations. Manghuang treating memory keepers as backdrop equals strangling civilization's own memory.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: 99% population potential locked by "no spiritual root" verdict
- **Anti-predation**: evolution qualification monopolized by spiritual-root threshold
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least universal-benefited humankind's lower-middle martial path; Manghuang did not even systematically give mortals martial path
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: root-less mortals in Manghuang are absent even from "Myriad Races" statistics
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: a civilization with 99% no road lacks openness resilience

The root-less commoners also have a political consequence: they have no voice in narrative. Manghuang world's decisions — war or peace, ascend or defend — all decided among cultivators; mortals do not even qualify to be consulted. A civilization where 99% population has no say, its "consensus" is only cultivators' consensus. Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad-Ranks republicanism includes mortals as voters; decisions truly become representative. A civilization without mortal voices is only a small group's civilization.

When the base is steady, the top is steady. Manghuang treating mortals as backdrop equals building the pyramid on sand. Heng Yuan Zhou propping up the base makes the whole civilization's tower stand steady.

## Conclusion

The no-spiritual-root system's cruelest thing is not not letting people cultivate, but judging them "unrelated to evolution" from birth. What Heng Yuan Zhou's Everyone-Has-a-Road guarantee wants is precisely to abolish this verdict — a civilization where 99% have no road, however strong, is only a very small civilization.
