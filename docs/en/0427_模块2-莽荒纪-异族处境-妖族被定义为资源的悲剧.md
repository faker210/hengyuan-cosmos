# Entry 427 | Manghuang System Decomposition · Alien Situation: The Tragedy of Demon Races Being Defined as Resources

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Manghuang Ji Full Decomposition (Entry 32) | Benchmark base: Documents 50, 52, 12

## Introduction: they are not races, they are walking medicine-herb cabinets

Among the six top-level axioms, the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings' equal evolution qualification. Manghuang world's aliens (demon tribes, alien beasts) have it worse than root-less mortals — mortals at least are still "people"; demon tribes are not even a race, only a "body-refining material cabinet." Their existence's meaning is defined as "being refined by human cultivators." This entry dissects this "resource-ification" tragedy. The law-first axiom is core. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = high-leaning-medium. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Demon races' resource-ification energy waste is reducing an entire intelligent race to "pill raw material": among demon tribes, clans that could have produced civilization, strong ones, independent cultivation systems, under the "material" definition are hunted to extinction. **Treating intelligent races as a mine to dig equals burning an entire race's civilizational potential into a pot of decoction.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How demon-race resource-ification manufactures regression:

1. **Ecology collapses**: high-tier fierce beasts hunted; biosphere broken; even the natural circulation humankind survives on collapses
2. **Racial hatred**: demon tribes long hunted; surely retaliate in great war; civilization internal friction
3. **Knowledge single**: demon-tribe ecological wisdom, bloodline inheritance ignored; humankind cultivates one road alone

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Behind demon-race resource-ification is species-discrimination jurisprudence:

- **"They are material"**: narrative from the start defines demon tribes as unintelligent, should-die, should-refine
- **Human exception**: all "spiritual root," "cultivation" rules only apply to humans
- **Ji Ning's dual-system superposition**: he refines body by fierce beasts; equal to personally eating demon tribes as material

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Manghuang demon-race setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Demon tribes' "intelligence level" swings with plot; early mindless as beasts, late occasionally humanlike
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Demon-race combat strength redefined with protagonist leveling
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high-leaning-medium**

Demon-race resource-ification also has a long-term consequence: it makes humankind lose the ability to dialogue with alien civilizations. Manghuang humans deal all their lives only with human cultivators; encountering demon tribes means fight; encountering aliens means kill. After several generations, humankind has not even grown the ability to negotiate with different civilizations. Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad-Races republicanism is not only being kind to demon tribes, but training humankind itself — learning to symbiosize with different beings is itself a kind of cultivation. Treating aliens as material, humankind itself also degenerates dialogue ability.

Demon-race resource-ification also makes Manghuang lose its ecological stabilizer. Without demon-race balance, some species overflow, some species go extinct; the biosphere collapses on its own. Heng Yuan Zhou's environmental-protection law is not only being kind to demon tribes, but preserving the whole world's ecological base.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

Treating aliens as friends, humankind can see its own problems.

Respecting demon tribes is essentially respecting life's diversity. A civilization that does not even respect nearby lives, how can it talk about Myriad-Races republicanism. Demon tribes' fate is the mirror of civilization's own cultivation.

Being kind to demon tribes is being kind to civilization's own ecological base.
