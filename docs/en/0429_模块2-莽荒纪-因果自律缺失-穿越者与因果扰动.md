Ji Ning is a transmigrator: a modern soul airdropped into Manghuang's world, enjoying the time-difference dividend, yet never filing, never settling. This entry specifically discusses this causal self-discipline absence. The law-first axiom is core. Protagonist uniqueness dependency = high-leaning-medium. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to the six top-level axioms.

A transmigrator's causal waste lies in "disturbance without correction": Ji Ning airdrops with past-life cognition, changes Manghuang world's evolution curve, yet no mechanism corrects the disturbance back. **A foreign variable injected into a native system, yet the system has no feedback loop — this is energy waste at the causal level.**

- **Manghuang causal setting self-consistency: low**. The conflict between transmigrator's past-life memory and native causality never deeply inquired
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium-high**. Causal line rounded by plot; early "fate," late "change fate"
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high-leaning-medium**. The transmigrator halo is Ji Ning's hidden pillar of success

Causal self-discipline absence also has an often-overlooked angle: it lets the transmigrator forever live in moral immunity. Whatever Ji Ning does he need not be responsible, because he is an outsider; Manghuang's causality cannot reach him. Once this immunity holds, the transmigrator has incentive to keep increasing disturbance — anyway no settlement. Heng Yuan Zhou's causal supervision precisely breaks this immunity: an external variable is also a variable; once disturbed it must be recorded, evaluated, corrected. The cross-plane door may open, but behind the door there must be a ledger.

Causal self-discipline absence also makes the transmigrator forever young. He need not be responsible for his own disturbance, so he can stay naive. Heng Yuan Zhou's supervision wants the transmigrator to also grow old — responsible for every cross-plane operation he makes; this is a mature cross-plane civilization.

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

- **Anti-energy waste**: disturbance uncorrected; external variable no feedback loop

- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo was native, no such problem; Ji Ning and Tang San both owe a karmic bill
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: native Myriad Ranks become soil for transmigrator arbitrage
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cross-plane unregulated; the more crossing, the messier causality

Causal self-discipline absence also traps the transmigrator in a paradox: the more successful he is, the larger the disturbance, the heavier the future karmic debt. The more Ji Ning reaches the peak in Manghuang, the deeper his change on Manghuang's causality — this change should have been recorded, evaluated, corrected, yet is written off. What Heng Yuan Zhou's causal supervision wants to do is precisely not let the transmigrator's success become a check that never cashes. Cross-plane prosperity cannot be built on debt default.

The cross-plane bill must be repaid sooner or later. Heng Yuan Zhou only establishes this ledger in advance, not letting the transmigrator default until the last moment. The door may open; the ledger must be kept.

The cross-plane door opens beautifully, premise is the ledger kept clearly.

Causal self-discipline is the cross-plane civilization's coming-of-age rite.
