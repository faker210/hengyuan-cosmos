## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones (Overall Conclusion)

Manghuang's energy waste is systemic: Controller-tier aptitude used to save one person; dual-system resource black hole devouring generations of offerings; Great-Expert battles' energy turning to dust; long lifespans spinning empty in closed-door instance-running. **A system that presses civilization-tier energy into the personal dantian inherently has negative energy utilization — the stronger the more wasted.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression (Overall Conclusion)

Manghuang's regression is three iron laws: a Great Expert falls = lineage break; a great calamity zeros out = evaporation; realms inflated = abandoned. Civilization curve is not ascending, but "spiral zeroing." Quest-Sediment-Domain simulation empirically: after Ji Ning's absence civilization returns to origin in about three great-calamity cycles.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking (Overall Conclusion)

Ji Ning is a "good-leaning predator" — he values loyalty and affection, yet stands atop the jurisprudence of "ownerless goods go to the capable." Dual-system one person eats double resources; demon tribes treated as material; women instrumentalized; root-less have no road; transmigrator does not settle causality — all lesions are this predatory jurisprudence's projections on different dimensions.

- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. Realm inflation + multi-book linkage + plot kidnapping triple overlay
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high-leaning-medium**. Lone-path cultivation style — civilization survival depends on Ji Ning personally reaching the top; he has Bodhi, Nüwa and other pillars beside him, hence rated "high-leaning-medium" not "extremely high"

Lone-path cultivation's ultimate limitation is not that Ji Ning is not good enough, but that a good protagonist should not carry the whole civilization alone. This is why all thirty-one dissections converge on "Myriad Ranks walk together."

## Conclusion

Manghuang's thirty-one dissections ultimately point to one sentence: **lone-path cultivation's ultimate limitation is not that the protagonist is not strong enough, but that only he is too strong.** Ji Ning is not a bad person; he is even that world's most affectionate, hardest-working, most protective person. But Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment never asks whether the protagonist is good, only whether the structure is stable. A system that bets civilization on one person's dantian — the day one person reaches the top is the day the whole cosmos becomes his backdrop. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is not a second Ji Ning, but Myriad Ranks walking together — this is why lone-path cultivation truly should be replaced.
