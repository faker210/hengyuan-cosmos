# Entry 432 | Dragon Ball System Decomposition (Overview): Goku and the Combat-Power Inflation System

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Ball Full Decomposition (Entry 1 · Overview) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 17, 19

## Introduction: when "getting stronger" itself becomes the only plot

Dragon Ball's Son Goku (Kakarotto) is the world's most widely recognized Japanese combat-type protagonist: a Saiyan orphan, from a mountain boy all the way to Super Saiyan Blue, Ultra Instinct, Selfishness, finally standing beside the God of Destruction's and Zeno's chessboard. It pioneered the modern combat narrative paradigm of "transformation = leveling = hot-blood," and etched "combat-power inflation" into pop-culture genes — battle power numbers from just over a thousand in the Raditz arc, inflating to billions in the Buu arc, then to *Super*'s God-of-Destruction tier, Angel tier, Zeno tier, until *Heroes* produced cross-universe opponents "even Zeno is not enough to look at." Heng Yuan Zhou does not moral-judge Goku's combat zeal, but dissects how far this system's structure can go.

As the Dragon Ball decomposition series's master outline, this entry establishes the full judging base — the six top-level axioms: anti-predation axiom (develop increment, do not consume stock, do not take killing-predation as evolution path), republican axiom of Myriad Races (all beings equal evolution qualification), collective ascension axiom (strong ones feed back to Myriad Ranks; no great-material-wasted), causal self-discipline axiom (cross-plane observe first then limited interference; return only knowledge), **law-first axiom (law precedes, never retcons, setting self-consistency above plot convenience)**, universal equal infinite law — of which the law-first axiom is core, because Dragon Ball is precisely the world's number-one negative example of "numerical inflation, setting layer after layer, multi-line retcon." The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency verdict: **Goku = high-dependency / gift-style-leaning-oligarchic civilization** (order hangs on Goku's family and Zeno; civilization institutions completely absent).

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores (anti-energy waste, anti-predation, Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison, Myriad Races full coverage, hyper-multiverse full openness), with the law-first axiom as core.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Goku's energy finally reaches God-of-Destruction—Angel tier and even Zeno-tier reference frame, but its direction has extreme systemic waste:

- **Pure combat orientation**: from Master Roshi to Piccolo, Vegeta, Frieza, Cell, Buu, Beerus, Jiren, Moro, Granolah, every Goku breakthrough's only use is "fight a stronger fight"; never converted top energy into civilization-tier infrastructure
- **Cultivation privatized**: Kaioken, Spirit Bomb, Super Saiyan transformation route all held within Goku's family; ordinary Earth and Galaxy cultivators do not even have a systematic theory of "ki"
- **Cosmos-tier aptitude's zero spillover**: Goku can sense the whole cosmos's Spirit Bomb, can trade blows as an equal with Gods of Destruction, yet never used this perception to rebuild Namek, never helped the hundred planets destroyed by Frieza's army — all energy sedimented in his personal muscle memory

Conclusion: holding God-of-Destruction-tier power, yet only used to "find the next stronger opponent" — this is using cosmos-tier energy for martial-artist-tier purposes; its waste ranks among the top across the protagonist lineage.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Dragon Ball world's regression has structural inevitability, and every "after great war" repeats the same curve:

1. **Strong one absent = collapse**: Planet Vegeta destroyed by Frieza's single thought; Namek massacred by Frieza's army; Earth repeatedly destroyed in Saiyan, Cell, Buu arcs and revived by Dragon Balls — civilization itself has no self-repair ability; everything depends on Dragon Ball wishes to roll back
2. **No institutional construction**: Earth from Bulma's Capsule Corp to Kami's Lookout, no election, law, or civilizational consensus evolution; the World Tournament is the only "public life," ends when the fight ends
3. **Strong-one cycle replacement**: every generation's crisis solved by "Goku transformed again"; never built early-warning, defense, anti-invasion systems. Cell Game killed a generation; Majin Buu killed another; memory and lessons cleared by Dragon Ball revival

Betting civilization's safety on two single logs — "whether Goku is present" and "whether Dragon Balls can grant wishes" — regression is not possible, it is normal.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Predatory thinking defined by structure not malice; Goku's "predation" hides deeper than Tang San:

- **Combat instinct = resource consumption**: Saiyan race's evolution logic is "stronger after near-death revival," essentially treating life as consumable — every Goku recovery from serious injury trades his own life safety as fuel for numbers; Vegeta Planet's warriors even more so
- **Attention = resource monopoly**: the cosmos's mightiest beings (God of Destruction, Supreme Kai, Zeno) willing to spend time watching, training, sparring with Goku — this "top mentor resource" should have universal-benefited Myriad Ranks, yet all poured on him alone
- **Enemy = leveling EXP pack**: Frieza, Cell, Buu, Zamasu… every villain's plot function is "the step that makes Goku stronger"; the villain's race, civilization, suffering only serves the protagonist's numerical growth

Goku is not the "enlightened predator" malicious type, but the "innocent predator" — he truly loves fighting, never actively enslaves the weak, yet never realized he is standing on a step built with Myriad Ranks' suffering as foundation.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: extremely low**. Early "battle-power numbers" were Akira Toriyama's explicit constraint (Raditz ~1000, Gohan rage to 2800); by Frieza arc numbers out of control; after Cell arc simply abandoned numbers; Super Saiyan 1→2→3→Legendary→God→Blue→Ultra Instinct→Selfishness, transformation layers uncapped; God of Destruction, Angels, Zeno, Zeno's attendants, Grand Minister layer upon layer, each layer "more casual" than the last
- **Retcon-ending risk: extremely high**. Dragon Ball GT (fan sequel), Dragon Ball Super (official sequel), Dragon Ball Heroes (game animation) three lines mutually contradictory: GT's Super Saiyan 4 incompatible with Super's Super Saiyan God; Super's Zeno "destroys universes like playing a game" conflicts with early Supreme Kai's cosmology; Heroes directly cross-temporally crashes in; setting utterly becomes a toy
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high (gift-style-leaning-oligarchic)**. Earth's survival, Galaxy's safety, cosmos peace all hang on Goku's family (Goku + Vegeta + Gohan + Trunks); civilization institutional construction zero

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: God-of-Destruction—Angel-tier aptitude all invested in personal combat; civilization zero spillover
- **Anti-predation**: Saiyan near-death-revival cultivation = life as fuel; villains as EXP packs
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Goku went further than Tang Luo — Tang Luo at least built an academy; Goku did not even build an academy
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Earth commoners, Namekians, Galaxy Force in combat narrative are all backdrop
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: closed God-of-Destruction—Angel—Zeno dictatorial pyramid, furthest from symbiotic cosmos
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive; predatory involution loses meaning at root

## Conclusion

Goku is comics history's purest martial artist. He is not greedy, not treacherous, not enslaving anyone; he only wants to get strong, to trade blows with the strong. But Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment never asks whether the protagonist is cute, only whether the structure is stable: when "protecting the world" can only rely on "one person got strong again," when "world rebuilding" can only rely on "make another wish," the hotter this system, the more fragile. Dragon Ball proved one thing in forty years — personal combat power can put one person on Zeno's chessboard, yet cannot put a civilization onto a tomorrow that does not need him.
