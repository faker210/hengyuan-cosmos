## Introduction: when cosmos-tier power serves only one martial-arts match

Heng Yuan Zhou judges a strong one's energy not by "how strong he can hit," but by "how much civilization gets stronger because of him." Goku's combat-power curve is the steepest in comics history: from a boy flipping cars bare-handed, to a Super Saiyan punching stars, to Super Saiyan Blue arm-wrestling Gods of Destruction, to the Ultra Instinct realm "even gods must study." This energy in the Myriad-Realms lineage is top-tier, yet his share directed to civilization is almost zero. This entry, by the law-first axiom (law precedes, never retcons) and anti-energy-waste lens, dissects Dragon Ball system's energy mismatch layer by layer.

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Goku's energy waste can be split into four dimensions:

- **Race dimension**: Saiyan near-death-revival gain is a cosmos-rare "life—combat" conversion mechanism, but it only serves the Saiyan race. Namekians, Earthlings, Frieza-army subordinate races — none gained theoretical or technical feedback from this biological miracle
- **Tier dimension**: Kaioken, Spirit Bomb, Instant Transmission, Ultra Instinct — these top skills all passed within Goku's family by mouth; Earth's "ki" theory remains at Turtle School's master-apprentice system, never textbook-ified, academified
- **Object dimension**: Beerus the God of Destruction willing to spar with Goku, Whis the Angel willing to coach him, Zeno willing to play Tournament of Power with him — the cosmos's top mentor resources and political resources all poured on one person; other cosmos beings do not even have auditor qualification
- **Use dimension**: every breakthrough's only use is "defeat the next villain." Goku never used Spirit Bomb to heal a destroyed planet, never used Instant Transmission to build a cross-star medical network, never used Ultra Instinct's perception to early-warn the next cosmos-tier disaster

Conclusion: **Zeno-tier aptitude, martial-artist-tier use.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Dragon Ball world's energy waste directly feeds historical regression:

1. **Knowledge does not spill**: every Goku breakthrough does not enter the public knowledge base; Earth civilization's cultivation level in forty years of plot almost stands still — Krillin, Tien Shinhan, Yamcha these early strong ones late become complete backdrop; not that they did not work hard, but the system did not give them a road
2. **Disasters repeat**: because Goku's energy never converted into a defense system, Frieza came, Cell came, Buu came, Beerus came, Moro came — the same disaster pattern with different villains acted for fifty years
3. **Wish rollback erases lessons**: after every disaster Dragon Balls revive the dead, restore cities; civilization has no "having suffered" memory; next time the same mistake is repeated

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Goku's predatory thinking is "innocent type," but structurally equally valid:

- **Top attention monopolized**: Whis, Beerus, Zeno could have spent time reforming Universe 10, balancing Universe 6 and 7 ecology, yet all used to "play" with Goku — this is personal occupation of cosmos-tier public resources
- **Training opportunities hoarded**: skills Goku learned in the Hyperbolic Time Chamber, on Whis's planet, on Yardrat, never systematically taught to Earth companions; Vegeta got to play because he chased to learn, not because Goku proactively opened up
- **Enemy suffering entertainment-ified**: Frieza destroying Planet Vegeta, Namek's entire race, in narrative ultimately all became "emotional fuel for Goku to become Super Saiyan" — Myriad Ranks' suffering compressed into the protagonist's pre-transformation backstory
