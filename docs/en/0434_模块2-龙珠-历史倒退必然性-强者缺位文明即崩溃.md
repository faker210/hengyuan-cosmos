# Entry 434 | Dragon Ball · Historical Regression Necessity: Strong One Absent and Civilization Collapses

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Ball Full Decomposition (Entry 3) | Benchmark base: Documents 03, 20, 49

## Introduction: after every great war, civilization starts from zero

Heng Yuan Zhou's historical view: whether a civilization is mature is not by how strong its strongest is, but by whether it can still run when the strongest is absent. Dragon Ball world is precisely this standard's negative specimen — its civilization curve is not ascending, but "destroy—wish-rebuild—destroy again—wish-rebuild again" cycle. This entry, by the law-first axiom (law precedes, never retcons) and collective ascension axiom (strong ones feed back to Myriad Ranks; no great-material-wasted), dissects why Dragon Ball world is "strong one present and civilization present; strong one gone and civilization collapses."

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Dragon Ball world's energy waste, the most glaring manifestation, is **strong ones never legislate for civilization**:

- Goku defeats King Piccolo, yet established no "anti-demon-invasion early-warning mechanism"
- Goku defeats Frieza, yet pushed no "anti-Frieza-army international convention"
- Goku defeats Cell, Buu, yet left no "what if Majin revives next time" contingency plan
- Even Piccolo, Kami, Supreme Kai these "gods," never formulated a cosmos-tier civilization governance framework

Energy all sedimented in muscle memory; institutional construction zero.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Dragon Ball world's historical regression is structurally inevitable:

1. **Revival erases history**: Dragon Ball wishes revive the dead, restore cities; civilization has no true "trauma memory"; next time the same mistake is repeated — this is the causal self-discipline axiom's complete opposite
2. **Commoners no armed ability**: Earth ordinary people in the Saiyan arc could only hide in houses; by Cell, Buu arcs directly one-shot killed; civilization has no self-organized defense
3. **God-tier stratum does not legislate**: Supreme Kai only "observes," God of Destruction only "destroys," Zeno only "wipes the table" — the whole cosmos has no "governor" role
4. **Technology single-point dependency**: Capsule Corp, Bulma's family is Earth's only tech engine; Bulma stays home, Earth tech does not advance
5. **School and education broken**: Gohan was a scholar seedling, yet repeatedly pulled back to the battlefield by wars; Earth has no public system to cultivate next-generation cultivators

Conclusion: **Dragon Ball world's "peace" was never institutional product, but villains not attacking yet.**

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Goku's predatory thinking on the "historical regression" dimension manifests as **irresponsibility for civilization's future**:

- **Father absent**: Gohan, Goten's growth repeatedly interrupted by Goku's "want to fight again"; after Goku died he should cultivate in Other World, yet repeatedly runs back to the human world "to see old friends"
- **No successor cultivation**: Goku only circles in the "can beat then beat, can't then train more" loop; never systematically cultivated a next generation that could take over Earth after he dies
- **Treats cosmos as playground**: Tournament of Power, Zeno's arena; Goku's every "I want to trade blows with the strong" bets the whole cosmos's survival — this is treating Myriad Ranks' fate as personal hobby

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: low**. "Dragon Balls can revive people" early had limits (one-year lifespan, only one wish); late limits repeatedly broken: Namekian Dragon Balls can grant three wishes, Frieza revived, Cell revived, Buu's pure good revived… revival rules entirely serve plot
- **Retcon-ending risk: extremely high**. GT turns Goku back into a child, Super rewrites Earth timeline back to "Goku still a martial artist," Heroes directly cross-temporal — historical regression in Dragon Ball series is not civilizational disease, but writers' common tool
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high (gift-style-leaning-oligarchic)**. Civilization survival entirely bet on Goku; no institutional backup

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Conclusion

Dragon Ball world's historical regression is not accident, but design. Every "peace" is built on the gamble that "Goku can transform next time"; every "rebuild" relies on wiping history and starting over. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants to ask: what if Dragon Balls can no longer grant wishes? What if Goku did not make it back this time? Dragon Ball series in forty years never answered these two questions — because its worldview never intended to let civilization stand on its own. Making civilization not depend on any one Goku is the true exit.
