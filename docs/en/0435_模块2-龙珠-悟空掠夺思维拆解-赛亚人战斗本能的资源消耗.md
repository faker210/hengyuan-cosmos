# Entry 435 | Dragon Ball · Goku Predatory-Thinking Decomposition: Resource Consumption of Saiyan Combat Instinct

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Ball Full Decomposition (Entry 4) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 19, 57

## Introduction: the kind warrior, the predatory structure

Heng Yuan Zhou's definition of "predatory thinking" does not take malice as standard, but structure: as long as a system's evolution path is built on consuming others, monopolizing resources, hoarding opportunities, however innocent and kind the protagonist himself, this thinking belongs to predation. Goku is one of comics history's kindest protagonists — he does not enslave, does not crave wealth, does not retaliate, even spared Frieza. But he stands on a predation system engine by Saiyan combat instinct without knowing. This entry, by the law-first axiom and anti-predation axiom, dissects this "innocent-type predation."

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Goku's combat instinct is itself an energy-waste device:

- **Combat consumption > construction output**: every Goku breakthrough is accompanied by planet-tier destruction — Namek wrecked, Earth wrecked, Supreme Kai realm wrecked — the energy he raises is far less than the energy he destroys
- **Near-death revival's life bet**: the Saiyan "near-death-revival stronger" mechanism essentially bets every breakthrough on "can I survive this time"; it is the least economical energy conversion
- **Opponent = energy source**: Goku's growth curve highly depends on "a stronger villain appears"; he never proactively opens a new cultivation path

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How Saiyan combat instinct feeds historical regression:

1. **Planet Vegeta itself is a predatory civilization**: Saiyans hired by Frieza's army to conquer planets and sell natives — this is institutionalized interstellar predation
2. **Goku continued this road**: though he does not invade planets, his obsession with "fighting the strong" turns every crisis into a "first beaten bloody, then counter-kill" loop; Myriad Ranks pay blood price for his growth
3. **Combat > construction values**: the whole Dragon Ball world's hero standard is "can fight"; scientists (Bulma), scholars (Gohan forcibly pulled back to the battlefield), doctors (Bulma's healer friends) are never protagonists; civilization builders are always supporting roles

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Goku's predatory thinking can be split into three layers:

- **Racial predation memory**: the Saiyan race itself was Frieza's army's "conquest tool"; Planet Vegeta's wealth built on a hundred planets' ruins. Though Goku lost his memory, he inherited this racial original sin — his "combat talent" itself is predatory civilization's legacy
- **Enemy = leveling fuel**: Frieza, Cell, Buu, Zamasu, Jiren… every villain's suffering history, every destroyed city, in narrative is only emotional fuel to "make Goku angry, make Goku transform." Myriad Ranks' blood compressed into the protagonist's power-up shot
- **Moral leniency = immunity**: Goku sparing Frieza, sparing Vegeta, sparing Buu (pure good), praised by readers as "kindness." But Heng Yuan Zhou wants to ask: every villain he spared continued doing evil later (Frieza comes out again in Super to cause trouble); who pays for this "kindness"'s price? The next batch of commoners Frieza kills

Goku is not an "enlightened predator," he is an **"innocent predator"** — he never questioned the loop of "can't win then train more, after training fight again," never realized this loop's foundation is Myriad Ranks' suffering.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: low**. Saiyan "near-death-revival stronger" early a rare mechanism; in Super repeatedly used by Vegeta, Gohan, Trunks; even Frieza by "four months of training" catches up to Super Saiyan Blue — rules completely become plot tool
- **Retcon-ending risk: extremely high**. GT's Super Saiyan 4 Saiyan-tail setting, Super's Super Saiyan God ritual, Heroes's "God Blue" stacking, mutually contradictory
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high (gift-style-leaning-oligarchic)**. Goku's "kindness" defaulted by the whole cosmos as justice's guarantee; no one questions

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: combat consumption far exceeds construction output; all energy for "fight another fight"
- **Anti-predation**: Saiyan race itself predatory civilization; Goku inherits the talent without reflection
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least proactively breaks predatory structure; Goku does not even reflect
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: planets conquered by Saiyans, commoners killed by villains, in narrative have no voice
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Saiyan combat instinct locked on the single line of "the stronger the more can fight"; no multi-path evolution
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

Goku's kindness is real, but his kindness has no structure. A good person innocently standing on predatory foundation is more dangerous than a malicious predator — because he makes predation look natural. Heng Yuan Zhou does not require Goku to become a saint, only requires him to ask: every time I get strong, whose body am I standing on? Ask this one sentence, Saiyan combat instinct truly begins to turn.
