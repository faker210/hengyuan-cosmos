## Introduction: when the numbers themselves become the enemy

Dragon Ball is comics history's first work to put "battle-power numbers" on the table, and the first to completely break those numbers. When Raditz pulled out the scouter and said the farmer's "power level 5," readers thought this was a rigorous numerical system; by Frieza's final form numbers jumped to 100 million; readers had given up calculating; by *Super*, concepts like "Super Saiyan Blue," "Ultra Instinct" completely detached from numbers, became "who plot needs who is strong." This entry takes the **law-first axiom** as core, dissecting Dragon Ball combat-power inflation's whole process, and why Heng Yuan Zhou's nine-realms system can root out this collapse.

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Combat-power inflation itself is the greatest energy waste:

- **Old numbers invalidated**: Master Roshi 139, Raditz ~1000, Nappa 4000, Vegeta 18000 — these early carefully built number ladders, after Frieza arc all invalidated; readers' early accumulated "number sense" zeroed in one go
- **Old strong ones devalued**: Tien Shinhan, Yamcha, Krillin these early strong ones, after combat-power inflation do not even have "sparring" qualification; their cultivation results narratively erased
- **Old threats devalued**: King Piccolo, Red Ribbon Army, Tao Pai Pai, these early late-BOSSES become jokes — villains later and later absurd, because early bosses are no longer impressive

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How combat-power inflation causes historical regression:

1. **Cultivation path single-ized**: because of number inflation, all cultivators compressed onto the single road of "compare numbers with Goku"; any cultivator not pursuing numbers (e.g., Crane School, Eight-Qigong-Cut style) loses meaning
2. **Ordinary people completely out**: when numbers inflate from hundreds to hundred-millions, ordinary people do not even have "participate in battle" qualification; civilization further slips to "few strong ones monopolize"
3. **History discontinuous**: because of number inflation, readers cannot judge "how strong is Goku now"; the worldview's historical feel reset repeatedly — this is law-tier historical regression

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Goku's predatory thinking in combat-power inflation:

- **Number monopoly**: Goku's number-growth curve far faster than companions; Vegeta chased decades barely caught up; Gohan, Trunks, Goten's growth curves all pressed by Goku
- **Enemy escalation = resource**: every new villain appears, its number must be "slightly higher than Goku," otherwise cannot continue — this means Dragon Ball world must continuously manufacture stronger enemies; Myriad Ranks continuously pay blood price for Goku's number inflation
- **Transformation layers plunder narrative right**: SSJ1/2/3, God, Blue, Ultra Instinct… each transformation layer is the personal narrative of "I broke through again"; Myriad Ranks have no place in the narrative
