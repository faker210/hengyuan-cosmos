# Entry 438 | Dragon Ball · Plot-Kidnapping-Setting Case Study: The Paradigm of Forced Transformation for Plot

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Ball Full Decomposition (Entry 7) | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 27, 19

## Introduction: can't win? Then transform one more layer

"Plot kidnaps setting" is the law-first axiom's number-one enemy: plot needs the protagonist to win, early iron laws give way; plot needs the villain stronger, realm temporarily adds a layer. Dragon Ball is this lesion's global textbook-level specimen — every "Goku can't win, then transform one more layer" is a textbook plot kidnapping. This entry, by the law-first axiom, dissects Dragon Ball history's five most typical plot-kidnapping incidents one by one.

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Plot kidnapping itself is energy waste:

- **Cultivation path uneconomic**: every Goku new transformation layer is not the natural result of long-term cultivation, but "can't win, temporarily awakened" — Hyperbolic Time Chamber training, Kaioken training, these early foreshadowing all invalidated in front of temporary transformations
- **Breakthrough = plot node**: breakthrough is no longer cultivation's result, but plot's metronome — readers see "Goku knocked down" and know "next page should transform"; narrative predictable to losing tension
- **Law replaced by emotion**: early laws "ki needs cultivation," "transformation needs conditions," in anger, grief, protecting-friends emotions all fail — emotion becomes the new breakthrough key

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How plot kidnapping feeds historical regression:

1. **Cultivators lose direction**: since "anger" can break through, why still cultivate? Earth cultivators see Goku transform by emotion, naturally abandon systematic training
2. **Law not serious**: when readers find law can be broken anytime, the worldview's credibility collapses — this is law-tier historical regression
3. **Villains instrumentalized**: villains' strength no longer determined by their cultivation history, but by "how many layers of transformation Goku must be pushed to"; villains reduced to plot metronomes

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Goku's predatory thinking in plot kidnapping:

- **Emotion = predation permit**: Goku's "to protect friends," "for Earth," "for justice" plot-endowed with anytime-breakthrough power — this power essentially predates the "breakthrough law"
- **Companions' emotions commandeered**: Gohan's anger (Cell arc), Vegeta's self-reproach (Buu arc), Trunks's despair (Android arc), all plot-commandeered as fuel for "let Goku or next generation transform" — companions' suffering becomes the protagonist's leveling resource
- **Villains' existence meaning plundered**: Frieza, Cell, Buu these villains should have their own storylines, but under plot kidnapping their only meaning is "push Goku to transform"

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: extremely low**. Five landmark transformations — SSJ1 (Krillin's death), SSJ2 (Gohan anger), SSJ3 (Other-World training), Super Saiyan God (ritual), Ultra Instinct (crisis enlightenment) — none is the natural result of systematic cultivation
- **Retcon-ending risk: extremely high**. "Super Saiyan" early a millennia legend; in Super becomes "Vegeta, Trunks, Gohan, Goten, Future Trunks all can transform"; legend reduced to standard equipment
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high (gift-style-leaning-oligarchic)**. Plot kidnapping's only beneficiary is Goku; all laws give way for him to win

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: temporary transformation invalidates all early cultivation foreshadowing; energy investment zeroed
- **Anti-predation**: villains and companions' emotions commandeered as leveling fuel
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's breakthrough at least relies on training; Goku on "anger"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Myriad Ranks in plot kidnapping only deserve to be "protected objects"; do not participate in breakthrough
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: breakthrough locked on single line of "transformation layers"; no multi-path
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

Dragon Ball's transformation culture influenced the whole Japanese-comics industry, but it also held up a mirror to all creators: pushing breakthrough by emotion, short-term hot-blood, long-term collapse. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is to write breakthrough as law — cultivation duration, mind-cultivation exam, contribution points triple threshold; none dispensable. Let the "can't win" plot dilemma force the author to design new institutions, not force the protagonist to temporarily transform one more layer — this is the law-first axiom's true meaning.
