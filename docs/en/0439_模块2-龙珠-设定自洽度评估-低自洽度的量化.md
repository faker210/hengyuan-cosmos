## Introduction: hold up a mirror with scores

Heng Yuan Zhou has for each benchmark IP a "setting self-consistency" scoring table: whether law is legislated first, whether expansion goes via appendix, whether plot gives way, whether foreshadowing is recovered, whether combat power is calculable. This entry, by the law-first axiom, puts Dragon Ball's forty years of all setting on this table for scoring, giving quantitative conclusions. Scores are not humiliation, but diagnosis — know where it is low, know how to fix.

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Low self-consistency itself is energy waste:

- **Reader trust cost**: every time readers see "Goku temporarily transformed again," trust in the worldview docks one point; forty years accumulated, Dragon Ball fans have immunized to "setting," only watch fights
- **Cross-work interpretation cost**: GT, Super, Heroes three works coexisting; fans every discussion of setting must first argue "which line counts" — this is huge cognitive tax
- **Newcomer threshold cost**: new readers want to enter, find they must watch original + Z + GT + Super + Heroes; timelines all pits — IP's propagation efficiency severely dragged by low self-consistency

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How low self-consistency feeds civilizational regression:

1. **Law untrustworthy**: when readers find law can be broken anytime, civilizational "contract spirit" collapses too — this is law-not-serious's projection inside the worldview
2. **Institutions cannot build**: a world where even "can people be revived" changes anytime, cannot build stable law, stable history, stable expectations
3. **Knowledge not accumulated**: because setting changes anytime, early "cultivation knowledge" may be invalidated late; civilization cannot settle cultivation experience

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Goku's predatory thinking in low self-consistency:

- **Rule-making right monopolized**: when can transform, when can revive, when can cross time — all decided by Goku (behind him the writers) — this is personal monopoly of rule-making right
- **Companion rules rewritten**: Vegeta's pride, Gohan's scholar dream, Trunks's future, these characters' initial rules repeatedly rewritten in plot to serve Goku's main line
- **Villain rules plundered**: Frieza's "absolute freeze," Cell's "absorption," Buu's "regeneration," these setting late all narratively weakened; villains' "hole cards" are only Goku's stepping stones
