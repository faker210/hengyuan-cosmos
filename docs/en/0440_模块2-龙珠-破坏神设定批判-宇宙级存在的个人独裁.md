# Entry 440 | Dragon Ball · God-of-Destruction Setting Critique: Personal Dictatorship of a Cosmos-Tier Being

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Ball Full Decomposition (Entry 9) | Benchmark base: Documents 49, 40, 05

## Introduction: when a god says "I'm going to sleep"

*Dragon Ball Super* introduced the God of Destruction Beerus — the most "politically incorrect" setting in Dragon Ball worldview: a cosmos-tier being capable of casually destroying planets, whose entire job is "decide whether to destroy by mood." He sleeps when he wants for centuries; when he wakes he finds planets to smash for fun; whoever he dislikes he makes disappear — no council, no law, no term, no appeal. This is not a god; this is a cosmos-tier dictator. This entry, by the law-first axiom and republican axiom of Myriad Races, dissects the God-of-Destruction setting's institutional lesion.

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The God of Destruction's energy level is cosmos-tier, but its direction is pure waste:

- **Destruction > construction**: Beerus's entire "job" is destruction; he never used this energy to build any planet, help any race
- **Waking = disaster**: every time Beerus wakes, the cosmos tenses; his "daily life" is Myriad Ranks' "disaster countdown"
- **Training Goku out of private interest**: Beerus spars with Goku not for cosmos peace, but "find someone fun to fight" — cosmos-tier public resources used for personal entertainment

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How God-of-Destruction dictatorship feeds historical regression:

1. **Myriad Ranks have no security expectation**: any race does not know whether Beerus will wake tomorrow, will take a fancy to their planet — civilization dares not do long-term construction
2. **No institutional constraint**: above the God of Destruction only Angels (observers) and Zeno (higher dictator); Myriad Ranks themselves have no constraint channel on the God of Destruction
3. **Power hereditary/appointed**: God of Destruction chosen as apprentice by previous God of Destruction; Supreme Kais chosen by Supreme Kais — cosmos governance stratum completely closed; Myriad Ranks have no participation right

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Goku's predatory thinking in his relationship with the God of Destruction:

- **Befriending a dictator as equals**: after Goku beat Beerus, Beerus stopped smashing Earth — this "get-out-of-jail-free card" was bought by Goku's strength, not by Myriad Ranks' institutions. That is, Earth's safety depends on whether Goku can keep beating Beerus
- **Training resources monopolized**: Whis sparring with Goku is essentially the God-of-Destruction system lending cosmos-tier mentor resources to one Saiyan on Earth — no other being in Myriad Ranks gets this treatment
- **Taking dictator as friend**: Goku never questions Beerus's dictatorial legitimacy, only cares "is it fun to fight him" — this is structural disregard of Myriad Ranks' suffering

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: low**. God of Destruction "destruction maintains cosmos balance" claim never truly executed in plot — Beerus smashes planets mostly "dislike," unrelated to cosmos balance
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. Super's "12 universes" setting after Tournament of Power directly wiped 8 by Zeno; universe count changes with plot
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high (gift-style-leaning-oligarchic)**. Earth safety relies on Goku's private relationship with Beerus, not institutional guarantee

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: cosmos-tier energy all used for destruction and personal entertainment
- **Anti-predation**: Myriad Ranks' planets and lives disposed of at the God of Destruction's whim
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least did not become a dictator; Beerus is institutionalized dictatorship
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Myriad Ranks have no constraint channel on the God of Destruction
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: 12 universes should have been multigovernance's rich ore, but written as 12 dictators' fiefs
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

The God-of-Destruction setting's appearance marks Dragon Ball worldview's upgrade from "Earth martial arts" to "cosmos politics" — but the direction it upgraded was higher-grade dictatorship, not higher-grade republic.
