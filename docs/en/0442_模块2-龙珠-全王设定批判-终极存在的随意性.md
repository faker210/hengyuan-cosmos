# Entry 442 | Dragon Ball · Zeno Setting Critique: The Arbitrariness of the Ultimate Being

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Ball Full Decomposition (Entry 11) | Benchmark base: Documents 40, 49, 05

## Introduction: when a child holds an eraser

Zeno is the apex of Dragon Ball worldview: one sentence and a universe vanishes; one wave and all villains are cleared away; he sits there like an innocent child, yet holds the power of life and death over twelve universes. This setting pushes "the ultimate being's arbitrariness" to the extreme — more terrifying than the God of Destruction's dictatorship is that above him there is a child who thinks "playing games is enough." This entry takes the **law-first axiom** as core, dissecting the Zeno setting's institutional terror.

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Zeno's energy is Dragon Ball worldview's absolute apex, but its direction is pure waste:

- **Game-style governance**: Tournament of Power's essence is "Zeno wants to watch fights"; 8 universes eliminated just because "Zeno feels there are too many"
- **Eraser-style clearing**: Zamasu, Infinite Zamasu cleared by Zeno's single finger, not by trial, but because "Zeno finds them annoying"
- **Childified ultimate power**: Zeno looks like a child, acts like a child, decides like a child — but behind this childlike arbitrariness are Myriad Ranks' billion-billion lives

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How Zeno's arbitrariness feeds historical regression:

1. **No safety bottom line**: any universe, any race does not know which day Zeno "feels too many" and wipes them out — civilization dares not do any long-term planning
2. **Law meaningless**: Zeno's will above all law, all rules, all history — however perfect Myriad Ranks' institutions, Zeno's one finger and they're gone
3. **Myriad Ranks completely powerless**: facing Zeno, God of Destruction, Angels, Goku all can only "smile along"; Myriad Ranks do not even have a channel to express demands

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Goku's predatory thinking in his relationship with Zeno:

- **Making friends with the ultimate dictator**: Goku reaches out and says "hello hello" to Zeno; Zeno thus takes a liking to Goku — this "friendship" makes Zeno not lightly move Earth, but this protection is non-institutional, private affection
- **Tournament of Power's wager**: Goku proposes Tournament of Power, the wager is 8 universes' survival — he treats Myriad Ranks' fate as a chip for "wanting to trade blows with the strong"; Zeno is just playing along with him
- **Accepting arbitrariness**: Goku never questions "by what right does Zeno decide universes' survival," only thinks "this kid is cute" — this is the gifted's structural dependence on the ultimate dictator

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: extremely low**. Zeno "supreme in all universes" setting directly conflicts with early Supreme Kai "creates universes" setting — who creates universes? No one can say clearly
- **Retcon-ending risk: extremely high**. Future Zeno, present Zeno, two Zenos, Zeno's attendants, number and relationships added with plot
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high (gift-style-leaning-oligarchic)**. Earth safety relies on Goku's private friendship with Zeno, not institutional guarantee

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: absolute apex energy used to play games; billion-billion lives treated as chips
- **Anti-predation**: Zeno arbitrarily eliminates universes is predation of Myriad Ranks' existence itself
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo however dictatorial only covers humans; Zeno's one finger wipes an entire universe
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Myriad Ranks in front of Zeno do not even have qualification to "be discussed"
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Zeno treats "multiverse" as a chessboard to erase at will, not a Myriad-Ranks-co-built home
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

The Zeno setting is Dragon Ball worldview's most terrifying stroke — it tells readers the cosmos's apex is not justice, not law, not republic, but a child who thinks "playing games is enough." Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is simple: any individual, even strong enough to wipe a universe with one finger, must be bound by law; any governance, even by the ultimate being, must go through Myriad-Ranks parliament consent. Letting a child hold an eraser is not the cosmos's cuteness, it is the cosmos's tragedy.
