## Introduction: blonde hair, the start of inflation

In 1991, Goku turned into a blonde Super Saiyan on Namek — this scene became comics history's watershed. It pioneered the modern combat narrative of "transformation = leveling = visual spectacle," but also from then let Dragon Ball's numerical system walk a road of no return: SSJ1 → SSJ2 → SSJ3 → SSJ4 (GT) → Super Saiyan God → Super Saiyan Blue → Ultra Instinct → Selfishness, transformation layers endless. This entry takes the **law-first axiom** as core, dissecting Super Saiyan as "the iconic mechanism of combat-power inflation."

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The transformation mechanism itself is an energy-waste device:

- **Old forms devalued**: every new transformation layer appears, the old form becomes "base state" — Goku's SSJ1 in front of SSJ2 becomes waste; SSJ2 in front of SSJ3 becomes waste
- **Cultivation path single-ized**: all Saiyans compressed onto the single road of "transformation layers"; any cultivator not pursuing transformation (e.g., Tien Shinhan) loses meaning
- **Energy uneconomic**: SSJ3's energy consumption enormous; SSJ Blue's even bigger — the stronger the transformation, the higher the maintenance cost; the lower the energy efficiency

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How transformation mechanism feeds historical regression:

1. **Legend devalued to standard equipment**: Super Saiyan early a "millennium legend"; in Super Vegeta, Trunks, Gohan, Goten, Future Trunks all can transform — legend reduced to bloodline standard
2. **Cultivators lose direction**: since transformation is the only leveling path, why practice anything else? Earth cultivators seeing Saiyan transformations naturally abandon systematic training
3. **Civilization tilts toward Saiyans**: because transformation mechanism only serves Saiyans, Earth civilization's cultivation resources tilt toward Goku's family; others marginalized

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Goku's predatory thinking in the transformation mechanism:

- **Transformation narrative right monopolized**: though Vegeta can also transform, the symbolic meaning of "Super Saiyan" is entirely defined by Goku — he is the first to transform, the most famous, the strongest
- **Transformation layers plunder evolution right**: Goku alone takes SSJ1/2/3/God/Blue/Ultra Instinct most layers; Vegeta chased decades barely tied — evolution right monopolized by protagonist
- **Enemy = transformation fuel**: every new transformation layer needs an "enemy pushed to desperation"; Myriad Ranks continuously pay blood price for Goku's every transformation layer
