# Entry 444 | Dragon Ball · Dragon Balls Setting Critique: Causal Disruption of Arbitrary Wishing

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Ball Full Decomposition (Entry 13) | Benchmark base: Documents 19, 49, 03

## Introduction: when wishes have no threshold

Dragon Balls themselves are this IP's name, also its most dangerous setting: seven balls gathered, summon the dragon god, grant any wish — revive people, exterminate a race, make someone young again, make the dead die again, restore the universe. No threshold, no review, no price (early "one-year lifespan" later also broken). This entry takes the **causal self-discipline axiom** as core, dissecting how "arbitrary wishing" thoroughly disrupts Dragon Ball world's causality.

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The wishing mechanism itself is energy waste:

- **Revival = history zeroed**: every wish to revive the dead erases civilization's trauma memory; history does not advance
- **Wish = resource allocation**: whoever gathers the balls takes the biggest cosmos-tier resource — this is naked resource competition, unrelated to cultivation
- **Dragon god's public resource privatized**: the dragon god should have been Myriad Ranks' public miracle, yet privately used by whoever gathers the balls — this public resource repeatedly diverted to private wishes

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How the wishing mechanism feeds historical regression:

1. **Causality arbitrarily modified**: dead can revive, cities can restore, time can reverse — civilization's law of causality is hollowed out by Dragon Balls
2. **Effort loses meaning**: since can wish revival, why cherish life? Since can wish restore, why build cities?
3. **Wish = power**: whoever gathers Dragon Balls has power to rewrite reality — this power does not rely on cultivation, contribution, legislation, only on "gather seven balls"

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Goku's predatory thinking in the wishing mechanism:

- **Revival resource repeatedly used**: Goku's party repeatedly gathers Dragon Balls to "revive companions"; this "revival right" used by them as public resource — but Frieza, Cell, Buu also wished back, proving this mechanism is not controlled by Goku at all
- **Wish = shortcut to solve problems**: after beating Buu, after beating Zamasu, after beating Jiren, it always ends "wish restore everything" — Goku never truly solves problems, just erases problems by wish
- **No reverence for causality**: Goku never reflects "did reviving these people disrupt causality" — he treats the dragon god as a tool person, uses it when he wants

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: low**. Earth Dragon Balls (one wish, one-year lifespan) → Namekian Dragon Balls (three wishes) → Super Dragon Balls (cross-universe wishes) → Black Star Dragon Balls (planet explodes after wish); rules added with plot
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. Revival rules repeatedly changed between original, GT, Super; Cell, Frieza, Buu's revival conditions inconsistent
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high (gift-style-leaning-oligarchic)**. Goku's party is Dragon Balls' main user; Myriad Ranks gather Dragon Balls far less

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: wishing zeroes history, invalidates effort, erases trauma memory
- **Anti-predation**: public miracle privately diverted; revival right repeatedly used by Goku's party
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least raised cultivation by effort; Goku solves problems by wishing
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: among Myriad Ranks only those who gather Dragon Balls can use the dragon god; the vast majority of beings have no access
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: wishing mechanism lets law of causality thoroughly collapse; cannot expand to stable multiverse
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

The Dragon Balls wishing mechanism is Dragon Ball worldview's most charming and most dangerous design — it realized childhood wishes, also hollowed out civilization's law of causality. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is the causal self-discipline axiom: any reality-rewriting operation must be filed, approved, trail-marked; revival is not forbidden, but must go legal process, must retain trauma memory, must let Myriad-Ranks parliament know. Let the dragon god return to "Myriad Ranks' public miracle" position, not a wish machine for whoever gathers the balls — this is the true way out for Dragon Balls' wishing mechanism.
