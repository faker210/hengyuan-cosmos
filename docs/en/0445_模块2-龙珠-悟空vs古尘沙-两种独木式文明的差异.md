# Entry 445 | Dragon Ball · Goku vs. Gu Chensha: Two Forms of Single-Log Civilization

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Ball Full Decomposition (Entry 14 · Cross-Protagonist Comparison 1) | Benchmark base: Documents 17, 04, 20

## Introduction: two people who "carry the world alone"

Gu Chensha (*Dragon Talisman*) is web-literature's pinnacle of single-log civilization: the first in history to cultivate the Heavenly Dao method, casually creates universes, "bears the sin for all beings." Goku is comics' pinnacle of single-log civilization: from boyhood fighting all the way to Zeno's chessboard; Earth's survival hangs on him alone. Both are "one person carries the world," but carry in completely different ways — Gu Chensha "lives for all beings," Goku "fights himself." This entry, by the law-first axiom and anti-predation axiom, compares these two single-log civilizations' structural differences.

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- **Goku's waste**: Zeno-tier aptitude for personal combat; civilization zero institutional spillover
- **Gu Chensha's waste**: cosmos-creation-tier aptitude for "bearing all beings' sin"; outsourcing all beings' karmic debt to one person — all beings lose the chance to bear it themselves, grow themselves
- **Comparison**: Gu Chensha at least "on behalf of" all beings did something (legislation, bearing sin, cosmos creation); Goku does not even "on behalf of," he only "fights" — but the two's common lesion: **all beings do not need to stand up themselves**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

- **Goku's world**: strong one absent = collapse; civilization has no institutions
- **Gu Chensha's world**: Gu Chensha present = cosmos present; Gu Chensha gone = all beings unsupported — single-log civilization's common fate
- **Difference**: Goku world's regression is "no protection and die"; Gu Chensha world's regression is "no one bears sin and collapses" — the former is defense vacuum, the latter is moral vacuum

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Goku's predation**: innocent type — enemies as EXP packs; training resources monopolized
- **Gu Chensha's predation**: noble type — "bear sin for all beings" looks compassionate, actually plunders all beings' chance to grow, reflect, bear it themselves
- **Comparison**: Goku's predation hidden in combat instinct; Gu Chensha's predation hidden in compassion — the latter more concealed, because he truly means well for all beings

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: both's energy not truly institutionalized; Goku for fighting, Gu Chensha for bearing sin
- **Anti-predation**: Goku plunders Myriad Ranks' suffering as leveling fuel; Gu Chensha plunders Myriad Ranks' growth opportunity
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least benefited humans; Goku and Gu Chensha both treat all beings as "objects I need to carry"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: both worlds' Myriad Ranks are "carried" objects, not co-build subjects
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: single-log civilization cannot truly expand, because everything hangs on one person
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

Goku and Gu Chensha are single-log civilization's two faces: one carries the world with fists, one carries the world with compassion. Both are great, but both made the same mistake — they let all beings believe "someone carrying for us is enough." Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: let all beings stand up themselves, not because strong ones are not compassionate, but because standing-up Myriad Ranks walk farther than carried Myriad Ranks.

One more layer: Goku's single-log is "combat-type" — he leaves after fighting, does not legislate, does not build; Gu Chensha's single-log is "sin-bearing-type" — he not only fights, he also carries all beings' karmic debt on himself. On the surface Gu Chensha is more "responsible," actually more dangerous: he outsources all beings' moral growth too. Goku at least let all beings retain the possibility of "fighting themselves" (though Earthlings cannot); Gu Chensha does not even let all beings reflect — "he's borne sin for us, what more should we think?" This is why Heng Yuan Zhou rates Gu Chensha's protagonist uniqueness dependency "extremely high," while Goku is only "high." Two kinds of single-log, one gentler than the next, also one harder to break than the next.
