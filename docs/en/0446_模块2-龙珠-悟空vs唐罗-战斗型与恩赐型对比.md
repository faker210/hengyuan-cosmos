# Entry 446 | Dragon Ball · Goku vs. Tang Luo: Combat-Type vs. Gift-Type Comparison

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Ball Full Decomposition (Entry 15 · Cross-Protagonist Comparison 2) | Benchmark base: Documents 17, 04, 08

## Introduction: one born to fight, one born to teach

Tang Luo (*I Can See Combat Power*) is web-literature's top universal-benefit protagonist: he breaks aristocratic monopoly, rebuilds academies, popularizes martial arts. Goku is comics' purest combat-type protagonist: he does not build academies, does not legislate, does not popularize, only looks for the next stronger opponent. One is "gift-type," one is "combat-type," but the two's civilizational end is strikingly similar — both bet order on themselves. This entry, by the law-first axiom and republican axiom of Myriad Races, compares these two protagonists' structural differences.

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- **Goku's waste**: Zeno-tier aptitude for personal combat; zero institutional spillover
- **Tang Luo's waste**: civilization-reconstruction-tier aptitude for "patching the old framework"; only benefits humans, only reaches ferocious realm
- **Comparison**: Tang Luo at least built academies, popularized mid-low-tier humans; Goku built nothing. From civilization-spillover angle, Tang Luo is a magnitude above Goku — but Tang Luo's "universal benefit" is still gift, not republic

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

- **Goku's world**: strong one absent = collapse; civilization has no institutions; disasters relied on wish rollback
- **Tang Luo's world**: strong one absent = regression; academies and popularization tied to Tang Luo alone; ferocious realm is the locked ceiling
- **Common lesion**: neither designed "make institutions not depend on oneself"

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Goku's predation**: innocent type — enemies as EXP packs; training resources monopolized
- **Tang Luo's predation**: enlightened type — he truly means well, but high-level Great Dao privately hoarded; racial exclusion; gift dependency
- **Comparison**: Goku's predation hidden in "I just want to fight"; Tang Luo's predation hidden in "I'm already very benevolent" — the latter harder to identify, because he really helped many people

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Goku for fighting, Tang Luo for patching old framework; neither truly institutionalized
- **Anti-predation**: Goku plunders enemies as leveling fuel; Tang Luo plunders high-level Great Dao as private hoard
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Goku is more primitive than Tang Luo — Tang Luo at least built an academy; Goku did not even build one
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: neither world truly includes Myriad Ranks; Tang Luo only benefits humans, Goku only protects Earth
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: neither system truly moves toward open multiverse
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

Goku and Tang Luo are the protagonist lineage's two poles: one pure to not build, one benevolent to only patch. Both are more likable than "evil protagonists," but Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment never looks at likability, looks at structure. Tang Luo took one more step than Goku (building an academy), but this step is still gift, not republic. Turning "the taught" into "co-builders" is Tang Luo and Goku's common next step.

One more stroke: Tang Luo's "universal benefit" at least has an institutional carrier (Peerless Academy); Goku's "protection" does not even have a carrier — Earth's defense system is Goku himself. This means after Tang Luo is absent, the academy's teachers and students can still continue part; after Goku is absent, Earth's defense system directly zeroes. From "institutional sediment" angle, Tang Luo walked at least ten years more than Goku. But both's end is the same: neither truly shattered protagonist uniqueness. Tang Luo's academy built by him, supported by him; Goku's Earth fought by him, defended by him. Difference only in "what was built," not in "who is carrying."
