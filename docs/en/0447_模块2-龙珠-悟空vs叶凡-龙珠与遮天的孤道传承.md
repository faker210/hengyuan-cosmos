## Introduction: two "epoch-defining" lone paths

Ye Fan (*Cover the Sky*) is oligarchic civilization's representative: he fights his whole life, in old age builds "Heavenly Court," titles himself "Emperor Ye," leaves a "ten-thousand generations of peace under the Ye family" layout. Goku is gift-style-leaning-oligarchic representative: he does not build a Heavenly Court, but Earth's survival hangs on him alone. Both are "epoch-defining strong ones," both walk the lone path, but walk differently — Ye Fan leaves institutions (albeit the Ye family's), Goku leaves nothing. This entry, by the law-first axiom and republican axiom of Myriad Races, compares these two lone paths' lineage differences.

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- **Goku's waste**: Zeno-tier aptitude for personal combat; civilization zero institutional spillover
- **Ye Fan's waste**: Red-Dust Immortal-tier aptitude for "Heavenly Court," but the Heavenly Court is the Ye family's — civilization-tier energy converted to family-tier power
- **Comparison**: Ye Fan at least built the "Heavenly Court" nominal governance structure; Goku did not even have that; but the two's common lesion: **order both hangs on one family / one person**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

- **Goku's world**: strong one absent = collapse; civilization has no institutions
- **Ye Fan's world**: after Ye Fan leaves, can the "Heavenly Court" continue? Cover the Sky's late period with Emperors emerging one after another, dark turmoils constant, proves "ten-thousand generations of peace" was only a brief moment when Emperor Ye was alive
- **Common lesion**: neither designed "make institutions not depend on the strong one"

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Goku's predation**: innocent type — enemies as EXP packs

- **Comparison**: Goku's predation is unconscious; Ye Fan's predation is conscious — he knows he is fighting for the world, only the world he fought for ultimately became the "Ye" family's

## Conclusion

Goku and Ye Fan are lone-path lineage's two faces: one wins the world with fists, one fights for the world with the Emperor path. Both are great, but both treated the world as "mine." Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: the world is not any one person's, even if he is Goku, even if he is Emperor Ye. Let Myriad Ranks themselves covenant, themselves legislate, themselves pass down — this is lone path's true end.

One more layer of comparison detail: Ye Fan at least left the nominal governance structure of "Heavenly Court," though it is surnamed Ye; Goku did not even leave "Heavenly Court," Earth scatters after battles. From "institutional legacy" angle, Ye Fan walked one more step than Goku — he knew to set up a "thing" to maintain order, only this "thing" is his own family's. Goku did not even have the consciousness of "setting up a thing"; he defaults "I'm here and it's fine." Both's common blind spot: they never thought of "setting up something that can run without me." Heng Yuan Zhou's Anchor Council + separation of powers is precisely this "something that can run without anyone."

One more layer worth noting: Ye Fan's "ten-thousand generations of peace under Ye" at least admitted the proposition that "peace needs governance," though his answer was "my Ye family governs." Goku never even admitted the proposition of "governance" — he thought after fighting, peace comes. From political-consciousness angle, Ye Fan is a magnitude more mature than Goku, but both's end is the same: neither transferred governance right from personal hands to institutions. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants to do is change both "my Ye family governs" and "my Goku protects" into "Myriad Ranks jointly govern."
