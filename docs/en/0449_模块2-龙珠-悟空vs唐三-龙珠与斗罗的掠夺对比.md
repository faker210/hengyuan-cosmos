Tang San (*Douluo Continent*) is institutionalized predation's representative: he wrote "kill to get spirit rings" into every realm's legal promotion procedure, enfeoffed Divine Realm positions by family private grant. Goku is innocent-type predation's representative: he does not actively predate, but his growth is built on "near-death revival + enemies as EXP packs." Both predate, but Tang San's predation is "open rules," Goku's predation is "hidden rules." This entry, by the law-first axiom and anti-predation axiom, compares these two predations' structural differences.

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. ## Conclusion

Goku and Tang San are the predation lineage's two poles: one innocently stands on predatory foundation, one consciously writes predation into rules. Neither is "evil," but both's systems make Myriad Ranks pay blood price. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is the anti-predation axiom — develop increment, do not consume stock, do not take killing-predation as evolution path. Whether innocent type or institutional type, as long as still consuming stock, still one step from Myriad-Ranks symbiosis.

One more layer: Tang San's predation at least has a clear "bill" — kill one spirit beast for one level; readers can calculate the price. Goku's predation is instead more concealed: he does not actively kill spirit beasts, but every time he gets stronger he needs "a stronger villain appears," and behind every villain is a destroyed city, a slaughtered race. Tang San's predation is written in rules; Goku's predation is hidden in narrative — what readers see is "Goku got angry again"; what they do not see is "to make Goku angry, the writer killed a city's worth of people." From predation's concealment, Goku is actually harder to identify than Tang San.
