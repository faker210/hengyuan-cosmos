# Entry 450 | Dragon Ball · Goku vs. Ji Ning: Transmigrator Comparison of Dragon Ball and Manghuang

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Ball Full Decomposition (Entry 19 · Cross-Protagonist Comparison 6) | Benchmark base: Documents 17, 04, 14

## Introduction: two strong ones who "lived two lives"

Ji Ning (*Manghuang Ji*) is transmigrator-type lone-path cultivation's representative: born with past-life memories, from Yushan Yu Kingdom all the way to Chaos Cosmos Controller, walking the route "personal cultivation—family lineage—cosmos control." Goku is comics-style transmigrator's counter-example: though a Saiyan orphan, completely no past-life memory, fighting up by combat instinct. Both are "two lives," but Ji Ning's transmigration is conscious, Goku's "transmigration" is amnesiac. This entry, by the law-first axiom and republican axiom of Myriad Races, compares these two lone-path cultivations' structural differences.

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

- **Goku's waste**: Zeno-tier aptitude for personal combat; civilization zero institutional spillover
- **Ji Ning's waste**: Chaos-Cosmos-Controller-tier aptitude for "family lineage" — Ji Ning converts cosmos-tier power into the Ji clan's private property
- **Comparison**: Ji Ning at least has "family lineage" consciousness; Goku's family lineage is only incidental; but the two's common lesion: **Myriad Ranks are not co-build subjects**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

- **Goku's world**: strong one absent = collapse; disasters relied on wish rollback
- **Ji Ning's world**: after Ji Ning becomes Controller, does the Manghuang cosmos truly enter Myriad-Ranks republicanism? The Ji clan's privatization is essentially taking cosmos-tier public resources into the family
- **Common lesion**: neither designed "make institutions not depend on the strong one"

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

- **Goku's predation**: innocent type — enemies as EXP packs
- **Ji Ning's predation**: transmigrator type — he is born with past-life memories and knowledge advantage; this "information gap" itself is unfair predation on the native world
- **Comparison**: Goku's predation is unconscious combat instinct; Ji Ning's predation is conscious information advantage — he knows how to cultivate, knows where treasures are, knows plot direction

## Conclusion

Goku and Ji Ning are lone-path cultivation's two faces: one fights up amnesiac, one cultivates up with memory. Both reach the top, but both's reaching the top did not let Myriad Ranks come up together. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: whether amnesiac or transmigrator, cultivation knowledge must be public; whether fought up or cultivated up, Myriad Ranks must walk together. Turning "I reach the top" into "Myriad-Ranks republicanism" is lone-path cultivation's true end.

One more layer: Ji Ning's transmigrator identity brought him two "unfair advantages" — past-life memory and foreknowledge of plot. This means every breakthrough, every treasure seizure in the Manghuang world is built on the premise "he knows the answer." This itself is unfair predation on the native world's original inhabitants. Though Goku has no past-life memory, his Saiyan bloodline gave him another "unfair advantage" — near-death-revival gain. Both's paths to the top differ, but essentially both are "born with an extra card." Heng Yuan Zhou asks not "should this card exist," but "does the person with this card make the card's face public to Myriad Ranks." Ji Ning did not; Goku did not either.
