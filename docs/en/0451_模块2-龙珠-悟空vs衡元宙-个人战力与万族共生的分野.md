## Introduction: when "protecting the world" no longer needs a superman

Goku vs. Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Universe) is this series's ultimate contrast: Goku represents "personal combat power carries the world" ultimate form; Heng Yuan Zhou represents "Myriad Ranks symbiotically co-build the world" network civilization. The two are not good-evil distinction, but structural distinction — in Goku's world, Earth protected by one Saiyan; in Heng Yuan Zhou's world, civilization sustained by Anchor Council, Symbiotic Court, Alaya Network, Myriad-Ranks republican co-build. This entry takes the **law-first axiom** as core, making this divide clear.

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## Conclusion

Goku and Heng Yuan Zhou are not a "who is stronger" comparison, but a "how should civilization be built" comparison. Goku proved one thing in forty years: one person can be strong enough to sit at Zeno's table, but he cannot make civilization no longer need him. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: let Myriad Ranks stand up themselves, not because Goku is not strong enough, but because standing-up Myriad Ranks walk farther than protected Myriad Ranks. This is personal combat power and Myriad-Ranks symbiosis's ultimate divide.

One more layer: in Goku's world, "protection" and "taming" are separated by only a thin line — if Myriad Ranks never need to stand up themselves, what distinguishes them from penned livestock? Heng Yuan Zhou does not forbid strong ones from protecting Myriad Ranks, but requires strong ones while protecting to "teach Myriad Ranks to protect themselves." Goku fought for forty years; Earthlings still cannot cultivate themselves, cannot early-warn themselves, cannot legislate themselves — this is "protection" turned into "taming." Heng Yuan Zhou's Anchor Symbiosis requires strong ones to be "scaffolding," not "cages": help Myriad Ranks stand up, then withdraw oneself.
