## Introduction: return "ki" to Myriad Ranks

The previous twenty entries dissected Dragon Ball system's lesions: energy waste, historical regression, predatory thinking, combat-power inflation, multi-line retcon, dictatorial pyramid. This entry does not scold again, but gives a **constructive alternative** — if let Heng Yuan Zhou's Anchor Symbiotic Civilization take over Dragon Ball world, how to transform this "personal-combat-power-oriented" system into a "civilizational" system. This entry takes the **law-first axiom** as core, giving a deployable transformation roadmap.

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones (Lesion Review)

Dragon Ball system's energy waste core is "ki" privatization:

- Ki theory only transmitted privately between Turtle School and Crane School masters and disciples
- Saiyan near-death-revival gain is a biological miracle, yet only serves the Saiyan race
- Ultra-Instinct-tier theory monopolized by the Angel clan
- Goku's every breakthrough does not enter the public knowledge base

Transformation direction: **change "ki" from master-apprentice private transmission to Myriad-Ranks public infrastructure.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression (Lesion Review)

Dragon Ball world's regression core is "no institutions":

- Disasters relied on wish rollback; civilization has no trauma memory
- Commoners have no armed ability; civilization has no self-organized defense
- God-tier stratum does not legislate; cosmos governance vacuum

Transformation direction: **change "Goku wins" to "Anchor Council legislates."**

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking (Lesion Review)

Goku's predatory thinking core is "combat instinct > construction":

- Enemies as leveling fuel
- Training resources monopolized
- Befriending dictators as equals
