## Introduction: install a brake on numerical inflation

Dragon Ball's combat-power inflation is essentially "no brake" — numbers from hundreds to ∞, transformation layers from 1 to 8+, revival rules from "one wish a year" to "revive whenever." Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: any energy change, any causal operation, any realm breakthrough, must all trail-mark in the Alaya Network, be anchor-calibrated, pass Symbiotic Court approval. This entry takes the **law-first axiom** as core, giving the institutional design to "stamp out combat-power inflation."

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones (Harm of No Supervision)

Consequences of Dragon Ball world without Alaya supervision:

- **Energy changes not trail-marked**: every Goku breakthrough, no one records "why broke through, what was the breakthrough cost"
- **Causal operations unapproved**: Dragon Ball wishing revives people, restores cities; no one approves "does this revival disrupt causality"
- **Realm breakthroughs uncalibrated**: SSJ1/2/3/God/Blue/Ultra Instinct, every breakthrough no anchor calibration; readers cannot judge "how strong is it now"
- **Conclusion**: energy growth without supervision = numerical inflation + causal collapse + setting self-consistency zeroed

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression (Harm of No Supervision)

How no Alaya supervision feeds historical regression:

1. **Knowledge not accumulated**: because energy changes not trail-marked, early "cultivation experience" may be invalidated late
2. **Causality arbitrarily modified**: revival, restoration, wishing, no trail-mark; civilization has no true history
3. **Strong ones unaccountable**: God of Destruction smashes planets, Zeno wipes universes; no supervision, no accountability

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking (Harm of No Supervision)

Goku's predatory thinking under no-supervision system:

- **Breakthrough unapproved**: every Goku temporary transformation, no one approves "does this breakthrough conform to law"

- **Training resources unaudited**: Whis sparring with Goku; no one audits "is this cosmos-tier mentor resource fairly distributed"
