# Entry 457 | Dragon Ball · Myriad-Ranks Republican Plan: Breaking Human/Saiyan-Centrism

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Ball Full Decomposition (Entry 26 · Alternative 6) | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 50, 52

## Introduction: when the "strongest race" becomes original sin

Dragon Ball world's racial order is: Saiyans (strongest combat race) > Earthlings/Namekians (ordinary intelligent races) > Frieza-army subordinate races (conquered) > others (backdrop). The Saiyan race itself was Frieza's army's conquest tool; though Goku lost his memory, he inherited this racial original sin. This entry takes the republican axiom of Myriad Races as core, giving the republican plan to "break human/Saiyan-centrism."

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Saiyan-centrism's energy waste:

- **Saiyan talent monopolized**: near-death-revival gain, Super Saiyan transformation route, all only serve Saiyans
- **Myriad-Ranks talent undeveloped**: Namekians' Dragon-Ball creation ability, Yardrats' Instant Transmission technology, Frieza-army subordinate races' various talents, all narratively marginalized
- **Racial stratification**: Frieza-army subordinate races defined as "conquered"; their civilizational value zeroed

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How Saiyan-centrism feeds historical regression:

1. **Myriad Ranks have no upward channel**: Saiyans can transform by bloodline alone; Myriad Ranks however hardworking cannot catch up — civilization tilts toward the Saiyan race
2. **Conquered have no voice**: Frieza-army subordinate races in narrative are only backdrop; their suffering unrecorded
3. **Racial conflict loop**: Saiyans were once Frieza's tool; though Goku defected, the Saiyan race's predatory history was never purged

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Goku's predatory thinking under Saiyan-centrism:

- **Accepting bloodline superiority**: Goku never questions "why Saiyans are born able to transform into Super Saiyan"; he defaults it as natural
- **Myriad-Ranks suffering forgotten**: Planet Vegeta destroyed by Frieza, but Goku never leads Namekians, Yardrats and other races invaded by Saiyans to seek justice
- **Saiyan = hero narrative**: the whole Dragon Ball world's hero narrative revolves around Saiyans; Myriad Ranks have no position in narrative
