# Entry 459 | Dragon Ball · Earth Setting Analysis: The Extreme Absence of Civilizational Construction

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Ball Full Decomposition (Entry 28 · Civilization Analysis 1) | Benchmark base: Documents 03, 36, 37

## Introduction: a planet living off the protagonist's protective shield

Earth in Dragon Ball is the most familiar yet most alien planet: readers have followed Goku on it for forty years, yet know nothing of its government, law, education, medical care. It has Capsule Corp, Bulma's family, the World Tournament, but it has no parliament, no court, no civilizational institutional construction. It is a planet "living off the protagonist's protective shield." This entry takes the republican axiom of Myriad Races as core, analyzing Dragon Ball Earth's civilizational construction absence.

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Earth's energy waste core is "technology and combat power disconnected":

- **Technology single-pointed**: Capsule Corp (Bulma's family) is Earth's only technology engine; Bulma stays home and Earth technology does not advance
- **Combat power single-pointed**: Earth's strongest combat power = Goku + Vegeta; ordinary cultivators' highest = Gohan/Piccolo level
- **Disconnect**: Earth's technology (capsule spaceship, time machine) and combat power (ki cultivation) completely disconnected; technology not converted into a civilizational defense system

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How Earth's civilizational construction absence feeds regression:

1. **No government**: Earth has no election, legislation, administration system; scatters after the World Tournament
2. **No education**: Earth has no cultivation academy; Turtle School, Crane School are master-apprentice systems; knowledge not public
3. **No medical care**: Earth has no public medical system; healing relies on Senzu beans (extremely low yield) or Bulma's healing devices
4. **No defense**: Earth has no interstellar early warning, no defense system; Saiyans come and they hide; Cell comes and they watch
5. **Conclusion**: Earth is not a civilization, it is "Goku's training ground"

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Goku's role in Earth's civilizational absence:

- **Accepting Earth has no institutions**: Goku never pushes Earth to establish any institution; he defaults "after fighting it's fine"
- **Treating Earth as training ground**: Goku returns to Earth to cultivate and fight, not to build
- **Senzu bean monopoly**: Senzu beans held by Korin/Yajirobe, extremely low yield; Earthlings injured have no public medical care

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Earth-setting self-consistency: low** (Earth's technology level over forty years fluctuates high and low; Bulma builds whatever she wants)
- **Civilizational construction self-consistency expected: high** (after Anchor-Symbiosis injection, Earth has stable government, education, medical, defense)
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium** (Earth setting changes with plot need; GT's future Earth and Super's future Earth contradictory)
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high** — Earth's survival hangs on Goku; no institutional backup

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: technology and combat power disconnected; energy not converted into civilizational infrastructure
- **Anti-predation**: Senzu bean monopoly, knowledge master-apprentice private transmission, Bulma's family monopolizing technology
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least built Peerless Academy; Dragon Ball Earth does not even have an academy
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Earth commoners under institutional absence have no self-organization ability at all
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Earth cannot independently survive under Goku absence, let alone move toward interstellar republicanism
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

Dragon Ball Earth is a specimen of "protagonist-protective-shield civilization": all its operation relies on Goku present; Goku absent and it collapses. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is not to make Goku stronger, but to make Earth no longer need Goku alone. Let Earth have its own parliament, own academy, own medical care, own defense — let Earth from "Goku's training ground" become "Myriad Ranks' home"; this is Dragon Ball Earth's true way out.

One more layer: Dragon Ball Earth's most absurd detail is — it experienced Saiyan invasion, Namek arc, Cell Game, Buu crisis, Beerus arrival, Tournament of Power; each time tens of millions died, but after battles cities build as usual, schools open as usual, economy runs as usual, as if nothing happened. This is not "strong resilience," this is "no true trauma." A civilization that truly experienced apocalyptic disaster leaves institutional reflection — establish early warning, reform education, improve medical care. Dragon Ball Earth left nothing, because the dead are revived, cities restored, reflection unnecessary. This is causal-self-discipline absence's fatal corrosion on civilizational construction.
