# Entry 460 | Dragon Ball · Saiyan Setting: The Predatory Essence of a Warrior Race

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Ball Full Decomposition (Entry 29 · Civilization Analysis 2) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 19, 57

## Introduction: a race whose trade is conquest

The Saiyans in Dragon Ball worldview are defined as a "warrior race": they are born with combat power, they love fighting, they are hired by Frieza's army to conquer planets, they get stronger by near-death revival. This setting writes "predation" into a race's biological instinct. Though Goku lost his memory and defected, he inherited this racial original sin. This entry takes the **anti-predation axiom** as core, dissecting the Saiyan setting's predatory essence.

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Saiyan setting's energy waste:

- **Combat talent wasted on conquest**: Saiyans' innate combat talent hired by Frieza's army for conquering planets and selling natives — this talent could have been used for Myriad-Ranks co-build
- **Near-death gain wasted on killing**: the biologically miraculous "near-death stronger" mechanism, used for the next conquest
- **Planet Vegeta civilization wasted**: the Saiyan race could have developed a unique cultivation civilization, but chose to be mercenaries

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How Saiyan predatory essence feeds historical regression:

1. **Planet Vegeta destroyed by Frieza**: Saiyans were mercenaries for centuries, ultimately silenced by their employer — the predator's fate
2. **Survivors are Goku and Vegeta**: two Saiyan survivors, one amnesiac became Earthling, one became villain then reformed — racial civilization completely broken
3. **Combat instinct unconverted**: Saiyans' instinct to love battle was never converted to "guard Myriad Ranks" duty; always stayed at "find a stronger opponent"

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Goku's predatory thinking under Saiyan setting:

- **Inheriting racial original sin**: Goku's combat talent, near-death gain, Super Saiyan transformation route, all inherited from a Saiyan race that lived by predation
- **Not reflecting**: Goku never reflects "is my talent from a predatory race"; he defaults "I'm a good person and that's enough"
- **Treating battle as purpose**: Saiyans' instinct to love battle manifests in Goku as "just want to fight the strong" — this instinct never redirected to "guard Myriad Ranks"

- **Saiyan setting self-consistency: low** (near-death gain rare early; late repeatedly used by Vegeta, Gohan, Trunks)
- **Racial setting self-consistency expected: high** (five genealogies equal; Saiyans as one branch have complete civilization lineage)

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: combat talent for conquest, not converted to Myriad-Ranks co-build
- **Anti-predation**: Saiyans were mercenaries for centuries; predatory history unpurged
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least proactively broke predatory structure; Saiyans are predatory structure itself
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Saiyans as one of Myriad Ranks should be equally included in republicanism, not race-centric
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Saiyans' combat instinct should be redirected to multi-path evolution, not single-dimension killing
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

The Saiyan setting is Dragon Ball worldview's deepest and most dangerous setting — it writes "predation" into a race's biological instinct. Goku is not a bad person, but he lives in a "battle = predation" system without knowing. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: Saiyans' combat talent can be retained, but must be redirected to guarding Myriad Ranks, not conquering planets; near-death gain can be retained, but must be made safe and technological, not betting lives. Let the warrior race from predators become guardians; this is the Saiyan setting's true redemption.
