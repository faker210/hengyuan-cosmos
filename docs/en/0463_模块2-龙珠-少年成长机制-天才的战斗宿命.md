## Introduction: when a scholar's dream is interrupted again and again

Gohan is Dragon Ball's most regrettable youth genius: at 4 years old could output 2800 combat power, at 7 fought Cell, was a scholar seedling, yet was pulled back to the battlefield by one great war after another. He represents Dragon Ball world's "youth growth mechanism" — genius youths are not cultivated into builders, but pushed onto the battlefield as consumables. This entry takes the republican axiom of Myriad Races as core, dissecting Dragon Ball's youth growth mechanism's combat fate.

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Gohan's energy waste:

- **Scholar dream interrupted**: Gohan wanted to be a scholar, yet was pulled to fight Cell, fight Buu, fight Beerus — his scholar talent completely wasted
- **Potential not fully developed**: Gohan's potential (unleashed by Old Kai) is Dragon Ball's most exaggerated, but this potential only used for "occasionally saving the day"; ordinarily he does not cultivate
- **Trunks's repetition**: Trunks crosses from the future to fight androids, after fighting goes back — his growth is also "fighting for fighting's sake"
- **Conclusion**: Dragon Ball's youth geniuses, all redirected to combat; none becomes a builder

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How youth growth mechanism feeds historical regression:

1. **Geniuses consumed on the battlefield**: the smartest, most promising youths, not cultivated into scientists/scholars/builders, but pushed onto the battlefield
2. **Next generation no lineage**: Gohan, Trunks, Goten, these next-generation geniuses have no systematic cultivation lineage; all rely on "fight once and temporarily comprehend"
3. **Education system absent**: Earth has no cultivation academy; youth geniuses all "pushed out by villains"

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Goku's role in youth growth mechanism:

- **Contradiction toward Gohan**: Goku both hopes Gohan studies, yet pulls him onto the battlefield again and again — he himself does not realize this contradiction
- **Not cultivating the next generation**: Goku never systematically cultivates any next-generation youth genius; Gohan, Goten, Trunks all "only appear when plot needs"
- **Treating youths as consumables**: letting Gohan fight Cell in the Cell Game is essentially treating a youth as combat consumable

## Conclusion

Gohan is Dragon Ball world's most regrettable youth genius — he could have been a scholar, yet was pulled onto the battlefield again and again. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: Youth Supremes can take "fast-track + quest-seeking" dual route; not every genius must be a combat consumable. Let Gohan study, let Trunks build, let Goten systematically cultivate — Dragon Ball world's next generation then truly has a future.

After Old Kai fully unleashed his potential, his strength could have been used to drive Earth civilization's leap — e.g., textbook-ify "ki" theory, democratize cultivation methods. But Dragon Ball plot only lets him "occasionally save the day"; ordinarily he is a scholar, in war he is a warrior, never combining the two. Heng Yuan Zhou's Youth Supreme dual route precisely solves this problem: youth geniuses can take fast-track combat route, can take quest-sediment route, can walk on two legs — not everyone must choose between "scholar" and "warrior."
