# Entry 464 | Dragon Ball · Commoners' Situation: Ordinary People's Helplessness in a Combat-Power World

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Ball Full Decomposition (Entry 33 · Civilization Analysis 6) | Benchmark base: Documents 08, 36, 41

## Introduction: when "protecting ordinary people" is only a line

Dragon Ball has a recurring line: "I fight to protect Earth's ordinary people." But Earth's ordinary people over forty years of plot were never truly protected — Saiyans come and they hide in houses; Cell comes and they watch the livestream; Buu comes and they are one-shot killed. They have no cultivation ability, no weapons, no early warning; they are only backdrop when villains destroy. This entry takes the republican axiom of Myriad Races as core, dissecting Dragon Ball commoners' helpless situation in a combat-power world.

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Commoners' energy waste:

- **Ordinary people no cultivation qualification**: Dragon Ball world's "ki" cultivation only transmitted privately between Turtle School and Crane School masters and disciples; ordinary people cannot even touch the basics
- **Technology not converted to defense**: Capsule Corp's technology only used to "build spaceships for Goku"; never builds defense weapons for commoners
- **Early-warning system zero**: Frieza comes, Cell comes, Buu comes; commoners every time "suddenly hear"; no early warning
- **Conclusion**: Dragon Ball world's ordinary people, at the combat-power level completely powerless

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How commoners' helplessness feeds historical regression:

1. **Civilization no self-organization ability**: commoners cannot cultivate, cannot early-warn, cannot self-rescue; disaster comes and they can only wait for Goku
2. **Casualty numbers meaningless**: Dragon Ball world every great war tens of millions die, but because can be revived, these casualties narratively zeroed
3. **Commoners no voice**: Earth government in forty years of plot never had a presence; commoners' demands no one represents

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Goku's role in commoners' helplessness:

- **Accepting commoners' helplessness**: Goku never pushes commoners to cultivate, never builds early-warning system; he defaults "I protect you and it's fine"
- **Treating commoners as chips**: Tournament of Power's wager is 8 universes' survival; Goku treats Myriad Ranks' fate as a chip for "wanting to trade blows with the strong"
- **No reflection after revival**: every great war relies on Dragon Balls to revive; Goku never reflects "why do commoners always die"

## Conclusion

Dragon Ball's "protect ordinary people" is only a line — ordinary people were never truly protected, they were only revived. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: let ordinary people themselves have roads. Basic methods universal, interstellar early-warning system, commoner representatives participate in parliament — let ordinary people from "protected backdrop" become "co-build subjects of civilization"; this is Dragon Ball commoners' true way out.

One more layer: Dragon Ball world's commoners have an extremely cruel detail — they do not even know what "ki" is. Turtle School's cultivation is master-apprentice secret transmission; the vast majority of Earth's ordinary people their whole life do not know "humans can fly." This means Dragon Ball world's commoners are not only "cannot beat villains"; they do not even know they can cultivate. Heng Yuan Zhou's road-for-everyone guarantee system (Doc 08) precisely breaks this "knowledge black box" — basic methods open to everyone, not because everyone can become Goku, but because everyone has the right to know "humans can fly." The right to know itself is Myriad-Ranks republicanism's bottom line.
