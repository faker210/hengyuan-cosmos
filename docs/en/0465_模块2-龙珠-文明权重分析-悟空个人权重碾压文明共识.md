## Introduction: when one person's will equals the whole civilization's will

Heng Yuan Zhou uses "civilizational weight" to measure an individual's decision-making share in civilization: in a mature civilization, any individual's weight should not exceed 5%, because civilizational consensus should be co-built by Myriad Ranks. Dragon Ball world's civilizational weight distribution is extreme: Goku alone's weight occupies over 90% of Earth civilization's decisions — whether to fight Frieza, whether to join Tournament of Power, whether to befriend Zeno, all decided by him alone. This entry takes the **law-first axiom** as core, analyzing Dragon Ball world's civilizational-weight imbalance.

This entry's judging base is the six top-level axioms and five mandatory cores, with the law-first axiom as core. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Civilizational-weight imbalance's energy waste:

- **Decision cost concentrated**: Goku alone decides "whether to fight Frieza," "whether to join Tournament of Power"; Myriad Ranks do not participate in decision-making
- **Decision risk concentrated**: Tournament of Power's wager is 8 universes' survival; Goku alone decides; Myriad Ranks bear consequences
- **Decision unaccountable**: Goku decides wrong (e.g., Tournament of Power almost destroys the universe), no one holds him accountable, no one vetoes

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

How civilizational-weight imbalance feeds historical regression:
