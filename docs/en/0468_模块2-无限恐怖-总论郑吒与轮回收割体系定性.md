# Entry 468 | Infinite Terror Series Overview: Zheng Zha and the Reincarnation-Harvest System Qualitative Assessment

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Infinite Terror Full Decomposition (Entry 1 · Overview) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 05, 17

## Introduction: when reincarnation becomes a slaughterhouse assembly line

*Infinite Terror* founded the "infinite-flow" web-novel paradigm: a group of ordinary people pulled into the "Main God Space," thrown into one movie-instance world after another, fighting life-and-death to trade for Genetic Locks and attribute points, growing stronger just to stay alive. As China Team captain, Zheng Zha grew from a homebody all the way to a strong one who could converse with "Saints"; he values brotherhood, dares take responsibility, can win the crowd — one of infinite-flow history's most charismatic protagonists. But Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe) does not give moral commendation for Zheng Zha's charisma, but dissects the "Main God Space" he stands on — it is essentially a harvest factory whose assembly line is reincarnation.

This entry as the Infinite Terror dissection series' general outline establishes the full judging base — the six top-level axioms: anti-predation axiom (develop increment, do not consume stock), republican axiom of Myriad Races (all beings equal evolution qualification), collective ascension axiom (strong ones feed back Myriad Ranks; no great material used small), causal self-discipline axiom (observe first then limited interference), **law-first axiom (law first, never retcon, setting self-consistency above plot convenience)**, universal equal infinite law — with law-first axiom as core, because the Main God Space is precisely the typical case of "manipulated by a higher being, rules changed with the manipulator's will"; Zheng Zha from start to finish does not know whose board he is playing on. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Protagonist uniqueness dependency judgment: **Zheng Zha = high-dependency / gift-style-leaning-oligarchic civilization** (China Team's survival tied to Zheng Zha alone; Main God rules tied to two unknown top layers: "Saints" and the "Main God").

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Zheng Zha's energy level is not low: five-stage Genetic Lock, replica battles, conversations with "Saints," final battle spanning countless instance worlds. But this energy level's direction has extreme waste:

- **Survival-oriented single-point consumption**: every time Zheng Zha breaks a Genetic Lock, its only use is "survive the next instance." He never converts this understanding of life-death rules into a mechanism that lets newcomers grow safely
- **Team resources personally centralized**: rarest side plots, best exchanges, strongest skills, all prioritized into Zheng Zha. China Team trained into a structure of "if captain strong then whole team strong; if captain falls then whole team dies"
- **Instance-world zero construction**: through *Alien*, *Ju-on*, *The Lord of the Rings* countless worlds; Zheng Zha never left knowledge, institutions, or aid in any instance world — those worlds to him are only "maps to grind monsters and points"

Conclusion: holding cross-reincarnation-tier energy level, yet only using it to "live longer in the slaughterhouse than others" — this is using a cosmos-tier proving ground as a personal survival arena.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

The Main God Space's regression is not a civilization-evolution problem; it is that from design it does not allow any civilization sediment:

1. **Reincarnation = zeroing**: every movie instance ends, team extracted, memory cut by rules, world line reset. China Team fought over a decade; not one instance world permanently improved because of them
2. **Newcomers fill veterans**: every old member dies, a new person pulled from reality fills the pit. Team always in the loop of "re-onboard — partial growth — battle loss — fill new"; civilization-level experience cannot accumulate
3. **Top-layer black box**: who the Main God is, why it built this space, where the Genetic Lock leads, all black boxes. No matter how strong Zheng Zha gets, he is only the mouse that runs fastest in rules written by others

Betting evolution on "can I live longer than last round"; every round is last round's zero-sum repetition — this is reincarnation harvest's institutional regression.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Zheng Zha is a man of loyalty; his predation hides in structure, not character:

- **Instance NPCs as resources**: in movie worlds, aliens, zombies, the Main God, the Ring, at the rule level all are EXP packs whose "kill gives points." Zheng Zha has loyalty to China Team, but to instance worlds' billion-billion beings has never had "symbiosis" consciousness
- **Teammates as combat parts**: Zhao Kongkong, Luo Gandao, Zero Point... every member's strongest exchange direction is allocated by "how to support Zheng Zha." Teammates' personal growth yields to team combat efficiency
- **Genetic Lock as predatory awakening**: the Genetic Lock's essence is "pressing one's own life potential"; opening more stages makes stronger but closer to collapse — this is burning one's own origin as fuel

Zheng Zha is not a bad person; he is the "excellent product" disciplined by the harvest system: the stronger he gets, the better this slaughterhouse runs.

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Main God Space Reincarnation Harvest | Anchor-Symbiosis Alternative |
|---------------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Life-death fight trades points | 300 Origin cultivation systems open; safe deduction, no life bets |
| Instance worlds as maps | Causal self-discipline axiom: cross-plane observe first then limited interference; only return knowledge without disturbing causality |
| Newcomers fill veterans loop | Road-for-everyone guarantee system; newcomers have complete growth period, not death-march period |
| Main God black-box manipulation | Law-first axiom: laws public, legislation first; no hidden manipulator |

Alternative's crux: turn "being the longest survivor in the slaughterhouse" into "Myriad Ranks collectively evolving under public laws."

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Genetic Lock stage-1 to stage-5 upgrade logic, instance difficulty curve broadly self-consistent early; but after "Saints," "Main God," "Box Theory" layer upon layer, upper-layer settings frequently overturn lower-layer explanations — the Main God starts as unknowable god, late becomes "we built it back then," then later becomes program in a higher box; setting keeps yielding to new suspense
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. Infinite-flow opened sequels, fan works, crossover works numbering in hundreds; *Infinite Terror* original work mid-late period combat-power inflation, new characters emerge endlessly, old supporting characters forgotten, old instance settings simplified; typical "plot kidnaps setting"
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high (gift-style-leaning-oligarchic)**. China Team's survival tied to Zheng Zha; Main God Space rules tied to unknowable top layer; once Zheng Zha falls, China Team disperses

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: cross-reincarnation-tier energy all poured into personal survival; instance worlds zero construction
- **Anti-predation**: NPCs as EXP packs, teammates as combat parts, Genetic Lock as origin burning
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least built an academy; Zheng Zha did not even build a "newcomer safety guide"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: instance worlds' billion-billion beings in reincarnation rules are only backdrop
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: under Main God black box, the evolution road for all participants is a dark box
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

Zheng Zha is a good person. In a factory whose rule is killing, he chose to protect teammates, remember the dead, refuse to become a monster. But Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment never asks whether the protagonist is hot-blooded enough, only whether the structure can sustain: when "staying alive" itself becomes the highest purpose, when "getting strong" is only for the next kill, when the entire cosmos is designed as a slaughter assembly line that never stops — then even the mouse that runs fastest is still a mouse. Infinite-flow's true way out is not running out of the Main God Space, but abolishing this space from the root.
