# Entry 469 | Infinite Terror · Energy-Waste Analysis: Genetic-Lock Aptitude Used for Personal Survival

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Infinite Terror Decomposition (Entry 2) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 15, 17

## Introduction: five-stage Genetic Lock, only used to "survive the next instance"

The Genetic Lock is *Infinite Terror*'s core power system: the human brain and body have layers of seals; breaking through in life-death extremities, each opened stage raises reaction, computation, life potential by an order of magnitude. One who reaches stage five can converse with "Saints." This system's ceiling is not low. But Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe) taking the six top-level axioms as ruler — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — will find a glaring fact: such a high energy level ultimately was used on one tiny thing: letting Zheng Zha alone survive the next movie. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

**1. Time-dimensional waste: every awakening only serves the next battle.** Stage-3+ Genetic Lock awakened ones already have "micro-particle-level manipulation" and "self-awareness externalization." If used for civilizational engineering, this ability could rebuild a planet's energy system; but every one of Zheng Zha's awakenings happens in the next life-death fight of *Transformers*, *The Lord of the Rings*, *Final Destination*; all energy of awakening pours out within dozens of minutes, leaving no civilizational sediment.

**2. Knowledge-dimensional waste: experience does not sediment.** China Team fought over a hundred instances; their understanding of alien ecology, ghost rules, magic systems, sci-fi technology is arguably the Myriad-Realms' broadest "cross-world encyclopedia." But all this knowledge exists only in team members' personal heads; after the instance ends, after battle losses, after memory splits, no *Cross-World Survival Manual* was systematically compiled for later people. Newcomers still have to die from scratch.

**3. Teammate-dimensional waste: the strongest trained into "Zheng Zha's accessory."** Zhao Kongkong's mental power, Luo Gandao's mech, Zero Point's sniping — every person's strongest direction was exchanged by "filling Zheng Zha's gaps." China Team never cultivates a second captain who can stand alone — because Main God Space rules do not need it, and Zheng Zha's own security sense does not need it.

**4. Instance-world-dimensional waste: billion-billion beings are only maps.** Zheng Zha enters *Lord of the Rings* world; he knows Frodo is destroying the Ring, knows Gondor is dying, but all his actions only for grinding side plots and exchanging points. He never brings real-world medicine, electricity, peace agreements back to any instance — at the rule level he even cannot do so.

Conclusion: five-stage Genetic Lock's energy level was compressed into "personal survival skill." This is the Myriad-Realms lineage's most typical "cosmos-tier aptitude, ant-tier use."

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

The Main God Space's "energy waste" is not accidental; it was designed. If Zheng Zha truly sedimented cross-world knowledge, truly cultivated a second and third captain, newcomer survival rate would skyrocket, and the Main God's "harvest efficiency" would drop. Reincarnation's essence requires participants always at the critical state of "barely surviving" — **wasting energy level is this system's function, not its defect.**

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Zheng Zha himself does not think he is predating. But structurally: he enjoyed the team's most concentrated resource exchange, best teammate coordination, most "protagonist halo," yet never fed back this concentrated resource as public good. He has affection for teammates, no sense for institutions — this is precisely the typical "benevolent predator": he makes those around him live slightly better, yet never thinks about making everyone not have to be pulled into this space.

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Main God Space Energy Use | Anchor-Symbiosis Alternative |
|-----------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Awakening only for next battle | Awakening feeds back civilizational engineering; energy level = infrastructure |
| Experience stored in personal brains | Alaya Network full recording; everyone can query |

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

After Quest Sediment Domain models the Main God Space, inject variable "Zheng Zha after every instance forcibly compiles a *Cross-World Survival Manual* and uploads it to the team public library." Simulation shows: after thirty instances, newcomers' first-round survival rate from 12% rises to 67%; team average energy distribution from "Zheng Zha 90%, others 10%" becomes "Zheng Zha 40%, others 60%" — **once energy waste is institutionally recycled, the same slaughterhouse can become a training camp.**

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Genetic Lock's own upgrade curve self-consistent, but upper-layer explanations for "why the human brain happens to have five locks," "why the Main God Space happens to select these people" are late covered by new settings
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium-high**. Original mid-late period new instances, new characters, new explanations emerge endlessly; early foreshadowed "box truth" rewritten multiple times
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high**. China Team's energy 90% concentrated on Zheng Zha; any split-force battle means that route is wiped out

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: five-stage Genetic Lock all poured into personal survival
- **Anti-predation**: cross-world knowledge privatized and zeroed by battle losses
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least publicized martial-arts knowledge; Zheng Zha did not even write a survival manual
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: instance worlds' billion-billion beings excluded from knowledge sharing
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: energy level cannot spill over to any external civilization
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

The Genetic Lock is a mirror. What it reflects is not how great human potential is, but: when a great potential is caged with "only to be used for surviving," it shrinks to the cage's shape. What Heng Yuan Zhou needs to do is tear down the cage — not make Zheng Zha live longer, but make everyone not have to enter this cage.
