# Entry 470 | Infinite Terror · Historical Regression Inevitability: The Reincarnation-Harvest Essence of the Main God Space

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Infinite Terror Decomposition (Entry 3) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 05, 19

## Introduction: reincarnation is not cultivation, it is slaughter

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — on the Main God Space specimen, reflect out a purest regression form: it does not even pretend to build civilization. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. It openly says it is a slaughterhouse; participants openly say they are betting their lives; all "growth" is only to make the next slaughter more watchable.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The Main God Space's energy waste is institutional. Every squad, every member, from the moment pulled into the space, their time, life, emotion all counted into the "instance — settlement — replacement" assembly line. China Team fought over a hundred movies; never left a permanent medical station, agriculture station, peace agreement in any instance world — **energy level not spilling over is written into Main God rules.** The stronger participants get, the more they must be thrown into the next more dangerous instance; energy level must immediately be consumed in the next kill; no sediment allowed.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

This entry's core. The Main God Space's regression mechanisms have four, all endogenous to structure:

1. **Zeroing mechanism**: instance ends = extraction. An ecological knowledge a member learned in *Alien* world, a war law witnessed in *Lord of the Rings* world, will not remain in the team in any inheritable form. Newcomers join; all from scratch.
2. **Replacement mechanism**: old member dies; the Main God randomly pulls a newcomer from reality to fill the pit. This means the team always has a large number of "zero-experience, zero-equipment, zero-consensus" members; civilization-level experience never catches up to battle-loss speed.
3. **Rule black box**: who the Main God is, why it harvests, where harvested energy goes, all unknowable. No matter how strong participants get, they only run faster under opaque rules. **Opaque, cannot be reformed.**
4. **Goal misalignment**: participants' highest goal is "survive," not "build something." A group where everyone only seeks self-preservation cannot spontaneously produce civilizational engineering.

Four mechanisms stacked result: the Main God Space runs for a thousand years, no essential difference from the first day — still newcomers die, veterans fight, top layer harvests. This is **reincarnation-harvest-type regression**: not civilization going downhill, but from design it does not allow uphill.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Zheng Zha does not realize that every time he "protects teammates," he maintains the assembly line's stability for the Main God: as long as China Team still turns in decent results, the Main God continues deploying more dangerous instances, harvesting more energy. The better Zheng Zha does, the higher the harvest efficiency. His brotherhood was used by the system as "glue that raises production capacity."

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain simulates three endings: A. Zheng Zha keeps winning forever; B. Zheng Zha eventually discovers the Main God truth and rebels; C. Main God broken through, participants released. Simulation shows: even if ending C achieved, the released ones — because long-lived under slaughterhouse rules — their organizational ability, knowledge system, emotional structure already deeply remolded; after returning to reality cannot directly convert to civilizational power — **reincarnation harvest's true harm is not physical death, but training a generation's souls into butchers. Participants long trained by the Main God Space have values remolded to "life-death as transaction, teammates as chips, enemies as EXP" — even if one day released back to reality, can no longer live peacefully. This is a deeper regression than physical death: civilization regressed to the point it no longer knows "how to coexist peacefully."**

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Explanations for "why the Main God selected Earthlings," "why use movies as instances" flip multiple times late
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. Infinite-flow sequels numerous; original late Box Theory layer upon layer; old settings repeatedly invalidated
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high**. China Team's survival tied to Zheng Zha; Main God rules tied to unknowable top layer

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: energy level force-zeroed by rules; no sediment allowed
- **Anti-predation**: reincarnation = institutionalized harvest; participants = fuel
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least let people not bet life-death for cultivation
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: pulled ones are only random samples; billion-billion beings have no right to choose
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: under Main God black box, cosmos truth closed to participants
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

Reincarnation harvest is all predatory civilizations' extreme form. It does not disguise, does not embellish, does not promise a future — it directly tells you: you are fuel. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is also direct: any structure that designs civilization as "always starting from zero," no matter how fast a champion it runs, is only an excellent employee of the slaughterhouse.
