# Entry 471 | Infinite Terror · Zheng Zha's Predatory-Thinking Decomposition: Personal Centralization of Team Resources

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Infinite Terror Decomposition (Entry 4) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 06, 26

## Introduction: the most loyal captain, also the most concentrated resource black hole

Zheng Zha is one of infinite-flow history's most loyal protagonists. He does not abandon teammates, does not pit newcomers, first thing after winning is "revive the fallen brothers." But Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — tells us: predatory thinking is not defined by character good/evil, but by **resource-flow structure**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. The better Zheng Zha's character, the less suspected the resource black hole he occupies.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Zheng Zha's resource concentration manifests in three places:

- **Priority allocation of exchange points**: points and side plots settled per instance allocated by "who can lead the team to survive"; Zheng Zha is always first in line. China Team's best equipment, rarest bloodline, top-tier skills, almost all hang on him.
- **Directional shaping of teammate abilities**: Zhao Kongkong trained as mental-power controller (filling Zheng Zha's reconnaissance gap), Luo Gandao trained as mech pilot (filling Zheng Zha's ranged firepower gap), Zero Point trained as sniper (filling Zheng Zha's precision-strike gap). China Team is not seven or eight independent strong ones, but "Zheng Zha's seven organs."
- **Monopoly of decision-making**: which instance to enter, which item to use, whom to sacrifice to save whom, Zheng Zha decides alone. On the battlefield this is efficient; but at civilization level it means the whole team's growth direction is locked by one person's judgment.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

A "captain-centralized" team's historical curve inevitably: when captain is present, whole team strong; once captain is heavily wounded, distracted, emotionally fluctuates, whole team immediately exposes gaps. China Team experienced more than once the loop of "Zheng Zha falls → whole team collapses → overturned only by revival." **Betting civilization on one person is betting civilization on a single log** — same lesion source as Tang Luo, Ye Fan, Gu Chensha, only Zheng Zha's log is wrapped most warmly in "brotherhood."

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

This entry's core. Zheng Zha's predatory thinking has three layers:

1. **Resource-layer predation**: the team's rarest resources flow to him alone. He is not embezzling — he uses these resources to fight, to tank damage, to save teammates. But "I used resources to save you" precisely proves resources in his hands are a "single-point amplifier," not "distributed infrastructure."
2. **Possibility-layer predation**: every China Team member could have walked his own strongest route. But under "cooperate with Zheng Zha to survive" rules, their possibilities compressed into accessories. Zhao Kongkong could have become her own strong one; Luo Gandao could have walked his own mech way — but the Main God Space does not need a second captain; Zheng Zha has no interest in cultivating a second.
3. **Emotion-layer predation**: the truer Zheng Zha's brotherhood, the more willingly teammates cede growth direction to him. This is the gentlest predation — **let you voluntarily hand over your own possibility out of being moved.**

Heng Yuan Zhou terminology: Zheng Zha is the warm variant of the "enlightened predator." He is not like Tang San who blatantly monopolizes opportunities, nor like Ye Fan who defaults "ten-thousand years of peace belongs to the Ye family"; he wraps predation in brotherhood as "we live together."

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Zheng Zha Centralization | Anchor-Symbiosis Alternative |
|--------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Points priority to captain | Joint contracting by more-work-more-reward; contribution records public |
| Teammates shaped by gaps | 20% activation strategy; everyone independently matures |
| Captain decides alone | Anchor Council collective legislation |
| Brotherhood = governance | Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision; not relying on personal emotion |
| Whom to sacrifice decided alone | Symbiotic Court ruling; right to life not bet on one person's judgment |

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain injects variable "Zheng Zha fully discloses every instance's exchange list, skill route, decision reasons, and forcibly cultivates two vice-captains." Simulation shows: after twenty instances, China Team produces three independent combat strengths each able to stand alone; team survival rate when Zheng Zha absent from 8% rises to 54% — **centralization is not efficiency, but putting eggs in one basket that will break.**

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium**. Team-battle internal rules broadly self-consistent, but "why Zheng Zha is always strongest" lacks systematic explanation; gradually sliding into protagonist halo
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium-high**. Original mid-late period new characters keep joining; old teammates' settings simplified or even forgotten
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high**. China Team's 90% combat strength concentrated on Zheng Zha; fits "gift-style-leaning-oligarchic" judgment

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: team resources compressed into one amplifier
- **Anti-predation**: teammates' personal possibilities structurally ceded
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least let students cultivate independently; Zheng Zha trains teammates into accessories
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: other squads outside China Team are backdrop in original
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: team capabilities cannot be distributively replicated
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

Zheng Zha is a captain worthy of respect. But respecting a good person is not endorsing his structure. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is not "a better captain," but "a team that can run without a captain" — because as long as there is still one person who must carry everyone's lives, everyone's lives hang on that one person's heartbeat.
