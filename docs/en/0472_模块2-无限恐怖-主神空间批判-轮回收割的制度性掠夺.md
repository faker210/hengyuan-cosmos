# Entry 472 | Infinite Terror · Main God Space Critique: The Institutional Predation of Reincarnation Harvest

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Infinite Terror Decomposition (Entry 5) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 03, 19

## Introduction: the best predation is making the predated think it is a fair game

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — on the Main God Space specimen, reflect out a highest-level predation form: it wraps predation as "fair competition." The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Points openly priced, exchange list public, rank by performance — on the surface everyone has equal opportunity; actually these rules themselves were designed by the harvester.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The Main God Space's waste is not someone's waste, but institutional "forced waste":

- **Time forcibly consumed**: participants' entire life cycle counted into instance assembly line; cannot be used for any external construction
- **Emotion forcibly consumed**: the loop of comrade sacrifice — revival — re-sacrifice, uses the most precious emotional energy to maintain combat will
- **Knowledge forcibly consumed**: cross-world experience zeroed by personnel battle losses

The Main God Space **needs** these wastes — because once participants truly sediment civilizational ability, harvest is no longer efficient.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

The Main God Space is a textbook of "institutional predation":

1. **Rules unilaterally made by top layer**: the Main God does not negotiate with participants; rules change at will.
2. **Benefits flow to black box**: participants fight life-death to trade points; where the points ultimately flow, what the Main God does with this energy, participants have no right to know.
3. **Participants have no exit right**: enter = bound; exit = death. This turns "cultivation" into a "bondage contract."
4. **No representation**: no squad, no strong one is allowed to participate in the Main God Space's legislation. No matter how strong China Team is, it is only an "excellent product," not a "stakeholder."

A deeper layer of institutional predation: the Main God Space does not allow participants to form a "community of interests." Any squad that truly establishes mutual-aid, transparent, shared institutions will lower the Main God's harvest efficiency — therefore Main God Space rules secretly encourage intra-team infighting, encourage betrayal, encourage "sell out teammates for points." This institutional design's purpose is precisely to prevent participants from awakening the consciousness that "we should change rules together." **The most brilliant predation is making the predated never be able to unite.**

Heng Yuan Zhou regards the Main God Space as the prototype specimen of all "institutional predation." It is not like Tang San's spirit rings that blatantly hunt, nor like Ye Fan's Great Emperor position that openly monopolizes — it wraps predation as "fair competition," wraps harvest as "growth game." This wrapping is more dangerous than direct predation, because it makes the predated believe "I am voluntary," "I can win," "if I win I am free." Heng Yuan Zhou's anti-predation axiom first opposes this "predation wrapped as fairness."

Four stacked: the Main God Space is not a civilization, it is a **company** — shareholders are the Main God, employees are participants, and employees cannot even read the pay stub. This company's entire "corporate culture" is making employees believe "hard work gets promotion, promotion gets freedom," yet never telling employees who owns the company or where profits flow.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Zheng Zha never questions the Main God rules themselves. His resistance logic is "I want to become strong enough that the Main God also has to listen to me" — this is still competing to be the biggest player within the game rules, not smashing the game. This is precisely predatory thinking's deepest layer: **it teaches you how to win, never teaches you how to refuse to accept.**

The Main God Space is the prototype of all "institutional predation." Its most frightening thing is not killing people, but making the harvested believe "this is fair, I can win, if I win I am free." Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: **true fairness is never winning within someone else's rules, but having the right to decide what the rules are.**
