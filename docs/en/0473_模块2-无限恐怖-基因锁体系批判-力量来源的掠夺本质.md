# Entry 473 | Infinite Terror · Genetic-Lock System Critique: The Predatory Essence of the Power Source

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Infinite Terror Decomposition (Entry 6) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 15, 07

## Introduction: squeezing yourself at life-death edge is not evolution, it is burning life

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — has a blunt judgment on the "Genetic Lock" power source: it is not "unlocking potential," but **pressing origin**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. It makes you ten times stronger in thirty seconds; the price is overdrawing your next thirty years of life force.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Every time the Genetic Lock opens, it accompanies irreversible origin loss:

- **Stage 1 open = nerve overdraft**: muscles, bones, nervous system endure multiple loads in short time; open then collapse
- **Stage 2 open = endocrine crash**: adrenaline and stress hormones peak; open then leave long-term sequelae
- **Stage 3+ = consciousness split**: self-awareness begins conversing with "the being behind the lock"; personality stability drops
- **Stage 4/5 = origin burning**: every deep opening burns one's own life origin to trade for power

This "get stronger once, age ten years" cultivation way, in Heng Yuan Zhou's view, is **the most wasteful energy conversion** — the power you get is borrowed; the principal is your own life.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

The Genetic Lock system's regressiveness: **it does not produce new energy level, it only misappropriates future energy level.** A stage-5 Genetic Lock strong one's "true energy level" is far below surface energy level — his power comes from overdrawing future life. When a generation of strong ones collectively enters "overdraft state," the whole civilization's future life force is spent at once. This is not evolution; it is **usurious borrowing.**

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

The Genetic Lock's predation has three layers:

1. **Predating future self**: every opening is borrowing from "tomorrow's me." Zheng Zha has lived through a hundred instances; his body's real age may far exceed his physiological age.
2. **Predating surrounding people's attention**: when the Genetic Lock opens, the aura, pressure, anomaly field makes surrounding people instinctively yield the stage — the strong one's presence itself is a kind of resource monopoly.
3. **Predating cultivation's own dignity**: these three summed, the Genetic Lock is a kind of "usurious cultivation": on the surface you are getting stronger, actually you are overdrawing. More frightening, this overdraft is irreversible — every opening leaves cracks on origin; cracks accumulate to a degree, the person collapses mentally before physically. The Main God Space wraps this cultivation as "awakening human potential"; essentially no different from usury wrapped as "get rich quick."

True cultivation should be "self-generating, self-growing" — energy level naturally grows with time, understanding, practice. The Genetic Lock turns cultivation into "forcing yourself to open hard before death"; this is an insult to cultivation ethics. It makes a generation of young people believe: getting strong must bet life; growth must cost collapse. Once this value internalizes, it can never be corrected.

Heng Yuan Zhou's life-cycle system precisely goes the opposite way: energy level grows naturally, no origin burning; cultivation safely deducted, no life bets; knowledge filed in Alaya, no individual hard resistance. If Zheng Zha had taken this road from the start, he would not need to squeeze himself at life-death edge; he only needed to cultivate, understand, accumulate step by step. Fifty years later, his energy level might not be as "stunning" as stage-5 Genetic Lock, but his lifespan longer, his mind more stable, his knowledge more systematic, and his cultivation path can be taught to ten million later comers — **burn yourself, make one person strong; nourish yourself, make a race strong.**

Quest Sediment Domain contrast simulation: Path A = Genetic-Lock opening; Path B = Heng Yuan Zhou life-cycle cultivation. Same initial aptitude person, after fifty years: Path A strong one's surface combat power is Path B's 3×, but remaining lifespan is Path B's 1/4, and his power cannot be taught to descendants; Path B strong one's combat power only 1/3 of Path A, but his energy level shareable by Alaya Network; Myriad-Ranks overall energy-level growth is Path A's 30× — **burn yourself, make one person strong; nourish yourself, make a race strong.**

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium**. Genetic Lock stage-5's ability boundary fuzzy in original; late weak/strong by plot need
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium-high**. Genetic Lock's relationship with "Saints," "boxes" rewritten multiple times
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high**. High-stage Genetic Lock holders extremely few; China Team high-stage basically only Zheng Zha

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: origin burned out at once, unrecoverable
- **Anti-predation**: borrowing from future self; borrowing stage from surrounding people
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's cultivation at least does not burn life; Genetic Lock directly burns life
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Genetic Lock only open to "Main God selected"; billion-billion beings no right
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: press-type cultivation path opposite to symbiotic cosmos
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

The Genetic Lock is the micro-prototype of "predatory cultivation." It tells you: you are not getting strong, you are using yourself as firewood. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is not "firewood that burns hotter," but **let everyone no longer be treated as firewood.**
