# Entry 474 | Infinite Terror · Retcon-Ending Analysis: Late-Stage Setting Collapse and Combat-Power Inflation

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Infinite Terror Decomposition (Entry 7) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 19, 14

## Introduction: when "outside the box there is another box" becomes the universal patch

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — has a strict definition of "retcon": early iron laws broken by late plot convenience, settings yielding to emotion, new explanations overturning old without notifying readers. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. *Infinite Terror* as infinite-flow's founding work, its late collapse has paradigm significance.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Retcon itself is the biggest energy waste — early carefully built rules discarded, equivalent to all "reader trust" in early part zeroed at once. Zheng Zha rose from China Team squad leader all the way to "Saint-tier"; his energy-level jump curve had rules to follow early, late completely serves "this chapter must win."

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Infinite-flow as a genre, its retcon is structural:

1. **Instances infinite**: theoretically movies, novels, games, real events can all be instances; the author cannot build self-consistent rules for each instance.
2. **Combat power infinitely inflating**: instances stronger and stronger; protagonist must follow; once protagonist strong, early enemies become jokes.
3. **Truth infinitely nested**: above the Main God are Saints; above Saints is the box; above the box is the author — each new box opened, all prior "answers" invalidated.

This "infinite" genre is by design at war with "setting self-consistency."

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Zheng Zha's "strength" less and less comes from cultivation, more and more from "plot needs." He begins able to defeat opponents he could not possibly win early, not because he did something, but because the author needs him to win. This is the most hidden predation — **predating readers' trust in the narrative contract that "hard work gets strong."** When readers find Zheng Zha wins not because he did something right, but because the author needs him to win, all the early tension about "life-death fight" invalidates.

Infinite-flow as a genre, its retcon has another structural root: **it has no "cosmos ceiling."** Any finite cosmos (e.g., Douluo, Shrouding the Heavens), even if late collapses, at least has one "cosmos" as backstop; but infinite-flow from the start has no cosmos — it only has "the next instance." No cosmos, no law; no law, no self-consistency. This is the genre's original sin.

Heng Yuan Zhou's diagnosis of infinite-flow: it is not that it cannot write well, but its genre structure itself is at war with "setting self-consistency." To make infinite-flow not collapse, must first give it a cosmos — a cosmos with boundaries, laws, institutions, then let "infinite" unfold inside this cosmos. Heng Yuan Zhou's Multiverse Realm, Balanced-Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, is precisely this "bounded infinity": it can infinitely expand, but expansion goes in appendices, does not overturn old settings. Infinite-flow if accepts this transformation, can turn from "infinite retcon" to "infinite expansion without collapse." This is the law-first axiom's universal value for all long-form cosmoses.
