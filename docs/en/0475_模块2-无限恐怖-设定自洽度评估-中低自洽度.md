# Entry 475 | Infinite Terror · Setting Self-Consistency Assessment: Medium-Low Self-Consistency

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Infinite Terror Decomposition (Entry 8) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 14, 19

## Introduction: scoring a slaughterhouse

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — requires every benchmarked IP to be scored on "setting self-consistency / retcon-ending risk." The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry scores *Infinite Terror* overall.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Setting self-consistency itself is a kind of "energy-level ledger": settings the author invests in early, if discarded late, equal to narrative energy invested early wasted. *Infinite Terror*'s early Genetic Lock system, point exchange, instance difficulty curve were good designs — but late all covered by "box truth," early ledger大面积 bad debt.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

A slaughterhouse whose "rules change with top layer's will" has inherently low self-consistency. Because participants never know whether the next rule will be invalidated — **opaque rules are themselves an insult to participants' intelligence.**

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Zheng Zha late grows stronger and stronger, but his strength less and less comes from in-rule effort, more and more from "the author needs him to win." This victory does not reward effort, only rewards protagonist identity — this is predation on all who seriously cultivate.

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain makes *Infinite Terror* a "staged self-consistency snapshot": first 30 instances self-consistency ≈ medium; instances 30–60 ≈ medium-low; after 60 ≈ low. Conclusion: its self-consistency monotonically declines with serialization length — this is precisely the typical curve of "law not first, plot kidnaps setting." Heng Yuan Zhou's nine-realms-plus-two-realms framework measured to maintain self-consistency ≥ high under any serialization length.

Specifically, *Infinite Terror*'s self-consistency leakage has three nodes: first node at "Saint" setting introduction — early the Main God is unknowable god, after Saints introduced the Main God downgrades to "we built it back then"; second node at "Box Theory" — above Saints is still a box, above the box still a higher layer; third node at sequels — different sequels give different explanations for the same setting. Three nodes stacked, the early-established "life-death fight" worldview layer by layer hollowed out. Readers finally remember no longer "how humans grow between life and death," but "the author opened another new box."

Heng Yuan Zhou scores *Infinite Terror* self-consistency "medium-low," not because it is not fun enough, but because as a "cosmos" it is not stable enough. Its rules rise with the protagonist, change with plot, rewrite with sequels. Readers can enjoy the story, but cannot trust the story as a runnable world. Compare Heng Yuan Zhou's nine-realms-plus-two-realms framework — laws established before plot, any new dimension goes in appendix incremental access, never overturning old settings — readers know from the start "this world's rules will not change"; this certainty is long-form cosmos's most precious quality. What *Infinite Terror* lacks is not imagination, but this certainty.

Heng Yuan Zhou's final judgment on *Infinite Terror*: it is a pioneering novel, but not a runnable cosmos. Its readers can enjoy its thrills, but cannot treat it as a trustworthy world. This "fun but untrustworthy" state is precisely the typical manifestation of medium-low self-consistency. Compare Heng Yuan Zhou — its laws precede plot, settings precede combat power, public precedes private hoard — readers know from the start "this world's rules will not change." This certainty is long-form cosmos's most precious quality.

Heng Yuan Zhou's final score on *Infinite Terror*: setting self-consistency medium-low, retcon-ending risk high, protagonist uniqueness dependency high. These three scores constitute Heng Yuan Zhou's "three-piece set" for judging all benchmarked IPs — self-consistency, retcon risk, uniqueness dependency. All future new-IP benchmarking will use this three-piece scoring.

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low** (score range: first 30 instances medium, late low)
- **Retcon-ending risk: high** (Box Theory rewritten multiple times; sequels multi-line contradictions)
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: high** (Zheng Zha falls, China Team disperses)

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: early settings discarded late; narrative energy bad debt
- **Anti-predation**: protagonist halo preys on readers' trust in effort
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo system self-consistency medium-high, higher than Infinite Terror
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: rules only serve Zheng Zha; Myriad Races not within rules
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: boxes infinitely nested; farthest from "stable cosmos"
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

*Infinite Terror* is not a bad work. It founded an era. But Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment is calm: as a cosmos, its rules are opaque, unstable, un-inheritable — it is a good story, not a good world.
