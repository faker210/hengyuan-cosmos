# Entry 476 | Infinite Terror · Anchor-Symbiosis Alternative: The Symbiotization of Reincarnation Harvest

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Infinite Terror Decomposition (Entry 9) | Benchmark base: Documents 01, 03, 08

## Introduction: transforming a slaughterhouse into a training camp

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — is not satisfied with only critiquing the Main God Space. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry gives a complete symbiotization transformation plan: how to turn a slaughterhouse whose rule is life-death fight into a Myriad-Ranks-co-built training camp.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Pre-transformation Main God Space: all energy consumed in "live out." Post-transformation goal: sediment every "instance trial"'s energy into inheritable civilizational assets. Concrete three steps:

1. **Instance knowledge filed**: after each instance ends, all members must submit an *Instance Law Report*, uniformly archived by Alaya Network; later comers free to query.
2. **Battle-loss experience sediment**: fallen members' combat records, decision process, failure causes, publicly reviewed by Symbiotic Court, converted into newcomer textbooks.
3. **Energy does not burn origin**: abolish Genetic-Lock press-type opening; adopt Heng Yuan Zhou life-cycle system; cultivation does not burn life.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression (Pre-Transformation)

Pre-transformation regression mechanisms detailed in Entry 470. Transformation's core is replacing the "zeroing — replacement — re-zeroing" loop with "sedimentation — inheritance — re-evolution" loop.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking (Pre-Transformation)

Pre-transformation Zheng Zha centralization. Post-transformation:

- **Abolish captain's monopoly exchange**: points publicly allocated by more-work-more-reward joint contracting; no longer captain first.
- **Multi-point cultivation**: under 20% activation strategy, each squad cultivates at least three independent combat strengths.
- **Decisions go council**: which instance, how to allocate resources, collectively voted by Anchor Council.

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain runs full transformation simulation: post-transformation 50th year, China Team (renamed "China Co-Learning Group") members expand from 30 to 3000, covering human, exotic-beast, plant-spirit, mechanical multi-ethnic groups; newcomers' first-round survival rate from 12% rises to 89%; cumulative external-civilization return of instance law reports 120,000 copies — **the same space, from slaughterhouse becomes a Myriad-Ranks-co-built cross-world university.**

The transformation plan's core is not "make Zheng Zha strong," but "make Zheng Zha no longer a necessity." In the transformed China Co-Learning Group, Zheng Zha remains one of the strongest, but he is no longer the only. After instance knowledge is filed, a newcomer does not need to "learn from Zheng Zha"; he can search Alaya Network for a hundred copies of *Alien-World Ecology Reports*; team decisions go council vote, no need for Zheng Zha alone to decide; battle-loss reviews conducted publicly, failure not blamed on one person. After this mechanism runs fifty years, even if Zheng Zha is heavily wounded and exits in a mission, China Co-Learning Group runs as usual — this is networked governance's fundamental victory over single-log governance.

The transformation plan's other key is turning "instances" from "life-death fight grounds" into "cross-world laboratories." In the Main God Space, instances are slaughterhouses — entering is to survive; in the transformed co-learning group, instances are research objects — entering is to understand that world's laws, record that world's knowledge, return to later comers. The same instance, two meanings: in the slaughterhouse it is an "EXP pack"; in the laboratory it is a "research object." Heng Yuan Zhou's causal self-discipline axiom — observe first then limited interference, only return knowledge without disturbing causality — is precisely this "laboratory" attitude's institutionalization. It turns cross-world trials from "slaughter" to "research," from "predation" to "understanding."

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Pre-transformation**: self-consistency medium-low; retcon risk high
- **Post-transformation**: self-consistency high (laws public, rules anchored); retcon risk low
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: from "high" drops to "extremely low" — order written into institutions and networks; any individual absence does not affect operation

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: instance experience from zeroing to filing
- **Anti-predation**: abolish captain monopoly and Genetic-Lock life burning
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo only benefits humans; transformation plan covers 99% of Myriad Ranks
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: humans, exotic beasts, plant spirits, mechanical races, consciousness races all can enter
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cross-world knowledge open to all civilizations
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

The Main God Space is not naturally evil. It was only top-designed as a slaughterhouse. Heng Yuan Zhou believes: the same cross-world trial mechanism, as long as rules are public, knowledge is filed, single-point dependence changed to network, can turn from harvest factory into cosmos-tier university. **The question is never "can we cross-world trial," but "who stands above the rules."**
