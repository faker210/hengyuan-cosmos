## Introduction: take Zheng Zha off the chessboard

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — requires every benchmarked system to do "Quest Sediment Domain simulation review." The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry uses a cruelest thought experiment to test China Team: **take Zheng Zha out of the team, what is left?**

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Quest Sediment Domain fully replicates China Team. At the 30th instance node, variable injected: Zheng Zha "withdraws due to injury" before instance starts, does not participate in this battle. Observation results:

- Instance advancement speed drops 73%
- Team decision time from average 40 seconds extends to average 11 minutes (no one dares decide)
- First occurrence of "member擅自 abandoning mission to self-preserve"
- At instance end battle-loss rate from normal 8% rises to 41%

Conclusion: Zheng Zha alone contributes China Team's about 70% combat strength and 90% decision-making power. He is not "captain"; he is "the team's operating system."

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

This data itself is the answer. A team's combat strength and decision-making power 90% concentrated on one person means:

- This person alive, team can fight
- This person wounded, team paralyzed
- This person dead, team disintegrates

This is not a team; this is **one person wearing seven people's shells.**

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Zheng Zha himself is not wrong. But his centralization made other members lose the opportunity to "become themselves." When Zhao Kongkong, Luo Gandao, Zero Point get used to "waiting for captain's order," they can never grow their own judgment muscles. **Predatory thinking's most hidden cost is making the protected lose growth ability.**

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan (This Entry's Core)

Review conclusions:

1. **Single-point dependence index**: Zheng Zha's contribution share to China Team ≈ 90%, belongs to "extremely high single-point dependence."
2. **Collapse threshold**: Zheng Zha absent more than one instance, team undergoes "decision paralysis — self-preservation split — battle-loss surge" three-stage collapse.
3. **Institutional substitution benefit**: after injecting Anchor-Symbiosis institutions (council + multi-point cultivation + Alaya knowledge filing), Zheng Zha-absent battle-loss rate from 41% drops to 6%, decision time stable within 90 seconds.
4. **Civilizational revelation**: any system equating "captain" with "team" is betting civilization on a single log.

The review also reveals a deeper mechanism: when Zheng Zha is present, China Team looks united; actually it is "united by captain's will." Once captain is absent, long-suppressed contradictions between members (Zhao Kongkong vs. Luo Gandao tactical differences, Zero Point vs. Qi Tengyi style conflicts) all erupt. This shows China Team's unity is not institutional unity, but personality unity — **a team sustained by one person's charisma; once the personality disperses, the team disperses.** What Anchor-Symbiosis institutions solve is precisely this "personality unity" unreliability: build unity on commonly followed rules, not on trust in a specific person.

Quest Sediment Domain also did a control experiment: let China Team start "institutional training" while Zheng Zha is present — after every instance forced to write review, forced to cultivate deputies, forced to publicize decision process. Thirty years later, China Team in an instance where Zheng Zha was absent, battle-loss rate only from normal 8% rises to 15%, far below "no institutional training"'s 41%. This control shows: single-point dependence is not unchangeable; it was just never seriously trained. **As long as from the start we take "do not depend on captain" as training goal, the team can grow distributed redundancy.** Zheng Zha's tragedy is not that he is too strong, but that he was never required to "not be strong."

This control experiment's revelation is far-reaching: single-point dependence is not innate, it is trained. As long as from the start we take "distributed" as training goal, any team can grow redundant ability not depending on one person. Zheng Zha and China Team just lacked this training — and this training is precisely what Anchor-Symbiosis institutions provide.
