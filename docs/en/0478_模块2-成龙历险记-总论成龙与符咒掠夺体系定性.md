# Entry 478 | Jackie Chan Adventures Series Overview: Jackie Chan and the Talisman Predation System Qualitative Assessment

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Jackie Chan Adventures Full Decomposition (Entry 1 · Overview) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 05, 18

## Introduction: twelve talismans, twelve zero-sum prizes

*Jackie Chan Adventures* stars archaeologist Jackie Chan, revolving around the "Twelve Talismans": Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig; each talisman grants a supernatural power — immortality, super strength, invisibility, shapeshifting, flight, fire-breathing, etc. The main plot is Jackie's side and dark forces (Valmont, Shendu, Demon Forces) contesting the talismans. This animation accompanied a generation's childhood, but Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe) taking the six top-level axioms as ruler — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — will find: the whole animation's bottom structure is a zero-sum contest around twelve "unreplicable supernatural resources." The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Jackie's energy comes from two things: kung fu + occasional talisman use. But this energy's direction is typical "personal-adventure-grade":

- **Talismans treated as props**: each talisman's power (e.g., Rat talisman gives life to inanimate objects; Horse talisman heals all injuries) could have been used for civilizational transformation — Horse talisman if used for public medical care, enough to eliminate half the world's diseases; Rat talisman if used for industry and agriculture, enough to reconstruct material production. But in the plot talismans are only "snatched back and forth"
- **Jackie himself zero construction**: he is an archaeologist, a cultural-relic protector, yet never systematized or publicized supernatural knowledge. Talismans found immediately handed in, handed in immediately sealed
- **Villains' energy equally wasted**: Shendu, Valmont, demons after getting talismans also only used them to "rule a small territory," never converting talisman power into civilizational engineering

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

The talisman system's regression mechanism is very clear:

1. **Resources zero-sum**: the Twelve Talismans are finite and unreplicable. One side gets, the other loses. This means all forces holding talismans are perpetually at war
2. **Power privately hoarded**: talisman power never publicly studied, never publicized. It is treated as "treasure," not "knowledge"
3. **Contest loop**: twelve talismans scatter — gather — scatter — gather; story loops; civilization never advances a step because of talismans

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Jackie's character is beyond reproach — he protects Jade, protects cultural relics, refuses to use supernatural power for personal gain. But structurally:

- **Talismans as resources**: Jackie's side's "protection" of talismans is essentially seizing resources from villains' hands, locking them in Section 13 vault. He did not make talisman power public, let all humanity study
- **Zero-sum as default**: the whole animation defaults "talismans can only be held by one side"; never appears the imagination of "all talisman power public"
- **Protagonist as arbitrator**: who should hold talismans, when to use, decided by Jackie's side. This is a "good-guy version" monopoly

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Horse talisman privately hoarded | Public medical system; healing ability universal |

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain simulates: if twelve talismans early in story were jointly publicized by "Jackie + Uncle + Section 13" to the global scientific community, what happens? Result shows: within thirty years, talisman power's principles deconstructed, replicated, popularized; all-humanity medical, transportation, energy revolutionizes; wars around talismans disappear — **once zero-sum resources are deconstructed into knowledge, they are no longer zero-sum.**

This simulation reveals Jackie Chan Adventures' key difference from other predatory IPs: Douluo's spirit rings, Shrouding-the-Heavens' Great Emperor position, Dragon Ball's transformations, all are "power endogenous to the person"; Jackie's talismans are "power external to objects." External-object power theoretically easier to publicize, study, replicate — it does not require hunting 100,000-year spirit beasts like spirit rings, nor does it require burning one's own life like Genetic Lock. As long as someone willing to hand over talisman principles, it can immediately convert to public knowledge. But Jackie's side never made this choice. This is not an ability problem, it is a choice problem.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium**. Twelve talismans' ability boundaries clear early; late weak/strong by plot need
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Multi-season animation's talisman, demon, mask settings occasionally contradictory, but overall within children's animation range reasonably self-consistent
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: medium-high**. Talisman contest's win/loss tied to Jackie personally; civilizational institution building zero

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: power enough to transform civilization treated as "props snatched back and forth"
- **Anti-predation**: talismans as zero-sum resources; good and bad guys both grabbing
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least publicized martial arts; Jackie did not even publicize talisman principles
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: talisman power only serves Earthling contest; alien/otherworldly/exotic-beast no share
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: power privately hoarded; far from symbiotic cosmos
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

Jackie is a good person. But "good guy snatches treasure" and "bad guy snatches treasure," under zero-sum structure, are the same thing. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is not "a better talisman-holder," but **let talismans no longer be treasure, but knowledge** — once power is publicized, deconstructed, replicated, no longer need Jackie to guard it with fists.
