# Entry 479 | Jackie Chan Adventures · Energy-Waste Analysis: Talisman Power Used for Personal Adventures

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Jackie Chan Adventures Decomposition (Entry 2) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 18, 15

## Introduction: twelve pieces of civilizational-grade power, only used for small fights

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — has a very direct judgment on "energy waste": when a power's civilizational use far exceeds personal use, its use for personal is waste. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Each of the Twelve Talismans is a specimen of this waste.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

When power enough to eliminate disease is locked in a vault, when power enough to extend lifespan is treated as a gimmick to "make an old man younger for a few minutes," civilization stops in place. Every time the Twelve Talismans appear, Earth should have leaped; but the story loops for hundreds of episodes, Earth is still the Earth that needs Jackie's fist to solve problems.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Jackie is not an active predator. But his "protect talismans" behavior, structurally equivalent to **privately hoarding civilizational-grade resources in Section 13**. He did not give talismans to the UN, to the scientific community, to any public institution — he locked them where his own people can control. This is "good-guy version hoarding."

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain control: Path A = original animation plot; Path B = talismans publicized. Path B fifty years later, Earth average lifespan from 70 years rises to 200 years, infant mortality drops to zero, global wars disappear (because no zero-sum resource worth fighting for). Path A fifty years later, Earth still grabbing talismans. **The same set of resources, two civilizational fates.**

This control's deep revelation: talisman power's waste is not because the power is not big enough, but because the power's use way is locked by "adventure narrative." In adventure narrative, talismans must be "props snatched back and forth" — because if it were publicly studied, the story would not be fun. But Heng Yuan Zhou asks: **a fun animation, and a world worth living in, which is more important?** Jackie Chan Adventures chose fun; Heng Yuan Zhou chose worth-living. This is not an aesthetic judgment, it is a value judgment.

The per-tally waste-ratio table also reveals a commonality: **none of the Twelve Talismans was used for "what it should have been used for."** Horse talisman should be for medical care, ended up occasionally healing wounds; Dog talisman should be for lifespan research, ended up making Uncle younger; Rat talisman should be for material production, ended up giving life to plush toys. This "wrong-direction use" is not accidental; it is adventure narrative's inevitable requirement — adventure narrative needs talismans to appear as "props," not as "infrastructure." What Heng Yuan Zhou needs to do is turn talismans from props back into infrastructure — let Horse talisman become the foundation of the global medical system, let Dog talisman become the starting point of lifespan research, let Rat talisman become the engine of the automation revolution.

## Conclusion

The Twelve Talismans are twelve mountains of gold. What Jackie did is treat every mountain of gold as "something bad guys must not get," lock it in his own warehouse. He did not do wrong — in a world where bad guys want to use the gold mountain to make cannons, this is the safest choice. But Heng Yuan Zhou asks: **why not smelt the gold mountain into a bridge, let everyone walk across?**
