# Entry 480 | Jackie Chan Adventures · Historical Regression Inevitability: Civilizational Stagnation of the Talisman Contest

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Jackie Chan Adventures Decomposition (Entry 3) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 05, 03

## Introduction: snatched for hundreds of episodes, civilization did not advance a step

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — judges whether a civilization "regresses" not only by whether it goes downhill, but more by whether it **stops rising due to internal structure**. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. *Jackie Chan Adventures*'s Earth is precisely this stagnation specimen: across hundreds of episodes, technology, medicine, social institutions near-zero progress.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The talisman contest itself is the biggest waste: both sides invest massive manpower, time, life, only to "prevent the other from getting it." This contest's net contribution to civilization is zero — even negative, because during the contest it destroyed massive cities, cultural relics, public facilities.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Stagnation mechanisms have three:

1. **Zero-sum resources lock cognition**: the whole society revolves around "who owns the talismans"; no one imagines "how to make talisman power public." Thought locked by zero-sum framework.
2. **Knowledge not filed**: Uncle as a magician, his magical knowledge all in his personal brain, never systematically recorded. Once he dies, the knowledge is gone.
3. **No institution building**: Section 13 is a secret agency, not responsible to the public, not subject to supervision, not cooperating with elected institutions. It is always "Jackie's gang" that decides.

Three stacked: after supernatural resources appear, Earth not only did not leap, but was dragged into the "grab — guard — grab — guard" loop. This is **civilizational-stagnation regression**: not regressing to backwardness, but regressing to spinning in place.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Jackie's side never asked "why talismans can only be grabbed." Their goal is "let our side grab." This is predatory thinking's bottom assumption — **default resources are zero-sum, default contest is reasonable, default publicity is impossible.**

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Talisman Zero-Sum | Anchor-Symbiosis Alternative |
|---------------------|-------------------------------|
| Talismans snatched back and forth | Talismans publicized; all humanity studies |
| Uncle's magic private transmission | Alaya Network; knowledge filed |
| Section 13 secret operation | Anchor Council public legislation |
| Zero-sum thinking | Develop increment; do not consume stock |

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain simulates: if from the start the story assumes "talisman power must be publicized," what happens? Result: contest disappears; science takes over talismans; thirty years civilizational leap. **Stagnation is not the talismans' curse, it is zero-sum thinking's curse.**

Notably, Earth in Jackie Chan Adventures is actually more "normal" than Earth in other IPs — it was not completely transformed by supernatural power, but also not destroyed by supernatural power. It stops at an awkward position of "has supernatural resources, but does not know how to use them." This awkwardness is precisely stagnation's mark: a civilization has the power enough to change itself, yet because thought is locked by zero-sum framework, refuses to use it. Heng Yuan Zhou calls this state "resource-rich civilizational poverty" — holding a gold mountain, yet still begging.

This "resource-rich civilizational poverty" has counterparts in other IPs: Douluo's spirit-beast forest resources abundant, but spirit beasts defined as "hunting targets"; Shrouding-the-Heavens' star-domain resources vast, but star domains defined as "Great Emperor battlefields"; Dragon Ball's cosmos infinite, but cosmos defined as "God of Destruction chessboard." Each IP has resources enough to leap, but each IP refuses to use because of thought-framework lock. Heng Yuan Zhou's anti-predation axiom — develop increment, do not consume stock — precisely wants to break this thought lock: **resources are not for grabbing, they are for building.**

Jackie Chan Adventures' stagnation is more hidden than Douluo's regression. Douluo at least "levels up" — though the leveling way is predatory; Jackie Chan Adventures does not even level up, it only "repeats." Across hundreds of episodes, Earth neither progresses because of talismans, nor is destroyed because of talismans — it stops in place. This stagnation itself is a regression, because a civilization after supernatural resources appears if it does not use them, it equals wasting a leap opportunity. Heng Yuan Zhou calls this waste "civilizational-level missed chance."

This "missed chance" is more regrettable than "regression" — regression at least has direction; missed chance has no direction. Earth in Jackie Chan Adventures clearly holds the power enough to change civilization, yet because thought-framework lock refuses to use it. What Heng Yuan Zhou needs to do is help it break this framework.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium**. Earth society after supernatural power appears has no reaction; itself not self-consistent
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**. Multi-season settings occasionally contradictory
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: medium-high**

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: contest process net destruction negative
- **Anti-predation**: zero-sum thinking defaults predation reasonable
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least built an academy; Jackie did not even publicize talisman principles
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: talismans only serve Earthlings
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: knowledge not filed; civilization not sedimented
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

A civilization does not automatically progress because treasures appeared. If it defaults "treasures can only be grabbed," then the more treasures, the more backward it is. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is not "the side that wins the grab," but **the civilization that turns treasures into knowledge.**
