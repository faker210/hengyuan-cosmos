# Entry 482 | Jackie Chan Adventures · Twelve Talismans Critique: The Predatory Essence of the Power Source

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Jackie Chan Adventures Decomposition (Entry 5) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 18, 10

## Introduction: where power comes from decides where civilization goes

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — has strict standards for "power source": if a power can only be "found," "grabbed," "inherited," but not "learned," "replicated," "publicly studied," then it is essentially predatory power. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. The Twelve Talismans are precisely specimens of this power.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

The Twelve Talismans' power source has three features, all pointing to predation:

1. **Unreplicable**: the twelve talismans are unique; a thirteenth cannot be made.
2. **Unlearnable**: power on the talismans cannot be obtained through cultivation, only "worn."
3. **Inseparable**: power bound to the talisman; talisman taken, power changes owner.

These three summed, mean talisman power **is essentially a "position resource"** — whoever occupies the talisman has power; whoever does not, has none. It is completely opposite to "knowledge": knowledge can be shared, copied, taught; talismans can only be contested, occupied, sealed.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Unreplicable power inevitably produces **monopoly**. Once one side long monopolizes talismans, it does not need to develop any "alternative technology." After hundreds of years, the whole civilization forms path dependence on talismans; without talismans, it cannot fight, cannot heal, cannot transport. **Building civilization on unreplicable resources is building civilization on sand.**

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Jackie's side's "protection" of talismans is essentially maintaining a "position resource"'s ownership. He never tried to "replicate talisman power" — because the whole worldview defaults talismans unreplicable. This default is predatory thinking's deepest soil: **it makes everyone feel "grabbing is the only solution."**

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

|----------------------|-------------------------------|
| Unreplicable | 300 Origin cultivation systems; learnable, teachable |
| Unlearnable | Alaya Network fully public |
| Bound to item | Power endogenous to person; not dependent on external objects |

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain control: Path A = talisman-type unreplicable power; Path B = Heng Yuan Zhou-type replicable cultivation system. Two paths run for a thousand years: Path A civilization stops in the "grab talismans" loop; Path B civilization's energy level rises ten-thousand-fold. **Power learnable, civilization can advance; power grabbable, civilization only spins in place.**

This comparison reveals a deeper principle: **power's source decides civilization's form.** When power comes from "learnable knowledge," civilization moves toward universalization — because the more people know knowledge, the stronger civilization; when power comes from "grabbable position," civilization moves toward contest — because the more people fight for position, the more chaotic civilization. Talismans are the latter, spirit rings are the latter, Genetic Lock is also the latter. Heng Yuan Zhou's 300 Origin cultivation systems are the former — it is learnable, teachable, replicable, universalizable; therefore it naturally leads to Myriad-Ranks republicanism.

This principle applies to all power systems: **judging whether a power system is predatory, do not look at how strong it is, look at whether it can be replicated.** Power that can be replicated, the more people have it, the stronger civilization; power that cannot be replicated, the more people fight over it, the more chaotic civilization. Talismans cannot replicate (only twelve), spirit rings cannot arbitrarily replicate (need to hunt 100,000-year spirit beasts), Genetic Lock cannot replicate (only burn one's own life), Tiandao Law cannot replicate (only Gu Chensha cultivated). Heng Yuan Zhou's 300 Origin systems are among the Myriad-Realms lineage the rare "replicable power" — this is no accident, it is design.

This "replicability test" is Heng Yuan Zhou's first cut for judging all power systems. Whether a power system passes this cut decides whether it is predatory or symbiotic. Talismans fail (only twelve), spirit rings fail (need to hunt spirit beasts), Genetic Lock fails (only burn oneself), Tiandao Law fails (only Gu Chensha cultivated). Heng Yuan Zhou's 300 Origin systems are the first system to pass this cut — learnable, teachable, replicable, universalizable. **Power that passes this cut is truly power belonging to Myriad Ranks.**

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium**. Talisman-unreplicable setting self-consistent within worldview, but this also means worldview ceiling low
- **Retcon-ending risk: medium**
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: medium-high**

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: unreplicable = un-universalizable = civilization locked
- **Anti-predation**: power essentially a "position resource"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's cultivation learnable, teachable; talismans only grabbable
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: talismans only change hands among Earth's few factions
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: power principles not public; far from symbiotic cosmos
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

The Twelve Talismans are the small specimen of "predatory power." It is not as grand as spirit rings, nor as life-burning as Genetic Lock, but it reveals a more plain truth: **if a power cannot be learned, it does not belong to everyone; it only belongs to the one who occupies it.**
