# Entry 484 | Jackie Chan Adventures · Anchor-Symbiosis Alternative: Civilizationalizing Talisman Power

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Jackie Chan Adventures Decomposition (Entry 7 · Conclusion) | Benchmark base: Documents 01, 03, 18

## Introduction: smelting twelve talismans into twelve bridges

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — is not satisfied with only critiquing talisman zero-sum. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry gives a complete transformation plan: how to turn twelve "treasures snatched back and forth" into twelve "bridges for Myriad Ranks to cross."

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Transformation three steps:

1. **Talisman principles publicized**: Horse talisman (healing), Dog talisman (longevity), Rat talisman (animate) as first batch publicized; global scientific community jointly studies.
2. **Replication and substitution**: through the "life-cycle system" among the 300 Origin cultivation systems, translate talisman power into learnable, teachable cultivation paths. The talismans themselves exit the historical stage.
3. **Civilizationalized application**: Horse talisman principle → global public medical care; Dog talisman principle → longevity research; Rat talisman principle → material-production automation.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression (Pre-Transformation)

Pre-transformation zero-sum loop detailed in Entry 480. Transformation's core is replacing "grab — guard — grab — guard" with "study — replicate — popularize."

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking (Pre-Transformation)

Post-transformation:

- **Abolish "talismans ours"**: talismans belong to no side; principles belong to all humanity.
- **Abolish Section 13 secret operation**: talisman research public, transparent, supervised.
- **Jackie role transformation**: from "the one who grabs talismans" to "the first propagandist for talisman civilizationization."

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain runs full transformation: post-transformation 30th year, Earth average lifespan from 70 years rises to 180 years; talisman-related crime rate drops to zero (because talismans no longer treasures); Jackie after retirement becomes a "cross-cultural magic history" popularization professor — **the same person, from adventurer to educator.**

The transformation plan's key is replacing the "talismans as treasure" cognition with "talismans as knowledge" cognition. Once this conversion completes, talismans no longer need to be grabbed — because the more people know knowledge, the more valuable it is, not the more depreciated. Horse talisman's healing principle once publicized, all humanity's doctors can study, improve, replicate; Dog talisman's longevity principle once publicized, all humanity's biologists can explore longevity boundaries; Rat talisman's animate principle once publicized, all humanity's engineers can think about new automation possibilities. **Treasure grabbed one less; knowledge publicized one more.** This is zero-sum turning non-zero-sum's essence.

The transformation plan also involves an institutional design: after talisman principles are publicized, who manages? Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is — no "manager" needed, because once principles are public, they become public knowledge; public knowledge is maintained by the public research community. Horse talisman's healing principle jointly researched, improved, annotated by global doctors; Dog talisman's longevity principle jointly explored, verified, corrected by global biologists; Rat talisman's animate principle jointly experimented, applied, extended by global engineers. This "distributed knowledge management" is safer, more efficient, more sustainable than Section 13's "centralized custody" — because it does not depend on any institution, any person, any vault.

Jackie's transformation from "adventurer" to "educator," this role change itself is the transformation plan's microcosm. He no longer needs to guard talismans with fists, because talismans are no longer treasures; he no longer needs to contest with Valmont, because the contested object no longer exists. He can spend his later life explaining to the world "what talisman power is, how to use, how to study" — this is more meaningful than him fighting a hundred battles. **A civilization's progress is not about how many battles its hero fights, but about when its hero can put down fists.**

The day Jackie puts down fists is the day Earth civilization truly begins to progress. Heng Yuan Zhou's transformation plan is not to make Jackie stronger, but to make Jackie no longer need to be strong.

- **Post-transformation**: self-consistency high; retcon risk low

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: talisman power from prop to infrastructure
- **Anti-predation**: principles publicized; zero-sum dissolves
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo only publicized martial arts; this plan publicizes supernatural power
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: talisman principles open to Myriad Ranks
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: power no longer Earth's monopoly
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

Twelve talismans need not be grabbed. They can be studied, replicated, popularized. Heng Yuan Zhou believes: **once treasure is understood as knowledge, it is no longer treasure — it becomes a bridge.** Jackie's fists should be used to open roads, not to guard warehouses.
