# Entry 485 | Dragon Rune Series Overview: Gu Chensha and Single-Log Civilization Qualitative Assessment

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Rune Full Decomposition (Entry 1 · Overview) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 05, 17

## Introduction: number one since ancient times, or the only one since ancient times

*Dragon Rune*'s Gu Chensha is web fiction's universally recognized combat-power ceiling: the first in history to cultivate Tiandao Law (Heavenly Dao Law), his saintly way is "sky-mending way" and "heaven-reforming way"; he vows to bear all sins and karmic retribution on behalf of all beings; late casually creates universes. He is broad-minded, ambitious, proposing the grand proposition "all beings between heaven and earth can become dragons." Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — does not make moral judgments on Gu Chensha's breadth, but dissects a sharpest question: **the greater he is, the more Myriad Races cannot do without him?** The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Heng Yuan Zhou's judging base has been upgraded to six top-level axioms.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Gu Chensha's energy level is among the highest tier in the Myriad-Realms lineage — Tiandao Law, cosmos creation, sky-mending, heaven-reforming. But this energy's direction is typical "single-log":

- **Civilization tied to one person**: he mends the sky, he reforms heaven, he bears sins for Myriad Ranks, he creates universes. All "civilization-level" actions done by him alone
- **Myriad Races do not participate**: he is willing to bear everything for Myriad Ranks — this sounds great, but equals outsourcing Myriad Ranks' subjectivity
- **No institution building**: after he creates universes, did not establish institutions letting Myriad Ranks self-govern in new universes

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Single-log civilization's regression mechanism is purest:

1. **Single-point dependence to the extreme**: once Gu Chensha falls, the whole cosmos collapses.
2. **Myriad Ranks trained as "the saved"**: Myriad Ranks got used to "Gu Chensha will carry it," lost the ability to carry it themselves.
3. **Gift = chain**: the more he gives, the more dependent Myriad Ranks become.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Gu Chensha is the highest form of "enlightened predator." He does not covet power, does not covet pleasure; what he covets is the moral high ground of "only I can carry it." **The other side of bearing sins for Myriad Ranks is robbing Myriad Ranks of the chance to atone themselves.**

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain simulates: Gu Chensha falls in a sky-mending. Result shows: the cosmos he created collapses within seven days; not one among Myriad Ranks can take over — because for ten thousand years all "key operations" done by Gu Chensha alone; Myriad Ranks never trained to "carry it themselves."

This simulation reveals single-log civilization's cruelest truth: **the greater Gu Chensha is, the more useless Myriad Ranks are.** He is not cultivating successors; he is cultivating an audience. After ten thousand years, Myriad Ranks watch him mend the sky, watch him reform heaven, watch him bear sins for them — they learned to look up, did not learn to act. When Gu Chensha finally falls, they can do nothing but cry. Heng Yuan Zhou calls this state "great tyranny": the greater the benefactor, the smaller the benefitted; the smaller the benefitted, the more dependent on the benefactor — a self-locked loop.

Gu Chensha vs. Tang Luo comparison established in Entry 251. Gu Chensha went further than Tang Luo — he not only universal-benefited, he also carries for Myriad Ranks; he not only built an academy, he also creates universes. But "going further" does not equal "going more right." Tang Luo at least let students cultivate themselves; Gu Chensha even cultivated for Myriad Ranks. Tang Luo's problem is "built academy but not institutions"; Gu Chensha's problem is more serious — he did not even build an academy; he directly lives for Myriad Ranks. **Tang Luo is "good-guy-version autocracy"; Gu Chensha is "saint-version autocracy."** The latter is harder to oppose than the former, because opposing a saint sounds like ingratitude. But Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment does not abandon structural critique because the other party is a saint — autocracy is autocracy, even wearing a saint's robe.

Gu Chensha in the five-level standard ranks "extremely high" — single-log civilization's ceiling. He went further than Tang Luo (high · gift-style), and is more dangerous. Because Tang Luo at least let students practice themselves; Gu Chensha even practiced for Myriad Ranks. **Tang Luo's problem is "built school but not constitution"; Gu Chensha's problem is "did not even build school, directly lived for Myriad Ranks."** This gap is the gap between "high" and "extremely high" — also between gift-style and single-log-style.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Tiandao Law, sky-mending, heaven-reforming, cosmos-creation boundaries late keep raising
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**. Combat power inflated to "casually create universes"; any new enemy must be bigger than universes
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: extremely high** (single-log; cosmos survival tied to Gu Chensha alone)

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: cosmos-creation-tier energy used to "carry for Myriad Ranks"; civilization zero institutions
- **Anti-predation**: bearing sins for Myriad Ranks = robbing Myriad Ranks' subjectivity
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Gu Chensha went further than Tang Luo, also more dangerous
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Myriad Ranks nominally "all can become dragons," actually all waiting for his gifts
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: single-log cosmos farthest from symbiotic network
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

Gu Chensha is web fiction history's greatest "good-guy ceiling." But Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment is cold: **a cosmos that needs the number-one in history to carry it is itself a failed cosmos.** True greatness is not carrying everything for Myriad Ranks, but letting Myriad Ranks learn they do not need you to carry it.
