# Entry 486 | Dragon Rune · Energy-Waste Analysis: Tiandao-Law Aptitude Used for Personal Sainthood

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Rune Decomposition (Entry 2) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 15, 17

## Introduction: the power of number one in history, only cultivated himself

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — judgment on "energy waste": if a power only cultivated one person, then no matter how big it is, it is waste. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Gu Chensha's Tiandao Law is precisely the extreme of this waste.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Gu Chensha's energy direction has four layers of waste:

- **Cosmos-tier aptitude, personal-tier use**: he can mend sky, reform heaven, create universes, but all these actions' purpose is "Gu Chensha himself becomes number one in history." Cosmos-tier energy compressed into one person's "cultivation milestone"
- **Myriad Ranks zero spillover**: the Tiandao Law he understood never transmitted to Myriad Ranks as public text. Myriad Ranks' understanding of the Dao still stops at "whatever Gu Chensha says"
- **Civilization zero infrastructure**: the universes he created have no schools, no hospitals, no councils — he created "space," did not create "society"
- **Cross-generation zero inheritance**: his ability travels with him alone; if he falls, all understanding goes with him

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

When a cosmos-tier understanding exists only in one person's brain, this understanding becomes his private property. Myriad Ranks can look up to him all they want, but only look up — **looking up is not learning; reverence is not inheritance.**

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Gu Chensha's predation is not greed for wealth, it is **"understanding right" predation**: Tiandao Law's true content, true path, true threshold, all defined by him alone. Myriad Ranks wanting to become dragons can only become in his way. This is the highest-level knowledge monopoly — **turning the Great Dao itself into personal private property.**

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Gu Chensha Personal Sainthood | Anchor-Symbiosis Alternative |
|--------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Tiandao Law in Gu Chensha's brain | 300 Origin systems fully public |
| Myriad Ranks look up to him | Alaya Network; knowledge learnable, teachable |
| He creates universes no society | Anchor Council + Symbiotic Court |
| Ability travels with him alone | Myriad-Ranks origin bidirectional cycle |
| Number one in history | 20% activation strategy; strong ones emerge in batches |

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain injects variable "Gu Chensha on the day he cultivates Tiandao Law, writes all understanding into public canon." Simulation shows: within a hundred years, among Myriad Ranks over a thousand strong ones reach Tiandao Law threshold; thousand strong ones co-build new universes; Gu Chensha himself from "the sole pillar" becomes "one of thousand founders" — **once energy publicized, single log turns into forest.**

This simulation's key: Tiandao Law's understanding itself does not depreciate because of publicity. Quite the opposite, publicity lets more people participate in understanding, supplement understanding, correct understanding — the more people participate, the richer, deeper, more reliable understanding becomes. Gu Chensha alone understanding Tiandao Law is one understanding; ten thousand people together understanding Tiandao Law is ten thousand understandings plus mutual dialogue and verification. **Knowledge is not like treasure; the more you divide, the less; knowledge is like fire; the more you spread, the brighter.** Gu Chensha privately hoarding Tiandao Law equals cupping a pile of fire in one person's arms — he himself is warm, Myriad Ranks still cold.

Gu Chensha's "understanding-right predation" is more thorough than Tang Luo's "knowledge monopoly." Tang Luo at least did not publicize mid-low martial arts; he at least publicized basic cultivation methods; Gu Chensha did not even publicize Tiandao Law's principles — he only let Myriad Ranks know "he cultivated it," did not let Myriad Ranks know "how to cultivate it." This "result public, process private" way is more dangerous than completely not publicizing: it lets Myriad Ranks know the road's endpoint exists, but does not let Myriad Ranks know where the road is. **The most thorough monopoly is not letting people not see the endpoint, but letting them see the endpoint yet not see the road.** Heng Yuan Zhou's 300 Origin cultivation systems are precisely opposite — it not only tells you where the endpoint is, it also publicizes every step in Alaya Network.

Gu Chensha's Tiandao Law private hoarding also causes a more hidden consequence: Myriad Ranks lose confidence in "cultivation" itself. When Myriad Ranks see Gu Chensha cultivated Tiandao Law, but do not know how, they will conclude "Tiandao Law only geniuses can cultivate." This conclusion once internalized, no one dares try again. **The biggest harm of privately hoarding the Great Dao is not that Myriad Ranks cannot learn, but that Myriad Ranks dare not learn.** Heng Yuan Zhou's public system precisely wants to break this "only geniuses can cultivate" fear — everyone has a road, everyone dares try.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. Tiandao Law boundary expands with Gu Chensha's personal promotion
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: extremely high**

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: cosmos-tier understanding only cultivated one person
- **Anti-predation**: the Great Dao privatized as private property
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least publicized mid-low martial arts; Gu Chensha did not even publicize Tiandao Law principles
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Myriad Ranks nominally all can become dragons, actually only look up
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: knowledge not publicized; cosmos not social
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

Tiandao Law is one of the peaks human imagination can reach. But Heng Yuan Zhou asks: **if this peak belongs to only one person, for Myriad Ranks, what difference is there from not existing?**
