# Entry 487 | Dragon Rune · Historical Regression Inevitability: Civilization Collapses Once Gu Chensha Is Absent

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Rune Decomposition (Entry 3) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 03, 29

## Introduction: remove the number-one-in-history from the cosmos

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — ultimate test for single-log civilization is the same thought experiment: **take the protagonist out of the cosmos, what is left?** Gu Chensha's answer is cruelest. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Quest Sediment Domain fully replicates the Dragon Rune world. At Gu Chensha's peak after "mending sky and reforming heaven," variable injected: Gu Chensha falls in a cross-cosmos operation, no longer exists. Observation results:

- Day 1: cracks appear at cosmos edge; no one can mend
- Day 7: karmic backlash; Myriad Races begin cursing each other because "no one bears sins"
- Day 30: Myriad Ranks' cultivation system overall confused; no one knows "Tiandao Law's next step"
- Day 100: cosmos begins collapsing

Conclusion: Gu Chensha alone bore four cosmos-tier functions: "sky-mending + sin-bearing + defining the Great Dao + maintaining order." Once he falls, four functions simultaneously vacant.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

This is single-log civilization's ultimate fate: **all of civilization's key functions concurrently held by one person.** The stronger he is, the less Myriad Ranks are needed; the less Myriad Ranks are needed, the less ability they have to take over. After a thousand years, the ability gap between him and Myriad Ranks already unbridgeable — he wants Myriad Ranks to take over, but they cannot.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Gu Chensha's "bearing sins for Myriad Ranks" sounds selfless, but it structurally completes a deepest predation: **he also undertook Myriad Ranks' opportunity to learn to take responsibility.** After a thousand years, Myriad Ranks not only cannot take over ability-wise, psychologically lost the consciousness of "I need to carry it myself."

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain control: Path A = Gu Chensha single-log; Path B = Anchor-Symbiosis type. Two paths run ten thousand years: Path A collapses within five hundred years after Gu Chensha falls; Path B continues rising. **Single-log civilization's lifespan is that single log's lifespan.**

This control also reveals a time-scale question: when Gu Chensha is alive, single-log civilization looks more "brilliant" than symbiotic civilization — because all energy concentrated on one person; his light outshines everything. But brilliance is unsustainable. Symbiotic civilization early looks less dazzling — because energy distributed across Myriad Ranks; no one emits that "number one in history" light. But distributed energy can last ten thousand, a hundred thousand, a million years; concentrated energy can only last Gu Chensha's lifespan. **Short-term brilliance, and long-term survival; Heng Yuan Zhou chooses the latter.**

This time-scale comparison is a wake-up call for all "protagonist as civilization" narratives. When Gu Chensha is alive, his light makes all readers believe "this cosmos will not collapse" — because there is Gu Chensha. But readers forgot to ask: what if Gu Chensha is gone? A cosmos that bets all key functions on one person, no matter how great that person, its lifespan does not exceed that person's lifespan. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: **a truly great cosmos should let its greatest person also exit at peace — because the cosmos does not need him to prop it; the cosmos can run itself.** Gu Chensha cannot do this, because from the start he never intended the cosmos to "run itself."

Quest Sediment Domain also did a control: let Gu Chensha from the second millennium start "consciously cultivating successors." Result shows: even starting cultivation from the second millennium, already too late — the first two thousand years' "single-person operation" already made Myriad Ranks completely lose autonomous operation ability; cultivated successors are only "miniature Gu Chensha," still single-log. **Single-log civilization's irreversibility is that it cannot cultivate forest — it can only cultivate more single logs.** What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is not "cultivate successors," but "build forest from the start."

This conclusion is a wake-up call for all single-log civilizations: Gu Chensha is not not great enough, he is too great — so great that Myriad Ranks do not need to grow on their own.
