# Entry 488 | Dragon Rune · Gu Chensha's Predatory-Thinking Decomposition: The Gift Essence of Bearing Sins for Myriad Ranks

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Rune Decomposition (Entry 4) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 26, 06

## Introduction: the greatest good deed, also the most hidden predation

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — judges "predatory thinking" not by motive good/evil, but by structural consequences. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Gu Chensha "vows to bear all sins and karmic retribution on behalf of Myriad Ranks"; motive purely good; but structural consequence is "undertaking" Myriad Ranks' subjectivity as well.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

"Bearing sins for Myriad Ranks"'s energy waste manifests in: it skips the causal process that Myriad Ranks themselves should learn, bear, grow. Myriad Ranks therefore never learn "why they were wrong, how to correct" — **energy used to carry for others equals carrying away people's growth right as well.**

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

When someone bears sins for you, Myriad Ranks lose the motivation to correct. After a thousand years, Myriad Ranks' moral level, responsibility consciousness, causal judgment not rise but fall — because "anyway someone carries it." This is the gift system's deepest poison: **it keeps the gifted permanently in a child's state.**

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

This entry's core. Gu Chensha's predation has three layers:

1. **Responsibility predation**: bearing onto himself the responsibility Myriad Ranks should bear.
2. **Growth predation**: carrying away what Myriad Ranks should learn from bearing.
3. **Subjectivity predation**: negating Myriad Ranks' qualification to "make their own choices, bear their own consequences."

Three stacked, "all beings between heaven and earth can become dragons" becomes empty talk — because becoming a dragon's premise is "walk by oneself," but Gu Chensha already paved the road, carried across the ditches, stepped on the thunder for you. Myriad Ranks are not becoming dragons; Myriad Ranks are **being made into dragons.**

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Gu Chensha Bears Sins for Myriad Ranks | Anchor-Symbiosis Alternative |
|------------------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| He carries everything | Myriad Ranks bear their own karma |
| Myriad Ranks do not grow | Causal self-discipline: observe first then interfere; let Myriad Ranks learn |
| Gift-style dragon-becoming | Myriad-Ranks republicanism: walk yourself, become yourself |
| Moral high ground monopolized | Morality is public issue, not one person carrying |
| He great, Myriad Ranks small | 99% Myriad-Ranks coverage; everyone has a road |

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain control: Path A = Gu Chensha bears sins for Myriad Ranks; Path B = Myriad Ranks bear themselves. Path A after a thousand years, Myriad Ranks' moral level stagnant, responsibility consciousness weak; Path B after a thousand years, Myriad Ranks' moral level autonomously rises, responsibility consciousness internalized. **Bearing sins for others raises a group of children who never grow up.**

This control also reveals a deeper ethical problem: Gu Chensha "bears sins for Myriad Ranks," sounds selfless, but it structurally completes a "de-sinification" of Myriad Ranks — Myriad Ranks need not be responsible for their own actions, because someone is responsible for them. This sounds compassionate, actually cancels Myriad Ranks' opportunity to learn "be responsible for yourself." A child whose parents backstop everything since childhood grows up not truly independent; a civilization backstopped by Gu Chensha for all sins, after ten thousand years, not truly autonomous either. **If compassion comes at the price of canceling subjectivity, it is not compassion, it is spoiling.**

Gu Chensha's "bearing sins for Myriad Ranks" is narratively extremely contagious — one person carrying the whole cosmos's sins is one of web fiction history's most tragic images. But Heng Yuan Zhou's structural analysis is unmoved by contagion: a tragic image, if at the price of canceling Myriad Ranks' subjectivity, it is tragic predation. **One person bearing sins for everyone sounds like a god; but if everyone therefore never learns to bear sins themselves, he is not a god, he is a nursemaid — and a bad nursemaid who lets all children never grow up.** What Heng Yuan Zhou wants is not "a god who bears sins for Myriad Ranks," but "a system that lets Myriad Ranks learn to bear sins themselves."

Gu Chensha's "bearing sins for Myriad Ranks" is narratively the highest-level predation — because it wears the most sacred robe. Other predators at least have the possibility of being opposed (Tang San's hunting can be opposed, Ye Fan's position can be questioned); Gu Chensha's sin-bearing almost cannot be opposed — who opposes "the one who bears sins for you"? This "unopposable predation" is the most stable of all predation forms. Heng Yuan Zhou to break it can only rely on one logic: **true compassion is letting Myriad Ranks learn to bear sins themselves, not bearing sins for them forever.**

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. "Number one in history carries for Myriad Ranks" itself unsustainable under civilizational logic
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: extremely high**

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: growth right carried away as well
- **Anti-predation**: greatest good deed = most hidden subjectivity predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least let students practice themselves; Gu Chensha does not even let students make mistakes
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Myriad Ranks gifted, not empowered
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: subjectivity not returned; cosmos is single log
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment on Gu Chensha is not that he is not good, but that he is **too good.** So good that Myriad Ranks do not need to be good; so good that the cosmos does not need to run itself. True symbiosis is not "I carry it for you," but "you have ability to carry it yourself, I carry it with you."

In the final analysis, Dragon Rune · Gu Chensha's predatory-thinking decomposition · the gift essence of bearing sins for Myriad Ranks described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
