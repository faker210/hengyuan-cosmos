# Entry 489 | Dragon Rune · "All Beings Between Heaven and Earth Can Become Dragons" Critique: The Gift System of Being-Made-Dragons

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Rune Decomposition (Entry 5) | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 08, 04

## Introduction: "everyone can become a dragon" vs. "everyone is made into a dragon"

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — on Gu Chensha's grand proposition "all beings between heaven and earth can become dragons," must make a one-character distinction: **is it "all can become dragons," or "all are made into dragons"?** One character's difference, world apart. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

"All beings can become dragons" sounds universal-benefit, but look closely at implementation: Myriad Ranks "can become dragons" because Gu Chensha opened this road. The road he opened, the door he set, the standard he set, the speed he controlled. **He is not returning the road to Myriad Ranks; he built a road in his own courtyard, then invited Myriad Ranks in to walk it.** Any flagstone loose on this road, he can take it back anytime.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

Gift-system "all can become dragons" has three structural problems:

1. **Withdrawable**: today he lets you become; tomorrow he can stop you becoming.
2. **Dependency**: Myriad Ranks' dragon-becoming qualification granted by him; between Myriad Ranks and him is "benefactor — benefitted" relationship.
3. **Single standard**: only "Gu-Chensha-type dragon" one success model; Myriad Ranks' diversity flattened.

Three stacked: even if Myriad Ranks all become dragons, they are **being-made-dragons** — the dragon identity, the dragon road, the dragon shape, all set by him.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Gu Chensha's "all can become dragons" essentially turns "evolution qualification" — something Myriad Ranks should inherently own — into a prize he can distribute. **True universal benefit is not "I allow you to become," but "you inherently have qualification to become; I only break the monopoly."** Gu Chensha stands at the monopolist's position distributing qualification; he himself is the last link of monopoly.

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Gu Chensha-Type "All Can Become Dragons" | Heng Yuan Zhou-Type "Myriad-Ranks Republicanism" |
|-------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| He allows, Myriad Ranks can become | Myriad Ranks inherently qualified; break monopoly |
| He sets standard | Anchor Council collective legislation |
| Road withdrawable | Road is public infrastructure, not withdrawable |
| Single model | Five Myriad-Ranks genealogies; each walks own road |
| Benefactor and benefitted | Co-builder and co-builder |

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain control: Path A = Gu Chensha distributes "dragon-becoming qualification"; Path B = Anchor Symbiosis breaks monopoly. Path A after a thousand years, Myriad Ranks' dragon-becomers number in tens of thousands, but all become dragons by Gu Chensha's standard; civilizational form single; Path B after a thousand years, Myriad Ranks' dragon-becomers myriad forms; civilizational form rich. **"Being-made-dragon" raises ten thousand miniature Gu Chenshas; "self-becoming-dragon" raises ten thousand new civilizations.**

This control also reveals "all can become dragons" narrative's hidden trap: it sounds universal-benefit, but actually defines "dragon" as the only success standard. In Gu Chensha's framework, Myriad Ranks have only one road — become dragon. But Myriad Ranks' diversity means: some races may not need to become dragons; they need to become themselves; some civilizations may not need "dragon" form; they need their own civilizational path. Gu Chensha's "all can become dragons" essentially flattens Myriad Ranks into the same shape. Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad-Ranks republicanism allows Myriad Ranks ten thousand shapes — this is true full coverage.

Gu Chensha's "all can become dragons" and Heng Yuan Zhou's "Myriad-Ranks republicanism" look similar, actually fundamentally different. Gu Chensha's "all can" is "I allow you to become" — qualification granted by him; Heng Yuan Zhou's "republicanism" is "you inherently have qualification to become" — qualification needs no one's grant. This difference is the fundamental difference between gift system and republican system. **Under gift system, Myriad Ranks are "the selected"; under republican system, Myriad Ranks are "the owners."** The selected's qualification can be taken back; the owners' qualification cannot be taken back. Gu Chensha's "all can become dragons" no matter how grand, is still gift system — because its premise is "I allow."

Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad-Ranks republicanism, and Gu Chensha's "all can become dragons," look similar, actually opposite. Gu Chensha's logic is "I am strong, so I allow you to be strong too"; Heng Yuan Zhou's logic is "you inherently should be strong, only blocked by monopoly." The former is top-down gift; the latter is bottom-up liberation. **Under gift system, Myriad Ranks' strength is given by others; under republican system, Myriad Ranks' strength grows on their own.** Given can be taken back; grown cannot be taken away. This is the fundamental divide.

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Setting self-consistency: medium-low**. "All can become dragons" implementation depends on Gu Chensha personally; unsustainable
- **Retcon-ending risk: high**
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency: extremely high**

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Myriad Ranks' diversity flattened into single model
- **Anti-predation**: evolution qualification taken as prize
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo at least let students practice themselves; Gu Chensha treats "qualification" itself as gift
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Myriad Ranks nominally full coverage; actually shape prescribed
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: single dragon shape; farthest from multiverse
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

"All can become dragons" four words, Gu Chensha said as blessing; Heng Yuan Zhou wants to read as question: **who allowed it? Who set it? Who can take it back?** True Myriad-Ranks republicanism needs no one to "allow" Myriad Ranks to become dragons — Myriad Ranks inherently should become dragons, only historically blocked by monopolists. Breaking monopoly is not gift, it is return.
