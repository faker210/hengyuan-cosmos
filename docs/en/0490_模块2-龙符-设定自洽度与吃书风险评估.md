# Entry 490 | Dragon Rune · Setting Self-Consistency and Retcon-Risk Assessment

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Dragon Rune Decomposition (Entry 6) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 14, 17

## Introduction: when the protagonist casually creates universes, how can the story continue

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — scores every benchmarked IP on "setting self-consistency / retcon-ending risk." The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry scores *Dragon Rune* overall.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones

Dragon Rune's combat-power inflation is top-tier in web-fiction history: Gu Chensha from human emperor all the way to Tiandao Law, sky-mending, heaven-reforming, casually creating universes. Every promotion has no explicit ceiling — since he can create universes, the next opponent must be bigger than universes. This "no-ceiling inflation" itself is setting self-consistency's terminal illness.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression

When the protagonist's power has no ceiling, the story has no "villain's reason to exist." Villains either stomped to death, or must be "monsters bigger than universes." The latter makes combat numbers meaningless; readers no longer believe any constraint.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking

Gu Chensha's victories less and less come from in-rule effort, more and more from "he is number one in history" this identity itself. This is the ultimate predation of readers' narrative trust.

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain makes *Dragon Rune* a "staged self-consistency snapshot": early ≈ medium; mid ≈ medium-low; late ≈ low. After Gu Chensha "casually creates universes," all early descriptions of "how hard cultivation is" invalidate. Conclusion: Dragon Rune is the typical case of "combat-power no-ceiling inflation."

Specifically, *Dragon Rune*'s self-consistency leakage has three nodes: first node at "Tiandao Law" establishment — protagonist from cultivator becomes the law itself; second node at "sky-mending heaven-reforming" — he from the law itself becomes the one who modifies laws; third node at "casually creating universes" — he from the law-modifier becomes the law-creator. Each node crossed, the early-established "cultivation difficulty" invalidates a layer. By the third node, readers already cannot judge "what is cultivation's meaning" — since the protagonist casually creates universes, do all those still cultivating hard have any value? This question, the original work does not answer.

Heng Yuan Zhou scores *Dragon Rune* self-consistency "low," because its inflation has reached "protagonist = law" level. When the protagonist casually creates universes, all descriptions of "cultivation" lose meaning — since cultivation's endpoint is "casually create universes," do all those still cultivating hard have any value? This question Douluo does not have (Douluo at least spirit rings still scarce), Shrouding the Heavens does not have (at least Great Emperor still has limits), Dragon Ball does not have (at least battles still have opponents). Dragon Rune pushed the protagonist to "omnipotent" position, and also abolished "cultivation" itself. **A cosmos that makes cultivation meaningless is a failed cosmos.**

Heng Yuan Zhou scores *Dragon Rune* self-consistency "low," among the Myriad-Realms lineage the lowest tier. Compare Douluo (medium-low), Shrouding (medium-low), Dragon Ball (extremely low but positioned as hot-blooded manga), Infinite Terror (medium-low), Dragon Rune's self-consistency problem is not its settings contradicting, but its protagonist already strong enough that "settings themselves lose meaning." When the protagonist casually creates universes, all descriptions of "cultivation," "realm," "breakthrough" become jokes — because readers know, the protagonist does not need these; he does as he wishes. **This is not setting self-consistency; this is setting cancellation.** Heng Yuan Zhou's law-first axiom precisely prevents this "setting cancellation" — laws once established in front, no matter how strong the protagonist, cannot exceed what laws allow.

Heng Yuan Zhou's final score on *Dragon Rune*: setting self-consistency low, retcon-ending risk extremely high, protagonist uniqueness dependency extremely high. These three "extremely high / low" constitute the Myriad-Realms lineage's most extreme case — Gu Chensha is single-log civilization's ceiling, and also Heng Yuan Zhou's critique's farthest bullseye.

Transformation plan see Entry 491. This is Heng Yuan Zhou's final diagnosis on the Dragon Rune world, and also single-log civilization's ultimate specimen.
