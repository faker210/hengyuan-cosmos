## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain runs full transformation: post-transformation first millennium, among Myriad Ranks over a thousand strong ones reach Tiandao Law threshold; Gu Chensha himself from "the sole pillar" becomes "one of thousand founders"; in a second-millennium "hypothetical fall," the cosmos runs safely — **single log becomes forest; forest is not afraid of any one tree falling.**

The transformation plan's key is not making Gu Chensha disappear, but making Gu Chensha from "the only" to "one of." He remains great — he is the first to cultivate Tiandao Law, the first to publicize Tiandao-Law understanding, the first to propose "sky-mending is engineering not feat." But he is no longer the only one. Over ten thousand new Tiandao-Law strong ones have their own understanding, own contributions, own creations. Gu Chensha's greatness is diluted by Myriad Ranks' greatness — this is not belittling him, this is fulfilling him. **A truly great person should hope his greatness is surpassed by more people, not fewer.**

The transformation plan also involves a psychological-level shift: Gu Chensha must step out of his self-identity as "number one in history." This is hardest for any strong one who reaches the peak — admitting he is not the only, admitting Myriad Ranks can reach the same height, admitting his greatness can be surpassed. But Heng Yuan Zhou believes: **true greatness is not "no one can surpass me," but "I am willing to let everyone surpass me."** If Gu Chensha can take this step, he from "single log" becomes "the starting point of the forest." He is still the first tree, but the forest will grow around him — this is far greater than him standing alone in an empty cosmos.
