Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — through per-entry decomposition of Tang Luo (252–287), Ye Fan (288–301), Tang San / Douluo, Ji Ning (406–431), Wukong (432–467), Zheng Zha (468–477), Jackie Chan (478–484), Gu Chensha (485–491), summarizes five predation modes. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. They are not moral judgments, but **structural classifications.**

Five predation modes' common waste: compressing energy that could have universally benefited Myriad Ranks into one person's or small circle's private property. The difference is only in "the way of compression."

## III. Five Predation Modes Typology

1. **Predation Mode 1: Resource-Capture Type (Tang San / Douluo)** — evolution path through slaughter, hunting, snatching spirit rings. Power comes from consuming stock.
2. **Predation Mode 2: Position-Monopoly Type (Ye Fan / Shrouding, Shi Hao / Perfect World)** — monopolize Great Emperor / Supreme position; ten-thousand years of peace surnamed "Ye" or "Shi." Power comes from institutional monopoly.
3. **Predation Mode 3: Origin-Burning Type (Zheng Zha / Infinite Terror Genetic Lock)** — press one's own life potential to trade for power. Power comes from borrowing from future self.
4. **Predation Mode 4: Zero-Sum Contest Type (Jackie Chan Adventures Twelve Talismans)** — repeatedly contest unreplicable zero-sum resources. Power comes from occupying position.
5. **Predation Mode 5: Gift-Bearing Type (Tang Luo, Gu Chensha)** — bringing civilization's key functions back to one person; bearing everything for Myriad Ranks. Power comes from outsourcing subjectivity.

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain runs "hundred-year simulations" for five modes respectively: A Resource-Capture type after a hundred years civilization battle-loss heavy; B Position-Monopoly type after a hundred years class solidification; C Origin-Burning type after a hundred years strong ones collectively premature aging; D Zero-Sum Contest type after a hundred years spinning in place; E Gift-Bearing type after a hundred years Myriad-Ranks ability regression. Heng-Yuan-Zhou-type symbiosis after a hundred years, Myriad Ranks' energy levels universally leap.

Five modes are not mutually exclusive; they often stack. Tang San's Douluo system is simultaneously A (hunting spirit beasts) and B (monopolizing spirit-master positions); Zheng Zha's Infinite Terror is simultaneously C (Genetic Lock burns life) and D (instance zero-sum); Gu Chensha's Dragon Rune is simultaneously E (gift-bearing) and B (monopolizing Tiandao Law). The heavier the stack, the more fragile civilization. What Heng Yuan Zhou needs is not eliminating one mode at a time, but fundamentally replacing the underlying logic of "predatory cultivation" — replaced with "develop increment, do not consume stock," all five modes lose their soil.

Five predation modes' deep commonality: they all understand "cultivation" as "acquisition" — acquire spirit rings, acquire position, acquire lifespan, acquire talismans, acquire gifts. Heng Yuan Zhou understands cultivation as "growth" — not acquiring external things, but developing internal abilities. This difference is fundamental: acquisition is zero-sum (you take more I take less), growth is non-zero-sum (my growth does not affect yours). Five predation modes all built on the wrong premise of "cultivation = acquisition." Heng Yuan Zhou's symbiotic cultivation is built on the correct premise of "cultivation = growth." Premise changed, all five modes invalidate.

Five predation modes' typology also has a practical use: it lets Heng Yuan Zhou when facing a new IP quickly judge "which predation modes this IP falls into." Tang San falls into A+B; Zheng Zha falls into C+D; Gu Chensha falls into E+B; Tang Luo falls into E; Ye Fan falls into B. After judging which modes, directly invoke corresponding symbiotic alternatives. **Typology is not academic classification; it is construction guide.**

Five predation modes' summarization ultimately points to a Heng Yuan Zhou core belief: **all predatory cultivation can be replaced by the same scheme — replicable, teachable, universalizable symbiotic cultivation system.** The 300 Origin cultivation systems are this scheme's concrete implementation.
