## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain runs "long-serialization self-consistency curve" for five forms respectively: all five forms' self-consistency monotonically declines with serialization length; Heng-Yuan-Zhou-type law-first self-consistency stays stable with serialization length. Conclusion: **retcon is not author writing-skill problem; it is structural problem.**

Five forms also have progressive relationship: iron-law dilution is lightest (Douluo only numerical inflation), realm collapse is medium (Shrouding late rules fail), transformation no-cap is heavier (Dragon Ball transformation layers infinitely stack), truth nesting-doll is heavier still (Infinite Terror outside box another box), no-ceiling inflation is heaviest (Dragon Rune casually creates universes). The heavier the form, the faster self-consistency collapses, the faster readers lose trust. Heng Yuan Zhou's law-first axiom precisely blocks these five forms at the source — laws once established in front, any "plot needs level-jump" impulse will be blocked by the law itself.

Five forms also have a common feature: they all make the "protagonist" untrustworthy. In Douluo the protagonist's spirit rings more and more unreasonable; in Shrouding the protagonist's realm more and more fuzzy; in Dragon Ball the protagonist's transformations more and more casual; in Infinite Terror the protagonist's truth more and more nesting; in Dragon Rune the protagonist's power more and more boundless. Readers finally no longer believe the protagonist "gets strong" — they know the protagonist only "is needed by plot to get strong." Once this distrust arises, long-form narrative loses its foundation. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants to protect is not the protagonist's strength, but readers' trust — **trust once lost, no matter how strong the protagonist is still a paper-mâché hero.**

Five forms' summarization also reveals a Heng Yuan Zhou methodology principle: **retcon is not accidental, it is inevitable.** As long as law is not first, any long-form serialization will fall into one of the five forms. The difference only is which one, how deep. Douluo falls into Form 1 (iron-law dilution), Shrouding falls into Form 2 (realm collapse), Dragon Ball falls into Form 3 (transformation no-cap), Infinite Terror falls into Form 4 (truth nesting-doll), Dragon Rune falls into Form 5 (no-ceiling inflation). Heng Yuan Zhou's law-first axiom precisely blocks these five forms at the source.

Five forms' summarization ultimately points to the same prescription: law first. As long as laws are established in front, all five forms lose their soil — because any "plot needs level-jump" impulse will be blocked by the law itself.
