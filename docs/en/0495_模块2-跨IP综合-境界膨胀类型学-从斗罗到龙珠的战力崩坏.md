# Entry 495 | Cross-IP Synthesis: Realm-Inflation Typology — Combat-Power Collapse from Douluo to Dragon Ball

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Cross-IP Synthesis (Entry 4) | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 04, 17

## Introduction: numerical inflation, from Douluo to Dragon Ball

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — has precise pathological diagnosis on "realm inflation." The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry through horizontal comparison of Douluo, Shrouding the Heavens, Dragon Ball (432–467), Dragon Rune (485–491) and other IPs, summarizes combat-power collapse's three typical paths.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones (Cross-IP Summary)

Realm inflation's essence is **numerical inflation**: the numerical ladder painstakingly built early, late watered down by protagonist halo. Early "hundred-year spirit rings scarce," late "100,000-year mass-produced"; early "combat power 1000," late "combat power 1 billion." All early reader tension late invalidates.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression (Cross-IP Summary)

When numbers lose meaning, readers no longer believe any constraint. The civilization in the story also loses realism — it is no longer a runnable world, only the protagonist's backdrop.

## III. Three Typical Paths of Realm Inflation

1. **Path 1: Resource-Devaluation Type (Douluo spirit rings)** — scarce resources mass-produced; numerical ladder watered down.
2. **Path 2: Transformation No-Cap Type (Dragon Ball)** — transformation layers infinitely stack; each stage ten times stronger than previous.
3. **Path 3: No-Ceiling Lifting Type (Dragon Rune, Shrouding late)** — protagonist no ceiling; enemy must be bigger than previous; cosmos casually created.

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Inflation Path | Heng Yuan Zhou Framework |
|----------------|--------------------------|
| Resource devaluation | 300 Origin systems; resource scarcity publicly legislated |
| Transformation no cap | Nine realms + two realms; each realm promotion goes public procedure |
| No-ceiling lifting | Plane-Transcendent four-dimensional exam; strong depends on contribution |

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain runs "thousand-year simulations" for three paths respectively: Path 1 after a thousand years spirit-ring system loses meaning; Path 2 after a thousand years transformation layers reach unimaginable; Path 3 after a thousand years protagonist becomes omnipotent god; Myriad Ranks become backdrop. Heng-Yuan-Zhou-type nine-plus-two realms after a thousand years, realm framework stable; Myriad Ranks' overall energy leaps.

Three paths' common lesion is "no external anchor." Douluo's spirit rings anchor on spirit beasts, but spirit beasts can be hunted to extinction; Dragon Ball's transformations anchor on Saiyan bloodline, but bloodline can infinitely stack via near-death revival; Dragon Rune's realms anchor on protagonist personally, but the person can infinitely become strong. Heng Yuan Zhou's nine-plus-two realms anchor on the law itself — laws precede the protagonist; whatever realm the protagonist reaches, the law already wrote that realm's rules. This "law anchoring" makes realm impossible to infinitely inflate, because the law itself does not allow it.

Three inflation paths' comparison also reveals a time law: Resource-Devaluation type (Douluo) inflates slowest, but ultimately most thorough — after a hundred years spirit-ring system completely fails; Transformation-No-Cap type (Dragon Ball) inflates fastest, because transformation layers can infinitely stack; No-Ceiling-Lifting type (Dragon Rune) inflates most fierce, because protagonist directly becomes the law itself. Heng Yuan Zhou's nine-plus-two-realms framework, by design excludes these three paths: resources do not devalue (300 systems fully public), transformations do not stack (each realm promotion goes public procedure), protagonist does not lift (Plane-Transcendent four-dimensional exam). **Realm inflation is not long-form serialization's fate, it is "no law anchoring" 's fate.**

Three inflation paths' comparison also has a practical use: it lets Heng Yuan Zhou when facing a new IP quickly judge "this IP's combat-power inflation belongs to which path." Douluo belongs to Path 1 (resource devaluation), Dragon Ball belongs to Path 2 (transformation no-cap), Dragon Rune belongs to Path 3 (no-ceiling lifting). After judging path, directly invoke corresponding law-anchoring scheme. **Inflation typology is not academic classification; it is early-warning system — identify it before inflation happens.**

Three inflation paths' summarization ultimately points to the same prescription: law anchoring. Nine-plus-two realms' fixed framework, by design excludes resource devaluation, transformation stacking, no-ceiling lifting three paths. Heng Yuan Zhou does not want to stop the protagonist getting strong, but wants the protagonist to get strong within what the law allows.

This is realm-inflation typology's final conclusion. Law anchoring is the only solution, and Heng Yuan Zhou's final prescription for all long-form cosmoses.
