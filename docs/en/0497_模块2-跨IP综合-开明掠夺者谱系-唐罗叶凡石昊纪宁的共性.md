# Entry 497 | Cross-IP Synthesis: The Enlightened-Predator Genealogy — Common Traits of Tang Luo / Ye Fan / Shi Hao / Ji Ning

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library | Series: Cross-IP Synthesis (Entry 6) | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 26, 05

## Introduction: what does the best predator look like

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — proposes the concept of "enlightened predator" (see Entry 261): they are not bad people; they sincerely do good, break old monopolies, universally benefit a part of people; but they never tore up the bottom jurisprudence and rewrote it. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry places Tang Luo (252–287), Ye Fan (288–301), Shi Hao (Perfect World), Ji Ning (406–431) four side by side, finding commonalities.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis of This System's Strong Ones (Cross-IP Summary)

Four enlightened predators' common waste: they all direct energy into "patching the old framework," not "overturning the old framework and rewriting." Tang Luo breaks aristocratic family monopoly but retains fierce-realm ceiling; Ye Fan puts down Dark Turmoil but ten-thousand years of peace surnamed Ye; Shi Hao saves the living from fire and water but the Supreme position still held by his family; Ji Ning transcends Three Realms but Three Realms order still set by him.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression (Cross-IP Summary)

Four common regression mechanisms: **they all bet order on themselves.** When they are alive, civilization looks prosperous; once they close-train / die, old structures immediately relapse.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking (Four Commonalities)

Four enlightened predators' commonalities:

1. **Break old monopoly, but retain new monopoly**: Tang Luo breaks aristocratic families, himself becomes new monopolist; Ye Fan puts down old aristocratic families, himself becomes new Ye Heavenly Emperor.
2. **Universal-benefit a part of people, but not Myriad Ranks**: Tang Luo benefits human mid-low tier; Ye Fan benefits human strong ones; Shi Hao benefits human common people; Ji Ning benefits Three Realms beings — all exclude 99% of Myriad Ranks.
3. **Build academy / build order, but not build institutions**: they built visible organizations, did not build invisible laws.
4. **Missing the "righteous rascal" step**: they are not "rascal" enough — dare not tear up old jurisprudence, dare not truly publicize the monopolized Great Dao.

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative

| Enlightened Predator | Anchor Symbiosis |
|----------------------|------------------|
| Break old monopoly leave new monopoly | Anti-monopoly in constitution |
| Universal-benefit a part | 99% Myriad-Ranks full coverage |
| Build organization not institution | Council + Court + Supervisory three-layer governance |
| Missing rascal step | Righteous rascal: tear up old jurisprudence |

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan

Quest Sediment Domain runs "absence simulation" for four respectively: Tang Luo absent → Wushuang Academy declines within ten years; Ye Fan absent → Dark Turmoil relapses within a hundred years; Shi Hao absent → Supreme system relapses within a thousand years; Ji Ning absent → Three Realms order chaos within a hundred years. Four's "enlightenment" all expire with their persons.

Four also have degree differences: Tang Luo closest to symbiosis (he at least built academy, publicized mid-low martial arts), Ye Fan next (he put down Dark Turmoil but ten-thousand years of peace surnamed Ye), Shi Hao again (he saved common people but Supreme position still held by him), Ji Ning farthest (he transcends Three Realms but Three Realms order still set by him). But no matter how great the degree difference, all four stop at the same threshold — **none of them turned himself from "the sole pillar" into "one of Myriad Ranks."** This threshold is the last step between enlightened predators and symbiotes. Cross over, it is Heng Yuan Zhou; cannot cross over, no matter how great still a single log.

Four enlightened predators' commonalities can also be summarized in one more concise sentence: **they all "did good deeds," but all "did not change institutions."** Tang Luo opened academy but did not establish constitution; Ye Fan settled peace but did not build court; Shi Hao saved common people but did not set supervision; Ji Ning transcended Three Realms but did not separate powers. Good deeds can make them loved by multitudes when alive, but institutional absence makes everything zero after they leave. What Heng Yuan Zhou needs to do is not deny the good deeds they did, but institutionalize the good deeds — let academy become constitution, let peace become court, let common people become supervision, let transcendence become power separation. **Good deeds walk with the person; institutions remain with the era.**

Four enlightened predators' genealogy also reveals a time law: the closer to modern protagonists, the more "enlightened" — Tang Luo more enlightened than Ye Fan, Ye Fan more enlightened than Shi Hao, Shi Hao more enlightened than Ji Ning. But "enlightenment" degree improvement always stays at "doing good deeds" level, never crosses into "building institutions" level. This shows web-fiction protagonists keep progressing in "goodwill," but in "structure" keep stepping in place. What Heng Yuan Zhou needs to do is upgrade this "goodwill progress" into "structural progress" — **not make the protagonist better, but make civilization not need the protagonist to be good.**

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment

- **Self-consistency law**: four's self-consistency all "medium" — self-consistent when alive; not self-consistent when absent
- **Retcon risk law**: four all accompanied by mid-late retcon
- **Protagonist uniqueness dependency**: Tang Luo / Ji Ning = high (gift-style), Ye Fan / Shi Hao = medium (oligarchic)

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: patch old framework, not overturn old framework
- **Anti-predation**: enlightenment is the gentlest predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is closest to symbiosis among four, still did not take the last step
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: four all stop at human race
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: four all still short of the last step to symbiotic cosmos
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

Enlightened predators are the best kind among predatory school. But Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment: **a "good predator" is still a predator.** Tang Luo, Ye Fan, Shi Hao, Ji Ning four, all short of the last step — turning oneself from "the sole pillar" into "one of Myriad Ranks." This step crossed out, it is Heng Yuan Zhou; cannot cross out, no matter how great still a single log.
