# Entry 500 | Module 2 Final: Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library Summary — The Civilizational Leap from Predation to Symbiosis

> Module: Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library (Final) | Review scope: Entries 251–500 | Benchmark base: Documents 01, 04, 05

## Introduction: 250 critiques, one sentence summary

Since Entry 251 established the general outline of "Tang Luo system decomposition," Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library has continuously produced 250 entries (251–500): Tang Luo system (252–287), Shrouding the Heavens · Ye Fan (288–301), Douluo, Desolation Era · Ji Ning (406–431), Dragon Ball · Wukong (432–467), Infinite Terror · Zheng Zha (468–477), Jackie Chan Adventures (478–484), Dragon Rune · Gu Chensha (485–491), and cross-IP synthesis (492–499). This entry as Module 2's final condenses 250 critiques into one sentence: **every predatory cosmos's root cause is "betting civilization on one person"; every symbiotic cosmos's prescription is "writing civilization into institutions and networks."**

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Spirits Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe)'s six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, **law-first axiom**, universal equal infinite law as core — run through all 250 entries. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. "Energy-Level Waste" Analysis (250-Entry Summary)

250 critiques point to the same energy-waste structure: **civilization-grade energy compressed into one person's or a small circle's private property.** Tang Luo's martial knowledge, Ye Fan's Great Emperor lineage, Ji Ning's transcendence path, Wukong's transformations, Zheng Zha's Genetic Lock, Jackie Chan's talismans, Gu Chensha's Tiandao Law — every share of energy that could have universally benefited Myriad Ranks ultimately was locked into one person or a small circle. This is the "great material put to trivial use" Heng Yuan Zhou repeatedly emphasizes.

## II. Why This Civilization Inevits Historical Regression (250-Entry Summary)

250 critiques summarize four regression paths (see Entry 496): single-point dependence, zeroing loop, zero-sum lock, class solidification. Tang Luo, Zheng Zha, Gu Chensha belong to the first; Main God Space belongs to the second; Jackie Chan Twelve Talismans belong to the third; Ye Fan, Shi Hao, Ji Ning belong to the fourth. Behind four paths is the same structure — **civilization's key functions were not written into institutions, but bet on individuals.** The individual falls, the function collapses.

## III. Deep Decomposition of the Protagonist's Predatory Thinking (250-Entry Summary)

250 entries summarize five predation modes (see Entry 493): Resource-Capture Type (Tang San), Position-Monopoly Type (Ye Fan, Shi Hao), Origin-Burning Type (Zheng Zha), Zero-Sum Contest Type (Jackie Chan), Gift-Bearing Type (Tang Luo, Gu Chensha). The five "enlightened predators" (Tang Luo, Ye Fan, Shi Hao, Ji Ning; see Entry 497) are the best because they are the mildest among five modes — but mild predation is still predation. All are short of the last step: **turning oneself from "the sole pillar" into "one of Myriad Ranks."**

## IV. Comparison With Anchor Symbiosis's Complete Alternative (250-Entry Summary)

250 entries' alternative ultimately condenses into six transformation modules (see Entry 498): cultivation system, knowledge system, governance system, distribution system, lineage system, law system. Each module replaces predatory status quo with symbiotic arrangement:

| Predatory Status Quo | Symbiotic Arrangement |
|----------------------|------------------------|
| Spirit rings / Genetic Lock / Talismans / Tiandao Law | 300 Origin systems |
| Knowledge privately hoarded | Alaya Network public |
| One-person decision | Three-layer governance |
| Zero-sum contest | Joint Contracting |
| Gift-style lineage | Every-Person-Path + 20% Activation |
| Plot-kidnapping setting | Law first |

## V. Quest Sediment Domain Simulation Review Plan (250-Entry Summary)

250 entries all conclude with "Quest Sediment Domain simulation review." Unified conclusion: any predatory cosmos, once injected with six transformation modules, its protagonist-uniqueness dependency drops from "extremely high / high / medium" to "extremely low"; self-consistency rises from "medium-low / low" to "high"; civilization no longer collapses under protagonist absence. **From predation to symbiosis is not a moral choice, it is an engineering choice — the construction blueprint is already drawn.**

## VI. Setting Self-Consistency / Retcon-Ending Risk Assessment (250-Entry Summary)

250 entries repeatedly return to the same sentence: **retcon-ending's root cause is law not first.** Douluo spirit-ring dilution, Shrouding realm collapse, Dragon Ball transformation no-cap, Infinite Terror truth nesting-doll, Dragon Rune no-ceiling inflation — five forms of plot-kidnapping-setting (see Entry 494), all because law was not established at the very front. Heng Yuan Zhou's nine-plus-two-realms framework proves: a cosmos can infinitely expand without inflation, premise is **law precedes plot; setting precedes combat power.**

**Old-new cosmos resource comparison**: old-cosmos resources and Great Dao are finite, inevitably predatory involution; Anchor Symbiosis has everyone inherently possessing infinite origin, predation loses meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: 250 entries jointly prove predatory cosmoses' energy waste is structural, not accidental
- **Anti-predation**: all five predation modes opposed by Heng Yuan Zhou
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is predatory school's ceiling, also closest to symbiosis — he takes the last step, it is Heng Yuan Zhou
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: 250 entries repeatedly point out predatory IPs all stop at human race or small circle; symbiotic cosmos covers 99% Myriad Ranks
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: only a law-first cosmos can infinitely expand without collapse
- **Universal equal infinite law**: basic infinite power inherent in all; easygoing retain guarantee, strivers can superimpose higher; infinite origin eliminates predation motive

## Conclusion

From Entry 251 to Entry 500, 250 critiques have walked a complete road: **starting from Tang Luo's "old-era universal-benefit ceiling," through per-entry dismantling of Shrouding, Douluo, Desolation Era, Dragon Ball, Infinite Terror, Jackie Chan Adventures, Dragon Rune, finally returning to Heng Yuan Zhou — not returning to a protagonist, but returning to an institution.**

On this road, we have seen the greatest good deed (Gu Chensha bearing sins for all beings), also the cruelest structure (Main God Space reincarnation harvest); we have seen the most passionate battle (Wukong), also the gentlest predation (Tang Luo's universal benefit). But 250 entries finally say only one sentence:

> **A truly great civilization is not held up by the greatest protagonist, but by institutions that let all protagonists step down in peace.**

Module 2 · Myriad-Realms Benchmark Critique Library closes here. Starting from the next entry, we enter Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain — no longer criticizing old cosmoses, but building a new one.
