# Entry 503 | Origin Acceleration Domain · Hundredfold Amplification of the Deduction-Iteration System

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 11, 16, 09

## Introduction: speeding up "thinking things through" a hundredfold

The Deduction-Iteration System is the first of the 300 Origin cultivation systems, ranking first among Origin Three-Basics (Document 11). Its core method is the "deduction-validation-iteration" three-ring loop, and its biggest lever is the hundredfold deduction amplification provided by Multiplication Fruit technology (Document 16). In the Origin Acceleration Domain, the two are superposed to engineering limit: cultivators no longer just individually get hundredfold amplification, but are organized into a civilization-grade deduction factory sharing acceleration resources.

Six top-level axioms remain the highest criteria in this superposition: anti-predation axiom requires hundredfold amplification come from law resonance and efficiency optimization, not devouring; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires deduction resources distributed by Joint Contracting, not a few geniuses' private property; collective ascension axiom requires all deduction results filed and shared; causal self-discipline axiom requires any interference scheme in deduction first observed, then limitedly executed; law-first axiom requires deduction ability's gear definitions stable and self-consistent, not arbitrarily raised by individual genius performance. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article focuses: where exactly is hundredfold amplification used in the Origin Acceleration Domain, and what civilization-grade outputs does it bring.

## I. Four Standard Moves of Personal-Level Hundredfold Amplification

Per Document 11, the Deduction-Iteration System's fifth realm (Origin realm) deduction ability can reach five hundredfold or more. In the Origin Acceleration Domain's 100× gear, a third-realm (Iteration realm) cultivator can stably obtain about hundredfold deduction ability; his standard moves at this point include:

1. **Multi-path parallel deduction**: simultaneously deduce three to five cultivation paths or technical routes, running the entire counterfactual chain of "what if I had chosen the other path" in consciousness, then select the optimal solution;
2. **Precision cascade**: refine energy-flow simulation from "rough trajectory" to "every energy particle's trajectory," exposing design flaws previously only discoverable via physical trial-and-error in consciousness in advance;
3. **Memory solidification**: insights produced in deduction are directly written into long-term memory, avoiding "thought it but forgot" after a hundred years of deduction;
4. **Cross-system migration**: apply deduction ability to the Life-Cycle System and Spatial-Legal Scheduling System, becoming a "meta-system" level optimizer.

These four moves at normal speed might take decades to master, but in 100× gear, a cultivator in one reality day (corresponding to a hundred days inside the Acceleration Domain) can complete the whole process from unfamiliar to proficient.

## II. Organizational Form of Civilization-Grade Deduction Factory

The Origin Acceleration Domain serves not only individuals. Its higher-level use is "civilization-grade deduction factory" — tens of thousands of deduction cultivators share the same Acceleration Domain, parallel-processing civilization-grade big problems:

| Task Type | Normal Cycle | After Hundredfold Cycle | Typical Output |
|-----------|--------------|--------------------------|----------------|
| New cultivation-technique draft | Decades | Months | 300-system expansion plan |
| New-energy tech pre-research | Centuries | Years | Law-resonance device iteration version |
| Cross-plane risk预案 | Years | Days | Crisis-response handbook |
| Civilizational error-correction simulation | Years | Days | Quest Domain simulation-review input model |

Organizationally, each deductor is responsible for a "sub-problem"; the Alaya Network automatically sutures sub-problem results. This is precisely the collective-ascension axiom's temporal expression: strong ones do not ascend alone, but converge deduction ability into a network, letting the whole civilization "think" together.

## III. Hundredfold Amplification's Boundaries: Why You Cannot Deduct Forever

Hundredfold amplification is not without cost. The Origin Acceleration Domain sets three hard boundaries:

- **Validation ring cannot be skipped**: Document 11 emphasizes "deduction-validation-iteration" three rings none dispensable. No matter how beautiful the deduction, without small-scale validation in isolated space, it does not count. This prevents "deduced for a hundred years in consciousness, deduced an empty方案 that violates real-world laws."
- **Reality-as-base cannot be broken**: all deduction must start from real-world laws (Document 09), no deduction into a metaphysical black box where "as long as setting says so, it holds."
- **Cooldown period cannot be skipped**: after continuous high-multiple deduction, must enter reality cooldown, letting consciousness-domain energy supply recover. This is anti-energy-waste self-protection, also the ethical valve preventing "deduction addiction."

## IV. Comparison With the Predatory School's "Deduction"

Predatory-school systems also have smart people, who can also deduce. But their deduction has two fatal problems: first, deduction's goal is "how to devour more, how to scheme harder"; the faster they deduce, the faster cosmic entropy increases; second, deduction results are locked into the protagonist's or sect's private vault, unusable by Myriad Ranks. Heng Yuan Zhou's hundredfold deduction is the opposite: goal is developing increment; results enter the Alaya Network for whole-network sharing; deductors earn resource returns via Joint Contracting, not by monopolizing technology and eating price spreads.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: compressing a hundred years into one year means wasting ninety fewer years of life and energy; parallel deduction lets ten thousand brains do one brain's hundred-year work
- **Anti-predation**: deduction goal is symbiosis and increment; deduction results public and shared; not oriented to slaughter and predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's deduction talent belongs only to the protagonist and his disciples; ordinary people do not even have the qualification to "be deduced about"; Heng Yuan Zhou's deduction ability is a basic cultivation technique, learnable by 99% Myriad Ranks
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: among five gene lineages, consciousness-information races naturally suit the Deduction-Iteration System; carbon-based matter races after training can also reach third realm or above
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: deduction algorithms, parallel-suturing protocols, memory-solidification technology all public; supports the full-chain innovation from Sacrificial Way to Balanced-Anchor hyper-multiverse realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Deduction-Iteration System's hundredfold amplification is the Origin Acceleration Domain's sharpest knife. But the knife's sharpness is not in how fast it can cut, but in whose hand holds it and where it cuts. In Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, this knife is held by Myriad Ranks, cutting toward "undeveloped increment" and "unsolved civilizational problems." The speed of thinking this through is a hundred times faster; the road walked after thinking it through can then truly be a hundred times faster.
