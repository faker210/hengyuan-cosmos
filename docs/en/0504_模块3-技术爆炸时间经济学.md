# Entry 504 | Origin Acceleration Domain · Time Economics of Civilizational Technological Explosion

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 16, 22, 15

## Introduction: when time becomes a resource that is priced, scheduled, and audited

"Time is money" is a metaphor in the mortal realm, but in the Origin Acceleration Domain it is a proper economics. If a cultivator spends one reality day in hundredfold flow, equivalent to living a hundred days in the Acceleration Domain, then these hundred days' consciousness energy, law-resonance share, isolated-space compute, and post-exit cooldown resources all must be priced, allocated, and audited. This is **time economics**.

Six top-level axioms are this economics's constitution: anti-predation axiom requires time resources allocated only to incremental development, not to predatory R&D; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires time resources allocated by qualification not by bloodline; collective ascension axiom requires time-investment outputs flow back to Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom requires time use not create causal overheating; law-first axiom requires time-pricing model stable and recalculable, not ad hoc. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article discusses: how the Origin Acceleration Domain makes "fast" into a civilizational business that does not waste, does not monopolize, does not drift.

## I. Time Resource Pricing Model

The Origin Acceleration Domain splits time into three priceable variables:

1. **Consciousness compute**: billed by gear multiplier. A 100× gear consciousness day's compute equals a hundred normal consciousness days' compute consumption.
2. **Law-resonance share**: resonance bandwidth occupied to maintain the Acceleration Domain's law network; apportioned by share.
3. **Isolated-space occupancy**: the Acceleration Domain is built in isolated space; space itself is a scarce resource, billed by occupied volume and duration.

Three summed, constitute the standard price of one "Acceleration-Domain consciousness day." This price is not decided by one person, but dynamically calculated by the Anchor Council based on the symbiotic network's real-time load, and publicly supervised by the Symbiotic Court. Any being, as long as earning enough "civilizational contribution" through the Joint Contracting mechanism (Document 22), can redeem corresponding acceleration time, **more work more gain, no birth-background look.**

This table's reading: **only when the "normal-cycle ÷ acceleration-cycle" multiple is large enough, and civilizational benefit positive, is it worth occupying Acceleration-Domain resources.** This is anti-energy-waste's concrete algorithm in time economics — unprofitable things, no matter how fast, are not done.

## III. Preventing "Acceleration Inflation"

The Acceleration Domain's biggest economic risk is "acceleration inflation": if everyone wants to open 1000× gear, law-resonance share will be instantly grabbed empty, prices soar, and finally only a few strong ones can afford it. Heng Yuan Zhou's countermeasure is triple:

- **Quota system**: each cultivator's annually redeemable acceleration time has an upper limit; excess requires higher contribution;
- **Priority system**: civilization-grade emergency tasks (rescue, disaster relief, risk预案) have priority over personal cultivation;
- **Cooldown system**: after high-multiple acceleration, mandatory cooldown period; during cooldown cannot open high-multiple gear again, preventing "involution-style acceleration."

This mechanism ensures the Acceleration Domain always remains civilization's public resource pool, not strong ones' private club.

## IV. Comparison With Tang Luo's "Technology Monopoly"

In the Tang Luo system, technological explosion only benefits the protagonist's lineage: new cultivation techniques, new pills, new arrays first enjoyed by the protagonist and his master's school; outer factions cannot use them for generations. Heng Yuan Zhou's time economics is the opposite: every new technology developed in the Acceleration Domain, once validated, enters the Alaya Network for whole-network opening; Myriad Ranks on the normal timeline can immediately use it. Technological-explosion dividends are not monopolized by one lineage, but eaten simultaneously by the whole civilization.

## V. Time Economics's Implications for Small Planes

For low-energy planes newly merged into the symbiotic network, this time economics has a particularly important implication: **it need not wait until it develops a complete Acceleration Domain of its own before talking about "efficiency."** Small planes can, per the standards published by the Anchor Council, first connect to the shared acceleration pool, redeeming acceleration time with technology or labor they contribute. This avoids the old-era pattern of "only strong ones qualified to research, weak ones only to be researched." Time resources, from a hoarded privilege, become a public good circulating by labor.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: input-output table ensures every bit of acceleration time spent on the highest civilizational-benefit direction; eliminates meaningless "fast for fast's sake"
- **Anti-predation**: acceleration resources come from symbiotic-network increment; pricing and allocation public and transparent; not allowed to redeem acceleration time by plundering others' contribution
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's tech dividends distributed by master-lineage and bloodline; outer factions cannot catch up for generations; Heng Yuan Zhou's tech dividends whole-network open; Myriad Ranks synchronously benefit
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five gene-lineage beings can all redeem acceleration time via contribution; plant spirits and planet consciousness's "time units" have dedicated conversion models
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: time-pricing model, quota rules, priority algorithms all public; any newly merged plane can connect per the same economics
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Technological explosion is not "faster is better," but fast where it cuts, fast in sharing, fast without waste. The Origin Acceleration Domain's time economics turns "fast" from a metaphysical talent into a civilizational engineering that can be accounted, allocated, audited. When fast itself is priced, civilization will not run itself empty in its wild sprint.
