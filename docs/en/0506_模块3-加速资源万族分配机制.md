# Entry 506 | Origin Acceleration Domain | Distribution Mechanism for Myriad-Races-Shared Acceleration Resources

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 22, 06, 94

## Introduction: fast resources — on what grounds to whom

The Origin Acceleration Domain's scarcest resource is not devices, but **acceleration time**. Law-resonance bandwidth, isolated-space compute, consciousness-domain energy supply — each is a hard constraint. If distribution is unfair, the Acceleration Domain will degenerate into strong ones' private club; the entire dual-track time domain's design becomes void. Six top-level axioms are the distribution mechanism's constitution: anti-predation axiom requires acceleration resources reward only incremental contribution, not predatory gains; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires five gene lineages, men and women, old and young, Youth Supremes and ordinary beings treated equally; collective ascension axiom requires acceleration resources tilt toward the direction of "lifting Myriad Ranks up together"; causal self-discipline axiom requires distribution process public and auditable; law-first axiom requires distribution algorithm stable and recalculable, not ad hoc favoritism. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article decomposes the Origin Acceleration Domain's distribution mechanism.

## I. Three Rulers of Distribution

Acceleration resource distribution does not look at birth, does not look at cultivation level, only at three rulers:

1. **Civilizational contribution**: accounted by the Joint Contracting mechanism (Document 22), recording the individual's incremental contribution to the symbiotic network — what was developed, how many lives saved, how many law anchors repaired;
2. **Task publicness**: the more public the task, the higher the priority. Saving a plane's disaster has priority over an individual's closed-door breakthrough;
3. **Racial coverage**: to ensure 99% full coverage, each Acceleration-Domain quota must reserve a certain proportion for historically marginalized races and individuals.

Three rulers combined constitute the "contribution—publicness—coverage" three-dimensional score. This score determines who can open which gear, for how long.

## II. Differentiated Conversion Across the Five Gene Lineages

Different races' "acceleration time" value differs; distribution must convert:

| Lineage | Time Conversion Coefficient | Note |
|---------|------------------------------|------|
| Carbon-based matter race | 1.0 | Baseline |
| Energy-condensation race | 0.7 | Consciousness naturally active; higher efficiency under same compute |
| Mechanical-construction race | 1.3 | Needs extra energy for chip computation; slightly higher conversion |
| Consciousness-information race | 0.5 | Almost pure consciousness existence; extremely high acceleration efficiency |
| Mixed-variant race | 1.0–1.5 | Case-by-case accounting by composition ratio |

Conversion coefficients ensure "high-efficiency races" do not monopolize resources because they are cheap, and "low-efficiency races" are not squeezed out of the Acceleration Domain because they are expensive. This is the republican axiom of Myriad Races's precise implementation at the distribution layer.

## III. Quotas, Rotation, and Relief

- **Base quota**: every being meeting the admission threshold gets a base acceleration time per year, ensuring everyone has some to use;
- **Contribution redemption**: exceeding the base quota redeemed with civilizational contribution; more work more gain;
- **Rotation system**: popular gears (100× and above) use queued rotation, eliminating strong ones' long-term occupation;
- **Relief channel**: planes or races facing civilization-level crisis may apply for emergency quotas, dispatched with priority after Anchor Supervisory Committee approval.

## IV. Comparison With Tang Luo's "Resource Master-Lineage System"

In the Tang Luo system, cultivation resources (including closed-door chambers, time-accelerated ley lines) are distributed by master-lineage, bloodline, contribution (to the sect); outer factions have almost no access. Heng Yuan Zhou's distribution mechanism replaces "sect loyalty" with "civilizational contribution," replaces "bloodline" with "coverage," replaces "exclusive occupation" with "rotation." A carbon-based youth from a remote plane, as long as he makes enough civilizational contribution, can queue in the same line as a Transform-Origin strong one.

## V. Grievance and Dynamic Adjustment of Distribution

Distribution is not set once. Every quarter, the Anchor Supervisory Committee reviews distribution results: if a race's actual usage long-term falls below quota, quota is too high; if a task type long-term cannot queue in, quota is too low. Review results public; Myriad-Rank representatives may propose adjustment motions at the Anchor Council. This dynamic adjustment ensures the distribution mechanism does not rigidify after a few years of operation, nor is it solidified into a new class by vested interests. Any being who believes they are unfairly excluded from a path may file a grievance with the Symbiotic Court; the Court has the right to require recalculation of their contribution and quota.

### Common Misconceptions in Distribution Clarified

During execution, three misconceptions need repeated clarification: first, "is base quota equal to egalitarianism" — no, base quota only guarantees entry rights; excess still redeemed by contribution; second, "will disadvantaged races be specially favored" — no special favoritism, but coverage correction, purpose is to make up historical arrears, not undifferentiated concession; third, "can quota be transferred" — no, acceleration time is tied to real-name identity; transfer is deemed covert trade, auto-identified by the Alaya Network. Clarifying these three is the premise for the distribution mechanism not being corrupted long-term.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: three-dimensional score directs acceleration time to the most public, highest-contribution tasks; eliminates strong ones hoarding
- **Anti-predation**: predatory gains do not count toward contribution; resources accumulated by devouring cannot redeem acceleration time
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's resources distributed by master-lineage; outer generations cannot catch up; Heng Yuan Zhou's resources distributed by contribution and coverage; newcomers and old races compete on the same field
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: differentiated conversion + base quota + relief channel, ensuring 99% races and individuals can actually enter the Acceleration Domain
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: distribution algorithm, conversion coefficients, queue rules all public; newly merged planes connect per the same rules
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Acceleration-resource distribution is the touchstone of whether the dual-track time domain truly belongs to Myriad Ranks. Three rulers — contribution, publicness, coverage — plus five gene-lineage differentiated conversion, make "fast" for the first time a public good of Myriad Ranks, not strong ones' private property.
