# Entry 507 | Origin Acceleration Domain | Alaya-Network Supervision of the Acceleration Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 21, 89, 49

## Introduction: where it is fast, there must be a tighter eye

The Origin Acceleration Domain compresses time a hundredfold, equivalent to amplifying causal bifurcations a hundredfold. In this space, any thought may evolve in consciousness into a virtual war; any deduction may create thousands of causal lines in isolated space. Without an eye fixed on it, the Acceleration Domain becomes an out-of-control explosives depot. Six top-level axioms are supervision design's highest principles: anti-predation axiom requires supervisors watch "whether anyone uses acceleration for predation"; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the supervision process itself supervised by Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires supervision serve "let Myriad Ranks rise together," not suppress innovation; causal self-discipline axiom requires supervision directly interface with time-paradox prevention; law-first axiom requires supervision rules precede, no retroactive加码 after the fact. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article describes Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's concrete operation in the Origin Acceleration Domain.

## I. Four Layers of Supervision

| Layer | Subject | Duty |
|-------|---------|------|
| Layer 1 | Anchor auto-monitoring | Real-time scan energy fluctuation, causal-bifurcation density, consciousness health |
| Layer 2 | Symbiotic Executive Committee commissioner | Handle anomaly alerts; decide downshift or pause |
| Layer 3 | Anchor Supervisory Committee | Review major acceleration tasks; approve 1000× gear use |
| Layer 4 | Symbiotic Court | Hear abuse cases; adjudicate grievances |

Four layers have intercommunication and power checks-and-balances; no single layer can alone decide a being's acceleration qualification, nor alone close an Acceleration Domain. This is fully consistent with Document 49's governance framework: legislation (Anchor Council), executive (Symbiotic Executive Committee), judiciary (Symbiotic Court), supervision (Anchor Supervisory Committee) four powers separated.

## II. What the Alaya Network Watches

The Alaya Network in the Acceleration Domain focuses on monitoring four signals:

1. **Causal-overheat signal**: whether deduction produces unauthorized interference schemes pointing to the real plane;
2. **Predatory-tendency signal**: whether deduction goals calculate "how to devour," "how to plunder";
3. **Consciousness-overload signal**: whether cultivators show consciousness disorder, memory dislocation, personality dissociation;
4. **Resource-anomaly signal**: whether anyone tries to bypass distribution mechanism, hoard acceleration time.

Any category of signal crossing threshold, auto-alert and triggers corresponding layer's review.

## III. Supervision Is Not Surveillance; It Is Protection

It needs emphasis that the Alaya Network's supervision in the Acceleration Domain is not "Big Brother" style surveillance. Its core purpose is protection:

- Protect cultivators themselves from high-multiple acceleration damaging consciousness;
- Protect the real plane from causal-bifurcation pollution;
- Protect Myriad-Races-shared resources from being occupied by a few;
- Protect the Acceleration Domain's output from being weaponized by predatory schools.

All monitoring data is used only for safety and audit, not for judging cultivators' thought tendencies, much less for political persecution. This is the republican axiom of Myriad Races's boundary in the supervision domain.

## IV. Comparison With Tang Luo's "Sect-Master One-Man Rule"

In the Tang Luo system, the time-acceleration chamber's supervision power is held by the sect master or protagonist; open when he wants, close when he wants, give to whom he wants, without any external constraint. Heng Yuan Zhou's Acceleration Domain supervision is distributed, multi-layer, grievance-able — any cultivator dissatisfied with a downshift or pause decision may appeal to the Symbiotic Court.

## V. Balance Between Supervision Data Transparency and Privacy

Most data the Alaya Network collects in the Acceleration Domain is metadata — causal-bifurcation density, energy-fluctuation curve, consciousness health indicators — these data are public to Myriad Ranks; anyone may consult a certain Acceleration Domain's operation report. But details involving a specific cultivator's personal experience are visible only to themselves and authorized mentors. This "operation data public, personal privacy protected" dichotomy is the republican axiom of Myriad Races's precise expression in the supervision domain: supervision must be visible, to prevent supervisors abusing power; privacy must be protected, to let cultivators in the Acceleration Domain think, err, grow in peace.

### Joint Conference Between Supervisors and the Supervised

Once a year, the Anchor Supervisory Committee convenes an "Acceleration-Domain User Representative Joint Conference." The meeting does not discuss technical parameters; it only listens to users: on which track did you encounter what problem, which rule do you find unreasonable, which process can be smoother. Meeting minutes public; reasonable suggestions land in next quarter's rule revision. This mechanism makes supervision not top-down command, but a pact co-shaped by the supervised. If supervisors cannot hear the supervised's voice, no matter how precise the algorithm will become disconnected bureaucracy after a few years.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: supervision auto-identifies meaningless high-multiple acceleration requests, directly rejecting, saving law-resonance bandwidth
- **Anti-predation**: predatory-tendency signal once appears immediately intervenes; no deduction of predation schemes allowed in the Acceleration Domain
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's supervision power concentrated in one sect master; Heng Yuan Zhou's supervision power four-power separated, multi-layer checks
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: monitoring treats five gene lineages equally; no green light for the strong, no barriers for the weak
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: monitoring algorithm, alert thresholds, grievance process all public; any plane connects to the same supervision standard
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The faster the place, the tighter the supervision needed; the tighter the supervision, the more it itself must also be supervised. Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision locks the Origin Acceleration Domain in a transparent cage — not to make it run slower, but to let it, when running fast, not hurt people, not drift, not become a few people's privilege.
