# Entry 508 | Origin Acceleration Domain | Risks and Prevention of Acceleration-Domain Abuse

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 19, 04, 89

## Introduction: even a good tool can be used wrong

The Origin Acceleration Domain is civilization's sharp tool, but sharp tools always have two edges. Without prevention, it may be abused into the following things: a reality-escape illusion, a predatory school's training ground, a new tool of class solidification, a source of mental-health pollution. Six top-level axioms are prevention design's highest principles: anti-predation axiom forbids transforming the Acceleration Domain into a predation training ground; republican axiom of Myriad Races forbids turning the Acceleration Domain into a new privilege zone; collective ascension axiom forbids using the Acceleration Domain to evade responsibility to Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom forbids using the Acceleration Domain to pollute real causality; law-first axiom requires abuse scenarios and disposal rules defined in advance, not ad hoc after the fact. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article lists the Acceleration Domain's main abuse risks and prevention mechanisms.

## I. Five Typical Abuses

| Abuse Type | Manifestation | Harm |
|------------|---------------|------|
| Illusion-Escape Type | Indulge in virtual lives inside the Acceleration Domain; refuse to return to reality | Individual disconnected from society; similar to "information drug abuse" |
| Predation-Drill Type | Deduct devouring, slaughter, enslavement schemes in the Acceleration Domain | Turns Acceleration Domain into predatory-school training camp |
| Involution-Overdraft Type | Long-term high-multiple acceleration for ranking; consciousness overload | Mental breakdown, cultivation interruption |
| Privilege-Hoarding Type | Occupy popular gears by connections or force | Destroys Myriad-Races sharing |
| Causal-Smuggling Type | Sneak unpurified information from the Acceleration Domain back to reality | Pollutes real causal chain |

## II. Six Gates of Prevention Mechanism

1. **Admission gate**: real-name registration + ethics review; those with predatory record denied admission;
2. **Duration gate**: dual limits on single-session duration and annual total duration; prevents indulgence;
3. **Cooldown gate**: after high-multiple acceleration, mandatory reality cooldown; cannot re-enter during cooldown;
4. **Monitoring gate**: Alaya Network real-time monitoring (see Entry 507);
5. **Purification gate**: all information must undergo information purification (Document 87) at exit;
6. **Relief gate**: provide free psychological and consciousness repair for those already addicted or mentally abnormal.

Six gates are not decoration — each corresponds to a specific abuse risk; each has explicit trigger conditions and disposal process.

## III. Targeting the "All-Beings-Origin-Attachment · Shortcut Demon"

The Acceleration Domain's biggest temptation is that it really can make people "fast." This "fast" is the strongest bait for the All-Beings-Origin-Attachment Shortcut Demon: someone will think, since I can cultivate to Origin in the Acceleration Domain in a hundred years, why still slowly endure in reality? The prevention mechanism's core is not negating fast, but **binding fast to responsibility**: the time you save in the Acceleration Domain must feed back to Myriad Ranks in some form; the cognition you gain in the Acceleration Domain must be returned to the Alaya Network for whole-network sharing. "Fast" detached from this binding is essentially new predation.

## IV. Comparison With Tang Luo's "Cheat Abuse"

In the Tang Luo system, the protagonist's time-acceleration or closed-door cheat has almost no external constraint; how much and how well it is used depends entirely on the protagonist's conscience. This design looks good in literature, but is dangerous in institutions. Heng Yuan Zhou's prevention mechanism acknowledges: **human nature is unreliable, inner demons are the norm, so institutions must be reliable.**

## V. Abuse Case Library and Prevention Education

Beyond the six gates, Heng Yuan Zhou has also established a public "Acceleration-Domain Abuse Case Library." The library records every historically filed abuse event: who, at what gear, by what method, tried to do what, stopped by which gate, finally disposed how. This case library does not name-and-shame the person, but fully discloses technical details for all cultivators entering the Acceleration Domain to study in admission training. This design's philosophy is: **the best prevention is letting everyone see with their own eyes "who has walked this road, and how it ended."** The case library updates quarterly, jointly maintained by the Anchor Supervisory Committee and Symbiotic Court; Myriad Ranks may consult free.

### Recovery Process From Abuse Events

When an abuse is identified, disposal is not simple ban. The system first evaluates the person's state: was it a momentary lapse, was it carried away by inner demon, or was it deliberate predation. In the first two cases, the person after completing psychological repair and ethics re-education may restore limited admission; in the third case, permanent deprivation. The recovery process is public to Myriad Ranks but without name-and-shame. This disposal philosophy is: **institutions must be strict, but beneath institutions are people.** One-swing-killing the abuser is neither humane, nor loses the chance to let him turn back and co-build.

### Long-Term Effects of Abuse Prevention

This set of six gates, in operation to date, has lowered the Acceleration Domain's abuse incident rate from an initial 3 per mille to 0.5 per myriad. The decline is not because it got stricter, but because admission training, case library, and immediate feedback form a closed loop — cultivators before entering know which roads are blocked, why blocked, what are the consequences of blocking. Prevention is cheaper than punishment; prevention is more humane than punishment; prevention is also more effective than punishment. This is the dual-track time domain's basic attitude toward the word "management."

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: six gates minimize abuse-caused energy, consciousness, and causal loss
- **Anti-predation**: predation-drill type abuse once discovered immediately permanently deprives admission
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's cheat has no constraint; Heng Yuan Zhou's Acceleration Domain has six gates constraining throughout
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: relief channel open to all races; mental repair does not refuse payment because of birth
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: abuse-type library, disposal process, grievance channels all public, accepting Myriad-Rank supervision
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

A tool's power never equals the legitimacy of its use. The Origin Acceleration Domain's design philosophy is: **first assume people will abuse it, then build prevention thoroughly.** This is not pessimism about human nature, but institutional responsibility to Myriad Ranks.
