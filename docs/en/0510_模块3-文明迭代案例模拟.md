# Entry 510 | Origin Acceleration Domain · Civilizational-Iteration Case Simulation in the Acceleration Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 11, 89, 75

## Introduction: use a hundred years of deduction to buy a plane's rebirth

No matter how much theory, nothing beats a real engineering case. This entry takes a typical "plane energy-upgrade" deduction task as an example, showing how the Origin Acceleration Domain compresses a civilization-grade project that normally takes decades into a few reality months, all without touching predation, without polluting causality. Six top-level axioms run through this case throughout: develop increment not predatory stock; let that plane's Myriad Ranks share the upgrade dividend; strong ones do not ascend alone but lift the whole plane; operations filed throughout, only knowledge returned; all deduction parameters stable and recalculable. The sixth, the universal equal infinite law, establishes the origin logic that everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Case Background: A Low-Energy Plane's Energy Bottleneck

Assume a carbon-based civilization plane (hereinafter Plane M) has developed to late industrial civilization; fossil energy depleted; new-energy technology long unable to break through; society begins to show internal division. If developing normally, Plane M may slide into long-term decline after two or three generations, even regressing into a predatory civilization. Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's task: without directly interfering with its internal politics, help it complete one energy iteration.

## II. Deduction Process Inside the Acceleration Domain

| Stage | Reality Duration | Inside-Acceleration-Domain Duration | Main Work |
|-------|------------------|--------------------------------------|-----------|
| Pure-observation phase | 3 days | 3 days (not accelerated) | Observe first per Document 83; map Plane M's energy structure |
| Model building | 2 days | 200 days | Use 100× gear to build digital model of Plane M's energy system |
| Scheme deduction | 5 days | 500 days | Parallel-deduce dozens of energy-upgrade routes |
| Risk filtering | 2 days | 200 days | Eliminate schemes likely to trigger social unrest or ecological collapse |
| Optimal selection | 1 day | 100 days | Select 3 most-stable schemes; hand to Plane M for self-selection |

The whole project reality duration about 13 days; accumulated inside Acceleration Domain about 1000 days (nearly three years of consciousness time).

## III. Output and Return

The Acceleration Domain does not directly send people to Plane M to do reform. Its output is three **validated technical solution packages**:

- A low-cost solar + fusion hybrid scheme;
- A medium-cost law-resonance miniaturization scheme;
- A high-cost but long-term-optimal spatial-legal-scheduling scheme.

Three schemes returned to Plane M as abstract knowledge after information purification; Plane M's own Myriad Ranks decide which to choose. Anchor Symbiotic Civilization only provides knowledge, does not choose for them — this is the embodiment of the Myriad-Ranks self-chosen-destiny mechanism (from Entry 525).

## IV. Causal Self-Check

After project completion, the Alaya Network does causal self-check on the whole process:

- Did it disturb Plane M's political direction? No; schemes only provide technology, do not designate a regime;
- Did it bring back Plane M's matter or life? No; only purified knowledge returned;
- Did it trigger a time paradox inside the Acceleration Domain? No; isolated space fully compliant throughout;
- Did it benefit the majority of beings on that plane? All three schemes are for all people, not only the upper class.

Self-check passed; project closed. Plane M over the next ten reality years self-selects a scheme, completes energy upgrade, joins the symbiotic network.

## V. Comparison With Tang Luo's "Protagonist Airdrop Reform"

In the Tang Luo system, when the protagonist encounters a backward plane, the usual approach is "let me teach you how to be human," directly shoving his own values and institutions over; the plane finally becomes the protagonist's vassal. Heng Yuan Zhou's approach is the opposite: the Acceleration Domain is only responsible for calculating the options clearly; the right to choose is returned to the plane itself. This is the joint requirement of the causal self-discipline axiom and the republican axiom of Myriad Races.

## VI. Case Replicability and Limitations

This Plane M case was written as a model not because it is perfect, but because it shows the complete dual-track collaboration process. Other planes encountering similar problems may reference this process, but cannot copy the result. Every plane's ecology, culture, energy structure differs; the Acceleration Domain's deduction must take that plane's real data as input; the Quest Domain's stress test must target that plane's specific risks. Treating process as template and result as answer is itself a violation of the reality-as-base principle. The case library's meaning is teaching people how to ask questions, not answering questions for them.

### Case Follow-Up Tracking

Ten years after Plane M joined the symbiotic network, the Anchor Council published a follow-up tracking report: that plane selected the second scheme (law-resonance miniaturization); energy upgrade went more smoothly than expected; social stability improved; Myriad-Ranks republicanism penetration rose from 30% to 70%. The report also records an unexpected finding: Plane M during upgrade spontaneously produced a small law-resonance application, which in turn contributed back to the symbiotic network. This finding confirms dual-track design's core judgment — **a civilization whose right to choose is respected does not only receive help; it also gives back to the helper.**

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: 13 reality days solved a decades-long civilizational problem; efficiency up a thousand times
- **Anti-predation**: energy schemes all based on incremental development; does not require Plane M to cede resources or submit
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo-style protagonist airdrop reform turns planes into vassals; Heng Yuan Zhou-style assistance turns planes into allies
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: three schemes all for Plane M's whole people, no favoritism to any class or ethnic group
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: whole deduction process, scheme templates, causal self-check checklist all public; other planes encountering similar problems can directly reuse
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

This case shows: the Origin Acceleration Domain's true power is not making one person strong, but letting an entire civilization, without being disturbed, find its own upgrade path. Fast is to shorten waiting; respect is to let the right to choose stay where it is. This is what civilization-grade iteration should look like.

In the final analysis, the civilizational-iteration case simulation described in this document is no isolated case — law self-consistency is backed by the law-first axiom and does not drift with narrative convenience.
