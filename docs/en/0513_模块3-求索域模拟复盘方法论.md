# Entry 513 | Quest Sediment Domain | Methodology of Quest-Domain Simulation Review

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 84, 87, 89

## Introduction: turn "the detours walked" into Myriad Ranks' textbooks

The Quest Sediment Domain serves not only individual trials; it also has a grander function — **Quest-Domain Simulation Review**. The so-called simulation review is: re-enact in a controlled environment failures, mistakes, disasters that have historically happened (including other planes, other civilizations, other timelines), letting contemporary cultivators experience as "participants," extracting lessons from it. This is the causal self-discipline axiom's core engineering of "thoroughly eradicating civilizational regression and historical reversal." Six top-level axioms run through this methodology: anti-predation axiom requires review not schadenfreude; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires lessons face Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires turning individual lessons into collective textbooks; causal self-discipline axiom requires review strictly isolated, not disturbing real history; law-first axiom requires review process stable and recalculable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article decomposes the Quest-Domain simulation review's standard methodology.

## I. Five-Step Review Process

| Step | Name | Key Action | Time Share |
|------|------|------------|------------|
| 1 | Source Purification | Strip emotional color and partisan bias from historical records; keep only factual skeleton | 10% |
| 2 | Environment Reconstruction | High-fidelity reconstruct the natural, social, psychological environment of the time in isolated space | 20% |
| 3 | Participant Immersion | Let trainees enter as the person concerned, facing the same choices | 50% |
| 4 | Difference Recording | Record all differences between trainee and historical real choice | 10% |
| 5 | Lesson Distillation | Write differences and results as "lesson cards," file into Alaya Network | 10% |

Five steps walked, a segment of history turns from "a story of the past" into "a textbook Myriad Ranks can learn from."

## II. Why Review Must Be Done in the Quest Domain, Not the Acceleration Domain

Review needs slow. Three reasons:

- **Emotional immersion needs time**: only at near-real flow rate can trainees truly feel the pressure, fear, temptation of the time; fast-forward will hollow out these feelings;
- **Causal density needs unfolding**: historical events' causal chains are long; fast-forward will compress intermediate links, making lessons shallow;
- **Risk isolation needs calm**: review is reprocessing of history; must have a slow, unemotional observer present, ensuring historical mistakes are not repeated.

This is why Quest-Domain simulation review is written into dual-track design — it is not the Acceleration Domain's byproduct, but an independent procedure that must be done slowly.

## III. Ethical Boundaries of Review

Simulation review is not taking historical suffering as entertainment. It has strict ethical boundaries:

- Reviews involving real deceased must pass their civilization's ethics review;
- Trainees may not in review impose new harmful dramatization on "historical figures";
- Review conclusions may not be used to provide legitimacy for invasion, predation, revenge;
- All review records open to Myriad Ranks, but original details desensitized as needed.

## IV. Comparison With "Historical Nihilism"

Predatory-school systems' attitude toward history is usually two kinds: either treat history as success-study material ("how the protagonist endured back then"), or treat history as nonexistent ("anyway my cheat is the biggest"). Heng Yuan Zhou's Quest-Domain simulation review is the opposite: treat history as teacher, failures as textbooks, detours as signposts. This is the causal self-discipline axiom's methodological promise of "thoroughly eradicating civilizational regression."

## V. Connection Between Review and Reality Teaching

Lesson cards produced by Quest-Domain simulation review are not left to gather dust in a warehouse. They are systematically incorporated into the symbiotic education system (Document 37): Sense-Origin students learn basic cases; Transform-Origin students learn complex cases; Boundary-Breaking students personally participate in constructing new cases. A lesson learned from review is far more effective than personally making a mistake in reality. This education method of "first learn others' mistakes, then walk one's own road" is the causal self-discipline axiom's implementation at the civilizational inheritance layer — Myriad Ranks need not reinvent lessons from their own blood and tears every generation.

### Review-Conclusion Update Mechanism

History does not end, and review conclusions are not final. Every epoch, the Anchor Council re-examines the classic case library: conclusions then thought "correct," under new laws and new cognition, do they still hold? For example, an early case judged "centralized power" as necessarily wrong; later cross-plane comparison found centralized power under specific ecologies is actually more stable. This self-renewal keeps the review library from becoming dogma, but living knowledge. The law-first axiom here is embodied: review conclusions themselves are also bound by law, may be corrected by new evidence.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: using one review to avoid Myriad Ranks repeating old mistakes is the most efficient energy saving
- **Anti-predation**: review explicitly shows predatory civilizations' inevitable end, letting trainees understand its absurdity from inside
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's protagonist rarely reviews his own mistakes; supporting roles' failures only set off the protagonist; Heng Yuan Zhou writes review into institutions
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: review case library covers Myriad Ranks' five gene-lineages' failure lessons, not limited to human race
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: five-step process, ethical boundaries, lesson-card format all public; any plane can organize its own review
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

History does not simply repeat, but it rhymes. Quest-Domain simulation review tells Myriad Ranks about the "rhyming" in advance, so every generation need not reinvent lessons from their own blood and tears. This is slow's greatest contribution to civilization — it does not directly produce power, but it lets power no longer be wasted on mistakes already made.
