# Entry 514 | Quest Sediment Domain | Process of Bringing Old Myriad-Realms Systems Into the Quest Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 30, 84, 88

## Introduction: turn the old world's stories into the new world's textbooks

Module 2 (252–500) systematically benchmarked old Myriad-Realms systems — Douluo, Shrouding the Heavens, Perfect World, Desolation Era, Dragon Ball, Infinite Terror, and others. These old systems ran in their own narratives for tens of thousands of years, accumulating a great many cultivators, a great many conflicts, a great many failure cases. Heng Yuan Zhou does not destroy these old worlds, but **brings them into the Quest Sediment Domain** as the material library of the civilizational error-correction project. Six top-level axioms are this process's highest principles: anti-predation axiom does not treat old-system beings as "NPCs" to consume; republican axiom of Myriad Races acknowledges old-system beings likewise qualify for evolution; collective ascension axiom requires turning old-system lessons into new-system nutrients; causal self-discipline axiom requires the inclusion process strictly follow cross-plane ethics; law-first axiom requires the inclusion process standardized and recalculable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article describes the standard process of bringing old Myriad-Realms systems into the Quest Domain.

## I. Five Stages of Inclusion

| Stage | Name | Main Work |
|-------|------|-----------|
| 1 | Identification and Filing | Lock target old-system plane; complete archaeological filing per Document 84 |
| 2 | Pure-Observation Phase | Observe first per Document 83; do not interfere with its internal causality |
| 3 | Voluntary Contact | Send invitation to that plane's representative civilization; it chooses for itself whether to be included |
| 4 | Instance Construction | Use Copy Mirror and isolated space to build a Quest instance, completely isolated from the original plane |
| 5 | Review and Feedback | Myriad Ranks simulate review in the instance; lessons distilled and returned to the original plane (with its consent) |

The whole process's keys are **voluntary** and **isolation**: old-system beings are not forcibly dragged into the Quest Domain; all deduction inside the instance does not affect the original plane's real causality.

## II. Differentiated Treatment of Three Types of Old Systems

Not all old systems are included the same way:

- **Still-running old systems**: only observation and knowledge return; do not build instances, avoiding dual timelines;
- **Already-collapsed old systems**: build complete Quest instances, for Myriad Ranks to deeply review collapse causes;
- **Fictional prototype planes** (e.g., from novel worlds): per Document 09's "reality-as-base" principle, included as thought-experiment material; not granted real-being status.

Differentiated treatment avoids "one-size-fits-all" crudeness, also avoids mixing fiction with reality.

## III. What Is Produced After Inclusion

After old systems are brought into the Quest Domain, they mainly produce three kinds of assets:

1. **Failure case library**: typical predation, collapse, historical-reversal cases in that system, for Myriad Ranks to learn;
2. **Reusable technical points**: that system's truly valuable cultivation techniques, arrays, social structures, after depredationization, incorporated into the 300-system expansion;
3. **Character growth samples**: representative figures in it (whether righteous or evil) as psychological and ethical education cases.

## IV. Comparison With the "Destroy the Old World" Approach

Predatory school's attitude toward old worlds is "if not submissive, destroy." Heng Yuan Zhou's attitude is the opposite: **the old world is not the enemy; it is the teacher.** Even a predatory old system, its failure itself is the most precious textbook. Destroy it, and the lesson disappears; bring it into the Quest Domain, and the lesson can be repeatedly learned by Myriad Ranks. This is the collective-ascension axiom's expression in the historical dimension — the strong do not erase the past, but lift the past into the future as well.

## V. Exit Right in the Inclusion Process

Once an old system is brought into the Quest Domain, it is not unable to exit. That plane's civilizational community at any stage may request "suspend inclusion"; the Anchor Council must respond within three reality months, and may not impose any resource or technical punishment on that plane for it. After exit, that plane may still maintain ordinary diplomatic relations with the symbiotic network. The exit-right design is to prevent "inclusion" from becoming another form of conquest — we invite you in as textbook, but you may say no at any time; if you say no, we still respect you.

### Cultural Protection During Inclusion

Old systems have their own literature, rituals, art. When brought into the Quest Domain, these cultural products are not cleared as "backward remnants," but completely copied into the instance, as part of that civilization's spiritual world for Myriad Ranks to learn. A predatory civilization's epic, even if its values are wrong, its artistic structure, emotional expression, worldview imagination still have aesthetic and cognitive value. Cultural protection makes inclusion not assimilation, but coexistence — we learn your lessons, but do not destroy your beauty.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the massive causality and experience old systems accumulated are not wasted; converted into civilizational textbooks
- **Anti-predation**: the inclusion process itself is anti-predation — does not draw their energy, seize their resources, force their transformation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo-style old-system protagonists tend to completely subdue or destroy divergent planes; Heng Yuan Zhou brings divergent planes in for learning
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: all beings in old systems — human race, exotic beasts, gods, planet consciousness — in the Quest Domain have a respected position
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: five-stage process, three differentiated treatments, instance-construction standards all public; supports infinite expansion
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The old world should not be buried, but read. The Quest Sediment Domain turns old Myriad-Realms systems into civilization's "off-campus classroom," letting Myriad Ranks, without paying a real price, finish the textbook others wrote with blood. This is the causal self-discipline axiom's gentlest yet most forceful implementation.

In the final analysis, the process of bringing old systems into the Quest Domain described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
