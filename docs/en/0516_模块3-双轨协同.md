# Entry 516 | Quest Sediment Domain | Dual-Track Collaboration Between the Quest Domain and the Acceleration Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Entry 501, Documents 25, 21

## Introduction: two tracks, one bloodline

The Origin Acceleration Domain and the Quest Sediment Domain are not two isolated places, but two organs on the same bloodline. If they each did their own thing, the Acceleration Domain would become an idle fantasy lab, and the Quest Domain would become a nostalgic nursing home. What really makes dual-track design work is the continuous collaboration between them. Six top-level axioms are the highest principles of collaborative design: anti-predation axiom requires data flow between the two tracks only to travel incrementally; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires both tracks' outputs face Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires both tracks' fruits feed each other; causal self-discipline axiom requires information between the two tracks strictly purified; law-first axiom requires collaboration protocols stable and recalculable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article decomposes the dual-track collaboration's information flow and energy flow.

## I. Three Main Information Flows of Collaboration

| Information Flow | Direction | Content | Frequency |
|------------------|-----------|---------|-----------|
| Scheme Flow | Acceleration Domain → Quest Domain | Deducted technical schemes, cultivation-technique drafts, engineering designs | Continuous |
| Lesson Flow | Quest Domain → Acceleration Domain | Reviewed failure cases, risk warnings, counterintuitive experience | Continuous |
| Archive Flow | Bidirectional | Epic archives, case library, individual growth curves | Monthly summary |

Three information flows are uniformly scheduled through the Alaya Network; all cross-track information undergoes information purification (Document 87 "only knowledge is returned"), without matter, energy, or life-form.

## II. Typical Work Cycle of Collaboration

A complete dual-track collaboration cycle usually looks like this:

1. The Acceleration Domain quickly generates a new scheme using hundredfold deduction;
2. The scheme is sent to the Quest Domain, "stress-tested" in a slow environment — let it run through simulated real society, real ecology, real human feelings;
3. The Quest Domain finds side effects the Acceleration Domain did not see, feeds lessons back;
4. The Acceleration Domain quickly revises the scheme based on lessons;
5. The revised scheme returns to the Quest Domain for validation;
6. Only after passing does it enter the real plane.

This cycle takes in both "fast" and "slow" advantages simultaneously: the Acceleration Domain is responsible for quickly spreading out possibilities; the Quest Domain is responsible for carefully screening possibilities. Without the former, civilization is too slow; without the latter, civilization is too brittle.

## III. Collaboration's Feedback Effect

Dual-track collaboration also has a hidden output: **it makes individual growth dual-track as well.** A cultivator who studied technology for ten years in the Acceleration Domain must spend a period in the Quest Domain, "growing" technology into his personality; conversely, a cultivator who sedimented for ten years in the Quest Domain, returning to the Acceleration Domain often can propose entirely new perspectives, because his cognitive thickness is something pure accelerators do not have. This two-way nourishment is something single-track civilization can never give.

## IV. Comparison With "Single-Track Acceleration" or "Single-Track Asceticism"

Single-track acceleration civilization has advanced technology but restless hearts; strong ones are hollow. Single-track asceticism civilization has thick hearts but stagnant technology; forever spinning in place. Heng Yuan Zhou's dual-track collaboration packs two civilizations' strengths into the same system, mutually correcting, mutually feeding. This is not compromise, but design.

## V. Collaboration's Failure Modes and Prevention

Dual-track collaboration is not error-free. Common failure modes are three: Acceleration-Domain output schemes pushed to reality before the Quest Domain has time to validate; Quest-Domain sedimented lessons not timely returned to the Acceleration Domain, causing the same mistakes to repeat in the next round of deduction; information purification between the two tracks incomplete, bringing things over that should not be brought. Against these three failures, the Alaya Network has set triple insurance: schemes must be signed off by the Quest Domain before returning to reality; lesson-return latency exceeding threshold auto-alerts; information purification reviewed by an independent second agency. Collaborative design acknowledges "people make mistakes," so insurance is thorough.

### Cost Accounting of Dual-Track Collaboration

Dual-track collaboration is not free. Data transmission, information purification, scheme sign-off between the two tracks all consume law-network bandwidth. The Anchor Council annually accounts collaboration costs, compares with collaboration benefits. Data shows: collaboration costs about 15% of dual-track total energy consumption, but it avoids the trial-and-error cost of directly pushing immature schemes to reality — the latter in old systems usually accounts for over 40% of total energy consumption. This account makes dual-track collaboration not a philosophical slogan, but an economic account that adds up.

### Dual-Track Collaboration and Civilizational Speed

Dual-track collaboration's final effect is not to make civilization run ten times faster, but to make civilization run ten times steadier. A civilization relying only on the Acceleration Domain can have a technological explosion in ten years, but collapse in twenty because it did not see side effects; a civilization relying only on the Quest Domain may not even take its first step in twenty years. Dual-track collaboration lets civilization, after completing a technological explosion in ten years, still have time for the Quest Domain to see side effects clearly, feed lessons back, revise schemes — then take the next step. Fast and steady, neither dispensable.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: schemes spread out in the Acceleration Domain, finely screened in the Quest Domain, avoiding directly pushing immature schemes to reality for trial-and-error
- **Anti-predation**: dual-track collaboration itself is a symbiotic mechanism — the fast do not look down on the slow; the slow do not envy the fast
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang Luo system either closed-door asceticism (single slow) or cheat burst (single fast), no collaboration; Heng Yuan Zhou institutionalizes both
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five gene-lineage beings may freely switch between the two tracks, choosing by their own rhythm
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: collaboration protocols, information-purification process, cycle standards all public
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The essence of dual-track design is not "having two domains," but "there is a living cycle between the two domains." The Acceleration Domain is the brain; the Quest Domain is the heart; the brain is responsible for thinking; the heart is responsible for feeling. A civilization with brain but no heart is cold; with heart but no brain is slow. Heng Yuan Zhou's dual-track collaboration gives civilization both brain and heart.
