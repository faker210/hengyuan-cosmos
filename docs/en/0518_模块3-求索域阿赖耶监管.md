# Entry 518 | Quest Sediment Domain | Alaya-Network Supervision of the Quest Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 21, 19, 49

## Introduction: the slow place also needs a gentle eye

If the Origin Acceleration Domain needs "tight" supervision (because it is fast, hot, causally dense), the Quest Sediment Domain needs "gentle" supervision. Its risk is not out-of-control causal explosion, but the individual being crushed by real pain in slow trials, trapped by indulgent illusions, harmed by deliberately manufactured suffering. Six top-level axioms are this gentle supervision's highest principles: anti-predation axiom forbids abusing the name of "trial" to mistreat the individual; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires supervision treat each individual equally; collective ascension axiom requires supervision serve growth not suppression; causal self-discipline axiom requires the trial environment not break real causality; law-first axiom requires supervision rules stable and unchanging. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article describes the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's operation in the Quest Sediment Domain.

## I. Four Focuses of Supervision

Quest-Domain supervision's focus differs from Acceleration-Domain supervision; it watches four things:

1. **Mental health degree**: monitor the trialist's emotional fluctuation, dissociation tendency, trauma response;
2. **Trial dosage**: check whether the current trial exceeds the individual's tolerance threshold;
3. **Voluntariness**: confirm the trialist can exit at any time, not forced by environment or others;
4. **Real-world anchoring**: ensure the trialist's connection to the real world is not broken.

Any item abnormal, the supervision layer has the right to immediately suspend the current trial and start psychological repair.

## II. Four-Layer Supervision Structure

| Layer | Subject | Duty |
|-------|---------|------|
| Layer 1 | Alaya auto-monitoring | Real-time scan psychological signals, trial dosage, voluntariness |
| Layer 2 | Senior trial mentor | One-on-one companionship; perceive details auto-monitoring cannot see |
| Layer 3 | Anchor Supervisory Committee | Approve high-intensity trial scenarios; accept grievances |
| Layer 4 | Symbiotic Court | Hear "trial harm" type cases |

The relationship between four layers is the same as the Acceleration Domain (see Entry 507), but the temperature differs — the Quest Domain's mentor is not a police, but a sparring partner.

## III. Exit Mechanism: Leave at Any Time

The Quest Domain's most critical supervisory design is **unconditional exit right**: any trialist, at any stage, without reason, may exit immediately. After exit the system automatically does a psychological reset, buffering overly strong emotional shocks from the trial, then sends back to reality. This fundamentally contrasts with predatory-style trials that "must clear the instance to leave" (common in Tang Luo systems).

The exit right is the republican axiom of Myriad Races's last guardrail in the Quest Domain: **no trial is worth exchanging for a being's mental health.**

## IV. Comparison With "Forced Asceticism"

Predatory-school systems often wrap the torment of disciples with "how to see rainbows without experiencing wind and rain" — severing meridians, throwing into forbidden lands, forcing into desperate straits. Heng Yuan Zhou's Quest Domain acknowledges the value of wind and rain, but refuses to manufacture wind and rain: wind and rain can be part of the trial, but dosage must be jointly decided by the individual and mentor, not unilaterally imposed by a lofty strong one.

## V. Supervisors Themselves Being Supervised

The Quest Domain's supervisors — whether the auto system, mentors, or committee — themselves are also being supervised. The Alaya Network monitors the statistical features of supervisory behavior: whether a certain mentor always tends to suspend trials, whether a certain committee always rejects exit applications, whether a certain type of case in the Symbiotic Court is systematically treated lightly or heavily. Any anomaly triggers independent investigation. This "supervisors also supervised" design is the republican axiom of Myriad Races's ultimate expression at the governance layer — no role can stand outside the rules, even if nominally he is there to protect others.

### Long-Term Support for Quest-Domain Psychological Repair

For individuals who suffered shocks in trials, after exiting the Quest Domain the system provides three reality years of long-term psychological support, not a one-time reset. During three years, he may at any time revisit a psychological counselor, retrieve trial memories for reprocessing, and get pre-check before re-entering the Quest Domain. This long-term support acknowledges: trauma cannot disappear by pressing a button at exit; growth needs continued care.

### Ethical Training for Quest-Domain Supervisors

Everyone who serves as a mentor in the Quest Domain must first complete one "being-trialed" — he himself must in a controlled environment experience a complete growth curve, feel what it is like to be supervised, protected, suspended. This training ensures mentors are not looking down at people from above, but people who have walked the same road. If a supervisor has never been supervised, his supervision easily becomes control; only if the supervisor himself has been supervised does he know where the boundary lies.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: timely suspend inappropriate trials, avoiding long-term repair cost after individual mental collapse
- **Anti-predation**: abuse in the name of "trial" explicitly forbidden; supervision layer has the right to intervene
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo master throwing disciples into forbidden lands is "cultivation"; Heng Yuan Zhou this behavior will be filed
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: psychological repair services open to all races, adapted by their spiritual structure
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: monitoring indicators, trial-dosage standards, exit process all public
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The fast place needs tight supervision; the slow place needs gentle protection. The Quest Sediment Domain's Alaya Network is not to control trialists, but to ensure as they slow down they are not submerged, not harmed, not forced. Only protected slowness is true slowness; only voluntary trials are true growth.
