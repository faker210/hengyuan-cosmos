# Entry 520 | Youth Supreme Growth Subplot · Ethical Boundaries of Trial Design

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 19, 18, 08

## Introduction: trials can be designed, but not abused

A Youth Supreme's growth usually needs trials — loss, setback, temptation, betrayal — these are all necessary material for personality growth. But "necessary" does not mean "the more the better," much less "the harsher the better." Predatory-school systems often romanticize trials, even treating tormenting disciples as "cultivation." Heng Yuan Zhou in the dual route explicitly draws ethical boundaries for trial design. Six top-level axioms are these boundaries' highest basis: anti-predation axiom forbids using the name of trial to predate the Youth Supreme's spirit; republican axiom of Myriad Races acknowledges the Youth Supreme's personality independence; collective ascension axiom requires trials serve growth not suppression; causal self-discipline axiom requires the trial environment controlled; law-first axiom requires boundaries stable and unchanging. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Four Ethical Red Lines

1. **Do not touch the bottom line of life**: do not design scenarios that truly cause death, disability, or permanent psychological trauma;
2. **Do not break real relationships**: trials may not target the Youth Supreme's real friends and family; may not exploit real relatives' deaths;
3. **Do not force**: the Youth Supreme has the right to refuse, exit, choose an alternative for the current trial;
4. **Do not humiliate**: trials may test will, may not publicly humiliate personality.

Four red lines guarded throughout by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision; any one breached, the trial immediately suspended, designer held accountable.

## II. Trial-Dosage Tiering

| Level | Content | Applicable Object |
|-------|---------|-------------------|
| Mild | Failure, rejection, small setbacks | All Youth Supremes' basic trials |
| Moderate | Losing virtual comrades, major decision mistakes | Transform Origin realm and above |
| Severe | Personally experiencing virtual civilization collapse, facing powerful temptation | Dao Origin realm and above, requires approval |
| Forbidden | Real relative harm, permanent trauma | Forbidden at any level |

Dosage tiering ensures trials match the growth stage — not letting a Sense-Origin youth experience Origin-realm-level psychological shock.

## III. Comparison With "Ascetic-Style Cultivation"

In the Tang Luo system, a master throws disciples into death forbidden lands, severs their meridians, lets them suffer humiliation, then euphemistically calls it "tempering the Dao heart." This design is cool in literature, a disaster in ethics. Heng Yuan Zhou explicitly opposes: **a true Dao heart does not need to be exchanged for with real suffering.** Trials in the Quest Domain are controlled, exit-able, backed up; their design purpose is to let the Youth Supreme grow resilience, not grind him into dust.

## IV. Trial-Dosage Review and Grievance

Trial-dosage tiering is not the mentor's call alone. Each time before designing a moderate-or-above trial, the scheme must be reviewed by the Anchor Supervisory Committee; if the trialist in the process feels dosage exceeds standard, he may at any time submit a "dosage review request" to the Alaya Network; an independent group, without interrupting the trial, reassesses whether the current scenario crossed a line. The trialist may also directly exercise the exit right, no reason needed. This review and grievance mechanism ensures "trial design" does not slide into "torment" during execution — institutions acknowledge that people who design trials may also misjudge, so a second pair of eyes is required.

### Cultural Relativity of Trial Design

What counts as "excessive trial" differs across races and cultures. A plant spirit may take "no sunlight for a quarter" as an endurable test, while a consciousness-information individual grown in warm water may experience the same as trauma. Trial-dosage standards are not human-centric; they are within each race jointly formulated by that race's representatives and psychological experts, then filed with the Anchor Supervisory Committee. In cross-race trials, dosage is calibrated by "the lowest-tolerating side." This relativity makes ethical boundaries truly enforceable, not empty words.

### Distinguishing Designed Trials From Real Trauma

The Quest Domain's controlled trials and real-world real trauma events have a boundary that must be strictly kept: the former is designed, exit-able, backed up; the latter is sudden, uncontrollable, with no one to catch. The trialist before entering the Quest Domain must understand this — the trials here are not the kind of suffering you encounter in reality, but a carefully packaged training. Confusing the two either underestimates the trial's value, or underestimates reality's cruelty. Distinguishing clearly is the mentor's first lesson.

Trial-design ethical boundaries are not slogans on the wall, but implemented on the signature sheet of every trial scheme — designer, reviewer, supervisor three-way signature; missing one cannot start. This process looks cumbersome, but makes "whether excessive" traceable and on record.

In the final analysis, trial-design ethical boundaries are not to protect the Youth Supreme from being hurt, but to protect him from being harmed. Injury is the price of growth; harm is institutional failure. Distinguishing the two is this design's entire meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: right-dosage trials are most efficient; excessive trials only consume repair resources
- **Anti-predation**: do not use the name of "cultivation" to predate the Youth Supreme's mental health
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo master's "tempering" often crosses lines; Heng Yuan Zhou has explicit red lines
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five gene-lineage Youth Supremes' "tolerance thresholds" differ; dosage adapted by lineage
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: four red lines, four dosage levels, approval process all public
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Trials are the material of growth, not the purpose of growth. Locking trials within ethical boundaries, the Youth Supreme can in a safe environment grow true resilience — rather than growing a body of abuse-trained bile.
