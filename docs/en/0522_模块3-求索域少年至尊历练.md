# Entry 522 | Youth Supreme Growth Subplot | Youth Supreme Trials in the Quest Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Entries 511, 517, 520

## Introduction: the fast person most needs to slow down

A Youth Supreme in the Acceleration Domain runs at several hundredfold speed; his power grows fast, but his personality, emotion, sense of responsibility cannot keep up. If not "ballasted" in the Quest Domain, they at high positions become the most dangerous kind of strong ones — with power, no weight. Six top-level axioms run through this entry: anti-predation axiom does not use the name of "for his own good" to force him to bear excessive trials; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires him in the Quest Domain get along with Myriad Ranks as an ordinary member; collective ascension axiom requires him after slowing down understand Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom requires the trial environment controlled; law-first axiom requires trial design stable and unchanging. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Three Types of Youth Supreme Trials in the Quest Domain

| Type | Content | Goal |
|------|---------|------|
| Common-Life Type | Live as an ordinary person in a virtual town for months | Experience ordinary beings' daily life |
| Collaborative Breakthrough Type | Team with ordinary cultivators on a task not depending on personal talent | Learn to rely on others |
| Failure-Setback Type | Let him repeatedly fail in fields he is not good at | Grind off "genius disease" |

Three trial types alternate, letting the Youth Supreme step out of the "I am the strongest" self-perception, becoming "one of Myriad Ranks" again.

## II. "De-Talenting" Design in Trials

After a Youth Supreme enters the Quest Domain, the system will **temporarily shield his realm advantage** — his speed, strength, perception all reduced to ordinary level. Not permanent deprivation, but during the trial let him re-experience "slow" and "weak." This design is the dual route's key: a person who has lived in talent advantage his whole life cannot truly understand the hardship of all beings.

## III. Rhythm Coordination With Accelerated Cultivation

A Youth Supreme's growth rhythm roughly is:

- Each completion of a stage in the Acceleration Domain (about 1–3 reality months), must complete a corresponding trial in the Quest Domain (about 1–3 reality months);
- Trial not ended, not allowed to enter the next acceleration stage;
- Trial report reviewed by peers, not mentor's call alone.

This mandatory alternation institutionally binds "fast" and "thick" together.

## IV. Comparison With Tang Luo's "Protagonist Incognito Visit"

In the Tang Luo system, the protagonist occasionally also makes "incognito visits," but essentially it is a plot the author arranged — he can at any moment show his identity and crush everything; finally this experience is used to set off his "benevolence." Heng Yuan Zhou's de-talenting trials truly bring him down, let him live among ordinary people, no back doors. This is not performance, it is training.

## V. Return After De-Talenting Trials

When a Youth Supreme returns from the Quest Domain to reality, the system does a "talent restoration" — gradually restoring the temporarily shielded realm advantage, not one-button opening. This transition process is itself part of the trial: let him re-acclimate to his power, but not allow him to immediately return to the old mode of "everything solved by talent." In the first three months after return, mentors deliberately arrange for him to handle some problems in ordinary ways, confirming he really brought the Quest Domain insights into reality, not forgetting as soon as out of the instance. This transition design prevents the split of "trial is trial, reality is reality."

### Companions' Source in De-Talenting Trials

The companions a Youth Supreme meets in de-talenting trials are not system-generated NPCs, but ordinary cultivators who signed up from Myriad Ranks — they are also receiving their own trials, just happened to be queued into the same scene as this Youth Supreme. This real encounter is the trial's key: the Youth Supreme is not talking to thin air; he is getting along with real beings with their own lives and purposes. This design makes "learn to collaborate with others" not a slogan, but something happening every day.

### Impact of De-Talenting Trials on the Youth Supreme's Family

A Youth Supreme undergoing long-term Quest-Domain trials, his family, friends, fellow disciples will also be affected. The system will in advance inform his relationship network: he has not disappeared, he is on a special trial; during the trial, do not encourage everyone to go to the Acceleration Domain to find him; after the trial he will return. This communication makes de-talenting not just the Youth Supreme's own matter, but an experience his surrounding relationship network shares. Respecting these relationships is also part of the trial.

The deepest meaning of de-talenting trials is letting the Youth Supreme upon leaving the instance understand: that he has come this far is not entirely because of himself. This understanding, more than any cultivation technique, prevents him from in the future becoming a strong one Myriad Ranks fear.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: temporarily put away the Youth Supreme's talent advantage, avoiding him always using talent to solve every problem
- **Anti-predation**: do not use "genius" identity to predate ordinary cultivators' dignity
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo protagonist's "experience the people's livelihood" is performance; Heng Yuan Zhou Youth Supreme's trial is real trial
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: companions he meets in the Quest Domain come from five gene lineages, not author-arranged tools
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: three trial templates, de-talenting parameters, review process all public
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Letting the fastest runner stop is not wasting his time, but giving him a chance to understand how others walked the road he ran. The Quest Sediment Domain's greatest value for Youth Supremes is not making him stronger, but making him more worthy of his strength.

In the final analysis, Youth Supreme trials in the quest domain described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
