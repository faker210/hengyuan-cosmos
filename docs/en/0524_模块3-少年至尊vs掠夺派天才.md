# Entry 524 | Youth Supreme Growth Subplot | Youth Supreme vs Predatory-School Geniuses

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Document 04, Module 2 series, Document 17

## Introduction: same starting talent, different fates

Youth Supremes and predatory-school geniuses often have strikingly similar starting points: extraordinary talent, fame at a young age, attention from all sides. Their fork is not in talent, but in growth path. Heng Yuan Zhou's dual route is essentially providing the "Youth Supreme" identity with a growth track completely different from predatory-school geniuses. Six top-level axioms are this comparison's highest framework: anti-predation axiom is the fork's root; republican axiom of Myriad Races is the fork's direction; collective ascension axiom is the fork's endpoint; causal self-discipline axiom is the guarantee that during forking they do not harm each other; law-first axiom is the guarantee that forking paths remain stable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Growth Comparison Table for Two Types of Genius

| Dimension | Predatory-School Genius | Heng Yuan Zhou Youth Supreme |
|-----------|-------------------------|------------------------------|
| Resource source | Predation, plunder, sect monopoly | Joint Contracting, Myriad-Ranks sharing |
| Speed | Accelerated by devouring, the more eaten the faster | Steadily accelerated by the dual route |
| Trials | Real suffering, exploited | Controlled trials, protected |
| State of mind | Suspicious, autocratic, paranoid | Collaborative, humble, responsible |
| Relation to Myriad Ranks | Myriad Ranks are nutrient or tools | Myriad Ranks are companions and service objects |
| Endpoint | Become new predators or be counter-devoured | Become a strong one who lifts Myriad Ranks up together |

## II. Where Is the Fork

Two types of genius's fork is not at some critical moment, but in every small choice:

- First time facing a weak person's cry for help: rob him or help him;
- First time discovering "swallowing one pill speeds ten years": swallow or not;
- First time being worshipped by multitudes: feel naturally noble, or feel responsibility heavy.

The dual route's role is, before these small choices appear, already arranging environment, institutions, mentors, letting the Youth Supreme more naturally walk toward the symbiotic side.

## III. Encountering Predatory-School Geniuses

Heng Yuan Zhou's Quest Domain will arrange Youth Supremes to encounter "predatory-school genius cases" — not really going to fight a predatory-school strong one, but in simulation review experiencing the other's life from the opponent's perspective. This design lets Youth Supremes understand: predatory-school geniuses are not born villains; they just had no other choice in that system. This understanding is not sympathy for predation, but eradicating from the root the arrogance of "I am nobler than him."

## IV. Encounter Protocol for Two Types of Genius

When Heng Yuan Zhou's Youth Supreme in a cross-plane task encounters a predatory-school genius, the dual-track design gives not "annihilation," but "contact protocol": first observe, then communicate, provide dual-route samples, invite voluntary experience. The protocol allows failure — not every predatory-school genius is willing to turn back. But as long as one is willing, the dual route opens to him, not permanently excluded because of his past predatory record. This design's undertone is: **today's predatory-school genius may be tomorrow's Myriad-Rank co-builder.** Forcing him into an enemy is the greatest energy waste.

### Conversion Data From Predatory-School Genius to Co-Builder

Heng Yuan Zhou has published a cross-epoch statistic: among all predatory-school geniuses who voluntarily contacted the dual route, about sixty percent after completing a full trial cycle chose to stay in the symbiotic network; about thirty percent after contact still chose to leave but no longer took hostile action; about ten percent conversion failed. This data does not beautify conversion difficulty, but it proves one thing: **build the road to the predatory-school genius's feet, and someone is willing to walk over.** Treating all predatory-school geniuses as enemies to annihilate is the greatest failure.

### The Dual Route's Attraction to Predatory-School Geniuses

The dual route does not attract predatory-school geniuses by slogans; it relies on a simple fact: in the dual route, a genius can still be fast, and need not pay the price of mental collapse, family-and-friends alienation, eventual backlash. For a predatory-school genius, this is a "cost-free strength" — the speed he used to trade for through predation, now can also get through symbiosis, without bearing predation's long-term bitter fruits. This attraction is more effective than any moral sermon.

Two types of genius's comparison ultimately point to one fact: the predatory-school genius's tragedy is not talent's tragedy, but path's tragedy. Change the road, the same talent can become Myriad Ranks' blessing. Making this fact clear is the Youth Supreme subplot's most important teaching.

Placing predatory-school geniuses side by side with Heng Yuan Zhou Youth Supremes is not to belittle anyone, but to explain: talent itself does not determine good and evil; the path does. This is the one sentence the entire Youth Supreme subplot most wants Myriad Ranks to remember.

- **Anti-energy waste**: direct the Youth Supreme's talent toward the symbiotic route, avoiding him ultimately becoming a predator needing correction

- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the dual route is open to Youth Supremes of all races, not only human
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: comparison table, fork-point analysis, simulation-review cases all public
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Talent is not fate; the path is. The gap between predatory-school genius and Heng Yuan Zhou Youth Supreme is not who is smarter, who fights better, but who was placed on which road. The dual route exists so that every talented youth need not become the next predator.
