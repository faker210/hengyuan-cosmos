# Entry 525 | Myriad-Races Self-Chosen-Destiny Mechanism · Overview — Returning the Right to Choose Destiny to All Beings

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 08, 40

## Introduction: the highest respect is giving up the right to decide

Predatory-school systems' attitude toward all beings' destiny, from the start, is decided: the protagonist is the heaven-chosen son; supporting roles are tools; mortals are dust; foreign races are enemies or slaves. The entire cosmos's destiny is decided by one person or one family. Heng Yuan Zhou's fundamental negation of this arrangement is embodied in one institution — **the Myriad-Races Self-Chosen-Destiny Mechanism**: every being, every race, every civilization has the right to choose which cultivation path to take, at what speed, what kind of life to live. Six top-level axioms are this mechanism's highest principles: anti-predation axiom forbids deciding destiny for others; republican axiom of Myriad Races acknowledges all beings' equal right to choose; collective ascension axiom requires self-chosen destiny's ultimate direction still co-building; causal self-discipline axiom requires the right to choose not be abused to harm others; law-first axiom requires choice rules stable and unchanging. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry as the Myriad-Races Self-Chosen-Destiny Mechanism's overview gives the overall design.

## I. Three Layers of Meaning of Self-Chosen Destiny

| Layer | Content | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Individual Layer | Each being self-chooses cultivation path and rhythm | Choose Acceleration Domain or Quest Domain |
| Racial Layer | Each race self-chooses civilizational development direction | Take the technology route or the law route |
| Civilizational Layer | Each plane self-chooses whether to join the symbiotic network | Autonomous decision, not forced |

Three layers of choice all protected by law; any strong one, any faction may not decide on their behalf.

## II. Choice Is Not Arbitrary, but Bounded Freedom

Self-chosen destiny does not equal "do whatever you want." The boundary is: **your freedom ends where another's freedom begins.** Specifically:

- You may choose not to cultivate, choose to be a mortal, choose an ordinary life — respected;
- You may not choose to predate, enslave, harm other beings — forbidden;
- You may not choose to impose your destiny on others — forbidden.

This is fully consistent with the Symbiotic Constitution (Document 40)'s basic spirit: freedom is freedom with boundaries; rights are rights parallel with responsibility.

## III. Comparison With Tang Luo's "Heaven-Chosen" Narrative

The Tang Luo system's core narrative is "heaven-chosen" — only the protagonist alone is chosen by destiny; everyone else's destiny is a backdrop. This narrative is cool in literature, a disaster in politics: it treats "being chosen" as the source of legitimacy, treats "not being chosen" as deserved. Heng Yuan Zhou's self-chosen-destiny mechanism is the reverse: **no one is specially chosen; every being is equally respected.**

## IV. Intergenerational Transmission of the Right to Choose

The Myriad-Races Self-Chosen-Destiny Mechanism also has a time dimension: the path the previous generation chose for the next cannot become the next generation's shackle. Every being, after reaching adulthood (the adulthood node defined by their race), has the right to re-examine the path family, race, civilization preset for him, and make his own choice. The system provides complete information support for this "re-choice" — laying out the pros and cons of the preset path, the possibilities of other paths. Intergenerational choice not free, self-chosen destiny is empty talk; self-chosen destiny must allow every generation to overturn the previous generation's decision, even if that decision was once right.

### Self-Chosen Destiny and Civilizational Diversity

The self-chosen-destiny mechanism also brings an unexpected dividend: civilizational diversity. When every plane, every race has the right to choose its own rhythm, technical direction, social structure, the entire symbiotic network presents plural forms unimaginable in the old era — some planes take the technology route, some take the law route, some take the fusion of both. This diversity is not chaos, but the natural result of the republican axiom of Myriad Races. It lets the symbiotic network when facing future unknown challenges, have more "alternative civilizations" to draw on.

### Self-Chosen Destiny and the Civilizational Compact

Self-chosen destiny is not anarchy. Every being while enjoying the right to choose, also signs a civilizational compact: your freedom ends where another's freedom begins. The compact is not a shackle, but the premise for self-chosen destiny to keep running. Without the compact, "I choose to predate" would also be called "my destiny my choice." The compact binds the right to choose with responsibility, letting self-choice be a true right, not indulgence.

The self-chosen-destiny mechanism's highest ideal is letting every being in Myriad Ranks be able to say: "This road I walk is one I chose myself." This sentence, in old systems almost no one had the qualification to say. It is not rhetoric; it is civilization's achievement.

Writing the self-chosen-destiny mechanism into Symbiotic Constitution Article 1 is not rhetoric; it means any behavior trying to deprive all beings' right to choose is unconstitutional. This is Heng Yuan Zhou's most solemn promise to Myriad Ranks.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: let every being walk the path suited to him, avoiding being stuffed by institutions into an unsuitable track
- **Anti-predation**: not deciding destiny for others is itself the greatest anti-predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is heaven-chosen narrative; Heng Yuan Zhou is self-chosen narrative
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five gene lineages, men and women, old and young, mortal and saint cultivators all have the right to choose
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: three-layer choice rules, boundary clauses, grievance channels all public
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Returning the right to choose to all beings is not civilization's weakness, but civilization's maturity. Heng Yuan Zhou does not try to arrange destiny for anyone; it only provides roads, protection, resources — as for which to walk, how fast, how far, the being himself decides.

In the final analysis, the Myriad-Races self-chosen-destiny overview described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
