# Entry 526 | Myriad-Races Self-Chosen-Destiny Mechanism | Self-Chosen Paths Between the Acceleration Domain and the Quest Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Entries 501, 525, Document 08

## Introduction: fast or slow is yours; the road is yours

The Myriad-Races Self-Chosen-Destiny Mechanism's first concrete implementation in the dual-track time domain is letting every being himself choose: **do I go to the Origin Acceleration Domain, or the Quest Sediment Domain, or both, at what rhythm.** This is not assigned by a mentor, not decided by talent, not arranged by institutions, but autonomously chosen by the being himself under informed conditions. Six top-level axioms run through this entry: anti-predation axiom does not force people onto a track; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all tracks open to all; collective ascension axiom requires whichever path chosen ultimately serves Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom requires the choice process transparent; law-first axiom requires choice standards stable and unchanging. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Four Typical Paths

| Path | Combination | Suited To |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| Pure-Acceleration Type | Acceleration-Domain primary, small amount of quest | Researchers, crisis responders, young geniuses |
| Pure-Quest Type | Quest-Domain primary, small amount of acceleration | Educators, mental workers, philosophers |
| Dual-Track Balanced Type | Acceleration and quest alternating | Most cultivators' recommended path |
| Real-Life Type | Does not enter any time domain; normal life | All beings choosing to be mortals |

Four paths have no hierarchy. An ordinary artisan choosing pure real life enjoys in Heng Yuan Zhou the same dignity as a Plane-Transcendent strong one choosing dual-track balanced.

## II. Informed-Consent Guarantee in the Choice Process

Self-choice is not blind choice. The system provides each chooser with:

- Current resource occupancy of each path;
- Growth data of past cultivators on that path;
- Potential risks and cooldown requirements;
- Cost of exit and switching.

Only after full information, is the choice recorded and respected.

## III. Path Is Switchable

Self-choice is not once in a lifetime. A being this year choosing pure acceleration, three years later may switch to pure quest; he may even repeatedly jump between the two time domains. The system does not punish "frequent path-changing," only reminds him "switching has cognitive cost." This flexibility is the republican axiom of Myriad Races's embodiment — people are not animals fixed by institutions; people can grow, can change, can re-know themselves.

## IV. Comparison With Tang Luo's "Path Decided by Talent"

In the Tang Luo system, path is decided by talent: you are a trash, you can only be a trash; you are a genius, the protagonist halo automatically arranges the path for you. Heng Yuan Zhou is the opposite: talent is only reference; the final path is your own choice. A being of "average talent" if choosing pure quest type, can also reach extremely high realms — just slower.

## V. Regret Mechanism for Path Choice

Self-choice does not mean cannot regret. The system provides a "regret window" for path switching: within one reality month right after switching, if the cultivator finds the new path unsuitable, may switch back to the old path at no cost; after one month, switching still allowed, but needs to complete a segment of cognitive integration, suturing the two paths' insights. This design acknowledges people's understanding of themselves can change; institutions should not force people to turn "wrong choice" into a lifetime shackle. The regret mechanism is the self-chosen destiny's gentle side — chosen can be changed, changed is not shameful.

### Path Choice and Cultivation Efficiency Relation

Self-chosen path does not equal "any choice works." The system based on the individual's past data gives a "recommended path" — not a command, but reference. A person strong in deduction talent but emotionally obtuse is recommended dual-track balanced rather than pure acceleration; a plant spirit is recommended quest-leaning. This recommendation does not decide for people, but helps people see themselves clearly. The republican axiom of Myriad Races is not laissez-faire, but respecting choice on the basis of full information.

### Path Choice and Age Relation

Different-age cultivators, the system gives different recommended paths. The young are encouraged to enter the Acceleration Domain more — because their consciousness plasticity strong, recover fast; middle-aged encouraged dual-track balanced; elderly encouraged quest-leaning — their experience more needs to settle into civilizational wisdom. This age-differentiated recommendation is not stereotype, but statistical law based on large data. Individuals may of course go against recommendation, but the system has the duty to make the statistical law clear.

Path-choice freedom is not one-time, but lifelong. Institutions do not allow any agency, any mentor, any "someone who has been there" in the name of "for your own good" to nail a being permanently to a path. The right to change paths is written into the Symbiotic Constitution.

Path-choice freedom ultimately lands on a plain fact: a person may for a lifetime not cultivate, only be an ordinary artisan, and be respected by the whole civilization. This is self-chosen destiny's hardest touchstone.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: choosing the path most suited to oneself is far more efficient than being forced into a universal path
- **Anti-predation**: do not decide paths for others; do not treat people as tools
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's path decided by talent and plot; Heng Yuan Zhou's path self-chosen by the individual
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: four paths all open to five gene lineages
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: path data, switching rules, risk warnings all public
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Returning the question of "fast or slow" to the beings themselves is the Myriad-Races Self-Chosen-Destiny Mechanism's first realization in the time dimension. Civilization does not decide for anyone how he should live — it only ensures all doors are open.

In the final analysis, self-chosen paths between the acceleration and quest domains described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
