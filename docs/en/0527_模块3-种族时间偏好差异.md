# Entry 527 | Myriad-Races Self-Chosen-Destiny Mechanism | Time-Preference Differences Across Races

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 94, 26, 05

## Introduction: time is not human time; rhythm is not human rhythm

The Myriad-Races Self-Chosen-Destiny Mechanism, to truly hold, cannot take the human race's time rhythm as default. Carbon-based matter races, energy-condensation races, mechanical-construction races, consciousness-information races, mixed-variant races — five gene lineages' understanding of "fast" and "slow" is completely different. If forcibly scheduling all races by human standard, self-chosen destiny degenerates into "chosen by human standard." Six top-level axioms are this entry's highest principles: anti-predation axiom forbids imposing human rhythm on foreign races; republican axiom of Myriad Races acknowledges five gene lineages' respective time natures; collective ascension axiom requires cooperation on the basis of respecting differences; causal self-discipline axiom requires adaptation process not break racial nature; law-first axiom requires adaptation model stable and unchanging. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Time-Preference Portraits of the Five Gene Lineages

| Lineage | Typical Time Preference | Acceleration-Domain Adaptation | Quest-Domain Adaptation |
|---------|-------------------------|--------------------------------|------------------------|
| Carbon-based matter race | Medium, with birth, aging, sickness, death | 10× to 100× | Months to years |
| Energy-condensation race | Consciousness naturally active, leans fast | Up to 500× | Lean short, months |
| Mechanical-construction race | Can run continuously long-term | Long-term 100× | Needs "shutdown thinking" |
| Consciousness-information race | Extremely fast, almost pure consciousness | 1000× gear common | Needs embodied trials |
| Plant spirit | Extremely slow, by seasons | Small acceleration gain | Naturally suited |
| Planet consciousness | Eon-scale | Acceleration almost meaningless | Sediments by eons |

## II. Relation Between Racial Preference and Individual Choice

Racial preference is statistical "default suggestion," not mandatory rule. A plant-spirit individual may completely choose to enter the Acceleration Domain; a consciousness-information individual may also choose long-term Quest-Domain sedimentation. The system only provides racial default suggestion; final choice still belongs to the individual. This is the republican axiom of Myriad Races's precise implementation at the racial layer.

## III. Time Coordination in Cross-Race Collaboration

Different races' rhythms differ greatly; collaboration inevitably produces friction of "carbon-based thinks consciousness race too fast; consciousness race thinks plant spirit too slow." Heng Yuan Zhou's solution is: **use the Alaya Network for time translation in collaboration** — translating different rhythms' thinking, decision, feedback into rhythms each side understands, not forcing one side to change its nature. This is dual-track collaboration's extension at the cross-race layer.

## IV. Comparison With Tang Luo's "Human-Centric"

The Tang Luo system defaults human as standard; other races are either human allies (assimilated by human values) or human enemies (annihilated). Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad-Races Self-Chosen mechanism is the opposite: **human is only one of five gene lineages; there is no human standard.**

## V. Time-Translation Ethics in Cross-Race Collaboration

When the Alaya Network does time translation, it must observe one ethic: **do not package any racial rhythm as "standard rhythm."** Translation is to let each other understand, not to turn one side into the other. A carbon-based cultivator interfacing with a consciousness-information collaborator, the system tunes both sides' time frequencies to an intermediate value, not letting the consciousness-information slow down, nor letting carbon-based forcibly accelerate. This intermediate value's choice is negotiated by both sides, not unilaterally decided by the system. Rhythm translation's ultimate purpose is collaboration, not assimilation.

### Planet Consciousness's Time Unit

Among all races, planet consciousness's time preference is most special: it thinks in geological ages; one "decision" may take ten thousand years. For it, the Acceleration Domain almost has no meaning — you cannot make a planet consciousness "fast-forward." The Quest Domain also has limited meaning for it, because it itself experiences everything on an eon scale. Heng Yuan Zhou designed "companion-style collaboration" for planet consciousness: do not urge it on human time, but on its eon scale, at its own rhythm provide knowledge, provide dialogue. This is the republican axiom of Myriad Races's expression on the most extreme time difference.

### Racial Time-Preference Evolution

Racial time preference is not eternal. A race after technological development may shift from "lean slow" to "lean fast"; another race after a civilizational collapse may shift from "lean fast" to "lean slow." The Alaya Network every ten years reviews each ethnic group's time-preference portrait, updates default suggestions. This dynamism acknowledges: races themselves are also growing; their time rhythm will change; institutions must change with it.

Five gene lineages' time differences are not defects, but wealth. A cosmos with only human rhythm is monotonous; a cosmos that contains plant spirits' slown and consciousness races' fast, truly deserves the name Myriad-Rank Republicanism.

Acknowledging racial time-preference differences is not to put Myriad Ranks into their respective time capsules, but to let different-rhythm races truly dialogue. Dialogue itself is Myriad-Rank Republicanism.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: schedule by racial rhythm, avoid using the wrong tool
- **Anti-predation**: do not impose human rhythm on foreign races
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is human-centric; Heng Yuan Zhou is Myriad Ranks each their own center
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five gene lineages + plant spirits + planet consciousness each have adaptation
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: time-preference portraits, translation protocols, adaptation standards all public
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

True Myriad-Rank Republicanism is not turning all races into human, but allowing each race to exist at its own time rhythm. The dual-track time domain wrote this into the foundation from design — fast and slow are not human privilege, but Myriad Ranks' respective choices.
