# Entry 528 | Myriad-Races Self-Chosen-Destiny Mechanism | Alaya Supervision of Self-Chosen Destiny

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 21, 40, 49

## Introduction: freedom needs guardrails, but guardrails cannot become cages

Once the self-chosen-destiny mechanism opens, people will worry: will someone use the name of "freedom" to predate others? Will someone use the name of "choice" to harm himself? Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is: **freedom must be supervised, but supervision itself must also be supervised.** The Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's role in the self-chosen-destiny domain is not to choose for beings, but to hold the line of "your freedom ends where another's freedom begins." Six top-level axioms are supervision's highest principles: anti-predation axiom is supervision's core goal; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires supervision equal for all races; collective ascension axiom requires supervision serve co-building not suppression; causal self-discipline axiom requires supervision process auditable; law-first axiom requires supervision rules stable and unchanging. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Three Supervision Goals

1. **Anti-coercion**: monitor whether any faction forces individuals to choose a path;
2. **Anti-harm**: monitor whether individuals make irreversible self-destructive choices;
3. **Anti-shifting**: monitor whether individuals' choices come at the cost of predating others.

## II. Four Supervision Layers

| Layer | Subject | Duty |
|-------|---------|------|
| Layer 1 | Alaya automatic monitoring | Sweep coercion signals, harm signals, shifting signals |
| Layer 2 | Myriad-Rank representative group | Accept individual appeals, mediate choice disputes |
| Layer 3 | Anchor Supervisory Committee | Review large-scale racial-level choice disputes |
| Layer 4 | Symbiotic Court | Adjudicate "self-chosen destiny infringed" cases |

## III. What Supervision Does Not Do

It must be made clear, the Alaya Network in the self-chosen-destiny domain **does not** do the following:

- Does not decide for the individual "which road is better";
- Does not record individual thought tendencies for judgment;
- Does not block individuals choosing "seemingly unprofitable" roads;
- Does not look down on a race because it chose "slow."

Supervision's purpose is to protect freedom, not restrict freedom.

## IV. Comparison With "Paternalism" Narrative

Predatory-school systems often in the name of "for your own good" decide for the weak — sect masters choose cultivation methods for disciples, dynasties choose destiny for the people, protagonists choose direction for the world. Heng Yuan Zhou's self-chosen-destiny supervision is the reverse: **"for your own good" cannot be the reason to deprive the right to choose.** If a being's choice will truly harm himself, what the system provides is information, advice, exit ramps, not forced blocking.

## V. Fast-Track for Supervision Grievances

Supervision disputes in the self-chosen-destiny domain are often time-sensitive — a being if being forced onto a path, delaying a week may be irreversible trauma. For this, the Symbiotic Court opened a fast track for "self-chosen destiny infringed" cases: from submitting grievance to initial adjudication no more than three reality days; in emergencies a temporary protection order may be issued within twenty-four hours, pausing the complained-of coercive behavior. The fast track's existence keeps supervision from becoming de facto indulgence in lengthy procedures.

### Anti-Coercion Institutional Backstop

The supervision mechanism has a hard rule of "anti-coercion": any agency, any mentor, any same-race elder may not in the name of "for your own good" choose a path for the individual. The system by design strictly separates "suggestion" from "decision" — suggestions can be unlimited; only the individual himself may press the confirm button. This rule is especially important in cross-race collaboration: history has repeatedly seen events of "strong ones out of goodwill deciding destiny for the weak"; Heng Yuan Zhou by institutions blocks this "well-meaning coercion" as well.

### Tension Balance Between Supervision and Self-Choice

Between self-chosen destiny and supervision exists natural tension: too loose, someone will be predated; too strict, it becomes deciding for people. Heng Yuan Zhou's balancing way is: **provide full information before choice occurs; after choice only intercept boundary-crossing behavior, do not intervene in the choice itself.** This is like the traffic light at an intersection — the light tells you which road is safe, but which road you take is still your own decision. Supervisors do not drive; they only do traffic lights.

Supervision's highest realm is making the supervised feel no supervision. When rules are internalized into common sense; when coercion only appears at the most extreme boundary-crossing moment, self-chosen destiny and external constraint no longer oppose, but jointly protect everyone's true freedom.

Supervision's ultimate confidence is that one day it can gradually fade — when self-chosen destiny becomes every being's second nature, supervision only appears at boundary-crossing moments. This is the day institutions hope to retire.

Supervisors are also mortals, will also err; putting supervisors themselves also under supervision, the whole system will not one day suddenly go bad. This is the republican axiom of Myriad Races's last line of defense in governance's deepest place.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: promptly stop coercion and shifting, avoid individuals and races' energy being predated
- **Anti-predation**: this entry's entire core is preventing predation in the name of "choice"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is paternalism; Heng Yuan Zhou is republicanism
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: supervision equal for all races, all individuals
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: three goals, four-layer structure, do-not-do list all public
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Self-chosen destiny is not anarchy, but freedom with guardrails. The Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's existence is not to keep people from choosing, but to let people truly choose — choose without being forced, without being deceived, without being predated.
