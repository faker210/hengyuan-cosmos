# Entry 530 | Experience-Argument · Overview — Why God-Speed Progress Is Not Hollow

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Entries 501, 512, Document 09

## Introduction: the sharpest challenge must be answered most seriously

The most common challenge to the Origin Acceleration Domain is: **if a person lives a hundred years in 100× time-flow, after coming out has he truly "lived"? Is he just an accelerated machine? Is his progress not hollow?** This challenge is very sharp, because it directly points to the dual-track time domain's philosophical root. Heng Yuan Zhou cannot fob it off with "anyway I just can"; it must from engineering, philosophy, institutions systematically argue: **why god-speed progress is not hollow.** Six top-level axioms are this argument's highest principles: anti-predation axiom requires "not hollow" not be built on predating others' experiences; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires "not hollow" hold for all races; collective ascension axiom requires "not hollow" ultimately serve Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom requires "not hollow" have real causal support; law-first axiom requires "experience" itself be a definable, measurable objective law, not a metaphysical slogan. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry as the experience-argument's overview gives the overall argument framework.

## I. What Exactly Is "Hollow Feeling"

To answer "why not hollow," first define "hollow feeling." It is not a metaphysical emotion, but three identifiable symptoms:

1. **Memory without thickness**: experienced many things, but none left a feeling;
2. **Choice without weight**: made many decisions, but none make oneself feel "this is my choice";
3. **No connection with others**: know many people, but no relationship is real.

Three symptoms appearing simultaneously is true hollowness. Conversely, as long as at least one symptom does not appear, the so-called "hollow" does not hold.

## III. Why "Fast" Itself Does Not Inevitably Cause Hollow

Some mistakenly think "fast" is the root of hollowness; not so. What truly causes hollow is not fast, but **fast divorced from reality** — like in the Tang Luo system the protagonist one-second sudden enlightenment via a golden finger, one-second gaining feelings via the author's arrangement. Heng Yuan Zhou's fast is **fast strictly constrained within reality** (see Entry 509): energy comes from real increments, knowledge returns to reality for validation, causality anchored to reality. This kind of fast, because of high efficiency, actually gives people more time to experience those things that "must be slow."

## IV. Comparison With the "Efficiency Equals Nihilism" Myth

Some modern thought trends oppose "efficiency" against "meaning," thinking everything calculable is hollow. Heng Yuan Zhou's dual-track design punctures this opposition: **efficiency is the means; meaning is the end.** The Origin Acceleration Domain handles the means; the Quest Sediment Domain handles the end; the two together are "efficient and not nihilistic."

## V. Counterexamples and Boundaries of the Experience Argument

Arguing "god-speed progress is not hollow" does not equal declaring "any god-speed progress is not hollow." Heng Yuan Zhou acknowledges: if a cultivator refuses the Quest Domain, refuses real anchor days, refuses emotional sampling, insists on running wild in the Acceleration Domain, he will indeed turn hollow. This hollow is not caused by acceleration itself, but by him actively giving up all anti-hollow designs. The experience argument's true claim is: **under the premise that institutions are fully built, god-speed progress is not hollow; but institutions cannot replace people in choosing to live.** Make this sentence clear, and the argument is complete.

### Philosophical Significance of the Experience Argument

Making "god-speed progress is not hollow" into an engineerable, measurable, publicly open proposition is itself the fundamental difference between Heng Yuan Zhou and old systems. Old systems argue progress via the author's one line "he was enlightened"; Heng Yuan Zhou argues via indicators, via data, via continuous tracking. This difference is not a stylistic difference, but a worldview difference: old systems believe feeling can be declared; Heng Yuan Zhou believes feeling must be designed, verified. The law-first axiom here extends to: **whatever is important must be engineerable.**

### The Experience Argument and Real Psychological Science

Heng Yuan Zhou's experience argument is not invented out of thin air; it corresponds to real-world psychological science: flow research proves experience depth comes from the balance of attention, feedback, challenge; memory research proves emotional intensity and time interval jointly determine memory retention; personality research proves self-continuity depends on stable social relations and daily anchors. The Origin Acceleration Domain's preservation mechanism is precisely these real laws' engineering. This is the reality-as-foundation axiom's another realization in the experience domain.
