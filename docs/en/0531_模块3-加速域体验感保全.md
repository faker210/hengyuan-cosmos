# Entry 531 | Experience-Argument | Experience-Preservation-Day Mechanism in the Acceleration Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Entries 503, 505, Document 16

## Introduction: in fast places, keep the feeling

The Origin Acceleration Domain accelerates consciousness a hundredfold; the biggest risk is: **in a hundred years thought about countless things, after coming out only remember a vague outline.** This is the Acceleration Domain-specific "experience dilution" problem. Heng Yuan Zhou designed for this a whole set of "experience-preservation-day mechanism" — inside the Acceleration Domain, use engineering means to keep key experiences. Six top-level axioms run through this entry: anti-predation axiom requires the preservation-day mechanism not steal others' experiences; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the mechanism apply to all races; collective ascension axiom requires preserved experiences ultimately returnable to Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom requires the preservation process not perturb real causality; law-first axiom requires the mechanism stable and recomputable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Three Causes of Experience Dilution

| Cause | Manifestation | Engineering Countermeasure |
|-------|---------------|-----------------------------|
| Memory overload | A hundred years of information exceeds consciousness bandwidth | Key-node memory fixing |
| Emotional blunting | High-frequency events weaken emotional response | Emotional sampling and replay |
| Self drift | After long acceleration forget "who I am" | Reality anchor day |

## II. Three Engineering Means of Preservation Days

1. **Memory fixing**: draw on the Multiplication Fruit technology's "deduction memory fixing" (Document 16), completely save each milestone node's consciousness state, replayable after exit;
2. **Emotional sampling**: the system at key nodes automatically collects the cultivator's emotional intensity, belief fluctuation, relationship changes, forming an "emotional curve";
3. **Reality anchor day**: every accumulated several consciousness days in the Acceleration Domain, mandatorily insert a "reality anchor day" — pause acceleration, return to the real plane to live one day, re-confirm who you are.

Three means combined, ensure the cultivator living a hundred years in 100× time-flow, after coming out still "remembers, feels, recognizes himself."

## III. Philosophical Meaning of the Preservation-Day Mechanism

The philosophy behind the preservation-day mechanism is: **experience is not a function of time length, but a joint function of memory, emotion, self-identity.** Merely lengthening time does not automatically thicken experience; engineering means must lock all three, and experience will not be diluted. This is completely different from the Tang Luo system's metaphysical description of "secluded for a hundred years, coming out remembering everything" — Heng Yuan Zhou acknowledges the problem exists, and gives an engineering solution.

## IV. Comparison With the "Acceleration Must Hollow" Myth

The "acceleration must hollow" myth is essentially directly applying a certain real-world experience (watching too many short videos makes you empty) to cultivation. But why real-world short videos are empty is precisely because they have no memory fixing, no emotional sampling, no reality anchor. Heng Yuan Zhou's Acceleration Domain does all three fully, and "hollow" is no longer inevitable.

## V. Racial Adaptation of the Preservation-Day Mechanism

Memory fixing, emotional sampling, reality anchor day — the three-piece set's parameters differ for the five gene lineages. The consciousness-information race's "memory fixing" is essentially data backup, frequency can be very high; carbon-based matter race's emotional curve is affected by physiological cycles, sampling must leave margin; mechanical-construction race has no "emotion" module, needs to use decision-weight changes instead of emotional sampling. The system does not apply one set of preservation-day parameters to different races, but calibrates by their consciousness structure. This adaptation is the republican axiom of Myriad Races's precise implementation in experience engineering — **do not let carbon-based "not hollow" standards demand a planet consciousness.**

### The Preservation-Day Mechanism and Personality Continuity

Living a hundred years in 100× time-flow, the biggest risk is not forgetting, but "am I still me." The reality anchor day's true role is not to let you go back to eat a meal, but to let you in a familiar real scene re-confirm: your body, your relationships, your daily life are all still in their original positions. This confirmation is the anchor of personality continuity. Without an anchor, no matter how thick memory fixing, it cannot stop the existential anxiety of "have I already become another person."

### Boundaries of the Preservation-Day Mechanism

The preservation-day mechanism cannot forge experience. It cannot turn an experience that never truly happened into a "seems to have happened" memory. It only locks and thickens truly happened experiences. If a cultivator in the Acceleration Domain only mechanically deduces, without truly investing any emotion and judgment, then memory fixing cannot fix thickness either. The mechanism is a guarantee, not a replacement. This must be made clear, otherwise preservation days will be misunderstood as "one-click obtaining experience."

The preservation-day mechanism is not omnipotent. It can lock already-happened experiences, but cannot replace you in experiencing. The cultivator must truly have invested, judged, struggled in the Acceleration Domain; only then does the mechanism have something to lock. This is its boundary, and its honesty.
