# Entry 533 | Experience-Argument | Quantitative Evaluation of Experience in the Dual-Track Time Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Entries 530, 531, 532

## Introduction: turn "not hollow" into measurable indicators

The last entry must solve the last problem: **on what basis do we say our dual-track time domain is truly not hollow?** No matter how much philosophical argument, it must ultimately land on measurable, auditable, publicly open indicators. Otherwise "experience preservation days" and "deep-experience design" become self-talk. Six top-level axioms are quantitative evaluation's highest principles: anti-predation axiom requires indicators not reward predatory "experience"; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires indicators fair to all races; collective ascension axiom requires indicators ultimately serve Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom requires the evaluation process itself not perturb causality; law-first axiom requires the indicator system stable and recomputable, not changed by emotion. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Four Dimensions of Experience Quantification

| Dimension | Indicator | Measurement Method |
|-----------|-----------|---------------------|
| Memory Thickness | Key-node memory completeness rate | Alaya Network comparison |
| Emotional Intensity | Emotional-curve fluctuation amplitude | Sampling replay |
| Self-Identity | Personality consistency before/after reality anchor day | Psychological assessment |
| Relation Authenticity | Proportion of relations built during trials still acknowledged after exit | Relation archive tracking |

Four dimensions jointly form the "Experience Index," 0–100 points. Below 60, this time-domain use is unqualified, needs review.

## II. Track-Specific Pass Lines

| Track | Pass Line | Note |
|-------|-----------|------|
| Origin Acceleration Domain (100× gear) | ≥70 | Acceleration itself dilutes experience, higher requirement |
| Origin Acceleration Domain (500×+) | ≥80 | High-gear risk larger |
| Quest Sediment Domain | ≥60 | Slowness naturally has thickness, lower requirement |
| Youth Supreme Dual-Route Composite | ≥75 | Youth Supreme growth cannot be hollow |

The pass line is not a punishment line, but an improvement line — below pass line no punishment, but mandatory arrangement of consultation and repair.

## III. How Evaluation Results Are Used

Evaluation results are not used to assess individuals, but to improve the system:

- A gear where consecutive multiple people fall below pass line indicates that gear's design has problems;
- A racial group universally below pass line indicates insufficient adaptation to that race;
- A trial template repeatedly going wrong indicates the template needs rewriting.

This usage is the collective ascension axiom's embodiment: **evaluation is not to judge people, but to improve civilization.**

## IV. Comparison With "By-Feeling" Evaluation

Predatory-school systems never quantify experience — the protagonist "feels" he is enlightened, and he is; supporting roles "feel" they progressed, and they did. This by-feeling evaluation inevitably degenerates into the strong one's word being law. Heng Yuan Zhou's quantitative evaluation makes this matter public, auditable, improvable, turning "experience" from a metaphysical feeling into a proper engineering discipline.

## V. Cross-Cultural Calibration of the Experience Index

Four dimensions' indicators at first glance look designed for humans; Heng Yuan Zhou for this established a cross-cultural calibration mechanism: each race's "memory completeness," "emotional intensity," "self-identity," "relation authenticity" all have that race's baseline; when evaluating, compare against baseline before scoring. A plant spirit's "emotional intensity" may never reach human fluctuation amplitude, but if it reaches ninety percent of the plant-spirit baseline, it passes. This calibration ensures the Experience Index does not become implicit human-centrism — measuring Myriad Ranks requires using Myriad Ranks' own rulers.

### Isolating the Experience Index From Individual Ranking

It must be especially emphasized: the Experience Index is not used for between-individual comparison, not for ranking, not for "you experienced more deeply than him." It is only a tool for the individual to compare against his own history. A cultivator with a low index will not be looked down on; the system only provides repair support. Turning the index into a new involution indicator is betrayal of the entire design. Indicators serve improvement, not discrimination — this is the republican axiom of Myriad Races's bottom line in quantitative evaluation.

### Long-Term Cultural Significance of the Experience Index

When a civilization continues measuring its own Experience Index for decades, it will obtain a unique civilizational self-cognition: which periods the civilization's overall experience was high, which low, high/low correlated with what social variables. This cognition more honestly answers "how are we doing" than any grand narrative. It lets civilizational progress look not only at power numbers, but at whether all beings are truly alive. This is the dual-track time domain's ultimate deliverable.
