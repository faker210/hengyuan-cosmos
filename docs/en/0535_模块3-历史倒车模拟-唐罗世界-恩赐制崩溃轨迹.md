# Entry 535 | Historical-Reversal Simulation · Tang-Luo World — Collapse Trajectory of the Bounty System

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 84, 87, 04, 22

## Introduction: when the bountifier leaves, what keeps the whole system going

The Tang Luo system is a typical example repeatedly dissected in Module 2: a strong one of the Gu Chensha style distributes cultivation resources as "bounty"; supporting roles enter by receiving rewards; mortals outside the system survive or die on their own. This structure looks efficient in the bountifier's prime, but buried a time bomb: **bounty is one-way, privately bestowed, shifts with the bountifier's will.** The historical-reversal simulation must answer a cold and clear question — once the bountifier leaves (falls, disappears, no longer cares), along what curve does the Tang-Luo world reverse on the time axis?

Six top-level axioms are this review's highest criteria: anti-predation axiom wants us to see through that the bounty system's essence is still "the strong monopolizing distribution"; republican axiom of Myriad Races wants us to count the vast majority blocked out under the bounty system; collective ascension axiom contrasts the bounty system's fate of "the strong alone reaching the top; all beings marking time"; causal self-discipline axiom constrains the whole simulation to run only in Quest-Sediment-Domain sandboxes, only knowledge returned without perturbing real planes; law-first axiom requires every step of the collapse trajectory deduced first by institution-energy causal chains, not plot-driven sudden collapse. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Three Layers of Fragility in the Bounty System

In the sandbox, the Tang-Luo world's institutions are restored to three layers; each layer has a clear reversal fuse:

1. **Distribution-layer fragility**: cultivation resources distributed by the bountifier's personal will, not joint-contracting more-work-more-pay (Document 22). Once the bountifier leaves, resource distribution immediately stalls; no one knows "who gets the next reward."
2. **Knowledge-layer fragility**: core techniques opened per the bountifier's mood; the Great Dao not publicly systematized. Recipients learned "results" not "principles"; once the wrong person is entrusted, knowledge is immediately lost.
3. **Heart-layer fragility**: all beings' expectation of the bountifier is built on the assumption "he will always reward." Once this assumption breaks, gratitude reverses into resentment in very short order; order collapses from within.

## II. The Collapse Curve: Five Stages

Sandbox deduction shows the bounty system's reversal is not a cliff, but a five-segment sliding curve:

| Stage | Time-Axis Manifestation | Social Signs |
|-------|--------------------------|--------------|
| I. Reward Vacuum | First generation after bountifier disappears | Panic at the top; scramble for stock resources begins |
| II. Private Bestowal | Second generation | Strong ones each recruit favorites; "little bountifiers" sprout everywhere |
| III. Law Disorder | Third generation | Old bounty order replaced by jungle law; private vengeance rampant |
| IV. Knowledge Break | Fourth to fifth generations | Core techniques completely lost; cultivation regresses to bloodline secret transmission |
| V. Full Degeneration | After tenth generation | Civilization degenerates into tribal warfare; even "there once was a bountifier" becomes myth |

The curve's most noteworthy point is **Stage II**: it does not immediately descend into chaos, but breaks the great bountifier into countless little bountifiers — this is precisely the breeding ground of "enlightened predators" and "righteous scoundrels." All beings' original-sin craving for shortcuts erupts centrally in this stage; everyone wants to be the next bountifier; no one wants to build true republicanism.

## III. Control Experiment With the Joint-Contracting System

In the same sandbox, researchers ran a control: replace the bounty system with joint-contracting more-work-more-pay (Document 22) and all-level Great-Dao openness; other conditions unchanged. Result: the time vector stayed positive; even amid resource crises, civilization self-repaired via incremental development and open knowledge iteration, no reversal ever again. Two control groups clearly prove: **the root of Tang-Luo reversal is not "the bountifier is not strong enough," but that the distribution system ties civilization's lifeline to one person.**

This also from the reverse confirms the Tang-Luo "extremely high protagonist-uniqueness dependence" verdict: a single-pillar system; once the single pillar falls, the mansion topples.

## IV. Review Results: Three Institutional Patches

The sandbox distills the collapse lessons into three patches directly usable in Civilizational Correction Engineering:

- **Depersonalize distribution**: no civilization may tie key resource distribution to a single strong one; must have institutional distribution mechanisms as backstop.
- **De-bounty knowledge**: the Great Dao and core techniques must be publicly systematized, written as texts anyone can self-learn, not "tailor-made" rewards.
- **De-heroify power**: governance relies on the four-power checks of the Anchor Deliberative Council, Symbiotic Executive Committee, Symbiotic Court, Anchor Supervisory Committee (Document 49), not a super hero as backstop.

All three generated within isolated sandboxes, following the "Knowledge-Only Return Principle" (Document 87), not perturbing a causal hair of the real Tang-Luo plane.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the bounty system wastes top strong ones' mental energy on "reward distribution" that institutions could automate; the sandbox proves this is civilizational-level energy waste
- **Anti-predation**: bounty looks kind; its essence is soft predation of monopolized distribution; the reversal curve proves it dies slower and more unjustly than hard predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: this article is Tang-Luo's own reversal dissection — its upper limit is not "can a stronger protagonist appear," but "what happens when the protagonist is gone"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the review registers mortals and foreign races deprived of evolutionary qualification under the bounty system one by one, verifying 99% coverage necessity
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: collapse-curve model, three institutional patches openly filed, for all symbiotic planes' self-check
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Tang-Luo world's reversal simulation teaches all civilizations that "pin hope on one super hero" the cruelest lesson: bounty can buy momentary prosperity, but cannot buy institutional continuation. When a civilization must rely on one person being ever-present to avoid regression, it has actually already begun regressing — just not yet seen.

In the final analysis, the Tang-Luo bounty-system collapse trajectory described in this document is no isolated case — it runs within Heng Yuan Zhou's overall coordinates; its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
