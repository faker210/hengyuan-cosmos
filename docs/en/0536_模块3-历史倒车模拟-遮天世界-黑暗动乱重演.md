# Entry 536 | Historical-Reversal Simulation | Shrouding-the-Heavens World — Recurrence of the Dark Turmoil

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 84, 87, 04, 05

## Introduction: every time an almighty nears death, civilization must be buried once more

On the Shrouding-the-Heavens system's time axis, the same tragedy repeats: ancient Great Emperors, Almighties near the end of their lifespan, either pass away or strike in the dark; the whole earth enters a "Dark Turmoil" — countless beings devoured, vast tomes burned in war, the civilization just accumulated forcibly zeroed, then the next generation begins from scratch. This is not occasional disaster, but a **periodic civilizational reversal**: every Great Emperor's lifespan cycle, civilization reverses a large stretch on the time axis. What reversal simulation asks is: can this periodic reversal be structurally avoided?

Six top-level axioms run through the whole review: anti-predation axiom directly points to Dark Turmoil's essence — a dying Almighty predating all civilization's stock to extend life; republican axiom of Myriad Races questions on what basis billions of beings must be buried for one Almighty's lifespan; collective ascension axiom points out the truly strong should have carried all beings forward, not bitten back at their deathbed; causal self-discipline axiom constrains the simulation to replay only in Quest-Sediment-Domain sandboxes, observe first then limited intervention; law-first axiom requires the "lifespan crisis — Dark Turmoil — civilization zeroed" causal chain defined first by law, not plot arranging each catastrophe. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Structural Causes of the Dark Turmoil

After sandbox restoration, the Dark Turmoil is not some Almighty's moral corruption, but institutional design's inevitability:

1. **Lifespan decoupled from power**: in this system, no matter how strong, power cannot buy reasonable lifespan extension; the strong one's only "lifespan extension" means is devouring others' origin.
2. **Knowledge buried with Great Emperors**: Great-Emperor-level insights mostly secret transmissions; once a Great Emperor passes, the highest cognition immediately breaks a generation; later generations' "from Qin-Huang to Han-Wu" repeated groping begins.
3. **All beings have no self-protection right**: civilization has no Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision; no one records an Almighty's depravity signs, no one warns at the first crisis.

Thus every "Almighty near death" equals a timed reversal. Civilization looks forward, but actually is forcibly zeroed every cycle.

## II. Replay Trajectory: Time Slices of a Complete Turmoil

The sandbox slices one Dark Turmoil into five time slices, for Quest-Domain juniors to personally experience:

| Slice | Stage | Reversal Signs |
|-------|-------|----------------|
| 1 | Signs of Almighty's life ending | Portents first appear; unease within sects |
| 2 | Dark strike | The Almighty to extend life predates a great region; beings suffer |
| 3 | Tomes burned | Ancient transmissions lost in war; knowledge-layer reversal |
| 4 | Heroes rise to fill the gap | Brief chaos; new oligarchic structure forms |
| 5 | Cultivate from zero | Later generations re-grope knowledge that could have been inherited openly |

Most shocking is Slice 5: breakthrough problems later-generation cultivators struggle with, sandbox comparison shows the answer is buried in the burned tomes. **Civilization forces tomorrow's people to re-solve the problems yesterday already solved** — this is reversal's most expensive, most absurd price.

## III. Control Experiment: What If a Symbiotic Network Existed

The sandbox ran a key control: on the same initial state, connect the Anchor Symbiotic Network and Alaya records; other things unchanged. Result:

- Lifespan problems solved by the Life-Cycle System (second Origin system)'s incremental development; Almighties need not extend life by devouring;
- Great Emperor insights uploaded in real time to Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision, publicly systematized; knowledge lost at a Great Emperor's death permanently retained;
- Almighty depravity signs caught in advance by the Alaya dashboard; Anchor Supervisory Committee intervenes timely.

Civilization never again shows periodic zeroing; time vector always positive. The control nails the conclusion: **Dark Turmoil is not Shrouding-the-Heavens' fate, but its institutional deficit's IOU.**

## IV. Review Results: Three Anti-Reversal Indicators

- **Lifespan-increment indicator**: monitor whether this civilization's lifespan-extension means are built on incremental development; once "extending life by devouring" signal appears, immediate warning.
- **Knowledge-retention-rate indicator**: monitor whether highest cognition is openly retained in real time on the Alaya Network; eliminate "person dies, Dao dies."
- **Almighty-depravity warning indicator**: full psychological and behavioral monitoring of strong ones near lifespan end; strangle depravity before they strike.

These three indicators have been connected to the Alaya Network's reversal dashboard for all symbiotic planes' self-check. All knowledge comes from isolated sandboxes, following the "Knowledge-Only Return Principle."

The sandbox's most silencing scene: later-generation cultivators staring at a broken stone tablet, struggling to deduce a long-lost basic technique — and the tablet's fragment is actually buried under the burned tomes of that turmoil three hundred years ago. Civilization did not lack the answer; it burned the answer itself. This detail is filed into the reversal-law library, reminding all civilizations: **the highest transmission is not to create stronger techniques, but not to burn the problems already solved.**

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: every Dark Turmoil is civilizational-level energy waste — burning the whole plane's eons of accumulation just to extend one Almighty's life
- **Anti-predation**: a dying Almighty devouring all civilization's stock is the predatory-school time view's nakedest form
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Shrouding-the-Heavens though multi-generational, still cannot escape the cycle of "the strong bury all beings"; proving merely changing the protagonist without changing institutions, reversal still replays
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the review categorically registers the billions devoured in the turmoil, verifying Myriad-Rank 99% coverage is not bounty but life-saving
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: turmoil time slices, three anti-reversal indicators openly filed, becoming all planes' public early-warning system
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Shrouding-the-Heavens' reversal simulation shows: as long as civilization suspends the highest strong one's lifespan problem unsolved, locks the highest cognition into personal secret hoards, it must periodically stage "the Almighty dies, civilization is buried." Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is not "pray for a benevolent Great Emperor," but use Life-Cycle incremental development to solve lifespan, use the Alaya Network to retain knowledge, use multi-layer supervision to snuff depravity — so Dark Turmoil loses its soil to replay in any symbiotic plane.
