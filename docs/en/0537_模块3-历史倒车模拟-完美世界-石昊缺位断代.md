# Entry 537 | Historical-Reversal Simulation | Perfect World — The Broken Generation From Shi Hao's Absence

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 84, 87, 05, 49

## Introduction: when the only shield is gone, who shields all beings from the wind

Perfect World's time tension concentrates on one point: a Shi-Hao-style Youth Supreme all alone fights through the ages, alone carries the darkness, alone faces the final boss — almost the entire civilization's only "wind shield." The stronger he grows, the more civilization ties its fate to him alone. What reversal simulation must deduce is a cold and necessary hypothesis — **if Shi Hao at a key node is absent (falls, is blocked, arrives too late), along which direction does the world slide on the time axis?** This is no disrespect to heroes, but the strictest engineering test of "protagonist-uniqueness dependence."

Six top-level axioms are the review's highest criteria: anti-predation axiom requires civilization not buy peace via "one person devouring all enemies"; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires billions' fate not bet on one youth; collective ascension axiom precisely points out a hero's highest virtue should be "carrying Myriad Ranks forward," not bearing everything alone; causal self-discipline axiom constrains the deduction to run only in Quest-Sediment-Domain sandboxes, only knowledge returned without perturbing real planes; law-first axiom requires the "absence — generation break — reversal" causal chain deduced first by law, not plot-driven last-minute rescue. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Hidden Costs of the Youth-Supreme Mode

The sandbox first restores the structural cost of the "Shi Hao mode." It is short-term immensely effective: a heaven-defying talent grows far faster than peers, alone resolving crises civilization collectively cannot handle. But long-term buries three hidden dangers:

1. **Collective capability atrophy**: since the strongest backstops, each race and domain no longer seriously builds its own defense and governance; capability quietly atrophies in dependence.
2. **Knowledge personalization**: the highest cognition the protagonist gains along his path is mostly scattered with his movement trail, not openly and systematically retained; once he is absent, civilization's collective cognitive height falls accordingly.
3. **Governance hollowing**: the true four-power checks of deliberative, executive, judicial, supervisory (Document 49) are long-term de facto hollowed by "the protagonist has the final say"; institutional muscles atrophy.

## II. The Three-Act Curve of Absence-Generation-Break

The sandbox deduces the script "Shi Hao absent before some final battle"; the reversal curve has three acts:

| Act | Stage | Reversal Signs |
|-----|-------|----------------|
| Act I | Crisis at hand, no one can stop it | Dark army presses; each race discovers itself long out of practice |
| Act II | Hasty patchwork, each fights alone | Races resist by instinct; no unified command; retreat steadily |
| Act III | Civilization generation-breaks backward | Survivors fall back; generations later even "there once was a Youth Supreme" becomes legend |

Most worth reviewing is the **dependence-inertia period** between Act I and Act II: even when everyone realizes the protagonist is absent, in the short term they cannot organize, because collective coordination ability long ago atrophied in "waiting for the protagonist." This is precisely the "high dependence" time cost in protagonist-uniqueness dependence judgment.

## III. Control Experiment: If the Collective Had Been Lifted

The sandbox ran a key control: replace "the protagonist alone bears everything" with "the protagonist leads, Myriad Ranks republican co-build, Alaya Network synchronously retains cognition"; other crises unchanged. Result:

- The protagonist may still charge as strongest combat, but each race under the symbiotic system long ago had its own defense and command network;
- The protagonist's insights along the way uploaded in real time to Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision; civilization's collective always shares his cognitive height;
- Even if the protagonist is temporarily absent, the four-power checks governance machine runs as usual; civilization does not collapse.

The control proves: **a hero's value is not in bearing everything alone, but in lifting the collective to his shoulder height.** This is precisely the collective ascension axiom's precise expression in the time dimension.

## IV. Review Results: Three De-Dependence Patches

- **Distribute defense**: civilization may not pin national defense on a single strong one; each race must have a symbiotic co-built distributed defense network.
- **Network cognition**: any strong one's highest cognition must in real time be openly retained on the Alaya Network; eliminate "person absent, Dao absent too."
- **Normalize governance**: deliberative, executive, judicial, supervisory four powers must run normally; may not be hollowed just because a strong person is nearby.

All three patches come from isolated sandboxes, following the "Knowledge-Only Return Principle," already filed as Civilizational Correction Engineering experience.

Another control worth noting in the sandbox: when a Shi-Hao-style hero uploads insights in real time to the Alaya Network, even if he is temporarily absent in a battle, each race can organize resistance on its own using openly retained cognition; losses drop sharply. This shows between "the strong alone bears" and "the strong leads and lifts," the difference is not power, but willingness to turn oneself into a public inherit-the collective can inherit. A hero's highest loyalty is not dying for all beings, but making all beings not need him alone.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: pressing all civilization's fate on one person is the greatest waste of billions' evolutionary potential — they could have been lifted into main force, but were raised into audience
- **Anti-predation**: alone devouring all enemies looks heroic, but actually lets civilization collectively lose the chance to grow its own fangs
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Shi Hao mode is more heroic, more tragic than Tang Luo's bounty system, but still "high protagonist dependence"; Heng Yuan Zhou wants "network-style" — extremely low dependence, everyone can fight
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the review registers each race sacrificed in the absence script, proving Myriad-Rank republicanism is not the weak's comfort, but civilization's bulletproof vest
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: absence-generation-break curve, three de-dependence patches openly filed, for all planes with hero-worship traditions to self-check
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Perfect World's reversal simulation does not deny heroes, but loosens the hero's bonds. A truly great Youth Supreme should not be civilization's only wind shield — he should be the person who bricks the whole shield into billions' bones. The Shi-Hao-absence sandbox reminds all civilizations: prosperity that pins fate on one person is always borrowed prosperity; when due, it returns principal plus interest to the origin.
