## Introduction: when cultivation treats the entire ecosystem as hunting ground, the ecosystem's revenge waits at the time axis's end

The Douluo system's cultivation foundation is "hunt spirit beasts, take spirit rings to advance." This means the whole spirit-master civilization defines the other half of the living world — spirit beasts — as pure consumables. What reversal simulation must deduce is this structure's time endpoint: **when spirit beasts are hunted near extinction, and spirit-master civilization loses advancement raw material, along which direction does it slide on the time axis?** This is a specimen case about "energy-cycle rupture": the day the stock a civilization relies on to advance is eaten dry by itself, is the day reversal begins.

Six top-level axioms run through the review: anti-predation axiom directly points to "taking spirit rings" as hunting stock, not developing increments; republican axiom of Myriad Races questions on what basis spirit beasts as sentient life are wholely treated as consumables; collective ascension axiom points out true evolution should not be built on another race's extinction; causal self-discipline axiom constrains deduction to run only in Quest-Sediment-Domain sandboxes; law-first axiom requires the "hunt — stock depletion — advancement gap — civilizational reversal" energy causal chain defined first by law. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Structural Death-Knot of Hunt-Style Advancement

After sandbox restoration, Douluo civilization's advancement system and spirit-beast population form a closed energy cycle (Document 15), but the direction is one-way extraction:

1. **Advancement depends on hunting**: every realm breakthrough needs a new spirit ring; raw material only comes from living spirit beasts.
2. **Irreversible population shrink**: high-tier spirit beasts are already rare, slow-growing; after targeted hunting, populations cannot naturally recover.
3. **No alternative increment**: the system never developed "artificial spirit rings" or energy-cycle alternatives; entirely fed by the hunting ground.

Thus the longer the time, the emptier the hunting ground. This is not some spirit master's evil, but the entire advancement economy mining a sentient ecosystem as infinite stock.

## II. Collapse Trajectory: Four-Step Eco-Civilization Double Reversal

The sandbox deduces the civilization curve after spirit-beast populations fall below critical:

| 1 | High-tier spirit beasts extinct | The top advancement starts "no rings to take" |

Most worth reviewing is Step 2: **when the hunting ground is empty, hunters start hunting hunters.** This is every predatory-school civilization's common turning point — after stock depletion, predation target turns from outside to inside; civilization worsens from "external predation" to "mutual slaughter."
