# Entry 539 | Historical-Reversal Simulation | Dragon-Ball World | Civilizational Nihilism After Power Inflation

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 84, 87, 04, 29

## Introduction: when power breaks through everything, what remains of civilization

On Dragon Ball's time axis there is a unique kind of reversal — it does not rely on war or ecological collapse, but on **endless power inflation**: the strong one's combat power doubles again and again; planets shatter like bubbles under fist winds; civilization's every institution, emotion, memory is diluted in the chase of "the next stronger enemy" to nearly zero. What reversal simulation asks is not "will civilization be smashed," but one layer deeper: **when power inflates to the point of casually erasing planets, why does civilization slide on the time axis toward a seemingly abundant but actually nihilistic regression?**

Six top-level axioms are the review's highest criteria: anti-predation axiom directly points to this "get stronger — destroy — get stronger — destroy" cycle's essence as unbridled release of stock; republican axiom of Myriad Races questions why billions of beings are just backdrops when the strong duel; collective ascension axiom points out that power, if not lifting civilization as a whole, is just making bigger ruins; causal self-discipline axiom constrains deduction to run only in Quest-Sediment-Domain sandboxes; law-first axiom requires the "power inflation — meaning emptied — civilizational nihilism" causal chain deduced first by law, not plot casually blowing up planets. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Structural Features of Power Inflation

The sandbox restores Dragon-Ball-style growth's structural features:

1. **Enemy ladder infinitely raised**: every enemy defeated, immediately a new enemy an order of magnitude stronger appears; civilization is forever dragged running forward.
2. **Power decoupled from construction**: all energy invested in "defeating the next opponent"; almost none on civilization construction, knowledge accumulation, ecological maintenance.
3. **Meaning hollowed by battle**: the entire reason to live becomes "don't be killed by a stronger enemy"; individuals and civilization both lose thickness beyond battle.

This is a paradox of "the faster it moves, the emptier it stops": civilization on the surface moves forward fast, but actually continuously reverses at the spiritual and institutional level.

## II. The Three-Segment Curve of Nihilistic Reversal

The sandbox slices this hidden reversal into three segments:

| Segment | Stage | Reversal Signs |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| I | Battle normalized | Peace becomes brief intervals; civilization construction always yields to war-prep |
| II | Individuals hollowed | The strong have almost no life content besides getting stronger; ordinary people become backdrop |
| III | Civilizational records disappear | No Alaya Network retains civilizational memory; history reshuffled after every great war |

Segment III is most worth reviewing: Dragon-Ball-style civilization has fought too many battles enough to destroy planets, but can almost not leave a complete civilizational history — because there is no systematic record; all meaning dissipates with the battlefield. **Civilization is blown apart spatially, erased temporally.**

## III. Control Experiment: Direct Power Toward Construction

The sandbox ran a key control: replace the "endless enemy ladder" with "power used for incremental development and Myriad-Rank co-building" — same combat power, used to transform planets, sustain ecosystems, publicly train systems. Result:

- Civilization is no longer chased, but begins to accumulate: technology, institutions, art, emotional memories continuously sediment;
- The strong one's power becomes a tool to lift Myriad Ranks, not a fist to destroy planets;
- The Alaya Network completely records every major event; civilization has continuous temporal memory.

The control proves: **the root of Dragon-Ball-style nihilism is not enemies too strong, but power's only outlet is "destruction."** Change the outlet to construction, and the same power turns from civilization's poison into civilization's fuel. This precisely answers the proposition "acceleration ≠ hollow": if fast is not anchored by sedimentation, fast itself is a kind of reversal.

## IV. Review Results: Three Anti-Nihilism Indicators

- **Power-outlet indicator**: monitor whether top strong ones' power mainly flows to destruction or construction; flow to destruction triggers warning.
- **Civilizational-memory retention indicator**: monitor whether this civilization has an Alaya-style continuous recording system; eliminate "fight and forget."
- **Non-battle thickness indicator**: evaluate whether individuals and civilization have institutional, artistic, emotional sediment thickness beyond battle; thinner is more dangerous.

Three indicators connected to the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision dashboard. All knowledge comes from isolated sandboxes, following the "Knowledge-Only Return Principle."

The sandbox's most desolate scene: a strongest warrior wins the final battle enough to destroy a galaxy, stands alone on scorched earth, but cannot remember what he left along the way besides getting stronger. He has no enemies anymore, nor stories. This image is repeatedly cited because it precisely draws power-inflation's endpoint: when every external goal is flattened by power, what remains is not freedom, but boundless emptiness. Why the Quest Sediment Domain is medicine for this kind of civilization is precisely because it forces people, outside of fast, to relearn slowness and remembering themselves.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: investing all power enough to transform planets into "defeat the next enemy" is the ultimate waste of cosmic-scale energy
- **Anti-predation**: destruction in endless battle is essentially unbridled consumption of the stock world; inflation is not development, it is overdraw
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's reversal is institutional collapse; Dragon Ball's reversal is spiritual emptiness; Heng Yuan Zhou's dual track uses the Quest Sediment Domain specifically to guard "slow thickness"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the review re-counts the billions of ordinary beings reduced to backdrop as civilizational subjects, opposing "strong duel, all beings run along"
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: nihilistic-reversal three-segment curve, three anti-nihilism indicators openly filed, for all battle civilizations' self-check
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Dragon Ball's reversal simulation reveals a most hidden kind of regression: it does not bleed, does not explode, looks passionate, but on the time axis drains civilization's meaning little by little. A civilization that uses all its energy to "not be killed by a stronger one," even if forever undefeatable, is already not a living civilization — it is just a still-running, memoryless battle machine. Heng Yuan Zhou uses the Origin Acceleration Domain to manage "fast" and the Quest Sediment Domain to guard "deep," precisely so civilization is neither knocked down nor hollowed out.
