# Entry 540 | Historical-Reversal Simulation | Wilderness Era World — Ecological Collapse of Predatory Cultivation

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 84, 87, 04, 15

## Introduction: when the entire plane is treated as a furnace to tear down and re-refine

Wilderness-style cultivation pushes "seizing heaven-and-earth's creation" to the extreme: spirit veins, cave-heavens, all-things' origin can be taken for me; mighty ones open sky and earth by smashing the old and re-refining a batch. What reversal simulation must deduce is this "predatory cultivation"'s time endpoint: **when cultivators treat the entire plane's spiritual qi and ecosystem as infinitely mineable ore, along which direction does the plane itself slide on the time axis?** It is the mirror of Douluo's "hunting-ground theory" — Douluo hunts sentient spirit beasts; Wilderness refines insentient heaven and earth, but structurally both eat stock.

Six top-level axioms run through the review: anti-predation axiom directly points to "seizing heaven-and-earth's creation" as predating the plane's stock, not developing increments; republican axiom of Myriad Races points out the myriad spirits nurtured by mountains, rivers, and spirit veins are not ownerless things; collective ascension axiom opposes "one general's merit with ten thousand bones withered, one immortal's merit with ten thousand lands withered"; causal self-discipline axiom constrains deduction to run only in Quest-Sediment-Domain sandboxes; law-first axiom requires the "mine — deplete — ecological collapse — cultivation loses foundation" energy causal chain defined first by law. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Three Layers of Overdraw in Predatory Cultivation

The sandbox restores three layers of overdraw in Wilderness-style cultivation:

1. **Spirit-vein overdraw**: top-grade spirit veins repeatedly extracted, severed; spiritual-qi density declines generation by generation; later generations' cultivation start gets harder and harder.
2. **Cave-heaven overdraw**: spiritual mountains and cave mansions occupied by mighty ones, enclosed as private property; ordinary cultivators have nowhere to go even for qi-rich places (the Great Dao occupied by kings).
3. **All-things-origin overdraw**: alchemy and artifact refining directly extract heavenly treasures' origin; the ecological chain shrinks synchronously on the three layers of "medicine field — spirit beast — spirit vein."

Three layers stacked; cultivation civilization seems strong ones emerge one after another, but actually makes the plane underfoot thinner and thinner.

## II. Ecological Collapse Curve: The Trilogy of Qi Decline

The sandbox deduces the civilization curve after spiritual-qi density falls below the cultivation threshold:

| Curve | Stage | Reversal Signs |
|-------|-------|----------------|
| I | Spirit veins depleted | High-tier veins successively go silent; mighty ones go out "to seek other treasure lands" |
| II | Cultivation generation-break | Native spiritual qi insufficient; new generations cannot even enter; native cultivation civilization shrinks |
| III | Plane barren | Lost spiritual nourishment; mountains wither, all things fade; once-cultivation holy lands become wasteland |

Most worth reviewing is Stage I's "go out seeking other treasure lands": after a plane is squeezed dry, predators do not reflect on structure, but replicate predatory means onto the next plane — this is precisely the time origin of cross-plane predatory colonization. **While reversing on its own plane, it sows reversal seeds onto more planes.**

## III. Control Experiment: Life-Cycle System's Incremental Spirit-Nourishing

The sandbox ran a key control: replace predatory mining with the Life-Cycle System (second Origin system) — spiritual qi is no longer extracted, but regarded as recyclable, renewable public resource; through the symbiotic network's incremental development, continuously nourish veins, nurture spirits, protect ecosystems. Result:

- Spirit veins get richer the more used; cave-heavens open to all beings (all-level Great-Dao openness);
- Cultivators symbiotic with ecosystems; alchemy and artifact refining use artificial synthesis to replace predating origins;
- Plane spiritual-qi density steadily rises; native new generations have roads to cultivate.

The control proves: **the root of Wilderness-style collapse is not "heaven-earth spiritual qi is finite," but treating spiritual qi as stock to be robbed, not nourished.** Nourished spiritual qi is inexhaustible; robbed spiritual qi empties the more it is robbed.

## IV. Review Results: Three Nourish-Take Balance Indicators

- **Spirit-vein regeneration-rate indicator**: monitor this civilization's spiritual-qi consumption vs regeneration ratio; long-term consumption greater than regeneration triggers warning.
- **Cave-heaven openness indicator**: monitor whether spiritual mountains and cave mansions are enclosed as private property by a few mighty ones; maintain Great-Dao openness.
- **Ecological coupling indicator**: monitor whether alchemy and artifact refining destroy ecosystems as raw material; mandate shift to artificial-synthesis incremental paths.

Three indicators connected to the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision reversal dashboard. All knowledge comes from isolated sandboxes, following the "Knowledge-Only Return Principle."

The sandbox also recorded a secondary consequence often ignored: after spirit veins deplete, what first withers is not mighty ones, but the low-tier beings living off mountain spiritual qi — insects, grasses, mountain sprites. No one speaks for them; they quietly vanish; by the time mighty ones notice below the mountain is barren, it is long past remedy. This observation is written as an ecological-ethics principle: **to see whether a cultivation civilization is healthy, do not look at how many mighty ones it has, but look at whether the low-tier beings under its feet are faring well.** Civilization's confidence lies precisely in the most inconspicuous places.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: treating a whole plane as a furnace to re-refine is the ultimate waste of cosmic-scale ecology — ore dug empty, not even reconstruction capital left
- **Anti-predation**: "seizing heaven-and-earth's creation" is stock predation wrapped in immortal glamour; the sandbox proves it inevitably leads to plane-level ecological collapse
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Wilderness is more "grand" than Tang Luo and Douluo, but cannot escape the fate of eating stock; Heng Yuan Zhou uses the Life-Cycle System to swap fuel at the root
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the review brings plant spirits and planet consciousness nurtured by mountains and rivers into symbiotic accounting, opposing treating heaven and earth as private property
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: qi-decline trilogy, three nourish-take balance indicators openly filed, for all cultivation planes' self-check
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Wilderness Era's reversal simulation tells all cultivation civilizations: the premise that you can "seize heaven-and-earth's creation" today is that tomorrow there is still heaven-and-earth for you to seize. Cultivators who dig the plane underfoot as a mine will eventually find themselves standing at the bottom of the pit they dug. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is not "cultivation forbidden," but use the Life-Cycle System to replace predatory mining with incremental nourishing — making spirit veins richer the more cultivated, cave-heavens wider the more opened; cultivation's endpoint is not a barren wasteland, but a lifted, nurtured, endlessly living Myriad-Rank home.
