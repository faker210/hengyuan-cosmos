# Entry 541 | Historical-Reversal Simulation | Generic Reversal Trajectory of Predatory-School Civilizations

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 84, 87, 15

## Introduction: strip off each one's skin, all predatory-school civilizations share the same skeleton

The preceding seven reversal sandboxes, from Tang Luo, Shrouding-the-Heavens, Perfect World, Douluo, Dragon Ball to Wilderness, vary in surface: some collapse in bounty vacuum, some perish in Almighty burial, some dry in ecological exhaustion, some hollow in power inflation. But stacking their time-axis curves together, researchers found an unsettling fact — **strip off each one's skin, all predatory-school civilizations share the same skeleton, sliding along the same generic reversal trajectory.** This entry distills that generic trajectory as the master diagnostic model for subsequent Civilizational Correction Engineering.

Six top-level axioms are the distillation's highest criteria: anti-predation axiom is this trajectory's fundamental reason for existing; republican axiom of Myriad Races is its antidote; collective ascension axiom is its mirror negative; causal self-discipline axiom constrains all distillation to run only in Quest-Sediment-Domain sandboxes; law-first axiom requires the generic trajectory as a causal model defined first by law, not altered by individual planes' accidental performance. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Generic Skeleton: Three Segments, Five Nodes

After cross-sandbox comparison, predatory-school civilization's reversal compresses into a "three-segment five-node" generic model:

| Segment | Node | Generic Mechanism |
|---------|------|-------------------|
| Segment 1: Eating Stock | ① Predatory Start | Opens with slaughter, devouring, enclosure; energy from stock |
| Segment 1 | ② Monopoly Reinforcement | Great Dao secretly hoarded, classes stratified; the strong lock the evolution channel |
| Segment 2: Turning Inward | ③ Stock Peaks | Predatable objects deplete; external dividends vanish |
| Segment 2 | ④ Turn Inward | Predation target shifts from outside to inside; mutual slaughter begins |
| Segment 3: Total Settlement | ⑤ Reversal Erupts | Institutions collapse, knowledge breaks, ecosystems wither; slide along time axis |

Five nodes in each concrete plane have different names (Tang Luo calls it "bounty vacuum," Shrouding-the-Heavens "Dark Turmoil," Douluo "spirit-beast extinction," Wilderness "spirit-vein depletion"), but the mechanism is identical. This is precisely the law-first axiom's victory: **setting self-consistency over plot convenience; structure does not change because of story packaging.**

## II. Why "Generic": Three Invariants

The generic trajectory holds cross-plane because predatory-school civilizations share three invariants:

1. **Energy direction invariant**: always "extract — concentrate — consume," not "develop — cycle — increment." Once energy direction is extraction, stock bottoming is a matter of time, not luck.
2. **Knowledge direction invariant**: always "secret hoard — monopolize — buried with the strong," not "open — network — retain across generations." Once knowledge is secretly hoarded, generation break is a matter of time.
3. **Governance direction invariant**: always "strong-person backstop — class stratification — no one corrects errors," not "four-power checks — Alaya records — continuous review." Once governance relies on a strong person, after reversal no one slams the brakes.

Three invariants are like three gravitational lines, pulling all predatory-school civilizations toward the same sliding curve. This is why changing protagonist, changing map, changing packaging cannot save them — same soup, different medicine.

## III. Mirror Comparison of Generic Trajectory and Symbiotic Civilization

Juxtaposing the generic trajectory and the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's time vector yields a clean set of mirrors:

| Dimension | Predatory Generic Trajectory | Anchor Symbiotic Civilization |
|-----------|-------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Energy | Extract stock | Develop increments; Myriad-Rank origin bidirectional cycle |
| Knowledge | Secret hoard, monopolize | Great Dao all-level open; Alaya network-wide sharing |
| Governance | Strong-person backstop | Four-power checks; Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision |
| Time | Thinner the more used | Dual-track sutured; thicker the more used |
| Endgame | Total settlement reversal | Collective ascension; always positive |

The mirror shows: whether a civilization reverses depends not on how many enemies it meets, or whether geniuses appear, but on what direction it sets these three invariants.

## IV. Review Results: A Generic Reversal Diagnostic Table

The sandbox compresses five nodes into a diagnostic table usable on any plane:

- **① Predatory-start self-check**: does this civilization's core energy come from slaughter and devouring?
- **② Monopoly-reinforcement self-check**: are the Great Dao and cultivation grounds secretly hoarded and enclosed by a few?
- **③ Stock-peak self-check**: are externally predatable objects decreasing?
- **④ Turn-inward self-check**: have "hunter hunts hunter" involution signs appeared?
- **⑤ Reversal-eruption self-check**: are institutions, knowledge, ecosystems showing irreversible decline?

Any one lighting a red light indicates the civilization has slid into some node of the generic trajectory; immediately launch Civilizational Correction Engineering (from Entry 542). This table all comes from cross-plane induction in isolated sandboxes, following the "Knowledge-Only Return Principle."

## Conclusion

The generic reversal trajectory of predatory-school civilizations is Heng Yuan Zhou's ultimate mathematical answer to "can predation ultimately win": no. Not because some hero is not strong enough, or some plane not rich enough, but because "eating stock, secretly hoarding knowledge, strong-person backstop" — these three invariants, on a long enough time axis, inevitably pull any civilization toward total settlement. Acknowledging this generic trajectory is not pessimism, but using it in reverse as a map: as long as the three invariants' directions are corrected one by one, any civilization can turn from the sliding curve to the ascension curve. This, is what Civilizational Correction Engineering must do.
