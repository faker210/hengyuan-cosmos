# Entry 545 | Civilizational Correction Engineering | The Law-First Axiom's Core Role in Correction

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 09, 97, 04, 89

## Introduction: what correction fears most is not failing to find errors, but messing up the laws to correct them

Civilizational Correction Engineering has a unique risk: it is itself a powerful cross-plane intervention capability. If the intervenor, to "save one civilization," casually alters that plane's underlying laws, what it saves may be a **freak whose laws contradict each other, liable to secondary collapse at any moment** — this is the realistic version of "plot kidnapping setting" and "retcon-and-bad-end." For this reason, among the six top-level axioms, the **law-first axiom** occupies the core position in correction engineering: law first, never retcon, never bad-end; setting self-consistency over plot convenience. The sixth axiom, the universal equal infinite law, establishes the origin logic that all beings inherently possess basic infinite power and predation loses meaning. This entry specifically discusses this axiom's ballast role in correction.

The other four axioms are also present: anti-predation axiom forbids "crude rescue" that disrupts laws as means; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires laws after repair still open to all beings; collective ascension axiom requires repaired laws support civilization's long-term ascension; causal self-discipline axiom requires law changes themselves be the largest causal operation, subject to the most careful filing and isolation; law-first axiom is all of this's highest criterion.

## I. Why Correction Most Easily "Retcons"

The sandbox shows a considerable proportion of failed correction interventions are not "wrong direction," but "broke the laws":

1. **Temporary patches**: to stop a reversal sign, temporarily stack a conflicting new rule, causing internal contradiction in the law system.
2. **Plot-style rescue**: to save a key person, temporarily relax his causal limits, breaking equal-application law consistency.
3. **Local optimum, global collapse**: a prescription effective in one plane copied to another plane with different law structure; poor fit instead accelerates collapse.

This is precisely "plot kidnapping setting"'s cross-plane-scale replica — for "plot" (this correction's success), sacrifice "setting" (law self-consistency).

## II. Three Gates of Law-First in Correction

The law-first axiom folds the correction process into three gates:

| Gate | Content | Prevents |
|------|---------|----------|
| Gate 1 | Model the laws first, then talk intervention | Prevents ad-hoc law changes |
| Gate 2 | Changes must pass setting-self-consistency review | Prevents patches conflicting |
| Gate 3 | Changes fully validated in isolated sandboxes | Prevents local prescription global collapse |

Only when all three gates pass, may a law-level correction land on the real plane. The Setting-Contradiction Self-Check Table (Document 97) is these three gates' concrete tool.

## III. Self-Consistency Review: Correction-Version "Retcon-and-Bad-End Risk" Assessment

Module 2 does "retcon-and-bad-end risk" assessment on heaven-and-earth systems; correction engineering applies this ability in reverse to itself — any proposed law change must first answer:

- Does this change conflict with existing laws?
- Will it produce unforeseen paradoxes in long-term deduction (link Document 89)?
- Is it still "reality-based" (Document 09), not an out-of-thin-air illusory rule?
- Can it support civilization's infinite expansion without collapsing?

Any one answered "no," the change is returned. This ensures correction engineering never, for "this time saved beautifully," buries the next larger collapse for civilization.

## IV. Law-First's Interlocking With the Other Four Axioms

Law-first is not isolated "technical purity," it tightly interlocks with the other four axioms:

- **With anti-predation**: law-self-consistent incremental development mechanism is more sustainable than "temporarily opening a predation cheat";
- **With Myriad-Rank republicanism**: once laws break precedent for one race, it breaks all beings' equal qualification;
- **With collective ascension**: only self-consistent laws can support civilization's infinite ascension; patched laws collapse halfway;
- **With causal self-discipline**: law changes themselves are the highest-level causal operation, requiring the strictest observe-first, isolation, filing.

Five axioms in correction engineering are not five side-by-side slogans, but a mutually locked net — remove any one, the other four loosen.

This ballast design in practice once saved an emergency: to stop bleeding fast, an executor proposed temporarily relaxing a causal-isolation rule to inject knowledge faster. Among the three gates, the self-consistency review on the spot stopped it — the sandbox showed the temporary relaxation would trigger a larger causal paradox three epochs later. So the engineering preferred to be half a beat slow, refusing to bury a timed bomb for civilization. This case later became the law-first axiom's most-cited footnote.

## V. How Law-First Avoids "The More You Save, The Messier It Gets"

Civilizational correction's most easily made mistake is rushing to see effects and skipping self-consistency review. The sandbox recorded a failed case: a team to quickly stabilize a collapsing plane, without adequate modeling, stacked three temporary rules; the three rules conflicted, and three months later the plane fell into a more complex paradox. Post-review, the root cause was not wrong direction, but acting before laws were self-consistent. The law-first axiom here plays the role of "half a beat slow" — prefer letting civilization slide a bit more, but before acting ensure the prescription carries no poison. This seeming delay is precisely the greatest responsibility.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: law-self-consistent one-time correct change, avoiding "every change collapses, repeated rework" civilizational-level waste
- **Anti-predation**: refuse "temporarily opening predation cheats to rescue"; insist replacing with self-consistent incremental laws
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style systems are precisely typical "plot kidnapping setting," for the thrill constantly breaking self-consistency bottom lines; correction engineering with law-first goes the opposite way entirely
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: laws uniformly apply to all beings, no exception for the strong, guaranteeing 99%-coverage fairness
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: three gates, self-consistency review checklist all public, becoming the common code of all cross-plane interventions
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The law-first axiom in Civilizational Correction Engineering plays the "ballast stone" role. It reminds all forces wanting to save civilization: saving people cannot turn them into monsters; correcting errors cannot turn the world into self-contradiction. A repair that truly lasts must be, on the premise of respecting that plane's law self-consistency, the minimal, most accurate, most reversible intervention. "Never retcon, never bad-end" — these eight characters look like writing norms, but are actually the life-or-death line of whether civilization can be truly saved.

In the final analysis, the law-first axiom's core role in correction described in this document is no isolated case — it runs within Heng Yuan Zhou's overall coordinates; always points toward the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's co-building direction; its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
