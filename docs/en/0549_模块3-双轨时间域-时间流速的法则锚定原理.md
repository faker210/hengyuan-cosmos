# Entry 549 | Dual-Track Time Domain | The Law-Anchor Principle of Time Flow Rate

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 20, 24, 09, 89

## Introduction: make time flow rate obey law, not anyone's mood

The previous entry covered how to compute the flow ratio; this entry answers a more foundational question: **what exactly is this thing called "time flow rate" pinned by?** If this question is not answered, so-called "speeding up time" is just a spell — fast today if you want fast, slow tomorrow if you want slow, then it is liable at any moment to be picked up by some strong one as a predation tool. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's answer is **law anchoring**: time flow rate is not a switch controlled by some person, but written into the law network, a constant range strictly managed by anchor-structure permissions. This entry dissects this anchoring principle.

Six top-level axioms run through: anti-predation axiom requires time flow rate not become the strong one's predation tool; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires anchoring permissions fairly open to all beings, not secretly hoarded; collective ascension axiom requires anchoring serve all-civilization iteration; causal self-discipline axiom requires any flow-rate adjustment leave traces, auditable; law-first axiom — this entry is its home court — requires every gear of time flow rate defined first by law, never retcon. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. What "Law Anchoring" Means

Law anchoring means: the physical quantity time flow rate is **written into the law network**, becoming a rule constrained by law itself, not some mighty one's personal ability. It has three features:

1. **Range fixed**: law only allows flow rate to float within the statutory range (e.g., 10:1 to 1000:1); crossing the boundary is rejected by law itself, no one needed to stop it.
2. **Permissions tiered**: adjusting flow rate requires corresponding law-anchoring permission; permissions granted by civilizational weight and responsibility (Document 20), not whoever has the biggest fist.
3. **No private bypass**: any private speed-up bypassing anchoring is identified by the law network as anomaly, immediately triggering temporal-paradox circuit-break (Document 89).

This turns "adjusting time" from a power privilege into a public operation constrained by law.

## II. The Three-Layer Structure of Anchoring

Law anchoring is implemented through a three-layer structure:

| Layer | Function | Analogy |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Law Layer | Defines flow rate's statutory range and boundary-crossing rules | Statute |
| Anchor Layer | Holds and verifies law-anchoring permissions | Law-enforcement organ |
| Conduction Layer | Actually applies calibrated flow rate to the isolated domain | Execution device |

Three layers connected by the anchor-energy conduction path (Document 24). The law layer says "how fast may one go"; the anchor layer says "are you qualified to adjust"; the conduction layer is responsible for "actually turning the flow rate over." Any layer failing, flow-rate adjustment cannot complete — this is precisely the structure preventing single-point abuse.

## III. Why It Must Be "Reality-as-Foundation"

Law anchoring has another hard constraint: it calibrates **real temporal laws**, not out-of-thin-air illusions. This strictly observes the "Reality-as-Foundation Fundamental Stance" (Document 09): the beings, energy, matter entering the time domain are all real; what is calibrated is only their relative time flow rate. This is critical — if what is anchored is an illusion, then "acceleration" becomes fake experience, deceiving both the individual and civilization's output; only anchored on reality can the Acceleration Domain's technical output truly be usable, truly feed back to Myriad Ranks.

## IV. Anchoring Permissions and Civilizational Weight

Who can apply to adjust higher flow-rate gears depends on their **law-anchoring permission**, and permissions are linked to "civilizational weight" (that individual/civilization's contribution and responsibility to Myriad-Rank co-building), not to combat power. This is a decisive design: it means the power to adjust time does not reward "who can fight more," but rewards "who contributes more to co-building, bears more responsibility." Thus if a strong one wants higher flow-rate gears, the only road is to lift Myriad Ranks, not to predate Myriad Ranks — the power direction is completely reversed.

Once someone questioned this design "too much trouble, might as well whoever has the biggest fist adjusts." The sandbox's answer is crisp: hand the power to adjust time to fists; within three epochs, the Acceleration Domain will become the strong ones' private property; all beings left further and further behind — that is precisely Tang-Luo-style monopoly replayed in time engineering. Law anchoring looks like a few more procedures, but actually swaps monopoly for procedures, swaps the jungle for rules. Slower to get started, but stable enough to use forever.

## V. When Anchoring Fails

Law anchoring is not infallible. Once anchor devices drift due to cross-plane interference, the system's first reaction is not "continue forcing the flow rate," but immediately slow down, fall back to a safety gear consistent with reality, then the anchor calibration team investigates. This "prefer slow down, not go wrong" downgrade strategy is the most critical fuse in the anchoring design. It acknowledges engineering will fail, so designs the failure cost as "return to origin," not "run with the error." Steady is always faster than accurate.

- **Anti-energy waste**: law anchoring locks flow rate within the statutory range, eliminating law-network overload and energy waste from private overclocking

The law-anchor principle of time flow rate answers the foundational question of "who manages time": not the strongest, but law itself. When time flow rate is written into the law network, strictly managed by anchor permissions, firmly pinned by the reality-as-foundation principle, "fast" is no longer a predatable privilege, but a publicly constrained, contribution-allocated civilizational engineering. The law-first axiom here is not a slogan, but lets the beast of time for the first time wear law's reins.
