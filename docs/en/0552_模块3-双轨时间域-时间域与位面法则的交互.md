# Entry 552 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Interaction Between the Time Domain and Plane Laws

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 80, 81, 09, 89

## Introduction: pack a 100× acceleration domain into a plane with different laws, what happens

The dual-track time domain does not float in a vacuum; it runs on **concrete planes**. Different planes have different law structures (Document 80): some energy-rich, some spatial rules peculiar, some time constants naturally different. Slip the Origin Acceleration Domain into a plane with a vastly different law structure; will the time domain's design be a poor fit? Between it and that plane's laws, is it master-slave, superposition, or mutual interference? This entry covers the interaction principle between the time domain and plane laws.

Six top-level axioms run through: anti-predation axiom requires the time domain not use "technical advantage" to bypass that plane's laws and indirectly predate; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires after interaction that plane's beings still enjoy equal qualification; collective ascension axiom requires the time domain serve that plane's civilizational lifting; causal self-discipline axiom requires interaction not perturb that plane's inherent causality; law-first axiom — home court once again here — requires the time domain always obey plane laws, not override them. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Interaction's General Principle: Time Domain Obeys Plane Laws

One general principle runs through all interactions: **the time domain is an "application layer" slipped over plane laws; plane laws are the bottom layer; the application layer may never rewrite the bottom layer.** This is consistent with the "Reality-as-Foundation Fundamental Stance" (Document 09). Concrete manifestations:

- The plane's energy rules, spatial rules, life-death rules are not overturned by the time domain;
- The time domain only calibrates the one parameter "time flow rate"; all other laws remain that plane's original;
- If some plane's laws fundamentally conflict with time-domain calibration, then that plane is unsuitable for opening an acceleration domain; better no than forced.

This principle eliminates the danger of "the strong forcibly rewriting others' world rules via time technology."

## II. Three Typical Interaction Situations

The sandbox归纳出 three typical situations of time-domain and plane-law interaction:

| Situation | Plane Feature | Interaction Result | Countermeasure |
|-----------|---------------|--------------------|----------------|
| Compatible | Conventional laws, energy-rich | Time domain smoothly superimposed | Normally open; monitor throughout |
| Adaptive | Peculiar laws (e.g., naturally high time constant) | Need recalibrating flow ratio | Recompute gears by that plane's constant |
| Conflict | Laws fundamentally opposed to time calibration | Superposition = collapse | Do not open; use Quest Domain slow-track review instead |

Three situations jointly show: **the time domain is not a universal plugin; it must humbly adapt to each plane's law personality.** This is precisely the law-first axiom — plane laws above time-engineering convenience.

## III. The Causal Firewall in Interaction

The time domain superimposed on a plane's biggest risk is "leaking" the Acceleration Domain's causal overheating into that plane's inherent causal flow. The interaction design uses a **causal firewall** to separate the two:

- High-density causal forks within the acceleration domain are confined to isolated space (Document 81's shuttling rules also require isolation);
- Only abstracted knowledge results allowed to cross the firewall back;
- Once causal-leakage signal is detected, immediately slow down or evacuate, prioritizing protecting the plane's inherent causality.

This makes the time domain like a "transparent cultivation hood over the plane" — acceleration happens inside the hood; the world outside the hood runs as usual, undisturbed.

## IV. Interaction Exit and Return

When some plane's acceleration-domain task is complete, the time domain does not "use it and leave, leaving a mess," but goes through the complete exit-and-return process:

1. Slow down to that plane's time constant;
2. Dismantle isolated space, restore that plane's law purity;
3. Return intra-domain output per joint contracting to that plane's beings;
4. Register on the Alaya Network all causal records of this interaction, for post-audit.

Clean exit is interaction ethics' last mile. It ensures the time domain is always that plane's civilization's "boost," not an "invasion" leaving aftereffects.

Worth mentioning is one "active evacuation": some plane performed well in the adaptation phase, but after a while its native laws evolved on their own to no longer be compatible with the acceleration domain. The team did not force maintenance, but per contingency plan orderly evacuated, slowed, returned output, letting the plane return to its own law rhythm. Some felt it a pity — after all quite an investment. But the law-first axiom decides: since the laws changed, let the plane walk itself. This restraint of "evacuate when told" wins more trust from plane beings than the technique of "lingering."

## V. "Understand the Plane's Language" in Interaction

Dealing with plane laws, technical skill alone is not enough; you also must learn to "listen." Each plane's laws have their own "temper" — some sensitive to energy disturbance, some zero-tolerance to causal rewriting. Before an interaction team takes post, it must first fully converse with that plane's laws in the sandbox, feel out its tolerance boundary, before daring to truly superimpose the time domain. This humility is written into protocol: no team may rush to station before "listening enough." Respecting a plane begins with first understanding its laws.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: adapt gears per plane laws, avoiding the double waste of "forcing acceleration in an incompatible plane"
- **Anti-predation**: the time domain obeys plane laws, does not use technical advantage to rewrite rules and indirectly predate
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo strong ones may wander into other worlds at will, rewriting rules to their own liking; Heng Yuan Zhou's time domain maintains humility toward each plane's laws
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: after interaction exit, output returned to that plane's all beings, not intercepted by strength
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: three interaction-situation classifications, causal-firewall norms, exit-return process all public
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Interaction between the time domain and plane laws tests a rare quality: **powerful yet humble.** A civilization with a hundredfold acceleration ability, yet knows to place itself in the application layer, letting each plane's bottom-layer laws continue to rule. This humility is not weakness, but the law-first axiom's true maturity — it knows any time operation wanting to override law will eventually be backlashed by law itself. A time domain that respects plane laws is a time domain civilization can safely rely on.
