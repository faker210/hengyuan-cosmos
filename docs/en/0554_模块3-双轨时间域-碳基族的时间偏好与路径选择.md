# Entry 554 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Carbon-Based Races' Time Preference and Path Choice

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 50, 51, 52, 16

## Introduction: finite-lifespan carbon-based life best knows "time is precious," and most fears "time is empty"

Among Myriad Races' five gene lineages (Document 50), the carbon-based matter race — human race, exotic beasts, plant spirits, many flesh-and-blood beings — has relatively the shortest lifespan, most sensitive to time's passage. Their preference in the dual-track time domain is highly representative: both extremely craving the "live more, learn more, do more" the acceleration domain brings, and fearing more than anyone else that after over-speeding they "go hollow." This entry analyzes the carbon-based race's time preference, and gives their best path choice between the two tracks.

Six top-level axioms run through: anti-predation axiom requires the carbon-based race's acceleration not rely on devouring lifespan; republican axiom of Myriad Races is this entry's direct source — the carbon-based race is only one of Myriad Ranks; its path does not constitute privilege; collective ascension axiom requires the carbon-based race's time choice ultimately feed back to Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom requires its track entry and exit leave traces throughout; law-first axiom requires path parameters calibrated first by law per carbon-based physiological traits. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Carbon-Based Race's Time Portrait

Per Myriad-Rank Lineages and Foreign-Race Symbiosis (Documents 50, 52), the carbon-based matter race's time traits are three:

1. **Finite lifespan**: the flesh ages and dies; time for them is a "consumable," a little less with every use.
2. **Thick experience**: birth, aging, sickness, death, joys and sorrows endow them with an extremely thick emotional and experiential layer; this is their gift.
3. **Metabolic constraints**: consciousness may be accelerated, but the flesh's material time needs freezing protection; physiological metabolism has a hard upper limit on continuous fast-track duration.

These three determine the carbon-based race's core contradiction in the dual track: **they most need fast, but most cannot bear staying fast.**

## II. The Carbon-Based Race's Preference: Fast to Live More, Slow to Not Live in Vain

The sandbox tracks the carbon-based race's track-entry choices, finding their preference stably points to one combination:

- **Fast track used to "catch up"**: put knowledge not had time to learn, R&D not had time to do in the limited lifespan, into the Acceleration Domain to make up — this is the direct response to "time is precious."
- **Slow track used to "have lived"**: raising children, emotional bonds, epic trials, firmly kept in the near-real-flow Quest Domain — this is the guardianship of "time is thick."

In other words, the carbon-based race instinctively understands: **fast is for "doing things," slow is for "living."** Reverse the two, and it is the "lived a hundred times, yet as if never lived" they most fear.

## III. The Carbon-Based Race's Recommended Path: Do-Fast-Live-Slow Type

Accordingly, the dual-track time domain recommends the "Do-Fast-Live-Slow" path for the carbon-based race:

| Stage | Domain | Flow Rate | Content |
|-------|--------|-----------|---------|
| Learning Period | Origin Acceleration Domain | 100× | Concentrate on absorbing public techniques and deductions |
| Growth Period | Quest Sediment Domain | Near-Real | Youth-Supreme-style trials; accumulate experience thickness |
| Output Period | Origin Acceleration Domain | On-Demand | Invest sedimented insights into R&D output |
| Feedback Period | Dual-Track Alternating | Flexible | Lift own race; return to Alaya Network |

The key is the "Growth Period" — the carbon-based race's Youth-Supreme trials firmly stay on the slow track. This both guards their most precious experience thickness, and avoids the common "grow up fast, hollow inside" ailment.

## IV. Two Special Protections for the Carbon-Based Race

The carbon-based race has finite lifespan; the dual-track time domain adds two special protections:

1. **Flesh freezing protection**: continuous fast-track duration strictly limited by physiological metabolic ceiling; at cap forced cooldown, preventing "consciousness fast, flesh wearing out";
2. **Experience-preservation priority**: when carbon-based races exit the fast track, the experience-preservation detection threshold is higher than other lineages — because their time is the most precious, they cannot afford to be hollow.

These two are not favoritism, but law calibration per carbon-based physiological traits — the law-first axiom requires parameters obey reality, not a uniform template.

One sentence a carbon-based cultivator is repeatedly reminded: **fast is borrowed time; slow is your own lifetime.** The hundred years of deduction in the Acceleration Domain is so that when you return to reality you live it out; if you only run wild on the fast track and refuse to sediment on the slow track, the borrowed time must eventually be returned, and returning it you will also owe a hollow stretch. Countless carbon-based predecessors, on the first homecoming, first reunion, first sunrise after exiting the fast track, truly understand the dual-track design's deep meaning — the ability it gives you to do more is so that you are more worthy to live that short one life.

## V. The Carbon-Based Race's Finite Is Precisely Civilization's Gift

Do not see "short lifespan" only as the carbon-based race's shortcoming. The sandbox finds: precisely because finite, the carbon-based race especially treasures time; the epics, emotions, choices it sediments are often thicker, truer than long-lived races. A life fiercely lived within finiteness, the memory it leaves, often moves even long-lived races. Thus the dual-track design does not try to "remake the carbon-based race into a long-lived race," but respects its finiteness, uses the fast track to win it time to do things, uses the slow track to let it live its finite life thickly. Finiteness is not defect; it is the carbon-based race's unique, burning time gift.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the Do-Fast-Live-Slow path puts the limited lifespan to the knife's edge, avoiding the carbon-based race "wasting its life on the wrong track"
- **Anti-predation**: the carbon-based race makes up for insufficient lifespan via consciousness acceleration, not by devouring others' lifespan
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo only lets the protagonist be "fast," mortals "slow" all their lives; Heng Yuan Zhou lets every carbon-based individual autonomously choose the Do-Fast-Live-Slow path
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: human race, exotic beasts, plant spirits, all carbon-based beings share this path, no distinction by strength
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Do-Fast-Live-Slow path table, two physiological-protection parameters all public, each ethnic group takes as needed
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The carbon-based race's time preference is precisely the most vivid footnote to the proposition "acceleration ≠ hollow." The shorter-lived the being, the more it understands slowness's preciousness — it wants fast, to do a bit more; it wants slow, to truly have lived once. The dual-track time domain's kindness to the carbon-based race is essentially acknowledgment that "finite life is also worth being treated solemnly." In Myriad-Rank Republicanism, even beings who can live only a short life both have the qualification to do things fast, and the right to live through slowly and thoroughly.
