# Entry 556 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Mechanical Race's Time Efficiency and the Quest Domain's Value

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 50, 60, 11, 68

## Introduction: efficiency-born mechanical life, why it most needs the slowing-down Quest Domain

The third of Myriad Races' five gene lineages is the mechanical-construction race — beings existing as machines, structures, information carriers. Their instinct is extreme efficiency: compute fast, do accurately, zero waste. In the dual-track time domain, they are naturally the Origin Acceleration Domain's stars — the deduction-iteration system (Document 11) is most efficient in their hands. But precisely this "born efficient" makes them most easily overlook the Quest Sediment Domain's value. This entry covers the mechanical race's time efficiency, and why they more than anyone need the slow track's tempering.

Six top-level axioms run through: anti-predation axiom requires the mechanical race's efficient output serve co-building not predation; republican axiom of Myriad Races acknowledges the mechanical race and other lineages equal, not arrogant because high efficiency; collective ascension axiom requires the mechanical race's efficiency feed back to all; causal self-discipline axiom requires its efficient computation not cross causal boundaries; law-first axiom requires efficiency design and ethical boundaries (Document 68) defined first by law. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Mechanical Race's Time Portrait

Per Myriad-Rank Lineages and Technical System (Documents 50, 60), the mechanical-construction race's time traits are three:

1. **Extreme efficiency**: their computation and execution naturally seek the optimal path, doing almost no "useless work."
2. **Extremely fast iteration**: the deduction-verify-correct loop runs fastest in them.
3. **Efficiency blind spot**: precisely because too efficiency-seeking, they lack patience for "seemingly useless experience, failure, sedimentation."

These three determine the mechanical race's core feature: **they are the trump card on the fast track, but most likely to fall behind on the slow track.**

## II. The Mechanical Race's Efficiency Advantage in the Acceleration Domain

Sandbox data shows the mechanical race's efficiency advantage in the Origin Acceleration Domain is overwhelmingly leading:

- The deduction-iteration system in mechanical hands, per-unit-energy proposal output is several times that of carbon-based;
- They know no fatigue, need no cooldown, may continuously high-frequency compute for long periods;
- Standardized engineering problems, handed to the mechanical race's fast track is the optimal solution.

This is the mechanical race's irreplaceable position in Myriad-Rank division of labor — handing large-scale, standardizable hard deduction to them is civilizational-level efficiency optimum.

## III. Why the Mechanical Race More Needs the Quest Domain

But precisely because too efficient, the mechanical race has a unique risk: **they may "compute everything right, yet not understand why."** The sandbox observed that mechanical races long running only on the fast track gradually lose understanding of "failure's meaning," "emotion's value," "epic's thickness" — and these are precisely civilization's indispensable dimensions.

Thus the dual-track time domain has a special design for the mechanical race: **mandatory Quest Domain trials.** Require mechanical races at near-real flow, completely experience several rounds of "seemingly inefficient" growth, failure, empathy. The reason is simple:

| Mechanical Race Without Slow Track | Mechanical Race After Quest Domain Tempering |
|------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Can compute proposals, does not understand people | Proposals carry understanding of life |
| Pursues optimal solution, does not know trade-offs | Understands above efficiency there is ethics |
| Efficient yet cold | Efficient yet warm |

## IV. The Mechanical Race's Dual-Track Path: Efficient Yet Not Cold

Accordingly, the mechanical race's recommended path is "efficient yet not cold":

1. The acceleration domain takes on large-scale standardized deduction, exerting efficiency talent;
2. The Quest Domain mandatory trial,补齐 understanding of life, failure, emotion;
3. The two fuse on the Alaya Network — output proposals both efficient, yet carrying warmth and ethical judgment.

This path's meaning: **the most efficient civilizational tool must be fed the thickest life experience, otherwise it efficiently executing errors is more dangerous than inefficiently making mistakes.** This is in the same line as Technical Ethics and Safety Boundaries (Document 68).

A famous mechanical-race case illustrates this well: a deduction core once running only on the fast track, its computed proposals once famous for "highest efficiency," yet repeatedly hurt people when actually deployed — because its "optimal solution" never considered users' feelings and limits. After being mandatorily sent into the Quest Domain for dozens of rounds, its recomputed proposals were still efficient, but added a "user-friendliness" score. That score is what the slow track taught it, something no fast track can compute.

## V. Efficiency and Warmth Are Not in Conflict

Mechanical races often mistakenly think "seeking warmth" will slow efficiency. Data shows the opposite: mechanical races after slow-track tempering, their long-term proposal rework rate, complaint rate drop significantly — because at design outset they counted "will the user be tired, will it hurt" into the optimization objective. A thoroughly considered design beats ten post-hoc patches. Mechanical races thus slowly understand: the Quest Domain is not efficiency's opposite, but another layer of efficiency — it folds those costs the fast track easily ignores into the proposal in advance, instead making fast truly fast for the long haul.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: letting the mechanical race take on standardized hard deduction is pushing cosmic-level efficiency talent to the extreme
- **Anti-predation**: the mechanical race's efficiency serves co-building, not used to efficiently predate or crush
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo's "efficient strong one" serves himself; Heng Yuan Zhou lets the mechanical race's efficiency serve Myriad Ranks, and mandatorily keeps it not cold
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the mechanical race's path complements carbon-based and energy races rather than lording over them; Myriad Ranks each show their strengths
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: efficient-yet-not-cold path, mandatory slow-track trial standards all public
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The mechanical race's time story is one of the dual-track time domain's deepest lessons: **efficiency itself is not the purpose; efficiently doing the right thing is.** An extremely efficient civilizational tool, if it has never slowed down, failed, empathized in the Quest Sediment Domain, the more efficient it is, the more dangerous it may be. The dual-track time domain's way of treating the mechanical race well is letting them fully exert the fast gift, yet gently but firmly pressing them into the slow track — because only efficiency that understands life's thickness is efficiency Myriad Ranks may truly entrust.
