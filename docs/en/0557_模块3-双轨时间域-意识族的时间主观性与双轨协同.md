# Entry 557 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Consciousness Race's Time Subjectivity and Dual-Track Collaboration

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 50, 21, 58, 89

## Introduction: time for them is half objective flow, half subjective feeling

The fourth of Myriad Races' five gene lineages is the consciousness-information race — beings whose main form is consciousness, information, thought. Their most peculiar feature: **time is subjective to them.** The same objective flow, an immersed consciousness race feels "fleeting"; a scattered consciousness race feels "days drag like years." This makes them in the dual-track time domain both the most flexible and the most easily led astray. This entry covers the consciousness race's time subjectivity, and how the dual track collaborates with it.

Six top-level axioms run through: anti-predation axiom requires the consciousness race not take spiritual erosion of others' consciousness as acceleration means; republican axiom of Myriad Races acknowledges the consciousness race's consciousness independence respected; collective ascension axiom requires the consciousness race's consciousness output shared via Alaya Network; causal self-discipline axiom — especially vigilance here — requires consciousness operations not perturb others' consciousness causality; law-first axiom requires subjective-time calibration rules defined first by law. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Consciousness Race's Time Portrait

Per Myriad-Rank Lineages and Dual-Soul Ethics (Documents 50, 58), the consciousness-information race's time traits are three:

1. **Time subjectified**: their "felt time" highly depends on consciousness focus; objective flow and subjective flow may deviate greatly.
2. **Consciousness as carrier**: they have no stable material boundary; exist in the consciousness domain, may deeply interconnect with the Alaya Network.
3. **Interconnection sensitive**: connection state with other consciousnesses, with the network, directly changes their time perception and stability.

These three determine the consciousness race's core feature: **they can both perfectly collaborate with the dual track, and most easily get lost in subjective time.**

## II. The Consciousness Race's Unique Position in the Dual Track

The consciousness race plays a unique "collaborator" role in the dual track:

- In the Origin Acceleration Domain, their subjective focus may further compress felt time, the best carrier for "deep thought" in deduction iteration;
- In the Quest Sediment Domain, their subjective-experience thickness is naturally extremely strong — one trial in their consciousness may sediment extremely deep insights;
- They deeply interconnect with the Alaya Network, the natural "interface" for knowledge transfer between the two tracks.

In a sense, **the consciousness race is the dual-track time domain's binder** — knowledge exchange between fast and slow tracks is largely completed via the consciousness race.

## III. Risks: Getting Lost in Subjective Time and Erosion

But precisely because time is subjective to them, the consciousness race faces two unique risks:

| Risk | Manifestation | Consequence |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| Self-Loss | Over-immersion in subjective time, cannot distinguish inside/outside domain | Time weightlessness, identity dispersal |
| Consciousness Erosion | Invade others' consciousness under "acceleration/interconnection" name | Crosses boundary into another predation |

The second risk especially touches the ethical red line — if the consciousness race "accelerates" via spiritual influence, memory tampering, consciousness harvesting, it is essentially predation on other races' consciousness causality. The dual-track time domain strictly fortifies per Dual-Soul Ethical Principle (Document 58) and Cross-Plane Ethics (Document 88): **only share one's own consciousness output; never read or alter others' consciousness.**

## IV. How the Dual Track Collaborates With the Consciousness Race

The dual-track time domain designs "subjective-anchored" collaboration for the consciousness race:

1. **Objective flow as anchor**: no matter subjective feeling, objective time flow is still law-anchored, preventing subjective dispersion from going out of control;
2. **Consciousness-boundary isolation**: between the consciousness race and others' consciousness there is an ethical isolation belt; interconnection only shares, does not invade;
3. **Alaya as comparison**: the consciousness race's subjective time records are compared with objective time; once deviation too large, remind it to return to the anchor.

This collaboration lets the consciousness race both fully exert subjective time's flexibility, yet not get lost in subjectivity, not turn flexibility into erosion.

The consciousness race also plays a unique role in the dual track — they are "translators of time experience." That "slow-down-to-understand thickness" carbon-based race hard to express, the "one-thought-becomes-obsession" energy race feels strange, the consciousness race can capture, abstract, translate into knowledge other lineages can understand, return to Alaya Network. Without this translation link, each ethnic group's time experience always talks past each other; precisely the consciousness race's subjective sensitivity lets Myriad Ranks for the first time have a shareable "time-feeling map."

## V. Reconciliation of Subjective and Objective

The consciousness race's peculiar experience finally teaches the whole dual-track system a lesson: time has never been purely objective cold graduation; it also has a subjective side. In the past people thought "calibrate flow rate accurately" was enough; the consciousness race reminds — the same objective flow, in different moods, is completely different time. Thus the dual-track design begins to reserve room for "subjective calibration" for each individual: objective law anchoring unchanged, but allow individuals within legal ranges to fine-tune their own subjective rhythm. Objective steadiness and subjective liveliness, for the first time reconciled in the time domain.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: letting the consciousness race take on the inter-track knowledge interface, turning consciousness-interconnection talent into civilizational efficiency
- **Anti-predation**: strictly guard consciousness boundaries, eliminating spiritual erosion, memory-tampering "consciousness predation"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo strong ones may arbitrarily manipulate others' minds; Heng Yuan Zhou uses ethical isolation belts to protect each consciousness's independence
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the consciousness race's time subjectivity is respected and calibrated, interconnects equally with other lineages
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: subjective-anchor three principles, consciousness-isolation norms, dual-soul ethical principle all public
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The consciousness race's time story touches the dual-track time domain's most subtle boundary: **time is both objective engineering parameter, and subjective life experience.** To the consciousness race, respecting its subjectivity is respecting its uniqueness as life; adding objective anchors and ethical boundaries to this subjectivity is so that flexibility does not become loss, interconnection does not become erosion. The dual-track time domain and the consciousness race's collaboration ultimately points to an ideal state — consciousness may freely be fast, freely be deep, yet never harm another consciousness's freedom.
