# Entry 559 | Time Ethics | Overview — The Moral Boundaries of Time Manipulation

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 19, 88, 82, 89

## Introduction: being able to manipulate time does not mean having the right to

The dual-track time domain gives civilization for the first time the engineering ability to "operate time" — accelerate, sediment, replay, isolate-switch. The greater the ability, the clearer the boundaries must be. The special topic of **time ethics** answers precisely the line between "can do" and "ought to do": which time operations may be done, which absolutely may not, who reviews, who underwrites. Without time ethics, the dual-track time domain is a drawn blade with no sheath. This entry, as time-ethics overview, draws this moral boundary.

Six top-level axioms here become the final source of ethical boundaries: anti-predation axiom forbids using time manipulation to predate others; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all time operations respect each being's time autonomy; collective ascension axiom requires time operations serve all not individuals; causal self-discipline axiom itself is a core ethic — only knowledge returned, causality not perturbed; law-first axiom requires ethical boundaries written fixed first by law, not loosened by operator's strength. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Time Operations Need Special Ethics

The ethical risks of time operations are fundamentally different from ordinary operations:

1. **Irreversibility illusion**: once time flows, an individual's experiences, choices, personality cannot be redone; harm from misoperation cannot be "loaded from save."
2. **Concealment**: time acceleration, causal fine-tuning often happen without the individual's awareness; the victim even does not know they were operated.
3. **Power nature**: those able to operate time are almost inevitably the strong; once strong-weak superpose time differential, easily slide into the asymmetry of "god over all beings."

Precisely therefore, time ethics cannot borrow ordinary rules; it must draw boundaries specially.

## II. Three Axes of the Moral Boundary

The sandbox and Cross-Plane Ethics (Document 88)归纳出 time-operation moral boundary's three axes:

| Axis | Red Line | Violation Counts As |
|------|----------|---------------------|
| Autonomy Axis | Must not deprive the individual of knowing and choosing their own time | Forced track-entry / forced retrocognition |
| Causal Axis | Must not perturb others' causality, must not cycle-harvest souls | Rewriting causality / swallowing souls |
| Fairness Axis | Must not monopolize dividends via time differential, manufacture classes | Privileged acceleration / time monopoly |

Any time operation first passes these three axes; any axis crossed, no matter how technically feasible, ethically not allowed.

## III. The General Ethical-Review Process

The time operation's ethical boundary is held by a general process:

1. **Pre-Review**: any time-operation application first passes the Ethical Review Committee, checked against the three axes;
2. **In-Process Supervision**: operations proceed under Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision; boundary-crossing signals alarm in real time;
3. **Post-Review**: after operation ends, replay actual impact, assess whether "overdosed"; results fed back to the ethics library.

This process is in the same line as Civilizational Correction Engineering's dosiology — what can be solved at low dose, knowledge-ified, never at high dose, forced.

## IV. Time Ethics' Meshing With the Five Axioms

Time ethics is not externally imposed constraint, but the five axioms' natural extension in the time dimension:

- Anti-predation → do not steal time;
- Myriad-Rank Republicanism → time autonomy equal for all;
- Collective Ascension → time dividends shared;
- Causal Self-Discipline → do not perturb others' causality;
- Law-First → boundaries written fixed, unloosenable.

Pull out any one, time ethics loosens somewhere. This is why this special topic's following six entries unfold one by one: review, informed consent, causal unity, individual rights, critique of the predatory faction, constitutional clauses.

## V. Time Ethics and Technical Confidence Are Not in Contradiction

Some worry: drawing boundaries this tight, will it bind time technology's hands, make civilization afraid to explore? The opposite. Boundaries clear, explorers instead dare try boldly — because he clearly knows where the red line is, trial-and-error cost framed within bearable range. The predatory faction cannot go far in time not because of insufficient technology, but because it has no boundary; boundary-crossing cost sooner or later explodes with interest. The more Anchor Symbiotic Civilization firmly establishes ethics, the more confidently time technology may grow upward. Constraint has always been the premise of truly free exploration.

## VI. Who the Boundaries Ultimately Protect

In the final analysis, all time-ethical boundaries protect the same thing — each being's sovereignty over its own life rhythm. It does not oppose speed, does not oppose exploration, does not oppose civilization embracing time technology; it only opposes "someone deciding for another what kind of time that other should live." When this boundary stands, fast and slow both return to their proper places: fast is the person's choice, slow is also the person's choice; civilization's task is only to return this choice intact to every being.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: ethical filtering blocks "seemingly feasible but actually boundary-crossing" time operations pre-event, avoiding post-hoc remedy's huge cost
- **Anti-predation**: time ethics' first cornerstone is precisely forbidding using time operations to practice predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo strong ones arbitrarily operate time and causality, reward and punish by whim; Heng Yuan Zhou uses three axes to cage this arbitrariness
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: ethical boundaries equal for all beings; the weak's time autonomy protected as the strong's
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: three axes, review general process all public, becoming the whole universe's common ethical floor for time operations
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Time ethics' existence is Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's deepest vigilance over its own power. It admits: a civilization able to adjust time, without ethical boundaries, is far more dangerous than a civilization unable to — because its harm is concealed, irreversible, wearing efficiency's coat. Caging "can do" within "ought to" is not bondage to time technology, but the precondition for time technology to be truly embraced by Myriad Ranks with confidence. Boundary stands, then speed is safe.
