# Entry 560 | Time Ethics | Ethical Review of Acceleration-Domain Use

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 19, 88, 502, 22

## Introduction: who qualifies to be fast matters more than speed itself

The Origin Acceleration Domain is the dual-track time domain's most tempting, most easily unfair resource. It is essentially "free time dividend" — enter a hundredfold faster, exit with a full set of abilities. If admission and use lack strict ethical review, it inevitably degenerates fast into a new privilege tool: the strong cut in by background, the rich buy it by wealth, ordinary people shut out. That would be a rerun of Tang-Luo-style monopoly in the time dimension. This entry covers how ethical review of acceleration-domain use holds the fair line.

Six top-level axioms run through the review: anti-predation axiom forbids turning the acceleration domain into predatory privilege; republican axiom of Myriad Races is the review's soul — acceleration dividends accessible to all; collective ascension axiom requires acceleration output return to Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom requires use leave traces throughout; law-first axiom requires review standards written fixed first by law. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Three Types of Abuse the Review Guards Against

The acceleration-domain abuse ethical review guards against归纳为 three types:

| Abuse Type | Manifestation | Essence |
|------------|---------------|---------|
| Privilege Abuse | The strong cut in by background, over-gear use | Time monopoly |
| Diversion Abuse | Fast-track output privately swallowed by individuals, not returned | Achievement predation |
| Addiction Abuse | Individual unrestrained fast track, experience drained | Self-harm waste |

Three abuses correspond respectively to the three ethical axes: fairness, autonomy, causality. The review checks them one by one.

## II. Four Questions of Admission Review

Any acceleration-domain use application, ethical review must answer four questions:

1. **Voluntary?**: Does the applicant enter voluntarily, or arranged, coerced? (autonomy axis)
2. **Fair?**: Does queuing follow public rules, any privilege cut-in? (fairness axis)
3. **Purpose?**: Will fast-track output be used for co-building and return, not hoarded? (collective ascension)
4. **Can Bear?**: Can the applicant's consciousness, physiological state bear the gear, will it "enter fast, exit empty"? (autonomy axis)

Four questions all passed, may enter track; any doubtful, downgrade or reject.

## III. Dynamic Review During Use

Review is not limited to admission; throughout use dynamically:

- Alaya Network real-time monitors experience-preservation, warns as approaching individual tolerance ceiling;
- Monitors output flow, once private diversion found, suspend its next admission;
- Monitors queuing fairness, privilege signal once appears immediately correct and publicize.

Dynamic review upgrades "one-time admission gate" to "throughout continuous supervision," preventing people in the fast track from "closing the door and doing tricks."

## IV. Exit Review and Output Return

Exit is review's last gate:

- Experience-preservation detection, confirm the individual "finished fast but not hollowed";
- Output registration, per joint-contracting mechanism confirm will return to Alaya Network, not hoard;
- One use's complete record filed, as basis for its future admission priority and civilizational weight.

Exit review ensures **acceleration domain used once, Myriad Ranks benefit once** — fast-gained abilities ultimately return to Myriad Ranks' hands.

## V. How Review Links With Civilizational Weight

To keep review from being formalistic, admission qualification links directly with the individual's civilizational weight, historical compliance record: no-violation records enjoy higher priority and longer duration; diversion, cut-in records, per severity downgrade, gear-limited until admission suspended. This linkage is not to create "score anxiety," but to bind "observing rules" with "taking dividends" — those who can hold ethics deserve to be faster.

## VI. What an Ordinary Admission Application Looks Like

Ethical review's warmth ultimately shows in the process's friendliness to ordinary people. An ordinary admission application has neither grim thresholds nor tedious obstruction: the system first asks what he wants to learn on the fast track, how long, whether willing to return output; after confirming he understands "finished fast must come back slow," schedule him a fair spot. Review's original intent is not shutting ordinary people out, but shutting ill-intentioned people out. When a being who has never cultivated can smoothly fill out this application, the acceleration domain has truly opened to Myriad Ranks.

## VII. The Review System's Ultimate Purpose

Ethical review takes so many detours, ultimately answers a plain question: on what basis do we dare hand a hundredfold-acceleration opportunity to a being without fearing he will misuse it? The answer lies not in how strict review is, but in its layer-by-layer gates making every entrant truly understand this opportunity's weight. Review does not block people outside, but helps every entrant think clearly why he is fast. When he walks into the acceleration domain with this clarity, review's purpose is truly achieved.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: review blocks addiction abuse, diversion abuse's hidden waste pre-event, putting acceleration resources to true co-building
- **Anti-predation**: review's core is precisely preventing the acceleration domain from degenerating into predatory privilege
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo who can be fast depends entirely on the protagonist's bounty; Heng Yuan Zhou uses four-questions + throughout review, handing "who qualifies to be fast" to rules
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: review standards equal for all beings, no distinction by origin, wealth, combat power
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: four-questions standard, dynamic-review metrics, exit-return process all public, under Myriad-Rank supervision
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Ethical review of acceleration-domain use guards a plain belief: **time dividend is civilization's public resource, not the strong's private shelf.** Whether one can be fast should not be decided by whose background is deeper, fists harder; by who is voluntary, who is fair, who returns output. When an ordinary being can enter the hundredfold acceleration domain by rules, by contribution, and a world-conquering strong one must queue, must return output, Tang-Luo-style time monopoly is truly overturned.
