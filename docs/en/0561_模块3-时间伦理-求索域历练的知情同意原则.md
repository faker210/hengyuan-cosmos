# Entry 561 | Time Ethics | Informed-Consent Principle for Quest-Domain Trials

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 19, 88, 37

## Introduction: slowing down to be tempered must also be something you yourself nod to

The Origin Acceleration Domain's ethical risk is "privilege monopoly"; the Quest Sediment Domain's ethical risk is the opposite — its risk is **treating people as tools to send to tempering**. Youth-Supreme hardship growth, old-system replay, deep trials, these are all valuable on the slow track, but if an individual is "arranged" into a suffering trial without knowledge, without voluntariness, then no matter how good the educational purpose, it slides into violation of the individual's time autonomy. This entry covers the informed-consent principle for Quest-Domain trials.

Six top-level axioms run through: anti-predation axiom forbids alienating individual trials into "being used"; republican axiom of Myriad Races treats the individual's informed consent as inalienable right; collective ascension axiom emphasizes trial purpose is lifting the individual not consuming him; causal self-discipline axiom requires trial arrangement not substitute for the individual's own choice; law-first axiom requires informed-consent process defined first by law. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Informed Consent: Trial Ethics' Cornerstone

The Quest Domain trial's peculiarity is it "has a cost" — suffering, failure, loss on the slow track are all real experience, not a game. Thus placing a person into such a trial must first satisfy informed consent:

1. **Informed**: the individual clearly knows this trial's goal, possible risks, approximate intensity;
2. **Consented**: the individual enters voluntarily after full information, not arranged, forced;
3. **Withdrawable**: the individual retains withdrawal right during the process, not "once in, not allowed out."

Informed-consent three elements are the dividing line between trial "education" not sliding into "abuse."

## II. Why Trials Most Easily Get "Arranged"

The sandbox finds Quest Domain trials most easily go wrong in practice, because they often wear the "for your own good" coat:

- Educators feel "this child should be tempered," so choose a suffering path for him;
- Replay tasks feel "this case is valuable," so stuff the individual into high-intensity situations;
- Youth-Supreme cultivation often in the name of "heaven will lay a great charge," decides for the youth how much hardship to eat.

These starting points may not be bad, **but deciding another's life suffering for him is itself deprivation of his time autonomy.** The informed-consent principle precisely stops this "well-meaning boundary-crossing."

## III. Tiered Adaptation of Informed Consent

Different individuals have different comprehension; informed consent also tier-adapts:

| Subject | Informed-Consent Form |
|---------|-----------------------|
| Mature Individual | Fully inform risks and gains, free choice |
| Youth Individual | Inform in ways he understands, guardian + ethics committee double gate |
| Special-Origin Individual | Adjust notification per its consciousness state, ensure true understanding |

Especially cautious of Youth-Supreme trials: tempering is a good thing, but **which hardship, how much, the youth himself must participate in deciding.** This is consistent with the Symbiotic Education System (Document 37) "guide not force" spirit.

## IV. The "Measure" in Trials: Preventing Harm Under the Name of Tempering

Beyond informed consent, trials themselves have ethical "measure" constraints:

- Must not arrange trials causing irreversible harm;
- Must not under "tempering" name repeatedly apply already-traumatizing situations;
- Trial intensity dynamically adjusted per individual tolerance; Alaya Network monitors psychological state throughout.

Informed consent guarantees "I am willing to go," intensity constraints guarantee "going won't be ruined" — together, Quest Domain tempering is growth, not harm.

## V. How Informed Consent Prevents "Well-Meaning Arrangement"

The hardest boundary-crossing to refuse in practice often wears "for your own good": a master picks the bitterest road for his apprentice, yet never asks if the apprentice is willing. Informed-consent principle's value lies in placing this hidden premise on the table — tempering may be designed, but choice must be returned. The Alaya Network at every trial application pushes "available paths, risks, withdrawal right" once in a form the applicant can understand, and leaves a trace of his confirmation. This confirmation record is later the hard evidence judging "this was his own choice" vs "arranged."

## VI. The Weight of Withdrawal Right

In informed consent, withdrawal right is the heaviest. A trial participant wanting to withdraw, no matter how "precious, how regrettable" the trial seems to others, must be immediately, blamelessly released. The sandbox finds: trial participants allowed to withdraw anytime, their persistence rate is actually higher — because he knows the road is his own choice, the suffering is his own acceptance. Conversely, those "once in, can't get out" temperings, even if content identical, only breed resentment not growth. Withdrawal right is not doubt of tempering, but trust in it: a road truly worth walking does not fear your turning away.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: informed consent + intensity constraints avoid the civilizational waste of "running the individual to ruin and restarting"
- **Anti-predation**: strictly forbid alienating individual trials into "being used, consumed," holding the individual's time autonomy
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style "heaven will lay a great charge" decides suffering for others; Heng Yuan Zhou returns the right to be tempered to the individual
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: informed consent tier-adapted to youth, special-origin individuals, leaving none behind
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: informed-consent three elements, tier-adaptation table, intensity-constraint standards all public
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Quest Domain trial informed-consent principle seems to slap a cumbersome procedure on "good tempering," but in fact guards civilization's most precious thing — **every being's decision over his own life rhythm.** Slowing down to be tempered is a good thing, but a trial whose participant is kept in the dark, pushed along, unable to withdraw even if he wants, no matter how "educational," is a deprivation. True trial must be one where the individual keeps eyes open, nods, and can turn away anytime. Those who temper you must first ask you; and you always retain the right to say "no."
