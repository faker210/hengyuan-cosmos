# Entry 562 | Time Ethics | Unity of Temporal Retrocognition and Causal Self-Discipline

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 82, 83, 87, 89

## Introduction: returning to the past, to learn, or to rewrite

Temporal retrocognition — returning to past time — is the one with greatest ethical tension among all time operations. It naturally tempts two impulses: one is "go back save that one who could have been saved," the other "go back fix that past mistake." The former is warm, the latter tempting, but once crossing the "rewriting" line, it shakes causality's foundation, sliding into temporal paradox and cycle harvesting. This entry covers how time ethics unifies temporal retrocognition with causal self-discipline — returning is allowed, but with what boundaries.

Six top-level axioms highly concentrated here: causal self-discipline axiom is this entry's protagonist — only knowledge returned, causality not perturbed; anti-predation axiom strictly forbids practicing harvesting via retrocognition; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires retrocognition not make decisions for any civilization; collective ascension axiom locates retrocognition's value in "learning" not "manipulating"; law-first axiom requires retrocognition rules written fixed first by the Temporal-Paradox Prevention Mechanism. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Retrocognition's Root Position: Learning, Not Rewriting

Time ethics' first setting on retrocognition: **retrocognition's only legitimate purpose is learning and archaeology, not rewriting history.** Per Basic Principles of Temporal Retrocognition (Document 82), the operator returning to the past plays the role of "honest observer," not "omnipotent corrector." This setting thoroughly separates retrocognition from "transmigrating to change fate" — the latter is precisely the predatory faction's cycle system's hotbed.

| Retrocognition Purpose | Legitimate? | Reason |
|------------------------|-------------|--------|
| Archaeological observation, recording truth | Legitimate | Only learning, no causal perturbation |
| Extracting shareable knowledge | Legitimate | Only-knowledge-returned principle |
| Saving a specific person/affair | Not Legitimate | Namely rewriting causality |
| Harvesting/diverting others' causality | Strictly Forbidden | Cycle harvesting |

## II. Observe-First-Limited-Intervention: Retrocognition's Operational Iron Rule

Legitimate retrocognition is also not random "running around," but constrained by the "Observe-First-Limited-Intervention Principle" (Document 83):

1. **Observe First**: enter the past, first fully record, understand its causal chain, not rush to act;
2. **Limited Intervention**: only when sandbox repeatedly verifies, and truly is a "salvageable key fork," allow extremely small knowledge injection;
3. **Minimum Dose**: intervention dose pressed to lowest, only transmit abstract laws, not make concrete choices for the other.

These three steps guarantee retrocognition even with intervention is light enough to almost not perturb the original causal flow.

## III. Temporal-Paradox Prevention: Unified Technical Guardrail

Retrocognition and causal self-discipline's unity ultimately relies on the Temporal-Paradox Prevention Mechanism (Document 89) technical guardrail as backstop:

- Any retrocognition operation proceeds within an isolation belt, with complete firewall from real causality;
- Once "rewriting" signal detected (the retrocognizer begins trying to change established facts), immediately circuit-break, forced extraction;
- Information carried by the retrocognizer passes a "content gate," only abstract knowledge allowed to flow back, sensitive causal content stays in the past.

Technical guardrail and ethical principle mutually lock: ethics says "should not rewrite," technology says "also cannot rewrite."

## IV. Why "Not Rewriting" Is True Compassion

Some question: clearly able to go back save someone, why not let save? Time ethics' answer:

- Rewriting one's causality, seemingly saving him, actually deprives him as life of complete experience and autonomy — including his right to have borne suffering himself;
- More dangerous, once "savable" allowed, retrocognition right inevitably flows to the strong; the weak's history randomly rewritten, the strong's history constantly beautified;
- True compassion is not changing fate for others, but returning the lesson, letting later generations not eat the same bitterness.

This is precisely "only knowledge returned"'s deep ethics: **respect every segment of history as a right of life truly having happened.**

## V. A Complete Portrait of a Compliant Retrocognition

To land principles, one compliant retrocognition may be depicted: a researcher returns to some civilization's ancient battlefield, first at an isolated observation post fully records both sides' causal chains and decision forks; he sees a tragedy that could have been avoided, per rules only returns to that civilization's later generations one abstract lesson — "that misjudgment came from falsely trusting an unreliable intelligence source," and absolutely does not go back to stop anyone at the time. He did not rewrite a specific person, yet let later generations avoid the same pit. This is "learning not rewriting"'s concrete look.

## VI. Why "Not Saving That Person" Is More Compassionate

Where this principle is most easily questioned is why it does not reach out facing a specific, savable person. The answer: that person saved by retrocognition, though survived, will never know his fate was rewritten by external force — his every later choice rests on a starting point not his own. This "being saved," seemingly compassionate, actually deprives him as subject of the complete right to walk through his own life. True respect is letting him live as himself in natural causality; leaving lessons for later generations, letting more not experience his suffering, is retrocognition's least-boundary-crossing goodwill.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: strictly forbid unbounded retrocognition rewriting, avoiding the huge waste of "recompute a whole causal segment for changing one detail"
- **Anti-predation**: thoroughly cut retrocognition from cycle harvesting, holding the bottom line of "going back only to learn"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo strong ones may arbitrarily transmigrate, rewrite, take disciples and change fate; Heng Yuan Zhou uses firewall + circuit-break to cage retrocognition in the study room
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: history treats all beings equally, not selectively rewritten by strength
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: retrocognition legitimate-purpose table, three-step operational iron rule, circuit-break thresholds all public
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The unity of temporal retrocognition and causal self-discipline gives a both restrained and gentle answer: returning to the past is allowed, but the one returning must be an honest student, not an omnipotent god. He may look, may record, may bring lessons back so later generations take fewer detours, but he cannot reach out to rewrite anyone's past life. Respecting history's causality is respecting every life that truly lived in it — this, is temporal retrocognition's deepest ethics.

In the final analysis, the unity of temporal retrocognition and causal self-discipline described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
