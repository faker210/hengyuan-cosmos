# Entry 563 | Time Ethics | Individual-Rights Protection in the Time Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 19, 26, 40, 88

## Introduction: when civilization can manipulate time, on what basis is the individual not treated as time's material

The dual-track time domain is a civilizational-level public project, but it is entered, experienced, borne by concrete individuals. No matter how grand the project, it may not dispatch the individual as "time's material." The time-ethics special topic must specifically cover **individual-rights protection**: in a civilization able to adjust time, arrange trials, share consciousness, what inviolable rights does an ordinary being retain over his own time. This is the Individual-Collective Balance Principle (Document 26) concretized in the time dimension.

Six top-level axioms run through: republican axiom of Myriad Races is this entry's protagonist — the individual's time rights do not disappear because of the collective's grandeur; anti-predation axiom forbids plundering individual time into public material; collective ascension axiom emphasizes lifting the individual is the collective's purpose not means; causal self-discipline axiom respects individual causal autonomy; law-first axiom writes individual rights into law, not temporarily canceled for collective needs. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Four Fundamental Rights of the Individual in the Time Domain

Time ethics归纳出 the individual's four fundamental rights in the time domain:

| Right | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Right to Know | Complete knowledge of time operations about to be experienced |
| Right to Choose | Voluntarily choose which track to enter, whether to participate, may refuse |
| Right to Withdraw | Retain withdrawal right during process, not "locked in time" |
| Right Not to Be Manipulated | One's time, causality, consciousness not secretly operated by others |

Four rights interlock; missing one, the individual may degenerate into "material used by the time project."

## II. How Rights Protection Prevents "the Collective Overwhelming the Individual"

Grand civilizational projects' most common ethical slide is "for the collective, sacrifice the individual." The time domain is especially dangerous because time operations are concealed, irreversible. Rights protection uses three mechanisms to prevent the slide:

1. **Rights Legalized**: four rights written into the Symbiotic Constitution Preamble (Document 40), not temporarily shelved because some project is "of great significance";
2. **Procedural Guarantee**: any time operation involving an individual must pass informed consent and ethical review; procedurally non-compliant = void;
3. **Redress Channels**: an individual believing his time rights violated may appeal to the Symbiotic Court, adjudicated independently by the judicial layer.

These three mechanisms guarantee: **no matter how grand the collective, it cannot legally cross the individual's time rights.**

## III. Each Track's Individual-Rights Focus

The dual tracks differ in individual-rights focus:

- **Origin Acceleration Domain**'s focus is "not pushed aside by privilege, not hollowed": the individual has fair-queuing right; on exit has experience-preservation right;
- **Quest Sediment Domain**'s focus is "not arranged to suffer, not forced to sediment": the individual has voluntary-participation right and withdrawal right.

Together, the individual whether on fast or slow track, neither has opportunities stolen, nor suffering stuffed at him.

## IV. The Dialectic of Rights and the Collective

Emphasizing individual rights is not encouraging selfishness, dragging the collective. Time ethics' stance is dialectical:

- Individual rights are the floor, collective goals may not break through it;
- But within rights, individuals are encouraged to voluntarily contribute to the collective — just as joint contracting rewards more work more gain; the more contribution, the higher civilizational weight;
- Collective ascension's true meaning is precisely **lifting every individual into a capable, dignified subject**, not crushing individuals to fill the collective's project.

Protecting individual rights ultimately makes the collective composed of "a group of dignified co-builders," not "a pile of dispatched material."

## V. Rights Are Not Islands, but Co-Building's Premise

Some worry emphasizing individual rights will make everyone look after himself, slow the collective. The opposite is true: the sandbox shows individuals with time rights fully protected, their co-building willingness and output quality are actually higher. The reason is simple — when a being knows his time will not be casually dispatched, his withdrawal right always available, he dares to throw himself fully into fast-track R&D, without always guarding against "being treated as material." Security itself is productivity. Individual rights are not the collective's opposite, but the foundation of a sustainable collective.

## VI. How the Rights List Specifically Lands

These four rights are not slogans on paper; they have everyday landing forms: before every track-entry, the system re-confirms in an understandable way "what you will experience, you may withdraw anytime, your consciousness will not be read"; after every time operation, the individual receives a concise receipt stating how his time was used. This receipt is his future appeal basis. When a being can anytime pull out a "my time ledger," he and the civilizational project finally stand on equal footing.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: protecting individual rights avoids the long-term waste of "running individuals to ruin, hearts scatter" — respected individuals are sustainable co-builders
- **Anti-predation**: individual rights directly negate the logic of "plundering individual time into public material"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system, supporting roles' time and fate are arranged at the protagonist's will; Heng Yuan Zhou uses four rights as backstop for every individual
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: four rights equal for all individuals, all lineages, no discount by strength, origin
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: four rights, three protection mechanisms, appeal process all public, written into the Symbiotic Constitution
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Individual-rights protection in the time domain is Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's final answer on "grand and individual" relations. It admits civilizational projects need collaboration, but absolutely does not admit collaboration may be at the cost of canceling the individual's time autonomy. In the dual-track time domain, an ordinary being may both voluntarily hand himself to hundredfold acceleration to fight for a future, and anytime turn away, say no, withdraw — he is always the master of his own time, not a dispatchable cog on civilization's clock. Such a civilization can be grand, and gentle.

In the final analysis, individual-rights protection in the time domain described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
