# Entry 564 | Time Ethics | Ethical Critique of the Predatory Faction's Time Manipulations

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 88, 82, 89

## Introduction: stealing others' time to be fast oneself is the most unjust kind of predation

Previous entries cover how Anchor Symbiotic Civilization regulates its own time operations; this one turns the gun outward, **critiquing the predatory faction system's time manipulations**. Because as long as the predatory faction still exists, time ethics cannot only say "how we do it," but must make clear "what they did wrong, why wrong, how far wrong." The predatory faction's abuse of time is the most concealed, most unjust of all predatory acts — because what it steals is not goods, but life's very length. This entry does this ethical accounting.

Six top-level axioms here become critique's yardstick: anti-predation axiom is critique's general outline; republican axiom of Myriad Races accuses it of trampling all beings' time equality; collective ascension axiom reveals it runs counter to lifting all; causal self-discipline axiom accuses it of perturbing causality, cycle harvesting; law-first axiom points out its operations inevitably cause temporal paradox and self-collapse. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Predatory Faction's Four Typical Time Operations

Cross-plane archaeology归纳出 the predatory faction's abuse of time concentrates in four typical forms:

| Operation | Manifestation | Ethical Qualification |
|-----------|---------------|------------------------|
| Lifespan Theft | Devour others' life length to extend self | Direct predation of life-time |
| Causal Rewrite | The strong arbitrarily rewrite history, others' fate | Deprivation of causal autonomy |
| Cycle Harvesting | Manipulate reincarnation, divert others' souls and merit | Cross-life predation |
| Time Monopoly | Monopolize acceleration resources; all beings can only slowly endure | Time privilege |

Four operations point to the same thing: **treating "time" as spoils that may be privately occupied, seized.**

## II. Why Lifespan Theft Is the Most Unjust Predation

The predatory faction's most common time operation is devouring others' lifespan to extend self. Ethical critique points straight at its guilt:

1. **Steals life itself**: goods stolen may be re-earned; lifespan devoured, that life's possibilities vanish forever;
2. **Strong bullying weak**: those able to steal lifespan must be the strong; victims have no power to resist, the most asymmetric predation;
3. **Breaks causality**: a life that should have run naturally is forcibly cut off, its causal chain broken, polluting the overall time flow.

By contrast, Anchor Symbiosis uses consciousness acceleration + flesh freezing to solve "want to live long, do more," without touching others' one bit of time — the two contrasted, superiority is self-evident.

## III. Cycle Harvesting: Cross-Life Predation

The deepest evil among the predatory faction's time operations is **cycle harvesting**: the strong manipulate reincarnation mechanisms, treating all beings' souls, merit, causality as recoverable private property. Ethical critique three questions:

- Reincarnation is all beings' natural causal flow; who gave the strong the right to "recycle"?
- The harvested cannot even autonomously choose their next life; talk of equal evolutionary qualification?
- This operation turns life and death itself into the strong's breeding ground, predation's ultimate form.

Precisely therefore, causal self-discipline axiom nails cycle harvesting as absolute forbidden zone — it is not "not quite good," but the original sin civilization spurns.

## IV. Time Monopoly: Taking All Beings' Slow Endurance as Privilege

The last critique is time monopoly: in the predatory faction system, time acceleration, the most precious resource, is monopolized by a few strong; myriad beings can only burn out their lives in slow cultivation with no end in sight. This seemingly steals no one, but is actually a **structural predation** — it deprives all beings' fair qualification to obtain time dividends. Heng Yuan Zhou's dual-track time domain's very existence is the answer to this monopoly: time dividends should be accessible to all.

## V. Critique Is Not Hostility, but Early Warning

This critique does not treat predatory-faction beings as enemies to be exterminated. Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, for individuals and civilizations led astray, always retains Civilizational Correction Engineering's exit — as long as willing to turn to symbiosis, history may be returned as lesson not settled. Critique targets the structure of "treating time as spoils," not the pitiful ones in that structure also pressed underneath. Drawing this clearly is important: opposing predation is not opposing the predator himself; receiving people out of the predation structure is critique's endpoint.

## VI. This Critique's Real-Life Landing

This critique is not to display historical evidence, but for every living being now: it must remind civilizations still shrouded by the notion "the strong may seize others' time" — stolen lifespan must eventually be returned, rewritten causality must eventually crack, harvested cycles must eventually break in their own hands. Critique's final landing is giving every predated being a fair cognition: the time you lost was not deserved; that is a debt not to be repudiated; and you standing on the symbiosis side today is refusing to pass this debt to the next generation. Axiom 6 landing: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; easygoing slackers have a floor; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more; predation loses meaning because all inherently possess infinity.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the other side of critique is proof — predatory-faction time operations inevitably lose on the total energy ledger, civilizational-level self-destruction
- **Anti-predation**: this entry is anti-predation axiom's total accounting in the time dimension; four operations each convicted
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style "bounty time, protagonist alone enjoys" is precisely the specimen of time monopoly; this entry openly convicts it
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: critique stands on the side of myriad beings whose lifespan was stolen, harvested, monopolized
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: critique conclusions convert to time-ethics prohibitions, written into the Symbiotic Constitution, announced cosmos-wide
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The ethical critique of the predatory faction's time manipulations ultimately lets a plain truth spread through the heavens: **time is not spoils, and life's length is even less something anyone may seize and keep.** Lifespan theft, causal rewrite, cycle harvesting, time monopoly — these four operations seem variously packaged, stripped to the bottom are one thing: the strong turn the weak's time into their own stepping stones. Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's attitude toward them leaves no ambiguity: never rerun in its own dual track, never recognize external predatory factions, and always ready to use Civilizational Correction Engineering to pull led-astray civilizations back on track. Because a civilization that treats time as spoils will ultimately be settled by time itself.

In the final analysis, the ethical critique of the predatory faction's time manipulations described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
