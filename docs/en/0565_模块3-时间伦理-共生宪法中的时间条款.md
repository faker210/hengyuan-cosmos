# Entry 565 | Time Ethics | Time Clauses in the Symbiotic Constitution

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 40, 92, 19, 88

## Introduction: turning time ethics from moral advocacy into binding constitutional law

Time-ethics entries have said so much "ought and ought not"; ultimately must answer a rule-of-law question: by what do these principles land? By moral consciousness? Predatory-faction history proves that relying on consciousness alone, the strong will inevitably cross boundaries in time. Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's answer is **writing time ethics into the Symbiotic Constitution** — turning "how to treat time" from an advocacy into legal clauses all must obey, violation = on trial. This entry sorts out the time-clause system in the Symbiotic Constitution.

Six top-level axioms here rule-legalize: anti-predation axiom becomes the time clauses' legislative basis; republican axiom of Myriad Races embodies as equal clauses for time rights; collective ascension axiom embodies as shared obligation for time dividends; causal self-discipline axiom directly becomes time-operation procedural clauses; law-first axiom itself is constitutional supremacy's jurisprudential basis. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Time Ethics Must Be Constitutionalized

Morality and law differ in "is there enforcement." Time operations carry extremely high risk, extremely great temptation; relying only on ethical advocacy is insufficient:

- The strong may feel "I contribute greatly, taking a bit more time what of it";
- Operators may feel "the purpose is good, rewriting his causality is fine";
- Ordinary people may not know their time rights are violated.

Only writing these into the constitution, paired with judicial redress, does time ethics have teeth. This is consistent with the Symbiotic Constitution Preamble (Document 40) "rights legalized, unconstitutional acts prosecuted" spirit.

## II. Four Blocks of Time Clauses

Time clauses in the Symbiotic Constitution归纳为 four blocks:

| Block | Core Clause | Protects |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Rights Block | Individual time four rights (know, choose, withdraw, not-be-manipulated) | Individual time autonomy |
| Procedural Block | Time-operation pre-review, in-process supervision, post-review | Causal self-discipline |
| Fairness Block | Time dividends distributed by contribution, privilege monopoly forbidden | Myriad-Rank Republicanism |
| Forbidden Zone Block | Strictly forbid lifespan theft, causal rewrite, cycle harvesting | Anti-predation |

Four blocks together cover four dimensions: individual, procedure, fairness, prohibition.

## III. Justiciability and Redress of Clauses

Constitutional clauses are not decoration; they are justiciable, with redress:

- Any individual believing his time rights violated may sue in the Symbiotic Court per constitutional clauses;
- Any time operation violating the procedural block, after trial may be declared void, and the operator held responsible;
- Those touching the forbidden-zone block, regardless of rank, stripped of law-anchoring permissions, permanently barred from time operations.

Justiciability turns constitutional time clauses from "words on the wall" into "weapons in hand" — ordinary beings can truly pick them up. This is consistent with the Legal-Provisions Index (Document 92) system.

## IV. Clauses Amending With Civilizational Ascension

Time clauses are not unchanging dead law. Law-first axiom requires them to **legally amend** with civilizational ascension:

- When dual-track technology, time ethics have new mature understanding, the Anchor Council amends clauses per constitutional-amendment procedure;
- Amendments still constrained by the five axioms; no clause may conflict with axioms;
- Amendment process throughout public, traceable, under Myriad-Rank supervision.

This guarantees the constitution both stable (not lightly changed) and growing (not rigid) — echoing "worldview may expand infinitely without collapse."

## V. How an Ordinary Being Uses These Clauses

In practice, clauses' meaning lies in: an ordinary individual never touching cultivation, if finds himself quietly stuffed into the fast track, forced into a trial, or occupying a fair queuing spot under "acceleration" name, he need not understand cultivation, need no background, only per constitutional time clauses may file in the Symbiotic Court. The court has a dedicated Time Division, adjudicated by judges independent of the executive layer. The strong before the Time Division are equal to a mortal. This is rule of law in the time dimension's most concrete look.

## VI. Time-Clause Amendment Precedent

Clauses were not always thus. At constitution-founding, there was once a loose clause "acceleration duration may be self-determined by units as needed"; after one epoch of operation it was found certain units had de-evolved it into "forced-overtime-style acceleration," so per constitutional-amendment procedure tightened to "individual daily fast-track duration cap." This amendment was fully recorded — it shows how constitutional clauses in practice have loopholes found, publicly discussed, legally patched. Time clauses are therefore not dead text carved on stele, but a living law that grows with civilization, yet always tethered by axioms.

## VII. Time Clauses' Relation to the Rest of the Constitution

Time clauses are not isolated; they mesh with the Symbiotic Constitution's other clauses — anti-monopoly, labor protection, individual rights. For example, time dividends distributed by contribution directly echo anti-monopoly clauses; individual withdrawal right directly echoes labor-protection clauses. Once time enters the constitution, it is no longer a technical topic, but becomes the whole civilizational value system's projection in the time dimension. Reading time clauses is actually reading the whole Symbiotic Constitution's soul through the mirror of time.
