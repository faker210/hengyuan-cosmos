# Entry 567 | Acceleration Domain | Sense-Origin Realm Adaptation — The Beginner Period's Acceleration Rhythm

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 16; Benchmark library: Entries 501, 502

## Introduction: which flow multiplier suits the first realm is a craft

Heng Yuan Zhou's (Wanling Hengmao Origin Universe) dual-track time domain does not "one-size-fits-all" stuff all cultivators into a hundredfold flow. The Origin Acceleration Domain's time-flow gear must strictly match the cultivator's realm — this is the law-first axiom's direct manifestation in the time dimension. Sense-Origin, as the nine realms' first realm, is the entry for all Myriad-Rank cultivators; its acceleration-rhythm design is especially sensitive: accelerate too fiercely, it hollows out the beginner's true bodily feeling of "energy entering the body for the first time"; accelerate insufficiently, it wastes the mature efficiency dividend of Multiplication Fruit Technicalization (Document 16).

Six top-level axioms jointly gate here: anti-predation axiom requires Sense-Origin acceleration come from law-efficiency improvement, not devouring others' time; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires this beginner acceleration rhythm open to 99% of Myriad Ranks, no noble children monopolizing the fast lane; collective ascension axiom requires beginner-period acceleration output ultimately feed back the whole cultivation community; causal self-discipline axiom requires beginners' acceleration operations recorded throughout by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision, preventing newcomers' misoperation from perturbing causality; law-first axiom writes "Sense-Origin adapted flow" as a law clause that may not be arbitrarily reset, never retconned. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry answers a concrete question: a cultivator who has just sensed wandering energy, in the Origin Acceleration Domain, at what rhythm should he begin his first class.

## I. Why Sense-Origin Cannot Directly Take Hundredfold Flow

Sense-Origin's core tasks are "perception awakening, basic absorption, energy discernment, foundation-condensation" (Document 14). These four highly depend on **true resonance between body and consciousness**: first sensing spiritual qi, first guiding energy into the body, first discerning law aura; these experiences constitute the cultivator's lifelong "origin memory." If directly applying default hundredfold flow, three systemic faults appear:

1. **Perception dulling**: consciousness under hundredfold flow forcibly stretched; the beginner instead cannot distinguish energy from hallucination in "normal time sense," mistaking consciousness noise for law aura.
2. **Body cannot keep up**: if energy-absorption rate is pulled to the limit by consciousness acceleration, the flesh (especially carbon-based life) cannot synchronously infiltrate, appearing as a tearing feeling of "consciousness ran ahead, body still in place."
3. **Origin-memory hollowing**: the beginner period spent under hundredfold flow, in retrospect like a fast-forwarded dream, lacking the weight of "first times" that constitute personality thickness. This directly conflicts with the core thesis "acceleration ≠ hollow."

Thus the Origin Acceleration Domain specially designs a **Beginner Gear** for Sense-Origin: 5× to 10× flow, with the first three days mandatorily at 1× real flow to complete "zero-point anchoring."

## II. Beginner Gear's Four-Stage Rhythm

| Stage | Duration (Real) | In-Domain Flow | Core Task | Exit Detection |
|-------|-----------------|----------------|-----------|-----------------|
| Zero-Point Anchoring | 3 days | 1× | Establish energy-perception baseline under normal time sense | Perception stability ≥ 60% |
| Gentle Acceleration | 7 days | 5× | Repeat basic absorption at 5× flow | Energy atlas初步成形 |
| Gear Probing | 14 days | 10× | Probe self tolerance ceiling | No body-tearing reports |
| Consolidation Transition | Long-term | 5–10× | Maintain Sense-Origin daily cultivation rhythm | May apply to enter Condensed-Origin acceleration gear |

This rhythm's essence is "slow first, then fast; from slow into fast." It is not punishment, but **experience-preservation engineering**: let the beginner complete the first time in true time sense, then let law acceleration take over the repetitive labor afterward. Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision real-time monitors each beginner's "time-sense anchoring index"; once the index falls below threshold, automatically slow to 1× until restabilized.

## III. Myriad-Rank Differentiated Beginner Rhythms

The republican axiom of Myriad Races requires this rhythm serve not just the human race. The five gene lineages (carbon-based matter, energy-condensation, mechanical-construction, consciousness-information, mixed-variant) have vastly different beginner tolerance:

- **Carbon-Based Matter Race** (human, beast, etc.): the flesh is the bottleneck; Beginner Gear takes the 5× ceiling, emphasizing flesh infiltration.
- **Energy-Condensation Race**: no flesh, consciousness sensitive; Beginner Gear takes 3×, preventing consciousness overshoot.
- **Mechanical-Construction Race**: hardware iteration fast, may bear 20×, but needs additional "emotion-module calibration."
- **Consciousness-Information Race**: most dangerous, consciousness flow itself extremely fast; Beginner Gear instead must drop to 0.5× (i.e., enter the Quest Domain slow segment) to avoid self-wrapping.
- **Mixed-Variant Race**: case-by-case verified by the Anchor Council, usually in the 5–10× range.

Plant spirits, planet consciousness and other strange life (Document 53) entirely do not follow the human rhythm; their "beginner" is measured in geological eras; the Origin Acceleration Domain opens a separate ten-thousand-year gear for them, preventing any Myriad-Rank lineage from being excluded from the acceleration dividend due to different time scales.

## IV. Fundamental Opposition to the Predatory Faction's Beginner Methods

In the old predatory-faction system, "beginner acceleration" meant two things: one was entering a grand sect by resource stacking (bounty-style); the other was devouring low-tier beings' energy for quick success (predatory-style). The former turned beginner qualification into a class ticket; the latter built the beginner on others' sacrifice. Heng Yuan Zhou's Sense-Origin acceleration gear seals both paths:

- Beginner-Gear resources distributed by the joint-contracting mechanism (Document 22), more work more gain, no look at origin;
- Beginner's energy comes from environmental incremental development and law cycle, traceable on Alaya, no one else's energy residue;
- Every beginner-period acceleration is recorded, becoming raw data for future civilizational-weight evaluation.

This is precisely anti-predation axiom's manifestation at the microcosm: **a person's first step to getting stronger must not be another's first step to getting weaker.**

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: do not waste hundredfold flow on the beginner period most needing slow experience; use 5–10× gear to precisely match perception tasks, avoiding law-network load overload
- **Anti-predation**: beginner acceleration all from law-efficiency improvement and incremental resources, zero devouring, zero bounty, Alaya-traceable
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style beginner is locked by sect resources and bloodline talent; mortals do not even have qualification to enter acceleration; Heng Yuan Zhou's Beginner Gear opens to 99% of Myriad Ranks, gear assigned by individual tolerance not origin
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five lineages and strange life each have dedicated Beginner Gear; consciousness-information race even reverse-slows, ensuring no lineage marginalized by time-scale difference
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Beginner-Gear parameters, monitoring algorithms, anchoring-index formula all public; anyone may recompute, audit; this is the first link in the open chain from basic techniques to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Sense-Origin acceleration rhythm is the whole dual-track time domain's first practice on the "fast and slow" philosophy. It tells every being just stepping onto the cultivation road: you deserve to be accelerated, but you also deserve to be treated slowly and seriously. Not chasing speed in the beginner period is precisely so that future speed has a non-hollow root.
