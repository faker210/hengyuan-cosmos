# Entry 568 | Acceleration Domain | Condensed-Origin Realm Adaptation — Acceleration of Inner-Circuit Construction

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 15; Benchmark library: Entry 567

## Introduction: the second realm's core is "making energy rotate on its own"

When a cultivator crosses Sense-Origin into Condensed-Origin, the task nature changes fundamentally: from "perceive and absorb" to "condense and inner-circulate" (Document 14). The so-called inner circuit means forming a closed loop between the meridians, dantian, and sea of consciousness that can self-rotate without external supply. Once this loop is built, the cultivator changes from "depending on heaven for food" to "self-circuit supply." Condensed-Origin is the bridging realm among the 300 origin cultivation systems, and also the realm most prone to qi deviation from impatience.

Six top-level axioms once again become design yardsticks: anti-predation axiom requires the inner circuit be built with incremental energy, not dismantling others' circuits and moving them into one's own body; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires inner-circuit construction methods fully public to Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires each cultivator's built inner circuit ultimately become a micro node of the Alaya Network; causal self-discipline axiom requires the acceleration domain monitor inner-circuit construction, preventing "circuit leakage" from polluting the environment; law-first axiom fixes "Condensed-Origin adapted flow" as a law clause, not arbitrarily reset for plot needs. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This entry focuses: in the Origin Acceleration Domain, at what speed should the inner circuit be built.

## I. Why the Inner Circuit Especially Consumes Time

The inner circuit is not an instantaneous action, but a **topology-forming process**. It requires energy to complete at least the four-node continuous flow of "absorb–transform–store–feed back" in the body, and this flow must stably run no less than a certain period before solidifying into body memory. At real flow, a human cultivator building a stable inner circuit usually takes three to five years.

In the Origin Acceleration Domain this can be compressed, but compression has physical limits:

- The thermodynamic response of energy-transform nodes has delay; no matter how fast consciousness, the meridians' "digestion" speed of energy does not change;
- Once inner-circuit topology solidifies it is extremely hard to modify; high-speed building means "too late to fix even if wrong";
- The inner circuit is deeply coupled with the cultivator's personality structure; a circuit built too fast reflects an "accelerated personality," prone to later emotional, ethical problems.

Thus Condensed-Origin's acceleration gear cannot copy Sense-Origin's gentle route, but must find a new balance between "topology trial-and-error" and "solidification speed."

## II. Condensed-Origin Acceleration-Gear Parameter Design

| Dimension | Parameter | Explanation |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Recommended Flow | 20×–50× | One gear above Sense-Origin, because inner circuit allows repeated trial-and-error |
| Trial Window | Each topology draft limited to 72 acceleration hours | Timeout forced rollback, avoiding solidifying wrong circuits |
| Solidification Observation Period | After draft built, 30-day real-flow cooldown | Use slow time to verify circuit stability |
| Inner-Circuit Redundancy Requirement | ≥ 30% | Prevent full-load rupture |
| Alaya Monitoring Frequency | Once per acceleration hour | High-frequency capture of circuit-leak signals |

This design's key innovation is "fast trial-and-error, slow solidification": in the acceleration domain use 20–50× flow to quickly try multiple inner-circuit topology drafts, each draft only runs 72 acceleration hours then rolls back; the truly selected optimal draft must be taken to real flow or the Quest Sediment Domain to cool for 30 days, confirming no leakage, no backlash, before allowing formal solidification. This moves engineering's "rapid prototype—slow verification" into cultivation.

## III. The Inner Circuit and Myriad-Rank Co-Governance

The inner circuit is not private. A stably running inner circuit is essentially a micro energy-self-consistent system; when millions, tens of millions of such systems run simultaneously, they form "circuit resonance" through the Alaya Network. Anchor Symbiotic Civilization deliberately uses this resonance:

- Each newly built inner circuit opens a read-only interface to the Alaya Network, for other cultivators to reference the topology;
- Circuits are allowed to do "circuit interlinking" under strict reporting, forming larger symbiotic circuits;
- Any attempt to close the inner circuit, disconnect from the network, is marked by the Anchor Supervisory Committee as "predatory-tendency risk."

This is precisely collective-ascension axiom's landing at the energy level: **your inner circuit is not your private property, but a cell of Myriad Ranks' great energy cycle.** The Origin Acceleration Domain in Condensed-Origin acceleration gear builds in this interface; the cultivator from day one gets used to "open circuits," not the old era's "closed-door" private dantian.

## IV. Opposition to the Predatory Faction's "Circuit-Dismantling-and-Relocation"

In the predatory-faction system, the most common shortcut is "seize others' golden core, dismantle others' circuit, move into own body." This approach seems efficient energetically, but actually:

1. Forcibly transplanted circuit topology is incompatible with the new host; must rupture within three years;
2. The dismantled one's energy residues long remain, polluting the new host's law perception;
3. It trains "circuit dismantlers," not "circuit builders"; once facing an opponent with no circuit to dismantle, they are helpless.

Heng Yuan Zhou in Condensed-Origin acceleration gear thoroughly negates this path: the Alaya Network recognizes any foreign circuit residue; once found, the cultivator must enter the Quest Sediment Domain for a not-short "circuit purification" trial, acceleration gear forbidden during purification. This mechanism from time allocation makes predatory circuit-dismantling's cost so high no one dares.

- **Anti-energy waste**: fast trial-and-error slow solidification avoids wasting time solidifying wrong topology; 30% redundancy avoids dual energy-and-time waste of full-load rupture
- **Anti-predation**: inner circuits all self-built; foreign residues forcibly purified; from time-resource allocation seals the "circuit-dismantling-relocation" shortcut
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style Condensed-Origin relies on master-sect empowerment, seizing others' golden core; circuits are bounty or plunder; Heng Yuan Zhou's inner circuit must be self-built and self-proven, Alaya-traced throughout
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five lineages carbon-based/energy/mechanical/consciousness/mixed each have inner-circuit topology templates; plant spirits' "root-circuit," planet consciousness's "geological circuit" also included in acceleration gear
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: topology draft library, trial-and-error algorithm, solidification-verification process all public; this is the mesoscopic link in the open chain from basic techniques to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Condensed-Origin acceleration rhythm is the key leap from "learner" to "self-consistent system." The Origin Acceleration Domain does not compress this into one second, but uses the "fast trial-and-error, slow solidification" two-stage design, both enjoying efficiency dividends and preserving the inner circuit's completeness as part of personality. An inner circuit self-built, open to Myriad Ranks, able to withstand slow cooldown, is the reliable foundation truly leading to Origin Realm.
