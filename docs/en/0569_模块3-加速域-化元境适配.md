## Introduction: the threshold from quantitative to qualitative change least tolerates rushing

After Condensed-Origin builds the inner circuit, the cultivator enters Transform-Origin. Transform-Origin's core is no longer "quantitative" accumulation, but "qualitative" leap: low-tier energy in the body must undergo structural phase transition, condensing from a diffuse state into a high-tier energy form able to carry law-will (Document 14). This phase transition physically resembles matter's three-state change — water to ice is not accomplished by pouring a few more cups, but by crossing a critical point. Once the critical point is forcibly crossed, what is obtained is not high-tier energy, but waste energy and hidden wounds.

Six top-level axioms here are highly sensitive: anti-predation axiom requires the critical energy needed for qualitative change all from incremental development, absolutely not allowing "borrowing" by devouring others' high-tier energy; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the qualitative-change critical-point algorithm public to Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires each qualitative change's high-tier energy sample returned to Alaya Network for Myriad Ranks' study; causal self-discipline axiom requires the qualitative-change process under multi-layer supervision, preventing phase-transition runaway from affecting the environment; law-first axiom writes "Transform-Origin adapted flow" fixed as a law clause, no acceleration may bypass the critical point. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Qualitative Change Especially Forbids "Fast"

Energy qualitative change differs fundamentally from inner-circuit construction. The inner circuit is a topology problem, may trial-and-error; qualitative change is a critical-point problem, **once wrong is irreversible**. In the Origin Acceleration Domain, this means several hard constraints:

1. **Critical point not compressible**: the law-resonance duration energy needs to cross the critical is a constant; consciousness acceleration cannot change law's own response speed;
2. **Phase-transition window extremely short**: once critical is touched, the window is only a few dozen acceleration hours; miss it and must re-accumulate;
3. **Phase-transition failure cost extremely high**: failure not only wastes energy, but leaves "phase-transition scars" in the meridians, recurring every later冲击 at higher tiers.

Thus Transform-Origin's acceleration-gear design思路 is completely opposite to Condensed-Origin: Condensed-Origin is "fast trial-and-error slow solidification"; Transform-Origin is "slow accumulation, fast crossing, long cooldown."
