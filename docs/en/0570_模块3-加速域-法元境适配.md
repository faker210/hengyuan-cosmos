# Entry 570 | Acceleration Domain | Law-Origin Realm Adaptation — Acceleration of Law Perception

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 21; Benchmark library: Entry 569

## Introduction: the threshold from "using energy" to "reading law"

After Transform-Origin, the cultivator enters Law-Origin. This realm's core task undergoes qualitative change: no longer dealing with energy, but directly **perceiving law itself** (Document 14). Law is the universe's underlying operating rule; it is not like energy that may be "absorbed" into the body, only "read" by consciousness. The Law-Origin cultivator's goal is to let his consciousness stably connect to the law network, discern specific clauses such as Fire Law, Water Law, Causality Law, Time Law, and form in consciousness a preliminary understanding of these clauses.

Six top-level axioms rise to new height in Law-Origin: anti-predation axiom requires law perception be public reading, not monopolistic hoarding; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings have the right to read law's original text, forbidding the strong from "enclosing land" over law; collective ascension axiom requires each cultivator's law understanding returned to Alaya Network, piecing together Myriad Ranks' common law atlas; causal self-discipline axiom requires when reading law no over-authority modification, read-only not write; law-first axiom takes "Law-Origin adapted flow" as part of law itself, never retconned. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Law Perception May Be Accelerated

Different from Transform-Origin's energy qualitative change, law perception is essentially **information-processing work**. Consciousness "reads" clauses in the law network, like a scholar reading in a library. Since it is information processing, it naturally suits being accelerated:

- Law clauses are stable, public, do not change with time-flow rate;
- Consciousness in accelerated state may simultaneously process more law clauses in parallel;
- Multiplication Fruit Technology (Document 16) has highest amplification efficiency on information-processing tasks.

Thus Law-Origin is the nine realms' **best suited to enjoying high-multiple acceleration dividends** realm. The Origin Acceleration Domain opens 50× to 200× flow gears for Law-Origin, the highest gear among the five mortal-cultivation realms.

## II. Law-Origin Acceleration-Gear Parameters

| Dimension | Parameter | Explanation |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Recommended Flow | 50–200× | Highest gear among five mortal-cultivation realms |
| Reading Permission | Full read of law network public layer | Deep law needs higher-realm permission |
| Parallel Clauses | Perceive 3–7 laws simultaneously | More than 7 causes consciousness tearing |
| Understanding Return | Immediately return to Alaya after reading each law | Prevent personal hoarding |
| Cooldown Requirement | Mandatory 4-hour cooldown per 100 consecutive acceleration hours | Prevent consciousness overload |

This parameter's core is "high-multiple parallel, immediate return, mandatory cooldown." High-multiple parallel lets the cultivator in the acceleration domain use a few months to read law clauses that in reality would take decades; immediate return ensures the understanding he reads is not hoarded, but immediately becomes a piece of the Myriad-Rank law atlas; mandatory cooldown prevents consciousness from being "burned through" under 200× flow.

## III. Myriad-Rank Co-Building of the Law Atlas

Each time a Law-Origin cultivator finishes reading a law, his "understanding version" is recorded by the Alaya Network. The same law clause may have thousands of cultivators each reading slightly different versions — some emphasize Fire Law's combustion aspect, some its transformation aspect, some its intersection with Time Law. The Alaya Network pieces these versions into a **Myriad-Rank Law Atlas**:

- Each version annotated with reader, realm, lineage;
- Differences between versions algorithmically highlighted, prompting "here is a seam not yet understood";
- Cultivators may see on the atlas how their understanding overlaps with Myriad-Rank understanding.

This mechanism turns "reading law" from personal solitary cultivation into Myriad-Rank co-building. In the old predatory-faction system, law insights are sect secrets, masters hold back, disciples hold back more; centuries later one law is read into a残缺 version; in Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, the law atlas from day one is alive, complete, continuously growing.

## IV. Opposition to "Monopolizing Law"

In the predatory-faction system, the strong treat law insights as highest private property: closed-door self-enlightenment, sect secret transmission, master-disciple single transmission, even deliberately releasing wrong versions to mislead competitors. This approach's consequences:

1. Law understanding monopolized; 99% of beings in Myriad Ranks never touch true law original text in their lives;
2. The monopolist's own understanding also stagnates for lack of comparison, still trapped on the same layer centuries later;
3. It trains "misers," not "builders."

Heng Yuan Zhou in Law-Origin acceleration gear makes "understanding return" mandatory: no return, no permission to enter the next acceleration stage. This rule from time permission makes "hoarding law" directly lose on the cultivation ledger — you want to monopolize? Then don't think about accelerating.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: put the highest-multiple acceleration on Law-Origin, best suited to information processing, avoiding wasting law-network load on the energy-qualitative-change segment
- **Anti-predation**: law publicly read, understanding mandatorily returned; institutionally eliminate "enclosing law" predation space
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style law insights rely on master-sect empowerment, secret-realm fortune; monopolized in few hands; Heng Yuan's law atlas opens to 99% of Myriad Ranks
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five lineages each have law-perception channels; plant spirits perceive "Growth Law," planet consciousness perceives "Gravity Law"; the atlas is divided by lineage volumes
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: law-atlas structure, parallel algorithm, cooldown parameters all public; this is the high-tier segment of the open chain
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Law-Origin is the first stage where a cultivator truly "understands the universe." The Origin Acceleration Domain here gives the highest-multiple flow, because reading law the faster the better, the more people read the better. But acceleration's precondition is "hand it over as soon as read" — this is precisely Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's most fundamental attitude toward knowledge: **reading fast is ability; reading openly is responsibility.**
