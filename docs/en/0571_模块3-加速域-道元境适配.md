# Entry 571 | Acceleration Domain | Dao-Origin Realm Adaptation — Acceleration of Dao-Principles Condensation

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 21; Benchmark library: Entry 570

## Introduction: the leap from "reading law" to "establishing a Dao-Principle"

Law-Origin lets the cultivator read law's original text; Dao-Origin requires him in his own consciousness to **condense a Dao-Principle belonging to himself** (Document 14). The so-called Dao-Principle is not law itself, but the cultivator's personalized understanding, application principle, and behavioral code of some law — it is the self-legislation of "how I will use this law." A Law-Origin cultivator of Fire Law, upon reaching Dao-Origin must answer: what do I burn with fire, what not, why burn, to what degree burn. Once this answer solidifies in consciousness, it is his Dao-Principle.

Six top-level axioms enter the core in Dao-Origin: anti-predation axiom requires the Dao-Principle be a "symbiotic Dao-Principle," may not write "predation is justice" into one's Dao; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires Dao-Principles coexist equally, forbidding one lineage's Dao-Principle lording over others; collective ascension axiom requires Dao-Principles ultimately serve Myriad Ranks' ascension, not personal flight; causal self-discipline axiom requires once a Dao-Principle is wrongly established, there must be a correction mechanism; law-first axiom takes "Dao-Origin adapted flow" as a law clause, never retconned. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Dao-Principle Condensation Needs "Half-Fast Half-Slow"

Dao-Principle condensation is a composite task: half is information integration (piecing together law understandings read in Law-Origin into a self-consistent system), half is personality legislation (internalizing the system into "I am this kind of person"). The former suits acceleration; the latter does not.

The Origin Acceleration Domain therefore designs a **two-stage flow** for Dao-Origin:

- **Dao-Principle Draft Stage**: 100× flow, use the acceleration domain to quickly integrate law understandings, write multiple Dao-Principle drafts;
- **Dao-Principle Legislation Stage**: 1× real flow, use months to years in real life to test drafts, confirming it truly fits one's personality.

This is in the same line as Condensed-Origin's "fast trial-and-error slow solidification," but more emphasizes legislation's solemnity: once a Dao-Principle solidifies in consciousness, it becomes the underlying code for all the cultivator's future behavior; if wrong, harder to fix than a wrong inner circuit.

## II. Dao-Origin Acceleration-Gear Parameters

| Stage | Flow | Output | Exit Standard |
|-------|------|--------|----------------|
| Draft Integration | 100× | 3–7 Dao-Principle drafts | Drafts logically self-consistent |
| Real-Life Test | 1× | Test records | Draft does not collapse in real conflict |
| Dao-Principle Solidification | 50× | Formal Dao-Principle | Alaya Network review passes |
| Dao-Principle Publication | Public | Myriad-Rank review | No objections during publication period |

Especially noteworthy is the "Dao-Principle publication" step: the cultivator's Dao-Principle after solidification must be published to Myriad Ranks, accept review. This is not formalism, but causal self-discipline axiom's direct manifestation — your Dao-Principle will affect how you use power in the future; Myriad Ranks have the right to know what it is before you use it.

## III. Symbiotic Dao-Principle's Review Standards

The Alaya Network has a set of automatic review standards for Dao-Principle drafts, three core articles:

1. **No predation**: the Dao-Principle may not contain clauses such as "using devouring others as the evolutionary path";
2. **No monopoly**: the Dao-Principle may not contain clauses such as "claiming law knowledge as private property";
3. **No exclusion**: the Dao-Principle may not contain clauses such as "some race naturally inferior to others."

Any one failing, the draft must be sent back for rewriting. This standard directly compiles the six top-level axioms into a Dao-Principle review algorithm — the cultivator wants to secretly stuff predation logic into his Dao? The algorithm is the first to refuse.

## IV. Opposition to "Heresy Dao-Principles"

In the predatory-faction system, Dao-Principle condensation is completely private: the strong legislate in closed-door cultivation, use it as established, no one governs. Thus history saw countless "heresy Dao-Principles" — proving the Dao by killing, by devouring, by world-destruction. These Dao-Principles may be self-consistent at the personal level, but at the civilizational level are disasters.

Heng Yuan Zhou's solution is not to forbid cultivators from legislating, but to place legislative power within Myriad-Rank co-governance framework: you may establish your Dao, but your Dao must withstand Myriad-Rank review, must conform to the five axioms, must leave traces on the Alaya Network. This is not suppressing individuality, but making individuality compatible with symbiosis.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: draft-stage high-multiple acceleration, legislation-stage real flow, avoiding compressing personality legislation into a consciousness game
- **Anti-predation**: Dao-Principle review algorithm automatically identifies predatory clauses, eliminating heresy Dao-Principles at the source
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style Dao-Principle relies on protagonist's comprehension self-enlightenment; if wrong no one corrects; Heng Yuan's Dao-Principle must be published to Myriad Ranks; if wrong someone corrects
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five lineages each have Dao-Principle templates; plant spirits' "Growth Dao-Principle," planet consciousness's "Balance Dao-Principle" also published
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Dao-Principle draft library, review algorithm, publication records all public; this is the high-tier segment of the open chain
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## VI. Dynamic Revision Mechanism After Dao-Principle Solidification

Dao-Principles after publication are not unchanging. Anchor Symbiotic Civilization designs a periodic review mechanism for solidified Dao-Principles: each time a cultivator experiences a major life turning point — helping others, erring, losing, bearing responsibility — he should proactively return to the Alaya Network, apply for a minor revision of his Dao-Principle. The revision process is the same as first legislation: draft-stage acceleration, real-life test, Myriad-Rank publication. This mechanism turns the Dao-Principle from a once-and-for-all manifesto into a living law that grows with life. In the predatory-faction system, the strong one's Dao-Principle once established never changes for life, even if long disconnected from reality; Heng Yuan Zhou allows Dao-Principles to grow with the cultivator; this is precisely the law-first axiom's true understanding of coherence — coherence is not unchanging, but able to maintain logical closure amid change.

## Conclusion

Dao-Origin is the key realm from "understanding the universe" to "defining how one treats the universe." The Origin Acceleration Domain here gives him efficiency to quickly integrate drafts, yet hands final legislative power back to real time and Myriad-Rank review. A Dao-Principle able to withstand publication is a Dao-Principle truly worthy of Origin Realm and higher realms.
