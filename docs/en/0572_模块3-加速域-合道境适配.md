# Entry 572 | Acceleration Domain | Dao-Integration Realm Adaptation — Acceleration of Law Fusion

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 21; Benchmark library: Entry 571

## Introduction: from "one Dao" to "multiple Daos fused"

Dao-Origin lets the cultivator establish his own Dao-Principle; Dao-Integration requires him to **fuse** this Dao-Principle with other laws, other Dao-Principles (Document 14). Dao-Integration is the sage-cultivation four realms' first realm, meaning the cultivator henceforth enters "sage" ranks. Fusion is not simple superposition, but requires originally independent law clauses to form a self-consistent whole in his consciousness — how do Fire Law and Water Law coexist? How do Time Law and Causality Law mutually verify? These questions must be answered in Dao-Integration.

Six top-level axioms in Dao-Integration enter the sage stage's first major exam: anti-predation axiom requires fusion be "symbiotic fusion," not "devouring other Daos"; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the cultivator's Dao-Principle fuse with Myriad-Rank Dao-Principles, not lord over; collective ascension axiom requires the fused law whole feed back to Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom requires the fusion process not perturb real causality; law-first axiom takes "Dao-Integration adapted flow" as a law clause, never retconned. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Law Fusion Especially Suits Acceleration

Dao-Integration's core task is "doing large-scale logical integration in consciousness." This is similar to Law-Origin's "reading," both information-processing tasks, but larger scale: Law-Origin reads single laws; Dao-Integration pieces dozens of laws into a self-consistent system. This task naturally suits high-multiple acceleration:

- Logical relations between laws are stable, do not change with time-flow rate;
- Consciousness in accelerated state may process more fusion branches in parallel;
- "Logical conflicts" during fusion may be quickly trial-and-errored in the acceleration domain.

Thus Dao-Integration is the sage-cultivation four realms' **best suited to high-multiple acceleration** realm. The Origin Acceleration Domain opens 100× to 500× flow for Dao-Integration.

## II. Dao-Integration Acceleration-Gear Parameters

| Dimension | Parameter | Explanation |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Recommended Flow | 100–500× | Highest gear among sage-cultivation four realms |
| Fusion-Branch Count | Explore 3–11 fusion paths simultaneously | More than 11 splits consciousness |
| Conflict Arbitration | Alaya Network real-time arbitration | Prevent fusion logic from self-contradiction |
| Fusion Cooldown | Per 500 consecutive acceleration hours, 24-hour cooldown | Prevent consciousness overheating |
| Fusion Output Return | Full return after fusion system complete | For Myriad-Rank reuse |

This parameter's key is "multi-branch parallel, network arbitration, full return." Multi-branch parallel lets the cultivator in the acceleration domain use a few months to try fusion paths that in reality would take centuries; network arbitration ensures he does not self-contradict during fusion; full return makes his fusion system immediately Myriad Ranks' public asset.

## III. Myriad-Rank Reuse of Fusion Systems

A Dao-Integration cultivator's completed fusion system is not his private property. The Alaya Network disassembles it into "fusion modules," opens to Myriad Ranks:

- Other Dao-Integration cultivators may directly call verified fusion modules, not start from zero;
- Technique masters develop new technique systems based on these modules;
- Fusion-failed cases also enter the library, as "civilizational correction" material.

This mechanism turns Dao-Integration from "personal enlightenment" into a "Myriad-Rank co-built fusion library." In the old predatory-faction system, Dao-Integration is the strong one's private result of centuries in closed-door cultivation; outsiders cannot even glance; in Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, fusion modules are public goods from day one.

## IV. Opposition to "Devouring Other Daos"

In the predatory-faction system, Dao-Integration's shortcut is "devouring others' Dao-Principles": seizing others' established Dao-Principles, forcibly merging into own consciousness. This approach's problems:

1. Forcibly merged Dao-Principles incompatible with own, long-term internal conflict;
2. The devoured one's Dao-Principle residues will pollute the fusion system, must rupture later;
3. It trains "robbers," not "fusors."

Heng Yuan Zhou in Dao-Integration acceleration gear does zero-tolerance detection of "foreign Dao-Principle residue": once found, immediately freeze acceleration permission, force entry into Quest Sediment Domain to walk the complete fusion process. This rule makes "Dao-devouring"彻底 lose on the time ledger.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: put the highest-multiple acceleration on Dao-Integration, best suited to information integration, avoiding invalid idling in consciousness
- **Anti-predation**: fusion all self-built; foreign Dao-Principle residue zero-tolerance; from time permission seals the Dao-devouring shortcut
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style Dao-Integration relies on protagonist's talent self-enlightenment; fusion is private; Heng Yuan's fusion modules open to 99% of Myriad Ranks
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five lineages' fusion modules each build libraries; consciousness-information race's "logical fusion," energy-condensation race's "resonance fusion" also included
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: fusion algorithm, module library, arbitration rules all public; this is the sage segment of the open chain
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## VI. Version Iteration of Fusion Modules

Fusion modules output by Dao-Integration are not final versions, but living documents under continuous iteration. The Alaya Network maintains version numbers for each fusion module: a cultivator finding a module malfunctioning in Quest-Domain life submits an issue; the acceleration domain releases a new version based on the issue; the new version after simulated verification replaces the old. This version iteration keeps fusion systems forever synchronized with Myriad Ranks' real use. In the predatory-faction system, a fusion system once self-enlightened by the strong is closed-garaged, not updated for centuries; Heng Yuan Zhou's fusion modules iterate openly like open-source software, sturdier the more used.

## Conclusion

Dao-Integration is the key realm from "one Dao" to "ten thousand methods returning to origin." The Origin Acceleration Domain here gives the sage stage's highest-multiple flow, because law fusion the faster the better, the more people try the better. But acceleration's precondition is "hand it over as soon as fused" — this is precisely Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's most fundamental attitude toward knowledge continued at the sage stage.
