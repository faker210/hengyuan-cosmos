# Entry 573 | Acceleration Domain | Boundary-Breaking Realm Adaptation — Acceleration of Plane Breakthrough

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 81; Benchmark library: Entry 572

## Introduction: the threshold from "within the plane" to "cross-plane"

After Dao-Integration, the cultivator enters Boundary-Breaking. This realm's core task is **breaking through the plane barrier** (Document 14): from the material plane into the energy layer, consciousness domain, and even other same-tier planes. Boundary-Breaking is the sage-cultivation four realms' second realm, and the cultivator's first true sense of "leaving the plane of one's birth." This matter is extremely sensitive energetically, causally, ethically — cross-plane is not just physical movement, but causality's cross-border.

Six top-level axioms in Boundary-Breaking enter the most sensitive zone: anti-predation axiom requires boundary-breaking be not "invading other worlds to plunder," but "going with goodwill, returning with knowledge"; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires cross-plane channels open equally to Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires boundary-breaking gains feed back the birth plane; causal self-discipline axiom requires cross-plane strictly obey "observe-first-limited-intervention, only-knowledge-returned, causality not perturbed" (Documents 83, 87); law-first axiom takes "Boundary-Breaking adapted flow" as a law clause, never retconned. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Boundary-Breaking Needs "Accelerated Observation, Slow Crossing"

The boundary-breaking task splits into two segments: the first is **observation** — in the acceleration domain using high-multiple flow to survey in detail the target plane's law structure, civilizational state, causal texture; the second is **crossing** — truly passing through the plane barrier into the target plane.

Observation is information processing, suits acceleration; crossing is causal intervention, does not suit acceleration. Thus Boundary-Breaking's acceleration-gear design principle is "fast observation, slow crossing, strict isolation":

- **Observation Segment**: 200–1000× flow, in the acceleration domain use a few days to complete years of survey of the target plane;
- **Crossing Segment**: 1× real flow, slowly cross the barrier in isolated space, Alaya Network watching throughout;
- **Isolation Segment**: after crossing stay in isolated space, confirm not carrying target-plane causal residues before allowing contact with local civilization.

This design strictly obeys Document 82 Basic Principles of Temporal Retrocognition and Document 89 Temporal-Paradox Prevention Mechanism: observation in the acceleration domain does not change the target plane; crossing-segment intervention is strictly limited to "only knowledge returned."

## II. Boundary-Breaking Acceleration-Gear Parameters

| Stage | Flow | Output | Exit Standard |
|-------|------|--------|----------------|
| Plane Survey | 200–1000× | Complete target-plane atlas | Atlas passes Alaya review |
| Barrier Crossing | 1× | Safe crossing | No causal residue carried |
| Isolation Observation | 1× | Observation records | No anomaly during isolation period |
| Knowledge Return | 500× | Return data packet | Data packet passes ethical review |

Especially emphasized: the Boundary-Breaking cultivator **must not** directly use the acceleration domain in the target plane. All his activities in the target plane must proceed at real flow; this is causal self-discipline axiom's hard constraint — you cannot run hundredfold speed to cause trouble in someone else's universe.

## III. Cross-Plane Knowledge Shared by Myriad Ranks

Knowledge the Boundary-Breaking cultivator brings back from the target plane is not personal private property, but Myriad Ranks' public asset:

- Knowledge data packets enter the library after ethical review, open to Myriad Ranks;
- If the target plane is a low-tier civilization, knowledge is only returned, not output (causality not perturbed);
- If the target plane is an already-contacted civilization, knowledge may be bidirectionally shared.

This mechanism turns boundary-breaking from "treasure-hunting expedition" into "civilizational exchange." In the old predatory-faction system, boundary-breaking is invasion; the strong barge into low-tier planes burning, killing, looting; in Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, boundary-breaking is going with respect, returning with knowledge, isolated throughout, traced throughout.

## IV. Opposition to "Plane Predation"

In the predatory-faction system, a Boundary-Breaking strong one's typical behavior is: barge into low-tier planes, plunder resources, enslave beings, devour origin. This behavior is彻底 sealed in Heng Yuan Zhou:

1. The acceleration-domain observation stage already marks the target plane's civilizational state; low-tier planes automatically enter "observe only, do not intervene" mode;
2. Crossing segment mandatory isolation, no direct contact allowed;
3. Once any predatory behavior found, the Anchor Supervisory Committee immediately revokes boundary-breaking permission; severe cases recover the realm.

Causal self-discipline axiom here is not a slogan, but written into every boundary-breaking operation's process.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: put high-multiple acceleration on the observation segment; the crossing segment firmly slows, avoiding causal overheating
- **Anti-predation**: boundary-breaking throughout isolated, knowledge returned, zero predation; from process seals plane invasion
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style boundary-breaking relies on protagonist's strength crushing other worlds; predation is the norm; Heng Yuan's boundary-breaking is civilizational exchange, Myriad Ranks equal
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five lineages each have boundary-breaking channels; consciousness-information race's "consciousness boundary-breaking," mechanical race's "engineering boundary-breaking" also included
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: plane atlas, isolation process, return standards all public; this is the cross-plane segment of the open chain
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## VI. Ethical Review of Cross-Plane Knowledge Return

Knowledge data packets brought back by Boundary-Breaking must pass Alaya Network's ethical review before return: confirm they contain no target-plane private information, do not expose low-tier plane location coordinates, do not bring military technology back to unenlightened civilizations. After review passes, packets enter the library graded by document. This step落实 causal self-discipline axiom to the finest detail: things you bring back from outside may trigger technology explosion in your own plane; must ensure the explosion does not hurt innocents.

## VII. Psychological Preparation for Boundary-Breaking Trials

Cross-plane is not only technical operation, but psychological test. During the time the cultivator waits in isolated space to cross, the Alaya Network proactively provides psychological-preparation resources: reading other boundary-breaking seniors' life journals, remote交流 with already-crossed cultivators, attending cross-plane psychological-preparation courses. This psychological-preparation step is often neglected by predatory-faction systems — the strong only teach how to break through the barrier, never how to face a strange universe. Heng Yuan Zhou writes psychological preparation into the boundary-breaking process, because a boundary-breaker not psychologically ready is more dangerous than a boundary-breaker not technically up to standard.

## Conclusion

Boundary-Breaking is the key realm where the cultivator for the first time walks out of his birth plane. The Origin Acceleration Domain here strictly limits high-multiple flow to the observation segment; the crossing segment firmly slows down, isolates. This is not conservatism, but awe of causality — **no matter how far you go, you cannot perturb others' civilizations at the cost.**
