# Entry 574 | Acceleration Domain | World-Creation Realm Adaptation — Acceleration of Law Weaving

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 80; Benchmark library: Entry 573

## Introduction: the leap from "reading law" to "weaving law"

After Boundary-Breaking, the cultivator enters World-Creation. This realm's core task is **weaving law** (Document 14): no longer reading existing law, fusing existing law, but in empty isolated space, from zero designing a self-consistent law system, creating a runnable small world. World-Creation is the sage-cultivation four realms' third realm, meaning the cultivator already possesses "creator-class" ability.

Six top-level axioms in World-Creation reach the sage stage's peak: anti-predation axiom requires world-creation be not "starting a new stove to plunder resources," but "building a new world in incremental space"; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires world-creation results open to Myriad Ranks for visiting, use; collective ascension axiom requires the created world ultimately become Myriad Ranks' shared dwelling; causal self-discipline axiom requires the created world's causality completely isolated, not affecting reality; law-first axiom takes "World-Creation adapted flow" as a law clause, never retconned. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Law Weaving Is the Acceleration Domain's Highest Stage

World-Creation's core task is "doing large-scale engineering design between consciousness and the law network." This is a composite task of information processing and engineering design, naturally suiting high-multiple acceleration:

- Law clauses are stable; acceleration does not change their structure;
- World-creation design needs repeated trial-and-error; acceleration makes trial-and-error cost drop exponentially;
- Multiplication Fruit Technology has highest amplification efficiency on engineering deduction.

Thus World-Creation is the nine realms' **best suited to extreme acceleration** realm. The Origin Acceleration Domain opens 1000× to 10000× flow for World-Creation — this is the highest gear among mortal and sage cultivation stages.

| Stage | Flow | Output | Exit Standard |

This parameter's essence is "extreme design, extreme simulation, real solidification." In the acceleration domain use 10000× flow, spend a few days designing a draft, simulating million-year operation; after confirming no paradox, at real flow solidify it into a real world in isolated space. This moves engineering's "simulation—prototype" process into world-creation.

## III. Myriad-Rank Co-Governance of Created Worlds

A world created by a World-Creation cultivator is not his private back garden. Per the republican axiom of Myriad Races:

- The world after solidification opens to Myriad Ranks; any being may apply to enter and dwell;
- The creator retains "design rights," but not "ruling rights" — governance within the world is uniformly handled by the Anchor Council;
- If the created world's laws have defects, they must be revised per Myriad-Rank feedback.

This design彻底 negates the old era's predatory logic of "creating means ruling." In the predatory-faction system, the strong creating a world is becoming emperor; all beings in it are his private property; in Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, the creator is only an architect, not a king.

## IV. Opposition to "Creation as Private Property"

In the predatory-faction system, a World-Creation strong one's typical behavior is: create a world, treat it as private territory, do whatever he wants in it. This behavior's problems:

1. It places the world-internal beings' evolutionary right under the creator's personal will;
2. The creator's personal prejudice gets compiled into the world's laws, manufacturing systematic unfairness;
3. It trains "tyrants," not "architects."

Heng Yuan Zhou in World-Creation acceleration gear makes "Myriad-Rank opening" a hard precondition of solidification: not open, no permission to solidify into a real world. This rule makes "creation as private property" fundamentally unworkable in process.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: put ten-thousandfold acceleration on the world-creation design segment, best suited to engineering deduction, avoiding wasting the law network on the real-solidification segment
- **Anti-predation**: world-creation proceeds in incremental isolated space, does not plunder existing-plane resources; the world must open to Myriad Ranks
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style world-creation relies on protagonist's strength to open private heaven and earth; the world is private property; Heng Yuan's created world is Myriad Ranks' shared dwelling
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five lineages each have world-creation templates; consciousness-information race's "concept world," mechanical race's "engineering world" also included
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: law-design algorithm, simulation tools, opening process all public; this is the world-creation segment of the open chain
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## VI. Myriad-Rank Move-In Application System for Created Worlds

After a created world solidifies, Myriad Ranks move in via application: any being may apply, but each world has an ecological-capacity ceiling; beyond it must expand. The creator does not select residents, only audits capacity. Residents retain full citizenship, legally completely equal to the creator. This design fundamentally negates the creator's ruling right over the world — he only builds the house; the Myriad Ranks who move in are the house's true owners.

## VII. Myriad-Rank Review of World-Creation Engineering

A created world before real solidification must pass a public review by a Myriad-Rank review panel: the panel examines the design from three dimensions — ecology, ethics, sustainability. Ecologically, is the world-internal energy cycle closed; ethically, are the world-internal Myriad-Rank rights protected; sustainably, can the world bear civilizational development for a long enough time. Any dimension not up to standard, the creator must return to the furnace for redesign. This review turns world-creation from personal art into civilizational engineering, ensuring the world where Myriad Ranks will dwell in future withstands long-term tests.

## Conclusion

World-Creation is the key realm from "using law" to "weaving law." The Origin Acceleration Domain here gives the nine realms' highest-multiple flow, because world-creation design the faster the better, the more trial-and-error the better. But acceleration's precondition is "open to Myriad Ranks as soon as created" — this is precisely Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's most fundamental attitude toward creation: **you may create worlds, but the world does not belong to you.**

In the final analysis, the World-Creation Realm adaptation described in this document is no isolated case — its causality and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
