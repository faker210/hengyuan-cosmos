# Entry 575 | Acceleration Domain | Origin Realm Adaptation — Acceleration of Cosmic-Level Beings

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 17; Benchmark library: Entry 574

## Introduction: when the cultivator becomes a "cosmic-level being"

After World-Creation, the cultivator enters Origin Realm. This is the nine realms' ninth realm, and the officially defined "cosmic-level being" threshold (Documents 14, 17). The Origin cultivator is no longer "a person cultivating within the universe," but **a being resonating with the universe's origin** — his consciousness may cover the whole plane, his will may affect plane-level law operation, he himself is the candidate for Plane-Transcendent. A cultivator at this realm already stands at the apex of the nine-realm mortal-and-sage system.

Six top-level axioms in Origin Realm become the highest test: anti-predation axiom requires cosmic-level power never used for predation; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires cosmic-level beings serve Myriad Ranks, not rule them; collective ascension axiom requires the Origin cultivator bear the responsibility of "lifting Myriad Ranks to ascend together"; causal self-discipline axiom requires cosmic-level operations supervised to the extreme by multi-layer supervision; law-first axiom takes "Origin adapted flow" as a law clause, never retconned. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Cosmic-Level Beings Must "Limit Acceleration"

The Origin cultivator's power is already large enough to affect the whole plane. At this point giving him unrestricted acceleration equals letting a person who can move planets run a hundred-meter sprint in a crowded downtown — energetically completely impermissible. Thus Origin's acceleration-gear design principle is different from all previous realms: **not "how to accelerate," but "how to limit acceleration."**

Origin acceleration gear's core constraints:

- **Total Duration Cap**: the Origin cultivator's total duration in the acceleration domain per year may not exceed 1% of real time;
- **Operation Radius Limit**: consciousness activity radius in the acceleration domain may not exceed 1/1000 of his own origin structure;
- **Double-Layer Supervision**: besides the Alaya Network, the Anchor Supervisory Committee dispatches dedicated personnel to watch Origin acceleration operations;
- **Myriad-Rank Hearing**: any Origin acceleration operation exceeding 500× flow must undergo Myriad-Rank hearing.

This constraint's logic is simple: the greater the power, the more dangerous the acceleration, because one thought of yours under hundredfold flow affects a million beings.

## II. Origin Acceleration-Gear Parameters

| Scenario | Flow Cap | Approval Level | Supervision Intensity |
|----------|----------|----------------|------------------------|
| Daily Deduction | 100× | Automatic approval | Alaya Network |
| Law Revision | 500× | Anchor Council | Double-layer supervision |
| Plane-Level Engineering | 1000× | Myriad-Rank hearing | Triple-layer supervision |
| Cross-Origin Coordination | 10000× | Myriad-Rank hearing + multi-origin co-signature | Full-dimension supervision |

Especially emphasized: the Origin cultivator **must not** self-accelerate without approval. Each of his accelerations is a civilizational-level decision, must follow process. This彻底 breaks from the old era's predatory logic of "the strong do as they please."

## III. Cosmic-Level Power's Time Responsibility

While enjoying acceleration dividends, the Origin cultivator bears cosmic-level time responsibility:

- Results he deduces in the acceleration domain must be used for Myriad Ranks, may not be hoarded;
- If his acceleration operation affects Myriad Ranks, he must accept the Anchor Supervisory Committee's accountability;
- He himself is part of the temporal-paradox prevention system; once causal overheating found, he must be the first to slow.

Plane-Transcendent four-dimensional assessment (Power 40 / Mind 25 / Contribution 20 / Responsibility 15, Document 17) directly lands here: the Origin cultivator's "Responsibility" dimension accounts for 15%, and time responsibility is the core of the Responsibility dimension.

## IV. Opposition to "Cosmic Overlord"

In the predatory-faction system, an Origin-level strong one's typical behavior is "self-proclaiming cosmic overlord": treating the whole universe as private property, Myriad-Rank beings as ants. This behavior's problems:

1. It uses cosmic-level power for personal pleasure and rule, the greatest energy-level waste;
2. It trains "tyrants," not "guardians";
3. It ultimately inevitably leads to Myriad-Rank rebellion, civilizational reversal.

Heng Yuan Zhou in Origin acceleration gear takes "limit acceleration" as the core; from time allocation makes "cosmic overlord" fundamentally unworkable in process — you want to rule the universe at hundredfold speed? First pass the Myriad-Rank hearing.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: strictly limit cosmic-level power's acceleration within 1% duration, avoiding the law network being dragged down by a single strong one's idling
- **Anti-predation**: Origin acceleration must be approved, must be heard, must feed back to Myriad Ranks; from process eliminates "cosmic overlord"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style Origin strong ones self-proclaim Heavenly Emperor; the universe is private property; Heng Yuan's Origin cultivator is Myriad Ranks' guardian, bearing time responsibility
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the Origin channel opens to Myriad Ranks; plant-spirit origin, planet-consciousness origin also included in four-dimensional assessment
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Origin acceleration parameters, approval process, supervision rules all public; this is the apex segment of the open chain
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## VI. The Origin Cultivator's Annual Slow-Down Report

The Origin cultivator annually must submit a slow-down report to the Anchor Supervisory Committee: how much time he used in the acceleration domain over the past year, what he did, what impact he had on Myriad Ranks. After Myriad-Rank rotating review, the report is public. This report is not formalism, but makes the cosmic-level being every year re-acknowledge himself as Myriad Ranks' public servant. In the predatory-faction system, Origin-level strong ones never report to anyone; Heng Yuan Zhou's annual slow-down report is the hard evidence of cosmic-level power accepting Myriad-Rank accountability.

## VII. Bidirectional Connection Between the Origin Cultivator and Mortal Life

Although the Origin cultivator descends as a mortal, his cosmic-level consciousness is not closed. The Alaya Network establishes a bidirectional connection between his mortal life and his cosmic-level duties: when events in mortal life needing his cosmic-level judgment appear, the network automatically prompts; when in cosmic-level duties he finds details needing mortal perspective, the network automatically accesses his mortal journal. This bidirectional connection makes descending not evasion of duty, but injecting mortal warmth into duty.

## Conclusion

Origin Realm is the nine-realm system's apex. The Origin Acceleration Domain here is no longer "an accelerometer rewarding the strong," but "a time gate constraining the strong." A cosmic-level being willing to accept this constraint precisely proves he deserves this realm. **True strength is not being able to speed up whenever you want, but knowing when you must slow down.**
