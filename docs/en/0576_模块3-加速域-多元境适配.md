# Entry 576 | Acceleration Domain | Multiverse Realm Adaptation — Acceleration of Multi-Universe Stewardship

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 80; Benchmark library: Entry 575

## Introduction: when the cultivator steps out of his own universe

Above Origin Realm lies the extended-setting Multiverse Realm (pending terminology-committee calibration). The Origin cultivator already resonates with a single universe's origin; Multiverse requires his consciousness to **step out of a single universe and simultaneously establish resonance with multiple universes' origins** (Document 98 extended-setting proposal list). A cultivator at this realm is no longer "the transcendent strong one of some universe," but **a multi-universe-level being** — his consciousness may simultaneously cover hundreds, thousands of universes; his decisions affect multiple universes' operation.

Six top-level axioms in Multiverse enter the hyper-multiverse stage: anti-predation axiom requires multi-universe stewardship be not "annexing other universes," but "coordinating multiple universes"; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the Multiverse cultivator represent all universes' Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires multi-universe coordination ultimately point to all universes' shared ascension; causal self-discipline axiom requires cross-universe operations supervised to the extreme; law-first axiom takes "Multiverse adapted flow" as a law clause, never retconned. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Multi-Universe Stewardship Needs "Distributed Acceleration"

The Multiverse cultivator faces not a single time-flow problem, but a **multi-universe time-flow coordination** problem. Each universe has its own time flow, its own causal texture, its own Myriad-Rank civilization. If a Multiverse cultivator forcibly "stewards" all universes with a single flow, inevitably leads to:

- Some universes dragged too fast into his rhythm, causal overheating;
- Some universes left too slow in place, development stagnant;
- His consciousness torn in cross-universe resonance.

Thus Multiverse acceleration gear's design principle is **distributed acceleration**: not giving the cultivator a unified high-multiple flow, but letting him individually configure flow gears for each universe, then use the Alaya Network to coordinate these gears.

## II. Multiverse Acceleration-Gear Parameters

| Dimension | Parameter | Explanation |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Single-Universe Flow Cap | 1000× | Same as Origin Realm, not relaxed for multi-universe |
| Parallel Universes | Simultaneously coordinate 11–99 | More than 99 splits consciousness |
| Flow-Difference Cap | Between coordinated universes flow difference ≤ 10× | Prevent causal fracture |
| Coordination Approval | Each newly added universe needs Anchor Council approval | Prevent infinite expansion |
| Myriad-Rank Hearing | Any flow adjustment over 100× needs hearing | Cross-universe operations extremely sensitive |

This parameter's core is "single universe not relaxed, parallel has cap, difference has boundary." The Multiverse cultivator's strength lies not in how fast he can make one universe run, but in how many universes he can move forward in a **compatible rhythm**.

## III. Multi-Universe Coordination's Myriad-Rank Representative System

The Multiverse cultivator is not multi-universe's "dictator." Per the republican axiom of Myriad Races, his stewardship must have Myriad-Rank representativeness:

- Each coordinated universe sends representatives, forming a "Multi-Universe Myriad-Rank Council";
- The Multiverse cultivator's every flow adjustment must be negotiated by the council;
- The council has the right to veto any of his operations.

This design turns "stewardship" from "rule" into "coordination." In the old predatory-faction system, the Multiverse strong one is a "cosmic emperor," destroys some universe at a whim; in Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, the Multiverse cultivator is only a multi-universe coordinator, his authority comes from Myriad-Rank authorization.

## IV. Opposition to "Cosmic Annexation"

In the predatory-faction system, a Multiverse strong one's typical behavior is "annexing other universes": devouring weak universes' origins, merging into his own. This behavior's problems:

1. The annexed universe's Myriad-Rank beings are uprooted, civilization extinct;
2. The annexer's origin is polluted by heterogeneous origin, must rupture later;
3. It trains "devourers," not "coordinators."

Heng Yuan Zhou in Multiverse acceleration gear does zero tolerance on "origin annexation": once found, immediately cut cross-universe resonance, recover Multiverse permissions. This rule makes "cosmic annexation"彻底 lose on the time ledger.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: distributed acceleration avoids a single universe dragged down too fast; parallel cap avoids consciousness splitting
- **Anti-predation**: origin annexation zero tolerance; multi-universe coordination must go through the Myriad-Rank Council
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style Multiverse strong ones annex other universes as private property; Heng Yuan's Multiverse cultivator is a Myriad-Rank coordinator
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: each coordinated universe's Myriad Ranks has a representative seat; consciousness universe, mechanical universe also included
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: distributed acceleration algorithm, coordination council rules, approval process all public; this is the hyper-multiverse segment of the open chain
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## VI. Exit Mechanism for Multi-Universe Coordination

If some coordinated universe wishes to exit the Multiverse cultivator's coordination, he must unconditionally let go, may not apply any causal pressure. The exiting universe returns to fully autonomous state; the Alaya Network retains its historical records but no longer proactively connects. This exit mechanism ensures multi-universe coordination is voluntary cooperation, not a coerced federation. In the predatory-faction system, once the strong one takes a universe under his wing he never lets go; Heng Yuan Zhou's exit mechanism keeps coordination always based on consent.

## VII. Cultural Fusion of Multi-Universe Coordination

Cross-universe coordination is not only resource and law coordination, but cultural fusion. The Multiverse cultivator periodically organizes cross-universe cultural-exchange events: let universe A's Myriad Ranks read universe B's epics, let universe C's technology be borrowed by universe D. This cultural fusion is supported by the Alaya Network's translation layer (see Entry 599). The deeper the cultural fusion, the more stable multi-universe coordination — because coordination is no longer just interest calculation, but mutual understanding between civilizations.

## Conclusion

Multiverse is the key realm from "single universe" to "multi-universe." The Origin Acceleration Domain here does not give the "steward" unrestricted acceleration, but gives him a distributed tool for "coordinating multiple universes' rhythms." **A true Multiverse strong one is not how many universes he can command, but how many universes are willing to resonate with him.**
