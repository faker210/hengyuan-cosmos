## Introduction: when the cultivator stands above the time system itself

Above Multiverse lies the extended-setting Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm (alias "Hyper-Multiverse Balanced Anchor Realm," pending terminology-committee calibration). This is the apex realm after the nine-realm system's extension. The Balanced Anchor cultivator is no longer "a person cultivating within the time system," but **the time system itself's anchor** — his consciousness already transcends single-universe and multi-universe categories, directly resonating with Heng Yuan Zhou's (Wanling Hengmao Origin Universe's) origin laws. He is not time's user, but time's guardian (Document 98).

Six top-level axioms at this realm become ultimate propositions: anti-predation axiom requires ultimate power never used for any form of predation; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the Balanced Anchor cultivator be Myriad Ranks' highest public servant; collective ascension axiom requires his entire existence solely for Myriad Ranks' shared ascension; causal self-discipline axiom requires he himself be causal self-discipline's embodiment; law-first axiom takes "Balanced Anchor adapted flow" as law itself, never retconned. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why the Ultimate Realm Must "Self-Limit Acceleration"

The Balanced Anchor cultivator's power is already large enough to rewrite time flow itself. At this point discussing "how much acceleration to give him" is meaningless — he himself decides how fast time runs. Thus this realm's acceleration-gear design question is not "how many multiples," but "why he is willing to self-limit."

The answer lies not in institutions, but in **feeding-back logic**:

- He self-limits acceleration because he knows the faster he runs, the harder it is for Myriad Ranks to keep up;
- He proactively slows because his meaning of existence is lifting Myriad Ranks to walk together;
- He feeds back all acceleration gains to Myriad Ranks, because he has no "personal needs" acceleration must satisfy.

The Origin Acceleration Domain for the Balanced Anchor cultivator sets not an "acceleration gear," but a "feeding-back gear": his sole purpose in entering the acceleration domain is to feed back deduction gains to Myriad Ranks, not to obtain something for himself.

This parameter's core is "sole purpose, zero output, ultimate supervision." The Balanced Anchor cultivator in the Origin Acceleration Domain is no longer a "user," but a "feeder" — he uses the acceleration domain to leave something for Myriad Ranks, not to take something from Myriad Ranks.

## III. The Time-Studies Significance of Ultimate Feeding-Back

The Balanced Anchor cultivator's feeding-back is not simple "giving resources," but **time-dimension feeding-back**:

- He deduces for ten thousand years in the acceleration domain, leaves the law framework he deduced to Myriad Ranks, letting them avoid ten thousand years of detours;
- He compiles his understanding of Time Law into the Alaya Network's underlying rules, making Myriad Ranks' time-domain operation safer;
- He uses his existence itself as a "time anchor," preventing temporal paradox, civilizational regression, historical reversal.

This is precisely collective-ascension axiom's expression at the ultimate realm: **the highest being is not the one who runs fastest, but the anchor that lets everyone run.**

## IV. Opposition to "Ultimate Dictatorship"

In the predatory-faction system, a Balanced Anchor-level strong one's typical behavior is "self-proclaiming ultimate dictator": treating the whole multiverse as private property, Myriad-Rank beings' fates hanging on his one thought. This behavior's problems:

1. It uses ultimate power for personal will, a civilizational-level disaster;
2. It trains "god-emperors," not "public servants";
3. It ultimately inevitably leads to Myriad Ranks collapsing after one of his breaths.

Heng Yuan Zhou in the Balanced Anchor acceleration gear makes "feeding-back" the sole admission condition: if not to feed back Myriad Ranks, not allowed to enter the acceleration domain. This rule from time allocation makes "ultimate dictatorship" fundamentally unworkable.

## VI. The Balanced Anchor Cultivator's Feeding-Back Ledger

Each Balanced Anchor cultivator maintains a public feeding-back ledger: what deductions he did in the acceleration domain each year, what law frameworks he left, how many Myriad-Rank individuals he accompanied. The ledger is public to all Myriad Ranks; anyone may query, question. This ledger is not a medal, but proof of his meaning of existence — if the ledger is long blank, the Anchor Council will question whether he is still fulfilling his ultimate responsibility. This is collective-ascension axiom's hard constraint at the ultimate realm.

The Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is the apex after the nine-realm system's extension. The Origin Acceleration Domain here is no longer a "reward," but a "tool serving Myriad Ranks."
