# Entry 578 | Quest Domain | Sense-Origin Realm Adaptation — Beginner Period's Deep Perception

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 53; Benchmark library: Entry 567

## Introduction: another way to walk the beginner period

The Origin Acceleration Domain designs "5–10× gentle acceleration" for Sense-Origin; the Quest Sediment Domain gives a completely different alternative — **0.3× to 1× slow deep perception**. This is not punishment, but respect for another type of cultivator: some are born unsuited to being accelerated; they need in normal or even slightly slow time sense to repeatedly chew "perceiving energy for the first time" until it becomes part of life.

Six top-level axioms take effect here as well: anti-predation axiom requires slow trials not be "punished slow endurance," but a proactively chosen true path; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the Quest Domain open to all beings, especially caring for life forms unsuited to acceleration; collective ascension axiom requires slow-trial insights ultimately returned to Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom requires slow trials not indulge, not disconnect from real causality; law-first axiom takes "Sense-Origin Quest Gear" as a law clause, never retconned. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Who Suits the Quest-Domain Beginner Path

The Quest-Domain Sense-Origin Gear is not "a substitute for the acceleration gear," but **another true path**. It suits three cultivator types:

1. **Consciousness-Sensitive Type**: consciousness itself flows fast, easily overshoots; acceleration instead makes them more anxious;
2. **Emotionally Rich Type**: need to sediment the emotional memory of "first perception"; acceleration would distort the memory;
3. **Strange-Life Type**: plant spirits, planet consciousness and other life's time scales are already measured in geological eras; acceleration is meaningless to them.

For these three types, 0.3× slowness is not slow, but **just right**. In slowness, they can see details flashed past in acceleration: the temperature of energy entering the body, the texture of law aura, the heartbeat at first resonance.

## II. Quest-Domain Sense-Origin Gear Parameters

| Dimension | Parameter | Explanation |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Recommended Flow | 0.3–1× | Close to or slightly slower than reality |
| Core Task | Deep perception, emotional record, insight sedimentation | Contrasts with acceleration gear's "task completion" |
| Trial Period | 1–10 real years | No speed pursuit |
| Exit Standard | Insight report passes Alaya review | Not "task complete," but "articulate insight" |
| Connection to Acceleration Domain | May switch to acceleration gear anytime | Two paths switchable anytime |

This parameter's essence is "not rushing time." In Quest-Domain Sense-Origin Gear, the cultivator has no KPI, no "must break through in so many years"; the only thing he must do is **feel through** perception. His insight report is recorded by the Alaya Network, becoming part of Myriad Ranks' perception textbook.

## III. The Insight Report's Myriad-Rank Value

The slow trial's most precious product is not "realm improvement," but the **insight report**. A cultivator spending ten years at 0.3× flow perceiving energy can write things far more delicate than a cultivator rushing over in two years at 5×:

- He can describe the "touch" of energy flowing in meridians;
- He can record the emotional fluctuations at first perception;
- He can compare perception differences across seasons, moods.

These details are unobtainable in the acceleration domain. The Alaya Network compiles these insight reports into the "Myriad-Rank Perception Studies" textbook, for all future beginners to read. This is precisely collective-ascension axiom's expression in the slow segment: **what one person slowly feels over ten years becomes a lamp for Myriad Ranks' beginner period.**

## IV. Opposition to "Ascetic Slow Endurance"

In the predatory-faction system, "slow" means punishment: erring and cast down to the mortal dust, insufficient resources only slow endurance, no talent only stacking time. This slowness is passive, painful, output-less.

Heng Yuan Zhou's Quest Domain slowness is completely different: it is **proactively chosen, output-producing, respected**. The cultivator enters the Quest Domain not because he is punished, but because he knows he needs to slow down; his slow trial is not meaningless endurance, but producing insight reports; his "slowness" is not looked down on by anyone, because the republican axiom of Myriad Races acknowledges: **those who walk slowly are also walking.**

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: letting consciousness-sensitive cultivators take the slow path, avoiding them being dragged into problems by acceleration, is itself energy saving
- **Anti-predation**: slow trials do not steal others' time, do not devour others' experiences, purely one's own insight
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style "slow" is punishment for no talent; Heng Yuan's Quest-Domain slowness is a proactively chosen true path, equal to the acceleration path
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: plant spirits, planet consciousness, consciousness-information race thrive in slowness; no lineage excluded
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: insight-report library, slow process, review standards all public; this is the slow segment of the open chain
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## VI. Exit and Transfer from Slow Beginner Period

If a cultivator in Quest-Domain Sense-Origin Gear finds he actually suits acceleration more, he may switch to acceleration gear anytime; vice versa. Between the two tracks there is no identity threshold, no one higher. This free switching itself is Myriad-Rank Republicanism's embodiment — you need not be assigned to a path, you may try yourself. The Alaya Network only reminds at switching: changing paths is not failure, but finding a rhythm more suited to yourself.

## VII. Mentorship in Slow Beginner Period

Quest-Domain Sense-Origin Gear is paired with a mentorship system: each beginner is guided by a cultivator who has already walked through this realm. The mentor does not teach technique, only how to feel — how to discern energy in slowness, how to record one's first times, how to get along with one's own impatience. Mentorship makes the slow beginner period not lonely, and gives the beginner's insights a mature reference. This system contrasts with the acceleration domain's self-learning: acceleration relies on algorithms; the Quest Domain relies on mentors.

## Conclusion

Sense-Origin's Quest Gear tells cultivators one thing: entering cultivation has not only "entering fast" one posture. Slowing down, feeling through the first time, is equally a true path. **The acceleration domain gives you efficiency; the Quest Domain gives you depth — both are Myriad Ranks' paths.**
