# Entry 579 | Quest Domain | Condensed-Origin Realm Adaptation — Experience Sedimentation of the Inner Circuit

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 15; Benchmark library: Entries 568, 578

## Introduction: after the inner circuit is built, you must "move in"

The acceleration domain designs "fast trial-and-error, slow solidification" for Condensed-Origin; the Quest Sediment Domain focuses on another matter: after the inner circuit is built, the cultivator must in slow time truly **"move into" it**. A built inner circuit, if only run through and tested in the acceleration domain, is still a separate skin from the cultivator's personality; only by running it, feeling it, adjusting it day after day at real flow does it truly become part of the cultivator's body.

Six top-level axioms continue here: anti-predation axiom requires experience sedimentation not be "devouring others' circuit experience," but one's own real experience; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires sedimented experience open to Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires inner-circuit daily insights returned to Alaya; causal self-discipline axiom requires the sedimentation period remain in contact with real causality; law-first axiom takes "Condensed-Origin Quest Gear" as a law clause, never retconned. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why the Inner Circuit Needs to "Move In"

The inner circuit is not a machine that once built is done, but a **living symbiotic system**. It mutually affects the cultivator's emotions, diet, sleep, relationships. A circuit run through in the acceleration domain may backfire in real life due to one argument, one sleepless night. The Quest-Domain Condensed-Origin Gear's task is to let the cultivator live long-term at real flow, observe the inner circuit's performance in various life situations:

- When emotionally agitated, is the circuit stable?
- When fatigued, does the circuit leak?
- When deeply interacting with others, does the circuit resonate?

These questions cannot be measured in the acceleration domain, because there is no real life there.

## II. Quest-Domain Condensed-Origin Gear Parameters

| Dimension | Parameter | Explanation |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Recommended Flow | 0.5–1× | Close to reality |
| Trial Period | 3–10 real years | Enough to cover the full life cycle |
| Core Task | Daily operation, situation recording, micro circuit adjustment | No breakthrough pursuit |
| Experience Product | Inner-Circuit Life Journal | Records circuit performance in various situations |
| Exit Standard | Journal passes Alaya review | Not "circuit stronger," but "circuit more mature" |

This parameter's essence is "living life." The cultivator in Quest-Domain Condensed-Origin Gear is not "cultivating," but "living" — he must work, socialize, face joys and sorrows, while observing how his inner circuit operates through all this. His life journal is his cultivation result.

## III. The Life Journal's Myriad-Rank Value

The inner-circuit life journal is a treasure the acceleration domain can never produce. A cultivator spending ten years recording "what happened to my circuit when heartbroken," "when helping others," "when erring" — these records' value to Myriad Ranks far exceeds a technique blueprint:

- It tells later-comers the inner circuit is not cold topology, but flesh-bound to life;
- It helps Myriad Ranks understand the real relation between "energy and emotion";
- It provides raw material for psychological healing, cultivation-ethics research.

The Alaya Network compiles these journals into "Myriad-Rank Cultivation Life Studies," required reading for all cultivators. This is precisely collective-ascension axiom's expression in everyday life: **your daily life can also become Myriad Ranks' textbook.**

## IV. Opposition to "Closed-Door Dead Practice"

In the predatory-faction system, Condensed-Origin's typical path is closed-door: lock oneself in a cave, ten years not seeing people, only practicing the circuit. This approach's problems:

1. On exiting closed-door, collapses at the first real-life encounter, because the circuit has not withstood life's test;
2. The closed-door practitioner disconnects from society, cultivation becomes escaping reality;
3. It trains "shut-in strong ones," not "life strong ones."

Heng Yuan Zhou in Quest-Domain Condensed-Origin Gear makes "life" the core requirement: pure closed-door forbidden, must run the circuit in real life. This rule makes "closed-door dead practice"彻底 lose on the experience ledger.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: let the circuit be matured in life, avoiding later collapsing in actual combat due to life fluctuations
- **Anti-predation**: experience all from one's own life, not devouring others' experiences
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style Condensed-Origin relies on closed-door ascetic practice, disconnected from life; Heng Yuan's Condensed-Origin requires maturing the circuit in life
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five lineages each have "life" forms; mechanical race's "social life," consciousness race's "conceptual life" also included
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: life-journal library, recording methods, review standards all public; this is the everyday segment of the open chain
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## VI. Ethical Review of Inner-Circuit Life Journals

Portions of life journals involving others must be anonymized: no recording neighbors' real names, no exposing others' privacy. The Alaya Network automatically reviews journals; unanonymized content is sent back. This protection lets cultivators dare run the circuit in real life, without worrying their cultivation records become tools of privacy violation. Causal self-discipline axiom here protects ordinary people around the cultivator.

## VII. Quarterly Sharing Sessions for Life Journals

Cultivators in Quest-Domain Condensed-Origin Gear attend a quarterly life-journal sharing session: everyone gathers, reads aloud the most felt journal passage of the quarter, comments on each other. Sharing is not assessment, but mutual inspiration — one person's inner-circuit problem may be a pit another once stepped in. Sharing sessions are anonymously hosted by the Alaya Network, protecting privacy while promoting real exchange.

## Conclusion

Condensed-Origin's Quest Gear tells cultivators: the inner circuit is not finished once built; you must live it into maturity in life. **A circuit stable through heartbreak, helping others, erring, is the circuit truly yours.**
