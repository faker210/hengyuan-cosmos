# Entry 582 | Quest Domain | Dao-Origin Realm Adaptation | Quest for Dao-Principle Condensation

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 21; Benchmark library: Entries 571, 581

## Introduction: before legislation, you must first "find yourself"

The acceleration domain designs "100× rapid drafts, real-life testing, Myriad-Rank publication" for Dao-Origin; the Quest Sediment Domain focuses on something more fundamental: before legislation, the cultivator in slow time must first **find himself**. Dao-Principles are not deduced from law clauses, but grow out of "who I am, what I care about, what I am willing to take responsibility for." These questions cannot be solved by acceleration, must be answered by long, winding, erring real life.

Six top-level axioms continue here: anti-predation axiom requires Dao-Principles grow out of one's own life, not copied; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires Dao-Principles withstand Myriad-Rank review; collective ascension axiom requires Dao-Principles ultimately serve Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom requires once a Dao-Principle is wrongly established there be a correction mechanism; law-first axiom takes "Dao-Origin Quest Gear" as a law clause, never retconned. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Legislation Must First "Find Yourself"

A Dao-Principle is the cultivator's legislation over himself. A person who has not found himself, the Dao-Principle he establishes is either copied, or a slogan. The Quest-Domain Dao-Origin Gear's task is to let the cultivator in slow time complete a full "self-archaeology":

- What do I truly care about?
- What am I willing to pay the price for?
- In what situations do I waver?
- What am I willing to take responsibility for to Myriad Ranks?

These questions have no standard answer, only to be tried over a lifetime.

## II. Quest-Domain Dao-Origin Gear Parameters

| Dimension | Parameter | Explanation |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Recommended Flow | 0.3–0.7× | Close to reality but slightly slower |
| Trial Method | Real-life trials | Not closed-door, but living, doing, erring, reflecting |
| Trial Period | 20–100 real years | Finding yourself has no timetable |
| Experience Product | Personal Dao-Principle embryo + life-case collection | Real cases supporting the Dao-Principle |
| Exit Standard | Dao-Principle embryo published to Myriad Ranks with no major objection | Ensures the Dao-Principle has real-life roots |

This parameter's essence is "legislating with life." The cultivator in Quest-Domain Dao-Origin Gear is not writing Dao-Principles in a study, but in real life doing, erring, bearing, then distilling these experiences into a Dao-Principle embryo. His Dao-Principle is not paper writing, but judgment bought with a lifetime's price.

## III. The Life-Case Collection's Myriad-Rank Value

The "life-case collection" attached to the Dao-Principle embryo is Myriad Ranks' most precious textbook. It records:

- How the cultivator chose in a specific situation;
- What consequences his choice brought;
- What he learned from the consequences;
- How this corrected his Dao-Principle.

These cases teach Myriad Ranks "how to establish Dao-Principles" better than any technique blueprint. The Alaya Network compiles them into "Myriad-Rank Dao-Principle Studies," required reading for all冲击 Dao-Origin. This is precisely collective-ascension axiom's expression in the ethical dimension: **one person's judgment bought with a lifetime becomes Myriad Ranks' reference when establishing Dao.**

## IV. Opposition to "Copying Dao-Principles"

In the predatory-faction system, Dao-Origin's typical path is "copying Dao-Principles": select a strong one's Dao-Principle and copy it, or in closed-door cultivation fabricate a pretty slogan out of thin air. This approach's problems:

1. Copied Dao-Principles do not fit own personality, collapse when things happen;
2. Fabricated Dao-Principles have no real-life roots, malfunction at the first concrete choice;
3. It trains "Dao-Principle imitators," not "true legislators."

Heng Yuan Zhou in Quest-Domain Dao-Origin Gear makes the "life-case collection" hard material for Dao-Principle review: a Dao-Principle embryo without enough real-case support, at publication必 be rejected. This rule makes "copying Dao-Principles" unworkable on the review ledger.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: supporting Dao-Principles with a lifetime of trials, avoiding establishing a paper blank
- **Anti-predation**: Dao-Principles grow out of one's own life, not copied, not stolen
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style Dao-Principles rely on protagonist's talent self-enlightenment, slogan-style; Heng Yuan's Dao-Principles supported by life cases, published to Myriad Ranks
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five lineages each have a "find yourself" path; plant spirits' "growth-style self-finding," planet consciousness's "balance-style self-finding" also included
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: case-library, Dao-Principle review methods, publication process all public; this is the ethical segment of the open chain
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## VI. Anonymization of Dao-Principle Life-Case Collections

Others involved in the case collection are all anonymized; real names are kept only in the Alaya Network's encrypted layer. When the cultivator publishes, he only shows case structures, not exposing specific parties. This protection lets cultivators dare write real experiences into Dao-Principles, without worrying about hurting those around them. Causal self-discipline axiom here simultaneously protects the legislator and the people around the legislated.

## VII. Life Apprenticeship for Dao-Principle Drafts

Dao-Origin Quest Gear besides writing case collections, also requires the cultivator to attend life apprenticeship: work for a time at Myriad Ranks' different posts — as doctor, farmer, teacher, engineer. Apprenticeship is not to learn skills, but to root the Dao-Principle in real human world. A cultivator who has not seen how ordinary people live a day, the Dao-Principle he establishes is doomed to hang in air. Life apprenticeship brings the Dao-Principle from the study back to earth.

## VIII. Mentorship for Dao-Principle Apprenticeship

Life apprenticeship is not wandering alone, but paired with a Dao-Principle mentor: the mentor himself has walked through Dao-Origin, seen countless Dao-Principles' successes and failures. Perplexities encountered during apprenticeship, the cultivator may anytime ask the mentor; the mentor also at key nodes reminds him which experiences are worth writing into the case collection. Mentorship keeps life apprenticeship from going astray, and lets Dao-Principle transmission continue in slowness.

## Conclusion

Dao-Origin's Quest Gear tells cultivators: before establishing a Dao-Principle, first live yourself clearly. **A Dao-Principle supported by a lifetime of cases is a Dao-Principle truly able to withstand Myriad-Rank review and the test of time.**
