# Entry 584 | Quest Domain | Boundary-Breaking Realm Adaptation | Trials of Plane Breakthrough

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 83; Benchmark library: Entries 573, 583

## Introduction: after crossing planes, you must "settle in"

The acceleration domain designs "fast observation, slow crossing, strict isolation" for Boundary-Breaking; the Quest Sediment Domain focuses on another matter: after truly stepping out of one's plane, the cultivator in slow time must **settle into another world**. Cross-plane is not a trip, but a **way-of-existence conversion** — you must in a strange universe, using its laws, its civilization, its time sense, relearn how to live. The more this is rushed, the less it is learned.

Six top-level axioms continue here: anti-predation axiom requires cross-plane trials be "being a guest," not "conquering"; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires equal interaction with local Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires trial gains returned to both planes; causal self-discipline axiom requires strict obedience to observe-first-limited-intervention, only-knowledge-returned; law-first axiom takes "Boundary-Breaking Quest Gear" as a law clause, never retconned. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Cross-Plane Must "Settle In"

After the Boundary-Breaking cultivator crosses the plane barrier, the greatest risk is not combat, but **culture shock**. Each plane has its own:

- Law-detail differences;
- Civilizational-form differences;
- Time-sense differences;
- Myriad-Rank relation differences.

No matter how detailed the atlas in the acceleration domain, it cannot replace "eating a year of meals in that world, making a few friends, making a few mistakes." The Quest-Domain Boundary-Breaking Gear's task is to let the cultivator settle into the target plane at real flow, as a long-term "learner."

## II. Quest-Domain Boundary-Breaking Gear Parameters

| Dimension | Parameter | Explanation |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Recommended Flow | 0.8–1× | Must live by local time sense |
| Trial Identity | Ordinary learner / laborer | Does not expose strong-one identity |
| Trial Period | 10–100 real years | Enough to learn a world |
| Core Discipline | Observe only, socialize only, do not intervene | Strictly obey causal self-discipline |
| Experience Product | Cross-Plane Life Journal | Records true understanding of another world |

This parameter's essence is "being a guest." The cultivator in Quest-Domain Boundary-Breaking Gear is not going to "inspect," but to "live" — he must find a local job, make a few friends, experience local joys and sorrows. His strong-one identity is deliberately hidden, because only when he lives as an "ordinary person" can he truly understand this world.

## III. The Cross-Plane Life Journal's Myriad-Rank Value

A cultivator spending decades living in another plane writing a "Cross-Plane Life Journal" is Myriad Ranks' most precious cross-plane-exchange material. It records not plane atlases (those are acceleration-domain products), but:

- How ordinary people of that plane live;
- How Myriad Ranks there interact;
- As an outsider, which cultural mistakes he made;
- What he learned from that world that can be brought back to his own.

The Alaya Network compiles these life journals into the "Myriad-Rank Cross-Plane Album," required reading for all cross-plane cultivators. This is precisely collective-ascension axiom's expression in the cross-plane dimension: **one person's life in another world becomes Myriad Ranks' window to understanding the multiverse.**

## IV. Opposition to "Plane Conqueror"

In the predatory-faction system, a Boundary-Breaking strong one's typical behavior after crossing is "conquest": on landing, show strength, establish authority, plunder resources. This approach's problems:

1. He forever only sees that world's "conquered side," not real life;
2. He turns local Myriad Ranks into enemies, not teachers;
3. It trains "plane officers," not "plane friends."

Heng Yuan Zhou in Quest-Domain Boundary-Breaking Gear makes "hide identity, ordinary life" a hard discipline: violators immediately recalled, cross-plane qualification canceled. This rule makes "plane conquest" fundamentally unworkable in process.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: let the cultivator truly understand another world, avoiding wrong cross-plane decisions from misunderstanding
- **Anti-predation**: cross-plane is being a guest, no plunder, no intervention
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style boundary-breaking relies on strength crushing other worlds; Heng Yuan's boundary-breaking relies on settling in to learn
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: every plane's Myriad Ranks are teachers; plant-spirit planes, consciousness-race planes also have life journals
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: life-journal library, guest discipline, recall mechanism all public; this is the cross-plane segment of the open chain
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## VI. Causal Cleaning of Cross-Plane Life Journals

If a life journal accidentally records sensitive target-plane information (such as low-tier civilization's technical weaknesses), before publication it must undergo causal cleaning: delete details that could be abused, retain only life experience. The Alaya Network's cleaning algorithm ensures life journals become a bridge of civilizational exchange, not a tool of technical theft. This step落实s the "only-knowledge-returned, causality-not-perturbed" axiom to the textual level.

## VII. Residence Certificates for Cross-Plane Life Journals

A Boundary-Breaking Quest Gear cultivator must continuously live in the target plane for the prescribed number of years, and obtain a residence certificate issued by local Myriad Ranks, before the trial is considered complete. The residence certificate is not formality, but local Myriad Ranks' recognition of whether he truly integrated. Without a residence certificate, the life journal he brings back is not collected by the Alaya Network. This hard metric prevents the cultivator's whirlwind-style inspection, forcing him to truly settle in.

## VIII. Feeding-Back After Cross-Plane Return

A cultivator completing a long-term life journal, on returning to his home plane, has the obligation to make a public report to his own ethnic Myriad Ranks: what he saw in the target plane, what he learned, what can be brought back. The report is not showing off, but letting one person's cross-plane experience become the whole home plane's wealth. This feeding-back obligation turns the boundary-breaking trial from personal adventure into civilizational exchange.

## Conclusion

Boundary-Breaking's Quest Gear tells cultivators: stepping out is not the end, but the beginning. **A true Boundary-Breaking strong one is not who can fight to another plane, but who can live like an ordinary person in another plane for decades.**
