# Entry 585 | Quest Domain | World-Creation Realm Adaptation | Creative Experience of Law Weaving

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 80; Benchmark library: Entries 574, 584

## Introduction: after world-creation, you must "be a resident"

The acceleration domain designs "1000–10000× extreme design, million-year simulation, real solidification" for World-Creation; the Quest Sediment Domain focuses on another matter: after the world is created, the cultivator in slow time must **be a resident of his own world**. A designed world, perfect on the blueprint, does not mean it works well in life. Only when the creator himself goes in to live for a time, living as an ordinary person, can he discover problems not seen on the design blueprint — this is precisely the most precious part of creative experience.

Six top-level axioms continue here: anti-predation axiom requires creative experience be "being a resident," not "being god"; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the creator live together with Myriad-Rank residents; collective ascension axiom requires creative experience feed back to Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom requires the world completely isolated; law-first axiom takes "World-Creation Quest Gear" as a law clause, never retconned. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why the Creator Must "Be a Resident"

The creator's most common mistake is "god's-eye view": he stands outside the world designing everything, yet does not know what it feels like to live inside. The Quest-Domain World-Creation Gear's task is to let the creator give up the god's-eye view, enter his own world, live as an ordinary resident for decades to centuries:

- Does the "emotion law" he designed truly make people happy in life?
- Does the "resource distribution" he designed truly fair in everyday life?
- Does the "growth path" he designed truly work for ordinary people?

Only after being a resident does he know whether his world is actually good.

## II. Quest-Domain World-Creation Gear Parameters

| Dimension | Parameter | Explanation |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Recommended Flow | 0.8–1× | Live by in-world time |
| Resident Identity | Ordinary labor resident | Does not expose creator identity |
| Trial Period | 100–1000 in-world years | Enough to experience the full life cycle |
| Core Task | Be a resident, discover design defects, record improvement points | Do not modify laws |
| Experience Product | Creator-Resident Journal | Records the world's gains and losses from a resident's view |

This parameter's essence is "forget you are the creator." The cultivator in Quest-Domain World-Creation Gear must deliberately forget he designed this world, feel joys and sorrows like a true resident. The complaints, surprises, confusion he writes in the journal are the most precious material for improving this world.

## III. The Resident Journal's Myriad-Rank Value

Creator-resident journals are "creation studies"' scarcest material. The acceleration domain produces "creation design studies"; the Quest Domain produces "creation residency studies." The Alaya Network pairs the two:

- One created world, paired with the creator's own centuries of resident journal;
- Future creators before designing, must read these journals, avoid repeating old mistakes;
- Design defects found in journals become improvement points for next-generation creation templates.

This is precisely collective-ascension axiom's expression in the creation dimension: **the hardships the creator himself ate as a resident become the textbook for all future creators.**

## IV. Opposition to "Creation as Dictatorship"

In the predatory-faction system, a World-Creation strong one's typical behavior is "creation as dictatorship": after making the world, hide on the god-throne looking down on all beings, never going in to live. This approach's problems:

1. The world's design defects are never discovered, residents suffer generation after generation;
2. The creator disconnects from his own created world, the world becomes his private toy;
3. It trains "creation tyrants," not "creation architects."

Heng Yuan Zhou in Quest-Domain World-Creation Gear makes "being a resident" a hard precondition of creation acceptance: a world the creator himself is unwilling to live in, no permission to open to Myriad Ranks. This rule makes "creation as dictatorship" unworkable on the acceptance ledger.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: let the creator himself discover design defects, avoiding Myriad-Rank residents trial-and-error for him
- **Anti-predation**: creation proceeds in incremental space; being a resident does not plunder
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style creation relies on the protagonist opening private heaven and earth then looking down on all beings; Heng Yuan's creation requires the creator himself be a resident
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five lineages' creators each have a "being a resident" way; consciousness race's "conceptual resident," mechanical race's "component resident" also included
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: resident-journal library, creation-acceptance standards, improvement process all public; this is the creation segment of the open chain
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## VI. World-Improvement Proposals From Creator-Resident Journals

Design defects found in resident journals, the creator must periodically organize into world-improvement proposals, revise laws after the Myriad-Rank Council's review. Revision does not change the world's isolation, but makes the world better and better to live in. This mechanism turns the created world from a one-shot deal into a continuously operated shared dwelling — the creator does not leave after handing in the assignment, but lives the world better together with Myriad Ranks.

## VII. The Created World's Trial-Operation Period

After a created world solidifies, a hundred-year trial-operation period is set: during the trial period Myriad Ranks may move in for free; the creator revises laws anytime based on feedback. After the trial period ends, the world officially opens for move-in. The trial period is the creator's main time being a resident, and the key process of the world turning from blueprint to real home. A created world without a trial-operation period is not allowed to officially open.

## VIII. Long-Term Tracking of Created Worlds

After the trial period ends, the creator still must do long-term tracking of his world: every hundred years submit a world-operation report, recording in-world Myriad Ranks' happiness, law stability, ecological health. Long-term tracking keeps the creator and world never decoupled, and lets world problems be found early. Tracking reports are public, under Myriad-Rank supervision.

## Conclusion

World-Creation's Quest Gear tells cultivators: having made the world, you must dare go in and live. **A world the creator himself is willing to live in for centuries deserves to be called Myriad Ranks' shared dwelling.**
