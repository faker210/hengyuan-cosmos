# Entry 586 | Quest Domain | Origin Realm Adaptation | Sedimentation of Cosmic-Level Beings

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 17; Benchmark library: Entries 575, 585

## Introduction: after becoming a cosmic-level being, you must "sink down"

The acceleration domain designs "limited acceleration, heavy supervision, feeding back to Myriad Ranks" for Origin Realm; the Quest Sediment Domain focuses on another matter: when the cultivator has already become a cosmic-level being, he in slow time must **sink himself down**. The greatest risk cosmic-level power brings is not abuse, but **floating** — after consciousness covers the whole plane, the cultivator easily disconnects from concrete, everyday, mortal life, becoming a "lofty abstract existence." The Quest-Domain Origin Gear's task is to pull him back to the ground.

Six top-level axioms continue here: anti-predation axiom requires sedimentation be "living together with Myriad Ranks," not "disconnecting from Myriad Ranks in closed-door cultivation"; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires cosmic-level beings interact equally with ordinary beings; collective ascension axiom requires insights during sedimentation feed back to Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom requires cosmic-level beings' daily operations under multi-layer supervision; law-first axiom takes "Origin Quest Gear" as a law clause, never retconned. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Cosmic-Level Beings Must "Sink Down"

The Origin cultivator's consciousness may cover the whole plane; this ability without constraint leads to several problems:

1. **Mortal experience lost**: he can no longer understand what "an ordinary person's day" feels like;
2. **Decisions abstracted**: his decisions increasingly disconnect from real life, becoming pure "plane-level calculation";
3. **Emotional break from Myriad Ranks**: he becomes a god off the chessboard, no longer a piece on it.

The Quest-Domain Origin Gear's task is to force him to redo being a mortal in slow time.

## II. Quest-Domain Origin Gear Parameters

| Dimension | Parameter | Explanation |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Recommended Flow | 0.8–1× | Live by mortal time sense |
| Identity Setting | Ordinary laborer | Does not expose Origin-Realm identity |
| Trial Period | 100–1000 real years | Enough to re-understand mortal life |
| Core Task | Work, socialize, experience joys and sorrows | Do not use cosmic-level power |
| Experience Product | Origin-Mortal Journal | Records cosmic-level being re-doing mortalhood |

This parameter's essence is "being human again." The cultivator in Quest-Domain Origin Gear must completely put away cosmic-level power, find an ordinary job, live an ordinary life. He may at year 200 finally re-experience the reality of "hungry, need to eat" — this re-experiencing is his most precious spiritual resource as Plane-Transcendent.

## III. The Origin-Mortal Journal's Myriad-Rank Value

An Origin-Mortal Journal written by a cosmic-level being re-doing mortalhood is Myriad Ranks' top-tier textbook on understanding "power and everyday life." It records:

- When you can cover a plane with one thought, why still seriously eat a bowl of rice;
- When you can arbitrarily modify law, why still obey ordinary people's rules;
- When you need no resources, why still participate in labor.

The answers to these questions are the core of the "Mind-Nature" dimension in Plane-Transcendent four-dimensional assessment (Power 40 / Mind 25 / Contribution 20 / Responsibility 15). The Alaya Network compiles Origin-Mortal Journals into "Myriad-Rank Transcendent Studies," required reading for all冲击 Origin Realm. This is precisely collective-ascension axiom's ultimate expression in the spiritual dimension: **the highest power must ultimately return to the most everyday life to be tested.**

## IV. Opposition to "Lofty Above"

In the predatory-faction system, an Origin-level strong one's typical state is "lofty above": living in celestial temples, not landing for millennia, knowing nothing of mortal affairs. This approach's problems:

1. His decisions disconnect from mortals' real life, ultimately brewing disaster;
2. He loses respect for individual life, treating Myriad Ranks as numbers;
3. It trains "gods in heaven," not "guardians on earth."

Heng Yuan Zhou in Quest-Domain Origin Gear makes "being human again" a hard requirement for maintaining Origin Realm: Origin-Realm cultivators long not participating in mortal life will be required by the Anchor Supervisory Committee to undergo mandatory trials. This rule makes "lofty above" unworkable institutionally.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: keep cosmic-level power connected with mortal experience, avoiding power becoming abstract idling disconnected from life
- **Anti-predation**: being human again does not plunder, but learns service
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style Origin strong ones live in temples looking down on all beings; Heng Yuan's Origin-Realm cultivators periodically descend as ordinary people
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five lineages' Origin beings each have a "being human again" way; plant-spirit Origin's "being grass again," planet-consciousness Origin's "being dust again" also included
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Origin-Mortal Journal library, trial standards, supervision rules all public; this is the apex segment of the open chain
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## VI. Anonymization and Publication of Origin-Mortal Journals

Ordinary residents involved in Origin-Mortal Journals are all anonymized, only event structures retained. Anonymized journals are public, letting Myriad Ranks see how cosmic-level beings take a meal, a labor seriously. This publicity itself is education: it tells every cultivator that the highest power does not disdain the most everyday life.

## VII. Rotation System for Origin-Realm Descent

An Origin-Realm cultivator's descent is not a one-time act, but a rotation system: descend once per millennium, each descent a century, return to origin then descend again. The rotation system ensures cosmic-level beings always stay in contact with mortal life, not forgetting the human world due to long elevation. Rotation-period mortal journals are public; Myriad Ranks may see how their guardian seriously lives an ordinary life.

## VIII. Cosmic-Level Application of Mortal Experience

Mortal experience accumulated during descent, after returning to origin, is systematically applied to cosmic-level decisions: when the Alaya Network discusses a plane-level policy, the Origin cultivator's mortal journal becomes important reference. He truly understands mortals, so his decisions do not disconnect from mortals. This is the bidirectional fulfillment of descent and cosmic-level duty.

## Conclusion

Origin Realm's Quest Gear tells cultivators: the greater the power, the more you must sink down. **A cosmic-level being willing to be a mortal again truly deserves to guard Myriad Ranks.**
