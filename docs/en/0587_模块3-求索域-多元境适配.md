# Entry 587 | Quest Domain | Multiverse Realm Adaptation | Epic of Multi-Universe Exploration

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 80; Benchmark library: Entries 576, 586

## Introduction: after traversing multiple universes, you must "tell stories"

The acceleration domain designs "distributed acceleration, multi-universe coordination, Myriad-Rank Council" for Multiverse; the Quest Sediment Domain focuses on another matter: when the cultivator has already walked many universes, he in slow time must **tell these journeys into an epic**. Multi-universe exploration's true value lies not in how many universes walked, but in what stories, what understandings, what discoveries of "Myriad-Rank commonality" he brought back from these journeys. These things must in slow time be sedimented into epic, before they can become Myriad Ranks' spiritual wealth.

Six top-level axioms continue here: anti-predation axiom requires the epic be "stories brought back," not "spoils seized"; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the epic open to Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires the epic become a bridge of Myriad Ranks' cross-universe understanding; causal self-discipline axiom requires narrative not distort, not exaggerate; law-first axiom takes "Multiverse Quest Gear" as a law clause, never retconned. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Multi-Universe Exploration Must "Be Told into Epic"

The more universes a Multiverse cultivator walks, the more easily he falls into "data-ification": universes become numbers, atlases, datasets. But universes are not data; universes are alive, storied. The Quest-Domain Multiverse Gear's task is to let him in slow time reduce data back into stories:

- How do that universe's Myriad Ranks live?
- Who did he meet in that universe?
- What did that universe teach him that no other universe could?
- Among all these universes, is there a common thread?

The answers to these questions are the multi-universe epic.

## II. Quest-Domain Multiverse Gear Parameters

| Dimension | Parameter | Explanation |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Recommended Flow | 0.5–0.8× | Slow down to recall, organize, narrate |
| Trial Method | Journey review, story organization, epic composition | No new journeys, only sediment existing |
| Trial Period | 1000–10000 real years | Epic composition has no timetable |
| Core Task | Reduce data back into stories, distill stories into epic | No exaggeration, no distortion |
| Experience Product | Multi-Universe Epic | A long scroll about Myriad-Rank commonality across universes |

This parameter's essence is "sedimentation." The cultivator in Quest-Domain Multiverse Gear no longer goes to new universes, but repeatedly chews, tells, revises journeys already walked. He may spend millennia completing one epic; that epic's thickness far exceeds the number of universes he walked.

## III. The Multi-Universe Epic's Myriad-Rank Value

A true multi-universe epic is Myriad Ranks' spiritual map for understanding "the multiverse." The acceleration domain produces "universe atlases" (data); the Quest Domain produces "universe epics" (stories). The Alaya Network pairs the two:

- One universe atlas, paired with the cultivator's centuries-written epic;
- Later-comers reading, see not only this universe's data, but also the imprint it left on some cultivator's life;
- The "Myriad-Rank commonality" distilled in the epic becomes the basis of cross-universe civilizational dialogue.

This is precisely collective-ascension axiom's expression in the epic dimension: **one person's walked road becomes Myriad Ranks' spiritual map for understanding the multiverse.**

## IV. Opposition to "Data Showing-Off"

In the predatory-faction system, a Multiverse strong one's typical behavior is "data showing-off": how many universes walked, how many strong ones fought, how many resources plundered, listed as a string of battle records. This approach's problems:

1. Universes become spoils to him, losing story-ness;
2. No matter how many universes he walks, he truly understands none;
3. It trains "universe hunters," not "universe storytellers."

Heng Yuan Zhou in Quest-Domain Multiverse Gear makes "epic" the core mark of Multiverse cultivation: a true multi-universe epic carries more weight than walking a thousand universes. This rule makes "data showing-off" unworkable on the spiritual ledger.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: sediment walked universes into epic, avoiding perfunctory idling
- **Anti-predation**: epics are stories brought back, not spoils seized
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style Multiverse strong ones show off battle records; Heng Yuan's Multiverse cultivators sediment into epic
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: in the epic each universe's Myriad Ranks are respectfully told; plant-spirit universes, consciousness-race universes also enter the scroll
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: epic library, narrative methods, revision rules all public; this is the epic segment of the open chain
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## V. Myriad-Rank Review of Multi-Universe Epics

After an epic's first draft is complete, it must be reviewed by the Multi-Universe Myriad-Rank Council: each universe written into the epic sends a representative to review, confirming the epic does not distort their civilization, does not write them as background panels. Only after review passes is the epic finalized. This mechanism ensures the epic is a respectful record, not an arrogant narration from the strong-one's perspective.

## VI. Continual-Revision Mechanism for Multi-Universe Epics

A multi-universe epic is not finished once written, but continually revised as the cultivator continues walking new universes. Each new universe walked adds a scroll to the epic. Continual revision must pass Myriad-Rank review (see Entry 587 V), ensuring the newly added scroll respects the new universe's civilization. An epic continually revised for millennia is itself living proof of Myriad Ranks' co-existence across universes.

## VII. Cross-Universe Readings of Epics

A completed multi-universe epic is read in various universes: not forced propagation, but voluntary recitation gatherings. Myriad Ranks from different universes gather, listen to an epic about their universe read aloud by another being. This cross-unverse recitation is one of Myriad-Rank Republicanism's most moving moments — the story you wrote, listened to in tears by strangers in another universe.

## Conclusion

Multiverse's Quest Gear tells cultivators: walking far is not as good as telling deeply. **A true multi-universe epic lets Myriad Ranks see their own shadows within it — this is cross-universe exploration's ultimate meaning.**
