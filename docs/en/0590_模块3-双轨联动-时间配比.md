# Entry 590 | Dual-Track Linkage | Acceleration/Quest Time Ratios Across Realms

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark library: Entries 501, 567–589

## Introduction: each realm's "fast and slow" ratio is different

Entry 589 gave the universal dual-track linkage procedure, but when the universal procedure lands at each realm, acceleration and quest time ratios are not the same. Entry-level realms need more consolidation, saint-realm cultivation needs more supervision, apex realms need more perpetuity. This entry gives the eleven-realm standard ratio table, and explains why it is arranged this way.

Six top-level axioms are the ratio table's highest basis: anti-predation axiom requires ratios not steal time; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires ratios adjustable per individual difference; collective ascension axiom requires ratios serve Myriad Ranks' overall rhythm; causal self-discipline axiom requires ratios prevent causal overheating; law-first axiom writes the ratio table as a law clause. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Basic Principles of Ratios

Ratios are not shot from the hip, but based on three principles:

1. **Task nature determines ratio**: information-processing tasks (reading law, designing) have high acceleration share; experience tasks (living, establishing Dao-principles) have high quest share;
2. **Higher realm leans more toward supervision**: mortal five realms lean acceleration, saint four realms start adding supervision, hyper-multiverse two realms lean perpetuity;
3. **Individual difference adjustable**: standard ratio is default, the Alaya Network may float up/down per individual state.

This table reveals a counter-intuitive pattern: **the higher the realm, the higher the quest share**. Because lower-realm problems are mainly "technology not known," can accelerate; higher-realm problems are mainly "understanding not deep, responsibility too heavy," must slow.

## III. Dynamic Adjustment of Ratios

Standard ratio is not fixed. The Alaya Network dynamically adjusts per cultivator's state:

- If he has low experience retention at some phase, automatically raise quest share;
- If he has high causal risk at some phase, automatically raise reality cooling share;
- If he has emergency rescue at some phase, temporarily raise acceleration share.

This dynamic adjustment ensures ratios serve the cultivator's current real needs, rather than rigidly executing the table.

## IV. Opposition to "Same Speed for All"

In the predatory-faction system, a cultivator's time ratio is entirely determined by birth: talents accelerate, ordinary beings slow, strong ones monopolize all acceleration. This approach's problems:

1. It turns time ratio into class privilege;
2. It does not consider task nature, blindly making strong ones fast and ordinary beings slow;
3. It trains "time aristocrats," not "time laborers."

Heng Yuan Zhou's ratio table is public to Myriad Ranks, allocating time by task nature not birth. **Same realm, Myriad Ranks enjoy same standard ratio — this is true republicanism of time resources.**

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: allocate fast/slow by task nature, avoiding empty acceleration on experience tasks
- **Anti-predation**: ratios public, no monopolizing acceleration
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style ratios by birth; Heng Yuan ratios by task, Myriad Ranks equal
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five lineages fine-tune standard ratios; plant spirits, planet consciousness each have adaptation
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: ratio table, adjustment algorithms, dynamic rules all public; this is the quantification segment of the open chain
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## VI. Myriad-Rank Consultation Mechanism for Ratios

The standard ratio table is reviewed by the Myriad-Rank Council every decade: each clan proposes adjustment suggestions based on its own cultivation practice, the Council synthesizes and revises the next-round ratio. The review process is public, any being may submit opinions. This periodic review ensures the ratio table always reflects Myriad Ranks' real needs, not some designer's one-shot pronouncement. In review, if some clan proposes its life-form needs a special gear, the Council must seriously listen, because time equality itself is the core of Myriad-Rank Republicanism.

## VII. Individual Trial-and-Error Right for Ratios

Standard ratio is default, but cultivators have individual trial-and-error rights: he may apply to deviate from standard ratio, e.g., at Sense-Origin try higher acceleration, see whether he tolerates. Trial-and-error has strict safety boundaries — Entry 594's five indicators monitor throughout; once a red light lights, immediately pull back to standard ratio. Trial-and-error right makes the ratio table both stable and flexible, respecting individual difference while not abandoning the safety bottom line.

## VIII. Long-Term Consequences of Ratio Deviation

Though trial-and-error rights allow deviating from standard ratio, long-term consequences of deviation are strictly recorded: if some cultivator's long-term deviation leads to indicator deterioration, the network uses his case as a negative example, reminding later-comers. This recording is not punishment, but makes the cost of trial-and-error Myriad Ranks' common experience. Trial-and-error rights and trial-and-error records run in parallel, so freedom does not lose control.

## IX. Ratio Linkage with Realm Breakthrough

Time ratio is not a static table independent of realm breakthrough, but a dynamic parameter linked with each breakthrough: on breakthrough eve, automatically raise quest share, letting the cultivator have enough time to digest; after breakthrough completes, automatically temporarily raise acceleration share, letting him quickly adapt to the new realm. This linkage makes the ratio table always serve the cultivator's current breakthrough state, rather than making breakthrough accommodate ratios. The Alaya Network automatically adjusts gears before/after each breakthrough; the cultivator need not manually apply.

## Conclusion

The dual-track ratio table tells cultivators: fast and slow are not preferences, but task requirements. **Slow more at entry, sink more at high realm — the allocation of time itself is cultivation.**
