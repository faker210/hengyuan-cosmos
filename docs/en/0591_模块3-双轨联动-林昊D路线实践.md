The preceding 35 entries spoke of institutions; this entry speaks of one concrete case: Lin Hao — protagonist of *Douluo: Ringless Heng-Lord*, Path-D (human-race artificial Dao-Principle spirit ring) representative cultivator — how he practices in the dual-track time domain. Six top-level axioms find their most concrete embodiment in Lin Hao: he walks Path D precisely for anti-predation — not swallowing spirit rings, not killing spirit beasts, using artificial Dao-Principle spirit rings to open the human race's own road; he walks Path D precisely for Myriad-Rank Republicanism — letting ring-less human race also cultivate; he walks Path D precisely for collective ascension — his path ultimately becomes Myriad Ranks' reusable template. ## I. Lin Hao's Specialness

Lin Hao differs from ordinary cultivators in three ways:

1. **Ringless start**: he has no natural spirit ring, must walk Path D of artificial Dao-Principle spirit rings;
2. **Path D unfinished**: Path D is a newly opened road, no mature template to copy;
3. **Lin Hao's identity**: he is Heng Yuan Zhou's anchor-type cultivator on the Douluo plane, bearing responsibility for bringing the symbiotic civilization into that plane.

These three specialnesses determine his dual-track practice cannot copy standard ratios, but must make personalized adjustments.

Lin Hao's Path D concretely fulfills anti-predation axiom in the dual-track time domain:
