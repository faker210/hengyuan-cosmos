## Introduction: an acceleration-domain solution to a technical problem

Entry 591 spoke of Lin Hao's overall practice in the dual-track time domain; this entry focuses on its most technical link: **Path D · human-race artificial Dao-Principle spirit ring's deduction process in the Origin Acceleration Domain**. This is the key step turning anti-predation axiom from moral claim into engineering solution — since we do not swallow spirit beasts' rings, where do rings come from? The answer: deduce them in the acceleration domain.

Six top-level axioms in this technical deduction become hard constraints: anti-predation axiom requires artificial rings zero-kill, zero-swallow; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires deduction schemes public to the human race and even Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires deduction results become public goods; causal self-discipline axiom requires deduction not disturb Douluo plane's native causality; law-first axiom requires deduction strictly obey Douluo plane's laws, no retcon. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. What Problem Artificial Dao-Principle Spirit Rings Must Solve

In Douluo's native system, spirit rings come from spirit beasts' post-death residue, essentially "kill one being to take its origin." Path D must replace it, solving three technical problems:

1. **The ring's law structure**: artificial rings must reproduce natural rings' law-bearing capacity;
2. **Human-ring compatibility**: artificial rings must not conflict with the cultivator's body;
3. **The ring's energy source**: cannot rely on killing spirit beasts, must come from incremental development.

These three problems at real flow might take centuries to trial-and-error one by one. The Origin Acceleration Domain compresses them into decades of accelerated time.

## II. Three Phases of Acceleration-Domain Deduction

| Phase | Flow | Core Task | Output |
|-------|------|-----------|--------|
| Law-Structure Reverse-Engineering | 1000× | In acceleration domain analyze natural ring's law structure | Ring structure model |
| Artificial-Scheme Design | 500× | Based on structure model design artificial ring schemes | Multiple candidate schemes |

This three-phase strictly follows Document 11's deduction iteration system: first reverse-engineer the natural object, then design the artificial object, then simulate in isolated space. The acceleration domain here is used on the cutting edge — because "analysis" and "simulation" are both information-processing tasks, naturally suited to high flow.

## III. The Deduction Result's Anti-Predation Attributes

Artificial Dao-Principle spirit ring schemes produced by acceleration-domain deduction, by design, have three anti-predation attributes:

- **Zero-kill**: the ring's energy comes from environmental incremental development, kills no spirit beast;
- **Zero-swallow**: the ring symbiotically merges with the cultivator, does not swallow others' origin;
- **Zero-monopoly**: the scheme is public through the Alaya Network to all human race, anyone may use.

These three attributes are anti-predation axiom's direct engineering compilation. Path D is not "we also make rings," but "we make a kind of ring that fundamentally does not plunder."

## VI. Ethical Boundaries of Artificial Dao-Principle Spirit Rings

Though artificial rings are zero-kill, the Alaya Network still sets ethical boundaries for them: must not manufacture rings exceeding the human race's current tolerance, must not forcibly promote to un-applied civilizations, must not use ring technology to control beings. These boundaries are written into scheme documents, public together with the technology. The more powerful the technology, the clearer the ethical boundaries must be — this is causal self-discipline axiom's expression in technical engineering. Any attempt to use artificial rings to control beings will be immediately stopped by the Anchor Supervisory Committee.

## VII. Phased Rollout of Artificial Rings

Path-D artificial Dao-Principle spirit rings are not one-time fully promoted, but phased: first batch only tested among human volunteers, verifying safety; second batch expanded to Myriad-Rank clans applying to join; third batch opened to all beings willing to walk Path D. Phased rollout keeps technical risk controllable, each batch's feedback used to improve the next. This engineering rhythm itself embodies dual-track time-domain thought — fast R&D, slow promotion, stable landing.

## VIII. User Feedback Loop for Artificial Rings

Each rolled-out batch of artificial rings comes with a user feedback channel: users encountering any problem during use may directly feedback to the acceleration-domain R&D team. Feedback is classified and processed, urgent issues immediately hot-fixed, non-urgent issues enter next-version planning. This user feedback loop makes artificial rings better and better with use, and makes users feel they are co-builders of the technology.

Path D's acceleration-domain deduction proves: anti-predation is not asceticism, but a more efficient engineering choice. **Artificial Dao-Principle spirit rings deduced over decades in the acceleration domain are faster, cleaner, more Myriad-Rank-owned than killing spirit beasts over centuries in reality.** This is the dual-track time domain's best answer to anti-predation axiom.
