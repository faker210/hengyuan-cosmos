## Introduction: how to know "after acceleration, not empty"

Entry 593 gave the quantification overview; this entry focuses on the acceleration domain's experience indicator system. The Origin Acceleration Domain is the higher-risk track in the dual system — a person staying long in 100× flow most easily suffers "entered fast, came out empty": consciousness came out, personality hollowed out. Therefore the acceleration domain's indicator system design is especially critical.

Six top-level axioms are the indicators' highest basis: anti-predation axiom requires indicators not reward "trading plunder for acceleration"; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires indicators public to Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires indicator data feed back to Alaya; causal self-discipline axiom requires collection not invade privacy; law-first axiom writes indicator thresholds as law clauses. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. How Indicators Are Collected

All five indicators are collected through structural signals, not reading specific thoughts:

- **Time-sense anchoring index**: monitor the cultivator's response latency to external time signals;
- **Memory continuity**: monitor memory-extraction completeness after acceleration;
- **Emotional fullness**: monitor response strength to standard emotional stimuli;
- **Causal-boundary sense**: monitor whether acceleration-domain rules are misapplied to reality;
- **Personality consistency**: monitor self-description stability before/after acceleration.

The entire collection process is automatically completed by the Alaya Network; the cultivator himself may also see his own indicator curves.

## III. Linkage of Indicators with Gears

The five indicators are not only monitoring, but also reverse-regulate acceleration gears:

- If time-sense anchoring index is high, tolerance is good, may suggest raising flow;
- If emotional fullness is low, near overdraft, automatically slow down;
- If causal-boundary sense drops, immediately exit, prevent bringing acceleration-domain behavior patterns back to reality.

This closed-loop regulation keeps acceleration gears always matching the cultivator's real state, rather than rigidly executing Entry 590's standard ratio.

## IV. Opposition to "Acceleration Addiction"

In the predatory-faction system, the acceleration domain (if it exists) becomes a hotbed of "acceleration addiction": once a cultivator tastes 100× flow, he refuses to return to slow, personality ultimately hollowed out. Heng Yuan Zhou's indicator system mechanistically blocks addiction:

- Any indicator falling below threshold forces exit, regardless of the cultivator's own wishes;
- After exit, mandatory cooling, no immediate re-entry;
- Only after indicators automatically recover during cooling may he apply for the next time.

This is not disrespecting the cultivator's freedom, but protecting him from being ruined by his own addiction.

## VI. Myriad-Rank Subgroup Calibration of Acceleration-Domain Indicators

Five indicators' thresholds are not one-size for all. The Alaya Network calibrates separately per five lineages: consciousness-information race's time-sense anchoring threshold is lower (because their consciousness flow is already fast), mechanical race's emotional fullness threshold needs redefinition (because their emotional expression differs). Subgroup calibration ensures indicators do not punish life-form differences, only identify true health risks. A plant spirit's emotional expression is annual rings, a mechanical race's is logs — indicators must respect this difference.

## VII. Experience Tracking After Acceleration-Domain Exit

After the cultivator exits the acceleration domain, the Alaya Network still tracks his experience indicators for a period — because some emptiness is not found in the acceleration domain, but only manifests after exit. If indicators decline during the tracking period, the network proactively suggests he arrange a period of Quest-Domain sedimentation. This post-exit tracking ensures acceleration aftereffects are timely discovered and handled.

## VIII. Retirement Mechanism for Acceleration Gears

If some cultivator long-term (e.g., ten consecutive times) does not meet acceleration-domain indicators, the Alaya Network suggests he consider retiring from acceleration gears, permanently transferring to the Quest Domain. Retirement is not punishment, but acknowledgment that his life form may not suit high-flow acceleration. After retirement he may still continue cultivating in the Quest Domain, without discrimination. This retirement mechanism makes acceleration gears a voluntary choice, not a mandatory obligation.

## IX. Emergency Intervention for Acceleration-Domain Indicators

If some indicator instantly drops below a dangerous threshold (e.g., causal-boundary sense abruptly drops), the Alaya Network does not wait for a periodic check, but immediately triggers emergency intervention: force-pause acceleration, start psychological counseling, contact the nearest master. Emergency-intervention mechanism ensures risks in the acceleration domain are responded to at second-level, rather than waiting until the next routine check. This second-level response is the dual-track time domain's bottom-line commitment to cultivator safety.

## Conclusion

The acceleration-domain experience indicator system tells cultivators: fast has boundaries, and the boundaries are determined by your real state. **Any one of the five indicators lighting red, you must slow down — this is not restricting your freedom, but ensuring when you come out, you are still you.**
