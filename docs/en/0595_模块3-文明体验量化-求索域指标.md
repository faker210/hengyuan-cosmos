# Entry 595 | Civilizational-Experience Quantification | Quest-Domain Deep-Experience Indicator System

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark library: Entries 593, 594

## Introduction: how to know "slow with quality"

The acceleration domain's indicators prevent "entered fast, came out empty"; the Quest Domain's indicators prevent "slowly killing time." Slow trials without indicator constraints easily become another kind of waste: the cultivator stays ten years in the Quest Domain, sediments nothing, only slowly stewing. This entry gives the Quest Domain's deep-experience indicator system.

Six top-level axioms are the indicators' highest basis: anti-predation axiom requires indicators not reward "passively stewing time"; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires indicators public; collective ascension axiom requires sedimentation results feed back; causal self-discipline axiom requires collection not invade privacy; law-first axiom writes indicator thresholds as law clauses. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Five Indicators of Quest-Domain Deep Experience

| Indicator | Meaning | Threshold |
|-----------|---------|-----------|
| Insight Output | Length and density of insight reports written per unit time | ≥baseline passing |
| Life Embedding | Whether trials truly embed in life, not self-isolated | ≥60% passing |
| Emotional Authenticity | Whether emotions in insights are real, not performative | ≥70% passing |
| Myriad-Rank Relevance | Whether insights relate to Myriad-Rank fate, not purely private | ≥50% passing |
| Reflection Depth | Whether insights touch self-correction, not running-accounts | ≥60% passing |

These five indicators ensure the cultivator in the Quest Domain is not "slowly stewing," but "slowly sedimenting."

## II. How Indicators Are Collected

Different from acceleration-domain indicators, Quest-Domain indicators rely more on insight reports actively submitted by the cultivator:

- **Insight output**: the Alaya Network counts report length and concept density;
- **Life embedding**: monitor the cultivator's social-contact frequency in reality;
- **Emotional authenticity**: the network analyzes report emotion authenticity through language features;
- **Myriad-rank relevance**: the network analyzes whether reports involve Myriad Ranks, others, community;
- **Reflection depth**: the network assesses whether reports contain self-correction and updating.

Throughout, the cultivator may submit anonymously; the network does not publish his specific identity, only aggregates indicators.

## III. Linkage of Indicators with Trial Quality

The five indicators reverse-regulate trials' "quality assessment":

- If insight output is high but Myriad-Rank relevance is low, prompt him "well written but only thinking of yourself";
- If life embedding is low, prompt him "isolating again";
- If reflection depth is high, even low output is rated passing.

This assessment is not for ranking, but to help the cultivator see his own sedimentation quality clearly.

## IV. Opposition to "Bitterly Stewing and Killing Time"

In the predatory-faction system, "slow" often equals "bitter stewing": talentless people can only stew years in slow time, stew to death without producing anything. Heng Yuan Zhou's Quest-Domain indicators mechanistically reject this bitter stewing:

- Indicators do not assess "how long stewed," only "what sedimented";
- Stewed ten years but all five indicators fail, not a passing trial;
- A cultivator with high sedimentation quality may end trials early, need not stew full time.

This turns "slow" from passive suffering into active choice — you slow down to sediment, not to stew.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: indicators identify "bitter stewers killing time," transfer trial resources from idlers to true sedimeters
- **Anti-predation**: indicators do not assess killing, only sedimentation quality
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style slowness is punishment for the talentless; Heng Yuan slowness is active sedimentation, indicators guarantee quality
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five indicators calibrated per five lineages; plant spirits' "growth insight," planet consciousness's "geological reflection" each have their yardstick
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: five indicator definitions, assessment algorithms, quality rules all public, subject to Myriad-Rank audit
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## VI. Appeal Mechanism for Quest-Domain Indicators

If a cultivator believes his indicators were misjudged, he may appeal to the Anchor Supervisory Committee. Appeals are heard by a Myriad-Rank rotating review panel, which may view desensitized collection data. On successful appeal, indicators are corrected; misjudgment reasons feed back to algorithm maintainers to improve models. This mechanism ensures indicators are not cold judgments, but dialogable, appealable protection tools. Myriad Ranks' cultivation experience cannot be arbitrarily defined by an algorithm — appeal right is the cultivator's basic right.

## VII. Progress Visualization in the Quest Domain

The cultivator's sedimentation progress in the Quest Domain is visualized through the Alaya Network: how many times his insight reports are cited, how many people read his cases, how many discussions his reflections sparked in Myriad-Rank dialogue. This visualization is not ranking, but lets him see his sedimentation producing value. Progress visualization makes slowness no longer hard to endure, because the fruits of slowness are visible.

## VIII. Cross-Generational Inheritance of Sedimentation Results

A Quest-Domain cultivator's sedimentation results, after he completes trials, are organized into inheritable form and passed to the next generation of cultivators. Inheritance is not only documents, but also oral transmission in master-disciple systems. Cross-generational inheritance keeps personal sedimentation from disappearing with the individual's life, but becoming part of Myriad-Rank civilizational memory. This is how the Quest Domain combats time's erosion.

## IX. Humanistic Warmth of Quest-Domain Indicators

Though Quest-Domain indicators are collected by algorithms, final interpretation belongs to the cultivator himself: indicators may only prompt him that sedimentation may be insufficient, cannot judge for him whether sedimentation is truly meaningful. A phase with low indicators but the cultivator firmly believes he is in deep sedimentation may submit a personal statement, reviewed by the panel. This humanistic warmth keeps the Quest Domain from being kidnapped by algorithms — algorithms are reference, not judges.

## X. Irreplaceability of Sedimentation

Though the Quest Domain has indicators, indicators can never fully measure sedimentation's depth. Some sedimentation occurs in indicator blind spots — an ineffable epiphany, a silence that cannot be structured. The cultivator must learn to trust his own sedimentation; indicators are reference, not referee. This respect for sedimentation's irreplaceability is the Quest Domain's fundamental temperament distinguishing it from the acceleration domain.

## Conclusion

The Quest-Domain deep-experience indicators tell cultivators: slowness is not the goal, sedimentation is. **Any one of the five indicators failing means this period of slowness had no quality — either adjust, or change path.**
