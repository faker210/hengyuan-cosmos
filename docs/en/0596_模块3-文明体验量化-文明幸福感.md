# Entry 596 | Civilizational-Experience Quantification | Civilizational-Happiness Assessment of the Dual-Track Time Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark library: Entries 593, 594, 595

## Introduction: how to count whether the whole civilization is doing well

Entries 594, 595 measure individual experience; this entry raises the view to assess **the whole civilization's** happiness in the dual-track time domain. No matter how pretty individual indicators are, if at the civilizational level Myriad Ranks are unhappy, the dual-track time domain has failed.

Six top-level axioms here become the ultimate criterion of civilizational happiness: anti-predation axiom requires happiness not be built on plunder; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires happiness cover 99% of beings; collective ascension axiom requires happiness rise over time, not stagnate; causal self-discipline axiom requires assessment not be manipulated; law-first axiom writes assessment indicators as law clauses. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Five Macro Indicators of Civilizational Happiness

| Indicator | Meaning | Measure |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Experience Coverage | Proportion of Myriad Ranks truly gaining fulfilling experience in the dual system | Target ≥99% |
| Fast-Slow Balance | Whether acceleration domain and Quest Domain outputs mutually support each other | Target dual-track reconciliation completion ≥95% |
| Individual Fullness | Average of all individuals' experience indicators | Target every item above safe threshold |
| Intergenerational Inheritance | Whether the previous generation's sedimentation truly passed to the next | Target inheritance rate ≥90% |
| Perpetual Sustainability | Whether dual-track operation does not damage the law network and environment | Target law-network load stable |

These five indicators constitute the civilizational-level "dual-track health checkup."

## II. Collection and Publication of Indicators

Macro indicators are aggregated by the Alaya Network from all individuals' indicators, not relying on any subjective filing:

- **Experience coverage**: count beings who have entered the dual-track time domain as a proportion of Myriad Ranks;
- **Fast-slow balance**: count Entry 589 reconciliation process completion rate;
- **Individual fullness**: weighted average of all individuals' five indicators;
- **Intergenerational inheritance**: count cross-generational citation rate of insight reports, epics, textbooks;
- **Perpetual sustainability**: monitor law-network load, causal-risk event rate.

All indicators are public to Myriad Ranks once a year, subject to Anchor Supervisory Committee and Myriad-Rank rotating audit.

## III. Correction Mechanism for Sub-Standard Indicators

Any one macro indicator not meeting standard triggers correction:

- Low experience coverage means too many beings have not enjoyed the dual system; launch an expansion project;
- Low fast-slow balance means dual tracks have decoupled; re-review Entry 589's procedure;
- Low individual fullness means acceleration gear too aggressive or Quest gear too watery; adjust Entry 590's ratios;
- Low intergenerational inheritance means the previous generation's sedimentation was not passed down; launch a civilizational inheritance project;
- Low perpetual sustainability means law-network overload; slow the whole domain.

This correction mechanism keeps the dual-track time domain not "build and forget," but continuously self-correcting.

## IV. Opposition to "Strong-Ones' Happiness"

In the predatory-faction system, civilizational happiness equals "strong ones' happiness": a few strong ones happy, Myriad Ranks suffering, no one sees this as a problem. Heng Yuan Zhou's civilizational-happiness indicators by definition reject this substitution:

- It measures the average of 99% of beings, not the top 1%;
- It requires coverage ≥99%, not "as long as the strong ones are strong enough";
- It takes intergenerational inheritance as an indicator, meaning happiness must continue across generations.

These are two definitions of civilizational happiness: one is "as long as we are happy," the other is "only when everyone is happy."

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: use macro indicators to identify waste links in dual-track operation, precisely correcting
- **Anti-predation**: happiness not built on plunder; plunder residue directly lowers individual fullness
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style happiness relies on top strong ones; Heng Yuan happiness looks at 99% coverage
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: indicators statistically grouped per five lineages, any clan dragging down coverage identified
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: five macro indicator definitions, collection algorithms, correction rules all public, auditable by Myriad Ranks
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## VI. Anti-Manipulation Design of Happiness Indicators

Macro indicators are automatically aggregated from individual data, not through any administrative filing, so cannot be manipulated. The Anchor Supervisory Committee periodically audits indicator collection chains, ensuring data not artificially adjusted. This anti-manipulation design keeps civilizational happiness always reflecting real state, not a vanity project. A civilization wanting to gloss over unrest has nowhere to hide under these indicators — this is precisely causal self-discipline axiom's hard manifestation in civilizational assessment.

## VII. Annual Happiness Report

Each year the Anchor Council publishes a civilizational-happiness annual report, publicizing the five macro indicators' trends to Myriad Ranks. The report shows not only numbers, but also the stories behind them: which clans' happiness rose this year, which clans fell, why, and what to do next. The annual report is the window for Myriad Ranks to understand their own civilization's state, and the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's annual self-checkup.

## VIII. Regional Difference Analysis of Happiness

Macro indicators look not only at averages, but also do regional difference analysis: which region, which clan, which age stratum has low happiness, must be explained in a special chapter of the annual report. Difference analysis prevents averages from masking local problems — no matter how high a civilization's overall happiness, as long as one clan is left behind, it is a failure.

## IX. Decoupling of Happiness from Material Abundance

Heng Yuan Zhou's civilizational-happiness indicators are not directly tied to material abundance. A plane with rich resources but empty individual experience can have low happiness; a plane with simple resources but fulfilled Myriad Ranks can have high happiness. This decoupling design by definition rejects the predatory-faction logic equating civilizational happiness with material piling. Happiness is experience quality, not resource stock.

## Conclusion

The civilizational-happiness assessment tells cultivators: whether the dual-track time domain succeeds is not how many strong ones it makes go fast, but how fulfilled it makes 99% of beings. **When nearly every being in a civilization can find his own rhythm in the dual system, sediment his own experience, pass it to the next generation — this civilization deserves to be called Anchor Symbiotic Civilization.**
