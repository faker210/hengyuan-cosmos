# Entry 597 | Myriad-Rank Republican Practice | Myriad-Rank Sharing of Acceleration-Domain Resources

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark base: Documents 18, 22; Benchmark library: Entry 501

## Introduction: how to distribute acceleration resources to call it Myriad-Rank Republicanism

The Origin Acceleration Domain is not an abstract "time tool"; behind it are real resources — law-network bandwidth, consciousness-energy supply, isolated-space compute, supervision compute. These resources are finite; how they are distributed to Myriad Ranks directly determines whether the dual-track time domain is truly republican. This entry answers: how acceleration-domain resources are shared with Myriad Ranks.

Six top-level axioms are the highest basis of distribution: anti-predation axiom requires distribution not steal or rob; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires 99% coverage; collective ascension axiom requires resources first used where they most feed back to Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom requires distribution process transparent and traceable; law-first axiom writes distribution rules as law clauses. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Four Categories of Acceleration-Domain Resources

| Resource Type | Meaning | Scarcity |
|---------------|---------|----------|
| Law-Network Bandwidth | High flow's occupation of the law network | High |
| Consciousness-Energy Supply | Accelerated consciousness's energy consumption | Medium |
| Isolated-Space Compute | Computing resources for simulation and deduction | Medium |
| Supervision Compute | Alaya Network monitoring overhead | High |

These four resources constitute the acceleration domain's "budget"; Myriad-Rank sharing means distributing within this budget.

## II. Three Principles of Myriad-Rank Sharing

1. **Distribute by task, not by birth**: whoever has a task truly needing acceleration (R&D, rescue, opening new paths) goes first, regardless of race or birth;
2. **Joint contracting, more work more gain**: contributors of greater contribution get more acceleration quota, corresponding to Document 22;
3. **Floor coverage**: every being yearly has a floor acceleration quota, ensuring 99% coverage, no "only strong ones have acceleration."

These three principles raise Entry 590's time ratios from the individual level to the civilizational level.

## III. Dynamic Adjustment of Quotas

Acceleration-domain resources are not fixed, but dynamically adjusted:

- When emergency rescue tasks arise, temporarily reallocate bandwidth to rescuers;
- When some clan's cultivators overall contribute high, next-round quota floats up;
- When some clan's experience indicators are low, next-round quota floats up to help them.

This dynamic adjustment ensures resources follow needs, not power.

## IV. Opposition to "Acceleration Privilege"

In the predatory-faction system, acceleration resources (if they exist) are privilege: sect elders monopolize acceleration chambers, the protagonist monopolizes by grace, ordinary beings cannot even touch the door. Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad-Rank sharing mechanistically rejects this privilege:

- Quotas public to Myriad Ranks, who got how much traceable;
- Floor coverage ensures no one excluded;
- Dynamic adjustment follows tasks and contribution, not power.

**Acceleration resources are public goods, not a noble club.**

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: resources follow tasks, idlers automatically get lowered quota
- **Anti-predation**: quotas public and traceable, no stealing or robbing
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style acceleration relies on sect privilege; Heng Yuan acceleration relies on tasks and contribution, Myriad Ranks floor
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five lineages, plant spirits, planet consciousness each have floor quota
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: four resource definitions, distribution rules, dynamic algorithms all public, auditable by Myriad Ranks
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## VI. Annual Public Audit of Quotas

All acceleration-domain resource distribution quota data is publicly audited to Myriad Ranks each year: who got how much, used on what task, produced what. Audit is performed by a Myriad-Rank rotating panel; found misappropriation recovered and held accountable. Public audit makes Myriad-Rank sharing of acceleration resources not just a slogan, but a supervised daily practice. Any being may consult the quota ledger, see where his jointly-contracted contribution was used — this is the double fulfillment of more-work-more-gain and Myriad-Rank Republicanism.

## VII. Emergency Allocation of Acceleration Resources

Besides annual quota allocation, the Alaya Network reserves emergency-allocation authority: when civilizational-level emergencies arise — e.g., some plane needs emergency technical rescue, some clan needs emergency technique R&D — the network may temporarily draw resources from non-emergency quotas to support. After emergency allocation, reasons must be publicly explained to Myriad Ranks, subject to after-the-fact audit. This mechanism gives acceleration resources both daily distribution order and emergency response capability.

## VIII. Acceleration Initiation for Newcomers

Every being newly entering the dual-track time domain has an acceleration initiation: the Alaya Network assigns a volunteer to show him the acceleration domain, explain rules, show cases, answer questions. Initiation is not sales, but lets newcomers choose whether to use acceleration on an informed basis. Informed choice is Myriad-Rank Republicanism's bottom line on time resources.

## IX. Intergenerational Inheritance of Acceleration Resources

Acceleration quotas do not disappear with the individual's death, but return to the public pool for reallocation. However, cultivators may pass their accumulated acceleration reputation to descendants — descendants inherit not specific quotas, but the reputation record of priority in obtaining quotas. This intergenerational inheritance makes acceleration-resource distribution balance publicity and continuity, avoiding resource complete break between generations.

## X. Long-Term Vision of Acceleration Sharing

Myriad-Rank sharing of acceleration-domain resources is not a phased policy, but a civilizational long-term vision: one day, all beings — regardless of birth, lineage, realm — can equally enjoy acceleration dividends, using saved time for cultivation and life. On that day, acceleration is no longer a few's privilege, but all beings' basic right. Today's quota system is precisely the transitional bridge to that vision.

## XI. Self-Repair of the Sharing System

If loopholes arise in the acceleration-resource sharing system — quotas misappropriated, distribution manipulated, newcomers discriminated — the Anchor Supervisory Committee must repair within an eon. Repair plans are public to Myriad Ranks, subject to review. Self-repair mechanism keeps the sharing system non-rigid, non-corrupt, always honest to Myriad Ranks.

## Conclusion

Myriad-Rank sharing of acceleration-domain resources tells cultivators: the dual-track time domain is not a small stove for strong ones, but Myriad Ranks' public facility. **Whoever has a real task goes first; everyone has a floor — this is true republicanism of time resources.**
