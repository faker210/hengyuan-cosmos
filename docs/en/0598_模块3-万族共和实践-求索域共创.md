# Entry 598 | Myriad-Rank Republican Practice | Myriad-Rank Co-Creation of Quest-Domain Trials

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark library: Entries 501, 578, 597

## Introduction: slow trials are not one person's matter

Entry 597 spoke of acceleration-domain resource sharing; this entry speaks of Quest-Domain trial co-creation. The Quest Sediment Domain looks more "individual" — one person slowly sediments, slowly realizes — but its results must be Myriad-Rank co-created. An insight written behind closed doors, without Myriad-Rank dialogue, without Myriad-Rank testing, is only a private reflection, not a civilizational treasure.

Six top-level axioms are co-creation's highest basis: anti-predation axiom requires co-creation not swallow others' insights; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires Myriad Ranks equally participate in dialogue; collective ascension axiom requires co-creation results feed back to all; causal self-discipline axiom requires dialogue not overstep boundaries; law-first axiom writes co-creation rules as law clauses. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. What Is "Myriad-Rank Co-Creation"

Myriad-Rank co-creation of Quest-Domain trials is not stuffing ten thousand people into a room to argue, but a three-layer structure:

1. **Individual sedimentation**: the cultivator himself writes insights in slow time;
2. **Myriad-Rank dialogue**: after insights are submitted, open to Myriad Ranks; other cultivators may comment, supplement, challenge;
3. **Joint revision**: after multiple dialogue rounds, insights are revised into a more mature version and archived.

One person's insight through these three layers turns from "private reflection" to "Myriad-Rank consensus."

## II. Technical Implementation of Co-Creation

| Link | Technology | Role |
|------|-----------|------|
| Insight Submission | Alaya Network anonymous upload | Protect privacy |
| Myriad-Rank Dialogue | Structured comment area | No arguing, only opinions |
| Consensus Formation | Algorithm aggregation | Find Myriad-Rank commonly accepted parts |
| Joint Revision | Original author and commenters collaborate | Revised version archived |

Throughout, the original author retains copyright, but the revised version is public to Myriad Ranks. This is the "selfless path" landing at the spiritual level.

## III. Co-Creation's Meaning for Myriad Ranks

Myriad-Rank co-creation is not to make one person's insight more perfect, but to let Myriad Ranks jointly grow in dialogue:

- Young cultivators may learn from elders' insights;
- Cultivators of different lineages may find commonalities in each other's insights;
- Old-system erroneous insights through dialogue are identified and corrected.

This is precisely collective-ascension axiom's expression in the spiritual dimension: **one person's depth becomes the whole's depth in Myriad-Rank dialogue.**

## IV. Opposition to "Closed-Door Enlightenment"

In the predatory-faction system, enlightenment is private: a strong one secludes for decades, the Dao he realizes is his private property, discussed with no one, challenged by no one. This approach's problems:

1. Closed-door enlightenment easily goes off track, no one corrects it;
2. Enlightenment results monopolized, Myriad Ranks cannot learn;
3. It trains "lone realizers," not "co-builders."

Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad-Rank co-creation mechanistically rejects closed-door enlightenment: an insight not through Myriad-Rank dialogue is not a passing trial. **You may slowly realize, but what you realize must be brought out to check with Myriad Ranks.**

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Myriad-Rank dialogue avoids one person realizing wrongly for decades before discovering
- **Anti-predation**: co-creation does not swallow others' insights, original author retains copyright
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style enlightenment relies on the protagonist closed-door self-realization; Heng Yuan enlightenment must have Myriad-Rank dialogue
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five lineages, plant spirits, planet consciousness all participate in dialogue, no clan excluded
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: dialogue rules, consensus algorithms, revision process all public, subject to Myriad-Rank audit
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## VI. Etiquette Rules for Co-Creation Dialogue

Myriad-Rank dialogue areas have strict etiquette rules: no personal attacks, no cross-lineage disparagement, no turning private reflections into weapons to attack others. Etiquette violators are temporarily removed from dialogue areas, re-educated. Etiquette rules protect co-creation from becoming an argument arena, letting cultivators of different lineages, different realms dare to express sincerely. Myriad-Rank co-creation's precondition is mutual respect among Myriad Ranks — without etiquette, dialogue degenerates into a battlefield.

## VII. Copyright Arrangements for Co-Creation Results

Mature insights produced by Myriad-Rank co-creation, copyright belongs to the original author, but usage rights belong to all Myriad Ranks. The original author may choose whether to sign on the co-creation result, or choose anonymity. This copyright arrangement both protects the creator's dignity, and ensures results are not privatized. The Alaya Network records contributor lists for each co-creation result, letting Myriad Ranks know whose life this wisdom came from.

## VIII. Newcomer Guidance in Co-Creation Areas

Myriad-Rank dialogue areas have newcomer guidance for newly joined cultivators: guiding him how to ask a good question, how to give constructive comments, how to accept others' challenges. Newcomer guidance keeps co-creation from disorder due to new members' awkwardness, and makes every new member feel welcome. Co-creation quality depends on every participant's cultivation, and cultivation comes from guidance.

## IX. Cross-Language Dissemination of Co-Creation Results

Insights produced by Myriad-Rank co-creation, through the Alaya Network's translation layer, are automatically translated into multiple civilizational languages, letting linguistically diverse Myriad Ranks all read them. Translation does not pursue perfection, but readability — a plant spirit's meditations, a mechanical race grasping the gist is enough, no word-by-word precision needed. Cross-language dissemination makes co-creation truly Myriad-Rank's, not some language circle's.

## X. Spiritual Heritage of Co-Creation

The most precious result of Myriad-Rank co-creation is not some specific insight document, but the spiritual disposition it cultivates: among Myriad Ranks, willingness to dialogue, willingness to listen, willingness to find consensus in disagreement. This spiritual disposition once cultivated, transcends specific co-creation activities, permeating every corner of civilization. This is co-creation's true heritage for the future.

## Conclusion

Myriad-Rank co-creation of Quest-Domain trials tells cultivators: slowness is not to hide away, but to sediment something that can dialogue with Myriad Ranks. **One person's insight, through Myriad-Rank dialogue, truly becomes civilization's wealth.**
