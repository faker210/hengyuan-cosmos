# Entry 599 | Myriad-Rank Republican Practice | Myriad-Rank Equality Mechanism in the Time Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain Civilizational-Experience System | Benchmark library: Entries 501, 597, 598

## Introduction: time equality is more fundamental than resource equality

Entry 597 spoke of resource sharing, Entry 598 of trial co-creation; this entry speaks of the most fundamental layer: **Myriad-Rank equality in the time domain**. If resources are unequal, Myriad Ranks may slowly catch up; but if time-feeling is unequal, Myriad Ranks form a fundamental estrangement between them — how can a carbon-based being living a century and a planet consciousness living millions of years possibly dialogue equally? The dual-track time domain must solve this time-scale problem.

Six top-level axioms here become the highest basis of time equality: anti-predation axiom requires not ranking by time scale; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires different time-scale Myriad Ranks dialogue equally; collective ascension axiom requires the time domain become a bridge for cross-scale dialogue; causal self-discipline axiom requires dialogue not disturb each other's causality; law-first axiom writes the equality mechanism as a law clause. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Roots of Time-Scale Inequality

Time-scale differences among Myriad Ranks are huge:

- Human race: hundred-year lifespan;
- Plant spirits: millennia to tens of millennia;
- Planet consciousness: geological eras;
- Consciousness-information race: instantaneous;
- Mechanical race: depends on hardware.

If dialoguing directly by real time, a hundred-year human is just a blink in the planet consciousness's eyes, cannot form an equal relationship. This is a physical problem Myriad-Rank Republicanism must solve.

## II. Cross-Scale Mechanisms in the Dual-Track Time Domain

The dual-track time domain achieves cross-scale equality through three mechanisms:

| Mechanism | Method | Effect |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| Flow Matching | Both sides in the dual system tune to relatively close "effective time sense" | Human accelerates 1000×, planet consciousness decelerates 1000×; their dialogue time-sense matches |
| Experience Alignment | Before dialogue, both sides first align "what counts as an event" | Human's lifetime and planet consciousness's eon structurally align |
| Rotating Listening | Short-lived race finishes speaking first, long-lived race then speaks, no interrupting | Short-lived voices not drowned out |

These three mechanisms let a hundred-year human and a planet consciousness truly dialogue equally, rather than one side speaking and the other spacing out.

## III. Relationship Between Time Equality and Realm Equality

Time equality is not "make everyone live the same length," but "let beings of different time scales have equal speech rights in the time domain":

- A hundred-year human in the acceleration domain has lived a thousand years of effective time, his experience is no less than the planet consciousness's;
- The planet consciousness in the Quest Domain decelerates to human scale, he can understand the hundred-year human's story;
- Both sides, with Alaya Network's help, translate their respective time experiences into structures the other understands.

This is Myriad-Rank Republicanism's true landing at the time dimension.

## IV. Opposition to "Time Hierarchy"

In the predatory-faction system, time scale directly equals rank: the longer you live, the more remarkable; short-lived beings' voices considered "brief and insignificant." This approach's problems:

1. It disparages short-lived races' experience as "a blink";
2. It makes long-lived races naturally arrogant, unable to truly dialogue;
3. It trains "time aristocrats," not "Myriad-Rank kin."

Heng Yuan Zhou's time-equality mechanism physically rejects this arrogance: a hundred-year human and a planet consciousness dialogue in the dual system with equal speech rights. **Living long does not mean you are right; living short does not mean you are insignificant.**

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: cross-scale dialogue lets short-lived races' experience not be wasted, long-lived races' arrogance not become an obstacle
- **Anti-predation**: not plundering speech rights by time scale
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style strong ones suppress by living long; Heng Yuan dual system lets hundred-year races and planet consciousness dialogue equally
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five lineages, plant spirits, planet consciousness, consciousness-information race all get equal seats in cross-scale dialogue
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: flow-matching algorithms, experience-alignment methods, rotating-listening rules all public, subject to Myriad-Rank audit
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## VI. Translation Layer for Cross-Scale Dialogue

The Alaya Network provides a translation layer for cross-scale dialogue: translating a human's lifetime into a planet consciousness's eon, translating consciousness-information race's instant into mechanical race's cycle. The translation layer does not change content, only transforms structure, letting both dialogue sides understand each other in their own time sense. This is Myriad-Rank Republicanism's gentle technical landing. Without the translation layer, hundred-year races and planet consciousness dialogue only talk past each other; with it, the time-scale chasm is truly crossed.

## VII. Ritual Sense of Cross-Scale Dialogue

Cross-scale dialogue is not only technical engineering, but has ritual sense: before each dialogue begins, both sides by their own traditions perform a meeting courtesy — human bows, plant spirit shows a new leaf, planet consciousness adjusts a gravitational fluctuation. These rituals make cross-scale dialogue not only information exchange, but a solemn meeting between civilizations. Ritual sense reminds every participant: you are dialoguing with a kin of a completely different time scale; this matter itself deserves to be taken seriously.

## VIII. Historical Archive of Time Equality

Each important cross-scale dialogue is recorded in the time-equality historical archive: who dialogued with whom, discussed what, reached what consensus. The archive is public to Myriad Ranks, becoming historical evidence of time equality from idea to practice. Myriad Ranks may look back: a hundred years ago how did hundred-year races and planet consciousness dialogue, a hundred years later how did dialogue deepen. The historical archive makes time equality not a slogan, but a traceable process.

## Conclusion

Myriad-Rank equality mechanism in the time domain tells cultivators: Myriad-Rank Republicanism is not only equality in resources, but more in time feeling. **The dual-track time domain's greatest invention is not making people faster, but letting Myriad Ranks of different time scales finally sit down and tell their stories equally.**
