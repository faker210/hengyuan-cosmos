## I. Basic Definition of More Work, More Reward

More Work, More Reward is the core distribution principle of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization: the distribution of scarce values such as cultivation resources, evolutionary opportunities, and social honors is based primarily on the actual contribution of individuals or groups to the symbiotic network. The greater the contribution, the larger the share obtained; the smaller the contribution, the smaller the share. More Work, More Reward opposes both the predatory distribution of unearned gains and the egalitarian distribution where working more or less makes no difference.

"Work" has a specific meaning in the symbiotic civilization: it refers not only to physical labor, but to all activities with positive contributions to the symbiotic network, including diligent cultivation, knowledge creation, technological innovation, anchor maintenance, civilizational governance, and racial collaboration. "Reward" also refers not only to material resources, but to comprehensive evolutionary support including evolutionary opportunities, anchor access permissions, mentorship qualifications, and priority in plane exploration.

## II. Theoretical Foundations of More Work, More Reward

The establishment of the More Work, More Reward principle is based on the following three theoretical considerations:

### 2.1 Incentive Theory: Contributions Need Positive Feedback

Evolution is a process requiring sustained investment. If contribution and reward are decoupled, individuals lose the motivation for sustained investment, and the evolutionary efficiency of the entire symbiotic network declines. By establishing a positive feedback loop between contribution and reward, More Work, More Reward motivates every life to maximize its own potential and create more value for the symbiotic network.

### 2.2 Fairness Theory: Distribution by Contribution Is the Most Basic Fairness

In the symbiotic civilization, fairness is not equal to egalitarianism. True fairness means every life's effort receives matching reward. A cultivator who reaches a high realm through diligent cultivation receiving more resources and opportunities is a reasonable reward for their effort; an individual unwilling to make any effort receiving only basic security is also an expression of fairness. More Work, More Reward protects the rights of the diligent and the creative.

### 2.3 Efficiency Theory: Resources Should Flow Where They Create the Most Value

Although cosmic cultivation resources can be continuously increased through technical means, they remain finite within a given space-time. The More Work, More Reward mechanism prioritizes allocating resources to individuals and groups with high contributions and efficiency, enabling finite resources to produce maximum evolutionary benefit. This is not an abandonment of the weak, but responsibility for overall evolutionary efficiency — only after overall efficiency improves can more resources be available to protect the weak.

## III. Quantitative Evaluation System for Contributions

To make the More Work, More Reward principle operable, the symbiotic civilization has established a systematic quantitative evaluation system for contributions:

| Contribution Type | Evaluation Dimension | Quantification Method |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Cultivation Contribution | Realm advancement speed, realm height, demonstration effect of breakthroughs | The anchor network automatically records cultivation data, converted into cultivation contribution value |
| Knowledge Contribution | Public sharing of cultivation insights, creation of new techniques, theoretical research results | Reviewed by the academic committee, valued by the influence and innovativeness of the knowledge |
| Technological Contribution | Technological innovation in law engineering, optimization of Multiplying Fruit applications, improvements to anchor hardware | Reviewed by the technology committee, valued by practical utility and promotion scope |
| Service Contribution | Mentorship hours, anchor maintenance workload, civilizational governance participation | The anchor network automatically records service duration and quality, converted into service contribution value |
| Ecological Contribution | Planet ecological restoration, plane energy circulation improvement, cosmic environmental protection | Evaluated by the ecology committee, valued by the scope and sustainability of ecological improvement |
| Collaborative Contribution | Participation in cross-racial cooperation projects, contributions to Republic of Myriad Races affairs | Evaluated by the Republic Assembly, valued by the depth and results of collaboration |

An individual's total contribution value is the weighted sum of all types of contribution values, with weights dynamically adjusted according to the needs of the civilization's development stage. Contribution values are settled quarterly, serving as the main basis for resource distribution in the following quarter.

## IV. The Output-Linked Contract System

The Output-Linked Contract is the concrete institutional design of the More Work, More Reward principle in the field of cultivation resource distribution, whose core idea is "contract output to groups, pay according to output, reward overproduction."

### 4.1 Basic Operation

1. **Resource Contracting**: The symbiotic civilization contracts cultivation resources within a certain region (such as spirit veins, medicine fields, energy mineral veins) to cultivator groups or racial communities
2. **Output Quota**: Set a reasonable output quota based on the resource's basic productivity and the contractor's capability
3. **Payment Linked to Output**: The resource share obtained by the contractor is linked to actual output; reaching the quota yields the basic share, and overproduction is rewarded proportionally
4. **Autonomous Operation**: Provided the contractor does not violate the anti-predation principle and ecological protection regulations, it has the right to autonomously decide resource development and utilization methods

### 4.2 Advantages of the Output-Linked Contract

- Directly links the utilization efficiency of collective resources with the contractor's personal interests, motivating meticulous cultivation
- Avoids the "tragedy of the commons" caused by equal distribution of resources, improving resource utilization efficiency
- Under the incentive of overproduction rewards, contractors are motivated to innovate technologically and optimize management
- Provides cultivators with a stable resource acquisition channel, reducing economic motivation for predatory behavior

### 4.3 Risk Protection

The Output-Linked Contract does not fully transfer risk to the contractor. The symbiotic civilization has established a "Contract Risk Protection Fund," providing basic protection to contractors in cases of force majeure natural disasters or plane fluctuations, ensuring they do not lose basic cultivation conditions due to accidents.

## V. Balance between Basic Security and More Work, More Reward

More Work, More Reward does not mean the symbiotic civilization is a complete "jungle society." On the contrary, More Work, More Reward must combine with the basic security of "A Path for Everyone" to achieve the unity of fairness and efficiency.

The symbiotic civilization has established an "Evolutionary Basic Security Line," ensuring that every life, regardless of contribution, can obtain the following basic protections:

- Free access to the basic methods of the Three Hundred Origin Cultivation Systems
- Minimum cultivation resource allocation (sufficient to sustain basic cultivation and survival)
- Basic medical and educational services
- Minimum-level access to the anchor network

Above the basic security line, resource distribution strictly follows the More Work, More Reward principle. This dual-layer structure of "bottom-line security + distribution by contribution" both protects the basic evolutionary rights of the weak and motivates the creation and contribution of the strong.

## VI. Mechanisms Preventing the Alienation of More Work, More Reward

More Work, More Reward may face the risk of alienation in practice, and the symbiotic civilization has established the following preventive mechanisms:

- **Anti-monopoly mechanism**: Prohibits individuals or groups from obtaining unreasonable excess contributions through monopolizing resources; the Anti-monopoly Law has explicit provisions
- **Intergenerational fairness mechanism**: Contribution values cannot be fully inherited, preventing class solidification caused by "inherited contribution"
- **Contribution authenticity review**: The anchor network cross-validates contribution data, preventing false reporting
- **Dynamic adjustment mechanism**: The weights and standards of contribution evaluation are periodically adjusted according to civilizational development needs, preventing excessive expansion of any one contribution type

## Conclusion

More Work, More Reward is the core principle of the distribution system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. It differs both from predationism's "the strong take all" and egalitarianism's "big rice pot"; rather, on the basis of basic security, resources and opportunities are distributed according to contribution, achieving the unity of fairness and efficiency. The ultimate goal of More Work, More Reward is to ensure that every life's effort has a return, and every life's potential can be fully realized.
