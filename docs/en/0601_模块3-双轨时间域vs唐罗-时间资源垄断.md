# Entry 601 | Dual-Track Time Domain vs Tang-Luo System | Tang-Luo Matchless Academy's Monopoly of Time Resources

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 16, 22, 04 | Series: Cross-Heavens Time-View Benchmark (Entry 1)

## Introduction: when "fast" becomes a few's privilege

Discussing the dual-track time domain, we cannot only speak of how it is designed, but must also answer a contrast question: **in a world without dual tracks, how is time distributed**. The Tang-Luo system (see Module Two 252–287 full teardown) gives the most representative answer — it runs a "Matchless Academy," packaging time acceleration, resource infusion, master's teaching into a kind of **grace**, distributed only to the very few chosen. The rest exhaust their lifespans in slow, endless suffering. This is the Tang-Luo upper limit at the time dimension.

Six top-level axioms here become the yardstick of contrast: anti-predation axiom requires acceleration come from incremental efficiency, not stealing others' time; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires time resources equally open to all beings; collective ascension axiom requires acceleration dividends feed back to Myriad Ranks, not feed one lineage; causal self-discipline axiom requires time scheduling not create class-solidifying causality; law-first axiom requires time-distribution rules stable and public, not temporarily tilted by protagonist plot. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry from the "time-resource monopoly" incision dissects the fundamental divergence between Tang-Luo system and the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization.

## I. Matchless Academy's Time Structure: An Acceleration Chamber Walled In

Tang-Luo Matchless Academy's essence is making "time" an **admission-only commodity**. Its time structure has three layers:

1. **In-school acceleration zone**: talents selected into the academy may obtain in arrays, secret realms, a cultivation flow several times outside; what others achieve in a lifetime, they achieve in years.
2. **Out-of-school suffering zone**: the unselected grope in the normal timeline, incomplete techniques, scarce resources, century after century treading water.
3. **Protagonist-only zone**: true top time dividends (grace, lucky encounters, elder empowerment) flow only to the protagonist, not even academy peers.

Between these three layers there are no passages, only walls. People in the acceleration chamber do not feed experience back to people outside the walls — because people outside are not "one of us." So time is cut into two halves: one belongs to talents, one to cannon fodder.

## II. Triple Cost of Time Monopoly

Tang-Luo-style time monopoly costs are deeper than simple resource plunder:

- **Absolute waste of lifespans**: beings outside the walls exhaust their lives in slow time; their lifespans could have been converted into effective cultivation time by the symbiotic network's lifespan-extension technology (Document 38), but are burned for nothing.
- **Causal class lock**: whether one is born inside or outside the walls almost determines how many times flow one may cultivate all life. This writes "birth" into time law, directly conflicting with Myriad-Rank Republican axiom.
- **Knowledge intergenerational gap**: in-school experience not fed back, out-of-school lessons not archived, the whole civilization's time utilization compressed to extremely low.

Heng Yuan Zhou judges this structure as **energy-level waste at the time dimension**: not energy insufficient, but time artificially locked in a few's hands.

## III. Anchor Symbiosis's Alternative: Breaking the Acceleration Chamber into Public Tracks

The dual-track time domain's replacement of Tang Luo is not "run Matchless Academy bigger," but fundamentally cancel the "walls":

| Tang-Luo Time Structure | Anchor-Symbiotic Alternative |
|-------------------------|------------------------------|
| In-school acceleration zone (grace admission) | Origin Acceleration Domain (contribution redeemed, joint contracting) |
| Out-of-school suffering zone (fend for self) | Quest Sediment Domain (slow trials also a legitimate path, guided and protected) |
| Protagonist-only zone (one monopolizes) | Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision (network-wide sharing, no one can monopolize) |
| Birth determines flow | Qualification determines flow; qualification accumulable by contribution |

The key difference: Tang Luo's "fast" is bestowed, so can be taken back anytime; Heng Yuan Zhou's "fast" is earned, per Document 22 more work more gain, anyone may redeem acceleration time by civilizational contribution. **Fast is no longer identity, but labor reward.**

## IV. Observation Indicators for Quest-Domain Replay

Replicating Tang-Luo's time structure in the Quest Sediment Domain, three indicator groups are focused:

1. **Inside-outside advancement gap**: acceleration zone vs suffering zone after a century's realm distribution gap, quantifying monopoly span.
2. **Outside lifespan loss rate**: unselected beings' effective cultivation time as proportion of total lifespan, quantifying energy-level waste.
3. **Experience feedback rate**: how much in-school-produced knowledge flows back outside — if near zero, confirms "time private retention."

The replay conclusion points to the same judgment: Matchless Academy is not inclusive education, but a machine discounting most people's time for a few.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: beings outside the walls' lifespans burned for nothing in slow suffering is the largest energy-level waste at the time dimension
- **Anti-predation**: acceleration chamber's flow is essentially implicit plunder of outside lifespans and opportunities; protagonist-only zone is even zero-sum
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Matchless Academy's time monopoly is precisely the Tang-Luo upper limit's projection at the time dimension — inclusion stops at the walls, outside never emerges
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: dual system open to 99% of Myriad Ranks, time qualification ignores bloodline and race; Tang-Luo Academy only accepts "one of us"
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: acceleration-domain flow parameters, redemption rules network-wide public; any merged plane may接入 by the same set of time economics
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Tang-Luo Matchless Academy proved one thing: **as long as time is a monopoly good, no matter how beautiful the academy walls, they are just outer packaging for plunder.** The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's dual-track time domain turns "fast" from grace to reward, from walls to public tracks — only then is time, the most fundamental means of production, for the first time returned to Myriad Ranks themselves.
