# Entry 603 | Dual-Track Time Domain vs Perfect World | Shi Hao's Alone-Deciding-Eternity Time Loneliness

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 17, 83, 03 | Series: Cross-Heavens Time-View Benchmark (Entry 3)

## Introduction: one person bearing all for eternity — epic or tragedy?

*Perfect World's* Shi Hao ultimately walks toward "alone-deciding eternity" — alone guarding the end of the river of time, holding off annihilation for the entire epoch, loved ones all gone, eternity walking alone. This is one of web novels' most tragic time pictures: an ultimate strong one nailing himself forever to time's farthest end, trading loneliness for all beings' survival. Heng Yuan Zhou does not intend to deny this tragic grandeur, but must structurally ask: **why must a civilization's survival be bet on one person alone bearing eternity?**

Six top-level axioms are the yardstick of this question: anti-predation axiom requires survival not cost one person swallowing eternity's causality; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the qualification of holding off disaster not belong only to one; collective ascension axiom requires strong ones lift Myriad Ranks along, not alone cover the rear; causal self-discipline axiom requires cross-time alone-deciding must be reported, observable, shareable; law-first axiom requires "why one person is necessary" first argued by law, not driven by tragic plot. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry dissects Shi-Hao-style "alone-deciding eternity" time structure and gives the dual alternative.

## I. Time Structure of "Alone-Deciding Eternity"

Shi-Hao-style alone-deciding is an extreme time-use method, its structure three:

1. **Single-point bearing**: all world-ending-level pressure on the timeline ultimately converges on him alone; Myriad Ranks retreat to safe zones, he alone faces the other end of the river of time.
2. **Eternal loneliness**: his time scale is pulled to equal the epoch's length; peers, loved ones, descendants are like morning dew before him; he lives yet equals having lost companions across all time.
3. **Causal solo-bearing**: the massive causal backlash from holding off disaster is not dispersed, not transferred, all recorded on his own causal ledger.

This structure pushes "guardianship" to aesthetic extreme, yet also evacuates "collective" to the extreme.

## II. Time Cost of Single-Point Bearing

Using the dual-track view to disassemble "alone-deciding eternity" costs:

- **Civilization loses its rear-guard echelon**: when all pressure flows to one, other strong ones need not train "disaster-bearing" ability. Once he misses, civilization has no second line of defense. This violates collective-ascension axiom — strong ones should cultivate thousands upon thousands who can stand side by side bearing things, not alone finish the job.
- **Time experience completely unbalanced**: he has eternity's length, yet loses the most basic experience of "spending time with others." The dual-track time domain's design premise (Entry 501) precisely opposes "fast to empty" or "long to lonely" — alone-deciding eternity is the latter's extreme.
- **Causal ledger unsustainable**: one person bearing eternity's causality, any calculation error may cause total collapse. This runs directly counter to causal self-discipline axiom's "first observe then limited intervention, disperse risk."

## III. Dual Alternative: Rear Guard Is Engineering, Not Lone Heroism

Anchor symbiosis's response to "world-ending-level threats" is not to reshape another Shi Hao, but to make "rear guard" a **distributed civilizational project**:

| Alone-Deciding-Eternity Structure | Dual Alternative Structure |
|-----------------------------------|----------------------------|
| One person blocks at time's end | Anchor Council + symbiotic court collective decision, rear-guard tasks decomposed to multiple echelons |
| Causal backlash solo-borne | Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision, causality dispersed to network-wide tolerable thresholds |
| Eternity alone walking, loved ones gone | Acceleration domain cultivates reserve corps, Quest Sediment Domain transmits rear-guard experience |
| Rely on tragic willpower to force it | Rely on law-first axiom to pre-deduce risks, engineered sharing |

The key difference: Shi-Hao-style is "**one person bears for eternity**"; Heng-Yuan-style is "**eternity together finishes the job**." The former is tragic; the latter is sustainable.

## IV. Tragedy ≠ Reasonable — Law-First Axiom's Correction

Law-first axiom here has a cold reminder: **a setting's "must be one person alone deciding" is often not because law says so, but because plot needs a tragic protagonist**. When we use the dual-track time domain to replay Perfect World's time structure, we find the question "why can't multiple echelons share?" the original text does not give a law-level hard constraint — it is more a narrative choice. This is precisely the source of retcon/abrupt-ending risk: "the only one person" driven by tragedy will eventually be overturned by new plot in sequels and crossovers.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: an eternal ultimate strong one's time only for solo rear guard; other strong ones lose disaster-bearing training, echelon gaps
- **Anti-predation**: single-point bearing implicitly contains "others protected in safe zones, need not bear," seemingly selfless, actually evacuating Myriad Ranks' responsibility capacity
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Shi Hao's "alone-deciding" and Tang Luo's "grace" are two sides of one coin — one monopolizes hard labor, the other monopolizes dividends, both refuse to let Myriad Ranks equally share
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: rear-guard ability opened to Myriad-Rank echelons for cultivation; planet consciousness, universe-origin beings may also share river-of-time pressure
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: risk contingency plans, rear-guard procedures all public and archived; any merged plane may接入 distributed defense, not wait for its own "Shi Hao"
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Shi Hao's eternal loneliness deserves salute, but should not be copied. Heng Yuan Zhou respects every strong one willing to hold off disaster for all beings, yet refuses to build civilization's survival on "some one person alone bearing eternity." The dual-track time domain's answer is: true guardianship is not living oneself as an isolated island, but letting Myriad Ranks stand side by side, watching over each other, in the river of time. Tragedy is remembered; institutions are what get continued.
