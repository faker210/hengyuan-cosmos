# Entry 604 | Dual-Track Time Domain vs Douluo Continent | Tang San's Transmigrator Time Privilege

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 82, 89, 87 | Series: Cross-Heavens Time-View Benchmark (Entry 4)

## Introduction: reborn with past-life memories — talent or time cheating?

*Douluo Continent's* Tang San, carrying past-life Mystic Heaven Skill memories and techniques reincarnated, equivalent to living one extra lifetime compared to everyone else on the starting line. This is web novels' classic "transmigration" template, and also an extremely hidden **time privilege**: his time does not start from the present, but is inherited from another timeline. Heng Yuan Zhou's dual-track time domain must ask: **is this "cross-timeline memory inheritance" causally and legally tenable?**

Six top-level axioms are the criterion of this question: anti-predation axiom requires past-life accumulation not become a dimensional-strike against present-life peers; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires evolution qualification not preset by "birth with a past life"; collective ascension axiom requires cross-timeline knowledge flow back to Myriad Ranks, not packed into one person's brain; causal self-discipline axiom requires any cross-time operation strictly reported, first observe then limited intervention; law-first axiom requires "whether memory-carrying transmigration is allowed" first defined by law, not default true by protagonist halo. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry dissects Tang-San-style time privilege and gives the dual-track and archaeology-protocol alternative.

## I. Three Layers of Transmigrator Time Privilege

Tang San's advantage is essentially a time-dimension asymmetry, its structure three:

1. **Memory inheritance**: past-life techniques, combat experience, world-view cognition brought into present life with the soul, equivalent to preinstalling an adult's cognitive system in infancy.
2. **Information-gap dividend**: he knows "what will happen in the future," always pre-positioning at key nodes, while peers walk blind in time.
3. **Psychological dimensional reduction**: he with two lifetimes of experience naturally suppresses peer youths in mindset, ruthlessness, scope.

These three layers stacked, the "transmigrator" gains an unfair advantage over locals from the time starting line.

## II. Causal Cost of Time Privilege

Reviewing transmigrator with causal self-discipline axiom (Documents 83, 89), problems immediately surface:

- **Causal pollution**: past-life memories come from another timeline; injecting them into present life equals pouring water from another river into a clean causal river — precisely the "foreign causal injection" Document 89's time-parox prevention warns of.
- **Locals' time stolen**: the opportunities, status, mentors Tang San grabs early through the information gap originally belonged to natives' natural growth opportunities on their own timeline. His "rebirth thrill" is built on locals being squeezed off the time track.
- **No reporting, no audit**: his cross-time operation never went through any "cross-plane/cross-time protocol" review; it is unsupervised private time travel.

Heng Yuan Zhou judges: transmigrator privilege is **original sin at the time dimension** — seemingly just a protagonist's golden finger, actually polluting the whole world at the causal layer.

## III. Dual-Track and Archaeology-Protocol Alternative: Knowledge May Feed Back, Time May Not Be Privately Carried

Anchor symbiosis does not forbid "obtaining knowledge from the past/elsewhere," but strictly distinguishes two things:

| Transmigrator Privilege | Anchor-Symbiotic Alternative |
|-------------------------|------------------------------|
| Carry memory rebirth, personally preinstall past life | Cross-plane archaeology (Documents 85, 87), only abstract knowledge fed back, not soul memories |
| Privately grab opportunities through information gap | Knowledge enters Alaya Network, shared by all Myriad Ranks, no one can monopolize by information gap |
| Cross-time unreported | Cross-time operation reported through Anchor Council, first observe then limited intervention |
| One person enjoys two lifetimes of experience alone | Quest Sediment Domain makes "two-lifetime comparison" a public teaching case |

The core difference: **knowledge may flow across timelines (protocol-fed back), but the individual may not privatize a timeline**. If Tang San were in Heng Yuan Zhou, his past-life Mystic Heaven Skill should enter the Alaya Network as abstract technique network-wide open, not locked in his own soul as a trump card.

## IV. Echo with the "Acceleration ≠ Emptiness" Proposition

Transmigrator privilege also bypasses the dual-track time domain's core proposition: **the time one must walk oneself, not one step may be saved**. Tang San skipped present-life cultivation experience "from not knowing to knowing," directly preinstalling past life — seemingly efficient, actually draining his real growth on the present-life timeline. The Quest Sediment Domain exists precisely to protect this "must be experienced oneself" experiential quality. Others' time, no matter how precious, cannot live for you.

## Brief Transition

Returning to the dual-track design's original intent: transmigrator privilege is denied precisely because it violates the Origin Acceleration Domain's open logic that "anyone may enter by contribution." Feeding past-life knowledge back desensitized through the Alaya Network lets Myriad Ranks grow on the same starting line through their own effort in Origin Acceleration Domain and Quest Sediment Domain, rather than by birth-like time cheating.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the transmigrator's information-gap dividend comes from unsupervised cross-time搬运, essentially implicit plunder of locals' opportunities
- **Anti-predation**: preinstalling past-life techniques = swallowing peers' growth opportunities on the time starting line, time-dimension zero-sum
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang San's "past-life golden finger" is the time-upgraded version of Tang Luo's "grace system" — grace from the sect becomes grace from a past life, monopoly essence unchanged
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: after knowledge feeds back to Myriad Ranks, any native may equally learn past-life wisdom, no "transmigration" privilege needed
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cross-time knowledge flow uniformly managed by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision; any plane may接入 by the same archaeology protocol
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The transmigrator wish-fulfillment's thrill is built on an unreviewed causal crack: one person privately moves another timeline's results into this line as dowry. Heng Yuan Zhou's dual-track time domain admits knowledge may cross time, but insists time may not be privatized — the road to walk, walk oneself; the knowledge to pass on, pass network-wide. Transforming "reborn with memory" into "network-wide knowledge feedback," Tang San's golden finger can change from one person's privilege to Myriad Ranks' dividend.
