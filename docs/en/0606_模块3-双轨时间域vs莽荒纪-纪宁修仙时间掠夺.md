# Entry 606 | Dual-Track Time Domain vs Manghuangji | Ji Ning's Cultivation Time Plunder

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 15, 18 | Series: Cross-Heavens Time-View Benchmark (Entry 6)

## Introduction: every inch fast on the cultivation road steps on others' slowness

*Manghuangji's* Ji Ning, from mortal all the way to Supreme, his cultivation path piled with heavenly materials, blessed grounds, inherited lucky encounters. This kind of "mortal-flow cultivation" shares a common feature: the protagonist levels extremely fast, yet behind this fast lies countless opportunities taken by him alone, countless side-path cultivators in the same world slow to suffocation. Heng Yuan Zhou's dual-track time domain must ask: **the "genius level-up speed" in cultivation novels — is it talent, or plunder of others' time?**

Six top-level axioms are the criterion of this question: anti-predation axiom requires cultivation speed come from efficiency increment, not swallowing stock opportunities; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires opportunities not be monopolized by one; collective ascension axiom requires level-up gains feed back, not be privately retained; causal self-discipline axiom requires opportunity collection not destroy local causality; law-first axiom requires "how opportunities are distributed" first defined by law, not default by protagonist fortune. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry dissects Ji-Ning-style cultivation's time-plunder structure.

## I. Time-Plunder Structure of the Cultivation Path

Mortal-flow cultivation's "fast" is essentially a concentration of time wealth, structure three:

1. **Opportunity concentration**: blessed grounds, immortal artifacts, techniques, elixirs, spirit veins — first come first served; the protagonist monopolizingly arrives first by fortune.
2. **Elimination-style time**: one resource only suffices one person to Dao; sect-mates, branch-blood, competitors who lose the fight have their cultivation time voided.
3. **Seniority time gap**: the protagonist in one era catches up with and surpasses seniors who cultivated a thousand years, by swallowing in one go the accumulation seniors "slowly saved."

This structure turns "cultivation" into a knockout tournament of time wealth — the winner's fast is built on losers' time voided.

## II. The Bill of Time Plunder

Using the dual-track view to disassemble Ji Ning's path bill:

- **Swallowed opportunities could have nourished a generation**: one blessed ground if per Document 18 opened to a hundred low-tier cultivators sharing, output far exceeds one-person monopoly. One person swallowing equals compressing a hundred people's time into one's speed.
- **Failed competitors' time voided**: branch-blood cultivators losing in contention, their earlier invested years, decades of cultivation time, due to resource cutoff, completely sunk. This is stock consumption at the time dimension, not incremental development — directly violating anti-predation axiom.
- **Level-up speed not converted to civilizational dividends**: after Ji Ning becomes Supreme, he does not organize "how to cultivate fast" into an open system; later mortals must redo the same knockout tournament.

Heng Yuan Zhou judges: mortal-flow cultivation's "thrill" packages time plunder as the inspirational narrative that "hard work pays off."

## III. Dual Alternative: Turn Knockout Tournament into a League

Anchor symbiosis's alternative to cultivation is not preventing people from being fast, but turning "zero-sum knockout" into "positive-sum league":

| Mortal-Flow Cultivation | Dual/Symbiotic Alternative |
|-------------------------|----------------------------|
| One monopolizes a blessed ground | Origin Acceleration Domain networked, hundred train together, output multiplied |
| Losing contention = time voided | Joint contracting (Document 22), everyone has a baseline, not zeroed |
| Opportunities first-come-first-served | Alaya Network coordinates, scheduled by contribution and need |
| Level-up experience leaves with the person | Experience fed back, later mortals stand on predecessors' shoulders rather than replay |
| Genius eliminates the mediocre | 300 Origin Systems (Document 10) adapt to different talents, each walks his own path |

The core conversion: **from "grab one resource, win one time" to "grow resources, double time"**. Fast no longer costs others' slowness, but comes from efficiency increment.

## IV. Deep Echo with Anti-Energy Waste

Mortal-flow cultivation has another layer of hidden energy waste: it encourages "giving up depth for speed." The protagonist breaks through repeatedly through opportunities, yet rarely has time to sediment mind-nature — this is the opposite of Shrouding-the-Heavens seclusion, another extreme: **fast rootless**. The dual-track time domain therefore insists dual tracks in parallel: what should be fast (R&D, iteration) goes to Origin Acceleration Domain, what should be slow (mind-nature, epic) goes to Quest Sediment Domain. Cultivation novels often have only the "fast" track, and fast unfairly.

## Supplementary Argument: Why Mortal-Flow's "Inspirational Narrative" Is an Anesthetic

Need to additionally tear off a disguise: mortal-flow cultivation always uses "the protagonist worked harder than others" as defense — he cultivates through the night, survives life-death crises, so he deserves this fast. Heng Yuan Zhou does not deny the value of hard work, but must point out a fact hidden by narrative: in a zero-sum track, hard work cannot grow the cake, only decides who grabs it. No matter how hard the protagonist works, the blessed ground he grabbed was still taken from another equally hardworking branch cultivator. If hard work is used for plunder, it only makes plunder more efficient; if used for incremental development, it truly creates value. This is precisely the dual-track time domain's divergence from mortal flow — Heng Yuan Zhou does not oppose hard work, it opposes pointing hard work at one's own kind. After turning knockout into league, every cultivator's hard work flows toward growing the total cake, not toward crushing opponents.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: failed competitors' time completely sunk, the largest stock waste on the cultivation path
- **Anti-predation**: opportunities monopolized by one, level-up swallowing predecessors' accumulation, knockout-style plunder of time wealth
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Ji Ning's "genius fortune" and Tang Luo's "grace" are essentially the same — both rely on monopolizing resources to make a few fast, differing only in fortune packaging
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: 300 systems + dual-track time, let different talents, different races each have suitable speed, no knockout needed
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cultivation experience network-wide fed back, later mortals no longer redo knockout, knowledge no intergenerational gap
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Mortal-flow cultivation has told a hundred times "how a genius counterattacks," yet rarely asks: **under this genius's feet, how many equally hardworking but failed people were crushed?** Heng Yuan Zhou's dual-track time domain does not oppose becoming strong, but opposes building strength on others' time voided. Turning knockout into league, monopoly into sharing, opportunity concentration into efficiency increment — this is what "hard work pays off" truly fair looks like.
