# Entry 607 | Dual-Track Time Domain vs Infinite Terror | the Main God Space's Time Harvesting

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 89, 04, 48 | Series: Cross-Heavens Time-View Benchmark (Entry 7)

## Introduction: throwing people into world after world to grind monsters and level up — who is harvesting time

*Infinite Terror's* "Main God Space" is a bizarre device: pulling real people into movie/novel worlds, trading life-death missions for reward points, then exchanging points for strength. On the surface, this is a "voluntary participation, distribution by labor" trial system — quite similar to Heng Yuan Zhou's Quest Sediment Domain. But the dual-track time domain must ask: **behind this seemingly fair loop, who is harvesting participants' time?**

Six top-level axioms are the criterion of this question: anti-predation axiom requires trial output not be skimmed/plundered by device operators; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires participation qualification and exit freedom; collective ascension axiom requires trial gains feed back to participants, not feed the device; causal self-discipline axiom requires cross-world traversal causal chain reported end-to-end; law-first axiom requires "why must life-death be staked" explained by law. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry dissects the Main God Space's time-harvesting structure.

## I. Time Structure of the Main God Space

The Main God Space is a designed "time processing plant," structure three:

1. **Forced entry**: participants are inexplicably pulled in, no negotiation, no exit right, life-death missions immediately begin.
2. **Instance timer**: each world instance has a strict time limit; participants must complete missions within time, else erased. Time pressure used as beast-taming.
3. **Reward skim**: reward points participants fight for their lives to earn, a portion settles in the Main God's hands as "exchange rates," "space maintenance fees," etc.; the Main God's true purpose never transparent.

This is a loop driven by time fear: the harder you fight, the stronger it grows, and you never know what it wants your time for.

## II. Mechanism of Time Harvesting

Heng Yuan Zhou uses two knives — causality and institution — to disassemble the Main God Space:

- **Exit right missing**: Quest Sediment Domain participants "voluntarily enter + may exit anytime" (Entry 501), while Main God Space participants are "forced entry + erasure threat." Presence or absence of exit right is the dividing line between civilizational trial and prison.
- **Goal opaque**: why the Main God exists, what it does with collected time and life, participants have no right to know. This violates law-first axiom — a civilizational-level device's operating purpose must be public and auditable.
- **Life-death as billing unit**: directly exchanging life risk for reward points equals reducing "people" to time currency. This fundamentally conflicts with Document 19 cultivation ethics' "people are not resources."

Heng Yuan Zhou judges: the Main God Space is the Quest Sediment Domain's **dark mirror** — similar in form (cross-world trials), opposite in law (one is public education, the other private harvesting).

## III. Dual/Quest-Domain Alternative: Transparent, Voluntary, Exitable

Anchor symbiosis does not oppose "cross-world trials" — the Quest Sediment Domain heavily uses simulation replays and isolated-space trials. The difference is in the institutional skeleton:

| Main God Space | Quest Sediment Domain |
|----------------|-----------------------|
| Forced entry, erasure threat | Voluntary entry, may exit anytime |
| Goal opaque, Main God privately retains | Purpose public, Alaya records throughout |
| Life-death for rewards, people become currency | Trials have safety nets, risks controlled and repairable |
| Skim feeds the device | Outputs feed back to Myriad Ranks, no one privately retains |
| No judicial remedy | Symbiotic court may appeal (Document 48), disputes adjudicated |

The core replacement: **change "being forced by an unknown entity to fight for your life" into "a public trial Myriad Ranks voluntarily join, transparently participate, may exit anytime"**. Trials may be thrilling, but must not be a prison.

## IV. Echo with Time-Paradox Prevention

The Main God Space's cross-world traversal is also a time-parox time bomb: participants carry one world's memories and items into the next, causal chains string across countless instances, yet no causal self-discipline mechanism controls them. Document 89's time-parox prevention requires: any cross-timeline operation must first observe, leave traces, be rollback-able. The Main God Space has precisely none of the three — it is not a trial device, but an unsupervised causal blender.

## Supplementary Argument: the Ethical Bottom Line of Cross-World Trials Is "Exit Right"

It is worth emphasizing that Heng Yuan Zhou does not oppose "growth under life-death pressure" itself — Quest Domain trials also have tension, have choice costs. The true dividing line is not "whether to fear death," but "whether one may leave." The Main God Space pulls exit rights out by the roots; trials degenerate from education to enslavement. The Quest Domain welds the exit anchor firmly in everyone's consciousness; trials always remain education. A person may sweat and weep in trials, but must always know "this is my choice, I may stop anytime." This ethical bottom line is the only real wall between civilizational trial and spiritual prison — once the wall falls, no matter how exciting the instance, it is a prison holding souls.

## Brief Transition

By contrast, the Quest Sediment Domain is safe precisely because it makes public all the Main God Space hid: trial purpose, risks, exit rights, feedback channels, all recorded on the Alaya Network, guarded by multi-layer supervision. The Origin Acceleration Domain and Quest Sediment Domain's every cross-world activity leaves traces on the Alaya Network, never letting participants face a black box.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: participants' life-fighting time is skimmed to feed an unknown device; massive life consumed in loops with no civilizational gain
- **Anti-predation**: forced entry + life-death exchange + opaque skim, institutional time harvesting
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Main God Space is more naked than Tang Luo — Tang Luo at least openly takes disciples; the Main God hides even its purpose, a "righteous rogue"-style harvest
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Quest Domain voluntarily open to Myriad Ranks; no one is forcibly pulled in, exit right sacred
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cross-world trial procedures public; any merged plane may接入 a transparent version of trials, not wait for its own "Main God"
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Main God Space proved one thing: **the form of cross-world trials, used well is the Quest Sediment Domain, used badly is a time harvester**. The difference is not "whether people bleed and sweat," but "who is present, for whom, whether one may exit." Heng Yuan Zhou's dual-track time domain insists: trials may be thrilling, institutions must be transparent; time may be spent in epics, not in prisons. Forcing the Main God Space offstage, returning trial sovereignty to participants — this is what cross-world time should look like.
