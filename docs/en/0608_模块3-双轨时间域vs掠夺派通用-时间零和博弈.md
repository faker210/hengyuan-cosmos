# Entry 608 | Dual-Track Time Domain vs Predatory Faction Generic | Time-as-Resource Zero-Sum Game

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 01, 15 | Series: Cross-Heavens Time-View Benchmark (Entry 8 · Conclusion)

## Introduction: when time is treated as the last remaining stock to grab

From Tang Luo to Shrouding the Heavens, from Perfect World to Douluo, from Dragon Ball to Manghuangji, to Infinite Terror, the preceding seven benchmarks point to the same underlying assumption — **in predatory-faction worlds, time itself is a zero-sum stock**: your fast is my slow; your opportunity is my loss; the extra hundred years you live is the hundred years I was squeezed off the time track. This entry, as the cross-heavens time-view benchmark series' conclusion, fully lays out this underlying assumption, and argues how the dual-track time domain fundamentally abolishes "time zero-sum."

Six top-level axioms are this conclusion's overall criterion: anti-predation axiom directly targets "develop increment, do not consume stock"; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires evolution qualifications not be mutually exclusive; collective ascension axiom requires time investment be made positive-sum; causal self-discipline axiom requires time scheduling not create zero-sum causality; law-first axiom requires "whether time is finite" first argued by law, not default by zero-sum intuition. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Predatory Faction's Time Zero-Sum Axiom

Predatory-faction worldviews share an unspoken axiom: **total time fixed, the more strong ones, the less each gets**. From this, a whole set of "reasonable" behaviors follows:

1. **Grab time = get strong**: seize others' lifespans, swallow others' opportunities, occupy others' blessed grounds, equals directly seizing the other's time.
2. **Elimination = purification**: losers' time voided is seen as "heavenly Dao's choice," the price of evolution.
3. **Monopoly = justice**: whoever is strong occupies, natural; the weak being slow is deserved.

Once this axiom stands, all cruelty is rationalized. Heng Yuan Zhou's work is to prove this axiom **wrong**.

## II. Why Zero-Sum Is Wrong: Time May Be "Grown"

Anti-predation axiom's (Document 04) core is: **develop increment, do not consume stock**. Time in the dual-track system is precisely the resource that best embodies this:

- **Efficiency may be multiplied**: the Origin Acceleration Domain relies on 100× deduction amplification (Document 16), amplifying the same wall-clock time's output a hundredfold — no more time spent, but "effective time" grown. This is increment, not zero-sum.
- **Outputs may be shared**: techniques and technologies practiced in the acceleration domain enter the Alaya Network network-wide open; one person practices = Myriad Ranks benefit. Knowledge's copy cost is zero, naturally positive-sum.
- **Depth irreplaceable but feedback-able**: Quest Sediment Domain trials are personal experience, non-transferable, but their lessons may be abstracted into knowledge feedback — detours you walked, later-comers need not re-walk.

Conclusion: **time is not a cake, cut one piece, one less; time is more like fire, lighting one illuminates the whole room**. The predatory faction mistakenly treats time as stock; the symbiotic faction treats time as increment — this is the two factions' fundamental divergence at the time dimension.

## III. Civilizational Consequences of Zero-Sum Game

As long as "time zero-sum" holds, civilization necessarily walks three dead ends:

1. **Class solidification**: first-time occupiers permanently lead; later-comers never get a chance — conflicts with Myriad-Rank Republican axiom.
2. **Knowledge gap between generations**: time and opportunities buried with the strong; civilization repeatedly starts from zero — conflicts with collective-ascension axiom.
3. **Strong-one loneliness**: to guard grabbed time, the strong must be wary of everyone, ultimately alone-deciding eternity or lone-path seclusion — conflicts with experience-sense preservation.

These three are precisely the lesions the preceding seven entries saw in Tang Luo, Shrouding the Heavens, Perfect World, etc. Zero-sum is not some work's bug, but the predatory-faction worldview's underlying virus.

## IV. How the Dual Track Fundamentally Abolishes Zero-Sum

The dual-track time domain's institutional design everywhere is "anti-zero-sum":

| Zero-Sum Predation Logic | Dual-Track Symbiosis Logic |
|--------------------------|----------------------------|
| Grab others' time for own use | Efficiency increment grows total time (Origin Acceleration Domain) |
| Failure = time voided | Joint contracting, everyone has a baseline not zeroed |
| Opportunities monopolized | Alaya Network coordinates, scheduled by contribution |
| Knowledge leaves with the person | Knowledge network-wide fed back, later-comers stand on predecessors' shoulders |
| Strong ones guard time in loneliness | Myriad Ranks share time dividends, collective ascension |

In one sentence: **the dual track is not "more fairly dividing that fixed cake," but "growing the cake, and proving it can be infinitely grown"**.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: zero-sum games waste massive time on mutual contention and elimination voiding; the dual track turns this time into incremental R&D
- **Anti-predation**: fundamentally denies "grab time = get strong," directs time use toward incremental development rather than stock plunder
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system is precisely "time zero-sum" typical — inclusion stops at the walls; the dual track proves time may be positive-sum, inclusion may be boundless
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: after time increment grows, 99% of Myriad Ranks benefit, no need to evolve by eliminating own kind
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the positive-sum time model may infinitely expand; any newly merged plane may接入 incremental time economy, not be dragged into zero-sum jungle
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The deepest fear in predatory-faction worlds is "time not enough to go around." So they grab, they kill, they squeeze their own kind off the track. Heng Yuan Zhou's dual-track time domain answers this fear with engineering: time is enough to go around — not by alms, but by efficiency growing it. When "your fast no longer equals my slow," predation's underlying logic collapses by half. This is why Module Three spends a full 33 entries on time: **whoever controls time's allocation method controls civilization's master switch of symbiosis or plunder.**
