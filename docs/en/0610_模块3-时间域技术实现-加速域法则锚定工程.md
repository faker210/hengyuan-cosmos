# Entry 610 | Time-Domain Technical Implementation | Acceleration-Domain Law-Anchoring Engineering

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 13, 64, 27 | Series: Time-Domain Technical Implementation (Entry 2)

## Introduction: how is the wall of 100× flow built

Once the Origin Acceleration Domain opens flow to 100×, one year inside, one day outside. This raises a fatal engineering question: **why doesn't the wall between this year and this day collapse?** Will someone rushing out from inside sweep outside causality along? Will outside natural disasters instantly flood into the 100×-flow domain? The answer is "law-anchoring engineering" — a time interface built out of the law network. This entry tells how this wall is built, tested, repaired.

Six top-level axioms run through the engineering: anti-predation axiom requires this wall only isolate causal risk, not knowledge feedback; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires wall space open by contribution, not privately occupied by the strong; collective ascension axiom requires wall maintenance shared by the whole network; causal self-discipline axiom requires the wall "isolate pollution," not "cut off contact"; law-first axiom requires wall thickness, leakage rate, lifespan all have law-level definitions, not by feel. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Three-Layer Wall of Law Anchoring

The Origin Acceleration Domain's boundary is not one membrane, but three nested walls:

1. **Spatial-jurisprudence wall**: based on the spatial jurisprudence scheduling system (Document 13) and isolated-space construction technology (Document 64), physically separating the acceleration domain from the real plane. Outermost, responsible for "matter" isolation.
2. **Time-flow wall**: inside the spatial wall, calibrate subjective/objective time multipliers, lock the inside-outside time interface. Middle layer, responsible for "time" isolation.
3. **Causal-filter wall**: innermost, only letting abstract knowledge pass (Document 87), intercepting concrete causal disturbances. Most critical layer, responsible for "fruit" filtering.

Three walls layer by layer: matter not mixed, time not cross, fruit not disturbed.

## II. Anchor-Node Deployment and Calibration

Law anchoring is not just drawing a circle, but jointly maintained by several "anchor nodes." Engineering points:

- **Node distribution**: anchor nodes uniformly deployed along the acceleration-domain boundary, spacing optimized by law-network simulation, eliminating boundary weak points.
- **Resonance calibration**: each node must maintain resonance sync with the law network; deviation over threshold triggers calibration (Document 27). This is why the Anchor Supervisory Committee periodically inspects — once anchoring drifts, 100× flow may "leak" a corner on some side.
- **Redundancy design**: key nodes equipped with backup nodes; single-point failure does not collapse the whole wall. This is engineering reliability's bottom line.

## III. Leakage Rate: An Engineering Indicator That Must Be Public

Law-anchoring engineering has an honest indicator — **leakage rate**, i.e., the proportion of outside causality seeping inside, or inside causality leaking outside. The engineering goal is not zero leakage (legally uneconomical), but pressing leakage within causal self-discipline axiom's acceptable threshold, and making this threshold public:

| Leakage Direction | Allowable Threshold | Consequence of Overrun |
|-------------------|---------------------|------------------------|
| Outside→Inside (disaster seepage) | <0.1% | Domain-internal R&D disturbed, isolation reinforcement started |
| Inside→Outside (causal leakage) | <0.01% | Immediately slow down, Alaya retrospective tracing |
| Knowledge feedback (expected pass) | 100% abstracted | Not intercepted, only desensitized |

Writing leakage rate into engineering indicators is law-first axiom's expression: **not "should be fine," but measurable, publishable numbers**.

## IV. Technical Interface with "Only-Knowledge-Returned"

The causal-filter wall's core technology is engineeringizing Document 87's "only-knowledge-returned principle." Everything produced inside the acceleration domain — techniques, data, emotional impact — is "desensitized" when crossing the causal-filter wall: concrete people, events, causal traces stripped, only abstract knowledge and patterns retained. Technically, this is done through the Alaya Network's semantic layering interface: knowledge layer passes, causal layer intercepts, emotional layer converted into safely digestible teaching cases. If this interface is poorly designed, the acceleration domain degenerates into another "Main God Space" (Entry 607).

## Supplementary Argument: The Philosophy of Boundary Engineering — Isolation ≠ Closure

Law-anchoring engineering is often misunderstood as "sealing the acceleration domain into an iron bucket." This is a misunderstanding. The three walls' purpose is not cutting inside-outside contact, but precisely controlling what may pass and what may not. Matter not mixed, time not cross, causality not disturbed — but knowledge may pass, experience may pass, distress signals may pass. A truly good isolation wall is not an airtight wall, but a wall that selectively breathes — it blocks pollution, lets value through. This "controlled permeability" is precisely the spatial jurisprudence scheduling system's (Document 13) essence: isolation is for safer opening, not for turning the acceleration domain into another privately hidden secret realm. Boundary's art lies in letting "fast" and "real" neither collide nor be separated.

## Brief Transition

It is worth adding that law-anchoring engineering not only guards the Origin Acceleration Domain, the Quest Sediment Domain also needs its slow isolation wall — both share the same set of anchor nodes and leakage-rate standards, only knob direction reversed. The fast and slow domains, one wall, same source.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: three walls configured by risk tier, not wasting top-tier anchoring in low-risk areas
- **Anti-predation**: causal-filter wall ensures domain-internal R&D results flow back as knowledge, not fenced into private retention
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's acceleration zone glosses over with "secret realm barriers"; Heng Yuan Zhou's boundary has leakage-rate indicators to check
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: wall specs adapt to different lineages — consciousness-information race's wall leans causal filtration, carbon-based race's leans physical isolation
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: anchoring parameters, leakage thresholds, calibration procedures all public; new planes may self-build acceleration domains by the same engineering standard
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

100× flow is safe not because protagonist halo shields it, but because a measurable, calibratable, repairable law wall steadily catches it. The Origin Acceleration Domain's true technical content is not "fast" itself, but "after going fast, not causing trouble." Law-anchoring engineering is the safety net that lets civilization dare to speed up.
