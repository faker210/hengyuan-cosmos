A cultivator spent one real hour in the Origin Acceleration Domain (100 hours inside), task complete, preparing to return to the real plane. This step out, his consciousness still sits in "100× time sense," but his body must adapt to normal time — this "time-difference return" is the most dangerous link in time-domain engineering. ## I. Three Basic Switch Types

Switching within the dual system, technically three types:

1. **Domain→Reality**: from acceleration or Quest domain back to normal plane. Most common, also most needing "time-difference shock" prevention.
2. **Acceleration↔Quest**: fast-track results enter slow-track testing, slow-track insights enter fast-track deduction. This is the dual-track closed loop's key channel.
3. **Same-gear different-domain**: migrating between two acceleration domains of same flow, lowest risk.

Three switch types share one "interface protocol," differing only in time-difference magnitude and causal coupling.

## II. Time-Difference Buffer Zone

Switching engineering's core is the **buffer zone** — a transitional space between two domains, letting subjective/objective time "align" in it, avoiding hard-switching tearing. Design:

- **Time gradient method**: buffer-zone flow smoothly transitions from source-domain multiplier to target-domain multiplier, not instant jump.
- **Consciousness re-anchoring**: participant's exit anchor (Entry 611) re-calibrates in the buffer zone, only released after confirming consciousness and body baseline aligned.
- **Causal reconciliation**: at switching instant do a "causal account" — verify source-domain outputs have been desensitized and fed back per protocol, no unprocessed causal residue before release.

Hard switching without buffer zone equals letting a person slam from 100× speed directly into normal; light symptoms vertigo and confusion, severe causal tearing.

## III. Switch Queues and Scheduling

When large numbers of cultivators simultaneously apply for switching, the spatial jurisprudence scheduling system (Document 13) does queue management. Scheduling principles:

| Scheduling Priority | Type | Explanation |

Scheduling itself follows joint-contracting logic — emergency tasks first, no strong-ones-cutting-line. This turns "switch right" from privilege into public service.

## IV. Interface with Cross-Plane Communication

Switching's other technical interface is cross-plane communication (Document 66). When cultivators inside the acceleration domain need to collaborate with the real plane, communication must pass causal filtration: **transmit information only, not time disturbance**. Technically, switching channels and communication channels share the same causal-desensitization layer — ensuring "inside speaks, outside listens," yet communication does not pull outside causality into 100× speed. ## Supplementary Argument: Switching Is the Dual Track's "Joint," Not "Door Crack"

A common misunderstanding: switching channel is just "in-out door," size doesn't matter. Engineering-wise this is wrong. The switching channel is the dual-track system's joint — all fast-track and slow-track matter, consciousness, knowledge exchange must pass here. If the joint wears, the whole dual track stiffens; if the joint leaks, the two tracks' causality mutually penetrates. Therefore switching engineering's precision requirement is no lower than the acceleration domain itself. Buffer-zone time gradient, consciousness re-anchoring, causal reconciliation — three procedures all indispensable, precisely to make this joint both flexible and tight. Designs treating switching as a door crack will ultimately leak causal chaos through the crack — this is engineering common sense bought with countless failed secret realms' lessons.
