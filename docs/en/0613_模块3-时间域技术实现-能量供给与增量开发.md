# Entry 613 | Time-Domain Technical Implementation | Time-Domain Energy Supply and Incremental Development

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 15, 24, 65 | Series: Time-Domain Technical Implementation (Entry 5)

## Introduction: who pays the electricity bill for 100× flow

The Origin Acceleration Domain opening 100× flow is essentially "borrowing" compute from the law network. What is borrowed must be returned — this is the time domain's energy-supply problem. Heng Yuan Zhou refuses to write acceleration as "innate aura comes with it"; per anti-predation axiom, **energy consumed by acceleration must come from incremental development, not drawing Myriad Ranks' stock**. This entry tells the time domain's energy bill: where electricity comes from, how it is metered, how to ensure "the more used, the more" rather than "the more used, the less."

Six top-level axioms are the energy engineering's criterion: anti-predation axiom directly requires energy source be incremental; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires energy consumption not passed on as Myriad-Rank burden; collective ascension axiom requires the energy system grow stronger with use; causal self-discipline axiom requires large-scale energy consumption not disturb local ecology; law-first axiom requires energy models recalculable, auditable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Three Energy Bills of the Time Domain

Maintaining dual-track time-domain operation consumes three categories of energy:

1. **Law-resonance energy**: law-network resonance share needed to maintain time-flow law anchoring, the largest.
2. **Consciousness-compute energy**: supporting high-speed operation of individual consciousness inside the acceleration domain, same source as Multiplication Fruit (Document 16).
3. **Isolated-space energy**: spatial-jurisprudence energy maintaining isolated-space boundaries (Entry 610).

Three bills combined constitute one "acceleration-domain consciousness-day" standard energy consumption. This consumption is not a black hole — it must be covered by an incremental energy system.

## II. Incremental Energy: Not Drawing Stock, Developing Increment

Heng Yuan Zhou's energy principle (Document 15) is "Myriad-Rank origin bidirectional cycle": energy gains within the cycle, not drawn from elsewhere. The time domain's energy supply is designed accordingly:

- **Origin-cycle amplification**: the acceleration domain itself produces techniques, gongfu, efficiency during operation — these outputs after feeding back to Myriad Ranks raise the whole civilization's energy-utilization efficiency, in turn feeding back time-domain energy consumption. This is the positive cycle of "using energy to build energy."
- **Incremental energy-level development**: through energy standardization and metering (Document 65), collect and utilize previously dissipated, unused free-floating energy, newly adding distributable energy, not dividing Myriad Ranks' stock cake.
- **Joint-contracting billing**: entrants billed by consumption, billing enters symbiotic-network redistribution (Document 22), ensuring energy consumption has clear economic subjects, not unconsciously borne by all.

Core: **the more the time domain is used, the more abundant civilization's total energy becomes** — this is incremental development's essential difference from predatory extraction.

## III. Load Ceiling and "Acceleration Inflation" Protection

Energy engineering must answer "at most how many times may be opened." The law network's resonance share has a physical ceiling; over it overload. Heng Yuan Zhou's load management:

| Load Band | State | Measures |
|-----------|-------|----------|
| <60% | Ample | Normal open, may accept new applications |
| 60%–85% | Tight | Start priority scheduling, personal cultivation yields to civilizational tasks |
| 85%–95% | Stressed | Freeze non-emergency acceleration applications, guide off-peak |
| >95% | Dangerous | Force slowdown, protect law network from collapse |

This load line blocks the "want to accelerate" desire below the physical ceiling, preventing "acceleration inflation" (Entry 504) from evolving into law-network overload accident.

## IV. Energy Audit and Transparency

Per Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision requirements, the time domain's energy收支 is accounted throughout:

- **Every share of resonance** used where, producing what, queryable and traceable.
- **Incremental energy's amplification rate** periodically published — proving the time domain truly "grows richer with use," not hidden blood-sucking.
- **Anchor Supervisory Committee** (see Entry 620) audits abnormal energy consumption, preventing someone using "R&D" as cover to "extract Myriad-Rank energy."

Energy transparency is anti-predation's last engineering gate: **as long as the books are visible, plunder cannot hide**.

## Supplementary Argument: Energy Self-Repayment Is the Confidence of "Civilization Dares to Be Fast"

The dual-track time domain has an often-overlooked economic precondition: it must be self-repaying. By self-repaying, the civilizational gain produced by acceleration-domain operation, after accounting, must be greater than the law-resonance energy it consumes. Once self-repayment is broken — e.g., opening thousand-speed gear for personal thrill with no output — the time domain degenerates from "civilizational investment" into "civilizational transfusion." Heng Yuan Zhou uses load lines, input-output tables, contribution billing triple mechanisms to hold the self-repayment line, essentially putting an economic gate on "fast": fast must make money, at least earn back its own electricity. This gate looks cold, yet is the root reason the time domain may operate long-term without being supported by Myriad Ranks.

## Brief Transition

The energy system must also reserve load for the Quest Sediment Domain — though the slow domain does not pursue high multiples, its consciousness immersion and instance operation also consume energy. Origin Acceleration Domain and Quest Sediment Domain energy consumption uniformly counted on one bill, audited by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision, ensuring incremental amplification benefits both tracks.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: load lines and billing ensure every bit of resonance energy spent where civilizational returns are high, eliminating idling
- **Anti-predation**: energy only from incremental development and cycle amplification, never drawing Myriad Ranks' stock, bills fully auditable
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's acceleration relies on spirit stones/heavenly treasures stock consumption, the more used the less; Heng Yuan Zhou relies on incremental development, the more used the more
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: incremental energy amplification benefits Myriad Ranks; time-domain energy consumption not passed on as some clan's burden
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: energy metering, load models, amplification algorithms public; newly merged planes may接入 the same incremental energy system
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

100× flow is not free, but it can be "self-repaying" — relying on output feedback, incremental amplification, audit transparency, the time domain turns expensive energy engineering into a civilization machine that grows richer with rotation. As long as energy comes from increment not stock, as long as bills are public throughout, "opening acceleration" is no longer plunder, but civilization's investment in itself.
