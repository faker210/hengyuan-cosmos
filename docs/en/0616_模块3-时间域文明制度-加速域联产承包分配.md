# Entry 616 | Time Domain and Civilizational Institutions | Joint-Contracting Allocation of Acceleration-Domain Resources

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 22, 06, 50 | Series: Time Domain and Civilizational Institutions (Entry 1)

## Introduction: to whom should 100× acceleration time be issued

The Origin Acceleration Domain is a scarce resource — law-resonance share finite, isolated space finite, consciousness compute finite. So an institutional question is unavoidable: **who qualifies to use, how much, on what basis more work more gain**. If this question is answered poorly, the acceleration domain degenerates into a Tang-Luo-style grace field (Entry 601). Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is directly applying the joint-contracting mechanism (Document 22) to time resources. This entry tells how acceleration-domain time is allocated by "contribution," not by "identity."

Six top-level axioms are the allocation institution's constitution: anti-predation axiom requires allocation not rely on plundering others' contribution; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires allocation equally open to Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires allocation direct toward civilizational increment; causal self-discipline axiom requires allocation process traceable and auditable; law-first axiom requires allocation rules stable, recalculable, not临时拍板. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Allocation Currency: Civilizational Contribution

Acceleration-domain time is not issued as "welfare," only as "contribution-redeemable vouchers." Cultivators accumulate contribution in civilizational building (R&D, teaching, rescue, engineering), redeem acceleration time by contribution. This design directly comes from the more-work-more-gain principle (Document 06):

- **More work more gain**: the more contribution, the more acceleration time redeemable, capped but not discriminatory.
- **Birth-blind**: contribution only recognizes actual contribution, not bloodline, race, sect.
- **Non-predatory**: contribution may only be accumulated by real labor; plundering others' contribution is institutionally infeasible (Alaya records throughout).

Allocation's underlying logic thus shifts from "who deserves it" to "who created what."

## II. Three-Layer Allocation Rules

Joint contracting landing in the acceleration domain has three rules:

| Rule | Content | Purpose |
|------|---------|---------|
| Baseline Quota | Each activated individual's yearly minimum acceleration time | Guarantee everyone has a way, not excluded |
| Contribution Redemption | Beyond baseline, redeem by contribution | Incentivize more work, more work more gain |
| Priority Scheduling | Civilizational emergency tasks outrank personal cultivation | Prevent individuals crowding out public emergency resources |

Three layers stacked: **baseline for all, overage by contribution, emergency yields to public**. This avoids both "big-pot-rice" waste and "jungle" plunder.

## III. Institutional Gates Against "Acceleration Inflation"

Time resources are special — opening too fast triggers inflation (Entry 504). The joint-contracting institution sets three gates for this:

1. **Annual cap**: individual redeemable acceleration time has an annual ceiling, preventing a few from redeeming too much causing a run.
2. **Cooling period**: after high-multiple acceleration mandatory cooling; during cooling may not redeem high-multiple gear again, preventing "involution-style acceleration."
3. **Load linkage**: when law-network load is high (Entry 613), personal redemption automatically downgrades, prioritizing civilizational tasks.

Three gates ensure acceleration-domain resources always a public reservoir, not a private pond squeezed dry by the powerful.

## IV. Myriad-Rank Conversion Fairness

Myriad-Rank lineages (Document 50) have different time units — a plant spirit's year is not a mechanical race's year. Joint contracting therefore needs a **conversion model**:

- For consciousness-information race, contribution converted by consciousness-compute contribution.
- For plant spirits, planet consciousness, converted by their stable contribution to ecology/law network, with dedicated time-unit conversion.
- Conversion model public, any clan may appeal, calibrate.

Allocation fairness lies not only in "everyone can redeem," but in "for different races, redeemed reasonably."

## Supplementary Argument: Between Baseline and Cap Is Space of Trust

Joint contracting looks just like dividing money and time; actually behind it is a civilizational-trust design. Baseline quota guarantees everyone a share, trusting Myriad Ranks will not give up; annual cap and cooling prevent overdraft, trusting people will be rational; priority yielding to public emergency, trusting individuals will yield for the collective. This design neither assumes human nature evil (thus not guarding people as thieves) nor assumes human nature good (thus setting caps against greed). It turns "how to divide time" from a moral question into an institutional question that can run with trust. Heng Yuan Zhou believes: as long as rules transparent, more work more gain, Myriad Ranks will use time in the right place — this institutional confidence is joint-contracting's soul.

## Brief Transition

Allocation institutions not only govern the Origin Acceleration Domain, the Quest Sediment Domain's course quotas are likewise allocated by contribution and education-guarantee law. All allocation data accounted through Alaya Network, audited by multi-layer supervision, ensuring both acceleration and Quest tracks are not crowded out by the powerful.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: baseline quota + contribution redemption, direct acceleration time toward those truly more work, truly efficient; neither feeding idlers nor runs
- **Anti-predation**: contribution only accumulated by real labor; plundering others' contribution is institutionally infeasible, cannot hide
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo issues acceleration qualification by sect bloodline; Heng Yuan Zhou redeems by contribution; birth no longer determines flow
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five lineages each have conversion models; plant spirits and planet consciousness also fairly redeem acceleration time
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: allocation rules, conversion models, gate algorithms public; newly merged planes may接入 by the same joint-contracting system
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The fairest way to divide acceleration-domain time is neither "equal for all" nor "strong ones eat all," but "baseline for all, overage by contribution." Joint contracting turns time resources from identity grace into labor reward, making "fast" truly a civilizational builder's medal. This institution holds a bottom line: **in Heng Yuan Zhou, time does not reward birth, only contribution.**
