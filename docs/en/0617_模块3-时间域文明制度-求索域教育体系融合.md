# Entry 617 | Time Domain and Civilizational Institutions | Fusion of Quest-Domain Trials with the Education System

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 37, 43 | Series: Time Domain and Civilizational Institutions (Entry 2)

## Introduction: pack "read ten thousand books" and "travel ten thousand miles" into one curriculum

The Quest Sediment Domain's slow trials, if treated only as personal private-cultivation space, waste its greatest social value — **it could be civilization's highest classroom**. Heng Yuan Zhou's symbiotic education system (Document 37) therefore formally includes the Quest Domain in the curriculum: Youth Supremes temper mind-nature inside, adult cultivators stress-test theories inside, the whole clan debriefs old systems inside. This entry tells how the Quest Domain fuses with education institutions, making "trials" no longer isolated cultivation, but a people-cultivating project.

Six top-level axioms are the education fusion's criterion: anti-predation axiom requires trial education not swallow participants' real life; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the classroom open to all beings; collective ascension axiom requires education results feed back network-wide; causal self-discipline axiom requires trial topics pass ethical review; law-first axiom requires the curriculum system stable and reproducible. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Fusion with Three Educational Stages

The Quest Domain in the education system承担 three stages:

1. **Initiation stage · Youth Supreme trials**: Youth Supremes (Entry Module One, type 3) experience "choice and cost" in controlled Quest-Domain instances, building first somatic feel of causality, responsibility. Instance difficulty graded, with mentors remotely陪伴.
2. **Advanced stage · Theory stress test**: cultivators put gongfu/theories learned in the Origin Acceleration Domain into the Quest Domain for slow real testing — book learning is shallow after all, must have fallen in real situations to count as learned.
3. **Postgraduate stage · Old-system debrief**: adult cultivators enter the Quest Domain, debrief Tang Luo, Shrouding the Heavens, Manghuangji etc. old systems' (Module Two) failure lessons, turning historical reverse-drives into living teaching material.

Three stages upgrade the Quest Domain from "cultivation chamber" to "people-cultivating classroom."

## II. Trial-Course Design Principles

Quest-Domain courses are not just making an instance for people to grind monsters, but follow three design principles:

| Principle | Meaning | Corresponding Axiom |
|-----------|---------|---------------------|
| Cost Controllable | In trials may experience loss, but no real irreversible damage | Myriad-Rank Republicanism, Causal Self-Discipline |
| Exit Reachable | Any stage may exit by exit anchor (Entry 611) | Myriad-Rank Republicanism |
| Lessons Feed Back | Insights produced by trials desensitized then enter Alaya Network, become public teaching material | Collective Ascension |

Three principles ensure the Quest Domain is a "classroom" not an "execution ground": may make people cry, not destroy people.

## III. Interface with Education-Guarantee Law

Education-Guarantee Law (Document 43) stipulates all beings' right to education. Quest-Domain fusion therefore must satisfy:

- **Free access**: basic trial courses included in compulsory education, applicable by identity, no extra charge — trials are not luxury goods.
- **Difference adaptation**: five lineages (Document 50) customize courses by perception mode; plant spirits' "growth course" and mechanical race's "choice course" differ in form.
- **Outcome certification**: those passing Quest-Domain trials, their insights and abilities after Alaya evaluation enter personal growth files, as basis for subsequent cultivation and contribution.

The right to education here concretizes as: **every being has the right, in a safe Quest Domain, to first walk the detours life must walk**.

## IV. Youth Supremes' "Trials" Are Not Abuse

It is worth specially distinguishing: the Quest Domain's youth trials include "trials" — setbacks, loss, choices. This is not abuse, nor Tang-Luo-style "genius must endure suffering to deserve strength" toxic chicken soup. The difference:

- Tang Luo's trials are **truly cruel, no backstop**, fail to endure and die;
- The Quest Domain's trials are **designed, with safety net, exitable**, tempering mind-nature, not life.

Trials' purpose is cultivating people, not elimination. This institutional boundary is supervised and enforced by the symbiotic court (Entry 619).

## Supplementary Argument: Educationization Turns Trials from "Private Cultivation" into "Public Heritage"

If the Quest Domain were only personal private cultivation, its value dissipates with the individual's end. After inclusion in the education system, the same trial may be "experienced" by tens of thousands of later generations repeatedly — the stumbles youth made in instances for predecessors, are their own future roads. This transformation's meaning is revolutionary: trials are no longer "I live it through for myself," but "Myriad Ranks live it through for each other." Predecessors ate the bitter fruit in the Quest Domain and made it a safe instance; later generations need not pay the same tuition with their lives in reality. Civilization's education thus for the first time achieves "experience compound interest" — each generation's trials let the next stand higher, fall lighter.

- **Anti-energy waste**: Quest Domain upgrades from "private chamber" to public classroom; one trial's output可供 ten thousand to learn

- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's trials are elimination-style; Heng Yuan Zhou's trials are people-cultivating-style, tempering mind-nature not life
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five lineages each have adapted courses; right to education not discounted by race

## Conclusion

The best education is letting people safely experience a "what if." The Quest Sediment Domain turns this "what if" into civilization's public classroom: youth learn choices inside, young adults validate theories inside, elders debrief history inside. When trials are included in the education institution, they are no longer an isolated cultivator's private matter, but a furnace for Myriad Ranks to jointly refine the next generation.
