# Entry 618 | Time Domain and Civilizational Institutions | Anchor-Council Legislation for Time-Domain Use

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 49, 40, 03 | Series: Time Domain and Civilizational Institutions (Entry 3)

## Introduction: who has the right to decide "how time is used"

Technology can build the time domain, but "who may use, how much, under what circumstances use is forbidden" — these are not technical questions, but legislative questions. Heng Yuan Zhou's legislative authority rests with the Anchor Council (Document 49). This entry tells how dual-track time-domain use rules are legislated: what to legislate, how to legislate, why must legislate rather than let the strong decide.

Six top-level axioms are legislation's spiritual source: anti-predation axiom written into time-use prohibition clauses; republican axiom of Myriad Races written into admission clauses; collective ascension axiom written into feedback obligations; causal self-discipline axiom written into reporting and rollback obligations; law-first axiom requires legislation itself stable, predictable, not changed by plot. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Legislation is the process of translating axioms into enforceable articles.

## I. What to Legislate: Four Laws for Time-Domain Use

Around the time domain, the Anchor Council enacts four mutually coordinating laws:

1. **Time Admission Law**: stipulates who may enter, on what basis, baseline quota (linking Entry 616 joint contracting).
2. **Time-Flow Law**: stipulates each gear's flow ceiling, load-linkage rules, cooling periods (linking Entries 609, 613).
3. **Causal Reporting Law**: stipulates any time-domain operation must be reported, traceable, rollback-able (linking Entries 614, 615).
4. **Time-Feedback Law**: stipulates domain-internal outputs must be desensitized and fed back to Alaya Network, private retention forbidden (linking Entry 614).

Four laws form a closed loop: **admission→use→report→feedback**, every ring has law to follow.

## II. How to Legislate: Legislative Procedure

Anchor Council legislation is not closed-door brainstorming; its procedure:

| Procedure Step | Content |
|----------------|---------|
| Proposal | May be proposed by Myriad-Rank representatives, Technical Committee, Symbiotic Executive Committee |
| Deliberation | Technical Committee assesses feasibility, Alaya provides historical data support |
| Hearing | Public hearing to Myriad Ranks, especially hearing affected clans' opinions |
| Vote | Anchor Council votes by civilizational weight, weights public |
| Promulgation | After law made, network-wide published, with version records, backward-compatible |

Legislation open and transparent is law-first axiom's institutional guarantee: **rules do not临时 tilt by some strong one's one word**.

## III. Why Legislate Rather Than Rely on the Strong's Self-Discipline

Predatory-faction worlds' common disease: how time resources are used all depends on the strong's mood. Tang Luo distributes opportunities by the protagonist's will; Shrouding the Heavens opens bans by the Great Emperor's mood. Heng Yuan Zhou insists on legislation for three reasons:

1. **Predictable**: Myriad Ranks know rules are stable, dare plan their cultivation lives, not lose everything because a strong one turns hostile.
2. **Anti-kidnap**: law-first axiom requires "plot may not kidnap settings" — legislation固化 rules, any plot event has no right to临时 change law.
3. **Correctable**: laws have versions, revision procedures; found unreasonable may be amended through due process, not overturned wholesale.

## IV. Legislation and Power Checks

Time-domain legislative power rests with the Anchor Council, but not monopolized: the Symbiotic Executive Committee enforces law, the symbiotic court adjudicates (Entry 619), the Anchor Supervisory Committee audits (Entry 620). This is precisely Document 49 "governance framework and power checks" at the time dimension: **legislative, executive, judicial, supervisory four powers separated, no one may monopolize time**.

Especially important: legislators themselves using the time domain are also bound by law — no "legislator immunity."

## Supplementary Argument: Legislation Turns "Temporary Grace" into "Permanent Right"

The time domain's most dangerous degradation is rules changing by the strong's mood. Today give you acceleration qualification, tomorrow take it back because you displease them — this is precisely Tang Luo's grace system's root disease. Anchor-Council legislation's fundamental meaning is turning time from "bestowed grace" into "legally protected right." Once in law, rules do not动摇 by any strong one turning hostile; Myriad Ranks dare bet their life planning on it. Legislation also附带 versions and revision procedures, found unreasonable may be improved through due process, not overturned wholesale. Rule-of-law stability is the precondition Myriad Ranks dare long-term invest in the time domain — without stable expectations, no matter how fast the track, no one dares board.

## Brief Transition

The four laws' scope covers Origin Acceleration Domain and Quest Sediment Domain throughout: admission law governs both tracks' quotas, causal-reporting law governs both tracks' operations. Once laws finalized, whether Origin Acceleration Domain's 100× flow or Quest Sediment Domain's slow trials, all run under the same rule of law; no one may grant extralegal grace by plot need.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: flow law, load law direct time resources toward civilizational high-return directions, institutionally eliminate idling
- **Anti-predation**: admission law, feedback law forbid private retention and monopoly, root out time monopoly at the legislative level
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo no legislation, time rules by protagonist's mood; Heng Yuan Zhou four laws, time rules stable and predictable
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: legislative hearings absorb Myriad-Rank representatives, admission law covers five lineages, no one legislatively excluded
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: four law texts network-wide public, newly merged planes may directly接入 this time rule-of-law
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The time domain is civilization's heavy instrument; heavy instruments must be guarded by law, not by the strong's mood. The Anchor Council enacts "admission, flow, reporting, feedback" into four interlocking laws, turning "how time is used" from grace into rule-of-law. When rules stable, public, predictable, Myriad Ranks dare bet their lives on this time track — this is civilization's greatest sincerity about "fast."
