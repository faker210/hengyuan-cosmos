# Entry 619 | Time Domain and Civilizational Institutions | Symbiotic-Court Adjudication of Time-Domain Abuse

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 48, 19, 58 | Series: Time Domain and Civilizational Institutions (Entry 4)

## Introduction: will someone 100× faster also do evil 100× faster

Legislation (Entry 618) made rules, but rules will be broken. The time domain's most dangerous point is: **an ill-intentioned cultivator, if he uses 100× flow on crooked paths, his evil speed is also amplified 100×** — he deduces forbidden arts, polishes predatory techniques, stockpiles private armies in the acceleration domain, while outside only a few days have passed. This "time-amplified evil" needs dedicated judicial response. This entry tells how the symbiotic court adjudicates time-domain abuse.

Six top-level axioms are adjudication's legal basis: anti-predation axiom is adjudication's highest charge source; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires adjudication treat Myriad Ranks equally; collective ascension axiom requires correction not mere punishment; causal self-discipline axiom requires adjudication retraceable, correctable; law-first axiom requires conviction standards stable, not law changed by person. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Four Categories of Time-Domain Abuse Crimes

The symbiotic court categorizes time-domain abuse into four:

1. **Time private-retention crime**: domain-internal outputs not fed back per the Time-Feedback Law, privately stockpiling gongfu, technology (linking Entry 614).
2. **Causal overreach crime**: deducing/practicing overreach behavior inside the domain (e.g., predatory cultivation, unreported cross-plane intervention), using acceleration as an evil accelerator.
3. **Qualification abuse crime**: forging contribution, defrauding acceleration quotas, using switching channels to smuggle un-desensitized causality.
4. **Experience-harm crime**: in Quest-Domain courses, under the name of "trials" doing abuse, depriving others of exit right (linking Entry 617).

Four crimes' common feature: **using public time resources for plunder or harm**.

## II. Adjudication's Evidence Base: Alaya Trace-Through

Time-domain adjudication's confidence comes from Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's全程 records. Anyone's every step in the domain — enter, deduce, exit, feedback — is evidence-queryable. Adjudication therefore does not rely on "confessions," but on:

| Evidence Type | Content |
|---------------|---------|
| Timestamp Records | When entered, when exited, how long stayed |
| Causal-Account Records | Whether domain-internal deduction overreached, whether outputs privately retained |
| Feedback Audit | Whether required feedback was made, whether anything smuggled |
| Experience Records | Whether in Quest-Domain courses others were harmed |

Complete evidence chain, conviction not by subjective judgment — this is precisely Document 48's judicial system "taking facts as basis" expression.

## III. Sentencing Principle: Correction First, Punishment Secondary

Heng Yuan Zhou's judiciary does not pursue "evil against evil," but correction (Documents 48, 19). Time-domain abuse sentencing is layered accordingly:

- **Minor** (e.g., feedback delay): deadline correction, deduct contribution, public admonishment.
- **Moderate** (e.g., privately retained outputs): forced feedback, suspend acceleration qualification for a period, order public remedial study.
- **Severe** (e.g., causal overreach, harming others): long-term deprivation of time-domain qualification, assigned by symbiotic court to service-oriented trials in the Quest Domain for correction.
- **Grave** (e.g., converting acceleration domain into a predatory workshop): per Anti-Predation Core Thesis strictly处置, clear anchoring permissions.

Sentencing's purpose is **let the abuser re-understand time's publicity**, not simply lock him up.

## IV. Contrast with Tang Luo's "Lawlessness"

In the Tang-Luo system, strong ones doing evil have almost no judicial constraint — the protagonist grabs what he wants, punishes whom he wants, no court. Heng Yuan Zhou's symbiotic court precisely fills this gap: **even an ultimate strong one abusing the time domain must stand trial**. In Plane-Transcendent four-dimensional assessment (Power 40 / Mind 25 / Contribution 20 / Responsibility 15, Document 17), Mind and Responsibility take 40 points — time-domain abuse directly counts into assessment, one vote may remove his position.

## Supplementary Argument: Judiciary Is the Moral Brake on "Fast"

100× flow unconstrained, one villain's destructive power also amplified 100×. The symbiotic court adjudicating time-domain abuse is essentially installing a moral brake on "fast." Its significance is not post-hoc punishment, but pre-hoc deterrence: knowing one's deduction of forbidden arts will be recorded throughout by Alaya, will go to court, most people will withdraw at the first thought. This deterrence works not by how harsh judges are, but by how complete the evidence chain is — timestamps, causal accounts, feedback records nowhere to hide. Heng Yuan Zhou believes a system transparent enough to have nowhere to hide is itself the best moral education. Judiciary here is not cold punishment, but the conscience insurance that lets civilization dare to speed up.

## Brief Transition

Adjudication objects include both those deducing forbidden arts in the Origin Acceleration Domain, and those depriving others' exit rights in Quest Sediment Domain courses. Both categories of crimes are supported by the Alaya Network's complete evidence chain — Origin Acceleration Domain and Quest Sediment Domain are two sets of clearly reconciled accounts in court.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: adjudication and correction pull abused time resources back to public use, not wasted on doing evil
- **Anti-predation**: four crimes directly target time plunder; the symbiotic court is anti-predation axiom's judicial teeth
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo strong ones lawless; Heng Yuan Zhou strong ones abusing time also go to court; no privilege before law
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: adjudication treats Myriad Ranks equally; harmed party regardless of race or clan may appeal
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: charge definitions, sentencing standards, adjudication procedures public; newly merged planes接入 the same time rule-of-law
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The faster the time domain, the more judiciary it needs. Otherwise 100× flow's endpoint is not civilizational technical explosion, but one person in a few days doing every evil. The symbiotic court, with trace-through evidence, correction-first sentencing, adjudication不分 strong or weak, installs a brake on "fast." **Daring to open speed to 100× is not only because technology can catch it, but because law can hold it.**
