# Entry 620 | Time Domain and Civilizational Institutions | Anchor-Supervisory-Committee Audit of the Time Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 49, 29, 27 | Series: Time Domain and Civilizational Institutions (Entry 5)

## Introduction: legislature, executive, judiciary all in place — who watches the three

The Anchor Council legislates (Entry 618), the Symbiotic Executive Committee enforces, the symbiotic court adjudicates (Entry 619). Separation of powers looks complete. But separation itself can go wrong — legislators partial, executives lax, judiciary corrupt. So a fourth power is needed: the independent audit of the **Anchor Supervisory Committee**. This entry tells how the Supervisory Committee audits every operation of the time domain, making "no one is an exception" real.

Six top-level axioms are audit's yardstick: anti-predation axiom is audit's red line; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires audit open to Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires audit drive improvement not targeting people; causal self-discipline axiom requires audit trace-through; law-first axiom requires audit standards stable, not stretching by the auditor's taste. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. What to Audit: Three Tables

The Anchor Supervisory Committee regularly audits three tables of the time domain:

1. **Allocation table**: against Entry 616 joint-contracting rules, review whether acceleration time is truly allocated by contribution, whether strong ones crowd out, whether contribution is forged.
2. **Feedback table**: against Entry 614 access protocol, review whether domain-internal outputs truly feed back to the Alaya Network, whether privately retained, whether smuggled un-desensitized causality.
3. **Energy-consumption table**: against Entry 613 energy engineering, review whether energy收支 is incrementally positive, whether under the name of R&D extraction is being done.

Three tables correspond to "allocation fair, outputs public, energy incremental" three vital points.

## II. How to Audit: Independent + Through-Out

The Supervisory Committee's audit has two features:

- **Independence**: the Committee does not belong to the Council or Executive Committee, directly responsible to Myriad Ranks, ensuring audit is not interfered with by the audited.
- **Through-out**: Alaya Network data is audit's factual basis (Entry 615); the Committee pulls all timestamps, causal accounts, feedback records, does not accept "inconvenient to check."

| Audited Object | Routine Frequency | Triggered Audit |
|----------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Ordinary cultivators | Sampling | On report or data anomaly |
| Executive Committee | Quarterly | After major accidents |
| Council legislation | Annual | On law revision |
| Transcendent-strength | Case-by-case | Linked to four-dimensional assessment (Document 17) |

Audit's edge **has no ceiling above, no floor below** — including auditing legislators themselves.

## III. Audit Output: Reports and Rectification

Audit is not going through the motions; its output is **audit report + rectification order**:

- **Report public**: each audit produces a report, desensitized then network-wide published, Myriad Ranks may query.
- **Rectification order**: on finding problems, issue time-limited rectification order to the audited unit, and track re-inspection.
- **Judicial referral**: finding suspected Entry 619 four-category crimes, refer to the symbiotic court.
- **Improvement feedback**: feed institutional loopholes back to the Council, drive law amendment — audit is also an input to institutional evolution.

Audit's final purpose is not targeting people, but making the time-domain system **rotate cleaner and fairer**.

## IV. Supervision of the Supervisory Committee

A sharp question: who supervises the supervisor? Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is **dual-layer supervision**:

- The Committee's own audit behavior is also recorded on the Alaya Network, may be pulled and reviewed by the Council.
- Committee members' appointment and removal involves Myriad-Rank representatives, not self-appointed.
- If the Committee itself is corrupt, it too is tried by the symbiotic court — auditors breaking the law, crime adds one grade.

This avoids the trap of "supervisors becoming a new privileged class."

## Supplementary Argument: Supervision Keeps the System From Becoming What It Hates

Any institution operating long risks sliding toward vested interests — legislators enrich themselves, executives go lax, judiciary corrupts. The Anchor Supervisory Committee's existence is like installing a never-closed mirror on the time-domain system. It regularly reflects out whether allocation has been manipulated, whether feedback has been intercepted, whether energy has been falsified. More critically, this mirror also reflects the supervisors: if supervisors are corrupt, they too are tried. This dual-layer design of "supervised are also supervised" eliminates the birth of a new privileged class. Civilization's trust in the time domain ultimately does not come from how well it legislated, but from whether someone is truly watching — and watching the watchers.
