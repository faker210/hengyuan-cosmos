# Entry 621 | Time Domain and Civilizational Institutions | Time-Domain Bias in the 20% Activation Strategy

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 23, 75, 26 | Series: Time Domain and Civilizational Institutions (Entry 6)

## Introduction: universal welfare is not equal effort — to whom should time resources be biased

Universal welfare is the bottom line of republican axiom of Myriad Races, but universal welfare is not "equal effort." A civilization in its early stage, if it spreads time resources evenly to everyone, instead no one can break through. So Heng Yuan Zhou has the **20% Activation Strategy** (Document 23): first concentrate resources to activate a batch of high-potential individuals, who then drive the whole. The time domain, as a key resource, naturally plays a "bias" role in this strategy. This entry tells how time resources are focusedly deployed, neither egalitarianism nor a new monopoly.

Six top-level axioms are bias's yardstick: anti-predation axiom requires bias not become plunder of the other 80%; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires biased objects by potential not birth; collective ascension axiom requires biased outputs must carry the whole along; causal self-discipline axiom requires bias process transparent and auditable; law-first axiom requires bias ratio, exit mechanism stable and predictable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. What to Bias: Put the Time Domain on the Cutting Edge

Under the 20% Activation Strategy, time-domain bias shows in three places:

1. **Origin Acceleration Domain quota bias**: activated high-potential individuals' yearly acceleration quota floats up, prioritized for high-multiple gears.
2. **Quest Domain mentor resource bias**: key trial instances open first to activated individuals, accompanied by senior mentors.
3. **Alaya deduction compute bias**: activated individuals' deduction projects prioritize network compute support.

Bias's purpose is clear: **let those most likely to break bottlenecks get the most time leverage**.

## II. Biased Objects: by Potential, Not Birth

Bias most easily slides into a new monopoly — if biased objects are fixed to a certain bloodline, a certain sect, it becomes a Tang-Luo remake. Heng Yuan Zhou's defense line:

| Bias Screening Standard | Content |
|-------------------------|---------|
| Potential Assessment | Comprehensively assessed by Alaya Network from growth trajectory, mind-nature, contribution |
| Dynamic In-Out | Activation list reassessed yearly; those falling behind exit, lagging entrants join |
| Birth-Neutral | Only potential and contribution count, not bloodline, race, lineage |
| Reversible Exit | Bias is phased investment, not lifelong privilege |

Key is **dynamic, reversible, by potential** — fundamentally different from Tang Luo's "chosen for life" grace system.

## III. Biased Outputs Must Carry the Whole Along

If bias only lets 20% ascend, it violates collective ascension axiom. Heng Yuan Zhou's hard constraint:

- Activated individuals' breakthrough results **must feed back to the Alaya Network per the Time-Feedback Law**, network-wide shared.
- Activated individuals have **teaching obligations**: organize their breakthrough experience into courses, nourishing the other 80%.
- Acceleration resources enjoyed during the bias period **count into their contribution**, to be repaid later by civilizational contribution — not free.

In other words, bias is "lending you resources to break through," not "bestowing you privilege." You break through, you must give the method back to everyone.

## IV. Bias and Individual-Collective Balance

Bias inevitably raises a tension: will there be a gap between biased and unbiased? This is exactly what Document 26 "individual-collective balance principle" handles. Heng Yuan Zhou's solution is **dual-track diversion**:

- Origin Acceleration Domain biases toward the 20%, helping them break through fast.
- Quest Sediment Domain opens to the 80%, guaranteeing their slow trials' right path — slow is not being abandoned.

Biased take the fast track, unbiased take the slow track; both tracks lead to Origin Realm, only rhythm differs. Bias is therefore not "abandoning the 80%," but "let the 20% scout first, then bring the 80% along."

## Supplementary Argument: Bias's Justice Lies in "Reversible"

The most easily criticized thing about resource bias is "why you first." Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is triple reversibility: list reassessed yearly, those falling behind exit, lagging join, bias is not lifelong; activated individuals have teaching obligations, must give breakthrough methods back to Myriad Ranks; biased resources count into contribution, repaid later by civilizational contribution. This triple reversibility turns "the rich first drive the rich later" from slogan into institution. Bias does not slide into a new monopoly not because biased individuals are virtuous, but because the institution does not allow him to occupy this position forever. Dynamic, reversible, by potential — this is the 20% Activation Strategy's fundamental difference from Tang Luo's "personally anointed protagonist."

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: bias concentrates time leverage at highest potential, far more efficient than evenly sprinkling pepper
- **Anti-predation**: bias by potential, dynamic and reversible, outputs must feed back; does not become 20%'s plunder of the 80%
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's "key cultivation" fixed to the protagonist's line; Heng Yuan Zhou's bias reassessed yearly, anyone may enter
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: bias screening birth-neutral; five lineages may enter the activation list by potential
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: bias standards, reassessment mechanism, feedback obligations public; newly merged planes may copy this "activate first then drive along"
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Addendum

It must be emphasized: bias is never the goal, only the means. The Origin Acceleration Domain hands leverage to high-potential individuals, the Quest Sediment Domain keeps the classroom for all; the two tracks ultimately point to the same result — the raising of Myriad Races' overall evolution speed, not a few running farther and farther ahead. Every bit of biased resources is converted on the Alaya Network into return to Myriad Ranks.

## Conclusion

Universal welfare is not average; bias is not monopoly — the narrow road between them is "break through by potential first, then drive Myriad Ranks." The 20% Activation Strategy hands the time domain's leverage to those most likely to break through, while using dynamic list, feedback obligations, teaching responsibility three locks to prevent bias from becoming new privilege. True universal welfare is not making everyone equally slow, but letting those with potential go one step faster, then paving the road for everyone.
