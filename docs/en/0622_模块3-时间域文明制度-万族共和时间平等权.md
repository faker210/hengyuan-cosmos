# Entry 622 | Time Domain and Civilizational Institutions | Time Equal-Right in Myriad-Race Republicanism

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 50, 08 | Series: Time Domain and Civilizational Institutions (Entry 7 · Conclusion)

## Introduction: even "time" being equal, then Myriad-Race Republicanism lands

Republican axiom of Myriad Races says "all beings have equal qualification to evolve." But "qualification," if not landed on the most concrete resource, is empty talk. In the time-domain age, the scarcest, most gap-widening resource is precisely **time** — who may enter the acceleration domain, who may take the Quest Domain directly determines who may evolve. So Myriad-Race Republicanism must concretize into a new right: **time equal-right**. This entry, as the civilizational-institution subseries' conclusion, argues this right's content and guarantee.

Among the six top-level axioms, republican axiom of Myriad Races is this entry's protagonist; the other four support it: anti-predation axiom requires time equality not achieved by stripping the strong; collective ascension axiom requires time equality lead to joint progress; causal self-discipline axiom requires equal-right not cause causal chaos; law-first axiom requires "equality"'s definition stable and operable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Three-Layer Meaning of Time Equal-Right

Time equal-right is not "everyone equally fast," but three layers of operable equality:

1. **Admission equality**: all beings, regardless of carbon-based, energy, mechanical, consciousness, mixed five lineages (Document 50), have the right to apply to enter the time domain, not refused by race.
2. **Basic-guarantee equality**: each activated individual enjoys baseline acceleration quota and basic Quest courses (linking Entries 616, 617), not excluded from time dividends.
3. **Rule equality**: time-domain law (Entry 618) treats Myriad Ranks equally, not biased in adjudication or audit by clan strength.

Three layers: **all may enter, all have baseline, all obey the same law**.

## II. Time Conversion for Special Races

Time equality is not "giving everyone identical quotas," because Myriad Ranks' time units fundamentally differ. Equal-right therefore requires **conversion fairness**:

| Clan Type | Time-Unit Feature | Equal Guarantee |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Carbon-based human race | Hundred-year scale | Standard quota |
| Energy-condensation race | Perceived time long | Quota converted by its rhythm |
| Mechanical-construction race | Time highly precise | Converted by compute |
| Consciousness-information race | Time nearly boundless | Converted by consciousness compute, prevent monopolization |
| Plant spirits / planet consciousness | Time extremely slow | Dedicated slow-gear Quest Domain, not left behind by fast track |

Conversion model public, appealable — **equality is "treated fairly by one's own time unit," not force-feeding the same number**.

## III. What Time Equal-Right Opposes

In practice, time equal-right mainly opposes three inequalities:

- **Anti-birth monopoly**: opposing Tang-Luo-style allocation of time qualification by bloodline, lineage (Entry 601).
- **Anti-species discrimination**: opposing excluding "non-humanoid" beings like plant spirits, planet consciousness from the time domain.
- **Anti-strong-one-takes-all**: opposing the powerful squeezing dry public acceleration quota by strength (Entry 616 load linkage).

Three oppositions are all written into time-domain law, backed by Supervisory-Committee audit (Entry 620).

## IV. Time Equal-Right and Collective Ascension's Unity

Some worry: giving all races equal time will slow the strong? Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is the opposite — **time equality is the precondition of collective ascension**:

- Excluded clans' talent and wisdom are wasted — this itself is energy waste.
- After equal opening, Myriad Ranks each evolve in time gears suited to themselves; outputs feed back through the Alaya Network (Entry 614), and the whole civilization's total output is actually larger.
- The 20% Activation Strategy (Entry 621) then biases on the basis of equality — "first guarantee everyone a road, then help able ones go first."

Time equal-right is therefore not egalitarianism, but **activating every piece on the Myriad-Race chessboard**.

## Supplementary Argument: Time Equality Is the Touchstone of Myriad-Race Republicanism

Myriad-Race Republicanism is often misunderstood as "lip-service equality." Time equal-right being the touchstone is precisely because time is scarcest and most gap-widening. Whether a clan is truly treated equally is more accurately seen in whether it may equally use the time domain than by any slogan. Heng Yuan Zhou therefore refines time equality into admission, baseline, conversion three layers, backed by supervisory audit. When even the slowest plant spirit and the most marginal mixed-variant race may evolve in adapted time gears, Myriad-Race Republicanism has fallen from manifesto into daily life. Time equal-right is not the endpoint; it is the hardest touchstone testing whether the whole civilization keeps its promise.

## Brief Transition

Time equal-right manifests in the Origin Acceleration Domain as everyone-redeemable quota, in the Quest Sediment Domain as everyone-choosable trial courses. The two tracks together form the complete picture of Myriad-Race time equality — whether carbon-based, energy, or consciousness-information race, each may find its own evolution rhythm in the Origin Acceleration Domain's fast and the Quest Sediment Domain's slow.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: liberating talent excluded by race and birth; time equality itself is the greatest anti-waste
- **Anti-predation**: time equal-right institutionally roots out birth monopoly and strong-one-takes-all; plunder loses soil
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo excludes Myriad Ranks (Module Two); Heng Yuan Zhou's time equal-right covers five lineages 99%
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: plant spirits, planet consciousness, exotic beasts, plant spirits all included; no one forgotten by time dividends
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: time equal-right written into the charter; conversion model public; newly merged planes automatically enjoy equal qualification
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Myriad-Race Republicanism is not a "we are equal" slogan; it must land on: every being may equally use the time domain. Time equal-right — all may enter, all have baseline, all obey the same law, converted by own time unit — hands the scarcest time resource, for the first time truly back to all beings. When even "time" is equal, Myriad-Race Republicanism has fallen from ideal into institution.
