# Entry 623 | Time Domain and Individual Cultivation | Personal Cultivation Plan in the Acceleration Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 11, 16, 14 | Series: Time Domain and Individual Cultivation (Entry 1)

## Introduction: entering the 100× acceleration domain, how to use these hundred days

Civilization has finished institutions; how should the individual conduct himself? A cultivator has obtained one hundred consciousness-days in the Origin Acceleration Domain. How should he arrange them? Dive in and grind blindly, or plan to put these hundred days on the cutting edge? This entry stands at the individual angle, telling personal cultivation plans inside the acceleration domain — the first link of the dual-track time domain falling from "civilizational engineering" to "personal daily life."

Six top-level axioms are the internal yardstick of personal plans: anti-predation axiom requires plans not steal others' time; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires plans fit the quota system; collective ascension axiom requires plan outputs feed back; causal self-discipline axiom requires plans not overreach; law-first axiom requires plans fit the cultivation-realm unified division (Document 14), not bouncing realms. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. A personal plan is ultimately a small gear in the civilizational system.

## I. Before Entering the Domain: Set Goals First, Then Turn the Knob

The biggest waste is entering the acceleration domain with a "go in and see" mindset. The right approach is to decide three things before entering:

1. **Goal gear**: this time, charging a realm bottleneck, researching a new gongfu, or drilling tactics? The goal determines flow gear and duration.
2. **Output list**: before entering, write clearly — when exiting, what to bring back (a gongfu draft, a deduction model, a realm breakthrough).
3. **Ethical pre-check**: confirm the goal does not involve predatory R&D; may enter only after Alaya pre-check passes.

These three steps turn "purposeless fast" into "purpose-directed fast."

## II. Time Allocation Inside the Domain: Three-to-Seven

A hundred consciousness-days inside the acceleration domain cannot all be spent charging realms. Heng Yuan Zhou's recommended allocation is **three-to-seven**:

| Time Share | Content | Purpose |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| 30% | High-intensity deduction / cultivation | Charge core goal |
| 50% | Verification, comparison, debrief | Prevent deduction from going off track (compare with Quest Domain) |
| 20% | Rest, record, organize notes | Prepare for feedback, prevent consciousness overload |

This ratio deliberately leaves large "non-sprint" time — because the easiest mistake at 100× flow is blindly sprinting, ending up with a pile of untested waste.

## III. Coordination with Realm Rhythm

Acceleration plans must obey the Nine Realms' objective rhythm (Document 14). Sense Origin, Condensed Origin and other first five mortal-realm stages rely on foundation grinding; **must not over-rely on the acceleration domain** — acceleration raises deduction, but cannot replace the body-grinding of "perceive, absorb, discern." The last four sage-realm stages (Dao Integration, Boundary-Breaking, World-Creation, Origin) are where the acceleration domain truly shows its skill.

So the key individual-plan judgment is: **at this stage, is the homework "should be fast" or "should be slow"**? Hard-opening acceleration at the stage that should be slow (e.g., Sense-Origin foundation) is like forcing seedlings up — precisely law-first axiom's expression at the personal level.

## IV. Anti "Acceleration Dependency"

Long soaking in the acceleration domain produces "real-time is too slow" anxiety, a new ailment of the time-domain age. Personal plans therefore build in anti-dependency:

- **Mandatory cooling**: after high-multiple acceleration, per institution mandatory cooling; during cooling do routine cultivation, regain real rhythm.
- **Regularly exit the domain**: even if task incomplete, periodically return to the real plane to live, maintain personality thickness.
- **Comparison-domain verification**: key outputs taken to the Quest Sediment Domain (Entry 624) for slow verification, prevent "what comes out fast is all castles in the air."

## Supplementary Argument: The Essence of Planning Is Respect for "Fast"

A common mistake is "entering the acceleration domain and naturally efficient." The opposite: 100× flow amplifies any unplanned chaos a hundredfold — the person who set no goal comes out only with a pile of busy garbage. The personal cultivation plan's three determinations (set goal, set output, set ethics) are essentially respect for "fast": precisely because fast is expensive, it cannot be used recklessly. The three-to-seven allocation deliberately leaves most time for verification and rest, admitting that "fast" also makes mistakes. Only those who know how to plan before speeding up have turned the acceleration domain into a lever; those who dive in and sprint hard have only sold their own time at a high price to inefficiency.

## Brief Transition

Throughout the plan's execution, the cultivator's every deduction and output is synchronously recorded on the Alaya Network, for later debrief and feedback. This personal plan is therefore not a private notebook, but a public sample witnessed by the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision, learnable network-wide.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: three goal-determinations before entering, three-to-seven allocation inside, putting hundred consciousness-days on the cutting edge
- **Anti-predation**: ethical pre-check + not stealing others' time; personal plans run within the quota system
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo protagonist charges by instinct upon entering a secret realm; Heng Yuan Zhou's individual speeds up scientifically by plan and ratio
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: plan methods open to Myriad Ranks; each race customizes its own "three-to-seven" by its own time unit
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: plan templates, output-list paradigms public; newly merged planes' cultivators may directly apply
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The acceleration domain's fast should not be blind fast. The personal cultivation plan converts "I have a hundred days" into "what to achieve, how to allocate, how to verify." Those who truly know how to use the acceleration domain are not those who soak longest inside, but those who bring back the cleanest, most usable results when exiting. Fast is a tool; planning is the skill.
