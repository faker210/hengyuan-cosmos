# Entry 626 | Time Domain and Individual Cultivation | Lin Hao's Dual-Track Time-Domain Cultivation Record

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 17, 26, 01 | Series: Time Domain and Individual Cultivation (Entry 4)

## Introduction: a concrete person, how to live the dual track as his own road

The previous entries discussed general methods; this entry lands on a concrete person — Lin Hao. As one of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's core practitioners, Lin Hao's use of the dual-track time domain is a living sample testing whether this system "really works on the individual." This entry, from a record perspective, debriefs how Lin Hao arranged his cultivation between fast track, slow track, normal, and distills reusable experience.

Six top-level axioms run through this record: anti-predation axiom embodied in his not swallowing others' opportunities; republican axiom of Myriad Races embodied in his not monopolizing acceleration resources; collective ascension axiom embodied in all his results fed back; causal self-discipline axiom embodied in his cross-time operations fully reported; law-first axiom embodied in his strictly following the Nine-Realm rhythm, not artificially ripening. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Lin Hao's Fast Track: Put 100× on the Cutting Edge

Lin Hao's use of the Origin Acceleration Domain strictly follows Entry 623's three pre-entries:

- **Clear goal**: he concentrates acceleration time on key bottlenecks of the deduction iteration system (First Origin System) — the self-consistency verification of Path D related Dao principles, not generic realm charging.
- **Output-oriented**: each exit must bring concrete output — a deduction model, a Dao-principle derivation, a counterintuitive discovery.
- **Ethical self-check**: his deduction always revolves around "symbiotic alternatives"; never in the acceleration domain deduced predatory gongfu.

His fast track is not "soaking long," but "calculating accurately" — the use the dual-track time domain recommends.

## II. Lin Hao's Slow Track: Experience Events and Temper the Heart in the Quest Domain

Beyond the fast track, Lin Hao equally values the Quest Sediment Domain:

- **Actively slow at bottlenecks**: when Dao-principle deduction technically passes but mind-nature is stuck, he does not force acceleration, but enters the Quest Domain to truly live through "what if I chose plunder?" "what if I ascended alone?" these hypotheses.
- **Debrief old systems**: he puts Tang Luo, Shrouding the Heavens, Manghuangji and other old systems' failures (Module Two) into the Quest Domain, as his "warning" course.
- **Lessons fed back**: the "why symbiosis is better than plunder" realized in the slow track is abstracted into articles, fed back to the Alaya Network, becoming Myriad Ranks' public teaching material.

His slow track is not escape, but "ballast" for the fast track.

## III. Lin Hao's Normal: Return to Reality to Live It Out

Most worth recording is that Lin Hao did not addict to any single track:

- He insists on living a normal life on the real plane, doing concrete civilizational-building work; fast-and-slow-track gains all must return to reality to be tested.
- He self-requires by the Plane Transcendent four-dimensional assessment (Power 40 / Mind 25 / Contribution 20 / Responsibility 15, Document 17) — Mind and Responsibility take nearly half, reminding him "fast is not everything."
- His dual-track rhythm is dynamic: stages differ, fast-slow-normal ratio adjusts accordingly, never dogmatic.

## IV. Record Distillation: Three Reusable Lessons

From Lin Hao's dual-track practice, three lessons useful to ordinary individuals may be distilled:

1. **Fast track produces solutions, slow track produces the true heart, normal produces results** — the three are indispensable; missing any is idling.
2. **Calculate clearly before speeding, live through clearly before teaching** — no unprepared battles, no teaching unexperienced Dao.
3. **Results must be fed back** — Lin Hao is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's core not because of how strong he personally is, but because he feeds every dual-track gain into the Alaya Network.

## Supplementary Argument: The Record's Value Is "Replicable" Not "Worshiped"

Lin Hao's record, if treated as a "protagonist legend" to worship, is completely misused. Its true value is replicable — any ordinary cultivator may, from his fast-slow-normal rhythm, three pre-entries, result feedback, distill methods he himself may use. Heng Yuan Zhou records the strong never to create gods, but to turn the road he trod into a bridge for later generations. Lin Hao's strength lies not in how much faster he is than others, but in feeding every dual-track gain into the Alaya Network, letting Myriad Ranks follow him and detour less. This is precisely the dividing line between "network-type strength" and "single-tree strength": the former makes oneself a section of the public road, the latter makes oneself the only lone peak.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Lin Hao concentrates acceleration time on highest-output Path D deduction; does not soak in useless fast
- **Anti-predation**: his fast track never deduces predatory gongfu; his slow-track trials do not swallow others' experiences
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Lin Hao's growth by dual-track + feedback, not Tang-Luo-style grace; his strength is "network-type" not "single-tree-type"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: his dual-track experience abstracted into public teaching material, nourishing Myriad Ranks, not a personal secret
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: this record itself is an open sample; newly merged planes' cultivators may directly reference his rhythm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Lin Hao's record proves one thing: the dual-track time domain is not a tool to open cheats for the protagonist, but a cultivation system ordinary people may also use well. The fast track helps him calculate clearly, the slow track helps him live through, the normal helps him land, feedback helps him amplify. He is not faster than anyone, only more knowing than everyone when fast, when slow, when to return gains to Myriad Ranks — this is the ideal cultivator the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization cultivates.
