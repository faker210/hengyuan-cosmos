# Entry 627 | Time Domain and Individual Cultivation | Deduction Optimization of Path D in the Time Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 11, 16, 08 | Series: Time Domain and Individual Cultivation (Entry 5 · Conclusion)

## Introduction: the road most needing deduction happens to use the domain best at deduction

Path D · human-race artificial Dao-principle spirit ring is a characteristic cultivation road Heng Yuan Zhou opens for the human race — not relying on innate martial-soul/bloodline talent, but relying on the deduction iteration system (First Origin System) to artificially construct Dao-principle spirit rings. This road's core difficulty is not "practice," but "calculate": each artificial Dao principle must be verified self-consistent through massive deduction, otherwise light symptoms fire deviation, severe symptoms become calamity. And **deduction is precisely the Origin Acceleration Domain's forte**. This entry tells how Path D uses the dual-track time domain to complete deduction optimization.

Six top-level axioms are Path D deduction's highest criterion: anti-predation axiom requires artificial Dao principles not swallow others' bloodline talent; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires Path D equally open to the untalented; collective ascension axiom requires deduction results network-wide shared; causal self-discipline axiom requires each artificial Dao principle causally verified; law-first axiom requires artificial Dao principles' self-consistency precede use, never retcon. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Path D Cannot Do Without the Acceleration Domain

Path D's essence is "use deduction to replace talent." Innate bloodline carries Dao principles by nature; Path D lacks this innate condition, can only "calculate" Dao principles through postnatal deduction. This road's deduction volume is enormous:

- Each Dao principle to be constructed, first simulate in the law network its compatibility with existing laws.
- Each conflict point, exhaust multiple correction schemes, verify one by one.
- Each correction, in turn, carry-test its impact on the whole spirit-ring chain.

This "hundred twists thousand turns" deduction, in normal time, may exhaust a lifetime — **it is precisely the Origin Acceleration Domain's 100× deduction amplification (Document 16) that turns Path D from "theoretically possible" to "practically feasible"**.

## II. Three-Track Coordination of Deduction Optimization

Path D's deduction optimization just uses up dual track + normal:

| Track | Role in Path D |
|-------|----------------|
| Origin Acceleration Domain | Mass parallel deduction: exhaust Dao-principle schemes, test compatibility, iterate correction |
| Quest Sediment Domain | Put deduction-passed Dao principles into simulated "real combat," see whether they hold in real cultivation situations |
| Real Plane | Real-person actual-cultivation verification: only after deduction and simulation both pass does one truly construct this spirit ring in reality |

Three tracks layer by layer gate: **acceleration domain exhaust, Quest domain verify truth, real plane land**. Any track failing does not enter the next.

## III. Safety Discipline of Deduction Optimization

Path D deduction's most dangerous place is "calculated wrong yet thought calculated right." So three disciplines:

1. **Self-consistency one-vote veto**: even if deduction passes, as long as causal self-discipline axiom testing finds the artificial Dao principle conflicts with existing laws, immediately discard and redo — law-first axiom above all.
2. **Small-step verification**: each spirit ring separately deduced, separately verified; do not construct the whole chain at once, lock risk locally.
3. **Network-wide review**: key Dao principles' deduction results feed back to the Alaya Network, multiple independent deductions in parallel review, prevent single-point deduction blind spots.

## IV. Path D and "Everyone-a-Road"

Path D + time-domain combination directly serves Document 08 "everyone-a-road" guarantee system:

- Human-race without innate martial-soul/bloodline talent, in Douluo-style systems had no future (Entry 604).
- In Heng Yuan Zhou, they may take Path D — through deduction iteration of artificial Dao-principle spirit rings, using "time + deduction" to make up for "talent absence."
- The Origin Acceleration Domain lowers deduction cost to affordable; Path D is therefore no longer the genius's patent, but the untalented's right path.

This is precisely the dual-track time domain's concrete contribution to republican axiom of Myriad Races: **it lets the old threshold of "talent" be crossed for the first time with "time"**.

## Supplementary Argument: Path D Is the Fundamental Denial of "Talent Divinely Ordained"

Path D's deep meaning far exceeds a new cultivation path. Philosophically it denies the predatory old law of "cultivation qualification ordained by talent" — in the past, human-race without martial-soul bloodline were sentenced "innately running alongside"; today, Path D tells them through deduction iteration: Dao principles do not come by reincarnation, they come by calculation. This is republican axiom of Myriad Races' hardest technological fulfillment. The dual-track time domain is this road's engine because deduction needs massive compute, and the acceleration domain precisely turns this compute into affordable reality. The old threshold of talent may for the first time be crossed by time and diligence — once this door opens, it never closes again.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the acceleration domain compresses Path D's massive deduction from "exhausting a lifetime" to affordable; time spent on the cutting edge
- **Anti-predation**: artificial Dao principles constructed by deduction, not swallowing others' bloodline talent; the untalented also have a road
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Douluo-style untalented have no future; Heng Yuan Zhou's Path D opens a door for them by time + deduction
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Path D's essence is "constructing Dao principles for the untalented"; it is Myriad-Race Republicanism's landing in cultivation technology
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Path D's deduction schemes, Dao-principle parameters network-wide open; any untalented may equally接入
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Path D is Heng Yuan Zhou's "everyone-a-road" promise's hard technological support, and the dual-track time domain is this road's engine. Without the acceleration domain's 100× deduction, Path D is only fantasy; without the Quest Domain's truth verification and real plane's landing, the acceleration domain's deduction also hangs in the air. Three-track coordination, strict discipline, results shared — Path D in the time domain, for the first time, turns "not enough talent, make up with time" from a slogan into a walkable road.
