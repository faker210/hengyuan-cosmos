# Entry 631 | Time Domain and Law-First Axiom | Self-Consistency Verification of Time-Domain Settings

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 97, 89, 09 | Series: Time Domain and Law-First Axiom (Entry 1)

## Introduction: such complex dual-track settings — will they fight themselves

Law-first axiom requires "laws first, never retcon, setting self-consistency above plot convenience." The dual-track time domain is Module Three's most complex engineering — hundredfold flow, causal filtering, Alaya feedback, fault rollback... the more links, the easier settings contradict themselves. This entry does a "self-consistency verification": check item by item whether the dual-track time domain's settings are internally consistent, conflict with the foundation, or hide "retcon" landmines. This is law-first axiom's direct landing in this module.

Among the six top-level axioms, law-first axiom is this entry's protagonist; the other four are the objects being verified: can anti-predation, republican of Myriad Races, collective ascension, causal self-discipline these four axioms simultaneously hold under dual-track settings without fighting? The sixth universal equal infinite law establishes the origin logic that all beings inherently possess basic infinite power and predation loses meaning.

## I. Verification Method: Four Questions

For each key setting of the dual-track time domain, test self-consistency with four questions:

1. **Conflict with the foundation?**: any contradiction with Documents 0–99's established laws?
2. **Does every link close?**: can acceleration domain, Quest domain, switching, feedback, fault links close the loop?
3. **Are special cases covered?**: do settings still hold under extreme cases (Myriad-Rank aliens, disaster faults)?
4. **Will it yield to plot?**: did settings leave a backdoor for "temporarily changed for wish-fulfilling plot"?

All four questions pass, then self-consistent.

## III. Identifying Potential "Retcon Landmines"

Self-consistency verification not only finds existing contradictions, but also identifies in advance "plot-kidnapping" landmines:

- **Landmine 1: protagonist-aura-style speed-up**. If some character, by plot need, bypasses quota, cooling, ethical review — this is retcon. Heng Yuan Zhou's defense: no exception before law (Entries 619, 620).
- **Landmine 2: acceleration used faster and faster**. If for thrill, load lines (Entry 613) are constantly broken, the law network must collapse. Defense: load lines are physical ceilings; plot has no right to break through.
- **Landmine 3: time-paradox beautification**. If for thrill, causal forks are ignored, it repeats Douluo's crossing mistakes (Entry 604). Defense: causal reporting and rollback are non-exemptible.

Marking these landmines in advance is law-first axiom's "nip in the bud" work.

## IV. Self-Consistency Is Civilization's Engineering Confidence

The dual-track time domain dares open speed to 100×; one source of confidence is this self-consistency verification: **settings hold on law, then dare let loose in engineering**. This fundamentally contrasts with predatory-faction worlds' "enjoy first, retcon and abandon later" — Shrouding's late-stage power inflation, Douluo's repeatedly crossed settings, are all costs of giving up self-consistency (Module Two analyzed in detail).

Heng Yuan Zhou's attitude: **each setting expansion first passes self-consistency**, rather slow than retcon.

## Supplementary Argument: Self-Consistency Is Dual Responsibility to Readers and the Future

Law-first axiom requiring self-consistency is often treated as "setting fetishism." Actually it is a kind of responsibility. A retcon-and-abandon worldview equals breaking faith with readers and later generations invested in it — today's setting changed for thrill, tomorrow makes countless people at a loss. The dual-track time domain insisting on item-by-item self-consistency checks, marking retcon landmines in advance, is responsible to the whole civilization and to future merged planes. Self-consistency does not bind imagination, but lets it go further: only when the laws underfoot do not collapse does civilization dare imagine an infinitely high future. Guarding self-consistency is guarding civilization's long dream from being woken by itself.

## Brief Transition

Specific to terminology: the Origin Acceleration Domain's hundredfold flow, the Quest Sediment Domain's slow immersion, and the Alaya Network access protocol between them, the three mesh in self-consistency — any one altered by plot, the other two must be synchronously re-verified. Law-first axiom is therefore not empty words; it requires the Origin Acceleration Domain, Quest Sediment Domain, Alaya Network as a whole to be continuously checked.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: self-consistency verification clears landmines in advance, avoiding rework and collapse caused by setting contradictions
- **Anti-predation**: verification blocks the "protagonist-aura speed-up" backdoor; predatory privilege has no setting space
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo/Shrouding sacrifice self-consistency for plot; Heng Yuan Zhou constrains plot for self-consistency
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: verification covers five lineages' special cases; no clan's settings omitted
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: self-consistency verification method public; any newly expanded settings self-check by the same standard
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The more complex the system, the more it needs self-consistency. The dual-track time domain uses "four questions" to verify item by item, confirming core settings close under foundational laws, and marking in advance landmines that may be plot-kidnapped. Law-first axiom here is not a slogan, but an engineering test each entry must pass. **Settings self-consistent, civilization dares infinite expansion; where it cannot be self-consistent, better not write.**

In the end, the time-domain law-first · self-consistency verification described in this entry is no isolated case — it always points toward the shared-building direction of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization.
