From Entry 501's overview to this entry, Module Three uses thirty-three entries to thoroughly explain "time," the most fundamental means of production. This entry, as the finale, introduces no new settings, but closes the whole module: what problem did the dual-track time domain actually solve? On what basis is it called "the ultimate solution to civilizational experience"? What is its relation to the six top-level axioms? Let us do a panoramic look back in the finale.

Six top-level axioms are the spine running through the whole module: anti-predation axiom says "acceleration by increment not by grabbing"; republican axiom of Myriad Races says "fast and slow are both Myriad Ranks' right paths"; collective ascension axiom says "fast track feeds slow track, slow track nourishes fast track"; causal self-discipline axiom says "time operations fully reported, rollback-able"; law-first axiom says "dual-track settings self-consistent, infinitely expandable." The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. Thirty-three entries are nothing but these five axioms fully unfolded in the "time" dimension.

## I. Three Core Problems the Dual-Track Time Domain Solves

Looking back at the whole module, the dual-track time domain centrally solves three problems predatory-faction worlds cannot solve:

1. **Time-allocation problem**: the predatory faction treats time as zero-sum stock (Entry 608), strong ones take all, weak ones are exhausted. The dual track makes time incremental — efficiency doubled, output shared, depth transmittable; time from zero-sum to positive-sum.
2. **Fast-slow contradiction problem**: single-track acceleration empties experience, causal overheating; single-track slowness technologically stagnates. The dual track packs "fast" into the Origin Acceleration Domain and "slow" into the Quest Sediment Domain, sewing into an organism by switching and feedback.
3. **Civilizational out-of-control problem**: the faster the more likely hollow, more likely predatory. The dual track uses law anchoring, causal filtering, fault rollback, legislative-judicial supervision (Entries 616–622), letting fast proceed within rule-of-law and engineering safety nets.

Three problems solved, civilizational experience is "ultimate."

The word "ultimate" does not rely on slogans, but on triple closure:

- **Logical closure**: five axioms all land in the time dimension, no one point falling empty.
- **Engineering closure**: from law definition to node deployment, from energy to fault, the whole chain operable (Entries 609–615).

Logic, engineering, institution triple closure, the dual-track time domain dares call "ultimate" — it is not some generation's protagonist's cheat, but a sustainable, supervisable, infinitely expandable civilizational infrastructure.

## Supplementary Argument: What the Dual Track Leaves Myriad Ranks Is a New Time Dignity

Module Three's ultimate concern, in the end, is "time dignity" four words. In predatory-faction worlds, an ordinary being's time is consumed, wasted, seized by the strong; in the dual-track time domain, his time is respected, planned, used to fulfill himself. Fast need not be privilege, slow need not be sin; fast and slow both lead to growth, both serve Myriad Ranks. This dignity, more fundamental than any gongfu, any realm — it is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's simplest yet most solemn promise to every being: your time belongs to you, and also to Myriad Ranks; it will be well used, never burned for nothing. This is what Module Three's thirty-three entries ultimately guard.
