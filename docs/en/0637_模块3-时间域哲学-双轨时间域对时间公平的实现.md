# Entry 637 | Time-Domain Philosophy | The Dual-Track Time Domain's Realization of "Time Fairness"

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 06, 22, 08 | Series: Time-Domain Philosophy (Entry 4)

## Introduction: time fairness is the last puzzle piece of Myriad-Race Republicanism

In resource-allocation philosophy, land fairness, wealth fairness, knowledge fairness have all been repeatedly discussed, but "time fairness" has almost never been seriously treated. Predatory-faction systems default time as a personal matter — how long you live is your business, how long you use is your business; no one feels this needs distributive justice. But the dual-track time-domain system reveals an overlooked truth: **when time flow may be technically regulated, time becomes an allocable resource, and the justice of time allocation is republican axiom of Myriad Races' final fulfillment in the time dimension**.

Six top-level axioms take effect here: anti-predation axiom requires time not be plundered and monopolized; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires time allocation equal to all beings; collective ascension axiom requires time investment ultimately serve joint progress; causal self-discipline axiom requires time allocation not create causal injustice; law-first axiom requires time-allocation law stable and self-consistent. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry asks: **how does the dual-track time domain realize time fairness? Why is this fairness the last puzzle piece of Myriad-Race Republicanism?**

## I. Three Historical Forms of Time Unfairness

In predatory-faction civilizations, time unfairness exists in three forms:

### 1.1 Lifespan Unfairness

The strong live forever; mortals go through birth, aging, illness, death. The strong live ten thousand years, mortals live a hundred — the same "lifetime," time windows differ a hundredfold. This gap is not the result of effort, but the product of predatory cultivation: the strong extend their own lifespan by devouring others' energy.

### 1.2 Flow Unfairness

The strong may seclude, may enter time secret realms, may use time-accelerating treasures; mortals may only labor in normal flow. Within the same physical time, the strong's effective cultivation time is mortals' hundredfold, thousandfold.

### 1.3 Exit Unfairness

The strong may exit time at any moment — use time stop, time reversal, reincarnation rebirth to evade errors and death; mortals have no such choice, one wrong step leads to eternal damnation.

Three unfairness stacked constitutes the predatory-faction system's most fundamental time injustice: **the strong through various means occupy Myriad Ranks' time**.

## II. The Dual-Track Time Domain's Three Corrections of Time Fairness

### 2.1 Qualification Fairness: All May Use

The Origin Acceleration Domain and Quest Sediment Domain's use qualification is equally open to all beings of Myriad Ranks. Regardless of race, birth, gender, as long as passing basic cultivation-ethical assessment, may apply. This directly corresponds to Document 08 "Everyone-a-Road Guarantee System."

### 2.2 Allocation Fairness: More Work More Gain

Time resources are not evenly allocated, but by contribution — consistent with Document 06 "More-Work-More-Gain Distribution Principle" and Document 22 "Joint-Contracting Mechanism." Higher-contribution cultivators obtain more acceleration-domain quotas, but these quotas must be used for outputs that feed back network-wide, not privately retained. Allocation differences come from contribution, not plunder.

### 2.3 Exit Fairness: All May Exit

In the Quest Domain's trial design, all participants hold unconditional exit right (linking Entry 520 trial-design ethical boundary). In the acceleration domain's deduction, cultivators may at any moment suspend and return to reality. No one is "trapped" in the time domain — this fundamentally contrasts with predatory-faction systems where "once the protagonist enters a secret realm he is forced to clear it."

## III. Quantitative Indicators of Time Fairness

The dual-track time domain's time fairness is not a slogan, but has quantitative indicators:

| Indicator | Definition | Target Value |
|-----------|------------|--------------|
| Time-Domain Qualification Coverage | Proportion of Myriad Ranks obtaining time-domain use qualification | ≥99% |
| Time-Quota Gini Coefficient | Inequality of acceleration-domain quota allocation among Myriad Ranks | ≤0.3 |
| Feedback Rate | Proportion of acceleration-domain outputs feeding back to the Alaya Network | =100% |
| Exit Rate | Actual freedom of Quest-Domain participants exercising exit right | =100% |
| Myriad-Rank Access Rate | Proportion of five lineages接入 the time domain | =100% |

These indicators are continuously monitored by the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision, regularly reported to the Anchor Council. Time fairness may be measured — precisely law-first axiom's expression in the allocation field.

## IV. Time Fairness and Intergenerational Fairness

Time fairness is not only intra-generational fairness, but also cross-generational fairness. In predatory-faction systems, the older generation of strong ones occupies time resources through plunder; the new generation of cultivators from the start faces a locked ceiling. The dual-track time domain's correction:

- Acceleration-domain outputs feed back network-wide; the new generation may directly stand on the previous generation's deduction results, no need to start from zero;
- The Quest Domain's civilizational memory sedimentation ensures each generation's Dao-realization results do not vanish with the individual's death;
- The joint-contracting mechanism ensures time resources are not permanently occupied by the previous generation, but dynamically adjust by contribution.

Intergenerational fairness is time fairness's extension in time depth — **today's time allocation may not be at the cost of the time possibilities of ten thousand generations later**.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: time fairness ensures every bit of time resource flows to contributors not plunderers, avoiding time inefficiently monopolized and wasted
- **Anti-predation**: directly cuts off "the strong monopolizing time," this most covert form of plunder; time fairness itself is anti-predation axiom's legislation in the time dimension
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system time allocation decided by force — whoever has bigger fists secludes longer; Heng Yuan Zhou's time allocation decided by contribution; force cannot redeem time
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: time qualification coverage ≥99%, Myriad-Rank access rate =100%; time fairness covers all beings of the five lineages
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: time-allocation law, quota formula, exit mechanism all public; newly merged planes接入 the same time-fairness system; the allocation infrastructure leading to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm may infinitely expand
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Time fairness is not an abstract philosophical ideal, but the dual-track time-domain system's institutional design goal. When qualification fairness, allocation fairness, exit fairness are simultaneously realized, time is no longer the strong's private property, but Myriad Ranks' public resource — **this is republican axiom of Myriad Races' true fulfillment in the time dimension**. Time fairness is the dual-track time-domain system's most profound political-philosophical promise.
