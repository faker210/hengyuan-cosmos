# Entry 638 | Time-Domain Philosophy | Philosophical Critique of Predatory-Faction Time-View

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 57, 19 | Series: Time-Domain Philosophy (Entry 5)

## Introduction: plunder is not only grabbing energy, but grabbing time

Predatory-faction systems' plunder of civilization is usually understood as grabbing energy, resources, territory. But the dual-track time-domain system reveals a deeper plunder dimension: **the predatory faction's most fundamental plunder object is time itself**. The strong through longevity arts, seclusion arts, reincarnation arts, time secret realms, take Myriad Ranks' time resources as their own. This time-plunder is more covert, more fundamental, harder to correct than energy-plunder — because it packages "grabbing time" as "cultivation."

Six top-level axioms here become critical weapons: anti-predation axiom is critique's fundamental stance; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires pointing out time-plunder's harm to Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires contrasting symbiotic alternatives; causal self-discipline axiom requires exposing how time-plunder distorts causal chains; law-first axiom requires arguing time-view's self-consistency at the law level. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry systematically critiques predatory-faction time-view's philosophical fallacies.

## I. Four Philosophical Fallacies of Predatory-Faction Time-View

### 1.1 Fallacy 1: Time-Private-Theory

The predatory faction defaults time as personal property — "my time is my business." But this ignores time's sociality: a civilization's time structure is jointly constituted by all beings; any individual's over-occupation of time affects the whole. Treating time as private property is the philosophical premise of time-plunder.

### 1.2 Fallacy 2: Time-Linear-Theory

The predatory faction defaults time as linear, non-renewable, so "who lives longer has the advantage." This linear-theory leads to unbridled pursuit of longevity arts — no one thinks "living longer" equals "living better," no one asks who bears the cost of "living longer."

### 1.3 Fallacy 3: Time-Zero-Sum-Theory

The predatory faction defaults time as zero-sum: I occupy one more share, others have one less share. This zero-sum assumption leads to a time arms race — the strong pursue longer lifespans, more seclusion time, rather than thinking how through incremental development to let everyone have more time.

### 1.4 Fallacy 4: Time-Power-Theory

The predatory faction defaults time allocation decided by martial force: whoever has bigger fists deserves more time. This thoroughly demotes time to power's appendage, completely canceling the justice dimension of time allocation.

## II. Civilizational Consequences of Predatory-Faction Time-View

Four philosophical fallacies stacked produce systematic civilizational consequences:

| Consequence Dimension | Specific Manifestation |
|-----------------------|------------------------|
| Intergenerational Solidification | Older-generation strong ones permanently occupy time resources; the new generation from the start faces a locked ceiling |
| Causal Distortion | Time reversal, reincarnation rebirth and other operations frequently disturb causal chains; history repeatedly tampered |
| Civilizational Stagnation | The strong seclude ten thousand years; civilization in physical time has actually not advanced |
| Ethical Vacuum | Grabbing time seen as "cultivation ability" not predatory act; no judicial constraint |
| Ultimate Nihilism | The strong live long enough to find immortality boring; civilization loses existence meaning (linking Entry 539 Dragon Ball world simulation) |

### Contrast Anchor with Heng Yuan Zhou's Dual-Track Time Domain

As a contrast anchor, Heng Yuan Zhou's dual-track time domain — Origin Acceleration Domain and Quest Sediment Domain — fundamentally opposes the above five consequences. In the Origin Acceleration Domain, time acceleration is not the strong's private occupation, but joint-contracting, more-work-more-gain public resource; in the Quest Sediment Domain, time sedimentation is not the strong's seclusion, but Myriad Ranks' shared deep-experience space; the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision fully record time use, eliminating ethical vacuum; causal-closure principle prevents causal distortion; the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's transcendent ones replace ultimate nihilism with perpetual construction. This contrast is not rhetoric, but structural replacement at the law level.

## III. Representative Cases of Predatory-Faction Time-View

### 3.1 Tang-Luo System: Grace-style Time Monopoly

In the Tang-Luo world, the protagonist obtains time-acceleration cultivation qualification through system grace, while ordinary cultivators have no such qualification. Time acceleration becomes a kind of "grace" — not a right, but a reward. This time monopoly's philosophical essence: time allocation decided by a higher will (system/protagonist); Myriad Ranks have no say.

### 3.2 Shrouding-the-Heavens System: Seclusion-style Time Consumption

In the Shrouding world, the strong casually seclude for several thousand years. The energy consumed by these seclusions, the space occupied, the delayed civilizational opportunity cost, are never calculated. Time in the Shrouding system is only "the strong's measuring unit," not a resource needing allocation.

### 3.3 Dragon Ball System: Cultivation-style Time Waste

In the Dragon Ball world, Goku and others enter the Hyperbolic Time Chamber to train one year; outside only one day passes. This time acceleration entirely serves individual martial-force提升; outputs do not feed back, are not shared, are not open. Time is treated as a "cultivation accelerator," never treated as a civilizational resource.

## IV. The Symbiotic Faction's Time-View Alternative (Preview)

Critique of predatory-faction time-view is not the goal itself; the goal is pointing to the alternative. The symbiotic faction's time-view (to be developed in detail in Entry 639) core proposition:

- Time is civilization's public resource, not personal private property;
- Time structure may through dual-track design simultaneously realize acceleration and sedimentation;
- Time allocation by contribution not martial force;
- Time investment serves civilizational increment not individual inflation.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: predatory-faction time-view wastes Myriad Ranks' time resources on individual martial-force inflation; critique's purpose is pointing out this waste's philosophical root
- **Anti-predation**: time-plunder is the predatory faction's most fundamental plunder form; this entry strips it from the packaging of "cultivation"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's grace-style time monopoly is predatory-faction time-view's archetype; this entry directly names and critiques it
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: time-plunder's victims are all beings of Myriad Ranks; critique covers the five lineages
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: predatory-faction time-view's law definition has fundamental defects in self-consistency (cannot explain time-allocation's justice); Heng Yuan Zhou's law-first axiom requires correcting this defect
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The predatory faction's time-view fundamental problem is not whether it is "fast" or "slow," but that it demotes time from civilization's public resource to the strong's private property. This demotion is the philosophical root of all time injustice. **Without critiquing time-view, one cannot truly critique the predatory faction** — because the plunderer's deepest plunder object is not energy, but time.
