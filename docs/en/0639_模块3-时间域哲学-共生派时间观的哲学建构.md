# Entry 639 | Time-Domain Philosophy | Philosophical Construction of the Symbiotic Faction's Time-View

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 01, 03, 25 | Series: Time-Domain Philosophy (Entry 6)

## Introduction: from critique to construction — the symbiotic faction's time-view's positive manifesto

Entry 638 critiqued the predatory faction's time-view's four philosophical fallacies. But critique is not the endpoint; construction is. The symbiotic faction's time-view must positively answer: **if time is not personal private property, what is it? If time allocation is not decided by force, by what? If time cannot be zero-sum occupied, how to let Myriad Ranks simultaneously have more time?** This entry, as the symbiotic faction's time-view's positive construction, gives a systematic answer.

Six top-level axioms here are not only critical weapons, but construction blueprints: anti-predation axiom defines time-resource development methods; republican axiom of Myriad Races defines time-resource allocation subjects; collective ascension axiom defines time-investment service direction; causal self-discipline axiom defines time-operation ethical boundaries; law-first axiom defines time-law self-consistency standards. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Five Core Propositions of the Symbiotic Faction's Time-View

### 1.1 Proposition 1: Time Is Civilization's Public Resource

Time does not belong to any individual, but to civilization as a whole. The individual's right to use time comes from civilization's authorization, not from force. This is not a moral ideal, but a law fact — time structure is part of the law network, and the law network belongs to Myriad Ranks (Document 20 anchor structure and hierarchy).

### 1.2 Proposition 2: Time May Be Incrementally Developed

The predatory faction defaults time as zero-sum; occupying more means grabbing more. The symbiotic faction believes that through law-level time-flow regulation, additional time windows may be created for civilization without reducing anyone's time. The Origin Acceleration Domain is this incremental development's technological implementation — hundredfold flow is not grabbed from mortals, but incremental space created through law engineering.

### 1.3 Proposition 3: Time Allocation by Contribution

Time resources' allocation is not by martial force, not by birth, not by grace, but by contribution. Contribution calculation is jointly completed by the joint-contracting mechanism (Document 22) and the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision. This ensures time flows to where civilizational increment contribution is greatest, not to the strongest fist.

### 1.4 Proposition 4: Time Investment Must Feed Back

Any output obtained in the time domain — gongfu, technology, Dao-realization — must feed back to the Alaya Network, network-wide shared. Privately retaining time outputs is equivalent to stealing public resources. This proposition directly binds time allocation with collective ascension axiom.

### 1.5 Proposition 5: Time Law Stable and Self-Consistent

Time's law definition — flow, allocation, switching, feedback — once legislated by the Anchor Council, never retcons, never arbitrarily changes. No plot need may kidnap time law. This is law-first axiom's direct implementation in the time domain.

## III. The Symbiotic Faction's Time-View and Myriad Ranks' Daily Life

The symbiotic faction's time-view is not a lofty philosophical construction; it directly lands as Myriad Ranks' daily experience:

- A human youth studying in Sense Origin Realm knows he may apply to the acceleration domain to deduce gongfu; outputs must feed back — this is propositions 3 and 4's daily fulfillment;
- A planet consciousness in long evolution needs a sediment period to realize planetary life; the Quest Sediment Domain provides it undisturbed immersion space — this is propositions 2 and 4's landing;
- An exotic beast clan choosing slow experience rather than accelerated cultivation, the Myriad-Ranks-Self-Chosen-Destiny mechanism (Entry 525) guarantees this choice — this is Proposition 1's "public resource" not equal to "forced averaging."

## IV. The Symbiotic Faction's Time-View Philosophical Uniqueness

Compared to various historical time philosophies, the symbiotic faction's time-view has several unique features:

1. **It is not pure speculation, but an engineering-implementable law system** — the Origin Acceleration Domain and Quest Sediment Domain are time-philosophy's technical embodiment;
2. **It does not pursue individual immortality, but civilizational perpetuity** — the time-view's service object is civilization as a whole, not the individual;
3. **It neither treats time as enemy nor as master, but as co-building material** — time is neither a passage to be fought against nor an eternity to be worshipped, but a resource Myriad Ranks may jointly develop.

The symbiotic faction's time-view is not the predatory faction's "improved version," but from the fundamental premise redefines time. When time shifts from "the strong's private property" to "civilization's public resource," when time allocation shifts from "force decides" to "contribution decides," when time investment shifts from "individual inflation" to "civilizational joint progress" — the dual-track time domain is no longer a technical facility, but a brand-new civilizational time philosophy. **This is one of Heng Yuan Zhou's most unique contributions to cosmic-civilizational thought**.
