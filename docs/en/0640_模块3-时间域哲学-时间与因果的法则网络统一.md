# Entry 640 | Time-Domain Philosophy | Law-Network Unity of Time and Causality

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 28, 47, 20 | Series: Time-Domain Philosophy (Entry 7)

## Introduction: move time, and you move causality — this is the law network's iron rule

In the predatory faction's time-view, time operations and causal operations are two things: seclusion is seclusion, changing history is changing history; the two are unrelated. But Heng Yuan Zhou's law network reveals a deeper structure: **time and causality are not two parallel lines, but two dimensions of the same relational web in the law network**. Any operation on time — acceleration, deceleration, reversal, branching — produces ripples in the causal network. This is not a "side effect," but the law network's basic structure.

Six top-level axioms are concentrated here: causal self-discipline axiom directly requires time operations not disturb causality; anti-predation axiom forbids using time operations to plunder causal chains; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires time-causal operations transparent to Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires time-causal operations serve civilizational increment; law-first axiom requires time-causal law definition stable and self-consistent. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry argues: **how do time and causality achieve unity in the law network? What does this unity mean for dual-track time-domain design?**

## I. The Law-Network Structure of Time and Causality

### 1.1 The Causal Network's Time Dimension

Causal chains are not "instantaneous"; they unfold in time. A cause produces an effect, between which a time interval is needed. This time interval is not neutral — it determines the causal chain's "conduction speed" and "intervention window." The Origin Acceleration Domain magnifies the causal chain's conduction speed a hundredfold: inside the acceleration domain, a cause producing an effect takes only one hundredth of outside time.

### 1.2 Causal Effects of Time Operations

Any time operation is not "idling":

| Time Operation | Causal Effect | Risk Level |
|----------------|---------------|------------|
| Acceleration-domain deduction | Causal chain closes in accelerated space, does not affect external causality | Low |
| Quest-domain immersion | Causal chain unfolds in simulated space, no trauma carried out on exit | Low |
| Time branching | Produces parallel causal lines, needs Alaya Network isolation | Medium |
| Time reversal | Rewrites existing causal chains, extremely high risk | Extremely High |
| Cross-plane time interference | Disturbs foreign-plane causal chains, needs strict reporting | High |

The dual-track time domain's design principle: **priority use of low-risk operations (accelerated deduction, immersive simulation), strictly restrict high-risk operations (reversal, branching, cross-plane interference)**.

### 1.3 The Alaya Network's Causal Audit

The Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision not only records time operations, but also audits their causal effects. Each entry into the acceleration domain, the Alaya Network pre-judges the causal ripple range this operation may produce; each exit from the Quest Domain, the Alaya Network confirms that the causal chain in the experience was not mistakenly brought into reality. This causal audit is causal self-discipline axiom's implementation at the technical level.

## II. Time-Causal Unity's Constraints on Dual-Track Design

### 2.1 The Acceleration Domain's Causal Closure Principle

All deduction, experiment, trial-and-error inside the Origin Acceleration Domain proceed in a causal-closed space. That is: causal chains produced inside the acceleration domain do not overflow to external reality. This is why the acceleration domain may "safely" do hundredfold deduction — its causal consequences are sealed inside the domain, do not change the external world's history.

### 2.2 The Quest Domain's Causal Desensitization Principle

Trial experiences inside the Quest Sediment Domain, before exit, must go through "causal desensitization": what the cultivator takes away is insight and wisdom, not traumatic causal traces. Death, failure, pain in simulation do not convert into real causal debt. This is completely different from the predatory faction's "traveler changes history" approach — the Quest Domain's causal chain closes in simulation, zeroed on exit.

### 2.3 Cross-Plane Time Operations' Reporting Principle

Cross-plane time operations (such as Entry 547 causal self-discipline axiom's correction application) must strictly follow: observe first, interfere later, only knowledge returned, causality not disturbed. Time-causal unity means: any cross-plane time operation may produce chain reactions in the foreign plane's causal network, so must be reported, must be limited, must be traceable.

## III. Time-Causal Unity's Philosophical Meaning

### 3.1 Opposing Time-Travel Fantasies

In predatory-faction systems, time travel, time reversal, parallel universes are casually used as "the strong's ability." But time-causal law-network unity tells us: **time reversal is not "returning to the past," but rewriting the causal chain**. Rewriting the causal chain's consequences cannot be borne by the individual — it affects all beings' fate in the causal network. Heng Yuan Zhou strictly restricts time reversal not because technology cannot do it, but because law does not allow it.

### 3.2 Opposing Time Zero-Sum Fantasies

The predatory faction defaults time as zero-sum. But time-causal unity reveals: through incremental development (acceleration domain) and deep sedimentation (Quest domain), time may increase without harming anyone. The causal network is not a closed zero-sum pool — it may be expanded through law engineering.

### 3.3 Supporting Time Fairness

Time-causal unity means: a strong one through time operations plunders not only time, but causal opportunities. The time he occupies more is essentially the evolutionary opportunity Myriad Ranks could have had in the causal chain. Time fairness is therefore not only a time-allocation problem, but a causal-justice problem.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: time-causal unity ensures acceleration-domain and Quest-domain time investment does not produce causal-overflow waste; every bit of time consumption produces corresponding increment in the law-closed space
- **Anti-predation**: cuts off the "time-reversal-changes-history" style of causal plunder; time-causal operations' impact on the causal network is strictly limited
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system time operations and causal operations are disconnected, the protagonist arbitrarily changes history with no consequence; Heng Yuan Zhou time-causal unity, any time operation has causal audit
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: time-causal operations treat Myriad Ranks equally; causal audit covers all time-domain participants
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: time-causal unity's law definition, audit protocol, reporting process all public; newly merged planes接入 the same time-causal law network; the causal infrastructure leading to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm may infinitely expand
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Time and causality in the law network are not two things, but two dimensions of the same relational web. The dual-track time-domain system is safe, controllable, sustainable precisely because from the start it considers time operations within the framework of the causal network — **the acceleration domain has causal closure, the Quest domain has causal desensitization, cross-plane has causal reporting**. This unity is not a restriction on time freedom, but its true guarantee: only when causality is not disturbed is Myriad Ranks' time safe.
