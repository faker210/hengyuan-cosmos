# Entry 641 | Time-Domain Philosophy | Symbiotic-Constitution Guarantee of Time and Free Will

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 40, 26, 29 | Series: Time-Domain Philosophy (Entry 8 · Philosophy-Series Conclusion)

## Introduction: a person decided by time — is he still a free person

Free will is one of philosophy's oldest questions. But the dual-track time-domain system raises a new free-will dimension: **does a cultivator decided by time flow still have free will?** If the acceleration domain magnifies human thought speed a hundredfold, and the Quest domain magnifies human experience depth a hundredfold, then human choices in these time structures — are they "free choices" or "choices decided by the time structure"? This question unresolved, the dual-track time domain is only a technical facility, not a civilizational institution.

Six top-level axioms here become free will's guarantee framework: anti-predation axiom forbids using time structure to control others; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings' time-choice right protected; collective ascension axiom requires time structure serve freedom not restrict it; causal self-discipline axiom requires time intervention not deprive others' choice space; law-first axiom requires time and free-will law definition stable and self-consistent. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry argues: **how does the dual-track time domain, at the symbiotic-constitution level, guarantee Myriad Ranks' time free will?**

## I. Triple Tension of Time and Free Will

### 1.1 Tension 1: Does Flow Acceleration Dilute Freedom

In the acceleration domain, a cultivator experiences a hundred years internally in one external day. Are the choices in these hundred years — what to deduce, what to experiment, what to abandon — still "his" choices? If the acceleration domain's algorithm automatically recommends the optimal path, and the cultivator just follows the recommendation, is his free will in the acceleration domain diluted?

Heng Yuan Zhou's solution: the Origin Acceleration Domain provides deduction assistance, but the final choice is always in the cultivator's hands. The algorithm recommends path A; the cultivator may choose path B, path C, even path D — as long as D's causal risk is within controllable range. The acceleration domain does not make decisions for you; it only unfolds your decisions a hundred times faster.

### 1.2 Tension 2: Does Quest Immersion Close Freedom

In the Quest Sediment Domain, the cultivator enters a simulated scene, experiences a trial. The scene's setting is designed by designers — then is the cultivator's "choice" in the scene just going through the motions in a preset script? Is this equal to depriving free will?

Heng Yuan Zhou's solution: the Quest Domain's scene design follows the "open framework" principle — given situations and resources, but not given scripts. How the cultivator acts in the scene, how he interacts with others, how he faces difficulties, are entirely his own decision. After the scene ends, the Alaya Network evaluates his choice quality, not whether he "followed the script."

### 1.3 Tension 3: Does Time Allocation Restrict Freedom

Time resources allocated by contribution — higher-contribution people obtain more acceleration quotas. Does this mean "people not working hard enough do not deserve more time"? Does this allocation restrict some people's time freedom?

Heng Yuan Zhou's solution: what time allocates is "extra acceleration quota," not "basic time right." All beings' basic time — the material plane's normal lifespan, basic cultivation time — is unaffected by contribution. Contribution only decides whether you may obtain **extra** acceleration-domain quota. More work more gain; not working not less gain. This is consistent with Document 26 individual-collective balance principle.

## II. The Symbiotic Constitution's Five Guarantees of Time Freedom

### 2.1 Guarantee 1: Time Choice Right

The Myriad-Ranks-Self-Chosen-Destiny mechanism (Entry 525)'s core is time choice right: every being may autonomously choose the acceleration route, sediment route, or dual-track parallel. No compulsion — you may reject the acceleration domain and choose to cultivate at your own rhythm in normal flow.

### 2.2 Guarantee 2: Exit Right

All time-domain participation is voluntary, and may exit at any time. In the Quest Domain's trials, participants have the right at any moment to suspend and return to reality — this clause is written into the trial-design ethical boundary (Entry 520), may not be overturned by any "plot need."

### 2.3 Guarantee 3: Right to Know

Time-domain participants have the right to know: the current domain's time flow, possible causal effects, the post-exit experience desensitization process. The right to know is not concealed — this is republican axiom of Myriad Races' direct implementation in the time domain.

### 2.4 Guarantee 4: Right of Appeal

If the time domain's design, allocation, operation is believed to have violated free will, participants may appeal to the symbiotic court. The Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's fully-recorded process provides the evidentiary basis for appeal.

### 2.5 Guarantee 5: Right of Refusal

Any being has the right to refuse being included in any time-domain project — even if this project is "obviously beneficial" to him. Parents may not decide for children; the strong may not decide for the weak; the majority may not decide for the minority. Time freedom is the individual's basic right.

## III. Time Freedom and Collective Ascension's Balance

Time freedom is not anarchism. The symbiotic constitution's design of time freedom is always within the individual-collective balance framework (Document 26):

| Individual Right | Collective Guarantee |
|------------------|----------------------|
| Choose which time route to take | Acceleration-domain and Quest-domain infrastructure provided by co-building |
| Exit the time domain | Post-exit experience desensitization guaranteed by the Alaya Network |
| Reject specific time projects | Rejection does not affect basic cultivation qualification |
| Appeal unfair time allocation | The symbiotic court provides independent adjudication channels |

This balance ensures: time freedom does not become time abuse; collective guarantee does not become time compulsion.

## IV. Contrast with the Predatory Faction's "Being Decided"

In predatory-faction systems, time's decisive effect on the individual is far stronger than in Heng Yuan Zhou:

- Travelers are decided by the system's fate (Tang-Luo-style grace);
- The protagonist is decided by protagonist aura on his growth path (Perfect-World-style arbitrational);
- Reincarnators are decided by past-life causality on this life's direction (Shrouding-style predestination).

In predatory-faction systems, the individual's time free will almost does not exist — you are decided by your birth, your golden finger, your fate. Heng Yuan Zhou's dual-track time domain precisely returns free will to the individual: **time structure is a tool, not master; you use time, not time uses you**.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: time freedom ensures cultivators autonomously choose the time route best suited to them, avoiding being forced into an unsuitable flow and wasting time
- **Anti-predation**: cuts off the "strong deciding time for the weak," this most covert time control; time choice right is every being's basic right
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo protagonist's time fate is decided by system grace; ordinary cultivators have no choice qualification; Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad Ranks have complete time choice, exit, refusal rights
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the five time-freedom guarantees equally apply to all beings of Myriad Ranks, regardless of race, strength, lineage
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the five time-freedom guarantees as symbiotic-constitution clauses all public; newly merged planes automatically apply; the time-freedom law system leading to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm may infinitely expand
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Time and free will are not opposites. A good time system does not eliminate time's influence on humans, but ensures humans remain their own masters in time. The dual-track time-domain system, through five constitutional guarantees of choice, exit, knowledge, appeal, refusal, ensures Myriad Ranks always retain free will in time structures. **This is why the dual-track time domain is not a prison, but freedom — a law-guaranteed, Myriad-Ranks-shared, infinitely expandable time freedom**.
