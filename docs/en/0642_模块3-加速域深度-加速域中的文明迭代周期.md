# Entry 642 | Acceleration-Domain Depth | Civilizational Iteration Cycles in the Acceleration Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 11, 10, 25 | Series: Acceleration-Domain Depth (Entry 1)

## Introduction: civilization does not advance linearly, but iteratively

Predatory-faction systems' civilizational-development narrative is usually linear: protagonist cultivates → gets stronger → fights monsters → levels up → gets stronger again. This linear narrative simplifies civilizational development into a monotonic process of "getting stronger." But Heng Yuan Zhou's Origin Acceleration Domain reveals civilization's true structure: **civilization does not advance linearly, but iteratively** — each iteration contains "raise question → deduce scheme → trial-verify → correct direction → sediment results" five stages, cyclically recurring, spiraling upward.

Six top-level axioms take effect here: anti-predation axiom requires iteration outputs serve incremental development; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires iteration results network-wide shared; collective ascension axiom requires iteration serve Myriad-Rank joint progress; causal self-discipline axiom requires iteration process not disturb others' causality; law-first axiom requires iteration-cycle law definition stable and self-consistent. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry disassembles the complete structure of civilizational iteration cycles in the acceleration domain.

## I. Five-Stage Model of Civilizational Iteration Cycles

### 1.1 Stage 1: Question Raising

Each iteration starts from a clear civilizational question. This question may come from: a real-plane technical bottleneck, a cultivation system's breakthrough need, a new contradiction arising in Myriad-Rank symbiosis. Question raising is not arbitrary — the Anchor Council, based on Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision data analysis, determines the direction civilization most needs to iterate.

### 1.2 Stage 2: Deduction Unfolding

After the question is determined, enter the Origin Acceleration Domain for hundredfold deduction. The deduction iteration system (Document 11) fully operates at this stage: cultivators inside the acceleration domain deduce multiple possible schemes, each scheme unfolded, analyzed, evaluated. This stage's output is a large candidate scheme set and corresponding risk predictions.

### 1.3 Stage 3: Trial-and-Error Verification

Candidate schemes may not directly apply to reality; they must undergo controlled trial-and-error inside the acceleration domain. Trial-and-error's purpose is to verify scheme feasibility, discover unforeseen side effects, test performance under extreme conditions. Schemes that fail trial-and-error are eliminated; successful ones enter the next stage.

### 1.4 Stage 4: Direction Correction

Schemes after trial-and-error often need correction: maybe parameter adjustment, maybe structural reconstruction, maybe complete restart. The corrected scheme is trial-and-errored again — this "trial-and-error-correction" cycle may repeat inside the acceleration domain until the scheme reaches acceptable maturity.

### 1.5 Stage 5: Sediment Feedback

The matured scheme exits the acceleration domain, passes Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's causal audit, and synchronously sediments insights in the Quest Sediment Domain, then feeds back to the real plane and the network-wide shared library. What feeds back is not only the scheme itself, but also deduction-process trial-and-error records, failure lessons, risk warnings — these "negative knowledge" are equally precious outputs of civilizational iteration.

## II. Time Structure of an Iteration Cycle

A complete civilizational iteration cycle's time structure in the acceleration domain:

| Stage | Intra-Domain Time Share | Corresponding External Time (100× Acceleration) | Key Output |
|-------|--------------------------|------------------------------------------------|------------|
| Question Raising | 5% | ~0.5 day | Question definition document |
| Deduction Unfolding | 30% | ~3 days | Candidate scheme set |
| Trial-and-Error Verification | 40% | ~4 days | Verification report |
| Direction Correction | 20% | ~2 days | Corrected scheme |
| Sediment Feedback | 5% | ~0.5 day | Feedback data packet |

A complete iteration cycle in the acceleration domain takes about ten days; outside only about 0.1 day passed — less than three hours.

## III. Iteration Cycles' Stacking and Compound Interest

A single iteration cycle's output is limited, but iteration cycles may stack: the first iteration's results become the second's foundation, the second's output becomes the third's starting point. This stacking produces compound-interest effects:

- First round: deduce basic scheme A
- Second round: deduce improved scheme A+ on A's basis
- Third round: deduce breakthrough scheme A++ on A+'s basis
- ……

Stack a hundred iterations inside the hundredfold acceleration domain; outside only ten days passed — but civilization within these ten days completed the iteration volume ordinary conditions would take ten years to complete. This is the Origin Acceleration Domain's civilizational acceleration principle.

## IV. Iteration Cycles and Myriad-Rank Participation

Civilizational iteration cycles are not a few elites' patent. Republican axiom of Myriad Races requires iteration process open to Myriad Ranks:

- Question-raising stage: Myriad Ranks may submit question suggestions through the Alaya Network;
- Deduction-unfolding stage: each clan's cultivators may apply to participate in deduction;
- Trial-and-error-verification stage: different lineages' cultivators may verify schemes from their own angles;
- Direction-correction stage: Myriad-Rank feedback is included in correction reference;
- Sediment-feedback stage: results network-wide shared, Myriad Ranks jointly use.

This open iteration ensures civilizational-development direction is not monopolized by a few, but truly reflects Myriad Ranks' common needs.

- **Anti-energy waste**: iteration cycles structure time investment — each stage's time has clear output, not wasted on aimless seclusion-style cultivation
- **Anti-predation**: iteration outputs all feedback network-wide; there is no case of "the strong deduce then privately hoard for self-use"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system the protagonist's cultivation is linear (cultivate → get stronger → fight monsters), no iteration structure; Heng Yuan Zhou's civilizational development is five-stage iterative, spiraling upward
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the iteration cycle's five stages all open to Myriad Ranks' participation, regardless of racial lineage
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the iteration cycle's five-stage model, time structure, stacking rules all public; newly merged planes may directly接入; the iteration infrastructure leading to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm may infinitely expand
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The civilizational iteration cycle is the Origin Acceleration Domain's core operating logic. It turns "fast" from directionless speed into a directed, structured, output-bearing civilizational driving force. **In the acceleration domain, time is not used to "endure," but to "iterate" — this is the acceleration domain's most fundamental contribution to civilizational development**.
