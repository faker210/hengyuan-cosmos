# Entry 643 | Acceleration-Domain Depth | The Technology-Explosion Model in the Acceleration Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 16, 15, 11 | Series: Acceleration-Domain Depth (Entry 2)

## Introduction: hundredfold iteration stacked with hundredfold feedback is the engine of technology explosion

Technology explosion in an ordinary civilization is a vague concept — referring to the phenomenon of technology suddenly accelerating. But in the Origin Acceleration Domain, technology explosion is not a vague phenomenon, but a deterministic process that may be modeled, predicted, engineering-guided. Its engine has two: hundredfold iteration (deduction-speed amplification) and hundredfold feedback (Myriad-Rank feedback-speed amplification). The two stack, and technological development shifts from linear growth to exponential explosion.

Six top-level axioms take effect here: anti-predation axiom requires technology-explosion results come from incremental development; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires technology results network-wide shared; collective ascension axiom requires technology explosion serve Myriad-Rank joint progress; causal self-discipline axiom requires technology explosion not produce uncontrollable causal overflow; law-first axiom requires technology-explosion model's law definition stable and self-consistent. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry disassembles the complete model of technology explosion in the acceleration domain.

## I. Three-Engine Structure of Technology Explosion

### 1.1 Engine 1: Deduction Acceleration Engine

The deduction iteration system (Document 11) operates under hundredfold flow, technology-deduction speed magnified a hundredfold. This means: a technology deduction that in an ordinary civilization takes a hundred years completes in one year (intra-domain time) in the acceleration domain; outside only about three days passed. Deduction acceleration is technology explosion's first driving force.

### 1.2 Engine 2: Feedback Acceleration Engine

After technology results feed back from the acceleration domain to the real plane, Myriad-Rank usage feedback is rapidly collected through the Alaya Network. In an ordinary civilization, a technology from release to collecting enough feedback data may take years; in Heng Yuan Zhou, feedback is real-time summarized through the Alaya Network; Myriad-Rank usage data, problem reports, improvement suggestions within hours may be transmitted back to the acceleration domain. Feedback acceleration makes technology iteration's direction correction more precise.

### 1.3 Engine 3: Cross-Disciplinary Fusion Engine

Inside the acceleration domain cultivators of the five lineages simultaneously converge — the human-race technical route, the exotic-beast race's natural route, the mechanical race's engineering route, the consciousness race's information route, the mixed race's cross-disciplinary route — under hundredfold flow they collide, cross-fuse. This cross-disciplinary fusion in an ordinary civilization may take hundreds of years to accidentally occur; inside the acceleration domain it may systematically arise within dozens of intra-domain years.

## III. Safety Boundaries of Technology Explosion

Technology explosion is not technology out of control. Technology explosion in the acceleration domain has three safety boundaries:

### 3.1 Causal-Closure Boundary

Technology deduction and experiments inside the acceleration domain proceed in a causal-closed space (Entry 640 time-causal unity); technology explosion's causal consequences do not overflow to external reality.

### 3.2 Feedback-Audit Boundary

When technology results exit the acceleration domain, they must pass Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's causal audit. Technologies that pass audit may feed back to the real plane; technologies that fail audit continue to be corrected inside the acceleration domain.

### 3.3 Ethical-Review Boundary

Technologies involving high-risk domains (such as cross-plane operations, large-scale energy scheduling) must pass the Anchor Council's ethical review before feeding back. Technology explosion may not break through ethical boundaries.

## IV. Technology Explosion and the Quest Domain's Coordination

Technology explosion does not mean civilization may "only want fast not deep." Technologies produced by the acceleration domain need the Quest Sediment Domain to digest:

- New technology's social impact needs deep simulation in the Quest Domain, evaluating its long-term impact on Myriad-Rank life;
- Ethical questions brought by new technology need philosophical contemplation in the Quest Domain, not only accelerated deduction;
- New technology's cultural acceptance needs immersive testing in the Quest Domain, ensuring Myriad Ranks are not overwhelmed by technology explosion.

Dual-track synergy: the acceleration domain is responsible for technology explosion's "fast," the Quest Domain for technology digestion's "deep."
