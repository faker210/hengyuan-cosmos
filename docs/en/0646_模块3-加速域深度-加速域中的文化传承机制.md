# Entry 646 | Acceleration-Domain Depth | Cultural-Transmission Mechanism in the Acceleration Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 37, 21, 05 | Series: Acceleration-Domain Depth (Entry 5)

## Introduction: when cultural change is a hundred times faster, how does transmission not break the thread

Technology may iterate a hundredfold, but may culture iterate a hundredfold? May a song, a tradition, a way of life rapidly update in hundredfold flow? If culture really accelerates a hundredfold along with technology, will civilization lose cultural continuity — within one generation culture changes, tradition breaks, identity blurs? This is the fundamental challenge the acceleration domain poses to cultural transmission.

Six top-level axioms take effect here: anti-predation axiom requires cultural transmission not be by plundering other cultures; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires Myriad-Rank clans' cultures equally transmitted; collective ascension axiom requires cultural transmission serve Myriad-Rank joint progress; causal self-discipline axiom requires cultural transmission not cut historical causality; law-first axiom requires cultural-transmission law definition stable and self-consistent. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry analyzes the special mechanism of cultural transmission in the acceleration domain.

## I. The Acceleration Domain's Triple Challenge to Cultural Transmission

### 1.1 Challenge 1: Intergenerational Cultural Fault

In an ordinary civilization, culture passes through generations: elders to juniors, each generation has enough time to digest the previous generation's cultural results and develop its own. But in the acceleration domain, a cultivator may within external months experience intra-domain decades of growth — his cultural experience has far surpassed the contemporaries left on the real plane. When he returns to reality, his and surrounding people's cultural languages already differ. Intergenerational cultural fault thus arises.

### 1.2 Challenge 2: Cultural Traditions Acceleratingly Dissolved

Technology explosion brings new ways of life, new values, new expression forms. Traditional cultural forms — rituals, festivals, customs — in rapid change are easily dissolved. Without protection, cultural traditions may within a few external generations disappear completely.

### 1.3 Challenge 3: Cultural Homogenization Risk

The acceleration domain converges Myriad-Rank cultivators; different cultures in hundredfold flow frequently collide. Collision may produce new cultures, but may also lead to cultural homogenization — all clans become the same culture, losing diversity. Republican axiom of Myriad Races requires protecting cultural diversity; this forms tension with the acceleration domain's homogenization tendency.

## II. Five Defense Lines of Cultural Transmission in the Acceleration Domain

### 2.1 Defense 1: Alaya Cultural Memory Repository

The Alaya Network not only records technology and causality, but also records culture. All cultural results — poetry, music, ritual, philosophy, custom — upon generation are collected into the Alaya cultural memory repository, permanently preserved. Even if some cultural form is no longer popular in reality, it in the Alaya Network remains queryable, learnable, revivable. This ensures cultural transmission does not completely break due to real-world change.

### 2.2 Defense 2: Quest-Domain Cultural Immersion Courses

The Quest Sediment Domain opens dedicated cultural immersion courses: cultivators may in equal-speed immersion experience different eras, different clans' cultural life. This immersive cultural education may not be completed in the acceleration domain — cultural experience needs time to feel — so it is placed in the Quest Domain. The acceleration domain produces cultural-analysis frameworks; the Quest domain produces cultural-experience depth.

### 2.3 Defense 3: Myriad-Rank Cultural Protection Zone

Outside the acceleration domain's influence range, a Myriad-Rank cultural protection zone is established: inside the protection zone time flow remains normal, cultural traditions continue at their own rhythm. Any clan may choose to place its cultural core area inside the protection zone, not swept along by the acceleration domain's change speed. This is the Myriad-Ranks-Self-Chosen-Destiny mechanism (Entry 525)'s expression in the cultural field.

### 2.4 Defense 4: Cultural-Diversity Assessment Indicators

The Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision continuously monitors cultural-diversity indicators: number of Myriad-Rank cultural forms, each culture's living-transmission population, cultural-innovation rate. When diversity indicators decline, the Anchor Council starts cultural-protection intervention. Cultural diversity is treated as civilizational health's core indicator, not an optional decoration.

### 2.5 Defense 5: New Culture's Symbiotic Fusion Mechanism

Cultural collision does not necessarily lead to homogenization. Heng Yuan Zhou designs a symbiotic-fusion mechanism: different cultures after colliding in the acceleration domain do not replace each other, but fuse to produce new cultural forms. This fusion is not averaging, but organic synthesis — retaining each's uniqueness, while producing new common expression.

## III. Time Allocation for Cultural Transmission

| Cultural Activity | Time-Domain Choice | Reason |
|-------------------|--------------------|--------|
| Cultural analysis, comparative study | Origin Acceleration Domain | May rapidly deduce cultural models |
| Cultural immersive experience | Quest Sediment Domain | Needs time to feel and realize |
| Cultural tradition protection | Myriad-Rank Cultural Protection Zone | Needs normal time flow |
| New cultural creation | Dual-track parallel | Accelerated deduction + sedimented insight |

## IV. Contrast with Predatory Faction's Cultural Eradication

In predatory-faction systems, cultural transmission's fate is extremely tragic:

- After the strong conquer a civilization, its culture is completely destroyed (devouring-style);
- The "cultural advantage" brought by protagonist aura crushes all other cultures;
- Technology develops faster than culture, leading culture to become technology's appendage.

Heng Yuan Zhou's cultural-transmission mechanism is precisely the opposite: culture not destroyed, not crushed, not appendaged — it is protected, immersed, fused. Culture is not civilization's decoration, but civilization's soul.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: cultural-transmission mechanism ensures cultural accumulation is not wasted because of the acceleration domain — the Alaya memory repository preserves all cultural results, does not discard because "outdated"
- **Anti-predation**: directly cuts off "the strong conquers culture then destroys it," this cultural plunder; Myriad-Rank cultures equally protected
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system culture is the strong's appendage, the conquered's culture crushed; Heng Yuan Zhou's five defense lines protect Myriad-Rank cultural diversity
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: cultural transmission covers all cultural forms of the five lineages, regardless of strength or size
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cultural-transmission five defense-line mechanisms, assessment indicators, protection-zone rules all public; newly merged planes automatically apply; the cultural infrastructure leading to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm may infinitely expand
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The acceleration domain may iterate technology a hundredfold, but may not iterate culture a hundredfold. Cultural transmission needs time to feel, to sediment, to continue. Heng Yuan Zhou uses five defense lines — Alaya memory repository, Quest-domain immersion, cultural protection zone, diversity assessment, symbiotic fusion — to ensure culture in the accelerating era does not break the thread. **Technology may be fast, culture must be deep; dual-track parallel, civilization has both speed and soul**.
