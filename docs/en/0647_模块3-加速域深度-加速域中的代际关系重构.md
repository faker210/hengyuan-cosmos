# Entry 647 | Acceleration-Domain Depth | Intergenerational Relationship Reconstruction in the Acceleration Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 36, 23, 08 | Series: Acceleration-Domain Depth (Entry 6 · Acceleration-Domain-Depth Conclusion)

## Introduction: when within one generation there is a "time gap," how are intergenerational relations defined

In traditional civilizations, intergenerational relations are divided by physiological age: parents' generation, children's generation; each generation experiences a similar length of time. But the Origin Acceleration Domain breaks this premise — a cultivator who stayed one year (intra-domain time) in the acceleration domain, outside only three days passed, but his psychological age, technical level, social experience may have grown decades. Meanwhile, contemporaries left on the real plane grew only three days. **Within one generation a huge "time gap" appears — intergenerational relations are no longer defined by physiological age, but by time-domain experience**.

Six top-level axioms take effect here: anti-predation axiom requires the acceleration domain not cause intergenerational plunder; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires intergenerational opportunity equality; collective ascension axiom requires the previous generation's results serve the next; causal self-discipline axiom requires intergenerational relations not create causal injustice; law-first axiom requires intergenerational-relation law definition stable and self-consistent. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry analyzes the intergenerational-relationship reconstruction driven by the acceleration domain.

## I. Three Assumptions of Traditional Intergenerational Relations and Their Collapse

### 1.1 Assumption 1: Contemporaries Experience Similar Time

Traditional assumption: the same generation grows in similar time flow, therefore has similar experience, similar cognition, similar social status. The acceleration domain breaks this assumption — among contemporaries, some have entered the acceleration domain, some have not; some entered multiple times, some only once. Their "effective experienced time" may differ a hundredfold.

### 1.2 Assumption 2: Elders Are Naturally Older Than Juniors

Traditional assumption: elders experience more time, therefore more experienced, wiser. The acceleration domain breaks this assumption: a young person who has entered the acceleration domain may technically deduction-experience far exceed an elder who never entered. "Old" no longer equals "experienced more."

### 1.3 Assumption 3: Intergenerational Transmission Is Unidirectional

Traditional assumption: elders transmit to juniors, direction top-down. The acceleration domain breaks this assumption: new technologies, new theories a young person deduces in the acceleration domain may be back-transmitted to elders who never entered the acceleration domain. Knowledge flow becomes bidirectional even multi-directional.

## II. Five Dimensions of Intergenerational-Relationship Reconstruction

### 2.1 From Age Authority to Contribution Authority

Intergenerational authority is no longer determined by age, but by contribution. A young person who produces a major technical breakthrough in the acceleration domain may in the Alaya Network's contribution ranking exceed many elders. This does not mean elders have no value — elders' value lies in mind-nature, wisdom, social experience, which the acceleration domain cannot accelerate. Intergenerational authority shifts from "age" to "each has strengths."

### 2.2 From Unidirectional Transmission to Network Sharing

Knowledge transmission is no longer the unidirectional chain of "elders teach juniors," but multi-point sharing in the Alaya Network. Any cultivator — regardless of age — inside the acceleration domain's outputs all feedback network-wide; the young may learn elders' wisdom, elders may also learn the young's technology. Intergenerational transmission becomes network sharing.

### 2.3 From Solidified Generations to Mobile Generations

Traditional generations are solidified: which generation you are born into is which. In the acceleration domain, generational labels become mobile: a cultivator may choose to grow in normal flow (keeping pace with contemporaries), or choose accelerated growth (on "effective experience" ahead of contemporaries). Generational belonging is no longer determined by birth year, but by time-domain experience.

### 2.4 From Intergenerational Conflict to Intergenerational Complementarity

Traditional intergenerational relations are full of conflict: elders' experience and juniors' new technologies contradict. Intergenerational relations in the acceleration domain move toward complementarity: elders' mind-nature wisdom and juniors' technical breakthroughs form complementarity, not conflict. The Quest Sediment Domain provides space for elders' mind-nature sedimentation; the acceleration domain provides space for juniors' technical deduction — two generations grow in their respective good time domains, then converge in the Alaya Network.

### 2.5 From Intergenerational Occupation to Intergenerational Feedback

The most fundamental reconstruction: in traditional society, the previous generation's strong ones limit the next generation's development by occupying resources (predatory-faction systems' intergenerational solidification). In Heng Yuan Zhou, the previous generation's acceleration-domain outputs all feedback network-wide; the next generation directly stands on the previous generation's results to continue iterating. Intergenerational relations shift from "the previous generation occupying the next generation's opportunities" to "the previous generation nourishing the next generation's development."

## IV. Contrast with Predatory Faction's Intergenerational Solidification

Predatory-faction systems' intergenerational relations are the worst: the older generation's strong ones permanently occupy resources and time; the new generation from the start faces a locked ceiling. In the Shrouding world, older-generation Great Emperors occupy the seats and do not leave; in the Tang-Luo world, the system grants grace to only the protagonist. Heng Yuan Zhou's intergenerational reconstruction is precisely the opposite: intergenerational relations from solidified to mobile, from occupation to feedback, from conflict to complementarity.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: intergenerational reconstruction ensures the previous generation's wisdom and the next generation's technology are both not wasted — two generations grow in their respective good time domains, then converge and complement in the network
- **Anti-predation**: cuts off "the previous generation's strong ones occupying time resources to limit the next generation"; output feedback ensures the next generation stands on the previous generation's shoulders
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system intergenerational relations solidify as "the system grants grace to only the protagonist"; other new generations have no opportunity; Heng Yuan Zhou's intergenerational relations are mobile, networked, feedback-oriented
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: intergenerational reconstruction covers all beings of Myriad Ranks; regardless of race, intergenerational relations are under the same legal framework
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: intergenerational-reconstruction law definition, guarantee mechanisms, arbitration processes all public; newly merged planes automatically apply; the intergenerational-relation system leading to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm may infinitely expand
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The acceleration domain breaks traditional intergenerational relations' three assumptions, forcing intergenerational relations from age authority to contribution authority, from unidirectional transmission to network sharing, from solidified to mobile, from conflict to complementarity, from occupation to feedback. This reconstruction is not "the young replacing the old," but letting each generation — whether taking the acceleration route or the sediment route — find its own position within the Myriad-Rank Republican framework. **Generations are no longer barriers, but complementarity; the time gap is no longer a conflict source, but a source of diversity**.
