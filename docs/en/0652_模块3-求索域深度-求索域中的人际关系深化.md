# Entry 652 | Quest-Domain Depth | Deepening of Interpersonal Relations in the Quest Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 26, 29, 52 | Series: Quest-Domain Depth (Entry 5)

## Introduction: real relations need time — they cannot grow in the acceleration domain

In predatory-faction systems interpersonal relations are functional: teammates, enemies, masters, disciples — everyone has a "use." When the use ends, the relation ends. The fundamental problem with this relational structure is: **it leaves no time and space for "non-utilitarian relations" to grow**. Real interpersonal relations — trust between friends, understanding between clans, tolerance between Myriad Ranks — cannot be rapidly built under hundredfold acceleration; they need time.

Six top-level axioms take effect here: anti-predation axiom requires relations not be based on using others; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires cross-clan relations develop equally; collective ascension axiom requires relationship deepening serve Myriad-Rank co-building; causal self-discipline axiom requires relationship development not create dependent causality; law-first axiom requires relationship-deepening law definition stable and self-consistent. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry analyzes the mechanism of interpersonal-relationship deepening in the Quest Sediment Domain.

## I. Three Time Dimensions of Relationship Deepening

### 1.1 Coexistence-Time Dimension

Real relations need coexistence time — not functional coexistence of "doing a mission together," but existential coexistence of "being together." The Quest Sediment Domain provides this coexistence space: cultivators may in the domain live with companions, face difficulties together, experience daily life together. This coexistence time does not exist in the acceleration domain — the acceleration domain's time is used for deduction and iteration, leaving no room for "just being together."

### 1.2 Conflict-Time Dimension

Relations deepen not only in favorable circumstances, but even more in conflict. Between two people, two clans, there are disagreements, contradictions, misunderstandings — how these conflicts are faced determines whether the relation deepens or breaks. The Quest Sediment Domain designs safe conflict scenarios: cultivators may in the simulated world experience relational conflict, in a controlled environment learn how to face disagreement, repair relations, find consensus across differences. This conflict practice may be enormously costly in reality, but in the Quest Domain it becomes a classroom for relationship deepening.

### 1.3 Sedimentation-Time Dimension

After shared experience, sedimentation time is needed to digest the experience in the relation. Companions who went through difficulty together need time after the difficulty ends to review, reflect, confirm each other's meaning. The Quest Sediment Domain's sedimentation period provides space for this relational digestion.

## II. Four Levels of Myriad-Rank Relationship Deepening

| Level | Relation Type | Implementation in the Quest Domain |
|-------|---------------|------------------------------------|
| Level 1 | Trust between individuals | Jointly experiencing epic scenes, building trust through choices |
| Level 2 | Understanding between clans | Cross-clan immersive experience — a human cultivator experiences an exotic-beast cultivator's life perspective |
| Level 3 | Consensus among Myriad Ranks | Multiple clans jointly facing simulated difficulty in the Quest Domain, negotiating solutions |
| Level 4 | Empathy between civilizations | Quest-Domain simulation-debrief of old civilizations' rise and fall, letting cultivators empathize with different civilizations' fates |

The four levels expand from individual to civilization layer by layer, constituting the complete path of Myriad-Rank relationship deepening.

## III. Ethical Boundaries of Relationship Deepening

Interpersonal-relationship deepening is not "forced closeness." Relationship development in the Quest Domain has clear ethical boundaries:

- **Voluntariness principle**: any relational interaction is voluntary; cultivators may not be forced to establish relations with others;
- **Exit principle**: cultivators may end a relational interaction at any time; there is no obligation of "once started cannot exit";
- **Respect for difference**: relationship deepening does not mean assimilation — two different individuals or clans may maintain difference while building a relation;
- **No-using principle**: relationship deepening does not take "using others" as purpose — this fundamentally opposes predatory relations (treating people as tools).

## IV. Contrast with the Predatory Faction's "Instrumental Relations"

In predatory-faction systems interpersonal relations are almost all instrumental:

- Teammates are "those to fight monsters with," scatter after the fight;
- Masters are "those who teach me cultivation methods," leave after learning;
- Enemies are "those to be defeated," forgotten after defeating.

There is no real trust, no deep understanding, no non-utilitarian connection. Such interpersonal relations are doomed to loneliness — the strong one lives ten thousand years with no one around him truly understanding him. The Quest Sediment Domain precisely provides the time and space to build real relations: coexistence, conflict, sedimentation — three time dimensions jointly cultivate deep relations that transcend utility.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: relationship deepening converts "seemingly useless" coexistence time into the trust foundation of Myriad-Rank collaboration — good interpersonal relations are the efficiency guarantee of Myriad-Rank co-building
- **Anti-predation**: directly cuts off the relationship predation of "treating people as tools"; all relations built on voluntary and respectful basis
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system interpersonal relations are functional (teammates, masters, enemies), without depth or trust; in Heng Yuan Zhou's Quest domain relations truly deepen in coexistence, conflict, sedimentation
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: relationship deepening covers all beings of the five lineages; cross-clan relations are formal teaching content in the Quest Domain
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: relationship-deepening four-level model, ethical boundaries, conflict-practice mechanism all public; newly merged planes接入 the same relationship-education system; the Myriad-Rank-relations infrastructure leading to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm may infinitely expand
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Real relations need time. Real relations cannot grow in the Origin Acceleration Domain — under hundredfold flow people are busy deducing and iterating, with no time "just being together." The Quest Sediment Domain uses three dimensions — coexistence time, conflict time, sedimentation time — to provide growth space for Myriad-Rank relationship deepening. In this space, trust between individuals, understanding between clans, consensus among Myriad Ranks gradually grow. **The acceleration domain lets Myriad Ranks work together; the Quest domain lets Myriad Ranks truly live together — this is the dual-track time domain's most profound contribution to Myriad-Rank relations. The Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision monitors the ethical boundaries throughout the relationship-deepening process**.
