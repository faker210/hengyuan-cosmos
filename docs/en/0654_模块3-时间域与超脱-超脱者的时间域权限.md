# Entry 654 | Time Domain and Transcendence | Time-Domain Authority of the Transcendent Ones

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 17, 49, 20 | Series: Time Domain and Transcendence (Entry 1)

## Introduction: the transcendent one is not time's master, but time's steward

In predatory-faction systems, transcendent ones (Great Emperors, Immortal Emperors, universe overlords) have absolute power over time — they may stop time, reverse time, create time secret realms. But this "absolute time power" is precisely the most dangerous place in predatory-faction systems: **a person with absolute power over time also has absolute power over Myriad Ranks' time fates**. Heng Yuan Zhou's setting of transcendent ones' time-domain authority fundamentally opposes this absolute power — the transcendent one is not time's master, but time's steward.

Six top-level axioms take effect here: anti-predation axiom requires transcendent ones not abuse time power to plunder Myriad Ranks; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires transcendent ones' time authority be supervised by Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires transcendent ones' time operations serve Myriad-Rank progress; causal self-discipline axiom requires transcendent ones' time operations not disturb causality; law-first axiom requires transcendent ones' authority law stable and self-consistent. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry defines the scope and constraints of transcendent ones' time-domain authority.

## I. Three-Layer Structure of Transcendent Ones' Time-Domain Authority

### 1.1 Layer 1: Basic Authority (all cultivators have)

As a cultivator, the transcendent one enjoys the same basic time-domain authority as Myriad Ranks: applying for Origin Acceleration Domain deduction, participating in Quest Sediment Domain immersion, using Alaya Network queries. This authority is neither expanded because of the transcendent one's status, nor reduced because of status — the transcendent one is first of all a member of Myriad Ranks.

### 1.2 Layer 2: Expanded Authority (obtained through contribution)

Because of their enormous contribution, transcendent ones may obtain larger time-domain quotas than ordinary cultivators: longer acceleration-domain usage time, higher flow-rate multiplier, more project participation rights. But these expanded authority still follow the joint-contracting mechanism — more work more gain, quotas tied to contribution. Expansion is not infinite, let alone "use however you want."

### 1.3 Layer 3: Stewardship Authority (conferred by post responsibility)

Transcendent ones of the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, as guardians of Myriad-Rank civilization, are conferred special time-domain stewardship authority: at moments of civilizational crisis, transcendent ones may call on more time resources to deduce response schemes; in cross-plane operations, transcendent ones may on behalf of Myriad Ranks coordinate at the time-domain level. But this stewardship authority is **post responsibility**, not personal privilege — its exercise must be reported, must be audited, must serve Myriad-Rank interests.

## II. Four Red Lines of Transcendent Ones' Time Authority

| Red Line | Content | Violation Consequence |
|----------|---------|----------------------|
| No monopolizing time | May not take acceleration-domain or Quest-domain resources as private property | Revoke expanded authority, symbiotic-court trial |
| No reversing causality | May not use time reversal to rewrite existing causal chains | Clear time-domain authority, handled per causal self-discipline axiom |
| No forcing others | May not force any being to enter or leave the time domain | Held accountable per the symbiotic constitution |
| No hoarding outputs | All outputs in the time domain must flow back to the Alaya Network | Forced backflow, contribution deducted |

The four red lines are monitored throughout by the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision. Transcendent ones have no extra-legal privilege — in the plane-transcendent four-dimensional assessment (power 40 / mind-nature 25 / contribution 20 / responsibility 15, Document 17), mind-nature and responsibility account for 40 points; any red line touched directly endangers the assessment.

## III. The Equality of Transcendent Ones and Ordinary Cultivators in the Time Domain

An important principle: before the time domain's basic rules, transcendent ones and ordinary cultivators are completely equal. This means:

- Transcendent ones may not skip the Alaya Network's causal audit;
- Transcendent ones may not bypass the symbiotic court's judicial authority;
- Transcendent ones may not monopolize knowledge outputs in the time domain;
- Transcendent ones may not make time choices for other beings.

This equality is not "modesty," but a law fact — the time domain's law definition treats all existents equally, does not differentiate by cultivation level. This is law-first axiom's expression in the power domain.

## IV. Contrast with the Predatory Faction's "Absolute Time Power"

| Dimension | Predatory-Faction Transcendent One | Heng Yuan Zhou Transcendent One |
|-----------|------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Time-Power Source | Personal cultivation / protagonist aura | Contribution + post responsibility |
| Time-Power Constraint | Unconstrained (do whatever) | Four red lines + Alaya audit + symbiotic court |
| Time-Output Ownership | Personal private hoarding | Network-wide backflow |
| Relation with Myriad Ranks | Myriad Ranks obey his time arrangement | Myriad Ranks supervise his time use |
| Time-Power Nature | Master power | Stewardship duty |

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: transcendent ones' time-domain authority is strictly limited to the range corresponding to contribution, preventing time resources from being inefficiently monopolized
- **Anti-predation**: the four red lines directly cut off all paths by which transcendent ones abuse time power to plunder Myriad Ranks
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo transcendent ones have absolute power over time with no one to constrain; Heng Yuan Zhou transcendent ones' time authority is supervised by Myriad Ranks, constrained by law, tracked by audit
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the three-layer time-domain authority structure equally applies to all beings of Myriad Ranks; transcendent ones enjoy no extra-legal privilege
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: transcendent ones' time-authority law definition, red-line list, audit mechanism all public; newly merged planes接入 the same authority-governance system; the authority framework leading to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm may infinitely expand
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The transcendent one is not time's master, but time's steward. Because of enormous contribution he may obtain a larger time quota; because of post responsibility he may obtain special stewardship authority — but he may never monopolize time, reverse causality, force others, hoard outputs. **In the time domain, the greater the transcendent one's power, the stricter the supervision — this is not distrust of the strong, but basic responsibility for Myriad Ranks' time resources**.
