# Entry 656 | Time Domain and Transcendence | Time Allocation for the Transcendent Ones' Eternally-Running Construction

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 17, 23, 06 | Series: Time Domain and Transcendence (Entry 3)

## Introduction: the transcendent one's time is not for resting, but for building

In predatory-faction systems, the standard state after a transcendent one reaches the peak is "AFK" — finding a place to sleep, seclude, wander. This state's philosophical meaning is: **the strong has already done everything; time for him is only consumption**. Heng Yuan Zhou's Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is precisely the opposite: the transcendent one's time is not for consuming, but for building — eternally-running construction. A true transcendent one always has a next step to do, a next layer to build, a next generation to guide.

Six top-level axioms take effect here: anti-predation axiom requires construction outputs come from incremental development; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires construction serve Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires transcendent ones carry Myriad Ranks forward together; causal self-discipline axiom requires construction not disturb others' causality; law-first axiom requires construction-direction law definition stable and self-consistent. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry analyzes the time-allocation logic in transcendent ones' eternally-running construction.

## I. The Time Structure of Eternally-Running Construction

The transcendent one's time is not infinite — even a Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm cultivator's effective construction time needs reasonable allocation. Time allocation follows this structure:

| Construction Direction | Time Share | Specific Content |
|------------------------|------------|------------------|
| Civilizational-Ascension Deduction | 30% | Deducting next-layer civilizational-upgrade schemes in the Origin Acceleration Domain |
| Infrastructure Maintenance | 25% | Maintaining time domain, Alaya Network, law anchoring and other infrastructure |
| Myriad-Rank Cultivation Guidance | 20% | Guiding new-generation cultivators, participating in Quest-Domain educational courses |
| Cross-Plane Coordination | 15% | Coordinating newly merged planes' entry, time-flow calibration |
| Self-Sedimentation | 10% | Personal Dao-realization and spiritual integration in the Quest Sediment Domain |

This ratio is not fixed — it dynamically adjusts according to civilization's current needs. But its basic principle is: **the transcendent one's time is mainly used to serve Myriad Ranks; self-sedimentation accounts for only the smallest share**.

## II. Why Eternally-Running Construction Does Not Burn Out

In predatory-faction systems the strong go AFK; the fundamental reason is "nothing new to do." Heng Yuan Zhou's transcendent ones never burn out, for three reasons:

### 2.1 Civilization Always Has a Next Layer

Heng Yuan Zhou's cultivation system is not "reaching the top then ending." Above the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm there is still infinite expansion space — new planes' entry, new clans' integration, new laws' improvement, new infrastructure's construction. Civilization always has a next layer to build, so the transcendent one always has something to do.

### 2.2 Construction Itself Is Meaning

Meaning construction in the Quest Sediment Domain (Entry 653) lets the transcendent one understand: construction is not a burden, but meaning itself. When a transcendent one knows what he is doing makes Myriad Ranks better, he does not need external motivation — construction itself is his life meaning.

### 2.3 Eternally-Running Does Not Mean Overwork

Eternally-running construction is not "working without rest." Time allocation reserves 10% self-sedimentation time — this is not rest, but deep spiritual integration. The transcendent one in the sedimentation period digests experiences from previous construction, adjusts direction, recharges for the next round of construction. This rhythm is not overwork, but a sustainable cycle.

## III. The Time Relation Between Eternally-Running Construction and Ordinary Cultivators

The transcendent one's eternally-running construction is not "one person doing everything." It complements Myriad Ranks' time allocation:

- Ordinary cultivators in the acceleration domain do their respective deduction and iteration;
- Transcendent ones in the acceleration domain do cross-domain, global deduction;
- Ordinary cultivators in the Quest domain do their respective Dao-realization and trial;
- Transcendent ones in the Quest domain do civilizational-level philosophical contemplation and epic guidance.

This complementarity ensures time resources at all levels are fully utilized — there is no waste of "the strong idling, mortals overworked."

## IV. Time Comparison with the Predatory Faction's "AFK Retirement"

| Dimension | Predatory-Faction Transcendent One | Heng Yuan Zhou Transcendent One |
|-----------|------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Post-Peak State | AFK sleep | Eternally-running construction |
| Time Use | Consumption (alive but not building) | Building (continuously creating increments for Myriad Ranks) |
| Relation with Myriad Ranks | Separated from Myriad Ranks | Carrying Myriad Ranks forward together |
| Burnout Reason | Nothing to do | None (civilization always has a next layer) |
| Time Evaluation | Time is a burden | Time is construction material |

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the transcendent one's time is all invested in constructive directions; there is no "AFK waste" — the highest strong one supported by Myriad Ranks creates increments for Myriad Ranks every minute
- **Anti-predation**: eternally-running construction's outputs all feed back network-wide; there is no case of "the strong builds then keeps it for his own enjoyment"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style transcendent ones have nothing to do after the peak; Heng Yuan Zhou transcendent ones' eternally-running construction always has a next step
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: eternally-running construction serves all beings of Myriad Ranks; the transcendent one's time allocation takes Myriad-Rank interests as the highest standard
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: eternally-running construction's time-allocation model, construction directions, adjustment mechanism all public; newly merged planes' transcendent ones automatically enter the same eternally-running construction system; the construction framework leading to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm may infinitely expand
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The transcendent one's time is not for resting, but for building. Eternally-running construction is not non-stop overwork, but under reasonable time allocation, continuously creating increments for Myriad Ranks. Civilization always has a next layer; construction always has a next step — this is the most fundamental difference between Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcendent ones and predatory-faction AFK retirees. **The predatory faction's strong one peaks at the endpoint; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcendent one peaks at the starting point — this is the time domain's final definition of the transcendent one**.
