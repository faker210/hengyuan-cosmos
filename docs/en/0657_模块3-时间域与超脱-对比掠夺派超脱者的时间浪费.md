# Entry 657 | Time Domain and Transcendence | Contrasting the Predatory-Faction Transcendent Ones' Time Waste

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 57, Entry 608 | Series: Time Domain and Transcendence (Entry 4)

## Introduction: the strongest ones waste the most time

The most ironic phenomenon in predatory-faction systems is: **those with the most time waste the most time**. A Great Emperor who lived ten thousand years slept for nine thousand of them; an Immortal Emperor who lived a hundred thousand years wandered for ninety-nine thousand of them. These strong ones' time resources — their energy consumption, their cosmic-space occupation, the ecological cost of maintaining their own existence — are all wasted.

Six top-level axioms here become analytical tools: anti-predation axiom requires examining the predatory essence of this time waste; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires asking whose interests the wasted time could have served; collective ascension axiom requires comparing the symbiotic faction's alternatives; causal self-discipline axiom requires tracking the causal consequences of time waste; law-first axiom requires analyzing at the law level why predatory-faction systems necessarily produce time waste. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry systematically contrasts the predatory-faction transcendent ones' time waste.

## I. Four Forms of Predatory-Faction Transcendent Ones' Time Waste

### 1.1 Form 1: Sleep Waste

The most common form of time waste: the strong one enters long sleep after reaching the peak. Older-generation Great Emperors in Shrouding routinely sleep for tens of thousands of years. The cosmic energy consumed during these sleeps, the cultivation resources occupied, the civilizational-development opportunities delayed — all are net waste. A sleeping strong one contributes nothing to civilization, but the resources he needs to maintain his own existence never stop consuming.

### 1.2 Form 2: Wandering Waste

The second form: the strong one does not sleep, but wanders aimlessly in the universe. In Dragon Ball, Goku's daily life in the peace era after defeating Buu is training and strolling. Training itself is not bad, but when training has no civilizational goal, no output backflow, no Myriad-Rank sharing, it is a personal time self-cycle — producing no civilizational increment.

### 1.3 Form 3: Maintenance Waste

The third form: after occupying the highest position, the strong one's main energy goes to "holding the position" — guarding against latecomers challenging, maintaining existing order, suppressing potential threats. This maintenance consumes the strong one's enormous time, but this time investment produces no civilizational increment — it only maintains the strong one's own monopolistic position.

### 1.4 Form 4: Nihilistic Waste

The deepest waste: after the strong one lives long enough, he finds everything meaningless. Time passing in this nihilistic state is the most thorough waste — not only no output, but consuming the strong one's spiritual energy to maintain the middle state of "not wanting to die and not wanting to live."

## II. Magnitude Estimate of Time Waste

| Waste Form | Single Waste Time | Corresponding Civilizational Opportunity Cost |
|------------|-------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| Great Emperor Sleeping | ~10,000–50,000 years | Time in which civilization could complete multiple technology upgrades |
| Strong One Wandering | ~1,000–10,000 years | Time in which civilization could raise generations of cultivators |
| Maintenance Consumption | Ongoing | All the time civilizational development is suppressed |
| Nihilistic State | Uncertain (possibly extremely long) | Time in which civilizational-spiritual resources are corrupted |

The sum of these time wastes constitutes predatory-faction systems' greatest hidden cost — **Myriad Ranks paid time resources, but received no civilizational return**.

As a contrasting anchor, Heng Yuan Zhou's Origin Acceleration Domain converts the transcendent one's time investment into hundredfold-iterated civilizational increments; the Quest Sediment Domain converts the transcendent one's sedimentation time into spiritual resources shared by Myriad Ranks; the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision records the transcendent one's time use throughout, ensuring zero waste; and the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's eternally-running construction system fundamentally negates the "post-peak AFK" time waste. The law-first axiom requires: the transcendent one's time allocation must serve Myriad Ranks; the "alive but not building" state may not be allowed to persist.

## III. The Predatory Essence of Time Waste

Time waste is not a "personal choice" — it is essentially predation:

- The energy consumed by a sleeping strong one comes from the environment Myriad Ranks survive in;
- The cultivation resources occupied by a wandering strong one could have been used to raise the new generation;
- The development possibilities civilization itself suppresses by a maintenance strong one;
- The confidence Myriad Ranks have in life's meaning corrupted by a nihilistic strong one.

Every form of time waste is a covert predation by the strong of Myriad Ranks' time resources — he takes the time Myriad Ranks could have used, then uses it to "do nothing."

## IV. Heng Yuan Zhou's Alternative (Review)

In contrast with predatory-faction time waste, Heng Yuan Zhou transcendent ones' eternally-running construction (Entry 656) invests all the transcendent one's time in constructive directions: civilizational-ascension deduction, infrastructure maintenance, Myriad-Rank cultivation guidance, cross-plane coordination, self-sedimentation. Same transcendent one, same long lifespan — the predatory-faction version sleeps; the symbiotic-faction version builds. **The difference is not in ability, but in the philosophy of time allocation**.

## Conclusion

The most absurd fact about predatory-faction systems is: the strongest ones waste the most time. Sleeping, wandering, maintaining, nihilism — four time-waste forms stacked constitute predatory-faction civilization's greatest hidden cost. These wastes are not "the strong one's freedom," but covert predation of Myriad Ranks' time resources. Heng Yuan Zhou's dual-track time-domain system precisely provides an alternative: the same transcendent one, in eternally-running construction converts every bit of time into a civilizational increment. **Not wasting time is the transcendent one's most basic responsibility to Myriad Ranks**.
