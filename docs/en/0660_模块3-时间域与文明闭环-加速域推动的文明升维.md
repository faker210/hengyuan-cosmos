# Entry 660 | Time Domain and Civilizational Closed Loop | Acceleration-Domain-Driven Civilizational Ascension

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 11, 23, 05 | Series: Time Domain and Civilizational Closed Loop (Entry 2)

## Introduction: civilizational ascension is not a one-step leap to heaven, but the natural result of hundredfold iteration

In predatory-faction systems "ascension" is a one-step leap — the protagonist suddenly breaks through, suddenly ascends, suddenly enters a higher tier. This one-step-leap ascension has two fundamental problems: first, it is unreplicable (only the protagonist may ascend); second, it is unstable (the civilizational foundation after ascension is unsound). Heng Yuan Zhou's stance is precisely the opposite: **civilizational ascension is not a one-step leap to heaven, but the natural result of countless iterations accumulating in the acceleration domain**.

Six top-level axioms take effect here: anti-predation axiom requires ascension come from incremental accumulation; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires ascension cover Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires ascension carry Myriad Ranks up together; causal self-discipline axiom requires ascension not create causal breakage; law-first axiom requires the ascension path's law definition stable and self-consistent. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry analyzes how the Origin Acceleration Domain drives civilizational ascension.

## I. Three Layers of Civilizational Ascension

### 1.1 Layer 1: Technical Ascension

The most basic ascension layer: improvement in technical level. The technology-explosion model in the acceleration domain (Entry 643) drives technology to iterate from basic to advanced level. The marker of technical ascension: Myriad Ranks' cultivation efficiency, energy-use efficiency, infrastructure efficiency comprehensively improve.

### 1.2 Layer 2: Institutional Ascension

After technical ascension reaches a certain level, social institutions must ascension synchronously (Entry 645 social-structure change). Driven by the acceleration domain, governance institutions, distribution institutions, education institutions, judicial institutions all upgrade from simple forms to complex forms. The marker of institutional ascension: the complexity of Myriad-Rank symbiosis rises; governance can cope with more complex civilizational states.

### 1.3 Layer 3: Spiritual Ascension

The deepest ascension: Myriad Ranks' spiritual realm, philosophical depth, art flourishing (Entry 651) comprehensively improve. Spiritual ascension is not completed by the acceleration domain alone — it requires dual-track synergy of the acceleration domain (providing material foundation) and the Quest Domain (providing spiritual depth). The marker of spiritual ascension: Myriad Ranks' depth of understanding life's meaning, degree of identification with Myriad-Rank symbiosis, level of understanding universal laws all comprehensively rise.

## II. The Iteration Mechanism by Which the Acceleration Domain Drives Ascension

Civilizational ascension is not completed at once, but gradually accumulated through iteration cycles (Entry 642):

| Iteration Stage | Ascension Contribution | Cumulative Effect |
|-----------------|------------------------|-------------------|
| Rounds 1–10 | Technical basic framework established | Civilization starts from low-technical level |
| Rounds 11–50 | Technology rapidly iterates; institutions begin adjusting | Technical level significantly improves |
| Rounds 51–100 | Technology matures; institutions fully ascension | Civilization enters new technology-institution balance |
| After Round 100 | Spiritual dimension begins to ascension | Civilization enters new stage of trinity of technology, institution, spirit |

Completing a hundred iterations inside the hundredfold acceleration domain, outside only about ten days have passed — but in these ten days, civilization completed the whole process from technical foundation to spiritual ascension.

## III. Myriad-Rank Coverage of Ascension

Acceleration-domain-driven ascension is not a few people's ascension, but Myriad Ranks' ascension:

- Technical-ascension outputs feed back network-wide from the Origin Acceleration Domain; the Quest Sediment Domain synchronously deepens Myriad Ranks' understanding of new technology; Myriad Ranks jointly use the more efficient cultivation system;
- Institutional-ascension result: Myriad Ranks live in a more complete governance framework;
- Spiritual-ascension results spread to Myriad Ranks through the Quest Domain; every being benefits.

This forms a fundamental contrast with predatory-faction systems where "the protagonist alone ascends; Myriad Ranks stay in place."

### III-Added: The Jump-Transition Markers of Ascension

Civilizational ascension is not continuous gradual change; it has clear jump-transition markers. When iteration accumulation reaches a certain threshold, civilization undergoes a step-jump level promotion: from low-technology civilization to medium-technology civilization, from simple institutions to complex institutions, from shallow spirit to deep spirit. These transition markers are continuously monitored by the Alaya Network — when key indicators (cultivation efficiency, institutional complexity, spiritual-depth indicators) continuously reach the threshold and stabilize for a period, the transition is automatically confirmed. The transition is not announced by some strong one, but automatically determined by the law network and data indicators — this ensures ascension is objective and measurable, not subjective boasting.

## IV. Sustainability of Ascension

Acceleration-domain-driven ascension has clear sustainability guarantees:

- **Energy sustainability**: the energy ascension needs comes from incremental development (Document 15), not plundering stock;
- **Law sustainability**: the ascension path's law definition is stable and self-consistent; it does not retcon settings because of ascension;
- **Social sustainability**: during ascension social structure synchronously changes (Entry 645), not creating social tearing;
- **Spiritual sustainability**: the Quest Domain guarantees spiritual depth during ascension, not creating "technically obese, spiritually anemic."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the acceleration domain's hundredfold iteration compresses ascension from "ten thousand years of waiting" to "completed in ten-plus days"; time resources are efficiently used
- **Anti-predation**: ascension outputs are network-wide shared; there is no case of "the strong ascend then monopolize the outputs"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system the protagonist one-step-leap ascends, unreplicable and unstable; Heng Yuan Zhou's acceleration-domain iterative ascension is replicable and stable
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: ascension covers all beings of Myriad Ranks, not a few people's privilege
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: ascension three-layer model, iteration mechanism, sustainability guarantees all public; newly merged planes接入 the same ascension path; the ascension system leading to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm may infinitely expand
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Acceleration-domain-driven civilizational ascension is not a one-step-leap miracle, but hundredfold iteration accumulation. Technical ascension, institutional ascension, spiritual ascension — three layers gradually unfold through iteration cycles; each iteration builds on the previous one. The greatest feature of this ascension way is: it is replicable, stable, and perpetual — **civilization does not ascension through some individual's miracle, but through Myriad Ranks' continuous iteration in the acceleration domain**.
