# Entry 663 | Time Domain and Law-First | Self-Consistency Proof of Time-Domain Laws

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 20, 49, 27 | Series: Time Domain and Law-First (Entry 1)

## Introduction: law-first axiom is not a slogan, but an engineering standard

Law-first axiom is one of the six top-level axioms. Its core requirement: **laws first, never retcon, never unfinished; setting self-consistency above plot convenience**. But "self-consistency" is not a vague philosophical concept — in the time domain's concrete settings, self-consistency must be engineering-proved, structurally tested, continuously maintained. This entry proves: why are the dual-track time domain's law settings self-consistent at the engineering standard?

Six top-level axioms here, law-first axiom is this entry's core testing standard. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. The other four axioms — anti-predation, republican of Myriad Races, collective ascension, causal self-discipline — constitute the content standard for time-domain law self-consistency. This entry structurally proves: the time-domain law settings satisfy all requirements of law-first axiom.

## I. Five Dimensions of Self-Consistency Testing

### 1.1 Dimension 1: Internal Consistency Test

All settings inside the time domain — Origin Acceleration Domain flow rate, Quest Sediment Domain immersion, switching process, allocation mechanism, backflow rules — must be consistent with each other, may not contradict. Test method: put all time-domain law clauses on one table, pairwise check for logical conflicts. After testing, there are no contradictions among the dual-track time domain's law clauses: acceleration-domain allocation rules do not conflict with backflow rules; Quest-domain exit right does not conflict with trial design; cross-plane reporting process does not conflict with causal-closure principle.

### 1.2 Dimension 2: Consistency with the Overall Law Network Test

Time-domain laws may not conflict with other parts of the law network. Test method: compare time-domain laws with Document 20 anchor structure and hierarchy, Document 15 energy-cycle law, Document 14 cultivation-realm division and other core laws. Conclusion: time-domain laws are the time-dimensional extension of the law network, do not conflict with any existing law.

### 1.3 Dimension 3: Causal Consistency Test

Each time-domain operation must have corresponding causal-effect definition (Entry 640 time-causal unity). Test method: list all time-domain operation types, confirm each operation has clear causal-effect description and audit mechanism. Conclusion: all operation types are covered by three handling ways — causal closure, causal desensitization, causal reporting.

### 1.4 Dimension 4: Expansion Consistency Test

Time-domain laws must support infinite expansion (Entry 664); new planes entering, new clans using, new functions developed may not lead to law contradiction. Test method: simulate new-plane entry scenarios, verify whether the existing law framework may naturally cover them. Conclusion: the law framework's open design ensures expansion does not produce contradictions.

### 1.5 Dimension 5: Governance Consistency Test

Time-domain laws must be consistent with the governance system — Anchor Council, Symbiotic Executive Committee, symbiotic court, Anchor Supervisory Committee. Test method: confirm the time domain's four-power division of legislation, administration, judiciary, supervision fully aligns with Document 49 governance framework. Conclusion: four-power division consistent; time-domain-abuse trial (Entry 619) seamlessly interfaces with the judicial system.

## II. Self-Consistency Maintenance Mechanisms

Self-consistency is not ended with one proof — it needs continuous maintenance:

| Maintenance Mechanism | Content |
|------------------------|---------|
| New-Law Review | Any new time-domain function proposal must first pass self-consistency review before project approval |
| Periodic Verification | Anchor Supervisory Committee periodically verifies time-domain laws' consistency with the overall law network |
| Conflict Warning | When a new cultivation system or technology may conflict with time-domain laws, the Alaya Network auto-warns |
| Correction Process | When self-consistency problems are discovered, the Anchor Council legislates correction; plot bypass is not allowed |

These maintenance mechanisms ensure time-domain laws always remain self-consistent — this is law-first axiom's implementation at the engineering level.

## III. Contrast with the Predatory Faction's "Retcon-and-Abandon"

Predatory-faction systems' setting self-consistency is generally low:

- Shrouding-the-Heavens: the power system set early is repeatedly broken later;
- Douluo: the spirit-ring system's rules change with the protagonist's needs;
- Dragon Ball: power numbers are completely incomparable; late stage completely collapses;
- Tang-Luo: the protagonist's abilities are arbitrarily added with plot needs.

The common problem of these systems: plot convenience kidnaps setting self-consistency — when the author needs a thrill he breaks the rules. Heng Yuan Zhou's law-first axiom precisely forbids this: **setting self-consistency above plot convenience**.

## IV. The Philosophical Meaning of Self-Consistency

Law self-consistency is not only a technical requirement, but a philosophical commitment: it means civilization's laws are not tools that may be arbitrarily changed, but the cornerstone on which civilization depends for survival. If laws may be changed with plot convenience, then Myriad Ranks' trust in laws collapses — no one will obey a rule that may be modified at any time. Law self-consistency is the premise of civilizational rule of law.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: self-consistency testing ensures time-domain laws do not produce chaos because of contradictions; every bit of time resource flows within the self-consistent law framework
- **Anti-predation**: law self-consistency ensures no "law loopholes" may be exploited for predation; all paths in the time domain are within the self-consistent law framework
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's settings are retconned with plot convenience, self-consistency extremely low; Heng Yuan Zhou law-first, setting self-consistency above all
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: self-consistency testing covers the time domain's application to all beings of Myriad Ranks; laws do not have contradictions because of different races
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: self-consistency testing's five-dimension model, maintenance mechanisms, correction process all public; newly merged planes接入 the same self-consistency guarantee system; the law-self-consistency framework leading to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm may infinitely expand
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Time-domain laws' self-consistency is not self-boasting, but proved through five dimensions of engineering testing: internal consistency, overall consistency, causal consistency, expansion consistency, governance consistency. These tests are not one-time — periodic verification, conflict warning, correction process ensure self-consistency continuously maintained. **Law-first axiom's core is not the empty phrase "laws are important," but the executable, testable, maintainable engineering standard "law self-consistency above plot convenience."** Time-domain laws have passed this standard — this is the dual-track time domain system's setting-self-consistency fundamental guarantee.
