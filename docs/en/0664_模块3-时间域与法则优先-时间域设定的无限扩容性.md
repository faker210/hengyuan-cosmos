# Entry 664 | Time Domain and Law-First | Infinite Expandability of Time-Domain Settings

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 20, 28, 23 | Series: Time Domain and Law-First (Entry 2)

## Introduction: a law system that cannot expand will ultimately be crushed by its own complexity

Predatory-faction systems' settings are often exquisite in early stages, collapsing in late stages. One of the fundamental reasons for collapse: **the setting system cannot expand** — new plot needs new settings, but new settings contradict old ones, so retcon-and-abandon is the only option. Heng Yuan Zhou's law-first axiom explicitly requires "the worldview may infinitely expand without collapsing." This entry proves: why does the dual-track time domain's setting system have infinite expandability?

Six top-level axioms take effect here: anti-predation axiom requires expansion not be achieved by predation; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires expansion cover newly merged planes' Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires expansion drive civilizational progress; causal self-discipline axiom requires expansion not create causal chaos; law-first axiom requires the expansion process be self-consistent and non-contradictory. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry analyzes the expansion structure of time-domain settings.

## I. Three Design Principles of Infinite Expandability

### 1.1 Principle 1: Modular Architecture

The time domain's settings are not monolithic, but modular: Origin Acceleration Domain module, Quest Sediment Domain module, allocation module, supervision module, cross-plane module — each module independently defined, independently maintained. When new functions are added, only new modules need to be added; internal structure of existing modules need not be modified. Modular architecture is the foundation of infinite expansion.

### 1.2 Principle 2: Standardized Interfaces

Modules connect through standard interfaces: time-domain switching protocol (Entry 612), Alaya-Network access protocol (Entry 614), causal-audit protocol — these interfaces are standardized; a new module may enter as long as it obeys interface specifications. Interface standardization ensures new functions do not require rewriting existing modules.

### 1.3 Principle 3: Parameterized Design

The time domain's key parameters — flow-rate multiplier, quota ceiling, audit threshold — are not hard-coded in laws, but exist as configurable parameters. When a new plane enters, appropriate parameters are configured according to its civilizational-development stage, rather than redesigning laws for each plane. Parameterized design ensures the law framework itself is unchanged; only parameters adjust with scenarios.

## II. Four Directions of Expansion Path

| Expansion Direction | Content | Expansion Method |
|---------------------|---------|------------------|
| New plane entry | New civilization merges into Heng Yuan Zhou | Cross-plane anchor connection + parameter configuration |
| New clan use | Myriad Ranks' new lineage discovered | Adapt existing modules + cultural calibration |
| New function development | Time domain adds new capabilities | New module + standard-interface entry |
| New-realm adaptation | Cultivation system expansion | Parameter adjustment + deep adaptation (Entries 578–588) |

All four expansion directions follow the "add without modify" principle: new things enter, but do not destroy existing structure.

## III. Self-Consistency Guarantee During Expansion

Infinite expansion does not equal infinite chaos. During expansion self-consistency is guaranteed by the following mechanisms:

- **New-module review**: any new module must pass Entry 663's five-dimension self-consistency test;
- **Interface-compatibility testing**: a new module must pass compatibility testing before entering standard interfaces;
- **Parameter-conflict detection**: conflicts between new parameter configurations and existing parameters are auto-detected by the Alaya Network;
- **Progressive rollout**: a new module first runs on a small scale; after confirming no contradiction, it is network-wide promoted.

These mechanisms ensure expansion does not produce self-consistency collapse.

## IV. Contrast Between Expandability and Predatory-Faction Systems

Why cannot predatory-faction systems expand?

- Shrouding-the-Heavens: the cultivation system set early is broken by new settings later, only retcon possible;
- Douluo: the spirit-ring system's rules change with the protagonist's needs; new abilities keep increasing but old rules are overturned;
- Dragon Ball: the power system is completely incomparable; whenever a new enemy appears, the old power ceiling must be broken;
- Tang-Luo: the protagonist's abilities are arbitrarily added with plot needs, with no law-framework constraint.

These systems' common problem: **no modularization, no standard interfaces, no parameterization — new content must modify old settings, so retcon is unavoidable**. Heng Yuan Zhou's modular architecture fundamentally avoids this problem.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: expandability ensures the time domain's law framework does not collapse because of complexity growth; each expansion is incremental construction, not requiring rebuilding from scratch
- **Anti-predation**: expansion is not achieved by plundering other systems' settings — Heng Yuan Zhou's expansion is endogenous and self-consistent
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system cannot expand — new content must retcon old settings; Heng Yuan Zhou's modular architecture may infinitely expand without collapse
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the four expansion directions all cover newly merged planes and newly discovered clans; Myriad-Rank scope continuously grows with expansion
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: expansion's three principles, four directions, self-consistency guarantee mechanisms all public; newly merged planes automatically enter the same expansion system; the expansion framework leading to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm may infinitely extend
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The time domain's infinite expandability comes from three design principles: modular architecture, interface standardization, parameterized design. These three principles ensure new content may enter without breaking old structure; new planes may enter without rewriting laws. **Law-first axiom not only requires existing settings self-consistent, but also requires the setting system may infinitely expand without collapsing — time-domain settings achieve this requirement through modular architecture.** A law system that may infinitely expand can support an infinitely broad civilizational territory — from the current range of 37 trillion light-years to the farther future, the time domain's law framework is always self-consistent, always open, always extendable.
