This entry systematically compares Heng Yuan Zhou's dual-track time domain's setting self-consistency with various major Myriad-World IPs' time settings. Comparison dimensions include: law-definition stability, time-allocation justice, causal consistency, expandability-without-collapse, retcon-and-abandon risk.

Six top-level axioms here serve as comparison rulers: anti-predation axiom tests whether time allocation is just; republican axiom of Myriad Races tests whether time qualification is open; collective ascension axiom tests whether time outputs are shared; causal self-discipline axiom tests whether time operations have causal constraints; law-first axiom tests whether settings are self-consistent and non-collapsing. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry uses comparison data to prove the dual-track time domain's self-consistency advantage.

## II. Self-Consistency Problems of Each IP's Time Settings

### 2.1 Shrouding-the-Heavens: Seclusion Time Has No Cost; Causality Has No Audit

In the Shrouding world, Great Emperors routinely seclude for thousands of years. Time cost is never calculated — energy consumed by seclusion, delayed civilizational development, occupied spatial resources, all ignored. Causal consistency almost does not exist: the river of time may be waded casually; history may be rewritten casually; no audit mechanism. Expandability extremely poor — late-stage new settings repeatedly break early-stage power systems.

### 2.2 Perfect World: The Time Loneliness of Alone-Decreeing-for-Eternity

Shi Hao alone decrees for eternity, one person guarding one era. This time setting's self-consistency problem: how does one person maintain an entire civilization's operation? The answer is "protagonist aura," not a law system. Time-allocation justice almost zero — all resources revolve around the protagonist.

### 2.3 Douluo Continent: The Spirit-Ring System Changes with the Protagonist

Douluo's spirit-ring system has clear rules in early stages, but as the protagonist's needs arise, rules are continuously modified. The traveler carries future memory to disturb causality, but no one audits — this is time privilege. Expandability poor: new spirit rings, new skills continuously break old rules.

### 2.4 Dragon Ball: The Time Chamber Is Used Without Restriction; Power Is Incomparable

Dragon Ball's Time Chamber is the most typical time resource — but it completely serves the protagonist's group, not allocated to others. Causal consistency zero: Trunks-line history is repeatedly rewritten. The power system is completely incomparable; retcon-and-abandon risk highest.

### 2.5 Tang-Luo: System-Grace-Style Time Monopoly

In the Tang-Luo world, accelerated-cultivation qualification is granted by the system as grace, not a right. Time allocation completely unjust — only the protagonist receives time acceleration. Causality un-audited; expandability extremely poor — protagonist's abilities arbitrarily added with plot needs.

## III. The Fundamental Reason Heng Yuan Zhou Leads in Self-Consistency

Heng Yuan Zhou leads in all five dimensions; the fundamental reason is:

- **Law definition stable**: law-first axiom ensures time-domain laws are not modified with plot convenience;
- **Time allocation just**: joint-contracting mechanism + Alaya audit ensures time is allocated by contribution;
- **Causally consistent**: time and causality are unified in the law network (Entry 640); all operations have causal handling;
- **Expands without collapse**: modular architecture (Entry 664) ensures new content enters without breaking old settings;
- **Retcon risk extremely low**: self-consistency five-dimension testing (Entry 663) continuously runs; problems are discovered and corrected early.

## IV. The Philosophical Meaning of Comparison

This comparison is not for "tearing one to praise another," but to prove a setting-theory principle: **a time setting's self-consistency depends on whether it treats time as law**. Myriad-World IPs treat time as "the strong one's ability," therefore self-consistency is not needed — the strong one does as he wishes. Heng Yuan Zhou treats time as "civilization's public resource," therefore self-consistency is required — public-resource allocation may not be changed casually. The dual-track design of Origin Acceleration Domain and Quest Sediment Domain, the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's full-process audit, law-first axiom's self-consistency guarantee, the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's infinite-expansion framework — these five-star dimensions in the comparison table are precisely what Myriad-World IP systems most lack. This is the manifestation of two time philosophies at the setting-theory level.
