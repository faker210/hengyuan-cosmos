# Entry 668 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Constitutional Guarantee of Individual Time Autonomy

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 40, 08, 26 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 2)

## Introduction: time is the individual's most fundamental property

In predatory-faction systems, time is something the strong may deprive at will — a mortal's whole life is, in an Immortal Emperor's eyes, but a snap of fingers; he does not even qualify to be consumed. Heng Yuan Zhou completely flips this premise: time is the individual's most fundamental property; the choice between acceleration domain and Quest domain is an individual's inalienable right. Six top-level axioms here land as constitutional clauses: anti-predation axiom forbids time predation; republican axiom of Myriad Races confirms all beings equally possess time rights; collective ascension axiom requires time rights correspond to co-building duties; causal self-discipline axiom requires time-right exercise not disturb others' causality; law-first axiom requires time rights' constitutional definition be stable, not modified with rulers' turnover. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry sorts out the constitutional guarantee system of individual time autonomy in the dual-track time domain.

## I. Three Layers of Connotation of Time Autonomy

### 1.1 Entry Right: Freely Choose Acceleration or Quest

Any being, after completing basic cultivation initiation, has the right to autonomously choose to enter the Origin Acceleration Domain for deduction, or enter the Quest Sediment Domain for trial, without any approval, without any "recommendation," without proving to anyone "I deserve to be fast." This is a complete negation of the old system's "entry threshold" — in the Tang-Luo world, ordinary people even need the protagonist's grace just to qualify to enter an academy; in Heng Yuan Zhou, qualification to enter the time domain is constitutionally conferred.

### 1.2 Exit Right: Leave Any Track at Any Time

The individual has the right at any moment to exit the Origin Acceleration Domain and return to normal flow; also to exit the Quest Sediment Domain and enter accelerated deduction. Exit may not be accompanied by punishment, may not deduct contribution, may not be recorded as "wavering." Exit right is more important than entry right — it prevents the individual from being "kidnapped" by any track into a tool.

### 1.3 Quota Right: Basic Time Unaffected by Contribution

Even if a being's contribution is extremely low, the constitution still guarantees his basic cultivation time and basic acceleration quota; he may not be deprived of time because he "produces little." This is a negation of the barbaric logic of "time = reward."

## II. Five Defense Lines of Constitutional Guarantee

| Defense Line | Content | Corresponding Axiom |
|--------------|---------|---------------------|
| Legislative defense | Symbiotic-constitution time chapter (Entry 686) expressly lists time rights | Republican of Myriad Races |
| Administrative defense | Symbiotic Executive Committee may not force-allocate individual pacing | Anti-predation |
| Judicial defense | Symbiotic court accepts time-right-infringement lawsuits (Entry 688) | Causal self-discipline |
| Audit defense | Anchor Supervisory Committee audits time-quota execution (Entry 689) | Law-first |
| Network defense | Alaya Network records individual time choices, traceable | Collective ascension |

The five defense lines are mutually independent and mutually checking: legislation sets boundaries; administration does not overstep; judiciary adjudicates disputes; audit checks execution; network leaves evidence. Any institution wanting to deprive some individual's time autonomy must simultaneously break through five defense lines — this is institutionally nearly impossible.

## III. The Boundary of Autonomy: Not Harm Others' Time Rights

Time autonomy is not absolute "do whatever you want." A cultivator has the right to deduce in the acceleration domain, but no right to occupy others' basic time quotas during deduction; has the right to trial in the Quest domain, but no right to drag others into his own trial script without their informed consent (Entry 561). Autonomy's boundary stops at the starting point of others' equal rights — this is precisely the fundamental difference between the symbiotic constitution and the predatory faction's "strong-man freedom."

## IV. Contrast with the Old System's Time Predation

In predatory-faction systems, time rights are held by the strongest: the emperor decides when the commoners "die"; the Immortal Elder decides when the mortals "become immortal"; the system decides when the protagonist "opens a cheat." The individual has no time autonomy, only an allocated fate. Heng Yuan Zhou returns time rights to the individual — not the strong one's grace, not the system's distribution, but constitutionally conferred, guarded by five defense lines.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the individual best understands his own rhythm; autonomous pacing wastes less than externally forced arrangement
- **Anti-predation**: institutionally roots out the possibility of "the strong plundering the weak's time"; five defense layers lock it down
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system time rights are completely monopolized by the system/protagonist; billions of beings are only time-background boards; in Heng Yuan Zhou time rights return to the individual
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: time autonomy covers 99% of Myriad Ranks, regardless of strength, race, or realm
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: time-right constitutional clauses are public and transparent; newly merged planes automatically inherit; no threshold toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Individual time autonomy is the starting point of all civilizational design in the dual-track time domain. Without this constitutional guarantee, the Origin Acceleration Domain would become a new tool for a few strong ones to squeeze the many's time; with this guarantee, the dual-track time domain truly becomes a Myriad-Ranks-Self-Chosen-Destiny paradise. Time belongs to every being — this is not benevolence, this is law.
