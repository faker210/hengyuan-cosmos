# Entry 669 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Democratic-Consultation Mechanism for Myriad-Rank Time Preferences

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 49, 29 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 3)

## Introduction: Myriad Ranks' understanding of "fast" and "slow" differs vastly

The carbon-based human race lives only a hundred years; the longing for "fast" is engraved in its genes. The energy-condensation race oscillates in units of ten thousand years; "fast" means nothing to it. The mechanical-construction race operates at millisecond precision; "slow" is, in its view, intolerable waste. If the same set of time quotas is forcibly imposed on Myriad Ranks, it equals forcing fish and birds to live by the same schedule. Six top-level axioms here raise consultation requirements: anti-predation axiom forbids forcing a clan to change its life rhythm in the name of "efficiency"; republican axiom of Myriad Races confirms each clan's equal say on time preferences; collective ascension axiom requires consultation results serve Myriad Ranks as a whole; causal self-discipline axiom requires consultation not break each clan's causal continuity; law-first axiom requires the consultation mechanism itself have stable procedures. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry establishes a democratic-consultation mechanism for Myriad-Rank time preferences.

## I. Why a Single Standard Cannot Be Used

The chronic malady of old cosmic systems is treating "the human-race strong one's time sense" as the whole universe's time standard. Predatory-faction Immortal Emperors measure mortals with their own lifespan scale, conclude "a mortal's life is not worth mentioning," and thereby rationalize all predation. Heng Yuan Zhou rejects this hegemony: time preference is civilization's inherent attribute, not a deviation to be corrected.

## II. Three-Layer Structure of the Consultation Mechanism

### 2.1 Base Layer: Each Clan Self-Reports Its Time-Preference Profile

Each major clan lineage (the five lineages — carbon-based, energy, mechanical, consciousness, mixed) upon entering the anchor network must submit a "Time-Preference Profile": this clan's typical lifespan, the physiological basis of time perception, the maximum tolerable acceleration-domain flow rate, the minimum Quest-domain sedimentation cycle, cultural attitudes toward "fast" and "slow." After verification by the Alaya Network, the profile enters the Anchor Council database.

### 2.2 Consultation Layer: Time-Quota Roundtable

The Anchor Council annually convenes a time-quota roundtable; the five lineages each send representatives to attend, comparing each clan's preference profile with that year's civilizational master-switch quota to negotiate an allocation plan. Consultation rules include:

- Basic quota first guaranteed by the 99%-coverage principle, then discuss excess allocation;
- No clan may have its basic quota cut because of "low efficiency";
- Consultation results must be approved by a two-thirds majority of clan representatives;
- Consultation records are network-wide public, subject to Anchor Supervisory Committee audit.

### 2.3 Arbitration Layer: Symbiotic Court's Time-Quota Adjudication

If roundtable consultation deadlocks, the symbiotic court adjudicates according to symbiotic-constitution time clauses. The adjudication does not pursue a mathematically "absolutely fair" solution, but pursues a bottom-line solution of "not harming any clan's basic time rights" — better to let powerful clans take a little less, than let vulnerable clans live one day less.

## III. Key Issues in Consultation

| Issue | Conflict Point | Consultation Direction |
|-------|----------------|------------------------|
| Acceleration-Resource Allocation | Mechanical clan wants fast; energy clan indifferent | Allocate by each clan's maximum tolerable flow rate, not forced |
| Quest-Cycle Arrangement | Carbon-based clan's lifespan is short; cannot afford long quest | Allow carbon-based clan to use acceleration domain to compensate quest time |
| Cross-Clan Collaboration Time Sync | How clans with different time perceptions coordinate | Alaya Network does time translation, does not change each one's flow rate |
| New-Generation Cultivation | Each clan's Youth Supreme growth rhythm differs | Design trial cycles differentially by lineage (Entries 519–524) |

## IV. Guarding Against "Majority Tyranny" in Consultation

The greatest risk of democratic consultation is major clans jointly bullying minor clans. Heng Yuan Zhou's defense mechanism: time-quota consultation does not adopt "winner-take-all" majority decision, but adopts a "floor + consultation" structure — basic quotas may not be cut by vote; only excess quotas enter consultation. This way, even if some clan is only one-thousandth of cosmic beings, its basic time rights remain inviolable.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: consultation makes each clan's time quota match its life rhythm, avoiding the absurd waste of "giving the mechanical clan a slow track, giving the human clan a ten-thousand-year-long track"
- **Anti-predation**: institutionally prevents powerful clans from partitioning vulnerable clans' time resources in the name of "efficiency"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system time resources are allocated by the system according to protagonist priority; Myriad Ranks are only background; in Heng Yuan Zhou Myriad Ranks' roundtable consultates, each clan has a say
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the consultation mechanism covers 99% of beings of the five lineages; the smallest clan also has a representative seat
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: consultation procedures, floor principle, arbitration rules all public; newly merged cosmos automatically enters the roundtable; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Time preferences have no superiority or inferiority, only difference. The democratic-consultation mechanism's purpose is not to make all races run by the same schedule, but to let Myriad Ranks of different life rhythms coexist respectfully in the same cosmos. Fast has fast's value; slow has slow's dignity — consultation is writing this dignity into the quota table.
