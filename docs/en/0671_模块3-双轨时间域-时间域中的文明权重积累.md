# Entry 671 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Accumulation of Civilizational Weight in the Time Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 29, 21, 25 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 5)

## Introduction: time is civilizational weight's "compound-interest account"

Civilizational weight is the anchor network's quantified ruler over each cultivator's and each clan's say in the Myriad-Rank Republican system. In predatory-faction systems, weight is decided by fist size — whoever can fight most gets to speak. Heng Yuan Zhou's civilizational weight is accumulated mainly by time: how many law increments are backflowed in the Origin Acceleration Domain; how many epic experiences are sedimented in the Quest Sediment Domain; how many Myriad-Rank users reuse them in the Alaya Network — these time-unit investments compound-interest into civilizational weight. Six top-level axioms here shape accumulation direction: anti-predation axiom forbids using predatory means to rapidly farm weight; republican axiom of Myriad Races ensures accumulation paths open to 99% of beings; collective ascension axiom lets weight serve the whole; causal self-discipline axiom lets accumulation not create causal overdraft; law-first axiom lets weight formulas stable and recalculable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry sorts out the accumulation mechanism of civilizational weight in the time domain.

## I. Each Track's Weight Contribution

### 1.1 Origin Acceleration Domain's Weight Contribution: Innovative Type

Each time a law increment deduced and produced in the acceleration domain is reused network-wide, it accrues civilizational weight for the producer. This kind of weight contribution is "explosive" — an epoch-making deduction may let a young cultivator's weight soar. But explosive contributions must pass the Alaya Network's "stability verification": after the law increment lands on the material plane without side effects and without triggering causal disorder, it formally enters weight accounting.

### 1.2 Quest Sediment Domain's Weight Contribution: Sedimented Type

Each time an epic experience, tribulation insight, or embodied knowledge of all beings' suffering sedimented in the Quest domain is later called and studied by later beings in the Alaya Network, it accrues civilizational weight for the one who sedimented it. This kind of weight contribution is "lingering" — an epic may still be read ten thousand years later; its weight contribution continues to release in ten-thousand-year units.

### 1.3 Dual-Track Weight Complementarity

Acceleration-domain weight rewards "new"; Quest-domain weight rewards "deep." A cultivator who takes only the fast track has high "innovation score" but low "sedimentation score"; a cultivator who takes only the slow track has high "sedimentation score" but low "innovation score." Complete civilizational weight requires accumulation on both tracks — this is precisely the balancing art (Entry 667)'s expression in the weight formula.

## II. Three-Layer Locking of Weight Accumulation

| Lock Layer | Content | Prevents |
|------------|---------|----------|
| Authenticity Lock | Alaya Network fully records time-domain experiences, non-tamperable | Faking experiences to farm weight |
| Benefit-Scope Lock | Weight accumulates only by Myriad-Rank benefit count, not by self-boasting | Self-aggrandizing weight |
| Anti-Predation Lock | Predation records permanently deduct weight, non-recoverable | Predating to trade for weight |

Three layers of locking ensure civilizational weight is not "self-proclaimed," but voted in by Myriad Ranks "with their feet."

## III. The "Slow-Variable" Constraint on Weight Accumulation

Civilizational weight most fears "getting rich overnight." Heng Yuan Zhou sets a "slow-variable" constraint on weight accumulation: a single year's weight increment may not exceed a certain proportion of the individual's historical weight total, preventing individual geniuses from obtaining excessive say through one acceleration-domain burst. This echoes the "mind-nature 25 points" in Entry 683's plane-transcendent four-dimensional assessment — say's growth must synchronize with mind-nature sedimentation; otherwise better not to issue it.

## IV. The Conversion of Weight to Say

Civilizational weight ultimately converts into two kinds of say: one, legislative voting weight in the Anchor Council; two, "recommendation slot" weight for individual outputs in the Alaya Network. But weight does not equal privilege — high-weight individuals bear higher responsibility assessments (Entry 683); the higher the weight, the stricter the audit. This cuts off the predatory-faction logic of "weight = privilege."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: weight accumulates by Myriad-Rank benefit scope; stamps out wasting computing power on self-entertainment-style deduction
- **Anti-predation**: predation records permanently deduct weight, letting predators never obtain say through fists
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system say is bestowed by the system; the protagonist's weight is naturally first; in Heng Yuan Zhou weight accumulates by time, counts by Myriad-Rank benefit, transparent and auditable
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: weight-accumulation paths are open to 99% of Myriad Ranks; carbon-based clans' life sedimentation, mechanical clans' efficiency innovation may all accrue weight
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: weight formula, three-layer locking, slow-variable constraint all public; newly merged planes automatically apply; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Civilizational weight is time's compound-interest settlement in the Myriad-Rank co-ruler system. It is not snatched by fists, not bestowed by the system, but bit by bit innovated in the acceleration domain and sedimented in the Quest domain. The longer the time, the more solid the contribution, the broader the benefit scope, the thicker the weight — this is the law guarantee that "time stands on the co-builders' side."
