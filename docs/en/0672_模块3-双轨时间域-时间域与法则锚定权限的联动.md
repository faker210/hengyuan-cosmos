# Entry 672 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Linkage Between Time Domain and Law-Anchoring Authority

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 20, 549, 552 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 6)

## Introduction: those who may regulate time must first understand laws

The essence of the Origin Acceleration Domain and Quest Sediment Domain is law-level regulation of local time flow. Who has the right to regulate time flow? This question is more fundamental than "can it be regulated." In predatory-faction systems, time-regulation power is held by the strongest — an Immortal Emperor may with a thought reverse a mortal's life and death. Heng Yuan Zhou puts this power into the "law-anchoring authority" system: every flow adjustment of the time domain must pass law-anchoring authority verification. Six top-level axioms are highly concentrated here: law-first axiom is the direct design blueprint; anti-predation axiom forbids using time-regulation power to plunder; republican axiom of Myriad Races confirms authority's open rules to Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom lets authority serve the whole; causal self-discipline axiom lets time regulation not disturb causality. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry sorts out the linkage mechanism between the time domain and law-anchoring authority.

## I. Tiers of Law-Anchoring Authority

According to Document 20 anchor structure and hierarchy, law-anchoring authority is divided by tier:

| Authority Tier | Adjustable Content | Holder |
|----------------|--------------------|---------|
| L1 Basic | Personal time-domain flow fine-tuning | All cultivators |
| L2 Clan-Level | Shared clan time-domain flow ratio | Clan council institutions |
| L3 Plane-Level | Entire plane time-domain master switch | Symbiotic Executive Committee |
| L4 Cross-Plane | Multi-cosmic time-domain coordination | Anchor Council + symbiotic court co-sign |
| L5 Hyper-Multiverse | Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's eternal time | Common will of all co-builders |

The higher the tier, the stricter the authority verification. L1 authority is auto-passed by the Alaya Network; L4 authority must be legislated by the Anchor Council and confirmed by the symbiotic court as causally non-disruptive before execution.

## II. Three Checks of Linkage

### 2.1 Law-Consistency Check

Any time-domain flow adjustment must pass the law network's consistency check: the new flow may not conflict with the plane's existing laws, may not cause energy non-conservation, may not trigger time paradoxes. The check is auto-executed by the law network; if not passed, execution is refused. This is law-first axiom's direct landing — plot may not kidnap laws; time-domain adjustment must obey laws.

### 2.2 Causal-Disturbance Check

Per causal self-discipline axiom, time-domain adjustment must assess its causal impact: will it accidentally rewrite some being's fate? Will it cause historical reversal? If the check result shows "causal disturbance exceeds limit," the adjustment plan must be returned for redo. Cross-plane time regulation is especially strict — only knowledge is returned, causality not disturbed (Entry 547).

### 2.3 Myriad-Rank-Impact Check

Time-domain adjustment affects not one person, but all beings of a clan or plane. The check must confirm: the adjustment does not harm any clan's basic time rights (Entry 668), does not create time discrimination (Entry 669).

## III. Dynamic Authority Promotion and Demotion

Law-anchoring authority is not lifelong. If a cultivator violates rules during time-domain operations (such as abusing acceleration resources, disturbing causality), his authority tier auto-demotes; if he is long-term compliant and makes major contributions to Myriad Ranks, his authority tier may promote. Promotion and demotion are fully recorded by the Alaya Network, audited by the Anchor Supervisory Committee, and do not change with individuals.

## IV. Contrast with the Old System

In predatory-faction systems, time-regulation power is "divinely conferred" — the strongest naturally has the right, no verification, no accountability. Heng Yuan Zhou turns time-regulation power into "law-conferred" — authority comes from the law network, constrained by laws, supervised by multiple layers. This fundamentally stamps out the theocratic time dictatorship of "one thought decides another's life and death."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: law-anchoring authority lets time regulation proceed only within law-permitted ranges; stamps out computing-power waste from repeated trial-and-error
- **Anti-predation**: three checks + tier authorization make time-regulation power unable to be privately abused as a predation tool
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system time (system acceleration) is completely commanded by the protagonist, with no checks; in Heng Yuan Zhou every time regulation passes three checks
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: L1 authority is open to all beings; L2–L4 authority is open to Myriad-Rank clans by contribution and responsibility
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: authority tiers, check rules, promotion/demotion mechanisms all public; newly merged cosmos automatically enters; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Behind the time domain is law; behind law is the anchor; behind the anchor is Myriad-Rank co-building. Law-anchoring authority turns the question "who may regulate time" from the strong one's will into law's rules. The time domain may be extreme speed, may be deep sediment, but every flow change must first pass law's test — this is law-first axiom's embodiment in the dual-track time domain.
