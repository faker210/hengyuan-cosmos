# Entry 673 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Alaya-Network Multi-Layer Supervision in the Time Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 21, 27, 29 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 7)

## Introduction: the time domain most easily harbors filth, so supervision must be strictest

The Origin Acceleration Domain flows extremely fast; a cultivator deduces, breaks through, produces inside it — outsiders cannot really see in real time what he is doing. The Quest Sediment Domain flows extremely slowly; a being experiences a hundred years of tribulation inside it — outsiders even harder to judge in real time whether he is trialing or being exploited. The time domain's "black box" attribute determines it is the place most likely to harbor filth. Six top-level axioms here require the strictest supervision design: anti-predation axiom requires supervising all predation behavior in the time domain; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires supervision equal for 99% of beings; collective ascension axiom lets supervision serve co-building; causal self-discipline axiom lets supervision itself not disturb causality; law-first axiom lets supervision rules stable and non-personal. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry establishes the complete system of Alaya-Network + multi-layer supervision in the time domain.

## I. Four-Layer Supervision Structure

### 1.1 Layer 1: Automatic Monitoring (Real-Time)

The Alaya Network automatically monitors all activities inside the time domain: whether energy-flow direction comes from predation, whether time-flow adjustments pass law checks, whether individual time choices are truly voluntary. Monitoring is fully automatic, no blind spots, not relying on people to report. Any anomaly auto-alarms and is recorded into files.

### 1.2 Layer 2: Ethical Review (Stage)

After a cultivator completes a stage in the time domain (such as an acceleration deduction cycle ending, a Quest trial exiting seclusion), he enters ethical review: reviewed for predation behavior during the period, for disturbing others' causality, for violating informed consent. Review is executed by the ethical body under the Symbiotic Executive Committee; results are public.

### 1.3 Layer 3: External Audit (Periodic)

The Anchor Supervisory Committee quarterly externally audits the time domain's overall operation: whether acceleration-resource allocation complies with more-work-more-gain, whether quotas are abused, whether time discrimination exists. Audit reports are network-wide public; anyone may check.

### 1.4 Layer 4: Myriad-Rank Hearing (Major Events)

If time-domain operations involve cross-plane or cross-cosmos (L4–L5 authority), or major violations occur, a Myriad-Rank hearing is convened: representatives of each clan, the symbiotic court, the Anchor Supervisory Committee, the cultivator involved jointly attend; openly debate, openly adjudicate. Hearing is the supervision system's highest layer, and also the direct embodiment of Myriad-Rank say.

## II. The Balance Between Supervision's "Transparency Principle" and "Privacy Protection"

Supervision does not mean monitoring everything. The Alaya Network's supervision of the time domain follows the "behavior logged, thought free" principle: energy activities, law operations, causal impacts of cultivators in the time domain are all logged (this is behavior supervision); but their inner thoughts, emotional experiences, private memories are not read (this is privacy protection). What is supervised is "what was done," not "what was thought." This boundary comes from causal self-discipline axiom — not disturbing others' inner world.

## III. Handling the Tension Between Supervision and Autonomy

Will strict supervision violate individual time autonomy (Entry 668)? Heng Yuan Zhou's handling: supervision targets behavior, not choices; targets violations, not preferences. A cultivator chooses to stay in the acceleration domain for a hundred years; as long as there is no predation, no causal disturbance, no quota abuse, the Alaya Network only records, does not intervene. Supervision is a "night watchman," not a "housekeeper."

## IV. Contrast with the Predatory Faction's "Unsupervised Freedom"

The predatory faction's so-called "freedom" is the strong one's unsupervised freedom — an Immortal Emperor may operate time at will with no one daring to ask. Heng Yuan Zhou's supervision system precisely puts this "strong-man freedom" into a law cage. The stricter the supervision, the safer the co-builders' time autonomy — because no one can secretly plunder your time.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: four-layer supervision stamps out violating idling, fake output, quota waste in the time domain
- **Anti-predation**: automatic monitoring + ethical review + external audit + Myriad-Rank hearing make predation behavior have nowhere to hide in the time domain
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system the protagonist's time operations (system acceleration) have no supervision; Myriad Ranks are crushed by time without knowing it; in Heng Yuan Zhou every time operation is under supervision
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: four-layer supervision covers 99% of Myriad Ranks, regardless of strength or race
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: supervision rules, transparency principle, privacy boundaries all public; newly merged planes automatically enter; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The time domain's freedom must take strict supervision as premise. Alaya-Network + multi-layer supervision is not distrust of cultivators, but guarding Myriad Ranks' time rights — it ensures the Origin Acceleration Domain does not become a new predation ground, the Quest Sediment Domain does not become a new exploitation den. The denser the supervision, the more real the freedom.
