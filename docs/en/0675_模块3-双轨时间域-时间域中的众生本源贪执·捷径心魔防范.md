# Entry 675 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Prevention of the Beings'-Origin-Attachment · Shortcut Inner Demon in the Time Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 19, 04, 508 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 9)

## Introduction: the acceleration domain is the shortcut inner demon's greatest hotbed

The beings'-origin-attachment · shortcut inner demon is every cultivator's deepest enemy. It is not an external monster, but the obsession hidden in every being's heart: why must others spend a hundred years cultivating, why can I not become immortal overnight? Why must others take the slow track, why can I not cut a corner directly? The Origin Acceleration Domain's existence precisely provides a "seemingly reasonable" outlet for this attachment — "I am accelerating legally, not plundering." If not guarded against, the acceleration domain becomes the shortcut inner demon's greatest hotbed: it makes cultivators think they are on the fast lane, but step by step fall into demonhood. Six top-level axioms here constitute defense lines: anti-predation axiom directly opposes shortcut predation; republican axiom of Myriad Races reminds cultivators "you are not the only protagonist"; collective ascension axiom lets desire point to co-building not monopolizing; causal self-discipline axiom lets impatience receive causal calibration; law-first axiom lets "shortcuts" be identified at the law level as false paths. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry establishes a prevention system for shortcut inner demons in the time domain.

## I. Four Disguises of the Shortcut Inner Demon in the Time Domain

### 1.1 Disguise 1: "Acceleration Is Not Predation"

The inner demon's talk: I am deducing legally in the Origin Acceleration Domain, devouring no one, why say I am attached? Prevention mechanism: the Alaya Network monitors the "causal source" of acceleration-domain output — if deduction material comes from unrevealed private-hoarded laws, from secret drawing from other planes, from shortcuts that bypass ethical review, even if formally inside the acceleration domain, it is still judged shortcut predation. The distinction between legal acceleration and illegal shortcut is not in location, but in source.

### 1.2 Disguise 2: "My Being Faster Than Others Means I Am Chosen"

The inner demon's talk: my acceleration-domain output efficiency is high, proving I am the chosen one and should enjoy more quota. Prevention mechanism: contribution coefficient is computed by Myriad-Rank benefit scope (Entry 670), not by personal-efficiency worship. High efficiency is only data, does not constitute privilege.

### 1.3 Disguise 3: "The Quest Domain Is Too Slow, I Cannot Wait"

The inner demon's talk: others quest for a hundred years; my lifespan is short and I cannot wait; I must skip sedimentation. Prevention mechanism: the Quest Sediment Domain's cycle may be adjusted by individual life rhythm (Entry 691 carbon-based clan life rhythm), but may not be skipped — mind-nature sedimentation is a law requirement, not optional decoration.

### 1.4 Disguise 4: "I Am Just Borrowing Predatory-Faction Experience"

The inner demon's talk: I study Tang-Luo and Shrouding-the-Heavens' predatory paths to be anti-predatory, not to really plunder. Prevention mechanism: Quest-domain simulation debrief (Entries 513–515) does study the predatory faction, but the purpose is correction, not imitation; the Alaya Network monitors whether the research direction slides from "critique" to "emulation."

## II. Three Gates of Prevention

| Gate | Position | Content |
|------|----------|---------|
| Entrance Gate | Before entering the time domain | Mind-nature questioning: why do you choose this track? Is it for co-building or for monopolizing? |
| Process Gate | During time-domain operation | Alaya Network monitors output source, efficiency motivation, emotional fluctuations |
| Exit Gate | After leaving the time domain | Ethical review + mind-nature assessment, confirm no traces of demon-entry |

The three gates are not "catching bad guys," but "holding up a mirror" — letting cultivators at each node have the opportunity for self-examination.

## III. The Inner Demon's Root: Anxiety About "Time Scarcity"

The shortcut inner demon's root is not greed, but deep anxiety about time scarcity: the carbon-based clan's lifespan is finite, fearing it is too late; the mechanical clan's efficiency-first, fearing being left behind; the consciousness clan's subjective time easily changes, fearing loss of control. Heng Yuan Zhou's radical cure is not "eliminating desire," but "eliminating scarcity anxiety" — basic time-right guarantee (Entry 668) lets every being not panic about "too late"; Myriad-Origin two-way cycle (Entry 674) lets the individual not monopolize everything. Anxiety eliminated, the inner demon naturally loses soil.

## IV. Contrast with the Predatory-Faction System

Predatory-faction systems do not guard against shortcut inner demons, but use them — they deliberately create time scarcity, deliberately shape "chosen protagonists," letting billions of beings under inner-demon drive plunder each other. Heng Yuan Zhou writes inner-demon prevention into every gate of the time domain, letting the acceleration domain be a co-building accelerator, not a conveyor belt into demonhood.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: shortcut inner demons waste computing power on false paths; preventing it is protecting computing power
- **Anti-predation**: directly opposes all covert predation, identifying even "shortcuts under legal cover"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system encourages the protagonist to take shortcuts (system grace); Myriad Ranks under inner-demon drive involute; in Heng Yuan Zhou the shortcut inner demon is listed as the number-one prevention object
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: inner-demon prevention is equal for 99% of Myriad Ranks, regardless of strength
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: four disguises, three gates, radical cure all public; newly merged planes automatically apply; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The faster the Origin Acceleration Domain, the more tempting the shortcut inner demon. Preventing it cannot rely on "banning acceleration," but on "staying awake in acceleration." Three gates, four-disguise identification, eliminating time-scarcity anxiety — these are not shackles, but protection. True freedom is still knowing why you are fast when you are at extreme speed.
