# Entry 676 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Cultivation of the "Righteous Rascal" in the Time Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 19, 05, 29 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 10)

## Introduction: the true co-builder must be one who "knows darkness but does not walk darkness"

The "righteous rascal" is Heng Yuan Zhou's distinctive personality archetype: he is not unacquainted with the predatory faction's ways — in the Quest Sediment Domain he has personally experienced, simulated, even played predators; he knows how darkness operates; but he chooses not to do so. This kind of "knowing black, guarding white" person is more reliable than the naive good person who has never seen darkness — because his righteousness is not ignorance, but choice. Six top-level axioms here shape cultivation direction: anti-predation axiom requires he ultimately stand on the anti-predation side; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires he escort 99% of beings; collective ascension axiom requires he convert experience into co-building experience; causal self-discipline axiom requires he not truly harm others when playing a predator; law-first axiom requires the cultivation process stable and controllable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry sorts out the cultivation mechanism of the "righteous rascal" in the time domain.

## I. Why Need a "Rascal" Not a "Good Person"

The naive good person cannot cope with the real predatory-faction game. Predators do not play by rules, leave no face, pick on weaknesses. If a co-builder only understands "building" not "predation," once encountering real predation he will be instantly pierced. Therefore Heng Yuan Zhou actively cultivates "righteous rascals" — he must in the Quest Sediment Domain fully experience the predatory faction's game environment, understand the other side's rhetoric, methods, psychology, then bring this understanding back to the symbiotic camp.

## II. Three Cultivation Stages

### 2.1 Simulation Stage: Predatory-Faction Debrief in the Quest Domain

In the Quest Sediment Domain's simulated environment (Entry 513 Quest-Domain simulation-debrief methodology), a cultivator is arranged to play a predatory-faction role: in a simulated Tang-Luo world, Shrouding world, experience "how the strong plunder the weak," "how the system bestows grace on the protagonist." Throughout the performance no real beings are harmed — all simulated objects are virtual characters generated by the Alaya Network. This stage's goal is "know black."

### 2.2 Enlightenment Stage: Compassion Arising from Predatory Experience

After simulation reaches a certain depth, the cultivator truly feels the plundered one's pain — even if the other is a virtual character, the emotional experience is real. This "empathy breakthrough" is the key enlightenment point: he finally understands predation is not "the strong one's freedom," but "the weak one's nightmare." After enlightenment, he actively chooses to return to the symbiotic camp. This stage's goal is "know black then guard white."

### 2.3 Practical Stage: Trial on the Anti-Predation Frontline

After enlightenment, the cultivator enters the anti-predation frontline: in the Origin Acceleration Domain he identifies predation disguised as deduction; in cross-plane patrols he identifies predation behavior; in Myriad-Rank hearings he testifies; in the symbiotic court he serves as juror. His "rascal" knowledge here converts into the ability to protect Myriad Ranks. This stage's goal is "using rascal's wisdom to do righteous things."

## III. Ethical Boundaries of Cultivation

Playing a predator does not equal allowing predation. The cultivation process has strict ethical boundaries (Entry 520):

- In simulation, real beings may not be used as predation objects;
- Performance depth is autonomously controlled by the cultivator; may exit at any time;
- After simulation ends, psychological counseling is mandatory, preventing "playing too deeply" from truly becoming a predator;
- Throughout, Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision monitors whether the cultivator slides from "playing" to "emulating."

## IV. The Righteous Rascal and the Lin Hao Archetype

Lin Hao (Entry 679) is the typical righteous rascal archetype: in multi-line growth he fully experienced the predatory faction's temptation, simulated the predatory faction's path, finally chose Anchor Symbiosis. He is not a born sage, but a co-builder who "knows black, guards white." This archetype can better hold Myriad-Rank republicanism in the complex cosmos than the "naive good person."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: cultivating co-builders who understand predation, compared with cultivating naive good people, can better cope with real games, reducing reconstruction waste after "being pierced"
- **Anti-predation**: understanding the predatory faction from "inside" makes anti-predation more effective
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system cultivates "chosen protagonists" who need not understand darkness because the system clears everything for them; Heng Yuan Zhou cultivates "righteous rascals" because he must face darkness himself
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: righteous-rascal cultivation is open to 99% of Myriad Ranks; each clan may produce its own escort
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: three-stage cultivation method, ethical boundaries, supervision mechanisms all public; newly merged planes automatically apply; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The righteous rascal is the personality Myriad-Rank symbiotic civilization most needs. He has not not seen darkness, but after seeing darkness still chooses light. The time domain — especially the Quest Sediment Domain — provides a safe training ground for this personality: know black in simulation, guard white in enlightenment, escort in practice. This is more durable than naive righteousness, more powerful than naive kindness.
