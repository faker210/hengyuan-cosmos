# Entry 677 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Conversion Mechanism for Enlightened Predators in the Time Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 05, 514 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 11)

## Introduction: to predators, Heng Yuan Zhou does not wipe them out, but gives a path of conversion

In the old cosmos, the predatory faction and the symbiotic faction are life-and-death enemies: the strong devour the weak; the weak, upon becoming strong, devour the even weaker; the cycle has no rest. Heng Yuan Zhou does not deny the predatory faction's existence — among multiverse residual civilizations there must still be beings with predatory thinking — but it rejects the "annihilate all" solution. Because eliminating predators is itself a kind of predation (violence in the name of justice). The path Heng Yuan Zhou gives is the "enlightened-predator conversion mechanism": let those predatory-faction strong ones who already realize the predatory road is impassable and are willing to turn to co-building complete conversion in the time domain. Six top-level axioms here constitute conversion legitimacy: anti-predation axiom does not indulge predation but also does not respond to violence with violence; republican axiom of Myriad Races acknowledges converters' equal evolution qualification; collective ascension axiom lets converters become co-building forces; causal self-discipline axiom lets the conversion process not create new causal debt; law-first axiom lets the conversion procedure stable and recalculable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry sorts out the enlightened-predator conversion mechanism in the time domain.

## I. Who Is an "Enlightened Predator"

An enlightened predator is not an ordinary predator, but a predatory-faction strong one with three characteristics:

1. **Already realizes the predatory road is a dead end**: he has lived through the predatory-faction system's collapse (such as the Tang-Luo world's grace-system collapse, the Shrouding world's dark unrest), personally seen that predation cannot continue;
2. **Willing to think about alternative paths**: he is not satisfied with "swap in another strong one and keep plundering," but willing to think "is there a non-predatory evolution path?";
3. **Willing to accept rule constraints**: he is willing to accept Alaya-Network + multi-layer supervision, willing to publicly audit his past predation records.

Three characteristics, all required. For diehard predatory-faction elements who refuse reflection, Heng Yuan Zhou does not force conversion, but quarantines and observes per causal self-discipline axiom.

## II. Five-Stage Conversion Process

### 2.1 Reception and Loss-Stop

The converter first enters the time domain's "loss-stop zone": his predatory behavior immediately stops; resources obtained through past predation are frozen pending audit. This stage does not hold him accountable, does not humiliate him, but also does not continue indulgence. Loss-stop is the premise of conversion — you cannot "pretend to convert" while still plundering.

### 2.2 Quest-Domain Personal Experience

The converter enters the Quest Sediment Domain, experiencing as a "plundered one" once more the suffering he had inflicted on others. This is not humiliation, but letting him emotionally truly understand predation's cost. The whole personal experience is conducted under Alaya-Network supervision; intensity controlled by ethical review.

### 2.3 Cognitive Reconstruction

After personal experience, the converter enters the cognitive-reconstruction stage: systematically studies Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's five axioms, the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle, the anti-predation core treatise. Learning is not indoctrination, but letting him himself deduce "if I had chosen co-building back then, what would civilization look like."

### 2.4 Trial-Building Contribution

After cognitive reconstruction, the converter completes one "co-building output" in the Origin Acceleration Domain: using his familiar deduction ability (the technical core of his former predatory ability) to produce a law increment for Myriad Ranks. The quality of this output is the key evidence of whether conversion succeeds.

### 2.5 Return and Supervision

After the trial-building contribution passes ethical review, the converter formally returns to the Myriad-Rank co-building system. But his civilizational weight (Entry 671) accumulates from zero — past predatory merits all do not count. At the same time, the converter accepts an enhanced supervision period of one cycle, confirming conversion is real and stable.

## III. The Anti-Predation Essence of the Conversion Mechanism

It must be emphasized: the conversion mechanism is not "forgiving predation," and certainly not "predatory merit gets amnesty." The essence of conversion is "let predators become co-builders, converting the negative energy predation produces into co-building positive energy." The causal debt of past predation is repaid by the converter's lifetime contribution — this better conforms to causal self-discipline axiom than simply killing him.

## IV. Contrast with the "Eliminate the Predatory Faction" Route

Predatory-faction systems advocate "eliminate heretics"; naive symbiotic factions may advocate "reform everyone." Heng Yuan Zhou's conversion mechanism lies between the two: does not eliminate (that is responding to violence with violence), does not reform (that is abandoning principles), but objectively converts by the five-stage process — those who can convert are given a road; those who cannot are quarantined. This is law-first axiom's expression in social governance.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: converting a predator's technical ability for co-building saves more computing power and energy than eliminating him
- **Anti-predation**: loss-stop + personal experience + audit + zero-based weight, does not indulge any predatory benefit
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system predators and plundered are fixed classes with no conversion channel; in Heng Yuan Zhou five-stage conversion lets predators re-enter co-building
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the conversion mechanism faces enlightened predators among multiverse residual civilizations, regardless of race
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: five-stage process, loss-stop rules, supervision mechanisms all public; newly merged cosmos automatically applies; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The enlightened-predator conversion mechanism is Heng Yuan Zhou's mature expression of "anti-predatory but not hostile to predators." It acknowledges the existence of predatory thinking, acknowledges the possibility of conversion, also acknowledges the difficulty of conversion. The time domain — especially the Quest Sediment Domain — provides a safe space for this conversion: understand suffering in personal experience, reconstruct paths in cognition, prove change in contribution. This is not benevolence; this is causal self-discipline.
