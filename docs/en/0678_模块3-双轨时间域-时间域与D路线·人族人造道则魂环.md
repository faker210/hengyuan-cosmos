# Entry 678 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Time Domain and Path D · Humankind's Artificial Dao-Principle Spirit Rings

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 08, 10 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 12)

## Introduction: humankind need not take the old road of "hunting spirit beasts"; the time domain makes "artificial Dao principles" possible

In the Douluo system, human cultivators must hunt spirit beasts to obtain spirit rings — this is a typical predatory-style evolution: spirit-beast life becomes the consumable material for humankind's leveling. Heng Yuan Zhou's Path D · humankind's artificial Dao-principle spirit rings fundamentally replace this predatory path: humankind need not hunt any being, but in the Origin Acceleration Domain, in a technologized way, "manufacture" Dao-principle spirit rings. The dual-track time domain plays a key role here — the acceleration domain provides deduction computing power; the Quest domain provides ethical calibration. Six top-level axioms are highly concentrated here: anti-predation axiom directly negates hunting spirit beasts; republican axiom of Myriad Races confirms spirit beasts and humankind's equal evolution qualification; collective ascension axiom lets humankind's Dao-principle spirit rings serve Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom lets artificial Dao principles not disturb natural causality; law-first axiom lets "artificial Dao principles" be valid at the law level. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry sorts out the linkage mechanism between the time domain and Path D.

## I. Path D's Core: Replace "Hunting" with "Manufacturing"

Traditional Douluo path: spirit beast → hunt → spirit ring → humankind levels. Its essence is devouring others' lives.

Path D: Origin Acceleration Domain → law deduction → artificial Dao-principle spirit ring → humankind levels. Its essence is developing incremental laws.

The difference between the two is not in result (both produce spirit rings), but in source (devouring vs. manufacturing). Path D uses technologized deduction to replace biological hunting, cutting off from energy-theory the predatory dependence on spirit beasts.

## II. The Time Domain's Three Roles in Path D

### 2.1 Origin Acceleration Domain: The Deduction Factory of Dao Principles

The "Dao-principle core" of artificial Dao-principle spirit rings needs to be repeatedly deduced in the Origin Acceleration Domain: what law structure may replace natural spirit rings? The deduction process consumes large amounts of computing power — the acceleration domain's high flow lets this deduction complete in a few years of real time, not hundreds. The acceleration domain is Path D's "factory."

### 2.2 Quest Sediment Domain: Ethical Calibration of Dao Principles

Dao principles deduced and produced may not be directly used — they must in the Quest Sediment Domain undergo ethical calibration of "will this indirectly harm anyone?": does the artificial Dao principle still indirectly draw on some clan's origin in energy? Does it create some hidden predation in law? Calibration is executed by the symbiotic court's ethical body. The Quest domain is Path D's "quality inspection."

### 2.3 Dual-Track Synergy: Technical Iteration of Dao Principles

The acceleration domain produces the first-version Dao principle → Quest-domain quality inspection feedback → acceleration domain iterates and improves. Dual-track cycles make Path D's Dao-principle spirit rings generation after generation purer and more efficient. This is precisely the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle (Entry 674)'s manifestation on humankind's cultivation path.

## III. Path D and "Spirit-Beast Liberation"

Path D's byproduct is spirit-beast liberation: when humankind no longer needs to hunt spirit beasts, spirit beasts obtain the qualification to evolve jointly with equal humankind. Under Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad-Rank Republican framework, spirit beasts are no longer "leveling material," but one of Myriad Ranks — they have their own cultivation path, their own time preference, their own Alaya-Network node. This forms a fundamental contrast with the Douluo world's "civilization collapses after spirit beasts are exterminated" (Entry 538 historical-reversal simulation).

## IV. Path D's Law Self-Consistency

It must be emphasized: artificial Dao-principle spirit rings are not "pseudo-spirit-rings," but law carriers fully valid at the law level. Law-first axiom requires: spirit rings produced by Path D must pass the law network's consistency check (Entry 672); their energy structure, law anchoring, causal impact are equivalent to natural spirit rings. After the check passes, Path D spirit rings and natural spirit rings have no functional difference, but are completely pure in source. This stamps out the plot-kidnapping setting of "artificial spirit rings are defective products."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: replacing biological hunting with technical deduction saves triple waste of spirit-beast life, computing power, and causal cost
- **Anti-predation**: cutting off from energy source the predatory dependence on spirit beasts is a model of anti-predation axiom on humankind's cultivation path
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system humankind must hunt spirit beasts to level; spirit beasts are consumables; in Heng Yuan Zhou Path D artificial Dao principles, spirit beasts are liberated as one of Myriad Ranks
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Path D humankind is only one of Myriad Ranks; spirit beasts, energy clans, mechanical clans all have their own "non-predatory leveling" paths
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Path D's deduction method, ethical calibration, law check all public; newly merged cosmos may directly adopt; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Path D · humankind's artificial Dao-principle spirit rings is the dual-track time domain's most direct technical response to anti-predation: it uses the acceleration domain's deduction computing power to replace the predatory path of "hunting spirit beasts" with the co-building path of "artificial Dao principles." Humankind no longer needs killing as the price of leveling — this is not moral preaching, but technical fact. The time domain makes all this possible.
