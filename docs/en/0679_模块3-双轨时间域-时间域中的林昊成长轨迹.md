# Entry 679 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Lin Hao's Growth Trajectory in the Time Domain

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 17, 05, 14 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 13)

## Introduction: Lin Hao is not a chosen protagonist, but a product of the dual-track time domain

Predatory-faction protagonists — Shi Hao, Ye Fan, Tang-Luo — are all "chosen": system-selected, bloodline awakened, opportunities heaven-sent. Heng Yuan Zhou's protagonist Lin Hao walks a completely different road: he has no heaven-sent system, no bloodline awakening; his growth is the product of the dual-track time domain's institutional design — deduced in the Origin Acceleration Domain, sedimented in the Quest Sediment Domain, supervised in the Alaya Network, finding direction in Myriad-Rank Republicanism. Six top-level axioms here explain why Lin Hao can succeed: anti-predation axiom lets him not depend on devouring; republican axiom of Myriad Races lets him not fight alone; collective ascension axiom lets his growth serve Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom lets him not disturb causality; law-first axiom lets his growth path stable and replicable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry sorts out Lin Hao's complete growth trajectory in the time domain.

## I. Lin Hao's Growth Is Not "Protagonist Aura," But Dual-Track Synergy

Lin Hao's growth trajectory may be summarized as a "dual-track spiral":

| Stage | Origin Acceleration Domain | Quest Sediment Domain | Output |
|-------|----------------------------|-----------------------|--------|
| Initiation Period | Basic deduction iteration | Sense-Origin Realm rooting | Perceptual awakening |
| Growth Period | Technical deduction acceleration | Tribulation trial | Mind-nature solidified |
| Breakthrough Period | Law deduction burst | Setback sedimentation | Realm leap |
| Contribution Period | Innovative law backflow | Epic experience sedimentation | Civilizational weight accumulates |
| Escort Period | Cross-plane deduction | Anti-predation frontline trial | Plane-transcendent strong one |

This trajectory is fundamentally different from predatory-faction protagonists' linear "steamroll all the way" trajectory — it is spiral: after each acceleration comes sedimentation; after each breakthrough comes reflection.

## II. How Lin Hao Walked Through the Shortcut Inner Demon

Lin Hao was not without shortcut temptation. In the growth period, he once faced the simulated test of "if accepting system grace may advance by a thousand miles in a day" (righteous-rascal cultivation, Entry 676). He personally experienced in the Quest Sediment Domain the consequence of the grace system — in the Tang-Luo world billions of beings abandoned by the system. This personal experience let him truly reject shortcuts. His choice is not the result of moral preaching, but of real experience in the time domain. This is precisely the dual-track time domain's design purpose: let correct choices be built on real experience, not on books.

## III. Lin Hao and "Protagonist-Uniqueness Dependency Degree"

Module Two (Myriad-World benchmarking critique library) proposed the five-tier standard of protagonist-uniqueness dependency degree: Gu Chensha-style extremely high, Tang-Luo-style high, Ye Fan-style medium, Heng Yuan Zhou-style extremely low. Lin Hao belongs to Heng Yuan Zhou-style extremely low — his growth does not depend on anything "only he can do." What he can do, any cultivator taking the dual-track time domain can do; the law increments he produces all flow back network-wide for Myriad Ranks to reuse. This means: Lin Hao is not an "irreplaceable protagonist," but "the first sample to run through the whole process of the dual-track time domain." This positioning is greater than "chosen protagonist" — because he proved the road is passable.

## IV. Lin Hao's Time-Allocation Art

Lin Hao's time allocation differs by stage: initiation period 90% Quest, 10% acceleration (rooting priority); growth period 50/50; breakthrough period 70% acceleration, 30% Quest (burst period); contribution period again 50/50 (sedimentation to prevent hollowing). This dynamic pacing (Entry 667 balancing art) is not his personal whim, but the anchor network's suggestion based on his mind-nature state, civilizational stage, Myriad-Rank needs — he autonomously chooses to adopt. This is precisely the model of "individual autonomy + network suggestion."

## V. Lin Hao's Endpoint Is Not "Becoming Immortal," But "Escorting"

Predatory-faction protagonists' endpoint is "becoming immortal" — becoming the strongest, looking down on all beings. Lin Hao's endpoint, after passing the plane-transcendent four-dimensional assessment, enters an escort post: continue deducing in the time domain, continue sedimenting in the Quest domain, continue contributing to Myriad-Rank uplift. He is not an endpoint, but "the dual-track time domain's runner-through + Myriad-Rank escort." This positioning lasts longer than "Immortal Emperor."

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Lin Hao's dual-track spiral makes acceleration and sediment complement each other, avoiding single-track waste
- **Anti-predation**: Lin Hao rejects shortcut temptation, walks the full co-building route
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo is the system bestowing grace on one protagonist; Lin Hao is the dual-track time domain system's runner-through — the road is institution, not the protagonist
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Lin Hao's trajectory is the road all Myriad-Rank beings may walk; he is only the first sample
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Lin Hao's growth path is completely public, replicable, may be promoted toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Lin Hao is not a chosen protagonist, but the dual-track time domain's first complete sample. His growth trajectory proves: an ordinary being with no system, no bloodline, no grace, as long as he takes the right dual track, guards his mind-nature, contributes to Myriad Ranks, may walk all the way to plane-transcendent strong one. This road is not exclusive to Lin Hao — it is open to 99% of beings. Lin Hao's greatness lies not in being unique, but in proving this road is walkable by everyone.
