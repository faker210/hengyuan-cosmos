# Entry 680 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Time Domain and the 300-Set Origin Cultivation System

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 10, 11, 08 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 14)

## Introduction: the 300-set system — every set needs the time domain as its carrier

The 300-set Origin Cultivation System defined in Document 10 falls into six categories of fifty sets each, covering different cultivation paths for Myriad Ranks. But no cultivation system is a "static manual" — it needs time to deduce, to sediment, to iterate. The Origin Acceleration Domain and Quest Sediment Domain are precisely the common "time-carrying layer" for these 300 sets. Six top-level axioms here shape the relationship between system and time domain: anti-predation axiom requires all 300 sets be non-predatory; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the 300 sets cover 99% of beings; collective ascension axiom requires outputs backflow; causal self-discipline axiom requires the system not disturb causality; law-first axiom requires the 300 sets' law definitions stable and self-consistent. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry sorts out the synergy between the time domain and the 300-set system.

## I. Distribution of the 300-Set System in the Time Domain

The six system categories, by their cultivation nature, form different fits with the dual-track time domain:

| System Category | Time-Domain Preference | Reason |
|-----------------|------------------------|--------|
| Deduction-Iteration (Sets 1–50) | Tilted to Origin Acceleration Domain | Core is computing deduction; acceleration domain directly amplifies |
| Life-Cycle (Sets 51–100) | Tilted to Quest Sediment Domain | Core is life rhythm; requires slow experience |
| Spatial-Law Dispatch (Sets 101–150) | Dual-track balance | Deduction + practice equally weighted |
| Mind-Nature Insight (Sets 151–200) | Tilted to Quest Sediment Domain | Requires deep experience |
| Law-Anchoring (Sets 201–250) | Tilted to Origin Acceleration Domain | Requires high-speed verification |
| Myriad-Building (Sets 251–300) | Dual-track equally weighted | Both deduction and social practice |

This distribution is not forced allocation, but the natural match between system nature and time-domain characteristics. Cultivators may still combine across categories (Entry 681 Origin-Three-Basic synergy).

## II. How the Time Domain Amplifies Every Set

### 2.1 The Acceleration Domain's Amplification of Deduction-Type Systems

Deduction-iteration systems (Set 1, one of the Origin Three-Basic) obtain a hundred-fold deduction amplification in the acceleration domain (Entry 503): laws that originally required a hundred years of deduction complete in one year inside the acceleration domain. This amplification is not "cheating," but lawful use of computing power.

### 2.2 The Quest Domain's Nourishment of Life-Type Systems

Life-cycle systems (Set 2, one of the Origin Three-Basic) obtain deep experience in the Quest domain: cultivators truly feel life's birth, aging, sickness, death, and cycles in slow trials, rather than "knowing" these concepts in the acceleration domain. The Quest domain turns life-type systems from knowledge into embodied knowing.

### 2.3 The Dual Track's Synergy for Spatial-Type Systems

Spatial-law dispatch systems (Set 3, one of the Origin Three-Basic) require deduction (designing spatial laws) + practice (dispatching on real planes); dual-track synergy lets the two calibrate each other.

## III. Time-Domain Pacing for System Combinations

A cultivator need not follow only one set; he may combine across categories. The time-domain pacing after combination is suggested by the anchor network based on the combination's nature: deduction + mind-nature combination tilts to Quest; deduction + law combination tilts to acceleration. Pacing suggestions are not mandatory; the cultivator decides autonomously.

## IV. Contrast with "Single-System Monopoly"

The 300 sets are distributed by nature to the Origin Acceleration Domain and Quest Sediment Domain; every set is carried, iterated, and optimized in the Alaya Network. This diversity itself is anti-predation — no single set may monopolize the cultivation path. The fit table between the 300 sets and the dual-track time domain is Myriad-Rank Republicanism's concrete landing on cultivation paths: whether you are a carbon-based clan or an energy clan, whether you prefer fast or slow, there is always a set and a time track prepared for you. More roads and wider roads is true "everyone has a road," and the true landing of Myriad-Rank Republicanism and time civilization.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the 300 sets matched to the time domain by nature, avoiding misallocation waste of "forcing mind-nature systems into the acceleration domain"
- **Anti-predation**: the 300 sets are fully public, fully free, fully non-predatory; root-and-branch stamps out "privately hoarding manuals"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system only the protagonist may learn the strongest manual; others cannot; Heng Yuan Zhou's 300 sets are open to all Myriad Ranks
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the 300 sets cover 99% of Myriad-Rank beings' different cultivation needs
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the 300 sets' fit table with the time domain is fully public; newly merged cosmos automatically applies; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The 300-set Origin Cultivation System is "the road"; the dual-track time domain is "time" — no matter how good the road, without time to walk it you will not arrive; no matter how much time, walking the wrong road you will not arrive. The synergy between the time domain and the 300 sets lets every set carry the cultivator forward in the most suitable time flow. This is Myriad-Rank Republicanism's concrete landing on cultivation paths.
