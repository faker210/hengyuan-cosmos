# Entry 682 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Time Domain and Multiplication-Fruit Technologization

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 16, 503, 11 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 16)

## Introduction: the Multiplication Fruit is not heaven-sent buff, but a technical product of the time domain and law network

In predatory-faction systems, "opening buff" and "hundred-fold amplification" are products of system grace — the protagonist gets them; others do not. Heng Yuan Zhou's Multiplication-Fruit Technologization turns this "grace" into "technology": it is no longer a few protagonists' exclusive, but a standard deduction tool all cultivators may use. The Origin Acceleration Domain is the Multiplication Fruit's main runtime — only in high flow may the hundred-fold deduction amplification exert its maximum effect. Six top-level axioms here explain why the Multiplication Fruit is possible: anti-predation axiom requires the Multiplication Fruit not plunder others; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires it open to 99% of beings; collective ascension axiom requires multiplication outputs backflow; causal self-discipline axiom requires multiplication not disturb causality; law-first axiom requires the multiplication principle stable and reproducible. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry sorts out the relationship between the time domain and Multiplication-Fruit Technologization.

## I. The Multiplication Fruit's Technical Essence

Per Document 16, the core of Multiplication-Fruit Technologization is "raising the computing efficiency of deduction by a hundred times." This is not mysticism, but the superposition of three technologies:

1. **The Origin Acceleration Domain's time flow**: one external year = a hundred years inside the domain;
2. **The Alaya Network's parallel computing**: Myriad-Rank nodes collaboratively deduce;
3. **Deduction-Iteration system's algorithm optimization**: Set 1 itself's hundred-fold iteration algorithm.

The three multiply together to produce "hundred-fold deduction amplification." No single condition suffices — this is precisely why the Multiplication Fruit must be technologized.

## II. The Multiplication Fruit's Use Rules in the Time Domain

### 2.1 Must Be Used Inside the Origin Acceleration Domain

The Multiplication Fruit consumes enormous computing power; only the acceleration domain's high-flow environment can carry it. Using the Multiplication Fruit at ordinary flow causes computing overload and causal disorder. Rule: the Multiplication Fruit is lawfully used only inside the acceleration domain.

### 2.2 Multiplication Outputs Must Backflow

Law increments produced by the Multiplication Fruit in the acceleration domain all backflow to the Alaya Network. This is the premise of using the Multiplication Fruit — you may not "secretly multiply" then monopolize results. Backflowed outputs are counted into the contribution coefficient per more-work-more-gain (Entry 670).

### 2.3 Multiplication-Strength Grading

| Multiplication Tier | Multiple | Applicable To | Approval |
|---------------------|----------|---------------|----------|
| L1 | 10x | All cultivators | Auto-pass |
| L2 | 50x | Advanced cultivators | Ethical review |
| L3 | 100x | Contributors meeting standards | Anchor Council filing |
| L4 | 1000x | Cross-plane major projects | Symbiotic court + Anchor Supervisory Committee co-sign |

The higher the multiple, the stricter the approval. This stamps out the predatory-faction scenario of "one person monopolizing a thousand-fold amplification."

## III. The Multiplication Fruit and the Quest Domain's Complementarity

The Multiplication Fruit is used only in the acceleration domain, but it cannot replace the Quest domain. A hundred-fold deduction lets you "know" a hundred times more laws, but cannot let you "embodied-know" a hundred years of life. After multiplying deduction in the acceleration domain, the cultivator must enter the Quest Sediment Domain for mind-nature calibration — otherwise he repeats "hollow inflation" (Entry 675 shortcut inner demon). The Multiplication Fruit is an accelerator, not a panacea.

## IV. Contrast with the Predatory Faction's "System-Gifted Hundred-Fold"

In predatory-faction systems, the hundred-fold amplification is the system's private grace to the protagonist; its use has no rules, no backflow, no supervision. Heng Yuan Zhou's Multiplication-Fruit Technologization turns "grace" into "tool": tiered multiples, output backflow, multi-layer supervision, open use. This is the fundamental flip from "protagonist privilege" to "public technology."

## V. The Multiplication Fruit's Safety-Redundancy Design

Multiplication-Fruit Technologization has multiple built-in safety redundancies: first, multiplication-strength grading (L1–L4) makes low multiples friendly to novices and high multiples open to seniors, preventing novices from using a thousand-fold amplification on day one and suffering mental collapse; second, the "stability verification" of multiplication outputs — law increments from hundred-fold deduction must land on the material plane without side effects before formally counting as contribution, preventing pseudo-multiplication where "deduction is beautiful but collapses on landing"; third, cooldown on multiplication use frequency — continuous use of high multiples forces entry into the Quest domain for sedimentation, preventing multiplication addiction. Triple redundancy ensures the Multiplication Fruit is a tool, not a drug.

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the Multiplication Fruit raises computing efficiency a hundred-fold, avoiding waste from repeated deduction
- **Anti-predation**: multiplication outputs must backflow; multiples must be approved; stamping out private monopolization
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system gives the protagonist a hundred-fold amplification alone; Myriad Ranks get none; Heng Yuan Zhou's Multiplication Fruit opens to Myriad Ranks by tier
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: L1 multiplication is open to all cultivators; 99% of beings may use it
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: multiplication principles, grading rules, approval processes all public; newly merged cosmos automatically applies; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Multiplication-Fruit Technologization is the dual-track time domain's model for turning "protagonist privilege" into "public technology." It lets hundred-fold deduction no longer be system grace, but a standard tool shared by Myriad Ranks. But multiplication must be rule-bound — tiered multiples, output backflow, Quest calibration. Otherwise, the faster the multiplication, the closer to demonhood.
