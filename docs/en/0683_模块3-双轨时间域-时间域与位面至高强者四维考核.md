# Entry 683 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Time Domain and the Four-Dimensional Assessment of Plane-Transcendent Strong Ones

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 17, 29, 671 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 17)

## Introduction: the time domain is both the examination hall and the timer of the four-dimensional assessment

Plane-transcendent strong ones — at the Origin Realm and above — are the core posts of Heng Yuan Zhou's governance system. Document 17 defines the four-dimensional assessment standard: Power 40 points, Mind-Nature 25 points, Contribution 20 points, Responsibility 15 points. The assessment of these four dimensions all requires time to complete: Power is deduced and verified in the Origin Acceleration Domain; Mind-Nature is sedimented and tested in the Quest Sediment Domain; Contribution is accumulated and computed in the Alaya Network; Responsibility is demonstrated in cross-plane escort. The dual-track time domain is both the "examination hall" and the "timer" of the four-dimensional assessment. Six top-level axioms here set assessment direction: anti-predation axiom requires the Power score exclude predatory gains; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the Contribution score benefit 99% of beings; collective ascension axiom requires the Responsibility score point to Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom requires the assessment process not disturb causality; law-first axiom requires assessment standards stable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry sorts out the linkage between the time domain and the four-dimensional assessment.

## I. The Four Dimensions' Assessment Venues in the Time Domain

| Dimension | Weight | Assessment Venue | Assessment Method |
|-----------|--------|------------------|-------------------|
| Power | 40% | Origin Acceleration Domain | Reproducibility of law deduction |
| Mind-Nature | 25% | Quest Sediment Domain | Mental stability after tribulation trials |
| Contribution | 20% | Alaya Network | Myriad-Rank benefit scope and reuse count |
| Responsibility | 15% | Cross-Plane Escort | Handling records of responsibility events |

Power is assessed on the fast track; Mind-Nature on the slow track; Contribution and Responsibility on the network and in practice. The four dimensions belong to different time domains; only dual-track synergy may complete the full assessment.

## II. How the Time Domain Prevents "Single-Dimension High Scores"

### 2.1 Preventing Predatory-Faction Strong Ones with "High Power, Low Mind-Nature"

Predatory-faction strong ones usually have full Power and extremely low Mind-Nature. Heng Yuan Zhou uses the Quest Sediment Domain as the Mind-Nature examination hall: even if you score full Power in the acceleration domain, if your Mind-Nature is unstable in the Quest domain (such as unreleased shortcut inner demons, lack of empathy), your four-dimensional total is directly disqualified. This institutionally stamps out the "one-punch Superman" style of plane-transcendent strong one.

### 2.2 Preventing Naive Good People with "High Mind-Nature, Low Power"

Conversely, if you score full Mind-Nature in the Quest domain but lack Power in the acceleration domain (unable to cope with real predatory threats), your four-dimensional total is also disqualified. What Myriad Ranks need is not "the good old man," but a strong one who may protect Myriad Ranks.

### 2.3 Preventing "Internet Celebrity Strong Ones" with "High Contribution, Low Responsibility"

If your Alaya backflow volume is large, but you avoid responsibility and shirk duty in cross-plane escort, your Responsibility score is directly zeroed. The Contribution score may not substitute for the Responsibility score.

## III. The Assessment's Time Cycle

The four-dimensional assessment is not a single exam for life, but dynamic assessment: Power is verified quarterly in the acceleration domain; Mind-Nature is sedimented and tested annually in the Quest domain; Contribution is accumulated in real time by the Alaya Network; Responsibility is assessed upon responsibility events. Dynamic assessment ensures plane-transcendent strong ones always maintain state in the time domain; they cannot "pass once and lie flat." This forms a fundamental contrast with the predatory faction's "after becoming a sage, enjoy eternal honor" — becoming a sage is not the endpoint, but the starting point of perpetual construction.

## IV. The Time Domain's "Slow-Variable" Protection for Assessment

Entry 671 mentioned civilizational weight's "slow-variable" constraint — single-year increment may not exceed a certain proportion of the historical total. This constraint also applies to the four-dimensional assessment: even if your Power skyrockets in the acceleration domain in one year, your Mind-Nature score must pass at least one Quest cycle before it may synchronously rise. This guarantees in time that "a strong one's Power and Mind-Nature grow together," preventing Power from running wild while Mind-Nature lags behind. The four-dimensional assessment is not "exam and done," but "dynamic and never graduated" — plane-transcendent strong ones always receive assessment in the time domain and may never lie flat.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the four-dimensional assessment ensures plane-transcendent strong ones do not "skew to one subject," avoiding wasting resources on a single dimension
- **Anti-predation**: the Power score explicitly excludes predatory gains; the Mind-Nature score specifically guards against predatory thinking
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's "supreme strong ones" rely on system-gifted Power; there is no Mind-Nature assessment, no Responsibility assessment; Heng Yuan Zhou's four dimensions are all required
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the four-dimensional assessment standard is consistent across Myriad-Rank clans, measured by the same ruler regardless of race
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the four-dimensional score sheet, time cycle, slow-variable constraint all public; newly merged cosmos automatically applies; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The four-dimensional assessment of plane-transcendent strong ones is the dual-track time domain's complete exam designed for the post of "strong one." Power is tested in the acceleration domain; Mind-Nature in the Quest domain; Contribution in the Alaya Network; Responsibility in cross-plane escort. All four dimensions are required; dynamic assessment never graduates. This is true "supremacy" — not the greatest Power, but full scores in all four dimensions.
