# Entry 684 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Time Domain and the 20% Activation Strategy

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 23, 08, 22 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 18)

## Introduction: with limited time-domain resources, first activate the 20% most able to produce

The 20% Activation Strategy is the core principle of Heng Yuan Zhou's resource allocation: at the early stage of limited resources, concentrate the finest resources on the 20% of beings most able to produce, letting them first run through paths and produce models, then the models drive the remaining 80%. This is not "discriminating against the 80%," but "using the 20%'s breakthrough to win the 80%'s liberation." The Origin Acceleration Domain, as the scarcest time resource, is precisely the main allocation object of the 20% Activation Strategy. Six top-level axioms here calibrate strategy direction: anti-predation axiom requires activation not exclude the unactivated; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the 80%'s basic rights remain intact; collective ascension axiom lets the 20%'s outputs drive the whole; causal self-discipline axiom lets the activation process not create causal fixation; law-first axiom requires the 20% ratio and mechanism stable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry sorts out the relationship between the time domain and the 20% Activation Strategy.

## I. The 20% Activation Strategy's Landing in the Time Domain

### 1.1 Who Are the 20%

The activated 20% are not selected by "talent," but by three criteria: co-building willingness + basic qualification + mind-nature stability. Those with high talent but unstable mind-nature are not chosen — they are potential predators (Entry 675). Cultivators with strong co-building willingness, qualified basic ability, and stable mind-nature are prioritized for Origin Acceleration Domain quotas.

### 1.2 Obligations After Activation

The 20% activated are not "enjoying privilege," but bearing dual obligations:

1. **Production obligation**: must produce reusable law increments in the acceleration domain, backflowed to the Alaya Network;
2. **Driving obligation**: the produced experience must be organized into textbooks for the 80% unactivated to learn.

These two obligations are written into the activation agreement; violators lose activation qualification.

### 1.3 The 80%'s Guarantee

The 80% unactivated are not abandoned: they still enjoy basic time rights (Entry 668), basic cultivation paths, open use of the Quest Sediment Domain. The 20% Activation Strategy is only "premium acceleration resources first go to the most able producers," not "strangle the unactivated."

## II. The 20% Strategy's Time-Domain Rhythm

| Stage | Time | What the 20% Do | What the 80% Do |
|-------|------|------------------|------------------|
| Launch | 0–10 years | Run through paths in the acceleration domain | Root in the Quest domain |
| Model | 10–30 years | Produce model laws and textbooks | Learn model textbooks |
| Diffusion | 30–100 years | Backflow mature paths network-wide | Progressively enter the acceleration domain |
| Universal Benefit | After 100 years | The 20% no longer special; everyone may enjoy acceleration | 99% of beings enter the dual track |

The key of rhythm design: the 20% "run through first," not "monopolize forever." After the universal-benefit stage arrives, the 20% Activation Strategy completes its historical mission and transitions to Entry 670's more-work-more-gain allocation.

## III. The 20% Strategy and the Fundamental Difference from Tang-Luo's "Single Protagonist"

Tang-Luo's "single protagonist" looks like the 20% activation, but its essence is completely different:

- Tang-Luo: the activated permanently monopolize, do not drive the 80%, and the 80% are backdrop;
- Heng Yuan Zhou: the activated run through first, must drive the 80%, and ultimately transition to universal benefit.

The difference lies in the "driving obligation" and the "universal-benefit endpoint." Tang-Luo has no endpoint; Heng Yuan Zhou has one. The entire legitimacy of the 20% Activation Strategy rests on these four words "universal-benefit endpoint" — if the endpoint is not universal benefit, the 20% degrades into a new elite privilege.

## IV. The 20% Strategy and the Law-First Axiom

The 20% Activation Strategy must be constrained by the law-first axiom: the 20%'s selection criteria, driving obligations, and universal-benefit timeline must be public and stable, and may not be arbitrarily expanded or shrunk for plot needs. Otherwise "20%" degrades into an excuse for "a few strong ones' privilege."

## V. The 20% Strategy's Exit and Evolution

The 20% Activation Strategy is not a permanent system. When the Myriad-Rank co-building civilization evolves to sufficient maturity — acceleration-domain computing power abundant, Alaya Network coverage complete, 99% of Myriad-Rank beings may autonomously access time resources — the 20% Activation Strategy automatically exits, transitioning to Entry 670's more-work-more-gain allocation. The exit timeline is evaluated annually by the Anchor Council: if 99% of beings have entered the dual-track time domain and acceleration resources are no longer scarce, the exit procedure starts. This is fundamentally different from the predatory faction's "elites privileged forever" logic — the 20% is a launch means, not an endpoint privilege.

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: giving scarce acceleration resources first to the most able producers is more efficient than equal distribution
- **Anti-predation**: the activated must produce + drive, no monopolization allowed; the 80%'s basic rights remain intact
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo is the protagonist permanently monopolizing; Heng Yuan Zhou is the 20% running through first then universal benefit
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: 20% activation faces all Myriad-Rank clans; 80% guarantee covers 99% of beings
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: 20% selection criteria, driving obligations, universal-benefit timeline all public; newly merged cosmos automatically applies; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The 20% Activation Strategy is the dual-track time domain's realistic choice in the limited-resource stage. It is not inequality, but "using the 20%'s breakthrough to win the 80%'s liberation." The key lies in: the 20% must bear production and driving obligations; the 80% must retain basic rights; ultimately it must move toward universal benefit. The time domain's scarcity determines this is a necessary road in the launch stage; and the law-first axiom ensures this road does not become permanent privilege.
