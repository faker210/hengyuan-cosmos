# Entry 685 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Time Domain and the Joint-Contracting Mechanism

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 22, 06, 25 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 19)

## Introduction: contract out the time domain's computing power to cultivators; settle output by more-work-more-gain

Joint contracting is Heng Yuan Zhou's foundational resource-allocation system (Document 22): a cultivator contracts a stretch of resources, uses it autonomously, settles by output. The dual-track time domain extends this institution to the time dimension — the Origin Acceleration Domain's computing power and the Quest Sediment Domain's trial space may both be "contracted out" to cultivators. Six top-level axioms here form contracting rules: anti-predation axiom forbids contractors using resources for predation; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires contracting rights open to 99% of beings; collective ascension axiom requires contracting outputs backflow; causal self-discipline axiom requires contracting not disturb causality; law-first axiom requires contracting contracts stable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry sorts out the linkage between the time domain and the joint-contracting mechanism.

## I. Two Forms of Time-Domain Contracting

### 1.1 Acceleration-Domain Computing-Power Contracting

A cultivator contracts a stretch of Origin Acceleration Domain computing power from the Symbiotic Executive Committee — for example, "100 hours of acceleration-domain deduction time." During the contract period, the cultivator autonomously decides deduction direction, usage, and output form. At contract end, settlement is by output quality:

- Law increments produced backflow to the Alaya Network, counted into the contribution coefficient per more-work-more-gain;
- If output quality is high, the next round's contracting quota auto-increases;
- If output quality is low (idling, pseudo-output), the next round's contracting quota shrinks.

### 1.2 Quest-Domain Trial Contracting

A cultivator contracts a stretch of Quest Sediment Domain trial space — for example, "a 50-year Quest-domain trial cycle." During the contract period, the cultivator autonomously designs tribulations, chooses companions, plans direction. At contract end, settlement is by sediment quality:

- Epic experiences sedimented backflow to the Alaya Network;
- Mind-nature growth passes ethical review;
- If "fake trials" appear during the trial (evading tribulations, self-deception), settlement is disqualified.

## II. Key Clauses of the Contracting Contract

| Clause | Content |
|--------|---------|
| Contracted Object | Specify acceleration-domain hours or Quest-domain years |
| Usage Right | Decide direction autonomously, but not for predation |
| Output Obligation | Must backflow to the Alaya Network |
| Settlement Method | Graded by quality, affecting next-round quota |
| Exit Clause | Cultivator may exit mid-contract, settled by used portion |
| Supervision Clause | Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision records throughout |

Contract clauses are public and transparent; cultivators are fully informed before signing. This is fundamentally different from the predatory faction's resource usage of "system allocation, accept or accept."

## III. The Contracting Mechanism's Anti-Predation Design

The joint-contracting mechanism's most critical anti-predation design is "output must backflow": a cultivator who contracts acceleration-domain computing power could theoretically secretly deduce a private manual then monopolize it. But the contracting contract explicitly requires output backflow — no backflow counts as breach, and future contracting qualification is canceled. This clause blocks the possibility of "private monopolization," ensuring contracting is "public tool for public use."

## IV. The Contracting Mechanism and the Lin Hao Archetype

Lin Hao (Entry 679)'s growth trajectory is precisely the typical application of joint contracting: at different stages he contracts different scales of acceleration-domain computing power, produces law increments, settles, then expands the next round's contract. His growth is not "system allocating resources," but the virtuous cycle of "contract–produce–settle–re-contract." This cycle maximizes individual initiative while ensuring output serves Myriad Ranks.

## V. The Contracting Mechanism's Dynamic Adjustment

The joint-contracting contract is not signed once for life. If a cultivator's output during the contract period far exceeds expectations, he may apply to expand the next round's contracting scale; if output is insufficient, the anchor network proactively intervenes for diagnosis — is it a direction problem, a mind-nature problem, or a resource misallocation? Diagnosis results are fed back to the cultivator, rather than simple punishment. This cycle of "contract + diagnose + adjust" lets the joint-contracting mechanism both guarantee individual initiative and prevent the individual from idling in the wrong direction. This forms a fundamental contrast with the predatory faction's resource model of "strong ones allocate, weak ones obey" — the contractor is not a distributed chess piece, but an autonomously operating co-builder.

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: contracting lets cultivators decide usage autonomously, fitting individual needs better than external allocation
- **Anti-predation**: output must backflow; stamping out private monopolization; supervision leaves traces throughout
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo resources are system-allocated; the protagonist has no contracting concept; Heng Yuan Zhou's cultivators contract time computing power with autonomy and control
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: contracting rights are open to 99% of Myriad Ranks; every clan may participate
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: contracting contracts, settlement rules, supervision clauses all public; newly merged cosmos automatically applies; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The joint-contracting mechanism turns time-domain resources from "allocation" into "contracting," turning cultivators from "allocatees" into "autonomous users." The virtuous cycle of contract–produce–settle–re-contract both releases individual initiative and guarantees Myriad-Rank interests through output backflow. This is the more-work-more-gain principle's concrete realization in the time dimension.
