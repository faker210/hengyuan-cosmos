# Entry 686 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Time Clauses of the Symbiotic Constitution

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 40, 03, 49 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 20)

## Introduction: time rights are not policy, but constitution

Policy may change with rulers; the constitution does not. Heng Yuan Zhou writes the dual-track time domain's core rights into the Symbiotic Constitution, making them inalienable fundamental law. Six top-level axioms here form the constitution's spirit: anti-predation axiom written as anti-time-predation clauses; republican axiom of Myriad Races written as time-rights equality clauses; collective ascension axiom written as time-output-backflow clauses; causal self-discipline axiom written as time-operation causality clauses; law-first axiom written as time-law stability clauses. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry sorts out the Symbiotic Constitution's core clauses on the dual-track time domain.

## I. The Six Cores of the Time Clauses

### 1.1 Time-Rights Equality Clause

"All beings, regardless of race, realm, or strength, enjoy equal basic time rights." This is the fundamental negation of the predatory faction's "strong ones have time, weak ones have no time." Basic time rights include: qualification to enter the Origin Acceleration Domain, qualification to enter the Quest Sediment Domain, and freedom to exit any time domain.

### 1.2 Anti-Time-Predation Clause

"It is forbidden to plunder others' time in any form, including but not limited to: forced acceleration, forced detention, depriving time quotas, using time as an exploitation tool." Time predation is a serious felony recognized by Heng Yuan Zhou, with sentencing second only to direct life deprivation.

### 1.3 Time-Output Backflow Clause

"All cultivation outputs in the Origin Acceleration Domain and Quest Sediment Domain, in principle, backflow to the Alaya Network for Myriad-Rank Republican use." Private monopolization of outputs is constitutionally forbidden.

### 1.4 Time-Operation Causality Clause

"Any time-domain flow adjustment must pass causal-disturbance assessment, and may not create historical reversal, cycle harvesting, or class fixation." This is the constitutionalization of the causal self-discipline axiom.

### 1.5 Time-Law Stability Clause

"The dual-track time domain's law definitions, flow ratios, and authority tiers are locked by the constitution and may not be changed except through constitutional amendment procedures." This is the constitutionalization of the law-first axiom — plot may not kidnap laws; legislators may not arbitrarily change them.

### 1.6 Myriad-Rank Consultation Clause

"Myriad-Rank time-quota allocation is round-table negotiated by the Anchor Council; basic quotas may not be voted down for reduction." This is the constitutionalization of the republican axiom of Myriad Races.

## II. The Time Clauses' Constitutional Rank

The time clauses occupy an extremely high rank in the Symbiotic Constitution — they stand alongside "right to life," "right to equality," and "anti-predation right," belonging to Chapter One "Fundamental Principles" of the constitution, not ordinary law. This means: any ordinary law, any administrative order, any judicial precedent may not conflict with the time clauses; conflicting provisions are void.

## III. The Time Clauses and the Five Lines of Defense

Entry 668 mentioned the five lines of defense for individual time autonomy (legislative, executive, judicial, audit, network); the time clauses are precisely the core of the legislative line. They turn "time belongs to the individual" from a policy promise into a constitutional fact. Among the five lines, the legislative line is the first and most fundamental.

## IV. The Time Clauses' Amendment Procedure

The time clauses are not eternally unchanging — the cosmos evolves, and time-domain rules may need adjustment. But the amendment procedure is extremely strict: it must pass three steps — two-thirds majority of the Anchor Council, constitutional review by the symbiotic court, and ratification by Myriad-Rank referendum. This stamps out the possibility of "legislators arbitrarily changing time rules." The law-first axiom requires rules stable, not rigid — the strictness of the amendment procedure itself is the guarantee of stability. Any attempt to plunder time by amending the time clauses will be intercepted in these three steps. The six sentences of the time clauses — time-rights equality, anti-time-predation, output backflow, causality assessment, law stability, Myriad-Rank consultation — together constitute the unshakable constitutional cornerstone of the dual-track time domain and the highest legal guarantee of Myriad Ranks' time rights.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: constitutionally stable time rules give cultivators long-term expectations, avoiding short-term speculation
- **Anti-predation**: anti-time-predation clauses + constitutional rank make time predation an irreversible felony
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system has no constitution; time rules are arbitrarily set by the system; Heng Yuan Zhou writes time rules into the constitution
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: time-rights equality clauses cover 99% of Myriad Ranks
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the full text of time clauses is public; newly merged cosmos automatically applies; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Symbiotic Constitution's time clauses are the fundamental law of the dual-track time domain. They write six sentences — "time belongs to the individual," "time may not be plundered," "outputs must backflow," "causality must be assessed," "laws must be stable," "Myriad Ranks must consult" — into Chapter One of the constitution. This is not rhetoric; this is law. With these six sentences, the dual-track time domain is elevated from a "technical arrangement" to a "civilizational institution."
