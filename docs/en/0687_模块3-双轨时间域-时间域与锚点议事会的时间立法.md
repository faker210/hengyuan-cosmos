# Entry 687 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Time Legislation by the Anchor Council

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 49, 40, 47 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 21)

## Introduction: the constitution is the outline; the Council is the detail — time legislation unfolds the outline into detail

The Symbiotic Constitution's time clauses (Entry 686) are fundamental law, but constitutional provisions cannot be directly operated. What unfolds constitutional clauses into executable rules is the Anchor Council's time legislation. As Heng Yuan Zhou's legislative body (Document 49 governance framework), the Council is responsible for turning constitutional principles such as "time-rights equality," "anti-time-predation," and "output backflow" into concrete time-quota allocation law, time-operation approval law, and time-dispute procedure law. Six top-level axioms here set legislative direction: anti-predation axiom requires legislation against time monopoly; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires legislation embody Myriad-Rank will; collective ascension axiom requires legislation serve whole uplift; causal self-discipline axiom requires legislation not disturb causality; law-first axiom requires legislation itself stable and predictable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry sorts out the Anchor Council's time-legislation work.

## I. Four Products of Time Legislation

### 1.1 Time-Quota Allocation Law

Unfolds the constitution's "basic time-rights equality" into a concrete quota table: how much basic Origin Acceleration Domain time each being gets monthly; how excess quotas are computed; how the annual civilizational master switch is set. The allocation law is revised annually; the revision process is publicly heard.

### 1.2 Time-Operation Approval Law

Unfolds the constitution's "time-operation causality assessment" into approval procedures: L1–L5 law-anchoring authority (Entry 672)'s approval conditions, approval bodies, approval time limits, appeal channels. The approval law ensures time operations are both efficient and controllable.

### 1.3 Time-Dispute Procedure Law

Unfolds the constitution's "time-rights equality" into dispute-resolution procedures: filing, hearing, and adjudication processes for time-quota disputes, time-operation torts, and time-discrimination cases. The procedure law guarantees every being has a channel for remedy when time rights are harmed.

### 1.4 Time-Discrimination Prohibition Law

Unfolds the constitution's "anti-time-predation" into anti-discrimination details: it is forbidden to reduce basic time rights on grounds of race, gender, realm, or contribution; it is forbidden to force a certain clan to take only the slow track; it is forbidden to implicitly discriminate in time quotas.

## II. The Democratic Procedure of Legislation

| Step | Content |
|------|---------|
| Proposal | Any Myriad-Rank representative or any clan council may propose a time bill |
| Hearing | The bill is publicly heard; representatives from Myriad-Rank clans state their views |
| Vote | The Anchor Council votes by civilizational weight (Entry 671), but basic-quota clauses may not be voted down for reduction |
| Constitutional Review | The symbiotic court reviews whether the bill violates the Symbiotic Constitution's time clauses |
| Promulgation | Constitutional bills are promulgated network-wide and take effect |

The core of the democratic procedure is "hearing" — time legislation affects Myriad Ranks, so Myriad Ranks must have a voice. This is fundamentally different from the predatory faction's "strong ones legislate, weak ones obey."

## III. Legislation's Prevention of "Time Monopoly"

The Anchor Council's most important anti-monopoly clauses in time legislation are:

- **Annual dispersion of acceleration resources**: any single cultivator's annual acceleration quota may not exceed a certain proportion of the total;
- **Quest-space openness**: the Quest Sediment Domain may not be long-term monopolized by any clan;
- **Alaya-Network neutrality**: Council members may not use legislative power to interfere with network records.

These clauses are written directly into the time-domain sub-chapter of the Anti-Monopoly Law (Document 47), stamping out "legislators becoming monopolizers." Once legislators themselves monopolize time resources, the time domain degrades from a Myriad-Rank paradise into a strong ones' private garden — anti-monopoly clauses lock this degradation out institutionally.

## IV. Legislation and the Law-First Axiom

The Anchor Council's time legislation is not "legislate whatever one pleases." Legislation must obey the law-first axiom: any time bill may not conflict with the dual-track time domain's law definitions, may not break the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle, may not violate causal self-discipline. If legislation conflicts with laws, the symbiotic court rules it void. This institutionally prevents "legislators kidnapping laws." The more transparent and law-bound legislation is, the more stable the dual-track time domain's rules.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: legislation standardizes time quotas and operation approvals, reducing repeated trial-and-error
- **Anti-predation**: time-discrimination prohibition law + anti-time-monopoly clauses block predatory paths
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system has no legislative body; time rules are arbitrarily set by the system; Heng Yuan Zhou's Council legislates democratically
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: legislative hearings cover all Myriad-Rank clans; basic quotas may not be reduced
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: legislative procedures, four bill types, anti-monopoly clauses all public; newly merged cosmos automatically applies; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Anchor Council's time legislation unfolds the Symbiotic Constitution's six sentences into ten thousand executable rules. Legislation is not for restricting freedom, but for letting every being have rules to follow, wrongs to appeal, and rights to protect in the time domain. The more transparent the legislation, the more stable the dual-track time domain.
