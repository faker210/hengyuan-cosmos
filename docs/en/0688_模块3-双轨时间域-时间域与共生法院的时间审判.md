# Entry 688 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Time Adjudication by the Symbiotic Court

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 48, 49, 48 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 22)

## Introduction: time disputes need a court; time predation needs adjudication

In the time domain's operation, disputes inevitably arise: unfair quota allocation, time-operation torts, cross-clan time discrimination, causal-disturbance controversies. These disputes may not be solved by fists — predatory-faction systems solved them by fists, and the result was billions of beings crushed. Heng Yuan Zhou entrusts the adjudication of time disputes to the symbiotic court, making time adjudication an independent judicial branch. Six top-level axioms here form adjudication principles: anti-predation axiom requires adjudication pursue time predation; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires adjudication equal to Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires adjudication serve co-building; causal self-discipline axiom requires adjudication itself not disturb causality; law-first axiom requires adjudication rules stable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry sorts out the symbiotic court's time-adjudication work.

## I. Three Categories of Time Adjudication Cases

### 1.1 Time-Quota Disputes

A cultivator or clan believes its time quota was improperly reduced or allocated unfairly, and sues the symbiotic court. The court, based on the Anchor Council's time-quota allocation law (Entry 687) and the Alaya Network's actual records, adjudicates whether the quota complies.

### 1.2 Time-Operation Torts

A cultivator's operation in the time domain harms others' rights — such as acceleration-domain deduction accidentally touching others' causality, Quest-domain trial forcing others into the drama — the victim files a tort suit. The court reviews whether the operation was approved, whether it passed causal-disturbance assessment, and whether it actually caused harm.

### 1.3 Time-Predation Trials

Criminal trials of time-predation behavior — forced acceleration, quota deprivation, using time as an exploitation tool. These are the most serious time cases, with sentencing second only to direct life deprivation. Trial focus is predation facts, causal damage, and possibility of reform.

## II. The Special Characteristics of Time Adjudication

Time adjudication differs from ordinary civil and criminal trials in three special ways:

### 2.1 The Unfalsifiability of Causal Evidence

Every operation in the time domain is recorded throughout by the Alaya Network (Entry 673), and causal trajectories may not be tampered with. The evidentiary basis for court adjudication is the Alaya Network's records, not party statements. This stamps out perjury, retractions, and denial.

### 2.2 Cross-Plane Jurisdiction

Time-domain operations may cross planes and cosmoses. The symbiotic court's jurisdiction covers all planes connected to the anchor network, not limited by a single plane's borders. Cross-plane cases are heard by the court's special tribunal.

### 2.3 Adjudication and Time Flow

Adjudication itself also proceeds in time. To prevent defendants from using time flow to delay trial, the court stipulates: time cases' hearing cycle is counted by the material plane's flow, and may not be extended because the defendant "kills time" in the acceleration domain. This stamps out the loophole of "running the law with time."

## III. The Link Between Adjudication and Conversion

For time predators, the court's adjudication is not mere punishment. If the defendant is an enlightened predator (Entry 677), the court's judgment links to the conversion mechanism: ordering him into the five-stage conversion process rather than simple imprisonment. The purpose of adjudication is not to annihilate the predator, but to repair damaged causality and rebuild broken relationships. This is fundamentally different from the predatory faction's judicial view of "annihilate heretics."

## IV. The Publicness of Adjudication

Time adjudication is fully public throughout: hearing records, evidence display, judgment reasons all go online for Myriad Ranks to inspect. Publicness is the root of preventing judicial corruption — with no backroom operations, there is no "strong ones walking free." This forms a fundamental contrast with the predatory faction's "court for the strong."

## V. Adjudication and the Law-First Axiom

Time-adjudication rules themselves are constrained by the law-first axiom: adjudication standards may not be arbitrarily changed; judgments may not be adjusted by party identity. If a "loophole in the law itself" is found during adjudication, the symbiotic court may not improvise, but must submit a legislative amendment recommendation to the Anchor Council. This stamps out the power expansion of "judicial legislation." The separation of four powers — adjudication, legislation, executive, audit — also holds in the time domain; no single power may dominate.

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: adjudication brings time disputes from "private settlement" to "public ruling," reducing internal friction from disputes
- **Anti-predation**: time-predation trials directly pursue time crimes, with clear sentencing
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system has no judiciary; the protagonist does as he pleases; Heng Yuan Zhou's symbiotic court adjudicates independently
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: adjudication is equal across Myriad-Rank clans, regardless of strength
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: adjudication rules, three case categories, public principle all public; newly merged cosmos automatically applies; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The symbiotic court's time adjudication is the judicial guarantee of the dual-track time domain. It turns time disputes, time torts, and time predation from "fist-solved" to "court-solved." Unfalsifiable causal evidence, cross-plane jurisdiction, adjudication decoupled from time flow — these designs make time adjudication truly Myriad Ranks' time court, not the strong ones' private hall.
