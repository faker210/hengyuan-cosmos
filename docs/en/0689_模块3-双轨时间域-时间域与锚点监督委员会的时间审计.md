# Entry 689 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Time Audit by the Anchor Supervisory Committee

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 49, 27, 673 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 23)

## Introduction: beyond legislation, executive, and judiciary, there is audit — whether time rules are obeyed must be checked by someone

The Anchor Supervisory Committee is an independent supervisory body in Heng Yuan Zhou's governance system (Document 49), standing alongside legislation (Anchor Council), executive (Symbiotic Executive Committee), and judiciary (Symbiotic Court), forming the separation of four powers. Whether the time domain operates compliantly, whether time quotas are abused, whether time legislation is enforced — the Anchor Supervisory Committee conducts independent audit of these. Six top-level axioms here form audit direction: anti-predation axiom requires audit discover time predation; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires audit accountable to Myriad Ranks; collective ascension axiom requires audit serve co-building; causal self-discipline axiom requires audit not disturb causality; law-first axiom requires audit standards stable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry sorts out the Anchor Supervisory Committee's time-audit work.

## I. Three Objects of Time Audit

### 1.1 Executive-Body Audit

Audits whether the Symbiotic Executive Committee's allocation of time quotas is compliant, whether time-operation approvals follow procedure, whether the 20% Activation Strategy (Entry 684)'s roster meets criteria. If executive bodies abusing allocation power is found, it is reported directly to the symbiotic court.

### 1.2 Cultivator Audit

Audits cultivators' time-domain usage by sampling ratio: whether Origin Acceleration Domain outputs truly backflow, whether Quest Sediment Domain trials are perfunctory, whether contracting contracts (Entry 685) are performed. Sampling audit does not target individuals, but launches case investigation once anomalies are found.

### 1.3 Network Audit

Audits whether the Alaya Network's records of the time domain are complete, whether they have been tampered with, whether multi-layer supervision (Entry 673) truly runs. Network audit is the supervision against "supervision failure."

## II. The Independent Status of Time Audit

The Anchor Supervisory Committee's independence is the premise of audit effectiveness:

- Not interfered with by the Anchor Council (the Council legislates and cannot supervise whether its own laws are obeyed);
- Not interfered with by the Symbiotic Executive Committee (the executive is the audited object);
- Not interfered with by the symbiotic court (the court adjudicates, does not audit);
- Directly accountable to Myriad-Rank public will; audit reports are network-wide public.

This independent status ensures audit is not "greased" by any power department.

## III. The Frequency and Depth of Time Audit

| Audit Type | Frequency | Depth |
|------------|-----------|-------|
| Routine Audit | Quarterly | 10% sampling |
| Special Audit | On demand | Targeted at specific clans/events |
| Comprehensive Audit | Annual | 100% coverage of high-authority operations |
| Myriad-Rank Hearing Audit | After major events | Full-process review |

Frequency and depth are designed both to ensure supervision density and to avoid excessive audit disturbing normal cultivation. The purpose of supervision is not to suspect every cultivator, but to make violations have nowhere to hide — innocent cultivators need not worry about audit, because the Alaya Network records facts as they are.

## IV. Handling Problems Found by Audit

Problems found by audit are handled in three categories:

- **Minor violations**: warning, rectification;
- **Serious violations**: transferred to the symbiotic court for trial (Entry 688);
- **Systemic defects**: submit legislative amendment recommendations to the Anchor Council (Entry 687).

Audit not only "finds problems" but also "pushes improvement" — when systemic defects are found, it pushes legislative refinement. This makes audit a driving force for institutional evolution.

## V. Audit and the Law-First Axiom

Audit standards themselves are constrained by the law-first axiom: audit rules may not be arbitrarily changed; audit conclusions may not be adjusted for plot needs. If audit finds "the rules themselves are problematic," it must go through legislative amendment procedures, rather than the audit body improvising. This stamps out the power expansion of "audit as legislation." Audit's independence, publicness, and stability combine, making time audit the last line of defense for Myriad Ranks' time rights — legislation makes rules, executive enforces, judiciary adjudicates, audit supervises; the four-power separation is indispensable.

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: after audit finds systemic defects, it pushes improvement, avoiding institutional waste
- **Anti-predation**: independent audit lets time predation have nowhere to hide
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system has no audit; system behavior is unsupervised; Heng Yuan Zhou has four-power separation + independent audit
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: audit reports are network-wide public; Myriad Ranks may supervise audit bodies
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: audit objects, frequency, handling methods all public; newly merged cosmos automatically applies; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Anchor Supervisory Committee's time audit is the fourth power of dual-track time-domain governance. Legislation makes rules, executive enforces, judiciary adjudicates, audit supervises — the four-power separation ensures every inch of time-domain rules has a guardian, every operation has a checker. Audit is independent of any power, directly accountable to Myriad-Rank public will. This is true "putting power in a cage."
