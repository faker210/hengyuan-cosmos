# Entry 690 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Time-Domain Adaptation to the Five Myriad-Rank Lineages

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 30, 527 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 24 · Overview)

## Introduction: the dual-track time domain is designed not for humankind, but for the five Myriad-Rank lineages

If the dual-track time domain served only humankind, it would merely be a "humankind accelerator"; it must serve the five Myriad-Rank lineages — carbon-based matter race, energy-condensation race, mechanical-construction race, consciousness-information race, mixed-variant race — to be true Myriad-Rank Republicanism. This entry, as an overview, sorts out the dual-track time domain's overall adaptation framework for the five lineages; details of each clan's life rhythm are in Entries 691–695. Six top-level axioms here form adaptation principles: anti-predation axiom requires adaptation not plunder any clan; republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the five lineages equal; collective ascension axiom requires adaptation serve the whole; causal self-discipline axiom requires adaptation not disturb each clan's causality; law-first axiom requires adaptation rules stable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Overview of the Five Lineages' Time Traits

| Lineage | Time Trait | Dual-Track Preference | Adaptation Focus |
|---------|------------|------------------------|------------------|
| Carbon-Based Matter Race | Finite lifespan, vivid life rhythm | Dual-track balance, Quest-leaning | Protect life rhythm |
| Energy-Condensation Race | No natural aging, oscillatory timing | Tilted to Origin Acceleration Domain | Solve "time-perception apathy" |
| Mechanical-Construction Race | Millisecond precision, efficiency above all | Tilted to Origin Acceleration Domain | Solve "slow = waste" obsession |
| Consciousness-Information Race | Subjective time, memory = time | Tilted to Quest Sediment Domain | Solve "subjective-time distortion" |
| Mixed-Variant Race | Multiple rhythms intermingled | Flexible dual track | Solve "rhythm conflict" |

This overview table is not forced allocation, but a basic reference for democratic consultation (Entry 669) — each clan adjusts at the round table according to its own reality.

## II. Three Principles of Adaptation

### 2.1 No-Forced-Assimilation Principle

The dual-track time domain does not force any clan to change its life rhythm. The mechanical clan need not force itself to be slow for "balance"; the consciousness clan need not force objective timing for "efficiency." Adaptation is "the dual track accommodating each clan," not "each clan accommodating the dual track."

### 2.2 Basic-Quota Floor Principle

Regardless of each clan's preference, the basic time quota may not be reduced (Entry 668). Even if the energy clan is uninterested in the acceleration domain, its basic quota is retained; even if the mechanical clan finds the Quest domain too slow, its basic Quest quota is retained. The floor ensures "uninterested" does not equal "deprived."

### 2.3 Cross-Clan Time-Translation Principle

When clans with different time perceptions collaborate, the Alaya Network provides "time translation": translating the mechanical clan's millisecond operations into rhythms the carbon-based clan may understand; translating the consciousness clan's subjective time into oscillations the energy clan may understand. Translation does not change each clan's flow; it only solves communication problems in collaboration.

## III. The Evolution Direction of Adaptation

The dual-track time domain's adaptation to the five lineages is not static. As the Myriad-Rank symbiotic civilization evolves, adaptation dynamically optimizes:

- After the carbon-based clan's lifespan extends, Quest-domain cycles may be adjusted;
- After the energy clan deepens interaction with the material layer, new time perceptions may develop;
- After the mechanical and consciousness clans fuse, mixed-clan time strategies become richer.

The evolution direction of adaptation is round-table negotiated by the Myriad-Rank Council (Entry 669), not unilaterally decided by any one clan.

## IV. Adaptation and Anti-Predation

The five lineages' adaptation is essentially the anti-predation axiom's expression in the time dimension: predatory-faction systems take humankind's (or one clan's) time sense as the universal standard, treating other clans' life rhythms as "inefficient" and plundering them. Heng Yuan Zhou acknowledges the equal value of each clan's time rhythm, letting the dual track accommodate each clan rather than each clan accommodating a single standard. This is Myriad-Rank Republicanism in the time dimension — no clan's time rhythm is "more advanced" than another; there are only "differences." Respecting differences is Myriad-Rank Republicanism's landing in time; acknowledging differences is the true ultimate goal of Myriad-Rank symbiosis and time civilization.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: adaptation matches each clan's time-domain quota to its life rhythm, reducing misallocation
- **Anti-predation**: rejects "single-time-standard hegemony," acknowledges each clan's time equality
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system takes the humankind protagonist's time sense as the sole standard; other clans have no time rights; Heng Yuan Zhou adapts to five lineages
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: adaptation covers 99% of beings across the five lineages
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: adaptation overview, three principles, evolution direction all public; newly merged cosmos automatically applies; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The dual-track time domain's adaptation to the five lineages is Myriad-Rank Republicanism's overview in the time dimension. It does not force assimilation, floors basic quotas, provides cross-clan translation — letting carbon-based, energy, mechanical, consciousness, and mixed clans all find their own rhythm in the dual track. Details of each clan are in the five following sub-entries.
