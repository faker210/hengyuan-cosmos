# Entry 691 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Time Domain and the Carbon-Based Clan's Life Rhythm

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 12, 07, 554 | Series: Five-Myriad-Rank-Lineage Adaptation (Entry 1)

## Introduction: the race with finite lifespan understands time's preciousness best

The carbon-based matter race — humankind, beasts, plant spirits, all life carried by carbon-based molecules — is the most lifespan-limited of the five lineages. Hundred-year lifespan, birth/aging/sickness/death, generational replacement — these "biological rhythms" are engraved in every carbon-based being's genes. The dual-track time domain's adaptation to the carbon-based clan is not core "help them live longer," but "respect their life rhythm": let them, within limited time, both enjoy the acceleration domain's efficiency and not lose the Quest domain's depth. Six top-level axioms here form adaptation direction: anti-predation axiom opposes predatory means of lifespan extension; republican axiom of Myriad Races confirms the carbon-based clan's equal evolution qualification; collective ascension axiom lets carbon-based life experience feed back to Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom lets lifespan extension not disturb natural causality; law-first axiom lets carbon-based time rules stable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry focuses on details of the carbon-based clan's life rhythm.

## I. Three Features of Carbon-Based Time Perception

### 1.1 Finiteness Drives "Urgency"

The hundred-year lifespan gives the carbon-based clan deep anxiety about "not enough time." This anxiety is double-edged: it drives the carbon-based clan to use time efficiently, but easily breeds shortcut inner demons (Entry 675) — "too late, must take a shortcut." The dual-track time domain's response is: basic time-right guarantee (Entry 668) + lawful acceleration-domain compensation, so the carbon-based clan need not "grab time" through predation.

### 1.2 Generational Replacement Drives "Experience Transmission"

The carbon-based clan renews each century; experience must continue through transmission. The dual-track time domain upgrades this transmission from "word of mouth" to "Alaya-Network backflow": one generation's deduction in the acceleration domain and sedimentation in the Quest domain all backflow to the network; the next generation stands directly on the previous one's shoulders. Generational replacement is no longer "experience reset," but "experience accumulation."

### 1.3 Life Rhythm Drives "Seasonal Sense"

The carbon-based clan's time perception syncs with seasons, day/night, growth and decay. This "seasonal sense" is absent from other lineages (such as the energy clan's oscillatory timing). The dual-track time domain preserves this perception for the carbon-based clan — day/night rhythm is maintained even in the acceleration domain; seasonal changes are maintained even in the Quest domain. It does not turn the carbon-based clan into "rhythmless computing bodies."

## II. Carbon-Based Dual-Track Pacing Recommendations

| Stage | Acceleration Domain | Quest Domain | Reason |
|-------|---------------------|--------------|--------|
| Initiation (0–20 years) | 20% | 80% | Root in life rhythm |
| Growth (20–50 years) | 50% | 50% | Dual-track parallel |
| Contribution (50–80 years) | 60% | 40% | Output maximized |
| Sedimentation (after 80) | 30% | 70% | Sediment to feed descendants |

Pacing dynamically adjusts by life stage, syncing with the carbon-based clan's natural rhythm. This is fundamentally different from the mechanical clan's "full-speed all along" and the energy clan's "full oscillation all along."

## III. The Ethical Boundary of Carbon-Based Lifespan Extension

The dual-track time domain does not proactively extend the carbon-based clan's lifespan — lifespan is part of the carbon-based life rhythm; forcibly extending it would break that rhythm. But through the Origin-Three-Basic's Life-Cycle system (Set 2), the carbon-based clan's healthy lifespan may naturally extend, and this extension does not change the essence of its life rhythm. The ethical boundary of lifespan extension: natural extension is allowed; predatory extension (devouring other clans' lives) is forbidden.

## IV. The Carbon-Based Clan Compared with Other Lineages

The carbon-based clan's "limited time" seems a disadvantage, but is actually an advantage — precisely because it is limited, every experience is especially precious; precisely because of replacement, generational experience renews. Predatory-faction systems regard "longevity" as the ultimate goal; Heng Yuan Zhou regards "depth within finitude" as value. The carbon-based clan's life rhythm itself is the best textbook of the Quest Sediment Domain. The dual-track time domain does not attempt to eliminate the carbon-based clan's finitude, but uses every second to the full within finitude — this is true respect for the carbon-based clan's life rhythm.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the carbon-based clan's finite lifespan ensures every experience is not wasted
- **Anti-predation**: predatory lifespan extension is forbidden; natural life rhythm respected
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system the protagonist is immortal; mortals' hundred years are like ants; Heng Yuan Zhou respects the carbon-based clan's life rhythm
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the carbon-based clan is only one of Myriad Ranks; its rhythm equals other lineages
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: pacing recommendations, lifespan boundaries, generational-transmission rules all public; newly merged cosmos automatically applies; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The carbon-based clan's life rhythm is the most "humanistic" branch of the dual-track time domain. Hundred-year finitude, generational replacement, seasonal sense — these are not "defects," but the carbon-based clan's unique time wisdom. The dual-track time domain does not attempt to transform the carbon-based clan into immortal computing bodies, but respects its rhythm, compensates its anxiety, transmits its experience. Limited, therefore precious; replaced, therefore renewed.
