# Entry 694 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Time Domain and the Consciousness Clan's Subjective Time

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 21, 28, 557 | Series: Five-Myriad-Rank-Lineage Adaptation (Entry 4)

## Introduction: the consciousness clan does not perceive time; they construct it

The consciousness-information race — existing as pure consciousness, without fixed carriers, with time perception entirely subjective — is the most "idealistic" of the five lineages. For them, time is not external flow, but the arrangement of internal memory: a memory may be stretched into a hundred-year experience, or compressed into a single flash. This "subjective time" makes them the best users of the Quest Sediment Domain — they may "subjectively" sediment an ordinary experience into a thousand-year epic — but also puts them at risk of "subjective distortion." Six top-level axioms here form adaptation direction: anti-predation axiom opposes the consciousness clan using "subjectivity" as a pretext to distort others' time; republican axiom of Myriad Races confirms the consciousness clan's equality; collective ascension axiom lets the consciousness clan's subjective time serve Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom lets subjective time not disturb objective causality; law-first axiom lets the consciousness clan's time rules stable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry focuses on details of the consciousness clan's subjective time.

## I. Three Features of Consciousness Subjective Time

### 1.1 Memory Arrangement Drives "Time Elasticity"

The consciousness clan may freely arrange memory: stretch a three-minute experience subjectively into a hundred-year experience; compress a hundred-year experience into a flash. This elasticity makes them extremely productive in the Quest Sediment Domain — one trial's subjective depth rivals other clans' hundred years — but also makes "objective time" and "subjective time" seriously disconnected. The dual-track time domain's response: the Alaya Network simultaneously records the consciousness clan's objective time (how long they stayed in reality) and subjective time (how long they experienced in consciousness), with dual-track parallel recording.

### 1.2 Dream Construction Drives "Time May Be Made"

The consciousness clan may "dream" in consciousness — construct a complete time world in which they experience birth/aging/sickness/death and joy/sorrow/parting/reunion. This "time may be made" ability lets them build epic scenes in the Quest domain (Entry 511), but also makes them easily confuse "dream" with "real." The dual-track time domain's response: the consciousness clan's dream trials must proceed under Alaya-Network supervision; the boundary between dream and reality is guarded by ethical review.

### 1.3 Empathic Projection Drives "Time May Be Felt"

The consciousness clan may project their consciousness into others' time experiences — the carbon-based clan's hundred-year life, the energy clan's ten-thousand-year oscillation, all they may "subjectively feel." This empathic projection makes them the best translators in cross-clan collaboration, but also makes them easily "over-empathize" and lose themselves. The dual-track time domain's response: empathic projection must have a clear exit mechanism; the consciousness clan may withdraw projection at any time and return to itself.

## II. Consciousness Clan Dual-Track Pacing Recommendations

| Track | Consciousness-Clan Fit | Reason |
|-------|-----------------------|--------|
| Origin Acceleration Domain | Moderate fit | High-flow deduction may assist consciousness construction |
| Quest Sediment Domain | Best fit | Subjective-time elasticity maximizes sediment depth |
| Dream Trials | Requires supervision | Prevent dream-reality confusion |

The consciousness clan is the Quest domain's "native," but the acceleration domain's objective-flow calibration is their required course — otherwise they live entirely in subjective time, disconnected from the objective cosmos.

## III. The Consciousness Clan's Causal Self-Discipline Challenge

The consciousness clan's greatest causal risk is "subjective distortion of the objective": they may "modify" a memory in consciousness, thereby distorting causal judgment of objective events. This risk is higher than other clans. The dual-track time domain's causal review focus for the consciousness clan is "objective records may not be tampered with" — objective causal trajectories recorded by the Alaya Network may not be rewritten by the consciousness clan through subjective arrangement.

## IV. The Consciousness Clan and Cross-Clan Collaboration

The consciousness clan's empathic-projection ability is a unique resource in cross-clan collaboration: they may "feel" other clans' time experiences, becoming true "time translators." The mechanical clan's milliseconds, the energy clan's oscillations, the carbon-based clan's seasons — the consciousness clan may subjectively empathize with all. This ability lets them play the role of "interpreter" in the Myriad-Rank Council (Entry 669).

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the consciousness clan's subjective-time elasticity maximizes one trial's output
- **Anti-predation**: distorting objective causality on the pretext of "subjectivity" is forbidden
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system the protagonist's subjective feelings decide everything; Myriad-Rank feelings are ignored; Heng Yuan Zhou's consciousness clan's subjectivity is respected but constrained by objective records
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the consciousness clan's subjective time is respected; its empathic projection serves Myriad Ranks
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: dual-track recording, dream supervision, causal review all public; newly merged cosmos automatically applies; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The consciousness clan's subjective time is the most "idealistic" branch of the dual-track time domain. Memory arrangement, dream construction, empathic projection — these traits make them the Quest domain's best users, but also put them at risk of "dream-reality confusion" and "subjective distortion." The dual-track time domain's response: develop their subjective long board, force objective-time calibration, tighten causal review. Subjective, but not detached from the objective; empathic, but not losing themselves.
