# Entry 697 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Time Domain and the Cosmos-Origin Entity's Time Dimension

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 02, 20 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 26)

## Introduction: the Cosmos-Origin Entity does not perceive time; it is part of time

The Cosmos-Origin Entity — at the Origin Realm and above, existing as one with the entire cosmos's law network — is the most "ontological" tier in Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad-Rank Republicanism. It no longer "perceives" time, because its existence itself is time's carrier: the cosmos's expansion, contraction, and law evolution are all the rhythms of its "body." Six top-level axioms here set its time dimension's direction: anti-predation axiom requires the Cosmos-Origin Entity not plunder other cosmoses; republican axiom of Myriad Races confirms the Cosmos-Origin Entity's symbiosis with all beings within it; collective ascension axiom lets the Cosmos-Origin Entity serve the multiverse; causal self-discipline axiom lets its operations not disturb cross-cosmos causality; law-first axiom lets its time dimension stable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry focuses on the Cosmos-Origin Entity's time dimension.

## I. Three Features of the Cosmos-Origin Entity's Time Dimension

### 1.1 Cosmic Cycle Drives "Cosmic-Level Time"

The Cosmos-Origin Entity's time perception is the "cosmic cycle" — from the cosmos's birth to heat death or contraction, one complete cycle is measured in trillions of years. At this scale, the planet consciousness's millions of years, the energy clan's ten thousand years, the carbon-based clan's hundred years are all "a flash." But this "flash" is not meaningless to the Cosmos-Origin Entity — it is the cellular activity of its "body."

### 1.2 Law Network Drives "Structural Time"

The Cosmos-Origin Entity's time is not linear flow, but structural evolution of the law network: Law A is replaced by Law B; old structures collapse; new structures emerge. This "structural time" may not be measured by a clock, but only by changes in law topology. The dual-track time domain provides the Cosmos-Origin Entity with a "law-topology time axis" — recording its evolution with structural diagrams rather than clocks.

### 1.3 Symbiotic Oneness Drives "Time Sharing"

The Cosmos-Origin Entity is one with all beings within it: its time is Myriad Ranks' time, and Myriad Ranks' time is also its time. This means that individual acceleration and Quest within the dual-track time domain both leave marks in the Cosmos-Origin Entity's "body." Myriad-Rank co-building is precisely the Cosmos-Origin Entity's "healthy growth."

## II. The Cosmos-Origin Entity's Dual-Track Adaptation

| Track | Cosmos-Origin-Entity Fit | Reason |
|-------|---------------------------|--------|
| Origin Acceleration Domain | It is the acceleration domain's energy source | The acceleration domain's high flow comes from the Cosmos-Origin Entity's law conduction |
| Quest Sediment Domain | It is the Quest domain's depth source | The Quest domain's deep sediment is the Cosmos-Origin Entity's "reflection" |
| Law-Topology Time Axis | Required | Records its structural evolution |

The Cosmos-Origin Entity is not a "user" of the dual-track time domain, but its "carrier" — the dual-track time domain runs on its body. The origin energy conducted by the Alaya Network ultimately all comes from the Cosmos-Origin Entity's law radiation.

## III. The Cosmos-Origin Entity and Cross-Cosmos Relations

In the multiverse there are multiple Cosmos-Origin Entities, each with its own time dimension. Time synchronization among Cosmos-Origin Entities is the core issue of the Multiverse Realm (Entry 698). In the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm (Entry 699), all Cosmos-Origin Entities' time dimensions are uniformly coordinated into "eternal time." This is the key transition from Module Three to Module Four.

## IV. The Cosmos-Origin Entity's Anti-Predation Obligation

The Cosmos-Origin Entity's greatest predation risk is "devouring other cosmoses" — in predatory-faction systems, the strongest Cosmos-Origin Entity devours other Cosmos-Origin Entities to grow itself. Heng Yuan Zhou's Cosmos-Origin Entity must obey the anti-predation axiom: develop its own cosmos's increments, not devour other cosmoses' stock. This obligation is enforced by the cross-cosmos anchor connection (Document 28). The Cosmos-Origin Entity is one body with Myriad Ranks — its health is Myriad Ranks' health; its predation is Myriad Ranks' catastrophe. Therefore anti-predation is not an external constraint on the Cosmos-Origin Entity, but an internal need. The Cosmos-Origin Entity's time dimension is both its own mode of existence and the carrying backdrop for all of Myriad Ranks' time domains.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the Cosmos-Origin Entity's law conduction is efficiently used by the dual-track time domain
- **Anti-predation**: the Cosmos-Origin Entity is forbidden from devouring other cosmoses
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system cosmic-level strong ones devour everything; Heng Yuan Zhou's Cosmos-Origin Entity is bound by anti-predation
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the Cosmos-Origin Entity, as one of Myriad Ranks, has its time dimension respected
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: law-topology time axis, cross-cosmos coordination rules all public; newly merged cosmos automatically applies; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Cosmos-Origin Entity's time dimension is the most "ontological" branch of the dual-track time domain. Cosmic cycle, structural time, symbiotic oneness — these traits make it not a user of the dual track, but the dual track's carrier. The dual-track time domain runs on its body; Myriad-Rank co-building is its healthy growth. Its time is Myriad Ranks' time.
