# Entry 698 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Time Domain and the Multiverse-Realm Cross-Cosmos Time

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 28, 02 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 27)

## Introduction: when a cultivator simultaneously lives in multiple cosmoses, how is time counted

The Multiverse Realm is an extended realm above Heng Yuan Zhou's nine realms (labeled "pending Terminology-Committee calibration"): the cultivator's consciousness and existence are no longer confined to a single cosmos, but may simultaneously span multiple cosmoses. This brings the dual-track time domain's most fundamental new question — how is cross-cosmos time synchronized? Each cosmos's time flow differs, each Cosmos-Origin Entity's time dimension differs, each planet consciousness's geological scale differs. When a cultivator simultaneously accelerates in Cosmos A, Quests in Cosmos B, and escorts in Cosmos C, how is his "time" counted? Six top-level axioms here form cross-cosmos-time rules: anti-predation axiom opposes cross-cosmos time predation; republican axiom of Myriad Races confirms Multiverse-Realm beings' equality with ordinary beings; collective ascension axiom lets cross-cosmos operations serve Myriad Ranks; causal self-discipline axiom lets cross-cosmos time operations not disturb causality; law-first axiom lets cross-cosmos time rules stable. The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry focuses on the Multiverse-Realm cross-cosmos time.

## I. Three Hard Problems of Cross-Cosmos Time

### 1.1 Flow-Asynchrony Problem

Cosmos A's Origin Acceleration Domain flows at a hundred times external; Cosmos B at a thousand times external; Cosmos C has no acceleration domain. A Multiverse-Realm cultivator active in three cosmoses simultaneously — three cosmoses' time flows are out of sync: he deduces a hundred years in Cosmos A, only ten years pass in Cosmos B, and a flash in Cosmos C. This asynchrony is not a problem, but the norm. The dual-track time domain's response: build a "cross-cosmos time ledger" for Multiverse-Realm cultivators — separately record his time experiences in each cosmos, not forcibly unify.

### 1.2 Cross-Cosmos Causality Problem

One time operation in Cosmos A may, through the cross-cosmos anchor connection (Document 28), ripple causality to Cosmos B. This cross-cosmos causal disturbance is the causal self-discipline axiom's most strictly constrained scenario. The dual-track time domain requires Multiverse-Realm cultivators' cross-cosmos operations to pass the symbiotic court's cross-cosmos causal assessment (Entry 688) — ten times stricter than single-cosmos operation review.

### 1.3 Identity-Consistency Problem

A Multiverse-Realm cultivator may have different identities, different cultivation paths, different social roles in different cosmoses. How to guarantee that "this cultivator accelerating in Cosmos A" and "that cultivator Questing in Cosmos B" are the same person? The dual-track time domain's response: the Alaya Network builds a "cross-cosmos identity anchor" for each Multiverse-Realm cultivator — regardless of which cosmos he is in or what identity he uses, his core consciousness, civilizational weight, and causal records are uniformly managed by the anchor.

## II. Cross-Cosmos Time's Dual-Track Adaptation

| Track | Cross-Cosmos Fit | Reason |
|-------|------------------|--------|
| Origin Acceleration Domain | May parallelize across multiple cosmoses | Accelerated deduction has no cross-cosmos conflict |
| Quest Sediment Domain | Must single-cosmos depth | Questing requires immersion, no distraction |
| Cross-Cosmos Causal Review | Required | Prevent cross-cosmos disturbance |

Multiverse-Realm cultivators may simultaneously conduct accelerated deduction in multiple cosmoses, but Quest Sediment must choose one cosmos for deep immersion — otherwise Questing becomes "sightseeing." This is the dual-track time domain's hard constraint on the Multiverse Realm.

## III. Cross-Cosmos Time and Myriad-Rank Deliberation

Multiverse-Realm cultivators' cross-cosmos activity involves multiple cosmoses' Myriad-Rank interests. Therefore cross-cosmos time operations may not be unilaterally decided by the cultivator — they must be reported at the Myriad-Rank Council round table (Entry 669), supervised by representatives of each clan. This prevents Multiverse-Realm cultivators from using cross-cosmos identity to "escape like a cicada shedding its shell" — doing bad things in Cosmos A then running to Cosmos C to continue cultivating.

## IV. Cross-Cosmos Time's Transition to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm

The Multiverse Realm's cross-cosmos time is the transition stage to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's eternal time (Entry 699). In the Multiverse Realm, cross-cosmos time is still a "multi-ledger" — each cosmos records separately; at the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, all ledgers are unified into "eternal time." This transition is not instantaneous, but the natural extension from Module Three to Module Four.

- **Anti-energy waste**: cross-cosmos time ledgers keep Multiverse-Realm cultivators' multi-line activity from chaos
- **Anti-predation**: cross-cosmos causal review strictly prevents cross-cosmos predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system the protagonist's cross-cosmos activity is unsupervised; Heng Yuan Zhou's Multiverse-Realm cultivators are under cross-cosmos review
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: cross-cosmos operations are supervised by the Myriad-Rank Council
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cross-cosmos ledgers, causal review, identity-anchor rules all public; newly merged cosmos automatically applies; may infinitely expand toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

The Multiverse Realm's cross-cosmos time is the dual-track time domain's key leap from "single cosmos" to "multi-cosmos." Flow asynchrony, cross-cosmos causality, identity consistency — each hard problem has its solution.
