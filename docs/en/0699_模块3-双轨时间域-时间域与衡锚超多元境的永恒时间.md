# Entry 699 | Dual-Track Time Domain | Time Domain and the Balanced-Anchor-Hyper-Multiverse Realm's Eternal Time

> Module: Module 3 · Dual-Track Time Domain | Benchmark base: Documents 14, 02, 20 | Series: Dual-Track-Time-Domain Overview Deepening (Entry 28 · Realm Final)

## Introduction: eternal time is not "time stopped," but "time fulfilled"

The Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm (alias "Hyper-Multiverse Balanced Anchor Realm") is the highest extended realm above the nine realms: the cultivator's existence transcends any single cosmos and any single time dimension, and is one with the entire Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's eternal laws. In this realm, time is no longer "linear flow," nor "cross-cosmos multi-ledger," but "eternal fulfillment" — past, present, and future are simultaneously present in eternal time. Six top-level axioms reach ultimate unity here: the anti-predation axiom expresses itself in eternal time as "no need to plunder"; the republican axiom of Myriad Races expresses itself as "Myriad Ranks as one body"; the collective ascension axiom expresses itself as "uplift is fulfillment"; the causal self-discipline axiom expresses itself as "causality self-abiding"; the law-first axiom expresses itself as "law is eternity." The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning. This entry focuses on the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's eternal time.

## I. Three Features of Eternal Time

### 1.1 Simultaneous Presence: Past, Present, Future Unified

In the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, the cultivator no longer "experiences" time — he "sees" time's full picture: an event's past, present, and future are simultaneously present in his consciousness. This is not prophesying the future; it is that the future already "exists" in eternity. Eternal time is not "time stopped," but "time fulfilled" — all time points are simultaneously present.

### 1.2 No Need to Plunder: Increment Development to the Extremes

In predatory-faction systems, the root of predation is "stock scarcity" — always feeling insufficient, must grab. In eternal time, what the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm cultivator sees is "infinite increments" — the law network's developable increments always exceed what has been developed. Since increments are infinite, predation loses its meaning. The anti-predation axiom is here elevated from "constraint" to "nature."

### 1.3 Myriad Ranks as One Body: Time Is Myriad Ranks

In eternal time, the cultivator is no longer the subject of "what I do with time," but the carrier of "time manifests Myriad Ranks through me." His time is one body with all of Myriad Ranks' beings' time — the carbon-based clan's hundred years, the energy clan's ten thousand years, the planet consciousness's millions of years, the Cosmos-Origin Entity's trillions of years, all simultaneously present in his eternal time. The republican axiom of Myriad Races is here elevated from "institution" to "existence."

## II. Eternal Time's Dual-Track Position

| Track | Position in Eternal Time |
|-------|--------------------------|
| Origin Acceleration Domain | No longer needed — acceleration is for deducing within finite time; in eternity there is no "finite" |
| Quest Sediment Domain | No longer needed — sedimentation is for accumulating within flowing time; in eternity there is no "flow" |
| Eternal Time | The dual track's ultimate fulfillment |

The Origin Acceleration Domain and Quest Sediment Domain "dissolve" into eternal time in the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm — not abolished, but fulfilled. The Alaya Network is no longer a "recording tool" in eternity, but Myriad Ranks' one-body consciousness itself. The acceleration domain's "fast" and the Quest domain's "deep" are simultaneously present in eternity. The dual-track time domain's endpoint is transcending the dual track.

## III. Eternal Time and the Transition to Module Four

The Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's eternal time is Module Three (Dual-Track Time Domain)'s endpoint and Module Four (Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library)'s starting point. The question Module Four must answer is: after reaching eternal time, what does the cultivator do? The answer is not "lie flat" — the cultivator in eternal time bears hyper-multiverse-level perpetual construction duties (Module Four will develop them). Eternal time is not an endpoint, but a new starting point.

## IV. The "Reachability" of Eternal Time

It must be emphasized: eternal time is not an esoteric beyond-shore, but the logical endpoint of the dual-track time-domain system. From the Sense-Origin Realm (Entry 100) to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm (Entry 699), the entire system is one continuous road — every step has law support, every step is reproducible, every step does not depend on protagonist aura. Lin Hao (Entry 679) walked through the first half; the second half is open to 99% of beings. Eternal time is not the privilege of a few chosen ones, but the ultimate direction of Myriad-Rank co-building.
