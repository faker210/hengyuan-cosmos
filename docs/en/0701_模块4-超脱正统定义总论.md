# Entry 701 | Transcendence Orthodox Definition · General Introduction — What Is True Transcendence

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 01, 17, 04

## Introduction: the two words "transcendence" stigmatized by web novels

"Transcendence" is the Myriad-Heavens web novels' cheapest and most abused word. After the protagonist reaches the peak, he always "transcends the mortal world," "transcends reincarnation," "transcends the Myriad Heavens," as if as long as his fist is big enough and his life long enough, sitting in some unnamed place is called transcendence. Heng Yuan Zhou's library, in Module 4's opening entry, must first nail these two words' orthodox definition; otherwise all later job duties, perpetual-motion construction, and idling critiques lose their yardstick.

The six top-level axioms at this moment hold the field together: the anti-predation axiom requires the transcendent one's power to have a clean source, not climbed over Myriad Ranks' bones; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the transcendent one's qualification be open to all beings, not a few chosen ones' patent; the collective-ascension axiom requires the transcendent one not fly away alone, but lift Myriad Ranks along to ascend; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the transcendent one's cross-plane operations be fully reported and not disturb causality; the law-first axiom requires "transcendence"'s own law definition be stable and self-consistent, never retconning and not drifting with plot.

Universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing/layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more higher-level infinity. Everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, so predation naturally loses meaning.

This entry answers a most fundamental question: in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, what counts as "true transcendence"?

## I: The Old Definition's Triple Bankruptcy

Web-novel systems' "transcendence" definition, seemingly varied, breaks open into only three layers:

1. **Power-dimension transcendence**: combat power crushes all enemies, lifespan crushes all time, and from then on no one can hurt him.
2. **Relation-dimension transcendence**: sever all bonds with the mortal world, relatives may die and causality may be cut, "unstained by the red dust."
3. **Rule-dimension transcendence**: above heavenly Dao, law, and reincarnation, with rules made and modified by himself.

These three definitions all go bankrupt from Heng Yuan Zhou's perspective. If power-dimension transcendence is built on predation, per the anti-predation axiom it is "advanced parasitism"; if relation-dimension transcendence manifests as abandoning Myriad Ranks to ascend alone, per the collective-ascension axiom it is a "deserter"; if rule-dimension transcendence manifests as one person privately holding law and rewriting at will, per the republican axiom of Myriad Races it is "oligarchic dictatorship." The three's common illness is understanding "transcendence" as "being rid of everything," not "taking responsibility for everything."

## II: Heng Yuan Zhou's Transcendence Definition: Responsible Transcendence

The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's orthodox definition is, word for word, the opposite of the old definition:

> **True transcendence is not being rid of Myriad Ranks, but becoming the permanent bearer of Myriad Ranks' civilizational weight; not fleeing law, but entering the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm and becoming a living anchor of the law network; not ending cultivation, but opening perpetual-motion construction, continuously doing work, continuously iterating, and continuously nurturing later people in the Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle.**

This definition has three indivisible components:

| Component | Old web-novel definition | Heng Yuan Zhou orthodox definition |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| Relation with Myriad Ranks | Sever bonds, ascend alone | Civilizational weight permanently bound; the stronger the more must feed back |
| Relation with law | Above law, rewrite at will | Enter the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, become a living law anchor |
| Relation with time | Immortal, idle retirement | Perpetual-motion construction, infinite iteration, the harder the stronger |

Miss any one of the three, and it is not true transcendence, but "pseudo-transcendence" — either running away, or lying flat, or monopolizing. These three pseudo-transcendences are precisely the objects Entries 702, 703, 704 will critique one by one.

## III: The Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm: The Transcendent One's Permanent-Post Realm

Per unified realm division, above the Nine Realms (Sense-Condensed to Origin) there are two more: the Multiverse Realm and the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm (first noted: also called "Hyper-Multiverse Balanced-Anchor Realm"; standard writing is Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm). Origin Realm is the "cosmic-tier" threshold, Multiverse Realm is the "cross-plane engineering" stage, and **the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is the transcendent one's permanent-post realm**.

In this realm, the cultivator is no longer "one person growing stronger," but becomes a living node in the law network:

- The personal Dao deeply fuses with cosmic-origin law; the Dao is the anchor, the anchor is the Dao;
- Its very presence calibrates the local law network, preventing law drift and historical reverse;
- Its civilizational weight is quantified in real time by the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision, accepted by Myriad-Rank representatives' deliberation;
- Its cultivation results (methods, law weaving, civilizational engineering) are opened network-wide via the Selfless Dao, for later people to use free.

That is, the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is not "cultivation's end," but "taking-up-post's start." If a person reaches this realm but refuses to take on job responsibilities, per the plane-sovereign strong-one four-dimensional assessment (power 40 / heart-nature 25 / contribution 20 / responsibility 15), his responsibility dimension is directly zeroed, not recognized as a transcendent one, only registered as a "high-risk individual."

## IV: The Transcendent One's Triple Identity Unity

The orthodox transcendent one in Heng Yuan Zhou simultaneously bears three identities; the three must be unified, none may be missing:

1. **Myriad-Rank symbiont**: his power comes from the Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle, and must also feed back to Myriad-Rank origin, not only in not out;
2. **Perpetual-motion builder**: his very existence is civilization-increment production; the stronger the more he must work, and the more he works the stronger;
3. **Law self-disciplinarian**: all his cross-plane and cross-time operations strictly follow causal self-discipline reporting, under Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision.

Behind these three identities is Heng Yuan Zhou's iron law that "individual combat power = destructive power × civilizational consensus" (see Entry 706). An individual with however great destructive power, if civilizational consensus approaches zero (i.e., Myriad Ranks do not consider his power lawful and do not accept his leadership), his actual combat power in the civilizational-weight system approaches zero — because he cannot call on the law network's public compute, nor receive the Alaya Network's legitimacy endorsement.

## V: Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: old web novels letting the strongest idle in retirement are cosmos-tier energy waste; Heng Yuan Zhou sets the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm as a permanent-post realm, putting the strongest's every bit of compute into civilization increment
- **Anti-predation**: the orthodox transcendent one's power source is all creation and cycle, not predation; those with an unclean source are directly zeroed in the civilizational-weight formula
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' "transcendence" is grace-style — the protagonist reaches the peak by Gu Chensha's grant, then locks later people in the fierce realm; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcendence is network-style — the strong come from Myriad Ranks and go to Myriad Ranks, with no one able to monopolize
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: transcendence qualification is open to all beings, with plant spirits, planet consciousness, cosmic-origin bodies, and multiverse-remnant civilizations all having a road into the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, not human aristocrats' exclusive
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the transcendent one's methods, law weaving, and civilizational engineering are all opened network-wide via the Selfless Dao, with hyper-multiverse-tier knowledge no longer privately hoarded
- **Universal equal infinity**: the universal equal infinite law — within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation naturally loses meaning

## Conclusion

What is true transcendence? In one sentence: **not being rid of everything, but taking responsibility for everything; not the end, but a post; not one person ascending, but lifting Myriad Ranks along to ascend.** This is the yardstick all of Module 4's 33 entries repeatedly argue. Entries 702 to 733 will use this ruler to measure one by one six pseudo-transcendences: running-away, lying-flat, monopolizing, idling, parasitizing, and aimless, measuring out their respective bankruptcies.
