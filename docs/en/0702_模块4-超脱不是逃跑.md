# Entry 702 | Transcendence Orthodox Definition · Transcendence Is Not Running Away — Critique of Individual-Escape Transcendence

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 83, 26

## Introduction: however far you run, you cannot run out of causality

"I have transcended the red dust, and from now on have no entanglement with the mortal world." — this sentence, in Myriad-Heavens web novels, is taken as a strong one's medal, but in Heng Yuan Zhou it is a **deserter's registration form**. Individual-escape transcendence is the most insidious of the three pseudo-transcendence brothers: it does not manifest as predation, nor as laziness; it wears the philosophical coat of "having seen through the world," yet does the deed of dropping Myriad Ranks and running off alone.

The six top-level axioms here simultaneously judge it: the anti-predation axiom does not directly name "running away," but the runner's power source is often stock accumulated by predation in early years; the republican axiom of Myriad Races asks — by what right do you alone leave, leaving Myriad Ranks who also gave and also have evolution qualification in place? The collective-ascension axiom most directly sentences it to death: the strong do not ascend alone. The causal self-discipline axiom adds the last blade: you think severing bonds severs causality? Causality does not vanish by your will; it is only thrown behind you, continuing to ferment in a more twisted way. The law-first axiom ensures the law definition that "running away is not transcendence" stays stable, not loosened by some web-novel protagonist's tragic ending.

Universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing/layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more higher-level infinity. Everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, so predation naturally loses meaning.

## I: Individual-Escape Transcendence's Three Typical Scripts

Dissecting the long web-novel river, individual-escape transcendence has three recurring scripts:

1. **Tribulation-in-the-mortal-world type**: the protagonist goes through life and death, relatives and friends die out, heart cold, "sees through the red dust," shatters the sky with one palm and leaves, leaving a devastated civilization to clean up the wreckage itself;
2. **Plane-ascension type**: the protagonist breaks through everything in the mortal realm, ascends to the upper realm, and the mortal realm from then on has nothing to do with him; breaks through again in the upper realm, ascends higher, dropping civilizations behind all the way;
3. **Autocratic-for-eons type**: the protagonist, to protect something, seals all enemies and all possibilities, alone guarding the end of the river of time, forbidding any later person to approach.

The three scripts look solemn, but are essentially the same: **packaging "I'm leaving" as "I've transcended," packaging "I've dropped Myriad Ranks" as "Myriad Ranks no longer need me."**

## II: Why Running Away Is Not Transcendence: Three Books of Causal Debts

Heng Yuan Zhou does not recognize running-away transcendence not by moralizing, but by accounting. Under the causal self-discipline axiom, the runner owes three books of debts never repayable:

| Account | Content | Consequence |
|------|------|------|
| Human-debt account | The causality mentors, fellow disciples, and clansmen gave on the cultivation road | All cut off, and you snap one ring in the Myriad-Rank origin cycle |
| Law account | Your plane was stable because of your presence; once you leave, the law anchor destabilizes | Local law drifts, and civilizational-regression risk rises exponentially |
| Example account | Countless later people take you as their model, learning "can't beat it so run, ascend and forget the root" | The whole civilization's strong-one ethics collapse, and the predation faction slips in |

Most critical is the third account. In the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's records, a strong one's "running away" is not a private choice, but a public event — it is seen by all beings network-wide, imitated by later people, interpreted by the predation faction as "the strong should do it this way." This is why the causal self-discipline axiom requires cross-plane operations strictly reported: you may not be unable to leave, you may not leave **without saying hello, without handover, without an anchor**.

## III: The Essential Difference from "Going Far to Cultivate"

Some will argue: doesn't Heng Yuan Zhou also do cross-plane exploration, also open new universes (see Post 719)? What's the difference between going far to cultivate and running-away transcendence?

There are three differences, each a hard standard:

1. **Whether an anchor is left**: the far-going cultivator must leave a law anchor and successor in the original plane, maintaining law stability; the runner snaps everything at once;
2. **Whether causality is reported**: the far-going cultivator reports the journey per observe-first-then-limitedly-intervene, completing handover before leaving; the runner disappears soundlessly;
3. **Whether the feedback loop is kept**: the far-going cultivator brings back knowledge, law-weaving experience, and incremental resources from new planes, feeding back to Myriad Ranks via the Selfless Dao; the runner only takes power, leaving not a thread.

All three present is called "opening up"; all three absent is called "running away." Heng Yuan Zhou does not oppose strong ones going far; Heng Yuan Zhou opposes **going far then cutting the loop back to the Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle**.

## IV: Lin Hao-Type Counter-Example: Writing "Running Away" as "Responsibility"

In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's narrative paradigm, protagonist Lin Hao's growth path is precisely the opposite of running-away transcendence: every realm breakthrough, he does not fly up one step then let go, but turns back to bring along fellow-realm latecomers. His "transcendence" is not flying to some higher unoccupied realm, but in the post of the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, weaving the Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle's loop tighter — activating one more plane, covering one more clan, opening one more method, and civilizational weight grows by one more point.

This is not moral loftiness, but law requirement. Per the plane-sovereign strong-one four-dimensional assessment, the responsibility dimension is 15% and heart-nature 25%; a runner's heart-nature score is pierced at the ethical-review stage: deep consciousness scanning reads out the "drop Myriad Ranks and ascend alone" subconscious, situational stress testing replays the choice of "you leave and the civilization behind collapses," and if he chooses "leave," he does not pass.

## V: Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the runner takes away lifetime cultivation's entire compute and law-weaving ability into ownerless land, a thorough waste of civilization increment
- **Anti-predation**: if the runner accumulated by predation in early years, his stock was unclean to begin with; running away does not repay, only makes the creditor unable to find him
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo protagonists ascend and cut contact, with the mortal realm locked in the fierce realm with no further improvement; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcendent ones before leaving must leave an anchor, a successor, and a knowledge loop
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: running-away transcendence leaves 99% of Myriad Ranks in place to fend for themselves, directly violating the Myriad-Rank 99% full-coverage promise
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the runner's methods and experience disappear with him, hyper-multiverse-tier knowledge is privately taken, openness goes to zero
- **Universal equal infinity**: the universal equal infinite law — within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation naturally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Transcendence is not a "I'm leaving." You may go to the farthest plane, to the newest universe, to law's wildest frontier — but as long as you are still doing work in the Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle, as long as you still keep the anchor, the successor, and the Selfless Dao loop, however far you go you are a transcendent one. Once you cut these three loops, however far you go you are only a deserter. **Running away is not transcendence; running away only drags the debt owed Myriad Ranks from today to tomorrow.**
