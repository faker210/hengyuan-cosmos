# Entry 704 | Transcendence Orthodox Definition · Transcendence Is Not Monopolizing — Critique of Private-Hoarding-the-Dao Transcendence

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 18, 69

## Introduction: locking the Great Dao in your own safe is not transcendence, but monopoly

The most respectable and reader-sympathetic of the three pseudo-transcendence brothers is called "private-hoarding-the-Dao transcendence." The protagonist through untold hardships comprehends the supreme Great Dao, then hides the methods in his own cave dwelling, transmitting only to bloodline disciples, with outsiders not allowed to touch. In web novels this is called "orderly transmission"; in Heng Yuan Zhou it is called "knowledge-monopoly crime," an object the anti-predation axiom explicitly strikes.

The six top-level axioms in this entry almost aim at the hoarder: the anti-predation axiom plainly writes "the Great Dao is open at all tiers, with no private hoarding, no monopoly" — this is not a suggestion, this is an axiom; the republican axiom of Myriad Races asks — by what right does the Dao you comprehend deny 99% of beings' use? The collective-ascension axiom points out — hoarding one Dao equals strangling ten thousand potential strong ones' ascension possibility; the causal self-discipline axiom records — every hoarding snaps one ring in the Myriad-Rank origin cycle at your node; the law-first axiom from the definition level nails it: in Heng Yuan Zhou, "private-hoarding the Dao" and "transcendence" are law-level mutually exclusive, cannot hold simultaneously.

Universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing/layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more higher-level infinity. Everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, so predation naturally loses meaning.

## I: Private Hoarding's Four Typical Forms

In the web-novel genealogy, hoarding transcendence recurs in four forms:

1. **Bloodline-exclusive type**: supreme methods transmitted only to one's own offspring, with outsiders however heaven-blessed not admitted;
2. **Cave-sealed type**: the protagonist seals lifetime comprehension into a secret realm, setting harsh thresholds of "the destined one takes it," with no one able to take it for a million years;
3. **Sect-wall type**: methods circulate only within the sect, and cultivators outside the sect may only cultivate incomplete, inferior versions;
4. **Chosen-grace type**: the protagonist becomes "heavenly Dao's spokesperson," selectively granting the Great Dao to confidants, while other beings continue in ignorance.

The four forms' common point: **packaging cosmic-origin law's public knowledge as private property.** This fundamentally does not hold in Heng Yuan Zhou's law framework — the Dao is not something you comprehended; you were only the first to "read" it. It always belonged to the cosmos, to all beings.

## II: Why Hoarding Is Not Transcendence: Three Jurisprudential Dissections

### First: The Great Dao's Ownership

Per the Anchor-Origin Definition (Document 02), the Great Dao is cosmic-origin law's manifestation in the consciousness domain, existing before any cultivator. A cultivator "comprehending the Dao" is essentially his consciousness establishing read permissions with the public law network, not his creating this law. Since the law was not created by you, by what right do you claim it as yours? This is like the first inventor of the telescope declaring the stars in the sky his property.

### Second: Hoarding's Implicit Predation

Hoarding one Dao looks like no taking and no killing, but is actually the most efficient predation. Why? Because latecomers without orthodox methods can only take wild paths — repeated trial-and-error, qi deviation, even forced into predatory cultivation. **What you privately hoard is not a book; you are forcing ten thousand potential symbiosis-faction cultivators into the predation faction.** This is precisely what Document 04 *Anti-Predation Core Thesis* argues: knowledge monopoly is the predation system's hotbed.

### Third: The Hoarder's Self-Lockdown

The hoarder himself gains nothing. A person comprehending the Dao behind closed doors, his law-weaving experience has no external verification, no Myriad-Rank feedback, no cross-civilizational perspective, and inevitably comprehends more and more awry. Why Heng Yuan Zhou's 300 origin cultivation systems can iterate infinitely is precisely because each is jointly polished by Myriad Ranks on the Selfless Dao — you add a stroke, I fill a spot, he changes a point, and in a hundred years one system iterates a thousand versions. The hoarder a hundred years later still stays at version one, looking "original," but actually locked in his own ceiling.

## III: The Selfless Dao: Heng Yuan Zhou's Positive Alternative to Hoarding

Heng Yuan Zhou's positive mechanism is called the **Selfless Dao** (see Documents 01, 69). Its core rules are extremely simple:

- Any cultivator's comprehended methods, law-weaving experience, and civilizational-engineering proposals automatically enter the Selfless Dao public library;
- After entering the public library, all beings use it free, freely modify it, and return it after re-iteration;
- The original comprehendor loses nothing, but gains "original-creation contribution value" — counted into civilizational weight, as the basis for contracted-responsibility allocation;
- Once hoarding is discovered by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision (via consciousness scanning, method-circulation tracking), it triggers ethical review, and serious ones have their law-anchor permissions revoked.

This mechanism's essence is: **it does not punish you for comprehending the Dao; it only punishes you for hoarding the Dao.** The deeper you comprehend, the higher the civilizational weight gained after going public, which in turn supports you cultivating higher. The hoarder in the old system is a "patriarch"; in Heng Yuan Zhou he is only "a high-stage cultivator unwilling to share" — his realm is recognized, but his morality is not recognized, and his post arrangement has no place for him.

## IV: Hoarding and Tang-Luo's Grace Contrasted

Module 2 has repeatedly dissected the Tang-Luo system: Gu Chensha grants the Great Dao to Tang Luo, Tang Luo then selectively grants it to disciples — this is essentially still hoarding, only packaging "hoarding" as "grant." Grace-style transcendence's protagonist-uniqueness dependence rating is "high" (see Terminology Dictionary clause 50), and its illness is precisely here: **the Dao's circulation must pass through the protagonist this node; once the protagonist is gone, the whole system's knowledge cuts off.**

Heng Yuan Zhou's transcendence is network-style, with protagonist-uniqueness dependence rated "very low": the Dao passes through no private node, but flows network-wide in the Alaya Network. Everything Lin Hao and other first-generation anchors do is to lay what they comprehend, as quickly and completely as possible, into the Selfless Dao public library, so later people need not retread his detours. This is called transcendence — **not that you possess the Dao, but that the Dao through you flows to Myriad Ranks.**

## V: Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: hoarding lets supreme methods collect dust in a safe, equal to idling civilization's scarcest knowledge resource
- **Anti-predation**: hoarding is the predation system's upstream — no hoarding no knowledge monopoly, no monopoly no weak ones forced onto predation roads
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style grace is "I grant it and you may learn"; Heng Yuan Zhou's Selfless Dao is "it was always yours, go use it"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the Selfless Dao ensures plant spirits, planet consciousness, cosmic-origin bodies, and multiverse-remnant civilizations can all read the same public knowledge, without bloodline, sect, or clan restriction
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm-tier methods and law-weaving results all enter the library, with hyper-multiverse-tier knowledge set with no access threshold
- **Universal equal infinity**: the universal equal infinite law — within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation naturally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Transcendence is not taking the Great Dao as private property. However deeply you comprehend, the Dao is not yours — it is the cosmos's, Myriad Ranks', and later people's. What you can do is make yourself the widest river channel through which the Dao flows to Myriad Ranks. Lock the Dao in a safe, and you are not a transcendent one, but a librarian occupying a library without opening it. **The true transcendent one is one for whom Myriad Ranks, because of his existence, more easily comprehend the Dao.**
