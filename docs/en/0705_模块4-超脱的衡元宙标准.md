# Entry 705 | Transcendence Orthodox Definition | Heng Yuan Zhou's Transcendence Standard — Myriad-Rank Symbiosis + Perpetual Construction

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 01, 05, 17

## Introduction: two hard bars; miss one and it is not transcendence

The previous four entries have dissected old web-novel pseudo-transcendences one by one: running-away, lying-flat, monopolizing. After dissection, rules must be established — by what standard does Heng Yuan Zhou recognize a person as "truly transcendent"? The answer is only two hard bars: **Myriad-Rank symbiosis + perpetual construction**. These two are not moral slogans, but law-level admission thresholds, written into the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's fundamental law, enforced throughout by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision.

The six top-level axioms in this entry converge into operational standards: the anti-predation axiom ensures the "clean source" premise of the two bars; the republican axiom of Myriad Races is directly the first bar's parent law; the collective-ascension axiom is directly the second bar's parent law; the causal self-discipline axiom is procedural protection during the two bars' execution; the law-first axiom ensures these two bars themselves are not modified by plot convenience, strong-one privilege, or any "special circumstance."

Universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing/layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more higher-level infinity. Everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, so predation naturally loses meaning.

## I: First Bar: Myriad-Rank Symbiosis

"Myriad-Rank symbiosis" as the transcendence admission standard has three operational layers:

### 1.1 Source Symbiosis

The transcendent one's entire power must be traceable to lawful inflow from the Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle, not predation, devouring, or monopolizing. The Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision does "source audit" on every candidate transcendent:

- Internal energy purity must reach 99%+, with no predation residual marks;
- The cultivation path must be taken from the public branches of the 300 origin cultivation systems, or anchor-certified self-created branches;
- Historical records show no active predation, no private hoarding, no cutting off Myriad Ranks' supply.

Any item failing, however great the power, is only registered as a "high-risk individual," not entering the transcendence sequence.

### 1.2 Relation Symbiosis

The transcendent one must maintain an active symbiotic relation with Myriad Ranks, not severing bonds and going alone. Specific indicators:

- His contribution value on the anchor network ranks in the civilization's top 20%;
- He has served at least in one Myriad-Rank clan as a guidance post, training post, or deliberation post;
- His methods, experience, and law-weaving results are all queryable in the Selfless Dao public library;
- His civilizational-weight panel is open to Myriad-Rank representatives, accepting periodic deliberation.

### 1.3 Coverage Symbiosis

The transcendent one's existence must objectively expand Myriad-Rank republicanism's coverage, not shrink it. A qualified transcendent one's lifetime activities must satisfy at least one:

- Brought some previously uncovered clan (plant spirits, planet consciousness, cosmic-origin bodies, multiverse-remnant civilizations, etc.) into the anchor network;
- Opened some previously locked civilizational tier (e.g., Tang-Luo-style fierce-realm ceiling);
- Gave some previously pathless special life form an orthodox cultivation system.

## II: Second Bar: Perpetual Construction

"Perpetual construction" as the transcendence admission standard likewise has three operational layers:

### 2.1 Post Perpetuity

The transcendent one must accept the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm as a **permanent-post realm**, not a temporary cultivation end. He must claim a specific post in the law network — law-network maintenance post, Myriad-Rank evolution guidance post, cross-plane anchor construction post, Alaya Network supervision post, civilization error-correction and historical-reverse prevention post, incremental-development and resource-creation post, Myriad-Rank-republicanism deliberation-coordination post, new-universe opening and law-weaving post, transcendent-one training and transmission post (see Entries 711–720).

A post is not nominal, but a contract. At least 30% of time and energy annually go to post work (following Document 17's strong-one duty system), and post performance enters civilizational weight.

### 2.2 Increment Perpetuity

The transcendent one's lifetime output must be **civilization increment**, not stock consumption. Judgment criteria:

- Do the new methods, new law-weaving schemes, and new civilizational engineering he created increase the Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle's total flow?
- Do the anchors, planes, and ecosystems he maintains make previously unstable systems more stable?
- Do the latecomers he cultivates start higher and go farther than he himself did?

If any one of the three questions is no, the transcendent one is at that stage in "stock consumption" state, and the Alaya Network automatically triggers a reminder, requiring him to adjust direction.

### 2.3 Iteration Perpetuity

The transcendent one himself may not stop iterating. In Heng Yuan Zhou's law definition, "a transcendent one who stops iterating" is a self-contradictory concept — because law itself is expanding infinitely, and if you do not follow law, you are left behind by law. This is not cruel; this is the law-first axiom's natural corollary: **the worldview may expand infinitely without collapse, meaning the transcendent one's post content must also expand infinitely.**

## III: The Two Bars' Synergy: Myriad-Rank Symbiosis Defines Direction, Perpetual Construction Defines Intensity

The two bars are not parallel, but a **direction and intensity** relation:

| | Myriad-Rank Symbiosis (direction) | Perpetual Construction (intensity) |
|---|---|---|
| Defines what | Whom power serves, to whom the Dao is open | How much power outputs, how fast the Dao iterates |
| Noncompliance manifests as | Running away, monopolizing, predation | Lying flat, idling, slumbering |
| Alaya Network judgment | Ethical review fails | Post performance substandard |
| Disposition | Does not enter transcendence sequence | Triggers post replacement |

A person may have immense power but wrong direction (predation-faction strong one), or right direction but insufficient intensity (retirement-style elder). Only pressing both bars simultaneously can leave the "true transcendence" position to those **with right direction, sufficient intensity, clean source, broad coverage**.

## IV: Connection with the Plane-Sovereign Strong-One Four-Dimensional Assessment

Heng Yuan Zhou's standard system is nested:

- Document 17's plane-sovereign strong-one four-dimensional assessment (power 40 / heart-nature 25 / contribution 20 / responsibility 15) is the **entry assessment**, eligible once Origin Realm is reached;
- This Entry 705's "Myriad-Rank symbiosis + perpetual construction" two bars is the **transcendence assessment**, entered once the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is reached;
- The two are directionally consistent, but the transcendence assessment is stricter on contribution and responsibility: not "top-20% level," but "post perpetuity, increment perpetuity, iteration perpetuity."

This nested design ensures: from Sense-Condensed Realm, every realm ascent deepens his binding with Myriad-Rank symbiosis and perpetual construction; by the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, "Myriad-Rank symbiosis + perpetual construction" is no longer an external assessment, but his life's own mode of existence.

## V: Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the two bars direct every bit of strong-one compute into "producing increment for Myriad Ranks," eliminating any form of energy idling
- **Anti-predation**: source-symbiosis audit cuts off predatory transcendence at the source, not allowing those who "ascend over Myriad Ranks' bones" into the transcendence sequence
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style transcendence's standard is "is the protagonist strong enough"; Heng Yuan Zhou's standard is "how much increment did the protagonist produce for Myriad Ranks" — the former looks at the individual, the latter at the network
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the coverage-symbiosis clause explicitly lists plant spirits, planet consciousness, cosmic-origin bodies, and multiverse-remnant civilizations as objects the transcendent one must serve
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the iteration-perpetuity clause requires the transcendent one's every output be queryable in the Selfless Dao public library, with hyper-multiverse-tier knowledge set with no access threshold
- **Universal equal infinity**: the universal equal infinite law — within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation naturally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Heng Yuan Zhou recognizes a person as truly transcendent not by how high his combat power, not by how long he has lived, not by how many enemies he has defeated. Only two: whether he symbioses with Myriad Ranks, whether he is in perpetual construction. Right direction, sufficient intensity, clean source, broad coverage — he is a transcendent one. Any one missing, however invincible, however immortal, however lonely, is only pseudo-transcendence. **This is Heng Yuan Zhou's final definition of the two words "transcendence."**
