# Entry 706 | Transcendence Orthodox Definition | The Transcendent One's Civilizational Weight — Individual Combat Power = Destructive Power × Civilizational Consensus

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 29, 17, 01

## Introduction: looking only at destructive power is the old world's algorithm

Web-novel systems evaluate a strong one by only one number: combat power. One fist shatters how many planets, one finger extinguishes how many galaxies; the bigger the number the stronger. This algorithm is thoroughly rewritten in Heng Yuan Zhou. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's formula is:

> **Individual effective combat power = destructive power × civilizational consensus**

This multiplication rather than addition means that as either variable approaches zero, effective combat power approaches zero. A strong one with off-the-charts destructive power but negative civilizational consensus, in Heng Yuan Zhou's ledger, is not "very strong," but "high-risk" — the harder he works, the greater the damage to civilization.

The six top-level axioms in this entry are simultaneously mathematized: the anti-predation axiom is the premise that "destructive power" must have a clean source; the republican axiom of Myriad Races is the legal source of "civilizational consensus"; the collective-ascension axiom requires the strong one's effective combat power ultimately serve ascension, not personal bravado; the causal self-discipline axiom constrains procedure when the strong one calls on destructive power; the law-first axiom ensures this formula itself is a law-level definition, not modified by any protagonist halo.

Universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing/layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more higher-level infinity. Everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, so predation naturally loses meaning.

## I: What Is Civilizational Weight: From "Destructive Power" to "Effective Combat Power"

First nail the terminology. **Civilizational weight** is Heng Yuan Zhou's quantified indicator of a cultivator's actual influence in the Anchor Symbiotic Network, computed in real time by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision. It is not a moral score, not a good-person card, but a **public-compute allocation voucher** — the higher the weight, the greater the law network's public compute you can call, cross-plane channel quotas, and contracted-responsibility resource-pool share.

The old system only computes destructive power; Heng Yuan Zhou puts destructive power in the civilizational-weight formula as multiplication:

| Factor | Definition | Range |
|------|------|----------|
| Destructive power | The maximum disturbance this individual can cause to the law network in extreme cases | 0 to +∞ |
| Civilizational consensus | Myriad-Rank representatives' and Alaya Network's composite score of his power's lawfulness, direction correctness, and contribution sustainability | -1 to +1 |
| Effective combat power | Destructive power × civilizational consensus | May be negative |

The key is civilizational consensus may be negative. A predation-faction strong one has immense destructive power, but his existence is recognized by Myriad Ranks as "harm," with consensus negative; his effective combat power on the civilization ledger is negative — that is, the stronger he is, the poorer civilization becomes because of him. This is not metaphor; this is how the Alaya Network actually keeps accounts.

## II: How Civilizational Consensus Is Computed: Five Sub-Items

Civilizational consensus is not voted out, but the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision computes by five sub-items weighted:

1. **Source lawfulness (weight 25%)**: whether power comes from the Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle, with no predation residual marks;
2. **Direction correctness (weight 25%)**: whether his daily work serves Myriad-Rank symbiosis, whether he refuses running away, lying flat, and monopolizing;
3. **Contribution sustainability (weight 20%)**: whether his post performance continuously outputs civilization increment, not one-off heroism;
4. **Coverage breadth (weight 15%)**: the number of clans, planes, and life forms his work benefits;
5. **Procedural compliance (weight 15%)**: whether cross-plane and cross-time operations strictly follow causal self-discipline reporting.

Any sub-item going to zero directly drags consensus down; source lawfulness alone being negative makes consensus directly negative. This algorithm ensures: **you cannot whitewash "long-term predation" by "occasionally doing good," nor bypass "wrong direction" by "invincible combat power."**

## III: Why Multiplication Not Addition

Designing it as multiplication is Heng Yuan Zhou's actuarial choice under the law-first axiom:

- If addition, a predation-faction strong one with immense destructive power, even with very low consensus, could still rank high on total score by destructive power — this is the mathematical root of the old world's "might makes right";
- Multiplication makes consensus the "switch" of destructive power: consensus negative, and however great destructive power is only a negative asset;
- Multiplication also keeps "right direction but small power" latecomers from being buried — as long as consensus is high enough, his effective combat power linearly amplifies with power growth, and the amplification direction is civilization increment.

First-generation anchors like Lin Hao, with limited early power, but with clean source, right direction, broad coverage, and procedural compliance, kept consensus above 0.9 long-term; later his destructive power grew with realm, and effective combat power soared synchronously — not because he suddenly turned fierce, but because his "fierceness" was from the beginning recognized by Myriad Ranks and lawfully called by the law network.

## IV: How Civilizational Weight in Turn Constrains the Transcendent One

Civilizational weight is not an award, but a **rein**. After the transcendent one enters the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, his civilizational-weight panel is open to Myriad-Rank representatives:

- The Myriad-Rank republican council may periodically deliberate his weight composition; any sub-item declining for two consecutive cycles triggers a post talk;
- Weight falling below a threshold automatically downgrades his law-anchor permissions — not punishment, but preventing a direction-drifting strong one from continuing to call public compute;
- What weight rise brings is not privilege inflation, but post upgrade: you can take on larger civilizational engineering, be responsible for more planes, train more transcendent ones.

This is the mathematized expression of "the stronger the more he must work": **civilizational weight is not a reward for working more, but a voucher for being able to lawfully keep working more.** If you don't work, weight drops; weight drops, your destructive power remains but effective combat power goes to zero, equal to self-disarming.

## V: Contrast with Tang-Luo and Gu Chensha Systems

Module 2 has repeatedly argued that the Tang-Luo system's protagonist-uniqueness dependence rating is "high," and the Gu Chensha system's is "very high." From the civilizational-weight view, these two systems' illness is exactly the same: **the whole civilization's "effective combat power" is compressed into one private node.** Gu Chensha's destructive power is immense, but his civilizational consensus is missing in the Myriad-Rank dimension — Myriad Ranks do not recognize him, they fear him. Under the multiplication formula, his "effective combat power" is actually only his personal destructive power; the whole network does not endorse him, and once he has a problem, civilization has no second node to take over.

Heng Yuan Zhou's network-style transcendence, with protagonist-uniqueness dependence "very low": the Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle shares civilizational weight across tens of millions of lawful nodes, and any one node going offline, the network runs as usual. Lin Hao is not "the only protagonist"; he is "the first to run the civilizational-weight formula through" — once he runs it through, the formula opens to all beings.

## VI: Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the multiplication formula directs "destructive power" into "effective combat power," eliminating meaningless consumption of pure destructive power on civilization
- **Anti-predation**: the source-lawfulness sub-item seals predators' whitewash path at the algorithm level, and predation-faction strong ones' consensus must be negative
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' combat-power algorithm is addition (big power = awesome); Heng Yuan Zhou's is multiplication (power must be consensus-recognized to be awesome)
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the coverage-breadth sub-item explicitly lists Myriad-Rank clans and life forms in consensus computation, and clans the strong one does not serve drag down his weight
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the civilizational-weight formula itself is queryable in the Selfless Dao public library, and all beings can compute their own weight accounts
- **Universal equal infinity**: the universal equal infinite law — within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation naturally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Individual combat power = destructive power × civilizational consensus. This formula is Heng Yuan Zhou's hardest rewrite of the word "strong one." It tells all cultivators: **your destructive power is your own affair; your civilizational consensus is Myriad Ranks' affair; only when Myriad Ranks recognize your direction may your destructive power be called combat power.** The transcendent one transcends not because his destructive power is greatest, but because his civilizational consensus is highest — so high that every bit of his destructive power is simultaneously Myriad Ranks' protective power.
