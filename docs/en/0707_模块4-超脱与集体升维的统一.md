# Entry 707 | Transcendence Orthodox Definition | The Unity of Transcendence and Collective Ascension

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 76, 26

## Introduction: one person ascending is not called ascension

Old web novels' "ascension" has an unspoken sub-rule: the protagonist alone ascends, and Myriad Ranks stay in place looking up. The protagonist ascends from the mortal realm to the upper realm, from mortal to immortal, from human to god; every level up drops the civilization behind by one level. This kind of "ascension," in Heng Yuan Zhou's terminology, is not called ascension, but **disconnection**.

Among the six top-level axioms, the collective-ascension axiom sentences this disconnection-style ascension to death: **the strong do not ascend alone, but lift Myriad Ranks forward together in evolution.** This entry argues: transcendence and collective ascension are not two things, but two sides of the same thing — a person who does not lift Myriad Ranks is not himself truly transcendent; a person truly transcendent, his very existence pushes Myriad Ranks to ascend. Among them, the sixth, the universal equal infinite law, establishes the origin logic that everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power and predation loses meaning.

Universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing/layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more higher-level infinity. Everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, so predation naturally loses meaning.

## I: The Old System's "Solo Ascension" Three-Layer Illusion

Dissecting solo ascension's illusion, there are three layers:

1. **Speed illusion**: the protagonist ascends fast, as if the whole civilization follows fast. Actually the civilization's average ascension speed is dropped by the protagonist, and the gap widens;
2. **Protection illusion**: after the protagonist becomes strong he "guards" civilization, and civilization under his wings stops evolving, finally becoming a greenhouse flower;
3. **Transmission illusion**: the protagonist transmits methods to one or two disciples, and calls it "successors exist." Actually 99% of the whole civilization is not covered.

The three layers jointly manufacture an illusion: as long as the protagonist is strong enough, civilization is safe. Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is: **the stronger the protagonist while civilization does not ascend, the more fragile civilization — because the whole civilization's safety is compressed onto one person's lifespan, one person's mood, one person's judgment.** This is precisely the civilizational-risk root of protagonist-uniqueness dependence "medium" to "very high" ratings.

## II: Collective Ascension's Law Meaning: The Myriad-Rank Origin Two-Way Cycle

Collective ascension is not a moral requirement, but a law-level energy-cycle requirement. Per the Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle principle:

- Myriad-Rank origin energy is continuously generated from all beings' daily cultivation, civilizational activities, and consciousness fluctuations;
- These origin energies flow through the anchor network into a public pool, then allocated to individuals needing breakthrough, construction, or rescue;
- The strong's cultivation consumes the public pool, but their post work, method openness, and law weaving must feed back to the public pool, forming a closed loop.

A person ascending alone equals drawing from the public pool a flow far beyond his due, without feeding back. This drawing shows no short-term problem, but on a million-year scale is **civilization's blood-making system's major hemorrhage**. Heng Yuan Zhou does not allow any strong one to keep this hemorrhage state long — the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision keeps real-time accounts of each strong one's "draw-feed ratio," and draw exceeding feed continuously past threshold automatically triggers a post talk.

## III: How the Transcendent One Lifts Myriad Ranks: Three Concrete Ways

"Lifting Myriad Ranks to ascend" is not empty talk; the transcendent one realizes it through three concrete ways:

### 3.1 Method-Level Lifting

The transcendent one reduces the law-weaving methods he comprehended and are practice-validated into public versions learnable by Myriad Ranks, laying them into the Selfless Dao public library. One important task of Lin Hao's generation of anchors is translating Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm-level law understanding into basic guides usable by Sense-Condensed, Condensed, and Transformation Realm cultivators — not letting Myriad Ranks reach heaven in one step, but letting Myriad Ranks stand on his shoulders every step.

### 3.2 Resource-Level Lifting

The transcendent one, through the incremental-development post (717) and new-universe opening post (719), continuously creates new cultivation resources, new planes, new energy sources, so the Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle's total flow continuously expands. This is not dividing the stock cake, but making the cake bigger — the anti-predation axiom's "develop increment, do not consume stock" lands at this layer.

### 3.3 Post-Level Lifting

The transcendent one, through the transcendent-one training and transmission post (720), consciously trains the next generation of plane-sovereign strong ones and next generation of transcendent ones. The 20% activation strategy's backbone echelon is lifted batch by batch this way. Every transcendent one's lifetime KPI has one hard indicator: **the number of plane-sovereign strong ones he personally trained and who passed the four-dimensional assessment.**

## IV: The Identity of Transcendence and Collective Ascension

This entry's core thesis: **transcendence and collective ascension are not two things, but the same thing.**

- If you truly transcend, your Dao has already fused with cosmic-origin law, and your very existence calibrates the law network — and the direct beneficiary of calibrating the law network is all Myriad Ranks;
- If you truly lift Myriad Ranks to ascend, the flow the Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle lets through you into the public pool grows larger and larger, and your Dao becomes more stable because of this continuous inflow — this is the mechanism itself of your "more transcendent."

Those who split these two things are either running-away transcendence (transcending but not lifting others) or ascetic sacrifice (lifting others but not transcending, burning oneself dry). Heng Yuan Zhou's orthodox position: **the two must happen simultaneously.** If you do not lift Myriad Ranks, you cannot cultivate up; if you cannot cultivate up, you cannot lift Myriad Ranks. This is a closed loop under the law-first axiom, not moralizing.

## V: Connection with the Four-Stage Ascension Route

Documents 31–35 and 79 describe the four-stage ascension route (realistic foundation → technology empowerment → consciousness evolution → interstellar symbiosis); from the transcendent-one perspective it means:

- Realistic-foundation stage: few transcendent ones, mainly early anchors laying the foundation;
- Technology-empowerment stage: transcendent ones emerge in batches, with Multiplication Fruit, copy-mirror, and isolated-space technologies developed;
- Consciousness-evolution stage: transcendent ones mainly work in the consciousness domain, with Alaya Network whole-network sharing becoming normal;
- Interstellar-symbiosis stage: transcendent ones as cross-plane anchors, sending ascension results to more universes.

At every stage, the transcendent one is not a bystander, but the **main bearer of that stage's ascension task**. They are not "ascension's result," they are "ascension's engine."

## VI: Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: collective ascension turns every bit of the transcendent one's compute into Myriad Ranks' ascension speed, eliminating solo-ascension-style civilization waste
- **Anti-predation**: lifting Myriad Ranks to ascend fundamentally eliminates "drawing from the public pool without feedback" solo ascension, and the Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle continuously closes
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style ascension is "protagonist takes disciples," disciples take disciples, and the pyramid tip is always Gu Chensha; Heng Yuan Zhou-style ascension is a network, and every Myriad-Rank level up moves the whole network up
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the three lifting ways of method, resource, and post all explicitly cover 99% of Myriad-Rank clans, not human patent
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the transcendent one's ascension experience is all opened via the Selfless Dao public library, with hyper-multiverse-tier ascension paths set with no threshold
- **Universal equal infinity**: the universal equal infinite law — within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation naturally loses meaning

## Conclusion

One person ascending is not called ascension, but escape. True ascension is, after you ascend, turning back to pull Myriad Ranks up one by one. The transcendent one transcends not because he is higher than Myriad Ranks, but because his existence makes Myriad Ranks as a whole higher. **Transcendence and collective ascension, in Heng Yuan Zhou's law definition, are the same verb.**
