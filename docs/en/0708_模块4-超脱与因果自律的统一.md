# Entry 708 | Transcendence Orthodox Definition | The Unity of Transcendence and Causal Self-Discipline

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 82, 83, 87

## Introduction: being able to rewrite causality does not mean having the right to rewrite it

Old web novels' most romantic imagination of "transcendence" is that the transcendent one may shuttle time at will, rewrite history, resurrect old friends, and reset reincarnation. This imagination treats "causality" as clay, to be kneaded however one likes. Heng Yuan Zhou's causal self-discipline axiom pours cold water on this imagination: **that you can operate causality does not mean you have the right to operate it; the stronger you are, the more your causality-operation permissions must be strictly reviewed.**

The six top-level axioms in this entry center on the causal self-discipline axiom, but the other four hold the field: the anti-predation axiom prevents the transcendent one from preying on other timelines' stock under the name of "rewriting history"; the republican axiom of Myriad Races asks — who gave you the power to rewrite the whole civilization's memory? The collective-ascension axiom requires cross-plane operations serve Myriad-Rank ascension, not personal obsession; the law-first axiom nails "causal self-discipline"'s law definition stable, not loosened by any protagonist's tragic story.

Universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing/layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more higher-level infinity. Everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, so predation naturally loses meaning.

## I: The Old System's "Recklessly Rewrite Causality" Three Disasters

In the web-novel genealogy, recklessly rewriting-causality transcendent ones repeatedly manufacture three disasters:

1. **Resurrection-obsession type**: the protagonist to resurrect an old friend regresses against the river of time, triggering ancestor-killing paradoxes and event paradoxes, with the whole civilization's causal chain twisted;
2. **Purge-and-restart type**: the protagonist is disappointed with the current civilization, directly resets the timeline, writing off billions of beings' suffering — also writing off their growth;
3. **Cyclical-harvesting type**: the protagonist treats reincarnation as his own farm, making Myriad Ranks repeatedly live and die, just to draw reincarnation energy for himself.

The three disasters' common illness: **treating causality as private territory, treating beings as his own props.** The Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision in Heng Yuan Zhou prohibits these three operations at the root.

## II: Causal Self-Discipline's Four Operating Iron Laws

Per Documents 82, 83, 87, 88, causal self-discipline has four iron laws for the transcendent one:

### 2.1 Observe First Then Limitedly Intervene

Any cross-plane or cross-time operation must first complete the pure-observation stage, confirming no major risk, before entering the limited-intervention stage. During observation the transcendent one may only look, not touch; during intervention he may only touch "non-critical nodes," not the causal main chain. This blocks "regressing against time on a whim."

### 2.2 Strict Reporting System

Every cross-plane shuttle, every time regression, every law-weaving operation of the transcendent one must, before acting, submit a report to the anchor network: operation purpose, impact scope, expected causal disturbance, emergency plan. Only after reporting passes may it be executed, with execution monitored in real time by the Alaya Network. This is not distrust of the transcendent one, but **causality's impact radius far exceeds the operator's own cognition** — you think you only touched a small node, but your small node may be the key clasp of the whole civilization's causal main chain.

### 2.3 Knowledge-Return-Only

In cross-plane and cross-time operations, what may be returned is only abstract knowledge, experience, and law-weaving schemes; no matter, energy, or living beings may be returned. This blocks the predatory shortcut of "moving resources from another timeline," and also blocks "pulling people from another plane as slaves."

### 2.4 Information Purification

Before returning knowledge, the source plane's law imprints must be completely stripped, keeping only pure semantics. This prevents cross-plane law pollution — a method you bring back from some high-magic plane, if carrying that plane's law fingerprint, transplanted to this cosmos may trigger law conflict.

## III: Why Transcendence and Causal Self-Discipline Must Be Unified

Some will ask: the transcendent one has already reached the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm; why still governed by these rules? Is this not self-contradictory?

Quite the contrary. **The stronger the transcendent one, the larger his disturbance radius on causality, and the more he needs self-discipline.** A Sense-Condensed cultivator throwing one wrong punch hurts at most a few people; a Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcendent one throwing one wrong punch may twist half a cosmos's causal main chain. The greater the power, the finer self-discipline's granularity must be — this does not weaken the transcendent one, this protects him from being backlashed by his own power.

Deeper: causal self-discipline itself is part of transcendence. A truly transcendent person understands causality deeply enough to **know which causality must not be touched**. He needs no one to govern him, because he himself is the strictest governor. The Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's existence is not because the transcendent one is untrustworthy, but because **the causal chain's complexity even the transcendent one himself cannot fully predict** — he needs the network as his external brain, helping him see the part of causality he cannot see.

## IV: Causal Self-Discipline and Historical-Reverse Prevention

Causal self-discipline's most core civilizational function is **historical-reverse prevention**. Documents 82–89 as a group repeatedly argue: cross-plane and cross-time operations' greatest risk is not the operation's own technical failure, but the operation causing civilizational regression — pouring advanced civilization's knowledge backward into a backward age, or polluting an advanced civilization with backward-age rules.

The transcendent one at this layer bears a special duty (detailed in Post 716): they are the front-line reviewers of cross-plane operations. They must judge whether an operation is "knowledge return, pushing ascension" or "causal disturbance, manufacturing reverse." Judge wrong, and the whole civilization may regress several epochs; judge right, and the Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle runs healthily at cross-plane scale.

## V: The Unity of Causal Self-Discipline and Myriad-Rank Republicanism

Causal self-discipline is not an isolated rule; it is deeply bound to the republican axiom of Myriad Races. Why may a transcendent one not recklessly rewrite causality? Because **causality is co-written by Myriad Ranks.** Every choice, every suffering, every growth of billions of beings is precipitated on the causal chain. The transcendent one has no right unilaterally to rewrite this joint work — he may, only after reporting, make limited, rollbackable micro-adjustments to non-critical nodes.

This is Heng Yuan Zhou's final answer to "resurrect old friends"-type obsession: you may study it with a hundredfold deduction, simulate it with a copy-mirror, memorialize it in an isolated space, but you may not drag a dead person back on the real causal chain — because that person's death has already become part of billions of others' causality; touching him is touching billions of others' lives.

## VI: Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: causal self-discipline directs the transcendent one's compute into "precise micro-adjustment," eliminating "crude reset"-style energy waste
- **Anti-predation**: the knowledge-return-only clause blocks at the root cross-plane resource predation and population-moving shortcuts
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo strong ones may cross timelines at will by protagonist halo; Heng Yuan Zhou transcendent ones report every step
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the causal chain is co-written by Myriad Ranks, and any cross-plane operation must consider impact on 99% of clans
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: causal self-discipline's four iron laws themselves are public in the Selfless Dao public library, and all transcendent ones work by the same rules
- **Universal equal infinity**: the universal equal infinite law — within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation naturally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Transcendence is not being able to do whatever one wants; transcendence is **knowing what one cannot do**. The stronger you are, the more causal nodes you may theoretically touch; but the more transcendent you are, the clearer you are which nodes must not be touched. Causal self-discipline is not a constraint on transcendence; it is the precondition for the transcendent one's lawful existence in the cosmic law network. **Power without self-discipline is called destruction; power with self-discipline is called transcendence.**
