# Entry 709 | Transcendence Orthodox Definition | The Unity of Transcendence and the Law-First Axiom

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 97, 14, 90

## Introduction: the axiom most overlooked by web novels

Among the six top-level axioms, the law-first axiom is the least romantic, most overlooked by web novels, yet hardest. It does not directly talk good and evil, nor Myriad Ranks, nor causality; it talks only one thing: **law comes first, never retconning, never ending unfinished; setting self-consistency above plot convenience, and plot may not kidnap setting.** This axiom is especially critical under the "transcendence" theme — because old web novels' greatest unfinished-ending risk appears precisely at "the protagonist transcends": when the author can no longer write, he lets the protagonist "transcend everything," and brushes all the holes he cannot round off with one line "he has already gone beyond law."

The six top-level axioms in this entry all hold the field, but the focus is the law-first axiom: the anti-predation axiom requires law definitions themselves not shelter predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires law definitions be equal to Myriad Races; the collective-ascension axiom requires law definitions serve ascension; the causal self-discipline axiom requires law definitions stable and executable; **the law-first axiom is the meta-rule of all this — it requires the other four axioms' expressions themselves be self-consistent, stable, and infinitely expandable.**

Universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing/layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more higher-level infinity. Everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, so predation naturally loses meaning.

## I: Old Web Novels' "Transcendence = Retcon" Three Typical Forms

"The protagonist transcends" in old web novels almost equals "setting collapsed." Three typical forms:

1. **Combat-power inflation type**: the protagonist is suppressed by some enemy early, but after "transcending" later, all early combat-power yardsticks are voided; enemies weaken, teammates become trash, and the worldview is reshuffled;
2. **Rules-are-all-powerful type**: after the protagonist "transcends law," whatever rule he dislikes may be changed at will, and whatever setting he needs may temporarily pop up;
3. **Ending-nihility type**: the story reaches the end, and the protagonist "transcends to a place even the author cannot reach," with all foreshadowing, all causality, all characters cleared by one line "he is no longer in this story."

The three typical forms' common essence: **the author uses "transcendence" as an all-purpose eraser, wiping off everywhere he cannot round out.** This, in Heng Yuan Zhou's terminology, is "plot kidnapping setting," the direct root of retcon-and-unfinished-ending risk.

## II: The Law-First Axiom's Hard Constraints on Transcendence

Heng Yuan Zhou's law-first axiom imposes five hard constraints on the concept of "transcendence" itself:

### 2.1 Transcendence May Not Stand Above Law

The four words "transcending law" are unlawful in Heng Yuan Zhou's law definition. The transcendent one enters the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm — he does not stand outside law, but becomes a living node of the law network. His relation with law is **deep fusion**, not **jumping outside**. This fundamentally eliminates the writing of "the protagonist shatters law with one punch."

### 2.2 Combat-Power Yardstick Does Not Float with the Protagonist

Heng Yuan Zhou's Nine-Realm division (Sense-Condensed to Origin) plus two realms (Multiverse, Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse) is a law-level definition, and any protagonist's growth must walk within this yardstick. One may not, because some plot segment needs it, let the protagonist suddenly "break through to a new realm even the author cannot clearly explain." Realms have boundaries, breakthroughs have standards, and four-dimensional assessment has procedure — this is what setting self-consistency approaching full marks means.

### 2.3 Foreshadowing Must Be Repaid

Old web novels wipe off foreshadowing with "transcendence." Heng Yuan Zhou does not allow it: every buried law clue, every mentioned plane, every introduced clan must be actually used, actually connected, actually delivered in later civilizational engineering. The worldview may expand infinitely without collapse, but expansion's direction may only be "adding new," not "changing old."

### 2.4 The Causal Chain May Not Be Reset

Per the causal self-discipline axiom (see Entry 708), the transcendent one may not reset timelines, may not rewrite the causal main chain. This at the law-first level means: **the story may not round itself off by "doing it over."** What has happened in causality has happened, and later plot must push forward on this fact.

### 2.5 Terminology Alignment

Heng Yuan Zhou has a Terminology Dictionary (Document 90), and all documents use the same terminology. "Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm" is "Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm"; it may not suddenly in some entry be written "Hyper-Multiverse Balanced-Anchor Realm" then treated as a new concept; "civilizational weight" is "civilizational weight"; it may not be renamed as a new mechanism. Terminology stability is the linguistic basis of setting self-consistency.

## III: Why the Law-First Axiom Is Transcendence's Meta-Rule

The law-first axiom is not a "writing technique"; it is part of Heng Yuan Zhou's cosmology. Why?

Because Heng Yuan Zhou is a cosmos that **may expand infinitely without collapse**. Its law network was not written once by some author, but continuously grows with civilizational engineering, plane opening, and clan incorporation. If law definitions themselves are unstable and may drift with plot, then every expansion tears open old setting once, and the whole cosmos collapses after ten expansions.

The transcendent one in this cosmos plays precisely the role of **a living anchor of the law network**. His very existence is the personification of law stability. If the transcendent one may stand above law and rewrite setting at will, then he is not a living anchor, but a living plot-kidnapper — wherever he goes, that place's law is torn by his personal will.

So the law-first axiom's requirement of the transcendent one is: **the more transcendent you are, the more you must obey law.** Not because you lack the ability to destroy law, but because your destruction will be amplified to the whole network. You are a living anchor; you move once, and the whole network shakes three times.

## IV: Contrast with Tang-Luo and Gu Chensha Systems

Module 2 repeatedly dissects: the Tang-Luo system's protagonist-uniqueness dependence is "high," and Gu Chensha's is "very high." From the law-first axiom view, these two systems' common illness is **the protagonist is law itself** — whatever the protagonist wants to change he changes, and law follows the protagonist. Such systems' setting-self-consistency score is extremely low, because all their "law" is temporary, depending entirely on what the protagonist needs at the moment.

Heng Yuan Zhou's network-style transcendence, with protagonist-uniqueness dependence "very low": law exists before the protagonist, and the protagonist is only the first to read law and make it public. Lin Hao is not law's master; he is law's first user, first commentator, first person to translate law into words Myriad Ranks can understand. He leaves, law remains; he changes, law does not. This is "law-first."

## V: Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: stable law means all cultivators' efforts settle on the same yardstick, not voided by retconning
- **Anti-predation**: law definitions do not shelter predation, and the predation faction cannot whitewash by "temporarily changing setting"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' law is the protagonist's servant; Heng Yuan Zhou's law is the protagonist's teacher — a student may not in turn change the teacher
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: terminology, realms, and assessment are cosmos-unified, and Myriad-Rank clans compete fairly by the same law
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: law itself is the most public knowledge, open to all beings via the Selfless Dao, not drifting with any protagonist
- **Universal equal infinity**: the universal equal infinite law — within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation naturally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Transcendence is not going beyond law; transcendence is **becoming law's most faithful executor**. The stronger you are, the less right you have to change rules at will — because you move once, the whole network follows. The law-first axiom tells all potential transcendent ones: **in Heng Yuan Zhou, the words "I have transcended all rules" are themselves the greatest violation.** True transcendence is you have lived yourself into part of the rules, not standing opposite them.

In the end, the unity of transcendence and the law-first axiom described in this entry is no isolated case — its causality and energy traces are throughout filed in the Alaya Network for the record, traceable and calibratable.
