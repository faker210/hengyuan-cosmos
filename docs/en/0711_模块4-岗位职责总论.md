# Entry 711 | Hyper-Multiverse Strong Ones' Post Duties · Overview — The Transcended Ones' Perpetual-Construction System

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 17, 49, 01

## Introduction: a transcended one is not a title, but a post table

The first three groups of Module Four (701–710) have fully explained "what true transcendence is." Starting at Entry 711, we enter the operational layer: what does a true transcended one specifically do? The answer is a **nine-post table**. These nine posts are not civilizational moral kidnapping, but the physical needs of Heng Yuan Zhou's law network — the law network has nine pits that must be filled; those who fill the pits are the transcended ones.

Six top-level axioms in this entry converge into post-design principles: the anti-predation axiom requires all posts' outputs be increments, not plunder; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires posts be open to all beings and post outputs serve all beings; the collective ascension axiom requires the nine posts form a closed loop jointly driving Myriad-Rank uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires every post's operation have a reporting procedure; the law-first axiom requires the nine posts' definitions stable, infinitely scalable, and not increased or decreased for plot needs.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## II. Four Principles of Post Design

### 2.1 Perpetual-Post System

The nine posts have no concept of "retirement." After entering the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, a transcended one may work one post for one civilizational cycle then apply to rotate, but may not abandon the post itself. This stamps out lie-flat-style transcendence of "work a few hundred years then go home to retire."

### 2.2 Multi-Person-Per-Post System

Each post is jointly borne by multiple transcended ones, forming internal checks. No single transcended one may alone decide a post's major operation — this corresponds to Document 49's governance framework: four-power separation of legislation (Anchor Council), executive (Symbiotic Executive Committee), judiciary (Symbiotic Court), supervision (Anchor Supervisory Committee) applies equally at the transcended-ones level.

### 2.3 Post-Rotation System

Transcended ones rotate among posts, avoiding long-term "department interests" forming on one post. A person switching from law-maintenance post to Alaya-supervision post deepens his understanding of the law network from both "internal" and "external" perspectives. This rotation itself is cultivation — the horizontal flow of post experience feeds back into his personal Dao.

### 2.4 Performance-Public System

Each post's performance is public to Myriad-Rank representatives through the civilizational-weight panel. The Myriad-Rank Republican Council may periodically review each post's output, cost, and risk; any post whose performance declines for two consecutive cycles triggers a post interview. This turns the transcended ones' work from "closed-door cultivation" into "public service."

## III. The Nine Posts' Linkage with the Myriad-Origin Two-Way Cycle

The nine posts jointly serve one ultimate closed loop: the **Myriad-Origin two-way cycle**.

- The maintenance, supervision, and correction posts are responsible for "guarding" the cycle against predatory factions, historical reversal, and law drift;
- The guidance, training, and deliberation posts are responsible for "unclogging" the cycle, letting Myriad Ranks' origin energy flow more smoothly into the public pool;
- The increment, frontier, and cross-plane posts are responsible for "expanding" the cycle, developing new energy sources, new planes, and new cosmoses, so the cycle's total flow continuously grows.

Three post types exert three forces, stretching the cycle larger and steadier. This is the post-study expression of "perpetual construction": not some one transcended one moving, but nine posts continuously doing work in the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle, spinning faster and steadier.

## IV. The Relationship Between Posts and Protagonist-Uniqueness Dependency Degree

Module Two has repeatedly argued: old web novels' malady is excessive protagonist-uniqueness dependency. The nine-post design structurally presses this dependency to "extremely low":

- The nine posts are jointly borne by multiple transcended ones; if any one transcended one leaves, others fill in;
- Post content is all public in the Selfless-Dao public library; any capable cultivator may learn, prepare, and take over;
- Post lineage is institutionalized through the training post, not relying on some strong one "fancying whoever to pass on."

This means: **Heng Yuan Zhou's civilizational security is not bet on any one person.** Lin Hao is the first-generation anchor, but he is not irreplaceable — the successor he trains will take over him; the successor's successor will take over the successor. This is true Myriad-Rank Republicanism's landing at the transcended-ones level.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the nine posts direct every Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcended one's computing power to concrete civilizational engineering, stamping out any form of computing idling
- **Anti-predation**: post design explicitly requires incremental development and forbids stock predation; any post operation must go through Alaya-Network + multi-layer supervision audit
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style civilization is held up by one protagonist; Heng Yuan Zhou-style civilization is held up by the nine-post network — with the protagonist absent, the network still runs
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the nine posts are open to all Myriad-Rank beings; post outputs serve 99% of clans
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: post manuals, post performance, post-training materials are all public in the Selfless-Dao public library
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

A transcended one is not a title, but a post table. The nine posts mesh with each other, perpetually rotate, and perform publicly, nailing the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's living anchors onto every key node of the law network. They do not retire, do not lie flat, do not monopolize — they work. This is Heng Yuan Zhou's post-study answer to "perpetual construction."
