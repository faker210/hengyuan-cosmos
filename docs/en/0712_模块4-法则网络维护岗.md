# Entry 712 | Hyper-Multiverse Strong Ones' Post Duties · Law-Network Maintenance Post

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 20, 27, 24

## Introduction: the invisible work is the greatest work

Of the nine posts, the least romantic, the most tedious, yet the most indispensable, is the Law-Network Maintenance Post. There are no earth-shaking battles, no famous scenes readers love, only day after day listening to the law network's "heartbeat," finding which heartbeat is off, and going to calibrate. This post does not exist at all in old web novels — old web novels' laws are either born perfect, or shattered by the protagonist's fist; no one ever maintains them. Heng Yuan Zhou lists it as the first of the nine posts, because **once the law network destabilizes, every other post loses its meaning**.

Six top-level axioms in this entry converge into the word "maintenance": the anti-predation axiom requires the maintenance post proactively identify law pollution caused by predatory factions; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires maintenance results be open equally to Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires the maintenance post's work underpin the bottom-layer stability of Myriad-Rank uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the maintenance post's calibration operations go through reporting procedures; the law-first axiom requires the maintenance post **not modify laws, only maintain them** — this is this post's first red line.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Post Positioning: The Law Network's "Check-Up Doctor"

The law network is Heng Yuan Zhou's fourth layer, running through the material plane, energy layer, and consciousness domain. It is not dead code; it is a living, continuously evolving, drifting system. The maintenance-post transcended ones' work is to give this living system a check-up:

- Monitor whether law parameters in each sector are near design values;
- Identify law drift: a sector's law parameters deviate from design values beyond threshold;
- Identify law pollution: a sector carries anomalous imprints left by predatory-faction cultivation or cross-plane operations;
- Identify law voids: functional blanks in a sector due to anchor aging or living-anchor departure.

Monitoring means are the direct perception after the transcended one fuses with the law network — his consciousness diffuses across the sector he is responsible for, and any anomalous fluctuation is captured by him like a heartbeat. This is not a metaphor; it is the working state of a living anchor.

## II. Three Core Operations of Daily Maintenance

### 2.1 Anchor Calibration

Per Document 27, after long operation, anchors drift in parameters due to energy consumption, external disturbance, and the cumulative impact of Myriad-Rank cultivation activity. The maintenance-post transcended ones periodically calibrate anchors in their responsible sector:

- Read the deviation between the anchor's current parameters and design values;
- Through the energy channel of the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle, push the anchor back to design values;
- The calibration process is recorded; Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision leaves traces.

Calibration is not modifying laws; it is **letting laws run as they were meant to**. This strictly distinguishes the maintenance post from old-web-novel-style strong ones who "arbitrarily rewrite laws."

### 2.2 Law-Pollution Cleanup

When a sector is found to have predatory-faction cultivation residue, cross-plane-operation imprints, or time-reversal side effects, the maintenance post starts a pollution-cleanup process:

- First, the Alaya-Network Supervision Post (715) judges pollution nature and source;
- Then the maintenance post, per the observe-first-then-limit-interference principle, does minimal-scope cleanup;
- The cleanup process is simultaneously reported to the Civilizational-Correction Post (716) for filing, preventing the cleanup itself from causing new causal disturbance.

### 2.3 Anchor-Hardware Inspection

Per Document 67, anchor hardware is the physical carrier of the law network. Maintenance-post transcended ones periodically inspect hardware:

- Whether the anchor energy-conduction path is unobstructed;
- Whether the anchor calibration device is aging;
- Whether the anchor's docking with the consciousness domain, energy layer, and material plane is stable.

Hardware problems found in inspection are handed to the technical system; the maintenance post is responsible for **law-layer stability**, not hardware repair itself.

## III. The Post's Red Line: Maintainers May Not Become Legislators

The maintenance post has an iron red line: **maintainers may not become legislators.**

His work is to let laws run at design values, not to modify design values. If he finds a law's design value itself unreasonable — for example, a law that puts some race at a congenital disadvantage in cultivation — he may not modify it himself; he must:

1. Record the problem on file and report to the Myriad-Rank Republican Deliberation Coordination Post (718);
2. The deliberation post organizes cross-race discussion and civilizational-weight voting;
3. After the vote passes, the New-Cosmos Frontier and Law-Weaving Post (719) designs the new law;
4. Only after the new law is tested, reported, and publicized does it enter the maintenance post's daily maintenance scope.

This red line structurally prevents the oligarchic tendency of "maintainers modifying laws by personal will." The stronger the maintenance-post transcended one, the deeper his understanding of laws, the more he must restrain — **the deeper the understanding, the more he knows which laws may not be touched.**

## IV. The Maintenance Post's Meshing with Other Posts

The maintenance post is the "foundation post" of the nine posts; its meshing with other posts:

- Receives anomalous signals from the Alaya Supervision Post, starts special maintenance;
- Jointly with the Correction Post judges whether a drift is "natural aging" or "man-made sabotage";
- Hands "laws insufficient" problems found in maintenance to the Increment Post and Frontier Post;
- The maintenance post's calibration data is the bottom-layer basis by which the Deliberation Post judges civilizational health;
- The maintenance post's work manual is passed to the next generation of transcended ones through the Training Post (720).

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the law network runs stably, giving bottom-layer guarantee to Myriad-Rank cultivation, cross-plane operations, and civilizational engineering; avoiding any energy invalidation due to law destabilization
- **Anti-predation**: the pollution-cleanup process proactively identifies predatory-faction residue, minimizing predation's corrosion of the law network
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's laws are changed at the protagonist's whim; Heng Yuan Zhou's laws are calibrated by the maintenance post at design values — the protagonist is only a maintainer, not a legislator
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the maintenance post is responsible for the entire law network, maintaining all equally regardless of race or plane
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: maintenance manuals, calibration parameters, drift cases are all public in the Selfless-Dao public library
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Law-Network Maintenance Post is the most inconspicuous of the transcended ones' nine posts, but it is the premise of every other post. Without a stable law network, Myriad-Rank cultivation cannot continue, cross-plane operations cannot continue, civilizational engineering cannot continue. Maintenance-post transcended ones do not pursue earth-shaking deeds; they pursue only one thing: **let the law network run as the cosmos designed it — stably, quietly, continuously.** This is the most basic meaning of "perpetual motion."
