# Entry 713 | Hyper-Multiverse Strong Ones' Post Duties · Myriad-Rank Evolution Guidance Post

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 08, 37, 50

## Introduction: the strongest ones do the most patient work

Of the nine posts, the one that most tests a transcended one's patience is the Myriad-Rank Evolution Guidance Post. A Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm strong one's daily work is not shattering stars, not crossing cosmoses, but sitting before a Sense-Origin Realm little life, listening to him describe the energy he perceives, helping him judge which cultivation system suits him. This never happens in old web novels — the stronger the protagonist, the more those around him only cry "senior spare my life"; no one actually asks him to sit down and lecture. Heng Yuan Zhou lists it as a core post, because **the speed of Myriad-Rank uplift depends not on how strong the strongest are, but on how well latecomers are guided**.

Six top-level axioms in this entry converge into the word "guidance": the anti-predation axiom requires the guidance post proactively identify and cut off latecomers' paths of being lured by predatory factions; the republican axiom of Myriad Races is directly this post's parent law — all beings possess equal evolution qualification; the collective ascension axiom requires the guidance post transform strong ones' power into latecomers' upward channel; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the guidance post not make choices for latecomers, only provide information; the law-first axiom requires all cultivation paths used by the guidance post come from the public, stable 300-set system.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Post Positioning: Myriad Ranks' "Navigator"

The Myriad-Rank Evolution Guidance Post transcended ones' work is to help every being who enters cultivation find the cultivation path best suited to himself. This is not classroom teaching, but **personalized navigation**:

- Assess the visitor's life form (humankind, alien beast, plant spirit, planet consciousness, Cosmos-Origin Entity, etc.);
- Assess his talent inclination (energy-perception type, law-understanding type, spatial-dispatch type, consciousness-resonance type, etc.);
- Assess his environmental conditions (energy density of his plane, available resources, time flow);
- Recommend the best-matched combination path from the 300-set Origin Cultivation System;
- Design short-, mid-, and long-term cultivation goals, tracking and adjusting.

This seems simple, but actually requires the transcended one to have deep understanding of every branch of the five Myriad-Rank lineages (carbon-based matter, energy-condensation, mechanical-construction, consciousness-information, mixed-variant). A transcended one who can only teach human cultivators is unqualified for this post.

## II. Three Core Tasks of the Guidance Post

### 2.1 Path Recommendation

This is the highest-frequency daily work. A visitor comes with confusion: which set should I cultivate? Should I take Deduction-Iteration or Life-Cycle? Where is my current bottleneck?

The guidance-post transcended one does not directly give answers, but through the anchor network pulls the visitor's historical cultivation data, talent assessment report, and current bottleneck analysis, then gives **three candidate paths** with their respective risks and returns. The right of choice is the visitor's — this is the causal self-discipline axiom's landing at the guidance post: we only backflow knowledge, we do not decide for you.

### 2.2 Deviation Correction

When a cultivator goes off track — for example, starts being lured by predatory factions, starts indulging shortcut inner demons, starts showing isolated self-cultivation tendency — the guidance-post transcended one proactively intervenes:

- Identify deviation signs through Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision;
- Talk as a mentor, no criticism, no humiliation, only facts;
- Help him analyze the cost of deviation, provide feasible paths to return to the right way;
- Throughout, no forced intervention; only speak when he is willing to listen.

### 2.3 Special-Life Adaptation

Per Documents 53 and 54, strange beings have their own identification and handling procedures. Guidance-post transcended ones design exclusive cultivation paths for special life forms:

- Plant spirits: based on the Life-Cycle system, modify energy-absorption channels;
- Planet consciousness: based on the Spatial-Law Dispatch system, modify consciousness diffusion;
- Cosmos-Origin Entities: based on the Deduction-Iteration system, modify their interface with origin;
- Multiverse residual civilizations: based on cross-plane anchor construction, help them integrate into the anchor network.

This category of work best embodies the depth of the republican axiom of Myriad Races — not transforming all life by the humankind template, but **finding each kind of life its own uplift road**.

## III. The Guidance Post's Link with the 20% Activation Strategy

Document 23's 20% Activation Strategy — where does its backbone come from? From the guidance post.

The strategy goal is to activate 20% of beings into plane-transcendent strong ones. This cannot rely on natural growth — someone must proactively identify suitable latecomers, guide them up, and lift them for a stretch. The guidance-post transcended ones are the core executors of this "identify–guide–lift":

- Each year identify seed candidates from Myriad Ranks with potential to attack the Origin Realm;
- Design exclusive cultivation paths for seed candidates, match them with Multiplication-Fruit Technology and joint-contracting resources;
- Provide mental and law-level escort at key breakthrough nodes;
- The plane-transcendent strong ones cultivated then guide the next generation of seeds.

This is a **self-replicating guidance network**. The guidance-post transcended one is not guiding Myriad Ranks alone; he is training thousands of secondary guides — this is the concrete mechanism of "carrying Myriad Ranks uplift."

## IV. The Guidance Post and the Selfless Dao

All work products of guidance-post transcended ones flow back to the Selfless-Dao public library:

- Personalized path-recommendation case library (desensitized);
- Deviation-correction methodology;
- Special-life adaptation design schemes;
- 20% Activation Strategy stage summaries.

After this knowledge flows back, other guidance-post transcended ones and other cultivators may learn and use it. The guidance post thus becomes **Myriad Ranks' largest cultivation-knowledge production workshop** — it produces not manuals, but the methodology of "how to help people find manuals."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the guidance post avoids talent mismatch, letting every cultivator's talent land on the best-suited path
- **Anti-predation**: the guidance post proactively cuts off latecomers' paths of being lured by predatory factions, reducing new increments of predatory-style cultivation at the source
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style guidance is "the protagonist passes it on to whoever he fancies"; Heng Yuan Zhou-style guidance is "the anchor network guides whoever it assesses" — not depending on any one person's eye
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the guidance post explicitly covers all life forms of the five lineages; special lives have exclusive paths
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: guidance methodology, case library, special-life adaptation schemes are all public in the Selfless-Dao public library
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The strongest ones do the most patient work. This is not putting a big talent to small use; this is putting a big talent to big use — **one transcended one cultivating a hundred plane-transcendent strong ones in a lifetime contributes a hundred million times more to Myriad Ranks than he himself shattering a hundred planets.** Guidance-post transcended ones do not pursue personal fame; what they pursue is: when this generation of transcended ones retires and rotates, there are already enough new transcended ones among Myriad Ranks to take over.
