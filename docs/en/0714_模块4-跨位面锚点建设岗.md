# Entry 714 | Hyper-Multiverse Strong Ones' Post Duties · Cross-Plane Anchor Construction Post

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 28, 80, 81

## Introduction: connecting isolated islands into a continent

If the Law-Network Maintenance Post is "defending," the Cross-Plane Anchor Construction Post is "opening." Its core work is to take the cosmos's isolated planes one by one — planes not yet connected to the anchor network, planes that are law-self-consistent but cut off from the outside, even planes that have drifted in the cosmos for eons — connect them to anchors one by one, joining them into a continent. This does not exist at all in old web novels: old web novels' planes are either the protagonist's leveling maps, where he leaves after fighting; or cages ruled by villains, shattered by the protagonist's fist. No one ever thought of connecting a plane **as a living civilizational unit** into the symbiotic network.

Six top-level axioms in this entry converge into the word "onboarding": the anti-predation axiom requires the onboarding process develop increments and not plunder the onboarded plane's stock; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings in the onboarded plane gain equal evolution qualification; the collective ascension axiom requires onboarding expand the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle's coverage; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the onboarding process strictly observe-first-then-limit-interference; the law-first axiom requires post-onboarding law docking not tear apart either side's self-consistency.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Post Positioning: The "Grid-Connection Engineer" of Plane Civilizations

The Cross-Plane Anchor Construction Post transcended ones' work is to safely, completely, and low-disturbingly connect a new plane to Heng Yuan Zhou's anchor network. This is not simply "opening a portal"; it includes a whole engineering process:

- **Survey**: pure-observation survey of the target plane, mapping its physical constants, energy density, time flow, consciousness activity;
- **Assess**: judge the plane's law self-consistency, civilizational development stage, whether native predatory-faction remnants exist;
- **Negotiate**: contact the native civilization's representatives (if it already has a civilization), explain the meaning and cost of onboarding;
- **Dock**: open a minimal interface on the law barrier, deploy anchor hardware, establish energy channels;
- **Fuse**: let the plane's origin energy join the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle, letting its beings begin enjoying the Selfless-Dao public library;
- **Trace**: the whole process is filed in Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision, auditable at any time.

## II. Six Iron Rules of the Onboarding Process

Cross-plane onboarding is a high-risk operation; this post has six iron rules:

1. **Observe before interfere**: the survey stage completes at least three full cycles of pure observation, without any interference;
2. **Do not disturb native causality**: the onboarding point is chosen at the edge of the native civilization's causal main chain, avoiding disturbance to its history;
3. **Do not plunder native stock**: after onboarding, only establish a two-way energy cycle, not draw out the native origin stock;
4. **Respect native self-determination**: if the native civilization refuses onboarding, do not force grid connection; only retain an observation channel;
5. **Law docking does not tear**: at onboarding, do law-fingerprint comparison; design a law-compatibility layer at the interface;
6. **Report and trace throughout**: every step of operation goes through causal self-discipline reporting; Alaya Network leaves traces.

## III. Why Onboarding Is a Key Link of Perpetual Construction

Cross-plane onboarding is the core means by which the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle expands total flow. Heng Yuan Zhou's cosmos keeps expanding — new planes, new cosmoses, new law regions keep emerging. If these newly emerging planes are not onboarded, they become **islands of the law network**: beings on the island live and die in isolation; energy on the island entropically dissipates in isolation.

Onboarding-post transcended ones connect these islands one by one into the continent, bringing three increments:

1. **Life increment**: billions of beings on the island gain cultivation paths, cultivation resources, and cross-plane channels;
2. **Energy increment**: the island's origin energy begins flowing into the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle, enlarging the public pool;
3. **Law increment**: the island's unique law combination, after compatibility-layer processing, becomes new material for the law network.

This is "develop increments, do not consume stock" landing at cross-plane scale — **onboarding is not possession, but symbiosis**.

## IV. Difference from the New-Cosmos Frontier Post

The Cross-Plane Anchor Construction Post (714) and the New-Cosmos Frontier and Law-Weaving Post (719) are often confused. The difference:

| | Cross-Plane Anchor Construction Post | New-Cosmos Frontier and Law-Weaving Post |
|---|---------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Work object | Planes that already exist | New cosmoses that do not yet exist and need weaving |
| Core action | Onboard, compatibilize, grid-connect | Design, weave, test |
| Risk type | Docking risk with native civilization | Self-consistency risk of new-law weaving |
| Output | Expanded Myriad-Rank coverage | Newly added law material library |

The two posts often cooperate: the Frontier Post first tests new laws in new cosmoses; the Construction Post then connects mature new laws into existing planes.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: connecting isolated planes' energy and life into the cycle avoids their entropic dissipation on the island
- **Anti-predation**: six iron rules explicitly forbid drawing out native stock and forced grid connection; onboarding is invitation, not occupation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style planes are the protagonist's leveling maps, left after fighting; Heng Yuan Zhou-style planes are Myriad Ranks' homes, left after onboarding
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the onboarding process explicitly covers every discovered plane, regardless of its civilizational stage, racial composition, or law combination
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: post-onboarding cultivation paths, law-compatibility layer, native-civilization knowledge all flow back to the Selfless-Dao public library
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Cross-Plane Anchor Construction Post is the frontline engineering post of Heng Yuan Zhou's territorial expansion. It does not pursue conquest; it pursues connection — joining islands drifting in the cosmos one by one into a continent of Myriad-Rank symbiosis. Every plane onboarded adds a water source to the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle; every plane onboarded adds a fulfillment of the republican axiom of Myriad Races. **This post's KPI is not how many enemies were shattered, but how many planes were onboarded.**
