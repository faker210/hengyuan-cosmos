# Entry 715 | Hyper-Multiverse Strong Ones' Post Duties · Alaya-Network Supervision Post

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 21, 29, 49

## Introduction: the strongest eyes watch our own people

Of the nine posts, the most controversial is the Alaya-Network Supervision Post. Because what it does is "monitor" — monitoring every cultivator's consciousness, behavior, and causality. In old web novels, this role is usually the villain: an omniscient, omnipotent "Heavenly Dao" watching the protagonist, striking him with lightning whenever he stirs. Heng Yuan Zhou sets it as one of the nine posts, personally held by transcended ones, because **supervision is not distrust of Myriad Ranks; it is protection of Myriad Ranks — especially protection of the transcended ones themselves**.

Six top-level axioms in this entry converge into the word "supervision": the anti-predation axiom requires the supervision post proactively identify predatory factions' lurking and disguise; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires supervision power itself also be supervised, not becoming a new oligarchy; the collective ascension axiom requires supervision data serve uplift decisions, not suppression; the causal self-discipline axiom is the supervision post's own code of conduct; the law-first axiom requires supervision algorithms themselves be public, auditable, and not privately modifiable by any one person.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Post Positioning: The Consciousness Domain's "Public Auditor"

The Alaya Network is Heng Yuan Zhou's consciousness-domain layer, carrying every Myriad-Rank cultivator's consciousness traces, cultivation records, causal operations, and public knowledge. The supervision-post transcended ones' work is not watching what Myriad Ranks think, but **auditing what Myriad Ranks do**:

- Track cultivators' energy flow: whether there is anomalous predation, whether there is energy of unknown origin flowing in;
- Track cross-plane operations: whether they are reported per causal self-discipline, whether they are within the reported scope;
- Track civilizational-weight computation: whether it is accurate, whether it has been tampered with, whether it reflects real contribution;
- Track consciousness-domain data: whether there is knowledge monopolization, darknet circulation, or privacy violation;
- Track the other eight posts: whether other transcended ones are working per post norms, whether there is post risk.

The supervision-post transcended ones' own operations are likewise recorded by the Alaya Network and audited by the Anchor Supervisory Committee. **The supervised are also supervisors; this is this post's first design principle.**

## II. Three Core Tasks of the Supervision Post

### 2.1 Anomaly Identification

Through Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision, supervision-post transcended ones identify three categories of anomalies:

- **Predation anomaly**: a cultivator's internal energy shows non-normal inflow; other cultivators around him show anomalous energy loss;
- **Causal anomaly**: a cross-plane operation was not reported; a timeline shows unreasonable disturbance; a causal main chain is distorted;
- **Weight anomaly**: a cultivator's civilizational weight seriously mismatches his actual contribution; data tampering may exist.

After anomaly identification, the supervision post does not directly enforce, but hands evidence to the symbiotic court (Document 48), letting the judicial system handle it per procedure. The supervision post is an auditor, not a police officer.

### 2.2 Consensus-Degree Audit

Every candidate transcended one's and every plane-transcendent strong one's civilizational consensus degree (see Entry 706) is audited by supervision-post transcended ones:

- Cross-check the raw data of the five sub-items (source legitimacy, direction correctness, contribution sustainability, coverage breadth, procedural compliance);
- Identify whether data has been manipulated, whether structural bias exists;
- Audit results serve as the Anchor Council's basis for deliberating transcended-one appointments.

### 2.3 Data Security

Consciousness-domain data is Myriad Ranks' public wealth. Supervision-post transcended ones are responsible for:

- Preventing consciousness-domain data from being privately extracted or moved cross-plane;
- Preventing consciousness-domain data from being monopolized by some post or clan;
- Preventing consciousness-domain data from being used for personality cult or political manipulation.

This category of work does not exist at all in old web novels — old web novels' "Heavenly Dao" only punishes the protagonist, does not protect Myriad Ranks' data rights.

## III. The Supervision Post's Meshing with Other Posts

The supervision post is the nine posts' "immune system"; its relationship with other posts:

- Receives law-pollution clues submitted by the Maintenance Post, doing consciousness-domain-level source tracing;
- Jointly with the Correction Post judges whether an anomaly is natural aging or man-made sabotage;
- Does ethical audit on the Guidance Post's and Training Post's work, preventing "mentor" from becoming "master";
- Does security audit on the Increment Post's and Frontier Post's new manuals, preventing new manuals from being abused;
- Does legality audit on the Deliberation Post's decisions, preventing Myriad-Rank Republicanism from becoming majority tyranny.

Supervision-post transcended ones do not participate in concrete construction, but their audit opinions affect all other posts' resource allocation.

## IV. Why Supervisors Must Be Transcended Ones, Not Machines

Some may ask: the Alaya Network itself is a system; why still need transcended ones to supervise? Why not fully automate?

The answer is: **the consciousness domain's complexity exceeds algorithmic boundaries.** A cultivator's consciousness struggling on the edge of predation, the causal consequences of a cross-plane operation, the ethical implications of a new manual — these all require transcended ones of the same realm and understanding to judge. Machines may identify "anomalous energy flow," but only transcended ones may judge whether this anomaly is "straying from the path" or "malicious predation."

This also means: supervision-post transcended ones bear the **highest ethical responsibility**. Every judgment they make may decide another cultivator's fate. Therefore supervision-post transcended ones' ethical review standards are the strictest of all posts — they themselves must be those with the cleanest source, straightest direction, and broadest coverage.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the supervision post identifies and blocks predation anomalies, avoiding Myriad Ranks' origin stock being secretly drawn out
- **Anti-predation**: the supervision post is the predatory faction's natural enemy at the consciousness-domain level; predators may fool others, but may not fool the Alaya Network
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo-style "Heavenly Dao" is the protagonist's private tool; Heng Yuan Zhou-style Alaya supervision is Myriad Ranks' public audit
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: supervision treats every cultivator equally; transcended ones themselves are also within supervision scope
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: supervision algorithms, audit processes, historical cases are all public in the Selfless-Dao public library, auditable by all beings
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Alaya-Network Supervision Post is the least popular of the nine posts, but the most indispensable. Without it, the other eight posts would slowly drift off course — the Maintenance Post might become law-modification, the Guidance Post might become mind control, the Increment Post might become predatory development. Supervision-post transcended ones are the external compass that keeps every other post on direction. **They do not build, but they keep building from going wrong.**
