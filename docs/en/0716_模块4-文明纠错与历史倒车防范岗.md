# Entry 716 | Hyper-Multiverse Strong Ones' Post Duties · Civilizational-Correction and Historical-Reversal-Prevention Post

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 82, 84, 89

## Introduction: the greatest fear is not the enemy, but walking backward ourselves

Of the nine posts, the one with the deepest historical responsibility is the Civilizational-Correction and Historical-Reversal-Prevention Post. Old web novels never worry about "civilizational regression" — the protagonist cheats all the way; civilization only advances, never retreats. But Heng Yuan Zhou's historical view is sober: **civilization does not necessarily advance; it may, due to cross-plane operational mistakes, due to predatory factions rekindling, due to a generation of strong ones collectively lying flat, regress by several epochs.** This post exists to, before, during, and after regression occurs, identify in time, brake in time, correct in time.

Six top-level axioms in this entry converge into the word "correction": the anti-predation axiom requires identifying whether predatory factions are reviving in civilization; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires correction not become majority tyranny, not cleanse dissent in the name of correction; the collective ascension axiom requires correction's goal be pulling civilization back onto the uplift track; the causal self-discipline axiom is the correction post's own work procedure — correction itself may not disturb causality; the law-first axiom requires correction schemes not violate the existing law network.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Post Positioning: Civilization's "Historical Doctor"

The correction-post transcended ones' work is not choosing direction for Myriad Ranks, but **diagnosing whether civilization is currently deviating from the uplift track**. They long-track a set of civilizational-health indicators:

- Whether the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle's total flow keeps growing;
- Whether 99% of clans' cultivation coverage meets standards;
- Whether plane-transcendent strong ones' generational replacement is normal;
- Whether the Selfless-Dao public library's manual-iteration speed is healthy;
- Whether cross-plane operations' reporting compliance rate stays high;
- Whether predatory-faction remnants' activity across civilization's layers is anomalous.

If any indicator declines for two consecutive cycles, the correction post starts a special investigation: natural fluctuation? external shock? or a systemic internal problem?

## II. Three Typical Forms of Historical Reversal

Correction-post transcended ones have repeatedly seen three reversals across the long river of history:

### 2.1 Knowledge-Backflow Type

A cross-plane operation backflows advanced-civilization knowledge into a backward era, causing the backward era's productivity and cognitive level to disconnect, and social structure to collapse. This is the accident the archaeological protocol most guards against — Documents 84 and 85 as a group exist to prevent it.

### 2.2 Strong-One-Restoration Type

A generation of transcended ones collectively leaves post (flees, lies flat, monopolizes); predatory-faction remnants from the old order seize the chance to restore, dragging Myriad Ranks back into the predatory cultivation system. This is the most realistic dangerous consequence of the pseudo-transcendence critiqued in Entries 702–704.

### 2.3 Causal-Distortion Type

A cross-plane operation disturbs the causal main chain, causing a civilization's historical memory to fault, heroes become villains, benefactors become enemies. This distortion is the most concealed — beings inside it feel no regression; instead they feel they are advancing.

The correction post's core work is to identify signs before these three reversals truly occur.

## III. Three-Level Response Mechanism for Correction

By risk level, the correction post has three levels of response:

### 3.1 Sign Level: Early Warning

Indicators anomalous but no actual harm yet. The correction post:

- Sends early warning to relevant posts;
- Organizes cross-post seminars to analyze root causes;
- Proposes improvement schemes, submits to the Anchor Council for deliberation.

### 3.2 Accident Level: Intervention

Reversal has occurred; Myriad Ranks have already been harmed. The correction post:

- Starts the observe-first-then-limit-interference process;
- Designs minimal-intervention schemes, touching only key nodes;
- The intervention process is monitored throughout by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision;
- After intervention, reassesses civilizational health, confirming reversal has been braked.

### 3.3 Catastrophe Level: Rollback

Reversal has distorted the causal main chain; normal intervention cannot restore. The correction post:

- Under Myriad-Rank Republican Council authorization, starts the causal-rollback procedure;
- The rollback scheme must be jointly signed by multiple transcended ones;
- The rollback process is public to Myriad Ranks, every step auditable;
- After rollback, does a full causal-chain check, ensuring no residual distortion.

## IV. Why Correction May Not Be Decided by a Single Transcended One

The correction post's most sensitive design is: **no single transcended one has the right to start correction alone.** The reason is simple: who judges "what is reversal"? Who judges "what is normal civilizational fluctuation"? If judgment rests in one transcended one's hands, he may define every change he dislikes as "reversal," locking civilization into his personal preferred state.

Therefore every start of the correction post must:

- Be based on objective data from Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision;
- Be jointly judged by multiple transcended ones across posts;
- Be deliberated and approved by the Myriad-Rank Republican Council;
- Be audited throughout by the Anchor Supervisory Committee.

This set of procedure looks slow, but it guarantees correction does not become new oppression.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: identifying reversal in time avoids Myriad Ranks consuming billions of years of accumulated civilizational fruits in regression
- **Anti-predation**: the biggest beneficiary of reversal is always predatory factions; the correction post structurally cuts off predatory-faction restoration
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style civilization, once regressed, has one protagonist decide how to rescue; Heng Yuan Zhou-style civilization's reversal is jointly decided by cross-post, cross-race, cross-supervision procedure
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: correction's object is civilization as a whole; correction decisions must involve Myriad-Rank representatives, not be unilaterally decided by some clan
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: correction cases, reversal-identification indicators, rollback procedures are all public in the Selfless-Dao public library
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Civilization does not automatically advance. It tires, gets lost, walks backward due to strong ones' dereliction. The correction-post transcended ones are those who timely pull the steering wheel when civilization may take a wrong turn. They do not create new directions; they only ensure civilization stays on the right direction. **This post is unromantic, but it is the last insurance that Myriad Ranks' billions of years of walking are not wasted.**
