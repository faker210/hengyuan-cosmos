# Entry 717 | Hyper-Multiverse Strong Ones' Post Duties · Increment-Development and Resource-Creation Post

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 10, 61, 04

## Introduction: if no new mine is opened, we can only divide the old cake

Of the nine posts, the most "outward-facing" is the Increment-Development and Resource-Creation Post. Its core proposition is: **the anti-predation axiom says "develop increments, do not consume stock" — then where do increments come from?** The answer is this post. It does not maintain existing laws, does not guide existing Myriad Ranks, does not audit existing data — it creates new cultivation resources, new manual systems, new civilizational engineering, letting the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle's total flow continuously expand. This almost does not exist in old web novels: old web-novel strong ones all divide the cake; no one truly makes a new cake.

Six top-level axioms in this entry converge into the word "increment": the anti-predation axiom is this post's parent law — stock predation is forbidden, so someone must develop increments; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires newly developed resources be equally open to all beings; the collective ascension axiom requires increments invested to drive whole uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires new development not disturb existing causality; the law-first axiom requires newly developed products be compatible with the existing law network.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Post Positioning: Myriad Ranks' "New-Cake Baker"

The Increment-Development Post transcended ones' work is to develop new flow sources for the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle. Specifically three fronts:

### 1.1 New Energy-Source Development

Heng Yuan Zhou's cosmos keeps expanding; new planes, new cosmoses, new law regions keep emerging. Increment-Development Post transcended ones:

- Survey newly emerged planes' energy characteristics;
- Design schemes to safely connect new-plane energy into the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle;
- Develop new energy-conversion technologies, converting energy that cannot be directly used into cultivation-usable energy.

The Multiplication Fruit (Documents 16, 61) is a classic case of increment development — technologizing the One Piece world's multiplication ability into hundred-fold deduction amplification is itself a successful increment development.

### 1.2 New-Manual Iteration

The 300-set Origin Cultivation System is not finalized. Increment-Development Post transcended ones:

- Identify existing systems' bottlenecks in practice;
- Design new manual branches and new combination paths;
- Verify new branches' safety through hundred-fold deduction;
- After testing passes, add new branches to the 300-set system public library.

This iteration lets the 300-set system evolve forever — it is not a fixed "manual list," but a living, continuously growing cultivation-knowledge base.

### 1.3 New Civilizational-Engineering Design

Cultivation is not only an individual affair. Increment-Development Post transcended ones also design civilizational-level engineering:

- New anchor-hardware schemes;
- New cross-plane communication protocols;
- New joint-contracting allocation algorithms;
- New symbiotic-education curriculum systems.

Every civilizational engineering landing pushes Myriad Ranks' uplift ceiling up one layer.

## II. Why Increments Are Perpetual Construction's Engine

Of the nine posts, the Maintenance, Supervision, Correction, and Deliberation posts are all "preservation" posts — they keep existing civilization from collapsing. The Guidance, Cross-Plane, and Training posts are "connection" posts — they connect Myriad Ranks more tightly. **Only the Increment-Development Post and the New-Cosmos Frontier Post (719) are truly "production" posts** — they create new things.

The key to perpetual construction being "perpetual" is here: if there is only preservation and connection, no production, the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle's total flow will one day hit a ceiling, then stagnate, then shrink, then regress. The Increment-Development Post is the one who continuously pushes the ceiling up. He pushes once; Myriad Ranks have more space; pushes again; more space. This "push the ceiling" action never stops, and perpetual construction truly holds.

## III. The Difference Between Increment Development and Predatory Development

Increment development is not "digging new mines everywhere." It is fundamentally different from predatory development:

| | Increment Development | Predatory Development |
|---|-----------------------|------------------------|
| Goal | Expand Myriad-Origin two-way cycle total flow | Grab others' stock into own hands |
| Method | Develop new energy sources, new manuals, new engineering | Devour, monopolize, exploit |
| Consequence | Myriad Ranks benefit whole | The strong get richer, the poor get poorer |
| Causal impact | Add new flow, do not disturb old causality | Draw out stock, distort old causality |
| Alaya record | Positive contribution | Predation record |

Increment-Development Post transcended ones must constantly remind themselves: **we are making a new cake, not grabbing the cake.** Once some development project begins to "consume stock" rather than "create increments," Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision auto-issues an early warning, requiring the project to pause and be reassessed.

## IV. Division of Labor with the New-Cosmos Frontier Post

The Increment-Development Post and the New-Cosmos Frontier Post (719) closely cooperate, but with different divisions:

- The Increment-Development Post develops new resources, new manuals, new engineering **within existing cosmoses**;
- The New-Cosmos Frontier Post weaves new laws in cosmoses **not yet existing**;
- New laws tested by the Frontier Post are handed to the Increment-Development Post to design into usable manual branches;
- Existing-law insufficiency discovered by the Increment-Development Post is handed to the Frontier Post to design new supplementary laws.

The two posts form a closed loop of "raise demand — frontier-verify — land and apply."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: increment development lets every new unit of computing power directly convert into Myriad-Rank-usable resources, not idle
- **Anti-predation**: increment development is the anti-predation axiom's positive practice — since we cannot grab stock, we make a new cake
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style strong ones divide the cake; Heng Yuan Zhou-style strong ones bake the cake; the former makes Myriad Ranks involute; the latter makes Myriad Ranks grow
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: newly developed manuals, energy, and engineering are all open to 99% of clans through the Selfless Dao
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: new manual branches, new energy-conversion technologies, new civilizational-engineering schemes all enter the library, no private hoarding
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Increment-Development Post is perpetual construction's engine. It is unromantic, undramatic, without protagonist aura; it simply, day after day, delivers new cultivation resources, new manual branches, and new civilizational engineering to Myriad Ranks. **Precisely because of such people, the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle may spin larger and larger, and Heng Yuan Zhou may truly be "perpetual."**
