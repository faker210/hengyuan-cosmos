# Entry 718 | Hyper-Multiverse Strong Ones' Post Duties · Myriad-Rank Republican Deliberation Coordination Post

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 03, 40, 77

## Introduction: the strongest mouth is used for the hardest persuasion

Of the nine posts, the one that most needs "speaking" is the Myriad-Rank Republican Deliberation Coordination Post. A Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm strong one's daily work is not solving problems with laws, but **solving problems through dialogue** — negotiating the disagreements among humankind, alien beasts, plant spirits, mechanical clans, consciousness clans, mixed-variant clans, one sentence at a time. This does not exist at all in old web novels: old web-novel strong ones resolve disagreements by fighting; once you win, the disagreement is gone. Heng Yuan Zhou lists it as a core post, because **disagreements may not be shattered; they may only be negotiated.**

Six top-level axioms in this entry converge into the word "republican": the republican axiom of Myriad Races is directly this post's parent law; the anti-predation axiom requires that in coordination the strong may not press down the weak; the collective ascension axiom requires coordination's ultimate goal be Myriad-Rank joint uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires coordination not substitute for each clan's own decisions; the law-first axiom requires coordination results conform to the constitution, not break the legal framework through negotiation skill.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Post Positioning: Myriad Ranks' "Chief Mediator"

The Deliberation Coordination Post transcended ones' work is cross-race, cross-plane, cross-civilizational-stage mediation and consensus crystallization:

- Mediate resource disputes between two clans: two clans sharing one plane, how is resource allocation decided?
- Mediate integration conflicts between a newly onboarded plane and old civilization: how are the new plane's customs and the old civilization's rules compatible?
- Mediate Myriad-Rank internal disagreement over major decisions: a cross-cosmos frontier scheme that Clan A supports and Clan B opposes, how to coordinate?
- Prepare pre-session work for the Anchor Council: sort out Myriad-Rank representatives' demands, put possible consensus and disagreement points on the table in advance.

This requires the transcended one not only strong, but also **able to listen, able to explain, able to endure**. He must, before a race that completely does not speak his logic, patiently hear their three-cycle statement, then explain his scheme back in language they may accept.

## II. Three Core Tasks of the Coordination Post

### 2.1 Dispute Mediation

This is the highest-frequency daily work. When disagreements over resources, laws, or paths arise between two clans, two posts, or two planes, the coordination-post transcended one intervenes:

- Hear both sides' statements separately, without bias;
- Organize both sides' core demands, bottom lines, and compromise ranges into a consensus document;
- Propose a compromise scheme both sides may accept;
- The mediation process is fully traced, as reference for future similar disputes.

### 2.2 Consensus Crystallization

Major civilizational decisions — such as whether to open a new round of 20% Activation Strategy, whether to onboard a controversial plane, whether to start a new-cosmos frontier — require Myriad-Rank representatives' consensus. The coordination-post transcended one:

- Translate complex decision options into language each clan may understand;
- Organize cross-race hearings, simulated deductions, risk assessments;
- Make the consensus-crystallization process public, so all beings may follow.

### 2.3 Boundary Guarding

The coordination-post transcended ones also have a hidden task: **guard the boundaries of Myriad-Rank Republicanism**. When some clan tries to use deliberation procedure to oppress another clan; when some post tries to bypass deliberation procedure and decide alone; when some extraordinary individual tries to impose his preference on Myriad Ranks — the coordination-post transcended one steps forward, calling a halt per constitutional procedure.

## III. Why Coordination Must Be Done by Transcended Ones

Why may the Deliberation Coordination Post not be held by ordinary politicians? Why must it be a transcended one?

Three reasons:

1. **Transcended ones are impartial**: he has reached the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm; his personal interests are already untied from any clan's interests; he has no motive to favor either side;
2. **Transcended ones see far**: his time-perception scale spans multiple civilizational cycles, able to see long-term interests behind short-term disputes;
3. **Transcended ones have credibility**: his civilizational weight comes from Myriad-Rank recognition; Myriad Ranks trust his judgment.

These three make the coordination-post transcended one the scarcest public property among Myriad Ranks — **he is not any clan's representative; he is Myriad Ranks' common translator.**

## IV. The Coordination Post's Meshing with Other Posts

The coordination post is the nine posts' "adhesive":

- Receives law problems submitted by the Maintenance and Correction posts, translating technical problems into deliberation language;
- Receives talent-allocation problems submitted by the Guidance and Training posts, coordinating each clan's demand for talent;
- Receives new-project schemes submitted by the Increment and Frontier posts, organizing Myriad-Rank representatives to deliberate;
- Feeds Council decision results back to the Supervision Post as audit basis;
- Feeds lessons learned in coordination back to the Training Post, cultivating the next generation of coordinators.

Without the coordination post, the other eight posts fight alone; with the coordination post, the nine posts become one machine.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: consensus crystallization lets Myriad Ranks' computing power be used in a common direction, not consumed in internal friction
- **Anti-predation**: the coordination process strictly forbids the strong pressing down the weak; forbids using force to replace negotiation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style disagreements are resolved by the protagonist beating people into submission; Heng Yuan Zhou-style disagreements are resolved by Myriad Ranks negotiating — beaten people are not persuaded; negotiated people are
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the coordination post explicitly serves every clan; small clans, newly onboarded clans, special-life clans all have representative rights
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: coordination cases, deliberation procedures, consensus documents are all public in the Selfless-Dao public library
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Deliberation Coordination Post is the "softest" of the nine posts, but it is the key to the nine posts truly running. It lets Myriad Ranks not be forcibly kneaded together by the law network, but choose to walk together. **Myriad-Rank Republicanism is not a slogan; it is one patient negotiation after another — and the person with the most weight at the negotiation table is the transcended one.**

In the end, the Myriad-Rank Republican Deliberation Coordination Post described in this entry is no isolated case — its causal and energetic traces are throughout filed in the Alaya Network, traceable and correctable.
