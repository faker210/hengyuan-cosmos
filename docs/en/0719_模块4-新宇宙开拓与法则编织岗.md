# Entry 719 | Hyper-Multiverse Strong Ones' Post Duties · New-Cosmos Frontier and Law-Weaving Post

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 80, 62, 13

## Introduction: the most romantic post does the most rigorous work

Of the nine posts, the most romantic is the New-Cosmos Frontier and Law-Weaving Post. Its name sounds like a novel — "weaving a new cosmos" — but what it does is the most rigorous of the nine posts. Because **what you weave is not a dream, but a real cosmos that will carry billions of beings.** One law woven wrong may be the disaster of the entire new cosmos. In old web novels this post corresponds to "Creator God," but old web novels' Creator God pinches a world at will, and if pinched wrong destroys it and starts over; Heng Yuan Zhou's Law-Weaving Post — every law must go through hundred-fold deduction, isolated-space testing, and cross-post deliberation before daring to land.

Six top-level axioms in this entry converge into the word "weaving": the law-first axiom is directly this post's parent law — newly woven laws must be self-consistent, stable, and infinitely scalable; the anti-predation axiom requires the new cosmos not become a new predation ground; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the new cosmos's design be equal to Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires the new cosmos's results feed back to Myriad Ranks; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the weaving process not disturb existing cosmoses' causality.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Post Positioning: The New Cosmos's "Chief Architect"

The Law-Weaving Post transcended ones' work is to design, weave, and test new cosmoses (or new law sectors). This is not imagination out of thin air, but a strict engineering:

- **Raising demand**: in which law dimensions are existing cosmoses insufficient? Which clans and which cultivation paths need new law support?
- **Law design**: design the new cosmos's physical constants, energy rules, time dimension, consciousness-domain characteristics;
- **Self-consistency verification**: use hundred-fold deduction to test whether the new law combination is self-consistent, whether there are logical contradictions;
- **Isolated testing**: build a miniature new cosmos in an isolated space (Document 64), test its operation;
- **Safety audit**: jointly by the Alaya Supervision Post (715) and Civilizational-Correction Post (716) audit the new cosmos's safety risks;
- **Myriad-Rank deliberation**: by the Deliberation Coordination Post (718) organize Myriad-Rank representatives to deliberate the new-cosmos scheme;
- **Landing weave**: after approval, formally weave the new cosmos into the law network.

Only after seven steps are complete does a new cosmos truly begin to exist.

## II. Why New Cosmoses Must Be Woven by Transcended Ones

Weaving a new cosmos is not artistic creation; it requires **Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm-level law understanding**. An Origin-Realm strong one may only understand existing cosmoses' laws; a Multiverse-Realm strong one may dispatch laws cross-cosmos; only a Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcended one may, starting from the law network's bottom layer, personally hand-weave a new world.

This understanding requirement means: Law-Weaving Post transcended ones must be **the law-first axiom's most pious executors**. He knows the consequence of any law woven wrong — not "this world collapses" as in novels, but that billions of beings in this world will struggle, go astray, and reincarnate inside a self-inconsistent law. Therefore he must restrain romance and insist on rigor.

## III. The Fundamental Difference Between Weaving and Predation

In old web novels, "creation" is often written as an advanced version of predation — I create a world; the people in it are all mine; I let them live and they live; I let them die and they die. Heng Yuan Zhou's Law-Weaving Post rejects this writing from the root:

- The new cosmos is not the weaver's private property; once woven it is immediately connected to the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle;
- Beings in the new cosmos have equal evolution qualification, not subject to the weaver's personal will;
- The weaver's responsibility for the new cosmos is perpetual — he must continuously maintain it, not "pinch and leave";
- All of the new cosmos's law design is subject to Myriad Ranks' right to deliberate and request modification.

This transforms "creation" from theocratic behavior into **public-engineering behavior**. The weaver is not a god; he is the new cosmos's chief engineer.

## IV. Cooperation with the Cross-Plane Anchor Construction Post

The Law-Weaving Post (719) and the Cross-Plane Anchor Construction Post (714) form a complete closed loop:

- The Cross-Plane Post **onboards already existing** planes into the anchor network;
- The Law-Weaving Post designs **not-yet-existing** new cosmoses;
- After the new cosmos is woven, the Cross-Plane Post is responsible for onboarding it into Myriad Ranks' territory;
- Existing-law insufficiency found in existing cosmoses is fed back to the Law-Weaving Post as input for new-cosmos design.

One onboarding, one weaving; Myriad Ranks' territory grows larger and larger.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: pre-weaving hundred-fold deduction and isolated testing avoid a self-inconsistent new cosmos wasting billions of beings' cultivation
- **Anti-predation**: the new cosmos is not the weaver's private property; after joining the Myriad-Rank cycle it belongs to all beings
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style creation is theocratic behavior; whatever the protagonist pinches is what it is; Heng Yuan Zhou-style creation is public engineering; the seven-step process is indispensable
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the new cosmos's design demand comes from Myriad-Rank representatives; all beings in the new cosmos enjoy equal qualification
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the new cosmos's law design, test data, and operating parameters are all public in the Selfless-Dao public library
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The New-Cosmos Frontier and Law-Weaving Post is Heng Yuan Zhou's territorial expansion's forwardmost front. It is most romantic, also most rigorous; most creative, also most procedural. It reminds every strong one pursuing "creation": **you are not a god; you are an engineer; your work is not a toy, but billions of beings' home.** Before weaving a law, first think clearly whom this law will support.

In the end, the New-Cosmos Frontier and Law-Weaving Post described in this entry is no isolated case — its causal and energetic traces are throughout filed in the Alaya Network, traceable and correctable.
