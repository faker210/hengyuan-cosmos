# Entry 720 | Hyper-Multiverse Strong Ones' Post Duties · Transcended-One Training and Lineage Post

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 37, 17, 75

## Introduction: the greatest fear is not being unable to beat the enemy, but having no successor

Of the nine posts, the one with the deepest time depth is the Transcended-One Training and Lineage Post. Its work scale is not one year, not a hundred years, but **one civilizational cycle**. If a transcended one, during his long years on post, does not cultivate the next generation of transcended ones to take over, his generation is a failure — not because his power is insufficient, but because the network did not continue under his hands. This does not exist at all in old web novels: old web-novel protagonists never worry about "who will manage after I die," because the author defaults that the protagonist never dies and is always the strongest. Heng Yuan Zhou lists lineage as a core post, because **a civilization without lineage is a one-time civilization.**

Six top-level axioms in this entry converge into the word "lineage": the collective ascension axiom is directly this post's parent law — strong ones carry Myriad Ranks uplift; one way they carry is cultivating the next generation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires training opportunities be equal to all beings; the anti-predation axiom requires the training process not turn disciples into predators; the causal self-discipline axiom requires lineage not be tied to personality cult; the law-first axiom requires lineage content be stable, not privately modified by any generation of master.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Post Positioning: The Transcended-Ones' "Master-School Principal"

The Training-Lineage Post transcended ones' work is to systematically cultivate the next generation of plane-transcendent strong ones and transcended ones. This is not one-to-one master-disciple, but **an institutionalized lineage system**:

- Identify potential seed candidates from Myriad Ranks (cooperating with the Guidance Post 713);
- Design long-term cultivation paths for seed candidates, spanning hundreds and thousands of years;
- Provide mentors, resources, and mental escort at key breakthrough nodes;
- After cultivating to the Origin Realm, arrange post internships and cross-post rotations;
- After passing four-dimensional assessment, formally enter the transcended-one sequence.

This requires the Training Post transcended one to have an ultra-long time view — he may watch a seed candidate cultivate from Sense-Origin Realm all the way to Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, spanning multiple of his own post cycles.

## II. Four Iron Rules of the Lineage System

### 2.1 No Personality Cult

Disciples are not the master's private property. Training Post transcended ones are strictly forbidden:

- Let disciples swear personal loyalty;
- Let disciples only learn the master's manual and not other public systems;
- Let disciples treat the master's will as law.

This structurally prevents "cult-leader lineage" — old web novels' master-disciple system often evolves into cult leader and cult followers; Heng Yuan Zhou does not allow it.

### 2.2 No Bloodlineism

Training targets are produced from Myriad Ranks by talent assessment, not by birth, race, or bloodline. A plant spirit, a planet consciousness, a mechanical-clan genius enjoy completely equal training opportunities with a humankind genius. This is the republican axiom of Myriad Races landing in the lineage field.

### 2.3 No One-Shot Bet

The Training Post transcended one does not "bet on one successor" for himself. He simultaneously cultivates a batch of seed candidates, letting them grow, compete, and complement each other across different posts. This prevents the common old-web-novel disease of "master picks the wrong successor; the entire sect collapses."

### 2.4 Training Content Must Be in the Selfless Dao

Every lesson the master teaches, every note he writes, every training scheme he designs, all flow back to the Selfless-Dao public library. Disciples are not only teachable by the master — on the Selfless Dao there are billions of "masters'" public teaching materials.

## III. The Training Post's Generational Link with the 20% Activation Strategy

The 20% Activation Strategy (Documents 23, 75) is not completed by one generation. It requires several generations of transcended ones relay:

- First-generation anchors (Lin Hao's generation): from 0 to 1, activate the first 1%;
- Second-generation transcended ones: from 1% to 10%, systematic cultivation;
- Third-generation transcended ones: from 10% to 20%, steady-state operation;
- Later transcended ones: maintain above 20%, push higher ratios.

Every generation of transcended ones' KPI includes a hard metric: **the number of plane-transcendent strong ones he personally cultivated who passed the four-dimensional assessment.** This figure is written into his civilizational weight, as the basis for whether he is qualified and whether he may be promoted to higher posts.

## IV. Why Lineage Is Perpetual Construction's Ultimate Guarantee

Of the nine posts, the other eight may be partially temporarily replaced by the system — the Maintenance Post may have automated assistance; the Supervision Post may have algorithmic early warning. But the Lineage Post cannot. It requires **a transcended one who has truly walked this road to personally accompany the next generation along it.** Algorithms may teach knowledge, but may not teach "how not to give up at the most desperate breakthrough node"; automation may give resources, but may not give that one "you're almost there, hold on" nudge.

Therefore the Lineage Post is perpetual construction's **ultimate guarantee**: as long as lineage is unbroken, even if some generation of transcended ones collectively leaves post, the network may keep spinning in the next generation's hands. If lineage breaks, no matter how perfect the post design, it is a castle in the air.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: lineage lets every transcended one's lifelong experience settle, not disappear with his departure
- **Anti-predation**: lineage content is all public on the Selfless Dao, preventing "master privately hides unique moves" monopolistic lineage
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style lineage is "the master picks one person to pass it to"; Heng Yuan Zhou-style lineage is "institutionally cultivate a batch to take over"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: training targets are selected by talent from Myriad Ranks' five lineages, not favoring humankind or any one clan
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: training materials, assessment standards, lineage experience are all public in the Selfless-Dao public library
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Training-Lineage Post is the nine posts' most time-deep. It does not pursue how strong this generation of transcended ones is; it pursues the next generation being stronger than this one. **A transcended one's true greatness is not how far he himself walked, but how many people he enabled to keep walking from where he reached.** Lineage unbroken; Heng Yuan Zhou always has tomorrow.

In the end, the Transcended-One Training and Lineage Post described in this entry is no isolated case — its causal and energetic traces are throughout filed in the Alaya Network, traceable and correctable.
