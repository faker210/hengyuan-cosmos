# Entry 722 | Transcended-Ones' Perpetual-Construction System · The Law Foundation of Perpetual Construction

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 02, 15, 24

## Introduction: perpetual motion is not a wish, it is a law

Entry 721 told the general framework of "the stronger, the more you must work." This entry digs one layer down: **why does this hold at the law level?** Perpetual construction is not civilization's moral requirement, not the Anchor Council's administrative order; it is Heng Yuan Zhou's cosmological natural law — after you enter the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, if you do not work, Dao unlinks from laws; if you work, Dao fuses with laws. This is not choice; this is physics.

Six top-level axioms all back this entry, but the focus is the law-first axiom: perpetual construction's law foundation must be self-consistent, stable, not drift with plot. The anti-predation axiom is this law foundation's directional constraint; the republican axiom of Myriad Races is its source of legitimacy; the collective ascension axiom is its long-term direction; the causal self-discipline axiom is its operating procedure; the law-first axiom guarantees "perpetual construction" itself is a law-level definition, not a policy slogan.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Law Foundation One: If Dao Does Not Advance, It Retreats

Heng Yuan Zhou's law network is not static; it keeps expanding. New planes are being onboarded, new manuals are iterating, new civilizational engineering is landing. This means **the law network's "current version" updates every moment.**

If a transcended one stops learning, stops working, stops interacting with the law network, his Dao stays at the old version. Compatibility between old and new versions decays over time — after a million years, his Dao may no longer dock with the current law network. This is "if Dao does not advance, it retreats." It is not punishment; it is a version-compatibility problem.

Old web novels' "eternal immortality" is a technical problem in Heng Yuan Zhou: you are immortal, but that does not mean your Dao is immortal. If you do not follow the law network, the law network will slowly leave you behind.

## II. Law Foundation Two: Living Anchors Must Continuously Do Work

Per Documents 20 and 27, living anchors in the law network (i.e., Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcended ones) are not static nodes. They must continuously do work — calibrate laws, maintain sectors, respond to anomalies. This doing-work itself is their mode of existence.

Why? Because the law network's stability is not automatically maintained. Myriad Ranks' cultivation activity, cross-plane operations, and time retrospection all disturb laws. If these disturbances are not continuously calibrated, they accumulate into drift; drift accumulates into accidents. The living anchor is the one who continuously calibrates.

Once you stop calibrating, your responsible sector begins to drift. Drift does not immediately collapse, but it slowly accumulates. After a million years, when you notice it again, you may have missed the best intervention window.

## III. Law Foundation Three: The Myriad-Origin Two-Way Cycle

Per Documents 15 and 24, Myriad-Origin energy is generated from all beings' daily activity, flows through the anchor network into the public pool, then is allocated to individuals in need. This cycle is Heng Yuan Zhou's energy underlayer.

The living anchor plays two roles in this cycle:

- **Node role**: he is a node of the cycle; his cultivation consumes the public pool;
- **Amplifier role**: his post work (law maintenance, increment development, new-cosmos weaving) expands the public pool's total flow.

These two roles must balance. If you consume more and amplify less, the cycle bleeds because of you; if you amplify more and consume less, the cycle fills because of you. Perpetual construction requires the transcended one to long-term maintain "amplification greater than consumption" — this is more-work-more-gain embodied at the energy underlayer.

## IV. Law Foundation Four: Co-Growth of the Consciousness Domain

Heng Yuan Zhou's consciousness domain (Alaya Network layer) is not a static database. It carries every Myriad-Rank cultivator's consciousness traces, cultivation experience, and law understanding. These traces continuously settle, recombine, and evolve.

If a transcended one stops inputting new content into the consciousness domain (his post-work experience, his law-weaving understanding, his civilizational-engineering schemes), his own connection with the consciousness domain becomes shallower. The consciousness domain is growing; he stands still; the connection naturally breaks.

Conversely, if he continuously inputs high-quality content into the consciousness domain, the consciousness domain becomes richer because of his input, and he himself fuses deeper with the consciousness domain because of this input. This is the law-foundation at the consciousness-domain level of "the more you work, the stronger you get."

## V. Synergy of the Four Law Foundations

The four law foundations are not four independent items; together they form a **self-consistent cosmological closed loop**:

1. If Dao does not advance, it retreats (law-version compatibility);
2. Living anchors must do work (law network's physical requirement);
3. The Myriad-Origin two-way cycle (energy underlayer);
4. Consciousness-domain co-growth (consciousness-layer synergy).

The four foundations together tell all potential transcended ones: **perpetual construction is not a question of whether you are "willing"; it is a question of whether you are "present."** Choosing to enter the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is equal to accepting this set of law foundations. You do not accept; you cannot enter. You have entered; you are within these laws.

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the law foundation requires living anchors continuously do work, preventing energy waste of "occupying an anchor post without working"
- **Anti-predation**: the cycle requires "amplification greater than consumption"; predatory-faction strong ones naturally cannot meet this condition
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style strong ones may stop; Heng Yuan Zhou-style strong ones cannot — this is a law-level difference, not a moral-level one
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the four law foundations treat all beings entering the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm equally, not favoring humankind
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the four law foundations themselves are public in the Selfless-Dao public library; any cultivator may study and prepare
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Perpetual construction is not civilization's requirement; it is the cosmos's law. The stronger you are, the deeper your fusion with this law; the deeper, the more you must work per law. This is not bondage; this is **the premise of your lawful existence in the cosmos as a living anchor.** Understand this, and you understand why Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended ones never complain of fatigue — they know "working" is not the price; it is their way of "being present" in the cosmos.
