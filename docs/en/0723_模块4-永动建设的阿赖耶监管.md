# Entry 723 | Transcended-Ones' Perpetual-Construction System | Alaya Supervision of Perpetual Construction

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 21, 29, 49

## Introduction: the strongest must also be watched by the ledger

Entry 722 discussed the law foundation of perpetual construction. This entry discusses the execution level: **who confirms that transcended ones are really working?** The answer is not self-discipline, not conscience, but Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision. Without supervision, perpetual construction degenerates into self-praise of "the strong say they are working." Heng Yuan Zhou does not accept self-praise — every unit of your work is recorded in the Alaya Network.

Six top-level axioms in this entry converge into the word "supervision": the causal self-discipline axiom is directly supervision's legal source; the anti-predation axiom requires supervision identify "fake work, real predation"; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires supervision data be public to Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires supervision serve the uplift direction; the law-first axiom requires supervision algorithms themselves be public and not privately modifiable.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Supervision Object: The Transcended One's "Work Ledger"

The Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision keeps a "work ledger" for every Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcended one. This ledger is not a moral score, but objective data:

- **Post hours**: the time and energy this transcended one actually invested in his post;
- **Post output**: which engineering he specifically completed, which problems he solved, how many people he cultivated;
- **Energy ledger**: the ratio between energy he consumed from the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle and energy he amplified;
- **Consensus ledger**: Myriad-Rank representatives' review of his work; Alaya Network's judgment of his direction;
- **Procedure ledger**: whether his cross-plane and cross-time operations were reported per causal self-discipline.

Five ledgers together are this transcended one's current "perpetual-construction health." If health declines for two consecutive cycles, post talk is auto-triggered.

## II. The Three-Layer Structure of Supervision

Alaya supervision is not one layer, but three layers stacked:

### 2.1 Automatic Layer: Algorithm Early Warning

The Alaya Network's basic algorithm runs 7×24:

- Anomalous energy flow;
- Post output mismatched with hours;
- Cross-plane operations not reported;
- Civilizational weight diverging from actual contribution.

For any anomaly, the algorithm auto-pushes an early warning; no human initiates.

### 2.2 Audit Layer: Transcended-Ones Mutual Review

Algorithm-flagged anomalies are re-reviewed by an audit group composed of transcended ones from other posts. For example, Maintenance Post anomalies are audited by Supervision Post transcended ones; Guidance Post anomalies are audited by Training Post transcended ones. **Transcended ones audit transcended ones** — because only those of the same realm may judge whether an operation is "reasonable simplification" or "lazy violation."

### 2.3 Public Layer: Myriad-Rank Representatives' Deliberation

Audit results are periodically submitted to the Anchor Council for Myriad-Rank representatives' public deliberation. Deliberation records are queryable in the Selfless-Dao public library. Any being may see: what some transcended one did this year, how Myriad-Rank representatives reviewed, whether post replacement was triggered.

The three layers together form a closed loop of "algorithms don't sleep, transcended ones mutually audit, Myriad Ranks publicly deliberate."

## III. Supervision Is Not Surveillance: It Protects the Transcended One and Also Limits Him

The Alaya-Network Supervision Post (715) has already discussed its meaning to Myriad Ranks in Entry 715. This entry emphasizes: **supervision is also protection for the transcended one himself.**

Why? Because perpetual construction is high-intensity work. If a transcended one long-term alone judges "am I doing enough," he slowly drifts off course — either overworks and harms himself, or deviates direction without knowing. The Alaya Network gives him an objective ledger, letting him know:

- You worked too much this cycle; time to rest;
- Your direction drifted this cycle; time to adjust;
- Your output was insufficient this cycle; time to fill in.

This ledger is not a whip; it is a dashboard. It lets the transcended one, during long perpetual construction, not get lost from "no feedback."

## IV. Supervision Red Line: Preventing Supervision Itself from Becoming Predation

The Alaya-Network Supervision Post transcended ones themselves are also supervised. This red line must be repeatedly emphasized:

- Supervision algorithms are public; any being may audit whether they have bias;
- Supervision data is public to Myriad Ranks; supervisors may not privately use data for self-interest;
- Supervisors' posts rotate periodically; no one may hold a supervision field long-term;
- Supervisors' judgments must leave traces, reviewable in hindsight.

This set of design prevents "supervisors becoming new oligarchs" — the most common tragedy in old web novels is that the supervisor finally becomes the master of the supervised. Heng Yuan Zhou structurally prevents this.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: supervision identifies "fake work, real slacking," preventing anchor posts being inefficiently occupied
- **Anti-predation**: supervision identifies "fake work, real predation," tearing off predatory factions' disguise
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style strong ones need no supervision, because he is the rule; Heng Yuan Zhou-style strong ones must be supervised, because he is only a living anchor
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: supervision data is public to Myriad Ranks; Myriad-Rank representatives have the right to deliberate any transcended one's work ledger
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: supervision algorithms, audit processes, historical cases are all public in the Selfless-Dao public library
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Perpetual construction cannot rely on self-discipline; it must rely on a ledger. The Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision is that ledger — it does not sleep, does not favor, does not get privately modified by any one person. It lets every transcended one know: how much you worked, Myriad Ranks watch; whether you worked correctly, laws watch; if you want to slack, the system reminds; if you want to drift, Myriad Ranks call a halt. **This is the institutional guarantee that perpetual construction may truly be "perpetual."**
