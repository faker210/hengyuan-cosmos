# Entry 724 | Transcended-Ones' Perpetual-Construction System | More-Work-More-Gain Allocation of Perpetual Construction

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 06, 22, 25

## Introduction: work is not asceticism, it is positive feedback

If perpetual construction has only the pressure of "you must work" without the feedback of "working pays off," it becomes hard labor, unsustainable for a million years. Heng Yuan Zhou's solution is **more-work-more-gain** — the more you work, the larger the public computable power you may dispatch; the larger your output, the higher your civilizational weight; the higher your weight, the larger the civilizational engineering you may take on. This is a positive feedback, not a punishment.

Six top-level axioms in this entry converge into the word "allocation": the anti-predation axiom requires allocation by contribution only, not by predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires allocation rules be equal to all beings; the collective ascension axiom requires allocation serve the uplift direction; the causal self-discipline axiom requires allocation processes be transparent and auditable; the law-first axiom requires allocation algorithms be stable, not adjusted by plot needs.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Core of Allocation: Civilizational Weight Is the Allocation Voucher

Heng Yuan Zhou's allocation is not "paying wages," but **civilizational weight as allocation voucher**.

- The higher your civilizational weight, the larger your share in the joint-contracting resource pool;
- The higher your civilizational weight, the larger the law-weaving engineering you may participate in;
- The higher your civilizational weight, the larger your voice in the Myriad-Rank Republican Council;
- The higher your civilizational weight, the more cross-plane-channel quotas you may dispatch.

This means: **allocation is not "rewarding your past contribution," but "giving you a larger stage to keep contributing."** Your weight is high not because you may enjoy, but because Myriad Ranks trust you to take on larger engineering.

## II. Three Quantitative Dimensions of More-Work-More-Gain

Per Documents 06 and 22, more-work-more-gain at the transcended-one level is concretely quantified in three dimensions:

### 2.1 Hours Dimension

The time and energy a transcended one actually invests on post. This is not "clocking in," but effective hours — how many anchors you actually calibrated on the Maintenance Post; how many latecomers you actually guided on the Guidance Post; how many new manuals you actually developed on the Increment Post.

### 2.2 Output Dimension

The efficiency with which hours convert into civilizational increments. Given the same hours, how many cultivators use the new manuals you developed; how many of the latecomers you guided pass four-dimensional assessment; how many planes onboard the new laws you wove. The output dimension matters more than the hours dimension — it measures "effective contribution."

### 2.3 Coverage Dimension

How many clans, how many planes, how many life forms your work benefits. A transcended one serving only humankind naturally has a lower coverage dimension than one serving Myriad Ranks. This encourages transcended ones structurally to expand work toward 99% of clans.

The three dimensions are weighted to yield this transcended one's current-period civilizational-weight increment. Increments accumulated are his total weight.

## III. Allocation's Anti-Matthew Principle

The old world's allocation is often Matthew-effect: the rich get richer; the poor get poorer. Heng Yuan Zhou's more-work-more-gain has a key correction — **anti-Matthew principle**:

- For latecomers: basic cultivation resources are prioritized, not monopolized by "arrivistes";
- For transcended ones: high weight brings not "private-property accumulation," but "larger public responsibility";
- For Myriad Ranks: the resource pool's total flow keeps expanding because of the Increment Post; it is not zero-sum.

This means: **your weight is high not because you "save more"; your weight is high because you "spend more on public engineering."** Civilizational weight may not be exchanged for private enjoyment; it may only be invested in the next round of construction.

## IV. The Meshing of Allocation with the Perpetual Cycle

The more-work-more-gain allocation mechanism is the gear of perpetual construction's positive-feedback cycle (see Entry 721):

```
work more → civilizational weight rises → larger public computable power to dispatch
 ↑                                                         ↓
 ←─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Without the allocation mechanism, this cycle cannot spin — you work half a day, computable power does not grow, next time you start from scratch. With the allocation mechanism, every time you work, your next work starts from a higher point. This is the mechanism foundation at the allocation level of "the more you work, the stronger you get."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: more-work-more-gain lets cultivators who maximize effective contribution get the largest computable power, preventing "muddling along and occupying resources"
- **Anti-predation**: allocation is by contribution only, not by predation — predatory-faction strong ones get no high weight in this algorithm
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style allocation is "the protagonist takes the lion's share, disciples drink soup"; Heng Yuan Zhou-style allocation is "keep ledgers by contribution, divide stages by weight"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the coverage dimension explicitly includes service to 99% of clans in allocation computation
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: allocation algorithms, weight ledgers, historical records are all public in the Selfless-Dao public library
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

More-work-more-gain is not a slogan; it is perpetual construction's allocation engine. It makes the transcended one feel, every time he works, "this time I have more drive than last"; it lets Myriad Ranks see that the larger the contributor's contribution, the larger his stage — not the more he enjoys.

This mechanism also has a social function often overlooked: it makes "being strong" no longer envied, but expected. In the old world, after a person becomes strong, those around him are instinctively wary — is he going to exploit us? Heng Yuan Zhou's more-work-more-gain allocation lets those around see: he became strong, his stage grew, his work grew, the civilizational fruits we enjoy also grew. Strength is no longer a threat; strength is an amplifier of public interest. **This is Heng Yuan Zhou's strong-one ethics: not making you poorer the more you work, but making you more able to keep working the more you work.**

In the end, the more-work-more-gain allocation of perpetual construction described in this entry is no isolated case — its causal and energetic traces are throughout filed in the Alaya Network, traceable and correctable.
