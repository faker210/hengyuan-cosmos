# Entry 725 | Transcended-Ones' Perpetual-Construction System | Perpetual Construction and the 20% Activation Strategy

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 23, 75, 17

## Introduction: 20% is not privilege, it is responsibility

The 20% Activation Strategy is a high-frequency term in Heng Yuan Zhou. Its original meaning: at the early stage of civilizational establishment, activate 20% of beings to the plane-transcendent strong-one level, as a backbone driving the remaining 80% to jointly uplift. This entry makes clear: **this 20% is not a chosen favorite, but a chosen builder.** The perpetual-construction system and the 20% Activation Strategy are two sides of the same thing — the 20% is the backbone, and the backbone's way of working is perpetual construction.

Six top-level axioms in this entry converge into the word "backbone": the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the 20% activation be a means, not an end; the end is 99%-clan full coverage; the collective ascension axiom is directly the 20% strategy's parent law; the anti-predation axiom requires this 20% not be activated by predation; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the activation process not disturb Myriad Ranks' natural rhythm; the law-first axiom requires the 20% ratio, paths, and assessment standards be stable.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Three-Layer Structure of the 20% Activation Strategy

Per Documents 23 and 75, the 20% Activation Strategy is not a vague number; it has a three-layer structure:

### 1.1 Short Term: 1% Seed Period

At the early stage of civilizational establishment, activate 1% of beings to the Origin Realm. This 1% is the first-generation anchors, the first batch of transcended ones, Lin Hao's generation. Their task is not to enjoy privilege, but **to make the road passable** — run the 300-set system smoothly, build the Selfless-Dao public library, erect the nine posts' prototype.

### 1.2 Mid Term: 10% Expansion Period

After the 1% seeds make the paths passable, activate 10% of beings to the Origin Realm. This 10% is the backbone ranks, beginning to hold the nine posts' formal work, beginning to cultivate the next generation of seeds, beginning to enlarge the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle's flow.

### 1.3 Long Term: 20% Steady-State Period

20% of beings reach the Origin Realm; civilization enters steady state. This 20% is the nine posts' personnel pool — Maintenance Post, Guidance Post, Supervision Post... all posts select from this pool. The remaining 80% of beings, under this 20%'s support, cultivate, live, and participate in civilization at their own rhythm.

## II. The Meshing of 20% and Perpetual Construction

The 20% Activation Strategy and the perpetual-construction system are not two independent projects; they mesh:

- Perpetual construction is the 20% transcended ones' **way of working**;
- The 20% activation is perpetual construction's **personnel-scale goal**.

Without the 20% personnel scale, perpetual construction is only a few first-generation anchors working; the scale cannot rise. Without the perpetual-construction way of working, the 20% strong ones are only 20% "retired elders"; even if the scale rises, it is useless.

The two together are Heng Yuan Zhou's complete answer to "Myriad-Rank uplift": **cultivate 20% willing to perpetually-build strong ones; let this 20% support the remaining 80%; let the whole civilization accelerate in positive feedback within perpetual construction.**

## III. 20% Is Not Privilege: Anti-Elitist Design

The 20% Activation Strategy is most easily misread as "cultivate 20% nobles." Heng Yuan Zhou structurally has anti-elitist design:

### 3.1 Public Assessment

The 20% selection standards (four-dimensional assessment: Power 40 / Heart-Nature 25 / Contribution 20 / Responsibility 15) are all public; no back-room dealing.

### 3.2 Mandatory Coverage

This 20% must cover all major clans across Myriad Ranks' five lineages; it may not concentrate in humankind, may not concentrate in some one race. If any major clan's share in the 20% is too low, the Guidance Post triggers special filling.

### 3.3 Exit Mechanism

Strong ones who enter the 20% are not lifetime tenure. If their civilizational weight keeps declining and post performance does not meet standards, they are rotated out of the 20% sequence, and more suitable cultivators fill in. The 20% is a dynamic pool, not a fixed identity.

### 3.4 Rights Guarantee for the 80%

The remaining 80% of beings do not lose any rights for not being 20% — the Everyone-a-Road Guarantee System (Document 08) ensures every being has basic cultivation conditions; the 20% are only "backbone," not "masters."

## IV. The Historical Depth of the 20% Activation Strategy

The 20% Activation Strategy is not completed by one generation. It requires several generations of transcended ones relay:

- First-generation anchors: from 0 to 1;
- Second-generation transcended ones: from 1% to 10%;
- Third-generation transcended ones: from 10% to 20%;
- Later transcended ones: maintain above 20%, push higher ratios.

Every generation of transcended ones' KPI includes "the number of plane-transcendent strong ones he cultivated." This figure is the 20% Activation Strategy's generational relay baton. Lin Hao's generation passes the baton; the next generation keeps running — this is perpetual construction embodied in the time dimension.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the 20% backbone ranks concentrate civilization's strongest computable power into perpetual construction, preventing strong-one idling
- **Anti-predation**: 20% selection is by contribution only, not by predation; predatory-faction strong ones cannot enter the backbone
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style elites are "the protagonist leads a few disciples"; Heng Yuan Zhou-style 20% is "backbone selected by assessment from Myriad Ranks" — the former relies on human feeling, the latter on institution
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the 20% composition must cover 99% of clans; it is not a humankind club
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: selection standards, assessment processes, backbone lists are all public in the Selfless-Dao public library
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The 20% Activation Strategy is not cultivating 20% nobles; it is cultivating 20% builders. Their privilege is not enjoyment, but work; their reward is not private property, but a larger stage.

This strategy's most exquisite point is that it completely welds the two identities of "strong one" and "builder." The old world's strong ones need not build — they obtained power by birth, by predation, by luck, with power decoupled from their contribution. Heng Yuan Zhou's 20% Activation Strategy welds the two from the moment of selection: you want to enter the 20%; you must pass four-dimensional assessment, must take a post, must accept Alaya supervision. After entering, you want to lie flat? The post-replacement procedure awaits. **The stronger this 20% is, the wider the remaining 80%'s uplift road — this is the final unity of perpetual construction and the 20% Activation Strategy.**

In the end, the perpetual construction and 20% Activation Strategy described in this entry is no isolated case — its causal and energetic traces are throughout filed in the Alaya Network, traceable and correctable.
