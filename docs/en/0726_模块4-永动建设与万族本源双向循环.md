# Entry 726 | Transcended-Ones' Perpetual-Construction System | Perpetual Construction and the Myriad-Origin Two-Way Cycle

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 15, 24, 25

## Introduction: perpetual motion is not a perpetual-motion machine, it is a water cycle

The word "perpetual motion" is most easily misread as "perpetual-motion machine" — consuming no energy, doing work forever. Heng Yuan Zhou's perpetual construction does not mean that. Its true physical model is **water cycle**: Myriad-Origin energy comes from Myriad Ranks, flows through the anchor network into the public pool, is allocated to transcended ones and cultivators, then through their post work, cultivation activity, and civilizational engineering, flows back to Myriad Ranks, and regenerates new origin energy. The cycle itself does not "create from nothing"; it only **makes the water cycle's efficiency higher, coverage wider, total amount larger.**

Six top-level axioms in this entry converge into the word "cycle": the anti-predation axiom requires the cycle go two-way, not one-way; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the cycle be open to all beings; the collective ascension axiom requires the cycle's direction be uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the cycle not be privately cut off by any one person; the law-first axiom requires the cycle's physical laws be stable.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Four Links of the Cycle

The Myriad-Origin two-way cycle consists of four links, none dispensable:

### 1.1 Generation Link

Billions of beings' daily cultivation, civilizational activity, and consciousness fluctuation continuously generate origin energy. This is the cycle's water source.

### 1.2 Collection Link

Origin energy flows from every plane and every clan through the anchor network into the public pool. This is the cycle's water-conveyance network.

### 1.3 Allocation Link

The public pool, per joint-contracting and more-work-more-gain principles, allocates energy to individuals in need of breakthrough, construction, or rescue. This is the cycle's water-supply station.

### 1.4 Backflow Link

Allocated energy, through cultivators' manual practice, transcended ones' post work, and civilizational-engineering output, flows back to Myriad Ranks again, becoming new origin energy. This is the cycle's evaporation and rainfall.

Perpetual construction can be "perpetual" precisely because these four links spin forever. Any link breaks; the cycle stops.

## II. The Transcended One's Dual Role in the Cycle

The transcended one is both **consumer** and **producer** in the cycle:

- As consumer: he draws allocated energy from the public pool, for cultivation and post work;
- As producer: his post work (law maintenance, increment development, new-cosmos weaving) enlarges the public pool's total flow.

Perpetual construction requires the transcended one to long-term maintain "output greater than consumption." A transcended one whose consumption exceeds output is a "leak point" in the cycle — he occupies a share of the public pool without making the pool larger. The Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision continuously monitors every transcended one's "production-consumption ratio"; long-term those whose expenses exceed income trigger post replacement.

## III. Why It Must Be "Two-Way"

The word "two-way" is this cycle's soul. The old world's energy flow is one-way — the strong draw energy from the weak, then leave after drawing. This one-way flow makes the strong rich in the short term, but makes the whole civilization poor in the long term: the weak have no energy, cannot cultivate, cannot generate new origin energy; the public pool dries up.

The two-way cycle structurally prevents this drying:

- The strong must backflow, otherwise his own Dao cannot sustain;
- The weak must have an entrance, otherwise he cannot generate new origin energy;
- Two-way flow lets the cycle grow larger and larger, not drier and drier.

This is Heng Yuan Zhou's anti-predation axiom ultimately landing at the energy underlayer: **predation is one-way drawing; symbiosis is two-way flow.**

## IV. How Perpetual Construction Expands the Cycle

The perpetual-construction system does not maintain the cycle's status quo; it must **continuously expand the cycle**. Ways of expansion:

### 4.1 Onboarding New Planes

The Cross-Plane Anchor Construction Post (714) onboards new planes into the cycle, bringing new water sources.

### 4.2 Developing New Energy Sources

The Increment Development Post (717) develops new energy-conversion technologies, turning unusable energy into usable.

### 4.3 Weaving New Cosmoses

The New-Cosmos Frontier Post (719) weaves new cosmoses, bringing brand-new law combinations and energy types.

### 4.4 Guiding New Cultivators

The Myriad-Rank Evolution Guidance Post (713) guides more beings into cultivation, enlarging the generation link's base.

The four actions together make the cycle's total flow rise a step every period. This is the true meaning of "perpetual" — **not that the same cup of water is never drunk up, but that more and more water sources are found.**

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the two-way cycle lets every unit of origin energy be used in flow, not settled or wasted
- **Anti-predation**: the two-way structure fundamentally prevents one-way predation; predatory-faction strong ones cannot sustain in this cycle
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style energy flow is "the protagonist draws from Myriad Ranks"; Heng Yuan Zhou-style energy flow is "Myriad Ranks and strong ones flow mutually"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the cycle is open to 99% of clans; every clan is both a water source and a water user
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the cycle's physical laws, allocation algorithms, and flow data are all public on the Selfless Dao
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Myriad-Origin two-way cycle is perpetual construction's energy underlayer. It tells all potential transcended ones: **you are not outside the cycle; you are inside the cycle.** Every unit of energy you draw from the cycle must flow back through your post work. You draw much and return much; the cycle grows because of you. You only draw and never return; the cycle shrinks because of you.

This cycle also has a time dimension often overlooked: it is not a one-time water flow, but an eons-long continuous water cycle. A transcended one on post for millions of years; every breath he takes, every calibration, every guidance, leaves traces in the cycle. These traces settle, becoming steps later-comers may step on. This is Heng Yuan Zhou's hardest energy-theory answer to the word "strong one" — **your strength is not your private property; it is living water the cycle lends you; living water must flow back to flow again.**
