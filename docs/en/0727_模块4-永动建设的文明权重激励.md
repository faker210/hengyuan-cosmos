# Entry 727 | Transcended-Ones' Perpetual-Construction System | Civilizational-Weight Incentive of Perpetual Construction

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 29, 06, 706

## Introduction: let those who work have a larger stage the more they work

Entry 706 already told the basic formula of civilizational weight: individual effective combat power = destructive power × civilizational consensus degree. This entry pushes it one layer further: **civilizational weight is not only a "combat-power formula"; it is also perpetual construction's incentive mechanism.** It does not evaluate after the fact whether you worked well; it continuously gives you feedback during and before work — you worked correctly, weight rises; weight rises, stage grows; stage grows, you may do larger work; do larger work, weight rises again. This is a positive feedback that makes the transcended one "want to work more the more he works."

Six top-level axioms in this entry converge into the word "incentive": the anti-predation axiom requires incentive reward contribution only, not predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires incentive rules be equal to all beings; the collective ascension axiom requires incentive direction be aimed at uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires incentive data be transparent; the law-first axiom requires incentive algorithms be stable.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Civilizational-Weight Dashboard: The Transcended One's "Instrument Panel"

Every Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcended one has a **civilizational-weight dashboard**. It is not private property; it is public to Myriad-Rank representatives. The dashboard shows five numbers in real time:

- Source legitimacy score (25%);
- Direction correctness score (25%);
- Contribution sustainability score (20%);
- Coverage breadth score (15%);
- Procedural compliance score (15%).

The five numbers are weighted to yield this transcended one's current-period total civilizational weight. Total weight determines the public computable power he may currently dispatch, the engineering scale he may participate in, and his voice in the Council.

This dashboard's purpose is to let the transcended one **always know where he is**. He need not ask others "how am I doing"; the dashboard tells him directly. If he wants to adjust direction, whichever sub-item is low on the dashboard, he fills that in. This is an "instrument panel" — not a whip, but navigation.

## II. Three Layers of Incentive

The civilizational-weight incentive acts simultaneously on three layers:

### 2.1 Immediate Layer: Post Feedback

Every thing he did on post today — calibrated an anchor, guided a latecomer, resolved a cross-plane dispute — is reflected on the dashboard within hours. Immediate feedback lets him know: "this thing was done correctly; that thing was done off course."

### 2.2 Cycle Layer: Post Performance

After a post cycle ends, his total performance is settled. Those with high performance are allocated larger engineering in the next post round; those with low performance receive post talk and direction adjustment.

### 2.3 Historical Layer: Civilizational Status

His lifelong civilizational-weight trajectory is written into his Alaya record. Later-comers, through the Selfless-Dao public library, may see this transcended one's life — where he came from, what he did, whom he cultivated, what he left. This is the highest layer of incentive: **not immediate computable power, but historical position.**

## III. Incentive Is Not Privilege: Weight May Not Be Exchanged for Private Property

The most critical red line of civilizational-weight incentive: **weight may not be exchanged for private enjoyment.**

- High weight does not mean you may live in a better cave dwelling or eat better spirit food;
- High weight does not mean you may make other cultivators obey you;
- High weight does not mean you may skip post rotation.

High weight means only one thing: **you may take on larger civilizational engineering.** It is a "stage amplifier," not an "enjoyment voucher." This structurally prevents "weight becoming a new rank privilege" — old-world rank privileges rotted precisely because high rank may be exchanged for private enjoyment; Heng Yuan Zhou's high weight may only be exchanged for larger responsibility.

## IV. The Meshing of Incentive with the Perpetual Cycle

Civilizational-weight incentive is perpetual construction's positive-feedback cycle's "instrument panel + accelerator":

- Instrument panel: the dashboard tells you in real time how you are doing;
- Accelerator: work well, weight rises, stage grows, next time you may do larger work.

Without the instrument panel, the transcended one does not know where he is; without the accelerator, the transcended one has no motivation to keep working. The two together are what let perpetual construction truly have "more drive the more you work."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: incentive directs the most effective builders to the largest stages, preventing "able people with nothing to do"
- **Anti-predation**: incentive is by contribution only, not by predation; predatory-faction strong ones' weight naturally cannot rise
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style incentive is "the protagonist rewards on a whim"; Heng Yuan Zhou-style incentive is "the dashboard auto-settles by contribution" — the former relies on human feeling, the latter on algorithm
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the dashboard is public to Myriad-Rank representatives; Myriad Ranks may see every transcended one's incentive process
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: weight algorithms, dashboard design, historical cases are all public on the Selfless Dao
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Civilizational-weight incentive makes perpetual construction no longer "the strong one's hard labor," but "the strong one's game" — work well, stage grows; stage grows, work better; work better, stage grows larger. This is a positive feedback that lets the transcended one stop.

But it always has one red line: weight is not exchanged for private property, only for larger responsibility. This red line looks harsh, but is actually the deepest protection for transcended ones — it lets transcended ones never be corrupted by "enjoyment." A transcended one of weight ten thousand and a transcended one of weight hundred live in the same cave dwelling, eat the same spirit food, wear the same clothes. The difference is only: the ten-thousand-weight one may dispatch ten thousand shares of public computable power; the hundred-weight one may dispatch a hundred shares. **Hold this red line, and incentive will not become a new rank privilege.**

In the end, the civilizational-weight incentive of perpetual construction described in this entry is no isolated case — its causal and energetic traces are throughout filed in the Alaya Network, traceable and correctable.
