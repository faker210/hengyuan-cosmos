# Entry 728 | Transcended-Ones' Perpetual-Construction System | The Parasitic Essence of Predatory-Faction Transcended Ones, by Contrast

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 07, 57

## Introduction: the most thorough controlled experiment

By now, Module 4 has thoroughly explained Heng Yuan Zhou's orthodox transcendence: Myriad-Rank symbiosis, perpetual construction, nine posts, perpetual cycle. The last entry does a **most thorough controlled experiment** — put the predatory-faction transcended one on Heng Yuan Zhou's ledger and see what he really is. The conclusion is two words: **parasite.** He is not another kind of transcendence; he is transcendence's opposite; he is not "another road"; he is the borer in this road.

Six top-level axioms in this entry almost aim directly at the predatory-faction transcended one: the anti-predation axiom is this controlled experiment's parent law; the republican axiom of Myriad Races asks on what basis; the collective ascension axiom judges his direction wrong; the causal self-discipline axiom records every extraction he makes; the law-first axiom ensures the law definition of "parasite" is stable.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Portrait of a Predatory-Faction Transcended One

What does a predatory-faction transcended one look like? On the surface he is much like an orthodox transcended one:

- Extremely high realm, usually also reaching Origin Realm, even Multiverse Realm;
- Extremely great power, destructive power no less than an orthodox transcended one;
- Extremely long lifespan, seemingly also "transcending life and death";
- Numerous disciples, seemingly also "lineage."

But opened up, his difference from the orthodox transcended one is at every key link:

| Link | Orthodox Transcended One | Predatory-Faction Transcended One |
|------|--------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Power source | Myriad-Origin two-way cycle | Devouring the weak, plundering stock |
| Post work | Perpetual construction across nine posts | Occupying the post without working, or using the post's name to do predation |
| Manual openness | Selfless-Dao public library | Privately hoarding bloodlines, monopolizing knowledge |
| Attitude to Myriad Ranks | Carrying Myriad Ranks uplift | Treating Myriad Ranks as nourishment |
| Civilizational weight | Positive, continuously rising | Negative,随时 zeroed |

## II. The Three Mechanisms of Parasitism

The predatory-faction transcended one's survival mechanism is typical parasitism:

### 2.1 Energy Parasitism

He does not draw energy through the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle's lawful channels, but **directly extracts the origin stock of other cultivators, other planes, other civilizations**. In his eyes, Myriad Ranks are not symbiotic partners; they are crops. He plants a crop, harvests when ripe, plants again after harvest.

### 2.2 Causal Parasitism

He hangs his own causality on Myriad Ranks' causal chain, making Myriad Ranks bear the causal consequences for him. He does one cross-plane operation, disturbing billions of beings' causality; he never reports, because reporting means he bears responsibility.

### 2.3 Knowledge Parasitism

He **reads** all public knowledge from the Selfless-Dao public library, but **never backflows** his own understanding, experience, and manuals. He is a reader who only goes in and never goes out; a black hole that only inhales and never exhales.

The three parasitisms together are the predatory-faction transcended one's true ecological niche: **he is not civilization's engine; he is civilization's parasite.**

## III. Why He Is Necessarily Judged Negative by Civilizational Weight

Per Entry 706's formula: individual effective combat power = destructive power × civilizational consensus degree.

The predatory-faction transcended one's destructive power is enormous, but his civilizational consensus degree under Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision is necessarily negative:

- Source legitimacy: his energy has predatory residual traces; sub-item is negative;
- Direction correctness: his daily work is extracting Myriad Ranks; sub-item is negative;
- Contribution sustainability: he produces no increments; sub-item is zero or negative;
- Coverage breadth: he serves himself, not Myriad Ranks; sub-item is zero;
- Procedural compliance: he does not report cross-plane operations; sub-item is negative.

The five together: his civilizational consensus degree is a large negative. Multiplied by his destructive power, his "effective combat power" on the civilizational ledger is **negative** — that is, the stronger he is, the poorer civilization becomes because of him.

## IV. The Parasite's Endgame

The predatory-faction transcended one is not eternal. His parasitic pattern has a fundamental death hole: **he depends on the host to live, but he will drain the host to death.**

- He draws out a civilization's origin stock; after drawing, this civilization dries up;
- A dried-up civilization cannot generate new origin energy; the public pool shrinks;
- The public pool shrinks; the next civilization he may draw is weaker;
- He has to go farther cosmoses, deeper planes to find new hosts;
- Eventually, the entire accessible cosmos is drained by him into a desert.

Old web novels' predatory protagonists' endgame is almost always this pattern: he grows stronger and stronger, but the civilization beneath his feet becomes emptier and emptier. He stands at the highest point, looking around with no one — this is not "the loneliness of invincibility"; this is **the inevitable result after a parasite eats its host up.**

## V. Why Heng Yuan Zhou Can Cure Parasitism

Heng Yuan Zhou structurally makes the parasitic pattern unsustainable:

1. **Source audit**: predatory residual traces cannot pass Alaya Network's consciousness scan;
2. **Energy supervision**: anomalous extraction auto-issues early warning, handed to the symbiotic court;
3. **Knowledge closed loop**: readers who only go in and never out are identified; civilizational weight is zeroed;
4. **Post contract**: parasites cannot enter the nine posts; no lawful post identity;
5. **Myriad-Rank consensus**: his civilizational consensus degree is negative; he cannot dispatch public computable power.

The five defense lines together: in Heng Yuan Zhou, predatory-faction transcended ones either reform themselves and join the symbiotic cycle, or are identified, quarantined, and stripped of power. **He has no space to "set up his own stove and be a parasite."**
