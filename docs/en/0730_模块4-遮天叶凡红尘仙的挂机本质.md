# Entry 730 | Critique of Hang-Up Retirement | The Hang-Up Essence of Shrouding the Heavens' Ye Fan as Mortal-World Immortal

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 17, 90

## Introduction: what the nine-dragon coffin pulled out is a hang-up endpoint

Shrouding the Heavens' ending is a classic sample of the web-novel hang-up-retirement paradigm. Ye Fan's journey — from Earth to Big Dipper, from mortal to Mortal-World Immortal, ultimately becoming the cosmos's topmost being. But Heng Yuan Zhou Library must measure with the ruler built in the previous 29 entries: **on Heng Yuan Zhou's ledger, what is Ye Fan's "Mortal-World Immortal"?** The answer: a hang-up sample under "medium" protagonist-uniqueness dependency — he is strong, but he does not lift people; he lives, but he does not work; he wins, but his civilization did not become better because of his win.

Six top-level axioms in this entry act as the critique ruler: the anti-predation axiom examines Ye Fan's power source; the republican axiom of Myriad Races asks whether his manuals were made public; the collective ascension axiom judges whether he carries Myriad Ranks; the causal self-discipline axiom examines his cross-plane operations; the law-first axiom examines Shrouding the Heavens' setting self-consistency.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Heng Yuan Zhou's Judgment of Ye Fan's "Mortal-World Immortal"

Per Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment standards, Ye Fan's "Mortal-World Immortal" realm has three problems:

### 1.1 Power Source Not Clean

Ye Fan's rise accompanies massive combat, devouring, and treasure-seizing. His power did not come through the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle's lawful channels, but grew in predatory fashion through life-and-death battles. Per Heng Yuan Zhou's source audit, his energy purity cannot reach 99%; he carries predatory residual traces.

### 1.2 Endpoint Is the Endpoint

Mortal-World Immortal is Shrouding the Heavens' top realm. After Ye Fan reaches Mortal-World Immortal, the story ends. He has no next step — no post, no nine-post rotation, no perpetual construction. His "transcendence" is a period.

### 1.3 Protagonist-Uniqueness Dependency "Medium"

Per Glossary Article 50, Shrouding the Heavens' Ye Fan's protagonist-uniqueness dependency rating is "medium" (oligarchic). His civilization revolves around him, but he is not an extreme oligarch of Gu Chensha's "everything belongs to me" kind; he has companions, disciples, and a cosmos. But he is still an oligarch — civilization's highest decision-making power rests in his hands alone.

## II. Ye Fan's Hang-Up Essence: Concrete Manifestations of the Four Symptoms

### 2.1 Myriad Ranks Stay Where They Are

After Ye Fan ascends, the cosmos behind him keeps running, but no longer uplifts because of him. Later generations only need to look up at the Mortal-World Immortal; no need to catch up. This is the "ceiling for later generations" discussed in Entry 729 — Ye Fan himself is the ceiling.

### 2.2 Knowledge Privatized

Ye Fan's manuals, his understanding of the Great Dao, his lifelong experience never entered a public "Selfless-Dao public library." They only circulate within his bloodline, his sect, his small circle. Ordinary beings among billions never read a single note of his experience in a lifetime.

### 2.3 Cross-Plane Operations Not Reported

Ye Fan's cross-cosmos travel, his historical intervention, his time-river operations all fail under Heng Yuan Zhou's causal self-discipline axiom. He does not report, does not audit, does not accept multi-layer supervision — he is his own rule.

### 2.4 Lonely Invincibility

The romantic feeling of Shrouding the Heavens' ending is essentially lonely invincibility. Ye Fan stands at the highest point; old friends have passed, peers have withered, later generations look up. This loneliness is taken by readers as "a strong one's medal"; Heng Yuan Zhou judges it as "hang-up retirement" — **he is not symbiotic with Myriad Ranks; he stands above Myriad Ranks.**

## III. What Happens If Ye Fan Enters Heng Yuan Zhou

Heng Yuan Zhou does not deny Ye Fan's power. If Ye Fan were placed into Heng Yuan Zhou's system:

- His destructive-power score would be extremely high;
- But his civilizational consensus degree would be pulled low at the source-audit stage;
- He could not pass the nine posts' ethical review;
- He would be classified as a "high-risk individual," not a "transcended one."

It is not that he is not strong enough, but that his strength is incompatible with Heng Yuan Zhou's law definition. **His strength is predatory strength; his transcendence is hang-up transcendence.** This is not a negation of the character Ye Fan; this is a critique of the paradigm he sits in.

## IV. Shrouding the Heavens' Book-Eating/Ending-Risk

Per Module 2's evaluation dimensions, Shrouding the Heavens' book-eating/ending-risk rating is "medium-high":

- The realm system set in the early period is constantly broken through and added to in the later period;
- The combat-power ruler floats with the protagonist's needs;
- Some foreshadowing in the ending is not fully recovered;
- Protagonist-uniqueness dependency is "medium"; civilization is deeply tied to Ye Fan personally.

Under the law-first axiom, Shrouding the Heavens' setting self-consistency cannot approach full marks. This is not disparagement; it is structural inevitability.

- **Anti-energy waste**: Ye Fan's Mortal-World Immortal computable power stops producing after reaching the summit; this is civilization-grade waste
- **Anti-predation**: Ye Fan's power source has predatory residual traces; Heng Yuan Zhou's source audit does not pass

## Conclusion

Ye Fan's Mortal-World Immortal is a strong-one romance under the Shrouding paradigm; it is a hang-up sample under the Heng Yuan Zhou paradigm. He is strong, he wins, he is immortal, but his strength did not convert into Myriad Ranks' uplift speed; his win did not convert into public knowledge; his immortality did not convert into sustained post work. **This is not Ye Fan's personal fault; this is the Shrouding paradigm's disease — and what Heng Yuan Zhou Library must do is cure this disease at the law level.**

In the end, the hang-up essence of Shrouding the Heavens' Ye Fan as Mortal-World Immortal described in this entry is no isolated case — its causal and energetic traces are throughout filed in the Alaya Network, traceable and correctable.
