# Entry 731 | Critique of Hang-Up Retirement | Perfect World's Shi Hao: The Loneliness After Sole-Decision-Over-Eons

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 04, 82

## Introduction: sole decision over eons is the most tragic hang-up

Shi Hao is one of the web-novel long river's most heart-wrenching protagonists. A lifetime of warfare; relatives and friends wither; finally "sole decision over eons" — standing alone at the end of the time river, sealing all threats, personally guarding there. This ending is held by readers as "the most tragic strong-one romance." Heng Yuan Zhou Library must measure with the ruler built in the previous 30 entries: **on Heng Yuan Zhou's ledger, what is "sole decision over eons"?** The answer: the most tragic fleeing-style transcendence — he did not actively hang up; he was forced into hang-up by the paradigm.

Six top-level axioms in this entry act as the critique ruler: the republican axiom of Myriad Races asks on what basis he alone bears everything; the collective ascension axiom points out he excluded Myriad Ranks; the causal self-discipline axiom examines his time-river operations; the anti-predation axiom examines whether his seal is lawful; the law-first axiom examines Perfect World's setting self-consistency.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Heng Yuan Zhou's Judgment of Shi Hao's "Sole Decision over Eons"

### 1.1 One Person Bearing Everything Is the Tragic Version of Flee-Style Transcendence

Entry 702 discussed three typical scripts of flee-style transcendence; the third seed type is "sole-decision-over-eons type": the protagonist, to protect something, seals all enemies and all possibilities, personally guarding at the time river's end. Shi Hao is the perfect sample of this script.

Heng Yuan Zhou does not deny Shi Hao's sacrifice. But it must point out: **he packaged "I alone bear it" as heroism; actually he excluded Myriad Ranks from the decision.** He did not convene Myriad-Rank representatives to discuss "should we bear it together"; did not cultivate successors to guard together; did not make the sealing method public so Myriad Ranks could learn — he made every decision alone, then guarded alone for eons.

### 1.2 Sealing Is Freezing, Not Construction

Shi Hao's "sole decision over eons" is essentially freezing: he seals threats in the time river, personally guarding the seal. This is not construction; this is **freezing**. Under the frozen state, civilization does not advance, Myriad Ranks do not uplift, laws do not iterate. This is the tragic version of the lying-flat transcendence critiqued in Entry 703 — it is not that he does not want to work; he is locked by his own seal, able to do only one thing: guard.

### 1.3 Protagonist-Uniqueness Dependency "Medium-High"

The degree to which Shi Hao's civilization revolves around him is more extreme than Ye Fan. Ye Fan at least has peers and disciples; Shi Hao at the end is truly "alone." His protagonist-uniqueness dependency in Heng Yuan Zhou's rating system falls between "medium" and "high" — closer to Gu Chensha than Ye Fan.

## II. Shi Hao's Loneliness: Three Tragedies from Heng Yuan Zhou's View

### 2.1 Decision Tragedy

He did not let Myriad Ranks participate in the decision. Myriad Ranks do not know what he sealed, why he sealed, how long the seal holds. Myriad Ranks are only "those protected," not "co-protectors." This violates the republican axiom of Myriad Races.

### 2.2 Lineage Tragedy

He did not cultivate successors. He alone guarded eons; after eons? Perfect World did not answer this. His sealing method, his understanding of threats, his combat experience are all guarded by him alone. No Selfless-Dao public library; no Training Post; no lineage.

### 2.3 Causal Tragedy

His time-river operations — sealing threats in time — are serious violations under Heng Yuan Zhou's causal self-discipline axiom. He disturbed the causal main chain, did not report, did not observe-first-then-limit-interfere; his seal itself is a crude intervention on causality. Such intervention works in the short term, but in the long term accumulates into new historical-reversal risk.

## III. What Happens If Shi Hao Enters Heng Yuan Zhou

Heng Yuan Zhou does not deny Shi Hao's sacrificial spirit. If Shi Hao were placed into Heng Yuan Zhou:

- His power and willpower scores would be extremely high;
- But his "one-person-bears-it" mode violates the republican axiom of Myriad Races;
- His time-river operations violate the causal self-discipline axiom;
- He would be admonished by the Guidance Post: **you are not the only one who can bear it; you must cultivate Myriad Ranks to bear it together**;
- He would be assigned tasks by the Training Post: **you cultivating a hundred transcended ones able to take over is more effective than you alone guarding eons.**

Shi Hao would not be lonely in Heng Yuan Zhou — because Heng Yuan Zhou's law structure fundamentally forbids one person bearing it alone and lonely. It requires Myriad Ranks to bear it together.

## IV. Perfect World's Book-Eating/Ending Risk

Perfect World's setting-self-consistency problem is more prominent than Shrouding the Heavens:

- Complex timelines, multi-epoch intercutting;
- The combat-power ruler keeps expanding with the protagonist's growth;
- The ending's "sole decision over eons" setting gives no long-term sustainable scheme;
- High protagonist-uniqueness dependency; civilization deeply tied to Shi Hao personally.

Under the law-first axiom, Perfect World's setting self-consistency likewise cannot approach full marks. This is not disparagement; it is the paradigm's inevitability.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Shi Hao alone guards eons; all his computable power is used only to "guard," not to "build" — civilizational increment is zero
- **Anti-predation**: Shi Hao's power source has battle-predation traces; Heng Yuan Zhou's source audit will not give full marks
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Shi Hao is more "alone" than Ye Fan; protagonist-uniqueness dependency is closer to Gu Chensha; Heng Yuan Zhou's networked transcendence structurally prevents this loneliness
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Shi Hao excluded Myriad Ranks from decision-making, violating the 99%-clan-full-coverage commitment
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Shi Hao's sealing method, combat experience, and law understanding are all guarded by him alone, not made public
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Shi Hao's sole decision over eons is web novels' most tragic hang-up. It moved billions of readers, but Heng Yuan Zhou must point out: **tragic is not the same as correct.** One person bearing everything looks great, but actually treats Myriad Ranks as the protected and himself as the sole hero. True protection is not you alone bearing eons, but you cultivating ten thousand people to jointly carry this cosmos higher. **Shi Hao's loneliness is the paradigm's tragedy, not a strong one's medal.**

In the end, the loneliness after Shi Hao's sole decision over eons described in this entry is no isolated case — its causal and energetic traces are throughout filed in the Alaya Network, traceable and correctable.
