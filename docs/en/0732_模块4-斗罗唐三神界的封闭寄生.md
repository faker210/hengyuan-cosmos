# Entry 732 | Critique of Hang-Up Retirement | Douluo's Tang San: The Closed Parasitism of the God Realm

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 47, 05

## Introduction: the most exquisite closed farm

If Ye Fan's hang-up is "the strong one lonely at the heights," and Shi Hao's hang-up is "tragically bearing it alone," then Tang San's God Realm is a more concealed, also more exquisite, kind of hang-up — **a closed parasitic farm.** By the Douluo series' Tales of the God Realm and Tang Clan Hero Legend, the God Realm has become the protagonist group and their descendants' private manor; beings of the mortal realm (Douluo Star) are locked beneath the God Realm, never climbing up. This is not transcendence; this is turning the entire civilization into the God Realm's nourishment farm.

Six top-level axioms in this entry act as the critique ruler: the anti-predation axiom is directly this critique's parent law; the republican axiom of Myriad Races asks on what basis the God Realm monopolizes the uplift channel; the collective ascension axiom points out the mortal realm is locked; the causal self-discipline axiom examines the God Realm's intervention on the mortal realm; the law-first axiom examines the Douluo system's book-eating/ending history.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Heng Yuan Zhou's Judgment of Tang San's God Realm

### 1.1 Closedness: A One-Way Valve Between Mortal Realm and God Realm

The Douluo system's God Realm is a closed structure:

- Mortal-realm cultivators wanting to become gods must pass the God Realm's selection;
- Selection standards are decided by existing gods in the God Realm;
- Divine positions are limited; old gods do not abdicate; new gods cannot rise;
- Once a God-Realm god becomes a god, he permanently dwells in the God Realm, decoupled from the mortal realm.

This is a **one-way valve**: the mortal realm may only send upward; the God Realm does not feed downward. Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad-Origin two-way cycle is here cut into one-way extraction.

### 1.2 Parasitism: The God Realm Is Nourished by the Mortal Realm

The God Realm's gods are not self-supporting. Their power, divine positions, and status are all built on the mortal realm's billions of beings' faith, cultivation, and sacrifice. The harder mortal-realm beings cultivate, the stronger the God Realm; but mortal-realm beings are forever locked under the ceiling of "cannot become a god."

This is the parasitism discussed in Entry 728: **the God Realm is the host; the mortal realm is the crop.** The crop is harvested when ripe; after harvest, it is planted again.

### 1.3 Protagonist-Uniqueness Dependency "High"

Per Glossary Article 50, the Douluo system's protagonist-uniqueness dependency rating is "high" (gift-style). Tang San, from being a two-lives person, cheats all the way, finally establishing the God Realm order. The entire God Realm revolves around the Tang family and around Tang San's will. No matter how many mortal-realm cultivators there are, they are only the God Realm's backdrop.

## II. The Douluo System's Book-Eating/Ending History

The Douluo system is the law-first axiom's most typical negative example:

- The first Douluo Continent's realm setting is constantly expanded in the second Peerless Tang Sect;
- The third Legend of the Dragon King and fourth Ultimate Douluo completely lose control of the combat-power ruler;
- The God Realm setting goes from nothing to something, from something to changed; each time it is temporarily adjusted for plot needs;
- Tang San's setting, from "transmigrator" to "God Realm enforcer" to "creation-level entity," eats its own books all the way.

This is the textbook case of Glossary Article 48's "book-eating/ending risk." The law-first axiom requires "never eat a book, never end in failure"; the Douluo system does the opposite — for plot convenience, it overturns its own setting again and again.

## III. What Happens If Tang San Enters Heng Yuan Zhou

Heng Yuan Zhou does not deny Tang San's personal ability. If Tang San were placed into Heng Yuan Zhou:

- His destructive-power score would be extremely high;
- But the "God Realm" structure he established violates the republican axiom of Myriad Races — on what basis do a few families monopolize divine positions?
- His extraction of the mortal realm violates the anti-predation axiom — is the mortal realm a crop?
- His repeatedly changing setting violates the law-first axiom — the Douluo system itself cannot pass the setting-self-consistency check;
- He would be reformed: either open the divine-position channel to Myriad Ranks and transform the God Realm into a nine-post-style public post, or be stripped of "divine-position monopoly," with the uplift channel taken over by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision.

Tang San in Heng Yuan Zhou would not be negated as a bad person, but the structure he established must be reformed.

## IV. The Douluo System's Pollution of Real-World Values

The Douluo system influenced a whole generation of readers. The values it transmits:

- The protagonist cheating is reasonable (two lives, Clear Sky Hammer twin spirits);
- The uplift channel is limited (limited divine positions);
- Those at the top monopolizing top positions is normal (God Realm eternal);
- Mortal-realm people cultivating hard is mainly for the chance to be chosen by those at the top.

This set of values seeping into reality is one of the cultural roots of "class solidification is natural." Heng Yuan Zhou Library's Module 4 opposes this set of values at the law level — **the uplift channel must cover 99%; the top must be open to all beings.**

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: mortal-realm billions' cultivation results are one-way-extracted by the God Realm; this is civilization-grade waste
- **Anti-predation**: the Douluo God Realm is the textbook sample of parasitic predation; Heng Yuan Zhou structurally prevents this ecology
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo is itself one of the critique targets; its protagonist-uniqueness dependency is "high"; Heng Yuan Zhou's networked transcendence is "extremely low"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the Douluo God Realm's divine positions are only open to a few chosen ones; Heng Yuan Zhou requires 99% of clans have roads
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the Douluo God Realm's manuals and law understanding only circulate within the God Realm; Heng Yuan Zhou requires the Selfless Dao be public across the whole network
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Tang San's God Realm is web-novel hang-up retirement's most exquisite, also most harmful, kind. It does not manifest as loneliness, does not manifest as tragedy, it manifests as "orderly" — but this order's essence is closed parasitism. Mortal-realm beings are locked in this order; God Realm gods hang up in this order. **What Heng Yuan Zhou must do is tear off this one-way valve and replace it with the two-way channel of the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle.**
