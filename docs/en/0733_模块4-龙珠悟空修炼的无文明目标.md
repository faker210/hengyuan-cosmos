# Entry 733 | Critique of Hang-Up Retirement | Dragon Ball's Goku: The Civilization-Goal-Less Cultivation

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 06, 26

## Introduction: the purest martial fan is also the purest goal-less

The last entry of 33 measures the ruler to a most special sample — Dragon Ball's Sun Wukong. Unlike Ye Fan, Shi Hao, and Tang San, Goku is not a predator, not an oligarch, not a parasitic farm owner. He is kind, upright, protects Earth, protects friends. But he has a problem Heng Yuan Zhou must point out: **he cultivated all his life, yet had no civilizational goal.** He grew strong to "fight stronger people," not to make anyone live better. His strength has no civilizational weight.

Six top-level axioms in this entry act as the critique ruler: the collective ascension axiom asks what he did after growing strong; the republican axiom of Myriad Races asks whom his strength benefited; the anti-predation axiom does not directly deny Goku, but asks whether his strength produces increments; the causal self-discipline axiom examines whether his battles disturbed causality; the law-first axiom examines Dragon Ball's setting self-consistency.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Heng Yuan Zhou's Judgment of Goku's "Cultivation"

### 1.1 A Pure Fighter, Purely Goal-Less

Goku's life is a pure fighter's life:

- He cultivated to grow strong;
- He grew strong to fight stronger enemies;
- After winning, he kept cultivating;
- Cultivated until the next enemy appeared, then fought.

There is no place for "civilization" in this loop. Earth was protected by him, but did Earth uplift because of his protection? No. Earth is still that Earth; technology is still that technology; people are still those people. Goku's strength did not convert into Earth's civilizational increment.

### 1.2 Power Source Relatively Clean, But Direction Empty

Unlike Ye Fan and Tang San, Goku's power source is relatively clean — he does not prey on the weak, does not monopolize knowledge, does not establish a God Realm. His power comes from cultivation, from life-and-death battles, from Saiyan talent. Per Heng Yuan Zhou's source audit, his energy purity gets a passing score.

But his direction gets zero. His strength serves no civilizational engineering, no Myriad-Rank uplift, no perpetual construction. Per Entry 706's formula: destructive power × civilizational consensus degree; his destructive power is enormous, but his civilizational consensus degree is near zero in the "direction correctness" sub-item — he does not know why he is strong, except "fighting is fun."

### 1.3 Protagonist-Uniqueness Dependency Between "Low" and "Extremely Low"

Dragon Ball's protagonist-uniqueness dependency in Heng Yuan Zhou's rating system is actually not high. Why? Because Dragon Ball's worldview is relatively open — Goku is not the only strong one; Vegeta, Piccolo, Gohan, Trunks, Son Goten... a whole crowd of Z-fighters are growing. Goku is not an oligarch; he is "the strongest companion." This is healthier than Ye Fan and Tang San.

But healthy does not mean qualified. Dragon Ball's problem is not "the protagonist is too alone," but "the protagonist is too strong yet does not know why he is strong."

## II. Goku's Goal-Lessness: Three Problems from Heng Yuan Zhou's View

### 2.1 Civilizational Increment Is Zero

Goku protected Earth for decades; Earth's civilizational level did not progress because of his protection. He beat Raditz, Vegeta, Frieza, Cell, Buu, Jiren... after every win, Earth is still that Earth. His strength did not convert into Earth's technological uplift, did not convert into Earthlings' cultivation popularization, did not convert into any civilizational engineering.

### 2.2 Failure to Cultivate Later Generations

Goku has sons Gohan and Goten, and a disciple Oob. But his cultivation of later generations is "bringing them along to fight," not "cultivating successors." Gohan originally wanted to be a scholar, pulled back by Goku to fight Cell; Goten's talent is extremely high, but Goku did not systematically cultivate him as "the next-generation guardian." Goku's cultivation is individualistic — he hopes later generations also be strong, but he does not know how to make them strong with direction.

### 2.3 Knowledge Not Made Public

Goku's combat experience, his understanding of ki, his mastery of Instant Transmission are all in his own head. He did not write them into teaching materials, did not enter any "Selfless-Dao public library." Earthlings wanting to cultivate can only fumble on their own — because Goku never teaches.

## III. What Happens If Goku Enters Heng Yuan Zhou

Heng Yuan Zhou very much likes people like Goku — kind, upright, not predatory, not monopolistic. If Goku were placed into Heng Yuan Zhou:

- He would be spotted at first glance by the Myriad-Rank Evolution Guidance Post (713): this is born Guidance-Post material;
- He would be assigned to the Guidance Post, specifically mentoring those young cultivators "who only know fighting but not why they fight";
- He would be assigned by the Training Post (720) to take disciples, writing his combat experience into teaching materials;
- He would be invited by the Increment Development Post (717) to participate in "new combat-system design" — converting his pure combat talent into teachable cultivation paths;
- He would discover: **it turns out that after growing strong, one may do so many things.**

Goku would not lose his combat passion in Heng Yuan Zhou. He would discover that fighting is no longer only "fighting stronger people," but "growing strong to protect Myriad Ranks." His purity would be directed toward perpetual construction.

## IV. Dragon Ball's Book-Eating/Ending Risk

Dragon Ball is a Japanese manga, not a web novel, but its paradigm influenced the entire web-novel world. Its setting-self-consistency problems:

- The combat-power ruler, from "beating King Piccolo" to "beating Frieza" to "beating Cell" to "beating Buu" to "beating Jiren," expands every step;
- Super Saiyan forms from 1 to 2 to 3 to God to Blue to Ultra Instinct, constantly added;
- Power-level numbers are basically abandoned after the Frieza arc, judged by "feeling."

Under the law-first axiom, Dragon Ball's setting self-consistency likewise cannot approach full marks. This is not disparagement; it is the inevitability of a long battle manga.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Goku's lifelong combat talent did not convert into civilizational increment; this is enormous energy waste
- **Anti-predation**: Goku's source is clean; Heng Yuan Zhou does not deny him — but his direction must be guided
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Goku's protagonist-uniqueness dependency is lower than Tang-Luo, but his civilizational weight is zero — strong without direction, like strong with predation, is equally unqualified
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Goku's strength only serves Earth, not benefiting Myriad Ranks — Heng Yuan Zhou requires 99% coverage
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Goku's combat experience is in his own head, not entering the Selfless Dao — Heng Yuan Zhou requires everything public
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The last entry of 33 lands on Goku to make one thing clear: **hang-up retirement is not equal to predation, not equal to oligarchy, not equal to parasitism. It may also be a kind, upright, non-predatory strong one who only knows fighting all his life, not knowing why he fights.** This kind of person is most worth sympathizing with, also most worth guiding — he is not a bad person; he simply has not found his civilizational goal. Heng Yuan Zhou's Guidance Post and Training Post are prepared for such people.

**This is the final conclusion of Module 4's 33 entries: true transcendence is, after you grow strong, knowing for whom you grow strong, why you grow strong, and what you do after growing strong.** Ye Fan, Shi Hao, Tang San, Goku — they are all strong, but none of them "transcends" in Heng Yuan Zhou's sense. Lin Hao and his generation of anchors walk another road: grow strong, then carry Myriad Ranks along.

In the end, the civilization-goal-less cultivation of Dragon Ball's Goku described in this entry is no isolated case — its causal and energetic traces are throughout filed in the Alaya Network, traceable and correctable.
