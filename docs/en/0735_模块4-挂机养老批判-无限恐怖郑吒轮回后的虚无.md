# Entry 735 | Critique of Hang-Up Retirement | Infinite Horror's Zheng Zha: The Nihilism After Breaking Reincarnation

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 82, 89

## Introduction: after punching through reincarnation, why only emptiness remains

Infinite Horror's narrative core throws protagonist Zheng Zha into an infinitely looping horror-dungeon system, struggling between life and death. By the story's thrill logic, Zheng Zha finally punching through the Main God Space, breaking reincarnation, and returning to reality is the happy ending. But under the scrutiny of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, after Zheng-Zha-style "punching through reincarnation," one precisely falls into another deeper pit: **the shackles of reincarnation are shattered, but no new construction goal takes over; only nihilism remains.**

Six top-level axioms back this entry: the anti-predation axiom examines whether his power source is built on dungeon slaughter; the republican axiom of Myriad Races examines whether "punching through reincarnation" benefits all trapped; the collective ascension axiom examines whether he lifted the entire trapped population out of reincarnation; the causal self-discipline axiom examines whether his breaking-reincarnation process disturbed causal chains; the law-first axiom examines whether the "reincarnation system" as a law device is self-consistent in its very definition.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Psychological Structure of Zheng-Zha-Style Nihilism

Zheng Zha's state after punching through reincarnation is not happiness, but emptiness. This nihilism has three psychological sources:

1. **Goal vacuum**: from day one in the Main God Space, "survive, grow strong, punch through the Main God" was the only goal. The moment the goal is achieved, over a decade of life's meaning suddenly hangs in midair;
2. **Comrades scattering**: partners who went through life and death together go their separate ways after reincarnation ends; the social network zeroes overnight;
3. **Skill mismatch**: the killing skills, team command, and extreme-survival ability cultivated in reincarnation are completely useless back in real society. A full set of dragon-slaying techniques, nowhere to be used.

The three stacked are essentially a psychological collapse after a "high-intensity stress system is suddenly withdrawn." Old web novels romanticize this collapse as "indifferent to fame and gain" or "retreat to the countryside," but in Heng Yuan Zhou's view this is a **typical pre-hang-up state** — the person has not yet lain flat, but the heart has.

## II. Law Critique of the Reincarnation System Itself

The deeper problem than Zheng Zha's personal nihilism is the Main God Space's definition as a reincarnation device at the law level. Per the law-first axiom, we must ask:

- **Who has the right to design reincarnation?** The Main God Space grabbing ordinary people as dungeon NPCs is essentially cross-plane population kidnapping, violating the causal self-discipline axiom;
- **Where does reincarnation's energy come from?** Energy generated by life-and-death battles in dungeons ultimately flows to the Main God, not the participants; this is typical reincarnation harvest — feeding a superior with beings' fear and death;
- **After punching through reincarnation?** The old story only answers "shatter"; it does not answer "what new order replaces it after shattering." Smashing the old machine is not building the new one.

Heng Yuan Zhou's characterization of the reincarnation system is very clear: **any reincarnation device that harvests beings' fear, death, and struggle as its energy source is a variant of predatory cultivation; it must be dismantled, not optimized.** After dismantling, the originally trapped beings are not "each going home," but enter Myriad Ranks' republican normal cultivation system, walking the right way of the 300-set Origin Cultivation System anew.

## III. The Real Root of Nihilism: No Perpetual-Construction Goal

The root disease of Zheng-Zha-style nihilism is that his entire life goal is "oppositional" — opposing the Main God, opposing monsters, opposing teammate betrayal. Oppositional goals have this trait: the instant the enemy disappears, the goal disappears. What the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization requires high-order individuals to have is **constructive goals** — construct new manuals, calibrate new laws, cultivate new successors, open new planes. Constructive goals have this trait: they do not depend on enemies; the more you work, the larger they grow; the more you work, the stronger you get.

| Dimension | Oppositional Goal (Zheng-Zha-Style) | Constructive Goal (Heng-Yuan-Zhou-Style) |
|-----------|------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Driving force | Fear, anger, revenge | Curiosity, responsibility, creativity |
| After the enemy vanishes | Goal vacuum, nihilism | Goal upgrades, keep building |
| Impact on Myriad Ranks | Zero-sum, life-or-death | Positive-sum, common increment |
| Ultimate form | Idle after fighting | Perpetual iteration |

If Zheng Zha were in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, his team-command ability, extreme adaptability, and grasp of life-death rhythm could all convert into a Quest-Sediment-Domain trial mentor or an Origin-Acceleration-Domain project commander. He would not need to "punch through the Main God" to find meaning; from day one he is given a construction goal, so there is no "after fighting, not knowing what to do" problem.

## IV. The Alaya Network's Repair of Reincarnation Trauma

What Zheng Zha brings out of reincarnation is not only a full set of skills, but a full set of trauma: PTSD, trust disorders, pessimistic expectations of human nature. In the old system, these traumas could only self-heal over time, even never heal. In Heng Yuan Zhou, the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision provides a systematic trauma-repair mechanism:

- **Causal-retrospection repair**: through the observe-first-then-limit-interfere principle, safely retrospective his trauma nodes, not changing historical facts, but changing his emotional response to history;
- **Symbiotic psychological aid**: the consciousness-information clan among Myriad Ranks excels at deep psychological healing, free to all reincarnation survivors;
- **Rebuilding the goal chain**: the Anchor Council's youth-development department custom-designs a constructive goal chain for him, escalating from short-term projects to long-term civilizational engineering.

The repair's endpoint is not making him "forget the past," but re-anchoring the extreme abilities cultivated in reincarnation onto a constructive direction.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Zheng Zha's full set of extreme-survival and team-command hard power, idling in nihilism, is typical big-talent-small-use; Heng Yuan Zhou converts this ability into civilizational-engineering command power
- **Anti-predation**: the Main God Space itself is a reincarnation-harvest device; Zheng Zha's power grew in a predatory system, and must go through Alaya Network source-tracing cleansing before entering the right way
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system traps people in a reincarnation-style gift chain; Zheng-Zha-style breaking reincarnation without a new order — both are "old machine dismantled, new machine not built"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Zheng Zha's trauma-repair path is open to all reincarnation survivors, regardless of race or plane; 99% Myriad-Rank coverage
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: all experience summarized from reincarnation systems on "how to resist harvest-type Main Gods" is opened across the Selfless-Dao network for all trapped planes' reference
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Zheng-Zha-style nihilism is not a strong one's privilege, but the old system's last trap for those who reach the summit: it makes you think you've beaten everything, when actually you've only beaten the enemy, and have not yet learned to build your own civilization. True transcendence is not staring blankly after shattering reincarnation, but immediately opening a new symbiotic order after shattering reincarnation — this is the meaning of the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's existence.
