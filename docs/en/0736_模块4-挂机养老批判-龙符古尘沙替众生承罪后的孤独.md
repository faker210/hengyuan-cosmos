# Entry 736 | Critique of Hang-Up Retirement | Dragon Talisman's Gu Chensha: The Loneliness After Bearing Sin for All Beings

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 03, 04, 49

## Introduction: one person bearing all sin — why is it not heroism but a disease

In the Dragon Talisman system, Gu Chensha is the extreme sample of a "single-pole" strong one — he must not only beat all enemies, but also bear sin for the entire world, carrying all beings' evil karma, causality, and guilt on his own shoulders. By web-novel narrative's romantic brushwork, this is "if I do not enter hell, who enters hell" highest compassion. But under the scrutiny of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, this choice of "bearing sin for all beings" is precisely the most concealed and most dangerous kind in the hang-up-retirement critique library: **it turns the strong one from a civilization co-builder into a lonely scapegoat, then drifts into closure within loneliness.**

Six top-level axioms must all be named: the anti-predation axiom examines whether "one person bearing sin" in disguised form exempts beings from their own responsibility; the republican axiom of Myriad Races examines whether this arrangement treats beings as infants needing salvation rather than equal co-builders; the collective ascension axiom examines whether one person bearing sin truly carries Myriad Ranks forward; the causal self-discipline axiom examines whether cross-plane causality may be borne by an individual alone; the law-first axiom examines whether "sin-bearing" as a law device is self-consistent in definition.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Three Logical Misunderstandings of Gu-Chensha-Style Sin-Bearing

Gu Chensha bearing sin for all beings, opened up, has three logical misunderstandings:

1. **Responsibility misplacement**: beings' evil karma is the causal consequence of their own actions; the strong one moving these consequences onto himself looks compassionate in the short term, but in the long term deprives beings of the chance to learn and grow from their own mistakes. It is equal to taking the exam papers for all examinees onto his own back; examinees never learn how to solve problems again;
2. **Causal monopolization**: one person bearing all beings' causality is equal to establishing an autocratic center in the causal network. Per the law-first axiom, the causal network must be distributed, not monopolized by a single point — otherwise once this point fails, the entire causal chain collapses;
3. **Emotional isolation**: long-term bearing pain, sin, and fear for beings creates a huge emotional gulf between the strong one and beings. It is not that he does not love beings, but that he is too far from them — too far to sense what beings truly need, only able to substitute "I think you need" for "you truly need."

The three misunderstandings stacked ultimately lead to Gu-Chensha-style loneliness: he stands at the highest point, carrying all beings' sin, yet no one can truly stand shoulder to shoulder with him. This loneliness is not a virtue, but a **governance-structure disease**.

## II. Why "One Person Bears It" Is Worse Than "Many Bear It"

Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad-Rank Republican logic and Gu-Chensha-style solitary-bearing logic are fundamentally opposed in governance structure:

| Dimension | Gu-Chensha-Style Solitary Bearing | Anchor-Style Co-Governance |
|-----------|-----------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Causal bearing | One person carries all karma | Distributed bearing, each responsible for own |
| Learning chance | Beings never learn | Beings grow through making mistakes |
| Risk resistance | Single-point failure = total collapse | Multi-layer supervision, mutual backup |
| Strong-one mindset | Lonely, exhausted, arrogant | Needed, accompanied, supervised |
| Final result | The strong one grows larger, beings grow smaller | The stronger the strong one, the stronger beings also |

The fatal problem of the Gu-Chensha model: it looks like saving beings, but actually **castrates beings' growth ability**. When beings get used to "someone will carry it for me anyway," they will not develop their own conscience, repair their own causality, or build their own civilization. Thousands of years later, the strong one is still carrying, and beings are still infants — this is not symbiosis; this is **permanent guardianship**.

## III. How Loneliness Degenerates into Hang-Up

Gu-Chensha-style loneliness will not stay at the emotional level of "lonely"; it will degenerate into hang-up retirement along a clear path:

1. **Stage One: Exhaustion** — carried too long, begins to feel beings are unworthy: "I carried so much for you, yet you don't even appreciate it";
2. **Stage Two: Isolation** — reduces direct contact with beings, only learns about the situation through subordinates; information begins to distort;
3. **Stage Three: Autocracy** — since you don't understand, I'll decide alone; you just follow orders;
4. **Stage Four: Hang-Up** — too lazy even to decide; sets a set of automatically running rules, retreats behind the scenes;
5. **Stage Five: Vacuum** — rules age, no one updates; civilization begins to rot from within.

This path repeats across web novels: Gu Chensha, Ji Ning, and more "compassion-type" summit-reachers almost all walk through these five steps. The only difference is time length. Heng Yuan Zhou lists "hang-up retirement" as the top pseudo-transcendence precisely because it is most concealed — wearing compassion's coat, doing evasion's work.

## IV. Heng Yuan Zhou's Alternative: Distributed Causal Bearing

The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's answer to "what to do when beings have evil karma" is not to find one strong one to bear it, but to establish a distributed causal-bearing mechanism:

- **Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision**: every being's causality is recorded, tracked, and has repair paths in the network; no individual needs to bear it on behalf of others;
- **Myriad-Rank Republican co-construction**: beings themselves participate in legislation, governance, and causal repair, learning in practice to be responsible for their own actions;
- **Symbiotic court adjudication**: evil-karma settlement goes through judicial procedure, adjudicated by a jury of Myriad-Rank representatives, not morally monopolized by one strong one;
- **Anchor Council legislation**: prevent evil karma from being produced at the institutional level, rather than wiping up after others.

If Gu Chensha appeared in Heng Yuan Zhou, his "bearing sin for all beings" compassion would not be denied, but would be re-directed: he is no longer a scapegoat, but **chief engineer of causal-repair engineering** — he designs institutions, trains teams, builds networks, letting tens of millions of beings themselves have the ability to repair their own causality. This is true compassion: not walking for people, but helping people grow legs.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Gu Chensha using all his computable power to scapegoat for beings, rather than to build institutions that let beings stand up on their own, is the most efficient energy waste
- **Anti-predation**: one person bearing sin looks compassionate, but actually in disguised form predates beings' right to grow — he takes beings' origin increment of "learning from mistakes"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system locks beings through gifts; the Gu-Chensha system locks beings through sin-bearing; different means, same result: beings are infants forever
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: distributed causal bearing is open equally to all beings; plant spirits and planet consciousnesses have their own causal-repair paths, not needing others to bear on their behalf
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: causal-repair institutional design, technical schemes, and historical cases are all opened across the Selfless-Dao network, not Gu Chensha's personal patent
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Bearing sin for all beings is not transcendence, but a kind of monopolism wearing compassion's coat — monopolizing the right to interpret pain, monopolizing the right to bear causality, monopolizing the right to adjudge "who is good and who is bad." True transcendence is not one person carrying the whole world, but letting everyone in the world have the ability to carry themselves.
