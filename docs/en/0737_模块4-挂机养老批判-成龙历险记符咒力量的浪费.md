# Entry 737 | Critique of Hang-Up Retirement | Jackie Chan Adventures: The Waste of Talisman Power

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 18, 47

## Introduction: twelve talismans, why are they only toys

The twelve talismans in Jackie Chan Adventures are the concrete carriers of twelve supernatural powers — Rat animation, Ox brute force, Tiger balance, Rabbit speed, Dragon explosive force, Snake invisibility, Horse healing, Sheep soul, Monkey shapeshifting, Chicken flight, Dog immortality, Pig lightning. Per setting, any one of these twelve powers, taken out, is enough to change a plane's civilizational trajectory. But in the original narrative, what did they finally become? They became "treasures" fought over, sealed, hidden; became props the villains fight over and the protagonist hides from.

Six top-level axioms must all be named: the anti-predation axiom examines the talismans' acquisition method; the republican axiom of Myriad Races examines whether these powers are open to all beings; the collective ascension axiom examines whether they were used to uplift the whole civilization; the causal self-discipline axiom examines whether talisman use disturbed plane causality; the law-first axiom examines whether the twelve talismans' law-level definition is self-consistent.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Four-Layer Structure of Talisman-Power Waste

The twelve talismans' waste is not "no one uses them," but "used in the wrong place." Opened up, four layers:

1. **Fighting-use**: talismans become villains' fighting prizes; whoever grabs them is tough, not whoever grabs them is responsible. Power becomes a chip of power, not a civilization's tool;
2. **Sealing-preservation**: after the protagonist gets talismans, the optimal move is to seal, hide, and let no one use them. This is equal to locking twelve civilization-changing powers in a safe on the grounds of "fear bad guys will use them";
3. **Individualized use**: each talisman's power is experienced by only a single user, not systematically disassembled, taught, or engineered. The Horse talisman's healing power did not develop into a Myriad-Rank-shared medical system; the Dog talisman's immortality did not develop into civilization-level life-extension engineering;
4. **Binary narrative**: talismans are either used by "good guys" as heroes or by "bad guys" to do evil; there is no third option — disassemble the talismans' power into law principles, making technology everyone may use.

## II. Why "Sealing Treasures" Is the Greatest Energy Waste

In old web novels and popular narrative, the most common plot is "protagonist gets treasure → seals it → villain comes to fight over it → protagonist wins → seals again." This loop itself is the greatest energy waste. Per Heng Yuan Zhou's standards:

- The **Horse talisman's healing power**, if disassembled into law principles, could be engineered into a Myriad-Rank-shared symbiotic medical system (benchmark Document 38), giving all beings stable self-healing ability, not locked in a small pebble;
- The **Dog talisman's immortality**, if incorporated into the Life-Cycle system's hyper-multiverse application, could be reformed into civilization-level longevity engineering, but must be paired with the constraint that "immortals must take on perpetual construction," otherwise it is a hotbed for hang-up retirement;
- The **Dragon talisman's explosive force**, if incorporated into the Spatial-Law Dispatch system, could be engineered into cosmos-level mining and stellar-engineering tools, not used for fighting;
- The **Snake talisman's invisibility**, if absorbed by consciousness-uplift systems, could develop into cross-plane information-concealment technology, protecting weak civilizations from being discovered by predators.

Every talisman sealed is equal to one civilization-level technology shelved. Twelve talismans sealed means twelve civilizational evolution paths simultaneously severed.

## III. The Deep Root Disease Exposed by Talisman Narrative: Power Privatization

The essence of the talisman problem is not whether talismans are too strong or too weak, but **power privatization**. In the original setting, talismans are "treasures" — whoever holds them owns them; others who want to use them must grab. Behind this is a deeply rooted old-thought: power is private property; the owner has the right to decide whether to let others use it.

Under Heng Yuan Zhou's law framework, this logic directly violates the anti-predation axiom and the republican axiom of Myriad Races:

- Any power sufficient to change civilizational trajectory, once its principles are discovered, must be public across the network;
- Power users must pass Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision ethical review, not "whoever grabs uses";
- Power's returns must feed back to Myriad Ranks, not become an individual hero's capital;
- It is forbidden to permanently seal power on the grounds of "fear bad guys use it" — the correct move is to make principles public, build supervision mechanisms, let Myriad Ranks all use it, not lock it up.

This is not naive. Per Anti-Monopoly Law (benchmark Document 47) logic, monopolization of any key technology causes civilizational rigidity; likewise, sealing any key power causes energy waste.

## IV. Heng Yuan Zhou's Talisman Reconstruction Scheme

If the twelve talismans appeared in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, their fate would be:

1. **Principle disassembly**: expert teams of consciousness-uplift, Life-Cycle, and Spatial-Law Dispatch among the 300-set Origin Cultivation System jointly disassemble each talisman's law encoding;
2. **Technologization**: transform talisman power from "carrier-dependent" to "replicable technology," no longer needing to hold the talisman itself;
3. **Network-wide openness**: technical schemes are freely open to Myriad Ranks through the Selfless Dao; any race, any individual may learn;
4. **Engineering application**: each talisman's power corresponds to a civilizational-engineering direction (medical, longevity, stellar engineering, information protection, etc.), incorporated into hyper-multiverse-grade civilizational-engineering projects;
5. **Supervision mechanism**: individuals using these powers are monitored throughout by the Alaya Network, preventing abuse.

After these five steps, talismans change from "contested treasures" into "civilization's public infrastructure." They will no longer be fought over by villains, because their principles already belong to everyone — you cannot steal knowledge that is already public.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: twelve civilization-level powers sealed, contested, and individually used is systemic waste of law resources; Heng Yuan Zhou engineers and publicizes them all
- **Anti-predation**: talisman narrative's underlogic is "whoever grabs uses," essentially power privatization; Heng Yuan Zhou requires all power principles be public across the network
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system locks power in a few hands through gift-style monopolization; talisman narrative locks power in a safe through "sealing treasures" — different tunes, equal artistry
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: talisman-power engineering applications cover 99% of Myriad Ranks; alien beasts, plant spirits, and planet consciousnesses all benefit from Horse-talisman-style self-healing technology
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: talisman law-principle disassembly reports are all opened across the Selfless-Dao network; hyper-multiverse-grade knowledge is no longer privately hoarded
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Talisans are not evil; sealing talismans is. Locking civilization-changing powers on the grounds of "fear bad guys use them" is equal to removing all lightbulbs on the grounds of "fear people will get shocked." The true solution is not sealing, but learning to use power safely — this is precisely what Heng Yuan Zhou's 300-set Origin Cultivation System and Alaya Network are designed to solve.
