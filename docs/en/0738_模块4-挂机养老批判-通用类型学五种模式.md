# Entry 738 | Critique of Hang-Up Retirement | General Typology — Five Hang-Up-Retirement Modes

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 17, 701

## Introduction: five ways to lie flat, the same root disease no matter how they vary

Entries 734–737 critiqued four concrete samples: Ji-Ning-style closure, Zheng-Zha-style nihilism, Gu-Chensha-style loneliness, talisman-style waste. But hang-up retirement is not an individual protagonist's accidental choice; it is a **cross-work, cross-plane, cross-setting universal phenomenon**. To systematically oppose it, we must distill its general typology — exactly how many "lying-flat poses" of hang-up retirement? What are each pose's identifying features, degeneration path, and civilizational cost?

Six top-level axioms here act as classification rulers: the anti-predation axiom judges each mode's power source; the republican axiom of Myriad Races judges each mode's degree of exclusion of Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom judges whether each mode carries Myriad Ranks forward; the causal self-discipline axiom judges whether each mode disturbs causality; the law-first axiom judges whether each mode is self-consistent at the law level.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Overview of the Five Hang-Up-Retirement Modes

Through Alaya Network cluster analysis of histories across planes, hang-up-retirement strong ones may be summarized into five general modes:

| No. | Mode Name | Core Action | Typical Sample |
|-----|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| A | Garden-Closure Type | Build a small world, retire with relatives/friends | Ji Ning, various Immortal Sovereigns retiring |
| B | Goal-Vacuum Type | No new goal after punching through enemies | Zheng Zha, reincarnation-system protagonists' endings |
| C | Single-Bearer Scapegoat Type | One person carries all karma, lonely self-consumption | Gu Chensha, compassionate saviors |
| D | Treasure-Sealing Type | Lock power up, refuse use | Talisman holders, treasure guardians |
| E | Rule-Auto Type | Set a set of rules, then wash hands and leave | Creators who set up Heavenly Dao and leave |

These five modes are not mutually exclusive; a strong one may stack two or three — for example, Gu Chensha is simultaneously C-type and A-type, both single-bearing and closed. But classification should still be by primary type.

## II. Type A: Garden-Closure Type

**Identifying features**: the strong one dwells long-term in a self-built independent space (small world, cave-heaven, eternal kingdom), maintaining physical and information distance from the original plane's beings.

**Degeneration path**:
1. Early stage: beautifully called "closed-door Dao-enlightenment," still periodically coming out to handle affairs;
2. Mid stage: all affairs handed to subordinates, only hearing reports; information begins to distort;
3. Late stage: completely no longer coming out; subordinates become new separatist forces;
4. Endgame: the small world decouples from the original plane; the original plane rots in a vacuum.

**Civilizational cost**: the strongest computable power goes offline; the law network loses a key anchor; predatory factions seize the chance.

**Counter-measure**: Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's living-anchor system — the strong one must take law-calibration duty for a star sector; his "closed-door Dao-enlightenment" results must be backfilled in real time through the Alaya Network, not offline.

## III. Type B: Goal-Vacuum Type

**Identifying features**: the strong one's entire life goals are oppositional (beat some enemy, break some system); after the goal is achieved, he falls into a goal-less state.

**Degeneration path**:
1. Early stage: brief emptiness after victory, beautifully called "relaxation";
2. Mid stage: starts finding new enemies to fight, but cannot find a worthy opponent;
3. Late stage: attention turns to personal indulgence (food, collecting, reproduction);
4. Endgame: becomes a pure hedonist, completely losing interest in public civilizational affairs.

**Civilizational cost**: construction ability is zero, even negative — to seek entertainment he may disturb plane causality.

**Counter-measure**: constructive goal-chain system — the strong one is implanted with long-term civilizational-engineering goals from Sense-Origin Realm onward, so there is no "after fighting, not knowing what to do" problem.

## IV. Type C: Single-Bearer Scapegoat Type

**Identifying features**: the strong one takes all beings' responsibility, causality, and suffering onto himself, beautifully called "compassion."

**Degeneration path**:
1. Early stage: sincerely wanting to share beings' burden;
2. Mid stage: discovering beings are "not up to it," beginning to despise beings' ability;
3. Late stage: changing from "bearing for beings" to "deciding for beings";
4. Endgame: becomes a de facto dictator, yet still feeling like a compassionate one.

**Civilizational cost**: beings' right to grow is deprived; the distributed causal network is monopolized by a single point; risk-resistance zeroed.

**Counter-measure**: distributed causal-bearing mechanism — causal repair goes through Myriad-Rank co-governance procedure; no individual may bear on behalf of others.

## V. Type D: Treasure-Sealing Type

**Identifying features**: the strong one holds civilization-level power (treasures, talismans, manuals, law fragments), but permanently seals it on the grounds of "fear of abuse."

**Degeneration path**:
1. Early stage: cautious use, only striking at critical moments;
2. Mid stage: finds a reason to completely seal, "rather not release than release wrongly";
3. Late stage: the sealing site becomes a new fighting focal point, instead inviting new war;
4. Endgame: the power ages within the seal, finally dissipating on its own; no one uses it.

**Civilizational cost**: civilization-level technology is permanently shelved; beings cannot benefit from it.

**Counter-measure**: power-principle network-wide openness system — sealing is not the answer; openness + supervision is.

## VI. Type E: Rule-Auto Type

**Identifying features**: after the strong one sets a set of Heavenly-Dao rules, reincarnation system, or causal laws, he believes "rules will run themselves," washes his hands and leaves.

**Degeneration path**:
1. Early stage: rules run well; everything looks automatic;
2. Mid stage: plane environment changes; rules begin to mismatch, but no one revises;
3. Late stage: rules age into shackles; beings are bound by old rules yet no one can change them;
4. Endgame: the rules themselves become the largest predation machine (old Heavenly Dao devours beings); the creator has long since vanished.

**Civilizational cost**: rules lack iteration mechanism; civilization is trapped by rules it itself made.

**Counter-measure**: law-revision democratic consultation mechanism + Alaya Network rule-self-consistency audit — rules are forever alive, requiring continuous revision and maintenance.

## VII. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the five modes are essentially all pulling overflowing computable power off the civilizational-increment production line; they are five faces of the same waste
- **Anti-predation**: each mode ultimately vacates a vacuum for predatory factions — Type A vacates space, Type B vacates goals, Type C vacates causality, Type D vacates technology, Type E vacates rules
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system is "gift-style monopolization"; the five hang-up modes are "lying flat after monopolizing" — the former locks beings out; the latter locks the strong one in
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the victims of the five modes are all Myriad Ranks — closed out the door, abandoned by vacuum, deprived of growth rights, shelved technology, trapped by aged rules
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the five modes' counter-measures all depend on hyper-multiverse-grade institutions — living anchors, goal chains, distributed causality, principle openness, law audit
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning
