# Entry 739 | Critique of Hang-Up Retirement | The Causal Chain of Hang-Up Retirement and Civilizational Reversal

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 82, 89

## Introduction: once the strong one retires, civilization reverses

The previous hang-up-retirement-critique entries focused on "the strong one personally" — Ji Ning closed the door, Zheng Zha stared blankly, Gu Chensha bore it alone, talismans were sealed. But hang-up retirement's true destructive power has never been only personal. It is like a row of dominoes: the first strong one lies down, knocking over the entire civilization's evolutionary direction. This entry draws the complete causal chain — from a strong one choosing to hang up to a civilization reversing into reincarnation harvest, exactly which steps in between?

Six top-level axioms here are the five rulers of this causal chain: the anti-predation axiom judges whether each step on the chain feeds predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races judges whether each step excludes beings; the collective ascension axiom judges whether each step blocks uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom judges whether cross-plane disturbance is controllable; the law-first axiom judges whether the entire causal chain is self-consistent and deducible at the law level.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Causal-Chain Overview: Seven Steps from Hang-Up to Reversal

Through Alaya Network retrospective analysis of histories across planes, the complete causal chain by which hang-up retirement causes civilizational reversal has seven steps:

| Step | Link | Core Event | Time Scale |
|------|------|------------|------------|
| 1 | Strong one goes offline | The high-position one chooses closed-door/retreat/single-bearing/sealing | Instant |
| 2 | Law vacuum | Star sectors that lost living anchors begin law drift | Centuries |
| 3 | Knowledge discontinuity | Peak Dao no longer public; later-comers have no road | Millennia |
| 4 | Power separatism | Mid-order strong ones fill the vacuum, each occupying a region | Tens of millennia |
| 5 | Predation rises | Separatists begin expanding by predation | Hundreds of millennia |
| 6 | Reincarnation solidifies | The predatory system self-hardens, forming reincarnation-harvest cycle | Millions of years |
| 7 | Civilizational reversal | The whole plane reverses from symbiotic civilization to predatory civilization | Eons |

The most frightening thing about this chain: **every step is not violent, every step "looks okay," but after seven steps, the plane is unrecognizable.**

## II. First Three Steps: From Offline to Discontinuity

**Step one · strong one offline** is the starting point of the whole chain. Reasons for choosing hang-up vary — tired, empty, lonely, fearing abuse — but the result is the same: the mightiest computable power is pulled off the public construction line. This step looks like a personal choice, but in the plane's law network, one Balanced-Anchor-level anchor going offline equals law calibration stopping across a star sector.

**Step two · law vacuum** appears within centuries. Without a living anchor continuously calibrating, local laws begin to drift: gravitational constant微调, uneven time flow, uneven spirit-energy density. These drifts are negligible early on, but their accumulation direction is **systematic disorder** — not that some one law is broken, but that the whole law network's coordination is declining.

**Step three · knowledge discontinuity** completes within millennia. After the strong one enters closed-door cultivation, his peak Dao, his understanding of laws, his self-invented cultivation system all stop updating and going public. Later Youth Supremes can only fumble through fragments; the ceiling is lower every generation. Ten thousand years later, this plane's peak combat power may be only one-tenth of pre-hang-up — not because talent worsened, but because **knowledge broke**.

## III. Middle Two Steps: From Separatism to Predation

**Step four · power separatism** plays out on the ten-thousand-year scale. After law vacuum and knowledge discontinuity stack, mid-order strong ones within the plane discover: the one sitting at the top no longer works; that position is empty — who sits? So various mid-order strong ones begin to separate a region, each building a sphere of influence. They may still fly the banner of "inheriting the predecessors' legacy," but essentially they are dividing the cake.

**Step five · predation rises** completes on the hundred-thousand-year scale. Competition among separatists quickly degrades from "who builds better" to "who grabs more." Because no living anchor at the top maintains anti-predation laws, separatists discover predatory cultivation is far faster and more satisfying than symbiotic cultivation. So the entire plane's cultivation atmosphere turns sharply: devouring others' energy, seizing resource veins, treating weak races as blood food — these predatory means suppressed before hanging up all rekindle.

## IV. Last Two Steps: From Solidification to Reversal

**Step six · reincarnation solidifies** completes on the million-year scale. The predatory system is not static; it self-hardens: predators build a whole set of "reasonable" institutions — Heavenly Dao, reincarnation, karmic retribution — to prove their predation is "Heaven-ordained." Beings are trained into subjects who accept "the weak deserve to be eaten"; the strong are trained into butchers who accept "might makes right is law." The whole plane enters a steady state: reincarnation harvest, cycle after cycle; no one remembers there once was a symbiotic civilization.

**Step seven · civilizational reversal** completes on the eon scale. This is when an Alaya Network archaeological team (benchmark Document 84) comes to survey; they find this plane's civilizational level is even lower than pre-hang-up: technology regressed, laws chaotic, beings benighted, predation solidified. A plane that once produced a Balanced-Anchor-level strong one has completely become a reincarnation-harvest ground.
