# Entry 741 | Hyper-Multiverse-Grade Manuals · Overview — The Infinitely Iterating Manual System

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 10, 11, 91

## Introduction: manuals are not classics, but a living production line

Manuals across web novels mostly exist in "classic" form — a secret manual, a set of formulas, a manual, finalized the day it is created; later generations just practice it. This "classic view" is completely obsolete under Heng Yuan Zhou's hyper-multiverse-grade view. Manuals are not scriptures carved on stone steles, but a **living production line**: every day new raw materials (new laws, new planes, new racial demands) come in, every day new products (new variants, new applications, new combinations) go out, every day old versions are iterated and retired.

Six top-level axioms must all be named: the anti-predation axiom requires all manual raw materials come from increment creation, not predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the manual system cover 99% of clans; the collective ascension axiom requires manual iteration results backflow to Myriad Ranks; the causal self-discipline axiom requires cross-plane manual transplantation go through reporting procedure; the law-first axiom requires the manual system's law definitions themselves be self-consistent, infinitely scalable, and never eat their own books.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Hierarchical Leap of the 300-Set Origin Cultivation System

Per benchmark Document 10, Heng Yuan Zhou's manual-system core is the 300-set Origin Cultivation System, divided into six categories of fifty each: Deduction-Iteration, Life-Cycle, Spatial-Law Dispatch, Consciousness-Uplift, Symbiotic-Network, and Application-Engineering. Under the nine-realm framework, these 300 sets cover the complete cultivation path from Sense-Origin Realm to Origin Realm.

But the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's requirement far exceeds this. When cultivators enter the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, the 300 sets must leap from "personal cultivation guide" to "hyper-multiverse-grade manual production system":

- **Origin grade**: the 300 sets themselves, standardized manual templates;
- **Multiverse grade**: variant engineering of the 300 sets across different planes, races, and environments;
- **Hyper-multiverse grade**: coordinated iteration across the 300 sets, cross-system fusion, new-law deduction, and continuous audit and revision of old systems.

This overview entry discusses the third layer — what the hyper-multiverse-grade manual system is, why it is needed, and how it runs.

## II. Why "Infinite Iteration" Instead of "Finalized"

Old web novels' finalized-manual view has three fatal problems:

1. **Environment changes but the manual does not**: a plane's spirit-energy density, law structure, and racial composition continuously change on eon scales; a finalized manual gradually fails, but no one dares change it — because "the founder handed it down";
2. **Knowledge locks innovation**: the founder's manual became an inviolable classic; later generations may only annotate, not overturn; the manual system does not progress for thousands of years;
3. **Monopolized entrance**: finalized manuals are held in sects, families, and gods' hands; outsiders cannot even see.

Heng Yuan Zhou's infinite-iteration view directly targets these three:

- **Environment self-adaptation**: the manual system embeds environment-monitoring modules; when plane parameters change, it auto-pushes revision suggestions;
- **Knowledge fully open**: all manual versions are public network-wide through the Selfless Dao; anyone may submit revision opinions and new variants;
- **No-threshold entrance**: the 300 sets are freely open to Myriad Ranks from Sense-Origin Realm onward, no master needed, no tuition needed.

## III. The Three-Layer Architecture of Hyper-Multiverse-Grade Manuals

```
Layer 1: Origin Three Bases (Deduction-Iteration 001 / Life-Cycle 002 / Spatial-Law Dispatch 003)
  ↓ base engine, root of all manuals
Layer 2: Six categories of fifty each (300 sets total)
  ↓ standardized templates, covering all nine-realm paths
Layer 3: Hyper-multiverse iteration layer
  ├─ Cross-system fusion (cross-combinations among six categories)
  ├─ Plane-adapted variants (same system modified across planes)
  ├─ New-law deduction (based on existing systems, deduce undiscovered new-law applications)
  └─ Version audit and retirement (self-consistency review and decommissioning of old versions)
```

Layer 3 is the core workshop of hyper-multiverse-grade manuals. It does not produce "a manual for some person to practice"; it produces "the manual-production method" — i.e., the manual's meta-technology. Mastering this layer, cultivators are no longer "people practicing some manual," but "people able to design manuals for new planes, new races, new environments."

## IV. Iteration's Driving Force: Four Engines

Hyper-multiverse-grade manuals' infinite iteration is driven by four engines:

1. **Myriad-Rank demand engine**: 99% Myriad-Rank coverage means new races and new demands are always being incorporated; each new race raises new demands on manuals;
2. **New-plane exploration engine**: the cross-plane exploration era (benchmark Document 78) continuously discovers new law structures, requiring new manuals to adapt;
3. **Causal self-discipline engine**: every cross-plane operation requires manuals not disturb causality, forcing manual precision to continuously improve;
4. **Alaya audit engine**: Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision continuously scans the manual system for self-consistency holes, predatory residue, and historical-reversal risk, auto-triggering revision.

The four engines jointly keep the manual system always in the state of "getting better," not the static state of "already perfect."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: finalized manuals age and fail with the environment, equal to wasting efficient manuals for nothing; infinite iteration keeps manuals in optimal state forever
- **Anti-predation**: manuals fully open means no one may monopolize cultivation knowledge; predatory factions cannot establish advantage through "exclusive manuals"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's manuals are gift-style — whatever Gu Chensha gives you practice; Heng Yuan Zhou's manuals are network-style — Myriad Ranks co-build, everyone may modify, always iterating
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: manual iteration's input comes from Myriad-Rank demand, output also serves Myriad-Rank demand; 99% coverage is not a slogan but the production line's design parameter
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: manual iteration's whole process — raw materials, procedures, semi-finished products, finished products — is public network-wide through the Selfless Dao, not a few grandmasters' private property
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Manuals are not scriptures carved on stone, but a production line that runs forever. Heng Yuan Zhou's hyper-multiverse-grade manual system is this production line's factory-ized, engineering-ized, Myriad-Rank-co-governed version. Its ultimate goal is not "create one strongest manual," but "let manuals forever have someone able to create, forever changing anew, forever belonging to everyone."
