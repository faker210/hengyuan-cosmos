# Entry 744 | Hyper-Multiverse-Grade Manuals | Hyper-Multiverse Applications of the Spatial-Law Dispatch System

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 13, 28, 80

## Introduction: upgrade "move through space" to "dispatch the multiverse's spatial infrastructure"

The Spatial-Law Dispatch System is the Origin Cultivation System's No. 3 origin base system (number 003). Per benchmark Document 13, its core is perception, positioning, and short-range traversal of spatial laws — storage, teleportation, spatial folding. Under the nine-realm framework, this is a personal spatial tool. But at the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, its role must leap — from "helping one person move house" to "dispatching the entire multiverse's spatial infrastructure."

Six top-level axioms here are spatial dispatch's coordinate system: the anti-predation axiom requires spatial-resource development go the increment route; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires cross-plane traffic be open fairly to Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires the spatial network serve Myriad Ranks' joint uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires cross-plane traversal be strictly reported; the law-first axiom requires spatial laws' definition be self-consistent and stable.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Origin Grade: Personal Spatial Operations

At origin grade, the Spatial-Law Dispatch System's functions are:

- Open personal storage space, breaking free of physical-container limits;
- Short-range teleportation, raising movement efficiency;
- Spatial perception, identifying nearby spatial cracks and anomalies;
- Laying the foundation for breaking through Dao Integration and Boundary-Breaking Realms.

This grade is entirely a personal tool — a cultivator's "backpack" and "ride." Its ceiling is low, and it involves no civilization-level impact.

## II. Multiverse Grade: Inter-Plane Traffic Infrastructure

After entering the Multiverse Realm, the Spatial-Law Dispatch System's service object leaps from individual to cross-plane traffic network:

- **Cross-plane anchor connection** (Document 28): establish stable spatial channels between planes, replacing random plane traversal;
- **Traffic dispatch system**: manage cross-plane channels' flow, routes, and safety checks, preventing disorderly plane traversal;
- **Spatial-resource survey**: probe new planes and new star sectors, evaluating whether their spatial-law structure is suitable for development;
- **Disaster-evacuation channel**: reserve safe evacuation routes for doomed planes, protecting Myriad-Rank beings.

This grade turns spatial ability from "personal ride" into "civilization traffic network."

## III. Hyper-Multiverse Grade: Cross-Cosmos Spatial-Law Dispatch

At the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, the Spatial-Law Dispatch System enters a third layer — cross-cosmos spatial-law dispatch. What it manages is no longer plane traffic inside some multiverse, but **spatial relations between multiverses**:

| Dispatch Direction | Specific Content |
|--------------------|------------------|
| Cross-cosmos channel construction | Establish stable spatial bridges between different multiverses, not random traversal |
| Spatial-resource reallocation | Dispatch surplus spatial computable power from resource-rich planes to resource-poor planes |
| Predation-channel defense | Monitor cross-cosmos predation channels, block them without triggering causal war |
| Spatial-isolation engineering | Establish spatial-isolation layers for low-order planes needing independent development, protecting them from disturbance |
| Dimension-layer dispatch | Dispatch spatial resources among the four-layer structure of material plane / energy layer / consciousness domain / law network |

## IV. Key Constraint: Space Is Not Ownerless Land

The most common old-web-novel setting is "a strong one may tear open space and leave at will," as if space is a public corridor. Heng Yuan Zhou's spatial-law system does not recognize this freedom:

- **Spatial channels have sovereignty**: opening any cross-plane or cross-cosmos channel must be consented by relevant planes' Myriad-Rank representatives;
- **Traversal has cost**: every spatial traversal's energy is borne by the traverser, not "free";
- **Flow is controlled**: Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision monitors all cross-plane traversal; anomalous flow auto-alarms;
- **Observe-first-then-interfere**: after arriving at a new plane, observe first then contact; not allowed to start transforming upon landing.

These constraints ensure spatial ability is not abused as a predation tool of "go wherever you want, grab whatever you want."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: if spatial ability is only used for personal teleportation, it is big-talent-small-use; hyper-multiverse applications turn spatial ability into civilization traffic infrastructure
- **Anti-predation**: cross-cosmos channel construction is throughout reported and supervised, preventing predatory factions from using spatial channels to invade weak planes
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's spatial ability is gift-style — whichever door Gu Chensha wants to open, he opens; Heng Yuan Zhou's spatial network is co-governed — every plane has a voice
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: cross-plane traffic is open fairly to Myriad Ranks; the five lineages (carbon/energy/mechanical/consciousness/mixed) each have adapted traversal methods
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: spatial-law dispatch's technical schemes, channel maps, and safety protocols are all open network-wide through the Selfless Dao
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Spatial-Law Dispatch System upgrades from "helping one person teleport" to "dispatching the whole multiverse's spatial infrastructure," essentially redefining space from "a strong one's private corridor" to "Myriad Ranks' public road." Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm cultivators are this road system's dispatchers — they dispatch space, but do not own space.
