# Entry 746 | Hyper-Multiverse-Grade Manuals | Details of the Fifty Symbiotic-Network Systems

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 10, 21, 25

## Introduction: cultivation is not one person's affair, but the whole network's

Among the 300-set Origin Cultivation System, the fifty Symbiotic-Network systems are Heng Yuan Zhou's core marker distinguishing it from all old web-novel systems. Old web-novel cultivation is essentially "one person's leveling game" — you beat monsters, you level, you grow strong. Symbiotic-Network systems fundamentally negate this premise: **cultivation is the network's affair, not the individual's.** You grow stronger; the network grows stronger by a fraction; the network grows stronger; you also grow stronger. Individual and network are a positive-sum cycle, not a zero-sum game.

Six top-level axioms here are Symbiotic-Network systems' skeleton: the anti-predation axiom requires energy flow in the network all be reciprocal, not predatory; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires network nodes be open equally to all beings; the collective ascension axiom requires the network's collective-uplift rate exceed the individual-uplift rate; the causal self-discipline axiom requires cross-network operations be reported; the law-first axiom requires network laws be self-consistent.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Fifty Symbiotic-Network Systems' Classification Framework

The fifty Symbiotic-Network systems, by network layer, are divided into five subgroups of ten each:

| Subgroup | Number Range | Core Direction |
|----------|--------------|-----------------|
| Personal Node | 201–210 | Onboard the individual cultivator into the symbiotic network as an effective node |
| Squad Collaboration | 211–220 | Small-team (3–10) cultivation coordination and energy sharing |
| City-State Interconnection | 221–230 | Cross-city-state, cross-race cultivation-resource interconnection and mutual aid |
| Plane-Level Net | 231–240 | The whole plane's cultivation-network construction and O&M |
| Cross-Cosmos Net | 241–250 | Cross-multiverse symbiotic-network interconnection and coordination |

The five subgroups, from "one person joins the net" to "the whole cosmos networks," form a complete symbiotic-network-construction ladder.

## II. Personal-Node Subgroup (201–210)

These ten systems solve the problem of "how the individual becomes an effective network node":

- **201 · Energy Two-Way Anchoring**: the individual cultivator's energy output and input interface with the network, both benefiting from and feeding back to the network;
- **202 · Contribution Auto-Ledger**: individual contributions to the network are automatically recorded, converting to civilizational weight;
- **203 · Demand Auto-Report**: individual cultivation-resource demands are automatically reported to the network, matched by the joint-production-contract mechanism;
- **204 · Knowledge-Sharing Interface**: new insights gained in personal cultivation are auto-uploaded to the network, for network-wide use;
- **205–210**: correspond to different node types' onboarding schemes (humankind nodes, ferocious-beast nodes, plant-spirit nodes, etc.).

This subgroup's core principle: joining the network is not one-way extraction, but a two-way cycle. You gain resources and knowledge from the network, and must also output contributions and insights to the network. Nodes that only take and never give are automatically down-weighted by the network.

## III. Squad-Collaboration Subgroup (211–220)

These ten systems solve the problem of "how small teams symbiose efficiently":

- **211 · Energy Complementary Cycle**: cultivators of different attributes in the team complement each other's energy, forming a closed loop;
- **212 · Risk-Sharing Mechanism**: the team's breakthrough-failure risk is borne by the whole team, not one person alone;
- **213 · Experience Sync**: team members' cultivation experience syncs in real time; one person's insight benefits all;
- **214 · Role Division**: divide labor by the 300-set system's different directions — deduction, life, space each hold their post;
- **215–220**: correspond to different team sizes and task types' collaboration schemes.

This subgroup directly opposes old web novels' "lone-cultivator supremacy" thinking. In Heng Yuan Zhou, squad collaboration's cultivation efficiency is always higher than lone cultivation — because of the network's compound-interest effect.

## IV. City-State Interconnection and Plane-Level Net Subgroups (221–240)

**City-State Interconnection (221–230)**: cross-city-state, cross-race cultivation-resource interconnection. For example, carbon-based humankind city-states' spirit-medicine gardens interconnect with plant-spirit city-states' seed vaults; mechanical-clan city-states' energy factories interconnect with energy-condensing-clan city-states' spirit veins. Resources are no longer monopolized by a single city-state, but flow network-wide.

**Plane-Level Net (231–240)**: the whole plane's cultivation-network construction:
- 231 · Plane Anchor Layout: deploy multiple Balanced-Anchor-level anchors within a plane, forming a distributed anchor network;
- 232 · Law-Monitoring Network-Wide: whole-plane real-time law monitoring, auto-alarm on anomalies;
- 233 · Resource Dispatch Center: whole-plane cultivation resources' unified dispatch and allocation;
- 234–240: correspond to different plane types' network-construction schemes (material plane, energy layer, consciousness domain, law-network layer each have adaptations).

## V. Cross-Cosmos Net Subgroup (241–250)

These ten systems are Symbiotic-Network class's highest order:

- **241 · Cross-Cosmos Anchor Interconnection** (detailed in entry 762): establish anchor connections between different multiverses;
- **242 · Cross-Cosmos Knowledge Sync**: cultivation knowledge from different cosmos is synced and shared through the anchor network;
- **243 · Cross-Cosmos Resource Dispatch**: dispatch energy, matter, manuals and other resources between cosmos;
- **244 · Cross-Cosmos Consensus Mechanism**: how Myriad-Rank representatives from different cosmos reach consensus on major issues;
- **245–250**: correspond to cross-cosmos network's security, defense, backup, and fault-tolerance directions.

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: lone cultivation's efficiency is far below network collaboration; Symbiotic-Network systems converge scattered computable power into a joint force
- **Anti-predation**: energy flow in the network is two-way reciprocal; no node may only take and never give
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system is an oligarchic network — Gu Chensha, one node, monopolizes the whole net; Heng Yuan Zhou is a distributed network — ten million nodes connect equally
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: network nodes are open to all beings; the five lineages each have onboarding schemes, 99% coverage
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the fifty systems are all freely public; network-construction technology itself is the network's public product
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The fifty Symbiotic-Network systems are Heng Yuan Zhou's "Myriad-Rank co-build" idea concretely realized at the manual level. Cultivation is no longer one person's affair, but the whole network's — you grow stronger by a fraction, the whole net grows stronger by a fraction; the whole net grows stronger by a fraction, you grow stronger by a fraction. This is the manual expression of the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle.
