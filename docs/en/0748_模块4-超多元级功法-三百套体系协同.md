# Entry 748 | Hyper-Multiverse-Grade Manuals | Hyper-Multiverse Coordination of the 300-Set System

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 10, 91, 21

## Introduction: three sets used separately are tools; 300 used together are civilization

The previous seven entries separately discussed the Origin Three Bases' hyper-multiverse applications (742–744) and the three categories' fifty-set details (745–747). But the 300-set system is not 300 isolated tools — they are a coordinated system. Use Deduction-Iteration alone, you are just someone who can compute; use Life-Cycle alone, you are just someone who lives long; use Spatial-Law Dispatch alone, you are just someone who can teleport. But when the 300-set system runs coordinated at hyper-multiverse grade, what they produce is no longer individual ability, but **civilization itself**.

Six top-level axioms here are coordination's coordinate system: the anti-predation axiom requires coordinated output energy flow be reciprocal; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the coordination process be transparent to Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires coordinated output exceed the sum of parts; the causal self-discipline axiom requires cross-system coordination's causal impact be controllable; the law-first axiom requires the coordination architecture itself be law-self-consistent.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Coordination Instead of Independence

If the 300 sets run independently, they hit three problems:

1. **Information islands**: conclusions computed by the Deduction-Iteration System are unknown to the Life-Cycle System; channels opened by the Spatial-Law Dispatch System are unknown to the Application-Engineering System;
2. **Duplicate labor**: different systems solve different sides of the same problem, wasting computable power;
3. **Side-effects not shared**: one system's operations may have side effects on another, but they do not know.

Hyper-multiverse coordination solves these three: let the 300 sets share information, share computable power, share side-effect warnings.

## II. Coordination's Three-Layer Architecture

```
Layer 3: Cross-System Fusion Protocol (hyper-multiverse grade)
  ├─ Deduction × Life: deduction results drive life-engineering optimization
  ├─ Space × Application: spatial channels support engineering logistics
  ├─ Consciousness × Symbiosis: consciousness network supports Myriad-Rank co-build
  └─ Any combination: cross-protocols among the six categories
Layer 2: Data-Sharing Bus (multiverse grade)
  ├─ The 300 sets share a data bus, exchanging status in real time
  ├─ Side-effect warnings auto-push to related systems
  └─ Public knowledge base for all systems to call
Layer 1: Each system runs independently (origin grade)
  └─ Each set cultivates and works by its own logic independently
```

Layer 1 is the base — each system must first be independently usable. Layer 2 is interconnection — data interflows. Layer 3 is fusion — cross-system coordination produces new value.

1. Spatial-Law Dispatch surveys the new plane's law structure;

The six categories coordinate in one project, producing not an isolated "manual," but a complete **new-plane development scheme**.

**Scenario 2: Crisis response**
1. Alaya Network detects a law anomaly;
2. Deduction-Iteration computes the crisis's impact scope and time;
3. Spatial-Law Dispatch opens emergency evacuation channels;
4. Life-Cycle launches wounded treatment;
5. Consciousness Uplift soothes the beings' emotion field;
6. Application-Engineering mobilizes defense-engineering resources;
7. Symbiotic-Network coordinates Myriad-Rank support.

Seven steps run simultaneously — not a linear flow but parallel coordination.

## IV. Coordination's Metric: the Coordination Efficiency Index

Heng Yuan Zhou uses the "Coordination Efficiency Index" (CEI) to measure the 300 sets' coordination degree:

- CEI=0: systems fully independent, no mutual exchange;
- CEI=0.5: data shared, but no joint action;
- CEI=1: able to handle common coordination scenarios;
- CEI=2: cross-system fusion produces new value;
- CEI=3: the 300 sets form a self-organizing system, able to auto-handle unknown crises.

Current Heng Yuan Zhou's CEI is about 2.5, still continuously rising. Every 0.1 rise in coordination efficiency raises the whole civilization's civilizational-weight net by about 3%.
