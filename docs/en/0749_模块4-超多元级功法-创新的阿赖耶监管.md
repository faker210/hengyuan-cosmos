# Entry 749 | Hyper-Multiverse-Grade Manuals | Alaya-Network Supervision of Manual Innovation

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 21, 49, 97

## Introduction: open innovation and safe supervision are not contradictory; they are two sides of the same thing

Heng Yuan Zhou advocates manuals be network-wide open, Myriad-Rank co-built, and infinitely iterated. But openness is not laissez-faire — any new manual's birth must pass strict self-consistency audit, ethical review, and safety testing. Otherwise, a flawed manual opened network-wide harms Myriad Ranks. This is the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's core duty in the manual-innovation field: **let innovation run fast, while ensuring the brakes always work.**

Six top-level axioms here are supervision's rulers: the anti-predation axiom requires new manuals have no predatory design; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires new manuals be equally usable by Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires new manuals serve the collective; the causal self-discipline axiom requires new manuals' cross-plane impact be controllable; the law-first axiom requires new manuals' law definitions be self-consistent and never eat their own books.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Supervision's Three Gates

Any new manual (whether individually original or cross-system fusion) entering the 300-set library must pass three gates:

| Gate | Review Content | Reviewer |
|------|----------------|----------|
| Gate 1 · Self-Consistency | Whether the manual's law definition is self-consistent; whether it has book-eating / ending-ruin risk | Alaya Network automatic audit |
| Gate 2 · Ethics | Whether the manual contains predatory design; whether it harms Myriad Ranks | Anchor Council Ethics Committee |
| Gate 3 · Safety | Whether the manual has backfire risk; whether it is safe to use | Symbiotic Executive Committee Test Group |

Only after all three gates pass may the new manual be officially numbered and filed, opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao. Any gate failing sends it back for revision.

## II. Gate 1: Automatic Self-Consistency Audit

The Alaya Network's self-consistency review of new manuals is fully automatic, mainly checking:

- **Law consistency**: whether the law definitions used by the new manual are compatible with the existing law network;
- **Logical closure**: whether the manual's cultivation path is complete, whether every step from entry to mastery has clear description;
- **Clear boundaries**: whether the manual's applicable and non-applicable scopes are clear;
- **Version compatibility**: whether the new manual conflicts with the existing 300 sets, whether mutual-exclusion marking is needed.

This gate directly corresponds to the law-first axiom: setting self-consistency above all plot convenience. A manual with logical holes, no matter how strong its effect, may not be filed — because it would "eat its own book" during use, leaving cultivators at a loss.

## III. Gate 2: Ethical Review

The Ethics Committee's review focuses on:

- **Predatory design**: whether the manual requires devouring others' energy, body-snatching, or resource plunder;
- **Threshold discrimination**: whether the manual is only open to specific races, genders, or classes;
- **Hidden backdoors**: whether the manual embeds the creator's backdoors (allowing remote control of cultivators);
- **Historical-reversal risk**: whether the manual would guide cultivators back onto the predatory old path.

Ethical review is the republican axiom of Myriad Races' gatekeeper. Any manual ethically problematic, no matter how perfect its self-consistency, may not be filed.

## IV. Gate 3: Safety Testing

The Test Group's safety testing focuses on:

- **Backfire test**: simulate worst-case cultivation results in virtual space, checking for fatal backfire;
- **Adaptation test**: test the manual's compatibility across the five lineages;
- **Extreme test**: test the manual's safety under cultivators' extreme emotions and environments;
- **Long-term test**: simulate the manual's state after 10,000 years of cultivation, checking for aging and drift.

After safety testing passes, the manual is marked "certified safe," for Myriad Ranks to use at ease.

## V. Supervision Does Not Limit Innovation

It must be emphasized: the three gates are not to block innovation, but to **let innovation run more safely.** Under the three gates, cultivators may freely propose any new manual concept — the Alaya Network gives self-consistency suggestions, the Ethics Committee points out ethical issues, the Test Group simulates risks. The cultivator revises per feedback, iterating repeatedly until passing the three gates. This process is far faster than the old system's "closed-door crafting" — because you do not have to step into pits alone; network-wide experts review with you.

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: ungoverned innovation produces many failed manuals, wasting computable power; the three gates ensure filed manuals are all high-quality
- **Anti-predation**: the ethics gate specifically blocks predatory design; predatory factions' new manuals simply cannot be filed
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's manual review is Gu Chensha's single decision; Heng Yuan Zhou's is three-gate distributed review; no one may decide alone
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: review standards treat all Myriad Ranks equally; any race's new manual goes through the same three gates
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: review standards themselves are network-wide public; anyone may submit review opinions and participate
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's role in the manual-innovation field is not police, but coach. It lets every cultivator wanting to innovate know: your concept will be reviewed by the network's best system, commented on by the best experts, tested by the best simulator. Pass the three gates, and your manual belongs to Myriad Ranks; fail, and you know where the problem is, may keep revising. This is the balance of openness and safety.
