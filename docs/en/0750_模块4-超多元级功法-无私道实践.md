# Entry 750 | Hyper-Multiverse-Grade Manuals | The Selfless-Dao Practice of Manual Openness

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 08, 18, 05

## Introduction: why the best manuals must be free

"Selfless Dao" is one of Heng Yuan Zhou's core moral practices, and the anti-predation axiom's concrete landing in the knowledge domain. Its principle is simple: **all knowledge, manuals, and technology beneficial to civilization must be network-wide free and open; no one may monopolize.** This is not charity; this is civilizational safety — because knowledge monopoly inevitably leads to class solidification, and class solidification inevitably leads to predation.

Six top-level axioms here are the Selfless Dao's skeleton: the anti-predation axiom requires knowledge not be monopolized; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires knowledge be open equally to all beings; the collective ascension axiom requires knowledge opening accelerate collective uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires knowledge propagation go through safe channels; the law-first axiom requires the "Selfless Dao" law itself be stably defined.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Old System's Three Knowledge-Monopoly Modes

Before discussing Selfless-Dao practice, first see how the old system monopolized knowledge:

1. **Sect monopoly**: manuals only transmit within the sect; outsiders wanting to learn must take a master, and taking a master requires loyalty;
2. **Bloodline monopoly**: manuals only transmit to specific bloodlines; outsiders, however strong, cannot learn;
3. **Chosen-one monopoly**: manuals only transmit to "the chosen son of Heaven"; others are not even worthy of knowing.

The three modes' common result: knowledge becomes a tool of power; whoever holds knowledge holds the right to rule. 99% of Myriad Ranks' beings are forever outside the door.

## II. The Selfless Dao's Five Practical Principles

The Selfless Dao is not a slogan; it has five operable practical principles:

| Principle | Content |
|-----------|---------|
| Full Publicity | All certified manuals are public in full text, no abridgment, no encryption |
| Zero Threshold | Learning requires no master, no fee, no loyalty |
| Improvable | Anyone may propose revisions to a manual; revisions are shared network-wide |
| Commercially Usable | Manuals may be used for commercial purposes, but commercial returns must feed back to civilization |
| Auditable | Manual usage data is network-wide queryable, preventing abuse |

The five principles together turn manuals from "private property" into "public knowledge."

## III. The Selfless Dao's Operating Mechanism

The Selfless Dao does not run on moral自觉; it has a whole set of operating mechanisms:

- **Filing means publicity**: after a new manual passes the three gates, it auto-enters the Selfless-Dao public library;
- **Contribution incentive**: cultivators who open their manuals have their civilizational weight auto-increased (because they contributed to Myriad Ranks);
- **Monopoly penalty**: anyone found privately hoarding manuals without publicity has civilizational weight auto-deducted; severe cases lose cultivation qualification;
- **Version management**: the Selfless-Dao library has mature version management; every revision is recorded and traceable;
- **Multi-language multi-species adaptation**: manual documents auto-translate into Myriad-Rank languages, adapting to the five lineages' reading habits.

## IV. The Selfless Dao's Relation to Cultivators Personally

Someone may ask: I worked hard to create a manual; why must it be free? Why can't I make a living from it?

Heng Yuan Zhou's answer:

1. **Your innovation did not start from zero**: you innovated standing on the shoulders of the 300-set Origin System, which is Myriad Ranks' co-built result. Your innovation itself already enjoyed Myriad Ranks' infrastructure; it should naturally feed back to Myriad Ranks;
2. **Publicity is not no return**: after opening a manual, the cultivator's civilizational weight rises sharply, and the resources exchanged by civilizational weight far exceed selling the manual for money;
3. **The cost of not publicizing is higher**: privately hoarding a manual means you have left the symbiotic network; your civilizational weight decays over time, finally losing your voice in Myriad-Rank co-governance.

In other words, the Selfless Dao does not "force you to give it away," but "publicizing is more profitable than hoarding." This is the result of institutional design, not moral kidnapping.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: privately hoarding a manual is equal to locking efficient technology in a safe, civilizational-grade energy waste; the Selfless Dao lets every manual serve Myriad Ranks
- **Anti-predation**: the Selfless Dao eliminates at root the possibility of "building predatory advantage through exclusive knowledge"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's knowledge is gift-style — whatever Gu Chensha gives, you learn; Heng Yuan Zhou's knowledge is open-source-style — Myriad Ranks co-build, everyone may use
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the Selfless-Dao library is open to 99% of Myriad Ranks; plant spirits, planet consciousnesses, and mechanical clans may all obtain it for free
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the Selfless Dao is a cross-plane, cross-cosmos opening system, not some plane's internal policy
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Selfless Dao is Heng Yuan Zhou's "Great Dao public" idea institutionalized. It does not run on sages' 自觉, but on a set of institutional design under which "publicizing is more profitable than hoarding." Under this institution, the best manuals are always free — because free is not charity, but civilization's most efficient knowledge-propagation method.
