# Entry 752 | Hyper-Multiverse-Grade Matter | Law-Encoding of New-Cosmos Matter

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 15, 80, 76

## Introduction: designing physical laws on a blank sheet

The previous overview discussed law-weaving's basic principles. This entry goes into concrete operation: when a Balanced-Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm cultivator sets out to open a new cosmos (or new plane), how does he, from zero, design a whole matter system for this new cosmos? This is not "moving over" the old cosmos's matter, but **encoding entirely new matter rules at the law layer** — like designing physical laws on a blank sheet.

Six top-level axioms here are encoding's coordinate system: the anti-predation axiom requires the new cosmos's matter system serve increment; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the new cosmos's matter be friendly to migrating Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires the new cosmos's matter system serve uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires causal isolation between new and old cosmos; the law-first axiom requires encoded laws be self-consistent.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The New-Cosmos Matter Encoding's Five Parameter Layers

Designing a matter system for a new cosmos requires encoding five parameter layers:

| Parameter Layer | Content | Example |
|-----------------|---------|---------|
| Basic Physics Layer | Light speed, gravitational constant, spatial dimensions, and other basic constants | Three or four dimensions? How fast is light? |
| Periodic-Element Layer | Matter's basic element types and properties | How many elements? How do elements react? |
| Energy-Conversion Layer | Energy-matter conversion rules | E=mc² or another formula? |
| Life-Adaptation Layer | The matter system's support for life forms | Carbon-based? Silicon-based? Energy beings? |
| Law-Interface Layer | The interface between matter and the cultivation system | How is spirit energy injected into matter? How are laws woven? |

The five layers, from "hard physics" to "soft interface," form the new cosmos's complete matter code.

## II. Encoding's Key Decision Points

In the encoding process, several key decision points directly determine the new cosmos's civilizational trajectory:

**Decision 1: Dimension choice**
- Three-dimensional space: suitable for carbon-based life, but physical complexity is limited;
- Four-dimensional space: high physical complexity, but life forms must adapt to extra dimensions;
- Mixed dimensions: material layer 3D, energy layer 4D, consciousness domain higher-dimensional — Heng Yuan Zhou's standard scheme.

**Decision 2: Energy-conversion rate**
- High conversion rate: high cultivation efficiency, but energy easily runs wild;
- Low conversion rate: slow cultivation but stable;
- Adjustable conversion rate: different regions have different rates — high in the Origin Acceleration Domain, low in the Quest Sediment Domain.

**Decision 3: Matter abundance**
- Extremely abundant matter: Myriad Ranks easily survive, but lack evolutionary pressure;
- Extremely scarce matter: high survival pressure, but easily breeds predation;
- Moderate abundance + weaving supplementation: Heng Yuan Zhou's standard scheme — natural matter is moderate, weave when insufficient.

## III. Encoding's Self-Consistency Verification

After law encoding completes, it must pass strict self-consistency verification (the law-first axiom's direct requirement):

- **Physical consistency**: are basic physical constants compatible? For example, do light speed and gravitational constant contradict?
- **Chemical consistency**: is the periodic table self-consistent? Do element reactions have dead loops?
- **Life consistency**: can the life-adaptation layer actually produce life, or is it only paper design?
- **Cultivation consistency**: is the cultivation system's law interface compatible with the matter system?
- **Long-term stability**: simulate after a billion years; is the matter system still stable, or will it spontaneously collapse?

Verification is auto-completed by the Alaya Network; failure sends it back for re-encoding.

## IV. Encoding's Myriad-Rank Participation

The new cosmos's matter encoding is not decided by some strong one on his own head. Per the republican axiom of Myriad Races:

- Representatives of races migrating to the new cosmos participate in encoding decisions;
- The encoding scheme is publicized network-wide, accepting Myriad-Rank opinions;
- Key parameters (dimensions, conversion rate, abundance) are decided by Myriad-Rank representative vote;
- After encoding completes, regular reviews revise per actual usage feedback.

This ensures the new cosmos's matter system is not "whatever the strong one likes gets encoded," but "whatever Myriad Ranks need gets encoded."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the new cosmos is encoded from zero, not copying the old cosmos's matter system, avoiding the old system's historical baggage
- **Anti-predation**: the new cosmos's matter system embeds anti-predation design — energy-conversion rate is adjustable, preventing predators from accumulating at high speed
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's "new world" is designed by Gu Chensha alone; Heng Yuan Zhou's new-cosmos encoding is Myriad-Rank co-governed
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: encoding already considers the five lineages' adaptation, not encoding first then making some lineage accommodate
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: encoding schemes, parameter choices, and verification reports are all open network-wide through the Selfless Dao
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

New-cosmos matter's law-encoding is one of Balanced-Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm cultivators' grandest engineering projects. It is not "build a new planet," but "write a new physics." Well encoded, Myriad Ranks flourish ten thousand years in the new cosmos; poorly encoded, the whole cosmos is broken from the physical ground up. This is the law-first axiom's weight in the engineering field.
