# Entry 753 | Hyper-Multiverse-Grade Matter | Cross-Plane Matter Transmission and Transformation

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 28, 81, 15

## Introduction: moving matter from one plane to another is not express delivery, but causal surgery

Old web novels' cross-plane matter moving is simply: storage ring absorbs, another realm releases. Under Heng Yuan Zhou's view, this is extremely crude — because different planes' law structures differ. Directly moving plane A's matter to plane B is like pulling a fish out of the sea and throwing it into the desert; the matter itself may directly collapse, and may also pollute plane B's ecology. Cross-plane matter transmission is not logistics, but **causal surgery**.

Six top-level axioms here are transmission's coordinate system: the anti-predation axiom requires transmission be reciprocal; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires transmission be fair to Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires transmission serve collective uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires transmission go through reporting and not pollute the receiving plane; the law-first axiom requires transmission laws be self-consistent.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Direct Moving Is Forbidden

Different planes' law structures differ; directly moving matter causes three problems:

1. **Matter instability**: plane A's law alloy, upon reaching plane B, may directly shatter because the gravitational constant differs;
2. **Ecological pollution**: plane A's plant seeds, upon reaching plane B, may grow wildly because they have no natural predators, destroying local ecology;
3. **Causal disturbance**: moving plane A's ore to plane B is equal to changing both planes' matter balance, possibly triggering unpredictable causal chains.

These three problems are why cross-plane matter transmission must go through the three-step process of "transform — transmit — re-adapt," not direct moving.

## II. The Three-Step Transmission Process

**Step 1 · Law Transformation**
At the departure plane, transform matter's law parameters into a "cross-plane compatible format." Like converting a file to a universal format, then converting back upon arrival. This step ensures matter does not collapse during transmission.

**Step 2 · Anchor Transmission**
Transmit matter through the dedicated channel of cross-plane anchor connection (Document 28). The channel's law parameters are specially calibrated, ensuring matter stays stable during transmission. Transmission volume, frequency, and item list are all reported to the Alaya Network.

**Step 3 · Landing Adaptation**
Upon reaching the receiving plane, transform matter from "cross-plane compatible format" into the receiving plane's local law parameters. Simultaneously run ecological-compatibility detection, ensuring no pollution of the receiving plane.

## III. Transformation Technology's Core: the Law Translator

Cross-plane matter transmission's core technology is the "Law Translator" — a law-engineering device deployed at both ends of the anchor channel. Its function is analogous to language translation:

- Translate plane A's matter law parameters into cross-plane universal parameters;
- After transmission completes, translate again into plane B's local parameters;
- Preserve the matter's core traits during translation, only adjusting environment-dependent parameters.

The Law Translator is maintained and upgraded by the Application-Engineering System (entry 747)'s 311–320 matter-engineering subgroup.

## IV. Transmission's Ethical Boundaries

Cross-plane matter transmission has strict ethical boundaries:

- **Transmit increment only**: only transmit woven new matter or surplus stock; do not "draw blood" from planes needing resources;
- **Two-way reciprocity**: whatever A transmits to B, B must transmit to A (or reciprocate through other means); one-way plunder is forbidden;
- **Detect before transmit**: before transmission, ecological-compatibility detection must be done;
- **Throughout reporting**: every batch of transmitted items has a manifest; the Alaya Network monitors throughout;
- **Emergency channel**: under disaster conditions, emergency transmission may be activated, but afterwards supplementary report and audit are required.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: direct moving causes matter collapse and ecological pollution, equal to waste; the three-step process ensures matter arrives safely
- **Anti-predation**: transmit increment only, two-way reciprocity; drawing blood from weak planes is forbidden
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's cross-plane matter is whatever Gu Chensha wants to move; Heng Yuan Zhou goes through reporting, detection, and reciprocity
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the cross-plane matter-transmission network is open fairly to Myriad Ranks; every race may apply for transmission
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the Law Translator's technical principles, transmission protocols, and safety standards are all open network-wide through the Selfless Dao
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Cross-plane matter transmission is not express delivery, but causal surgery. Well-done surgery lets Myriad Ranks exchange matter for mutual prosperity; poorly done surgery, at best matter is damaged, at worst ecological disaster, causal war. Heng Yuan Zhou's three-step transmission process and Law Translator are what make this surgery precise, safe, and auditable.
