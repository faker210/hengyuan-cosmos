# Entry 755 | Hyper-Multiverse-Grade Matter | The Myriad-Rank Sharing Mechanism of Matter Engineering

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 22, 05, 08

## Introduction: once matter is woven, how to distribute it fairly to Myriad Ranks?

The previous four entries covered matter's law-weaving, new-cosmos encoding, cross-plane transmission, and increment development. But after all these technical problems are solved, there remains the most critical problem: **woven matter, how to distribute it to Myriad Ranks?** If distribution is unfair, all previous effort is wasted — the strong weave matter and take it all; the weak get nothing; how is this different from the old system?

Six top-level axioms here are distribution's skeleton: the anti-predation axiom requires distribution forbid plunder; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires distribution fairly cover 99%; the collective ascension axiom requires distribution accelerate collective uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the distribution process be transparent and auditable; the law-first axiom requires distribution laws be self-consistent and stable.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Three Principles of Myriad-Rank Sharing

Matter engineering's Myriad-Rank sharing has three basic principles:

| Principle | Content |
|-----------|---------|
| Distribution by Need | Basic survival materials distributed by need, not by power |
| More-Work-More-Gain | Advanced engineering products distributed by contribution; more contributors receive more |
| Network-Wide Transparency | Distribution process and results are public network-wide, subject to audit |

The three principles together both guarantee basic fairness (by need) and retain incentive (more-work-more-gain), while ensuring transparency (network-wide queryable).

## II. Distribution's Three-Layer Architecture

**Layer 1 · Basic Materials Distributed by Need**
Myriad Ranks' basic survival materials (food, shelter, medical care, basic cultivation resources) are distributed by need. Any race, any individual, as long as there is need, may apply; no fee, no queue, no reading faces. This layer is supported by the Symbiotic Guarantee System (benchmark Documents 38–45).

**Layer 2 · Engineering Products Distributed by Contribution**
Advanced engineering products (law alloys, cross-plane vehicles, advanced manual materials) are distributed by civilizational weight. The more you contribute to civilization, the higher your civilizational weight, the more advanced products you may apply for. This layer is supported by the Joint-Production-Contract Mechanism (Document 22).

**Layer 3 · Special Resources Distributed by the Council**
Extremely special resources (Balanced-Anchor-level law crystals, cross-cosmos anchor access rights, etc.) are uniformly distributed by the Anchor Council according to civilizational-engineering needs, not by individual weight, but by project needs.

## III. Myriad-Rank Differentiated Distribution

Myriad Ranks' five lineages have different matter needs; the distribution mechanism must be differentiated:

| Lineage | Basic Needs | Advanced Needs |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| Carbon-based Material Clan | Food, shelter, medical care | Spirit-medicine gardens, cultivation chambers |
| Energy-Condensing Clan | Stable energy field | Spirit-vein access rights |
| Mechanical-Construct Clan | Energy supply, maintenance materials | Law-alloy upgrade parts |
| Consciousness-Information Clan | Computable-power access, knowledge storage space | Consciousness-domain bandwidth |
| Mixed-Mutation Clan | Comprehensive of the above | Customized schemes |

Differentiated distribution ensures every race obtains materials suited to itself, rather than humankind's standard force-fitted to all races.

## IV. Distribution's Audit and Correction

The Myriad-Rank sharing mechanism is not one-time; it has continuous audit and correction:

- **Alaya Network automatic audit**: every quarter auto-scan distribution data, checking for anomalous concentration, whether any race is marginalized;
- **Myriad-Rank representative complaint channel**: any race believing distribution is unfair may complain through the Anchor Council;
- **Annual distribution review**: every year convene a Myriad-Rank representatives' assembly, reviewing that year's distribution effect, adjusting next year's scheme;
- **Extreme-case emergency distribution**: under disasters, wars, and other extreme cases, activate emergency distribution, prioritizing basic survival.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: unfair distribution leads to the strong hoarding and the weak lacking; both sides are waste; Myriad-Rank sharing puts every unit of matter where it is most needed
- **Anti-predation**: the distribution mechanism institutionally eliminates the need to "grab" — distribution by need guarantees basic needs; more-work-more-gain incentivizes advanced needs
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's matter distribution is gift-style — Gu Chensha is in a good mood and grants a little; Heng Yuan Zhou is institutional — by need, by contribution, network-wide transparent
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the five lineages each have distribution schemes; 99% coverage is not a slogan but an institution
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: distribution laws, distribution data, and audit reports are all public network-wide through the Selfless Dao
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Matter engineering's Myriad-Rank sharing mechanism turns "matter creation" from "the strong build, the strong take" to "Myriad Ranks build, Myriad Ranks enjoy." Woven matter is no longer a few people's private property, but civilization's public wealth. This is the anti-predation axiom's final landing in the distribution domain — no grabbing, because there is enough to go around; no hoarding, because the network is transparent.
