# Entry 756 | Hyper-Multiverse-Grade Rules · Overview — Infinite Scalability of the Law Network

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 20, 40, 97

## Introduction: laws are not dead rules carved on stone steles, but a living network

Old web novels' understanding of "laws" is analogous to physical laws — they are out there; you obey or violate them, but you cannot change them. Heng Yuan Zhou's hyper-multiverse-grade rules view overturns this premise: **laws are not eternally unchanging physical laws, but a living network jointly maintained by the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization and the cosmos's origin.** It may scale up, be revised, and be optimized, but may not be wantonly destroyed — because the law-first axiom requires it always be self-consistent.

Six top-level axioms here are the law network's skeleton: the anti-predation axiom requires laws not protect predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires laws be co-governed by Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires laws serve uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires law revision go through procedure; the law-first axiom itself is the law network's highest law.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Old Law View's Three Misconceptions

1. **Laws are eternally unchanging**: believing laws have been this way since the cosmos's birth, never changing. In reality laws evolve with civilizational evolution.
2. **Laws are set by the strong**: believing Heavenly Dao and laws were set by some ancient god or creator, and later generations may only obey. In reality laws are Myriad Ranks' co-built product.
3. **Laws cannot be revised**: believing laws, once set, cannot be changed, otherwise it is "defying Heaven." In reality aged laws must be revised, otherwise they become shackles.

## II. Heng Yuan Zhou's Law-Network View

Heng Yuan Zhou understands laws as a **living network**, with several key traits:

- **Distributed**: laws are not concentrated in some single "Heavenly Dao," but distributed across innumerable anchor nodes;
- **Iterable**: laws are revised with civilizational evolution, not static;
- **Auditable**: every law's history, revision records, and current version are all queryable;
- **Co-governed**: law-making and revision are jointly participated by Myriad-Rank representatives, not decided by one person alone;
- **Self-Primary**: the law-first axiom itself is the meta-law, above all concrete laws.

## III. The Law Network's Hierarchical Structure

```
Layer 1: Meta-Laws (the five axioms, including the law-first axiom)
  ← un-revisable; the foundation of the entire law network
Layer 2: Myriad-Rank Republican Charter-Level Laws
  ← extremely hard to revise; requires 2/3+ passage of the Myriad-Rank Representatives' Assembly
Layer 3: Department-Level Laws (cultivation, resources, medical care, education, etc.)
  ← revisable; goes through normal legislation procedure
Layer 4: Operation-Level Rules (concrete operation procedures, technical standards)
  ← quickly revisable; responding to actual needs
```

The four-layer structure ensures: core principles stay stable, concrete rules flexibly adjust.

## IV. The Meaning of Infinite Scalability

"The law network's infinite scalability" has two layers of meaning:

1. **Horizontal scalability**: as new planes, new races, and new civilizations join, the law network's coverage continuously expands;
2. **Vertical scalability**: as cultivators' realms rise, increasingly detailed new laws are born in the law network, covering increasingly deep cosmic patterns.

The two layers of scalability proceed simultaneously, ensuring the law network always keeps up with civilizational development, rather than civilization being trapped by aged laws.

Understanding laws as "dead rules carved on stone steles" is the old system's most stubborn misconception. Words on steles do not change, but the cosmos changes, Myriad Ranks change, technology changes. Heng Yuan Zhou sees laws as a living network: each law is a node; nodes have dependency relations; new laws entering must pass self-consistency checks; outdated old laws may be retired through revision procedure. The network has redundancy — the same axiom may be guaranteed by multiple departmental laws from different angles; removing one does not collapse the whole system; the network has versioning — every node's modification leaves a trace; problems may be rolled back. This networked law view is the root reason Heng Yuan Zhou can support "infinite scalability": dead rules only get thicker as carved; a living network only gets wider as woven.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: rigid old laws become civilizational shackles, continuously wasting computable power; the iterable law network lets rules always serve civilization
- **Anti-predation**: laws embed the anti-predation axiom from design; they are not the old Heavenly Dao of "the strong protecting predators"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's laws are gift-style — whatever Gu Chensha sets is law; Heng Yuan Zhou's laws are co-governed — Myriad Ranks co-build, Myriad Ranks jointly revise
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: law-making and revision are open to 99% of Myriad Ranks; every race has a voice
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: law texts, revision records, and audit reports are all public network-wide through the Selfless Dao
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The law network is not dead rules carved on stone steles, but a living, growing, revisable network. Its infinite scalability ability is the institutional guarantee that Heng Yuan Zhou civilization may perpetually develop at hyper-multiverse scale — as long as laws grow with civilization, civilization will not be trapped by its own rules.
