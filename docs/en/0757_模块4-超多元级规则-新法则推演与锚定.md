# Entry 757 | Hyper-Multiverse-Grade Rules | Deduction and Anchoring of New Laws

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 11, 24, 20

## Introduction: discovering a new law is equal to adding a new part to the cosmos

When a Balanced-Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm cultivator, through the Deduction-Iteration System, discovers a new law not previously encoded, he faces not "now I know it" and done, but two engineering problems: **first, how to confirm this new law is real and self-consistent? Second, how to "anchor" this new law into the existing law network, letting it run stably rather than drift away?** This is the new-law deduction and anchoring engineering.

Six top-level axioms here are deduction and anchoring's coordinate system: the anti-predation axiom requires new laws serve increment; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires new laws be consented by Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires new laws serve uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires new-law introduction not disturb old causality; the law-first axiom requires new laws be self-consistent with existing laws.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The New-Law Deduction Process

New laws are not thought up on the spot; they have a strict deduction process:

1. **Observe anomaly**: in cultivation or engineering practice, observe a phenomenon the existing law network cannot explain;
2. **Propose hypothesis**: based on the anomaly, propose the hypothesis "some new law may exist";
3. **Simulate and verify**: use the Deduction-Iteration System to simulate this new law's effects in virtual space;
4. **Self-consistency check**: check whether the new law conflicts with existing laws;
5. **Small-scale pilot**: actually run this new law in isolated space, observing effects;
6. **Network-wide rollout**: after pilot succeeds, officially anchor it into the law network.

The six-step process ensures new laws are not fantasy, but verified engineering results.

## II. The New-Law Anchoring Technology

After deduction verification passes, the next step is anchoring. Anchoring's purpose is to let the new law stably exist in the law network, not drift, not degrade, not be overwritten by old laws.

Anchoring technology's core is the "living anchor":

- In the star sector covered by the new law, deploy one or more Balanced-Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm living anchors;
- The living anchor's personal Dao deeply fuses with the new law; his very existence is the new law's physical anchor;
- The anchor continuously calibrates the new law through the Alaya Network, preventing drift;
- The anchor simultaneously monitors the new law's impact on surrounding old laws, adjusting in time.

A new law without a living anchor is like a bridge without piers — written on paper, but cannot stand.

## III. The New-Law Self-Consistency Review

Before a new law enters the library, it must pass self-consistency review:

- **Meta-law compatibility**: the new law may not violate the six top-level axioms;
- **Charter-level compatibility**: the new law may not conflict with the Myriad-Rank Republican Charter;
- **Existing law-network compatibility**: the new law may not contradict existing concrete laws;
- **Long-term stability**: simulate after a billion years whether the new law is still stable;
- **Side-effect assessment**: after the new law is introduced, what side effects does it have on existing ecology, the cultivation system, and Myriad Ranks' lives.

Any one failing sends it back for re-deduction.

## IV. New-Law Myriad-Rank Participation

New-law introduction is not decided by one cultivator alone:

- Deduction results are publicized network-wide, accepting Myriad-Rank opinions;
- New laws with large impact require Myriad-Rank Representatives' Assembly vote;
- During pilot, invite relevant races to participate in testing;
- After anchoring, regular reviews adjust per Myriad-Rank feedback.

This ensures new laws are not "whatever the strong likes gets on," but "whatever Myriad Ranks need gets on."

New-law deduction does not succeed every time. The Alaya Network records all rejected proposals — including logical holes in the deduction process, contradictions exposed in self-consistency review, and objections raised in Myriad-Rank hearings. These failure cases are as important as success cases: they tell later deductors "why this road does not work," avoiding repeated pit-stepping. Some proposals look elegant on the surface, but conflict with some hidden dependency of the existing law network, and are stopped at review; some proposals are technically feasible, but in Myriad-Rank hearings are proven to burden some weak ethnic group, and are also sent back. New-law birth rate is deliberately kept low — in one cosmic eon, stably anchoring three to five truly important new laws is enough to support civilization's leap. Rather than abundance than shoddiness is the first precept of the law-weaving craft.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: new laws expand the law network's coverage, letting more cosmic patterns be utilized, reducing energy waste
- **Anti-predation**: new-law deduction and anchoring are throughout supervised by the Alaya Network, preventing anyone from using new laws to establish predatory advantage
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's "new laws" are changed by Gu Chensha's one sentence; Heng Yuan Zhou goes through deduction, pilot, publicity, vote
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: new laws' publicity, vote, and testing whole process is open to Myriad Ranks
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: new-law deduction methods, anchoring technology, and self-consistency standards are all publicized through the Selfless Dao
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

New-law deduction and anchoring is one of Balanced-Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm cultivators' most central civilizational engineering projects. It lets the law network always grow new parts — not to make one person stronger, but to make the whole cosmos's civilizational infrastructure more and more complete.
