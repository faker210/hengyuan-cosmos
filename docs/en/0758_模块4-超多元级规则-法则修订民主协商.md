# Entry 758 | Hyper-Multiverse-Grade Rules | The Democratic Consultation Mechanism for Law Revision

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 49, 40, 48

## Introduction: old rules not revised are civilization's shackles

The law network needs infinite scalability, and also needs continuous revision. Laws set in one era may no longer apply in the next. Without a revision mechanism, the law network accumulates more and more rigidity, finally becoming civilization's shackles. Old web novels are full of such examples — the "Heavenly Dao" set in ancient times still runs in the Dharma-Ending Age, suppressing all new things as "defying Heaven." Heng Yuan Zhou's solution is to establish a democratic consultation mechanism for law revision.

Six top-level axioms here are the revision mechanism's skeleton: the anti-predation axiom requires revision not protect predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the revision process have Myriad-Rank participation; the collective ascension axiom requires revision serve uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires revision not cause historical reversal; the law-first axiom requires revised laws still be self-consistent.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. When Revision Is Needed

Law revision is usually triggered by four kinds of triggering events:

1. **Environmental change**: plane parameter changes (spirit-energy density, astral cycles) cause old laws to no longer apply;
2. **Technological progress**: new technology (such as law-weaving) makes some clauses of old laws obsolete;
3. **Myriad-Rank feedback**: Myriad Ranks discover holes or unfairness in old laws during use;
4. **Alaya audit**: Alaya Network self-consistency audit discovers contradictions in old laws.

Any one triggering event may start the revision process.

## II. The Four-Level Revision Procedure

| Level | Revision Content | Passage Standard |
|-------|------------------|------------------|
| Level 1 | Operation-level rule revision | Department committee approval |
| Level 2 | Department-level law revision | Anchor Council vote |
| Level 3 | Charter-level law revision | Myriad-Rank Representatives' Assembly 2/3 passage |
| Level 4 | Meta-law revision | Forbidden — meta-laws may not be revised |

The four-level procedure ensures: minor tweaks are fast and flexible; major overhauls are cautious and democratic; core principles are unshakable.

## III. The Democratic Consultation Process

Taking Level 2 revision (department-level laws) as an example, the complete process is:

1. **Proposal**: any cultivator, any race, any Alaya Network node may propose a revision proposal;
2. **Publicity**: the proposal is publicized network-wide, accepting Myriad-Rank comments and revision suggestions;
3. **Hearing**: the Anchor Council organizes a hearing; relevant racial representatives speak;
4. **Revision**: revise the proposal per hearing opinions;
5. **Vote**: the Council votes; majority passes;
6. **Trial operation**: trial-run one cycle, collecting feedback;
7. **Formal effective**: after trial-run succeeds, formally effective.

The whole process is transparent throughout; records are all filed.

## IV. Revision and Historical Continuity

Revising laws does not equal overturning and starting over. Heng Yuan Zhou has two principles guaranteeing historical continuity:

- **No retroactivity**: new laws do not trace past actions, only bind the future;
- **Version archiving**: every law's historical versions are all preserved, archaeology and legal tracing may all query.

This ensures revision is "forward-looking," not "old-account-settling."

Regular revision goes through the seven-step process, but civilization does not always play by regular rules. When some plane suffers law-level mutation, predatory-faction remnants cross-cosmos infiltrate, or Alaya audit discovers an emergency contradiction, Heng Yuan Zhou allows an "emergency revision channel" to be opened: the proposer directly petitions an emergency session of the Anchor Council; hearing and voting complete within 24 shichen; the vote threshold rises from majority to two-thirds; and passage must be followed by three cycles of trial operation, with Myriad-Rank Representatives' Assembly ratification before formalization. The emergency channel exists to prevent "going through procedure until civilization has already had an accident," but the high threshold and ratification mechanism prevent its abuse into "using the name of emergency to practice autocracy." Minority protection is equally key: if a revision vote involves some ethnic group's core cultural rights, that ethnic group holds a "cultural veto" — even if the Myriad-Rank Assembly passes by two-thirds, as long as the affected ethnic group's representatives' assembly vetoes, the revision may not be forcefully landed on that ethnic group's plane.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: a law network without a revision mechanism accumulates more and more rigidity, continuously wasting computable power; democratic consultation keeps laws always adapted to reality
- **Anti-predation**: the revision mechanism allows Myriad Ranks to revise old laws protecting predation, institutionally continuously anti-predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's law revision is Gu Chensha's single decision; Heng Yuan Zhou is proposal, publicity, hearing, vote, trial-run
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the revision process is open to Myriad Ranks; every race may propose, attend hearings, and vote
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: revision process, revision records, and revision results are all public network-wide through the Selfless Dao
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The democratic consultation mechanism for law revision is the institutional guarantee that makes the law network "living." Old rules must be replaceable by new rules, otherwise civilization will be trapped by its own past. Heng Yuan Zhou's revision mechanism is not "change at will," but "change cautiously, change openly, change together with Myriad Ranks."
