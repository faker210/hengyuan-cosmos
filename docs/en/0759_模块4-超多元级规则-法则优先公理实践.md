# Entry 759 | Hyper-Multiverse-Grade Rules | The Rule Practice of the Law-First Axiom

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 97, 04, 40

## Introduction: plot may not kidnap setting; setting must kidnap plot

The law-first axiom is one of the six top-level axioms; its statement is: **laws first, never eat books, never end in ruin; setting self-consistency is above plot convenience; plot may not kidnap setting; the worldview may infinitely scale without collapsing.** This axiom appears repeatedly in web-novel critique, but how does it actually land in Heng Yuan Zhou's rules system? This entry discusses the whole process by which the law-first axiom goes from "slogan" to "institution." The sixth, the universal equal infinite law of all beings, establishes the origin logic that everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power and predation loses meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

Six top-level axioms here are practice's skeleton — this entry's protagonist is the law-first axiom itself; the other four axioms act as supporting accompaniment.

## I. Why the Old System Eats Its Own Books

Old web novels eat their books and end in ruin because of "plot kidnapping setting": the author first thinks of a thrill point, then reverse-engineers setting to fit that thrill. Thrill A needs the protagonist to suddenly grow stronger, so add a mysterious lineage; Thrill B needs the villain to suddenly weaken, so add an old internal injury. Setting is like dough, kneaded this way and that by plot, finally self-contradicting, readers abandoning the book.

Heng Yuan Zhou's law-first axiom is to institutionally cut this cycle: **set setting first, then write plot. Setting does not change; plot must unfold within the setting framework.**

## II. Law-First's Three Operating Principles

**Principle 1: Setting Freeze Period**
Any major setting (new realm, new law, new manual) enters a "freeze period" after official release; during the freeze period it may not be modified. Unless through the democratic-consultation revision process described in entry 758, setting does not change.

**Principle 2: Plot Obeys Setting**
When plot needs conflict with setting, change the plot, not the setting. For example: the protagonist may not suddenly gain an ability that does not exist in the setting to beat the enemy, but must use existing abilities to find a way to win.

**Principle 3: Infinite Scalability Without Collapse**
New setting may be added, but must pass self-consistency check (entry 97 Setting-Contradiction Self-Check Table), ensuring no contradiction with old setting. Scalability is addition, not rewriting.

## III. Law-First's Landing in Manual Engineering

In manual engineering, the law-first axiom concretely manifests as:

- **Manual release means freeze**: after a manual passes the three-gate review and is released, core setting freezes;
- **Revision goes through procedure**: when a manual needs modification, go through entry 758's democratic-consultation revision process;
- **Testing first**: any manual effect must pass virtual-space testing before actual use;
- **No "temporary cheat" allowed**: a cultivator may not, at a critical moment, suddenly use an ability that never appeared.

## IV. Law-First's Landing in Civilizational Engineering

In civilizational engineering, the law-first axiom manifests as:

- **Engineering design document first**: any civilizational engineering (new-cosmos opening, cross-cosmos anchor-network construction) first produces a design document; construction may start only after the design document passes self-consistency review;
- **Construction does not deviate from design**: during construction, if design changes are needed, formal change procedure must be followed; on-site arbitrary changes are not allowed;
- **Acceptance per design document**: after engineering completes, acceptance is per the design document; "good enough" is not allowed.

Take a concrete book-eating scenario: some plane's supreme strong one, when deducing a manual a hundred years ago, temporarily set "this plane's spirit veins only recognize humankind bloodline"; a hundred years later, this plane newly gives birth to a highly gifted demon-clan Youth Supreme; per the old setting he simply cannot cultivate. If it were the old web-novel system, the author would either let the Youth Supreme "lucky-adventure change fate" (eat the book), or let him end in tragedy (readers dissatisfied). Heng Yuan Zhou's approach: this temporary setting should never have been anchored as a "law" from the start; it is only an operation-level rule, abolished through Level-1 revision; simultaneously the Youth Supreme's cultivation path is re-planned by the Myriad-Rank Evolution Guidance Post, requiring no lucky-adventure comeback. Law-first here is not an abstract principle, but gives every setting a clear level, revision path, and responsible person — when problems arise, no author has to wipe them up; the system itself fixes the bug.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: eaten-book and ruin-ending setting equals all previous writing wasted; law-first ensures every piece of setting is permanently valid
- **Anti-predation**: plot may not open backdoors to thrill the protagonist, preventing predatory settings highly dependent on "protagonist uniqueness"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's setting is whatever Gu Chensha wants to change; Heng Yuan Zhou's setting-freeze period + revision process ensures setting is not kidnapped by plot
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: setting freeze and revision process are transparent to Myriad Ranks; anyone may see why setting is the way it is
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the Setting-Contradiction Self-Check Table (Document 97) and revision process are all publicized through the Selfless Dao
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

The law-first axiom is not a creative slogan; it is Heng Yuan Zhou's entire rules system's meta-rule. It ensures Heng Yuan Zhou's worldview may infinitely scale without collapse — because every scaling goes through self-consistency check, every modification goes through democratic procedure. In this sense, the law-first axiom is Heng Yuan Zhou civilization itself's "bug-prevention mechanism."
