# Entry 760 | Hyper-Multiverse-Grade Rules | Alaya Audit of Rule Self-Consistency

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 21, 97, 49

## Introduction: no matter how good the rules, without audit they slowly rot

The law-first axiom requires rules be self-consistent, but self-consistency is not once-and-for-all. As the law network infinitely scales, continuously revises, and cross-plane transplants, it may at any moment produce new contradictions, new holes, new inconsistencies. This requires a continuously running audit system — not audited once a year, but real-time audited, network-wide audited, automatically audited. This is the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's core function in the rules domain.

Six top-level axioms here are audit's coordinate system: the anti-predation axiom requires audit identify predatory residue; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires audit identify discriminatory clauses; the collective ascension axiom requires audit identify hang-up clauses; the causal self-discipline axiom requires audit identify causal holes; the law-first axiom itself is audit's highest standard.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Audit's Four Tiers

The Alaya Network's rule-self-consistency audit is divided into four tiers:

| Tier | Frequency | Audit Content |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| Real-Time Scan | Every second | Anomalous fluctuations and conflicts in law operation |
| Daily Report | Daily | Initial self-consistency screening of that day's newly added and revised laws |
| Quarterly Audit | Quarterly | Full law-network self-consistency scan |
| Annual Review | Annual | Deep audit + Myriad-Rank representatives participating in review |

The four tiers, from real-time to annual, ensure problems are discovered and handled early.

## II. Real-Time Scan: the Alaya Network's Automatic Patrol

The Alaya Network is a distributed law-monitoring system. Its real-time scan functions include:

- **Law-conflict detection**: whether two laws produce contradictions in some concrete scenario;
- **Energy-anomaly detection**: whether there is anomalous energy flow during law operation (possibly predatory behavior);
- **Drift detection**: whether some law's actual effect has deviated from its designed definition;
- **Coverage-blind-spot detection**: whether some behaviors, some races, some scenarios are outside law coverage.

Problems found by real-time scan auto-generate audit work orders, dispatched to relevant handling teams.

## III. Quarterly Full Audit

The quarterly full audit is jointly executed by the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision:

1. **Full law list**: pull all currently effective laws, classify by tier;
2. **Cross-comparison**: compare pairwise, checking for contradictions;
3. **Version tracing**: trace each law's historical revisions, checking for historical leftover contradictions;
4. **Simulated operation**: simulate all laws running simultaneously in virtual space, checking for conflicts;
5. **Generate audit report**: list all discovered problems, sorted by severity.

The audit report is publicized network-wide, subject to Myriad-Rank supervision.

## IV. Annual Review: Myriad Ranks Participate

The annual review is deeper than quarterly audit, and has Myriad-Rank representatives participating:

- The Alaya Network submits the annual audit master report;
- The Myriad-Rank Representatives' Assembly reviews by domain (cultivation, resources, medical care, education, etc.);
- Each discovered problem's solution is discussed;
- Major problems trigger entry 758's law-revision process;
- Review results are publicized network-wide.

The annual review is not only technical audit, but also Myriad Ranks' collective confirmation of the law network.

## V. Application of Audit Results

Audit is not for picking faults, but for improvement:

- **Minor problems**: auto-fix or minor repair;
- **Moderate problems**: trigger formal revision process;
- **Severe problems**: suspend related laws' execution, trigger emergency revision;
- **Systemic problems**: trigger the law network's architecture-level review.

Audit results are simultaneously counted into the Anchor Council's legislative reference, helping legislators understand the law network's weak links.

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: inconsistent laws cause execution chaos, repeated enforcement, and mutual contradiction, wasting large amounts of computable power; audit ensures the law network is clean and efficient
- **Anti-predation**: audit specifically scans whether some law protects predatory behavior, whether some hole is exploited by predatory factions
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's "audit" is Gu Chensha alone looking; Heng Yuan Zhou is Alaya Network automatic + Myriad-Rank representatives participating
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: audit reports are publicized network-wide; Myriad-Rank representatives participate in annual review; every race may influence law improvement
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: audit methods, audit standards, and audit reports are all public network-wide through the Selfless Dao
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Alaya audit of rule self-consistency is the law network's "immune system." It does not passively wait for rules to have problems then fix them, but actively, continuously scans, detects, and repairs. With this immune system, Heng Yuan Zhou's law network may always stay self-consistent through infinite scaling — it will not eat its own books, will not end in ruin, will not become civilizational shackles through aging.
