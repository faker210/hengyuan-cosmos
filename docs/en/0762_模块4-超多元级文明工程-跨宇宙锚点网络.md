# Entry 762 | Hyper-Multiverse-Grade Civilizational Engineering | Cross-Cosmos Anchor-Network Construction

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 28, 20, 78

## Introduction: connect isolated cosmos into one network

Heng Yuan Zhou's ultimate infrastructure is not some plane's spirit-vein network, not some multiverse's anchor system, but the **cross-cosmos anchor network** — connecting different multiverses through living anchors, forming a symbiotic network covering all known cosmos. This is the grandest project in civilizational engineering, and also the key step for Myriad-Rank Republic to upgrade from "one cosmos's republic" to "multi-cosmos republic."

Six top-level axioms here are network construction's skeleton: the anti-predation axiom requires the network transmit only increment; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires every cosmos's civilization have a node; the collective ascension axiom requires the network accelerate collective uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires inter-cosmos connections not disturb each other's causality; the law-first axiom requires network protocols be self-consistent.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why the Cross-Cosmos Anchor Network Is Needed

A single cosmos's anchor network, no matter how complete, has a ceiling:

- **Resources finite**: a single cosmos's matter, energy, and law resources are finite;
- **Isolation risk**: if one cosmos has a problem, there is no external support;
- **Knowledge islands**: different cosmos's civilizations do not know each other's existence;
- **Predation escape**: after predatory factions are driven out of one cosmos, they may escape to another.

The cross-cosmos anchor network solves these four problems: resource interflow, disaster mutual rescue, knowledge sharing, predators nowhere to flee.

## II. The Network's Three-Layer Architecture

```
Layer 3: Cross-Cosmos Backbone Network
  ← backbone anchors connecting different multiverses
Layer 2: Intra-Multiverse Network
  ← inter-plane network inside each multiverse
Layer 1: Plane Local Network
  ← inter-city-state network inside each plane
```

The three-layer network interconnects step by step from local to cross-cosmos. This entry focuses on Layer 3.

## III. Backbone Anchor Selection Criteria

Cross-cosmos backbone anchors are not chosen at random; their criteria are extremely strict:

- Cultivator has reached the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm;
- Civilizational weight ranks in Myriad Ranks' top 0.01%;
- Heart-nature tested for 10,000+ years, no predatory record;
- Rich cross-cosmos operation experience;
- Passed by Myriad-Rank representative vote.

Every backbone anchor is Myriad Ranks' most reliable node, tested over ten thousand years and through countless events.

## IV. Network Protocol: Rules of Cross-Cosmos Communication

The cross-cosmos anchor network follows strict network protocols:

- **Transmit knowledge, not intervention**: cosmos share knowledge, technology, and early warnings, but do not directly interfere with each other's internal affairs;
- **Observe-first-then-contact**: before a new cosmos joins the network, observe first then contact;
- **Peer connection**: every cosmos node in the network has equal status; there is no "big-brother cosmos";
- **Disconnection right**: any cosmos, if it believes the network connection harms its interests, may apply to disconnect, but must go through consultation procedure.

After the cross-cosmos backbone network is built, the real test is O&M. Distances between backbone anchors are measured in cosmic eons; law-parameter drift at any one node may cause the entire network to oscillate. Therefore, Heng Yuan Zhou additionally sets up "Watcher Node Clusters" outside the backbone network — composed of dozens of lower-order plane-supreme strong ones forming a rotating team, monitoring 24/7 the backbone anchors' energy waveforms, law compatibility, and information latency. Once some backbone node shows anomaly, the Watchers complete initial judgment within half a cosmic eon, then nearby transcended-one teams intervene to calibrate. Bandwidth allocation is not egalitarian: newly joined cosmos enjoy bandwidth tilt in the first three eons, helping their civilization quickly catch up; mature cosmos' bandwidth returns to baseline, yielding resources to rising cosmos. This dynamic scheduling avoids the Matthew effect of "old cosmos always occupying the main road, new cosmos unable to get on the fast lane," keeping the cross-cosmos network always weak-tilted in its public attribute.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: isolated cosmos fight separately, with massive duplicate construction; the cross-cosmos network lets knowledge, resources, and experience be shared network-wide
- **Anti-predation**: after predatory factions are driven out of one cosmos, they have nowhere to flee, because the whole network is linked
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's "cross-cosmos" is Gu Chensha alone going wherever he wants; Heng Yuan Zhou is a co-built, shared network
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: every cosmos's Myriad Ranks have their own node representatives, not humankind cosmos calling the shots
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: network protocols, access standards, and data formats are all public network-wide through the Selfless Dao
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

The cross-cosmos anchor network is Heng Yuan Zhou's "Myriad-Rank Republic" idea's infrastructure for expanding from one cosmos to multi-cosmos. It is not conquering other cosmos, but connecting other cosmos — linking isolated islands into a continent, linking separately-fighting civilizations into a community of shared destiny.
