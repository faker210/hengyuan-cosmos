# Entry 763 | Hyper-Multiverse-Grade Civilizational Engineering | Hyper-Multiverse Landing of the Myriad-Rank Republican Charter

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 03, 40, 77

## Introduction: how does one charter fit the whole multiverse?

The Myriad-Rank Republican Charter (benchmark Document 03) is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's fundamental law. But writing the charter on paper is easy; how to make it truly land at hyper-multiverse scale across cosmos, planes, and races? This is one of civilizational engineering's core tasks: **not to photocopy the charter ten thousand times and distribute to every plane, but to engineer, technologize, and institutionalize the charter's principles, letting it auto-run in any newly joined cosmos.**

Six top-level axioms here are the charter's landing skeleton — the charter itself is the five axioms' codified expression. The sixth, the universal equal infinite law of all beings, establishes the origin logic that everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power and predation loses meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Charter's Landing in Three Layers

The Myriad-Rank Republican Charter's hyper-multiverse landing is divided into three layers:

| Layer | Content | Tool |
|-------|---------|------|
| Legal Layer | Charter clauses translated into each cosmos's readable legal system | Myriad-Rank Law Translator |
| Technical Layer | Charter principles embedded in technical systems (Alaya Network, anchor network) | Technical embedded check |
| Cultural Layer | Charter ideas propagated to every race through the education system | Myriad-Rank Symbiotic Education |

The three layers advance simultaneously, ensuring the charter is not a scrap of paper.

## II. Legal Layer: From Master Outline to Local Detailed Rules

The charter is a master outline; its landing needs layer-by-layer refinement:

- **Master-outline layer**: the Myriad-Rank Republican Charter (Document 03) itself;
- **Department-law layer**: labor law, education law, medical law, housing law, etc. (Documents 41–46);
- **Plane layer**: each plane formulates implementation detailed rules per local conditions;
- **Cosmos layer**: cross-cosmos treaties, cross-cosmos arbitration rules.

Each layer's refinement must go through entry 758's democratic consultation process; top-down on-the-spot decisions with bottom-up copy are not allowed.

## III. Technical Layer: Charter Principles Embedded in Systems

More important than legislation is embedding charter principles in technical systems:

- **Alaya Network embedded axioms**: as the network runs, it auto-checks whether behavior violates the five axioms;
- **Civilizational weight auto-calculation**: contribution and predation are auto-counted into weight, requiring no manual law enforcement;
- **Cross-cosmos protocol embedded charter**: in the cross-cosmos anchor network's protocol, charter principles are the unmodifiable underlayer.

Technical embedding's benefit: law enforcement does not depend on people; the system runs automatically. Even if some person wants to violate the charter, he cannot bypass the technical system's check.

## IV. Cultural Layer: Charter Ideas Propagated Through Education

Finally, the charter must become Myriad Ranks' cultural consensus:

- The Symbiotic Education System (Document 37) teaches Myriad-Rank Republican ideas from early childhood;
- In Myriad-Rank history courses, predatory civilizations' failure lessons are used as teaching material;
- Every newly joined cosmos's civilization first undergoes a period of "charter education" before formally joining the network.

What the charter's landing fears most is not not landing, but "mechanical landing" — copying charter text verbatim to every plane, resulting in serious conflict with local ecology. Heng Yuan Zhou resolves this with "unified principles, autonomous detailed rules": the charter's six top-level axioms and core rights clauses are unmodifiable bottom lines, must land verbatim; but concrete implementation detailed rules, festival arrangements, custom articulation, are formulated by that plane's Myriad-Rank representatives per local conditions. This is like the same constitution; at a cold-zone plane and a tropical plane, at gaseous life and carbon-based life, landing details may differ a thousand ways, but the constitution's spirit does not move a hair. The technical-layer translator only converts axiom clauses into coding that the plane's law system can read; it does not decide detailed rules for that plane. Detailed rules must go through entry 758's democratic consultation process, negotiated by local beings themselves — this is Myriad-Rank Republic's most concrete cash-out at hyper-multiverse scale.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: a charter that does not land is a scrap of paper; technical embedding + cultural propagation makes it truly run
- **Anti-predation**: the charter's core is anti-predation; technical embedding ensures predatory behavior is auto-identified by the system
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's "laws" are Gu Chensha's single decision; Heng Yuan Zhou's charter is Myriad-Rank co-built, technically embedded, not autocratic
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: during charter landing, every race participates in translation and detailed-rule formulation
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the charter's full text, department laws, and implementation detailed rules are all public network-wide through the Selfless Dao
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Myriad-Rank Republican Charter's hyper-multiverse landing is the process of turning a "constitution" into an "operating system." It does not rely on forceful promotion, but on technical embedding + democratic consultation + cultural education, letting Myriad Ranks, without realizing it, already live under the charter. This is the ultimate landing of institution — you do not feel it, but it is everywhere.
