# Entry 765 | Hyper-Multiverse-Grade Civilizational Engineering | New-Cosmos Opening and Colonial Critique

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 78, 83, 88

## Introduction: opening is not colonization; construction is not occupation

New-cosmos opening is civilizational engineering's natural extension — when a cosmos's civilization develops to a certain degree, it naturally explores toward new cosmos. But the word "opening," in old history, is full of blood: opening equals colonization; colonization equals plundering natives, plundering resources, exterminating local civilization. Heng Yuan Zhou's new-cosmos opening engineering, from design, takes "anti-colonization" as an iron rule. This entry draws a line: **Heng Yuan Zhou's opening is co-built opening, not predatory colonization.**

Six top-level axioms here are opening's skeleton: the anti-predation axiom directly forbids colonization; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires new-cosmos indigenous races have a voice; the collective ascension axiom requires opening serve both sides' uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires opening observe-first-then-interfere; the law-first axiom requires opening's rules be self-consistent.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Old Colonial Opening's Three Crimes

Old history's "opening," disassembled, has three crimes:

1. **Genocide**: treating indigenous races as barbarians, military extermination or expulsion;
2. **Resource plunder**: continuously shipping new cosmos's resources back to the old cosmos, local area becoming a mine;
3. **Cultural extermination**: forcefully imposing the old cosmos's culture, religion, and institutions, exterminating local civilization.

Heng Yuan Zhou's new-cosmos opening, institutionally, completely forbids these three.

## II. Heng Yuan Zhou's Five Iron Rules of Opening

**Iron Rule 1: Indigenous Sovereignty**
If a new cosmos has indigenous people, they hold complete sovereignty over their own cosmos. The opening party may only apply for cooperation, may not forcefully occupy.

**Iron Rule 2: Observe-First-Then-Contact**
Per entry 83's observe-first-then-limit-interfere principle, first observe at a safe distance, confirm indigenous people's civilizational level, social structure, and needs, then decide whether to contact.

**Iron Rule 3: Only Backflow Knowledge, Do Not Disturb Causality**
Per entry 87's knowledge-backflow-only principle, exchanges with indigenous people are limited to knowledge sharing, not changing the other's historical trajectory, social structure, or cultural traditions.

**Iron Rule 4: Resources Two-Way Reciprocal**
If resources need to be obtained from the new cosmos, it must be through equivalent exchange; may not take for free. Resource-development returns mostly belong to indigenous people.

**Iron Rule 5: Voluntary Network Joining**
Indigenous people may independently decide whether to join the cross-cosmos anchor network. Not joining is also welcome; joining is also equal.

## III. Opening's Standard Process

1. **Discovery**: the Spatial-Law Dispatch System discovers a new cosmos;
2. **Observation**: long-term observation at a safe distance, mapping the new cosmos's law map;
3. **Contact**: if there are indigenous people, peaceful contact through the Consciousness-Uplift System;
4. **Consultation**: negotiate cooperation methods with indigenous representatives;
5. **Co-building**: if indigenous people agree, co-build the new cosmos's infrastructure;
6. **Exit mechanism**: indigenous people may at any time require exiting cooperation; the opening party must unconditionally withdraw.

## IV. Why Heng Yuan Zhou Does Not Need Colonization

Someone may ask: Heng Yuan Zhou has so many people, such large resource demand, why not directly colonize new cosmos?

The answer is: **colonization's economic logic is already obsolete.**

- Heng Yuan Zhou has the Increment-Development Principle (entry 754); matter may be woven from the law layer, no need to grab from other cosmos;
- Heng Yuan Zhou has the cross-cosmos anchor network (entry 762); knowledge and technology may be shared, no need to occupy;
- Colonization's costs (war, suppression, rule) are far higher than cooperation's costs (consultation, sharing, co-building).

Colonization was cost-effective in the old era — because matter was finite, technology backward, information disconnected. In Heng Yuan Zhou's hyper-multiverse era, colonization is a losing deal.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: colonial war's computable-power consumption is far higher than co-built cooperation; anti-colonization is the most economical opening method
- **Anti-predation**: anti-colonization is anti-predation's extension at cosmic scale
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's "opening" is Gu Chensha occupying, and it's mine; Heng Yuan Zhou is co-building, cooperating, volunteering
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: new cosmos's indigenous races are members of Myriad Ranks; opening must respect their sovereignty
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: opening process, ethical principles, and contact protocols are all public network-wide through the Selfless Dao
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

New-cosmos opening is not colonization; construction is not occupation. Heng Yuan Zhou's opening method treats new cosmos's indigenous people as equal cooperators, not conquered ones. This is not only a moral requirement, but an economic calculation — in the hyper-multiverse era, co-building is always more cost-effective than plunder.
