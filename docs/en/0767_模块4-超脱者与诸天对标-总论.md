# Entry 767 | Transcended Ones and the Heavens · Cross-IP Benchmark Overview — Cross-IP Comparison of the Transcendence Definition

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 01, 04, 701

## Introduction: measure the cosmos' protagonists on the same ruler

The first half of Module 4 (701–766) has already built the ruler of "what is true transcendence": bearing transcendence, civilizational weight, Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, perpetual construction, Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision. From this entry begins a new working segment — inviting those across the cosmos universally recognized "transcended ones" one by one in front of this ruler, measuring whether they truly transcended, to which step, and on which ceiling they are stuck.

Six top-level axioms here simultaneously act as ruler ticks: the anti-predation axiom measures power's origin; the republican axiom of Myriad Races measures qualification openness; the collective ascension axiom measures whether the strong one carries Myriad Ranks along; the causal self-discipline axiom measures whether cross-plane operations are reported and controllable; the law-first axiom measures whether the "transcendence" definition itself is self-consistent and will not eat its own book. This entry is the overview of this whole group of benchmark articles; first set up the comparison framework, then entries 768–776 cut case by case.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Cross-IP Comparison Is Needed

Cosmos web novels each have their own "transcendence" narrative: Shrouding's Mortal-World Immortal, Perfect World's above-Sacrifice-Dao, Douluo's God Position, Manghuang Ji's Eternal Peak, Dragon Ball's Super Saiyan God, Infinite Horror's Reincarnator Transcendence, Dragon Talisman's Gu Chensha, Jackie Chan Adventures' talisman ultimate form. Each IP is self-consistent internally, and readers buy it. But once placed side by side, three problems immediately appear:

1. **Definitions contradict**: work A's "transcendence" is severing bonds; work B's "transcendence" is protecting relatives and friends; both cannot be true simultaneously;
2. **Ceilings differ**: some works' protagonist topping is the endgame; some works' protagonist may continue dimension-uplifting after topping, but the uplift direction is "stronger individual combat power," not "greater civilizational responsibility";
3. **Myriad-Rank dimension generally absent**: almost all old IP's "transcended ones" are humankind-male chosen ones; plant spirits, planet consciousnesses, and cosmos-origin bodies do not even have qualification to compete.

Cross-IP comparison's meaning is to spread out these mutually contradictory "transcendence" narratives, using Heng Yuan Zhou's bearing transcendence as the sole judge, finding their shared blind spot — this blind spot is Module 4's orthodox-definition space to fill.

## II. Comparison Framework: Five Dimensions

The cross-IP comparison framework established here is fixed at five dimensions; each later benchmark article scores by these five dimensions:

| Dimension | Core Question | Heng Yuan Zhou Full-Marks Standard |
|-----------|---------------|------------------------------------|
| Origin Dimension | Where does power come from? | All from the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle, zero predatory residue |
| Openness Dimension | To whom is qualification open? | 99% Myriad-Rank coverage, no race/gender/origin threshold |
| Uplift Dimension | Does the strong one carry Myriad Ranks along? | The strong one does not ascend alone; civilizational engineering is shared by all |
| Causal Dimension | Are cross-plane operations reported? | Observe-first-then-limit-interfere; only backflow knowledge, do not disturb causality |
| Self-Consistency Dimension | Is setting stable? | Law-first axiom; never eat books, never drift with plot |

The five dimensions are not equal-weight. The origin dimension is a one-vote-veto item — as long as power's origin has predatory residue, no matter how high the other four dimensions, it is only an "advanced predator," not entering the transcended-one candidate list. This is fully consistent with entry 706's civilizational-weight formula: origin-legality subitem negative; consensus degree directly negative; effective combat power negative.

## III. Nine Benchmark Samples' Positioning Preview

Entries 768–776 will handle the following nine cosmos-recognized "transcended ones" one by one:

- **Tang Luo** (Dragon Talisman): gift-system ceiling — the protagonist tops through a higher being's gift, then locks later-comers in the fierce realm;
- **Ye Fan** (Shrouding): oligarchic lone-Dao — one person carrying the whole era; Dao not open, authority not delegated;
- **Shi Hao** (Perfect World): single-tree autocracy — above-Sacrifice-Dao one person decides life and death; Myriad Ranks are only protected objects;
- **Tang San** (Douluo Continent): predatory closure — the God-Position system is essentially closure after predatory upgrade;
- **Goku** (Dragon Ball): aimless combat-power inflation — combat power inflates infinitely but has no civilizational-construction goal;
- **Ji Ning** (Manghuang Ji): cultivation-style closure — closed-door retirement after eternal peak (complementary to entry 734's hang-up critique angle);
- **Gu Chensha** (Dragon Talisman): gift-style sin-bearing — bears sin on behalf of all beings but treats them as children;
- **Zheng Zha** (Infinite Horror): reincarnation harvest — the reincarnation system itself is a harvesting machine;
- **Jackie Chan** (Jackie Chan Adventures): talisman resource waste — civilization-level power used as a toy.

The nine samples cover nine typical ailments of "gift / oligarch / single-tree / predatory / inflation / closure / sin-bearing / harvest / waste," exactly corresponding to the old world's "transcendence" narrative's nine bankruptcy paths.

## IV. Comparison's Methodological Discipline

Cross-IP comparison most easily makes two mistakes; this entry first sets rules:

1. **Do not use Heng Yuan Zhou's moral height to over-criticize old IP**: old IP's protagonists are already heroes within their planes; this entry criticizes the structural defect of their "transcendence definition," not denying their personal struggle. Tang Luo, Ye Fan, Shi Hao are all worthy of respect in their own stories, but respect does not equal acknowledging their transcendence definition holds.
2. **Do not do cheap rehabilitation for old IP**: every benchmark must land on "if this protagonist appeared in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, how should his path be modified" — giving constructive alternative solutions, not only scolding without building. This is the causal self-discipline axiom's embodiment in the critique domain: only backflow knowledge, do not disturb old planes' causality.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the nine samples' shared waste is locking overflowing-plane computable power into an individual-narrative endpoint; Heng Yuan Zhou requires this computable power enter perpetual construction
- **Anti-predation**: the origin dimension is one-vote-veto; at least four of the nine samples' power origins carry predatory residue, directly eliminated
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is the "gift system" extreme among the nine samples; this entry places him first in the benchmark sequence as the old ceiling's baseline
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the nine samples are all humankind-male chosen ones; the Myriad-Rank dimension is collectively absent; this itself is old IP's "transcendence" narrative's greatest structural defect
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the comparison framework itself (the five-dimension scoring table) is open network-wide through the Selfless Dao; any being may use the same ruler to measure any strong one of any IP
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Cross-IP comparison is not to step on the cosmos' protagonists, but to raise the threshold of the word "transcendence." The old world wrote "transcendence" as an individual narrative's endpoint; Heng Yuan Zhou writes "transcendence" as civilizational engineering's starting point. Nine benchmark articles use nine mirrors to illuminate what this starting point should look like.
