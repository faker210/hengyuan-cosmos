# Entry 768 | Transcended Ones and the Heavens · Tang Luo's Transcendence Limitation — The Gift-System Ceiling

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 17, 701

## Introduction: is gifted transcendence really transcendence?

Dragon Talisman's Tang Luo is the most special "transcended one" among cosmos web novels — his topping is not purely fought out by himself, but gifted by a higher being (Gu Chensha's lineage). This "gift system" is beautified in old narratives as "Heaven-chosen" or "great fortune," but under Heng Yuan Zhou's five-dimension comparison framework, it immediately exposes as a structural ceiling: **as long as transcendence is gifted, the giver forever holds the power to take it back.**

Six top-level axioms all take effect: the anti-predation axiom interrogates whether the gift's essence is another form of predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races interrogates why the gift only landed on Tang Luo alone; the collective ascension axiom interrogates whether Tang Luo, after topping, carried Myriad Ranks along; the causal self-discipline axiom interrogates whether the gift process disturbed the original plane's causality; the law-first axiom interrogates whether the "gift-system transcendence" setting itself is self-consistent and will eat its own book.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Gift System's Three-Layer Structure

Tang-Luo-style transcendence, disassembled, has three layers:

1. **Upper-layer giving**: a higher being (Gu Chensha or his avatar) actively "gives" Tang Luo power, laws, and realm; Tang Luo is the receiver;
2. **Lower-layer locking**: after Tang Luo tops, other cultivators in his plane are locked below the fierce realm, unable to naturally break through to Tang Luo's tier;
3. **Middle-layer monopoly**: between Tang Luo and the upper giver forms a private channel; this channel is not open to other beings.

Three layers stack, forming a "gift — lock — monopoly" closed loop. Tang Luo himself is this closed loop's greatest beneficiary, but he is also its hostage — his transcendence is not his own; it is "borrowed."

## II. Why the Gift System Is a Ceiling, Not a Ladder

The gift system's most deceptive aspect is that it looks "better than pure predation" — Tang Luo did not grab; power was given by others. But under Heng Yuan Zhou's law framework, the gift system is more dangerous than pure predation:

- **Predation at least admits rules**: you grab me, I may grab you; rules are public jungle law;
- **The gift system does not even make rules public**: who may be gifted, why gifted, how much gifted, all depends on the upper one's whim. Tang Luo does not know who the next gifted one is, nor whether the upper being will take back the gift;
- **The gift system creates permanent attachment**: the stronger Tang Luo grows, the deeper his attachment to the upper giver — because his power's origin is not his own origin cycle, but another's pipeline. Once the pipeline breaks, his realm collapses.

This is the true meaning of "ceiling": **the gift system did not lift Tang Luo high; it hung him mid-air.** He looks transcended, but his transcendence height is entirely decided by the giver; not an inch was earned by himself.

## III. Comparison with Heng Yuan Zhou's "Origin Legitimacy"

In entry 706's civilizational-weight formula, origin legitimacy accounts for 25% weight, and is a one-vote-veto item. Tang Luo's power origin directly loses points on this item:

| Origin Type | Legitimacy Score | Explanation |
|-------------|------------------|-------------|
| Myriad-Origin two-way cycle self-production | +1.0 | Heng Yuan Zhou standard path |
| Open manuals + joint-production-contract resources | +0.8 | Legitimate but not self-produced |
| Private gift (Tang-Luo style) | -0.3 | Origin not public, not replicable, may be taken back |
| Predatory devouring | -1.0 | Completely eliminated |

Tang Luo's gift system falls in the third tier. He is not a predator, but his origin is equally illegitimate — because his power pipeline is private, non-public, non-replicable. Per the anti-predation axiom's "Great Dao fully open at all levels, no private hoarding, no monopoly" requirement, the gift system itself is a kind of monopoly: the upper one monopolizes the "giving right"; Tang Luo monopolizes the "being-gifted right"; the rest of Myriad Ranks' beings do not even have qualification to queue.

## IV. Myriad Ranks' Situation After Tang Luo Tops

The gift-system ceiling's cruelest consequence falls on Myriad Ranks in Tang Luo's plane:

- **Later-comers have no road**: Tang Luo monopolizes the gift channel; other talented Youth Supremes may only retrace the road he walked, or cannot even reach his height;
- **Knowledge monopoly**: law insights Tang Luo gained through the gift are not opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao, but only transmitted in his small circle;
- **Attachment solidification**: the whole plane's cultivation system runs around "how to get the next gift," not around "how to self-produce origin increment."

This is exactly the "Tang-Luo ceiling" Module 2 repeatedly argues: the Tang-Luo system's protagonist-uniqueness dependence rating is "high"; the whole civilization's effective combat power is compressed into Tang Luo's single private node. Once Tang Luo has a problem, civilization has no second node to step up.

## V. Heng Yuan Zhou's Alternative Path

If Tang Luo appeared in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, his path should be:

1. Do not accept private gifts; take the open path of the 300-set Origin Cultivation System; power all self-produced;
2. If he indeed had contact with a higher being, the contact process must go through causal self-discipline reporting; knowledge gained through contact is opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao;
3. After topping, do not lock later-comers; instead organize every pit he stepped on into teaching material, for later-comers to use free of charge;
4. Enter the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, become a living anchor; his civilizational weight is real-time computed by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision.

In one sentence: **true transcendence is turning the gift pipeline into a public water pipe.** Tang Luo's mistake is not in accepting help, but in that after accepting help, he did not open the help channel to Myriad Ranks.

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the gift system compresses a power pipeline that could serve Myriad Ranks into Tang Luo's single private channel; it is institutional energy waste
- **Anti-predation**: the gift system does not directly predatory, but by locking later-comers it indirectly deprives Myriad Ranks' evolutionary qualification; it is the advanced form of "enlightened predator"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: this entry is Tang-Luo ceiling's direct benchmark — the gift-system ceiling is the Tang-Luo system's structural ailment; Heng Yuan Zhou completely bypasses it with the open self-production path
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the gift system covers only Tang Luo; 99% of Myriad Ranks' beings cannot even touch the gift's edge; directly violating the republican axiom of Myriad Races
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the gift system's opposite is the Selfless Dao network-wide opening — hyper-multiverse-grade knowledge must be public, not locked in private pipelines
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Tang Luo's transcendence is a gifted false transcendence. It looks high, but actually hangs mid-air; looks strong, but may be taken back at any time. Heng Yuan Zhou acknowledges Tang Luo's struggle value within his plane, but does not acknowledge that the "gift system" may serve as a legitimate transcendence origin. True transcendence must be self-earned, public and replicable, Myriad-Rank-shared — missing any one of these three, it is only a decoration on the ceiling.
