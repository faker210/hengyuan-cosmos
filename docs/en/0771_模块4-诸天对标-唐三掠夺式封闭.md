# Entry 771 | Transcended Ones and the Heavens · Tang San's Transcendence Limitation — Predatory Closure

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 05, 701

## Introduction: a god hunted into being — is he a god or a hunter?

Douluo Continent's Tang San, from Holy Spirit Village's waste martial soul to Sea God and Asura God dual god positions, is web-novel history's most successful Youth-Supreme sample. But his whole cultivation path is built on one act completely legitimized by old narratives — **hunting spirit rings**. Every level up, he must hunt a spirit beast, taking its spirit ring as promotion material. In old narratives this is called "beating monsters and leveling up"; under Heng Yuan Zhou's five-dimension comparison framework, this is **predatory closure**: first use predation to complete primitive accumulation, then use the god-position system to close the whole accumulation process, leaving later-comers no second road.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom directly judges the spirit-ring system; the republican axiom of Myriad Races judges why spirit beasts, as one of Myriad Ranks, may only serve as material; the collective ascension axiom judges whether Tang San, after becoming a god, carries Myriad Ranks along; the causal self-discipline axiom judges the causal disturbance of cross-plane god-position inheritance; the law-first axiom judges whether the "god positions may only be chosen by gods" setting is self-consistent.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Predatory Closure's Two-Layer Structure

Tang-San-style transcendence, disassembled, has two layers:

1. **Bottom layer: predatory primitive accumulation** — spirit-ring hunting is essentially the carbon-based material clan's systematic slaughter of the energy-condensing clan (spirit beasts). Spirit beasts are not enemies, but Myriad Ranks' equal member; in the Douluo system they are only defined as "material";
2. **Top layer: god-position closure** — after Tang San becomes a god, the god-position inheritance system is fixed: god positions are finite, may only be chosen by the current god, and mortal cultivators must follow the spirit-ring system to climb to the god-position door. The whole system becomes a closed loop of "first predatory accumulation, then closure and lock."

Two layers stack, more insidious than pure predation: pure predators at least admit they are grabbing; Tang-San-style system wraps predation as "Heaven-ordained cultivation method," letting later-comers hunt spirit beasts with no psychological burden.

## II. Myriad-Rank Judgment of the Spirit-Ring System

The republican axiom of Myriad Races is the hardest ruler. Per Heng Yuan Zhou's five Myriad-Rank lineages (Document 50), spirit beasts belong to the energy-condensing clan, with status completely equal to the carbon-based material clan (humans). But in the Douluo system:

- Spirit beasts have no cultivation right — their only social function is being hunted to produce spirit rings;
- Spirit beasts have no representative right — Spirit Hall, the Seven Great Sects, imperial courts, none sets a seat for spirit beasts;
- Spirit beasts have no feedback channel — humans take spirit rings, spirit bones, and skills from spirit beasts, but never feed back any knowledge, resources, or protection to the spirit-beast race.

This is not a question of "humans stronger than spirit beasts"; this is the problem of **defining an entire Myriad-Rank race as another race's cultivation consumable**. Per the anti-predation axiom's "not using slaughter and plunder as an evolutionary path" requirement, the spirit-ring system does not hold at the law layer at all — every level of promotion is built on another race's death.

## III. God-Position Closure's Ceiling Effect

After Tang San becomes a god, the problem does not end, but escalates:

- **God-position number fixed**: Douluo God Realm's god positions are finite, not open. Mortal cultivators climbing to the top must still queue for a god-position vacancy;
- **Inheritance designation**: who receives a god position is decided by the current god, not competed for by mortal cultivators by ability;
- **Cultivation path locked**: to become a god, one must take the spirit-ring path; not taking this path, no matter how talented, one cannot climb to the god-position door.

Three closures stack, forming the Douluo system's ceiling. Tang San himself is this system's greatest beneficiary, but once the system is built, later-comers are locked on the "hunt-spirit — rank-up — wait-for-god-position" track, with no escape room. This forms the sharpest contrast with Heng Yuan Zhou's "300-set Origin Cultivation System fully open."

## IV. Tang-San-Style Closure vs. Heng Yuan Zhou Myriad-Rank Common Progress

| Dimension | Tang-San-Style Predatory Closure | Heng Yuan Zhou Myriad-Rank Common Progress |
|-----------|----------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Energy source | Hunt spirit beasts for spirit rings | Myriad-Origin two-way cycle, self-produce increment |
| Myriad-Rank status | Spirit beasts = material, no rights | 99% Myriad-Rank coverage, all races equal |
| Promotion path | Single spirit-ring path, locked | 300 sets, freely choose and combine |
| Top position | Finite god positions, designated inheritance | Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm living-anchor network, no upper limit |
| Knowledge openness | Sect private hoard, God Realm closed | Selfless Dao network-wide opening |

The comparison table's core gap is **whether Myriad Ranks have the right "not to be eaten."** The Tang-San system turns spirit beasts' lives into humankind's promotion fuel; Heng Yuan Zhou treats every Myriad-Rank race as a co-evolution partner.

## V. Heng Yuan Zhou's Alternative Path

If Tang San appeared in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization:

1. His Blue Silver Grass and Clear Sky Hammer talents take the open path of the 300-set Origin Cultivation System; he does not hunt any spirit beast;
2. He establishes a symbiotic agreement with the spirit-beast race: spirit beasts, as energy-condensing clan representatives, participate in the Anchor Council, sharing cultivation resources;
3. His Sea God and Asura God insights do not become "finite god positions," but are organized into law-weaving teaching material, opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao;
4. After entering the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, he becomes a living anchor; his civilizational weight is real-time computed by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision;
5. His cross-plane (mortal — God Realm) operations strictly go through causal self-discipline reporting, not disturbing mortal causality.

In one sentence: **true Sea God is the god protecting all beings in the sea, not the god refining beings in the sea into spirit rings.**

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the spirit-ring system burns the spirit-beast race's evolutionary potential entirely as humankind's promotion fuel; it is cosmic-scale racial energy waste
- **Anti-predation**: spirit-ring hunting is institutional predation; Tang San is this predatory system's greatest product; this entry makes direct judgment on it
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is the gift-system ceiling (power given); Tang San is the predatory-system ceiling (power grabbed); both ceilings do not let Myriad Ranks walk on their own
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the spirit-beast race in the Douluo system does not even have "coverage" qualification; directly violating the republican axiom of Myriad Races
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the god-position closure system locks hyper-multiverse-grade knowledge into finite god positions; Heng Yuan Zhou completely bypasses it through Selfless Dao network-wide opening
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Tang San is a hero in old narratives, but his hero's road steps on an entire race's bones. Heng Yuan Zhou does not deny his personal struggle; what Heng Yuan Zhou wants to deny is this civilizational structure of "relying on hunting one of Myriad Ranks' races to fulfill one race." True transcendence is not one person stepping on spirit beasts to become Sea God, but Myriad Ranks together evolving separately in the origin two-way cycle, not devouring each other. Tang-San-style closure's ceiling must be completely overturned by Myriad-Rank Republic's floor.
