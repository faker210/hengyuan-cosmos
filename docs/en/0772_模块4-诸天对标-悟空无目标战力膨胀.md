# Entry 772 | Transcended Ones and the Heavens · Goku's Transcendence Limitation — Aimless Combat-Power Inflation

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 17, 701

## Introduction: strong beyond measure, then what?

Dragon Ball's Goku is cosmos web novels' patriarch of "combat-power inflation" narrative. From Saiyan to Super Saiyan God to Ultra Instinct, his combat-power number has no upper limit; enemies are stronger wave after wave; he bursts out stronger wave after wave. In old narratives this is called "never-giving-up warrior race." But under Heng Yuan Zhou's five-dimension comparison framework, Goku-style "infinite growth" precisely exposes the fourth transcendence limitation — **aimless combat-power inflation**: power grows exponentially, but what to do after growing stronger has no answer.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom measures Goku's power origin; the republican axiom of Myriad Races measures whether he benefited Myriad Ranks after growing stronger; the collective ascension axiom measures whether his combat power has converted into civilizational increment; the causal self-discipline axiom measures his repeated power-ups' disturbance of Earth's causality; the law-first axiom measures whether the "combat power infinitely inflates yet has no goal" setting is self-consistent.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Aimless Combat-Power Inflation's Three Traits

Goku-style transcendence, disassembled, has three traits:

1. **Combat power no upper limit**: no law stipulates where Goku's combat-power ceiling is; however strong the enemy, however high he bursts;
2. **Goal purely adversarial**: his growth is always to "beat the next enemy"; after enemies are beaten, find the next enemy; no constructive goal;
3. **Civilizational contribution zero**: after he grows stronger, Earth is still the same Earth; after the Z-Fighters finish fighting, they go back to farming; no civilizational engineering advances because he grew stronger.

Three traits stack, forming a floating state of "always preparing for war, always fighting, after fighting waiting for the next fight." Goku is not a bad person, but his strength is a one-way account to civilization.

## II. Why "Pure Combat Growth" Does Not Hold in Heng Yuan Zhou

Old narratives take "combat is cultivation" as gospel; Heng Yuan Zhou points out its three structural problems:

- **Combat is consumption, not production**: every power-up consumes massive planets, cities, even galaxies; after winning, these consumptions do not auto-return. Every time Goku grows stronger, it corresponds to a civilization-level destruction bill;
- **Enemies are manufactured**: if Goku did not keep growing stronger, there would be no enemy worthy of him. Old narratives, to maintain his "growth line," continuously manufacture stronger enemies, equal to continuously manufacturing disasters for the world so he has fights to fight;
- **Growth has no landing point**: after Goku grows to Super Saiyan God, he still only takes his son fishing and farms. His strength has not converted into any civilizational increment — no teaching material, no law-weaving, no public works.

These three together are "aimless combat-power inflation"'s essence: **strong is strong, but strength has no meaning.** It is not that combat is wrong, but that after combat there must be construction; otherwise combat is only consumption.

## III. Goku-Style Inflation's Causal Cost

From the causal self-discipline axiom, Goku-style power-up has a hidden layer of cost: every time he expands the battlefield to the whole galaxy "to protect Earth," he disturbs the whole plane's causal network.

| Event Type | Causal Disturbance Scale | Consequence |
|-------------|---------------------------|-------------|
| Fight Raditz | Earth-level | City destruction, population casualties |
| Fight Vegeta | Galaxy-level | Large-scale Earth surface destruction |
| Fight Frieza | Planet-level | Namek destroyed, race genocide |
| Fight Cell | Earth-level | Androids out of control, humankind-extinction risk |
| Fight Majin Buu | Cosmos-level | Earth destroyed, souls devoured |

Every "victory" accompanies a civilization-level destruction. The higher Goku's combat power, the bigger his destruction bill per fight. Heng Yuan Zhou's "observe-first-then-limit-interfere; only backflow knowledge, do not disturb causality" forms the sharpest contrast with Goku-style "smash through first, talk later."

## IV. Comparison with Heng Yuan Zhou Perpetual Construction

Heng Yuan Zhou does not want "stronger warriors," but "stronger builders":

| Dimension | Goku-Style Aimless Inflation | Heng Yuan Zhou Perpetual Construction |
|-----------|-------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Growth direction | To beat the next enemy | To advance the next civilizational engineering |
| Combat nature | Destructive consumption | Limited interference, reported and controllable |
| Civilizational contribution | Near zero | Every unit of computable power invested in civilizational increment |
| Knowledge lineage | Master-disciple oral transmission, unsystematic | Selfless Dao network-wide opening |
| End state | Always waiting for the next enemy | Perpetual construction, no endpoint but with KPIs |

The comparison table's core gap is **whether power has a landing point.** Goku's power lands in the punch's swing; after swinging, it's gone; Heng Yuan Zhou transcended-one's power lands on civilizational engineering's advancement; after pushing, it leaves permanent increment.

## V. Heng Yuan Zhou's Alternative Path

If Goku appeared in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization:

1. His combat talent takes the open path of the 300-set Origin Cultivation System, not relying on high-consumption "life-death-edge power-up" modes;
2. His combat power converts into civilizational engineering: use combat power to open new star-sector planes, calibrate the law network, protect Myriad-Rank migration channels, not to beat the next enemy;
3. His "combat intuition" is organized into teaching material, opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao, for Myriad-Rank cultivators to learn;
4. After entering the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, he becomes a living anchor; his civilizational weight is real-time computed by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision;
5. Every "strike" of his goes through causal self-discipline reporting, only interfering to a limited degree within Myriad-Rank consent.

In one sentence: **true strength is not beating the next enemy, but being strong enough that no enemies are needed yet Myriad Ranks still live better.**

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Goku's combat-power inflation every time accompanies planet-level destruction bills; it burns evolutionary computable power on fighting
- **Anti-predation**: Goku's origin is fairly clean in the old system, but to maintain his "growth line" he continuously manufactures enemies, objectively manufacturing disasters for the plane
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is gift system (power given); Goku is aimless inflation (power fought but no idea what for); both transcendences have no landing point
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Goku's combat only benefits a corner of Earth; plant spirits, planet consciousnesses, and Myriad Ranks on other planes did not even receive his "protection"
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Goku's combat insights should be network-wide open; aimless inflation locks them in master-disciple oral transmission; later-comers may only burst out themselves again
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Goku is the old world's most lovable strong one — he is pure, kind, and loves combat. But "loving combat" is not "knowing construction." Heng Yuan Zhou does not deny Goku's strength; what Heng Yuan Zhou asks is: **after being strong, then what?** Smashed through Frieza, smashed through Cell, smashed through Buu, then what? True transcendence is not always finding the next enemy, but being strong enough that one day the world no longer needs enemies, and you still have work to do.
