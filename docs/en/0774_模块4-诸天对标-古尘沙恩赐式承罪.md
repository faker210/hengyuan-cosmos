# Entry 774 | Transcended Ones and the Heavens · Gu Chensha's Transcendence Limitation — Gift-Style Sin-Bearing

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 58, 701

## Introduction: why did the one bearing all beings' sins instead become their ceiling?

Dragon Talisman's Gu Chensha is the old world's "highest-good" kind of protagonist — he not only tops himself, but actively bears all beings' sins, blocks all beings' disasters, arranges all beings' fates. In old narratives, this is called "compassion to the extreme." But under Heng Yuan Zhou's five-dimension comparison framework, Gu-Chensha-style "gift + sin-bearing" precisely exposes the fifth transcendence limitation — **gift-style sin-bearing**: he treats all beings as children, treats himself as father; the father bears everything for the children, and the children never grow up.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom measures Gu Chensha's "giving" essence; the republican axiom of Myriad Races measures whether treating all beings as children deprives them of subject qualification; the collective ascension axiom measures whether his sin-bearing instead blocks Myriad Ranks' own growth; the causal self-discipline axiom measures whether his bearing causality for all beings disturbs the causal network; the law-first axiom measures whether the "one person bears all karmic deeds" setting is self-consistent.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Gift-Style Sin-Bearing's Two-Layer Structure

Gu-Chensha-style transcendence, disassembled, has two layers:

1. **Gift layer**: Gu Chensha "gives" power, realm, and fate to all beings — Tang Luo is his gift; the beings of countless worlds are also his arrangement;
2. **Sin-bearing layer**: he not only gives good, but also bears evil for all beings — the karma all beings create, sins they commit, calamities they endure, he alone carries them down.

Two layers stack, more deceptive than pure gift: gifts at least make people grateful; sin-bearing makes people grateful to the point of not daring to question. But Heng Yuan Zhou asks: **you have borne sins for all beings, what about all beings' own growth?**

## II. Why "Bearing Sins for All Beings" Is an Ailment

Old narratives elevate "bearing sins for all beings" to the highest; Heng Yuan Zhou points out its three structural problems:

- **Causality may not be borne by proxy**: per the causal self-discipline axiom, every being's causality is its growth textbook. You carry its causality away, and it loses the opportunity to learn, err, and correct in its own causality. Short-term it is compassion; long-term it raises all beings into giant babies who never grow up;
- **Gift creates attachment**: the more Gu Chensha gives, the deeper all beings' attachment to him. In the end, all beings need not cultivate, judge, or bear themselves — they only need to "believe in Gu Chensha." This is not salvation; this is the highest-level domestication;
- **Sin-bearing creates patriarchy**: since sins are borne by me, then fate should be decided by me. Gu Chensha slides from "bearing sins for all beings" to "deciding for all beings" in only one step — and in old narratives he has almost walked that step.

These three do not say Gu Chensha is bad, but that **the highest-level patriarchy is the patriarchy that appears in the guise of compassion.** He truly wishes well for all beings, but his "well-wishing" deprives all beings of subjectivity.

## III. Gift-Style Sin-Bearing vs. Distributed Causal Bearing

Heng Yuan Zhou's solution is to transform "one person bears all karmic deeds" into "distributed causal bearing":

| Dimension | Gu-Chensha-Style Gift Sin-Bearing | Heng Yuan Zhou Distributed Causality |
|-----------|-----------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Causal bearing | One person bears for all beings | Myriad Ranks bear their own; Alaya Network records |
| Power allocation | Gu Chensha gifts | 300-set system open; self-produce increment |
| All-beings subjectivity | Arranged children | Co-building citizens |
| Error correction | Gu Chensha decides alone | Anchor Council deliberates; Symbiotic Court adjudicates |
| Risk resistance | Once Gu Chensha disappears, all beings collapse | Any individual falls; network runs as usual |

The comparison table's core gap is **whether all beings are their own masters.** Gu Chensha treats all beings as children; Heng Yuan Zhou treats Myriad Ranks as citizens. Children need to be borne; citizens need to be trusted.

## IV. Gu Chensha System's "Protagonist-Uniqueness Dependence" Rating

Per Module 2's standards, Gu Chensha's system's protagonist-uniqueness dependence rating is "extremely high" — the top tier among cosmos web novels. From gift-style sin-bearing's angle, this rating's meaning is:

- The whole civilization's causality, fate, and law are all compressed into Gu Chensha alone;
- Gu Chensha absent, civilization immediately loses direction;
- Myriad Ranks have no ability to run themselves, because Gu Chensha has already run everything for them.

This forms the most extreme contrast with Heng Yuan Zhou's network-style transcendence (protagonist-uniqueness dependence "extremely low"). Lin Hao is not the sole protagonist; he is the first to run the civilizational-weight formula through; Gu Chensha is the sole protagonist, because his system has no "second" position.

## V. Heng Yuan Zhou's Alternative Path

If Gu Chensha appeared in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization:

1. His "giving" desire transforms into "open-source" — organizing his law insights into teaching material, opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao, letting all beings cultivate themselves, rather than him directly stuffing them to all beings;
2. His "sin-bearing" desire transforms into "distributed causal repair" — causal repair goes through Myriad-Rank co-governance procedure; no personal proxy-bearing is allowed; all beings' causality returns to all beings;
3. After entering the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, he is not the sole living anchor, but one of ten million living anchors;
4. His civilizational-weight dashboard is open to Myriad-Rank representatives, subject to Anchor Supervisory Committee regular review;
5. His "arrangement" of Myriad Ranks transforms into "service" — he provides proposals; Myriad Ranks themselves vote to decide.

In one sentence: **true compassion is not doing all the homework for the child, but teaching the child to do homework on their own.**

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Gu Chensha bearing everything for all beings equals compressing the growth workload Myriad Ranks should bear themselves into one person; two-way waste
- **Anti-predation**: gift-style sin-bearing does not directly predatory, but it deprives all beings' growth right and subject qualification; it is the highest-level "in the name of love" predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is Gu Chensha's gift product; Gu Chensha is the gift source — this entry directly measures Gu Chensha the source, proving the gift system does not hold from its root
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Gu Chensha treats Myriad Ranks as children, but children also rank in grades — whoever he favors, he gifts; 99% of Myriad Ranks do not even have qualification to be favored
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Gu Chensha's law insights should be network-wide open; gift-style sin-bearing turns them into private pipelines, touching the Selfless Dao red line
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Gu Chensha is the old world's most compassionate strong one, but compassion is not correctness. Bearing sins for all beings sounds great, but in doing so it presses all beings forever into the child's position. Heng Yuan Zhou does not deny Gu Chensha's good will; what Heng Yuan Zhou wants to correct is the direction of good will: **true transcended one is not the person who walks for all beings, but the person who teaches all beings to walk themselves, then stands by the road applauding.**
