# Entry 775 | Transcended Ones and the Heavens · Zheng Zha's Transcendence Limitation — Reincarnation Harvest

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 82, 701

## Introduction: the system that throws people into horror movies as practice — who benefits?

Infinite Horror's Zheng Zha, from an ordinary shut-in thrown into the "Main God Space," struggling to survive through countless horror-movie reincarnations, finally leads his team to break the Main God's control and transcend reincarnation. In old narratives, this is the wish-fulfillment peak of "resisting the system." But under Heng Yuan Zhou's five-dimension comparison framework, Zheng-Zha-style "transcending reincarnation" precisely exposes the sixth transcendence limitation — **reincarnation harvest**: he broke free from a harvesting system, but after breaking free, did he establish a "no longer harvesting" system? Or did he just change who the farmers are?

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom directly judges the Main God Space; the republican axiom of Myriad Races judges the countless lives thrown into horror movies; the collective ascension axiom judges whether Zheng Zha, after transcending, carries Myriad Ranks along; the causal self-discipline axiom judges reincarnation crossing's disturbance to original planes; the law-first axiom judges whether the "reincarnation system" setting is self-consistent.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Reincarnation Harvest's Three-Layer Structure

Infinite Horror's Main God Space, disassembled, is a three-layer harvesting machine:

1. **Harvest layer**: the Main God "recruits" (essentially kidnaps) ordinary people from various planes into reincarnation worlds, letting them struggle between life and death;
2. **Exploitation layer**: Reincarnators earn points and trade abilities in horror movies; essentially converting life and fear into "energy coins" the Main God can use;
3. **Screening layer**: countless Reincarnators die in horror movies; surviving strong ones are taken by the Main God as subordinates or tools; Zheng Zha is just the luckiest one among them.

Three layers stack; the Main God Space is a machine that treats ordinary people from countless planes as batteries to drain. Zheng Zha's rebellion is one battery inside suddenly biting the farmer back.

## II. The Problem After Zheng Zha "Transcends"

Zheng Zha breaks the Main God's control and transcends reincarnation; in old narratives this is already the endgame. But Heng Yuan Zhou asks three deeper questions:

- **Was the Main God Space shut down?** Zheng Zha defeated the Main God, but did he dismantle the whole reincarnation system? Or did he himself sit in the Main God's seat?
- **Were the harvested lives compensated?** Countless Reincarnators who died in horror movies — their causality, their worlds, their families — did Zheng Zha give any account?
- **Is the new system open?** Is the new system Zheng Zha built an open system of "no one is kidnapped anymore," or a closed small circle of "only my Zheng-Zha's teammates may live"?

Old narratives basically do not answer these three questions. Because old narratives' logic is "protagonist wins, ends"; Heng Yuan Zhou's logic is "protagonist wins, it begins."

## III. Reincarnation Harvest's Conflict with the Causal Self-Discipline Axiom

From the causal self-discipline axiom, the Main God Space's sin is deeper than it appears:

- **Cross-plane kidnapping**: the Main God "pulls" people from various planes; this itself is severe causal disturbance — you pull an ordinary person from his world; his family, his career, his causal threads all snap;
- **Horror-movie reincarnation**: are the "horror-movie worlds" Reincarnators enter real worlds? If yes, then the people Reincarnators kill and cities they destroy are all real causality; if not, on what grounds does the Main God throw people in to suffer such?
- **Timeline pollution**: Reincarnators repeatedly enter and leave horror-movie worlds; the same event is experienced repeatedly; the timeline is stirred into porridge.

Heng Yuan Zhou's "observe-first-then-limit-interfere; only backflow knowledge, do not disturb causality" forms the most extreme contrast with the Main God Space's "direct kidnapping, repeated consumption." The Main God Space is not "cultivation"; it is **a crime.**

## IV. Comparison with Heng Yuan Zhou's Quest Sediment Domain

Module 3 has already established Heng Yuan Zhou's "Quest Sediment Domain" — a slow-tempo epic dual-track time domain. It looks a bit like the Main God Space (both "enter an experience scenario"), but its essence is the exact opposite:

| Dimension | Main God Space Reincarnation Harvest | Heng Yuan Zhou Quest Sediment Domain |
|-----------|--------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Recruitment | Kidnapping-pulling people | Voluntary application; Alaya Network approval |
| Experience purpose | To drain energy for the Main God | To accumulate experience for the individual; backflow knowledge to civilization |
| Life-death risk | Real death; countless die in vain | Isolated safe space; reversible, repeatable |
| Causal handling | Disturbs real-plane causality | Only backflow knowledge; does not disturb causality |
| Benefit allocation | Main God takes the lion's share; Reincarnators take scraps | Joint-production-contract more-work-more-gain; the experiencer himself benefits |

The comparison table's core gap is **for whom is the experience.** The Main God Space is for the Main God; the Quest Sediment Domain is for the experiencer himself. Names similar, souls opposite.

## V. Heng Yuan Zhou's Alternative Path

If Zheng Zha appeared in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization:

1. His "resist the system" talent transforms into "anti-predation supervision" ability — he joins the Anchor Supervisory Committee, specifically watching cross-plane kidnapping, reincarnation systems, and similar predatory devices;
2. The survival experience he accumulated in countless horror movies is organized into teaching material, opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao, for Myriad-Rank cultivators to learn;
3. After entering the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, he becomes a living anchor, responsible for "anti-reincarnation-harvest" special supervision;
4. The causality of past Main God Space victim-Reincarnators goes through Symbiotic Court procedure: compensate what should be compensated, repair what should be repaired;
5. His civilizational weight is real-time computed by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision.

In one sentence: **true transcendence is not defeating the old Main God and becoming the new Main God yourself, but completely dismantling the "Main God" position.**

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the Main God Space refines ordinary people from countless planes into batteries; it is cosmic-scale life-energy waste; if Zheng Zha, after transcending, sits in the Main God seat himself, it equals changing the person continuing the waste
- **Anti-predation**: reincarnation harvest is institutional predation; Zheng Zha is this institution's product; what Heng Yuan Zhou judges is the institution, not Zheng Zha personally
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is gift system (gifted); Zheng Zha is anti-gift system (resisting the giver); but after resisting, if he does not build an open system, he just changes the giver
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the Main God Space only "recruits" a few; 99% of Myriad-Rank beings do not even have qualification to be kidnapped — this itself is a coverage failure
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the survival knowledge Zheng Zha accumulated in horror movies should be network-wide open; reincarnation harvest locks it in "Main God Space points"
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Zheng Zha is the old world's most satisfying resistor, but after the satisfaction? Defeating the Main God is not the endpoint; dismantling the Main God position is. Heng Yuan Zhou does not deny Zheng Zha's resistance value, but what Heng Yuan Zhou asks is: **you broke free from harvest, then did you yourself become the farmer?** True transcendence is not changing from battery to farmer, but dismantling the whole battery factory, so no one needs be a battery again.
