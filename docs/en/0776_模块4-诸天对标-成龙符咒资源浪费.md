# Entry 776 | Transcended Ones and the Heavens · Jackie Chan's Transcendence Limitation — Talisman Resource Waste

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 18, 69, 701

## Introduction: the Twelve Talismans — why can't they be all humankind's infrastructure?

Jackie Chan Adventures' Twelve Talismans each carry civilization-grade supernatural power — Rat Talisman animates, Ox Talisman is super-strength, Tiger Talisman balances yin-yang, Rabbit Talisman is super-speed, Dragon Talisman breathes fire... Twelve combined are enough to reshape Earth's whole technology and civilization landscape. But in old narratives, what is the fate of these twelve talismans? Fought over by the Dark Hand, thrown around by Jackie, finally sealed by Uncle's one line of magic. Under Heng Yuan Zhou's five-dimension comparison framework, this exposes the seventh transcendence limitation — **talisman resource waste**: civilization-grade resources fought over like toys, finally sealed in an antique shop's basement.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom measures talisman-fighting's essence; the republican axiom of Myriad Races measures why talisman power is not open to all humankind; the collective ascension axiom measures the civilizational progress talismans should have driven; the causal self-discipline axiom measures talisman power's disturbance to the real world; the law-first axiom measures whether the "talisman power used arbitrarily then sealed arbitrarily" setting is self-consistent.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Talisman Resource Waste's Three-Layer Structure

The Twelve Talismans' fate in old narratives, disassembled, has three layers of waste:

1. **Fight waste**: heroes and villains fight season after season over "monopolizing" talismans; every fight causes city destruction, casualties — destruction that could have been avoided;
2. **Misuse waste**: whoever grabs talismans uses power for small things — Dark Hand uses Rabbit Talisman to rob banks, Valmont uses Dragon Talisman to threaten governments; no one thinks to use Rabbit Talisman to revamp global logistics, Dragon Talisman to solve the energy crisis;
3. **Seal waste**: finally the talismans are sealed; no one may use them. Civilization-grade power gathers dust in the basement.

Three layers stack; twelve talismans went from "human-civilization-upgrade keys" to "villain toys" and "Uncle's collection." This is not resource utilization; this is resource slaughter.

## II. Why "Sealing the Talismans" Is Not the Solution

Old narratives treat "sealing the talismans" as justice's victory — bad guys can't use them, good guys don't either, everyone's safe. But under Heng Yuan Zhou's framework, sealing is the worst choice:

- **Power principle not publicized**: why do talismans have this power? What's the principle? Can it be replicated? Old narratives never explain, because explaining would require opening;
- **Use threshold privatized**: talismans may only be activated by specific people (Jackie, Uncle, Jade); ordinary people cannot even touch them;
- **Seal creates new fights**: the seal location itself becomes the new fight focus — Dark Hand, unable to grab talismans, grabs the seal location; war never stops.

Heng Yuan Zhou's solution (Document 69 · Technology-Opening and Sharing Mechanism) is the exact opposite: **power principles network-wide publicized; use supervised by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision; whoever wants to use it applies; what public engineering it goes to is decided by the Anchor Council.** Sealing is not the answer; opening + supervision is.

If the Twelve Talismans appeared in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, they would not be sealed, but disassembled into twelve public technologies:

|----------|-------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Rat | Animates inanimate objects | Mechanical-constructed clan (Document 50 lineage) civilization interface |
| Ox | Super-strength | Heavy engineering, disaster rescue, interstellar colonization |

Twelve talisman powers, each could become a public engineering project. Locking them in the basement equals sealing twelve gold mines in sand.

## IV. The Limit of Jackie Chan-Style "Protection"

Jackie Chan in old narratives is the hero who "protects talismans from bad guys." But Heng Yuan Zhou points out: **protecting only without using is itself a waste.** Jackie protected for twelve seasons; the talismans are still those twelve talismans; human civilization did not take one more step because they existed.

This is consistent with entry 706's civilizational-weight formula: talismans' "destructive power" is enormous, but their "civilizational consensus degree" is zero — because they have not been used by any public engineering, have not benefited any Myriad-Rank being. Under the multiplication formula, the twelve talismans' effective combat power on the civilizational ledger is zero: they are in the basement, same as nonexistent.

## V. Heng Yuan Zhou's Alternative Path

If Jackie Chan appeared in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization:

1. The twelve talismans' power principles go through technical reverse-engineering, opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao;
2. Each talisman corresponds to a public engineering project; the Anchor Council allocates use quotas;
3. Jackie's "adventure-protection" experience transforms into a "crisis response" post — he joins the Symbiotic Executive Committee, responsible for frontline rescue of cross-plane sudden disasters;
4. Uncle's "magic" knowledge is organized into teaching material, opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao, not hoarded in an antique shop;
5. Talisman use is supervised throughout by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision; any abuse is real-time intercepted;
6. If Jackie continues improving during talisman use, he may eventually enter the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, becoming the public-ization-of-talisman-power living anchor.

In one sentence: **true protection of a talisman is not locking it away, but letting it serve Myriad Ranks.**

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: twelve civilization-grade talismans sealed and gathering dust is sealing twelve gold mines in the basement; cosmic-scale resource waste
- **Anti-predation**: talisman fighting itself is predation — heroes and villains both want to monopolize; no one wants to open
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is gift system (power given and locked); talismans are resource system (power exists but sealed); two wastes' essence is the same: power does not flow to Myriad Ranks
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: talisman power only benefits Jackie's family and the Dark Hand; plant spirits, planet consciousnesses, and Myriad Ranks on other planes cannot even see them
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: talisman principles should be network-wide open; seal-style protection turns them into antique-shop private hoards, touching the Selfless Dao red line
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Jackie Chan is the old world's most lovable hero — kind, brave, protecting the weak. But his way of protecting is "hiding power away," not "using power out." Heng Yuan Zhou does not deny Jackie's protection value, but what Heng Yuan Zhou asks is: **after protecting for twelve seasons, what did the talismans drive?** True transcendence is not locking civilization-grade power in the basement, but disassembling it into public engineering, letting the twelve talismans become Myriad-Rank-shared infrastructure.
