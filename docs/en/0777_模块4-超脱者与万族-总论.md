# Entry 777 | Transcended Ones and Myriad Races · Overview — Reconstructing the Relationship Between Transcended Ones and Myriad Ranks

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 01, 701

## Introduction: the old world places transcended ones above Myriad Ranks; Heng Yuan Zhou places them among Myriad Ranks

Entries 767–776, nine benchmark articles, measured the cosmos' old IPs' "transcended ones" one by one, and measured out a shared ailment: **all old IPs' transcended ones are above Myriad Ranks; none is among Myriad Ranks.** Tang Luo gifts above Myriad Ranks; Ye Fan carries the flag above Myriad Ranks; Shi Hao decides alone above Myriad Ranks; Gu Chensha bears sins above Myriad Ranks — their relationship with Myriad Ranks is all vertical, top-down. This entry, as this group's overview, must completely reconstruct this vertical relationship into a horizontal one. The Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcended one's relationship with Myriad Ranks is not "king and subjects," but "co-builder and co-builder."

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom requires the reconstructed relationship contain no predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races is reconstruction's jurisprudential base; the collective ascension axiom requires the reconstructed relationship serve common uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the reconstruction process not disturb Myriad Ranks' causality; the law-first axiom requires the reconstructed relationship be self-consistent at the law layer.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Old Relationship's Three Old Templates

The old world's transcended-one/Myriad-Rank relationship, no matter how varied, always falls on three templates:

1. **Ruler template**: the transcended one is Myriad Ranks' king; Myriad Ranks are subjects. Representatives: Shi Hao, various Heavenly Emperors;
2. **Protector template**: the transcended one is Myriad Ranks' shield; Myriad Ranks are the protected. Representatives: Ye Fan, Goku;
3. **Giver template**: the transcended one is Myriad Ranks' father; Myriad Ranks are raised children. Representatives: Gu Chensha, Tang Luo.

Three templates look different, but their essence is the same — **transcended one above, Myriad Ranks below.** In vertical relationships, Myriad Ranks are always the passive party: ruled, protected, raised. This relationship's result: the stronger the transcended one, the weaker Myriad Ranks; the more responsible the transcended one, the less responsible Myriad Ranks.

## II. Heng Yuan Zhou's New Relationship: Co-Builder Relationship

The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's new relationship turns vertical completely horizontal:

> **The transcended one is not Myriad Ranks' king, not Myriad Ranks' shield, not Myriad Ranks' father, but Myriad Ranks' co-builder — a co-builder who happens to hold a higher post, larger computable power, and heavier responsibility.**

The new relationship has three components:

- **Same post, different rank**: the transcended one and Myriad Ranks are in the same "Myriad-Rank Republican co-building" post system, only differing in rank. Higher rank means heavier responsibility, not higher status;
- **Two-way cycle**: the transcended one draws power from the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle, and must feed power back into the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle. Not one-way giving, but two-way cycle;
- **Mutual supervision**: Myriad Ranks supervise the transcended one (civilizational-weight dashboard open); the transcended one also serves Myriad Ranks (post performance accountable to Myriad Ranks). Not one-way rule, but mutual accountability. This mutual supervision lands through Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision, traceable, auditable, non-repudiable.

Three components stack; the transcended one and Myriad Ranks' relationship changes from "father and son" to "coworkers" — except this coworker happens to have 10,000 times your computable power.

## III. Relationship Reconstruction's Three Law-Science Consequences

Turning vertical into horizontal produces three hard consequences at the law layer:

1. **The transcended one no longer has a "veto right"**: the old world's transcended one decides Myriad Ranks' fate with one sentence; in the new relationship the transcended one only has proposal and vote rights, equal to Myriad-Rank representatives;
2. **Myriad Ranks are no longer "the protected"**: Myriad Ranks have their own causality, their own responsibility, their own growth right; the transcended one may not bear it by proxy;
3. **The transcended one's power legitimacy comes from Myriad-Rank authorization**: per entry 706's civilizational-weight formula, the transcended one's "effective combat power" must be multiplied by "civilizational consensus degree"; consensus degree zero → effective combat power zero.

These three consequences are not moral requirements, but law-layer definitions. Any transcended one wanting to return to the old template directly loses points in the civilizational-weight formula — once points fall below threshold, law-anchoring permissions auto-downgrade.

## IV. Six Sub-Articles' Positioning Preview

Entries 778–783, six sub-articles, will handle the new relationship's six specific dimensions one by one:

- **Representation qualification** (778): on what grounds does the transcended one represent Myriad Ranks? On what grounds do Myriad Ranks recognize him?
- **Feedback obligation** (779): what must the transcended one give back to Myriad Ranks? How much?
- **Supervision mechanism** (780): how do Myriad Ranks supervise the transcended one? How is the transcended one held accountable?
- **Education responsibility** (781): what must the transcended one teach Myriad Ranks? How?
- **Dispute mediation** (782): when Myriad Ranks fight among themselves, on what grounds does the transcended one mediate? By what procedure?
- **Cultural protection** (783): how does the transcended one protect Myriad Ranks' cultural diversity? How to prevent "strong-one culture" from devouring "weak-one culture"?

Six dimensions together are the complete operation manual of "transcended-one/Myriad-Rank co-builder relationship."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: old templates lock the transcended one's computable power into three vertical posts (rule/protect/gift); the new relationship releases this computable power into Myriad-Rank co-building horizontal networks
- **Anti-predation**: old templates' vertical relationship is essentially "strong-one's structural domination of the weak"; the new relationship eliminates this domination from its root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is the giver template's extreme — he acts as father above Myriad Ranks; Heng Yuan Zhou pulls him down to be a coworker
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the new relationship covers 99% of Myriad Ranks' life forms; plant spirits, planet consciousnesses, and cosmos-origin bodies all have seats in the co-building network
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the new relationship's entire institutional design (representation qualification, feedback obligation, supervision mechanism, etc.) is opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The transcended-one/Myriad-Rank relationship is Module 4's most central relationship. The old world places transcended ones above Myriad Ranks; Heng Yuan Zhou places them among Myriad Ranks. This position's move is not a moral posture, but a law rewrite — it decides whether the transcended one's power is used to press Myriad Ranks or to lift Myriad Ranks.
