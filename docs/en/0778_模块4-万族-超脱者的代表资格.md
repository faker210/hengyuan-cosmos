# Entry 778 | Transcended Ones and Myriad Races · The Transcended One's Myriad-Rank Representation Qualification

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 49, 777

## Introduction: who grants the transcended one qualification to represent Myriad Ranks?

The old world's transcended one is naturally Myriad Ranks' representative — he is strongest, so he represents. This "strong-one-is-representative" logic is completely abolished in Heng Yuan Zhou. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization must answer a harder question: **one person strong enough for the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm — on what grounds does he represent Myriad Ranks? Why do Myriad Ranks recognize him?** This entry answers this "representation qualification" question.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom requires the representation qualification's origin be clean; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the qualification be open to Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires the qualification serve common uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the qualification-acquisition process not disturb Myriad Ranks' causality; the law-first axiom requires the qualification's law definition be self-consistent and repeatable.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Representation Qualification's Four Hard Thresholds

Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended one must simultaneously cross four hard thresholds to gain Myriad-Rank representation qualification:

1. **Origin threshold**: all power from the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle, zero predatory residue. Those with improper origins cannot even enter the representation-qualification application stage;
2. **Coverage threshold**: his work history must have benefited over 99% of Myriad Ranks' life forms (five lineages: carbon-based material, energy-condensing, mechanical-constructed, consciousness-information, mixed-mutated), not only humankind;
3. **Consensus threshold**: the "civilizational consensus degree" in the civilizational-weight formula must be maintained above 0.8 for three consecutive cycles, and the coverage-breadth subitem must not fall below 0.7;
4. **Procedure threshold**: his cross-plane and cross-time operations must be 100% reported through causal self-discipline, with zero violation records.

Four thresholds, no substitute. Any one unmet, no matter how strong the transcended one, he is only an "individual strong one," without Myriad-Rank representation qualification.

## II. Why "Strong" Is Not Qualification

The old world's logic is "strong = representative"; Heng Yuan Zhou's logic is "strong ≠ representative." Three reasons:

- **Strength only shows large destructive power, not correct direction**: a strong one with enormous destructive power but wrong direction representing Myriad Ranks equals selling Myriad Ranks out. Entry 706's multiplication formula already mathematizes this;
- **Strength is private ability; representation is public authorization**: one person cultivating to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is his own business, but if he wants to speak for Myriad Ranks, he must go through Myriad-Rank authorization. Authorization is not automatic; it is procedural;
- **Strength changes; qualification must be stable**: strong ones make mistakes, go astray, get eroded by inner demons. If "strong = representative," then once he goes astray, Myriad Ranks are mis-represented.

## III. Representation Qualification's Acquisition Procedure

Representation qualification is not a one-time exam for life tenure, but goes through a dynamic procedure:

1. **Application**: the transcended one actively submits a representation-qualification application to the Anchor Council, attaching his complete civilizational-weight dashboard;
2. **Deliberation**: Myriad-Rank representatives deliberate separately by the five lineages — carbon-based material representatives review his origin; energy-condensing representatives review his direction; mechanical-constructed representatives review his procedure; consciousness-information representatives review his coverage; mixed-mutated representatives review his ethics;
3. **Public notice**: after deliberation passes, the representation qualification is publicized on the Alaya Network for one cycle; any Myriad-Rank being may raise objection;
4. **Authorization**: after public notice with no objection, the Anchor Council issues a representation-qualification credential, formally granting the transcended one qualification to speak, propose, and vote at the Myriad-Rank level;
5. **Annual review**: representation qualification is annually reviewed each cycle; any subitem slipping out of threshold suspends qualification; after rectification, it resumes.

## IV. Representation Qualification's Limits

Gaining representation qualification does not equal gaining ruling power. Heng Yuan Zhou's representation qualification has three hard limits:

- **No veto right**: the transcended one as representative has only one vote in the Anchor Council, equal to other Myriad-Rank representatives. His "strength" does not give him extra votes;
- **Fixed term**: representation qualification is not life tenure; each term has a clear duration; at term end, he must reapply;
- **Recallable**: over one-third of Myriad-Rank representatives jointly petitioning may launch a recall procedure. Once recall passes, representation qualification immediately terminates.

These three limits ensure the transcended one is always Myriad Ranks' "employee," not Myriad Ranks' "master."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the old world letting the strongest naturally represent Myriad Ranks equals wasting representation qualification on someone whose "direction is unverified"; Heng Yuan Zhou uses four thresholds to filter out those truly worthy
- **Anti-predation**: the origin threshold directly seals predatory strong ones' representation qualification — your power was grabbed; you have no qualification to represent the Myriad Ranks you once robbed
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo naturally "represents" his plane because he is strongest; Heng Yuan Zhou requires Tang Luo to cross four thresholds, otherwise he is only a high-risk individual
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the coverage threshold explicitly requires benefiting 99% of the five-lineages' life forms, directly ensuring representation qualification does not only serve humankind
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: representation qualification's entire procedure, standards, and deliberation records are opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao; any Myriad-Rank being may audit
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The transcended one's Myriad-Rank representation qualification is not a "strong-one-natural" privilege, but a "Myriad-Rank-common-authorization" post. Qualification may be gained, annually reviewed, and recalled. This turns the transcended one from "Myriad Ranks' master" into "Myriad Ranks' employee" — an employee who happens to have 10,000 times the computable power. No matter how strong the employee, he must obey the employer.
