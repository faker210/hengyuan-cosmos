# Entry 779 | Transcended Ones and Myriad Races | The Transcended One's Myriad-Rank Feedback Obligation

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 25, 22, 777

## Introduction: come from Myriad Ranks, go back to Myriad Ranks

A transcended one's power is not conjured out of thin air by himself. Every wisp of spiritual energy he absorbed on his cultivation road, every thread of law, every moment of sudden enlightenment — behind it all is the whole Myriad-Origin two-way cycle transfusing him. Heng Yuan Zhou therefore has an iron rule: **power that came from Myriad Ranks must return to Myriad Ranks.** This is the transcended one's Myriad-Rank feedback obligation. This entry breaks this obligation into a quantifiable, auditable, accountable post description.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom is the feedback obligation's flip side — only-in-no-out is predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races is the feedback obligation's jurisprudential source; the collective ascension axiom is the feedback obligation's direction; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the feedback process not disturb Myriad Ranks' causality; the law-first axiom requires the feedback obligation be quantifiable and executable at the law layer.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Feedback Obligation's Four Hard Indicators

After entering the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, the transcended one's feedback obligation is real-time quantified by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision on four hard indicators:

1. **Knowledge feedback rate**: new cultivation manuals, new law-weavings, and new civilizational-engineering proposals produced during his cultivation must be opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao. Feedback rate = opened knowledge / total knowledge output; red line is 95%;
2. **Computable-power feedback amount**: a fixed proportion (baseline 50%) of his personal computable power must be invested in public civilizational engineering — law calibration, star-sector maintenance, new-recruit training, etc. Computable-power feedback amount is auto-accounted by Alaya Network;
3. **Resource feedback amount**: in his joint-production-contract resource pool, a proportion (baseline 20%) must be fed back to Myriad Ranks' weak-coverage races (plant spirits, planet consciousnesses, low-order alien races) development funds;
4. **Time feedback amount**: each year he must spend a fixed duration (baseline 30%) on frontline service — stationed in Myriad-Rank clans, teaching, mediating; he cannot only issue orders from high-level meeting rooms.

Any one of the four indicators below red line for two consecutive cycles triggers a post interview; below red line for four consecutive cycles downgrades civilizational weight.

## II. Why Feedback Is Obligation, Not Gift

The old world calls the strong helping the weak "gift"; Heng Yuan Zhou calls it "obligation." One character's difference, worlds apart:

- **Gift is decided by the giver**: how much the strong one gives, what he gives, to whom he gives, all depends on his mood;
- **Obligation is owed to the receiver**: Myriad Ranks have a legitimate claim on the transcended one's feedback; it is not "begging him to give," but "he must give."

This difference is already mathematized in entry 706's civilizational-weight formula: the origin-legality subitem requires the transcended one's power all come from the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle. Since power's origin is Myriad Ranks', feedback is not "extra good deed," but "paying back the debt." A transcended one who does not pay back directly loses points on origin legality.

## III. Feedback Is Not One-Way Alms

The feedback obligation is most easily misunderstood as "the strong give alms to the weak." Heng Yuan Zhou must clarify: **feedback is not alms, but cycle.**

The Myriad-Origin two-way cycle's way of working is:

1. Every Myriad-Rank being produces origin increment at his own post;
2. Increment converges through Alaya Network to the anchor network;
3. The anchor network distributes increment to cultivators in need — including transcended ones;
4. The transcended one uses this increment to cultivate, producing higher-tier knowledge and computable power;
5. The transcended one feeds knowledge and computable power back into the network;
6. The network again distributes this feedback to every Myriad-Rank being.

In this cycle, the transcended one is not "giving Myriad Ranks grace," but "a high-power node on the cycle chain." He feeds back because his position is on the cycle chain — if he does not feed back, the cycle breaks, and his own origin also cuts off.

## IV. Feedback Obligation and the Justified Rascal

The feedback obligation has a special function: it is the institutional tool against "justified rascals."

"Justified rascal" refers to strong ones who, under the banner of "for your own good," actually pull resources toward themselves. The feedback obligation's four hard indicators turn "for your own good" from slogan into numbers — you say you're good for Myriad Ranks; then what's the knowledge feedback rate? Computable-power feedback amount? Resource feedback amount? Time feedback amount? Numbers insufficient, no matter how many fine words, useless.

This is Heng Yuan Zhou's way against "justified rascals": **don't listen to what you say; look at what the Alaya Network recorded.**

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the feedback obligation pulls the transcended one's computable power, knowledge, resources, and time from the private domain back into the public cycle, eliminating only-in-no-out energy waste
- **Anti-predation**: the feedback obligation's flip side is predation — only-in-no-out is advanced predation; four hard indicators directly seal this road
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo took everything from the Myriad-Origin cycle, yet locked later-comers in the fierce realm; Heng Yuan Zhou uses four feedback indicators to force him to pay back
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: resource feedback amount explicitly requires covering Myriad Ranks' weak-coverage races, ensuring feedback does not only flow around strong ones
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: knowledge feedback rate requires over 95% of hyper-multiverse-grade knowledge to be opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao, eliminating private hoarding
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The transcended one's feedback obligation is not a high moral requirement, but a law-layer hard indicator. Four numbers sit on the Alaya Network; more is contribution, less is violation. Power that came from Myriad Ranks must return to Myriad Ranks — this is not sentiment, this is a ledger. The numbers on the ledger are more honest than any manifesto.
