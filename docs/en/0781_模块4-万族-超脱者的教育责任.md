# Entry 781 | Transcended Ones and Myriad Races | The Transcended One's Myriad-Rank Education Responsibility

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 37, 69, 777

## Introduction: the strongest must be a teacher

The old world's transcended ones never taught. Their Dao comes from "sudden enlightenment"; written out, others still cannot understand; their experience is forged "between life and death"; told, others still cannot learn. So old-world transcended ones never systematically educate Myriad Ranks — Myriad Ranks may only "gaze at the imperial posture" and figure it out themselves. Heng Yuan Zhou completely flips this: **the strongest must be a teacher.** This entry breaks the transcended one's education responsibility into an operable teaching post description.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom requires education hoard nothing; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires education open to 99% of Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires education serve common uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires education content not disturb Myriad Ranks' causality; the law-first axiom requires the education system itself be self-consistent, replicable, and iterable.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Education Responsibility's Three Tiers

The transcended one's Myriad-Rank education responsibility has three tiers, no tier substitutable:

1. **Knowledge tier: teaching-material-ization** — the transcended one's every step of insight, every breakthrough, every pit must be organized into teaching material, opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao. Teaching material is not "quotation-style," but structured complete teaching files with prerequisites, steps, cases, and assessment;
2. **Method tier: pedagogy** — teaching material alone is insufficient; the transcended one must also teach "how to learn": how to sense energy, how to discern laws, how to avoid inner demons, how to judge whether one has gone astray. The method tier is more important than the knowledge tier;
3. **Lineage tier: apprenticeship** — the transcended one must have a fixed apprenticeship quota (baseline 3–5 per cohort), personally guiding later-comers past key breakthrough points. Apprenticeship is not "gift," but post obligation.

Three tiers stack: knowledge tier solves "what is there"; method tier solves "how to learn"; lineage tier solves "who guides you."

## II. Why "Sudden Enlightenment Cannot Be Transmitted" Is an Excuse

The old world uses "sudden enlightenment cannot be transmitted" as an excuse to refuse systematic education. Heng Yuan Zhou punctures this excuse:

- **Sudden enlightenment cannot be transmitted because you did not organize it**: you say your Dao cannot be transmitted because you did not spend time breaking it into teachable steps. True Great Dao must be teachable — the law-first axiom requires settings be self-consistent; self-consistent settings can necessarily be written as teaching material;
- **Sudden enlightenment cannot be transmitted equals knowledge monopoly**: if your Dao is truly only understandable by you, then it is not a "Dao," but "your private experience." Private experience not opened is exactly the "monopoly" entry 704 criticizes;
- **Sudden enlightenment cannot be transmitted blocks later-comers**: you enlightened for a lifetime, later-comers must re-enlighten the same thing. This is not "respecting the student's aptitude"; this is wasting Myriad Ranks' time.

Heng Yuan Zhou's stance is clear: **Dao that cannot be written as teaching material does not count.** What you insight, you must be able to teach; cannot teach means you have not truly understood.

## III. Education Responsibility and Myriad Races Full Coverage

The transcended one's education responsibility especially emphasizes tilt toward Myriad Ranks' weak-coverage races:

- **Plant spirits**: the transcended one must develop cultivation teaching material suited to plant-spirit life forms; cannot only teach the carbon-based-material-clan package;
- **Planet consciousnesses**: the transcended one must design for planet consciousnesses a complete advancement path from "unconscious planet" to "awakened planet consciousness";
- **Consciousness-information clans**: the transcended one must cooperate with consciousness-information-clan representatives to translate cultivation teaching material into a format consciousness-form can receive;
- **Mixed-mutated clans**: the transcended one must design cultivation paths compatible with multiple lineages for mixed-mutated clans.

Tilt is not "caring," but "filling gaps." 99% Myriad-Rank coverage is not a slogan, but a hard indicator in the transcended one's annual education work plan.

## IV. Education Responsibility and All Beings' Origin Attachment · Shortcut Inner Demon

The transcended one's education responsibility has a hidden function: **it is the public defense line against the "shortcut inner demon."**

Myriad-Rank beings on the cultivation path are most easily tempted by "quick success," "devouring," and "back-door." The meaning of the transcended one's systematic education is to give later-comers a "right road" — when the right road is clear enough, easy enough, and fair enough, the shortcut inner demon's temptation is much weaker.

This is why Heng Yuan Zhou requires transcended ones to teach: **not teaching is helping the shortcut inner demon steal students.** You don't open the door, predators will open it; you don't teach the right road, evil roads will grow grass.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: if the transcended one's insights are not teaching-material-ized, they dissipate with him personally; teaching-material-ization lets one insight serve Myriad Ranks for ten thousand years. Transcended ones who enter the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm especially must teach, because they hold hyper-multiverse-grade knowledge
- **Anti-predation**: education hoards nothing, directly sealing the predatory path of "knowledge monopoly"; if you don't teach, others will snatch it by predation. Education output is reverse-counted into civilizational weight; the more you teach, the higher the weight
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's Dao was transmitted only to Tang Luo (because it was given by Gu Chensha); Heng Yuan Zhou requires transcended ones to teaching-material-ize the Dao and transmit it to all Myriad-Rank beings
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: education responsibility explicitly requires developing adapted teaching material for all five-lineages' life forms, ensuring 99% coverage
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: all teaching material is opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao, zero threshold, zero fee, zero discrimination. Teaching-material usage is counted by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision, as the basis for the transcended one's education performance
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The strongest must be a teacher. This is not a moral call, but a post hard indicator. What you insight, you must teach; cannot teach means you have not truly understood. Heng Yuan Zhou does not acknowledge "untarnsmissible sudden enlightenment" — true Great Dao must be writable, teachable, replicable, and iterable. This is the true meaning of "Myriad Ranks have a road."
