# Entry 782 | Transcended Ones and Myriad Races | The Transcended One's Myriad-Rank Dispute Mediation

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 48, 49, 777

## Introduction: on what grounds does the strong adjudicate for the weak?

Myriad Ranks inevitably have disputes — resource allocation, territory disputes, cultural conflicts, causal compensation. The old world's solution is "whoever has the biggest fists decides." Heng Yuan Zhou's solution is procedural justice, but procedural justice needs someone to execute it; the transcended one is the key post in this execution segment. This entry must answer: **on what grounds does the transcended one adjudicate Myriad Ranks' disputes? By what procedure? How to prevent him himself from becoming a new overbearing adjudicator?**

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom requires mediation itself contain no predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires mediation be equal to all Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires mediation serve uplift not suppression; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the mediation process not disturb causality; the law-first axiom requires the mediation procedure be self-consistent at the law layer, not bypassable by any transcended individual.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Mediation's Three Roles

The transcended one is not the sole adjudicator in Myriad-Rank disputes; he has three roles, switched by dispute nature:

1. **Mediator**: for resource and territory disputes between Myriad-Rank races, the transcended one participates as a neutral third party. He does not adjudicate, only chairs dialogue, letting both sides negotiate a solution themselves;
2. **Witness**: when the dispute involves cross-plane causal disturbance, the transcended one, thanks to Alaya Network's full-record capability, may serve as a "fact witness" providing causal evidence. He does not judge right/wrong, only provides facts;
3. **Executor**: when the Symbiotic Court has already issued an adjudication, but the losing side refuses to execute, the transcended one may, as a law-anchor, execute the adjudication — but he may only execute the court's adjudication, not fabricate adjudications himself.

The three roles' core difference: **the transcended one does not fabricate rulings; he only helps rulings land.** The power to fabricate rulings rests with the Symbiotic Court, not the transcended one.

## II. Why the Transcended One Is Not Allowed to Directly Adjudicate

The old world's thinking is "the strong adjudicates fastest" — the transcended one is strongest, his one sentence decides right/wrong. Why does Heng Yuan Zhou refuse?

- **Direct adjudication destroys procedural justice**: the strong one's one sentence, the weak have no chance to defend. This is not adjudicating; this is crushing;
- **Direct adjudication creates attachment**: Myriad Ranks get used to "complain to the transcended one," rather than going through institutional procedure. Over time, institutions idle, everything depends on the transcended one's personal energy;
- **Direct adjudication is non-replicable**: the transcended one today in a good mood adjudicates one case, tomorrow in a bad mood adjudicates another; standards inconsistent. Procedural justice's core is consistent standards, not the strong one's wisdom.

## III. Mediation's Procedure

The transcended one participating in Myriad-Rank dispute mediation must go through this procedure:

1. **Acceptance**: either party to the dispute may file a mediation application to the Symbiotic Court; the transcended one does not proactively take cases;
2. **Recusal**: if a dispute party has a direct interest relationship with the transcended one (same clan, same lineage), the transcended one must recuse;
3. **Fact-finding**: the transcended one pulls Alaya Network records, reconstructs causal facts, and does not make value judgments;
4. **Mediation dialogue**: the transcended one chairs both sides' dialogue, helping both sides find a consensus solution;
5. **Solution submission**: the consensus solution is submitted to the Symbiotic Court for review; after the Court rules, it takes effect;
6. **Execution supervision**: if one side does not execute, the transcended one executes per the Court's ruling, and records the execution process.

Six-step procedure ensures the transcended one is always in an "assisting" position, not a "leading" position.

## IV. Mediation's Ethical Red Lines

The transcended one participating in mediation has three ethical red lines he must not cross:

- **No favoritism**: must not lean toward a clan because it is close to him. Alaya Network records the leaning distribution of every mediation; favoritism is auditable by data;
- **No surrogate decision**: must not bypass both sides and directly give a "you should do this" solution. The solution must be negotiated out by both sides themselves; the transcended one only helps;
- **No threat**: must not use "I am stronger than you" as a mediation chip. Once power threat is used, it immediately triggers an Anchor Supervisory Committee interview.

Three red lines rest on one principle: **the transcended one's mediation power comes from his neutrality and procedure, not his fists.**

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the old world uses fists to adjudicate, talks after fighting, energy all wasted on fighting; Heng Yuan Zhou uses procedural mediation, saving energy for construction. The Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcended one's mediation computable power all comes from the civilizational-weight formula's legitimate allocation
- **Anti-predation**: the strong one adjudicating itself is predation — because he only listens to what he wants to hear, only adjudicates what he wants to adjudicate; procedural mediation seals this road
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's one sentence decides Myriad Ranks' fate; Heng Yuan Zhou requires the transcended one only assist the Court, not fabricate rulings himself
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the mediation procedure is equally open to all Myriad-Rank races, with no "strong-clan priority." The mediation process is recorded throughout by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: all mediation records, factual evidence, and adjudication results are opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao; Myriad Ranks may inspect
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The transcended one mediates Myriad-Rank disputes not because he is strongest, but because he is most neutral, most evidence-bearing, most able to land. But "most able to land" does not equal "most able to adjudicate" — adjudication power rests with the Symbiotic Court, not the transcended one. The transcended one is the hand that executes, not the brain that judges. No matter how strong the hand, it must obey the brain.
