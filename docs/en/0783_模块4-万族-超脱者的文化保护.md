# Entry 783 | Transcended Ones and Myriad Races | The Transcended One's Myriad-Rank Cultural Protection

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 52, 777

## Introduction: why strong-one culture must not devour weak-one culture

Myriad Ranks coexist; the biggest long-term risk is not war, but cultural assimilation. The strong's culture (humankind cultivation, technological expansion, single aesthetics) will flood the weak's culture (plant spirits' rhythm, planet consciousnesses' silence, consciousness-information clans' logical beauty) like a flood. The old world never protects weak-one culture — wherever the strong walk, culture follows; the weak either learn the strong or disappear. Heng Yuan Zhou flips this: **one of the transcended one's core duties is protecting Myriad-Rank cultural diversity.** This entry breaks this duty into operable protection engineering.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom requires cultural protection target "cultural predation"; the republican axiom of Myriad Races is cultural protection's jurisprudential source; the collective ascension axiom requires cultural diversity serve uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires cultural protection not forcibly remake weak-one culture; the law-first axiom requires the cultural-protection mechanism be self-consistent and sustainable at the law layer.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Cultural Protection's Three-Layer Structure

The transcended one's Myriad-Rank cultural protection is a three-layer structure:

1. **Record layer**: completely record every Myriad-Rank clan's culture (language, art, cultivation method, social organization, religious philosophy), storing it in Alaya Network's cultural archive. Recording is not for "display," but for "not losing";
2. **Nursery layer**: for endangered-culture clans (small population, fragile transmission chain), the transcended one must provide resources, space, and education protection, ensuring their culture is not swallowed by strong-one culture;
3. **Exchange layer**: organize Myriad-Rank cultural exchange — not "let the weak learn the strong," but "let the strong also learn the weak." Humankind must learn long-time-horizon rhythm perception from plant spirits, learn holistic view from planet consciousnesses.

Three layers stack: record layer solves "not lose"; nursery layer solves "not die out"; exchange layer solves "not isolated."

## II. Why Strong-One Culture Inevitably Devours Weak-One Culture

Without the transcended one's active protection, cultural assimilation is inevitable. Three reasons:

- **Population scale gap**: strong clans have large populations and reproduce fast; weak clans have small populations. Over time, culture is naturally swallowed by the majority;
- **Technological generation gap**: the strong have cultivation systems and cross-plane channels; the weak do not. To survive, the weak actively abandon their own culture to learn the strong's;
- **Aesthetic single-ization**: the strong's aesthetics (combat, conquest, efficiency) diffuse through Alaya Network; the weak slowly feel their own culture is "backward" and "useless."

Three reasons stack; cultural assimilation is the entropy-increasing direction. Without external intervention, diversity inevitably moves to uniformity. The transcended one's cultural protection is anti-entropy-increasing engineering.

## III. Cultural Protection's Concrete Engineering

Cultural protection is not shouting slogans, but a series of concrete projects:

| Project | Content | Responsible Post |
|---------|---------|------------------|
| Cultural Archive | Full record of all Myriad-Rank clans' culture | Transcended one + consciousness-information clan representative |
| Nursery Special Zone | Independent development space for endangered-culture clans | Transcended one + Anchor Council |
| Cultural Translation Network | Mutual translation of Myriad-Rank cultures, not only weak→strong | Transcended one + language-lineage representatives |
| Cultural Festivals | Every Myriad-Rank clan has its own annual cultural celebration | Transcended one + Symbiotic Executive Committee |
| Cultural Impact Assessment | Any cross-cultural engineering must pass cultural impact assessment | Transcended one + Symbiotic Court |

Five projects together turn "protecting cultural diversity" from sentiment into executable, auditable public engineering.

## IV. Cultural Protection and the "Justified Rascal"

Cultural protection is the field most easily exploited by "justified rascals." Under the banner of "protecting weak-one culture," they actually isolate the weak, blocking the weak's autonomous evolution. Heng Yuan Zhou must distinguish two kinds of "protection":

- **True protection**: give the weak resources, space, education, letting them themselves choose what to keep and what to change;
- **False protection**: seal the weak in a "cultural museum," not letting them contact new technologies and new ideas, beautified as "authentic."

The transcended one's cultural protection must be true protection. The judgment standard is simple: **does the protected clan itself have choice?** They may keep if they wish, change if they wish, disappear if they wish — the transcended one only provides options, does not force results.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: cultural diversity is civilization's "gene pool"; losing a clan loses a set of evolutionary solutions; cultural protection is the biggest gene-pool investment
- **Anti-predation**: cultural predation is more covert than resource predation — the strong does not grab your things, only makes your culture disappear; cultural protection directly fights this predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system strong-one culture crushes everything; weak-one culture does not even have qualification to be recorded; Heng Yuan Zhou lists cultural protection as a transcended-one hard indicator
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: cultural protection explicitly covers all five-lineages' clans, including the weakest plant spirits and planet consciousnesses
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the cultural archive is opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao; all Myriad-Rank beings may inspect, learn, and appreciate. The Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcended one's cultural-protection performance is directly counted into civilizational weight
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Myriad-Rank cultural diversity is Heng Yuan Zhou civilization's gene pool. Losing a clan's culture is not losing a song or a language, but losing a unique evolutionary solution. The transcended one's cultural-protection duty is to guard this gene pool — not seal it in a museum, but let every set of culture have space to grow freely. The strong may be strong, but the strong have no right to leave the world with only one look of the strong.
