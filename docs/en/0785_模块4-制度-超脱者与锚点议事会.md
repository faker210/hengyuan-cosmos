# Entry 785 | Transcended Ones and Institutions | The Transcended One's Relationship with the Anchor Council

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 40, 49, 784

## Introduction: the transcended one in the legislative body has only one vote

The Anchor Council is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's legislative body — all Myriad-Rank laws, rules, and conventions are discussed, voted, and promulgated by it. The old world's transcended ones are naturally "legislators" — their word is law. Heng Yuan Zhou must be clear: **the transcended one in the Anchor Council is not the speaker, not the chairperson, only an ordinary representative with one vote.** This entry pins this relationship down.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom requires legislation contain no predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires legislative power belong to Myriad-Rank representatives; the collective ascension axiom requires legislation serve uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires legislation not create causal loopholes; the law-first axiom requires legislative procedure be self-consistent and not manipulable by any transcended individual.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Transcended One's Three Rights in the Council

The transcended one, as a Myriad-Rank representative (qualified per entry 778's representation-qualification procedure), has three rights in the Anchor Council:

1. **Proposal right**: the transcended one may submit bill proposals to the Council. A proposal needs no co-sponsor, but also does not auto-enter the agenda — any representative's proposal goes through the same procedure;
2. **Vote right**: the transcended one casts one vote in voting, equal in weight to other representatives' one vote. No "transcended-one vote counts double";
3. **Floor right**: the transcended one may speak in the Council, but speaking duration is the same as other representatives; "because strong, talk half an hour more" is not allowed.

Three rights together, the transcended one in the Council is "a representative with slightly more seniority." His seniority may make his proposals more likely to be taken seriously, but cannot make his vote heavier.

## II. The Transcended One's Three Limits in the Council

Beyond rights, there are three limits:

- **No veto right**: the transcended one may not veto bills the Council passes. Once a bill passes, the transcended one also must obey it;
- **No dissolution right**: the transcended one may not dissolve the Council because it "does not listen." The Council's existence is guaranteed by the Symbiotic Constitution;
- **No temporary-legislation right**: the transcended one may not bypass the Council to "temporarily legislate." Any rule change must go through normal legislative procedure.

Three limits ensure the Council is always Myriad Ranks' legislative body, not the transcended one's rubber stamp.

## III. Special Procedure for Transcended-One Proposals

Because the transcended one has large computable power and broad vision, his proposals indeed carry more technical weight than ordinary representatives'. Heng Yuan Zhou therefore establishes a "special procedure for transcended-one proposals," but this procedure is **stricter**, not looser:

1. The transcended one's proposal must attach complete technical argumentation (law impact, causal disturbance, Myriad-Rank coverage assessment);
2. The proposal must go through at least one complete deliberation cycle; no "emergency passage";
3. The proposal must accept cross-examination by Myriad-Rank representatives; the transcended one must answer on the spot;
4. When the proposal is voted, the transcended one must recuse from the vote he leads (conflict-of-interest recusal).

The special procedure's logic is: **transcended-one proposals have large impact, so must be more cautiously reviewed.** Not "the transcended one's word counts," but "the transcended one's words must be even more seriously reviewed."

## IV. The Council's Reverse Power Over the Transcended One

The relationship is two-way. The transcended one is limited in the Council, and the Council also has reverse power over the transcended one:

- **Legislative power constrains the transcended one**: laws the Council passes, the transcended one must obey. If he breaks law, the Council may launch impeachment;
- **Budget power constrains the transcended one**: the Council approves the public computable power and resource quota the transcended one's post needs. The transcended one "wants to work" must have the Council approve the budget;
- **Supervisory power constrains the transcended one**: the Council may regularly summon the transcended one, requiring him to report post performance.

Reverse power ensures the transcended one and the Council mutually constrain each other, not the transcended one unilaterally directing the Council.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the old world letting transcended ones casually legislate equals wasting legislative power on personal will; Heng Yuan Zhou uses procedure to give legislative power to Myriad-Rank representatives
- **Anti-predation**: legislative power rests in the Council, not the transcended one, sealing the path of "the strong legislating to prey on the weak"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's word is law; Heng Yuan Zhou requires Tang Luo have only one vote in the Council
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the Council represents Myriad Ranks' five lineages; the transcended one may not represent any single clan monopolizing legislation
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Council deliberation records and full bill text are opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao. The transcended one's every vote and every proposal in the Council is recorded by Alaya Network, counted into his civilizational weight
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The transcended one in the Anchor Council is not speaker, not chairperson, only an ordinary representative with one vote. He may propose, may speak, may vote, but may not veto, may not dissolve, may not temporarily legislate. Legislative power belongs to Myriad-Rank representatives, not any one person — even if that person is strong enough for the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm.
