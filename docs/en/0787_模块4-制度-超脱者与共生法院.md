# Entry 787 | Transcended Ones and Institutions | The Transcended One's Relationship with the Symbiotic Court

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 48, 40, 784

## Introduction: even the strongest must stand in the dock

The Symbiotic Court is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's judicial body — hearing all cases violating Myriad-Rank law. The old world's transcended ones never enter court, because he is strongest and courts cannot reach him. Heng Yuan Zhou must squarely answer: **one person strong enough for the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm — can he stand in the dock?** The answer: yes, and must be able to. This entry pins the transcended-one/Symbiotic-Court relationship down.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom requires courts directly judge predatory behavior; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires courts be equal to all Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires judicial fairness serve uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the judicial process not disturb causality; the law-first axiom requires judicial procedure be self-consistent and not bypassable by any transcended individual.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Transcended One's Dual Identity in Court

The transcended one in the Symbiotic Court has dual identity:

1. **Citizen identity**: the transcended one, as a Myriad-Rank citizen, is subject to Myriad-Rank law. If he breaks law, the Court hears him;
2. **Witness identity**: the transcended one, thanks to Alaya Network's full-record capability, may serve as a fact witness in others' cases providing causal evidence.

Two identities must not be confused: the transcended one may not avoid prosecution because "I'm a witness," nor refuse to testify because "I'm a transcended one."

## II. Special Procedure for Transcended-One Cases

Transcended-one cases, because the defendant's destructive power is enormous, have special procedure, but this procedure is **stricter**, not looser:

- **Panel escalation**: transcended-one cases must be heard by the highest collegial panel, not a基层 court sole judge;
- **Full public**: the entire trial process of transcended-one cases is live-streamed through Alaya Network; all Myriad-Rank beings may attend;
- **Superior review**: after first-instance judgment, it must be reviewed by a superior court before taking effect;
- **Execution isolation**: after judgment takes effect, the execution phase takes isolation measures, preventing the transcended one from disturbing the plane during execution.

The special procedure's logic is: **the transcended one has large destructive power, so trying him must be more cautious, more public, more secure.** Not "transcended-one extra-legal mercy," but "transcended-one in-law strict trial."

## III. The Transcended One May Not Interfere with the Court

The hardest rule: **the transcended one may not interfere with the Court.**

- May not ask for a lighter sentence because "I am strong";
- May not ask for the case to be dropped because "I did it for your own good";
- May not ask for a continuance because "I can affect causality."

Any act of court interference is itself a new crime — "interfering with judicial independence," heard by a higher court. This rule ensures the Court is always Myriad Ranks' judicial body, not the transcended one's private court.

## IV. The Court's Five-Tier Sentencing for the Transcended One

The Court's sentencing for the transcended one, by severity, has five tiers:

1. **Warning**: minor violation; written warning, recorded;
2. **Compensation**: caused causal damage; compensates the victim party with origin increment;
3. **Downgrade**: law-anchoring permissions downgraded; part of public computable power reclaimed;
4. **Suspension**: transcended-one post suspended; suspended-duty investigation;
5. **Expulsion**: transcended-one qualification canceled; treated as an ordinary citizen.

Five-tier sentencing ensures the judiciary has teeth, yet has gradation. The transcended one is not "tried and killed in one instance," but "tier by tier."

After judgment there is still the execution problem. Ordinary cultivators not obeying judgment, the Court may force; but if the transcended one refuses compensation and does not accept rotation downgrade, the Symbiotic Court cannot rely on "having a fight" to execute — that equals returning to predation logic. Heng Yuan Zhou's solution is "weight execution": once a Court judgment takes effect, Alaya Network auto-adjusts the transcended one's civilizational weight, freezes his cross-plane deployment permissions, downgrades his law-anchoring operation level, until he fulfills the judgment. In other words, no one needs to force him; the system will make him "unable to continue." If he tries to cut off his connection to Alaya Network to evade execution, that itself is a first-degree felony; the Anchor Supervisory Committee will launch a joint investigation. This "rely on the system, not force" execution method is where the Court truly has teeth against the strongest.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the old world letting the strongest evade trial equals letting predatory behavior go unpunished; Heng Yuan Zhou uses the Court to put predators in the dock
- **Anti-predation**: the Court directly judges predatory behavior; predatory transcended ones have no place to hide before the law
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is law in his plane; Heng Yuan Zhou requires Tang Luo stand in the dock for trial
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the Court has equal jurisdiction over all Myriad-Rank beings; the transcended one may not be an exception
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: transcended-one cases are fully public throughout; full judgment text is opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao. Judgment results directly affect his civilizational weight
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Even the strongest must stand in the dock. This is not distrust of the transcended one, but respect for judicial independence. A court that may try even transcended ones is Myriad Ranks' true court. If a court may only try ordinary people, not the strongest, then it is not a court, but a shrine for the weak. The Symbiotic Court's judgments are not affected by any transcended one's strength, only by evidence and law — this is Heng Yuan Zhou's hardest-ever execution of "equality before the law."
