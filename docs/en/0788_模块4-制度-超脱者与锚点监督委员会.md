# Entry 788 | Transcended Ones and Institutions | The Transcended One's Relationship with the Anchor Supervisory Committee

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 49, 27, 784

## Introduction: who watches the transcended one?

The Anchor Supervisory Committee is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's supervisory body — specifically watching all public-post holders, preventing power abuse. Among the four institutional bodies, it has the most direct relationship with the transcended one: **the transcended one is the Supervisory Committee's primary supervision object.** The old world has no such body, because the old world believed "the strongest needs no supervision." Heng Yuan Zhou must squarely answer: **why does the strongest need supervision most?** This entry pins this relationship down.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom requires supervision directly target predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires supervision right belong to Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires supervision serve uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires supervision not create causal loopholes; the law-first axiom requires the supervision mechanism be self-consistent and not bypassable by any transcended individual.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Supervisory Committee's Four Grips on the Transcended One

The Anchor Supervisory Committee's supervision of the transcended one has four concrete grips:

1. **Daily audit**: weekly automatic audit of the transcended one's Alaya Network operation logs, checking for anomalous cross-plane operations;
2. **Quarterly weight audit**: quarterly audit of the transcended one's civilizational-weight five-dimension composition; any subitem declining triggers an early warning;
3. **Annual public hearing**: once a year public hearing; the transcended one must in person report post performance to Myriad-Rank representatives;
4. **Random fly-by inspection**: unscheduled, unannounced fly-by inspection, checking the transcended one's actual work state.

Four grips together; the transcended one is watched every day — but this watching is not "suspecting he's a bad person," but "ensuring his power always stays within Myriad-Rank authorization."

## II. Why the Strongest Needs Supervision Most

The old world believes "the strong need no supervision"; Heng Yuan Zhou's logic is the exact opposite:

- **The greater the destructive power, the greater the risk**: one of the transcended one's operation errors may disturb an entire star sector. An ordinary person's error ruins at most a room; the transcended one's error ruins a galaxy. Risk is proportional to destructive power;
- **The greater the power, the greater the corruption temptation**: public computable power, cross-plane channels, and law permissions the transcended one may mobilize are trillions of times those of ordinary people. Temptation is also trillions of times;
- **The more people worship, the easier to go astray**: the transcended one, gazed up at by Myriad Ranks for long, easily develops the "I am right" self-hallucination. Supervision is the external brake installed on this hallucination.

Three reasons together: **it is not that the transcended one's character is flawed, but that the transcended one's post risk is too large.** Risk large, supervision must be strict.

## III. The Transcended One's Cooperation Obligation to the Supervisory Committee

The transcended one has an obligation to cooperate with the Supervisory Committee, may not refuse:

- **May not refuse audit**: Alaya Network logs are fully auto-recorded; the transcended one has no right to delete, hide, or encrypt;
- **May not refuse hearing**: the transcended one must attend the annual public hearing, may not delegate another to report on his behalf;
- **May not refuse fly-by inspection**: during fly-by inspection the transcended one must open his work space, operation records, and computable-power invocation list;
- **May not retaliate against supervisors**: any retaliatory act against Supervisory Committee members triggers Symbiotic Court criminal prosecution.

The cooperation obligation ensures supervision is not "going through the motions," but real supervision.

## IV. The Supervisory Committee's Reverse Relationship with the Transcended One

The relationship is two-way. The transcended one cooperates with the Supervisory Committee, and the Supervisory Committee must also protect the transcended one:

- **No indiscriminate harassment**: the Supervisory Committee may not frequently harass because "the transcended one is strong"; audit rhythm is stipulated by institutions;
- **No leaking transcended-one privacy**: audit records are only for public accountability, not for private snooping;
- **Timely feedback**: if audit finds problems, the transcended one must be promptly informed, given rectification opportunity; no "reckoning after autumn."

Reverse protection ensures supervision is "accountability tool," not "targeted-persecution tool."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the Supervisory Committee prevents transcended ones from using public computable power for private purposes; it is the "auditor" of computable-power use
- **Anti-predation**: daily audit directly watches cross-plane operations; predatory transcended ones have no place to hide under audit
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system no one supervises Gu Chensha; Heng Yuan Zhou uses the Supervisory Committee to completely abolish the old precedent of "the strongest is unsupervised"
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the Supervisory Committee is composed of Myriad-Rank five-lineage representatives, not the transcended one's peers
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: supervision's entire procedure, standards, and audit results are opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao. The Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcended one is the Supervisory Committee's primary audit object
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The strongest needs supervision most. Not because his character is bad, but because his destructive power is large, temptation is large, and he easily self-deifies. The Anchor Supervisory Committee is Heng Yuan Zhou's external brake for this risk — the brake is not distrust of the driver, but because this car runs too fast.
