# Entry 789 | Transcended Ones and Institutions | The Transcended One's Legal Constraints and Immunity Boundaries

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 40, 48, 784

## Introduction: does the transcended one have "extra-legal territory"?

Previous entries repeatedly say "the transcended one and Myriad Ranks are subject to the same law." But a real-world question cannot be avoided: **does the transcended one, in emergencies, have brief legal immunity?** For example, when a plane-level disaster is imminent and the transcended one has no time to go through reporting procedure, may he strike first? This entry answers this "immunity boundary" question — not denying "equality before the law," but drawing a narrow door for emergencies.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom requires immunity contain no predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires immunity be fair to all Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires immunity serve uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires immunity be strictly limited to emergency scope; the law-first axiom requires immunity boundaries be clear at the law layer, not expandable.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Immunity's Three Statutory Conditions

Heng Yuan Zhou does not practice "transcended-one extra-legal mercy," but indeed grants an extremely narrow statutory immunity — "emergency-avoidance immunity." This immunity takes effect only when three conditions are simultaneously met:

1. **Urgency**: a plane-level disaster (star-sector-level causal fracture, cross-plane pollution outbreak, Myriad-Rank-level life threat) is occurring; without immunity there is no time;
2. **Necessity**: the action the transcended one takes is the only means at the time to stop the disaster; there is no alternative;
3. **Immediacy**: immunity is only valid while the disaster is occurring; once the disaster passes, immunity immediately terminates.

Three conditions, no substitute. Any one unmet, the transcended one has no immunity and must go through normal procedure.

## II. Immunity's Three Prohibited Expansions

Immunity has strict prohibited-expansion rules:

- **No temporal expansion**: immunity only covers while the disaster is in progress; it may not extend to "aftermath" after the disaster ends. Aftermath must go through normal procedure;
- **No scope expansion**: immunity only covers the minimal action necessary to stop the disaster; no taking the immunity to "conveniently" do other things;
- **No identity expansion**: immunity belongs only to the transcended one personally taking emergency action; it may not extend to his co-disciples, same clan, or subordinates.

Three prohibitions ensure immunity is a narrow door, not a wide-open back door.

## III. Mandatory Procedure After Immunity

Immunity is not "free pass." After the emergency-avoidance immunity, the transcended one must go through the following mandatory procedure:

1. **Immediate report**: within one cycle after the disaster ends, the transcended one must submit a complete action report to the Anchor Supervisory Committee;
2. **After-the-fact audit**: the Supervisory Committee audits all operations during the immunity period, confirming whether the three conditions were truly met;
3. **Causal repair**: causal disturbance caused by the immunity action, the transcended one must repair in subsequent post work;
4. **Public notification**: the audit result is publicly notified to Myriad Ranks, accepting Myriad-Rank representatives' cross-examination.

Four-step procedure ensures immunity is not an "evasion channel," but "act first, report later."

## IV. Behaviors Absolutely Not Immune

Some behaviors, even in emergencies, the transcended one absolutely may not be immune for:

- **Predatory behavior**: any form of predation, devouring, seizure is never within immunity scope;
- **Depriving Myriad-Rank growth right**: making decisions for Myriad Ranks, bearing causality for Myriad Ranks, is never within immunity scope;
- **Rewriting history**: directly rewriting cross-plane timelines is never within immunity scope;
- **Exterminating clans**: any form of genocide is never within immunity scope.

Four red lines are Heng Yuan Zhou's "absolutely non-immune list." Even if the sky falls, the transcended one may not step on these four lines. Stepping on them, even to "save the world," is still treated as crime.

Immunity is not a "merit-free token," but "temporary right-of-way in emergencies." Every use of immunity must accept double audit after the fact: Alaya Network's behavioral-data audit, and the Anchor Supervisory Committee's ethical audit. Audit conclusions are three-tiered — compliant, blemished, overreach. Compliant leaves no stain; blemished requires supplementary explanation and is recorded in file; overreach not only voids immunity, but adds accountability as ordinary violation. The key principle: immunity only covers "the portion of behavior that had to be done to stop a greater evil"; anything beyond this scope is never immune. For example, destroying an empty building to stop a predatory faction's nuke may be immune, but conveniently grabbing resources inside the building is not. The boundary is drawn extremely finely, leaving no "state of emergency" imaginative room for anyone.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: immunity boundaries prevent transcended ones from abusing power under the pretext of emergencies, steering computable power onto the right path
- **Anti-predation**: predatory behavior is absolutely non-immune, sealing the "emergency predation" excuse from the legal layer
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system the transcended one has no legal constraint at all; Heng Yuan Zhou grants only an extremely narrow emergency-avoidance immunity door
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: immunity applies to all Myriad-Rank beings; the transcended one enjoys no special immunity right
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: all immunity rules, cases, and audit results are opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao. When the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcended one exercises emergency-avoidance immunity, it is recorded throughout by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision, counted into civilizational weight after the fact
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Does the transcended one have extra-legal territory? There is one extremely narrow door — emergency-avoidance immunity. But this door only opens when the sky falls, closes the moment the sky stops, and behind the door there is mandatory audit, causal repair, and public notification. Beyond that, the transcended one and Myriad Ranks are subject to the same law, with no privileges. The narrow door is for saving people, not for letting people go.
