# Entry 790 | Transcended Ones and Institutions | The Transcended One's Checks-and-Balances Mechanism

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 49, 40, 784

## Introduction: four bodies, how do they watch each other?

Entries 785–789, five entries, separately covered the transcended one's relationship with the Anchor Council, Symbiotic Executive Committee, Symbiotic Court, Anchor Supervisory Committee, and legal immunity boundaries. This entry, as this group's closing, places the four bodies on one chart, seeing how they check each other, ensuring the transcended one does not turn any body into a private tool.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom requires checks-and-balances directly target power concentration; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires checks-and-balances serve Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires checks-and-balances not become internal attrition; the causal self-discipline axiom requires checks-and-balances not create causal loopholes; the law-first axiom requires the checks-and-balances structure be self-consistent at the law layer, not bypassable by any transcended individual.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Four Bodies' Power Distribution

First look at what power each of the four bodies holds:

| Body | Core Power | Constraint on the Transcended One |
|------|------------|-----------------------------------|
| Anchor Council | Legislative power, budget power, impeachment power | Legislation constrains transcended one; approves transcended-one budget; impeaches transcended one |
| Symbiotic Executive Committee | Administrative power, post-assignment power, assessment power | Assigns work to transcended one; assesses transcended one; hires/dismisses transcended one |
| Symbiotic Court | Judicial power, adjudication power, judgment-enforcement power | Tries transcended one; sentences transcended one; enforces judgment against transcended one |
| Anchor Supervisory Committee | Supervision power, audit power, hearing power | Audits transcended one; hears transcended one; reports transcended one |

Four power modules, held by four independent bodies respectively. The transcended one wanting to turn any one body into a private tool will be watched by the other three.

## II. Checks-and-Balances' Four Loops

Checks-and-balances among the four bodies run on four loops:

1. **Council legislates → Committee executes → Court adjudicates → Supervisory Committee audits**: transcended one wants to change law, Council does not approve; wants to bypass execution, Court sues him; wants to interfere with judiciary, Supervisory Committee audits him;
2. **Committee assesses → Council impeaches → Court sentences → Supervisory Committee enforces**: transcended-one post performance poor, Committee deducts points; continuously poor, Council impeaches; impeachment passes, Court sentences; judgment enforced, Supervisory Committee watches;
3. **Court tries → Supervisory Committee audits → Council legislates → Committee executes**: Court sentences transcended one, Supervisory Committee audits the trial process; trial unfair, Council legislates law revision; after revision, Committee executes;
4. **Supervisory Committee discovers → Council investigates → Court adjudicates → Committee rectifies**: Supervisory Committee finds problems, Council launches investigation; investigation confirms, Court adjudicates; after adjudication, Committee rectifies.

Four loops together form a "you-in-me, me-in-you" checks-and-balances network. The transcended one wanting to find a loophole at any link will be plugged by the other three links.

## III. The Transcended One's Position in the Checks-and-Balances Network

In this checks-and-balances network, the transcended one is not the center, but a node. His position is:

- **In the Council**: an ordinary representative with one vote (entry 785);
- **In the Executive Committee**: a senior technical employee (entry 786);
- **Before the Court**: a citizen who may be tried (entry 787);
- **Before the Supervisory Committee**: an audited object (entry 788).

Four identities together; in the checks-and-balances network the transcended one is a "four-subjected" figure: subject to legislation, subject to administrative assessment, subject to judicial trial, subject to supervisory audit. He is not the checks-and-balances network's center; he is the checks-and-balances network's subjected object.

## IV. The Checks-and-Balances Mechanism's Three Design Principles

Heng Yuan Zhou's checks-and-balances follows three design principles:

1. **Distrust principle**: by default anyone may abuse power, including the transcended one. Institutional design's starting point is "assume the transcended one will go astray," then use institutions to catch him;
2. **Redundancy principle**: every power module has a backup — the Council has Myriad-Rank-representative backup, the Executive Committee has post backup, the Court has collegial-panel backup, the Supervisory Committee has lineage backup. Any one point failing, the network runs as usual;
3. **Transparency principle**: the four bodies' entire operation process is open to Myriad Ranks through Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision. Transparency itself is the greatest check — Myriad Ranks can see, so power dares not abuse.

Three principles together; checks-and-balances does not rely on "finding a good person to manage bad people," but on "institutions not assuming good people."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the checks-and-balances mechanism prevents public computable power from being abused by private power, ensuring computable power serves Myriad Ranks
- **Anti-predation**: four loops directly seal the transcended one's path to prey on Myriad Ranks through any body
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system four bodies are unified (Gu Chensha alone legislates, administers, adjudicates, and supervises); Heng Yuan Zhou separates four powers
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: all four bodies are composed of Myriad-Rank representatives; the transcended one may not monopolize any one
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: checks-and-balances' entire rules and operation records are opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao. The Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcended one's every step in the checks-and-balances network is recorded; civilizational weight real-time reflects his checked state
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Four bodies watch each other; the transcended one is watched by four bodies simultaneously. This is Heng Yuan Zhou's ultimate answer to "absolute power corrupts absolutely": **absolute power does not exist, because power from the start is separated, watching each other, and transparent to Myriad Ranks.** No matter how strong the transcended one, he is only a node on this checks-and-balances network — a node may shine, but may not leave the network.
