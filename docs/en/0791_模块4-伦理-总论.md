# Entry 791 | Transcended Ones and Ethics · Overview — The Transcended One's Ethical System

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 19, 88, 701

## Introduction: why is the strongest one's ethics stricter than ordinary people's?

The old world's strong ones have no ethical constraints — he is strongest, what he does is right. Heng Yuan Zhou must squarely answer: **one person strong enough for the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm — should his ethical standards be higher or lower than ordinary people's?** The answer: higher. Not because his character is better, but because his destructive power is larger. This entry, as this group's overview, must lay out the transcended-one ethical system's overall framework.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom is the ethical system's bottom line; the republican axiom of Myriad Races is the ethical system's source; the collective ascension axiom is the ethical system's direction; the causal self-discipline axiom is the ethical system's operational norm; the law-first axiom requires the ethical system itself be self-consistent, executable, and not rewritable by any transcended individual.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why the Strong's Ethics Must Be Stricter

The old world believes "the strong are free"; Heng Yuan Zhou's logic is the exact opposite:

- **The greater the destructive power, the greater the mistake cost**: an ordinary person's mistake ruins a room; the transcended one's mistake ruins a star sector. Cost is proportional to destructive power, and ethical standards must be proportional too;
- **The more gazed up at, the easier to self-deify**: the transcended one, gazed up at by Myriad Ranks for long, easily feels "whatever I do is right." The ethical system is the external brake on this self-hallucination;
- **The greater the ability, the greater the temptation**: the transcended one can do so much — rewrite history, adjust causality, rearrange rules. The greater the temptation, the more ethical red lines are needed.

Three reasons together: **it is not that the transcended one's character is bad, but that the transcended one's post risk is too large.** Risk large, ethical standards must be high.

## II. The Transcended-One Ethical System's Four Pillars

The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's transcended-one ethical system has four pillars:

1. **Non-interference principle** (entry 792): the transcended one by default does not interfere with Myriad Ranks' natural development;
2. **Limited-interference boundary** (entry 793): when Myriad Ranks actively request or disaster is urgent, limited interference is allowed, but with strict boundaries;
3. **Causal self-discipline practice** (entry 794): all cross-plane, cross-time operations are strictly reported; observe-first-then-interfere; only backflow knowledge, do not disturb causality;
4. **Attachment-and-rascal prevention** (entries 795, 796): prevent the transcended one himself from being eroded by "all-beings' origin attachment · shortcut inner demon," and prevent him from becoming a "justified rascal."

Four pillars together form a complete ethical system of "non-interference primary, limited-interference auxiliary, causal self-discipline throughout, inner defense as backstop."

## III. The Ethical System and Civilizational Weight's Relationship

The ethical system is not an abstract moral requirement; it hooks directly into entry 706's civilizational-weight formula:

- Violating non-interference principle → procedure-compliance subitem deducted;
- Overstepping limited-interference boundary → direction-correctness subitem deducted;
- Missing causal report → procedure-compliance subitem directly zeroed;
- Attachment or rascal behavior → origin-legality subitem deducted; in severe cases directly negative.

Ethical deductions directly cause civilizational-weight decline; weight decline causes law-anchoring permissions to downgrade. This is what "ethics is not a slogan, but a ledger" means.

## IV. Five Sub-Articles' Positioning Preview

Entries 792–796, five sub-articles, will handle the four pillars' concrete operations one by one:

- **Non-interference principle** (792): the jurisprudential basis and operational boundary of default non-interference;
- **Limited-interference boundary** (793): when may one interfere? To what extent?
- **Causal self-discipline practice** (794): concrete reporting procedure for cross-plane operations;
- **All-beings' Origin Attachment Prevention** (795): how the transcended one himself is not eroded by the shortcut inner demon;
- **Justified Rascal Cultivation** (796): how to prevent the transcended one from becoming an "in-the-name-of-love" predator.

Why must ethical standards rise with power? The reason is simple: an ordinary person's one greedy thought takes at most one extra bite of food; a transcended one's one greedy thought may move an entire plane's spiritual vein for private use. Ethical constraint intensity must be proportional to destructive power. Therefore Heng Yuan Zhou's ethical system is not "equal to everyone" — ordinary people only need to obey bottom-line law; the transcended one must additionally carry four layers of obligation above the bottom line: active reporting, active recusal, active stepping down, active stepping up. These four layers of obligation are not moral kidnapping, but a post contract: you took the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's authority, and automatically signed accepting these four layers of constraints. Whoever does not want constraints may at any time downgrade and retire, returning to the ranks of ordinary cultivators; no one stops him.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the ethical system prevents the transcended one from using computable power to do things that disturb Myriad Ranks' causality; it is the computable-power "steering wheel"
- **Anti-predation**: the non-interference principle from its root seals "I did it for your own good so I decide for you" this advanced predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system the transcended one has no ethical constraints; Heng Yuan Zhou uses four pillars to raise ethical standards to the highest
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the ethical system applies equally to all Myriad-Rank beings; the transcended one may not be an exception
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the ethical system's entire rules, cases, and audit records are opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao. Ethical execution is recorded throughout by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The strongest one's ethics must be stricter than ordinary people's. Not because his character is bad, but because his destructive power is large, temptation is large, and he easily self-deifies. Four pillars together form an ethical net of "do not strike casually, striking has boundaries, striking must be reported, inner mind has defense." No matter how strong the transcended one, he is in this net.
