# Entry 792 | Transcended Ones and Ethics | The Transcended One's Non-Interference Principle

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 83, 88, 791

## Introduction: the strongest one's least-appropriate act is deciding for the weak

The old world's strong ones' greatest impulse is "let me help you" — help you fight enemies, help you set direction, help you choose fate. This impulse sounds kind, but in Heng Yuan Zhou's ethical system it is the first felony. The non-interference principle this entry discusses is to completely call a stop to this impulse: **the transcended one by default does not interfere with Myriad Ranks' natural development, even if Myriad Ranks' development direction looks "unwise."**

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom treats non-interference as anti-predation's first defense line; the republican axiom of Myriad Races is the non-interference principle's jurisprudential source; the collective ascension axiom requires non-interference ≠ non-service; the causal self-discipline axiom is the non-interference principle's operational norm; the law-first axiom requires the non-interference principle be self-consistent and executable at the law layer.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Non-Interference Principle's Jurisprudential Base

The non-interference principle is not "indifference," but based on three jurisprudential judgments:

1. **Myriad Ranks have autonomous growth right**: every clan, every being has the right to grow at its own pace, by its own path, through its own mistakes. The transcended one walking for him, he never learns to walk himself;
2. **Causality may not be borne by proxy**: Myriad Ranks' causality is Myriad Ranks' growth textbook. The transcended one carrying causality away equals confiscating the textbook;
3. **"I did it for your own good" most easily becomes predation**: historically, every transformation of alien races, every arrangement of the weak, started with "for your own good" and ended with "you were eaten by me."

Three judgments together: non-interference is not not-caring, but respect. Respecting that Myriad Ranks are their own masters.

## II. The Non-Interference Principle's Four Concrete Prohibitions

The non-interference principle lands as four concrete prohibitions:

- **Do not fight Myriad Ranks' wars for them**: wars between Myriad-Rank clans, the transcended one does not proactively intervene. He may mediate (entry 782), but does not fight one side for the other;
- **Do not choose Myriad Ranks' Dao for them**: what cultivation path, what social organization, what cultural direction Myriad Ranks choose, the transcended one does not choose for them. He may provide information, not make decisions;
- **Do not rewrite Myriad Ranks' history for them**: Myriad Ranks' history has detours and suffering; the transcended one does not travel back to "correct" it. Historical suffering is Myriad Ranks' growth resource;
- **Do not decide Myriad Ranks' future for them**: Myriad Ranks' future is explored by Myriad Ranks themselves; the transcended one does not "plan" where they should go.

Four prohibitions together; the transcended one's attitude to Myriad Ranks is "I watch from the side, you walk yourself."

## III. Why "Watching Without Helping" Is the Hardest Ethics

The non-interference principle sounds simple, but is extremely hard to do. Because it requires the transcended one to restrain a kindest impulse: **seeing the weak suffer, reaching out to help.**

Heng Yuan Zhou's position: this kind impulse must be restrained. Reasons:

- **You help this once, next time he waits for you to help again**: Myriad Ranks develop dependence on "waiting for the transcended one to save them," and never grow up;
- **The direction you help may not be the direction he wants**: what you think is good, he may not think is good. Your "help" may be his "disaster";
- **Your "help" disturbs his causality**: you intervene once, his causal thread deviates once. Deviate too many times, his history is no longer his history.

This is why the non-interference principle is the transcended-one ethics' first principle: **it requires the transcended one watch Myriad Ranks suffer, yet not reach out.** This is not cold-bloodedness, but the highest-level respect.

## IV. The Non-Interference Principle and the "Feedback Obligation" Relationship

Entry 779 covered the transcended one's feedback obligation; this entry covers the non-interference principle. The two look contradictory — one side must feedback, one side must non-interfere. Heng Yuan Zhou must clarify the difference:

- **Feedback is providing resources and knowledge**: the transcended one opens teaching material, computable power, and resources to Myriad Ranks; Myriad Ranks themselves decide whether to use and how to use;
- **Non-interference is not using for Myriad Ranks**: the transcended one does not decide for Myriad Ranks "you should use this teaching material on this path."

The dividing line: **what to provide is the transcended one's obligation; how to use is Myriad Ranks' right.** The transcended one puts the toolbox at Myriad Ranks' door, then turns and walks away; whether Myriad Ranks take it, how they take it, where they take it, the transcended one does not manage.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the non-interference principle prevents the transcended one from using computable power to do for Myriad Ranks what they should do themselves; it is respect for Myriad Ranks' computable power
- **Anti-predation**: the non-interference principle from its root seals "for-your-own-good" style advanced predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo arranges everything for Myriad Ranks; Heng Yuan Zhou requires the transcended one return choice to Myriad Ranks
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the non-interference principle applies equally to all Myriad-Rank clans; no "strong clans may be helped, weak clans do not deserve respect"
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the non-interference principle's entire rules and cases are opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao. If the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcended one violates the non-interference principle, his civilizational weight is directly deducted; operations recorded by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The strongest one's least-appropriate act is deciding for the weak. The non-interference principle is not indifference, but the highest-level respect — it believes Myriad Ranks have the ability to walk their own road, even if taking detours or wrong roads. Detours are also roads; wrong roads are also textbooks. What the transcended one may do is put the toolbox at the door, then step back.
