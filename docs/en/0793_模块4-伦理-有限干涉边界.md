# Entry 793 | Transcended Ones and Ethics | The Transcended One's Limited-Interference Boundary

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 83, 86, 792

## Introduction: non-interference does not mean never striking

Entry 792 covered the non-interference principle, but a real-world question cannot be avoided: **if Myriad Ranks are being slaughtered by predators, if cross-plane pollution is devouring a galaxy, if civilization-level disaster has already occurred, does the transcended one still not interfere?** Of course not. The limited-interference boundary this entry discusses answers: under the non-interference principle's broad framework, in what circumstances may the transcended one strike? To what extent? This entry draws this boundary clearly.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom requires interference directly target predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires interference have Myriad-Rank consent; the collective ascension axiom requires interference serve uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires interference be strictly bounded; the law-first axiom requires boundaries be clear at the law layer, not expandable.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Limited Interference's Three Statutory Conditions

The transcended one may strike and interfere only when three statutory conditions are simultaneously met:

1. **Disaster condition**: a plane-level or cross-plane disaster is occurring — predatory-faction slaughter, cross-plane pollution, law-network fracture, civilization-level life threat;
2. **Request condition**: the affected Myriad-Rank clan actively requests the transcended one's intervention, or Alaya Network monitors a Myriad-Rank-wide consensus requesting intervention;
3. **Proportionality condition**: the action the transcended one takes is the minimal action necessary to stop the disaster, not expanded.

Three conditions, no substitute. Any one unmet, the transcended one continues non-interference.

## II. Interference's Four Boundaries

Even when three conditions are met, interference has four hard boundaries:

- **Minimal boundary**: only do what is necessary to stop the disaster; no "conveniently" doing other things. Stop the slaughter, do not by the way transform the rescued clan;
- **Short-term boundary**: interference only lasts until the disaster ends. The disaster stops, the transcended one immediately withdraws, does not stay to "guide";
- **Knowledge boundary**: knowledge gained during interference is backflowed to Myriad Ranks through the Selfless Dao, but the order established during interference is not imposed on Myriad Ranks;
- **Isolation boundary**: interference is conducted as much as possible in isolated space (Document 64) or low-pollution zones, reducing disturbance to the original plane's causality.

Four boundaries together; interference is "hemostasis," not "surgery." After hemostasis, the doctor leaves; the patient recovers on his own.

## III. Post-Interference Withdrawal Mechanism

The link where interference most easily goes wrong is **withdrawal**. Many interferers after striking do not leave, stay to "guide," finally becoming occupation. Heng Yuan Zhou's withdrawal mechanism has three steps:

1. **Withdrawal declaration**: after the disaster ends, the transcended one publicly declares interference ended, withdrawing all non-essential personnel and computable power;
2. **Handover list**: the temporary order, temporary facilities, and temporary communications established during interference are completely handed over to Myriad Ranks' own management;
3. **Withdrawal audit**: the Anchor Supervisory Committee audits the whole interference process, confirming whether it truly "fought and left," not "lingered."

Three-step withdrawal ensures interference is "helped and left," not "helped and occupied."

## IV. Relationship with Entry 789's Immunity Boundary

Entry 789 covered "emergency-avoidance immunity"; this entry covers "limited interference." Their relationship:

- **Emergency-avoidance immunity** is the law layer — the transcended one in emergencies may bypass normal legal procedure;
- **Limited-interference boundary** is the ethics layer — even when immunity takes effect, interference itself has boundaries.

Immunity is "may act first"; boundary is "how far to act." Both stack: the transcended one may strike in emergencies, but the strike must be within limited boundaries. Immunity grants the right to strike; the boundary limits the strike's scope.

After interference ends there is still an after-the-fact review. Every time the transcended one exercises the limited-interference right, he must within one cycle after interference ends submit a complete report to the Anchor Supervisory Committee: whether the interference trigger condition was truly met, whether interference means were minimized, whether local causality was disturbed after interference, whether withdrawal was clean. The Supervisory Committee cross-checks with Alaya Network data, confirming the report matches actual behavior. If "in the name of interference but actually predation" or "excessive interference causing local civilizational autonomy damage" is found, not only is immunity withdrawn, but the transcended one's civilizational weight is retroactively adjusted. After-the-fact review locks interference power in a cage — you may strike in emergencies, but every motion of your strike will be reckoned after the fact.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: limited interference puts computable power on disasters truly needing it, avoiding computable power being wasted on "guiding Myriad Ranks"
- **Anti-predation**: interference directly targets predatory factions, while preventing interference itself from becoming new predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo arranges everything for Myriad Ranks; Heng Yuan Zhou requires the transcended one only strike during disasters, then leave
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: interference applies equally to all Myriad-Rank clans; no "this clan deserves rescue, that clan deserves it"
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: interference's entire cases and boundary-execution records are opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao. When the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcended one exercises limited-interference right, his civilizational weight must cover the relevant clans
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Non-interference does not mean never striking. Myriad Ranks being slaughtered, galaxies being polluted, laws fracturing — in these times the transcended one must strike. But striking has boundaries: minimal, short-term, only backflowing knowledge, isolated operation, fight and leave. Interference is hemostasis, not occupation. After hemostasis, how the wound heals is Myriad Ranks' own business.
