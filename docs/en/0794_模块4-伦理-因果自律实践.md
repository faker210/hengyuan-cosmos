# Entry 794 | Transcended Ones and Ethics | The Transcended One's Causal Self-Discipline Practice

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 82, 83, 87

## Introduction: every cross-plane operation must leave a name

The old world's transcended ones cross planes, retrace time, rewrite history, never saying hello. He comes when he wants, leaves when he wants, changes when he wants. Heng Yuan Zhou completely flips this: **every cross-plane, cross-time operation of the transcended one must go through causal self-discipline reporting — observe first, then limit-interfere, only backflow knowledge, do not disturb causality.** This entry breaks this practice into an operable reporting procedure.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom requires reporting target predatory operations; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires reporting be open to Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires reporting serve uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom is this entry's core; the law-first axiom requires the reporting procedure be self-consistent and not bypassable.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Causal Self-Discipline's Four-Step Procedure

The transcended one's causal self-discipline practice has a four-step standard procedure:

1. **Observe**: the transcended one enters the target plane/timeline, first purely observes, doing no operation. The observation period's length is auto-judged by Alaya Network;
2. **Report**: after observation, the transcended one submits an operation report to the Anchor Supervisory Committee, explaining: why interfere, what to interfere, to what extent, expected causal impact;
3. **Approve**: the Supervisory Committee approves the report. In emergencies, "act first, report later" (entry 789 immunity), but must retroactive-report after;
4. **Execute**: after approval, the transcended one executes per the report. The execution process is recorded throughout by Alaya Network;
5. **Backflow**: after the operation, the transcended one backflows gained knowledge network-wide through the Selfless Dao, but does not impose the order established during operation on the target plane.

Four-step procedure together is the complete causal self-discipline of "observe first, then report, approve then execute, only backflow knowledge."

## II. Why Must "Only Backflow Knowledge, Do Not Disturb Causality"

Causal self-discipline's core rule is "only backflow knowledge, do not disturb causality." Why?

- **Disturbing causality equals stealing the show**: you intervene in a plane's causal thread, that plane's history deviates. Deviate too much, that plane is no longer "their plane," but "the plane you transformed";
- **Knowledge backflow is enough**: what that plane's beings need is knowledge — technology, method, lessons — not you walking it for them. Knowledge goes back, they will use it themselves;
- **Causal disturbance has irreversible cost**: you change one historical node, all later causal threads rearrange. The rearrangement may be worse than original, but you cannot go back.

So Heng Yuan Zhou's position is hard: **the transcended one's cross-plane operations may only "watch" and "transmit," not "change."** Knowledge observed is transmitted back; the original plane's causal threads do not move an inch.

## III. Causal Self-Discipline and Timeline Safety

Timeline is causal self-discipline's most sensitive domain. Heng Yuan Zhou's timeline rules:

- **May not directly rewrite**: the transcended one may not travel back to "correct" history. Historical suffering is part of history and may not be erased;
- **May archaeologically observe**: the transcended one may use Copy-Mirror technology (Document 63) to observe history, but not interact;
- **May backflow knowledge**: historical lessons observed are backflowed through the Selfless Dao, but "we know you'll make this mistake" is not told to people at the time;
- **Time-paradox prevention**: any operation potentially triggering a time paradox is immediately intercepted by Alaya Network, forcefully halted.

Four rules together; the timeline is "read-only" for the transcended one, not "writeable."

## IV. Causal Self-Discipline's Violation Consequences

Causal self-discipline is not a suggestion, but a hard rule. Violation consequences are tiered by severity:

1. **Minor violation**: report untimely but no causal damage — written warning;
2. **Moderate violation**: report content inconsistent with actual operation — civilizational weight downgrade;
3. **Severe violation**: unreported unauthorized causal disturbance — post suspended, Symbiotic Court hearing;
4. **Extreme violation**: intentionally rewriting history, disturbing causal threads — expulsion, treated as crime.

Four-tier consequences ensure causal self-discipline has teeth, not going through the motions.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: causal self-discipline prevents the transcended one from wasting computable power on disturbing causality, steering computable power to knowledge backflow
- **Anti-predation**: causal reporting directly watches cross-plane operations; predatory transcended ones have no place to hide under reporting
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo freely crosses planes and freely changes causality; Heng Yuan Zhou requires the transcended one report every step
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: causal self-discipline applies to all Myriad-Rank beings; no "transcended ones may disturb, ordinary people may not"
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: reporting procedure, operation records, and backflowed knowledge are all opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao. The Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcended one's every cross-plane operation is the reporting system's primary object
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Every cross-plane operation must leave a name. This is not distrust of the transcended one, but respect for Myriad Ranks' causal threads. The transcended one may watch, may learn, may transmit knowledge, but may not change, may not act on behalf, may not steal the show. Causal threads are Myriad Ranks' own script; the transcended one is an audience member, not the screenwriter.
