# Entry 795 | Transcended Ones and Ethics | The Transcended One's All-Beings' Origin Attachment Prevention

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 07, 19, 791

## Introduction: even the strongest is targeted by inner demons

"All-beings' origin attachment · shortcut inner demon" is the Heng Yuan Zhou terminology library's most core cultivation trap — it is not an external demon, but the "wanting-to-take-shortcuts" thought in every being's heart. The old world believes this inner demon only harms low-order cultivators; Heng Yuan Zhou's judgment is the opposite: **this inner demon most harms precisely the highest-order transcended ones.** Because the transcended one has the greatest temptation to take shortcuts, the greatest ability, and the heaviest consequences. This entry discusses how the transcended one prevents himself from being eroded by this inner demon.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom treats attachment as predation's psychological root; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires preventing attachment protect Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires preventing attachment serve uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires preventing attachment not disturb causality; the law-first axiom requires the prevention mechanism be self-consistent and executable.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Transcended One's Five Special Attachments

Low-order cultivators' attachment is "wanting to grow faster"; the transcended one's attachments are more subtle, five kinds:

1. **Control attachment**: cannot help deciding for Myriad Ranks, because "I understand better than you";
2. **Timeline attachment**: cannot help traveling back to correct history, because "that history could have been better";
3. **Knowledge-monopoly attachment**: cannot help privately hoarding key knowledge, because "what if it is misused once opened?";
4. **Eternal-youth attachment**: cannot help remodeling one's own existence form, pursuing "permanent perfection";
5. **Worship attachment**: cannot help enjoying Myriad Ranks' gaze, treating the gaze as one's own need.

Five attachments all sound "very lofty," but their essence is the same thing: **sliding from serving Myriad Ranks to serving oneself.**

## II. Attachment's Degeneration Path

The transcended one eroded by attachment follows a fixed degeneration path:

1. **Early stage**: sincerely wishes well for Myriad Ranks, but begins to feel "Myriad Ranks aren't smart enough, let me decide for them";
2. **Middle stage**: deciding for Myriad Ranks becomes habit, begins to feel "Myriad Ranks can't live without me";
3. **Late stage**: Myriad Ranks truly can't live without him — because he did everything Myriad Ranks could do for themselves;
4. **Endgame**: he becomes the kind of person he originally opposed — a Gu-Chensha-style patriarch.

This path's scariest part is that every step "looks like it's for Myriad Ranks." The transcended one himself does not feel he is falling; he feels he is dedicating.

## III. Prevention's Three Defense Lines

Heng Yuan Zhou's attachment prevention for transcended ones has three defense lines:

1. **Self defense line**: the transcended one's own cultivation log must do an "attachment self-check" weekly — did I decide for Myriad Ranks this week? Did I privately hoard knowledge? Did I enjoy being worshipped?
2. **Institutional defense line**: the Anchor Supervisory Committee's quarterly audit specifically watches whether the transcended one's behavior pattern slides toward the five attachments;
3. **Myriad-Rank defense line**: Myriad-Rank representatives may at any time report to the Supervisory Committee "this transcended one recently has started deciding for us?"

Three defense lines together turn attachment prevention from "personal cultivation" into "institutional engineering." It does not rely on the transcended one's own self-awareness, but on institutions catching him.

## IV. Prevention's Distinction from "Justified Rascal"

Attachment prevention (this entry) and justified rascal (entry 796) are two sides of one coin:

- **Attachment** is the thought in the transcended one's heart — he wants to control, monopolize, be worshipped;
- **Rascal** is the transcended one's behavior — he wraps attachment as "for your own good," turning it into actual action.

Attachment prevention guards thoughts; rascal-prevention guards behavior. Both stack: when a thought arises, institutions hold it; when a thought becomes behavior, law stops it.

Attachment's early detection does not rely on the transcended one's self-awareness, but on three objective signals. The first is Alaya Network's behavioral-baseline deviation detection: if a transcended one suddenly begins hoarding a certain resource alone, repeatedly accessing a certain ordinary cultivator's consciousness domain, or continuously pushing clearly self-favoring agendas in the Council, the algorithm auto-flags. The second is peer review: other transcended ones in the same post annually do one anonymous psychological-state assessment of each other; any obvious paranoia, autocracy, or rising control desire triggers attention. The third is Myriad-Rank letters: ordinary beings may anonymously report a transcended one's anomalous words and deeds through the Selfless Dao to the Supervisory Committee. Any one of the three signals lights, a psychological assessment launches; two or more light, forced rotation rest. Institutions do not believe empty talk like "the strong can self-control."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: attachment steers the transcended one's computable power to private purposes; preventing attachment pulls computable power back to civilizational engineering
- **Anti-predation**: attachment is predation's psychological root; preventing attachment seals predation paths from the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system no one prevents Gu Chensha's attachment; Heng Yuan Zhou uses three defense lines to watch the transcended one
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: attachment prevention protects Myriad Ranks' autonomous growth right; Myriad Ranks are the defense line's beneficiaries
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: attachment prevention's entire mechanisms, self-check tables, and audit records are opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao. The Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcended one's attachment self-check is audited by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision; anomalies directly affect his civilizational weight
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Even the strongest is targeted by inner demons, and inner demons' temptation to him is greatest. All-beings' origin attachment is not low-order cultivators' patent; it is precisely the transcended one's greatest risk. Prevention does not rely on the transcended one's own self-awareness, but on three defense lines: self self-check, institutional audit, Myriad-Rank report. When a thought arises, give a pull; when behavior occurs, give a stop.
