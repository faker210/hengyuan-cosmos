# Entry 796 | Transcended Ones and Ethics | The Transcended One's Justified-Rascal Cultivation

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 19, 04, 795

## Introduction: the most dangerous predation is predation wearing "for your own good" skin

"Justified rascal" is the Heng Yuan Zhou terminology library's most venomous word — it refers to strong ones who, under the banner of "for your own good," "for Myriad Ranks," "for justice," actually pull resources, power, and causality toward themselves. This kind of person is most dangerous, because he does not feel he is predating; he feels he is dedicating. This entry discusses how the transcended one recognizes, prevents, and even cultivates out this "justified rascal," and how institutions prevent the transcended one himself from becoming him.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom directly judges the justified rascal; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires preventing rascals protect Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires preventing rascals serve uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires preventing rascals not disturb causality; the law-first axiom requires the prevention mechanism be self-consistent and executable.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Justified Rascal's Five Identifying Traits

The justified rascal is not recognizable by ordinary people, because his behavior looks all good things. Heng Yuan Zhou gives five identifying traits:

1. **Catchphrase is "for your own good"**: everything he does must add "this is for you/for Myriad Ranks/for everyone";
2. **Refuses data audit**: you ask him to speak with numbers, he says "you don't understand the big picture";
3. **Monopolizes interpretation right**: what is "good" is defined by him. You disagree, it is "you don't understand me";
4. **Creates attachment**: the more he helps you, the more you cannot leave him. He is not helping you become independent, but helping you become dependent on him;
5. **Unaccountable**: what he does you may not audit, because "he has already given so much."

Five traits together; the justified rascal is a person who is "morally impeccable, behaviorally suspicious." You scold him, Myriad Ranks feel you are ungrateful; you investigate him, he says you don't trust him.

## II. Why "Cultivating" the Justified Rascal Is Ironic

This entry's title says "justified-rascal cultivation," which is ironic — Heng Yuan Zhou is not cultivating justified rascals, but **cultivating the ability to recognize justified rascals**, and **preventing the transcended one himself from being cultivated into a justified rascal**.

"Cultivation" holds in two senses:

- **For Myriad Ranks**: Heng Yuan Zhou wants to cultivate Myriad Ranks' ability to recognize justified rascals — when a strong one says "for your own good," Myriad Ranks should learn to ask: where are the numbers? Where is the audit? Where is the choice?;
- **For the transcended one**: Heng Yuan Zhou wants to cultivate the transcended one's vigilance toward himself sliding into rascal — when you find yourself starting to say "for your own good" without presenting data, you may be becoming a rascal.

## III. How Institutions Prevent the Transcended One from Becoming a Rascal

At the institutional level, Heng Yuan Zhou has three tools to prevent the transcended one from becoming a justified rascal:

1. **Data replaces slogans**: entry 779's feedback obligation's four hard indicators turn "for your own good" into numbers. What's the knowledge feedback rate? What's the computable-power feedback amount? Numbers insufficient, no matter how many fine words, useless;
2. **Audit replaces trust**: entry 780's supervision mechanism turns "trusting he is a good person" into "auditing what he did." Alaya Network trace-keeping, not relying on his self-awareness;
3. **Choice replaces arrangement**: entry 792's non-interference principle returns "deciding for Myriad Ranks" to Myriad Ranks themselves. He does not choose for you; you choose yourself.

Three tools together; the justified rascal has no survival space in Heng Yuan Zhou's institutions — because his "for your own good" must speak with numbers, his behavior must be audited, he may not decide for Myriad Ranks.

## IV. How Myriad Ranks Recognize Rascals Around Them

Heng Yuan Zhou also teaches Myriad Ranks to recognize rascals around them, three questions:

- **First ask numbers**: you say you're good for me, then where is the data of you being good for me? How many clans benefited? How many resources fed back?
- **Second ask choice**: you say you're good for me, then did you give me a choice? Or did you decide for me?
- **Third ask exit**: you say you're good for me, then I ask you to exit, will you exit?

Three questions cannot be answered, no matter how melodious the "for your own good" is, it is a rascal.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the justified rascal wraps computable power as "dedication," actually pulling it to private purposes; institutions pull it back with numbers
- **Anti-predation**: the justified rascal is the highest-level predation — he does not grab your things; he grabs your choice; institutions directly seal it
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Gu Chensha is cosmos web novels' biggest justified rascal; Heng Yuan Zhou uses the trio of data, audit, and choice to defend against this
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the ability to recognize rascals is open to all Myriad-Rank beings; the weak may also see through the strong's "for your own good"
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the rascal-recognition three questions, audit tools, and feedback indicators are all opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao. The Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcended one's civilizational weight is the best "rascal recognizer" — numbers insufficient, no matter how many fine words, useless
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The most dangerous predation is predation wearing "for your own good" skin. The justified rascal does not grab your things; he grabs your choice. Heng Yuan Zhou's way against him is simple: don't listen to slogans, look at numbers; don't believe in trust, rely on audit; don't allow arrangement, return choice. When a strong one says "for your own good," ask three questions — where are the numbers? Where is the choice? Where is the exit? Those who cannot answer, no matter how melodious, are rascals.
