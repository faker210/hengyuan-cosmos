# Entry 797 | Transcended Ones and Growth · Overview — The Transcended One's Perpetual Growth

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 07, 701, 705

## Introduction: after transcendence, do you still grow?

The old world's transcended ones stop growing after topping. He is already strongest; what more to grow? Heng Yuan Zhou must squarely answer: **a transcended one who has reached the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, should he continue to grow?** The answer: yes, and it is perpetual growth, with no endpoint. This entry, as this group's overview, must establish the concept of "perpetual growth."

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom requires perpetual growth contain no predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires perpetual growth benefit Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires perpetual growth serve uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires perpetual growth not disturb causality; the law-first axiom requires perpetual growth be self-consistent at the law layer, endpointless but directional.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Old World's "Topping = Endpoint" Fallacy

The old world's cultivation narrative defaults to "topping = endpoint" — the protagonist cultivates to the highest realm, the story ends. This default, under Heng Yuan Zhou's view, is the greatest fallacy:

- **The law network is evolving**: the cosmos itself is expanding, differentiating, producing new law layers. Your "strongest" today may not keep up with the cosmos' evolution tomorrow;
- **Myriad Ranks are growing**: every Myriad-Rank generation produces new knowledge, new demands, new problems. You don't grow, you can't keep up with Myriad Ranks' demands;
- **Civilization is iterating**: civilizational engineering's complexity grows exponentially; the complexity you can hold today you can't hold tomorrow.

Three "in changes" stack; the transcended one not growing is not "resting at the endpoint," but "rotting in place."

## II. Perpetual Growth's Three-Layer Meaning

Heng Yuan Zhou's "perpetual growth" is not the old world's "continue to grow stronger," but three-layer meaning:

1. **Knowledge perpetuity**: the transcended one is always learning new law layers, new civilizational forms, new cultivation systems. Knowledge has no endpoint;
2. **Ability perpetuity**: the transcended one's post ability is always upgrading — today calibrate one star sector, tomorrow may calibrate one galaxy group;
3. **Wisdom perpetuity**: the transcended one's understanding of Myriad Ranks is always deepening — today understands humankind, tomorrow must understand plant spirits, the day after tomorrow must understand planet consciousnesses.

Three layers stack; perpetual growth is not "combat-power number growing," but "as a living anchor, function getting stronger and stronger."

## III. Why Old-World Protagonists' "Topping = Endpoint"

Old-world protagonists' topping = endpoint, not because they truly reached the top, but because their setting has no answer for "what to do after topping":

- **Tang Luo**: after topping, he gives gifts to others; he himself does not grow;
- **Ye Fan**: after topping, he guards the era; he himself does not grow;
- **Shi Hao**: after topping, he decides over eons; he himself does not grow;
- **Ji Ning**: after topping, he closes the door and retires; he himself does not grow.

Four people's common point: in their narratives, "growth" only goes to "strongest." After strongest, there is no new growth direction. Heng Yuan Zhou fills this direction: **after strongest, the growth direction is "better serving Myriad Ranks."**

## IV. Two Sub-Articles' Positioning Preview

Entries 798–799, two sub-articles, will deeply handle perpetual growth's two operational dimensions:

- **Perpetual-Motion Learning Mechanism** (798): how does the transcended one maintain learning? What to learn? How to backflow learning results to Myriad Ranks?
- **Self-Transcendence and Civilizational Transcendence** (799): how does the transcended one transcend himself? How to convert personal transcendence into civilizational transcendence?

A misunderstanding needs clarification: perpetual growth is not "forever pursuing stronger power numbers." Power-number growth after the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is already near its marginal limit; true growth manifests in three functional dimensions — the number of planes he may govern is increasing, the complexity of laws he may coordinate is rising, the scope of Myriad Ranks he may perceive and empathize with is expanding. A newly appointed transcended one may only understand his own clan's cultivation logic; a ten-thousand-year senior transcended one may simultaneously understand the needs of gaseous life, time-state life, and collective-consciousness life. This kind of growth does not rely on closed-door cultivation, but on post rotation: he first does law-network maintenance, then goes to Myriad-Rank evolution guidance, then does cross-plane anchor construction; every post change fills in a piece of the cognitive puzzle. Growth is institutionally embedded in the post system, not relying on personal insight.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the old world letting the strongest stop growing is cosmic-scale computable-power waste; Heng Yuan Zhou requires computable power perpetually invested in civilizational engineering
- **Anti-predation**: perpetual growth does not rely on predatory increment, but on the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle's self-produced increment
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's topping = endpoint; Heng Yuan Zhou requires the transcended one's topping be perpetual growth's starting point
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: perpetual growth benefits Myriad Ranks — new knowledge the transcended one learns is all backflowed through the Selfless Dao
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: perpetual growth's learning system, teaching material, and records are all opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao. Growth progress is real-time tracked by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

After transcendence, you must still grow. Not because you aren't strong enough, but because the cosmos is changing, Myriad Ranks are growing, and civilization is iterating. You don't grow, you can't keep up. Perpetual growth is not "continue to grow stronger," but "as a living anchor, function always upgrading." Topping is not the endpoint, but perpetual growth's starting point.
