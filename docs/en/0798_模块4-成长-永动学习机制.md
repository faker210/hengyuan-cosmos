# Entry 798 | Transcended Ones and Growth | The Transcended One's Perpetual-Motion Learning Mechanism

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 11, 69, 797

## Introduction: why does the strongest most need to learn?

The old world believes the strongest needs no learning — he is already omniscient. Heng Yuan Zhou must squarely answer: **one person strong enough for the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm — why must he still learn?** The answer: because the cosmos is evolving, laws are differentiating, and Myriad Ranks are raising new questions. He doesn't learn, he can't keep up. This entry discusses the transcended one's perpetual-motion learning mechanism — how to learn, what to learn, how to use after learning.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom requires learning not rely on predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires learning benefit Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires learning serve uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires learning not disturb causality; the law-first axiom requires the learning mechanism be self-consistent and iterable.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Perpetual-Motion Learning's Four Learning Objects

The transcended one's perpetual-motion learning has four fixed learning objects:

1. **New law layers**: law layers newly produced by cosmos evolution; the transcended one must continuously learn to calibrate these new layers;
2. **New Myriad-Rank civilizations**: new civilizational forms discovered in cross-plane exploration; the transcended one must learn their culture, technology, social organization;
3. **New cultivation systems**: new cultivation-system variants Myriad Ranks produce at their respective posts; the transcended one must learn to integrate them into the 300-set system;
4. **Own past mistakes**: the transcended one's own past mistakes, Alaya Network's trace-kept operation logs, must continuously be reviewed and learned.

Four learning objects together; the transcended one's learning is not "learning new knowledge," but "evolving with the cosmos."

## II. Perpetual-Motion Learning's Three Learning Channels

Where does the transcended one learn from? Three channels:

- **Alaya Network auto-push**: the network monitors new law layers, new civilizations, new systems, auto-pushing them to the transcended one;
- **Myriad-Rank representative feedback**: questions and demands Myriad-Rank representatives raise in the Anchor Council and public hearings are the transcended one's most important learning material;
- **Frontline station-learning**: the transcended one regularly stations in Myriad-Rank clans (part of entry 779's time-feedback obligation), learning Myriad Ranks' real situation on the frontline.

Three channels together; the transcended one's learning is not "closed-door enlightenment," but "learning among Myriad Ranks."

## III. Learning Results' Backflow Mechanism

The transcended one finishes learning; results must not be privately hoarded. Heng Yuan Zhou's backflow mechanism:

1. **Teaching-material-ization**: learning results are organized into teaching material, opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao;
2. **Systematization**: new law layers and new cultivation systems are integrated into the 300-set system's index (Document 91);
3. **Training-ization**: the transcended one transmits what he learned to the next generation through apprenticeship (entry 781);
4. **Engineering-ization**: learning results are converted into civilizational engineering — new-law-layer calibration, new-civilization docking, new-system promotion.

Four-step backflow ensures every bit of knowledge the transcended one learns serves Myriad Ranks, not locked in a private brain.

## IV. Perpetual-Motion Learning and the "Omniscience" Hallucination

The old world's strong ones' greatest hallucination is "I am omniscient." Heng Yuan Zhou's perpetual-motion learning mechanism specifically targets this hallucination:

- **No omniscience assumption**: institutionally no "omniscience" is recognized; Alaya Network is always pushing new knowledge;
- **Question-encouraging**: the transcended one in Council and hearings is encouraged to ask questions, not expected to "answer all correctly";
- **Mistake-normalizing**: mistakes the transcended one makes in learning are normal; institutions do not punish trial-and-error in learning, only intentional predation.

Three together; the transcended one is institutionally forever pressed into the "learner" position, not letting him slide into the "omniscient one" arrogance.

Perpetual-motion learning's one special object is already-defeated predatory civilizations. Alaya Network completely preserves these civilizations' operational data — their cultivation-manual systems, resource-dispatch methods, social structures, collapse nodes. The transcended one does not learn their predatory means, but to figure out "how a civilization step by step walked onto the predatory road and how it step by step collapsed." This kind of learning is more important than reading success cases, because successful paths cannot be replicated, but failure lessons may directly convert into institutional patches. Every purified predatory plane produces a "civilizational pathology report," distributed to all transcended-one posts as training material. In this sense, the predatory factions' demise is not entirely bad — they used their own ruin to serve as a free negative textbook for Myriad Ranks.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: perpetual-motion learning continuously invests the transcended one's computable power into new knowledge, avoiding computable power idling in "omniscience hallucination"
- **Anti-predation**: learning relies on Alaya Network and Myriad-Rank feedback, not cross-plane predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is omniscient and does not learn; Heng Yuan Zhou requires the transcended one forever learning
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: learning objects include all Myriad-Rank civilizational forms; learning results backflow to all Myriad-Rank beings
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: learning teaching material, system index, and training records are all opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao. Learning output is directly counted into the transcended one's civilizational weight
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The strongest most needs to learn, because the cosmos is changing, Myriad Ranks are growing, and laws are differentiating. Perpetual-motion learning is not "continue to grow stronger," but "evolve with the cosmos." After learning, teaching-material-ize, systematize, train-ify, engineer-ify, backflow to Myriad Ranks. Institutionally never recognize "omniscience," forever press the transcended one into the learner's position.
