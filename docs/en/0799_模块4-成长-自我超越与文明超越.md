# Entry 799 | Transcended Ones and Growth | The Transcended One's Self-Transcendence and Civilizational Transcendence

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 07, 797, 701

## Introduction: transcending oneself is for transcending civilization

The old world's "transcendence" is "transcending others" — transcending enemies, peers, ceilings. Heng Yuan Zhou's "transcendence" has two layers: **self-transcendence** and **civilizational transcendence**. The former is the transcended one continuously transcending his own limits; the latter is the transcended one converting personal transcendence into civilization's overall transcendence. This entry discusses the relationship between these two transcendences.

Six top-level axioms all line up: the anti-predation axiom requires transcendence contain no predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires civilizational transcendence benefit Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom is civilizational transcendence's core; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the transcendence process not disturb causality; the law-first axiom requires the two transcendences' relationship be self-consistent at the law layer.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Self-Transcendence's Three Directions

The transcended one's self-transcendence is not "combat-power number growing," but three directions:

1. **Cognitive transcendence**: continuously transcending one's own understanding of Myriad Ranks — from "only understand humankind" to "understand five lineages," from "understand now" to "understand history and future";
2. **Ethical transcendence**: continuously transcending one's own ethical limits — from "not predating" to "non-interference," from "non-interference" to "proactive feedback";
3. **Post transcendence**: continuously transcending one's own post ability — from "calibrate one star sector" to "calibrate one galaxy group," from "bring 3 disciples" to "train a generation of transcended ones."

Three directions together; self-transcendence is "as a living anchor, functional upgrade."

## II. Civilizational Transcendence's Three Directions

The transcended one's self-transcendence ultimately must convert into civilizational transcendence. Civilizational transcendence's three directions:

1. **Law-network upgrade**: the law network the transcended one calibrates gets more stable and broader; the whole civilization's law infrastructure upgrades;
2. **Myriad-Rank coverage upgrade**: the Myriad-Rank clans the transcended one serves get more; 99% coverage approaches 100%;
3. **Knowledge-system upgrade**: knowledge the transcended one backflows gets more systematic; the 300-set system continuously iterates; later-comers' starting point gets higher.

Three directions together; civilizational transcendence is "the whole Myriad-Rank civilization's water level rising."

## III. The Two Transcendences' Relationship

Self-transcendence and civilizational transcendence are not two things, but two sides of one thing:

- **Civilizational transcendence without self-transcendence is empty talk**: you yourself don't grow, what do you use to lead civilization to grow?
- **Self-transcendence without civilizational transcendence is waste**: you yourself grow, but don't convert into civilizational increment, equal to growing in vain.

Heng Yuan Zhou's formula: **self-transcendence × civilizational conversion = true transcended growth.** No matter how large self-transcendence is, civilizational conversion zero, product zero. This is the same logic as entry 706's civilizational-weight formula (destructive power × civilizational consensus degree). The conversion process of the two transcendences is recorded throughout by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision, non-fabricable, non-interruptible.

## IV. Why Old-World Protagonists Have No Civilizational Transcendence

Old-world protagonists have self-transcendence (combat power growing stronger), but civilizational transcendence is almost zero:

- **Tang Luo**: he grew stronger, but civilization did not grow stronger — Myriad Ranks are still locked in the fierce realm;
- **Ye Fan**: he grew stronger, but civilization did not grow stronger — Myriad Ranks are still the protected;
- **Shi Hao**: he grew stronger, but civilization did not grow stronger — Myriad Ranks are still autocratically decided;
- **Goku**: he grew stronger, but civilization did not grow stronger — Earth is still that Earth.

Their self-transcendence did not convert into civilizational transcendence. Heng Yuan Zhou requires every bit of the transcended one's self-transcendence have a corresponding civilizational-conversion indicator.

Between the two transcendences there is a positive-feedback loop. New problems the transcended one encounters in civilizational transcendence — such as law conflicts of a newly accessed cosmos, special difficulties of a weak race — will in turn expose his own cognitive blind spots, pushing him to self-transcend; and new abilities and new perspectives he gains after self-transcendence can help him solve civilizational-engineering problems he could not solve before. Old-world protagonists only walk the "self-transcendence" line, growing stronger yet lonelier, because their growth has no external object to feedback; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended ones must tie the two lines together, self-transcendence's depth is tested by civilizational-transcendence results, civilizational-transcendence's height is limited by self-transcendence's ceiling. Either leg short, the other cannot walk far.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: self-transcendence not converting into civilizational transcendence is computable-power waste; Heng Yuan Zhou uses conversion indicators to tie the two transcendences together
- **Anti-predation**: transcendence relies on the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle, not predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo only has self-transcendence (combat power growing stronger), no civilizational transcendence; Heng Yuan Zhou requires both transcendences happen simultaneously
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: civilizational transcendence directly benefits all Myriad-Rank clans
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: transcendence's entire indicators, teaching material, and engineering records are opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao. The Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcended one's two transcendences (self × civilizational) are perpetual growth's ultimate ruler
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Transcending oneself is for transcending civilization. Self-transcendence is the means; civilizational transcendence is the end. The two transcendences tied together is true perpetual growth. Old-world protagonists only did the first half; Heng Yuan Zhou requires the transcended one do the second half too — and the second half is true transcendence.
