> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 01, 701, 705

## Introduction: three hundred thousand words, rewriting the word "transcendence" from scratch

From 701 to 800, a full hundred entries (this batch is 767–800, continuing 701–766), the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization did one thing: redefine from head to toe the word "transcendence," which web novels had stigmatized. This entry, as Module 4's first-half closing, must systematically summarize these hundred entries' results — how the old definition went bankrupt, how the new definition was established, and what the new definition's complete system looks like.

Six top-level axioms here serve as the summary's main thread: anti-predation, republicanism of Myriad Ranks, collective ascension, causal self-discipline, law-first. All hundred entries' argumentation ultimately converges on these five axioms.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Old Definition's Sixfold Bankruptcy

Module 4's first half (701–766) first did the "break" work — bankrupting the old world's "transcendence" definition six times:

1. **Escape-style transcendence**: severing bonds, ascending alone — entry 702;
2. **Lie-flat-style transcendence**: hang-up retirement, no more construction — entry 703;
3. **Monopoly-style transcendence**: privately hoarding cultivation manuals, knowledge monopoly — entry 704;
4. **Hang-up-style transcendence**: after strength reaches the extreme, going offline — entries 734–738, five types;
5. **Parasitic-style transcendence**: topping by predation or gift — entries 768–776, nine benchmarks;
6. **Aimless-style transcendence**: combat-power inflation with no civilizational direction — entry 772.

Sixfold bankruptcy together; the old world's "transcendence" narrative is completely liquidated.

## II. The New Definition's Fivefold Construction

After breaking, Module 4's second half (767–800) did the "establish" work — Heng Yuan Zhou's bearing transcendence's fivefold construction:

1. **Benchmark reconstruction** (767–776): using the five-dimension comparison framework, measuring nine cosmos protagonists one by one, measuring out the old ceiling's nine ailments;
2. **Myriad-Rank relationship reconstruction** (777–783): pulling the transcended one from "above Myriad Ranks" to "among Myriad Ranks," establishing co-builder relationship;
3. **Institutional-position reconstruction** (784–790): pulling the transcended one from "above institutions" to "within institutions," four powers separated, mutually checked;
4. **Ethical-boundary reconstruction** (791–796): establishing five ethical defense lines — non-interference, limited interference, causal self-discipline, attachment prevention, rascal recognition;
5. **Perpetual-growth reconstruction** (797–799): flipping "topping = endpoint" to "topping = starting point," self-transcendence × civilizational transcendence.

Fivefold construction together; Heng Yuan Zhou's "bearing transcendence" from a slogan becomes an operable, auditable, executable institutional system.

## III. The New Definition's Core Formula

All Module 4 argumentation ultimately converges on one formula:

> **Individual effective combat power = destructive power × civilizational consensus degree** (entry 706)

This formula is Heng Yuan Zhou's hardest rewrite of the word "strong." It tells all cultivators:

- Your destructive power is your own business;
- Your civilizational consensus degree is Myriad Ranks' business;
- Only when Myriad Ranks recognize your direction does your destructive power deserve to be called combat power;
- Consensus degree negative, no matter how large the destructive power, it is a negative asset.

Around this formula, Module 4 established a complete set of supporting institutions: five-dimensional civilizational weight, four bodies, seven Myriad-Rank relationships, five ethical defense lines, perpetual-growth two transcendences.
