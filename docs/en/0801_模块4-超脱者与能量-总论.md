# Entry 801 | Transcended Ones and Energy · Overview — The Transcended One's Energy-Operation System

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 15, 04, 24

## Introduction: the transcended one is not energy's endpoint, but the energy cycle's hub

The predatory system's imagination of "transcendence" is: the strong absorb enough energy, then turn themselves into an energy black hole, severing the cycle with all beings. Heng Yuan Zhou completely negates this definition. In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's orthodox framework, **the transcended one is not energy's endpoint, but the highest-level hub node of the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle** — the scale of energy he absorbs, purifies, dispatches, and feeds back far exceeds ordinary cultivators, but every bit of energy he handles must run on the track of "creating increment, not consuming stock."

Six top-level axioms all take effect in this entry: the anti-predation axiom requires the transcended one's energy origins all be clean, not swallowing a thread of other clans' origin; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the transcended one's energy system open interfaces to all beings, no private spirit veins; the collective ascension axiom requires the transcended one's energy dispatch ultimately serve Myriad Ranks' simultaneous uplift, not solo ascent; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the transcended one in cross-plane energy dispatch strictly report, observe first then limited-interfere; the law-first axiom ensures the transcended one's energy-operation system itself is a law-level definition, not eaten-book-changed by plot convenience.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article, as the "Transcended Ones and Energy" sub-series overview, systematically sorts out the transcended one's energy-operation panorama at the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm level: what energy he handles, what channels he runs, what boundaries he keeps, what increment he produces.

## I. The Transcended One's Energy-Operation Four Layers

Per the Energy-Cycle and Transformation Law (Document 15) and Anchor Energy-Conduction Path (Document 24), the transcended one's energy operation from low to high divides into four layers; each layer corresponds to one link of the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle:

| Layer | Name | Operation Content | Corresponding Cycle Link |
|-------|------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| Layer 1 | Inner-cycle layer | In-body origin energy's purification, storage, and daily dispatch | Individual absorption—purification—storage—application—feedback |
| Layer 2 | Anchor-docking layer | Accessing the symbiotic network's public energy pool through high-tier anchors | Group cycle and synergy gain |
| Layer 3 | Plane-dispatch layer | Cross-star-sector, cross-plane energy surplus-shortfall adjustment and law-engineering power supply | Cosmic-scale "origin release—layered flow—cyclic gain—return to origin" |
| Layer 4 | Origin-creation layer | Under law-anchor authority, participating in energy-increment development and new-cosmic-grade energy-circuit weaving | Increment development · energy creation |

Ordinary cultivators throughout their lives mainly operate in layers 1 and 2; plane-sovereign strong ones (Origin Realm) enter layer 3; but the transcended one entering the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is formally authorized to operate in layer 4. Layer 4's core is not "moving more energy," but **making the energy cycle itself produce increment** — this is precisely the anti-predation axiom's fulfillment at the highest energy level.

## II. Seven Energy Types the Transcended One Handles

Per Document 15's energy classification, the transcended one across four layers simultaneously handles seven energy types: spirit qi, law force, life energy, consciousness energy, matter energy, origin energy, anomalous energy. Unlike ordinary cultivators only touching the first three, the transcended one must have complete identification, transformation, and control ability for the latter four.

Three key principles:

- **Origin transparency**: every bit of energy entering the transcended one's operation system carries an origin marker in Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision. Predatory residue cannot hide in this marker system — this is the causal self-discipline axiom's technicalized implementation at the energy layer.
- **Transformation trace-keeping**: transformation between different energy types (e.g., law force → origin energy), transformation efficiency, loss destination, byproduct destination all recorded. Multiplication Fruit technology's hundredfold amplification only raises transformation efficiency, never creating energy out of nothing — this is respect for the energy-conservation law.
- **Feedback mandatory**: every cycle the transcended one operates, he must feed back purified energy to the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle at no less than a certain ratio. The feedback ratio is not a suggestion, but one of the conditions for maintaining law-anchor authority — feedback substandard, authority auto-downgrades.

## III. Energy Conservation and Increment Creation's Unity

The old system's greatest logical loophole is: either admit energy conservation (then on what grounds does the protagonist grow stronger as he fights?), or secretly break conservation (protagonist-aura-style star-absorbing method). Heng Yuan Zhou uses the law-first axiom to completely resolve this contradiction:

- **Base layer**: energy conservation law may not be violated. The transcended one cannot create energy out of nothing; all operation is within the conservation framework.
- **Increment layer**: through the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle, life's ordered cultivation activity may offset entropy increase and raise the cosmos' overall order — this is not "creating energy," but **weaving energy that would otherwise dissipate into background radiation back into a cycle life may use**. The transcended one's layer-4 work is amplifying this increment effect.

In other words, the transcended one's "energy creation" is not creation out of nothing, but **entropy-reduction engineering**. He takes energy lost in cosmic disorderly dissipation and re-includes it in a benign cycle through law-anchor authority. This both holds conservation, and fulfills the anti-predation axiom of "developing increment without consuming stock."

## IV. Energy Operation's Supervision Boundaries

The transcended one's energy operation is under triple supervision:

1. **Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision**: real-time monitoring of energy flow, origin, destination; abnormal fluctuation auto-alerts;
2. **Anchor Supervisory Committee**: periodic audit of the transcended one's energy accounts, checking whether the feedback ratio meets standard;
3. **Myriad-Rank Republican Council**: for plane-level-or-above large-scale energy dispatch, exercises final approval power.

These three layers of supervision ensure: no matter how powerful the transcended one's energy system, it will not become a new "energy pixiu" — only in, no out, monopolizing the public energy pool. In the Tang-Luo system, the plot of strong ones monopolizing heaven-and-earth spirit qi is institutionally impossible in Heng Yuan Zhou's law network.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the transcended one's energy operation centers on entropy-reduction engineering, recycling dissipated energy, eliminating meaningless waste at any energy level
- **Anti-predation**: the energy-origin marker system technically seals predatory residue; every bit of energy the transcended one handles has a checkable origin and traceable destination
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system, the higher strong ones level up the more they monopolize energy; in Heng Yuan Zhou, the higher the transcended one levels up the more he expands the cycle — the former is a black hole, the latter is a hub
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the transcended one's energy interface opens to all Myriad-Rank life forms; carbon-based, energy-condensed clans, mechanical-constructed clans, consciousness-information clans, mixed-mutated clans may all access
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the transcended one's energy-operation principles, transformation formulas, and supervision rules are all queryable in the Selfless Dao public library; no classification, no monopoly
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The transcended one's energy-operation system is essentially magnifying the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's scale. He is not energy's endpoint, but the cycle's highest hub. The more energy he handles, the higher his purification efficiency, the fuller his feedback ratio, the more vigorous Myriad Ranks' energy cycle, the more thorough the cosmos' entropy reduction — this is "transcendence" at the energy dimension's orthodox definition: not leaving the cycle, but shouldering the cycle.
