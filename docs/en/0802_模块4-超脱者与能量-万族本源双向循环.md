# Entry 802 | Transcended Ones and Energy | Hyper-Multiverse Practice of the Myriad-Origin Two-Way Cycle

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 15, 05, 25

## Introduction: the two-way cycle is not a slogan, but the transcended one's daily operation manual

The Myriad-Origin two-way cycle is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's energetics core, and the anti-predation axiom's direct expression at the energy dimension. But at the ordinary cultivator level, this cycle mainly manifests as the individual closed loop of "absorb—purify—feedback"; only when the cultivator enters the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm and holds law-anchor authority does he truly begin operating this two-way cycle at the **hyper-multiverse scale** — not one plane's cycle, but a giant cycle network across planes, cosmic structures, and life forms.

Six top-level axioms run through this entry: the anti-predation axiom requires the two-way cycle's "out" and "in" be equal, never allowing only-in-no-out; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires cycle channels open equally to all Myriad-Rank lineages; the collective ascension axiom requires the cycle's net effect continuously push the cosmos' overall entropy reduction and uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires cross-plane cycle operations strictly report, only backflow knowledge not disturb causality; the law-first axiom requires the two-way cycle's operation rules themselves be law-level definitions, not privately tampered with by any strong one.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article focuses on one question: in hyper-multiverse practice, how does the transcended one concretely run the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle.

## I. The Two-Way Cycle's Three Hyper-Multiverse Scales

Ordinary cultivators' cycle is the two-way of "individual → anchor"; the transcended one's cycle spans three scales:

| Scale | Cycle Range | Operating Subject | Core Task |
|-------|-------------|-------------------|-----------|
| Micro scale | Individual in-body inner cycle | The transcended one himself | Energy purification, impurity removal, life-energy self-cycle |
| Meso scale | Cross-plane energy surplus-shortfall adjustment | Transcended one + plane core anchor | Energy-surplus planes transmit to shortage planes, maintaining each plane's cycle balance |
| Macro scale | Multiverse-grade origin circuit weaving | Transcended-one group + cosmic origin anchor | Weave dissipated energy from scattered cosmoses back into a benign cycle, pushing overall entropy reduction |

Three scales are not split, but projections of the same two-way cycle at different spatial scales. The transcended one's operation at any one scale is synchronously reflected to the other two scales through Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision — this is the "one hair moves the whole body" hyper-multiverse practice.

## II. The "In" Side: How the Transcended One Receives Myriad-Rank Energy

The two-way cycle's "in" side is not predation, but **receiving purified energy Myriad Ranks voluntarily flow in**. Concrete channels include:

- **Public energy pool surplus inflow**: secondary energy spilled when cultivators on each plane break through, and surplus from daily cultivation contributions, after being gathered level by level through the anchor network, partly enter the transcended one's dispatch system;
- **Consciousness-domain resonance confluence**: consciousness-energy resonance generated by Myriad Ranks collectively cultivating in consciousness domains, flowing through consciousness-domain interfaces into the transcended one's energy system;
- **Law-network calibration backflow**: after the transcended one participates in law fine-tuning, the law network itself produces "calibration dividend" energy backflow;
- **Cosmic origin anchor seepage-down**: origin energy the cosmic origin anchor seeps down to high-tier transcended ones at a fixed ratio.

Key boundary: all "in" has **quota ceilings**. The transcended one may not unlimitedly draw from the public energy pool — his draw quota is dynamically determined by his civilizational weight and post needs; the higher the weight, the heavier the post, the larger the quota, but there is always a ceiling. This institutionally eliminates the predatory cycle of "strong ones draw more and more, weak ones are left with less and less."

## III. The "Out" Side: How the Transcended One Feeds Back Myriad-Rank Energy

The two-way cycle's "out" side is the transcended one's energy feedback to Myriad Ranks, and the key to whether this cycle may be called "two-way." Feedback forms include:

1. **Law-engineering power supply**: provide origin-grade energy injection for cross-plane anchor construction, plane-law maintenance, new-cosmos weaving;
2. **Later-comer support**: seep purified energy down to Myriad-Rank cultivators still in the mortal-cultivation stage, lowering evolution thresholds — this is "everyone has a road" fulfilled at the energy level;
3. **Ecological restoration power supply**: conduct energy-ecological restoration on planes and planets whose energy cycle has been damaged;
4. **Uplift thrust output**: inject maximally purified origin energy into the cosmic uplift circuit, pushing the cosmos overall toward higher dimensions.

The feedback ratio's bottom line is directly linked to law-anchor authority: below threshold, authority downgrades; continuously substandard, Anchor Supervisory Committee review launches. This is not a moral requirement, but a law-level hard constraint.

## IV. The Two-Way Cycle's Gain Effect: Why "Going Back and Forth" Is Stronger Than "One-Way Storage"

The Myriad-Origin two-way cycle at the hyper-multiverse scale produces a key effect: **synergy gain**. When the transcended one's "in" and "out" form a stable closed loop, the cycle itself produces an amplification effect — drawing 100 units of energy, after purification, dispatch, feedback returning to the transcended one's system, may become 105 units. These 5 units of increment are not produced out of nothing, but:

- Reducing energy-loss that would dissipate in cross-plane transmission through channel optimization;
- Settling Myriad-Rank cultivators' contributed "purification labor" back into the cycle in energy form;
- Incorporating entropy-reduction dividends released during law-network calibration into the circuit.

This is the anti-predation axiom's deepest fulfillment at the energetics level: **increment comes from the cycle itself, not from predating others.** Predatory cultivators think they are "earning," but actually they are cutting the cycle off and creating loss; the transcended one looks like he is "giving," but actually he is making the cycle produce increment and making everyone richer.

- **Anti-energy waste**: the two-way cycle minimizes cross-plane transmission loss, recycling dissipated energy into usable increment, eliminating every energy-level leak

- **Myriad Races full coverage**: cycle channels open to all Myriad-Rank five lineages; each clan's cultivators' contributed energy and received feedback are treated equally
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the two-way cycle's operation protocol, gain formula, and quota rules are all queryable in the Selfless Dao public library; any transcended one may reproduce them
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The Myriad-Origin two-way cycle in hyper-multiverse practice is not an abstract civilizational ideal, but an energy operation manual the transcended one executes every day. "In" has quota, "out" has bottom line, cycle has gain, whole process has supervision — these four conditions together constitute complete hyper-multiverse two-way cycle practice.
