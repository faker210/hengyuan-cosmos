> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 15, 20, 09

## Introduction: conservation is not a shackle, but the law network's self-consistency cornerstone

Web-novel systems' easiest collapse point is energy conservation. The author writes "energy conservation" today; tomorrow the protagonist swallows stars and realms — the number-one source of book-eating/stale-ending risk is setting not matching plot. Heng Yuan Zhou uses the **law-first axiom** to block this risk from the root: energy conservation is not a "reference clause," but the law network's underlying hard constraint; any transcended one's any operation may not bypass it.

But holding conservation does not mean civilization stops advancing. Heng Yuan Zhou's solution: **within the conservation framework, through continuous law-network optimization, push energy's usable efficiency infinitely high.** This is "the unity of energy conservation and the law network" — conservation is the skeleton, the law network is the flesh; the transcended one's job is to let skeleton and flesh grow together.

Six top-level axioms in this entry centrally embody the law-first axiom's power: the anti-predation axiom requires increment only come from efficiency optimization, not predating stock; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires efficiency-improvement results under the conservation framework be shared by Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires the law network continuously evolve to support cosmic uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom restrains the transcended one from disturbing others while fine-tuning the law network; the law-first axiom itself is the meta-rule for all this — setting self-consistency above plot convenience.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Energy Conservation's Three-Layer Position in the Law Network

Energy conservation is not an isolated physical law; it occupies three layers in Heng Yuan Zhou's law network:

| Layer | Conservation Position | Meaning | May the Transcended One Touch It |
|-------|----------------------|---------|-----------------------------------|
| Underlying hard constraint | Total energy neither arises nor perishes | The cosmos' total energy is constant, may not be created or destroyed | Absolutely may not be touched |
| Middle-layer rule | Transformation efficiency has an upper limit | Loss during energy transformation is inevitable, but optimizable | May optimize efficiency, may not break through the physical upper limit |
| Upper-layer engineering | Entropy reduction may be industrialized | Through life's ordered activity, the cosmos' overall order may rise | The transcended one's core working surface |

The three-layer structure's key: **underlying does not move, middle may be optimized, upper may be built.** The transcended one may not cheat at the underlying level (creation out of nothing), but he may at the middle push efficiency to the theoretical limit, and at the upper take entropy-reduction engineering to industrial scale. This is conservation and evolution's unity — conservation not broken, but usable energy within the conservation framework grows infinitely.

## II. How the Law Network Bears Conservation: The Anchor's "Three Cores Two Rings"

Per Anchor Structure and Levels (Document 20), the anchor's "three cores two rings" architecture is energy conservation's technical implementation at the infrastructure layer:

- **Law core**: stores conservation law's operating parameters. The law core's calibration ring continuously works, ensuring conservation parameters do not drift — once drifting, energy transformation will show systematic deviation.
- **Energy core**: responsible for energy reception, transformation, output, strictly running per conservation formula: output energy + loss energy = input energy. The energy core's multi-type compatibility ability maximizes energy utilization without breaking conservation.
- **Information core**: records every energy transformation's input, output, loss data, for Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision audit.

The transcended one's law-anchor authority is essentially being authorized to participate in the law core's calibration and fine-tuning. This authority must be granted by multi-dimensional assessment (power 40% / heart-nature 25% / contribution 20% / responsibility 15%) precisely because: **if a person may modify conservation parameters, he could create or destroy energy out of nothing — this is cosmos-level danger.** So the authority's grant threshold is extremely high, supervision extremely strict, and it may be withdrawn at any time.

## III. How Is "Infinitely Growing Within the Conservation Framework" Possible

This is this entry's core theoretical question. Conservation means total energy unchanged; then how can civilization develop infinitely? Heng Yuan Zhou's answer has three layers:

1. **Usable energy ≠ total energy**: of the cosmos' total energy, the vast majority exists as high-entropy form (background radiation, disorderly dissipation), unusable by life. The transcended one through entropy-reduction engineering converts this "dormant energy" into usable energy — total energy unchanged, but the usable-energy pool may infinitely expand.
2. **Efficiency improvement has no upper limit**: although single transformation efficiency has a theoretical upper limit, the law network itself is continuously evolving — new transformation paths are discovered, new resonance modes established, new energy types tamed. Every law-network optimization lets the same input produce more usable energy.
3. **Life participation is a multiplier**: Document 15 already demonstrates that a lifeless cosmos' energy cycle is one-way decay (entropy increase); when a large amount of life through symbiotic cultivation participates in the cycle, life's ordered activity may offset or even reverse entropy increase. The transcended one's job is amplifying this multiplier — making Myriad Ranks' ordered activity contribute more and more to the cosmos' whole.

Three layers together: **total energy conserved, but usable energy, utilization efficiency, and order all three dimensions may infinitely grow.** This is "conservation and law-network unity"'s true meaning.

## IV. Law-Level Defense Against "Book-Eating/Stale-Ending Risk"

The law-first axiom requires "never eat books, never go stale." The unified structure of energy conservation and the law network is itself defense against book-eating risk:

- **Underlying hard constraint unchanged**: no matter how plot develops, total energy neither arises nor perishes — this is written dead in the law core; any author-style "protagonist suddenly breaks conservation" is directly vetoed by the law network.
- **Middle-layer rules may evolve but may not jump**: transformation-efficiency improvement must be achieved stepwise through technical iteration, may not suddenly jump from 30% to 90% — Multiplication Fruit technology's hundredfold amplification is "efficiency amplification," not "skipping intermediate steps."
- **Upper-layer engineering may infinitely expand**: new cosmos, new plane, new energy-type development may proceed infinitely, not getting stuck because "energy is insufficient."

This structure ensures: the worldview may infinitely expand without collapsing. The author (and transcended one) may open new content in any direction, but will never hit the dead wall of "conservation broken."
