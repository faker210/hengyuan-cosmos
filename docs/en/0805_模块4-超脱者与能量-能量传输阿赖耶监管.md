# Entry 805 | Transcended Ones and Energy | Alaya Supervision of Energy Transmission

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 21, 28, 19

## Introduction: wherever energy flows, supervision must follow

The transcended one dispatches energy at the hyper-multiverse scale; daily energy flow handled is measured in planetary annual output. Energy transmission of this scale, without supervision, is a new predation risk — strong ones could entirely under the name of "energy dispatch" actually do "energy interception." Heng Yuan Zhou's solution: **Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision is embedded throughout every energy-transmission channel**, letting supervision follow wherever energy flows.

Six top-level axioms in this entry directly land as supervision mechanisms: the anti-predation axiom is supervision's legislative purpose — preventing energy transmission from becoming a predatory channel; the republican axiom of Myriad Races is supervision's jurisprudential source — energy is Myriad Ranks' public wealth, dispatch must be known to Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom is supervision's efficiency goal — supervise without obstructing uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom is supervision's operational norm — observe first then limited-interfere, only backflow knowledge not disturb causality; the law-first axiom ensures supervision rules themselves are law-level definitions, not bypassable by any transcended one.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article focuses: in cross-plane, cross-cosmic-structure energy transmission, how Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision concretely operates.

## I. Energy Transmission's Three Channels and Supervision Levels

Per Cross-Plane Anchor Connection (Document 28), energy-transmission channels divide into three types, supervision levels differ accordingly:

| Channel Type | Stability | Bandwidth | Supervision Level | Supervision Focus |
|--------------|-----------|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Permanent trunk connection | Permanently stable | Cosmic-grade torrent | Cosmic origin anchor + Alaya Network + Myriad-Rank Council | Flow quota, origin audit, destination approval |
| Temporary branch connection | Days to months | Star-sector grade | Plane core anchor + Anchor Supervisory Committee | Task report, usage duration, shutdown confirmation |
| Probe micro-connection | Interruptible at any time | Extremely low | Automated probes + Alaya Network auto-alert | Abnormal fluctuation, unauthorized energy extraction |

Three channels' supervision principle is identical: **flow transparent, origin traceable, destination approved, abnormal alarms.** The difference is only in approval level and granularity — trunk channels need cosmic-level approval, branches need plane-level approval, micro-connections are auto-managed by the system.

## II. Alaya Supervision's Five Technical Links

Alaya Network's supervision of energy transmission is not after-the-fact audit, but **real-time embedded in the transmission link**. Five technical links:

1. **Source tagging**: the moment energy leaves the source plane/source anchor, Alaya Network auto-stamps an origin marker — which anchor, which plane, which cultivator group contributed, at what time. The marker is carried throughout the energy flow, non-erasable.
2. **Path tracing**: as energy flows through each level of anchor, path nodes auto-record. If energy goes from Plane A to Plane B, which galaxy hub anchors and which star-sector nodes it passes through mid-way, the whole process leaves traces.
3. **Destination approval**: after energy reaches the destination, the receiver must state the use — is it cultivation use, law-engineering power supply, or ecological restoration. Unappoved energy cannot be drawn from the channel; it auto-backflows.
4. **Loss audit**: loss rate during transmission is computed in real time. If the loss rate is abnormally high, it may mean someone is intercepting energy in the channel — the system auto-alerts.
5. **Closed-loop confirmation**: after energy use completes, the user submits a usage report; Alaya Network compares actual usage with approved usage, confirming the closed loop. Deviation exceeds threshold, an Anchor Supervisory Committee investigation launches.

Five links together constitute "source—path—destination—loss—closed loop" whole-link supervision. The transcended one may dispatch enormous energy, but he cannot hide the energy's origin, cannot intercept the middle segment, cannot false-report the energy's destination.

## III. Cross-Plane Energy Transmission's Causal Self-Discipline Constraints

Energy transmission is not only a physical problem, but also a causal problem. The transcended one moves energy from one plane to another, changing two planes' energy ecology, further affecting the cultivation trajectories of Myriad-Rank beings in both planes — this is causal disturbance.

The causal self-discipline axiom requires: cross-plane energy transmission must follow three rules:

- **Observe before interfere**: before transmission must observe both planes' energy-ecology baseline through Alaya Network, assessing impact scope and degree after transmission;
- **Only backflow knowledge, do not disturb causality**: if the transmission purpose is to help a weak plane, prioritize transmitting knowledge of "how to produce energy yourself" (cultivation methods, technical schemes), rather than direct transfusion — direct transfusion makes the helped plane dependent, and long-term causality is worse;
- **Reporting system**: all cross-plane energy transmission must be reported to the Anchor Supervisory Committee; major transmissions (exceeding 10% of plane-level bandwidth) require Myriad-Rank Republican Council approval.

These three rules turn energy transmission from "strong ones dispatching resources at will" into "Myriad Ranks informed, scientifically assessed, limited-interference" civilizational engineering.

## IV. Supervision and Efficiency's Balance: Why Stricter Is Not Always Better

Some worry: such strict supervision will it slow energy-transmission efficiency? Heng Yuan Zhou's design principle is **supervision does not slow, transparency does not interfere**:

- Energy transmission's physical channel itself is unaffected by supervision — tagging, tracing, and audit are all information-layer operations, not occupying energy bandwidth;
- The approval mechanism is achieved through automated pre-approval — transmissions within regular quotas auto-pass, only over-quota or abnormal patterns trigger manual approval;
- Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's design is "observational" rather than "approval-type" — it mainly records and warns, not blocks normal energy dispatch.

This design ensures: supervision's existence does not lower energy-transmission efficiency, but supervision's deterrence makes anyone trying to use transmission channels for predation have nowhere to hide.

Alaya supervision of energy transmission is the causal self-discipline axiom's technicalized fulfillment at the energy dimension.
