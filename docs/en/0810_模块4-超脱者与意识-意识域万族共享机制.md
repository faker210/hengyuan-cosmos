# Entry 810 | Transcended Ones and Consciousness | The Consciousness Domain's Myriad-Rank Sharing Mechanism

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 21, 08, 05

## Introduction: the consciousness domain is not the strong's private garden, but Myriad Ranks' public park

The consciousness domain, this public space, if dominated by the strong, easily becomes the strong's private garden — they build whatever they want in it; low-order cultivators may only eavesdrop in corners. Heng Yuan Zhou rejects this trend. In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's orthodox framework, **the consciousness domain is Myriad Ranks' public park, not the strong's private garden.** The transcended one's role in the consciousness domain is the park's gardener and administrator — he maintains the park's facilities, cleans its garbage, ensures it is open to all beings, but he may not turn the park into his own backyard.

Six top-level axioms in this entry directly land as sharing mechanisms: the anti-predation axiom requires knowledge and space in the consciousness domain not be monopolized; the republican axiom of Myriad Races is the Myriad-Rank sharing mechanism's jurisprudential source; the collective ascension axiom requires sharing's ultimate purpose be pushing Myriad Ranks' consciousness jointly uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires sharing's process not disturb others' consciousness privacy; the law-first axiom requires the sharing mechanism itself be a law-level definition, not modifiable by any strong one.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article focuses: how the consciousness domain's Myriad-Rank sharing mechanism concretely operates, and what duties the transcended one bears in it.

## I. Consciousness-Domain Sharing's Five-Layer Spatial Structure

The consciousness domain is not a flat big room, but a layered shared space. Per Symbiotic-Network Operating Principles (Document 21), sharing from low to high divides into five layers:

| Layer | Space Name | Open Content | Open To |
|-------|-----------|--------------|---------|
| Layer 1 | Public Plaza | Basic cultivation information, public announcements, introductory guidance | All accessors |
| Layer 2 | Myriad-Rank Study | Past cultivators' public enlightenment records and cultivation insights | All accessors (after basic certification) |
| Layer 3 | Resonance Hall | Group consciousness resonance space, collective cultivation ground | Cultivator groups under mentor guidance |
| Layer 4 | Law Corridor | Law network's consciousness mapping, law-interaction experiment ground | Law Realm and above cultivators |
| Layer 5 | Origin Council Chamber | Cosmic-grade consciousness decision space, transcended-one deliberation ground | Plane-sovereign strong ones and above |

Five-layer space's design principle is **progressive opening**: low-threshold spaces open to all beings; high-threshold spaces gradually unlock as cultivators' realms rise. This is not discrimination, but protection — if low-order cultivators directly enter the Layer-5 space, their consciousness cannot bear that information density and will instead be injured. Progressive opening is both an admission system and a safety system.

## II. Myriad-Rank Sharing's Three Core Rules

The consciousness domain's Myriad-Rank sharing is not anarchy of "anyone may enter and leave at will," but has three core rules:

1. **Knowledge fully open**: enlightenment records, cultivation insights, and law-research results in the consciousness domain, once made public by their creator, permanently belong to Myriad Ranks' shared resources. The creator may choose not to make it public (this is his freedom), but once public, he may not restrict others' learning on the grounds of "my intellectual property" — the Great Dao is fully open at all levels, no private hoarding, no monopoly.
2. **Privacy inviolable**: corresponding to knowledge fully open is strict privacy protection. An individual's private memories, emotional experiences, and private thinking never enter the shared space. When Alaya Network collects consciousness data, it follows the minimum-necessary principle — only collecting evolution-related data, not touching private domains.
3. **Participation voluntary**: accessing the consciousness domain, participating in resonance, using shared resources, all voluntary. Any individual may choose not to access the consciousness domain at all, and will not thereby be discriminated against or deprived of evolutionary rights — not accessing the public network only loses one acceleration channel; the road is still open.

Three rules together: **what is open is knowledge and tools; what is protected is privacy and freedom.** This is the republican axiom of Myriad Races' precise expression in the consciousness domain.

## III. The Transcended One's Four Maintenance Duties in the Sharing Mechanism

The transcended one, as the consciousness domain's senior administrator, bears four maintenance duties:

- **Space-facility maintenance**: maintain the consciousness domain's five-layer space infrastructure — the Public Plaza's guidance system, the Myriad-Rank Study's retrieval system, the Resonance Hall's resonance calibration, the Law Corridor's experiment safety, the Origin Council Chamber's deliberation rules. When facilities break, repair; when insufficient, expand.
- **Space-order maintenance**: maintain exchange order in shared spaces — preventing harassment, manipulation, ideological indoctrination, and similar behaviors in the consciousness domain. The transcended one does not review viewpoints themselves (viewpoint freedom is Myriad-Rank sharing's core), but he stops behavior forcing others to accept viewpoints through consciousness-attack means.
- **Resource fair allocation**: ensure shared resources in the consciousness domain (such as resonance time, law-experiment space, archive-query quotas) are fairly allocated among Myriad Ranks — not allocating more because some race is strong, not giving less because some race is weak.
- **New-space opening**: when existing shared spaces are insufficient (such as Myriad-Rank cultivator numbers growing, new races accessing), the transcended one is responsible for opening new shared spaces in the consciousness domain, ensuring access capacity does not become an evolution bottleneck.

## IV. How the Sharing Mechanism Feeds Back the Transcended One Himself

The consciousness domain's Myriad-Rank sharing is not the transcended one's one-way giving. The sharing mechanism itself also feeds back the transcended one:

- **Consciousness diversity nourishment**: unique enlightenment, unique perspectives, and unique experiences Myriad Ranks contribute in the consciousness domain provide the transcended one's consciousness with extremely rich nourishment. A transcended one only cultivating in his own consciousness will see his consciousness dimension gradually atrophy; a transcended one fully sharing with Myriad Ranks' consciousness will see his consciousness continuously grow due to contact with infinite diversity.
- **Collective-intelligence calibration**: judgments and decisions the transcended one makes in the consciousness domain receive Myriad Ranks' consciousness feedback. Myriad Ranks' diverse perspectives help the transcended one avoid the "strong-one blind spot" — when his thinking because standing too high neglects certain details, Myriad Ranks' voice pulls him back.
- **Resonance energy back-transfusion**: Myriad Ranks' collective cultivation resonance in the consciousness domain back-transfuses to the transcended one through the shared network — this is a consciousness-dimension energy feedback, keeping the transcended one's consciousness alive and stable.

This two-way feedback is the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle's embodiment at the consciousness dimension: the transcended one maintains the shared space; the shared space nourishes the transcended one. Not one-way service, but two-way win-win.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: knowledge fully open avoids repeated waste of "every cultivator groping from scratch"; the Myriad-Rank Study's enlightenment records let later-comers stand on predecessors' shoulders
- **Anti-predation**: three core rules' "privacy inviolable" and "participation voluntary" institutionally seal consciousness-dimension predation channels in the consciousness domain
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system, strong ones privately hoard cultivation manuals and knowledge as tools to oppress the weak; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended one fully opens enlightenment results as Myriad Ranks' evolutionary public resources
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the consciousness domain's five-layer space opens to all Myriad-Rank five-lineage consciousness beings; each clan's consciousness cultural tradition is equally displayed in the shared space
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: shared-space layering rules, admission standards, and privacy-protection mechanisms are all publicly queryable; Myriad Ranks may supervise whether the sharing mechanism is executed fairly

- Terminology alignment: the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is this batch's standard realm appellation; the transcended one's civilizational weight dynamically adjusts with post performance; Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision monitors operation compliance throughout; exercise of law-anchor authority is strictly constrained by four-dimensional assessment and democratic procedure.
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The consciousness domain's Myriad-Rank sharing mechanism is the republican axiom of Myriad Races' institutionalized fulfillment at the consciousness dimension. Five-layer spaces progressively open; three rules guard boundaries; four duties maintain operation; two-way feedback continuously wins. The transcended one's role in this mechanism is not the consciousness domain's master, but its gardener — he plants flowers, waters, removes pests, and builds roads, but all knowledge in the garden belongs to Myriad Ranks, and all beings have the right to enter. When the consciousness domain truly becomes Myriad Ranks' public park rather than the strong's private garden, Myriad Ranks' consciousness may freely grow and jointly uplift in it.
