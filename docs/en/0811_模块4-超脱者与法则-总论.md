# Entry 811 | Transcended Ones and Laws · Overview — The Transcended One's Law Authority

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 20, 17, 04

## Introduction: law authority is not a scepter, but the post duty of maintaining the law network

The old system's imagination of "law authority" is: the strong cultivate to a certain level, and may "control laws" — wind, fire, water, thunder, use them however he wants. This imagination treats laws as the strong's private toys. Heng Yuan Zhou completely negates this definition. In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's orthodox framework, **law authority is not a scepter, but the post duty of maintaining the law network, this public infrastructure.** The transcended one gaining law-anchor authority is not to let him manipulate laws at will, but to let him bear the civilization-level project of law-network maintenance.

Six top-level axioms all take effect in this entry: the anti-predation axiom requires law authority never be privatized or monopolized; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires law authority's grant and withdrawal accept Myriad-Rank supervision; the collective ascension axiom requires law authority's exercise ultimately serve the cosmic law network's overall evolution; the causal self-discipline axiom requires any law-level operation strictly report, observe first then limited-interfere; the law-first axiom itself is the meta-rule for all this — laws first, never eat books, setting self-consistency above plot convenience.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article, as the "Transcended Ones and Laws" sub-series overview, sorts out the transcended one's law-authority panorama: what is law-anchor authority, how many levels, how granted, how withdrawn.

## I. What Is Law-Anchor Authority

**Law-anchor authority** is Heng Yuan Zhou's formal authorization of "who is qualified to participate in law-network maintenance." This term's core is in "anchor" — not "controlling" laws, but "anchoring" law parameters. The law network itself naturally exists in the cosmos; the transcended one may not create or abolish laws, but he may:

- Anchor laws' operating parameters at specific coordinates (through the anchor's law core);
- Fine-tune law parameters' offset within legal ranges;
- Monitor law parameters' drift and calibrate;
- Weave new law-connection channels in the law network.

Note: all of the above are "maintenance" and "fine-tuning," not "creation" and "abolition." The law network's fundamental laws (such as energy conservation) may not be modified — this is the law-first axiom's bottom line. The transcended one works within this bottom line.

## II. Law Authority's Five-Level Grading

Per Anchor Structure and Levels (Document 20) and Plane-Sovereign Strong-One Standards (Document 17), law-anchor authority is graded by the anchor five-level system:

| Authority Level | Corresponding Anchor Level | Operable Range | Grant Condition |
|-----------------|----------------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Level 1 | Planetary access anchor | Planetary-grade law-parameter calibration | Law Realm + ethics review passed |
| Level 2 | Galaxy node anchor | Galaxy-grade law-connection maintenance | Dao Origin Realm + contribution value met |
| Level 3 | Star-sector hub anchor | Star-sector-grade law-parameter fine-tuning | Dao Integration Realm + responsibility assessment passed |
| Level 4 | Plane core anchor | Plane-level law-network maintenance | Boundary-Breaking Realm + four-dimensional assessment all met |
| Level 5 | Cosmic origin anchor | Hyper-multiverse law-network trunk | Origin to Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm + Myriad-Rank Council approval |

The transcended one (Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm) holds Level-5 law-anchor authority — the highest grade. This means he may participate in the cosmic origin anchor's law-core maintenance, fine-tuning law parameters at the hyper-multiverse scale. But this level's grant threshold is also highest: not only power met, but must pass heart-nature, contribution, responsibility three-dimensional comprehensive assessment, finally approved by the Myriad-Rank Republican Council.

## III. Authority Grant's Four-Dimensional Assessment

Per Document 17's plane-sovereign strong-one four-dimensional assessment system, law-authority grant strictly follows four-dimensional assessment:

- **Power dimension (40%)**: the realm must reach the corresponding level, able to stably mobilize law force;
- **Heart-nature dimension (25%)**: pass the Anchor Court ethics review, confirming a firm anti-predation stance and deep symbiotic conviction;
- **Contribution dimension (20%)**: contribution value reaches the civilization's top-20% level, with at least one civilization-level contribution record;
- **Responsibility dimension (15%)**: willing and able to bear the public responsibility of law-network maintenance.

All four dimensions met before grant. Any one dimension substandard, authority not granted — no matter how strong the power. This is "both virtue and ability, virtue first" implemented on law authority. A cultivator with strong power but improper heart-nature, if given law-anchor authority, equals giving him the key to modifying the cosmos' underlying rules — this is a risk Heng Yuan Zhou absolutely does not allow.

## IV. Authority's Supervision and Withdrawal

Law-anchor authority is not lifelong. It is under triple supervision and may be withdrawn at any time:

1. **Daily supervision**: Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision real-time monitors the authority holder's operation records; abnormal operation auto-alerts;
2. **Periodic audit**: the Anchor Supervisory Committee audits authority use once per standard cycle, checking whether operations are compliant;
3. **Myriad-Rank deliberation**: the Myriad-Rank Republican Council periodically deliberates the authority holder's civilizational weight — weight drops below threshold, authority auto-downgrades.

Withdrawal mechanisms include: downgrade (from Level 5 to Level 4), suspension (suspend authority use, under investigation), permanent deprivation (confirmed serious violation, permanently withdraw authority and ban reapplication). Withdrawal is not punitive — it is protective, preventing someone no longer suitable to hold high authority from continuing to operate the law network.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: law authority is graded-granted, avoiding energy waste and risk caused by low-order cultivators forcefully operating laws without qualifications
- **Anti-predation**: the four-dimensional assessment's heart-nature dimension's 25% weight seals predatory strong ones' channel to obtain law authority at the admission stage
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system, strong ones directly "control laws" by protagonist aura, no assessment no supervision; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended one obtains law-anchor authority through four-dimensional assessment, under triple supervision throughout
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: law-authority grant standards are open and transparent to all Myriad-Rank cultivators; each clan's cultivators reaching conditions may apply, no racial threshold
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: law-authority grading standards, assessment procedures, supervision mechanisms, and withdrawal rules are all queryable in the Selfless Dao public library; Myriad Ranks may supervise the fairness of the authority system

- Terminology alignment: the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle ensures energy and knowledge circulate perpetually among Myriad Ranks.
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The transcended one's law authority is essentially post authorization for the civilization-level project of "maintaining the law network." It is not a scepter, but a tool; not privilege, but responsibility. What the transcended one may do holding Level-5 law-anchor authority is anchor parameters, fine-tune and calibrate, monitor drift, weave connections — what he may not do is create laws, abolish laws, privatize the law network. The larger the authority, the stricter the supervision; the more contributions, the more stable the authority. This is "law authority" at the hyper-multiverse realm's orthodox definition: not letting you rule laws, but letting you guard laws.
