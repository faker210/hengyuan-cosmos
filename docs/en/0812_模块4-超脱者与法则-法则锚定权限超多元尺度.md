# Entry 812 | Transcended Ones and Laws | The Hyper-Multiverse Scale of Law-Anchor Authority

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 20, 28, 17

## Introduction: from one planet to the multiverse — law anchoring's scale leap

Law-anchor authority is not only a matter of "size," but also of "scale." A cultivator calibrating law parameters on a planetary anchor and a transcended one fine-tuning the hyper-multiverse law network on a cosmic origin anchor operate on entirely different magnitudes in object operated, complexity faced, and risk borne. This article specifically discusses this **scale problem**: when law-anchor authority leaps from the planetary scale to the hyper-multiverse scale, what does the transcended one face, and where is his authority boundary.

Six top-level axioms run through this entry: the anti-predation axiom requires the larger the scale the less it may be privatized — the hyper-multiverse-scale law network belongs to all Myriad Ranks; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires hyper-multiverse-scale law operations be known to Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires the scale leap serve the cosmos' overall uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires hyper-multiverse-scale law operations follow the strictest reporting approval; the law-first axiom ensures law self-consistency during the scale leap is not broken.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Five Scale Layers of Law-Anchoring Operation

Law-anchoring operation by spatial scale divides into five layers; each layer's operation content and risk level differ:

| Scale Layer | Operation Space | Typical Operation | Risk Level |
|-------------|-----------------|-------------------|------------|
| Planetary scale | Single planet's surface and near-earth | Planetary law-parameter calibration, local law-connection maintenance | Low |
| Galaxy scale | Single galaxy range | Galaxy law-network connection, galaxy-grade law-drift correction | Low-mid |
| Star-sector scale | Cross-galaxy range | Star-sector law-parameter fine-tuning, star-sector law-consistency calibration | Mid |
| Plane scale | Single plane's whole domain | Plane law-network maintenance, plane-law compatibility adjustment | High |
| Hyper-multiverse scale | Cross-plane, cross-cosmic-structure | Hyper-multiverse law-network trunk calibration, new-cosmic-structure law weaving | Extreme |

The first four scales may be operated by plane-sovereign strong ones (Origin Realm); the fifth scale — hyper-multiverse scale — may only be participated in by transcended ones entering the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm. This is not because the hyper-multiverse scale needs stronger "power," but because it needs higher **system understanding**: the transcended one must simultaneously understand multiple planes' law differences, cross-plane law-compatibility patterns, and the overall architecture of cosmic-structure laws.

## II. Hyper-Multiverse-Scale Operation's Three Special Challenges

Hyper-multiverse-scale law anchoring has three special challenges over the first four scales:

1. **Cross-plane law compatibility**: different planes' law parameters naturally differ (Document 28 already demonstrates that plane barriers consist of law differences). When the transcended one operates the law network at hyper-multiverse scale, he may not forcibly unify each plane's law parameters — that would destroy each plane's law uniqueness. His work is **building compatibility channels amid differences**, letting planes of different law systems interconnect while keeping their own law character.
2. **Causal threads' cross-plane extension**: adjusting one law parameter at planetary scale has limited causal impact range. But at hyper-multiverse scale, a law fine-tuning's causal impact may span multiple planes — the transcended one must anticipate this cross-plane causal thread's trend and follow the causal self-discipline axiom for the strictest reporting.
3. **Multi-cosmic-structure parallel maintenance**: Heng Yuan Zhou is not a single cosmos, but a multiverse structure. When the transcended one operates at hyper-multiverse scale, he must simultaneously maintain law-network consistency across multiple cosmic structures — may not attend to one and neglect the other.

Three challenges' common point: **the larger the scale, the less operations may be "taken for granted," the more systematic, prudent, and fully argued engineering-style operations are needed.**

## III. Hyper-Multiverse-Scale Operation's Authority Boundary

The transcended one has law-anchor authority at hyper-multiverse scale, but this authority has clear boundaries. What he may and may not do:

| May Do | May Not Do |
|--------|------------|
| Calibrate hyper-multiverse law-network parameter drift | Modify the cosmos' fundamental laws (such as energy conservation) |
| Weave cross-plane law-compatibility channels | Abolish any plane's unique law system |
| Lay law skeletons in new cosmic structures | Force-unify one plane's law parameters to other planes |
| Monitor the hyper-multiverse law network's global health | Use law operations for personal gain |
| Initiate law-revision proposals (follow democratic procedure) | Privately modify laws without Myriad-Rank approval |

This boundary's core logic: **the hyper-multiverse-scale law network is Myriad Ranks' common infrastructure; the transcended one is the maintainer, not the owner.** He may repair, adjust, expand, but may not change the fundamentals, may not dismantle others', may not use it privately.

## IV. Hyper-Multiverse-Scale Operation's Approval Procedure

Because hyper-multiverse-scale law operations carry extremely high risk, the approval procedure is also strictest:

1. **Proposal stage**: the transcended one submits an operation proposal, including operation objective, technical scheme, projected impact scope, risk assessment;
2. **Quest Domain simulation review**: the proposal undergoes full-dimensional deduction in the Quest Sediment Domain — deducing operation impact under different scenarios, identifying potential risks;
3. **Myriad-Rank Republican Council review**: Myriad-Rank representatives review the proposal, focusing on whether it is compatible with Myriad-Rank interests, whether hidden predatory risk exists;
4. **Pilot operation**: after review passes, first pilot in a small range, verifying operation effects match the simulation deduction;
5. **Full promotion**: after the pilot passes, the operation formally implements, monitored throughout by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision.

Five-step approval ensures: hyper-multiverse-scale law operation is not one transcended one's personal will, but the result of Myriad Ranks' common decision.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: hyper-multiverse-scale operation's pre-operation simulation review avoids energy waste from "taken-for-granted operations causing large-area law-network failure"
- **Anti-predation**: the authority-boundary table seals at the law level the channel of the transcended one using hyper-multiverse authority for private gain
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system, strong ones after leveling up directly "control multiverse laws," no boundary no approval; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended one at hyper-multiverse scale follows five-step approval for every operation
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: hyper-multiverse-scale law-compatibility channels open to all Myriad-Rank planes and all races, not excluding any plane from the multiverse law network
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: five scale layers, three special challenges, authority boundary, and approval procedure are all publicly queryable; Myriad Ranks may supervise the legitimacy of hyper-multiverse law operations

- Terminology alignment: the transcended one's civilizational weight dynamically adjusts with post performance; the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle ensures energy and knowledge circulate perpetually among Myriad Ranks.
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Law-anchor authority's hyper-multiverse scale is the transcended one's grandest, most delicate, and most constrained working surface. The operation space he may touch spans planes and cosmic structures, but the bottom line he may not touch is equally clear: do not modify fundamental laws, do not destroy plane character, do not pursue private gain, do not bypass Myriad-Rank approval. The larger the scale, the stricter the boundary; the stronger the ability, the more elaborate the procedure. This is law-anchor authority's orthodox positioning at the hyper-multiverse realm: **you may touch the most things, but you may not touch what should not be touched.**
