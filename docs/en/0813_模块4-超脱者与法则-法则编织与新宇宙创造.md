# Entry 813 | Transcended Ones and Laws | Law Weaving and New-Cosmos Creation

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 13, 14, 28

## Introduction: weaving laws is not being a creator god, but being a cosmic-scale engineer

The old system's imagination of "creating a cosmos" is: the strongest one punches apart the old world, then willfully kneads a new one — he is the god of the new world; all beings in the world he creates are profoundly grateful. This imagination turns "creating a cosmos" into the highest stage for personal cult. Heng Yuan Zhou completely negates this definition. In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's orthodox framework, **law weaving and new-cosmos creation are not a god's personal genesis, but a cosmic-scale project with Myriad Ranks' common participation.** The transcended one is this project's chief engineer, not the creator god; the law skeleton he weaves serves Myriad Ranks' habitation and evolution, not his personal genesis thrill.

Six top-level axioms centrally embody in this entry: the anti-predation axiom requires new-cosmos creation not consume the old cosmos' stock resources; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the new-cosmos design scheme have Myriad-Rank participation and results shared by Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires new-cosmos creation serve Myriad Ranks' common uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the new and old cosmos' causal boundary be strictly isolated; the law-first axiom requires the new cosmos' law system itself be self-consistent and not conflict with the existing law network.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article focuses: the transcended one's concrete engineering role in law weaving and new-cosmos creation.

## I. New-Cosmos Creation's Four Engineering Stages

Per Spatial-Law Dispatch System Detailed (Document 13) and Cultivation-Realm Unified Division (Document 14), new-cosmos creation is not done in one step, but four engineering stages:

| Stage | Name | Core Work | Participating Subjects |
|-------|------|-----------|------------------------|
| Stage 1 | Site selection and exploration | Explore spacetime coordinates suitable for opening a new cosmos within the multiverse structure | Transcended one + spatial-law dispatch team |
| Stage 2 | Law-skeleton weaving | On selected coordinates weave the new cosmos' law underlying architecture | Transcended one (Level-5 law-anchor authority) + multi-plane sovereign strong ones |
| Stage 3 | Energy-circuit laying | On the law skeleton lay energy-cycle channels, establishing the new cosmos' energy base | Transcended one + energy-engineering team |
| Stage 4 | Ecology and consciousness access | Introduce life ecology, establish consciousness-domain interfaces, access Alaya Network | Myriad-Rank representatives + transcended one |

Four stages' key: **the transcended one leads stages 2 and 3's law engineering, but stage 4 must have Myriad-Rank participation.** The new cosmos is not the transcended one's private estate; it is a new home built for Myriad Ranks — Myriad Ranks have the right to participate in design, the right to inhabit, the right to evolve in it their own way.

## II. Law Weaving's Engineering Principle

Law weaving is new-cosmos creation's most core and delicate link. It is not "write whatever law you think of," but strict engineering design:

- **Self-consistency check**: every law in the new cosmos must not contradict other laws. If Law A says "energy conservation," Law B may not allow "creation out of nothing" — the law network's interior must be logically self-consistent. This check is completed through the deduction-iteration system in Quest Sediment Domain simulation review.
- **Compatibility reservation**: the new cosmos' law skeleton must reserve compatibility interfaces with the existing multiverse law network — in the future the new cosmos will access the cross-plane anchor network; incompatible laws cannot connect.
- **Myriad-Rank adaptability**: law design must consider Myriad Ranks' different life forms' survival needs — carbon-based life needs material plane physical laws; energy-condensed clans need energy-layer law support; consciousness-information clans need consciousness-domain law interfaces. Law weaving is not designed only for humankind.
- **Scalability**: the law skeleton may not be written dead — it must leave room, letting the new cosmos in the future upgrade law parameters as Myriad Ranks evolve, not getting stuck in civilizational development because "laws are written dead."

Four design requirements together: **law weaving is engineering design, not artistic creation.** What it pursues is not "how beautiful the laws I designed are," but "can Myriad Ranks live and evolve well inside."

## III. The New Cosmos and Old Cosmos' Causal Isolation

New-cosmos creation has an extremely critical causal self-discipline requirement: **a strict causal isolation layer must be established between the new and old cosmos.**

The reason is simple: if there is no causal isolation between the new and old cosmos, then everything happening in the new cosmos will through causal threads affect the old cosmos. A cultivator in the new cosmos falling into qi deviation, a plane's laws collapsing — if these causal events conduct back to the old cosmos, they will cause unpredictable interference to Myriad Ranks' survival in the old cosmos.

Causal isolation layer design:

- **Spacetime isolation**: the new cosmos' spacetime coordinates are completely independent of the old cosmos; between them only approved anchor channels connect.
- **Law isolation**: the new cosmos' law system and the old cosmos' law system are disconnected at the isolation layer, not directly interacting.
- **Energy isolation**: the new cosmos' energy cycle closes inside the new cosmos, not sharing an energy pool with the old cosmos (cross-plane energy dispatch goes through anchor channels, under strict supervision).
- **Consciousness isolation**: the new cosmos' consciousness domain and the old cosmos' consciousness domain connect through Alaya Network's isolation buffer zone, not directly merging.

The isolation layer ensures: the new cosmos is Myriad Ranks' new home, not the old cosmos' risk-transfer site. Problems in the new cosmos are solved inside the new cosmos; the old cosmos is not causally polluted by the new.

## IV. The New Cosmos' Myriad-Rank Co-Governance Mechanism

After new-cosmos creation completes, its governance power belongs to no single transcended one, but to the Myriad-Rank republican system:

- **New-Cosmos Council**: representatives of each clan inhabiting the new cosmos form a council, participating in the new cosmos' governance.
- **Anchor-network access**: the new cosmos accesses Alaya Network per standard procedure, accepting the same multi-layer supervision as the old cosmos.
- **Law-revision right**: the new cosmos' law-parameter revision follows Myriad-Rank democratic procedure (see entry 814), not decided by the transcended one alone.
- **Open admission**: the new cosmos opens admission to all Myriad-Rank life forms — cultivators wanting to move into the new cosmos apply per standard procedure.

This co-governance mechanism ensures: after the new cosmos is created, it is not the transcended one's "genesis souvenir," but a new home Myriad Ranks jointly use.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: law weaving's pre-operation self-consistency check and simulation review avoid the enormous energy waste of "unreasonable law design causing new-cosmos reconstruction"
- **Anti-predation**: new-cosmos creation uses increment-developed spacetime coordinates and energy circuits, not consuming the old cosmos' stock resources
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system, strong ones after creating a new world become its creator god; all beings are grateful; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended one after weaving the law skeleton returns it to Myriad-Rank co-governance; he is only the chief engineer
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: law weaving's Myriad-Rank adaptability requirement considers all five-lineage life forms' survival needs; the new cosmos opens admission to all races
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: four engineering stages, law-weaving four requirements, causal-isolation-layer design, and co-governance mechanism are all publicly queryable; Myriad Ranks may supervise the new-cosmos project

- Terminology alignment: the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is this batch's standard realm appellation; the transcended one's civilizational weight dynamically adjusts with post performance; Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision monitors operation compliance throughout; the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle ensures energy and knowledge circulate perpetually among Myriad Ranks.
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Law weaving and new-cosmos creation in Heng Yuan Zhou is not "a creator god's personal show," but a cosmic-scale project with Myriad Ranks' common participation. What the transcended one does is the chief engineer's work — he explores coordinates, weaves law skeletons, lays energy circuits, but the new-cosmos design scheme has Myriad-Rank participation, habitation eligibility is equal for Myriad Ranks, governance power is co-governed by Myriad Ranks. What he weaves is not his own god-kingdom, but Myriad Ranks' new home. This is "genesis" at the hyper-multiverse realm's orthodox definition: not that you created the world then ruled it, but that you created the world for Myriad Ranks then returned it to them.
