# Entry 814 | Transcended Ones and Laws | The Democratic Procedure of Law Revision

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 03, 40, 29

## Introduction: laws may not be privately modified by the strong — this is Heng Yuan Zhou's ultimate negation of "god-modifies-law"

One of the old system's greatest institutional loopholes is that laws may be modified at will by the strongest. The protagonist cultivates to the peak, feels a certain law is not handy, raises his hand and modifies it — this kind of writing reduces "laws" to the strong one's modeling clay. Heng Yuan Zhou jointly seals this loophole with the **republican axiom of Myriad Races** and the **law-first axiom**: laws may not be modified, but modifying laws must follow Myriad Ranks' democratic procedure; no transcended one has the right to privately revise laws.

Six top-level axioms in this entry centrally embody the republican axiom of Myriad Races: law-revision right belongs to all Myriad Ranks; the transcended one is only the proposer and executor, not the legislator. The anti-predation axiom requires law revision not serve predators' private gain; the collective ascension axiom requires revision's purpose be pushing the cosmos' overall evolution; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the revision process be transparent to Myriad Ranks; the law-first axiom ensures the revision procedure itself is a law-level definition, which even transcended ones may not bypass to directly modify laws.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article focuses: how Heng Yuan Zhou's law-revision democratic procedure concretely operates.

## I. Law Revision's Scope: What May Be Changed, What May Not

Not all laws may be revised. Heng Yuan Zhou divides laws into two categories:

| Law Category | Examples | May Be Revised | Revision Condition |
|--------------|----------|----------------|--------------------|
| Fundamental laws | Energy conservation, anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races | Not revisable | No procedure may modify fundamental laws |
| Operating laws | Energy-transformation efficiency parameters, consciousness-domain interface standards, anchor-calibration frequency | Revisable | Follow complete democratic procedure |

Fundamental laws are Heng Yuan Zhou's "constitution"; they define the cosmos' basic nature — energy conservation inviolable, anti-predation unshakable, Myriad-Rank equality inviolable. Once these laws are revised, the whole civilization's foundation shakes. Operating laws are the cosmos' "administrative regulations"; they define concrete operational parameters and standards — these may be revised according to civilizational-development needs.

This distinction itself is the law-first axiom's embodiment: **setting self-consistency above all — if fundamental laws may be casually changed, the setting is no longer self-consistent.**

## II. Law Revision's Six-Step Democratic Procedure

Operating-law revision must follow a six-step democratic procedure:

1. **Proposal initiation**: any cultivator (not limited to transcended ones) may initiate a law-revision proposal. The proposal must explain revision reason, projected effect, impact scope.
2. **Myriad-Rank representative review**: the proposal is submitted to the Myriad-Rank Republican Council, reviewed by each clan's representatives. Review focus: whether revision complies with fundamental laws, whether it benefits Myriad Ranks' overall interest, whether hidden predatory risk exists.
3. **Quest Domain simulation review**: the proposal is deduced in Quest Sediment Domain simulation review — if laws are revised per the proposal, what impact will it have on all cosmic layers? Deduction results serve as reference for Myriad-Rank representatives.
4. **Myriad-Rank referendum**: after review passes, the proposal enters the Myriad-Rank referendum stage. Cultivators on planes and in races affected by the revision all have voting rights. Voting results are weighted by civilizational weight (see entry 706).
5. **Public notice and buffer period**: after referendum passes, enter the public-notice period. During the notice period any cultivator may raise objections. After the notice period ends a buffer period is set, giving Myriad Ranks time to adapt to the coming law changes.
6. **Formal revision**: after the buffer period ends, laws are formally revised. The revision process is executed by the transcended one — he is the technical operator of law revision, not the decision-maker.

Six-step procedure's core logic: **the transcended one revises laws, but does not legislate.** What he executes is Myriad Ranks' will, not his own.

## III. The Transcended One's Role in the Revision Procedure

The transcended one's role in law revision is very clear: **technical executor**, not political decision-maker. Concrete duties:

- **Proposal technical assessment**: when a proposal involves law technical details, the transcended one is responsible for assessing the proposal's technical feasibility — whether the revision may be realized in the existing law network, how large the technical risk, what supporting engineering is needed;
- **Simulation-review operation**: Quest Sediment Domain simulation review's concrete operation is completed by the transcended one — he converts the proposal into a simulation model, runs the deduction, outputs a deduction report;
- **Revision technical execution**: after Myriad-Rank referendum passes, the transcended one executes concrete parameter revision in the law network — he is the law network's "construction crew," not the "design party";
- **Post-revision monitoring**: after revision completes, the transcended one continuously monitors the law network's operating state, confirming revision effects match the simulation deduction, reporting promptly when deviation appears.

Four duties together: the transcended one is law-revision engineering's chief engineer — he understands technology, can operate, can monitor, but he does not decide "whether to revise." "Whether to revise" is Myriad Ranks' business.

## IV. Why a Democratic Procedure Is Necessary: Historical Lessons Against "Strong Ones Modifying Laws"

Heng Yuan Zhou insists on the law-revision democratic procedure because the old system's historical lessons are too deep:

- **Consequence 1 of strong ones privately modifying laws: law collapse.** In the old system, some strong one feels a certain law is not handy, raises his hand and modifies it — but the law he modified has deep connections with other laws; the change triggers a chain reaction, and the whole law network shows systematic collapse.
- **Consequence 2: law bias.** When strong ones modify laws, they inevitably favor themselves and their allies — the strong one's race benefits; other races are marginalized. This is the disease of "legislators are the privileged class."
- **Consequence 3: historical regression.** When strong ones modify laws, they may write back the old era's predation-ism laws — because the strong one's interests do not align with Myriad Ranks' interests.

The democratic procedure institutionally prevents these three consequences: one more review step is one more layer of technical-risk gatekeeping; one more referendum step is one more layer of Myriad-Rank-interest guarantee; one more notice-buffer step is one more layer of historical-regression defense.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: pre-revision simulation review avoids repeated waste of "after revising find it wrong and change back"; the public-notice buffer period avoids systematic disturbance caused by law mutation
- **Anti-predation**: the six-step procedure's Myriad-Rank representative review and referendum stages institutionally seal the channel of strong ones using law revision for private gain
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system, strong ones modify laws whenever they want, no review no referendum; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended one revising laws must follow a six-step democratic procedure; he is only the technical executor
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: referendum rights open to all Myriad-Rank races, all planes, all life forms; groups affected by the revision all have voting rights
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the six-step procedure, fundamental vs. operating law classification, and the transcended one's technical role are all publicly queryable; Myriad Ranks may supervise the fairness of the revision procedure

- Terminology alignment: the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is this batch's standard realm appellation; Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision monitors operation compliance throughout; the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle ensures energy and knowledge circulate perpetually among Myriad Ranks; exercise of law-anchor authority is strictly constrained by four-dimensional assessment and democratic procedure.
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The law-revision democratic procedure is the republican axiom of Myriad Races' institutionalized fulfillment at the law dimension. It tells all cultivators: laws are not the strong one's modeling clay, but Myriad Ranks' common contract. Fundamental laws may not be changed; operating laws may be changed but must follow procedure. The transcended one's role in it is chief engineer — he revises laws, but does not legislate; he operates, but does not decide. When law-revision right truly belongs to all Myriad Ranks, the law network will not be reduced to the strong one's private instrument, and civilization will not go into historical regression because of some strong one's personal will.
