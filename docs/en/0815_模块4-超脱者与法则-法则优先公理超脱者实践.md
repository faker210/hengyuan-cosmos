# Entry 815 | Transcended Ones and Laws | The Transcended One's Practice of the Law-First Axiom

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 09, 03, 709

## Introduction: the law-first axiom is not a slogan, but a multiple-choice the transcended one must make every day

The law-first axiom is the most special of the six top-level axioms — unlike the other four that directly prescribe "what to do," it prescribes "the priority among all prescriptions": **laws first, never eat books, never go stale; setting self-consistency above plot convenience; plot may not hijack setting.** This axiom sounds abstract, but in the transcended one's daily work, it is a multiple-choice faced at every decision moment. The sixth, the universal equal infinite law, establishes the origin logic that all beings inherently possess basic infinite power and predation loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

Six top-level axioms in this entry centrally discuss the law-first axiom itself: why does it stand above the other four? Because if the setting is not self-consistent, the other four axioms cannot stably run — energy conservation broken, the anti-predation axiom loses its physical base; laws may be casually changed by plot, and the republican guarantee becomes empty talk. So the law-first axiom is the meta-axiom; it guards the other axioms' stability.

This article focuses: in daily work, how the transcended one turns the law-first axiom from "text written in the charter" into "practice executed at every decision."

## I. The Law-First Axiom's Three-Layer Meaning

The law-first axiom has three progressive layers of meaning:

| Layer | Meaning | Concrete Requirement |
|-------|---------|----------------------|
| Layer 1: laws above plot | The development of any civilizational event may not violate established law setting | If plot needs conflict with laws, change the plot, not the laws |
| Layer 2: self-consistency above convenience | Temporarily modifying setting for plot convenience is the highest-level violation | Temporarily changing setting = book-eating; once it occurs, must be publicly corrected |
| Layer 3: expansion without collapse | The worldview may infinitely expand, but the expanded part must be self-consistent with existing setting | When new content conflicts with old setting, old setting prevails, adjust new content |

Three layers together: **laws are hard constraints; plot is soft variables.** All the transcended one's work proceeds within the law framework — he may not, because "a hero moment is needed," let some cultivator violate energy conservation and suddenly grow stronger; may not, because "a moving moment is needed," let some already-dead cultivator violate causality and resurrect. Laws are the ceiling; plot may not break through the ceiling.

## II. Three Typical "Plot-Hijack" Scenarios the Transcended One Faces

The law-first axiom in practice mainly guards against three typical "plot-hijacks-setting" scenarios:

1. **Scenario 1: law conflict in emergency rescue.** In a plane crisis, the transcended one faces a choice — follow legal procedure (may be too late to rescue), or bypass laws and directly rescue (violate procedure). The law-first axiom requires: bypassing laws to directly rescue is a violation. The correct approach is finding the fastest path within the law framework — emergency channels, plan activation, linked dispatch; these are fast-response mechanisms within the law framework, not "temporarily breaking laws."
2. **Scenario 2: setting conflict in major breakthrough.** Some cultivator's breakthrough direction is inconsistent with the established law definition — for example, he claims he "broke energy conservation." The law-first axiom requires: this kind of "breakthrough" is not recognized. The cultivator must either adjust his own understanding, or propose modification suggestions through legal law-revision procedure (entry 814), rather than personally announcing "I broke through the laws."
3. **Scenario 3: causal conflict in historical events.** Cross-plane exploration discovers some historical event contradicts existing historical records. The law-first axiom requires: do not rush to "rewrite history" to accommodate new discoveries, but first verify the new discovery's authenticity — if the new discovery is indeed reliable, systematically correct historical records through civilizational-correction procedure (entry 828), not casually change a detail.

Three scenarios' common solution: **when encountering conflict, do not move laws; move the plan.** Laws are constants; the plan is the variable — always adjust the plan to fit laws, never adjust laws to fit the plan.

## III. The Transcended One's "Book-Eating" Self-Review Mechanism

Before executing any law-level operation, the transcended one must perform a "book-eating risk" self-review. Review checklist:

- Does this operation contradict an established fundamental law? If yes, stop the operation.
- Does this operation require temporarily modifying some operating law? If yes, transfer to the law-revision democratic procedure (entry 814); do not do a temporary modification.
- Will this operation's consequences contradict existing historical records? If yes, first verify, then correct; do not casually change history.
- Will this operation give some cultivator "abilities laws do not allow"? If yes, refuse to grant.

Four review questions; any one answered "yes," the operation immediately pauses. This is not cowardice, but the law-first axiom's daily discipline — **better slower than book-eating.** Eat a book once, the law network's credibility drops one point; eat ten, the whole civilization's setting foundation shakes.

## IV. Why the Transcended One Must Take the Lead in Obeying the Law-First Axiom

The transcended one is law-anchor authority's holder — he theoretically has the ability to "operate laws." Precisely because of this, he even more may not become "the first person to break laws." Three reasons:

- **Demonstration effect**: the transcended one is Myriad Ranks' example. If he himself does not obey the law-first axiom, Myriad-Rank cultivators will feel "laws are for ordinary people; strong ones are exceptions" — this is the breeding ground for predation-ism's revival.
- **Trust base**: Myriad Ranks gave law-anchor authority to the transcended one; the trust base is that he will not abuse this authority. If he modifies laws for convenience, Myriad Ranks' trust in him instantly collapses — his civilizational weight directly zeroes.
- **System stability**: the law network is a self-consistent system. The transcended one's one private law modification may trigger chain reactions he did not anticipate. Book-eating's cost is not just "setting inconsistency," but the whole law network's systematic risk.

So, the transcended one obeying the law-first axiom is not because "he doesn't dare," but because **he is the person who most clearly understands the cost of violating laws.**

- **Anti-energy waste**: book-eating self-review avoids repeated engineering waste of "after revising find it wrong and change back," and also avoids systematic repair costs caused by law conflicts
- **Anti-predation**: the law-first axiom seals the channel of "strong ones under plot-need name bypassing laws to predate" — you may not rob by saying "plot needs it"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo authors change setting for thrill points at any time; book-eating/stale-ending is the norm; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended one takes "not eating books" as daily discipline, every operation passing book-eating-risk review
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the law-first axiom applies equally to all Myriad-Rank cultivators — transcended ones are no exception, ordinary cultivators are no exception; before laws there is no privileged class
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the law-first axiom's three-layer meaning, three typical scenarios, and self-review checklist are all publicly queryable; Myriad Ranks may supervise whether the transcended one strictly obeys

- Terminology alignment: the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is this batch's standard realm appellation; Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision monitors operation compliance throughout; the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle ensures energy and knowledge circulate perpetually among Myriad Ranks.
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The transcended one's practice of the law-first axiom turns "setting self-consistency above plot convenience" from a manifesto into discipline at every decision moment. In daily work the transcended one faces countless temptations of "should we bypass laws for convenience," and every time he chooses "not bypass," he adds a brick to civilization's setting foundation. He is not incapable of bypassing laws — he is law-anchor authority's holder, he has that ability — but he chooses not to bypass, because he most clearly knows: **laws bypassed once, civilization shrinks one inch.** When the transcended one takes the lead in guarding the law-first axiom, Myriad Ranks' civilizational mansion may forever not collapse amid infinite expansion.
