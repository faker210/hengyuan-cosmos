# Entry 816 | Transcended Ones and Planes · Overview — The Transcended One's Plane Management

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 17, 80, 28

## Introduction: the transcended one is not a plane's lord, but its guardian and server

The old system's imagination of "plane manager" is: the strong one conquers a plane, self-proclaims lord, all beings on the plane pay tribute and are ruled by him. This imagination turns plane management into territory occupation. Heng Yuan Zhou completely negates this definition. In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's orthodox framework, **the transcended one's relationship to a plane is not lord-to-territory, but guardian-to-home.** The transcended one manages a plane not to occupy its resources and population, but to guard the plane's law stability and serve Myriad-Rank beings' evolutionary needs on the plane.

Six top-level axioms all take effect in this entry: the anti-predation axiom requires plane management never become predation under plane-patronage; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings on the plane equally participate in plane governance; the collective ascension axiom requires plane management's ultimate goal be pushing plane civilization and the cosmos overall uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires cross-plane management operations strictly report; the law-first axiom requires plane-management rules themselves be law-level definitions, not changed by the transcended one's personal will.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article, as the "Transcended Ones and Planes" sub-series overview, sorts out the transcended one's plane-management panorama at the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm level.

## I. Plane Management's Five Functional Dimensions

Per Plane-Sovereign Strong-One Standards (Document 17) and Plane Theory and Classification (Document 80), the transcended one's plane management covers five functional dimensions:

| Functional Dimension | Management Content | Corresponding Anchor Level |
|----------------------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| Law stability | Maintain the plane's law parameters without drift or collapse | Plane core anchor (Level 2) |
| Energy dispatch | Monitor the plane's energy cycle, adjust surplus and shortfall | Plane core anchor + star-sector hub anchor |
| Civilizational guidance | Guide the plane's civilizational-development direction, prevent historical regression | Plane core anchor + Alaya Network |
| Safety protection | Resist external plane threats (invasion, law pollution, energy storms) | Plane core anchor + cross-plane connection |
| Interconnection | Connect the plane to the cross-plane anchor network, participate in multiverse symbiosis | Cross-plane anchor connection |

Five functional dimensions' common trait: **all are service-type management, not rule-type power.** The transcended one manages the plane not because the plane belongs to him, but because the plane needs someone to guard it — he is the guardian, not the lord.

## II. Plane Management and the Old System's "Plane Hegemon"'s Essential Difference

Put the transcended one's plane management side by side with the old system's "plane hegemon"; the distinction is clear:

| Dimension | Old-System Plane Hegemon | Heng Yuan Zhou Plane Guardian |
|-----------|--------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Relationship to plane | Possession — the plane is my private estate | Guardianship — the plane is Myriad Ranks' home |
| Relationship to beings | Ruling — all beings are my subjects | Serving — all beings are my service objects |
| Resource use | Predation — plane resources are my monopoly | Dispatch — plane resources go through Myriad-Rank cycle |
| Management method | Autocracy — I have the final say | Co-governance — Myriad-Rank representatives participate in decisions |
| Exit mechanism | May not exit — what I conquered is mine | May exit — post rotation, weight decline means handover |

The most critical difference is in the last row: **the plane-management post is rotatable.** Old-system plane hegemons after conquering never leave, ruling forever; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended one after managing a plane for several standard cycles rotates the post, handing it to the next — because plane management is a post duty, not a hereditary fief.

## III. How Many Planes Does a Transcended One Manage: Quota and Rotation

How many planes does one transcended one simultaneously manage? This is not unlimited; there is a clear quota:

- **Base quota**: a transcended one entering the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm has a base management quota of 1 plane;
- **Weighted quota**: weighted by civilizational weight — the higher the weight, the more planes he may manage. But even the highest-weight transcended one manages no more than 7 planes simultaneously;
- **Rotation mechanism**: after each management cycle ends, the relationship between the transcended one and the managed plane is reassessed — if the plane's civilization has matured and may operate autonomously, the transcended one hands over the management post and goes to a plane that needs him more.

Quota and rotation's design logic: **prevent the transcended one from becoming a "plane hoarder."** In the old system, the more planes a strong one conquers the more imposing; in Heng Yuan Zhou, the more planes a transcended one manages, the heavier his burden — not a reward, but a responsibility. The more planes you manage, the more energy you must invest, the greater your obligation to Myriad Ranks.

## IV. Plane Management's Supervision Mechanism

The transcended one's plane management is under triple supervision:

1. **Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision**: real-time monitoring of the plane's law state, energy flow, civilizational-development trend; anomalies auto-alert;
2. **Anchor Supervisory Committee**: periodic audit of the transcended one's operation records in plane management, checking compliance;
3. **Plane Myriad-Rank Representative Assembly**: Myriad-Rank representatives on the plane periodically evaluate the transcended one's management performance — if the plane's beings are broadly dissatisfied, post adjustment triggers.

Triple supervision ensures: no matter how long the transcended one manages a plane, he will not become a "plane local emperor." His management performance is evaluated by the managed (Myriad Ranks); his operation records are audited by the network; his post tenure is decided by weight and performance.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: quota and rotation mechanisms prevent the transcended one from over-expanding management scope and degrading management quality, and also prevent plane resources from being wasted by inefficient management
- **Anti-predation**: triple supervision + co-governance mechanism institutionally seal the channel of "manager becomes lord"; resources and beings on the plane are not privatized by the manager
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system, strong ones after conquering a plane self-proclaim hegemon and rule hereditarily; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended one manages a plane on rotation, performance, handover
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: plane management's five functional dimensions all face all Myriad-Rank beings on the plane; each clan's needs are considered in management
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: plane-management functional standards, quota rules, supervision mechanisms, and rotation procedures are all queryable in the Selfless Dao public library; Myriad Ranks may supervise management fairness

- Terminology alignment: the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle ensures energy and knowledge circulate perpetually among Myriad Ranks; exercise of law-anchor authority is strictly constrained by four-dimensional assessment and democratic procedure.
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The transcended one's plane management is essentially the post-performance of the civilization-level service of "guarding the plane." He is not the plane's lord, but its guardian; not all beings' ruler, but their server. He manages the plane because the plane needs someone to guard laws, dispatch energy, guide civilization, defend threats, connect networks — when he does these five things well, Myriad Ranks on the plane may evolve at ease. When the transcended one turns plane management from "possession" into "service," the title "plane-sovereign strong one" truly deserves the word "sovereign" — sovereign above, but not riding on all beings' heads.
