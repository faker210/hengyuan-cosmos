# Entry 817 | Transcended Ones and Planes | The Plane-Sovereign Strong One's Four-Dimensional Assessment

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 17, 29, 23

## Introduction: looking only at power is the old world's algorithm for selecting strong ones

The old system selects strong ones by one metric: combat power. Whoever has the bigger fist is sovereign. The consequence of this selection algorithm: the strongest on a plane is often the most dangerous person — his power has no heart-nature constraint, no contribution requirement, no responsibility binding. Heng Yuan Zhou completely rewrites this algorithm with the **four-dimensional assessment system**: power is only the base; heart-nature, contribution, and responsibility are equally hard indicators; all four dimensions met before recognition as a plane-sovereign strong one.

Six top-level axioms in this entry centrally embody the republican axiom of Myriad Races: the strong-one selection standard is not "who fights best," but "who serves Myriad Ranks best." The anti-predation axiom is the heart-nature dimension's assessment core; the collective ascension axiom is the contribution dimension's assessment direction; the causal self-discipline axiom is the responsibility dimension's assessment content; the law-first axiom ensures the four-dimensional assessment standard itself is a law-level definition, not modified by any strong one's rise.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article focuses: how the plane-sovereign strong one's four-dimensional assessment operates at the transcended-one (Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm) level.

## I. Four-Dimensional Assessment's Weight Design

Per Plane-Sovereign Strong-One Standards (Document 17), four-dimensional assessment's weight design is:

| Dimension | Weight | Assessment Core | Consequence of Substandard |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|------------------------------|
| Power | 40% | Realm, energy, law-manipulation ability | Substandard = insufficient power, no qualification to discuss others |
| Heart-nature | 25% | Anti-predation stance, ethics adherence, mental maturity | Substandard = high-risk individual; the stronger the more dangerous |
| Contribution | 20% | Actual contribution to civilization, Myriad Ranks, symbiotic network | Substandard = power decoupled from civilization; great talent wasted |
| Responsibility | 15% | Willingness and ability to bear public responsibility | Substandard = refuses service; strong-one obligations unfulfilled |

Weight design's logic is very clear: **power is a threshold, not a high-score item.** The 40% power weight only means "you must have this base," but the remaining 60% heart-nature, contribution, and responsibility are what decide whether you may become a true "sovereign strong one." A cultivator with full marks in power but zero in heart-nature scores only 40 total — he does not reach the 80 passing line.

## II. Heart-Nature Dimension: Why 25% Weight Is Heavier Than It Looks

The heart-nature dimension's 25% weight looks not high, but it has a special property: **one-vote veto.** As long as the heart-nature dimension has "predatory residue" or "anti-symbiotic stance," no matter how high the other three dimensions, recognition is denied — not points deducted, but direct veto.

The heart-nature dimension's assessment content includes:

- **Historical behavior review**: all major behavior records throughout history are called up; any predatory behavior, any persecution record?
- **Deep consciousness scan**: under complete voluntariness, scan the cultivator's inner true thoughts, confirming his stance is not disguised.
- **Situational stress test**: set extreme moral dilemmas in a simulated environment, observing the cultivator's true choices under pressure.
- **Long-term observation**: for controversial candidates, conduct long-term observation over years to decades, confirming his heart-nature is stable.

This assessment ensures: **you cannot pass the heart-nature gate by "pretending to be a good person."** Stress tests and long-term observation will expose disguise — a person who under extreme dilemmas chooses predating to save himself, his "symbiotic conviction" is only a mask.

## III. Contribution Dimension: From "Personal Cultivation" to "Civilizational-Level Contribution"

The contribution dimension's 20% weight assesses the cultivator's actual contribution to civilization. The key standard: **contribution value reaches the civilization's top-20% level, with at least one civilization-level contribution record.**

Contribution types include:

- Cultivation contribution: new cultivation-manual creation, cultivation-theory innovation;
- Knowledge contribution: enlightenment opening, education and training, academic research;
- Technical contribution: law-engineering technology, Multiplication Fruit technology optimization;
- Service contribution: anchor maintenance, civilizational governance, mentor guidance;
- Ecological contribution: planetary ecological restoration, plane energy-cycle improvement;
- Guardianship contribution: resisting external threats, disaster rescue.

Six contribution types' common trait: **all are "given out," not "taken in."** Your contribution to civilization is measured by what you did for Myriad Ranks, not by how strong you yourself are. A person who cultivates to the peak alone but never shares, never serves, his contribution value is zero — his power has not converted into civilizational increment.

## IV. Responsibility Dimension: Quantifying the Strong-One Obligation System

The responsibility dimension's 15% weight assesses the cultivator's willingness and ability to bear public responsibility. The core standard: **at least 30% of time and effort annually invested in public responsibility.**

Concrete responsibility types include: anchor guardianship, civilizational advisory, mentor transmission, plane inspection, crisis response, law participation. The transcended one at the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm has stricter responsibility-dimension assessment — his public responsibility is not just 30%, but his post itself is public responsibility.

The responsibility dimension's only 15% weight is not because responsibility is unimportant, but because: **the responsibility dimension is more "bottom-line" in nature.** If you completely refuse to bear public responsibility, no matter how high the other three dimensions, recognition is denied — this is another one-vote-veto item. The 15% weight distinguishes "basically qualified," but for "complete responsibility-refusers" it is zero tolerance.

## V. The Transcended One's Four-Dimensional Assessment: Stricter Than the Origin Realm

The transcended one entering the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm already passed the four-dimensional assessment once at the Origin Realm. But after promotion to transcended one, the assessment standard upgrades:

- **Power dimension**: no longer just "reach Origin Realm," but requiring law-anchor capability at the hyper-multiverse scale;
- **Heart-nature dimension**: from "firm anti-predation stance" upgraded to "withstanding power corrosion at the hyper-multiverse scale" — the transcended one's law-anchor authority is too large; heart-nature testing is also greater;
- **Contribution dimension**: from "civilization top-20%" upgraded to "have cross-plane-grade civilizational contribution records";
- **Responsibility dimension**: from "30% time invested in public responsibility" upgraded to "post is public responsibility" — the transcended one's whole existence is public service.

The upgraded assessment essentially asks one question: **when you hold hyper-multiverse-grade power, can you still keep the original heart of being a Myriad-Rank server?** Keep it, you continue as transcended one; cannot keep it, authority downgrades, post hands over.

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: four-dimensional assessment ensures those recognized as sovereign strong ones have their power steered to civilizational service, not idle or abused — eliminating great-talent-wasted
- **Anti-predation**: the heart-nature dimension's one-vote veto seals predatory strong ones' promotion channel at the admission stage
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo strong ones automatically become sovereign just by power size; Heng Yuan Zhou's plane-sovereign strong ones must meet all four dimensions; heart-nature substandard directly vetoed
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: four-dimensional assessment is open to all Myriad-Rank cultivators; each clan's cultivators meeting standards may be recognized, no racial threshold
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: four-dimensional assessment's weights, standards, procedures, and veto mechanisms are all publicly queryable; Myriad Ranks may supervise assessment fairness

- Terminology alignment: the transcended one's civilizational weight dynamically adjusts with post performance; Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision monitors operation compliance throughout; the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle ensures energy and knowledge circulate perpetually among Myriad Ranks.
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The plane-sovereign strong one's four-dimensional assessment is Heng Yuan Zhou's hardest institutional definition of "strong." Power 40% is only the threshold; heart-nature 25% is the soul; contribution 20% is the value; responsibility 15% is the commitment. The four dimensions united are true "sovereign." The old system looks only at power; Heng Yuan Zhou weights all four — this difference determines: the old system's sovereign strong ones are plane hegemons; Heng Yuan Zhou's sovereign strong ones are plane guardians. The transcended one transcends not because his power is largest, but because he is all-four-excellent — his power, heart-nature, contribution, and responsibility all point to the same direction: serving Myriad Ranks.
