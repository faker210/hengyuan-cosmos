# Entry 818 | Transcended Ones and Planes | Cross-Plane Anchor Construction and Maintenance

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 28, 20, 27

## Introduction: cross-plane anchors are not building a road, but building a whole cross-dimensional traffic network

Planes were originally isolated from each other — each plane has its own spacetime, laws, and energy environment. To let Myriad Ranks freely travel, dispatch energy, and share knowledge across multiple planes, cross-plane anchor connections must be built. This is not as simple as building a road, but building a whole cross-dimensional traffic network. The transcended one is this network's chief engineer.

Six top-level axioms in this entry land as engineering standards: the anti-predation axiom requires anchor construction not predate any plane's resources; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the anchor network open equally to all Myriad-Rank planes; the collective ascension axiom requires the anchor network serve the cosmos' overall uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires cross-plane channel construction strictly report, observe first then build; the law-first axiom requires anchor-construction engineering strictly follow established technical standards, not cut corners.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article focuses: the transcended one's engineering practice in cross-plane anchor construction and maintenance.

## I. Cross-Plane Anchor Construction's Engineering Procedure

Per Cross-Plane Anchor Connection (Document 28), cross-plane anchor construction divides into five engineering steps:

| Step | Name | Core Work | Participants |
|------|------|-----------|--------------|
| Step 1 | Site exploration | Scan unknown planes through probe micro-connections, assess their law parameters, energy environment, life forms | Exploration team + transcended one |
| Step 2 | Spacetime bridging | Use the spatial-law dispatch system to build a spacetime bridge between two plane anchors, breaking through spacetime barriers | Transcended one + spatial-law team |
| Step 3 | Law compatibility | Bidirectionally calibrate both-end anchors' law cores, bringing law parameters to compatibility | Transcended one (Level-5 law-anchor authority) |
| Step 4 | Energy conversion | Establish multi-form energy-conversion protocol, letting energy freely circulate between both ends | Energy-engineering team + transcended one |
| Step 5 | Channel stabilization | Continuously inject stable energy, bringing the channel into stable operation, completing network access | Anchor team + Alaya Network acceptance |

Five-step procedure's key: **every step needs the transcended one's law-anchor authority participation, but it is not just the transcended one alone doing it.** He is chief engineer, below him is a complete engineering team — spatial-law team, energy-engineering team, anchor team. This is not one person's heroism engineering, but Myriad Ranks' collaborative systems engineering.

## II. Three Connection Types' Differentiated Construction Strategy

Not all cross-plane connections are built to the same standard. Per Document 28, three connection types' construction strategies are entirely different:

- **Permanent trunk connection**: built only between strategically important, frequently exchanged planes. Construction cost is extremely high; requires multi-plane-sovereign strong ones' coordination. After completion, permanently stable, annual interruptions not exceeding 1 hour. This connection is the cross-plane network's "expressway."
- **Temporary branch connection**: flexibly built per task needs. For example, an exploration team needs temporary communications, or some plane needs emergency energy delivery. Closed after the task completes. This is the cross-plane network's "temporary byroad."
- **Probe micro-connection**: built in large numbers during early exploration, only used to probe unknown planes' basic situation. Bandwidth extremely low, stability extremely poor, but construction cost low. This is the cross-plane network's "reconnaissance drone."

The transcended one's depth of participation differs in the three connection constructions: permanent trunk connections require him to personally participate in law-compatibility calibration; temporary branch connections he only approves; probe micro-connections are completed by automated probes, he only needs to monitor global state.

## III. Anchor Maintenance's Daily Work

Construction completion is not the end. Cross-plane anchors need continuous maintenance:

- **Parameter calibration**: anchors' law cores, energy cores, and information cores need periodic calibration (Document 27), preventing parameter drift from causing channel instability. The transcended one is responsible for setting calibration cycles and standards.
- **Load balancing**: when some cross-plane channel's usage exceeds designed bandwidth, the transcended one coordinates adjacent channels to divert flow, or expands the main channel.
- **Fault repair**: when a channel shows interruption or anomaly, the transcended one organizes an emergency repair team to locate the fault point and repair the channel.
- **Security upgrade**: as technology advances and security threats change, the transcended one is responsible for upgrading the channel's encryption, intrusion detection, and quarantine-inspection systems.

Daily maintenance workload is enormous — the larger the cross-plane network, the more nodes need maintenance. This is why the 20% Activation Strategy cultivates a large number of plane-sovereign strong ones to share the load — the transcended one cannot maintain the whole network alone.

## IV. Anchor Construction's Causal Self-Discipline Requirements

Cross-plane anchor construction is not "build whenever you want." The causal self-discipline axiom requires the construction process follow three rules:

1. **Observe before build**: before formally building a connection, must fully observe the target plane through probe micro-connections — are law parameters stable? Is there native life? Is there danger? Building without observation may alarm native life or introduce unknown risk.
2. **Native informed consent**: if the target plane already has intelligent life inhabiting it, building a cross-plane connection must inform the plane's residents and obtain their consent. You may not secretly build a road at someone else's doorstep.
3. **Minimal-interference principle**: during construction, the causal impact on the target plane is minimized — before construction completes, do not deliver large-scale energy to the plane, do not introduce foreign species, do not change its law parameters.

Three rules ensure: cross-plane anchor construction is civilizational expansion, not civilizational invasion.

- **Anti-energy waste**: three connection types' differentiated construction avoids wasting expensive permanent trunks between unimportant planes
- **Anti-predation**: native informed consent and the minimal-interference principle institutionally prevent anchor construction from becoming predation of unknown planes
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system, strong ones tear plane barriers by personal will, no observation no approval; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended one building cross-plane anchors follows a five-step engineering procedure + three causal-self-discipline rules
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the anchor network opens to all Myriad-Rank planes and all races; Myriad-Rank residents in connected planes may freely use channels
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: construction procedure, connection types, maintenance standards, and causal-self-discipline rules are all publicly queryable; Myriad Ranks may supervise construction behavior

- Terminology alignment: the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is this batch's standard realm appellation; the transcended one's civilizational weight dynamically adjusts with post performance; Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision monitors operation compliance throughout; the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle ensures energy and knowledge circulate perpetually among Myriad Ranks.
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

Cross-plane anchor construction and maintenance is the cross-dimensional traffic network the transcended one builds for Myriad Ranks. He surveys terrain, builds bridges, calibrates laws, converts energy, maintains roads — he is chief engineer. When this road is built well, Myriad Ranks may freely travel, dispatch energy, and share knowledge in the multiverse.
