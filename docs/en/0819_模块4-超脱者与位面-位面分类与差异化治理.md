# Entry 819 | Transcended Ones and Planes | Plane Classification and Differentiated Governance

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 80, 30, 17

## Introduction: a thousand planes have a thousand ways of living — the transcended one may not measure all planes with one ruler

The old system's handling of planes is very rough: the strong one arrives at a plane, transforms it by his own standard — this plane's cultivation system must be like mine, laws must follow mine, civilization must follow the route I planned. This "great unification" thinking turns a rich cosmos into monotonous copy-paste. Heng Yuan Zhou rejects this practice. In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's orthodox framework, **planes are not uniform templates, but homes with their own character.** The transcended one's governance is not "transform all planes into the same," but "respect each plane's differentiated development within a unified framework."

Six top-level axioms in this entry centrally embody the republican axiom of Myriad Races: Myriad Ranks' diversity itself is the cosmos' wealth, not to be smoothed out. The anti-predation axiom requires differentiated governance not become "unequal treatment, discriminating against the weak"; the collective ascension axiom requires differentiated governance's ultimate goal still be pushing overall uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires cross-plane governance not disturb each plane's natural development trajectory; the law-first axiom requires differentiated governance's classification standard itself be a law-level definition, not casually classified by the transcended one's personal preference.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article focuses: how the transcended one conducts differentiated governance of different plane types.

## I. Planes' Four Basic Types

Per Plane Theory and Classification (Document 80) and Five Symbiotic-Civilization Typology Overview (Document 30), planes in Heng Yuan Zhou by development stage and civilizational maturity divide into four basic types:

| Plane Type | Development Stage | Civilizational State | Transcended-One Governance Focus |
|-------------|-------------------|----------------------|----------------------------------|
| Nascent plane | Newly opened or newly discovered | No civilization or civilization in embryo | Provide basic protection, do not interfere with natural development |
| Growing plane | Civilization initially established | Cultivation system forming | Guide direction, provide public infrastructure |
| Mature plane | Civilization highly developed | Cultivation system complete, Myriad-Rank republicanism running well | Maintain stability, promote innovation |
| Declining plane | Civilization showing regression signs | Law drift, energy-cycle deterioration | Emergency intervention, ecological restoration, prevent historical regression |

Four types are not fixed labels — a plane's type changes with civilizational development. The transcended one's governance strategy must adjust accordingly: a nascent plane grows into a growing plane; a growing plane matures into a mature plane; a mature plane in trouble may also degrade into a declining plane.

## II. Differentiated Governance's Four Principles

The transcended one's differentiated governance of different plane types follows four principles:

1. **Minimal-intervention principle**: for nascent and growing planes, the transcended one intervenes as little as possible — only strikes when civilization faces an existential-level crisis; daily development lets the plane go its own way. Over-intervention is like pulling up seedlings to help them grow.
2. **Infrastructure principle**: for growing planes, the transcended one's main work is providing public infrastructure — anchor access, cultivation-system opening, public energy-pool construction. When infrastructure is in place, plane civilization will grow by itself.
3. **Innovation-guarantee principle**: for mature planes, the transcended one's work shifts from "construction" to "guarantee" — guaranteeing civilizational innovation is not locked by old paths; guaranteeing new cultivation systems and new civilizational experiments have trial-and-error room.
4. **Emergency-repair principle**: for declining planes, the transcended one must quickly intervene — law-stability repair, energy-cycle reconstruction, civilizational-correction engineering. A declining plane without timely repair may collapse in a short time.

Four principles' common logic: **what the plane needs, the transcended one provides; what the plane does not need, the transcended one does not over-manage.** Governance is not "how I want to do it," but "what the plane needs me to do."

## III. Differentiated Governance and Myriad-Races-Full-Coverage's Unity

Some worry: will differentiated governance become "unequal treatment" — strong planes get more care, weak planes are neglected? Heng Yuan Zhou's design is exactly the opposite:

- **Resource allocation linked to need**: anchor resources, energy quotas, and technical support a plane receives are allocated per its development stage's actual needs — nascent planes most need basic protection, so basic-protection resources take priority; mature planes most need innovation guarantee, so trial-and-error room takes priority. Not allocated by strength, but by need.
- **Development rights absolutely equal**: no matter the plane type, the evolutionary rights of Myriad Ranks on the plane are absolutely equal. A cultivator on a nascent plane and a cultivator on a mature plane have exactly the same qualification to access Alaya Network, right to use public cultivation resources, and voting right in Myriad-Rank Republican deliberation.
- **Progress channels open**: a plane's type is not a lifelong label — any plane may through civilizational development go from nascent to mature. The transcended one's governance work is ensuring this progress channel is always open.

Differentiated governance ≠ discrimination. It is "on-demand service," not "strength-based allocation."

## IV. The Transcended One's Plane-Inspection System

Differentiated governance requires the transcended one periodically inspect each plane, understanding the plane's real state — he may not sit in an office judging by data, but must perceive on the ground. Inspection system:

- **Inspection frequency**: mature planes inspected once per standard cycle; growing planes once per half-cycle; nascent and declining planes once per quarter;
- **Inspection content**: law stability, energy-cycle state, civilizational-development trend, Myriad Ranks' living conditions, anchor-network operating state;
- **Inspection method**: the transcended one blends into plane life as an ordinary cultivator, not revealing identity — only in real daily life may he see the real effect of plane governance;
- **Inspection report**: after inspection, submit a written report recording the plane's state, discovered problems, and suggested adjustment plans.

The inspection system ensures: the transcended one's governance decisions are not based on abstract data, but on personal experience of the plane's real state. He is not a manager watching fire from across the shore, but a guardian walking into the fire.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: differentiated governance ensures resources are invested where each plane most needs them, avoiding waste caused by "one-size-fits-all" resource misallocation
- **Anti-predation**: need-based allocation + equal development rights institutionally prevent the transcended one from favoring strong planes and sacrificing weak planes
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo strong ones transform all planes by their own standard, uniform; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended one respects each plane's differentiated development, each with character
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Myriad-Rank cultivators on all four plane types are covered by governance; no matter plane size, strength, newness, treated equally
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: plane-classification standards, four governance principles, and the inspection system are all publicly queryable; each plane may supervise whether governance is fair

- Terminology alignment: the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is this batch's standard realm appellation; the transcended one's civilizational weight dynamically adjusts with post performance; Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision monitors operation compliance throughout; the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle ensures energy and knowledge circulate perpetually among Myriad Ranks; exercise of law-anchor authority is strictly constrained by four-dimensional assessment and democratic procedure.
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Plane classification and differentiated governance is the republican axiom of Myriad Races' refined fulfillment at the plane dimension. A thousand planes have a thousand ways of living; the transcended one does not try to turn them into one way. He manages nascent planes lightly, paves roads for growing planes, guarantees mature planes, and rush-repairs declining planes — governance strategy follows the plane's actual needs, not his personal preference. When every plane may develop at its own pace and with its own character, the multiverse truly becomes Myriad Ranks' home — not a home carved from one mold, but a home of a hundred flowers blooming.
