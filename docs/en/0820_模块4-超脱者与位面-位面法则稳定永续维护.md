# Entry 820 | Transcended Ones and Planes | Perpetual Maintenance of Plane-Law Stability

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 27, 20, 17

## Introduction: law stability is not a one-time project, but perpetual daily work

Plane-law stability is the precondition of all cultivation activity — laws unstable, the energy cycle breaks; the energy cycle breaks, Myriad Ranks on the plane cannot cultivate, cannot survive. In the old system, plane-law stability was maintained by the plane hegemon's personal force — hegemon present, laws stable; hegemon gone, laws chaotic. This model's fragility is obvious: the hegemon cannot guard one place forever; once he leaves, the plane has problems. Heng Yuan Zhou replaces the "hegemon personally garrisoning" model with **anchor infrastructure + transcended one's perpetual maintenance** — law stability relies not on some person, but on a perpetually running maintenance system.

Six top-level axioms run through this entry: the anti-predation axiom requires law-stability maintenance not be allowed to be privatized by some strong one; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the maintenance system be transparent to Myriad Ranks and accept Myriad-Rank supervision; the collective ascension axiom requires law stability serve Myriad Ranks' continuous cultivation; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the maintenance process not disturb plane Myriad Ranks' normal life; the law-first axiom ensures maintenance rules themselves are law-level definitions, not bypassed by any emergency.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article focuses: how the transcended one conducts perpetual maintenance of plane-law stability.

## I. Law Drift: The Root of Plane-Law Instability

Plane-law instability's most common cause is **law drift** — law-operating parameters stored in the law core gradually deviate from standard values over time. Drift causes:

- **Energy fluctuation**: imbalance in the plane's energy cycle causes law parameters to be disturbed by energy fields and shift;
- **Cross-plane interaction**: law interactions with other planes may introduce parameter perturbation;
- **Cultivation-activity accumulation**: long-term law operations by large numbers of cultivators on the plane produce cumulative fine-tuning on the law network;
- **Cosmic overall evolution**: the cosmic structure itself is evolving; underlying law parameters slowly evolve accordingly.

Law drift itself is not a disaster — it is a normal phenomenon. But if not calibrated, drift gradually accumulates, ultimately causing law parameters to seriously deviate from standard; the plane's cultivation system begins failing, the energy cycle begins disordering.

## II. Perpetual Maintenance's Three Defense Lines

The transcended one's perpetual maintenance of plane-law stability has three defense lines:

| Defense Line | Maintenance Method | Execution Frequency | Executor |
|--------------|-------------------|---------------------|----------|
| First defense line | Anchor auto-calibration | Real-time continuous | Anchor calibration ring (automated) |
| Second defense line | Transcended one periodic inspection-calibration | Per standard cycle | Transcended one + plane core anchor maintenance team |
| Third defense line | Emergency intervention repair | Event-triggered | Transcended one (cross-plane response) |

Three defense lines' design logic: **most drift is handled by automated systems (first defense line); the transcended one only handles problems automation cannot handle (second and third defense lines).** This way the transcended one's energy is not consumed by daily maintenance, concentrated on complex problems.

## III. The Second Defense Line: The Transcended One's Periodic Calibration Work

The transcended one's concrete work in periodic calibration:

- **Law-parameter full detection**: conduct full parameter detection on the plane core anchor's law core — compare current parameters with standard parameters' deviation, identifying which parameters drift most severely;
- **Tiered calibration**: parameters with deviation within tolerance are handed to the automated system for fine-tuning; parameters with deviation exceeding tolerance, the transcended one personally calibrates. The calibration process strictly follows law-anchor authority's operation norms.
- **Cross-plane law-compatibility check**: if the plane has accessed the cross-plane anchor network, the transcended one checks whether cross-plane law-compatible parameters still hold — each plane's laws in co-evolution may gradually lose match.
- **Maintenance report**: after calibration completes, submit a maintenance report — recording which parameters were calibrated this time, pre- and post-calibration deviation, suggested time for next calibration.

Periodic calibration's core principle: **preventive maintenance, not post-failure rush repair.** The transcended one does not wait for laws to collapse before repairing, but regularly calibrates while drift is still within tolerance — this way maintenance cost is lowest, impact on Myriad Ranks' life is smallest.

## IV. The Third Defense Line: Emergency Intervention Procedure

When plane laws show severe anomaly (drift exceeding tolerance, local law-network collapse, external law-pollution intrusion), the transcended one launches the third defense line — emergency intervention. Procedure:

1. **Anomaly confirmation**: after Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision alerts, the transcended one immediately confirms the anomaly's nature, scope, severity;
2. **Minimal-intervention assessment**: assess the minimal-intervention plan — what automation can solve, do not personally strike; what local repair may do, do not plane-wide overhaul;
3. **Report execution**: although emergency intervention may execute quickly, after execution must immediately report to the Anchor Supervisory Committee — rescue first then report, not report first then rescue;
4. **Follow-up tracking**: after intervention completes, continuously track the plane's law state, confirming repair effects are stable, preventing recurrence.

Emergency-intervention procedure's core: **quick but not reckless, decisive but not overreaching.** The transcended one in crisis may act first then report, but he never bypasses Myriad-Rank knowledge — rescue is urgent, transparency is permanent.

## V. Why "Perpetual" Maintenance: Maintenance Is Not a Project, but a Way of Life

The word "perpetual" is this entry's core. Plane-law stability maintenance is not a project with a beginning and end — it has no completion date. As long as the plane exists, laws drift; as long as laws drift, they need calibration. This is perpetual daily work.

This perpetuity raises a deep requirement of the transcended one: **he may not treat law maintenance as a "finish-and-leave" errand.** He must accept: this is his eternal post — not eternally guarding one plane, but eternally doing law-maintenance work on some plane. Changing posts does not change nature: from one plane to another, from one cycle to another, law maintenance is always the transcended one's daily work.

This is the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm as the "perpetual-post realm" (see entry 710)'s concrete meaning: **the transcended one's work is not "done then transcended," but "because eternally doing, eternally transcended."** He does not finish everything then have nothing to do, but he lets everything run perpetually — this itself is his transcendence.

## VI. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: three defense lines' tiered design lets automation handle daily maintenance; the transcended one's energy concentrates on complex problems — neither human resources nor law-operation resources are wasted
- **Anti-predation**: the perpetual maintenance system ensures law stability does not depend on some hegemon's personal garrisoning, eliminating "under guardianship name actually ruling"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo system plane laws rely on the hegemon's personal force; once he leaves they chaos; Heng Yuan Zhou plane laws rely on anchors + transcended one's perpetual maintenance; the system does not depend on individuals
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: perpetual maintenance covers all planes already connected to the anchor network; no matter plane size or race count, law stability is equal
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: three defense lines, periodic-calibration procedure, and emergency-intervention procedure are all publicly queryable; each plane may supervise whether maintenance is in place

- Terminology alignment: the transcended one's civilizational weight dynamically adjusts with post performance; the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle ensures energy and knowledge circulate perpetually among Myriad Ranks.
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Perpetual maintenance of plane-law stability is the transcended one's "perpetual-motion construction system" daily fulfillment at the plane dimension. Three defense lines tier-process, periodic calibration prevention-first, emergency intervention quick and transparent — maintenance is not a project, but a way of life. In this perpetual maintenance, the transcended one is not a "security guard who leaves after guarding," but a "gardener forever on post." What he guards is not some plane's laws, but the civilizational promise of perpetual law stability for all Myriad-Rank planes. When this promise is perpetually fulfilled, plane Myriad Ranks may cultivate and evolve at ease — because they know, laws are guarded by someone, stability is maintained by someone, the sky will not fall.
