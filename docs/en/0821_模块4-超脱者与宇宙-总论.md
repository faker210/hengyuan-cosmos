# Entry 821 | Transcended Ones and the Cosmos · Overview — The Transcended One's Cosmic-Grade Duties

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 00, 17, 20

## Introduction: the transcended one is not the cosmos' master, but its gardener

The old system's imagination of a "cosmic-grade strong one" is: he stands at the cosmos' peak, looks down on all beings, the whole cosmos is his back garden. This imagination treats the cosmos as the strong one's private estate. Heng Yuan Zhou completely negates this definition. In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's orthodox framework, **the transcended one's relationship to the cosmos is not master-to-property, but gardener-to-garden.** The cosmos is not the transcended one's private estate, but Myriad Ranks' common home; the transcended one guards the cosmos not because the cosmos belongs to him, but because the cosmos needs someone to guard it.

Six top-level axioms all take effect in this entry: the anti-predation axiom requires cosmic-grade power never be privatized; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires cosmic governance power belong to all Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires the transcended one's cosmic-grade duties serve the cosmos' overall uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires cosmic-grade operations follow the strictest reporting approval; the law-first axiom ensures cosmic-grade duties' definition itself is law-level setting, not changed by any plot convenience.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article, as the "Transcended Ones and the Cosmos" sub-series overview, sorts out the transcended one's cosmic-grade duties panorama at the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm level.

## I. Cosmic-Grade Duties' Five Layers

Per Heng Yuan Zhou Cosmos Overview (Document 00), the transcended one's cosmic-grade duties from micro to macro divide into five layers:

| Layer | Duty Content | Corresponding Cosmic Structure |
|-------|--------------|-------------------------------|
| Micro layer | Guard individual cultivators' safety and growth | Material plane · individual |
| Meso layer | Maintain plane civilization's healthy development | Material plane · civilization |
| Macro layer | Maintain the cross-plane law network's stability | Law network · cross-plane |
| Hyper layer | Push the cosmos overall to high-dimensional uplift | Cosmic origin · uplift |
| Cosmic layer | Participate in the multiverse structure's interconnection and co-rule | Multiverse · structure |

The first three layers have been detailed in previous entries (plane management, law maintenance, cross-plane construction). This article focuses on the latter two layers: **cosmic uplift** and **multiverse interconnection** — these are duties only Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcended ones may bear.

## II. Why Cosmic-Grade Duties Are Needed

Some ask: matters on planes are already enough, why does the transcended one still need to worry about "cosmic-grade" matters? The answer: **the cosmos itself is also aging.** Any cosmos not actively maintained will along the entropy-increase law head toward heat death — energy dissipated, laws decayed, life extinct. Cosmic-grade duties exist precisely to counter this natural trend.

Concretely, cosmic-grade duties address three natural trends:

- **Entropy-increase trend**: the cosmos' total energy under natural conditions continuously dissipates into unusable background radiation. The transcended one's duty is to counter entropy increase through the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle's entropy-reduction engineering (see entry 803);
- **Law-aging trend**: the cosmos' law parameters over time gradually drift; the law network's operating efficiency gradually declines. The transcended one's duty is to delay law aging through law-calibration maintenance (see entry 820);
- **Isolation-fragmentation trend**: planes and cosmic structures in the multiverse naturally tend to isolation — plane barriers thicken, exchanges dwindle. The transcended one's duty is to counter fragmentation through cross-plane anchor construction (see entry 818).

Three trends not countered, the cosmos slowly dies from within. The transcended one's cosmic-grade duties are to let the cosmos "live" longer and "live" better.

## III. Cosmic-Grade Duties and Individual Cultivation's Difference

An individual cultivator's goal is "make myself stronger"; the transcended one's cosmic-grade duties are "make the cosmos better." These two goals are not contradictory, but their directions are entirely different:

- Individual cultivation: energy flows into his own body; his own realm rises;
- Cosmic-grade duties: energy is dispatched through him into the Myriad-Rank cycle; the cosmos' order rises.

A transcended one who only focuses on individual cultivation, his power stays at the Origin Realm — because he does not participate in cosmic-grade duties, his civilizational weight cannot rise, law-anchor authority cannot rise. Only by unifying individual cultivation and cosmic-grade duties — **the purpose of growing stronger is to be able to bear greater cosmic-grade duties** — may he truly enter the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm.

This is the collective ascension axiom's deep meaning: **the strong do not ascend alone, but lift Myriad Ranks forward into evolution.** The transcended one's power is not his personal achievement, but the tool by which he bears cosmic-grade duties.

## IV. Cosmic-Grade Duties' Responsible Subject: Not One Person, but a Group

Cosmic-grade duties' scale is too large — too large for any single transcended one to bear alone. Heng Yuan Zhou's design: **cosmic-grade duties are jointly borne by a group of transcended ones, completed through division of labor and collaboration.**

Division-of-labor method:

- Different transcended ones are responsible for law maintenance in different cosmic regions;
- Different transcended ones are responsible for civilizational guidance of different plane types;
- Different transcended ones are responsible for different lines of the cross-plane anchor network;
- Major cosmic-grade projects (such as cosmic-uplift promotion, new-cosmos weaving) are coordinated by multiple transcended ones.

Group division of labor ensures: any one transcended one goes offline, cosmic-grade duties do not stop — others may fill in. This is network-style transcendence (protagonist-uniqueness dependency extremely low) at cosmic-grade duties' embodiment.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: cosmic-grade duties' group division of labor avoids management inefficiency caused by a single transcended one's energy being over-dispersed
- **Anti-predation**: cosmic-grade duties' public design ensures cosmic energy and laws are not privatized by any single transcended one
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system, cosmic-grade strong ones alone rule the cosmos, Myriad Ranks prostrate; Heng Yuan Zhou's cosmic-grade duties are borne by group division of labor, Myriad-Rank co-governance
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: all cosmic-grade duties' results (law maintenance, uplift promotion, interconnection construction) benefit all Myriad-Rank life forms
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cosmic-grade duties' five layers, division-of-labor method, and responsibility system are all publicly queryable; Myriad Ranks may supervise duty performance

- Terminology alignment: Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision monitors operation compliance throughout.
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The transcended one's cosmic-grade duties are essentially the post-performance of the civilization-level mission of "guarding the cosmos." He is not the cosmos' master, but its gardener; not all things' sovereign, but all things' sustainer. What he guards is not some plane, some race, but the whole cosmos' perpetual operation — countering entropy increase, delaying aging, preventing fragmentation. When a group of transcended ones jointly bear cosmic-grade duties, the cosmos is no longer a wilderness left to age naturally, but a garden Myriad Ranks jointly cultivate. The transcended one transcends not because he stands at the cosmos' peak, but because he squats down and, together with Myriad Ranks, waters, weeds, and prunes the cosmos.
