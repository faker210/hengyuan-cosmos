# Entry 822 | Transcended Ones and the Cosmos | The Depersonified Understanding of Cosmic Will

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 00, 09, 21

## Introduction: the cosmos has no personality, the cosmos only has laws

The old system's handling of "cosmic will" is very casual: the cosmos is a conscious being; it has preferences, emotions, intentions — it chooses protagonists, it sends calamities, it judges all beings. This personification treatment turns the cosmos into a super big shot — it is stronger than all strong ones because it is "will itself." Heng Yuan Zhou completely negates this understanding. In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's orthodox framework, **cosmic will has no personified subject — the cosmos has no preference, no emotion, no private intention. It only has laws.**

Six top-level axioms in this entry centrally embody the law-first axiom: the cosmos' operation is not "some will's arrangement," but "the law network's natural running." The anti-predation axiom requires demoting the cosmos from "personified sovereign" to "depersonified law system" — this way no one may impersonate "cosmic will" to predate; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires cosmic governance power belong to Myriad Ranks, not to "cosmic will"; the collective ascension axiom requires the cosmos' evolutionary direction be jointly pushed by Myriad Ranks; the causal self-discipline axiom requires causal threads' running be a law's natural result, not "the cosmos' arrangement"; the law-first axiom is this entry's entire content's meta-rule.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article focuses: why the transcended one must understand cosmic will in a depersonified way, and how this understanding changes his working style.

## I. Personified Cosmic Will's Three Historical Harms

Personified "cosmic will" in the old system caused three historical harms:

| Harm | Manifestation | Consequence |
|------|---------------|-------------|
| Harm 1: impersonating will | Strong ones claim "I represent cosmic will," endorsing their predatory behavior | Predation is packaged as "heaven's will"; resisting predation becomes "defying heaven" |
| Harm 2: will hijacking | When plot needs it, "cosmic will" suddenly sends calamities, grants opportunities | Myriad Ranks' fate is randomly manipulated; civilization has no autonomy |
| Harm 3: personification corruption | If the cosmos truly has personality, its personality will corrupt — favoring some races, partial to some strong ones | The cosmos itself becomes the biggest predator and corruptor |

Three harms' common root: **once the cosmos is understood as a personified subject, it gives ambitious people the chance to impersonate it, gives plot an excuse for random manipulation, gives "heaven's will is justice" predation logic a breeding ground.**

## II. Depersonified Understanding: The Cosmos Is the Law Network's Natural Running

Heng Yuan Zhou's orthodox understanding of "cosmic will" is: **the so-called "cosmic will" is not some conscious subject making decisions, but the law network's natural running trend at the macro level.** Concretely:

- **The cosmos has no preference**: laws treat all beings equally — energy conservation does not stop constraining you because you are the protagonist; the republican axiom of Myriad Races does not stop applying because you are the villain.
- **The cosmos has no emotion**: the cosmos does not "rage," does not "rejoice," does not "disappoint." Cataclysms are not the cosmos' punishment; opportunities are not the cosmos' gifts — they are the law's running natural results.
- **The cosmos has no intention**: the cosmos does not arrange anyone's fate. A cultivator's fate is jointly determined by his own choices + the laws' natural running, not "a script the cosmos wrote."

This understanding demotes the cosmos from "personified sovereign" to "depersonified law system." It is cold, but it is just — because it is impartial.

## III. Depersonified Understanding's Change to the Transcended One's Working Style

The transcended one understanding cosmic will in a depersonified way, his working style undergoes three fundamental changes:

1. **Do not impersonate the cosmos**: the transcended one never says "I represent cosmic will." What he says is "per the law-first axiom, this serves Myriad Ranks' interests." His authority does not come from "I am the cosmos' spokesperson," but from "my operation is within the law framework and approved by Myriad Ranks."
2. **Do not wait for heaven's will**: the transcended one does not expect "the cosmos will solve the problem itself." The cosmos is a law system; it does not proactively do things — all maintenance, calibration, and repair require the transcended one and Myriad Ranks to proactively do. Heaven will not help you; you must help heaven yourself.
3. **Do not interpret divine will**: when a cataclysm or opportunity occurs, the transcended one does not interpret from "the cosmos' intention" angle — he analyzes from "the law network's natural running" angle: which law-parameter drift caused this cataclysm? Which energy-cycle optimization brought this opportunity? Once analyzed clearly, prescribe the right medicine.

Three changes together: **the transcended one's relationship to the cosmos is engineer-to-machine — he does not worship the machine, does not beg the machine, does not interpret the machine's "mood"; he understands the machine's running laws, then maintains and optimizes it.**

## IV. Depersonified Understanding and Myriad-Rank Republicanism's Unity

Some feel: understanding the cosmos as a depersonified law system will it make the cosmos "cold" and leave Myriad Ranks without belonging? Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: **depersonified ≠ cold; it equals just.**

- Precisely because the cosmos has no personality, it does not play favorites — laws treat all Myriad-Rank life forms equally; this is true "Myriad-Rank republicanism" at the cosmic level's guarantee.
- Precisely because the cosmos has no intention, Myriad Ranks truly own fate autonomy — the cosmos does not arrange your fate; you and Myriad Ranks jointly decide your own fate.
- Precisely because the cosmos has no emotion, the causal law is truly reliable — causality does not change because of "the cosmos' mood"; whatever cause you plant you get whatever effect, fair and predictable.

A depersonified cosmos is warmer than a personified cosmos — because its warmth does not come from "god's favor," but from "laws' justice."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: not waiting for heaven's will means all maintenance is proactively executed, not wasting time on "waiting for the cosmos to get better itself"
- **Anti-predation**: not impersonating cosmic will institutionally seals the logic of "I represent heaven's will so predation is reasonable"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo protagonists are favored by "cosmic will," with built-in protagonist aura; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended ones do not impersonate heaven's will, do not wait for favor, all achievements rely on proactive work within the law framework
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the depersonified cosmos treats all Myriad-Rank life forms equally; there is no question of "which race the cosmos favors"
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cosmic will's depersonified understanding, three historical harms, and three working changes are all publicly queryable; Myriad Ranks may supervise whether the transcended one obeys

- Terminology alignment: the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is this batch's standard realm appellation; the transcended one's civilizational weight dynamically adjusts with post performance; Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision monitors operation compliance throughout; the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle ensures energy and knowledge circulate perpetually among Myriad Ranks; exercise of law-anchor authority is strictly constrained by four-dimensional assessment and democratic procedure.
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

The depersonified understanding of cosmic will is the law-first axiom's hard-core fulfillment at cosmic-philosophy level. The cosmos has no personality, the cosmos only has laws; the cosmos has no preference, the cosmos only has patterns; the cosmos has no intention, the cosmos only has running. The transcended one understanding the cosmos in this way, he does not impersonate heaven's will, does not wait for divine grace, does not interpret divine will — what he does is the law engineer's work: understand patterns, maintain the system, optimize running. When the transcended one and Myriad Ranks both understand the cosmos in a depersonified way, the cosmos is no longer some big shot's back garden, but the law system Myriad Ranks jointly run. This system does not favor anyone, but it is just to all — this is Myriad-Rank republicanism at the cosmic level's ultimate guarantee.
