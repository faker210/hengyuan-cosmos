# Entry 823 | Transcended Ones and the Cosmos | The Collective Promotion of Cosmic Uplift

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 23, 15

## Introduction: cosmic uplift is not one person ascending alone, but Myriad Ranks moving upward together

The old system's imagination of "cosmic uplift" is: the strongest one breaks through to a higher dimension alone, then the cosmos "follows in uplift" because of his breakthrough. This imagination turns cosmic uplift into an individual-heroism byproduct. Heng Yuan Zhou completely negates this definition. In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's orthodox framework, **cosmic uplift is not one person's matter, but a civilization project collectively promoted by Myriad Ranks.** No matter how strong one person is, his power is only enough to lift himself up; only the uplift project with Myriad Ranks' joint participation may lift the whole cosmos up.

Six top-level axioms in this entry centrally embody the collective ascension axiom: the strong do not ascend alone, but lift Myriad Ranks forward into evolution. The anti-predation axiom requires the uplift process not predate other races' evolutionary achievements; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the uplift project be Myriad-Rank-participated and Myriad-Rank-benefited; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the uplift process not disturb non-participating clans' natural development; the law-first axiom requires the uplift's law path itself be self-consistent, no skipping steps.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article focuses: how the transcended one organizes and promotes the collective project of cosmic uplift.

## I. Cosmic-Uplift Principle: Why Collective Promotion Is Needed

Per Energy-Cycle-and-Transformation Laws (Document 15), cosmic uplift's essence is **the cosmos' overall orderliness increase.** What does orderliness increase rely on? Not some strong one's energy, but **large numbers of beings' orderly cultivation activity** — every cultivator's cultivation is a tiny contribution to the cosmos' orderliness; billions of cultivators' contributions accumulate to push the cosmos' overall uplift.

This is why cosmic uplift must be collectively promoted:

- One strong one's orderliness contribution, relative to the whole cosmos' scale, is infinitesimal — he himself uplifts, the cosmos' orderliness only rises by one-hundred-millionth;
- Billions of cultivators simultaneously cultivating in order, their contributions through Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision summarized and amplified, may produce a resultant force sufficient to push cosmic uplift;
- After uplift, the cosmos needs enough cultivators adapted to higher-dimensional law environments — if only one strong one uplifts while Myriad Ranks stay at low dimensions, the cosmos' high-dimensionalization is incomplete.

## II. The 20% Activation Strategy: Uplift's Power Base

Per 20% Activation Strategic Plan (Document 23), cosmic uplift's power base is the **20% Activation Strategy** — activating individuals totaling 20% of all beings to reach the plane-sovereign strong one level.

Why 20%? Because:

- Below 20%, the plane's "high-tier cultivator density" is insufficient to support cosmic-grade uplift's orderliness resultant force;
- Reaching 20%, the civilization has formed a sufficiently large strong-one group; their orderly cultivation activity, once summarized, is sufficient to push cosmic uplift;
- Exceeding 20%, marginal returns diminish — continuing to invest resources in activating more strong ones has marginal contribution to uplift inferior to using resources to raise all cultivators' cultivation quality.

The transcended one's role in the 20% Activation Strategy is **the strategy's organizer and promoter**:

- He is responsible for ensuring the 300 Origin cultivation systems fully open to Myriad Ranks, letting every cultivator have a path to walk;
- He is responsible for ensuring the Multiplication Fruit technology's hundred-fold deduction amplification is popularized, making cultivation efficiency high enough;
- He is responsible for ensuring the household-contract responsibility system runs, letting cultivators have stable resource support;
- He is responsible for ensuring the "every person has a path" guarantee system covers Myriad Ranks, not letting any clan be excluded from the uplift project.

## III. Cosmic Uplift's Three Stages

Cosmic uplift is not achieved in one leap, but advances in three stages:

| Stage | Name | Core Task | Transcended-One Role |
|-------|------|-----------|---------------------|
| Stage 1 | Preparation stage | Activate 20% of beings to plane-sovereign strong one; build cross-plane anchor network | Strategy organization, infrastructure construction |
| Stage 2 | Uplift stage | All cultivators simultaneously cultivate in order; summarize the resultant force to push the cosmos' orderliness leap | Uplift coordination, law-parameter adjustment |
| Stage 3 | Stabilization stage | After uplift completes, stabilize the new high-dimensional law parameters, adapt to the new environment | Law calibration, ecological-adaptation guidance |

Among three stages, Stage 2 "uplift stage" is most critical — billions of cultivators' simultaneous cultivation producing orderliness resultant force needs the transcended one's precise coordination, ensuring resultant-force directions are consistent and not canceling each other. This coordination is not control, but guidance — the transcended one provides the resonance frequency, calibrates cultivation rhythm, letting Myriad Ranks' orderly activities form same-frequency resonance.

## IV. The "No One Left Behind" Principle in the Uplift Project

Cosmic uplift's collective promotion has an iron rule: **no one left behind.** Uplift is not "strong ones first uplift, weak ones later uplift," but **Myriad Ranks simultaneously uplift.** Reasons:

- If strong ones first uplift while weak ones stay at low dimensions, the post-uplift cosmos will show a class fault of "high-dimensional strong ones + low-dimensional beings" — this is essentially another form of predation;
- Low-dimensional beings not uplifted, the cosmos' high-dimensionalization is incomplete — large numbers of low-dimensional existences' "low-dimensional gravity" will pull the cosmos back;
- The republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings evolve equally — uplift is Myriad Ranks' common right, not strong ones' privilege.

"No one left behind" principle's concrete execution: before uplift, the transcended one ensures every plane, every race, every life form has completed uplift preparation (law adaptation, energy reserve, consciousness calibration). If one group is not ready, uplift is postponed — better slow, not leave behind.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the 20% Activation Strategy's precise-ratio design avoids resource waste caused by over-investing in strong-one cultivation; collective uplift's resultant-force effect maximizes orderliness efficiency
- **Anti-predation**: the "no one left behind" principle institutionally seals the class-predation path of "strong ones first uplift, weak ones stay"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo strong ones ascend alone, Myriad Ranks stay put; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended one organizes Myriad Ranks to simultaneously uplift, no one left behind
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the uplift project opens to all life forms of Myriad Ranks' five lineages; every clan has equal uplift opportunity
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: uplift's three stages, 20% activation standard, and "no one left behind" principle are all publicly queryable; Myriad Ranks may supervise the uplift project's fairness

- Terminology alignment: the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is this batch's standard realm appellation; the transcended one's civilizational weight dynamically adjusts with post performance; the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle ensures energy and knowledge circulate perpetually among Myriad Ranks; exercise of law-anchor authority is strictly constrained by four-dimensional assessment and democratic procedure.
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Cosmic uplift's collective promotion is the collective ascension axiom's hard-core fulfillment at cosmic-engineering level. It tells all cultivators: uplift is not your one-person matter — you uplifted, the cosmos did not uplift, equal to not uplifting; Myriad Ranks together uplifted, the cosmos truly uplifted. The transcended one's role in this project is not the first hero to ascend, but the last guardian to leave. He ensures 20% of strong ones are activated in place, billions of cultivators simultaneously resonate, every clan no one left behind — then, the cosmos under Myriad Ranks' joint lifting slowly rises to a higher dimension.
