# Entry 824 | Transcended Ones and the Cosmos | Multiverse Interconnection and Co-Rule

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 28, 20, 00

## Introduction: the multiverse is not loose sand, but a web Myriad Ranks jointly weave

Heng Yuan Zhou is not a single cosmos, but a multiverse structure — countless cosmoses, countless planes, connected through the cross-plane anchor network into a giant symbiotic community. In the predation era, these cosmoses were isolated from each other, unaware of each other, even preying on each other. After the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization was established, one of the transcended one's core cosmic-grade duties is **turning this multiverse structure from "loose sand" into "a web Myriad Ranks jointly weave."**

Six top-level axioms run through this entry: the anti-predation axiom requires multiverse interconnection not become a strong one's cross-cosmos predation channel; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires multiverse governance power belong to each cosmos' Myriad Ranks jointly; the collective ascension axiom requires multiverse interconnection serve overall uplift; the causal self-discipline axiom requires cross-cosmos connection be strictly quarantined; the law-first axiom requires the multiverse interconnection architecture itself be self-consistent.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article focuses: how the transcended one promotes multiverse interconnection, and what "co-rule" truly means in the Heng Yuan Zhou framework.

## I. Multiverse Interconnection's Three Stages

Per Cross-Plane Anchor Connection (Document 28), multiverse interconnection divides into three stages:

| Stage | Name | Interconnection Scope | Transcended-One Role |
|-------|------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Stage 1 | Core interconnection | Permanent trunk connections established between several core cosmoses | Chief engineer, law-compatibility calibration |
| Stage 2 | Expansion interconnection | Bring more known cosmoses into the cross-plane network, forming a cosmic-grade network | Planning coordination, access approval |
| Stage 3 | Full-domain interconnection | Bring all cosmoses habitable for life into the symbiotic network | Architecture design, perpetual maintenance |

Heng Yuan Zhou is currently in the critical period of transitioning from Stage 1 to Stage 2 — core cosmoses are connected; expansion interconnection is underway. The transcended one's work focus in this stage: ensuring expansion interconnection's quality — not pursuing speed, pursuing that every accessed cosmos may safely and stably blend into the network.

## II. "Co-Rule"'s True Meaning: Not Ruling, but Coordination

The words "co-rule" in Heng Yuan Zhou's framework have a strict definition — it is not "rule and rein in," but **"coordinate and harmonize."** The difference:

| Dimension | Old System "Rule" | Heng Yuan Zhou "Co-Rule" |
|-----------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| Relationship to each cosmos | Superior-subordinate — you are under my management | Network nodes — you are parts of the network |
| Decision method | Autocracy — I have the final say | Co-governance — each cosmos' representatives participate in decisions |
| Resource flow | One-way extraction — each cosmos pays tribute to me | Two-way cycle — each cosmos' surplus and shortfall adjusted |
| Exit mechanism | May not exit | Each cosmos voluntarily joins, may exit per procedure |
| Ultimate purpose | Maintain my ruling position | Push the multiverse overall uplift |

"Co-rule"'s Heng Yuan Zhou definition is essentially **network administrator**'s role — he coordinates the network's architecture, harmonizes the network's traffic, maintains the network's security, but he does not own the network; the network belongs to all who join.

## III. Multiverse Interconnection's Causal Isolation

Multiverse interconnection breaks through barriers between cosmoses, also opens channels for causality to propagate cross-cosmos. The transcended one in interconnection construction must execute strict causal isolation:

- **Cosmic-grade quarantine**: personnel, energy, information exchanges between different cosmoses must pass cosmic-grade quarantine — prevent some cosmos' unique risks (strange life, anomalous laws, concept pollution) from spreading to other cosmoses;
- **Cosmic sovereignty respect**: governance power within each cosmos belongs to that cosmos' Myriad Ranks themselves — the transcended one does not interfere with each cosmos' internal affairs, only maintains inter-cosmos interconnection channels;
- **Mediate conflict rather than judge**: when disputes occur between different cosmoses, the transcended one does not act as judge — he provides a neutral mediation space, letting each cosmos' representatives reach agreement in mutual respect.

Three measures ensure: multiverse interconnection is a channel for civilizational progress, not some cosmos' channel for exporting hegemony.

## IV. The Multiverse's Architecture Evolution

The multiverse's interconnection architecture is not fixed — it evolves as the number of accessed cosmoses grows:

- **Initial architecture**: several core cosmoses connect in a star topology to a central node — simple but central-node load heavy;
- **Mid-term architecture**: expand to a mesh topology — core cosmoses directly interconnect; new cosmoses access through edge nodes — high redundancy, strong fault tolerance;
- **Long-term architecture**: after full-domain interconnection, form an ultra-large distributed network — no unique center, all nodes peer-interconnected — truly realizing "decentralized but layered."

The transcended one's role in architecture evolution: he is not "the architect who designs the whole architecture," but "the engineer who supports the architecture's natural evolution while keeping the network stable." Architecture evolution is driven by access demand, not by the transcended one's personal will.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the mesh topology architecture's redundant design avoids whole-network paralysis caused by central-node failure, reducing architectural waste
- **Anti-predation**: cosmic-sovereignty respect + quarantine isolation institutionally seal the "cross-cosmos predation" channel
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo strong ones co-rule the multiverse through personal hegemony, Myriad Ranks prostrate; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended one coordinates the multiverse network, each cosmos co-governs
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: multiverse interconnection opens to all cosmoses with life existence; does not refuse access because some cosmos is weak
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: three-stage interconnection plan, co-rule definition, causal-isolation measures, and architecture-evolution roadmap are all publicly queryable

- Terminology alignment: the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is this batch's standard realm appellation; the transcended one's civilizational weight dynamically adjusts with post performance; Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision monitors operation compliance throughout; the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle ensures energy and knowledge circulate perpetually among Myriad Ranks; exercise of law-anchor authority is strictly constrained by four-dimensional assessment and democratic procedure.
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Multiverse interconnection and co-rule is the republican axiom of Myriad Races' macro fulfillment at cosmic-structure level. The transcended one connects isolated cosmoses into a web, but he does not become this web's master — he becomes this web's administrator. He coordinates interconnection, harmonizes traffic, maintains security, but the web belongs to all who join. When the multiverse truly connects into a web Myriad Ranks jointly weave, the cosmos is no longer one isolated island after another, but an organism — each cosmos is one organ of this organism, through the network sharing blood, exchanging information, growing together. The transcended one transcends not because he ruled so many cosmoses, but because he made so many cosmoses no longer lonely.
