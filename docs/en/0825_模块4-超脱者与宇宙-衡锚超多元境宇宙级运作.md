The Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is **the starting point of cosmic-grade operation**: entering this realm is not "you may finally rest," but "you finally begin the real work."

Six top-level axioms as conclusion are again run through in this entry: the anti-predation axiom, the republican axiom of Myriad Races, the collective ascension axiom, the causal self-discipline axiom, the law-first axiom — five axioms are not the cultivator's "entrance exam," but the transcended one's "lifelong work code." Entering the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is not passing an exam, but receiving a lifelong post.

This article focuses: the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's cosmic-grade operation panorama — what the transcended one does every day, why, and for how long.

## II. Cosmic-Grade Operation's Daily Rhythm

The transcended one's work has no "nine-to-five" — the cosmos does not rest, neither does he. But this does not mean he runs 24 hours nonstop. His operating rhythm is **rhythmic**:

- **Daily maintenance rhythm**: law calibration, channel inspection, consciousness-domain monitoring — these are routine work ongoing every day; automated systems handle most, the transcended one only handles anomalies;
- **Periodic engineering rhythm**: new-plane access, new-cosmos weaving, law revision — these are large projects advanced per standard cycle, with clear timetables;
- **Event-response rhythm**: plane crises, law anomalies, external invasions — these are unpredictable sudden events; the transcended one responds at any time.

Three rhythms stack to form the transcended one's work rhythm. He is not always busy — most of the time, automated systems handle routine work; he only needs to focus on anomalies and projects. But he is always "on post" — because he is a cosmic-grade guardian; when the cosmos has a problem, he is the first responder.

## III. Why "Eternal Post": The Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's Essence

The Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is defined as the "perpetual-post realm" (see entry 710) for the core reason: **cosmic-grade operation has no completion date.** Laws always need calibration, planes always need guardianship, civilization always needs maintenance, uplift always needs promotion — this work cannot be finished, nor should it be.

If cosmic-grade operation one day were "finished," that would mean:

- The law network is so perfect it needs no maintenance — but the law network is always evolving, always needing calibration;
- Plane civilization is so perfect it needs no guidance — but civilization is always developing, always needing direction;
- Cosmic uplift is so complete it needs no promotion — but the cosmos' uplift is infinite; every dimension has a higher dimension.

So the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcended one is always working. Not that he is "trapped in" this realm, but he **chooses** to stay at this post — because he knows, if he stops, the cosmos begins to age; if he slacks, Myriad Ranks lose a layer of guardianship. This "always working" is not forced toil, but proactive taking-up — he chooses to be the cosmos' gardener because he loves this garden.

## IV. Cosmic-Grade Operation's View of Happiness: Why Eternal Work Is Happiness

The old system's imagination of "eternal work" is painful — always working either kills you with exhaustion or bores you to death. Heng Yuan Zhou's understanding of eternal work is entirely different:

- **Work content does not repeat**: the cosmos is evolving, Myriad Ranks are evolving, planes are developing — the transcended one faces new problems every day. He is not repeating the same operation, but continuously responding to new challenges.
- **Work results are perceptible**: the laws he calibrates guard Myriad Ranks' cultivation; the channels he maintains connect Myriad Ranks' exchanges; the uplift he promotes lets Myriad Ranks evolve — every piece of his work has real, perceptible results.
- **Work partners are not lonely**: he is not working alone — billions of cultivators are cultivating, thousands of transcended ones are collaborating, Myriad Ranks are co-evolving. He is one member of this giant community.
- **Work meaning does not dry up**: his work serves Myriad-Rank symbiosis and cosmic uplift — this is an infinite meaning source, not drying up because "it is finished."

Eternal work is not punishment, but **the highest-grade happiness** — because it turns "work" from a livelihood means into an existential meaning. The transcended one does not need to "retire," because his work is his life.
