# Entry 828 | Transcended Ones and Civilization | Civilizational Correction and Prevention of Historical Regression

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 08, 26

## Introduction: civilization fears not taking detours most, but going back to the old road

In the old system, civilizational development often shows "historical regression" — hard-won civilizational progress, because some strong one rises, some institution fails, some war breaks out, all reverses. Back to slavery, back to predatory cultivation, back to class solidification. Once such regression occurs, how many epochs' civilizational accumulation all clears to zero. Heng Yuan Zhou takes "preventing historical regression" as one of civilizational construction's core tasks. **The transcended one's role in it is the civilizational-correction engineer — he does not forbid civilization from taking detours, but ensures detours walked may be returned from, old roads walked may be pulled back.**

Six top-level axioms in this entry centrally embody the causal self-discipline axiom: cross-plane operations strictly report, observe first then limitedly interfere, thoroughly rooting out civilizational regression, historical reversal, reincarnation harvesting, and class solidification. The anti-predation axiom is correction's direction — regression toward predation is absolutely not allowed; the republican axiom of Myriad Races is correction's subject — correction is participated by Myriad Ranks, not autocratically decided by the transcended one; the collective ascension axiom is correction's goal — pulling the regressed civilization back onto the uplift track; the law-first axiom ensures the correction procedure itself is self-consistent.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

This article focuses: how the transcended one recognizes civilization's wrong direction, how he executes correction, and how he prevents historical regression.

## I. Historical Regression's Five Typical Patterns

Per Anti-Predation Core Thesis (Document 04), historical regression has five typical patterns:

| Regression Pattern | Manifestation | Root Cause |
|--------------------|---------------|------------|
| Pattern 1: institutional regression | Myriad-Rank republican institutions abolished; dictatorship or hegemon rule restored | Power concentration out of control |
| Pattern 2: cultivation regression | Anti-predation cultivation system abandoned; predatory cultivation restored | Short-cut inner demon recurrence |
| Pattern 3: class solidification | Evolution channels re-monopolized; Myriad Ranks' 99% coverage collapses | Established-interest groups blockade |
| Pattern 4: knowledge blockade | Public knowledge library privatized; cultivation system again becomes a few's patent | Knowledge monopoly recurrence |
| Pattern 5: causal harvesting | Strong ones again begin cross-plane predation, reincarnation harvesting of the weak | Predation ideology resurgence |

Five patterns' common root: **old-era inertia.** The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization has not been established long enough; the old era's predation ideology, institutional memory, and behavioral inertia have not completely disappeared. Once civilization loosens, old inertia raises its head.

## II. The Correction Mechanism's Three Defense Lines

The transcended one's prevention of historical regression has three defense lines:

| Defense Line | Mechanism | Executing Subject |
|--------------|-----------|-------------------|
| First defense line | Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's real-time early warning | Automated monitoring system |
| Second defense line | Myriad-Rank Republican Deliberative Assembly's institutional checks and balances | Myriad-Rank representatives |
| Third defense line | The transcended one's limited intervention and correction | The transcended one (plane-sovereign strong one or above) |

Three defense lines' design logic: **most regression sprouts are stopped at the first and second defense lines; the transcended one's third defense line is the last resort, not the first resort.** If everything requires the transcended one personally strike to correct, it means the first two defense lines have failed — that is a bigger problem.

## III. The Transcended One's Correction Procedure

When regression sprouts break through the first two defense lines, the transcended one launches the third defense line — limited intervention and correction. The procedure strictly follows the causal self-discipline axiom:

1. **Observation confirmation**: the transcended one first through Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision comprehensively observes the regression's nature, scope, severity — do not rush to strike; see clearly first.
2. **Myriad-Rank consultation**: after observation completes, consult with the affected plane's Myriad-Rank representatives — correction is Myriad Ranks' matter; the transcended one is only an assistor. If Myriad-Rank representatives themselves are already correcting, the transcended one does not intervene.
3. **Limited intervention**: if Myriad Ranks themselves cannot correct (e.g., regression has become institutionalized, established-interest groups too powerful), the transcended one conducts limited intervention — the intervention target is to restore institutional balance, not to overthrow and start over.
4. **Aftermath repair**: after correction completes, assist Myriad Ranks in repairing damage caused by regression — affected cultivators, destroyed energy cycle, polluted consciousness domain.
5. **Prevention reinforcement**: analyze why the regression happened, why the first two defense lines did not stop it; reinforce weak links — prevent next time's regression.

Five-step procedure's core: **observe first, then consult, limitedly interfere, aftermath-repair, prevention-reinforce.** The transcended one does not rush in and overthrow everything — what he does is minimal intervention, helping Myriad Ranks themselves pull civilization back on track.

## IV. Why Regression Cannot Be "Once-and-For-All" Prevented

Some ask: since regression is so dangerous, why not institutionally lock it dead, making regression impossible? Heng Yuan Zhou's answer: **cannot.** There are three reasons:

- **Myriad Ranks' freedom is civilization's core value.** If institutions are locked dead to where Myriad Ranks have no room to make mistakes, then Myriad Ranks lose their right to autonomous choice — this itself is another form of oppression.
- **Regression's root is in people's hearts, not in institutions.** Institutions may limit predatory behavior, but cannot eliminate predatory thought. Predatory thought may only be dissolved through education, culture, and example, not locked dead by institutions.
- **Correction itself is civilization's learning process.** If civilization never makes mistakes, never corrects, it will never learn how to self-correct. Appropriate mistakes and corrections are necessary stages of civilizational evolution.

So the transcended one's way of preventing historical regression is not "make regression impossible," but "let regression, once it occurs, be corrected as soon as possible, and after correction not repeat."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: real-time early warning ensures regression is discovered early; correction cost is lowest; prevention reinforcement avoids repeated waste caused by the same regression recurring
- **Anti-predation**: five regression patterns all point to predation resurgence; the correction mechanism's core is pulling predation back to symbiosis
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo civilizational regression is forcibly suppressed by the protagonist's personal power, treating symptoms not the root; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended one through three defense lines + five-step procedure helps Myriad Ranks self-correct
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the correction procedure faces all Myriad-Rank clans — any clan's regression sprouts will be monitored; each clan has participation right during correction
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: five regression patterns, three defense lines, and five-step correction procedure are all publicly queryable; Myriad Ranks may supervise whether correction is fair

- Terminology alignment: the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is this batch's standard realm appellation; the transcended one's civilizational weight dynamically adjusts with post performance; the Myriad-Origin two-way cycle ensures energy and knowledge circulate perpetually among Myriad Ranks; exercise of law-anchor authority is strictly constrained by four-dimensional assessment and democratic procedure.
- **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity — infinite origin inherent in all; predation naturally loses its meaning

## Conclusion

Civilizational correction and historical-regression prevention is the causal self-discipline axiom's institutional fulfillment at the civilizational dimension. The transcended one is not civilization's police — he does not watch Myriad Ranks every day catching mistakes. He is civilization's gardener — he pulls weeds as they sprout, treats pests as they appear, but he lets the garden grow by itself. He knows: civilization will make mistakes; what matters is not not making mistakes, but being able to correct mistakes after making them, and not repeating them after correcting. When three defense lines and the five-step procedure run perpetually, historical regression is no longer civilization's death sentence, but civilization's growing pains — having suffered, having learned, civilization becomes stronger.
