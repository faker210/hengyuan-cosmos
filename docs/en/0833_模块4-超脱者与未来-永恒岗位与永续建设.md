## I. Eternal Post's Definition: Not "trapped at post," but "choosing to stay at post"

The words "eternal post" in the old system sound like a curse — eternally trapped in work, unable to be liberated. Heng Yuan Zhou's definition of eternal post is entirely different:

- **Not trapped**: the transcended one may at any time choose to exit the post — his civilizational weight declines, his power wanes, his will changes, any may hand over the post. No chains lock him.
- **It is choice**: he chooses to stay, because he sees this work's meaning — Myriad Ranks need him, the cosmos needs him, he himself has found existential value in this work.
- **It is growth**: eternal post is not "forever doing the same thing" — the cosmos is changing, Myriad Ranks are changing, problems are changing, his work content is always updating. He continuously grows in work, not consumed in repetition.

Three elements together: **eternal post is perpetual taking-up under free choice.** He may leave, but he chooses to stay; he may rest, but he chooses to work; he may monopolize power, but he chooses to serve Myriad Ranks. This is the words "eternal" true meaning — not forced, but voluntary; not endpoint, but road.

## III. The Transcended One's "Successor" Mechanism

Eternal post is not "the same person forever doing it." The transcended one's perpetual construction needs generations of transcended ones' relay. This requires a **successor mechanism**:

- **Cultivating juniors**: every transcended one has the duty to cultivate the next generation of transcended ones — guiding promising cultivators to complete consciousness uplift (entry 809), accumulate civilizational weight, pass four-dimensional assessment (entry 817);
- **Post handover**: when the current transcended one for any reason needs to leave the post (power wane, will change, new-post need), he hands over work to a successor who has already passed assessment — the handover process is smooth, transparent, Myriad Ranks informed;
- **Experience transmission**: experience, lessons, and insights the transcended one accumulated at the post are archived through Alaya Network (entry 830) — the successor does not need to grope from zero; he stands on his predecessor's shoulders.

The successor mechanism ensures: **perpetual construction does not depend on some specific transcended one's eternal existence.** Transcended ones age, leave, hand over — but the post always has someone; perpetual construction never stops. This is network-style transcendence at the time dimension's fulfillment: does not depend on any one person; the system runs perpetually.
