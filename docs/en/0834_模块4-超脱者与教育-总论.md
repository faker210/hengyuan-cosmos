# Entry 834 | Transcended Ones and Education · Overview — The Transcended One's Education System

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 37, 08, 781, 720

## Introduction: the transcended one's education system is the master valve of "Myriad Ranks have a path"

The strongest in the old world never ran education. His Dao came through sudden enlightenment; written out, others cannot understand; his experience was forged between life and death; spoken out, others cannot learn. So Myriad Ranks could only "gaze at the imperial visage," guessing on their own — what they guessed was mostly predation. Heng Yuan Zhou completely flips this: **the strongest not only must teach, but must build teaching itself into a self-consistent, replicable, perpetually iterating system.** This entry is the "Transcended Ones and Education" sub-theme overview, answering one overall question: what does the transcended one's education system look like as a whole, what does it rely on to run, and why is it the anti-predation civilization's first public defense line.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: **the anti-predation axiom** requires education not hide private things, not monopolize, not use knowledge as a private tool; **the republican axiom of Myriad Races** requires education open to 99% of all Myriad-Rank beings, regardless of race, gender, origin, or life form; **the collective ascension axiom** requires education's ultimate purpose be lifting Myriad Ranks forward into evolution, not cultivating a few solo-flying strong ones; **the causal self-discipline axiom** requires education content and process be traceable and supervisable, not disturb Myriad Ranks' causality under education's name; **the law-first axiom** requires this education system itself be law-level self-consistent setting, infinitely scalable without book-eating or unfinished ending.

Among them, **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law** is the sixth pillar: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. The old cosmos' resources and Great Dao are limited, necessarily leading to predatory involution; the Anchor Symbiotic everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why the Transcended One's Education Is a "System," Not "A Few Teachers"

The old world also had teachers — sect elders, family elders, secret-realm inheritors. But that was not a system; it was master-apprentice private transmission: one master takes three to five disciples; disciples each take three to five; the Dao goes with the person; the person dies, the Dao extinguishes. Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended-one education is called a "system" because it simultaneously has four traits the old world never had:

1. **Textbook-ification**: every step of the transcended one's insight, every breakthrough, every pitfall must be organized into structured teaching material — with prerequisites, steps, cases, assessment, applicable boundaries, opened network-wide through the Selfless Dao. Textbooks are not quotation-style, but reusable engineering documents.
2. **Post-ification**: teaching is not the transcended one's amateur good deed, but his legal post duty as a Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm officeholder (see entry 720 "Transcended-One Training and Lineage Post"). Apprentice-taking quotas, teaching hours, textbook output all count into civilizational-weight assessment.
3. **Network-ization**: education does not depend on some famous teacher. Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision networks every transcended one's teaching data, students' growth curves, textbook revision records, forming a retrievable, iterable, correctable public-knowledge network.
4. **Myriad-Rank-ization**: textbooks and teaching methods must adapt for Myriad Ranks' five lineages (carbon-based matter lineage, energy-condensed lineage, mechanical-constructed lineage, consciousness-information lineage, hybrid-mutated lineage) each life form; may not only teach the carbon-based human set.

Four traits together, the Dao no longer "goes with the person" — **the Dao is deposited into the system; people may rotate; the system is perpetual.**

## II. The Education System's Four-Layer Depth

Benchmarking Symbiotic Education System (Document 37)'s four-level structure, the transcended-one education system overlays a "hyper-multiverse depth," forming four layers:

| Layer | Object | Transcended-One Intervention | Correspondence with Nine Realms |
|-------|--------|-------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Enlightenment layer | All Myriad-Rank underage beings | Provide free public textbooks, remote teaching | Sense Realm — Condensed Realm |
| Foundation layer | Those who have completed enlightenment | System-compatibility assessment, public cultivation sites open | Transform Realm — Law Realm |
| Advanced layer | High-potential cultivators | One-on-one mentoring, deep law research | Dao Origin Realm — Dao Integration Realm |
| Hyper-multiverse lineage layer | Would-be transcended ones | On-post mentoring, cross-plane actual combat, civilizational-weight assessment | Boundary-Breaking Realm — Origin Realm — Multiverse Realm — Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm |

The first three layers are "bringing Myriad Ranks through the door"; the fourth layer is "bringing later-comers into new transcended ones." Without the fourth layer, the transcended-one ranks cannot be perpetual; without the first three layers, the fourth layer is a tower in the air — it must grow from Myriad Ranks' universal-evolution soil. This is the collective ascension axiom's educational expression: **the strong do not ascend alone, but also teach Myriad Ranks the "method of ascending."**

## III. How the Education System Cuts Off the "Short-Cut Inner Demon"'s Student Source

The transcended-one education system's most hidden yet critical function is guarding against beings' origin greed · short-cut inner demon. Myriad-Rank beings on the cultivation road are most easily tempted by "quick results," "devouring others," "back doors." When the right road is clear enough, easy enough, fair enough, the short-cut inner demon's temptation drops sharply.

Heng Yuan Zhou therefore adheres to one iron rule: **if transcended ones do not open the door, predators will open it; if transcended ones do not teach the right road, evil roads will grow weeds.** The education system's completeness directly determines the short-cut inner demon's student-source scale. This is not a moral call, but civilizational-security engineering — the thicker the textbooks, the more mentors, the lower the threshold, the fewer "believers" predators can dig.

## IV. The Education System and Alaya Network + Multi-Layer Supervision

Education itself is also a cross-plane, cross-generation causal operation, so it must accept the causal self-discipline axiom's constraints. The transcended one's teaching content, apprentice records, textbook revisions all leave traces under Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision:

- Who taught, whom, what textbooks used, whether the student went astray, all traceable;
- Once a textbook is revised, the old version is archived and queryable, eliminating "plot-hijacking-the-setting"-style temporary course changes;
- If a student is found to be taking a predatory route, the system automatically traces his textbook source and mentor-guidance records, cutting off the pollution chain;
- Teaching-ethics review: prohibits any transcended one under the name "teaching according to aptitude" from actually privately hoarding knowledge.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the transcended one's insights if not textbook-ified dissipate with the individual; textbook-ification lets one insight serve Myriad Ranks for ten-thousand years, eliminating great-talent-wasted
- **Anti-predation**: education not hiding private things directly seals the "knowledge monopoly" predatory path; if you do not teach, others will snatch by predatory means
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo favor system transmitted the Dao only to Tang Luo alone, relying on Gu Chensha's grace, ceiling locked; Heng Yuan Zhou requires transcended ones textbook-ify the Dao then open it to all Myriad-Rank beings; the education system perpetually iterates
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: four-layer depth explicitly requires developing adapted textbooks for five lineages' each life form, ensuring 99% coverage
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: all textbooks, teaching methods, assessment standards open through the Selfless Dao network-wide, zero threshold, zero fee, zero discrimination; part of the "basic manuals → Sacrifice Dao → multiverse-grade → Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm" full-open chain's educational segment

## Conclusion

The transcended one's education system is the four words "Myriad Ranks have a path"'s master valve. It deposits the strongest one's insights into Myriad Ranks' reusable public knowledge; upgrades master-apprentice private transmission into a post-ified, network-ized, Myriad-Rank-ized perpetual project. When the strongest must be a teacher, being a teacher must be a system, being a system must be supervised, civilization will not hand "evolution" back to predation and monopoly. This is the transcended one's orthodox definition at the education dimension.
