# Entry 835 | Transcended Ones and Education | Establishment of the Transcended-One Training Academy

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 37, 17, 720, 834

## Introduction: an academy is not "a cave-dwelling sect taking disciples," but engineering batch-producing qualified transcended ones

The old world cultivated the next generation of strong ones by "cave-dwelling sect taking disciples" — one strong one occupies a mountain, takes three to five closed-door disciples, resources privately transmitted, lineage transmitted by mood. The result: once the strong one dies, the lineage breaks; whether the disciple succeeds depends entirely on the master's whim. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants to cultivate is not "some strong one's inheritor," but **generation after generation of qualified transcended-one post officeholders.** This requires running an academy — turning "how to become a transcended one" from private experience into public engineering. This entry explains why the Transcended-One Training Academy must be established, by what standards, and what prevents the academy itself from degrading into a new sect monopoly.

Six top-level axioms all take effect: **the anti-predation axiom** requires the academy not run sect private hoarding, not monopolize resources, doors open to Myriad Ranks; **the republican axiom of Myriad Races** requires enrollment not ask origin or race, only potential and heart-nature; **the collective ascension axiom** requires the academy's output be not "stronger individuals," but "more qualified co-building post-holders"; **the causal self-discipline axiom** requires the academy's teaching and assessment throughout traceable; **the law-first axiom** requires the academy's cultivation standard itself be law-level self-consistent, not casually changed by plot needs.

Among them, **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law** is the sixth pillar: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. The old cosmos' resources and Great Dao are limited, necessarily leading to predatory involution; the Anchor Symbiotic everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Necessity of Establishment: Why "Cave-Dwelling Sect Taking Disciples" Can No Longer Continue

Leaving "cultivating transcended ones" to private cave-dwelling sects, the old world left three bloody lessons:

1. **Lineage dies with the person**: the strong one dies from sudden enlightenment; the disciple may not catch it; a millennium of lineage returns to zero overnight. This is the largest source of energy-level waste — one insight served one person, then dissipated forever.
2. **Selection by mood**: whom to take as disciple depends entirely on the master's likes and dislikes; truly good seedlings may be missed because "not pleasing"; opportunists rise through taking sides.
3. **No unified standard**: what counts as a "qualified transcended one"? No unified standard. The result is a batch of "strong but without responsibility" individuals — combat power enough, but heart-nature and civilizational-weight assessment not passing, ultimately becoming hang-up-retirement-style strong ones (see entry 729 critique series).

The fundamental reason for academy establishment is to engineer these three things: **lineage deposition, selection standardization, unified qualification line.**

## II. The Academy's Positioning and Admission

The Transcended-One Training Academy is not an ordinary elite school; it is the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's "post-qualification" training and certification institution. Its positioning has three:

- **The hyper-multiverse lineage layer's main entrance**: takes high-potential cultivators from the nine realms' mid-to-late stage (Dao Origin Realm and above), delivering qualified seedlings to Boundary-Breaking, World-Creation, Origin, Multiverse Realm, even Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm;
- **Post-duty training ground**: students do not only study cultivation, but also intern at posts — law-network maintenance, Myriad-Rank evolution guidance, Alaya-Network supervision, civilizational correction and other post rotations (corresponding to entries 711–720's post-duty system);
- **Qualification-line certification office**: students graduating must pass the "plane-sovereign strong one's four-dimensional assessment" (Power 40 / Heart-nature 25 / Contribution 20 / Responsibility 15, see Document 17), none may be missing.

Admission standards have only two hard lines: **potential meets standard** and **heart-nature meets standard.** The former looks at cultivation talent and system-compatibility assessment; the latter looks at whether there is a predatory record and whether one identifies with the republican axiom of Myriad Races. Origin, race, wealth, gender are never thresholds — this is the republican axiom of Myriad Races' direct landing.

## III. The Academy's Curriculum Structure

The academy's curriculum is not "deeper manuals," but four modules' combination:

| Module | Content | Proportion | Corresponding Axiom |
|--------|----------|-----------|---------------------|
| Power module | Hyper-multiverse-grade manuals, law weaving, new-cosmos frontier technology | 40% | Collective ascension |
| Heart-nature module | Anti-predation ethics, short-cut-inner-demon recognition, beings' origin greed · causal self-discipline | 25% | Anti-predation |
| Contribution module | Post internship, Myriad-Rank feedback, increment-development actual combat | 20% | Collective ascension |
| Responsibility module | Civilizational-weight assessment, supervision obedience, historical-correction awareness | 15% | Causal self-discipline |

This proportion directly copies the plane-sovereign strong one's four-dimensional assessment — **power is only four-tenths; heart-nature, contribution, responsibility together are six-tenths.** The academy from curriculum structure itself negates the old definition of "strong is transcendence": a student with full combat power but without responsibility and contribution cannot graduate.

## IV. Three Locks Preventing the Academy from Degrading into a New Sect

The academy itself may also degenerate. The old world's sects are from "teaching community" degraded into "resource-monopoly organization." Heng Yuan Zhou for this applied three locks:

1. **Faculty rotation lock**: transcended ones teaching at the academy is post duty, term system, rotated at term end; not allowed to long-term occupy the lectern forming "faction." Teaching results count into civilizational weight, but seniority is not ranked by number of disciples.
2. **Resource-open lock**: all academy manuals, textbooks, teaching videos open through the Selfless Dao network-wide; off-campus cultivators and on-campus students enjoy equal knowledge access. The academy only provides "guidance and practical training," does not monopolize "knowledge itself."
3. **Alaya supervision lock**: enrollment, assessment, graduation whole process leaves traces under Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision. Any "pre-decided," "recommended admission," "discriminating against some race" anomaly signals auto-trigger the Anchor Supervisory Committee's investigation.

Three locks combined, the academy is a "public practical-training ground," not "some faction's private school."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the academy deposits the strong one's private lineage into standardized courses; letting one insight perpetually serve Myriad Ranks, eliminating "person dies Dao extinguishes" maximum waste
- **Anti-predation**: three locks seal the academy's degradation into sect-monopoly possibility; knowledge opens network-wide, no sect private hoarding
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: under the Tang-Luo favor system, the next generation of strong ones through Gu Chensha's one-time grace succeeds; no standardized cultivation, ceiling locked; Heng Yuan Zhou uses the academy to engineer and perpetually batch-produce qualified transcended ones
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: enrollment only looks at potential and heart-nature, not race origin; curriculum adapts for Myriad Ranks' five lineages' life forms
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: academy knowledge opens network-wide, practical-training post rotations public, graduation standard four-dimensional queryable

## Conclusion

Establishment of the Transcended-One Training Academy marks "how to become a transcended one" from private experience into public engineering. It uses standardized selection, four-dimensional assessment, and three locks to ensure output is not "stronger individuals," but "qualified co-building post-holders." When the strongest's cultivation may also be engineered, supervised, and perpetually iterated, the transcended-one ranks will not break generations; Myriad Ranks will always have qualified leaders.
