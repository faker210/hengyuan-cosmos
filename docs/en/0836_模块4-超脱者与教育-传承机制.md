# Entry 836 | Transcended Ones and Education | The Transcended One's Lineage-Transmission Mechanism

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 25, 710, 720, 781

## Introduction: lineage does not die with the person is the transcended one's transmission first principle

The old world's most bitter loss is not war, but "person dies Dao extinguishes" — what one strong one spent a lifetime comprehending goes with him into the grave; later-comers must stumble over the same pit anew. Heng Yuan Zhou was able to push civilization to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm not by some strong one's dramatic emergence, but by a **lineage-transmission mechanism that lets the Dao flow perpetually.** This entry does not discuss the academy (that is batch cultivation), but specifically discusses "how the Dao itself is transmitted among transcended ones, between transcended ones and later-comers" — transmission mechanism's legal basis, structure, and anti-generation-break design.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: **the anti-predation axiom** requires transmission not hide private things, not run "transmit inside not outside"; **the republican axiom of Myriad Races** requires transmission face all qualified ones, not choose inheritors by bloodline or sect; **the collective ascension axiom** requires transmission's purpose be letting later-comers stand on predecessors' shoulders to continue uplift, not becoming predecessors' shadows; **the causal self-discipline axiom** requires the transmission process leave traces, preventing "off-track lineage" from polluting descendants; **the law-first axiom** requires the transmission mechanism itself be a stable self-consistent law-level institution, not collapsing due to personal handover.

Among them, **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law** is the sixth pillar: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. The old cosmos' resources and Great Dao are limited, necessarily leading to predatory involution; the Anchor Symbiotic everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Transmission Mechanism's Three-Layer Structure

Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended-one transmission divides into three layers simultaneously, none dispensable:

1. **Knowledge-layer transmission — textbook network**: the transcended one's insights must be textbook-ified, deposited into the Selfless Dao's public textbook library. This layer does not depend on any specific person; anyone may retrieve. This is "the Dao does not extinguish"'s chassis.
2. **Method-layer transmission — master-apprentice mentoring**: textbooks alone cannot teach the "feel." Every on-post transcended one bears a fixed apprentice quota (baseline 3–5 per term), hand-in-hand bringing later-comers through key breakthrough points — those judgments textbooks cannot fully write, only felt in real decisions.
3. **Post-layer transmission — succession handover**: the transcended one as a Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm perpetual-post officeholder (entry 710), his post duty is not private estate, may not be "his when he dies." The post must have a succession echelon; before leaving the post, complete knowledge, authority, and relationship-network full handover.

Three layers stack: the knowledge layer ensures "the Dao does not extinguish"; the method layer ensures "the Dao is learnable"; the post layer ensures "the post is not vacant." The old world only had the second layer (and did it poorly), the third layer completely absent — so once a strong one falls, the whole system empties.

## II. Succession System: A Post Is Not Private Estate, May Not Be Monopolized

The transmission mechanism's hardest rule is **the post-succession system.** It must answer a question the old world never answered: when a transcended one "retires" or "ascends," who takes his post?

Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is: post-echelon system. Every transcended post has 1 principal, 2–3 alternates, several reserves; alternates auto-promote through post rotation and civilizational-weight assessment. When the principal leaves, follow the standard handover process:

- Authority transfer: law-anchor authority, Alaya-Network supervision authority, cross-plane dispatch authority transfer per procedure, no back doors left;
- Relationship transfer: Myriad-Rank clan representatives, cross-plane anchor contacts, in-progress project list, item-by-item handover;
- Cognition transfer: unwritten judgments, historical pitfalls, unresolved contradictions, archived in "successor memorandum" form.

This directly seals the old world's "sole decision over eons" mode — you leave, the post remains, order does not collapse.

## III. Why Not Run a "Single Inheritor"

The old world favors "the sole inheritor": an unparalleled strong one transmits his lifelong cultivation and robe-bowl only to one disciple. Heng Yuan Zhou explicitly opposes:

- **Single-point failure risk**: if the sole inheritor midway goes astray, falls, or has corrupt heart-nature, the whole lineage breaks. This is betting civilization's future on one person.
- **Heart-nature monopoly risk**: the sole inheritor is indoctrinated from childhood with "you are heaven-chosen"; extremely easy to breed beings' origin greed · short-cut inner demon and "protagonist-uniqueness dependency."
- **Myriad-Rank exclusion risk**: choosing only one inheritor equals excluding all other qualified ones among Myriad Ranks.

Heng Yuan Zhou's approach is **multiple mentoring, echelon succession, open competition.** Lineage is not some person's "inheritance," but a public river — anyone may flow in, no one may monopolize.

## IV. Transmission and Causal Self-Discipline: Preventing "Off-Track Lineage" from Polluting Descendants

Transmission is cross-generational causal operation. If some transcended one's insights carry untested bias, even residual predatory thinking, and he transmits it without textbook-ification and supervision, it will pollute generation after generation of later-comers. Heng Yuan Zhou's solution:

- Before textbooks enter the library, they must pass Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's "ethics and self-consistency review"; content conflicting with fundamental laws may not enter the library;
- The apprentice-mentoring process throughout leaves traces; the master's every key guidance is traceable; once the master is found to go astray, cut the pollution chain in time;
- The student has the right to raise objection to the master's guidance, submitting to the Symbiotic Court for review — the student is not the master's private estate.

This way, transmission is not "replicating another me," but "handing the tested Dao to a younger, possibly stronger generation."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: three-layer transmission lets one insight flow perpetually, eliminating "person dies Dao extinguishes" — the old world's largest energy-level waste
- **Anti-predation**: multiple mentoring, open competition, seal "single inheritor"-style lineage monopoly; knowledge layer opens network-wide
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's Dao through Gu Chensha's one-time grace to Tang Luo; no echelon, no succession; once the protagonist changes, generations break; Heng Yuan Zhou uses post-echelon system to let lineage be perpetual
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: transmission faces all qualified ones, not selected by bloodline or sect; alternates selected from Myriad Ranks
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: textbook layer opens network-wide, succession standards and handover process public, Alaya supervision queryable

## Conclusion

The transcended one's transmission mechanism answers "how does civilization not regress when a strong one dies." It uses knowledge-layer, method-layer, post-layer triple structure; uses echelon succession to replace single inheritor; uses Alaya supervision to cut off off-track lineage. Lineage is no longer some person's private estate, but a public river Myriad Ranks jointly build and flow perpetually. When the strongest ages, retires, uplifts, while the Dao and post are perpetual, civilization truly owns a future not kidnapped by any individual's fate.
