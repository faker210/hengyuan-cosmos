# Entry 837 | Transcended Ones and Education | The Transcended One's Guidance of Youth Supremes

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 08, 23, 37, 713

## Introduction: the sharpest seedling most needs to be led onto the right road

Youth Supreme — a being who in youth already shows extraordinary cultivation talent, breakthrough speed far exceeding peers — is Myriad Ranks' evolution's sharpest, and also most dangerous, force. The old world had only two attitudes toward Youth Supremes: either snatch them as thugs, cauldrons, "luck-sons" to harvest; or crown them "heaven-chosen," letting them swell, ultimately letting them grow into new predators. Heng Yuan Zhou's attitude is a third: **acknowledge their talent, guide their heart-nature, forge the sharpest seedling into a sword of co-building, not a blade chopped at Myriad Ranks.** This entry specifically discusses how the transcended one guides Youth Supremes, and why this guidance itself is the anti-predation civilization's key project.

Six top-level axioms centrally embody: **the republican axiom of Myriad Races** requires the Youth Supreme's talent belong not to him personally, still less to some force, but to Myriad Ranks' public evolutionary resource; **the anti-predation axiom** requires never treating Youth Supremes as "cauldrons" or "luck" to harvest; **the collective ascension axiom** requires guidance's purpose be letting him lift Myriad Ranks forward together, not ascend alone; **the causal self-discipline axiom** requires guidance of Youth Supremes throughout traceable, preventing exploitation; **the law-first axiom** requires the "quick + quest" dual-route design itself be self-consistent, not opening back doors for the protagonist by plot needs.

Among them, **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law** is the sixth pillar: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. The old cosmos' resources and Great Dao are limited, necessarily leading to predatory involution; the Anchor Symbiotic everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Youth Supremes Are Dangerous

The Youth Supreme's danger is not in his strength, but in his "speed." The faster the breakthrough, the more easily heart-nature polishing is skipped; the more he is coddled, the more easily "I am extraordinarily talented" is misread as "I may be the exception." A large number of predatory protagonists in the old world grew up from the Youth Supreme road:

- **Short-cut inner demon's high-incidence zone**: the higher the talent, the more easily "I am special," "taking short cuts is only natural" greed breeds;
- **Monopolizers' prey**: old sects, old clans rush to take him, essentially snatching a "future combat-power asset," turning him into private property;
- **Post-swelling disaster**: if a Youth Supreme reaches the peak quickly without accomplished heart-nature, what backfires is not only him, but the whole plane around him.

Therefore, **guidance of Youth Supremes the earlier the better** — before his heart-nature solidifies, teach clearly the questions of "where does power come from, where does it go."

## II. Dual-Route Design: Quick and Quest in Parallel

Heng Yuan Zhou adopts for Youth Supremes the "quick + quest" dual route (this is Youth-Supreme-exclusive design, see Module 1 related entries). The transcended one's guidance is to let these two lines neither be biased nor neglected:

| Route | What It Solves | How the Transcended One Guides |
|-------|----------------|--------------------------------|
| Quick route | Seize the talent window, quickly solidify realms, not waste genius | Provide optimal textbooks, simulated training, precise Q&A; use the Multiplication Fruit deduction engine to help him avoid detours |
| Quest route | Prevent "fast but empty"; arrange at key nodes slow trials, ordeals, and responsibility | After his breakthrough, arrange post internship, Myriad-Rank service, independently facing causal trials, polishing heart-nature |

Quick is not spoiling-to-death; quest is not making things difficult. Missing either: only quick raises "strong but without responsibility" monsters; only quest wastes genius's talent window. The transcended one's mastery lies in **letting him go fast, while going correctly.**

## III. Guidance's Three Iron Rules

The transcended one guiding Youth Supremes has three insurmountable iron rules:

1. **Do not feed "exclusive food"**: never treat private resources or private manuals given only to this youth as "cultivation." What he may learn, other Myriad Ranks may learn; what public resources he may use must be within public standards. Talent may be tilted, but knowledge may not be privately hoarded.
2. **Do not enclose "uniqueness"**: never tell him "you are heaven-chosen," "you are the one." Such words are beings' origin greed · short-cut inner demon's best nourishment. What Heng Yuan Zhou wants to cultivate is "a leader who runs one step ahead among Myriad Ranks," not "the sole protagonist."
3. **Do not indulge swelling**: if a Youth Supreme shows predatory tendency or corrupt-heart-nature signs, the transcended one has the duty to immediately intervene — suspend his acceleration, arrange heart-nature assessment, if necessary report through Alaya supervision. Guidance is not "accompany him to grow stronger," but "ensure he goes right."

## IV. Guidance and the 20% Activation Strategy

Youth Supremes are one of "first activating that 20%" core objects in the 20% Activation Strategy (Document 23). Leading this batch of high-potential seedlings onto the right road early has the highest input-output ratio: a correctly guided Youth Supreme after adulthood may drive his clan and plane's universal evolution.

But tilting is not privilege. What Youth Supremes enjoy is "obtaining guidance earlier," not "monopolizing guidance." He goes fast because of good talent + correct method; he has no qualification to deprive other slower beings' road — this is the "every person has a path" bottom line entry 839 will discuss.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: guiding Youth Supremes early, directing the sharpest talent to co-building, avoiding genius growing into predators or being harvested by old forces, eliminating the largest talent waste
- **Anti-predation**: three iron rules seal the predatory path of "treating Youth Supremes as cauldrons, private property, heaven-chosen to harvest"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: under the Tang-Luo favor system, Youth Supremes through Gu Chensha's one-time feeding to the top; no fast-slow dual line, no heart-nature assessment; Heng Yuan Zhou uses quick + quest dual route, both protecting talent and polishing heart-nature
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Youth Supremes' talent is viewed as Myriad Ranks' public evolutionary resource; guidance faces each clan's Youth Supremes, not exclusive to humans
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: textbooks Youth Supremes may learn open network-wide; the tilting he enjoys is a "time window," not a "knowledge privilege"

## Supplement: Youth-Supreme Guidance's Post Metering and Supervision

This guidance is by no means the transcended one's momentary whim good deed, but his post duty as a Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm officeholder. Apprentice quotas, guidance duration, heart-nature-assessment pass rate all count into his civilizational weight — guidance proper, Youth Supreme goes right, weight rises; guidance improper, seedling goes astray, weight falls. The entire guidance process leaves traces under Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: who is guided by whom, at what node received heart-nature assessment, whether predatory signs appeared, all traceable. This both avoids the transcended one "taking disciples by preference," and prevents Youth Supremes from being led astray by some master's bias. The Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm perpetually having qualified leaders relies precisely on this metered, supervised guidance system, not on accidentally appearing geniuses.

## Conclusion

The Youth Supreme is the fastest wind in Myriad Ranks' evolution. The wind itself has no direction; it may blow up harvest, and may also overturn the roof. The transcended one's guidance of Youth Supremes is to install a rudder on this fastest wind: acknowledge his talent, accelerate his growth, and more nail his heart-nature onto Myriad Ranks' republican right road. When the sharpest seedling is forged into a sword of co-building, not a blade chopped at Myriad Ranks, civilization truly grips its own future.
