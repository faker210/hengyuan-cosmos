# Entry 838 | Transcended Ones and Education | The Transcended One's Knowledge Popularization to Myriad Ranks

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 18, 50, 69, 781

## Introduction: if knowledge does not go to the countryside, Myriad Ranks are forever at the foot of the mountain

The old world's knowledge has "walls." Top manuals hidden in sect forbidden grounds; core insights locked in strong ones' secret chambers; even "how to cultivate," the most basic matter, had to be bought by kowtowing, risking life, acting as a grandson for half a sentence. Myriad Ranks' bottom beings do not not want to evolve; they simply do not know "the road exists." Heng Yuan Zhou tears down this wall — **the transcended one must not only write out what he has comprehended, but actively deliver it to every Myriad-Rank being's doorstep.** This entry specifically discusses the "knowledge popularization" segment: how the transcended one translates hyper-multiverse-grade knowledge into something Myriad Ranks may reach and use.

Six top-level axioms centrally embody **the republican axiom of Myriad Races**: all beings own equal evolutionary qualification; knowledge is this qualification's precondition; **the anti-predation axiom** requires the Great Dao full-level open, no private hoarding no monopoly; **the collective ascension axiom** requires knowledge popularization serve Myriad Ranks' universal uplift; **the causal self-discipline axiom** requires popularization process not mislead, not incite; **the law-first axiom** requires knowledge expression self-consistent and accurate, not spreading half-baked settings.

Among them, **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law** is the sixth pillar: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. The old cosmos' resources and Great Dao are limited, necessarily leading to predatory involution; the Anchor Symbiotic everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Popularization Is Not "Alms," But Civilizational-Security Engineering

Calling knowledge popularization "care for the weak" is still condescending. Heng Yuan Zhou's positioning of knowledge popularization is harder: **it is civilizational-security engineering.**

- The more widespread knowledge, the fewer the short-cut inner demon's student source — when the right road is known to all, no one walks the evil road;
- The more widespread knowledge, the thicker Myriad Ranks' "universal-evolution chassis" — the anchor network's effectiveness positively correlates with accessed beings' quantity and quality (Document 08);
- The more widespread knowledge, the harder class solidification — once the bottom knows the road exists and may obtain textbooks, they will not long stay in "abandoned" state.

So popularizing knowledge is not the transcended one's good deed, but his responsibility for civilization's overall stability.

## II. Knowledge Popularization's Four Tiers

The transcended one faces Myriad Ranks' five lineages, countless life forms and cognitive levels. Popularization may not be one-size-fits-all; it divides into four tiers:

| Tier | Object | Form | Goal |
|------|--------|------|------|
| Enlightenment tier | Beings not yet entered the door | Diagrams, stories, sensory demonstrations | Let them know "the road exists" |
| Entry tier | Newly entered cultivators | Structured textbooks, basic methods, online Q&A | Let them truly start walking |
| Advanced tier | Those with some realm | Systematic courses, simulated training, mentor matching | Let them walk steadily, not astray |
| Frontier tier | Research-type cultivators | Hyper-multiverse-grade manuals, law-engineering documents | Let knowledge continue pushing forward |

Four tiers share the same knowledge source (Selfless Dao public textbook library), only expression depth differs. This ensures: a bottommost plant spirit, and a topmost researcher, read the same set of Dao — only the former reads the "entry version," the latter the "complete version."

## III. "Translating" for Myriad Ranks' Five Lineages

Popularization's greatest technical difficulty is **translation.** Carbon-based humans' textbooks, plant spirits cannot read; energy-condensed lineage's cultivation descriptions, mechanical-constructed lineage cannot execute. The transcended one must take the lead, translating knowledge into each life form's receivable format:

- **Carbon-based matter lineage**: conventional text, diagrams, hands-on demonstrations suffice;
- **Energy-condensed lineage**: textbooks need translation into "frequency-rhythm" consciousness-state expression;
- **Mechanical-constructed lineage**: need translation into "parameter-instruction" structure-state expression;
- **Consciousness-information lineage**: need direct connection to the consciousness domain, using intent rather than text to transmit;
- **Hybrid-mutated lineage**: need to develop compatible multi-lineage composite expression, customized per life form.

Translation is not "translating language," but "translating life forms." This only the transcended one taking the lead may do fully — this is also why knowledge popularization is the transcended one's legal duty, not something ordinary teachers may independently complete.

## IV. Popularization and Causal Self-Discipline: Do Not Mislead, Do Not Incite

Knowledge popularization also has risks. If expression is improper or taken out of context, hyper-multiverse-grade knowledge may be misunderstood and abused. Heng Yuan Zhou therefore requires:

- All popularization content is consistent with formal textbooks; not "simplified to distortion" in transmission;
- Popularization materials reviewed by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision; versions that may induce predation, incite antagonism, or mislead causality may not be published;
- Popularization to Myriad Ranks only returns "knowledge," does not make decisions on behalf — each being still chooses which road to walk (see Document 87 "knowledge-only return principle").

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: let sleeping Myriad-Rank potential be awakened, universally access the anchor network, thicken civilizational-evolution chassis
- **Anti-predation**: knowledge openness itself is anti-predation — once the wall falls, the "knowledge private hoarding" predatory path is gone
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's core knowledge relies on Gu Chensha's grace; ordinary people do not even know the road; Heng Yuan Zhou actively delivers knowledge to every Myriad-Rank being's doorstep
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: four tiers + five-lineage translation ensure 99% coverage; plant spirits, planetary consciousnesses, consciousness-information lineage may all reach it
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: all textbooks open through the Selfless Dao network-wide; tier versions share one source; frontier knowledge sets no threshold

## Supplement: Knowledge Popularization's Metrics and Endpoint

Popularization is not "after distributing textbooks done"; it has its own metrics and endpoint. The anchor network counts each plane's, each clan's textbook reach rate, mastery rate, advancement conversion rate; these data flow into the transcended one's civilizational-weight assessment — broader coverage, higher conversion, heavier weight. Popularization's endpoint is not "everyone has read," but Myriad Ranks' 99% truly "have a road to walk, have a will to walk." When a most remote plant-spirit clan may also free-retrieve adapted textbooks and complete system-compatibility assessment, popularization truly lands. This is also the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's "hyper-multiverse" meaning — it is not only the strong ones' realm, but the degree to which Myriad Ranks as a whole are illuminated by knowledge.

## Conclusion

If knowledge does not go to the countryside, Myriad Ranks are forever at the foot of the mountain. The transcended one's knowledge popularization to Myriad Ranks is turning "evolution right" from a promise into something every being may truly obtain. It does not rely on alms, but on engineered four tiers and cross-lineage translation; does not rely on slogans, but on Alaya supervision preventing misleading. When every Myriad-Rank being knows the road exists, may obtain textbooks, and can understand, "Myriad Ranks have a path" changes from words on the wall into a road underfoot.
