# Entry 842 | Transcended Ones and Technology | Perpetual Iteration of Multiplication-Fruit Technologization

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 16, 11, 61, 68

## Introduction: why one "opening buff" can evolve into a perpetual-evolution engine

In the old worldview, an "opening buff" is one-time: the protagonist picks up a treasure, eats a divine pill, has fun in the early stage, later still has to cultivate hard. Heng Yuan Zhou completely transforms the Multiplication Fruit "opening buff" into something else — it is not only opening fun, but technologized into a **perpetually iterating deduction engine**, becoming the whole civilization's evolution accelerator. This entry explains: how the transcended one turns the Multiplication Fruit from "one person's cheat" into Myriad Ranks' shared, self-iterating public infrastructure.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: **the anti-predation axiom** requires this accelerator not be monopolized, shared network-wide; **the republican axiom of Myriad Races** requires it serve Myriad Ranks, not only strong ones' private cheat; **the collective ascension axiom** requires its iteration direction point to common uplift; **the causal self-discipline axiom** requires deduction results not be directly used as action basis, must be reality-validated; **the law-first axiom** requires the engine's multiplication mechanism be self-consistent and stable, not collapse with version iteration.

Among them, **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law** is the sixth pillar: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. The old cosmos' resources and Great Dao are limited, necessarily leading to predatory involution; the Anchor Symbiotic everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. From "Fruit" to "Engine" Technologization Leap

Document 61 makes it clear: the Multiplication Fruit is not for eating, but walks the three steps of "law extraction — mechanism analysis — engineering reconstruction." Its essence is locked as **exponential amplification of information-processing efficiency** — within the same time, completing a hundred-fold parallel deduction path. This leap's key is:

- Transform "personal special ability from eating the fruit" into "public compute anyone may call";
- Transform "one-time stat spike" into "continuously outputting deduction acceleration";
- Transform "protagonist-exclusive cheat" into "whole-civilization evolution infrastructure."

This is why it is an "opening buff" but not "used up at opening" — after being engineered, every generation is getting stronger.

## II. Three-Tier Deployment and Perpetual Iteration

The deduction engine adopts a "core — array — network" three-tier architecture (Document 61), and on this basis perpetually iterates:

| Deployment Tier | Parallelism | Served Object | Iteration Direction |
|-----------------|-------------|---------------|---------------------|
| Personal-terminal type | 100x base value | Individual cultivator | Optimize portability, lower consciousness load |
| Research-center type | 1000x+ | Research institutions | Expand array scale, fuse more laws |
| Anchor-integrated type | Global | Anchor network globally | Provide global deduction capability for the whole network |

"Perpetual iteration"'s key is: the engine not only runs faster and faster, but also returns every deduction's data to the anchor network, feeding itself with Myriad Ranks' real cultivation data. It is not a static machine, but a **public intelligence that gets smarter the more it is used.**

## III. Why It Is "Perpetual" Rather Than "One-Time"

Multiplication-Fruit technologization's ability to be perpetual roots in what it serves — the cultivation system itself — being infinitely iterable:

- The deduction engine helps cultivators verify paths; verification results are returned, optimizing next deduction;
- The engine helps research accelerate law discovery; new laws in turn become new deduction material;
- The engine helps crisis rehearsal; rehearsal experience deposits into new safety boundaries.

This is a positive feedback: **used more → data thicker → deduction more accurate → evolution faster → produces more data.** As long as Myriad Ranks are still evolving, this engine always has an iteration direction. It is not a "use-and-discard" opening prop, but civilization's perpetual-motion construction (from entry 721) compute heart.

## IV. Ethics Boundary: The Accelerator May Not Be Used to Open Evil Roads

The stronger the accelerator, the more we must hold the ethics red line (Document 68). Heng Yuan Zhou's boundaries on deduction-engine use:

- **Do not deduce predation**: the engine may not be used to deduce predatory cultivation paths or solutions harming others;
- **Do not replace reality**: deduction results must be reality-validated before execution; "using deduction to replace actual combat" is prohibited;
- **Do not overload**: personal-terminal parallelism sets an upper limit, preventing individual deduction overload from damaging consciousness;
- **Supervise throughout**: high-risk deductions (cross-plane, time-rewind type) proceed under Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision.

The accelerator is for "walking the right road faster," not "walking the evil road faster."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: transforming a one-time opening buff into a perpetual accelerator turns a hundred-fold deduction capability from personal cheat into public infrastructure
- **Anti-predation**: engine shared network-wide, does not deduce predatory paths, seals "using acceleration cheat to monopolize evolution" predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's ability relies on Gu Chensha's one-time bestowal, ends when used; Heng Yuan Zhou's engine perpetually iterates, stronger and more accurate the more used
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: personal-terminal type faces all cultivators, adapted per life form, not exclusive to strong ones
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: engine architecture, parameters, and ethics boundaries all public, continuously iterating as civilization upgrades

## Supplement: Engine Publicization and Weight

After the deduction engine changes from "personal cheat" to "public infrastructure," every iteration no longer belongs to some person. The engine's deployment breadth, cultivation time saved for Myriad Ranks, and demonic-obsession accidents avoided are all converted into the transcended one's civilizational weight — the more inclusive and safer this engine, the higher the weight of the transcended one driving it. The engine's ethics boundaries are also written into Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: once someone is found using it to deduce predatory paths, immediately suspend the account and trace back. Precisely because this smarter-and-smarter machine is publicized, supervised, and metered, it will not degenerate into the strong ones' monopolized acceleration privilege, but truly become the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's shared evolution heart.

## Conclusion

Multiplication-Fruit technologization's perpetual iteration is Heng Yuan Zhou's "opening buff" philosophy's ultimate demonstration: not leaving magical power to the protagonist alone, but decomposing it into mechanisms, engineering it into an engine, opening it to Myriad Ranks, then letting it self-iterate on Myriad Ranks' data. When an accelerator that gets smarter the more it is used serves everyone, civilization's evolution speed enters a positive loop. This is what an "opening buff" should look like in a symbiotic civilization — not pleasure for a moment, but benefiting Myriad Ranks and iterating forever.
