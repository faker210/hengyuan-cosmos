## Introduction: the most dangerous experiments must be done in a "cage"

Once any civilization is strong enough to operate laws, it holds dynamite sufficient to destroy itself. The old worldview never faces this squarely — the protagonist casually emits power and shatters half a continent, yet no one ever asks "what if the experiment explodes?" Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is: **outside the main plane, build a law-isolated "cage."** This is isolation space. This entry explains: how the transcended one uses isolation-space technology to cage the whole civilization's adventure, experiment, and exploration safely.

Six top-level axioms centrally embody **the causal self-discipline axiom** and **the law-first axiom**: high-risk operations must proceed within controlled space; isolation degree is a law-level parameter; **the anti-predation axiom** requires dangerous experiments not harm innocent Myriad Ranks; **the republican axiom of Myriad Races** requires safety be protection for all beings; **the collective ascension axiom** requires adventure's purpose still be common uplift.

Among them, **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law** is the sixth pillar: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. The old cosmos' resources and Great Dao are limited, necessarily leading to predatory involution; the Anchor Symbiotic everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. What Isolation Space Is: Three Isolations

Document 64 defines it clearly: isolation space is an independent space instance outside the main plane, with high law-isolation degree. It achieves "three isolations":

- **Law isolation**: laws operating inside do not permeate the main plane; main-plane law changes do not interfere inside;
- **Matter isolation**: no natural matter-exchange channel; all entry/exit goes through controlled interfaces;
- **Consciousness isolation**: internal consciousness activity is not directly perceived by the main plane's consciousness domain, preventing consciousness pollution.

Standard isolation space uses seven law barriers (matter block, energy attenuation, law filter, consciousness shield, time buffer, spatial lock, anchor fixation), achieving 99.97%+ comprehensive isolation degree. **Its essence is installing a leak-proof valve on civilization's curiosity.**

## II. Civilizational Applications: Isolation Space Is Not Only a Laboratory

Isolation space's applications in hyper-multiverse civilization far exceed "doing experiments":

| Application Scenario | How Used | Whom Protected |
|----------------------|----------|----------------|
| High-risk law experiments | Test new laws in B-grade+ isolation space; even if it explodes, does not pollute the main plane | Main-plane Myriad Ranks |
| Replication-Mirror mirror reconstruction | Replicas are rebuilt in A-grade isolation space; research does not affect the original | The alien land under study |
| Strange-energy containment | Dangerous energy contained in isolation space, does not leak out | Everything around |
| New-civilization rehearsal | Simulate a new institution / new law in isolation space, then promote after validation | Real civilization |

## III. Time-Flow Rate: Civilization's "Safe Accelerator"

One undervalued capability of isolation space is **adjustable time-flow rate** (1:100 to 100:1). This lets the transcended one do what the old world could not:

- An experiment requiring a century to validate runs a millennium in isolation space; only a decade passes in the main plane;
- A new institution's "trial run" walks through several generations in isolation space; the main plane is unscathed;
- Dangerous reactions may be "slowed down," leaving time to handle.

Time-flow rate stacked with isolation degree, isolation space becomes civilization's "safe sandbox" — **trial freely; trial-and-error cost goes to zero.** This is entry 765's technical confidence opposing "crude colonial-style frontier": want to open a new cosmos? First simulate it running ten thousand years in isolation space.

## IV. Safe Destruction: The Cage Must Also Be Dismantlable

Isolation space is not a permanent building; it has a retirement process. Technology ethics requires:

- After experiments complete, replicas are safely destroyed per standard process, leaving no residue;
- At retirement, first dispose of internal contents, then lift barriers layer by layer, finally reclaim the space anchor;
- After destruction, run residue scan, confirming no space debris leaked back to the main plane.

"Buildable" also must be "dismantleable clean" — otherwise isolation space itself becomes a new pollution source.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: freely trial-and-error in isolation space, downgrading "one failure destroys civilization" high risk to bearable experiment cost
- **Anti-predation**: dangerous experiments caged, do not harm innocent Myriad Ranks, do not use others as experiment cost
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style strong ones casually emit power shattering continents, never considering experiment safety; Heng Yuan Zhou uses isolation space to cage all high-risk
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: isolation space protects all beings in the main plane; personal cultivation buffer rooms also open to Myriad Ranks
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: isolation-space principle, seven barriers, time-flow rate, and destruction process all public

## Supplement: The Cage's Operation and Weight

Isolation space is not "build then leave alone"; its operation itself is the transcended one's post. Every isolation space's isolation degree, time-flow rate, destruction records are real-time monitored under Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision; any barrier-leak sign immediately alerts. The transcended one operating isolation space and caging dangerous experiments is counted into civilizational weight by the civilizational risk avoided — the steadier the protection, the higher the weight. This set of mechanisms lets the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm dare to think and dare to try, yet always trial in a safe sandbox. The cage is not a chain restraining curiosity, but a runway letting curiosity run wildly without worry.

## Conclusion

Isolation-space technology is a powerful civilization's "safety redundancy." It tells Myriad Ranks: true transcendence is not "strong enough to do whatever one wants," but "strong enough to know how to cage danger." When the transcended one's every high-risk operation proceeds in isolation space, every trial-and-error does not harm the innocent, every abandoned space is dismantled clean, civilization grips the balance of power and restraint. This is the causal self-discipline axiom's final landing in physical space.
