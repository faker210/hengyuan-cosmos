# Entry 845 | Transcended Ones and Technology | The Fusion of Transcended Ones and Technological Civilization

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 21, 60, 766, 840

## Introduction: cultivation and technology are the same thing in Heng Yuan Zhou

In the old worldview, cultivation and technology are two mutually exclusive roads: either cultivate immortals, or build starships; choose one and despise the other. Cultivators think technology is "side door"; technologists think cultivation is "superstition." Heng Yuan Zhou completely heals this rift — **cultivation is technology, technology is cultivation; the transcended one is the chief engineer of both's fusion.** This entry concludes the "Transcended Ones and Technology" sub-theme, explaining why this fusion is inevitable, what civilization looks like after fusion, and what prevents fusion imbalance.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: **the anti-predation axiom** requires the fused technological civilization not monopolize or privatize; **the republican axiom of Myriad Races** requires fusion serve all Myriad-Rank beings; **the collective ascension axiom** requires fusion push common uplift; **the causal self-discipline axiom** requires high-risk operations in fusion be supervised; **the law-first axiom** requires the interface between cultivation and technology be self-consistent at the law layer, not written as a collage of "cultivating immortals while opening cheats."

Among them, **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law** is the sixth pillar: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. The old cosmos' resources and Great Dao are limited, necessarily leading to predatory involution; the Anchor Symbiotic everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, predation naturally loses its meaning.

Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Cultivation and Technology Must Fuse

Fusion is not chasing fashion, but mechanism dictates it. Documents 60 and 21 reveal: cultivation's essence is law operation; technology's essence is also law operation —

- Cultivators perceive laws with the body; technology scans laws with arrays;
- Cultivators break bottlenecks with comprehension; technology validates in parallel with deduction engines;
- Cultivators use consciousness to affect energy; technology uses interfaces to connect consciousness to the network.

Both study the same set of cosmic underlying code; only tools differ. Heng Yuan Zhou only connects two sets of tools: **cultivation provides "why"; technology provides "how to build"; cultivation provides "boundaries"; technology provides "amplification."** Walked separately, each has a ceiling; fused together, they enter the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm.

## II. Civilizational Form After Fusion: Cultivation Is Infrastructure

Once fusion completes, civilization's form changes:

| Old Era | After Fusion (Heng Yuan Zhou) |
|---------|-------------------------------|
| Cultivation is personal private matter | Cultivation is public infrastructure; methods free, interfaces public |
| Technology is another road | Technology is cultivation's amplifier; multiplication engine available to all |
| Strong ones alone in closed-door cultivation | Strong ones engineer at posts, leave traces in the network |
| Curiosity = stepping in and destroying | Curiosity = Replication Mirror + isolation-space low-pollution exploration |
| Power = private estate | Power = civilizational weight; distributed by contribution, supervised by Myriad Ranks |

In one sentence: **cultivation is no longer "one person becoming stronger"; technology is no longer "another group building things"; the two combine into one "Myriad Ranks jointly build" assembly line.**

## III. Fusion's Three Interfaces

Fusion concretely lands through three interfaces:

1. **Perception-scan interface**: the cultivator's sensory capability is technologized into array scanning, letting law information be read by machines;
2. **Enlightenment-deduction interface**: personal sudden enlightenment is replaced by the multiplication engine's parallel deduction; enlightenment changes from "a century of closed-door cultivation" to "a hundred days of validation";
3. **Consciousness-network interface**: individual consciousness connects to the collective through Alaya Network; personal insights deposit into public textbooks at any time.

Three interfaces together are entry 748 "three-hundred systems coordination" at the technical layer — three-hundred cultivation systems are no longer three isolated islands, but welded by technology into one web.

## IV. Preventing Fusion Imbalance: Technology May Not Overpower Cultivation

Fusion also has risks. The biggest risk is "technology overpowering cultivation" — everything handed to machines; people no longer cultivate, no longer comprehend, no longer bear responsibility; civilization degenerates into compute puppets. Heng Yuan Zhou's defense line:

- Technology only amplifies, does not replace: deductions must be reality-validated; interfaces must be personally called by cultivators;
- Frontier creation still relies on people: new cosmoses, new axioms' opening still requires the transcended one to comprehend in responsibility;
- Alaya supervision: the more powerful the technology, the stricter the heart-nature and contribution assessment of users.

Fusion's purpose is "making people stronger," not "making people exit the stage."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: cultivation and technology fusion opens the ceilings of two sets of tools, releasing single-path development waste
- **Anti-predation**: the fused technological civilization shares network-wide, no monopoly, no private hoard
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system cuts cultivation from technology; power relies on grace; Heng Yuan Zhou integrates cultivation and technology; Myriad Ranks jointly build
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: fused infrastructure opens to all five-lineage beings, interfaces adapted per life form
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: perception, enlightenment, consciousness three interfaces' technical documents all public, infinitely iterating with civilization

## Supplement: Fusion Process's Supervision and Direction

Once fusion accelerates, the more we must prevent two imbalances: "technology overpowering cultivation" or "cultivation resisting technology." Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision continuously monitors: are cultivators still personally comprehending; is technology replacing rather than amplifying the human heart; are interfaces open to Myriad Ranks. The transcended one's results in driving fusion are counted into civilizational weight by its effect of "making people stronger rather than making people exit." This set of supervision ensures fusion always serves Myriad Ranks' full uplift, not turning people into compute appendages. The Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is both powerful and humane precisely because every fusion step is calibrated in direction this way.

## Conclusion

The fusion of transcended ones and technological civilization is Module 4's technology sub-theme endpoint, and also Heng Yuan Zhou's "cultivation is technology" worldview manifesto. It welds personal enlightenment with public engineering, bodily cultivation with the law network, individual sudden enlightenment with Myriad-Rank consensus into one perpetually iterating assembly line. When cultivators no longer despise technologists, technologists no longer question cultivators, and the strongest transcended one is simultaneously the best engineer, civilization truly reaches the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's threshold.
