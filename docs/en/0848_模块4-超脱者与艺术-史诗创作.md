# Entry 848 | Transcended Ones and Art | The Transcended One's Epic Creation

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 70, 93, 847, 858

## Introduction: whoever tells stories holds Myriad Ranks' hearts

The old world's epics were written by hegemons. It praises heroes opening territory, forgets clans crushed; it writes predation as great achievement, writes sacrifice as dust. Myriad Ranks growing up listening to such epics will genuinely believe "the strong predate as a matter of course." Heng Yuan Zhou must rewrite epics — not write a new book "praising the transcended one," but write **Myriad Ranks' real journey from predation to symbiosis** into singable, recognizable, generation-after-generation narrative. This entry explains: why must the transcended one personally create epics, and what is the fundamental difference between his epic and the old world's hero epics.

Six top-level axioms centrally fulfill **the republican axiom of Myriad Races**: the epic's protagonist must be Myriad Ranks, not some hero; **the anti-predation axiom** requires the epic not write predation as great achievement; **the collective ascension axiom** requires the epic point to future common uplift; **the causal self-discipline axiom** requires the epic based on real history, not fabricated deification; **the law-first axiom** requires the epic be self-consistent with chronicles, not written as contradictory romance.

Among them, **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law** is the sixth pillar: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. The old cosmos' resources and Great Dao are limited, necessarily leading to predatory involution; the Anchor Symbiotic everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, predation naturally loses its meaning.

Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Epics Matter

An epic is not a "pleasant story"; it is **civilization's operating system.** What stories a civilization listens to determines what it believes in its subconscious:

- The old world listens to "the single protagonist defying heaven," and expects a unique hero, accepts oligarchy;
- Heng Yuan Zhou must write "Myriad Ranks co-build symbiosis," so Myriad Ranks believe they also have a share.

Epics sink into hearts deeper than law — law tells you "do not rob"; epics make you "not want to rob." This is why entry 859 must discuss "historical responsibility," and the epic is its artistic vehicle.

## II. Transcended-One Epic's Four Narrative Disciplines

The transcended one creating epics is bound by four disciplines:

1. **Myriad Ranks are the protagonist**: do not write "some transcended one alone saves the cosmos"; write "Myriad Ranks co-build, everyone lifts civilization forward." The transcended one in the epic is one of the builders, not the autocratic savior.
2. **True not deified**: the epic is based on chronicle (Document 93) and era division (Document 70)'s real thread; does not fabricate myths like "the transcended one extinguishes Myriad Ranks with one palm." It allows literary quality, but does not violate historical facts.
3. **Do not evade suffering**: the epic does not whitewash the old era's predation and civilizational regression; writes them as lessons, letting Myriad Ranks remember why they cannot turn back.
4. **Point to the future**: the epic does not only tell the past; it must sing out the conviction "Myriad Ranks must still walk forward together," becoming the next generation's spiritual compass.

## III. The Epic's Function: The Casting Mold of Common Memory

The epic in Heng Yuan Zhou has three practical functions:

| Function | Effect | Corresponding |
|----------|--------|---------------|
| Identity | Let each clan see itself in the same epic | Myriad-Rank republicanism |
| Education | Sing axioms and lessons into young people's hearts | Education (entry 834) |
| Cohesion | Provide "why we persist" in crisis | Collective uplift |

A good epic beats ten thousand lectures. It lets Myriad Ranks emotionally "become one family," not just legally "be treated equally."

## IV. Epic and Alaya Supervision: Do Not Fabricate, Do Not Manipulate

Epics act on hearts, therefore are also causal operations. Heng Yuan Zhou requires:

- The epic creation process cross-checks with historical archives; major plots may not be deified out of thin air;
- The epic is not used to incite hatred or provoke clan antagonism — it praises symbiosis, does not render resentment;
- Epic versions are publicly revised with history; old versions archived, eliminating "changing history for plot needs."

Art most easily becomes a tool for manipulating hearts, so it is most supervised.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: cast Myriad Ranks' common journey into an epic, letting generation after generation directly inherit through singing, no need to repeat exploration
- **Anti-predation**: the epic writes predation as lesson not great achievement, cutting "hero = predator" from the narrative root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo epic writes one defying heaven, sole-venerating Myriad Ranks; the Heng Yuan Zhou epic writes Myriad Ranks co-build, everyone lifting
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: each clan has its place in the epic, not written as a single protagonist's monologue
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: epic text, historical evidence, and version revisions all open network-wide

## Supplement: Epic's Historical Supervision and Weight

Epics sing stories, but guard truth. Every key plot of the epic must cross-check with chronicle and era division; this cross-check is completed by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: once excessive deification or predation whitewashing is found, immediately return for revision. The transcended one's epic-creation quality — whether true, whether inspiring Myriad Ranks, whether pointing to the future — is counted into his civilizational weight. Once the epic becomes Myriad Ranks' recognized public narrative, it is no longer some poet's private estate, but the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's spiritual compass passed generation to generation. Precisely because the epic is both tuneful and trustworthy, Myriad Ranks in singing will truly take symbiosis as their own origin and destination.

## Conclusion

Whoever tells stories holds Myriad Ranks' hearts. The transcended one's epic creation is an art project liberating Myriad Ranks from predatory narrative — it no longer praises the autocratic savior, but sings the co-building Myriad Ranks; no longer writes predation as great achievement, but as lesson; no longer serves the strong's self-deification, but serves the next generation's spiritual compass. When Myriad Ranks sing the epic "we walked to today together, and must walk forward together," symbiosis is no longer institution, but bloodline identity.
