## Introduction: the cosmos' underlying layer is "circulation," not "conquest"

The old worldview's cosmology is the conqueror's cosmology: the strong conquer the weak, the higher devour the lower; the whole cosmos is like a food-chain pyramid, lonelier the higher you go. Heng Yuan Zhou's cosmology is completely the opposite — **the cosmos' underlying layer is not the food chain, but bidirectional circulation.** This is symbiosis philosophy. This entry explains: how the transcended one, at the hyper-multiverse scale, constructs "symbiosis" from a moral slogan into a philosophy about the cosmos' essence.

Six top-level axioms centrally embody **the collective ascension axiom** and **the republican axiom of Myriad Races**: symbiosis philosophy's cosmology is these two axioms' metaphysical foundation; **the anti-predation axiom** is symbiosis' direct corollary; **the causal self-discipline axiom** requires respecting circulation at the cross-plane scale, not interrupting it; **the law-first axiom** requires symbiosis cosmology be self-consistent and stable.

Among them, **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law** is the sixth pillar: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. The old cosmos' resources and Great Dao are limited, necessarily leading to predatory involution; the Anchor Symbiotic everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Symbiosis Philosophy's Core: Myriad-Rank Origin Bidirectional Circulation

Documents 02 and 15 reveal symbiosis philosophy's core — **Myriad-Rank origin bidirectional circulation.** Its meaning is:

- Myriad-Rank individuals cultivate, drawing energy from the cosmos origin;
- After individuals complete cultivation, they feed back to the origin, making the origin fuller;
- The fuller the origin, the more Myriad Ranks it can nourish;
- This is a "individual → collective → cosmos → individual" positive cycle.

The old world's model is "stock allocation" — energy is just that much; if you have more, I have less, so one must grab. Symbiosis philosophy's model is "increment circulation" — energy turns more and more through circulation; no need to grab. **Once the cosmology changes, predation changes from "inevitable" to "redundant."**

## III. Why Symbiosis Outperforms Conquest: An Efficiency Argument

Symbiosis philosophy is not only moral preaching; it has a cold efficiency argument:

- **Conquest model's ceiling**: the stock you may devour is limited, and the devouring process damages ecosystems; the more you devour, the less; ultimately exhausted;
- **Symbiosis model's ceiling**: circulation is incremental; the more Myriad Ranks participate, the greater the output; no ceiling;
- **Long-term**: any conqueror ultimately cannot outrun the symbiont — because the symbiont stands on the "turning more and more" curve.

This is entry 853's to-be-expanded "anti-predation ultimate argument" embryo. **Symbiosis is not kinder, it is more sustainable.**

## IV. Symbiosis Philosophy and the Individual's Position

Symbiosis philosophy is most easily misunderstood as "eliminating the individual." Heng Yuan Zhou clarifies:

- Each individual in the circulation is a real link — without individuals, circulation breaks;
- The individual is not asked to "sacrifice himself to fulfill the collective," but "fulfill himself in circulation";
- Document 26's individual-collective balance principle is symbiosis philosophy's guardrail: circulation amplifies the collective, also protects the individual.

Symbiosis is not "Myriad Ranks are one iron plate," but "Myriad Ranks are a living circulation; every cell matters."
