> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 57, 728, 852

## Introduction: why predation is "losing" in the ultimate sense

The old worldview never doubts predation. It defaults: power is taken by grabbing; the strong prey on the weak as a matter of course. All wish-fulfillment protagonists are essentially "high-level predators." What Heng Yuan Zhou must do is not morally scold predation, but philosophically completely prove: **predation is not only shameful, but in the ultimate sense a losing and self-destructive thing.** This entry gives anti-predation philosophy's ultimate argument — why predatory cultivation cannot reach the end, why strong ones who "harvest all beings" are ultimately backfired by causality. This is the entire Module 4's most fundamental theoretical cornerstone.

Six top-level axioms centrally burst forth: **the anti-predation axiom** is the argument object itself; **the causal self-discipline axiom** is the argument's core mechanism; **the collective ascension axiom** is predation's opposite; **the republican axiom of Myriad Races** is what predation would destroy; **the law-first axiom** requires this argument be logically rigorous, self-consistent, and unassailable.

Among them, **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law** is the sixth pillar: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. The old cosmos' resources and Great Dao are limited, necessarily leading to predatory involution; the Anchor Symbiotic everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Predation's Triple Self-Destruct Structure

Why can predatory cultivation (devouring others' energy, seizing resources, harvesting fortune) not reach the top? Because it has a triple self-destruct structure:

1. **Energy-structure self-destruction**: predated energy is "exogenous," incompatible with one's own origin. Document 04 already argues: predatory cultivators' energy has "original sin"; the higher and deeper, the greater the hidden wound; ultimately stuck before the Origin Realm — this is the ceiling root of Tang Luo, Tang San and their ilk.
2. **Causal-structure self-destruction**: every predation leaves a causal account in Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision. The old world says "kill and set fire, gold belts at the waist"; that is because the ledger has not settled; Heng Yuan Zhou's causal law is perpetual — what you harvest will ultimately flow back to you at greater cost. This is entry 859 "historical correction"'s account to be settled.
3. **Ecological-structure self-destruction**: predation damages Myriad-Rank origin bidirectional circulation (entry 852). Every being you devour leaves one less link in the circulation; the more you devour, the more the circulation you depend on dries up. **The predator is digging the root under his own feet.**

## II. Why "Harvesting Fortune" Is the Greatest Self-Deception

The old worldview has a tempting claim: the protagonist harvests Myriad Ranks' fortune, therefore he is strong. Heng Yuan Zhou must burst this greatest self-deception:

- **Fortune is not stock, it is circulation's product.** The better Myriad Ranks are, the more prosperous the circulation, the more abundant the fortune; you harvesting Myriad Ranks is cutting the circulation's source;
- **The stronger the harvester, the greater the backfire.** After he squeezes Myriad Ranks dry, he himself becomes water without a source — Ye Fan's loneliness, Zheng Zha's nothingness after reincarnation harvest, are essentially this;
- **The Dark Substitution Body's lesson** (Document 57): an existence born by devouring others' origin is from the root diseased; however strong, it is a cosmic lesion, not the cosmic direction.

## III. Ultimate Argument: Predation Is Doomed Before the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm

Pushing triple self-destruction to the limit gives anti-predation's ultimate argument:

| Comparison | Predatory Path | Symbiotic Path |
|------------|----------------|----------------|
| Energy | Exogenous, hidden wounds | Endogenous, pure |
| Causality | In debt, must repay eventually | Contribution, accumulating civilizational weight |
| Circulation | Damaged, narrower and narrower | Maintained, wider and wider |
| End point | Exhaustion, loneliness, crash | Perpetuity, symbiosis, uplift |

Conclusion: **the predator may win for a while, but he cannot win to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm.** Because what that realm requires is precisely what he lacks most — circulating with Myriad Ranks, trusted by Myriad Ranks, clean causality. The stronger the predator, the farther he is from that realm. This is why entry 728 judges predatory-school transcended ones essentially "parasitic" — he is not transcended, he is a tumor living off the host.

## IV. This Argument's Practical Significance

This is not a logic game in a study. It directly answers Myriad Ranks' deepest doubt: "the strong prey, what do we do?" Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is: **do not fear; predation in the ultimate sense is doomed to lose.** You do not have to imitate predators to win — walk the symbiotic road and outrun predators long-term. This conviction itself is anti-predation's public defense line.

Anti-predation philosophy's ultimate argument is Heng Yuan Zhou's deepest overturn of the old worldview.
