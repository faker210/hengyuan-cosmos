# Entry 855 | Transcended Ones and Philosophy | The Philosophical Depth of the Law-First Axiom

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 97, 709, 759, 851

## Introduction: even the act of "setting a cosmos" itself must obey laws

Among the five axioms, the most special is **the law-first axiom**. It does not directly talk ethics like the first four; it talks a meta-rule — **laws are above all, including the storyteller himself.** The old worldview's most shameful place is "plot hijacking the setting": the author, for wish-fulfillment, today says the protagonist is invincible, tomorrow lets him suffer a loss; today sets a rule, tomorrow casually breaks it. This is "setting-breaking and rushed ending." Heng Yuan Zhou elevates this into the highest axiom: setting self-consistency is above plot convenience; the worldview may infinitely scale without collapsing. This entry digs deep into this axiom's philosophical depth.

Here the law-first axiom itself is the theme; it simultaneously guards the other five — precisely because law is first, anti-predation, Myriad-Rank republicanism, collective uplift, and causal self-discipline will not have their settings temporarily altered by "plot needs."

Among them, **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law** is the sixth pillar: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. The old cosmos' resources and Great Dao are limited, necessarily leading to predatory involution; the Anchor Symbiotic everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. What "Law-First" Means

The law-first axiom's core has three layers:

1. **Laws are the cosmos' underlying code**: they do not change with any individual will — even the strongest transcended one may not bypass laws to do things (entries 809, 814).
2. **Setting self-consistency is above all**: if a new plot conflicts with existing laws, change the plot, not the law. This is Document 97 Setting-Contradiction Self-Check Table's philosophical source.
3. **May infinitely scale without collapsing**: law-first guarantees the worldview may continuously add new content, new cosmoses, new levels, yet always be logically self-consistent.

Why is it an "axiom" not a "suggestion"? Because as long as laws may be casually changed by the strong, the entire cosmos' certainty is gone — Myriad Ranks cannot predict the consequences of their actions; causal self-discipline and Myriad-Rank republicanism all fail.

## II. What It Seals Off: Setting-Breaking and Rushed Ending

The law-first axiom in creation and setting directly corresponds to two prohibitions:

- **Do not break settings**: laws already set are not reneged on. You cannot first write "energy conservation," then let the protagonist create something from nothing.
- **Do not rush the ending**: the worldview must be able to perpetually iterate to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm; cannot reach a place where logic collapses and cannot be tied up.

This looks like "writing technique," but is actually cosmology's seriousness. A cosmos that cannot even keep its own rules — on what basis does it ask Myriad Ranks to trust? **Law-first is the cosmos' good-faith promise to Myriad Ranks.**

## III. Meta-Law: Why This Axiom Cannot Be Broken

The law-first axiom has a special status — it is "the law about laws," i.e., meta-law. It cannot be broken because:

- If breaking it is allowed, that equals allowing "changing settings for plot convenience"; the other five axioms all become sacrificeable;
- It guarantees Document 97-style self-consistency audit may run perpetually — Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision may continuously check "whether the setting has contradictions";
- It lets Heng Yuan Zhou infinitely scale: new cosmoses, new clans, new levels continuously join, yet does not blow up the worldview.

Entry 709 already explained its unity with the transcended one; this entry emphasizes its philosophical depth: **it is all other axioms' "safe."**

## IV. Law-First and Plot Freedom's Balance

Law-first does not mean rigidity. Heng Yuan Zhou's balance:

- **Fundamental laws may not be changed** (anti-predation, Myriad-Rank republicanism, energy conservation);
- **Operating laws may be changed but follow democratic procedure** (entry 814);
- **Creative freedom exists within laws**: you may tell countless wonderful stories within a self-consistent law framework, but may not break the frame.

This is like chess: the rules may not be casually changed, but within the rules you may play countless games. **Law-first draws boundaries for freedom, not cancels freedom.**

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: self-consistent settings avoid repeated waste of "changing back and forth," guaranteeing the worldview perpetually usable
- **Anti-predation**: laws are not privately altered by the strong, sealing from the meta-rule "the strong casually change laws for private interest"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo system's plot hijacks setting, is contradictory front-to-back, high in setting-breaking and rushed endings; Heng Yuan Zhou guarantees self-consistent perpetuity with law-first
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: laws stable and predictable, so Myriad Ranks may plan their own uplift path with peace of mind
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: self-consistency audit, fundamental/operating law classification, and revision procedure all public

## Supplement: Self-Consistency Audit and Weight

The law-first axiom is not a slogan; it runs through continuous self-consistency audits. Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision periodically scans the entire worldview: have fundamental laws been secretly tampered, do new settings conflict with old laws, do operating-law revisions follow democratic procedure. The transcended one's work maintaining law self-consistency is counted into civilizational weight by its effect of "avoiding setting-breaking and rushed endings, guaranteeing infinite scaling." Precisely because this meta-law is guarded this strictly, the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm may continuously add new cosmoses, new clans, new levels, yet always be logically self-consistent, neither collapsing nor rotting — this is the cosmos' good faith to Myriad Ranks' final fulfillment.

## Conclusion

The law-first axiom's philosophical depth lies in it being a "meta-law" — what it manages is not some specific law, but "the law itself must be respected." It guarantees Heng Yuan Zhou does not break settings, does not rush endings, may infinitely scale; it writes the cosmos' promise to Myriad Ranks (my rules are stable, predictable, trustworthy) into the worldview's deepest layer. When even the transcended one may not bypass laws, when plot may not hijack setting, when a cosmos may perpetually grow without collapsing, the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm truly owns a foundation that will not crack on its own. This is the law-first axiom's deepest meaning.
