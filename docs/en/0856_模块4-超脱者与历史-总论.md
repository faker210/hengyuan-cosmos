# Entry 856 | Transcended Ones and History · Overview — The Transcended One's View of History

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 70, 93, 716, 860

## Introduction: if one lives long enough, one must take responsibility for history

The old worldview's strong ones are history's amnesiacs. He lives tens of thousands of years, yet never summarizes — he only remembers whom he fought, whom he beat; does not remember how many Myriad Ranks died in these wars. So history repeats itself again and again: civilization just flourished, yet is pulled back to barbarism by some strong one's ambition. Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended one lives longer than anyone, therefore he must possess a true view of history — **not only remember the past, but take responsibility for the past, and above all prevent the past's tragedies from repeating.** This entry is the "Transcended Ones and History" sub-theme overview, explaining how the transcended one views history, and how his view of history differs from the old world's "the strong are history" view.

Six top-level axioms centrally fulfill **the causal self-discipline axiom**: history is causality's accumulation; the transcended one must take responsibility for history; **the anti-predation axiom** requires history not be written by predators; **the republican axiom of Myriad Races** requires history's protagonist be Myriad Ranks; **the collective ascension axiom** requires history point to progress not cyclical regression; **the law-first axiom** requires the chronicle be self-consistent, not tampered.

Among them, **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law** is the sixth pillar: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. The old cosmos' resources and Great Dao are limited, necessarily leading to predatory involution; the Anchor Symbiotic everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why the Transcended One Most Needs a View of History

The transcended one lives too long. A mortal living a century may remember one era; a transcended one living ten thousand years has personally seen several civilizations' rise and fall. If he has no view of history, he will:

- Mistake old era's errors as "common sense," inadvertently repeating them;
- Forget what Myriad Ranks paid for today, compromise too easily;
- Lose direction in eternity, slide into hang-up retirement (entry 729).

**The view of history is the transcended one's time coordinate.** Without it, the stronger he is, the more lost he becomes.

## II. The Transcended One's View of History Has Three Basic Stances

Documents 70 Era-Division Master Table and 93 Chronicle Brief Table lay the transcended one's view of history's three stances:

1. **History belongs to Myriad Ranks, not the strong**: the chronicle records how Myriad Ranks walked all the way, not some protagonist's rise story.
2. **History has direction**: Heng Yuan Zhou's history is not "long-divided must reunite, long-reunited must divide" cycle, but an upward arrow from predation to symbiosis.
3. **History must be true**: the chronicle is based on facts, not deifying, not whitewashing, not tampering (entry 848 already emphasized epics aligning with historical facts).

## III. History as Public Memory

History in Heng Yuan Zhou is not scholars' study; it is public memory:

| History's Function | Effect |
|--------------------|--------|
| Lesson | Let Myriad Ranks remember the price of civilizational regression and reincarnation harvest |
| Identity | Let Myriad Ranks know "where we came from" |
| Early warning | Identify "historical reversal"'s early signals |
| Responsibility | Let the transcended one be responsible for the past and the future |

This is why the transcended one must set the "civilizational-correction and historical-reversal-prevention post" (entry 716) — history is not past tense, it is an early-warning system living in the present.

## IV. View of History and Time Domains

Heng Yuan Zhou has the Origin Acceleration Domain and the Quest Sediment Domain (Module 3); the transcended one's view of history must also balance between these two domains:

- In the acceleration domain, history is quickly iterated and quickly learned;
- In the sediment domain, history is slowly chewed and deeply comprehended;
- The two combined, history is neither forgotten nor hastily simplified.

The transcended one's view of history also has a special requirement: he must simultaneously live in three time scales. At the individual scale, he remembers his own cultivation journey from childhood to now; at the civilizational scale, he understands Myriad-Rank symbiosis's every turning point from foundation to today; at the cosmic scale, he perceives the multiverse's long process from isolation to interconnection. If the three scales are severed, problems arise — living only at the individual scale becomes a self-pitying isolated sovereign; living only at the civilizational scale becomes a grand-narrative machine ignoring individual suffering; living only at the cosmic scale becomes a void existence insensible to human warmth. Heng Yuan Zhou requires the transcended one hold the three scales simultaneously in his heart; this is his internal coordinate system when making any historical decision.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: deposit civilization's rise-and-fall lessons into public memory, avoiding Myriad Ranks repeatedly paying tuition
- **Anti-predation**: history written by Myriad Ranks, not tampered by predators into "great achievements"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-style strong ones have no view of history, repeat errors; Heng Yuan Zhou uses chronicle and prevention posts to hold direction
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: history records each Myriad-Rank clan's contribution, not only the strong ones' achievements
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: chronicle, era division, and historical early-warning mechanism all public

## Supplement: The View of History's Post and Weight

The view of history is not scholars' private interest; it is the transcended one's post. He is responsible for maintaining the chronicle, identifying reversal signals, explaining the road traveled to Myriad Ranks; this work is counted into civilizational weight by its effect of "preventing civilization from taking a backward road." Every chronicle revision, every historical judgment leaves traces under Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision, preventing history from being tampered or deified. When the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's transcended one, after ten thousand years, still remembers the road and takes responsibility for the past, he will not let civilization lose its way in eternity — history becomes the compass that keeps him from sinking in the torrent of time.

Also because of Axiom 6, historical narrative no longer serves only the strong ones' achievements: every being's inherent basic infinity is history's legitimate protagonist; the chronicle must record Myriad Ranks' ordinary people's road stroke by stroke, not only write the strong ones' family tree.

## Conclusion

If one lives long enough, one must take responsibility for history. The transcended one's view of history rewrites history from "the strong ones' family tree" into "Myriad Ranks' public memory" — it is true, has direction, serves the present. When the transcended one remembers how civilization once regressed and how Myriad Ranks once suffered, he will not let history repeat; when he remembers how Myriad Ranks once jointly built, he has the reason to continue forward. History is the compass by which the transcended one does not lose himself in eternity.
