## I. Regression's Four Historical Roots

Benchmarking Document 71 Pre-Anchor Era and entry 739 Civilizational-Regression Causal Chain, civilizational regression has four intertwined roots:

1. **The strong re-monopolize**: one generation of strong ones finally opened knowledge; the next generation of strong ones takes it back into private vaults; knowledge monopoly rebuilds; class re-solidifies.
2. **Predation restoration**: Myriad Ranks have just tasted symbiosis' benefits; old predatory rhetoric ("the strong should take more," "harvest fortune") is dug up again; short-cut inner demons spread again.
3. **Causal accounts unsettled**: causality owed by old-era predation has not been settled; it flows back in new forms, creating new suffering.
4. **Supervision laxity**: once supervision slackens, the strong start bypassing rules, hollowing out institutions bit by bit.

The four often flare together: monopoly paves the way for predation, predation creates enmity, enmity is covered by lax supervision, ultimately civilization one day suddenly crashes back to barbarism.

The old worldview comforts itself: "the world's great trend, long-divided must reunite, long-reunited must divide" — treating regression as inevitable natural law. Heng Yuan Zhou firmly opposes:
