## II. Historical Construction's Principle: Truth Above Beautification

Constructing symbiotic-civilization history is not equal to "writing a beautified history." The transcended one insists:

- **Do not evade darkness**: the Pre-Anchor Era's predation, sacrifice, and regression are faithfully recorded;
- **Do not deify heroes**: record key contributors, but place them within the "Myriad Ranks jointly build" framework;
- **Do not omit struggle**: civilization is not a smooth road; record roads taken wrong, pits stepped on;
- **Align with laws**: every key node in the chronicle has a corresponding explanation at the law layer and causal layer.

Truth is historical construction's first principle — because entry 848 said epics must be based on truth; entry 856 said history must serve early warning. A beautified history neither educates Myriad Ranks nor early-warns regression.
