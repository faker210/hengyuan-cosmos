# Entry 860 | Transcended Ones and History | The Perpetual Mechanism of Historical Correction

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 716, 760, 857, 859

## Introduction: civilization must be able to "correct its own errors," not rely on luck

The old worldview's civilizational correction relied on luck — a good protagonist appeared to right the error; if not, it rotted all the way. Heng Yuan Zhou does not bet on luck. It must build a **perpetually running correction mechanism**: even if no savior appears, civilization may itself discover errors, itself correct, itself not repeat. This entry concludes the "Transcended Ones and History" sub-theme, explaining how this historical-correction mechanism runs perpetually, and how it turns civilization from "progressing by luck" to "progressing by institution."

Six top-level axioms centrally embody **the law-first axiom** and **the causal self-discipline axiom**: the correction mechanism itself is a law-level stable institution; it records causality and corrects deviation; **the anti-predation axiom** requires correction point to clearing predation legacies; **the republican axiom of Myriad Races** requires correction be Myriad-Rank-participated, not autocratically decided by the strong; **the collective ascension axiom** requires correction serve perpetual progress.

Among them, **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law** is the sixth pillar: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. The old cosmos' resources and Great Dao are limited, necessarily leading to predatory involution; the Anchor Symbiotic everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Correction Must Be "Perpetual" Not "One-Time"

Historical correction does not end with some "righting chaos." Errors continuously arise — new strong ones, new temptations, new institutional loopholes. Therefore correction must be perpetual:

- **One correction is not enough**: today monopoly is cleared; tomorrow it may appear again;
- **Reliance on heroes is unreliable**: cannot expect a good protagonist in every era;
- **Institutions must run themselves**: civilization must possess the ability to "self-correct."

This is why it is called a "perpetual mechanism" — it is a closed loop always running, not a one-time movement.

## II. Correction Mechanism's Four-Link Closed Loop

Historical correction's perpetual operation relies on a four-link closed loop:

| Link | What Is Done | Who Does It |
|------|--------------|-------------|
| Monitor | Identify regression signals, anomalous data, predatory苗头 | Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision, Anchor Supervisory Committee |
| Diagnose | Judge whether this is a real problem, where the root is | Civilizational-correction post (entry 716) |
| Correct | Under Myriad-Rank democratic procedure, correct institutions, clear legacies | Anchor Council + Symbiotic Executive Committee |
| Review | Record this correction, deposit into textbooks and early warning | Transcended one + chronicle maintenance |

Four links walked, return to monitoring — closed loop perpetual. Any link stops, correction fails.

## III. Correction and Alaya Self-Consistency Audit

Correction's technical chassis is entry 760's "self-consistency Alaya audit." Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision continuously scans:

- Whether the law layer has contradictions (law-first axiom);
- Whether the causal layer has unsettled old predation accounts;
- Whether the institutional layer has secretly hollowed-out links;
- Whether the historical layer has tampered or beautified records.

Anomaly found, auto-alert, enters diagnosis. **Correction does not rely on human conscience, but on the network's automatic patrol.**

## IV. Correction Does Not Create New Harm

Correction itself may become harm — using "correction" name to conduct purges and rectifications. Heng Yuan Zhou's discipline:

- Correction follows Myriad-Rank democratic procedure, not autocratically decided by some transcended one;
- Correction's goal is "repair institutions, clear legacies," not "settle accounts with people";
- The corrected party has appeal right; the Symbiotic Court arbitrates (entry 787);
- Correction process leaves traces, preventing it from becoming new persecution.

Correction is "treating disease" for civilization, not "operating to the bleeding."

The correction mechanism also has an easily overlooked function: it is Myriad Ranks' "reverse supervision" of the transcended one. The transcended one lives too long; things he did and errors he made may himself have beautified or forgotten; but Alaya Network's historical archives will not. Myriad Ranks may through the correction mechanism bring up again a historical error made by some transcended one ten thousand years ago during his term, requiring the current transcended one, representing the system, to make compensation and correction. This turns "merits and demerits cannot offset each other" from slogan into institution — your construction merit back then is merit; your decision error back then is error; two accounts are settled separately. This tracing right makes every sitting transcended one dare not act rashly, because he knows someone will still dig up his old accounts ten thousand years later.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: perpetual correction lets civilization treat small diseases early, avoiding small errors dragging into civilizational-regression's big waste
- **Anti-predation**: the mechanism continuously clears predation legacies, not letting monopoly and harvest rear again
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo civilization relies on protagonist luck to correct errors; if wrong, rots; Heng Yuan Zhou relies on closed-loop institutions to perpetually self-correct
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the correction mechanism protects all Myriad-Rank beings, especially groups affected by old harm
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: monitor, diagnose, correct, review four links all public and queryable

The correction process follows Myriad-Rank democratic procedure, under Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision; the corrected party has appeal right.
