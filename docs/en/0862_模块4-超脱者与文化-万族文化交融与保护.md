# Entry 862 | Transcended Ones and Culture | Intermingling and Protection of Myriad-Rank Cultures

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 50, 52, 861, 865

## Introduction: intermingling is not assimilation; protection is not isolation

Myriad-Rank cultures are naturally colorful. Carbon-based humans have song and dance; plant spirits have growth rhythms; consciousness-information clans have dream art; mechanical-construct clans have structural aesthetics. The old world's strong ones, facing this diversity, have only two crude choices: either use force to force Myriad Ranks to "civilize" (assimilation), or treat alien clans as outsiders to isolate (discrimination). Heng Yuan Zhou must walk a third road — **intermingle without assimilation, protect without isolation.** This entry explains: how the transcended one lets each Myriad-Rank clan's culture both keep its own uniqueness and nourish each other in exchange.

Six top-level axioms centrally fulfill **the republican axiom of Myriad Races**: each clan's culture is equal, no high-low; **the anti-predation axiom** opposes using culture as a weapon to conquer; **the collective ascension axiom** lets cultural intermingling serve common progress; **the causal self-discipline axiom** requires cultural exchange not forcibly change others; **the law-first axiom** requires protection and intermingling principles be stable and self-consistent.

Among them, **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law** is the sixth pillar: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. The old cosmos' resources and Great Dao are limited, necessarily leading to predatory involution; the Anchor Symbiotic everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, predation naturally loses its meaning.

Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Difference Between Intermingling and Assimilation

This is this entry's most critical divide:

| Dimension | Assimilation (Old World) | Intermingling (Heng Yuan Zhou) |
|------------|--------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Purpose | Let a few cultures cover the many | Each clan learns from each other |
| Result | Cultural simplification | Culture richer |
| Power relation | Strong culture crushes weak | Equal exchange |
| To Myriad Ranks | Loss of diversity | Richer symbiotic circulation |

Assimilation is "I turn you into me"; intermingling is "we jointly grow something new." **Heng Yuan Zhou wants the latter.**

## II. Protection: Each Clan's Culture Is Myriad Ranks' Common Wealth

Protecting Myriad-Rank cultures is not "preserving specimens," but recognizing:

- Each clan's culture is its unique understanding of the cosmos — a plant spirit's understanding of time is what humans cannot give;
- Cultural extinction equals losing one way of seeing the world — this is Myriad Ranks' common loss;
- Weak clans' cultures need protection more, because they most easily disappear under strong cultures.

The transcended one takes the lead, providing endangered-clan cultures with documentation, transmission, and resource support, letting them continue growing within Myriad-Rank republicanism.

## III. Intermingling: Let Culture Grow in Exchange

Protection does not mean sealing each clan's culture in a glass cabinet. Heng Yuan Zhou encourages intermingling:

- Myriad Ranks daily exchange on the cross-plane anchor network; cultures naturally influence each other;
- Each clan's art (entries 846–850) learns from each other, producing new mixed art;
- Symbiotic culture (entry 863) as the "greatest common divisor," provides a common base color for diverse cultures.

Intermingling's goal is not "become the same," but "find the common in the different, keep the different in the common."

## IV. Preventing Cultural Hegemony

Intermingling also has risks — strong cultures may imperceptibly crush weak cultures. Heng Yuan Zhou's defense line:

- Do not judge some clan's culture as "higher" — cultures have no superiority or inferiority, only character;
- Leave sufficient protection resources and display space for weak cultures;
- Cultural evaluation right belongs to each clan itself, not arbitrated by the transcended one or majority clans.

This foreshadows entry 865's "cultural-diversity law guarantee" — it must write this protection into law.

The healthy sign of intermingling is both sides may "take something" from each other without losing themselves. A carbon-based race absorbing gas-form life's musical form, in turn fusing this musical form into its own rituals — this is intermingling; if this race's children from then on only listen to gas music and no longer tell their own clan's epics, that is being assimilated. The transcended one's role is not to act as referee stipulating who should influence whom, but to monitor intermingling's direction — when some culture's output shows overwhelming asymmetry (e.g., some human plane's cultural products occupy 80% of other planes' attention), start cultural hedging mechanism, supporting weak-culture output channels. Intermingling must be free, but freedom's end may not be extermination.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: preserving Myriad-Rank diverse cultures preserves countless unique ways of seeing the world, not wasted in assimilation
- **Anti-predation**: oppose cultural conquest and cultural hegemony; intermingle not assimilate
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo civilization centers on itself to assimilate alien clans; Heng Yuan Zhou lets Myriad-Rank cultures intermingle equally
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: five-lineage each clan's culture is protected and may participate in intermingling
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cultural archives, intermingling mechanism, and protection policy all public network-wide

## Supplement: Intermingling and Protection's Supervision and Weight

Protecting diversity and promoting intermingling may not rely on good intentions alone. Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision continuously watches: whether strong cultures quietly crush weak cultures, whether endangered-clan cultures receive resources, whether intermingling is voluntary. The transcended one's work guarding cultural diversity is counted into civilizational weight by its effect of "letting each clan be beautiful in its own way and all beauties together." This set of supervision lets the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm avoid the two extremes of assimilation and isolation — neither letting the strong conquer with culture, nor letting weak cultures wither in isolation. When Myriad Ranks' spiritual world is both rich and equal, Myriad-Rank republicanism truly becomes spiritual reality.

Intermingling and protection must also not forget Axiom 6: different life forms' inherent basic infinities differ; plant spirits' slowness, consciousness clans' stillness, are all unique rhythms worth protecting, not to be crushed by a unified striver standard.

## Conclusion

Myriad-Rank cultures' intermingling and protection is "Myriad-Rank republicanism" fulfilled in the spiritual domain. It neither lets the strong assimilate alien clans with culture, nor lets weak cultures wither in isolation; it lets each clan keep its uniqueness, yet nourish each other in exchange. When plant spirits' growth rhythms, consciousness clans' dream art, and humans' song and dance are each beautiful on the symbiotic-culture base color and all beauties together, Myriad-Rank republicanism for the first time is no longer only a political arrangement, but spiritual reality.
