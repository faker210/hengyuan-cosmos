# Entry 863 | Transcended Ones and Culture | Symbiotic Culture's Creation and Dissemination

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 01, 838, 848, 861

## Introduction: Myriad Ranks need a new "common language"

Myriad Ranks each have their own culture, but to jointly build one cosmos, they need a "common cultural base color" above each clan — it does not replace each clan's traditions, but lets Myriad Ranks "be able to converse" spiritually. This is **symbiotic culture.** It distills the five axioms into values Myriad Ranks may feel, trust, and pass on. This entry explains: how the transcended one creates this new culture, and how he disseminates it to every corner of Myriad Ranks.

Six top-level axioms centrally embody — symbiotic culture is precisely these six axioms' embodiment in the spiritual domain: **anti-predation** values, **Myriad-Rank-republican** identity, **collective-uplift** direction, **causal-self-discipline** reverence, **law-first** good faith, all condensed into symbiotic culture.

Among them, **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law** is the sixth pillar: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. The old cosmos' resources and Great Dao are limited, necessarily leading to predatory involution; the Anchor Symbiotic everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. What Is Symbiotic Culture

Symbiotic culture is not another "clan culture"; it is Myriad Ranks' **common base color**:

- **Core values**: co-build and share, Myriad-Rank equality, increment creation, anti-predation;
- **Common symbols**: anchor, bidirectional circulation, the Alaya Network's public emblems;
- **Common festivals**: commemorate Myriad-Rank republicanism's establishment, celebrate common-uplift milestones;
- **Common narrative**: the "we walked together" sung by epic (entry 848).

Its relationship to each clan's culture is "base color and picture" — each clan's culture is still a rich picture; symbiotic culture is the base color that lets all pictures hang together.

## II. How to Create: From Axioms to Life

Symbiotic culture may not just shout slogans out of thin air; it must from axioms step by step land in life:

| Layer | From Axiom to Symbiotic Culture |
|-------|--------------------------------|
| Values | Anti-predation → "robbing is losing"; Myriad-Rank republicanism → "we are equal" |
| Institutions | Every person has a path, more-work-more-pay, civilizational weight |
| Art | Civilizational aesthetics, epic, consciousness art |
| Daily | Festivals, customs, praise words in language |

Creating symbiotic culture is letting the five axioms, from charter text, seep into Myriad Ranks' daily language, festivals, and aesthetics.

## III. How to Disseminate: Culture Goes to the Countryside

Having created symbiotic culture, it must still be brought before Myriad Ranks. This is the same logic as entry 838 "knowledge popularization":

- **Four-tier dissemination**: enlightenment, entry, advanced, frontier, corresponding to different cognitive levels;
- **Cross-lineage translation**: translate symbiotic culture into forms perceptible to five-lineage life forms;
- **Borrow daily carriers**: naturally disseminate through education, epic, festivals, art, not relying on forced inculcation;
- **Alaya traces**: dissemination process does not manipulate or incite, under supervision.

**Disseminating symbiotic culture is letting it "grow out," not "pour in."**

## IV. Symbiotic Culture Is Not the Only Culture

Be especially wary: symbiotic culture is a "common base color," not "the only culture." It does not replace each clan's tradition (entry 862), but lets multiple cultures have a basis for common dialogue. If symbiotic culture becomes "the only correct culture," it degenerates into new cultural hegemony — this is what Heng Yuan Zhou firmly opposes.

Symbiotic culture's dissemination most fears two distortions. The first is "fundamentalism" — turning axioms into rigid dogma, requiring Myriad Ranks to express them in completely identical rituals, language, and festivals; as a result symbiotic culture instead becomes new cultural hegemony. The second is "dilution" — during dissemination, to please various audiences, softening the hard cores of anti-predation and Myriad-Rank republicanism into correct nonsense like "everyone should be friendly." Heng Yuan Zhou's solution: the core may absolutely not be diluted; expression must be fully plural. The same axiom of "universal equal infinite law for all beings" may be composed into a symphony on a human plane, into wave-form poetry on a gas-form life plane, into a collective dream on a hive-mind plane — forms vary a thousand ways, the core does not move a bit. The transcended one during dissemination only assesses core fidelity, does not interfere with specific forms of expression.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: use common culture to organize Myriad Ranks' spiritual force, forming synergy not internal friction
- **Anti-predation**: symbiotic culture from the heart layer confirms "robbing is losing," more root-cure than law
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo civilization has no common culture, Myriad Ranks attack each other; Heng Yuan Zhou uses symbiotic culture to bind Myriad Ranks
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: symbiotic culture is translated per five lineages, facing all clans for dissemination
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: symbiotic culture's core, symbols, and dissemination mechanism all public and participable

## Supplement: Symbiotic Culture's Dissemination Supervision and Self-Consistency

Disseminating symbiotic culture must "grow out" not "pour in." The whole dissemination process leaves traces under Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: whether it is used to manipulate hearts, whether some clan is forced to accept, whether it conflicts with fundamental laws. Symbiotic culture's dissemination effect — Myriad Ranks' recognition of co-building values — converts into the transcended one's civilizational weight. And the reason symbiotic culture may continuously grow is precisely because it obeys the law-first axiom: it does not conflict with fundamental laws; it continuously self-revises with Myriad Ranks' life experience, yet always holds the anti-predation, Myriad-Rank-republican base color. The Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's spiritual base color is therefore both stable and vivid.

When disseminating symbiotic culture, Axiom 6 must be explained thoroughly: symbiosis is not forcing everyone to strive to the top, but letting everyone inherently possess a guaranteed infinity, each superimposing at their own rhythm — strivers may go higher, easygoing ones are not eliminated.

This is symbiotic culture's softest yet firmest base color — it does not eliminate anyone, only invites everyone at their own rhythm to flow into common uplift.

## Conclusion

Symbiotic culture's creation and dissemination is condensing the five axioms from charter text into Myriad Ranks' common spiritual base color. It lets Myriad Ranks, while keeping their own traditions, possess a common identity of "we are one"; it naturally grows through education, epic, and festivals, not forced inculcation. When Myriad Ranks in daily life naturally believe "co-building is better than predation, republicanism is better than autocracy," symbiosis internally transforms from institution into culture. This is the most far-reaching spiritual project the transcended one leaves Myriad Ranks.
