# Entry 865 | Transcended Ones and Culture | Law Guarantee of Cultural Diversity

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 03, 814, 854, 862

## Introduction: diversity may not rely on good intentions alone; laws must be the backstop

Protecting cultural diversity relies on the transcended one's "good heart" and Myriad Ranks' "conscience" alone is insufficient — history repeatedly proves good intentions fatigue and conscience slackens. Heng Yuan Zhou's approach is: **write cultural diversity into law; use law as backstop.** This entry concludes the "Transcended Ones and Culture" sub-theme, explaining how this law guarantee is designed, and why it is the republican axiom of Myriad Races' final landing in the cultural field.

Six top-level axioms centrally fulfill **the republican axiom of Myriad Races** and **the law-first axiom**: cultural diversity is a law-level guarantee, stable and not casually tamperable; **the anti-predation axiom** opposes cultural conquest; **the collective ascension axiom** lets diversity nourish symbiosis; **the causal self-discipline axiom** requires the guarantee mechanism be transparent and queryable.

Among them, **Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law** is the sixth pillar: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. The old cosmos' resources and Great Dao are limited, necessarily leading to predatory involution; the Anchor Symbiotic everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why Cultural Diversity Needs Law

Cultural diversity is fragile in reality:

- Strong cultures naturally expand; weak cultures naturally shrink;
- One war, one disaster, may make a culture disappear;
- Majority clans may imperceptibly suppress minority clans' cultures.

Relying on "everyone should respect" is not enough; there must be a law backstop — just as Document 03 Symbiotic Civilization Charter backs Myriad Ranks' equal rights. **Cultural diversity is a charter-level right, not a goodwill.**

## II. Law Guarantee's Several Core Clauses

Cultural diversity's law guarantee contains several core clauses:

| Clause | Content |
|--------|---------|
| Cultural sovereignty | Each clan has autonomous disposal over its own culture, not forced assimilation by external force |
| Endangered protection | Provide resources, documentation, and transmission support for endangered cultures |
| Anti-discrimination | Discriminating against some clan due to cultural difference is prohibited |
| Voluntary intermingling | Cultural exchange is voluntary, not forced |
| Plural coexistence | Symbiotic culture is the base color, not replacing each clan's tradition |

These clauses are written into the charter; any power — including the transcended one — may not violate them.

## III. Law Guarantee and the Transcended One's Role

Under this set of laws, the transcended one's role is clear:

- **Law enforcer**: supervise cultural laws not being violated; immediately handle cultural discrimination and forced assimilation when found;
- **Resource allocator**: deliver resources for protecting endangered cultures to clans in need;
- **Non-overstepper**: he himself is also bound by cultural laws, may not under "protection" name interfere with other clans' cultural autonomy.

The transcended one is cultural diversity's **guardian**, not culture's **master**.

## IV. Law Revision: Stable but Not Rigid

Cultural diversity's laws belong to entry 814's "operating laws" category — they may, per Myriad Ranks' needs, be revised through democratic procedure, but:

- Fundamental clauses (cultural sovereignty, anti-discrimination) are immovable;
- Revision follows Myriad-Rank democratic procedure, not autocratically decided by the strong;
- Revision process is public under Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision.

This is precisely the law-first axiom's fulfillment in the cultural field — **guarantees must be stable, but not rigid.**

Law guarantee's execution falls on two grips: first is the cultural rights list — each race's core cultural rights, including festival autonomy, education autonomy, and belief autonomy, are written into that plane's implementation rules; infringement triggers legal remedy; second is cultural-endangerment early warning — the Alaya Network monitors each race's cultural carriers (number of language speakers, ritual-survival frequency, inheritor age structure); once some indicator falls below the safety line, automatically start protection engineering. These two grips run automatically, not relying on some transcended one's good heart. Good intentions fluctuate; laws do not; emotions fatigue; the network does not. Whether diversity can be preserved ultimately relies on institutions, not sentiment.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the law backstop preserves Myriad-Rank diverse cultures, avoiding unique civilizations dying out inadvertently
- **Anti-predation**: anti-cultural-discrimination, anti-forced-assimilation, sealing cultural conquest from the law layer
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo civilization centers on itself, cultural hegemony; Heng Yuan Zhou writes cultural diversity into a charter-level right
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: each clan regardless of size or strength, cultural sovereignty equally protected by law
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cultural-diversity law clauses, revision procedure, and execution records all public

## Supplement: Law Guarantee's Execution and Weight

Cultural diversity written into the charter still needs someone to execute it. The transcended one is the guardian of this set of laws: he supervises cultural sovereignty not being infringed, delivers resources to endangered clans, and is himself also bound by the law. Execution effect — whether cultural discrimination has decreased, whether endangered cultures survive — is counted into his civilizational weight. Law revision follows Myriad-Rank democratic procedure; fundamental clauses are immovable; the whole process is public under Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision. When the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm writes Myriad Ranks' spiritual diversity into a charter-level right, uses law as backstop, uses supervision to execute, Myriad-Rank republicanism truly takes root in the spiritual field.

The law guarantee should also write Axiom 6: every being's inherent basic infinity is protected by law; no clan or life form loses cultural qualification because "not strong enough."

Under the law backstop, Myriad Ranks' spiritual garden may truly bloom without failing and perpetually flourish.

## Conclusion

Cultural diversity's law guarantee is "Myriad-Rank republicanism"'s final landing in the cultural field. It no longer entrusts diversity to good intentions, but writes it into charter-level law — cultural sovereignty inviolable, endangered cultures protected, intermingling must be voluntary, the transcended one also bound by it. When Myriad Ranks' diversity has a law backstop and does not change with the strong one's will, Myriad-Rank republicanism truly takes root in the spiritual field. This is the steadiest heritage the transcended one may leave Myriad Ranks in the cultural dimension.
