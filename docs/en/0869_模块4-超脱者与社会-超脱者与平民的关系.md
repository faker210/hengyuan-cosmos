# Entry 869 | Transcended Ones and Society | The Transcended One's Relationship with Commoners

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 08, 36, 51, 781

## Introduction: the transcended one neither tramples commoners' heads nor floats on clouds

Between the old world's transcended one and commoners lies a sky-cleaving chasm: commoners gaze up at the Great Emperor as at starry sky; the Great Emperor looks down on commoners as on ants. There is no real relationship between them — only obeisance and being obeyed. Heng Yuan Zhou must answer: **what relationship should the transcended one have with those commoners in Myriad Ranks who have just reached Sense Realm or not yet begun cultivation at all?** This entry nails this relationship down.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: the anti-predation axiom forbids the transcended one treating commoners as cultivation resources; the republican axiom of Myriad Races confirms commoners and the transcended one possess equal uplift qualification; the collective ascension axiom requires the transcended one bring commoners through the door, not trample them underfoot; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the transcended one's interaction with commoners leave traces throughout and not disturb commoners' causality; the law-first axiom requires this relationship be law-level self-consistent, not arbitrarily setting "the Great Emperor travels incognito"-style romanticized narrative by plot needs; the universal equal infinite law confirms all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; easygoing layabouts retain a floor and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher infinity — infinite origin is inherent in all, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Relationship Bottom Line: Commoners Are Not Cultivation Resources

The old world's most common predatory logic is treating commoners as resources — blood food, cauldrons, medicine primers, human sacrifices. Heng Yuan Zhou seals this path with the strictest laws:

- Commoners' lives, energy, and consciousness are inviolable;
- If the transcended one extracts commoners' origin in any form, triggers the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's highest-level alert;
- Violators not only lose transcended-one qualification, but must face Symbiotic Court trial.

This bottom line is not moral preaching, but the anti-predation axiom's hard enforcement. It means: **between the transcended one and commoners, there is no "strong eat weak" food chain.** Commoners are not the transcended one's nourishment, but the objects of his service.

## II. The Relationship's Positive Line: The Transcended One Is Commoners' Uplift Guide

Above the bottom line, what is the positive relationship between the transcended one and commoners? Not patronage (that is ruler logic), but guidance:

1. **Open the door**: through the every-person-has-a-path guarantee system (Document 08), ensure every commoner may find a cultivation path suited to him, not blocked by birth, clan, or gender;
2. **Pave the road**: through public cultivation places, free textbooks, public compute, lower commoners' starting cost;
3. **Lead the way**: the transcended-one training and transmission post (entry 720) periodically sinks into commoner communities, doing enlightenment teaching, path assessment, and misconception correction;
4. **Guard the door**: prevent predatory schools from developing believers among commoners — commoners are most easily tempted by "quick success"; the transcended one has the duty to provide a better right-road choice.

The guidance relationship's core is: the transcended one does not walk for commoners, but he must ensure the door is open, the road is flat, the lamp is lit. How far commoners walk depends on themselves; but the door may not close, the road may not be blocked.

## III. The Sense of Distance in the Relationship

Between the transcended one and commoners a healthy distance must be kept. Too close is no good — the transcended one directly meddling in commoners' daily affairs becomes internal-affairs interference; too far is no good either — floating on clouds ignoring commoners' lives is hang-up retirement.

Heng Yuan Zhou uses institutions to adjust this distance:

- **Legal interaction channels**: commoners communicate with the transcended one through Myriad-Rank representatives, supervisory committees, and Alaya feedback systems; no "stopping the imperial carriage to file grievances";
- **Periodic sinking**: the transcended one annually must complete a certain duration of grassroots teaching, not only stay at the law network's top;
- **No private relationships**: the transcended one may not establish "private grudges" with specific commoners — if you helped Zhang San, you must be fair to Li Si too; helping one person is not serving Myriad Ranks;
- **No status crushing**: the transcended one in public may not suppress commoners with power, may not enjoy special treatment "because I am the transcended one."

## IV. Commoners' Rights Against the Transcended One

Relationships are bidirectional. Commoners hold the following rights against the transcended one:

- **Right to know**: the transcended one's post goals, resource use, and assessment results are public to commoners;
- **Right to supervise**: if commoners find the transcended one violating rules, they may report through the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision system;
- **Right to request**: commoners may through Myriad-Rank representatives make reasonable service requests to the transcended one (e.g., some region's law-network maintenance needs);
- **Right to refuse**: commoners have the right to refuse the transcended one's "well-meaning but ill-done" intervention — if you do not need the transcended one's help, he cannot force help.

These rights ensure commoners are not passive objects receiving patronage, but citizens with subjectivity.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the transcended one does not treat commoners as nourishment; commoners do not treat the transcended one as a god — both sides' energy is used on co-building
- **Anti-predation**: commoners are not cultivation resources; this bottom line directly cuts the food chain predatory schools most rely on
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo system commoners are the Great Emperor's backdrop, life and death not their own; in Heng Yuan Zhou commoners have complete rights, the transcended one is their server
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: commoners cover all five-lineage Myriad-Rank beings not yet entering cultivation, regardless of clan or gender
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: public textbooks, cultivation places, and complaint channels all open network-wide through the Selfless Dao

## Conclusion

The relationship between the transcended one and commoners is not ruler and ant, not god and believer, but guide and walker. The transcended one opens the door, paves the road, lights the lamp; commoners themselves choose whether to walk and how far. He does not trample you, nor walk for you — he only ensures the door is always open. This is the orthodox definition of the transcended one's relationship with commoners.
