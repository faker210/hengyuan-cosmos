# Entry 870 | Transcended Ones and Society | The Transcended One's Relationship with Mid-Level Cultivators

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 36, 14, 22, 872

## Introduction: mid-level cultivators are civilization's waist — the transcended one may not press the waist

The old world's cultivation society is a pyramid: the top is the Great Emperor; the middle is sect elders and family strong ones; the bottom is billions of mortals. This pyramid's waist — those cultivators with some cultivation far from the top — is the group most easily overlooked and most easily sacrificed. Heng Yuan Zhou must make clear: **mid-level cultivators (roughly corresponding to the Transform Realm through Dao Integration Realm range) are civilization's waist; the transcended one's attitude toward them directly determines whether civilization is mobile or solidified.** This entry explains this relationship.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: the anti-predation axiom forbids the transcended one treating mid-level cultivators as "harvestable leeks"; the republican axiom of Myriad Races confirms mid-level cultivators are Myriad Ranks' evolutionary backbone; the collective ascension axiom requires the transcended one lift mid-level cultivators upward, not press down on them; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the transcended one's resource allocation with mid-level cultivators be traceable; the law-first axiom requires the class-mobility mechanism be law-level self-consistent; the universal equal infinite law confirms all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; easygoing layabouts retain a floor and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher infinity — infinite origin is inherent in all, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Who Are Mid-Level Cultivators

Per Unified Cultivation-Realm Classification (Document 14), mid-level cultivators roughly correspond to the Transform, Law, Dao Origin, and Dao Integration ranges — they have passed early mortal cultivation, have considerable cultivation and social-contribution capability, but are far from the Boundary-Breaking and above sage-cultivator level.

They are:
- Sect backbone lecturers, workshop master technicians, city enforcers;
- Cultivation academy upper-grade students, cross-plane exploration team captains;
- Technical backbone in every industry, primary bearers of grassroots governance.

Civilization's daily operation relies not on the tower-top transcended one, but these waists. Waist hard → civilization stable; waist broken → civilization topples.

## II. The Old World's Two Harms to Mid-Level Cultivators

The old world has two systematic harms to mid-level cultivators:

1. **Harvest type**: tower-top strong ones treat mid-level cultivators as "harvestable middle resources" — send them to die, make them take the blame, ambush them during breakthrough to snatch treasures. Mid-level cultivators have barely climbed halfway up the mountain; one word from a tower-top strong one wipes it all out.
2. **Suppression type**: tower-top strong ones deliberately lock the ceiling halfway up, letting mid-level cultivators never reach the top. Because if too many people come up, tower-top power dilutes. Thus "poor families rarely produce noble sons" becomes "the middle rarely produces strong ones."

Both harms' essence is the same: **the tower top treats the middle as threat, not as echelon.**

## III. Heng Yuan Zhou's Positive Relationship: Echelon Relationship

Heng Yuan Zhou defines the transcended one's relationship with mid-level cultivators as "echelon" — the transcended one is not a ceiling pressing on their heads, but a guide standing before them:

- **No harvest**: mid-level cultivators' breakthrough results belong to themselves; the transcended one intercepts in no form;
- **No limitation**: the ceiling is open to all; mid-level cultivators may go upward once qualified; the transcended one does not set limits by person;
- **Give resources**: the household-responsibility mechanism (Document 22) ensures mid-level cultivators' labor receives reasonable return, no need to "hand up to the sect" or "present tribute to elders";
- **Give channels**: cultivators at Dao Origin and Dao Integration stages may through the transcended-one training and transmission post (entry 720) receive one-on-one guidance, entering the preparation channel for Boundary-Breaking.

The echelon relationship's core is: **the transcended one treats mid-level cultivators as future transcended-one reserve, not as consumable supplies.** Today's mid-level cultivator is tomorrow's post officeholder.

## IV. How the Transcended One Avoids "Middle Solidification"

The greatest risk of class solidification comes from the transcended one himself — he may unconsciously favor "his own people" (same clan, same lineage, same path cultivators), causing resources and opportunities to concentrate in a small circle. Heng Yuan Zhou prevents this with the following mechanisms:

- **Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision**: the transcended one's resource-allocation records leave traces throughout; if systematic bias toward some clan or lineage appears, automatically trigger review;
- **Rotation system**: the transcended one periodically changes responsible region and post, avoiding staying in one place too long and forming a "circle";
- **Open review**: mid-level cultivators' promotion review involves multi-party representatives; the transcended one has only one vote;
- **Appeal channel**: unfairly treated mid-level cultivators may appeal to the Symbiotic Court.

These mechanisms ensure the transcended one does not become a new "sect master" — turning middle cultivators into his private army.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: mid-level cultivators are civilization's largest talent pool; no harvest, no suppression is the greatest energy saving
- **Anti-predation**: cut the tower-top harvest-middle food chain, ensuring middle layer's labor fruits belong to the middle layer itself
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo grace system, middle cultivators rise by grace; the ceiling is locked by Gu Chensha alone; in Heng Yuan Zhou the middle rises by more-work-more-pay, the ceiling is open to all
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: mid-level cultivators cover five Myriad-Rank lineages, impartial
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: promotion channels, review standards, and resource-allocation rules all open network-wide through the Selfless Dao

## Conclusion

Mid-level cultivators are civilization's waist. The transcended one's attitude toward the waist determines whether civilization is mobile or solidified. Heng Yuan Zhou requires the transcended one be an echelon builder, not a harvester, not a suppressor, not a circle boss. Waist hard, civilization stands straight. This is the orthodox definition of the transcended one's relationship with mid-level cultivators.
