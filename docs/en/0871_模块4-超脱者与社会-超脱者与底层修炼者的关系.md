# Entry 871 | Transcended Ones and Society | The Transcended One's Relationship with Bottom-Level Cultivators

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 08, 36, 18, 23

## Introduction: bottom-level cultivators are not civilization's garbage bin — the transcended one has a floor-guarantee duty

The old world's bottom-level cultivators are civilization's sacrifices: they cultivate in the worst environments, with the worst resources, on the most dangerous roads, then are silently erased in some sect competition, some secret-realm scramble. No one remembers their names; no one cares whether they live or die. Heng Yuan Zhou must reverse this narrative: **bottom-level cultivators are not civilization's garbage bin, but Myriad Ranks' evolutionary soil — the transcended one owes them floor-guarantee duty.** This entry explains this relationship.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: the anti-predation axiom forbids the transcended one treating bottom-level cultivators as cannon fodder; the republican axiom of Myriad Races confirms the lowest beings also have equal uplift qualification; the collective ascension axiom requires the transcended one tilt resources toward the bottom, not concentrate them at the tower top; the causal self-discipline axiom requires floor-guarantee resource allocation be traceable and not misappropriated; the law-first axiom requires the floor-guarantee mechanism be law-level self-consistent, not hollowed out by "strong-first" inertia; the universal equal infinite law confirms all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; easygoing layabouts retain a floor and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher infinity — infinite origin is inherent in all, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Who Are Bottom-Level Cultivators

Bottom-level cultivators refer to those with the worst cultivation conditions, latest start, most scarce resources, including but not limited to:

- Native-civilization cultivators from undeveloped planes not yet touching the anchor network;
- Cultivators who lost all resources due to war, disaster, or Dark-Substitution-Body events;
- Members of clans most deeply oppressed by the old world among Myriad Ranks' five lineages;
- Cultivators who started late in age, have low aptitude assessment but firm will;
- Strange life forms among mixed-mutant clans not yet identified, not yet granted legal identity.

Their common feature is: **in the old-world system, they almost could not rise.** Not because they are incapable, but because resources, opportunities, and teachers are all monopolized by the upper layer.

## II. Why the Old World Abandoned the Bottom

The old world's abandoning the bottom is not accidental, but systemic design:

- Resources limited, strong-first — give resources to high aptitude, "highest return rate";
- Time limited, quick-success first — teaching a bottom-level cultivator ten years is worse than waiting for a genius a hundred years;
- Logic limited, elite-first — civilization is driven by geniuses; the bottom is only base mass.

This set of "elite logic"'s result is: Myriad Ranks' 99% potential evolvers are wasted at the bottom. This is precisely beings' origin attachment / short-cut inner demon's most fertile soil — bottom-level cultivators, since the right road does not work, naturally take the evil road. Predatory schools forever have fresh recruits because the old world systemically pushes the bottom into predatory schools' arms.

## III. Heng Yuan Zhou's Floor-Guarantee Mechanism

Heng Yuan Zhou's floor guarantee for bottom-level cultivators is not charity, but civilizational-safety engineering:

1. **20% activation strategy** (Document 23): targetedly deliver 20% of public cultivation resources to bottom regions, ensuring the most resource-poor groups are activated first;
2. **Public cultivation places sink**: set up free cultivation stations in bottom planes, disaster zones, undeveloped areas, equipped with basic teachers;
3. **Identification and referral**: Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision automatically scans high-potential individuals among bottom-level cultivators, proactively offering upgrade channels — no need for them to "crawl up by themselves";
4. **Suffering compensation**: cultivators damaged by civilizational disaster or Dark-Substitution-Body events receive extra restoration resources and acceleration channels.

The floor-guarantee mechanism's logic is: **bottom-level cultivators are not "worth pity," but "civilization cannot give up."** Giving them up is giving up Myriad Ranks' 99% evolutionary potential.

## IV. The Transcended One's Concrete Duties in Floor Guarantee

The transcended one bears an irreplaceable role in the floor-guarantee mechanism:

- **Personally sink**: the transcended one periodically teaches at the lowest cultivation stations; not sending a proxy, but going himself;
- **Open increments**: the transcended one develops new resources, new laws, new cultivation places in bottom regions, not "taking a share" from upper-layer stock;
- **Clear predatory schools**: the bottom is where predatory schools are most active; the transcended one has the duty to clear predatory-school strongholds developing believers in the bottom;
- **Witness and record**: the transcended one turns bottom-level cultivators' growth cases into textbooks, letting Myriad Ranks see "the bottom may also transcend."

The floor-guarantee mechanism is not charity, but investment. Every resource delivered to the bottom today is a seed of Myriad Ranks' evolutionary potential tomorrow. A bottom-level cultivator lifted up may in the future open up a brand-new law direction; an overlooked bottom-level genius may be dug up by predatory schools and become civilization's enemy. This account is a bargain whichever way you calculate — the question has never been "can we afford it," but "are we willing to spend."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: bottom-level cultivators are the vast majority of Myriad Ranks; giving them up is wasting 99% of evolutionary potential
- **Anti-predation**: pull the bottom out of predatory schools' recruitment pool; the better the right road is to walk, the fewer walk the evil road
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo grace system only graces a few "chosen ones"; the bottom is always at the bottom; Heng Yuan Zhou uses the 20% activation strategy for targeted floor guarantee; the bottom has a road
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: floor guarantee faces all five-lineage bottom beings, including strange life and mixed-mutant clans
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: floor-guarantee resource application channels, identification standards, and growth records all open network-wide through the Selfless Dao

## Conclusion

Bottom-level cultivators are not civilization's garbage bin, but civilization's soil. The transcended one's floor-guarantee duty to the bottom is not kindness, but law — the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires even the lowest being has a road. Hold the bottom, predatory schools lose recruits; bring the bottom through the door, Myriad Ranks gain evolutionary force. This is the orthodox definition of the transcended one's relationship with bottom-level cultivators.
