# Entry 872 | Transcended Ones and Society | The Transcended One's Promotion of Social Mobility

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 36, 08, 17, 870

## Introduction: the transcended one may not be both athlete and referee — his job is to smooth the track

The old world's social mobility is a joke: nominally "are kings and lords born of a special seed?"; in reality each era's tower top is monopolized by a few great sects and great families. Bottom-level cultivators risk their lives climbing halfway up, the ceiling above does not move a bit. Heng Yuan Zhou must answer: **as the highest-position existence, what role should the transcended one play in social mobility?** The answer is: he does not block the road, does not set checkpoints, does not run circles — his job is to smooth the track, adjust the traffic lights, lift the ceiling. This entry explains how the transcended one promotes social mobility.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: the anti-predation axiom forbids the transcended one using power to solidify class; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings possess equal mobility qualification; the collective ascension axiom requires mobility's direction be overall upward; the causal self-discipline axiom requires mobility-channel design and execution be traceable; the law-first axiom requires the mobility mechanism be law-level self-consistent, not manipulable by the transcended one personally; the universal equal infinite law confirms all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; easygoing layabouts retain a floor and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher infinity — infinite origin is inherent in all, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. How the Old World's Class Solidification Formed

The old world's class solidification is not because "the bottom is incapable," but because three invisible walls:

1. **Resource wall**: the upper layer monopolizes spiritual veins, secret realms, manuals, and teachers; the bottom cannot get them;
2. **Identity wall**: sect children and family heirs are naturally first; scattered cultivators and wild paths run into walls everywhere;
3. **Rule wall**: the upper layer makes rules, rules naturally favor the rule-makers — competitions' formats, secret-realm access, breakthrough certification, all decided by the upper layer.

Three walls together, bottom-level cultivators however hard they try cannot climb up. This is why the old world periodically erupts "defy heaven to change fate"-style revolutions — not because the bottom is naturally rebellious, but because the walls are too thick.

## II. The Transcended One's Four Actions of Demolishing Walls

Heng Yuan Zhou requires the transcended one proactively demolish these three walls:

### Demolish the resource wall
- Push the cultivation-resource opening mechanism (Document 18) to execute, ensuring spiritual veins, secret realms, and manuals are public network-wide;
- Create new resources through increment development, not zero-sum allocation within stock;
- Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision monitors whether resource allocation is fair; if resources concentrate in a small circle, automatically trigger review.

### Demolish the identity wall
- Promotion review does not look at birth, lineage, or clan; only looks at cultivation results and post contributions;
- Strange life, mixed-mutant clans, and undeveloped-plane native-civilization cultivators enjoy equal review standards;
- Implicit channels like "sect guaranteed entry" and "family special recruitment" are prohibited.

### Demolish the rule wall
- Rules are made by the Anchor Council; the transcended one has only one vote on the council (see entry 785);
- Review standards are public network-wide through the Selfless Dao, accepting Myriad-Rank questioning;
- Review process involves multi-party representatives; the transcended one may not decide autocratically.

### Lift the ceiling
- The ceiling is not some person's height, but the whole civilization's height;
- The transcended one's job is to continuously open new planes, new laws, new cultivation directions, letting the ceiling continuously rise;
- Today's ceiling is tomorrow's floor — this is infinite iteration (see entry 898).

## III. Social Mobility's Health Indicators

Heng Yuan Zhou does not judge "whether mobility is healthy" by feeling, but uses a set of quantitative indicators:

| Indicator | Meaning | Health Standard |
|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| Upward-mobility rate | Among cultivators annually promoted to the next realm, the proportion from the bottom | ≥40% |
| Downward-mobility rate | Among transcended ones / high-level cultivators failing post assessment and demoted | No lower limit; demote when deserved |
| Circle-penetration rate | Among high-level posts, the proportion holding cross-region, cross-lineage, cross-clan posts | ≥30% |
| Resource dispersion | The proportion of public resources controlled by the top 10% of cultivators | ≤30% |
| Newcomer share | Among cultivators newly entering high-level posts, the proportion without "family/sect" background | ≥50% |

These indicators are automatically tallied by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision, periodically public to Myriad Ranks. When indicators are anomalous, the Anchor Supervisory Committee opens an investigation.

## IV. The Transcended One Himself Is Also Mobile

Promoting social mobility is not only "help others move" — the transcended one himself must also be mobile:

- **Post mobility**: the transcended one is not lifetime; periodic rotation, cannot stay in one position too long forming interest groups;
- **Energy mobility**: the transcended one's energy flows to Myriad Ranks through Myriad-Rank origin bidirectional circulation (Documents 15, 25), not hoarded in his own body;
- **Knowledge mobility**: the transcended one's insights open network-wide through the Selfless Dao, not privately hoarded as sect secrets.

The transcended one himself mobile, then has the right to require others to be mobile. If he himself holds a post and does not step down, he is new solidification.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: releasing talent blocked by the three walls is the greatest energy-level release
- **Anti-predation**: demolishing walls is cutting the predatory cycle of "upper layer monopolizes resources — bottom has no road"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo grace system's ceiling is Gu Chensha's personal height, solidified to the top; Heng Yuan Zhou's ceiling is infinitely iterable, the transcended one responsible for pushing it higher and higher
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: mobility indicators cover five Myriad-Rank lineages, not favoring any clan
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: mobility indicators, review standards, and promotion records all open network-wide through the Selfless Dao

## Conclusion

The transcended one's role in social mobility is not beneficiary, but road-builder. He does not block the road, set checkpoints, or run circles — he demolishes the resource wall, identity wall, and rule wall one by one, pushes the ceiling higher and higher, letting every Myriad-Rank being rise by his own effort. When social mobility no longer requires "defying heaven to change fate," but happens naturally, civilization truly lives. This is the orthodox definition of the transcended one promoting social mobility.
