# Entry 874 | Transcended Ones and Economy | The Increment-Development Economic Model

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 717, 754, 719

## Introduction: do not grab the old cake, only make new cake — increment development's economic philosophy

The old world's economic growth has only one road: grab. Grab spiritual veins, secret realms, mineral veins, population. Grab it and prosper for a while; fail to grab and wait to be grabbed by others. Heng Yuan Zhou completely reverses this: **economic growth relies not on grabbing stock, but on creating increments — make new cake, not cut old cake.** This entry explains increment development as an economic model: how it runs, why it is sustainable, what the transcended one does in it.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: the anti-predation axiom is increment development's philosophical root — do not consume stock, do not prey on the weak; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires increment results be shared by Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires increment development serve overall evolution; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the increment-development process be traceable and not disturb causality; the law-first axiom requires increment development's law framework be self-consistent and infinitely scalable; the universal equal infinite law confirms all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; easygoing layabouts retain a floor and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher infinity — infinite origin is inherent in all, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. What Is Increment Development

Increment development is not "use stock sparingly," but **create resources that did not originally exist.** It has three layers:

1. **Discover increments**: open new planes, new cosmoses, new energy belts, discovering natural resources not yet used by any civilization;
2. **Create increments**: through law weaving, "design" resources that could not originally exist — new energy forms, new material structures, new cultivation materials;
3. **Transform increments**: transform existing low-value resources into high-value resources through technical means (e.g., Multiplication Fruit technologization, energy standardization).

Three layers together, increment development's essence is: **turn "nothing" into "something," turn "little" into "much," turn "low value" into "high value."** This is not zero-sum game; this is positive-sum creation.

## II. Increment Development's Economic Cycle

Increment development is not a one-time "treasure discovery," but a sustainable economic cycle:

```
New-plane frontier → resource preliminary development → technical processing raises value → Myriad-Rank shared allocation
     ↑                                                                ↓
     └──── Myriad-Rank evolution feeds back new-plane-frontier capability ←──┘
```

This cycle's key is: **each round of increment development raises Myriad Ranks' overall evolutionary level, thus making the next round of increment development more capable.** This is positive feedback — the more developed, the stronger; the stronger, the more capable of developing. It stands in fundamental opposition to predatory economy's negative feedback (the more grabbed, the weaker; the weaker, the more need to grab).

## III. The Transcended One's Concrete Work in Increment Development

The transcended one bears the core role in the increment-development economic model:

### New-Cosmos Frontier Post (entry 719)
- Search the multiverse for newly openable planes;
- Assess the new plane's law self-consistency, resource potential, causal risk;
- Draft the frontier plan, report to the Anchor Council for approval.

### Law Weaving
- Design a stable law framework for the new plane, ensuring new resources do not "collapse once developed";
- Deduce mathematical models for new energy forms, ensuring metrology, transmission, and distribution.

### Increment-Resource Creation
- Use the deduction-iteration system (Document 11) to deduce synthesis paths for new materials and new energy;
- Use the life-cycle system (Document 12) to design renewable resource loops;
- Use the spatial-law dispatch system (Document 13) to optimize cross-plane resource transmission.

### Alaya Network + Multi-Layer Supervision
- Record every step of increment development throughout, ensuring no overstepping to prey on stock;
- Monitor whether increment allocation is fair, preventing new resources from again being monopolized by small circles.

## IV. The Essential Difference Between Increment Development and the Old World's "Extraction"

| Dimension | Old-World Extraction | Heng-Yuan-Zhou Increment Development |
|-----------|----------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Object | Existing stock | Newly created / newly discovered |
| Competition | Grab territory | Open new territory |
| Environment | Predatory extraction, use-and-discard | Cyclic development, renewable |
| Result allocation | Strong ones take all | Myriad Ranks share |
| Sustainability | Collapse when stock exhausted | Infinite iteration |
| Causal impact | Disturb local causality | Observe first, limited intervention |

The difference's core is: **old-world extraction is "take"; increment development is "make."** What is taken always has an end; what is made may be infinite.

The reason increment development's economic cycle is sustainable lies in its connecting "discovery—creation—transformation" three links. Discovering a new plane is only the first step; transform the new plane's resources into energy usable by Myriad Ranks, transform energy into cultivation results, transform cultivation results into frontier capability — every step makes civilization as a whole stronger, not some person stronger. This is Myriad-Rank origin bidirectional circulation (Documents 15, 25)'s concrete expression in the economic field.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: do not waste energy on stock contest; invest all in increment creation
- **Anti-predation**: increment development fundamentally eliminates predation's economic motive — no need to grab
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: the Tang-Luo economy relies on Gu Chensha's personal grace, the grace amount limited; Heng Yuan Zhou relies on the transcended-one team's continuous increment frontier, the economy has no upper limit
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: increment results are shared by Myriad Ranks, allocated through the household-responsibility mechanism
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: increment-development technology, new-plane list, and allocation rules all open network-wide through the Selfless Dao

## Conclusion

The increment-development economic model turns "grabbing cake" into "making cake." It does not rely on predation of stock, but on opening new planes, weaving new laws, transforming new resources. The transcended one's role in it is core engineer — he frontiers, he weaves, he creates, but he does not possess, monopolize, or take all. When the cake is always getting bigger, Myriad Ranks no longer need to tear at each other. This is the orthodox definition of the increment-development economic model.
