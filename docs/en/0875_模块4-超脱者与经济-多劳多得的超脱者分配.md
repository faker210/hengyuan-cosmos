# Entry 875 | Transcended Ones and Economy | More-Work-More-Pay Transcended-One Allocation

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 06, 22, 724, 727

## Introduction: the transcended one is not a retired cadre — how much he takes depends entirely on how much he did

The old world's allocation to the strong is "status allocation": what realm you are in, what treatment you get. The Great Emperor lives in an imperial palace, uses immortal artifacts, receives Myriad Ranks' offerings — not because he did much work, but because he is strongest. Heng Yuan Zhou reverses this: **how much the transcended one takes depends not on how strong he is, but on how much work he did.** This is more-work-more-pay's concrete execution on the transcended one. This entry explains the transcended one's allocation mechanism.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: the anti-predation axiom negates the "strong take all" allocation logic; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires allocation be fair and open; the collective ascension axiom requires allocation incentivize Myriad-Rank co-building; the causal self-discipline axiom requires allocation be traceable throughout; the law-first axiom requires allocation laws be self-consistent, not manipulated by personal ties; the universal equal infinite law confirms all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; easygoing layabouts retain a floor and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher infinity — infinite origin is inherent in all, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why the Old World's Allocation Is Inevitable Unfair

The old world's allocation rules look also very "reasonable":

- Sect-competition winner takes the first-place prize;
- Secret-realm exploration first-come-first-served;
- Higher cultivation gets more resources.

But these rules' premise is "fair starting point" — and the starting point is never fair. Great-sect children have the best resources from childhood; scattered cultivators start behind the starting line from birth. So-called "fair competition" only wraps starting-point unfairness in rules.

A deeper problem is: **allocation decoupled from contribution.** A Great Emperor may have sat in the imperial palace for a hundred thousand years, doing nothing, but his offerings do not decrease a bit. A bottom-level cultivator may have worked in a workshop a lifetime, retiring with nothing. This is not more-work-more-pay; this is more-grab-more-pay.

## II. Heng Yuan Zhou's Allocation Formula

Heng Yuan Zhou's allocation formula for the transcended one is very simple:

**Receipt = post-contribution score × increment-development score × civilizational-weight coefficient × supervision score**

The four variables' meanings:

1. **Post-contribution score**: quantified score of the transcended one's completing legal post duties (law maintenance, Myriad-Rank guidance, cross-plane construction, etc.);
2. **Increment-development score**: quantified score of the transcended one opening new resources, new planes, new laws;
3. **Civilizational-weight coefficient**: comprehensively calculated from the transcended one's historical contributions, post difficulty, Myriad-Rank evaluation;
4. **Supervision score**: Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's compliance score; deductions for violations, bonuses for cooperation.

This formula's core is: **no look at birth, lineage, or clan; only look at contribution.** Do more → take more; do less → take less; do nothing → demote.

## III. Several Allocation Hard Rules

More-work-more-pay is not empty words; it has several hard rules:

- **No ceiling, but a protected floor**: take as much as contribution warrants; no "transcended-one salary ceiling"; but if post assessment is unqualified, the floor lowers until transcended-one qualification is revoked;
- **Stock does not participate in allocation**: the transcended one may not allocate existing stock — his receipt must come from increment development. This ensures he does not compete with Myriad Ranks over stock;
- **Allocation public**: every transcended one's allocation detail is public network-wide through the Selfless Dao; Myriad Ranks may query;
- **Myriad-Rank evaluation counted**: the allocation formula includes Myriad-Rank satisfaction score; not "self-work-self-evaluate";
- **Allocation tied to cultivation resources**: the transcended one's cultivation resources are not "free supply," but disbursed from allocation — the more he does, the more cultivation resources.

## IV. Allocation Differences Between Transcended Ones

More-work-more-pay means allocation differences between transcended ones — this is not inequality, this is reasonable incentive:

- A transcended one who opened a new cosmos takes far more than one who only does routine maintenance on old planes;
- A transcended one who trained ten new transcended ones takes more than one who did not teach a single disciple;
- A transcended one who stepped forward in crisis and protected Myriad Ranks takes more than one who usually "slacks off."

The difference's purpose is not to create classes, but **incentivize behavior**: you want more? Then do more work, open more, teach more. Not go grab, monopolize, or run small circles.

The allocation formula's transparency is critical. If how much the transcended one takes is not public, Myriad Ranks will suspect — "is he embezzling?" "is he favoring someone?" Public allocation details turn suspicion into data, turn data into trust. Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision ensures data is not falsified; Selfless Dao network-wide opening ensures everyone can query. When allocation is transparent, Myriad Ranks will not internally wear down over "unfairness."

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: allocation tied to contribution, eliminating the energy waste of "holding a post without working"
- **Anti-predation**: allocation comes from increment not stock; the transcended one need not prey on Myriad Ranks to support himself
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo's allocation is grace system — he gives whomever he gives, the standard in his heart; Heng Yuan Zhou's allocation is formula system — four variables all public, the standard in Myriad Ranks' hands
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the allocation formula treats transcended ones from five Myriad-Rank lineages equally
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the allocation formula and every transcended one's allocation detail open network-wide through the Selfless Dao

## Conclusion

More-work-more-pay's execution on the transcended one turns "status allocation" into "contribution allocation." How much the transcended one takes depends not on how strong he is, but on how much work he did; not on who he is, but on how much increment he created. Do more → take more; do nothing → demote. This is not coldness; this is fairness — true Myriad-Rank republicanism means even the strongest must work for his own keep. This is the orthodox definition of transcended-one allocation.
