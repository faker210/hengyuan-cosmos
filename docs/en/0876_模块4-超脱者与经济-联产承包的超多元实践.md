# Entry 876 | Transcended Ones and Economy | The Hyper-Multiverse Practice of Household-Responsibility Contracting

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 22, 06, 724, 18

## Introduction: contract the "land" to those who farm best — household-responsibility at hyper-multiverse scale

Household-responsibility contracting at the mortal level is a plain truth: divide land to those who farm best, more harvest, more kept. Heng Yuan Zhou scales this plain logic up to hyper-multiverse scale: **"contract" planes, law segments, and resource belts to the transcended ones best at maintaining them; increment output goes to the contractor as incentive; stock belongs to Myriad Ranks in common.** This entry explains household-responsibility contracting's concrete practice at the transcended-one level.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: the anti-predation axiom requires contracting not become occupation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires contracted land not become private property; the collective ascension axiom requires contracted output serve overall evolution; the causal self-discipline axiom requires contracting boundaries be clear and traceable; the law-first axiom requires the contracting system be law-level self-consistent; the universal equal infinite law confirms all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; easygoing layabouts retain a floor and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher infinity — infinite origin is inherent in all, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. What Is Hyper-Multiverse-Scale Household-Responsibility Contracting

Household-responsibility contracting in Heng Yuan Zhou's base version (Document 22) refers to cultivation resources allocated by the principle of "who develops, who benefits, more work more pay." At the transcended-one level, this principle is scaled up to:

- **Plane contracting**: a newly opened plane is contracted to one transcended one (or a team) for development; increment output is proportionally allocated to the contractor as incentive;
- **Law-segment contracting**: maintenance, iteration, and optimization of some law-network segment is contracted to a dedicated transcended one; maintenance quality counts toward civilizational weight;
- **Resource-belt contracting**: cross-plane resource-belt development is contracted to a transcended-one team; output allocated by contribution;
- **Clan-guidance contracting**: evolution guidance for some Myriad-Rank clan is contracted to a transcended one; guidance effectiveness counts in assessment.

Contracting is not possession. The contractor has management right and revenue-sharing right over the "contracted land," but no ownership — ownership always belongs to Myriad Ranks.

## II. The Responsibility Contract's Three Cores

### Clear responsibility
Every contract clearly states: what the contractor is responsible for, what the goals are, how long the term, what the assessment standards are, what happens if not met. No vague zones.

### Revenue tied to output
The contractor's revenue is directly tied to output:
- Exceeding goals → the excess is proportionally rewarded;
- Meeting basic goals → receive base allocation;
- Failing goals → allocation deducted; severe cases have the contracting right withdrawn.

### Supervision in place
The contract leaves traces throughout under Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision:
- All contractor operations are traceable;
- Output data is automatically tallied, not self-reported;
- Myriad-Rank representatives may at any time question the contractor's performance.

## III. The Boundary Between Contracting and Monopoly

Household-responsibility contracting is most easily misunderstood as "disguised monopoly" — I contracted this plane, this plane is mine. Heng Yuan Zhou uses three firewalls to draw the line between contracting and monopoly:

1. **Ownership not transferable**: the contractor has only management right, no ownership. The plane always belongs to Myriad Ranks; the contracting right automatically returns at term end;
2. **Stock not withheld**: stock resources already on the contracted land may not be withheld for the contractor's own use; what he may take is only the increment part;
3. **Monopoly review**: if one transcended one contracts more than a certain number of planes / law segments / resource belts, Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision automatically triggers anti-monopoly review, preventing "super landlords."

Three firewalls ensure contracting is a responsibility system, not enfeoffment.

## IV. The Special Challenges of Hyper-Multiverse Practice

Doing household-responsibility contracting at hyper-multiverse scale is far more complex than mortal scale:

- **Cross-plane contracting**: the contractor may need to simultaneously manage several planes with different law frameworks, requiring extremely strong law comprehension;
- **Heterogeneous resource contracting**: different clans' resource forms are completely different (energy clans need no mineral veins; consciousness clans need no material resources); the contracting plan must be customized per clan;
- **Long-cycle contracting**: plane development may span several thousand years; the contractor's lifespan is not the problem, but the contract's continuity is — therefore the contractor must train a successor;
- **Causal contracting**: contracting an undeveloped plane is itself a causal operation; it must first follow the "observe first, limited intervention" procedure (entry 897).

These challenges require the transcended one not only to have power, but project-management ability, cross-civilization communication ability, long-cycle planning ability. This is why the transcended one is not a mere "cultivator," but a post officeholder.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: contracting resources to those best at maintaining them is far more efficient than centralized management
- **Anti-predation**: contracting only takes increment share, does not touch stock, eliminating "contracting turns into occupation"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in the Tang-Luo grace system resources are allocated by Tang Luo alone, inefficient and unfair; Heng Yuan Zhou uses household-responsibility to contract resources to those who farm best, more work more pay
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: contracting plans are customized for five Myriad-Rank lineages' life forms, no one-size-fits-all
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: contracts, output data, and allocation details all open network-wide through the Selfless Dao

## Conclusion

Household-responsibility contracting's hyper-multiverse-scale practice turns "more work more pay" from slogan into institution. It contracts planes, law segments, and resource belts to the transcended ones best at maintaining them, letting them produce more and take more; meanwhile using three firewalls to prevent contracting from turning into monopoly. Contracting is not possession; a responsibility system is not enfeoffment — this is household-responsibility contracting's orthodox expression at the transcended-one level.
