# Entry 877 | Transcended Ones and Economy | Eliminating Resource Monopoly

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 47, 18, 04, 876

## Introduction: the transcended one is strongest — therefore he is least allowed to monopolize

The old world's economic monopoly is a natural result: whoever is strongest monopolizes. Spiritual veins held in hand, manuals locked in the vault, secret realms set as private property. Monopolists beautifully call it "transmission," but essentially it is turning Myriad Ranks' evolutionary resources into the family's private property. Heng Yuan Zhou must answer: **a transcended one as strong as the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is himself the greatest monopoly risk source — how does the system prevent him from monopolizing?** This entry explains this question.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: the anti-predation axiom directly negates monopoly itself; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires resources belong to Myriad Ranks; the collective ascension axiom requires resources flow to serve the whole; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the monopoly-prevention mechanism be traceable; the law-first axiom requires anti-monopoly laws be self-consistent, not bypassable by the transcended one; the universal equal infinite law confirms all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; easygoing layabouts retain a floor and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher infinity — infinite origin is inherent in all, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Three Forms of Monopoly

Resource monopoly in the old world has three forms; Heng Yuan Zhou has targeted prevention for each:

### Physical monopoly: holding spiritual veins without developing
- Old world: great sects occupy the best spiritual veins, only use for their own; outsiders who want to touch are killed;
- Heng Yuan Zhou: spiritual veins belong to Myriad Ranks; any cultivator (including the transcended one) has only usage right, no ownership right. Long-undeveloped veins are automatically withdrawn and reallocated.

### Knowledge monopoly: locking manuals in the vault
- Old world: sect manuals are transmitted only within inner sects; outsiders cannot even see them;
- Heng Yuan Zhou: all manuals open network-wide through the Selfless Dao. The transcended one's insights must be textbook-ified before public (see entries 720, 781). Privately hoarding manuals counts as monopoly behavior.

### Channel monopoly: controlling secret-realm entrances
- Old world: secret-realm entrances guarded by great sects, pay to enter;
- Heng Yuan Zhou: secret realms are public resources; entrances are uniformly dispatched by the Alaya Network, entry qualification allocated by contribution and need. The transcended one sets no checkpoints, collects no fees.

## II. Why the Transcended One Is the Greatest Monopoly Risk

This is not pleasant to say, but must be said: **the transcended one himself is monopoly's greatest risk source.**

The reason is simple:
- He is strongest; if he wants to monopolize, no one can stop him;
- He lives longest; he has enough time to build a monopoly system;
- He has the broadest vision; he knows which resources are most valuable;
- He has the most disciples; monopoly can pass down through lineage generation after generation.

The old world's Great Emperors, every one was a successful monopolist. They monopolized spiritual veins, monopolized manuals, monopolized secret realms, monopolized the qualification of "who may cultivate." Then they beautifully called it "transmission."

Heng Yuan Zhou cannot, because the transcended one is strong, assume he will not monopolize — **the system must assume he will, then design sufficiently strong prevention.**

## III. Five Defense Lines Against Monopoly

Heng Yuan Zhou sets five defense lines against the transcended one's monopoly:

### Line 1: Ownership inviolable
All cultivation resources' ownership belongs to Myriad Ranks, not any individual. The transcended one has only usage and management right, no ownership right. This is a constitutional-level provision (Document 40 Symbiotic Constitution General Outline).

### Line 2: Alaya Network + Multi-Layer Supervision
All the transcended one's resource-use behavior leaves traces throughout. The Alaya algorithm automatically detects anomalous patterns:
- Is the number of resources under some transcended one's name continuously growing?
- Is there "only in, no out" hoarding?
- Are resources being directionally routed to specific groups through lineage relationships?

### Line 3: Anti-Monopoly Law
Anti-Monopoly Law (Document 47) expressly stipulates:
- No individual may monopolize more than a certain proportion of public resources;
- Monopoly behavior once discovered, forced breakup + withdrawal + penalty;
- The transcended one violating anti-monopoly law faces a higher penalty standard than ordinary cultivators — because his damage is greater.

### Line 4: Rotation System
The transcended one periodically changes posts and responsible regions, preventing staying in one place too long and forming "territorial consciousness." Rotation frequency is automatically adjusted by the Alaya Network based on risk assessment.

### Line 5: Myriad-Rank Supervision
Myriad-Rank representatives have the right to question the transcended one's resource use. If the Myriad-Rank Representative Assembly considers some transcended one under monopoly suspicion, it may open an independent investigation.

## IV. Balancing Monopoly Prevention and Household-Responsibility Contracting

Household-responsibility contracting (entry 876) gives the transcended one some resource-management right, while anti-monopoly must prevent him from monopolizing — how to balance these two?

The answer is: **contracting is a time-limited, scope-bounded, assessed responsibility system; monopoly is time-unlimited, scope-unbounded, unconstrained occupation.** The key to system design is strictly confining "contracting" within the responsibility framework:

- Contracting has a contract term, automatically returns at expiration;
- Contracting has a clear scope, may not overstep and expand;
- Contracting has assessment standards, failure returns the contracting right;
- Contracting has Alaya supervision, anomalies automatically alert.

Contracting is responsibility; monopoly is infringement — the boundary between the two is dead-drawn by the system.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: monopoly locks resources in few people's hands, wasting Myriad Ranks' evolutionary potential; opening resources is the greatest energy saving
- **Anti-predation**: monopoly is essentially institutionalized predation — turning public resources into private property
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is the greatest monopolist; he monopolized the Dao, monopolized resources, monopolized the qualification of "who can become strong"; Heng Yuan Zhou uses five defense lines to ensure no one — even as strong as the transcended one — can monopolize
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: anti-monopoly faces all Myriad-Rank resource forms, no clan or plane distinction
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: anti-monopoly law, resource lists, and use records all open network-wide through the Selfless Dao

## Conclusion

The transcended one is strongest, therefore he is least allowed to monopolize. The old world's Great Emperors monopolized one more than the next; Heng Yuan Zhou uses five defense lines to block all monopoly roads: ownership belongs to Myriad Ranks, Alaya full-process supervision, anti-monopoly law expressly prohibiting it, rotation preventing rooting, Myriad-Rank supervision as backstop. Contracting is responsibility not private property; opening is principle not grace. This is the orthodox definition of eliminating resource monopoly.
