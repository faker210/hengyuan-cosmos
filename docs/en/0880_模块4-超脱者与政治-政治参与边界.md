# Entry 880 | Transcended Ones and Politics | The Transcended One's Boundary of Political Participation

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 49, 878, 881, 789

## Introduction: the transcended one may vote, but may not canvass — precise graduations of political participation

The old world's problem is: the transcended one either does not participate in politics at all (closed-door cultivation, ignore the sky falling outside), or takes over politics completely (whatever he says is law). There is no middle state. Heng Yuan Zhou must precisely mark the graduation line of the transcended one's political participation: **what he may do, what he may not do, where the boundary lies.** This entry is the precise map of this boundary.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: the anti-predation axiom requires the boundary itself be anti-predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the boundary protect Myriad Ranks' political subjectivity; the collective ascension axiom requires participation not deviate from the ascension direction; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the boundary be enforceable and traceable; the law-first axiom requires the boundary be law-level self-consistent, leaving no gray zones; the universal equal infinite law confirms all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; easygoing layabouts retain a floor and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher infinity — infinite origin is inherent in all, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. What May Be Done: The Transcended One's Legitimate Political Participation

### 1. Voting right
As a Myriad-Rank citizen, the transcended one has the right to participate in Anchor Council representative elections and votes. His one vote weighs the same as any other citizen's one vote.

### 2. Proposal right
The transcended one may submit bill proposals to the council, following normal procedure (see entry 785). Proposals must attach technical justification and accept public review.

### 3. Speech right
In the invited technical-explanation segment, the transcended one has the right to speak. Speech duration is the same as other representatives.

### 4. Petition right
Like ordinary citizens, the transcended one may submit petitions to the council through formal channels.

### 5. Eligibility right
The transcended one may run for Myriad-Rank representative. But once elected, his rights and duties in the council are identical to other representatives.

## II. What May Not Be Done: The Transcended One's Political Forbidden Zones

### 1. May not use force to pressure
The transcended one may not display force, threaten, or in any form imply "I am stronger than you" in political activity. His force is invisible on political occasions.

### 2. May not form political organizations
The transcended one may not form parties, political factions, or any organization targeting political influence. He may join Myriad-Rank representatives' discussions, but may not organize them.

### 3. May not control media / public opinion
The transcended one may not use Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's technical advantage to manipulate public opinion — e.g., targeted push, post deletion, search-trend guidance.

### 4. May not privately confer benefits
The transcended one may not exchange resources, contracting rights, or promotion opportunities for political support. This is political corruption.

### 5. May not interfere with elections
The transcended one may not stand with, endorse, or use prestige to influence some candidate's election result. Elections are Myriad Ranks' affair.

### 6. May not bypass institutions to directly administer
The transcended one may not "because the council discussion is taking too long" directly issue orders. Any administrative decision must follow the Symbiotic Executive Committee procedure.

## III. The Boundary's Gray Zones and Adjudication

Some situations are not fully on the "may" or "may not" list, e.g.:

- The transcended one published at an academic conference a paper on some policy's technical impact — does it count as "political speech"?
- The transcended one at a private gathering discussed some bill with friends — does it count as "private lobbying"?
- The transcended one in a media interview answered political questions — does it count as "opinion manipulation"?

These gray zones are not judged by the transcended one himself, but adjudicated by the **Symbiotic Court Political Division**. The adjudication standard is: **did this behavior in substance affect Myriad Ranks' political autonomous decision?** If yes, overstepped; if no, not overstepped.

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision automatically flags gray-zone behavior and submits it to the Political Division for pre-review.

## IV. The Boundary's Dynamic Adjustment

The political-participation boundary is not fixed. With civilizational development, technological progress, and new planes joining, new political-participation forms may appear (e.g., cross-plane remote voting, consciousness-domain political discussion). The boundary is legislatively adjusted by the Anchor Council — the transcended one in the council has only one vote and cannot expand his own power.

The adjustment principle is: **any political-participation behavior not expressly permitted by legislation, the transcended one uniformly does not do.** This is the opposite of ordinary citizens' "what is not prohibited is permitted" principle — the transcended one is "what is not permitted is not permitted," because his behavior has too great an impact.

The precise boundary's significance is: it lets the transcended one not need to guess every day "may I do this." On the list, may do; not on the list, may not; gray zones, go to court. This kind of certainty protects both the transcended one and Myriad Ranks — the transcended one knows he will not accidentally overstep; Myriad Ranks knows the transcended one will not suddenly overstep.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: precise boundaries let the transcended one concentrate energy on post fulfillment, not waste on political activity
- **Anti-predation**: the boundary directly cuts the "force exchanged for political power" path
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo has no boundary — he does whatever he wants; Heng Yuan Zhou draws the transcended one a precise boundary map
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the boundary applies to all transcended ones, no exception for clan, contribution, or seniority
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: boundary lists and gray-zone adjudication cases all open network-wide through the Selfless Dao

## Conclusion

The transcended one's political-participation boundary is a precise map: vote, propose, speak, petition, be elected — permitted; pressure, form parties, control opinion, confer privately, interfere with elections, bypass institutions — prohibited. Gray zones are adjudicated by the court, the boundary adjusted by legislation, and the transcended one follows "what is not permitted is not permitted." Boundaries clear, Myriad Ranks' politics will not be crushed by one person's force. This is the orthodox definition of the transcended one's political-participation boundary.
