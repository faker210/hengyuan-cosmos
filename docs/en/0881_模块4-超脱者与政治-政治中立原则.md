# Entry 881 | Transcended Ones and Politics | The Transcended One's Political Neutrality Principle

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 49, 880, 792, 789

## Introduction: the transcended one does not take sides — because his force standing on any side will crush the scale

The old world's strong ones naturally take sides. If he is friendly with this sect, he helps this sect beat that sect; if he takes a fancy to some dynasty, he supports it to unify the world. His force is like a weight on a balance — put on whichever side, that side wins. Heng Yuan Zhou must take this weight off the balance: **the transcended one in Myriad-Rank politics must maintain neutrality, not take sides, not favor, not choose a side.** This entry explains this neutrality principle.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: the anti-predation axiom requires neutrality so as not to abet tyranny; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the transcended one treat all Myriad-Rank clans equally; the collective ascension axiom requires neutrality serve overall ascension not local interest; the causal self-discipline axiom requires neutrality behavior be traceable; the law-first axiom requires the neutrality principle be law-level self-consistent; the universal equal infinite law confirms all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; easygoing layabouts retain a floor and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher infinity — infinite origin is inherent in all, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Why the Transcended One Must Be Neutral

The transcended one's neutrality is not a moral choice, but a physical necessity:

- **He is too heavy**: one transcended one's force suffices to affect any political struggle's result. He stands on whichever side, that side wins — this is not fair competition, this is force crushing;
- **He is too long**: the transcended one lives tens of thousands of years; the political force he sides with today may tomorrow become the new oppressor. Long-term siding inevitably alienates;
- **He is too broad**: the transcended one simultaneously faces countless clans, planes, and issues. He cannot have a stance on every issue — if he forcefully does, his stances will self-contradict;
- **He is a public resource**: the transcended one is a public resource co-built by Myriad Ranks, not any political faction's private tool.

Four reasons together: **the cost of the transcended one taking sides is too great, too great for Myriad Ranks to bear.**

## II. The Neutrality Principle's Three-Layer Meaning

### Layer 1: No favoritism
The transcended one does not choose a side in Myriad Ranks' internal political struggles. Two clans argue over resource allocation; the transcended one does not help either — he provides technical data, but does not take a side for either.

### Layer 2: No adjudication
The transcended one does not serve as the "final appellate judge" for Myriad Ranks' internal political disputes. Disputes are negotiated by the council, adjudicated by the Symbiotic Court; the transcended one does not overstep.

### Layer 3: No preference
The transcended one in resource allocation, post arrangement, and promotion review does not show preference for specific clans, regions, or lineages. Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision detects systematic bias in allocation data.

Three layers together: **neutrality is not indifference, it is fairness.** The transcended one's love for Myriad Ranks does not favor any one child.

## III. Neutrality's Exceptions: When May One Not Be Neutral

The neutrality principle is not absolute. Under two situations, the transcended one may break neutrality:

### Exception 1: Predatory-school invasion
If external predatory-school forces invade Myriad-Rank-republican territory, the transcended one must resist with all his strength — this is not taking sides, this is self-defense. Neutrality does not apply to life-or-death external threats.

### Exception 2: Predatory behavior appears within Myriad Ranks
If some group within Myriad Ranks is preying on other groups (e.g., a large clan oppressing a small one), the transcended one must stop it. This is not taking sides, this is law enforcement — the anti-predation axiom outranks the neutrality principle.

The two exceptions' common feature: **neutrality protects fair competition within Myriad-Rank republicanism, not predators' "rights."** When neutrality itself becomes abetting predation, neutrality yields to anti-predation.

## IV. Neutrality vs. Technical Opinion

Maintaining neutrality does not equal not expressing technical opinions. The transcended one may say:

- "This plan has X risks at the law level";
- "That plan's causal disturbance range is Y";
- "According to Alaya data, plan Z's long-term effect is better."

These are technical fact statements, not political stances. The distinction is:
- Technical statement: "This road does not work at the law level" — neutral;
- Political stance: "I support this road because it benefits my friend" — not neutral.

The transcended one does only the former, not the latter.

Neutrality is not indifference. The transcended one's love for Myriad Ranks is embodied in his placing all his energy on post fulfillment — maintaining the law network, guiding Myriad-Rank evolution, opening new planes. This work itself is the greatest love for Myriad Ranks. He need not take sides in political struggles to prove whom he loves — every law segment he repairs, every class he teaches, is love.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: do not waste the transcended one's force on Myriad Ranks' internal political struggles
- **Anti-predation**: neutrality prevents the transcended one from becoming some side's predatory tool
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's "neutrality" is actually selective grace — he helps whoever he wants; Heng Yuan Zhou's neutrality is institutional, not biased by person or matter
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: neutrality means equal treatment of five lineages
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: neutrality-principle execution records and exception-triggering cases all open network-wide through the Selfless Dao

## Conclusion

The transcended one's political neutrality is not indifference, it is fairness. He does not take sides, favor, adjudicate, or prefer; he provides technical facts, does not express political stances. The only exceptions are external predatory invasion and internal predatory behavior — then neutrality yields to self-defense and law enforcement. When the strongest does not take sides, Myriad Ranks may decide their own fate. This is the orthodox definition of the transcended one's political neutrality principle.
