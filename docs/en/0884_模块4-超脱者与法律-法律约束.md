# Entry 884 | Transcended Ones and Law | Legal Constraints on the Transcended One

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 19, 40, 789, 883

## Introduction: what may be done and what may not — the transcended one's legal-constraint list

Entry 883 explained the transcended one's equal status before the law. This entry is more concrete: **what exactly does the law constrain in the transcended one's behavior? Which behaviors are permitted, which are forbidden, which require approval?** This is an operable legal-constraint list.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: the anti-predation axiom is the constraint's core content; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires constraints protect all Myriad-Rank beings; the collective ascension axiom requires constraints not obstruct legitimate ascension work; the causal self-discipline axiom requires constraint execution be traceable; the law-first axiom requires constraint clauses be self-consistent, leaving no loopholes; the universal equal infinite law confirms all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; easygoing layabouts retain a floor and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher infinity — infinite origin is inherent in all, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Absolute-Prohibition List (Red Lines That May Not Be Touched)

The following behaviors are uniformly prohibited regardless of reason:

1. **Predating beings' origin**: extracting, devouring, or transforming other beings' origin energy in any form — including but not limited to blood sacrifice, body seizing, devouring, sucking;
2. **Breaking the law network**: deliberately damaging anchor connections, law nodes, Alaya infrastructure;
3. **Intervening with causal lines**: cross-plane operations without approval, disturbing uncontacted civilizations' historical lines;
4. **Monopolizing knowledge resources**: privately hoarding manuals, sealing secret realms, refusing the public-opening duty;
5. **Crushing others with force**: threatening, intimidating, or suppressing other beings with force in any social interaction;
6. **Falsifying Alaya records**: modifying, deleting, or fabricating behavioral records in the Alaya Network;
7. **Sheltering predatory schools**: knowing a predatory-school member but not reporting, not stopping, even providing protection.

Seven red lines; touching any one immediately triggers the highest-level legal procedure — revocation of transcended-one qualification, opening of criminal trial.

## II. Restricted-Approval List (Things That Need Approval)

The following behaviors are not themselves illegal, but must go through approval:

| Behavior | Approval Body | Approval Standard |
|----------|---------------|--------------------|
| Cross-plane operation | Anchor Supervisory Committee | Observe first, limited intervention; causal impact assessment |
| New-plane frontier | Anchor Council | Law self-consistency; no native-civilization conflict |
| Law-revision proposal | Anchor Council | No conflict with existing laws; Myriad-Rank-representative review |
| Large-scale energy deployment | Symbiotic Executive Committee | Energy scale, environmental impact, timing |
| Contact with uncontacted civilizations | Anchor Council + Ethics Review | Compliance with observe-first-limited-intervention principle |

Approval is not a formality. Each approval has complete technical justification, risk assessment, and Myriad-Rank-representative questioning.

## III. Duty-of-Care List (Special Duties the Transcended One Bears Because of Greater Ability)

Duties ordinary cultivators do not have, the transcended one must bear:

1. **Proactive-reporting duty**: if the transcended one discovers predatory-school activity, law anomalies, or civilizational-regression signs, he must proactively report — he may not treat it as "not my business";
2. **Prudent-exercise duty**: when exercising any post authority, the transcended one must be prudent to a standard beyond ordinary people. He is ten thousand times stronger than ordinary people, so his "carelessness" is also ten thousand times more dangerous than ordinary people's;
3. **Weak-protection duty**: when the transcended one is present and some weak being is being violated, he has the duty to stop it — he may not pretend not to see;
4. **Textbook-ification duty**: the transcended one's insights must be organized into textbooks and public; he may not privately hoard;
5. **Cooperation-with-investigation duty**: when Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision or the Symbiotic Court investigates, the transcended one must fully cooperate; he may not refuse on the grounds of "busy cultivating."

## IV. Balancing Constraints and Freedom

Legal constraints are not meant to tie the transcended one up. Heng Yuan Zhou insists: **constraints are for the transcended one to work better, not to stop him from working.**

- Daily post fulfillment does not require approval for everything — within post-authorization scope he has freedom of action;
- In emergencies he may act first then report — but must immediately supplement the report afterward;
- Technological innovation is unrestricted — so long as red lines are not touched, the transcended one may freely explore new directions.

The constraint's logic is: **it governs how you use force, not whether you have force.** That you have force is your affair; how you use force is the law's affair.

The existence of the constraint list is itself protection for the transcended one. Without a list, the transcended one guesses every day "may I do this"; with a list, he may put energy into working, not into figuring out the rules. Good constraints do not tie hands and feet, but let people concentrate attention on the right direction.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: a clear constraint list lets the transcended one not guess "may I do this," saving cognitive energy
- **Anti-predation**: seven red lines directly seal the behavioral patterns predatory schools most rely on
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo has no constraint list — he does whatever he wants; Heng Yuan Zhou lists three clear lists for the transcended one
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: constraints apply to all transcended ones, no exception by clan, contribution, or seniority
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: constraint lists, approval processes, and penalty cases all open network-wide through the Selfless Dao

## Conclusion

The transcended one's legal constraints are a crystal-clear list: seven red lines absolutely prohibited, five categories of behavior requiring approval, five duties borne because of greater ability. Constraints are not a binding down, but for the transcended one to use force in the right place. When the strongest knows what may not be done, Myriad Ranks may rest assured. This is the orthodox definition of the transcended one's legal constraints.
