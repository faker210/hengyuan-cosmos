# Entry 886 | Transcended Ones and Law | The Transcended One's Relationship with the Symbiotic Constitution

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 40, 03, 883, 763

## Introduction: the Symbiotic Constitution was not written by the transcended one — the transcended one is also under the constitution

The Symbiotic Constitution is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's fundamental law; all laws, rules, and covenants derive from it. The old world's Great Emperors were often "constitutional fathers" — they made the fundamental law, they interpreted the fundamental law, they could also amend the fundamental law. Heng Yuan Zhou must make clear: **the Symbiotic Constitution was not written by the transcended one; the transcended one is also under the constitution.** This entry explains the fundamental relationship between the transcended one and the Symbiotic Constitution.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: the republican axiom of Myriad Races is the constitution's legitimacy source — the constitution is Myriad Ranks' covenant, not some person's grace; the anti-predation axiom is the constitution's core content; the collective ascension axiom is the constitution's purpose; the causal self-discipline axiom requires constitution execution be traceable; the law-first axiom requires the constitution itself be law-level self-consistent and infinitely iterable; the universal equal infinite law confirms all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; easygoing layabouts retain a floor and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher infinity — infinite origin is inherent in all, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Symbiotic Constitution's Legitimacy Source

The Symbiotic Constitution (Documents 40, 03)'s legitimacy does not come from some transcended one's "imperial decree," but from:

1. **Myriad-Rank consensus**: five Myriad-Rank-lineage representatives jointly make it through the Anchor Council;
2. **Historical practice**: from the Anchor Awakening Era (Document 72) to the establishment of Myriad-Rank republicanism (Document 77), the constitution gradually took shape through long symbiotic practice;
3. **Alaya verification**: constitutional clauses pass Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's causal verification, confirmed not to produce civilizational regression;
4. **Continuous amendment**: the constitution is not fixed; the amendment procedure is led by the council.

What did the transcended one do in the constitution-making process? He provided technical support — law self-consistency checks, causal impact assessment, technical feasibility justification. But he did not "write down" the constitution; he may not "imperially decree" it.

## II. The Transcended One's Three Identities Under the Constitution

### Identity 1: Constitution observer
The transcended one must obey the Symbiotic Constitution. All rights and duties stipulated by the constitution, the transcended one follows in full. He may not on the grounds "I am the transcended one" refuse to obey constitutional clauses.

### Identity 2: Constitution guardian
The transcended one has the duty to maintain the constitution from being broken. If someone (including internal Myriad-Rank schemers) tries to overthrow the constitution, hollow out the council, abolish the supervision mechanism — the transcended one must stop it.

### Identity 3: Constitution-amendment participant
The transcended one may participate in constitutional amendment through normal legislative procedure — propose, discuss, vote; he has only one vote. He may not unilaterally amend the constitution.

Three identities together: **the transcended one is under the constitution, but he also maintains the constitution. He is not the constitution's master; he is the constitution's citizen and guardian.**

## III. Three Things the Transcended One May Not Do

### 1. May not interpret the constitution to serve himself
The constitution's interpretation power belongs to the Symbiotic Court Constitutional Division, not the transcended one. The transcended one may not say "I understand the constitution this way" to find a basis for his own behavior.

### 2. May not suspend the constitution
In emergencies, some laws may be temporarily adjusted — but the constitution may not be suspended. The transcended one may not on the grounds "situation is urgent" stop the constitution's effectiveness.

### 3. May not legislate bypassing the constitution
Any rule change must conform to the constitutional framework. The transcended one may not bypass the constitution to "temporarily legislate" — the bill he proposes, if unconstitutional, the council may not pass either.

## IV. Constitutional Amendment and the Transcended One

The constitution may be amended — civilization develops, the constitution also needs iteration. But the amendment procedure is very strict:

1. A constitutional-amendment proposal must be co-signed by at least a certain proportion of Myriad-Rank representatives;
2. The proposal must go through at least two complete review cycles;
3. The amendment must pass Myriad-Rank referendum (hyper-multiverse-scale popular vote);
4. After amendment it must pass Alaya causal verification.

The transcended one may participate in this process — he may propose, give technical explanations, vote. But he may not unilaterally amend the constitution, nor use prestige to push a "quick amendment."

The constitution is alive — it iterates with civilizational development. But iteration must follow procedure: council proposal, Myriad-Rank referendum, Alaya verification. The transcended one may not "feel the constitution is outdated" and change it himself. The constitution's authority comes from it being Myriad-Rank consensus, not from any one transcended one's recognition.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the constitution is the crystallization of Myriad-Rank consensus; the transcended one need not "reinvent the wheel"
- **Anti-predation**: the constitution itself is the anti-predation fundamental law; the transcended one being under the constitution means he also may not predate
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is the constitution — what he says is the fundamental law; Heng Yuan Zhou places Tang Luo under the constitution, he also may not violate it
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the constitution applies to all five Myriad-Rank-lineage beings, including the transcended one
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the full constitution text, amendment history, and referendum results all open network-wide through the Selfless Dao

## Conclusion

The Symbiotic Constitution was not written by the transcended one; the transcended one is also under the constitution. He is the constitution's observer, guardian, and amendment participant — but not the constitution's master. He may not interpret the constitution to serve himself, suspend the constitution, or legislate bypassing the constitution. When the strongest also must obey the fundamental law, civilization truly has a root. This is the orthodox definition of the transcended one's relationship with the Symbiotic Constitution.
