# Entry 890 | Transcended Ones and Military | Symbiotic Resolution of Civilizational Conflict

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 889, 894, 897

## Introduction: civilizational conflict is not either fight or surrender — there is a third road

The old world handling civilizational conflict has only two options: fight or surrender. Win and colonize; lose and be colonized. Heng Yuan Zhou must find a third road: **neither fight nor surrender, use symbiotic means to resolve conflict.** This entry explains how the transcended one, as conflict mediator, finds symbiotic solutions between civilizations.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: the anti-predation axiom negates "winning makes it right"; the republican axiom of Myriad Races confirms all civilizations have the right to exist; the collective ascension axiom requires conflict resolution serve common evolution; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the mediation process be traceable; the law-first axiom requires the mediation framework be self-consistent; the universal equal infinite law confirms all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; easygoing layabouts retain a floor and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher infinity — infinite origin is inherent in all, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Roots of Civilizational Conflict

Between civilizations' conflicts, on the surface are resource contest, territorial dispute, ideological opposition. Dig one layer deeper, roots are often:

1. **Information asymmetry**: two civilizations do not understand each other, mistaking the other's normal behavior for hostility;
2. **Different development models**: predatory civilizations and symbiotic civilizations necessarily conflict — because symbiotic civilizations will not let predatory civilizations prey;
3. **Living-space overlap**: two civilizations' natural expansion directions collide;
4. **Historical hatred**: distrust left over from past conflicts.

Different conflict roots, different resolution methods. The transcended one may not use one set of methods for all conflicts.

## II. The Five-Step Symbiotic-Resolution Process

### Step 1: Information isolation and observation
The transcended one first does not intervene, but observes both sides through Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision:
- Each side's demands;
- Each side's bottom line;
- Each side's internal political structure.

### Step 2: Representatives of both sides dialogue
The transcended one arranges both sides' representatives to dialogue on neutral ground:
- The transcended one only chairs, does not adjudicate;
- Both sides state demands and concerns;
- The transcended one provides technical data (resource distribution, law boundaries, etc.), does not provide a stance.

### Step 3: Symbiotic-solution design
If dialogue progresses, the transcended one assists in designing a symbiotic solution:
- Resource sharing rather than division;
- Law compatibility rather than opposition;
- Joint development rather than separate development;
- Mutual opening rather than closed-door isolation.

### Step 4: Phased implementation
After the solution is designed, implement in phases:
- Start from low-risk cooperation fields (knowledge exchange, technology sharing);
- Gradually expand to resource development, security cooperation;
- Ultimately form long-term symbiotic relations.

### Step 5: Alaya supervision
The whole process leaves traces under Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision:
- Prevent either side from using dialogue to delay and prepare for war;
- Supervise whether solution execution is fair;
- Record both sides' treaty-keeping.

## III. When Mediation Does Not Apply

Symbiotic resolution is not omnipotent. The following situations do not apply mediation:

- **Predatory-school invasion**: the other side's purpose is predation; mediation is asking a tiger for its skin — must self-defend and counterattack;
- **Dark-Substitution-Body spread**: not civilizational conflict, but infection problem; must isolate and eliminate;
- **One side refuses dialogue**: the other side does not want to talk at all; mediation is out of the question;
- **Conflict involves civilizational life-or-death**: if one side wants to exterminate the other, mediation does not apply — must first stop the extermination behavior.

When mediation does not apply, the transcended one's role shifts from mediator to defender (see entries 888, 891).

## IV. Symbiotic Resolution's Historical Achievements

In Heng Yuan Zhou's history, civilizational conflicts successfully resolved through symbiotic means include:
- Two energy-coagulating clans contesting the same star-vein: ultimately designed a joint cyclic-utilization solution;
- A carbon-based material clan and a mechanical-construct clan competing for some planet's resources: ultimately developed a layered-utilization solution;
- Two consciousness-information clans incompatible due to different law frameworks: ultimately designed a consciousness-domain translation protocol.

Success's key is not the transcended one "adjudicating," but his helping both sides see the possibility of "coexisting without fighting."

Symbiotic resolution's success key is not how strong the transcended one is, but whether he is willing to spend time. Many conflicts escalate to war precisely because neither side has time to sit and talk. The transcended one has lived for thousands of years; what he has most is time — he may spend ten years mediating one conflict, without rushing.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: do not waste energy on civilizational war; use on symbiotic construction
- **Anti-predation**: do not recognize "winning is justice"; use symbiotic solutions to replace predation logic
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo handles civilizational conflict by "if you disobey, beat you until you obey"; Heng Yuan Zhou uses mediation, dialogue, symbiotic solutions
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the resolution process faces all civilizations, including heterogeneous life forms
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: mediation records, symbiotic solutions, and execution supervision all open network-wide through the Selfless Dao

## Conclusion

Civilizational conflict is not either fight or surrender; there is a third road — symbiotic resolution. The transcended one chairs but does not adjudicate, provides data but not a stance, assists in designing symbiotic solutions. When mediation does not apply (predatory invasion, Dark Substitution Body, refusal of dialogue), only then shift to defense. If you can not fight, do not fight; if you can symbiose, do not war — this is the orthodox definition of the transcended one handling civilizational conflict.
