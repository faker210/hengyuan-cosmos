# Entry 891 | Transcended Ones and Military | The Transcended One and Plane Defense

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 816, 817, 888, 889

## Introduction: a plane is not a single plane — it is a mutually supporting web

The old world's plane defense is single-point: one plane broken through, all beings inside suffer. Heng Yuan Zhou upgrades defense from "single-point" to "network": **each plane is not an isolated island, but a node on the anchor network; nodes mutually support and back each other up.** This entry explains how the transcended one builds and maintains this plane-defense web.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: the anti-predation axiom requires defense be used only for self-defense; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the defense web protect all Myriad-Rank planes; the collective ascension axiom requires the defense web serve overall evolution; the causal self-discipline axiom requires defense deployment be traceable; the law-first axiom requires the defense architecture be self-consistent, producing no new weak points; the universal equal infinite law confirms all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; easygoing layabouts retain a floor and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher infinity — infinite origin is inherent in all, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. The Three-Layer Structure of Plane Defense

### Layer 1: Plane self-defense
Each plane bears primary responsibility for its own security:
- The plane's own cultivators undertake basic defense tasks;
- The plane's own law network is maintained by the plane's own team;
- The plane's own anomalies are monitored by the plane's own Alaya node.

This layer ensures "matters at home are managed at home," not depending on the transcended one watching around the clock.

### Layer 2: Cross-plane mutual-aid web
Planes support each other:
- Neighboring planes establish emergency support channels;
- One plane attacked, surrounding planes automatically respond;
- The transcended one as quick-reaction force, the "last line of defense" above cross-plane support.

### Layer 3: Transcended-one strategic deterrence
The transcended one as the highest deterrence:
- When the defense web is broken through, the transcended one acts directly;
- The transcended one's existence itself is a warning to invaders;
- The transcended one is not stationed at some plane — he is at the network hub, going where needed.

## II. The Defense Web's Technical Infrastructure

Plane defense is not carried by the transcended one alone; it has a complete set of technical infrastructure:

- **Anchor network**: cross-plane connection backbone, also defense-signal transmission channel;
- **Alaya monitoring web**: 24×7 monitoring each plane's anomalous energy fluctuations and law disturbances;
- **Early-warning system**: automatically alerts relevant planes and the transcended one after detecting invasion signs;
- **Fast channel**: cross-plane rapid deployment channel for the transcended one and support forces;
- **Distributed backup**: each important plane's law data has redundant backups on other planes.

## III. The Transcended One's Concrete Duties in the Defense Web

1. **Design defense architecture**: according to each plane's characteristics, design adapted defense plans;
2. **Maintain law boundaries**: ensure boundaries between planes are clear, not exploitable by invaders;
3. **Train defense forces**: help each plane cultivate its own defense team;
4. **Quick reaction**: after early warning, arrive on scene within prescribed time;
5. **Post-war repair**: after repelling invasion, help the plane repair the law network and resume normal operation.

## IV. The Asymmetry Between Defense and Attack

A core principle of plane defense is: **the defender naturally has advantage over the attacker.**

- The defender knows his terrain; the attacker does not;
- The defender has local law bonuses; the attacker does not;
- The defender may predict attack routes; the attacker must feel his way;
- The defender has Alaya early warning; the attacker does not.

The transcended one's job is to maximize this "defense advantage," making invaders pay a price far exceeding their gain. When the price of invasion is high enough, predatory schools will not come.

The defense web's highest realm is "subduing the enemy without fighting." When invaders discover attacking this civilization costs ten times the gain, they leave on their own. The transcended one's job is not to win wars, but to make wars impossible to start.

The defense web's other advantage is "scalable." Newly joined planes need not redesign their defense system — they directly plug into the existing network, automatically gaining early warning, support, and backup capability. The larger the network, the safer each node.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: distributed defense is more energy-efficient than centralized defense — no need for the transcended one to be everywhere
- **Anti-predation**: the defense web makes predatory schools unprofitable, eliminating invasion motive economically
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo's defense is him alone carrying everything — when he is absent everything is lost; Heng Yuan Zhou's defense is networked, the transcended one is only one layer
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the defense web covers all Myriad-Rank planes, including preliminary protection for undeveloped planes
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: defense architecture, early-warning standards, and drill records all open network-wide through the Selfless Dao

## Conclusion

Plane defense is not a single-point city wall, but a mutually supporting web. Plane self-defense, cross-plane mutual aid, transcended-one strategic deterrence — three-layer structure plus Alaya monitoring and fast channels, make every Myriad-Rank plane no isolated island. The transcended one's role in it is architect, quick-reactor, post-war repairer — not a 24/7 bodyguard. When the defense web is sturdy enough, the price of invasion becomes too high for anyone to afford. This is the orthodox definition of plane defense.

In the final analysis, the transcended-one-and-military / transcended-one-and-plane-defense described in this entry is no isolated case — its causal and energy-level traces are filed throughout in the Alaya Network, traceable and verifiable.
