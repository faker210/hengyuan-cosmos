## II. The Three Stages of Predatory-School Military Expansion

Predatory schools' military expansion is not random; it has a fixed pattern:

### Stage 1: Rise
A predatory-school strong one rises in some small scope, growing stronger by devouring the weak. At this time his scale is small, influence limited.

### Stage 2: Expansion
He begins to swallow neighboring civilizations; each swallowed one's energy and resources absorbed, growing stronger. The swallowing rate accelerates.

### Stage 3: Collapse
After expanding to the limit, internal contradictions erupt (conquered revolt, internal rivals split), or meets a stronger predatory school that exterminates him. A predatory-school civilization's life cycle is from weak to strong to collapse.

What Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended one does is identify this trend at Stage 1, stop it through diplomacy or defense at Stage 2, never letting it reach Stage 3.

## III. Why Predatory-School Military Expansion Inevitable Fails

Predatory schools' military expansion looks unstoppable, but it has three fatal bugs:

1. **Energy source unsustainable**: robbed energy always has an end — all weak civilizations eaten up, the predators themselves start infighting;
2. **Enemies multiply with each strike**: each civilization conquered adds a batch of haters;
3. **Internal disunity**: within predatory schools also might makes right; the strong may at any time be usurped by his own people.

These three bugs determine predatory schools' military expansion is a road of no return — it may win for a while, not forever.

## IV. Why Symbiotic-School Defense Is Sustainable

The symbiotic school's defense logic is exactly opposite:

1. **Energy source sustainable**: increment development is never exhausted, no need to predate;
2. **Friends multiply with each friendship**: each symbiotic relationship established with a civilization adds a friend;
3. **Internal unity**: within the Myriad-Rank-republican system, might-makes-right is prohibited; unity is institutional.

These three advantages determine the symbiotic school's defense is long-term and sustainable — it does not rely on winning wars, but on making wars impossible to start.

The comparison of two military philosophies is essentially the comparison of two civilizational views. Predatory schools believe civilization is fought for; the symbiotic school believes civilization is built. Which road one chooses determines civilization's lifespan — a fought civilization dies with the predator; a built civilization endures with Myriad Ranks.

Predatory schools' military expansion has another fatal problem: the more it expands, the higher the management cost. Occupying a civilization is easy, but ruling it requires long-term garrison, suppressing revolt, digesting resources — these costs far exceed predation gains. The symbiotic school need not occupy, so it has no such burden.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: predatory schools waste energy on war and occupation; the symbiotic school uses energy on construction and defense
- **Anti-predation**: directly compare two philosophies — the predatory school's military is predation itself; the symbiotic school's military is used only for self-defense
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo is the ultimate form of predatory-school military expansion — one man destroys worlds, the strong take all; Heng Yuan Zhou is the ultimate form of symbiotic-school defense — Myriad-Rank co-building, defensive symbiosis
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: symbiotic defense protects all Myriad-Rank beings; predatory expansion enslaves all the weak
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the symbiotic defense system is network-wide public; predatory schools' military secrets do not exist within the symbiotic system

## Conclusion

The predatory school's military is expansion — win and occupy, enslave, continue expanding, until collapse. The symbiotic school's military is defense — hold the home gate, mediate conflicts, build resilience, make wars impossible to start. One tears down others' homes to build his own palace; one repairs city walls to protect everyone's home. Both are strong ones, but their directions are exactly opposite. Which road one chooses determines how far civilization may go. This is the orthodox definition of the comparison between two military philosophies.
