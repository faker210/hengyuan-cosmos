# Entry 894 | Transcended Ones and Diplomacy | Law Basis of Cross-Plane Diplomacy

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 28, 13, 812, 893

## Introduction: diplomacy is not a banquet — it is docking at the law level

Cross-plane diplomacy is not as simple as envoys from two states visiting each other; the universal equal infinite law confirms all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; easygoing layabouts retain a floor and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher infinity — infinite origin is inherent in all, predation naturally loses its meaning. Different planes' law frameworks are completely different; two civilizations wanting to dialogue must first solve "how to dock at the law level." Heng Yuan Zhou's cross-plane diplomacy is built on a rigorous law basis. This entry explains this basis.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: the law-first axiom is this entry's core — diplomatic agreements must be self-consistent at the law level; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires law docking serve equal dialogue; the anti-predation axiom requires law docking not be manipulated by one side; the collective ascension axiom requires law docking promote common evolution; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the law-docking process be traceable.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. Law Differences Between Different Planes

Cross-plane diplomacy's first problem is: different planes' laws are fundamentally different.

- **Different energy laws**: Plane A's spiritual energy may not exist at all on Plane B;
- **Different time laws**: Plane A's one day may be one year on Plane B;
- **Different consciousness laws**: Plane A's individual consciousness may be collective consciousness on Plane B;
- **Different causal laws**: Plane A's causal lines may not hold on Plane B.

These differences mean: **beings from two planes cannot directly understand each other.** Not language mismatch, but law-level mismatch.

## II. The Three-Layer Law-Docking Protocol

Heng Yuan Zhou solves the law-docking problem through a three-layer protocol:

### Layer 1: Law translation layer
The transcended one is responsible for "translating" one plane's law framework into an expression another plane may understand:
- Energy-conversion protocol: how different planes' energy forms are equivalently converted;
- Time-synchronization protocol: how planes with different time flows coordinate schedules;
- Consciousness-mapping protocol: how beings with different consciousness structures understand each other.

### Layer 2: Law compatibility layer
On the basis of translation, design a law framework under which two planes may coexist:
- Cross-plane passage protocol: how laws adapt when one side enters the other's plane;
- Cross-plane trade protocol: law rules for cross-plane energy and matter transmission;
- Cross-plane legal protocol: which set of laws applies to disputes.

### Layer 3: Law anchoring layer
Ultimately, through the anchor network (Document 28), connect two planes' law frameworks:
- Anchor connection point: a stable channel between two planes;
- Law arbitration mechanism: when laws conflict, the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision arbitrate;
- Joint law revision: major law changes require mutual consultation.

## III. The Law-First Axiom's Execution in Diplomacy

The law-first axiom's expression in cross-plane diplomacy is:

- **Diplomatic agreements must be feasible at the law level**: may not sign an agreement that is "law-level impossible";
- **Law self-consistency takes precedence over diplomatic convenience**: may not neglect law conflicts for diplomatic results;
- **Law revision must follow proper procedure**: may not temporarily alter laws because of diplomatic needs;
- **Alaya verifies diplomatic agreements**: before signing, the agreement must pass causal-impact assessment.

This means: diplomacy is not whatever the transcended one wants to sign — what he signs must stand at the law level.

## IV. The Diplomatic Agreement's Life Cycle

A cross-plane diplomatic agreement is not finished once signed; it has a complete life cycle:

1. **Observation period**: first observe the other plane's law characteristics through the Alaya Network;
2. **Design period**: design the law-docking plan;
3. **Negotiation period**: both sides negotiate agreement clauses;
4. **Signing period**: formally sign, Alaya records;
5. **Execution period**: execute per agreement, Alaya supervises;
6. **Revision period**: revise the agreement based on execution;
7. **Exit period**: agreement expires or both sides agree to terminate.

Each stage has law-level checkpoints, ensuring the agreement does not collapse due to law conflicts.

The difficulty of law docking is often underestimated. Beings from two planes may not even share the concept of "time" — Plane A feels time is linear; Plane B feels time is cyclic. The transcended one must find a dialogue basis between such fundamental differences; this is harder than translating any language.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: law docking greatly lowers cross-plane exchange costs
- **Anti-predation**: law agreements ensure cross-plane trade does not become predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo needs no law docking — he alone crushes all planes; Heng Yuan Zhou must design a law framework for every diplomatic partner
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: law-docking protocols are customized for five Myriad-Rank-lineage life forms
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: law-docking agreement full texts and technical documents open network-wide through the Selfless Dao

## Conclusion

Cross-plane diplomacy's law basis is the three-layer protocol of law translation, law compatibility, law anchoring. It ensures civilizations from different planes may dialogue, cooperate, and coexist at the law level. Diplomacy is not a banquet; it is law engineering — every word signed must stand at the law level. The law-first axiom here means: diplomatic results may not come at the cost of law self-consistency. This is the orthodox definition of cross-plane diplomacy's law basis.
