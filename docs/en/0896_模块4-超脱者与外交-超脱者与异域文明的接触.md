# Entry 896 | Transcended Ones and Diplomacy | The Transcended One's Contact with Exotic Civilizations

> Module: Module 4 · Transcendence Orthodox Definition Library | Benchmark base: Documents 53, 54, 893, 897

## Introduction: first contact — diplomacy's most dangerous and most important moment

The first dialogue with an exotic civilization never contacted before is the most delicate moment in all multiverse diplomacy. Done well, two civilizations henceforth symbiose; done poorly, it may trigger war, causal contamination, even civilizational destruction. This entry explains how the transcended one handles contact with exotic civilizations.

Six top-level axioms simultaneously take the stage: the causal self-discipline axiom is this entry's core — first contact most easily disturbs causality; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires exotic civilizations also have equal rights; the anti-predation axiom requires contact not become conquest; the collective ascension axiom requires contact promote common evolution; the law-first axiom requires contact laws be self-consistent; the universal equal infinite law confirms all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; easygoing layabouts retain a floor and are not eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher infinity — infinite origin is inherent in all, predation naturally loses its meaning.

The universal equal infinite law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will never be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes can superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. Infinite origin is inherent in all, so predation naturally loses its meaning.

## I. First Contact's Risks

First contact with an exotic civilization has four main risks:

1. **Causal contamination**: our appearance may completely change the other civilization's historical direction;
2. **Law conflict**: our law-existence mode may be incompatible with the other's;
3. **Misjudgment**: the other side mistakes our goodwill for hostility, or vice versa;
4. **Predatory-school infiltration**: predatory schools may disguise themselves as "friendly civilizations" to approach us.

## II. The Observation Phase Before Contact

Before formal contact, the transcended one must first conduct long-term observation through Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision:

- The other civilization's development stage (pre-industrial? pre-cultivation? already cultivating?);
- The other civilization's social structure (individual? collective? hive?);
- The other civilization's law characteristics (energy? consciousness? matter?);
- The other civilization's external attitude (closed? expanding? curious?).

The observation period may last hundreds of years — do not rush to contact; first figure out what the other side is.

## III. Tiered Response to Contact

Based on observation results, the transcended one adopts different contact strategies:

| Other Civilization State | Contact Strategy |
|--------------------------|------------------|
| Pre-cultivation stage, not ready for contact | Do not contact, continue observing |
| Already cultivating, curious and friendly | Limited contact, gradually establish relations |
| Already cultivating, with predatory tendency | Do not contact, maintain defense |
| Dark Substitution Body / non-civilizational existence | Isolate, eliminate, or transfer |

Tiered response ensures contact does not "rush all in at once" — but chooses the safest strategy based on the other's state.

## IV. The Transcended One's Code of Conduct During Contact

In first contact, the transcended one must observe:

1. **Do not display force**: may not display power in first contact — that is a threat;
2. **Do not change causality**: do not provide the other side technology or knowledge that may change their historical direction;
3. **Do not accept unilateral demands**: do not agree to any of the other side's demands just out of curiosity;
4. **Do not rush to deepen**: first contact only establishes a basic communication channel, signs no agreement;
5. **Alaya records throughout**: every step of contact is on record.

First contact's most easily-made mistake is "over-enthusiasm." The transcended one, out of curiosity, immediately displays all technology, all force, all knowledge — scaring the other side, or making them dependent. The right approach is restraint: only establish a communication channel, do not provide substantive content, wait until the other side is ready.

When contacting exotic civilizations, the most taboo is "projecting oneself onto others." What we feel is friendly behavior, the other side may feel is offense; what we feel is normal technology, the other side may feel is threat. The transcended one must give up the assumption "my way is right," understanding contact from the other side's perspective.

When contacting exotic civilizations, the transcended one also needs to beware of the "projection effect" — projecting one's own intentions onto the other side. We think the other side is expressing friendliness; actually they may only be stating facts; we think the other side is threatening; actually they may just be unaccustomed to direct communication. This kind of misjudgment can only be eliminated through long-term observation.

Contact with exotic civilizations also needs to consider time differential. Some civilizations' response cycle is a hundred times ours — we feel negotiations are settled, the other side is still internally discussing. The transcended one must adapt to this time differential, not push the other side at his own pace.

This patience itself is a kind of power — more powerful than eagerness for quick success.

## V. Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: long-term observation saves enormous trial-and-error cost over blind contact
- **Anti-predation**: contact does not become conquest — do not swallow the other side because they are weak
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang Luo directly crushes exotic civilizations; Heng Yuan Zhou observes first then tiered contact
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: contact strategies are customized for five Myriad-Rank-lineage life forms
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: observation records and contact reports open network-wide through the Selfless Dao

## Conclusion

Contact with exotic civilizations is diplomacy's most delicate moment. First long-term observation, then tiered response; during contact do not display force, do not change causality, do not rush to deepen. First contact is not the moment to make friends, but the moment to establish a communication channel — a true relationship may only be built after multiple contacts. It may not be rushed, nor erred. This is the orthodox definition of the transcended one's contact with exotic civilizations.
