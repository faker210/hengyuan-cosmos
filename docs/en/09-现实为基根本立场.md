## I. Basic Definition of Reality as the Foundation

Reality as the foundation is the fundamental epistemological stance and methodological principle of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, meaning that all theoretical constructions, institutional designs, cultivation practices, and technological innovations of the civilization must take the real world as the only true foundation and the ultimate standard of verification. "Reality" refers to the objective material world and energy world that can be perceived, measured, and verified — the actual arena where life survives and evolves.

The stance of reality as the foundation contains three layers of meaning: first, the real world is the only true world; fictional worlds (including worlds in novels, myths, and fantasies) are products of thought experiments and do not possess objective reality; second, the settings of fictional worlds can serve as sources of inspiration and tools of thought experimentation, but cannot be directly used as the basis of reality — they must pass through the verification and transformation of real laws; third, things that do not yet exist in reality can be reasonably deduced and created based on verified objective laws, but such creation must submit to the test of practice.

## II. Why Reality as the Foundation Must Be Upheld

### 2.1 Preventing the Confusion of Fiction and Reality

In cultivation civilizations, the settings of fictional worlds often possess powerful allure. Many cultivators draw cultivation inspiration from novels, myths, and legends, which is itself normal. But if the settings of fictional worlds are directly equated with real laws, serious problems will arise: cultivating according to fictional settings may lead to deviation; designing institutions according to fictional settings may depart from reality; treating fictional figures as real idols may lead to spiritual loss. The primary function of the stance of reality as the foundation is to draw a clear line between fiction and reality, preventing their confusion.

### 2.2 Ensuring the Safety and Effectiveness of Cultivation Practice

Cultivation is a serious practice involving the Origin of life and the operation of energy; any erroneous theory may lead to serious consequences. The cultivation systems in fictional worlds are often designed by authors for plot needs and may not conform to the real laws of energy operation and life evolution. If cultivators copy fictional settings indiscriminately, at best the cultivation is ineffective and time is wasted; at worst, energy disorders, cultivation deviation, and even threats to life occur. The stance of reality as the foundation requires that all cultivation theories must pass through the verification and validation of reality, ensuring the safety and effectiveness of cultivation practice.

### 2.3 Maintaining the Pragmatism of Civilizational Institutions

The design of civilizational institutions must be based on objective factors such as real social conditions, resource situations, and racial relations. If institutions are designed apart from reality, relying only on beautiful imagination or fictional settings, they will become castles in the air, unable to be implemented. The stance of reality as the foundation requires that all institutional designs start from reality, pass through the test of practice, and be continuously adjusted and improved according to changes in reality.

### 2.4 Providing Reliable Methodology for Innovation and Creation

Reality as the foundation is not about restricting innovation and imagination, but about providing reliable methodology for innovation and imagination. Fiction can serve as a source of inspiration and a tool of thought experimentation, but true creation must be based on a profound understanding of real laws. Things that do not exist in reality can be created as long as they conform to objective laws; but if they violate objective laws, no matter how beautiful the imagination, they cannot become reality. The stance of reality as the foundation combines "bold imagination" with "careful verification," building innovation on a solid foundation.

## III. Principles for Handling the Relationship between Fiction and Reality

| Principle | Meaning | Specific Requirements |
|------|------|----------|
| Principle of Distinction | Strictly distinguish fictional settings from real laws | Any theory must clearly mark whether its source is fictional inspiration or real verification |
| Principle of Reference | Fiction can serve as a source of inspiration and thought experimentation | Encourage drawing inspiration from fiction, but it must be transformed through real laws |
| Principle of Verification | All theories must be verified by real practice | Unverified theories can only serve as hypotheses, not as the basis for guiding practice |
| Principle of Transformation | Fictional settings must be transformed into forms conforming to real laws | Re-express fictional concepts in the language of real energetics and law studies |
| Principle of Boundary | Rights and obligations in fictional worlds do not extend to reality | Do not recognize the validity of identities, contracts, and causality from fictional worlds in reality |

## IV. "Supplement What Fiction Lacks with Reality; Create What Reality Lacks through Law-Based Upgrading"

This is the core operational principle of the stance of reality as the foundation, containing two aspects:

### 4.1 Supplement What Fiction Lacks with Reality

When the inspiration or settings obtained from fictional worlds are incomplete, incoherent, or non-conforming to real laws, they should be supplemented and corrected with knowledge and laws already verified in reality. For example, a cultivation system in fiction may only describe the division of realms without explaining the mechanism of energy operation; in this case, the real theory of energy circulation should be used to supplement its operating mechanism; a technology in fiction may violate the basic law of energy conservation; in this case, real laws should be used to correct its settings.

The process of supplementation is not a simple splicing of fiction and reality, but rather, using real laws as the framework, reorganizing the reasonable elements of fiction into a coherent theoretical system that conforms to real laws.

### 4.2 Create What Reality Lacks through Law-Based Upgrading

When a certain thing or capability does not yet exist in reality, but its possibility of existence can be deduced from verified objective laws, reasonable upgrading and creation can be carried out based on laws. This kind of creation is not fantasy out of thin air, but a logical extension and technological realization based on existing laws.

For example, the technological application of the Multiplying Fruit is a typical case of "creation through law-based upgrading." The Multiplying Fruit originally came from the settings of fictional works, but researchers of the symbiotic civilization re-expressed its core mechanism (multiplication amplification) in the language of real energetics and law studies, and developed engineering-implementable multiplication technology based on the laws of energy amplification and law resonance. This process both utilized fictional inspiration and strictly followed real laws.

The results of creation must undergo the test of practice. Only creations verified as effective, safe, and stable in reality can be incorporated into the formal system of the symbiotic civilization.

## V. Applications of Reality as the Foundation in Various Fields

### 5.1 In the Field of Cultivation

All cultivation systems must pass through a "real verification" process: from theoretical construction to small-scale experiments, then to large-scale promotion; each stage must have measurable and reproducible effect data. Cultivation methods not verified by reality can only serve as "experimental techniques," marked with risk warnings, and cannot be promoted as formal systems.

### 5.2 In the Field of Technology

All law engineering technologies must follow the development process of "theoretical feasibility — experimental verification — engineering realization — safety assessment." The theoretical basis of technology must be verified objective laws; the effects of technology must have measurable indicators; the safety of technology must undergo strict assessment.

### 5.3 In the Field of Social Institutions

All social institutional designs must be based on real social surveys and data analysis. Major institutional reforms must first conduct small-scale pilots, evaluate effects, and then gradually promote. The evaluation standards of institutions must be quantifiable real indicators, not abstract ideas or fictional settings.

### 5.4 In the Field of History and Archaeology

Research on historical events and ancient civilizations must be based on physical evidence and verifiable historical materials. Content in legends and myths can serve as objects of cultural research but cannot be directly treated as historical facts. Archaeological work follows the low-pollution protocol of "Replication Mirror + remote archaeology in Isolation Space, transmitting only knowledge back," ensuring archaeological activities do not change historical reality.

## VI. Deepening Understanding of the Concept of "Reality"

It should be particularly noted that "reality" in the stance of reality as the foundation is not a static, closed concept, but a dynamic concept that is continuously understood and continuously expanded.

First, the scope of reality continuously expands with the development of civilization. Phenomena once considered "supernatural" have been incorporated into the category of reality with deeper understanding of energy and laws. The anchor mechanism, plane traversal, consciousness-domain interaction, etc., may seem "fictional" to low-level civilizations, but objectively exist in the reality of high-level civilizations.

Second, understanding of real laws is continuously deepening. Theories considered "laws" today may be revised or replaced by deeper theories in the future. The stance of reality as the foundation does not mean clinging to existing understanding, but upholding the fundamental method of "practice as the sole criterion for testing truth," continuously deepening understanding of reality.

Finally, reality contains infinite possibilities. Based on existing laws, we can deduce many possibilities not yet realized and turn them into reality through technical means. Reality as the foundation is not about restricting possibilities, but about ensuring that the realization of possibilities is built on a reliable foundation.

## Conclusion

Reality as the foundation is the fundamental stance of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, the starting point and destination of all its theories and practices. It requires us to have both open imagination, drawing inspiration from fiction and legends, and rigorous pragmatism, placing all inspiration under the test of real laws. Supplement what fiction lacks with reality; create what reality lacks through law-based upgrading — this principle organically unifies imagination and reality, inspiration and laws, creation and verification, providing reliable methodological guarantees for the continuous evolution of the symbiotic civilization.
