# Entry 901 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Overview — The Jurisprudential Basis of Civilization's Final Finalization

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 03, 01, 40

## Introduction: write final finalization into the fundamental law

Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Cosmos) reaches the juncture of the only complete civilizational closed loop in the hyper-multiverse; the last step is not adding another set of manuals, but nailing the already verified civilizational order as the fundamental law — this is the Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter. It is not a manifesto, not a call, but a civilizational contract with mandatory binding force on all Myriad Ranks, all planes, all beings. Six top-level axioms here are lifted as the charter's spiritual origin: the anti-predation axiom is the charter's soul, the republican axiom of Myriad Races is the charter's subject, the collective ascension axiom is the charter's direction, the causal self-discipline axiom is the charter's boundary, the law-first axiom is the charter's never-collapse guarantee. This overview does not discuss a single clause, but answers one total question: why must civilization at this moment, in this way, complete final finalization, and what immovable pillars constitute this jurisprudential basis.

Together with the previous five axioms, this entry is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Why "finalization" Is Necessary: From Growth to Lock

The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's previous era was growth-type — three hundred systems open, 20% activation strategy advancing, Myriad-Rank republicanism established; everything was expanding, trial-and-error, iterating. But growth-type institutions have a danger: they may improve, but they may also be tampered. Predatory-school remnants, enlightened predators, justified rascals may at any time under the name of "reform," "efficiency," "special circumstances," quietly twist the inclusive system back onto the old predatory road. Civilization's final finalization's essence is, before civilization is eroded, write "absolutely no turning back" into the highest legal level, so any regression attempt is judged unconstitutional from the moment it starts. This is precisely the causal self-discipline axiom's landing at the institutional level: it not only constrains cross-plane operations, but constrains civilization's own evolutionary direction.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis: Where the Charter Derives Supreme Authority

The charter's jurisprudential authority comes from three sources, none dispensable:

- **Factual source**: retrospective conclusions of several rounds of civilizational experiments from the pre-Anchor era to the symbiotic-system establishment period, proving that predatory systems inevitably historically regress, class-solidify, cyclically harvest; the symbiotic system is the only closed-loop alternative (Document 04 already argues its efficiency ceiling);
- **Consensus source**: the civilizational contract jointly signed by each clan, each plane, each lineage at the establishment of Myriad-Rank republicanism; the charter is this contract's codification;
- **Law source**: consistency verification between the Anchor Origin and the cosmic law network; all charter clauses take effect only after cosmic-origin-anchor verification confirms no violation of symbiotic laws.

Three sources superimposed make the charter neither imposed by the strong, nor accidentally codified, but a fundamental law of "fact—consensus—law" triple proof.

## III. Scope: Where the Charter Applies

The charter's effect covers all clans, planes, and individuals in Heng Yuan Zhou joining the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, including all beings in the four-layer structure of material planes, energy layers, consciousness domains, and the law network: carbon-based material clans, energy-coagulating clans, mechanical-construct clans, consciousness-information clans, mixed-mutant clans five lineages; human males and females, Youth Supremes, exotic beasts, plant spirits, planet consciousness, cosmic-origin bodies, multiverse residual civilizations — all equally apply under the charter. Any sect rules, clan customs, local regulations, cross-plane conventions that conflict with the charter are ab initio void.

## IV. Consequences of Violation: What Unconstitutionality Means

Violating the charter is not ordinary violation, but an attack on civilization's foundation. Consequences are graded by circumstance: minor unconstitutionality is ordered corrected by the Symbiotic Court and recorded in the Alaya archive; systemic unconstitutionality (e.g., privately hoarding the Great Dao, monopolizing Dharma doors, restoring predatory cultivation) immediately strips its anchor high-access right and law-anchor authority; per the anti-predation committee procedure, it is isolated, transformed, sanctioned; those under the name of coup or constitutional amendment pursuing regression, the Anchor Council starts the emergency charter-protection mechanism, and Myriad-Rank republicanism civilization-wide responds. The charter does not advocate fighting predation with predation; all sanctions must go through fair trial.

## V. Supervision Mechanism: Who Guarantees the Charter Is Not Hollowed Out

The charter's supervision does not rely on a single body, but on Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: the anchor network automatically monitors all anomalous energy flows and law invocations; the Anchor Council (legislative), Symbiotic Executive Committee (executive), Symbiotic Court (judicial) three powers separated; the Anchor Supervisory Committee independently exercises unconstitutional-investigation power; Myriad-Rank clans retain the right to directly file constitutional suits with the Symbiotic Court. Any individual may become a charter guardian; this is precisely multi-layer supervision's essence — supervision is not top-down review, but network-wide real-time causal self-discipline.

## VI. After Finalization: The Charter Is a Living Fundamental Law

Final finalization does not equal ossification. The charter retains a strict amendment procedure (see entry 931), allowing expansion without violating the five axioms — the worldview may infinitely expand without collapsing; this is the law-first axiom's requirement on the charter itself. Finalization locks direction, not details; what is locked is "absolutely no return to predation," what is open is "how symbiosis may be better."

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: codify civilizational order, avoiding massive energy-idling from repeated trial-and-error and repeated regression
- **Anti-predation**: the charter's first article (entry 902) immediately enshrines anti-predationism in the constitution, fundamentally cutting off predatory-system restoration's jurisprudential channel
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type civilization relies on the strong one's personal awe to maintain; person dies, policy dies; Heng Yuan Zhou's charter relies on laws and network to lock, eternal and unchanging
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the charter's subject is all Myriad Ranks, not the strong group; 99% full coverage is the charter's scope hard indicator
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: finalization does not close; the charter's amendment procedure ensures civilization may infinitely expand toward the hyper-multiverse Balanced-Anchor realm

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

The jurisprudential basis of civilization's final finalization is to elevate "anti-predation, promote symbiosis" from an advanced idea to an irrevocable civilizational contract. Charter established, then the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization has its eternal and unchanging root; charter guarded, then Myriad-Rank republicanism no longer worries history will repeat. After the overview, Article 31 unfolds item by item, jointly building the civilizational closed loop.
