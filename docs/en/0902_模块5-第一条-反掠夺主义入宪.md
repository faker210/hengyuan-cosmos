# Entry 902 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 1 — Enshrining Anti-Predationism in the Constitution

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 47, 19

## Introduction: write "do not predate" into civilization's first article

The reason the charter's first article is enshrining anti-predationism is that before all institutional design, one must first answer what civilization stands on. Among the six top-level axioms, the anti-predation axiom holds the first place — develop increments, do not consume stock; do not use slaughter and predation as the evolutionary path; the Great Dao is fully open at all levels, no private hoarding, no monopoly. Placing this article first means all other charter articles are its unfolding and guarantee: Myriad-Rank republicanism is anti-predation's unfolding at the subject layer; collective ascension is anti-predation's unfolding at the direction layer; causal self-discipline is anti-predation's unfolding at the boundary layer; the law-first axiom is anti-predation's unfolding at the stability layer. Article 1's enshrinement equals declaring: the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization from birth draws a line with the predatory system.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article's jurisprudential basis directly comes from the Anti-Predationism Core Thesis (Document 04)'s triple-fallacy argument: predationism misjudges the cosmos as a finite hunting ground (cosmology fallacy), places the strong's evolutionary right above the weak's right to life (ethics fallacy), mistakes predation as the most efficient path (efficiency fallacy). The thesis already proves predatory cultivation has four ceilings — impure origin, refining consumes spirit, resistance cost, ability degradation; the higher one goes, the more impassable. Therefore declaring predation illegal is not moral high-talk, but a legislative choice based on objective cosmic laws — in Heng Yuan Zhou predation is "this road does not go"; constitutional enshrinement only writes this objective fact down.

## II. Normative Content: Six Prohibited Predation Forms

Article 1 uses enumeration plus catch-all to constitutionally prohibit all the following forms:

| Form | Constitutional Prohibition |
|------|-----------------------------|
| Energy predation | Directly devouring others' cultivation energy and origin |
| Fortune predation | Secret arts stealing clan or individual fortune |
| Dharma-door monopoly | Using transmission authorization in exchange for loyalty, monopolizing the Great Dao |
| Plane colonization | Conquering low-tier planes to predate resources and population |
| Spiritual enslavement | Soul control, turning beings into cultivation tools |
| Resource draining | Unrestricted extraction, causing plane-membrane collapse |

Any cultivation system taking predation as its core is uniformly declared illegal; there is no excuse space for "gray predation," "limited predation," or "just predation."

## III. Scope

This article applies to all individuals, clans, sects, and civilizational institutions, regardless of realm. Origin Realm, plane-sovereign strong ones, and Sense-Realm new cultivators are equally bound by this article; the higher the realm, the heavier the violation consequence — because the strong one's predation exponentially amplifies damage to the network. This article also applies to cross-plane and cross-time operations: predating in other planes or historical periods is as guilty as predating in the home plane at present.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Violating Article 1 is one of the charter's most serious unconstitutionality types. The consequence ladder is: all predated gains uniformly recovered; origin forcibly purified and residual traces of others' will removed; predatory cultivators stripped of corresponding realm qualification, transferred to ethics-transformation procedure; those causing the predated's death or plane-membrane damage, the Symbiotic Court per anti-predation clauses imposes life-long limits on their anchor access; those styling themselves "enlightened predators" or "justified rascals," using clever means to circumvent enumerated clauses, are pursued under the catch-all clause; no exemption because means are covert.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed throughout by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: the anchor network automatically compares energy sources; any foreign energy carrying the original owner's residual will immediately triggers an alert; the Myriad-Rank-republican council's anti-predation committee under it is dedicated to investigation; the Symbiotic Court sets a predation-cases dedicated tribunal; each clan retains direct-report and constitutional-suit rights. Supervision does not rely on the parties' voluntary surrender, but network real-time causal monitoring — in Heng Yuan Zhou predation is almost impossible to leave no traces; this is precisely the technical basis for anti-predation's constitutional enshrinement truly landing.

## VI. After Constitutional Enshrinement: From Prohibition to Culture

Article 1's constitutional enshrinement is not the end, but turning anti-predation from an external prohibition into Myriad Ranks' inner culture. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, at every new cultivator's initiation rite, has them publicly confirm: every bit of energy they henceforth cultivate comes from creation and circulation, not from devouring others. This ritual looks formal, but actually nails a red line deep in consciousness — even if later reaching the highest realm, facing short-cut inner-demon temptation, this red line will still be awakened. The Alaya Network is responsible for external monitoring; this red line etched into initiation memory is responsible for inner self-discipline; internal and external joint force makes "not using slaughter and predation as the evolutionary path" truly become civilization's collective instinct.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: predated gains require enormous effort to refine and purify; Article 1's constitutional enshrinement eliminates this double waste at the source
- **Anti-predation**: this article is the anti-predation axiom itself, the whole charter's prohibition core
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems default the strong may take; Heng Yuan Zhou's Article 1 directly negates "the strong may take" jurisprudence
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the protected object is not some clan, but all possible predated ones, 99% coverage
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the Great Dao's full-level openness is anti-predation's positive expression, opposing predatory private hoarding

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Article 1's constitutional enshrinement is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's total break with the old cosmic view. It tells Myriad Ranks: in Heng Yuan Zhou, the only legitimate way to evolve is to develop increments and co-build circulation; any attempt to walk the old road, from the moment the pen touches paper, is unconstitutional.
