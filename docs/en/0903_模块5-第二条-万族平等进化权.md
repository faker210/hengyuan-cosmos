# Entry 903 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 2 — Myriad Ranks' Equal Evolutionary Right

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 50, 08

## Introduction: before the evolutionary right, all Myriad Ranks are equal

If Article 1 prohibits "grabbing," Article 2 guarantees "everyone may enter." The republican axiom of Myriad Races among the six top-level axioms is here enshrined: all beings have equal evolutionary qualification, 99% full coverage of Myriad Ranks; human males and females, Youth Supremes, exotic beasts, plant spirits, planet consciousness, cosmic-origin bodies, multiverse residual civilizations all have a road; break the strong's monopoly on knowledge and the Great Dao. This article answers a question the predatory era avoided for eons: is the evolutionary right the strong one's privilege, or all life's natural right? The charter's answer is the latter, and writes it as an inalienable basic right.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Myriad-Ranks Co-Advancement Basic Program (Document 05) and the Every-Road-Available Guarantee System (Document 08): the evolutionary right in cultivation civilizations is not just a development right, but a survival right — those of low realm have extremely low survival probability before plane fluctuations, energy storms, and alien invasion. Equalizing the evolutionary right is essentially bringing every life's basic survival right under charter protection. Initial endowment differences are objective facts, but institutions' responsibility is to make up differences, not amplify them.

## II. Normative Content: Four-Layer Meaning of Equality

Article 2's "equality" is not egalitarianism, but four-layer progressive equality:

1. **Qualification equality**: all beings before the evolutionary right are equal, no discrimination by clan, form, origin, initial realm;
2. **Admission equality**: the 300 origin cultivation systems' basic Dharma doors are free and open to all, no sect threshold, no loyalty condition;
3. **Opportunity equality**: the anchor network provides system-compatibility assessment, providing targeted support for low-endowment individuals, making up the difference rather than laissez-faire;
4. **Relief equality**: when the evolutionary right is infringed, all beings enjoy equal rights to appeal to the Symbiotic Court.

## III. Scope

This article covers all life in the five lineages, especially tilting toward historically suppressed groups: low-endowment individuals old systems deemed "trash," impoverished clans overall lacking resources, post-disaster groups who lost homes to disasters, disabled cultivators, strange life not fitting conventional systems. This article does not deprive a clan of its road because it is "temporarily behind"; on the contrary, the further behind, the more targeted support.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Any refusing to open Dharma doors on grounds of clan purity, noble/mean birth, or talent level, depriving cultivation qualification, or setting evolutionary thresholds constitutes unconstitutionality. Consequences: discriminatory clauses are ab initio void; the directly responsible person's anchor-access permission is downgraded; the institution concerned is ordered to rectify within a time limit and publicly correct; those inciting clan segregation under the theory of "species superiority," attempting to rebuild evolutionary monopoly, are pursued under anti-predation clauses (Article 1) catch-all, because Dharma-door monopoly is precisely one of predation's important forms.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article's supervision relies on the Every-Road-Available Guarantee System's four-layer structure to land: the basic-guarantee layer bottoms out, the targeted-support layer makes up differences, the opportunity-expansion layer nurtures excellence, the achievement-incentive layer rewards excellence; all four layers are tracked for coverage by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision. The Anchor Supervisory Committee periodically publishes each clan's and each group's evolutionary-right realization rate; 99% coverage is the hard indicator; any group's coverage long below threshold triggers special correction.

## VI. A Plant Spirit's Initiation Right

To make Article 2 concrete, imagine an example: a plant spirit just awakening consciousness, with no human form, no meridians; under the old system it would not even count as a cultivator. Under Article 2, it has the right to pass the anchor network's system-compatibility assessment, be matched to the life-cycle-type system, starting from the Sense Realm. No one asks which forest it came from, whether it has a human master, or whether it is worth cultivating. This example shows the equal evolutionary right is not an abstract slogan, but every life once judged by old systems as unable to cultivate, may for the first time hear in Heng Yuan Zhou "you too may." It is precisely these once-impossibles that constitute Myriad-Rank republicanism's most solid base color.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: individuals deemed trash may precisely be geniuses; this article re-includes these buried evolutionary potentials into civilization's general ledger
- **Anti-predation**: Dharma-door monopoly is one of six predation forms; this article dismantles it at the admission end
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems commoners enter by grace, ceiling locked; Heng Yuan Zhou admission sets no threshold, the road is open to all
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: this article is "99% full coverage"'s direct constitutional expression
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the equal evolutionary right synchronously covers newly joined multiverse life as civilization expands

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Myriad Ranks' equal evolutionary right is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's identity marker distinguishing it from all predatory civilizations. When a plant spirit, a planet consciousness, the humblest carbon-based individual may freely step onto the Sense Realm's first step, Myriad-Rank republicanism truly goes from slogan to fact.

In the final analysis, Article 2 · Myriad Ranks' equal evolutionary right described in this entry is no isolated case — law self-consistency is caught by the law-first axiom, and does not drift with narrative convenience.
