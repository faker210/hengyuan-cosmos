# Entry 904 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 3 — The Collective-Ascension Obligation

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 17, 25

## Introduction: the strong do not ascend alone

The collective-ascension axiom among the six top-level axioms is enshrined in Article 3: the strong do not ascend alone, but lift Myriad Ranks forward together; Myriad-Rank republican co-construction, Alaya network-wide sharing; put an end to using great material for trivial purposes. The predatory era's ultimate dream is "one person achieves the Dao" — the strong one breaks through the plane, becomes an immortal alone, leaving the whole plane's beings in place. This article judges this dream as civilization's shame. It stipulates: in Heng Yuan Zhou, the greater the force, the greater the obligation to lift Myriad Ranks up together; ascending alone is not glory, but unconstitutionality.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Myriad-Ranks Co-Advancement Basic Program (Document 05) and the Symbiotic Feedback Cycle Mechanism (Document 25). The anchor network's effectiveness is positively correlated with the number and quality of connected lives: a few strong ones ascending alone actually drains the network's core nodes, breaking the whole plane's cycle. From energetics, ascending alone turns increments into stock, turns co-construction into monopoly; it is homologous and contrary to the anti-predation axiom. Collective ascension is not moral kidnapping of the strong, but the cosmic energy-cycle law's objective requirement on the strong.

## II. Normative Content: Three Forms of the Obligation

Article 3 concretizes the collective-ascension obligation into three executable duties:

1. **Transmission duty**: the higher the realm, the more they must publicly return cultivation insights and Dharma-door innovations to the Alaya Network for network-wide reuse; no private hoarding;
2. **Driving duty**: plane-sovereign strong ones must annually invest a certain proportion of time and energy in public duties (mentorship, anchor maintenance, plane inspection);
3. **Lifting duty**: when breaking through to high tiers, they must feed back their own origin force to the plane cycle, driving co-plane beings' realms to rise with the tide, rather than withdrawing alone.

## III. Scope

This article applies to Dao Integration Realm and above, especially Boundary-Breaking, World-Creation, and Origin Realm strong ones, and all individuals with cross-plane influence. The higher the realm, the greater the obligation proportion. Sense-Realm new cultivators have no such duty, but enjoy the right to be driven — rights and duties are gradient-allocated by realm. This article also binds all special strong ones such as Lin Hao's route and the D route: the more unique the strong one, the less exemption for ascending alone.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Achieving breakthrough without returning, having ability without driving, ascending and withdrawing from the plane, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: their personal breakthrough results are not certified, the Alaya Network does not register them; those forcibly ascending alone and draining the plane's origin are treated as predation against the plane's beings, handled under Article 1 anti-predation clauses; those with egregious circumstances are stripped of the law-anchor authority corresponding to their achieved realm, ordered to first complete the driving duty before advancing further.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is automatically measured by the contribution dimension within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: whether personal breakthrough is returned, whether the plane is fed back, whether public duty is borne, all recorded by the network. Plane-sovereign strong ones' four-dimensional assessment (see entry 911)'s "contribution 20%, responsibility 15%" two dimensions are precisely this article's formalization. The Anchor Supervisory Committee monitors the energy flow after a strong one's breakthrough — whether breakthrough energy backflows to the plane cycle, or is drained to a private domain, clear at a glance.

## VI. The Tens of Millions Behind One Breakthrough

Article 3's weight can only be seen placed inside a real breakthrough. When a Dao Integration Realm strong one prepares to step into Boundary-Breaking Realm, under the old system he would seclude himself swallowing, quietly leave, leaving plane beings in place; while in Heng Yuan Zhou, he must first return the insights from his breakthrough to the Alaya Network, then feed his origin force back to his plane, letting the tens of millions who cultivated with him rise with this backflow. His breakthrough is no longer one person's highlight, but the springboard for tens of millions advancing together. The collective-ascension obligation is precisely turning this picture of one person breaking through and Myriad Ranks benefiting, from moral advocacy into an unshirkable legal responsibility.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: put an end to "using great material for trivial purposes"; let every bit of the strong one's force feed back to the whole network, rather than being consumed alone in a private domain
- **Anti-predation**: ascending alone is essentially draining the plane's stock; this article characterizes it as homologous with predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type protagonists ascend and plane beings stay in place; Heng Yuan Zhou strong ones' breakthrough must lift Myriad Ranks along
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: those lifted are not the strong one's own clan, but all beings on the same plane
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: ascension results are shared network-wide, belonging to the high-tier link of the fully-open chain

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

The collective-ascension obligation is Heng Yuan Zhou's rewrite of the words "the strong." Here, the strongest is not the one who walks farthest, but the one who brings the most people to the distance. The strong do not ascend alone; Myriad Ranks share this ascent.

In the final analysis, Article 3 · Collective-Ascension Obligation described in this entry is no isolated case — law self-consistency is caught by the law-first axiom, and does not drift with narrative convenience.
