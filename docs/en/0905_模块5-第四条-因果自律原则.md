# Entry 905 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 4 — The Causal Self-Discipline Principle

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 83, 87, 89

## Introduction: being able to act is not the same as being allowed to act

The causal self-discipline axiom among the six top-level axioms is enshrined in Article 4: cross-plane operations strictly reported, observe first then limited intervention, only return knowledge without disturbing causality; thoroughly eradicate civilizational regression, historical reversal, cyclical harvesting, and class solidification. Heng Yuan Zhou's strong ones may cross planes, trace time, but "can" has never meant "may." This article stipulates freedom's boundary: the greater the ability, the deeper the awe for the causal chain, the humbler the operation. It is the charter's most "restrained" article, yet the one that best embodies civilization's maturity.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Observe-First-Limited-Intervention Guideline (Document 83), the Knowledge-Return-Only Principle (Document 87), and the Time-Paradox Prevention Mechanism (Document 89). Its philosophical foundation is cognitive humility: even plane-sovereign strong ones cannot fully master all causal associations of one time node or one plane civilization. Any direct intervention may trigger unpredictable chain reactions. Writing causal self-discipline into the charter is precisely solidifying this awe for cosmic complexity into an insurmountable operational red line.

## II. Normative Content: Four Iron Laws of Causal Self-Discipline

Article 4 unfolds causal self-discipline into executable operational iron laws:

1. **Reporting iron law**: all cross-plane, time-tracing operations are reported in advance to the Time-Law Committee / Plane Administration Bureau; no approval, no operation;
2. **Observation iron law**: observation duration is no less than 1% of the target period, no less than 72 hours of the home plane, covering the five dimensions of matter, energy, life, consciousness, causality;
3. **Limitation iron law**: intervention scope does not exceed 1% of the permitted area, degree is bound by intervention thresholds, time does not exceed 10% of observation duration;
4. **Return iron law**: only return knowledge, do not disturb the target plane's causal direction, do not bring in material energy, do not alter its historical nodes.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all individuals and teams with cross-plane, time-tracing ability, regardless of whether their identity is archaeological team, diplomat, rescuer, or cultivator. Plane-sovereign strong ones and Multiverse Realm strong ones without exception — the stronger the ability, the larger the causal-influence radius, the stricter the review. Those unreported privately crossing or privately intervening, regardless of whether the motive is good or evil, are uniformly unconstitutional.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Unreported operations, intervening with insufficient observation, breaking through intervention thresholds, attempting to rewrite other planes' history through intervention, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: immediately suspend operations and forcibly withdraw; those already causing causal deviation trigger causal-correction procedures, and the operator bears the correction cost; those triggering time paradoxes, historical forks, or cyclical-harvest-type disturbances are pursued heavily under Article 1 anti-predation and Article 8 historical-reversal prevention; those with serious circumstances permanently lose cross-plane operation qualification.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by the time-operation archive library within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: all cross-plane operations leave traces throughout; observation duration, intervention thresholds, causal-deviation amplitude are automatically compared; the anchor network real-time monitors anomalous causal-chain fluctuations; any over-threshold disturbance freezes subsequent operations; the Time-Law Committee periodically audits all time-operation archives, putting an end to "secret crossing."

## VI. An Impulse Held Back

Article 4's most vivid footnote is an impulse held back. A young strong one observing another plane witnesses an innocent civilization about to be destroyed by internal strife, and instinctively wants to immediately act to rewrite the ending. Per the causal self-discipline principle, he is calmly reminded by the network: first complete five-dimensional observation, submit assessment, obtain permission, then make limited intervention within a thousandth of the range. In the end he only returned one piece of knowledge sufficient for that civilization to avoid the disaster on its own, rather than forcibly deciding for it. This hold-back did not hold back kindness, but held back the causal explosion of good intentions going bad. Causal self-discipline is precisely the awe that must be retained even for the kindest impulse.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: causal disturbance triggers massive uncontrollable chain corrections; this article locks this hidden waste in advance
- **Anti-predation**: cyclical harvesting and historical manipulation are essentially predation on other planes' causality; this article positively prohibits them
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type protagonists freely traverse and rewrite history with no consequences; Heng Yuan Zhou every operation is bound by reporting, threshold, and return triple constraints
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: what is protected is not only the home plane, but all beings in all other planes and all historical periods
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: self-discipline does not equal closure; the reporting system ensures exploration infinitely expands without collapse

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

The causal self-discipline principle is Heng Yuan Zhou's final answer to the "force and boundary" relationship. Being able to act is civilization's glory; knowing restraint before causality is the mark of civilization's maturity. This principle held, cross-plane exploration may be both free and safe.

In the final analysis, Article 4 · Causal Self-Discipline Principle described in this entry is no isolated case — law self-consistency is caught by the law-first axiom, and does not drift with narrative convenience.
