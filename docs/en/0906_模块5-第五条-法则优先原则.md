# Entry 906 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 5 — The Law-First Principle

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 97, 02, 99

## Introduction: laws first, never retcon

The law-first axiom among the six top-level axioms is enshrined in Article 5: laws first, never retcon, never fizzle; setting self-consistency takes precedence over plot convenience; plot may not kidnap setting; the worldview may infinitely expand without collapsing. This article looks like it "governs stories," but actually governs civilization itself — Heng Yuan Zhou's entire cultivation system, governance system, and cross-plane system are a strictly defined set of "laws." Once laws may be temporarily altered for convenience, the whole civilization's foundation will collapse. This article elevates "self-consistency" from a writing requirement to a civilizational-level legal principle.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Anchor Origin Definition and Essence (Document 02) and the Setting-Contradiction Self-Check Table (Document 97). The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's entire institutions are built on a deducible, verifiable, reproducible law network. If allowing temporary law changes for expediency equals admitting the law network may be arbitrarily tampered by power — this is precisely the predatory-era strong ones' legacy of "words become laws, change on command." Law-first is precisely placing law stability above all expediency and convenience.

## II. Normative Content: Four Requirements of Law-First

Article 5 unfolds law-first into four hard requirements:

1. **Definition stability**: the nine-realm division, 300 systems, four-dimensional assessment and other core definitions, once established, may not be temporarily altered except through legal procedure;
2. **Self-consistency priority**: any new policy, new system, new event may not conflict with existing laws; when conflict arises, existing laws prevail, rather than breaking precedent for new events;
3. **Plot yields**: the "stories" in civilizational evolution (personal legends, sudden events) may not kidnap laws; special cases are not written into general rules;
4. **Expansion without collapse**: the worldview may infinitely expand with civilization toward Multiverse Realm and Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, but every level of expansion must pass self-consistency verification.

## III. Scope

This article binds all legislation, administration, judiciary, and cultivation practice: the Anchor Council's legislation, the Symbiotic Executive Committee's policies, the Symbiotic Court's precedents, and plane-sovereign strong ones' law fine-tuning must all proceed within existing law frameworks. No institution, no strong one has the privilege of "temporarily changing laws." Ordinary cultivators equally apply — their cultivation behavior may not violate law definitions, nor claim on grounds of personal insight that "laws change for me."

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Temporarily changing laws on grounds of expediency, breaking precedent to protect a favored strong one, using special cases to force general rules, and forcibly making laws accommodate plot when plot conflicts with law, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: the relevant change is ab initio void, reapplied per existing laws; the responsible person is recorded in the Alaya archive's "law-stability" credit item; those causing plane-law disorder through unauthorized law changes bear repair responsibility and, by circumstance, are merged into anti-predation pursuit.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is automatically executed by the law network within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: before any law invocation, fine-tuning, or new policy goes live, it first passes anchor-origin self-consistency verification; conflict items are directly blocked; the Setting-Contradiction Self-Check Table (Document 97) serves as a normalized self-check tool; the Anchor Supervisory Committee sets up a dedicated law-stability review post, periodically auditing all law-change records, ensuring "retcon and fizzle" is institutionally impossible.

## VI. A Precedent-Set Request Refused

The law-first axiom in reality often manifests as a precedent-set request refused. During some major crisis, someone proposed temporarily modifying the realm division, letting a batch of sub-standard individuals go into battle early as emergency. The Anchor Court rejected it on grounds of law-first: emergencies may mobilize all resources within existing systems, but may not temporarily change laws for this. Afterwards it was proven that, relying on total mobilization within the existing law framework, the crisis was likewise resolved, while law definitions' stability was not torn open. This refusal tells Myriad Ranks: the more perilous the moment, the more plot convenience may not kidnap setting — once laws break precedent for one crisis, countless crises will come asking for a second precedent.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: laws changing overnight makes network-wide cultivators lose their bearings; this article zeroes out this systemic waste
- **Anti-predation**: "words become laws" is the predatory legacy of the strong monopolizing laws; this article judges it unconstitutional
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems随时改上限、改规则 as the protagonist needs (retcon); Heng Yuan Zhou laws are stable, not changing for anyone's convenience
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: law stability lets even the lowest-tier new cultivators trust that the road is predictable, not abandoned by temporary rerouting
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: precisely because laws are stable, civilization dares to infinitely expand toward the hyper-multiverse without collapsing

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

The law-first principle is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's confidence in reaching the hyper-multiverse's only complete closed loop. As long as laws are not kidnapped by any individual or any plot, this civilization may remain self-consistent through infinite expansion — this is the legal guarantee of "never fizzling."
