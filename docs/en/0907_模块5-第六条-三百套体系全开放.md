# Entry 907 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 6 — Full Opening of the 300 Systems

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 10, 18, 69

## Introduction: the Great Dao open at all levels, no private hoarding, no monopoly

The anti-predation axiom among the six top-level axioms lands in Article 6 on the most concrete institution: the Great Dao open at all levels, no private hoarding, no monopoly. The 300 origin cultivation systems are the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's core legacy for Myriad Ranks, six categories each 50 systems, from deduction-iteration, life-cycle to spatial-law dispatch, covering all possible evolutionary directions. This article stipulates: these 300 systems' all levels — from basic Dharma doors to above-Sacrifice-Dao, multiverse-grade, all the way to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm — are fully open to Myriad Ranks; no person, no sect may privately hoard any level within them.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the 300 Origin Cultivation Systems Overview (Document 10), the Cultivation-Resource Opening Mechanism (Document 18), and the Technology Opening and Sharing Mechanism (Document 69). Dharma-door monopoly is one of six predation forms: a few monopolize the Great Dao, using transmission authorization in exchange for loyalty, inevitably causing class solidification. Constitutionalizing full opening is precisely eradicating from the root the old order of "transmit within, not outside; transmit to the legitimate heir, not the bastard." Systems are the public fruits of cosmic laws, not any individual's private property.

## II. Normative Content: Three-Layer Meaning of Full Opening

Article 6 refines "full opening" into three-layer commitments:

1. **Basic layer free**: the 300 systems' basic Dharma doors are free and open to all life, no fee, no loyalty, no qualification review;
2. **All-level progression**: not only the basic layer, but advanced, high-tier, and hyper-multiverse levels open synchronously with civilization's progress; no "hidden ultimate version";
3. **Compatibility inclusion**: the anchor network provides system-compatibility assessment, helping every being pick the combination best suited to themselves from the 300, rather than one-size-fits-all.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all individuals and institutions: no cultivator may seal systems on grounds of "lineage," no sect may retain unique secrets on grounds of "ancestral instructions," no institution may delay high-tier opening on grounds of "security." The D route · human-made Dao-rules soul rings, Lin Hao route and other special systems, as supplements outside the 300, equally apply the full-opening principle (see entries 921, 922).

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Privately hoarding systems' core layers, using transmission authorization in exchange for loyalty, charging for basic Dharma doors, deliberately delaying high-tier opening, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: hoarded parts are forcibly returned to the Alaya Network and opened network-wide; relevant rights are uniformly void; those profiting from monopoly have gains recovered under Article 1 anti-predation clauses; those causing Myriad Ranks to stagnate from not obtaining systems, the institution head bears responsibility. This article has no "well-meaning hoarding" exception.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by the system-opening ledger within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: the 300 systems' every layer, every level's opening status is transparent network-wide; if any layer is "taken down" or "classified," the network automatically alerts; the Anchor Supervisory Committee periodically audits the opening rate; full-level opening is the hard indicator; each clan retains the right to directly apply to retrieve any level's Dharma door; a rejected application immediately triggers a constitutional suit.

## VI. A Public Ultimate Version

Article 6's most persuasive picture is an ultimate version finally made public. In the old age, top-tier manuals' ultimate layer was always privately hoarded by sects, claiming not transmitted to non-core disciples; in Heng Yuan Zhou, every layer of the 300 systems, including the parts leading to multiverse-grade and the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, hangs on the Alaya Network synchronously with civilization's progress. A Sense-Realm new cultivator just initiated can, like reading a textbook, see the scenery at this road's highest point. This transparency is itself the strongest motivation — he knows the road is real, open, not a scam, so he is willing to give his life to it. Full opening turns hope itself into a public good.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: a system used by only one person is waste; network-wide reuse amplifies value to the extreme
- **Anti-predation**: this article is the Selfless Dao's direct codification, positively dismantling Dharma-door monopoly
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems top-tier manuals are only transmitted to the destined protagonist; Heng Yuan Zhou's 300 systems all levels are free to Myriad Ranks
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the 300 systems' six categories are precisely designed to cover five lineages and all endowments
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: this article itself is the hyper-multiverse full-open chain's direct expression in cultivation systems

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Full opening of the 300 systems is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's most generous and most clever institutional design. Return the Great Dao to Myriad Ranks, and Myriad Ranks will use collective evolution to push civilization toward the hyper-multiverse — this is precisely the reverse verification of "the selfless achieves its own."

In the final analysis, Article 6 · Full Opening of the 300 Systems described in this entry is no isolated case — law self-consistency is caught by the law-first axiom, and does not drift with narrative convenience.
