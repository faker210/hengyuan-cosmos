# Entry 908 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 7 — Alaya-Network Multi-Layer Supervision

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 21, 49, 20

## Introduction: entrust supervision to the whole network, not to some upright official

For the charter to land, it relies not on one institution per clause, but on a supervision network running through all Myriad Ranks — Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision. Article 5 has already legislated law-first, Article 4 has already legislated causal self-discipline, but if no one watches laws and causality, they may still be circumvented. Article 7 stipulates: the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's every exercise of power, every energy flow, every law invocation is under the Alaya Network's full-process trace and multi-layer supervision. Supervision is not a few inspectors' business, but a business every network node may see and may report.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Symbiotic Network Operating Principles (Document 21), the Governance Framework and Checks and Balances (Document 49), and the Anchor Structure and Levels (Document 20). The Anchor Council legislates, the Symbiotic Executive Committee administers, the Symbiotic Court adjudicates; separation of three powers is itself one layer of supervision; and the Alaya Network overlays the three powers with a layer of "network-wide visible, full-process trace" technical supervision. Jurisprudentially, power is supervisable because every exercise of it leaves a trace on causality and energy that the network may capture.

## II. Normative Content: Five-Layer Structure of Multi-Layer Supervision

Article 7 refines multi-layer supervision into five mutually independent yet mutually interlocking layers:

1. **Network layer**: the Alaya Network automatically monitors energy flows, law invocations, cross-plane operations; anomaly immediately alerts;
2. **Legislative layer**: the Anchor Council periodically reviews whether executive and judicial branches are constitutional;
3. **Executive layer**: the Symbiotic Executive Committee's internal hierarchical auditing;
4. **Judicial layer**: the Symbiotic Court accepts constitutional suits, independently adjudicates unconstitutional acts;
5. **Civil-rights layer**: any Myriad-Rank individual or clan may directly report, directly sue, no level-by-level approval needed.

The five layers are not subordinate to one another and serve as mutual backups; if any layer fails, the other layers may still fill in.

## III. Scope

This article's supervision covers all public power and all individuals with cross-plane influence: the three-power institutions, plane-sovereign strong ones, cross-plane operation teams, special-route strong ones, all under supervision. Especially high-realm strong ones — the greater their force, the higher the monitoring level, not the higher the "exemption from inspection." Ordinary individuals' private-cultivation space is protected, but once public-energy invocation and law use are involved, it enters supervision's field of view.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Using office to block traces, bribing supervision nodes, tampering with Alaya archives, retaliating against reporters, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: relevant records prevail by the network's original traces; tampering is void; blocking and retaliation are pursued heavily; the responsible person's supervision-exemption qualification is permanently cancelled; if one supervision layer is wholly hollowed out, the Anchor Council starts emergency charter protection, with the remaining layers and the civil-rights layer temporarily taking over that layer's functions.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article's "self-supervising" closed-loop design is its essence: the Alaya Network itself is also supervised — the network's algorithms and nodes are periodically audited by a technical supervision committee composed of clan representatives, preventing the unsolvable loop of "who supervises the supervisor." The civil-rights layer's reporting channel is open to all beings, anonymous and real-name both allowed, report handling results publicized network-wide. Multi-layer supervision's ultimate meaning is: no layer, no node stands above the network itself.

## VI. A Five-Layer Coordinated Correction

Article 7's power can only be seen in a real correction. Some administrative institution, when allocating resources, quietly lowered a weak clan's weight. The first to detect was not some inspector, but the network layer automatically discovering that clan's receipt curve was anomalous; the civil-rights layer immediately had that clan's individuals directly report; the judicial layer accepted the constitutional suit; the legislative layer ordered review; the executive layer was forced to rectify and open the accounts. Among the five layers, no single layer alone turned the tide, but they filled in for each other, no one could be bribed. This is precisely multi-layer supervision's essence — it does not expect an upright official to appear; what it designs is a structure where any layer failing does not cause overall collapse.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: full-process trace avoids repeated investigation and repeated auditing; one record is usable network-wide
- **Anti-predation**: anomalous energy flows automatically alert; predation is almost impossible to hide
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems rely on the protagonist's consciousness or sect-master awe; Heng Yuan Zhou relies on network-wide multi-layer supervision, depending on no upright official
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: reporting rights belong to everyone; supervision is not a few inspectors' monopoly
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly joined multiverse life automatically connects to the network layer; supervision extends as civilization expands

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Alaya-Network multi-layer supervision is the charter's last mile from paper to reality. No matter how well laws are written, if no one watches they will be circumvented; with this five-layer interlocking web, the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization may truly achieve "the net of heaven has wide meshes, yet lets nothing through" — and this web is not for oppression, but for guarding Myriad-Rank republicanism.
