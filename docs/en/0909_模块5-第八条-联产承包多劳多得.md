# Entry 909 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 8 — Contract-Responsibility and More-Work-More-Pay

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 06, 22, 26

## Introduction: distributive justice is civilization's lasting base

Myriad-Rank republicanism cannot only talk sentiment, it must also talk distribution. Article 8 stipulates: the distribution of evolutionary resources is mainly based on the individual's contribution to the symbiotic network; the greater the contribution, the more the gain; at the same time a basic guarantee line is set, ensuring those with little contribution also have a road. This is contract-responsibility and more-work-more-pay. It and Article 2's every-road-available are twin clauses — every-road-available solves fairness bottom-guarantee, more-work-more-pay solves efficiency motivation; without it, every-road-available degenerates into egalitarianism; without the latter, more-work-more-pay degenerates into might makes right.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the More-Work-More-Pay Distribution Principle (Document 06), the Resource-Distribution Contract-Responsibility Mechanism (Document 22), and the Individual-Collective Balance Principle (Document 26). The anti-predation axiom opposes predation without labor, not the recognition of differences; on the contrary, it insists differences must come from one's own effort and contribution, not from violence or birth. More-work-more-pay is legalizing the cosmic energy-cycle law that "contribution deserves return" — those who invest in the cycle deserve more backflow from the cycle.

## II. Normative Content: How Contract-Responsibility Runs

Article 8 concretizes the distribution system:

1. **Contract-responsibility**: the individual "signs and contracts" a segment of cultivation-contribution tasks with the network (e.g., maintaining a segment of anchor, mentoring a batch of new cultivators, deducing a manual set);
2. **Metered pay**: contribution is automatically measured and weighted by the Alaya Network across six categories — cultivation, knowledge, technology, service, ecology, guardianship;
3. **More pay**: the higher the contribution, the higher the treatment — cultivation resources, anchor high-access, space-creation rights;
4. **Bottom line**: even with zero contribution, Article 2's basic guarantee line does not move; no "starve if you do not work."

## III. Scope

This article applies to all individuals and institutions entering the cultivation cycle. Contribution measurement treats Myriad Ranks equally: human deduction, exotic-beast guardianship, plant-spirit ecological restoration, planet-consciousness plane stability, all converted at unified weights. This article also applies to progressive adjustment for high contributors — levying a certain proportion of contribution-adjustment tax on high contributors, feeding back to the every-road-available guarantee system, preventing more-work-more-pay from alienating into wealth concentration.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Forging contribution, falsifying results to defraud resources, subcontracting contracted tasks for profit, discriminatorily suppressing some clan's contribution weight, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: falsified gains recovered, contribution records zeroed; those using office to privately distribute resources have their distribution right revoked and, by circumstance, merged into anti-predation pursuit; no institution may set "weight by clan"; weight looks only at contribution, not birth.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by the contribution-measurement system within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: contribution data is automatically collected and network-wide queryable; no manual black boxes allowed; the Anchor Supervisory Committee periodically audits distribution fairness, publishes each clan's and group's contribution-gain curve, and discovers systematic suppression of some clan's contribution triggers correction. The adjustment-tax and guarantee-line income and expenditure are publicly accounted by the Myriad-Rank-republican council's guarantee committee.

## VI. Fairness on a Reconciliation Statement

Article 8 landing in daily life is a reconciliation statement everyone may check. Every cultivator opens the anchor interface and may see what tasks he has contracted this period, what category of contribution he produced, how many resources were converted, and what he gained back per more-work-more-pay. Exotic-beast plane guardianship, plant-spirit ecological restoration, human manual deduction are converted into the same ruler. This transparency makes more-work-more-pay no longer pretty words from the strong, but an account every being may check and be convinced by. When all beings believe gains match effort, they will willingly feed more energy back into the cycle; civilization's distributive justice is thus day after day consolidated in every reconciliation.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: distribute by contribution, directing resources to those who create more value; putting an end to egalitarian idling
- **Anti-predation**: more-work-more-pay opposes getting something for nothing; it and Article 1's predation prohibition are two sides of the same coin
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems resources rely on the destined protagonist's aura; Heng Yuan Zhou relies on contribution measurement; everyone may earn more through real work
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the six-category contribution measurement leaves value-creation entrances for all five lineages
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly joined multiverse life automatically connects to contract-responsibility; contribution is measurable, more-pay is available

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Contract-responsibility and more-work-more-pay give Myriad-Rank republicanism both a fair bottom line and a running motivation. Once distributive justice stands firm in the charter, civilization will not weary from "doing more or less the same," nor suffocate from "the strong keep getting stronger."
