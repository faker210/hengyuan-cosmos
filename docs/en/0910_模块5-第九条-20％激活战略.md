# Entry 910 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 9 — The 20% Activation Strategy

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 23, 17, 75

## Introduction: why activate the top 20% of strong ones first

Civilizational ascension needs a power foundation, and that foundation cannot rest on one or two saviors. Article 9 writes the 20% activation strategy into the charter: within the first era after civilization's founding, activate 20% of all beings to the plane-sovereign strong-one level. This is not elitism; on the contrary — it is precisely to prevent strong-one monopoly. If the whole civilization has only one savior, he is both savior and the sole dictatorship hidden danger; only when 20% of all beings become qualified strong ones does civilization's power base become wide enough, so it does not collapse because of any one person's fall.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the 20% Activation Strategy Planning (Document 23), the Plane-Sovereign Strong-One Standards (Document 17), and the 20% Activation Strategy Implementation (Document 75). Its internal logic is the collective-ascension axiom: the cosmos's overall ascension cannot rely only on a few sovereign strong ones, but requires universal evolution of all Myriad-Rank beings; the Alaya Network's effectiveness is positively correlated with the number and quality of connected life. Activating 20% of strong ones is precisely raising the network's core-node density above the safety threshold.

## II. Normative Content: Three-Step Activation Pace

Article 9 quantifies the activation strategy into three steps:

1. Short-term (within 100 years of civilization's founding): activate 1% of all beings to Origin Realm, establishing the first batch of guardian skeletons;
2. Mid-term (within 500 years): activate 10%, forming a strong-one network, preventing individual strong-one power monopoly;
3. Long-term (within 1000 years): activate 20%, providing a sufficient power foundation for the cosmos's overall ascension.

The strategy relies on five pillars: the 300 systems' opening, Multiplication-Fruit technology popularization, contract-responsibility mechanism, every-road-available guarantee, and mentor network.

## III. Scope

This article's "20%" covers all Myriad Ranks, especially emphasizing each clan must have its own sovereign strong-one representative, rather than human race monopolizing strong-one quotas. Low-endowment individuals and impoverished clans are equally within activation targets — activation is not picking geniuses and eliminating the weak, but using systems to hold up as many as possible. This article sets no clan-quota ceiling, only a floor of "no clan may be absent."

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Deliberately suppressing activation rate, concentrating strong-one quotas on some clan or surname, obstructing others' breakthrough to preserve monopoly, depriving the weak of activation opportunities on grounds of "insufficient talent," all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: the relevant institution is ordered to rectify within a time limit, and activation rate is included in its accountability indicators; those artificially creating "the one destined" and creating protagonist-uniqueness dependence are pursued heavily under class solidification (entry 930) and anti-predation.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by the activation-rate ledger within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: each clan's and group's proportion reaching Origin Realm is real-time statistically tracked, and the gap to short/mid/long-term targets is publicized network-wide; the Anchor Supervisory Committee monitors strong-one quotas' clan distribution, and if some clan's strong ones are long absent, targeted support is given. Eras with substandard activation rates are held accountable by the Myriad-Rank-republican council against the relevant executing institution.

## VI. Seeing the Strategy Through a Forest

The 20% activation strategy's meaning is best analogized with a forest. If the whole forest has only one tallest big tree, one storm may destroy all; if 20% of trees all grow to a considerable height, the forest has a self-repair skeleton. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization spending a thousand years pushing 20% of beings to plane-sovereign levels is not to create a 20% privileged class, but to prepare for cosmic ascension a strong-one forest that will not be uprooted by one disaster. When each clan has its own representative tree in this forest, no one may threaten the whole forest with a single lone tree — this is precisely the activation strategy's anti-monopoly deep meaning.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: Multiplication-Fruit technologization and mentor network maximize strong-one cultivation efficiency, avoiding inefficient groping
- **Anti-predation**: widely distributing strong ones is precisely to put an end to a single predator monopolizing the network by force
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems produce one protagonist after eons; Heng Yuan Zhou activates 20% of beings to sovereign levels in a thousand years, strong ones emerge in succession
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: activation rate is statistically tracked across Myriad Ranks; no clan may be absent
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the wider the strong-one base, the more civilization dares to expand toward the hyper-multiverse

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

The 20% activation strategy is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's institutional answer to the "savior narrative." It does not bet on one person, it bets on Myriad Ranks; it does not rely on one ceiling, it relies on a forest. When 20% of all beings become guardians, cosmic ascension is truly irreversible.
