# Entry 911 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 10 — Four-Dimensional Assessment of Plane-Sovereign Strong Ones

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 17, 04, 29

## Introduction: sovereign strong ones — all four dimensions, none may be missing

Article 9 solved "how many strong ones to cultivate"; Article 10 solves "what counts as a qualified strong one." Plane-sovereign strong ones correspond to Origin Realm and above, Heng Yuan Zhou's topmost power group. The predatory era's strong-one standard had only one clause — whoever has the biggest fist is sovereign; charter Article 10 changes it to four-dimensional assessment: Force 40, Heart-nature 25, Contribution 20, Responsibility 15. Force is only the entry ticket; heart-nature is the soul; contribution is value; responsibility is the load-bearing. A cultivator with the biggest fist but evil heart-nature and refusal to bear responsibility is in Heng Yuan Zhou not called a sovereign strong one, but a "high-risk individual."

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Plane-Sovereign Strong-One Standards (Document 17). Its jurisprudential core is "the strong one's responsibility exceeds his rights" (Document 04 core proposition three): the higher the realm, the greater the responsibility to the symbiotic network, not the greater the right to predate. The four-dimensional weight design itself is anti-predation — the heart-nature dimension alone holds 25, contribution and responsibility together 35; although the force dimension holds 40, it is only the basic threshold, and after crossing the force line it is the other three dimensions that truly separate people.

## II. Normative Content: Four Dimensions' Weights and Passing Lines

| Dimension | Weight | Passing Points |
|-----------|--------|----------------|
| Force | 40% | Reaches Origin Realm, stably mobilizes origin force, energy purity 99%+ |
| Heart-nature | 25% | Passes ethics review, never predated, does not use predation to save itself under extreme duress |
| Contribution | 20% | Contribution value in civilization's top 20%, at least one civilizational-level contribution record |
| Responsibility | 15% | Willing and able to bear plane-level public duty, annually investing no less than 30% of time |

All four dimensions must pass to qualify; force passing but heart-nature, contribution, or responsibility failing any one, downgrade to "high-risk individual" under supervision.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all cultivators wishing to obtain the formal "plane-sovereign strong one" title and corresponding rights (resource priority, law-participation right, civilizational decision-making right, etc.). The title is not life-long; four dimensions are annually re-reviewed: those whose heart-nature, contribution, and responsibility dimensions continuously decline, title and treatment are synchronously downgraded. This article applies especially strictly to special strong ones such as Lin Hao route and D route — their force dimension is naturally prominent, so assessment focus precisely falls on heart-nature and responsibility.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Forging assessment records, disguising consciousness to pass ethics review, refusing public duty after obtaining the title, using title privileges to actually predate, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: title revoked, relevant rights stripped; those cheating in ethics review have their consciousness deep-scan conclusions re-examined, and if necessary pursued under anti-predation clauses; high-risk individuals refusing transformation face cultivation restrictions and plane isolation.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: force data comes from network energy monitoring, contribution data from six-category automatic contribution measurement, responsibility data from public-task attendance records; only the heart-nature dimension is independently completed by the Anchor Court's ethics review committee through four means — historical review, voluntary consciousness deep-scan, situational stress tests, and long-term observation. The four-dimensional data is not subordinate to one another and cross-verified, preventing single-dimension score farming.

## VI. A Genius Sent Back

The four-dimensional assessment's most weighty example is a genius sent back. He scored full marks in force, but in the heart-nature review's stress test, under extreme duress chose to sacrifice the innocent to save himself. The assessment committee did not accommodate him for his force, but moved him from the sovereign-strong-one roster into high-risk-individual supervision. This send-back declared to all civilization: in Heng Yuan Zhou, no matter how big the fist, it cannot buy the word "qualified." The heart-nature dimension's one-vote veto lets all strong ones wanting a shortcut understand — force is the entry ticket; passing it only proves you qualify to take the exam, and what truly decides whether you may become a guardian is whom you are willing to use this force to guard.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: direct strong-one resources toward "qualified guardians" not "force monsters"; putting an end to using great material for trivial purposes
- **Anti-predation**: the heart-nature dimension directly one-vote-vetoes predatory stance; it is anti-predation's implementation at the strong-one level
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type sovereign strong ones only compare fists; Heng Yuan Zhou's four-dimensional assessment demands virtue-and-ability, with virtue first
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: each clan's strong ones are assessed by the same four-dimensional ruler; no clan double standards
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: four-dimensional standards are synchronously calibrated as civilization expands, no dual tracks re-set when new systems appear

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

The four-dimensional assessment is Heng Yuan Zhou's rewrite of the word "sovereign." True sovereignty is not the person with the greatest force, but the person in whom force, heart-nature, contribution, and responsibility are four-in-one. This standard held, the strong-one group is civilization's guardian, not the knife hanging above Myriad Ranks' heads.
