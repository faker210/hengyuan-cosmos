# Entry 912 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 11 — The Every-Road-Available Guarantee

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 08, 43, 44

## Introduction: do not give up on any potential evolver

If Article 2 declares "the evolutionary right is equal for all," Article 11 turns this right into the real institution of "a road to walk." It stipulates: every life in Heng Yuan Zhou, regardless of clan, birth, endowment, realm, or wealth, must receive an evolutionary path suited to itself — a Dharma door to study, resources to use, a mentor to ask, an environment to cultivate, institutions to rely on. This is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's simplest and most solemn promise to Myriad Ranks: no one will be abandoned by the cultivation system.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Every-Road-Available Guarantee System (Document 08). Its jurisprudence lies in: the evolutionary right in cultivation civilizations is the right to life; low-realm beings have extremely low survival probability before cosmic dangers; initial endowment differences objectively exist, but institutions' responsibility is to make up differences, not amplify them; abandoned individuals are civilization's potential loss, and many "trash" simply have not found the road suited to them. Universal evolution is even more the foundation of cosmic ascension — network effectiveness is positively correlated with the number of connected life.

## II. Normative Content: Four-Layer Guarantee Chain

Article 11 refines the guarantee system into a four-layer progressive structure:

1. **Basic guarantee**: free Dharma doors, minimum resource allocation, basic medical care and education, no threshold for all;
2. **Targeted support**: extra make-up for low-endowment individuals, impoverished clans, post-disaster groups, disabled cultivators, strange life;
3. **Opportunity expansion**: advanced opportunities, cross-plane exchange, special-program participation rights, bridging for those with potential;
4. **Achievement incentives**: high-tier resources, anchor high-access, civilizational honors, rewarding those with outstanding contributions.

The four layers are bottom-guarantee, make-up, nurturing excellence, and incentive, ring-linked.

## III. Scope

This article tilts toward vulnerable groups: those with poor innate aptitude, those overall lacking resources, those who lost homes to disaster, the physically and mentally disabled, strange life not fitting conventional systems. Targeted support is not permanent welfare, but helping them reach a basic evolutionary level before transitioning to more-work-more-pay. This article also applies to accepted individuals from multiverse residual civilizations (see entry 926), additionally providing system-compatibility assessment.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Refusing to provide basic guarantee on grounds of "insufficient talent" or "humble birth," systematically discriminating against targeted-support recipients, diverting guarantee resources to other uses, running "guarantee-quota trading," all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: guarantee resources are replenished, the responsible person bears misappropriation responsibility; those deliberately creating "a road exists but you may not walk it" obstacles are pursued as double unconstitutionality under Article 2's equal evolutionary right and this article.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by the coverage-rate ledger within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: basic-guarantee coverage rate, targeted-support arrival rate, each clan's evolutionary-right realization rate are real-time statistically tracked; any layer long below threshold triggers special correction; the guarantee system's income and expenditure is managed by the Myriad-Rank-republican council's guarantee committee, with accounts periodically network-wide open to supervision by all beings.

## VI. A Hand Not Given Up

Article 11's softest moment is a hand not given up. A child with extremely poor innate aptitude, long judged by the old system as hopeless in this lifetime, in Heng Yuan Zhou is caught by the targeted-support layer: extra resources, one-on-one mentor, a cultivation plan tailored for him. Years later he may not become a sovereign strong one, but steadily reached the Law Origin Realm and became a mentor in a public cultivation room. This scene shows the every-road-available guarantee does not judge success by achievement rate — its success is letting every hand reaching out not be left cold in the air. Civilization's warmth lies precisely in those lives doomed never to become geniuses, yet still treated well.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: do not bury any potential genius; count evolutionary potentials discarded by old systems into civilization's general ledger
- **Anti-predation**: bottom-guarantee lets the weak not risk predation for a livelihood, dissolving predation motivation from the demand side
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems' bottom relies on grace, supply may be cut off at any time; Heng Yuan Zhou's guarantee is a legal right, inalienable
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: this article is "99% coverage"'s direct landing at the social-security layer
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly joined multiverse life automatically enters the four-layer guarantee chain, seamlessly connecting to civilization

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

The every-road-available guarantee is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's softest yet hardest part. Soft in its promise to every weak one; hard in that this promise is written into the charter. When the humblest life also firmly believes "there is a road," Myriad-Rank republicanism truly has the people's heart as its base.
