# Entry 913 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 12 — Myriad-Rank Origin Bidirectional Cycle

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 15, 25, 12

## Introduction: not one-way transfusion, but bidirectional cycle

Predatory systems' energy relation is one-way — the strong drain from the weak, the more drained the drier. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's energy relation is bidirectional cycle: the strong one's origin feeds back the network; the network then distributes increments to Myriad Ranks; Myriad Ranks' evolution in turn feeds back the network. Article 12 writes this "Myriad-Rank-origin bidirectional cycle" into the charter; it is the entire civilization's blood circulation system. Without it, Article 3's collective ascension cannot land; Article 8's more-work-more-pay has nothing to distribute.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Energy Cycle and Transformation Law (Document 15), the Symbiotic Feedback Cycle Mechanism (Document 25), and the Life-Cycle System Details (Document 12). Its jurisprudential core is the anti-predation axiom's "develop increments": the cosmos is not a static resource container, but a life body that may be wholly ascended through orderly symbiosis. Predators consume stock and evolutionary potential; symbiotic civilization enlarges the cycle and produces increments, letting every participant benefit from the larger cycle.

## II. Normative Content: Four Links of the Bidirectional Cycle

Article 12 breaks the cycle into four supervisable links:

1. **Upstream**: the origin a cultivator gains returns to the network by contribution;
2. **Amplification**: the network, through anchors and the law network, converts returned origin into increments (Multiplication-Fruit-type technical amplification);
3. **Downstream**: increments are distributed back to Myriad Ranks along two lines — more-work-more-pay and every-road-available;
4. **Feedback**: Myriad Ranks' universal evolution raises network effectiveness, further amplifying the cycle.

Four rings connect head to tail, forming an undepletable positive feedback, not a one-outlet-only drainage pipe.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all life and institutions connected to the anchor network: from Sense-Realm new cultivators to Origin Realm strong ones, every cultivator's energy in-and-out is within the cycle. This article also binds cross-plane resource allocation — resource flow between planes must be bidirectional cycle; one-way draining of some plane (plane colonization's essence) is prohibited. The D route's human-made Dao-rules soul rings, Lin Hao route and other special systems' energy output must likewise return to the cycle, no private domains allowed.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Intercepting and privately hoarding origin that should return, only taking from the network and never upstream, one-way draining some plane's resources, contaminating cycle purity by mixing in predated gains, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: intercepted parts are forcibly returned; contaminated parts are network-wide purified; those under the name of "self-contained cycle" actually running independent predation are pursued heavily under Article 1 anti-predation and Article 3 collective-ascension obligation.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by the energy-flow monitoring within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: every node's upstream and downstream flow is real-time recorded; nodes that are out of budget and long only-take-no-give automatically alert; the Anchor Supervisory Committee monitors each plane's energy income and expenditure; if some plane is one-way drained, protection is immediately started. Cycle purity is guaranteed by energy-standardized metering (Document 65); once predatory residues are mixed in, network-wide tracing is triggered.

## VI. One Cycle's Start and Return

Article 12 may be understood through one complete energy journey. A new cultivator's hard-won thread of origin returns to the anchor network per contract-responsibility; the network through law amplification turns it into increments usable by Myriad Ranks; part of this increment returns to him per more-work-more-pay, part through every-road-available bottom-line goes to the lowest new life; that new life later grows and sends its own origin back to the network. This thread of energy completes one loop, from one person becoming Myriad Ranks', yet with ten times more value than if left in his body. This is the bidirectional cycle — not who supports whom, but everyone together making the cake bigger and bigger.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the bidirectional cycle amplifies every individual's cultivation results into network-wide increments, putting an end to energy idling in private domains
- **Anti-predation**: the cycle institutionally negates "draining stock," only allowing "producing increments"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems strong ones eat fatter and fatter, planes drain drier and drier; Heng Yuan Zhou's bidirectional cycle, Myriad Ranks rise with the tide
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the cycle's beneficiaries are all Myriad Ranks, not the cycle's designers
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the cycle expands as new planes and new clans connect; the larger the scale, the stronger the effectiveness

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Myriad-Rank-origin bidirectional cycle is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's heart. Once the heart stops, civilization regresses to predatory stagnant water; once the heart beats, every life's effort pushes the whole cosmos to ascend. This cycle held, civilization is alive.
