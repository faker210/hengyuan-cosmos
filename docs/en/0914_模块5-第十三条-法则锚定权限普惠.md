# Entry 914 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 13 — Universal Access to Law-Anchor Authority

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 20, 24, 27

## Introduction: connecting to the anchor is the admission right every being deserves

Law-anchor authority refers to which layer of the anchor network a cultivator connects to, and how wide a range of laws he may mobilize. In the predatory era, connecting to high-tier anchors was top sects' patent; bottom beings could not even touch the most basic anchor. Article 13 universalizes this authority: every life reaching the corresponding realm has the right to connect to the corresponding tier of anchor network; no person may set up barriers. The anchor is not the strong one's private property, but Myriad Ranks' public infrastructure.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Anchor Structure and Levels (Document 20), the Anchor Energy-Conduction Path (Document 24), and the Anchor Calibration and Maintenance (Document 27). The anchor network is Myriad Ranks' symbiosis's physical foundation; its effectiveness rises with the number of connected life. Monopolizing connection authority in a few hands both violates the republican axiom of Myriad Races and is energetically inefficient — the denser the network nodes, the steadier the cycle. Therefore, universal access is a right for Myriad Ranks, and a gain for the network.

## II. Normative Content: Tiered Access, Level-by-Level Unlocking

Article 13 grades connection authority by realm and unlocks level by level:

1. Sense to Condensed / Transform: connect to basic anchors, satisfying daily cultivation and compatibility assessment;
2. Law to Dao Origin: connect to regional anchors, may participate in regional law insights;
3. Dao Integration to Boundary-Breaking: connect to plane anchors, may participate in plane law fine-tuning;
4. World-Creation to Origin: connect to cosmic anchors, participate in cosmic-law-level operations.

Grading asks only realm and certification, not clan, birth, or lineage. Reach the level and connect; no queue, no attached loyalty.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all life reaching the corresponding realm, including all forms of five lineages, Youth Supremes, planet consciousness, and accepted individuals from multiverse residual civilizations. This article especially protects new life "connecting from zero" — no discrimination because historically never connected. High-realm strong ones may not "book the venue" of low-realm anchors; they must leave capacity for new connectors.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Refusing level-reachers access on grounds of "qualification review" or "limited quotas," renting anchor capacity for profit, blocking specific clans from access, artificially lowering new connectors' permission tier, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: access right is immediately restored, losses during the blocked period are converted into compensation; those trading anchor-access rights for loyalty are pursued under Dharma-door monopoly (Article 1).

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by the access ledger within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: each anchor's access list and capacity occupation are transparent; the time new level-reachers take to apply for access is automatically statistically tracked, overdue immediately alerts; the Anchor Supervisory Committee monitors each clan's access-rate distribution, and discovering some clan is systematically locked out triggers correction. The anchors' own calibration and maintenance are the responsibility of professional institutions; connectors have no right to alter node parameters without authorization.

## VI. One Access Not Stopped

Article 13's most concrete fulfillment is one access not stopped. A young person from a marginal plane just breaking through to Law Origin Realm, per rules applies to connect to a regional anchor. Under the old system he would be told quotas are full, queue a hundred years first; in Heng Yuan Zhou, the system verifies his realm has reached standard and immediately opens. He is not blocked outside the anchor door because born on a marginal plane with no big-faction background. Universal law-anchor authority is precisely delivering cultivation civilization's most core infrastructure, like water and air, equally to every level-reacher's hand. The door is always open; it only asks whether you have walked there, not where you came from.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: nodes not wasted, network effectiveness maximized; an idle anchor is the whole cycle's loss
- **Anti-predation**: access monopoly is Dharma-door monopoly's replica at the infrastructure layer; this article positively dismantles it
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems access the Great Dao by the protagonist's destined fate; Heng Yuan Zhou reach-level-connect, universal to Myriad Ranks
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the tiered access table treats five lineages equally
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: new planes and new clans upon access enjoy the same-tier authority, no extra thresholds

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Universal law-anchor authority turns cultivation civilization's most core infrastructure into a public good. When every level-reacher may fairly connect to anchors, "the Great Dao is simple and accessible to Myriad Ranks" is no longer empty talk, but reality happening every day.
