# Entry 915 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 14 — The Cross-Plane Operation Reporting System

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 81, 88, 28

## Introduction: every cross-plane trip must leave a name

Article 4's causal self-discipline has already established the principle; Article 14 lands it as a concrete administrative procedure: the reporting system. Heng Yuan Zhou's strong ones may traverse planes, but any cross-plane operation — regardless of purpose being diplomacy, rescue, archaeology, or cultivation — must in advance be reported to the Plane Administration Bureau, stating purpose, personnel, time limit, expected impact; no approval, no action. Reporting is not distrust of strong ones, but because cross-plane operations have an enormous causal-influence radius; one impulsive traversal may contaminate two planes.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Plane-Traversal Rules (Document 81), the Cross-Plane Ethics Guidelines (Document 88), and the Cross-Plane Anchor Connection (Document 28). Cross-plane operations directly touch the causal self-discipline axiom: only return knowledge, do not disturb causality. The reporting system turns this axiom from moral consciousness into administrative precondition — not "do it then file afterward," but "approved then allowed to move." Its jurisprudence is cognitive humility: even plane-sovereign strong ones cannot fully pre-judge traversal's full causal consequences; advance reporting is precisely to let professional institutions intervene in assessment.

## II. Normative Content: Five Requirements of Reporting

Article 14 requires every cross-plane report to state:

1. **Operation purpose**: diplomacy, rescue, archaeology, cultivation, or cross-plane trade;
2. **Personnel list**: who goes, each one's realm and authority;
3. **Time limit**: estimated entry and departure time points;
4. **Expected impact**: the disturbance range the target plane may suffer;
5. **Return method**: the commitment to only return knowledge, no material energy.

All five required for acceptance; after approval a cross-plane travel permit is issued.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all individuals and teams with cross-plane traversal ability, whether home-plane residents or external missions. Plane-sovereign strong ones and Multiverse Realm strong ones' reporting review is stricter — because their causal-influence radius is larger. Traversing privately without a travel permit, regardless of reason, is uniformly unconstitutional. This article does not apply to emergency rescue: but rescue must immediately file a post-report and accept causal assessment.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Traversing without approval, falsifying report information, overstaying beyond permitted purpose and time limit, using cross-plane channels to smuggle predated gains, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: forcibly withdraw, travel permit suspended; falsification and smuggling are pursued heavily under Article 1 anti-predation; those whose private traversal causes causal contamination, the operator bears all causal-correction costs and is permanently restricted from cross-plane qualification.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by the cross-plane travel ledger within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: every traversal's start, end, personnel, and time are fully traced, automatically compared with report records, mismatch immediately alerts; the Plane Administration Bureau sets up dedicated approval and inspection posts; the Time-Law Committee periodically audits reporting compliance, putting an end to "secret passages."

## VI. An Approved Travel Pass

Article 14 in reality is an approved travel pass. An archaeological team plans to go to an unfamiliar plane; in their report form they clearly write purpose, personnel, 72-hour observation period, commitment to only return knowledge; after submission it is quickly approved. This travel pass is not distrust, but a joint protection — protecting them from causing a causal accident due to momentary impulse, and protecting that unfamiliar plane from unauthorized entry. When cross-plane exploration becomes a rule-following, everyone-certificated daily routine, freedom need not come at the cost of adventure. The reporting system looks tedious, but actually makes every departure leave with peace of mind and return clean.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: advance assessment avoids catastrophic correction after cross-plane operations, zeroing out hidden costs
- **Anti-predation**: smuggling and plane colonization both need cross-plane channels; the reporting system places these channels under monitoring
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type protagonists traverse freely with no one managing; Heng Yuan Zhou every traversal has a report, time limit, and inspection
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: reporting applies equally to Myriad-Rank missions and individuals, no exemption because of a large clan
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the reporting system lets cross-plane exploration infinitely expand without losing control

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

The cross-plane operation reporting system is the reins Heng Yuan Zhou puts on free exploration. The reins are not to trap exploration, but to let exploration go on forever — the farther one goes, the more one must know where one came from, where one is going, and what one left behind.
