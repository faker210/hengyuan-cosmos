# Entry 916 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 15 — Observe First, Then Limited Intervention

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 83, 84, 89

## Introduction: observe without touching, measure without disturbing

Article 14 governs "whether you may go"; Article 15 governs "what to do after going." It writes the Observe-First-Limited-Intervention Guideline (Document 83) directly into the charter: observe without touching, measure without disturbing, examine before moving, move with measure. For an unfamiliar plane or a historical period, Heng Yuan Zhou's strong ones may only watch first; only after watching, understanding, and assessing, may they make a limited move. This is not timidity, but the deepest awe for the causal law.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Observe-First-Limited-Intervention Guideline (Document 83), the Archaeology Agreement General Outline (Document 84), and the Time-Paradox Prevention Mechanism (Document 89). Its jurisprudence is cognitive humility: even plane-sovereign strong ones cannot fully master all causal associations of one time node or one plane civilization. Direct intervention may trigger unpredictable chain reactions; full observation is the only reliable means to minimize risk. The causal self-discipline axiom is here concretized into an operational process.

## II. Normative Content: Hard Indicators for Observation and Intervention

Article 15 quantifies the process into hard indicators:

1. **Observation duration**: no less than 1% of the target period, and no less than 72 hours of the home plane's standard time;
2. **Observation dimensions**: matter, energy, life, consciousness, causality five dimensions, none dispensable;
3. **Observation neutrality**: pure passive perception, no communication, no traces, no hints, no thought waves released;
4. **Intervention scope**: no more than 1% of the permitted area;
5. **Intervention degree**: bound by intervention threshold (area total energy × causal-sensitivity coefficient × 1/10000);
6. **Intervention time**: no more than 10% of observation duration, and no more than 24 hours.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all cross-plane expeditions, time retrogression, archaeology, and diplomatic operations. The higher the civilization level and the more critical the historical node, the higher the causal-sensitivity coefficient, the lower the intervention threshold — at critical nodes almost no substantial energy injection is allowed. This article applies equally to plane-sovereign strong ones and ordinary cultivators; thresholds are not relaxed because the strong are bold.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Intervening with insufficient observation, missing a dimension, disturbing the target during observation, breaking through intervention scope or threshold, overstaying in the target period, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: immediately suspend and forcibly withdraw; the causal deviation already caused starts a correction procedure, and the operator bears correction costs; those triggering time forks or cyclical-harvest-type disturbances are pursued heavily under Article 4 and historical-reversal prevention.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: observation duration, five-dimensional coverage, and intervention thresholds are automatically verified by anchor anchoring devices; intervention-permitted area and energy output are real-time compared; all operations are recorded in the time-operation archive library for permanent preservation; the Time-Law Committee independently inspects anomalous intervention behavior.

## VI. A Pair of Eyes That Watched for 72 Hours

Article 15's most patient picture is a pair of eyes that watched for 72 hours. The observer is required, in pure passive state, to watch a target period's matter, energy, life, consciousness, causality five dimensions for the full minimum duration. Others may feel this is too long and too slow, but precisely these 72 hours let him avoid later filling a precipitancy pit with countless expensive causal corrections. Observing first is not hesitation, but compressing the cost of ignorance to the minimum. Heng Yuan Zhou's strong ones thus develop a peculiar humility: the longer they watch, the more they know they should not rush to act; the clearer they see, the more they understand that even acting must act with restraint.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: full advance observation avoids expensive post-hoc causal correction; it is the most cost-effective energy saving
- **Anti-predation**: random intervention and rewriting other planes' history is essentially predation on their causality; this article positively prohibits it
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type protagonists break into other worlds and freely rewrite plot; Heng Yuan Zhou five-dimensional observation, threshold constraints, time-limited withdrawal
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: what is protected is all beings in all unfamiliar planes and all historical periods
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the observe-intervene closed loop lets civilization infinitely explore more unknown planes without collapsing

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Observe first, limited intervention is Heng Yuan Zhou's mature posture for exploring the cosmos. It tells Myriad Ranks: curiosity is no sin, but curiosity with humility deserves the infinite cosmos. First understand the world, then talk about changing the world.
