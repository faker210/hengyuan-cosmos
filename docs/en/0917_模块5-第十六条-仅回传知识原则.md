# Entry 917 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 16 — The Knowledge-Return-Only Principle

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 87, 69, 84

## Introduction: bring back only knowledge, nothing else

Article 15 says "how to watch, how to move"; Article 16 says "what to bring back after watching." It stipulates: the only permitted product of cross-plane and time-tracing operations is knowledge — observations' knowledge may be returned to the Alaya Network for Myriad Ranks to learn, but material, energy, beings, or fortune from the target plane may not be taken away, nor one's own causality implanted. This is the causal self-discipline axiom's final lock at the "results" link. Knowledge is an increment; the more it is shared, the more it grows; matter and causality are stock; taking one thing away disturbs a whole patch.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Knowledge-Return-Only Principle (Document 87), the Technology Opening and Sharing Mechanism (Document 69), and the Archaeology Agreement General Outline (Document 84). Its jurisprudence is: cross-plane operations' legitimate purpose is seeking knowledge and mutual aid, not transport and predation. Returning knowledge network-wide both expands Myriad Ranks' cognition and does not disturb the target plane's causality; while carrying back any material or energy establishes new causal entanglement between two planes, sowing seeds of future conflict and colonization.

## II. Normative Content: What to Return, What Not to Return

Article 16 draws the return boundary:

- **May return**: observed knowledge, laws, cultural records, technical principles, all entered into the Alaya Network for network-wide sharing;
- **Forbidden to take**: target plane's primordial matter, energy veins, individual beings, fortune crystals, law fragments;
- **Forbidden to implant**: do not implant one's own plane's people, objects, values into the target plane, do not "make decisions" for it;
- **Exception channel**: only when the target plane actively requests and its own clan self-government agrees, may the cross-plane aid procedure be followed, separately approved.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all cross-plane operation teams and individuals. Archaeological teams, diplomatic missions, cultivation tour groups apply equally. Seemingly harmless acts like "casually taking an antique as a souvenir" are equally forbidden — a souvenir is also stock transport. This article does not exclude broad knowledge sharing: on the contrary, returned knowledge is fully open to Myriad Ranks; this is precisely cross-plane operations' true value.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Privately carrying material or energy out of the target plane, secretly bringing back individual beings, implanting home-plane influence without consent, running plane colonization under the name of "scholarship," all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: brought-back items are forcibly returned or harmlessly disposed of, and the relevant knowledge-return qualification is suspended; those causing target-plane ecological or causal damage are pursued heavily under plane colonization (Article 1) and causal disturbance (Article 4).

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: cross-plane entry and exit's material and energy carried amount is scanned and compared by anchor anchoring devices, excess immediately intercepted; returned knowledge's catalog is publicized network-wide, subject to each clan's supervision on "whether private goods are hidden"; the time-operation archive library records every operation's actual products for post-audit.

## VI. A Hand Returning Empty

Article 16's most moving picture is a hand returning empty. A cultivator walked through over a dozen other planes, and when leaving took nothing — no casually picked antique, no rare treasure, no stone wanting to keep as a souvenir, only returned with a belly full of learned knowledge. This knowledge is entered into the Alaya Network; Myriad Ranks henceforth have the wisdom of over a dozen more planes. Returning with empty hands looks like gaining nothing, but actually brings back the most precious thing, without leaving a wound on any plane. Knowledge-return-only makes the explorer's far journey a pure gift, not another gentle predation.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: knowledge copies at zero cost, material transport is expensive; returning only knowledge is the most economical exploration
- **Anti-predation**: this article directly blocks the old colonial road of "looting things under the guise of exploration"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type protagonists loot wherever they go, carrying away piles of treasures; Heng Yuan Zhou brings back only knowledge, departing with empty hands, returning with a belly full of learning
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: returned knowledge is fully open to Myriad Ranks; exploration results belong to all
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: knowledge sharing makes every cross-plane exploration a civilizational cognitive increment

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

The knowledge-return-only principle is the "gentlemen's agreement" Heng Yuan Zhou lays down for cross-plane exploration. It lets Myriad Ranks safely see every corner of the cosmos, without worrying any viewing turns into another colonization. Bring only wisdom, not a single inch of land — this is true civilizational confidence.
