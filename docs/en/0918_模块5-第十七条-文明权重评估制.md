# Entry 918 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 17 — The Civilizational-Weight Assessment System

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 29, 05, 77

## Introduction: voting voice is allocated by symbiotic contribution

Myriad Ranks deliberate Myriad Ranks' affairs, but how is Myriad Ranks' opinion measured? Article 17 introduces the civilizational-weight assessment system: each clan's and each plane's voting voice in Myriad-Rank-republican decisions is allocated not by fist size, not by history length, but by its symbiotic contribution to the symbiotic network and symbiosis-degree assessment. This institutionally prevents the oligarchization of "the strong have loud voices" — voting voice follows contribution, not force.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Symbiosis-Degree Assessment System (Document 29), the Myriad-Ranks Co-Advancement Basic Program (Document 05), and the Founding of Myriad-Rank Republicanism (Document 77). Its jurisprudence is in the same line as Article 8's more-work-more-pay: the greater the contribution, the greater the share in distribution and decision. But unlike personal contribution, civilizational weight assesses the whole clan/plane's degree of feedback to the network — a civilization long symbiosing with the network, ecologically stable, knowledge-sharing, contributing in crisis, should have greater voting voice than a civilization that only takes and does not give.

## II. Normative Content: How Weights Are Assessed and Used

Article 17 breaks the weight system into three links:

1. **Assessment dimensions**: clan/plane symbiosis degree (energy return flow, knowledge-sharing volume, ecological stability, crisis contribution, inter-clan integration);
2. **Weight conversion**: assessment results converted into decision weight, weight dynamically floats with annual symbiosis degree;
3. **Decision application**: the Myriad-Rank-republican council deliberates by weight, but every clan retains the one-person-one-vote basic voting right; weight only amplifies contributors' influence on public issues, does not strip any clan's basic voice.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all clans and planes joining Myriad-Rank republicanism. Weight does not discriminate against "young civilizations": newly joined civilizations have a novice protection period, with basic weight bottom-lined, not losing voice because of low starting point. This article also binds the human race — as the pioneer, the human race's weight may not be permanently inflated by seniority, but must be maintained by continuous symbiotic contribution.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Manipulating weight assessment, forging clan symbiosis-degree data, coercing deliberation results by force, turning weight into hereditary privilege, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: relevant deliberation resolutions are void, weights are re-assessed; the manipulator's decision qualification is suspended; those using force to veto Myriad-Rank-republican resolutions are pursued heavily under undermining Myriad-Rank-republican order.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by the symbiosis-degree assessment system within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: weight data comes from automatic network collection, not manual declaration; the Anchor Supervisory Committee annually publishes each clan's weight distribution, anomalous weight changes are automatically audited; any clan disagreeing with its own weight may file an assessment review with the Symbiotic Court.

## VI. A Vote Not by Loudness

Article 17 changed Myriad-Rank-republican deliberation's appearance. In the past, the loudest voice and hardest fist's civilization steered the agenda; now, each civilization's voting voice is calculated by its degree of feedback to the symbiotic network. A small clan quietly restoring its plane's ecology excellently and sharing knowledge year-round may have greater influence on public issues than a big clan that only takes. This does not mean big clans lose voice — it still has its bottom-lined one vote. Weight follows contribution; deliberation changes from comparing whose voice is loudest to comparing who has done most for everyone. Civilization thus is willing to do more, not roar more.

## VII. Axiom 6's Projection in the Weight System

The universal equal infinite law reminds us: civilizational weight only affects the size of deliberative influence, never affects any being's inherently possessed basic infinite power. Easygoing layabouts even with humble contribution and weak weight still have their floor infinity intact, and are not eliminated because of low weight; strivers and Supremes though high weight have only superimposed more levels of infinity, not gained one bit more of the basics than others. Weight measures construction contribution, not the worth of life; the two are never conflated.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: voting voice allocated to civilizations truly feeding back the network yields higher decision quality, fewer detours
- **Anti-predation**: weight follows contribution not force; from the decision end, the strong oligarch is put an end to
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems the strong one's word is law; Heng Yuan Zhou voting voice assessed by symbiotic contribution, dynamically floating
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: new civilizations have bottom-lined weight, not squeezed out the door by established civilizations
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly joined multiverse civilizations automatically enter assessment, gaining voting voice by contribution

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

The civilizational-weight assessment system makes Myriad-Rank-republican deliberation both efficient and fair. It does not pursue absolute average — contributors speak a bit more; nor does it pursue the strong eating everything — every clan has a bottom-lined one vote. Voting voice allocated by symbiosis, civilization will never degenerate into a few people's club.
