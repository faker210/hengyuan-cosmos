# Entry 919 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 18 — The Transcended One's Perpetual-Construction Obligation

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 17, 95, 79

## Introduction: transcendence is not lying flat, but perpetual construction

The old age's imagination of "transcendence" is climbing to the peak then washing hands and quitting, hanging up retirement, as cosmos sovereign sunbathing. Article 18 completely negates this imagination: in Heng Yuan Zhou, true transcendence is not escape, not lying flat, but perpetual construction. The transcended one — Multiverse Realm, Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm strong ones — has mastered near-infinite force, and thus bears near-infinite construction duty. The greater the force, the longer the construction; never retire. This is the collective-ascension axiom's extension at the highest realm.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Plane-Sovereign Strong-One Standards (Document 17)'s "strong-one duty system" (annually investing no less than 30% of time in public duty), extending it to hyper-multiverse strong ones. Its jurisprudence is: the transcended one could transcend precisely because the Myriad-Rank symbiotic network held him up; he did not climb up out of thin air, but Myriad Ranks together lifted him up. Having received this great grace, turning around to lie flat and enjoy is ethically a variant of Article 3's "ascending alone." The anti-predation axiom requires the strong one's force serve Myriad Ranks' common evolution; perpetual construction is precisely this requirement's highest form.

## II. Normative Content: Three Categories of Perpetual-Construction Duty

Article 18 concretizes the transcended one's obligation into three categories of never-ceasing construction duty:

1. **Law engineering**: continuously iterate hyper-multiverse-grade manuals, material rules, civilizational engineering, pushing laws to better evolution;
2. **Civilizational guardianship**: guard the planes and anchors under responsibility, monitor external threats and plane stability;
3. **Torch transmission**: cultivate the next generation of sovereign strong ones, especially tilting toward low-endowment and weak clans.

The transcended one may rotate responsible directions, but may not withdraw from construction entirely; may rest and adjust, but "perpetual" means his duty never ends.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all transcended ones breaking through Origin Realm and entering Multiverse Realm and Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm. The higher the realm, the larger the construction duty scale. This article especially binds those ancient strong ones of "transcendence then hermitage" — in Heng Yuan Zhou there is no such option as hermitage. Lin Hao, D-route strong ones and other special transcended ones are equally within the perpetual-construction obligation, no exemption because of special route.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

After transcendence completely withdrawing from public construction, using force for private-domain pleasure, refusing to transmit and cultivate descendants, evading all duty on grounds of "I have already transcended," all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: his transcended-realm certification and treatment are suspended, and the Alaya Network records his "construction absenteeism"; those with serious circumstances and force strong enough to endanger the network yet refusing construction are supervised as high-risk individuals, and in extreme cases a force-restraint procedure is started — not punishment, but preventing a huge force from idling into a hidden danger.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by the transcended-one duty ledger within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: each transcended one's law-engineering output, guardianship records, and transmission results are annually statistically tracked; the Anchor Supervisory Committee monitors the transcended one's energy flow; those long only enjoying not constructing automatically alert; the Myriad-Rank-republican council periodically hears the transcended ones' construction reports.

## VI. A Guardian Who Never Retires

Article 18 makes Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended ones sharply distinct from old-age sovereigns. The old age's strong ones climb to the peak then find a pure land to hang up retirement, handing the cosmos back to war and predation; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended ones are bound to posts by the perpetual-construction obligation: iterating manuals, guarding anchors, cultivating descendants, until civilization reaches higher dimensions. He does not lack the right to rest, but he understands he could transcend because Myriad Ranks together lifted him up, and this force must be returned. The never-retiring transcended one turns near-infinite force into an eternally-under-construction project — this is the most responsible use of infinite force.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: near-infinite force if lying flat is the cosmos's greatest energy waste; perpetual construction channels it into infinite output
- **Anti-predation**: judges the predatory legacy of "transcendence means occupying resources and enjoying" as unconstitutional
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type Gu Chensha, Ye Fan-type sovereigns hang up after transcendence; Heng Yuan Zhou's transcended ones perpetually construct, iterating without cease
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the transcended one's construction results — law engineering, guardianship, transmission — benefit all Myriad Ranks
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: hyper-multiverse-grade manuals and engineering continuously open and iterate; civilization never caps

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

The transcended one's perpetual-construction obligation is Heng Yuan Zhou's ultimate answer to "what to do after success." In predatory civilization, success is the end and the start of enjoying; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, success is only the start of greater responsibility. True transcendence is always under construction, always on the road.
