# Entry 920 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 19 | Dual-Track Time-Domain Use Right

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 82, 24, 79

## Introduction: fast track and slow track, chosen by beings themselves

The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization has prepared two time tracks for cultivators: the Origin Acceleration Domain lets civilization iterate at extreme speed; the Quest Sediment Domain lets life slowly temper epics. Article 19 stipulates the use right of these two time domains — everyone has the right to use them, but how to use, how long, where to use, has rules. Time is the cosmos's scarcest resource; the dual-track system lets beings want both efficiency and experience, without having to choose one.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Time Retrogression Basic Principles (Document 82) and the dual-track design of time domains. Its jurisprudence is: different life has different needs for "time density" — some clans have short lifespans and want fast-forward for development; some civilizations value experience and want to walk slowly. Forcing all life to cultivate at the same time pace is itself a kind of discrimination. The dual-track system is the republican axiom of Myriad Races' unfolding in the time dimension: there is more than one evolutionary path, and more than one time pace.

## II. Normative Content: Dual-Track Definitions and Use-Right Rules

Article 19 regularizes dual-track use:

1. **Origin Acceleration Domain**: high-magnification deduction acceleration, suitable for manual iteration, batch cultivation, civilization extreme advancement; use right opened by contribution and quota;
2. **Quest Sediment Domain**: normal time flow, suitable for tempering, insights, growth epics; naturally belongs to all life's "real time";
3. **Self-selection principle**: individuals may self-select switching between two tracks, but switching must be reported, to prevent arbitrage using the dual tracks;
4. **Non-withdrawal principle**: no individual may use the acceleration domain to detach from reality and be irresponsible to sediment-domain beings — speed is to better come back and construct.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all cultivators. The Youth Supreme's hardship-growth branch may unfold in the Quest Sediment Domain (see Module 3); high-contribution strong ones may apply for more acceleration quotas. This article especially binds the evasive behavior of "hiding in the acceleration domain and not coming out": the acceleration domain is a tool, not a hermitage. Dual-track use right treats Myriad Ranks equally; no quota tilt by clan.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Arbitraging using the dual-track time differential, forging use quotas, hiding in the acceleration domain to evade the collective-ascension obligation, conducting predatory quick-cultivation in the acceleration domain, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: dual-track use right is suspended, acceleration gains are re-verified as "detached from sediment"; those using the acceleration domain for predatory quick-cultivation are pursued heavily under Article 1 anti-predation and Article 3 collective-ascension obligation.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by the time-domain use ledger within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: every individual's stay duration and switching records on both tracks are fully traced; acceleration quotas are linked to contribution and automatically calculated; the Anchor Supervisory Committee monitors anomalous individuals "long hiding in the acceleration domain," promptly reminding them to return to the sediment domain to fulfill duties.

## VI. Two Youth on Two Roads

Article 19 lets Myriad Ranks for the first time have the freedom to choose time. A short-lived energy clan may desperately iterate in the Origin Acceleration Domain, covering in a hundred years what other clans take a thousand; an experience-valuing consciousness clan is content to slowly temper in the Quest Sediment Domain, living every growth as an epic. Between the two tracks, individuals may freely switch with reporting, neither sacrificing experience for efficiency, nor being left behind by the times for experience. The dual-track system's deepest kindness is: it acknowledges different life has different desires for time density, then returns the choice to the person, rather than prescribing one way of living for everyone.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the acceleration domain is used for manual iterations truly needing speed-up, avoiding all things idling inefficiently on a single slow track
- **Anti-predation**: blocks the loophole of "predating via time differential"; the dual track is a construction tool, not an arbitrage tool
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems' time flow follows the author's arrangement (plot convenience); Heng Yuan Zhou's dual track is fixed by laws, chosen by beings
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the dual track satisfies clans of different lifespans and paces; everyone has a suitable time track
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: as civilization expands, time domains may continue adding new tracks, no capping by the dual track

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Dual-track time-domain use right is Heng Yuan Zhou's unique answer to "time fairness." It lets those wanting speed speed up, those wanting slowness slow down, and uses reporting and the non-withdrawal principle to prevent the dual track from becoming a back door for evading duty. Fast or slow, both are roads Myriad Ranks chose themselves.
