# Entry 921 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 20 | Legalization of the D-Route Human-Made Dao-Rules Soul Rings

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 10, 51, 91

## Introduction: write the human race's original route into the charter

In the old Douluo system, soul rings came from hunting soul beasts — naked predation. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization opens a brand-new route for post-humanity: the D route · human-made Dao-rules soul rings — not by hunting, but by law engineering, using technical means to generate Dao-rules soul rings, transforming "soul rings" from predation's product into creation's product. Article 20 formally legalizes this route: it is not a side door, but a legitimate evolutionary path the human race contributes to Myriad Ranks outside the 300 systems.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the 300 Origin Cultivation Systems Overview (Document 10)'s "remaining numbers for special clans and special routes" design and the Human-Race Positioning and Traits (Document 51). Its jurisprudential core is the anti-predation axiom's "develop increments": the old soul-ring system at the cost of soul-beast life; the D route through law engineering creates increments, eradicating from the root the predatory chain of "hunting to level up." Legalizing it is legally confirming that "human-made Dao-rules soul rings" have equal status with natural soul rings and with paths within the 300 systems.

## II. Normative Content: Three Layers of Legalization

Article 20 makes the D route's status clear:

1. **Legitimate path**: human-made Dao-rules soul rings, as a human-characteristic evolutionary path, are protected by the charter; no institution may dismiss them as "pseudo-cultivation";
2. **Unified standards**: D-route cultivators and other-path cultivators are on the same ruler for realm certification, contribution measurement, rights and duties;
3. **Full-chain opening**: the D route's law-engineering technology, like the 300 systems, is fully open to Myriad Ranks; no human patent;
4. **Anti-predation bottom line**: no link of the D route may regress to the old method of "hunting soul beasts, devouring origin"; once discovered, handled under Article 1.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all human cultivators taking the D route, and to other clans willing to adopt this technology. The D route is not the human race's "private plot" — after full technology opening, any clan may draw on it. This article also binds the D route's developers: no new monopoly on grounds of "exclusive technology."

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Belittling the D route as pseudo-cultivation, refusing to certify its realm, restricting D-route cultivators' rights on clan grounds, re-sealing and monopolizing D-route technology, or actually hunting under the D-route name, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: discriminatory practices are corrected, D-route rights are restored; technology monopoly is handled under Article 6's full-opening principle; those regressing to hunting are pursued heavily under Article 1 anti-predation.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: D-route cultivators' realm certification goes through a unified process; their soul rings' origin is network-verified as human-made Dao-rules rather than hunted; the Anchor Supervisory Committee monitors whether D-route technology is truly fully open, discovering closure triggers correction.

## VI. The First Ring No Longer Draws Blood

The D route's legalization's most far-reaching significance is making the first soul ring no longer draw blood. In the old system, every new spirit master's start meant one soul beast's death; under the D route, a youth's first condensing a Dao-rules soul ring relies on a human-made object generated by law engineering, with no being bleeding for it outside the window. This change looks like only a technical replacement, but actually transforms an evolutionary road built on hunting into a new road built on creation. More precious is that this technology is fully open to Myriad Ranks — any clan unwilling to exchange life for power again may take this road.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: law engineering batch-generates soul rings, far more efficient than hunting one by one, and without sacrificing life
- **Anti-predation**: transforming soul rings from "hunt products" into "creation products" is anti-predation's thorough transformation on the martial-soul system
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type soul rings rely on hunting soul beasts and might makes right; Heng Yuan Zhou's D route human-made Dao-rules, learnable by Myriad Ranks
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: D-route technology is fully open; other clans may also adopt it, not human-exclusive
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the D route, as a supplementary path, together with the 300 systems constitutes an open system

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Legalizing the D route · human-made Dao-rules soul rings is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's example of transforming the old world's most typical predation — hunting soul beasts — into creation. It proves: even the bloodiest old setting may, under the anti-predation axiom, be rewritten into a Myriad-Rank-co-created new path.
