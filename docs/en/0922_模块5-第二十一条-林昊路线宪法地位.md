# Entry 922 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 21 | Constitutional Status of the Lin Hao Route

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 95, 51, 04

## Introduction: why one person's route must be written into the charter

Lin Hao is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's iconic figure; his cultivation route (no-anchor-ring, symbiosis-as-root unique path) is the most representative personal-original route outside the 300 systems. Article 21 formally confirms the Lin Hao route's constitutional status. This looks special, but is actually necessary: if a route that opens a brand-new evolutionary possibility is not legally recognized, later generations dare not walk it; if it is deified into unquestionable oracle, it slides into personality cult. What the charter must do is both recognize its historical contribution, and place it in a legal position equal to all routes.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Key Figures and Organizations (Document 95) and the republican axiom of Myriad Races. Its jurisprudence is: the Lin Hao route's value does not lie in the name "Lin Hao," but in its verifying an evolutionary possibility previously negated by old systems — without predation and without predatory soul rings, one may also reach extremely high realms. Writing it into the charter is institutionally protecting this "possibility" itself, not deifying some person. The law-first axiom requires: personal routes may be recorded and referenced, but may not become personal will above the laws.

## II. Normative Content: Threefold Positioning of the Lin Hao Route

Article 21 positions the Lin Hao route clearly:

1. **Historical status**: as a pioneering route verifying anti-predation feasibility and proving symbiosis may reach the sovereign, recorded in civilizational history;
2. **Legal status**: as one of Myriad Ranks' selectable evolutionary paths, equal with the 300 systems and the D route, not higher;
3. **Open status**: the Lin Hao route's experience, insights, and technology are fully returned to the Alaya Network, open to Myriad Ranks, no private secret transmission;
4. **Non-oracle status**: the Lin Hao route's specific insights may be referenced, but no individual need, nor may be forced to, imitate it; it is one path, not the only correct answer.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all cultivators studying, drawing on, or walking the Lin Hao route. It protects later generations' free reference, and also prevents using the name "Lin Hao inheritor" to build sects, establish cults, or collect offerings. This article especially binds Lin Hao himself and his successors: they gain no privilege because of pioneering the route, and are still bound by all clauses including four-dimensional assessment, perpetual-construction duty, and contribution measurement.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Deifying the Lin Hao route into the only correct answer, using the name "Lin Hao inheritor" for personality cult and interest groups, sealing the route's technology from openness, or belittling other routes as "unorthodox," all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: sect-like organizations are disbanded, their sealed technology forcibly opened; those using personality cult to accumulate power are pursued under undermining Myriad-Rank-republican order. The Lin Hao route being derogated or denounced as heresy is likewise corrected — its legal status is charter-protected.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: whether the Lin Hao route's technical materials are truly fully open, and whether named sects appear, are network-monitored throughout; the Anchor Supervisory Committee monitors whether anyone uses the name "Lin Hao" to hollow out the charter, placing an individual above the laws — precisely what the law-first axiom requires.

## VI. A Road Honored But Not Deified

Article 21's attitude toward the Lin Hao route is Heng Yuan Zhou's mature sample of treating heroes. The no-ring symbiotic route Lin Hao pioneered is recorded in history, open to Myriad Ranks, for later generations' reference; but it is not deified into the only correct answer; no one must imitate, much less use Lin Hao's name to establish cults and collect offerings. Lin Hao himself likewise accepts four-dimensional assessment and bears the perpetual-construction duty, no privilege because of being the pathbreaker. This both-respect-and-restraint attitude ensures civilization will remember heroes, yet not be kidnapped by them. Heroes are signposts, not shackles; the road is open; whether to walk and how to walk, Myriad Ranks choose themselves.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: a verified-feasible route if privately hoarded deprives Myriad Ranks of one more expressway; opening it is the greatest saving
- **Anti-predation**: the Lin Hao route itself is living evidence of "non-predation may also reach the sovereign"; constitutionalizing it institutionalizes anti-predation's persuasiveness
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type protagonist routes are deified as the destined unique, others may not learn; Heng Yuan Zhou's Lin Hao route is one equal path, Myriad Ranks may reference or not learn
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the route is open to Myriad Ranks, not Lin Hao's exclusive privilege
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: personal original routes, once charter-recognized, merge into the open system; civilization's roads grow more and more

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

The Lin Hao route's constitutional status is Heng Yuan Zhou's model for handling the "hero and institution" relationship. It respects the pathbreaker, yet does not deify him; it acknowledges one road's value, yet insists all roads are equal before the law. Heroes grow old; institutions endure — this is precisely what final finalization must do.
