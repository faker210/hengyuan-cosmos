# Entry 923 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 22 | The Youth-Supreme Cultivation Mechanism

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 10, 37, 17

## Introduction: Youth Supremes, both quick and questing

Youth Supremes — those who display extraordinary cultivation talent while young — are civilization's most precious yet most carefully treated seeds. Article 22 stipulates the Youth-Supreme cultivation mechanism: not the old age's "genius = predation machine" road, but the "quick + quest" dual route in parallel. On one hand use Multiplication Fruit and mentor network to accelerate growth; on the other hand in the Quest Sediment Domain retain their hardship tempering, avoiding a genius rocketing up yet with empty heart-nature and growing into a high-risk individual.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the 300 Origin Cultivation Systems Overview (Document 10)'s "Youth-Supreme-exclusive dual route" design, the Symbiotic Education System (Document 37), and the Plane-Sovereign Strong-One Standards (Document 17)'s heart-nature dimension. Its jurisprudence is: talent is talent, not a get-out-of-jail-free card. If a Youth Supreme only quickens without tempering, his heart-nature dimension (25 of the four-dimensional assessment) will necessarily fail, eventually growing into a high-risk individual with great force yet unreliable. The dual route is precisely to let genius be both fast and upright.

## II. Normative Content: How the Dual Route Runs

Article 22 breaks Youth-Supreme cultivation into dual tracks:

1. **Quick line**: enjoy Multiplication-Fruit-technologized hundredfold deduction amplification, top-mentor matching, targeted resource allocation;
2. **Quest line**: in the Quest Sediment Domain retain the hardship-growth branch, experiencing real predicaments, failures, and responsibility; heart-nature and responsibility are simultaneously tempered;
3. **Synchronized assessment**: quick progress and quest tempering are assessed by proportion, neither dispensable; those only quickening without questing are not granted high certification;
4. **Anti-predation lock**: Youth Supremes from initiation receive anti-predation ethics education; no matter how high the talent, they may not predate for quick results.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all youths identified with Supreme talent, across the human race and the five lineages. This article especially protects Youth Supremes from old forces "picking the peaches" — no one may dig a Youth Supreme out of the cultivation mechanism to cultivate their own thug and force them onto a predatory road. The Youth Supreme himself retains an exit mechanism; talent is no reason for compulsion.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Cultivating Youth Supremes as predation tools, opening a "heart-nature inspection-exempt" back door for them, poaching Youth Supremes for private forces, using talent theory to force them to bear tasks against their will, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: relevant cultivation relationships are voided, the Youth Supreme returns to the universal cultivation mechanism; those using Supreme talent as leverage for personal control are pursued under spiritual enslavement (Article 1).

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: the Youth Supreme's quick and quest dual-track progress is synchronously recorded, and the heart-nature dimension is included in four-dimensional pre-assessment; the Anchor Supervisory Committee monitors whether the Youth Supreme's cultivation environment is healthy, discovering exploitation or control immediately intervenes to protect.

## VI. A Genius Well Protected

Article 22's most precious picture is a genius well protected. A youth awakening Supreme aptitude young, both enjoys Multiplication Fruit and top-mentor acceleration, and is placed in the Quest Sediment Domain to experience real failure, responsibility, and predicament. He is not dried up in haste as a predation tool because of extraordinary talent, nor poached by old forces as a private soldier. The dual route lets his force and heart-nature grow together; by adulthood, both fast and upright. The Youth-Supreme mechanism proves: talent is worth accelerating, but more worth protecting — a genius well protected will grow up to protect Myriad Ranks in turn.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: a genius wasted on a predatory road is civilization's greatest loss; the dual route lets his talent truly serve symbiosis
- **Anti-predation**: from the cultivation source, puts an end to the old logic of "genius = predator seedling"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type protagonists rise young and are swept by fate into predation; Heng Yuan Zhou's Youth Supremes dual route, heart-nature and force grow in step
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Supreme-talent identification covers Myriad Ranks, not only human youths
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cultivation technology and experience are network-wide shared, every clan may identify and cultivate its own Supreme

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

The Youth-Supreme cultivation mechanism is Heng Yuan Zhou's most responsible attitude toward "genius." It acknowledges genius, accelerates genius, yet does not indulge genius; it makes talent Myriad Ranks' wealth, not one person's or one group's private soldiers. When the youth are strong, Myriad Ranks are strong.
