# Entry 924 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 23 | Protection of Myriad-Rank Culture

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 52, 05, 35

## Introduction: symbiosis is not assimilation

Myriad-Rank republicanism pursues common evolution, but common evolution is not melting Myriad Ranks into one shape. Article 23 stipulates protection of Myriad-Rank culture: every clan's and plane's unique culture, beliefs, lifestyles, and aesthetic traditions are all charter-protected; forced assimilation under the names of "advanced," "unified," or "efficiency" is prohibited. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization wants Myriad Ranks' symbiosis and common prosperity, not Myriad Ranks' uniformity. Diversity itself is civilization's wealth.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Alien-Race Classification and Symbiosis (Document 52), the Myriad-Ranks Co-Advancement Basic Program (Document 05), and the "autonomy-and-co-governance-combined principle" (Document 03). Its jurisprudence is: the republican axiom of Myriad Races includes not only equal evolutionary right, but also the equal right to preserve one's own culture. Forced assimilation is just like predation — both are the strong imposing their own standards on others. Cultural diversity also provides civilization with more evolutionary possibilities; a homogenized civilization's risk-resistance is far below a plural civilization's.

## II. Normative Content: Four Bottom Lines of Cultural Protection

Article 23 draws cultural protection's boundaries:

1. **Autonomy right**: each clan, without violating the charter, enjoys autonomy over internal culture, institutions, and customs;
2. **No forced assimilation**: prohibited to require alien clans to abandon their culture or adopt other clans' lifestyles on any grounds;
3. **Record and transmission**: each clan's cultural classics, crafts, and traditions are archived and protected by the Alaya Network, preventing loss;
4. **Free cross-boundary exchange**: clans may voluntarily exchange, draw on, and merge culture, but merging must be voluntary and may not be imposed.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all clans and planes joining Myriad-Rank republicanism. It especially protects weak, young, small-population clans — these clans' cultures are most easily diluted by strong civilizations. This article does not protect bad customs that "in the name of culture actually predate": if some clan's customs include human sacrifice, enslavement, or predatory cultivation, that bad custom is not culturally protected and is handled under Article 1.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Forced assimilation, banning alien languages and customs, using cultural-superiority theory to marginalize weak clans, destroying alien cultural classics, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: relevant policies are revoked, damaged cultural records are restored through Alaya archives; those under the name of "cultural purification" actually running clan oppression are pursued heavily under anti-predation and anti-discrimination.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by the cultural archive library within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: each clan's cultural classics are fully archived, and loss risk automatically alerts; the Anchor Supervisory Committee monitors each clan's cultural survival state, discovering some clan's culture systematically suppressed immediately intervenes; each clan retains the right to sue the Symbiotic Court for cultural assimilation.

## VI. An Odd Flower in a Hundred-Flower Garden

Article 23 makes Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad-Rank symbiosis like a hundred-flower garden, not a uniform lawn. A clan from a cold plane preserves its unique culture of ice-carved narratives; a consciousness-information clan continues its poems transmitted only in dreams. No one forces assimilation because they differ from the mainstream; their classics are archived by the Alaya Network, and loss risk automatically alerts. Civilization thus gains countless ways of seeing the world, and risk-resistance also strengthens. True confidence never fears others being different; symbiosis's highest realm is precisely letting Myriad Ranks stand side by side, yet each beautiful, each appreciating the other.

## VII. Axiom 6's Projection in Cultural Protection

The universal equal infinite law adds another bottom line to cultural protection: regardless of whether a clan's culture flourishes or declines, the basic infinite power inherent in each of its members does not increase or decrease because of it. No matter how niche or obscure a culture, its bearers still retain floor infinity, and are not judged eliminable because of a small clan. Cultural protection is thus not only an aesthetic choice, but also a solemn recognition of every ordinary being's inherent infinity.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: wiping out one culture wipes out one unique way of seeing the world; this article preserves this cognitive diversity
- **Anti-predation**: cultural assimilation is a form of spiritual predation; this article positively prohibits it
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type civilizations take the strong's culture as the only standard; Heng Yuan Zhou protects Myriad-Rank cultural diversity, each enjoying its own beauty
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: from large clans to small clans, culture is uniformly protected
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: every new civilization accepted has its culture brought under protection; the civilizational hundred-flower garden blooms ever fuller

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Protection of Myriad-Rank culture is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's maturity marker. A truly powerful civilization does not fear others being different, but takes pride in others being different. Symbiosis's highest realm is Myriad Ranks standing side by side, yet each beautiful.
