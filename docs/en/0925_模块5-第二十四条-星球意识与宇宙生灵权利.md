# Entry 925 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 24 | Rights of Planet Consciousness and Cosmic Beings

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 53, 54, 50

## Introduction: planets are also right-bearing "persons"

In the predatory era, a planet was only a resource mine, only a battlefield, only an object to be shattered and mined. Article 23 treats culture as a right; Article 24 goes a step further: planet consciousness and cosmic beings themselves are right subjects. A planet that has conceived consciousness, a cosmic-origin body coagulated with spirit, is not an ownerless vein, but a life with perception and a right to life. Mining it is not digging resources, but harming a person.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Strange-Life Identification Standards (Document 53), the Three Categories of Strange-Life Handling Process (Document 54), and the consciousness-information clan and mixed-mutant clan classifications in the Myriad-Rank Lineage Overview (Document 50). Its jurisprudence is: the republican axiom of Myriad Races' "all beings" is not limited to human form or carbon-based. Whatever has perception, self, and a will to survive, whether its form is an individual, clan, planet, or cosmic-origin body, is within equal evolutionary-right coverage. Treating planets as objects is the predatory cosmology's last remnant.

## II. Normative Content: Three Rights of Planets and Cosmic Beings

Article 24 concretizes this right-subject status:

1. **Right to life**: planet consciousness and cosmic beings are not harmed by unrestricted mining, improper plane warfare, or plane-membrane tearing;
2. **Right to information**: before developing their planet/region, they must inform the planet consciousness of development content and hear its will;
3. **Right to participation**: major decisions involving the planet's overall fate, the planet consciousness participates in deliberation as a right subject.

Planet consciousness also bears corresponding duties — its stable output is part of Myriad Ranks' symbiotic cycle; rights and duties are reciprocal.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all planets, cosmic beings, and origin bodies identified as possessing consciousness. For celestial bodies whose consciousness is not yet confirmed, first assess per strange-life identification standards; do not pre-assume they are "just rock." This article especially forbids treating consciousness-bearing planets as "resource draining" (one of six predatory forms) mines.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Harming planet consciousness, forcibly mining without informing, tearing plane membranes, enslaving cosmic beings as tools, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: development is immediately suspended, and the damaged planet consciousness is ecologically restored; those causing planet-consciousness death or plane collapse are pursued heavily under resource draining and spiritual enslavement (Article 1).

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by the strange-life identification and archive system within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: all planet consciousnesses are archived and registered, and their health is continuously monitored; before any major development action, the system automatically verifies whether the information and deliberation procedure has been fulfilled; the Anchor Supervisory Committee liaises with planet-consciousness representatives, accepting their right claims.

## VI. A Mining That Greets First

Article 24 changed how humans relate to planets. When a civilization prepares to develop resources on a planet, they no longer treat it as a silent mine, but first inform the planet's consciousness of the plan, hear its will, deliberate boundaries. The planet consciousness has the right to say no, or demand restoration compensation. This habit turns mining from harm into dialogue. A respected planet will more stably output origin to the network; a drained planet will only crack into silence. Treating planets as right subjects ultimately protects not only the planet, but also the very foundation civilization itself lives on.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: harming a spiritual planet equals destroying a huge symbiotic node; this article puts an end to this ruinous waste
- **Anti-predation**: restoring planets from "mine" to "life" is anti-predation's extension at the ecological layer
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems shatter planets and crush planes at will; Heng Yuan Zhou planet consciousness has rights, development requires deliberation
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: this article expands right subjects from human-shaped beings to planets and cosmic beings, fuller coverage
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly discovered cosmic beings automatically enter right-subject assessment

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

The rights of planet consciousness and cosmic beings are the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's final expansion of the "life" concept. When even a planet is respected as a right subject, Myriad-Rank republicanism truly lives up to the words "Myriad Ranks" — it is not ten thousand human races, but all spiritual beings in the cosmos.
