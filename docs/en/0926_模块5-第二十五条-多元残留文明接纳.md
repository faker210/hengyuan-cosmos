# Entry 926 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 25 | Acceptance of Multiverse Residual Civilizations

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 54, 55, 56

## Introduction: survivors at the cosmos's end also deserve a road

Heng Yuan Zhou is not the only cosmos. In the hyper-multiverse view, there exist residual civilizations and surviving individuals from other universes, other cycles, other shattered planes. Article 25 stipulates acceptance of multiverse residual civilizations: these survivors at the cosmos's end are not refused because they come "from outside" or carry old-cosmos causal residues. Myriad-Rank republicanism's gate is open to all suffering beings — this is "99% full coverage"'s final extension.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Three Categories of Strange-Life Handling Process (Document 54), the Anomalous-Energy Classification and Traits (Document 55), and the Three Categories of Anomalous-Energy Handling Process (Document 56). Its jurisprudence is the thoroughness of the republican axiom of Myriad Races: the equal evolutionary right is not discounted because of "which cosmos one comes from." A soul surviving an old-cosmos destruction is as qualified as a newborn in this cosmos to step onto the evolutionary road. Refusing acceptance equals admitting "outside the cosmos there are no Myriad Ranks," which contradicts hyper-multiverse full openness's direction.

## II. Normative Content: Three-Stage Acceptance Process

Article 25 regularizes the acceptance process:

1. **Identification**: per strange-life and anomalous-energy standards, identify the residual civilization's nature, causal residues, and danger level;
2. **Purification and adaptation**: harmlessly purify old-cosmos causal residues they carry, and match them to a suitable cultivation system;
3. **Acceptance and integration**: through the every-road-available guarantee system arrange an entry path for them, bring them into Myriad-Rank republicanism with equal rights.

Acceptance is not unconditional: if the residual civilization itself is predatory and arrives bringing destruction, first isolate and transform; if transformation fails, then handle per Article 1.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all residual civilizations and individuals arriving from other planes, other universes, shattered cycles. It especially tilts toward trauma-bearing survivors: providing restorative targeted support, rather than vigilantly rejecting on grounds of "unknown origin." This article also binds internally — no clan may discriminate against residual-civilization individuals as "outsiders."

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Refusing basic guarantee to residual life on grounds of "unknown origin," directly annihilating residual civilizations as enemies, using their unfamiliarity with rules to actually predate, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: refusal is corrected, residual individuals receive bottom-line guarantee; those attacking residual civilizations without identification and purification procedures are pursued under violating causal self-discipline and anti-predation.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: residual civilizations' identification, purification, and acceptance are fully archived throughout; post-acceptance integration is tracked, discovering discriminatory treatment immediately intervenes; the Anchor Supervisory Committee liaises with residual-civilization representatives, guaranteeing their lawful rights.

## VI. A Ticket to the End of the Cosmos

Article 25's warmest picture is a ticket handed to the cosmos's end. A clan surviving shattered cycles in an old-cosmos destruction arrives in Heng Yuan Zhou, carrying wounds and old-cosmos knowledge. It will not be vigilantly rejected as an outsider, but first identified, its causal residues purified, a suitable system matched, then through every-road-available guarantee connected to civilization. The old-cosmos lessons it brings become the Alaya Network's precious cognitive increment. Heng Yuan Zhou thus opens its door to all wandering survivors — come in, there is a road here. This breadth is precisely why Myriad-Rank republicanism deserves the words "Myriad Ranks."

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: a civilization surviving destruction carries old-cosmos precious knowledge and lessons; accepting it is accepting cognitive increment
- **Anti-predation**: refusing survivors and annihilating all outsiders is cosmos-scale predation; this article corrects it
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems annihilate outsiders not of our clan; Heng Yuan Zhou first identifies, purifies, accepts, giving survivors a road
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: this article extends "Myriad Ranks" to residual civilizations outside the cosmos, extreme coverage
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the acceptance mechanism extends infinitely with hyper-multiverse exploration; the door never closes

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Acceptance of multiverse residual civilizations is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's ultimate proof of breadth. It does not draw the cosmos as a closed circle, but says to all suffering, surviving, wandering beings: come in, there is a road here. Myriad-Rank republicanism thus deserves the words "Myriad Ranks."
