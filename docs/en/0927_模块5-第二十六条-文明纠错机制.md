# Entry 927 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 26 | The Civilizational Error-Correction Mechanism

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 27, 97, 25

## Introduction: civilization must know how to advance, and even more how to turn back

Final finalization does not mean forever correct. No matter how perfect an institution, running long enough will drift; no matter how advanced a direction, in execution it will go off. Article 26 stipulates the civilizational error-correction mechanism: the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization must have a normalized mechanism to discover errors, admit errors, and correct errors, rather than wait until terminally ill to reform passively. A civilization that can correct errors is the one that truly lasts.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Anchor Calibration and Maintenance (Document 27), the Setting-Contradiction Self-Check Table (Document 97), and the Symbiotic Feedback Cycle Mechanism (Document 25). Its jurisprudence is the law-first axiom's extension: law stability does not mean never correcting — when actual operation deviates from law expectations, deviation discovery and calibration must be allowed. Error correction is not overthrowing the charter, but keeping the charter vital in operation. This does not contradict "never retconning": retconning is chaotic change for plot convenience; error correction is calibrating deviation per procedure.

## II. Normative Content: Four Links of the Error-Correction Mechanism

Article 26 closes the error-correction loop:

1. **Monitoring**: the Alaya Network continuously compares actual operation with charter expectations, automatically alerts on discovered deviation;
2. **Identification**: the Anchor Supervisory Committee judges the deviation's nature — execution drift, institutional loophole, or environmental change;
3. **Correction**: execution drift orders the executing institution to rectify; institutional loophole starts legislative patching; environmental change submits to the charter-amendment procedure;
4. **Publicization**: correction results are network-wide publicized for Myriad Ranks' review.

Four links ensure error correction is both timely and not overstepping.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all links of civilizational operation: cultivation practice, distribution systems, cross-plane operations, strong-one assessment, cultural policy. Error correction targets institutions not individuals — discovering some executing institution long drifting, what is corrected is the mechanism, not just swapping one person. This article also allows ordinary individuals and clans to submit error-correction suggestions; everyone is an eye of error correction.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Discovering deviation yet concealing and not reporting, labeling error-correction suggestions as "undermining stability," using administrative power to suppress error-correction voices, or after correction surreptitiously regressing, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: concealment and suppression are pursued heavily, relevant deviations are ordered corrected within a time limit; those repeating after correction hold the institution head responsible. This article allows no "stick with the error" excuse.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by the deviation-monitoring system within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: the gap between expectation and reality is continuously quantified, deviation over threshold triggers; the Anchor Supervisory Committee sets up a dedicated error-correction acceptance post; the Myriad-Rank-republican council periodically reviews error-correction reports, ensuring the error-correction mechanism itself is not hollowed out.

## VI. A Deviation Publicly Admitted

Article 26's rarest moment is a deviation publicly admitted. After some new policy ran for several generations, the Alaya Network discovered it had unexpectedly squeezed a marginal group. The Anchor Supervisory Committee did not cover up, but publicly quantified the deviation, admitted the judgment error, ordered rectification, and reported results to all civilization. This public apology not only did not damage authority, but made Myriad Ranks trust this institution even more — it even dares to put its own errors on the table. A civilization that can correct errors is more trustworthy than one that never errs, because the former knows it will change, while the latter only covers errors bigger and bigger.

## VII. Axiom 6's Projection in the Error-Correction Mechanism

The universal equal infinite law requires error correction especially bottom-guarantee: when deviation appears, those most impacted are often low-contributors such as easygoing layabouts. The error-correction mechanism must first confirm that these floor-infinity-retaining individuals have not been institutionally squeezed out of the cycle. What is corrected is deviation; what is guarded is every being's innate basic infinity; error correction itself must never become secondary harm to the weak.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: small errors uncorrected drag into catastrophe; timely error correction kills massive rework in the bud
- **Anti-predation**: predatory resurgence often appears quietly as "execution drift"; the error-correction mechanism specifically watches for this
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems after setting collapse brazenly patch (retcon); Heng Yuan Zhou per procedure admits and calibrates deviation
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: every clan may submit error-correction suggestions; error correction is not a top-level privilege
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the error-correction mechanism continuously calibrates as civilization expands, ensuring no drift toward the hyper-multiverse

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

The civilizational error-correction mechanism is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's soberest understanding of the word "perfect." It does not pretend it never errs, but builds an institution that lets errors hide nowhere and be corrected anytime. It can advance, and can turn back; dares to finalize, and dares to calibrate — this is the civilization that can reach the ultimate closed loop.
