# Entry 928 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 27 | Historical-Reversal Prevention

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 71, 72, 89

## Introduction: the greatest fear is crawling out only to crawl back

The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization spent the entire long span from the Pre-Anchor Era to the Anchor-Awakening Era crawling out of the predatory-system mire. Article 27 stipulates historical-reversal prevention: a permanent mechanism must be built to prevent civilization from quietly regressing to predation, monopoly, and might-makes-right at some future moment. Civilization may progress, and may also regress; history is not a one-way street. Without prevention, the higher it climbs the harder it falls.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Pre-Anchor Era (Document 71), the Anchor-Awakening Era (Document 72), and the Time-Paradox Prevention Mechanism (Document 89). Its jurisprudence is the causal self-discipline axiom's fourth mission — thoroughly eradicating civilizational regression and historical reversal. The Pre-Anchor Era's retrospective proves: predatory systems are not one-time errors, but temptations that repeatedly restore. Enlightened predators and justified rascals constantly package old logic with "special circumstances," "efficiency first," and "inevitable." Preventing historical reversal is precisely permanently fortifying against this restoration impulse.

## II. Normative Content: Three Gates of Prevention

Article 27 breaks the prevention mechanism into three gates:

1. **Thought gate**: symbiotic education makes the historical lesson that "predation necessarily regresses" a required course, making Myriad Ranks cognitively reject old-logic resurgence;
2. **Institution gate**: charter Article 1 enshrines anti-predation, so any reversal from its start constitutes unconstitutionality;
3. **Monitoring gate**: the Alaya Network monitors whether monopoly, inequality, and one-way upstream appear in system operation as reversal indicators; once they surface, immediately correct.

Three gates lock the reversal road from cognition, law, and monitoring simultaneously.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all stages of civilizational evolution. It especially watches for "old predation packaged in new form": old sect monopoly becomes today's "technology patent wall"; old plane colonization becomes today's "cross-plane resource cooperation." As long as the essence is predation, no matter how new the form, it is treated as reversal. This article applies equally to all institutions, all strong ones, and all new ideologies.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Pushing civilizational policy toward predation, hollowing out anti-predation clauses on grounds of "efficiency" or "special circumstances," beautifying the Pre-Anchor Era's predatory system, providing theoretical packaging for historical reversal, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: relevant policies are immediately revoked, and promoters are pursued under undermining civilizational order; thought-level beautifying speech is corrected through education and public debate, not by suppressing discussion.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by the reversal-indicator monitoring within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: monopoly degree, inequality degree, one-way-upstream degree, Dharma-door opening rate and other key indicators are continuously quantified, worsening trends automatically alert; the Anchor Supervisory Committee periodically conducts "historical-reversal risk assessment" and reports to the Myriad-Rank-republican council; the Quest Domain simulation retrospective (Module 2) continuously reenacts old-system collapse, providing real-world teaching material for prevention.

## VI. An Alarm Behind the Three Gates

Article 26 in practice is an alarm with three gates simultaneously alert. When some new formulation, under the name of efficiency first, tries to re-limit Dharma-door opening scope, the thought gate immediately brings up Pre-Anchor Era monopoly-led-collapse historical teaching material; the institution gate points out this has touched Article 1; the monitoring gate simultaneously discovers that group's opening-rate indicator is declining. Three gates jointly snuff this reversal seedling in the bud. Historical reversal never happens overnight; it always quietly tests in a new coat. Precisely because of these three permanently taut gates, civilization, before every test, may calmly say: this road we have walked; it does not go.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: once civilization reverses, all prior evolutionary achievements go to waste; preventing reversal is the greatest energy protection
- **Anti-predation**: this article is the anti-predation axiom's institutional landing at the "restoration prevention" dimension
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems cycle back and forth, dynasties rise and fall, strong and weak swap, yet might makes right throughout; Heng Yuan Zhou permanently eliminates this cycle with three gates
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: reversal's victims are always Myriad Ranks' weak; this article protects the broadest beings
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the prevention mechanism ensures civilization never regresses while infinitely expanding toward the hyper-multiverse

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Historical-reversal prevention is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's permanent vigilance about "who we are." It reminds Myriad Ranks: progress is not automatic, happiness is not eternal; one lapse and you slip back into that man-eating old cosmos. With three gates standing here, civilization may confidently keep walking forward.
