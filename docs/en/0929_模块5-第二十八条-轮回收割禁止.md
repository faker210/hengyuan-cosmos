# Entry 929 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 28 | Prohibition of Cyclical Harvesting

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 82, 90

## Introduction: reincarnation is no one's cropland

Predatory systems' most hidden yet deepest evil is turning reincarnation into a harvesting ground: the strong manipulate reincarnation, letting beings cultivate and grow life after life, then at some node harvest in one sweep the origin, souls, and fortune accumulated across lives. Beings think they are cultivating in reincarnation, but are actually farming for someone else. Article 28 declares cyclical harvesting illegal — reincarnation is beings' right to natural soul circulation, not any strong one's private farm.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Anti-Predationism Core Thesis (Document 04)'s "fortune predation" and "spiritual enslavement" two forms, and the Time Retrogression Basic Principles (Document 82). Its jurisprudence is one of the causal self-discipline axiom's ultimate missions: eradicating cyclical harvesting. Cyclical harvesting is the deepest predation because it crosses life and death, manipulates causality, and treats beings across countless reincarnations as one-time raw material. Heng Yuan Zhou acknowledges reincarnation's objective existence, yet firmly denies that anyone enjoys a harvesting right over reincarnation.

## II. Normative Content: Specific Boundaries of Harvesting Prohibition

Article 28 draws the red line in the reincarnation domain:

1. **Harvesting prohibited**: no person may manipulate the reincarnation mechanism to intercept beings' origin, soul, and fortune accumulated in reincarnation for own use;
2. **Reincarnation farm prohibited**: no designing "raise a life, harvest a life" cultivation scams, inducing beings to be planted unknowingly;
3. **Memory tampering prohibited**: no tampering beings' reincarnation memory for harvesting purposes, making them unknowing nourishment;
4. **Natural circulation protected**: reincarnation as a natural soul mechanism operates free from any strong one's private-interest manipulation;
5. **Knowledge exception**: only research into reincarnation laws themselves (knowledge) is allowed; harvesting technology exploiting this is strictly forbidden.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all individuals and institutions touching reincarnation, soul, and life-death mechanisms, especially watching sovereign strong ones who command time and soul laws. The more capable an entity is of manipulating reincarnation, the more strictly this article binds them — because they have both the ability and the motive to harvest. This article also binds hypocrites running harvesting under the banners of "salvation" or "deliverance."

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Manipulating reincarnation harvesting, designing reincarnation farms, tampering reincarnation memory, cultivating with souls as raw material, all constitute some of this article's most serious unconstitutionality. Consequences: the relevant reincarnation mechanism is forcibly reset, harvested souls and origin are returned as much as possible; perpetrators are handled under anti-predation crime's top-tier clauses — because they cross life and death and harm beings across countless reincarnations, the harm far exceeds ordinary predation. This article allows no "for the greater good" exception.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by the reincarnation-monitoring subsystem within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: reincarnation flow, soul direction, and anomalous interception points are monitored throughout; any invocation of the reincarnation mechanism is strictly reported (see cross-plane operation reporting system); the Anchor Supervisory Committee and Time-Law Committee jointly inspect reincarnation anomalies, and once harvesting traces are found, trace them network-wide.

## VI. A River No One May Ever Touch

Article 28 guards a river no one may touch. The river of reincarnation flows quietly, countless souls birth and perish within it, naturally circulating. In the old cosmos, some strong ones once spread nets in the river, treating souls cultivated and grown life after life as autumn-harvest crops, reaped on schedule. Heng Yuan Zhou marks this river as an absolute no-go zone: any anomalous interception of reincarnation flow is network-traced; any harvesting hypocrisy under the banner of deliverance and salvation is thoroughly exposed. This river belongs to every soul in it, not any strong one standing on the bank. Guarding this river is guarding every life's dignity in every life.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: cyclical harvesting drains countless lives' effort in one sweep, the cosmos-scale's most horrifying energy waste; this article eradicates it
- **Anti-predation**: this article is the anti-predation axiom's final blockade at the life-death-reincarnation dimension
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in Tang-Luo-type systems reincarnation and transmigration are often used by strong ones as pieces and nourishment; in Heng Yuan Zhou reincarnation is beings' right, forbidding anyone to harvest
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: what is protected is every soul in reincarnation, regardless of clan, regardless of life or death
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: reincarnation laws may be studied and knowledge-shared, but harvesting technology is never open

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Prohibition of cyclical harvesting is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's final iron gate guarding beings' souls. It tells all existences in the cosmos: reincarnation may be studied, knowledge may be shared, but beings' souls, no one may think of harvesting as crops. This held, civilization truly takes responsibility for every life in every life.
