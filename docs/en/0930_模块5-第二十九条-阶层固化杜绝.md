# Entry 930 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 29 | Elimination of Class Solidification

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 36, 47, 08

## Introduction: the greatest fear is "dragons beget dragons, phoenixes beget phoenixes"

Even after anti-predation and Dharma-door opening, civilization may still degenerate into another kind of shackle: class solidification. The strong's descendants are always strong; the bottom's descendants never turn over; the evolutionary channel nominally open is actually blocked layer by layer by family, lineage, and resources. Article 29 stipulates elimination of class solidification: the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization must maintain an eternally upward flowing channel, and never allow a rigid caste-like class to form.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Social Structure and Class Mobility (Document 36), the Anti-Monopoly Law (Document 47), and the Every-Road-Available Guarantee System (Document 08). Its jurisprudence is the combined force of the republican axiom of Myriad Races and the anti-predation axiom: Dharma-door monopoly inevitably leads to class solidification, and class solidification is essentially structural predation of bottom beings' evolutionary right. More-work-more-pay allows differences, but does not allow differences to be frozen by family and passed down generation after generation. A civilization no longer flowing is a civilization chronically regressing.

## II. Normative Content: Four Hard Measures Against Solidification

Article 29 concretizes anti-solidification measures:

1. **Admission looks not at birth**: the evolutionary channel recognizes only effort and assessment, not family, lineage, or sect background;
2. **Resources are not hereditary**: cultivation resources, anchor permissions, and civilizational status are dynamically allocated by contribution, not hereditarily transmitted;
3. **Upward channel open**: the every-road-available guarantee system continuously bridges for the bottom, with targeted support tilting toward low-endowment and impoverished individuals;
4. **Downward responsibility**: those in high strata who long contribute nothing have their resources and status dynamically downgraded per more-work-more-pay; no lifetime aristocrats.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all clans and all strata. It especially watches for "strong-one familialization" — strong ones transmitting resources, Dharma doors, and status to descendants, forming new aristocratic clans. This article also protects the bottom: no one is defaulted to "such is the ceiling" because of humble birth. Youth-Supreme cultivation, the D route, and the Lin Hao route are all open to all births, with no gate of family.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Running aristocratic heredity, setting evolutionary-channel limits by birth, suppressing the bottom's ceiling on family grounds, privately granting public resources to one's own children, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: hereditary privileges are abolished, relevant resources return to the public cycle; those artificially creating class barriers are pursued under Dharma-door monopoly and class predation. This article recognizes no "aristocratic family" special status in the cultivation system.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by the class-mobility ledger within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: each clan's and group's bottom-upward flow rate and resource allocation's birth-correlation are continuously quantified; flow rate long stagnant triggers intensified targeted support; the Anchor Supervisory Committee monitors for new-aristocrat seedlings, discovering early and correcting early.

## VI. An Upward Channel That Never Caps

Article 29 guards an upward channel that never caps. In Heng Yuan Zhou, a life born at the very bottom is not defaulted to a fixed ceiling because of family; a strong one standing at the top, if long contributing nothing, also has resources and status dynamically downgraded per more-work-more-pay. Aristocratic clans are not hereditary, resources are not privately transmitted, Dharma doors look not at birth. This channel's existence lets every bottom believe effort works, and lets every top not dare slacken. Civilization is thus forever young — as long as the bottom forever has a road and the top never entrenches, this civilization will not one day suddenly discover it has quietly grown old and quietly hardened.

## VII. Axiom 6's Projection in Anti-Solidification

The universal equal infinite law is anti-solidification's deepest basis: since every being is innately endowed with basic infinite power, any institution permanently locking some people at the bottom is deprivation of this established right. The reason classes must flow is that no one deserves elimination, and no one innately deserves height — basic infinity belongs to all; differences lie only in how much is superimposed, not whether there is any.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: solidification locks nine-tenths of potential at the bottom; this article releases these frozen evolutionary potentials
- **Anti-predation**: class solidification is structural predation; this article eradicates it at the social-structure level
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems birth determines ceiling, sect is class; Heng Yuan Zhou admission looks not at birth, the upward channel is eternally open
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the flow channel is open to all Myriad Ranks' bottom
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly joined civilizations' bottom individuals equally enjoy the upward-mobility right

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

Elimination of class solidification is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's safety valve for maintaining vitality. As long as a civilization's bottom forever has a road and its top is never hereditary, it is forever young and forever upward. Solidification is the start of civilization's aging; anti-solidification is civilization's elixir of immortality.
