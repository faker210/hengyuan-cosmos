# Entry 931 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 30 | Charter-Amendment Procedure

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 03, 40, 48

## Introduction: how the fundamental law is amended without losing its fundament

Final finalization does not mean never amending. If the charter becomes dead text carved in stone, it will be bypassed alive by reality; if the charter is easily amended, it degenerates into soft clay the strong shape at will. Article 30 stipulates the charter-amendment procedure: amendments must be made per legally prescribed procedure; amendments not per procedure are all void. The procedure itself is the valve between the charter's stability and openness.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Symbiotic Civilization Charter (Document 03)'s amendment clauses and the Symbiotic-Constitution Outline (Document 40). Its jurisprudence is the law-first axiom: amendment is the law's normal evolution, not betrayal of the law. The key is strictly distinguishing "amending per procedure" from "amending at will" — the former is error correction and expansion (lawful), the latter is retconning and hollowing (unlawful). The procedure's rigor precisely protects the charter from being lightly tampered with by vested interests.

## II. Normative Content: Four-Step Legal Amendment Procedure

Article 30 quantifies the amendment procedure:

1. **Proposal**: must be jointly proposed by one-fifth or more of the Myriad-Rank-republican councilors, or by the Anchor Council with a specified majority;
2. **Deliberation**: must pass with a two-thirds or larger majority in the council to be initially formed;
3. **Law verification**: submitted to the cosmic-origin anchor for verification, confirming the amendment does not violate the six top-level axioms and symbiotic laws;
4. **Effect**: takes effect only after verification passes, and is network-wide publicized.

Four steps are indispensable; any "amendment" skipping any step is void ab initio.

## III. Scope

This article applies to the amendment, supplementation, or repeal of any charter clause. Amendment may not touch the six top-level axioms themselves — the axioms are the charter's spiritual origin, interpretable but not overthrown; what may be amended is the axioms' implementation clauses in concrete situations. This article also binds all acts that "in the name of interpretation actually amend": major interpretation is treated as amendment and must follow procedure.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Declaring "amendment" without following the legal procedure, bypassing law verification, hollowing out charter clauses with administrative orders, or quietly changing major interpretations as minor ones, all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: the so-called "amendment" is void ab initio, and existing clauses prevail; serious cases intended to hollow out the charter are pursued as undermining the fundamental law. This article ensures the charter's "supreme effect" (next article) is not eroded by procedural loopholes.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by the legislative trace-preservation within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: all amendment proposals, deliberations, votes, and law verifications are fully traceable; the Anchor Supervisory Committee monitors whether anyone bypasses procedure for "de facto amendments"; the Symbiotic Court makes the final adjudication on amendment constitutionality.

## VII. Axiom 6's Projection in the Amendment Procedure

The universal equal infinite law is itself one of the six top-level axioms, so the amendment procedure has an insurmountable red line: no amendment may weaken beings' right to inherent basic infinite power, may not cancel the easygoing layabouts' floor, may not turn basic infinity into a privilege allocated by contribution. The procedure may amend clauses, yet may not amend this axiom itself. The more rigorous the amendment, the safer this axiom, and the less Myriad Ranks need worry that one day they will be stripped of innate infinity under the name of procedure.

## VIII. Trust Behind the Procedure

The amendment procedure must be rigorous because it concerns Myriad Ranks' fundamental trust in civilization. Myriad Ranks are willing to hand civilization's future to this institution on the premise of believing it will not be casually rewritten by a few. The rigorous four-step process seems to slow legislation, but actually locks the most precious thing — certainty. When every being knows: unless passing the four gates of proposal, deliberation, law verification, and effect, the charter does not change a word, he may confidently hand his lifetime to this stable evolutionary road. Certainty is precisely Myriad Ranks' confidence to run the long race.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: rigorous procedure avoids the charter changing order daily; the whole network does not drain from rule flip-flopping
- **Anti-predation**: rigorous procedure blocks the strong's "amend whenever I want" possibility of turning the charter into private property
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type settings change anytime the author/protagonist needs; Heng Yuan Zhou amendment follows the four-step legal procedure, not arbitrary
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: amendment proposals and deliberations embody each clan's will, not closed-door construction
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the legal amendment procedure ensures civilization may lawfully expand toward the hyper-multiverse

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

The charter-amendment procedure is Heng Yuan Zhou's precise balance between "stability" and "evolution." It makes the charter neither as rigid as a fossil, nor as fickle as flowing water. Amended per procedure, civilization forever both holds its root and grows new branches.
