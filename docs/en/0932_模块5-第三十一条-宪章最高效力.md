# Entry 932 | Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter · Article 31 | The Charter's Supreme Effect

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 03, 48, 49

## Introduction: above everything stands only the charter itself

Article 30 governs "how the charter is amended"; Article 31 governs "how high the charter stands." It stipulates: the charter is Heng Yuan Zhou's supreme law, with effect higher than all laws, all institutions, all strong ones, and all conventions. Any norm conflicting with the charter is void ab initio. This is no slogan — it is paired with constitutionality review and constitutional litigation, turning "supreme effect" from a paper title into enforceable reality.

This article is from beginning to end placed under the six top-level axioms — the anti-predation axiom draws the bottom line, the republican axiom of Myriad Races establishes the subject, the collective-ascension axiom sets the direction, the causal self-discipline axiom guards the boundary, the law-first axiom preserves self-consistency; the five together constitute this article's insurmountable spiritual boundary.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. Jurisprudential Basis

This article is based on the Symbiotic Civilization Charter (Document 03)'s constitutionality-review system, the Judicial System and Adjudication Mechanism (Document 48), and the Governance Framework and Power Checks (Document 49). Its jurisprudence is the law-first axiom: the charter itself is the law's total expression among beings. No institution and no strong one may place himself outside the charter — the Anchor Council may not ride above it, the Symbiotic Executive Committee may not bypass it, plane-sovereign strong ones may not be exempt. Supreme effect means: in Myriad-Rank republicanism, the only highest is not some person, but the laws commonly established.

## II. Normative Content: Three Layers of Supreme-Effect Guarantee

Article 31 concretizes supreme effect:

1. **Conflict void**: all local regulations, sect rules, clan customs, cross-plane conventions conflicting with the charter are void ab initio;
2. **Constitutional review**: all laws and policies, before taking effect, must pass the Anchor Court's constitutionality review;
3. **Constitutional litigation**: any being who believes a public-power act is unconstitutional may directly file constitutional litigation with the Anchor Court, without level-by-level approval.

Three layers of guarantee make the charter's supreme effect directly invocable by ordinary beings.

## III. Scope

This article applies to all subjects in Heng Yuan Zhou, including the tripartite institutions, all clans, all planes, and all individuals and strong ones. This article especially makes clear: no institution or individual stands "above the charter" — even Lin Hao who pioneered a route, even Transcended Ones who wield hyper-multiverse power, are equally bound by the charter. Planet consciousness and accepted multiverse-residual individuals likewise have the right to file constitutional litigation.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

Enacting or applying unconstitutional norms, refusing constitutionality review, suppressing constitutional litigation, or declaring with institutional authority "the charter does not bind me," all constitute unconstitutionality under this article. Consequences: unconstitutional norms are immediately void, relevant acts are revoked; those using power to override law or placing themselves outside the charter have their power qualifications suspended, and are pursued heavily as undermining the fundamental law.

## V. Supervision Mechanism

This article is executed by the constitutionality-review and litigation system within Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: all higher norms are automatically compared against the charter, and conflicts are blocked; the constitutional-litigation channel is open to all beings, and handling results are network-wide publicized; the Anchor Court independently exercises charter-interpretation power, free from any administrative interference.

## VI. A Litigation the Weak Won

Article 31's most powerful moment is a litigation the weak won. An ordinary cultivator from a marginal plane, believing some public policy discriminates against his group, directly files constitutional litigation with the Anchor Court. Not because of his low realm, not because he faces a high-and-mighty institution, the suit is accepted, and finally the policy is ruled unconstitutional and void. This judgment tells Myriad Ranks: in Heng Yuan Zhou, the charter is not wall decoration for the strong, but a weapon every weak person may use against the strong. When the weakest party may also use the supreme law to win over the strongest party, law-supremacy truly turns from slogan into reality.

## VII. Axiom 6's Projection in Supreme Effect

The universal equal infinite law grants every being a supreme-law weapon against discrimination: when any institution or any strong one tries to deny some individual's inherent basic infinite power, that individual may directly file constitutional litigation under the charter's supreme effect. Supreme effect thus belongs not only to grand clauses, but ultimately lands on the most concrete individual — no matter how easygoing, how ordinary, his innate share of basic infinity is guarded by the entire charter's supreme effect.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: all subjects obey the same supreme law, avoiding system internal friction from normative conflict
- **Anti-predation**: supreme effect equals declaring "no one may stand above the anti-predation axiom"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in Tang-Luo-type systems the strong speak the law into being and are themselves the law; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone is bound by the charter, including the strongest
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: constitutional-litigation right belongs to all; the weak may also use the charter against the strong
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: supreme effect covers every newly incorporated plane and civilization, without exception

- **Universal equal infinity**: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning

## Conclusion

The charter's supreme effect is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's rule of law apex. When Myriad Ranks confirm — the only highest is the common law, not any single strongest individual — this civilization truly escapes the cycle of "rule by man." Law supreme, and Myriad-Rank republicanism is firm through the ages.
