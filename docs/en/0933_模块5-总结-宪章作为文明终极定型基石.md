## Introduction: thirty-one articles, laying civilization's last cornerstone

From Entry 901's general introduction to Entry 932's supreme effect, the Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter has thirty-one articles in total, together with the general introduction and summary, constituting the complete jurisprudential closed loop of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's final finalization. Reaching this entry, we answer Module 5's master question: why is this charter the cornerstone of Heng Yuan Zhou's march toward the hyper-multiverse's only complete civilizational closed loop? The answer is: the six top-level axioms — anti-predation axiom, republican axiom of Myriad Races, collective-ascension axiom, causal self-discipline axiom, law-first axiom — have, at the four levels of idea, institution, technology, and supervision, all been realized as enforceable, remediable, supervisable legal clauses. What remains is to hold them.

Together with the previous five axioms, this article is also bound by Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a guaranteed basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; predation thus fundamentally loses meaning.

## I. The Internal Structure of the Thirty-One Articles

These thirty-one articles are not scattered listings, but a self-consistent edifice:

- **Foundation layer** (902–905): anti-predation into the charter, Myriad-Rank equality, collective ascension, causal self-discipline — four axioms directly into the charter;
- **Operation layer** (906–919): law priority, system full openness, multi-layer supervision, contracted responsibility, 20% activation strategy, four-dimensional assessment, every road available, bidirectional cycle, anchor universal access, reporting system, observe-first, knowledge-return-only, weight assessment, Transcendent perpetual motion — how institutions run;
- **Distinctive layer** (920–926): dual-track time domain, D route, Lin Hao route, Youth Supreme, cultural protection, planet consciousness, residual civilizations — full coverage of paths and subjects;
- **Defense layer** (927–930): error correction, reversal prevention, harvesting ban, anti-solidification — four permanent mechanisms blocking every regression;
- **Capping layer** (931–932): amendment procedure, supreme effect — letting this edifice both stand firm and grow.

## II. Why This Is "Final Finalization"

Old-age civilizations repeatedly collapsed because they built order on "some strong one" — order exists when the strong one exists, order collapses when the strong one perishes. Tang-Luo-type systems' protagonist-uniqueness dependence is extremely high, the whole civilization hangs on one person. Heng Yuan Zhou's final finalization moves order from "person" to "law":

- No longer relying on a savior, but on the broad strong-one base of 20% activation;
- No longer relying on personal moral conscience, but on Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision;
- No longer relying on luck not turning bad, but on the four permanent lines of error correction, reversal prevention, harvesting ban, and anti-solidification;
- No longer fearing plot kidnapping settings, but locked down by the law-first axiom.

Thus, civilization's stability no longer depends on any individual, but on a law system that self-runs, self-corrects, and self-reinforces. This is the "closed loop" — input Myriad Ranks, output ascension, with no external dependency and no fatal single point in between.

## III. The Closed Loop's Final Verification

Whether a civilizational closed loop is complete depends on whether five questions all have answers:

1. Where does power come from? — develop increments, bidirectional cycle (913), not predating stock;
2. Who is qualified to use it? — Myriad Ranks full coverage, every road available (903, 912);
3. How are the strong managed? — four-dimensional assessment, perpetual construction (911, 919);
4. What if wrong? — error-correction mechanism, amendment procedure (927, 931);
5. Will it regress? — reversal prevention, harvesting ban, anti-solidification (928, 929, 930).

Five questions all have institutional answers, and all are enforceable and remediable under Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision — this is the hyper-multiverse's only complete civilizational closed loop's jurisprudential completion state.

## IV. The Road After Finalization

Final finalization is not the end, but a new starting point. What the charter locks is direction; what it opens is infinite future: civilization will, along the amendment procedure, continue expanding toward the Multiverse Realm and the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm; Myriad Ranks, under the charter, will implement every clause more finely. Heng Yuan Zhou will not stop here — it has only finally gained feet that will not slip, and may confidently walk deep into the cosmos.

## VI. Above the Cornerstone, Still a Long Road

It must be emphasized that writing the charter as civilization's final-finalization cornerstone by no means civilization stops here. The cornerstone's function is to let the building grow ever higher, not to build the building into a tomb. Under this charter, Myriad Ranks still infinitely climb toward the Multiverse Realm and the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm; outside the 300 systems new roads will still grow; the dual-track time domain will still hold more diverse ways of living. What the charter locks is only that direction Myriad Ranks absolutely will not return to — predation, monopoly, harvesting, regression. Every other possibility, it hands over freely. The cornerstone is firm; the long road still goes forward.
