# Entry 934 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · General Introduction | Final Finalization of the Heavenly-Dao Rules

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 03 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: writing anti-predation into the cosmos's laws themselves

Heng Yuan Zhou (Wanling Hengmao Origin Universe)'s civilizational final finalization, after the Myriad-Rank Republican Civilizational Charter (from Entry 901) completed the institution-layer lock, leaves one last and highest project — sublimating anti-predation from "human law" into "Heavenly-Dao rules." Human law may be overthrown by the strong's force and tampered with by conspiracy; once Heavenly-Dao rules are anchored in the cosmic-law network, they resonate with causality and the anchor origin, and any who act against the trend bear law's counter-restraint from the very moment of their rising thought. This is the master proposition of the Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules.

The six top-level axioms are here as a whole written into the Heavenly-Dao layer: the anti-predation axiom is this series' spiritual body, the republican axiom of Myriad Races is the object the rules protect, the collective-ascension axiom is the direction the rules guide, the causal self-discipline axiom is the boundary of the rules' operation, and the law-first axiom is the meta-rule under which this series stands — precisely because the law comes first and setting self-consistency outranks all, Heavenly-Dao rules may never retcon and endure through ages. This general introduction does not legislate article by article, but first makes clear: what is the relationship between Heavenly-Dao rules and the charter, on what basis do they bind Myriad Ranks, and how do they interlock with Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: Three Layers of Heavenly-Dao Rules

The Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules total thirty-one rules, plus this general introduction and the closing summary (Entry 966), constituting a complete system. Its structure is three layers:

- **Body layer (Rules 1–5)**: increment-development iron law, no-consumption-of-stock iron law, no killing-and-predation as evolutionary path, Great Dao full-level openness, no private hoarding no monopoly — answering "where does power come from";
- **Subject layer (Rules 6–12)**: Myriad Ranks 99% full coverage, all beings' equal evolutionary qualification, strong ones do not ascend alone, lifting Myriad Ranks up together, Myriad-Rank republican co-construction, Alaya network-wide sharing, no great-material-for-small-use — answering "to whom does power belong, and whom does it serve";
- **Boundary layer (Rules 13–31)**: cross-plane reporting, observe-first-then-limited-intervene, knowledge-return-only-without-causality-disturbance, eradicating civilizational regression/historical reversal/cyclical harvesting/class solidification, law comes first, never retcon, setting self-consistency, plot does not kidnap settings, worldview may infinitely expand, predatory cultivation prohibited, enlightened-predator conversion, just-rascal cultivation, shortcut inner-demon prevention, civilizational-weight assessment, law-anchor-authority universal access, Myriad-Rank-origin bidirectional cycle — answering "how is power bound."

Thirty-one rules link ring by ring; missing any one leaves a seam in the system.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis: From Where Heavenly-Dao Rules Gain Binding Force

Heavenly-Dao rules' authority is not "some god said so," but the result of a triple argument:

1. **Direct corollary of the law-first axiom**: the cosmic-law network itself precedes all strong ones; predatory systems could run rampant only because the old cosmos never wrote anti-predation into the law layer. Anchor-origin verification shows anti-predation is fully aligned with the cosmic-origin cycle direction;
2. **Iron evidence from historical retrospective**: the endgame retrospective of all Pre-Anchor Era predatory civilizations (Tang-Luo, Douluo-type sects, Shrouding-the-Sky-type forbidden zones) shows predatory paths necessarily self-exhaust, and anti-predation is the only direction that can close the loop (Document 04 has proven its efficiency ceiling);
3. **Law-ification of the Myriad-Rank contract**: the civilizational contract signed when Myriad-Rank republicanism was established, after Anchor Council legislation and Symbiotic Court judicial confirmation, is "engraved" into the law network by the Anchor Supervisory Committee's petition to the cosmic-origin anchor; from then on, defying Heavenly-Dao rules disturbs the anchor.

## III. Execution Mechanism: How Heavenly-Dao Rules Land

Heavenly-Dao rules do not rely on fantastical vague means like "heavenly punishment," but on precise causal engineering:

- Each rule corresponds to a set of monitoring nodes in the Alaya Network, real-time marking all energy flows, law invocations, and causal disturbances that contradict it;
- Rule triggering is graded early-warning: thought stage (inner-demon prompt) → behavior stage (anchor freezes part of permissions) → accomplished stage (Symbiotic Court prosecution), embodying causal self-discipline's "prevention first, sanction after";
- The executing subject is not some Heavenly Emperor, but the Anchor Council (law interpretation), Symbiotic Executive Committee (law enforcement), and Symbiotic Court (judicial) tripartite's extension at the Heavenly-Dao layer, avoiding "in the name of heavenly punishment actually running dictatorship."

## IV. Consequences of Violation: Graded Costs of Defying Heavenly Rules

- **Light**: occasional greed, local overstep, gets an Alaya Network inner-demon warning, is forced into the Quest Sediment Domain to reflect, and is not recorded as original sin;
- **Medium**: systematic private hoarding, monopoly, unreported cross-plane interference, has law-anchor permissions frozen, high-level access stripped, and is transferred into the enlightened-predator conversion program;
- **Heavy**: restoring predatory cultivation, manufacturing cyclical harvesting, pushing civilizational regression, is isolated, tried, and converted under the Anti-Predation Committee procedure; never counter-killing with killing.

Heavenly-Dao rules' ultimate consequence is not soul-and-spirit annihilation, but "losing all symbiotic-network benefits" — in an anchor civilization, this is equivalent to being expelled from the evolutionary expressway.

## V. Supervising Subject: Who Guards the Heavenly Dao

Supervision is borne by Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision: anchor-network automatic monitoring is the first layer, each clan's autonomous supervision is the second, the Anchor Supervisory Committee's independent investigation is the third, and Myriad Ranks' direct Heavenly-Dao litigation to the Symbiotic Court is the fourth. The Heavenly Dao belongs to no single Transcended One; the Heavenly Dao belongs to all Myriad Ranks — this is precisely the fundamental difference between Heavenly-Dao rules and the old cosmos's "Heavenly Dao is some great-power's will."

- **Anti-energy waste**: writing anti-predation into the Heavenly-Dao layer once for all eradicates predatory systems' hundreds-of-millions-of-years repeated restoration, saving immeasurable civilizational-level trial-and-error energy
- **Anti-predation**: this series' thirty-one rules all revolve around "develop increments, consume no stock, take no killing as evolution"; they are the anti-predation axiom's Heavenly-Dao embodiment; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root

- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: Heavenly-Dao rules allow the worldview to expand infinitely; newly added planes and lineages automatically enter rule application without collapsing
