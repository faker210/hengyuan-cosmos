# Entry 935 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 1 | The Increment-Development Iron Law

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 15, 04 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: power's first legitimate source is "new creation"

Of the Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules' thirty-one, the first must answer "where should cultivators obtain power." The old cosmos's default answer is zero-sum — you gain one more, others lose one more, so killing, devouring, and preying become the only evolutionary shortcut. Heng Yuan Zhou's Heavenly-Dao answer is: all energy and law amplification needed for cultivation must first come from the cosmos's inherently increment-developable part, not from slicing a chunk off stock distribution. This is the Increment-Development Iron Law; it is the anti-predation axiom's first cornerstone at the energy layer.

The six top-level axioms simultaneously activate here: the anti-predation axiom directly requires "develop increments, consume no stock"; the republican axiom of Myriad Races ensures increments' dividends are shared by Myriad Ranks, not monopolized by the strong; the collective-ascension axiom makes the increment project itself the heavenly ladder lifting Myriad Ranks; the causal self-discipline axiom requires increment development not disturb existing causal chains; the law-first axiom lets "increment"'s definition, measurement, and boundary be nailed first by law, never changing for plot convenience.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: What Counts as "Increment"

The Increment-Development Iron Law's content breaks into three executable criteria:

1. **Free energy the cosmos may inherently develop, law nodes not yet lit, and space-laws that may be remade** are regarded as increments and developed first;
2. **Stock energy and established achievements already belonging to some being, civilization, or planet consciousness** may not be extracted, transferred, or nibbled under any name;
3. **The same energy flow may only "create one direction once"**: after the developer gains increment returns, that direction must be registered in the Alaya Network as "symbiotic benefit"; secondary resale for profit is forbidden.

Take the three origin bases: the deduction-iteration system (001) develops law blanks in the cosmos not yet exhausted by deduction; the life-cycle system (002) develops life energy that self-generates and self-grows; the space-law scheduling system (003) schedules unoccupied space positions — all three are increments, none extracts stock.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis: Why the Cosmos Can Afford "Everyone Taking Increments"

Increment development is not moral beautification, but a physical fact proven by the Energy Cycle and Transformation Law (Document 15) and the Multiplication-Fruit technologized principle (Document 16):

- The cosmic-law network contains massive "unlit nodes" whose energy reserves far exceed all beings' current total consumption;
- Deduction iteration may multiply research efficiency (Multiplication Fruit has been technologized into hundredfold deduction amplification), turning "time" itself into an increment;
- The anchor-origin feedback cycle (Document 25) amplifies each correctly developed increment, via the Myriad-Rank-origin bidirectional cycle, into whole-system benefit.

The old cosmos was forced to prey because it treated the cosmos as a fixed-size pie; Heavenly-Dao Rule 1 points out: the cosmos is a wok that grows its own dough.

## III. Execution Mechanism: How Increments Are Identified, Registered, Allocated

- The anchor network real-time draws the "unlit-law map," publicly listing developable increments to all beings, free to claim;
- Development projects are compensated per contracted-responsibility more-work-more-pay; increment returns are shared by the developer and the civilizational community he belongs to;
- Before any development action starts, the Alaya Network automatically verifies: does the energy used come from the increment pool? Does it touch the stock red line? Touching freezes it;
- The 20% activation strategy (Document 23) ensures increment-development resources first flow to the 80% not yet initiated, rather than continuing to feed the already strong.

## IV. Consequences of Violation: What Happens When Stock Is Treated as Increment

- **Light**: unknowingly misusing stock energy, forced rollback and compensatory development of equal increments, no original sin pursued;
- **Medium**: knowingly violating, under the name of "developing increments" actually extracting some clan's stock, has law-anchor permissions frozen and is transferred into Entry 960's enlightened-predator conversion program;
- **Heavy**: systematically draining some plane's or planet consciousness's stock energy to manufacture cyclical harvesting, is isolated and tried under the Anti-Predation Committee procedure.

Rule 1's sanction logic is "restore original + make up increments," not destruction — the goal is to guide the astray energy flow back into the symbiotic cycle.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Supervision has four parties: Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision automatically marks energy flow as the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee does quarterly audits of the increment list and stock red line as the second layer; the extracted party (planet consciousness, weak clan) may directly file Heavenly-Dao litigation with the Symbiotic Court as the third layer; the Anchor Council interprets "increment" definition and prevents the rule from being technically evaded as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: locking the development object to "unlit nodes" eliminates mutual-destruction-style internal friction from fighting over stock
- **Anti-predation**: cutting off predation's necessity at the energy source — since increments are infinite, predation has no "survival necessity" excuse; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems allocate cosmic resources as private property, with the strong monopolizing increment entrances; Heng Yuan Zhou's increment list is network-wide public, the weak claim first
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: increment-development qualification is open to Myriad Ranks 99%; plant spirits, planet consciousness, and consciousness-information clans all have dedicated increment maps
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated multiverse residual civilizations automatically gain increment-access channels, no limit by civilization age

## Conclusion

The Increment-Development Iron Law answers civilizational finalization's first question: where does power come from. The answer is "newly made, not stolen." With the source correct, the following thirty-one rules have roots.
