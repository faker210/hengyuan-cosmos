# Entry 936 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 2 | The No-Consumption-of-Stock Iron Law

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 25, 04 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: what already belongs to someone, not an inch may move

Rule 1 says "power must come from new creation"; Rule 2 immediately adds the other half — "what others already have, not an inch may move." The Increment-Development Iron Law and the No-Consumption-of-Stock Iron Law are two sides of one coin: the former governs source legitimacy; the latter governs boundary sacredness. The old cosmos's countless tragedies were precisely not from lacking increments, but from the strong not even sparing stock: devouring others' cultivation, refining living souls, treating a planet as cultivation fuel. Heavenly-Dao Rule 2 must thoroughly seal this kind of "even grabbing the rice in the pot" behavior at the cosmic-law layer.

The six top-level axioms run through it: the anti-predation axiom directly negates the old logic that "consuming stock is evolution"; the republican axiom of Myriad Races declares each being's established achievements are his inalienable evolutionary assets; the collective-ascension axiom requires stock not only not be consumed, but be protected and transmitted; the causal self-discipline axiom writes "not touching others' stock" as a hard boundary of cross-plane interference; the law-first axiom lets "stock" 's ownership, measurement, and disputes be adjudicated first by law, not by fist size.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: The Stock List and No-Touch Clauses

The No-Consumption-of-Stock Iron Law stipulates:

1. All beings' already-cultivated cultivation, already-comprehended laws, already-accumulated memory and soul-spirit are regarded as their "non-consumable stock";
2. All planet consciousnesses, civilizational communities, and clan lineages' multi-generational accumulated achievements are regarded as "collective stock," equally protected;
3. No cultivation method, formation, treasure, or realm advancement may take "sacrificing others," "refining soul-spirits," or "devouring origin" as energy source or cost;
4. Allowed exchange is limited to mutual-benefit cooperation of "increment for increment"; stock may transfer only under the owner's complete voluntariness and without harming third parties, and transfer must leave a trace in the Alaya Network.

In one sentence: stock may be studied, researched, and praised, but may not be eaten, drained, or taken away.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis: Why Stock Is Sacred

This rule's jurisprudence is more than moral; it has three hard arguments:

- **Feedback-cycle argument**: the Symbiotic Feedback Cycle Mechanism (Document 25) proves power gained by consuming stock is doubly backlashed in the causal loop — the stronger the predator, the fiercer the backlash; this is precisely the mechanism root of predatory systems' inevitable historical regression;
- **Self-growth argument**: the Self-Growing Evolution Law (Document 07) points out each being's stock is the crystal of years of self-growth; forcibly draining it equals breaking his causal trunk, causing irreparable origin incompleteness;
- **Civilizational-continuity argument**: once collective stock (civilizational classics, planetary ecology, clan gifts) is consumed, later Myriad Ranks have no chance to stand on predecessors' shoulders, and civilization necessarily breaks across generations.

## III. Execution Mechanism: How Stock Is Guarded

- The Alaya Network builds a "stock archive" for every being and every civilization, marking the causal ownership of their cultivation, soul-spirit, and memory;
- All cultivation energy input must pass a "stock-verification gate": if the energy's source traces to some stock subject, it is automatically rejected and alerted;
- Voluntary transfer has triple protection: owner's soul-spirit confirmation, no minor/unconscious being harmed, Symbiotic Court online witness;
- The Quest Sediment Domain retains "predation-scenario simulation," letting cultivators intuitively see the long-term causal bill of consuming stock, eradicating motivation cognitively.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: carelessly touching others' stock (e.g., a cultivation method inadvertently absorbing a neighbor's dissipated energy), forced stripping, equal compensation, one inner-demon record;
- **Medium**: designing "pseudo-voluntary" contracts, using illusions to lure others into yielding stock, has law-anchor permissions stripped and enters Entry 960's conversion program;
- **Heavy**: those taking devouring, refining, and sacrifice as routine cultivation means are recidivist predatory cultivators, handled specially under Rule 25 (Entry 959).

Sanction's core is "restore stock ownership," not simple annihilation — the consumed stock energy, after causal retrogression, must as much as possible be returned to the original owner or his civilizational community.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision is the first line: the stock-verification gate automatically rejects as the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee spot-checks "voluntary transfer" authenticity as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court accepts stock-ownership disputes as the third layer; each clan's autonomy assembly exercises protection over its own collective stock as the fourth layer. Rule 2 especially authorizes weak clans, plant spirits, and planet consciousness to directly sue the strong, with no "station too low to sue" threshold.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: avoiding civilizational-level energy idling from "kill-or-be-killed" zero-sum games
- **Anti-predation**: writing "no consuming stock" as cosmic hard law; predators' energy channels to act are sealed; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in Tang-Luo-type systems the weak are the strong's "stock batteries,"随时可取; in Heng Yuan Zhou the weak's stock is Heavenly-Dao protected, and the strong reaching out is unconstitutional
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the protected object applies uniformly from the human race to planet consciousness, plant spirits, and consciousness-information clans, with no station discrimination
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated cross-plane civilizations' stock automatically enters the protection list, not regarded as ownerless because they have "not yet submitted"

## Conclusion

Stock is the road Myriad Ranks each have walked. Rule 2 declares: no one has the right to eat the road others have walked as his own. With stock held, Myriad-Rank republicanism has a true "every person" to speak of.
