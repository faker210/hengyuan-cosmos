# Entry 937 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 3 | No Killing-and-Predation as an Evolutionary Path

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 19, 04 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: killing is not cultivation, but having strayed from the road

Nearly all old-cosmos cultivation systems default to one unwritten rule: cultivation is fought for, realms are climbed on the bones of enemies. Realm-leaping slaying, massacring hostile sects, devouring the defeated's origin — killing and predation are not only tolerated, but even beautified as the mark of a "genius." Heavenly-Dao Rule 3 must de-name this unwritten rule at the root: an evolutionary path's legitimacy depends not on whom you defeated, but on how much increment you developed and how much you fed back to Myriad Ranks. Taking killing and predation as an evolutionary path is not "ruthless means" in Heng Yuan Zhou, but "the wrong road."

The six top-level axioms are nailed here: the anti-predation axiom directly declares "no killing-and-predation as evolutionary path"; the republican axiom of Myriad Races makes the slain no longer stepping stones but equal subjects; the collective-ascension axiom changes evolution's opponent from "others" to "unlit laws"; the causal self-discipline axiom requires all strife be limited to just defense and may not be lethal; the law-first axiom lets "lawful evolutionary paths" be defined first by law, preventing the strong from inventing new predation tricks.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: Three Path Prohibitions

1. **No killing beings as a necessary condition for realm advancement**: no lawful cultivation method exists that "must slay N people to break through";
2. **No predating the defeated's cultivation, treasures, or soul-spirit as victory spoils**: a just-defense victor may not draw any evolutionary resource from the defeated;
3. **No "kill count," "war-merit board," or "stepping on others to rise" as promotion basis**: all promotion looks only at increment-development contribution and Myriad-Rank feedback degree.

Allowed strife is limited to: Quest Domain simulation retrospective (simulated duels in the text do not touch real causality), skill sparring, just defense. Casualties from sparring and defense are reviewed case by case by the Symbiotic Court for overreach.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis: Why the Killing Path Is a Dead End

- **Efficiency-ceiling argument**: Document 04 has already proven predatory cultivation's returns drop sharply as realms rise, while backlash rises exponentially; the higher the realm, the more dead-ended — this is its "efficiency ceiling";
- **Causal-compound-interest argument**: the Symbiotic Feedback Cycle Mechanism points out each killing-and-predation knots an evil cause on the causal web; it does not vanish with the strong one's death, but is reclaimed with principal and interest across reincarnation — so-called "cyclical harvesting" is precisely the old cosmos's collective reckoning with killers;
- **Evolution-direction argument**: the cosmic-law network rewards "lighting new nodes," not "extinguishing old nodes"; extinguishing others' law nodes is a net loss to the cosmos, naturally giving no feedback.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- All breakthrough applications must submit an "energy source and causal cleanliness" report; the Alaya Network automatically verifies: does breakthrough energy come from increments? Are there killing-predation residues?
- War merit and kill-count "achievements" are not recorded as positive contribution in Alaya archives, but are listed as inner-demon risk items;
- The Quest Domain simulation system specially provides "killing-fantasy experience": letting cultivators walk a killing-evolution road in simulation, see its endgame collapse with their own eyes, then voluntarily choose the symbiotic road;
- Cultivation-ethics courses (Document 19) list "killing is not evolution" as a required course at all realms.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: sparring overreach causing injury, forced medical treatment, inner-demon warning, recorded in ethics archive;
- **Medium**: under the name of "just defense" actually preying, has law-anchor permissions frozen and enters enlightened-predator conversion (Entry 960);
- **Heavy**: organizing, participating in, or promoting a cultivation system centered on killing-and-predation, handled specially under Rule 25 (Entry 959) as predatory cultivation, isolated and tried.

Heavenly-Dao rules do not stop killing with killing: sanctions themselves never use execution, refining, or other means, but cut off its predatory energy source, letting it relearn within the symbiotic network.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision automatically verifies breakthrough causality as the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee specially audits "war-merit promotion" as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates killing disputes as the third layer; each clan's elder assembly and Youth-Supreme supervision corps exercise ethics supervision over their own disciples as the fourth layer. Rule 3 especially establishes a "victim causal retrogression" mechanism: the descendant civilizational communities of historically slain beings have the right to collectively prosecute.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: mutual-destruction-style consumption from killing-predation both sides is the cosmos's most expensive zero-sum game; Rule 3 once for all cancels its legality
- **Anti-predation**: directly prohibiting the "defeat—predate—evolve" chain; the core ritual the predation faction most relies on is legislatively abolished; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in Tang-Luo-type systems realm-leaping slaying makes one a genius, and the defeated have no right of defense; in Heng Yuan Zhou slaying is not merit, and excessive defense is instead tried
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the human race, alien beasts, plant spirits, and planet consciousness's "life stock" is equally protected; no "demons may be killed, spirits may be eaten" customary exemption
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated multiverse residual civilizations with their own killing traditions first enter the Quest Domain for reform, then take incremental evolution; individuals are not punished for their old customs

## Conclusion

Killing and predation are not the strong one's medal, but a strayed road. Rule 3 changes evolution's opponent from other beings to unlit law nodes — Myriad Ranks henceforth stand side by side conquering the unknown outward, not devouring one another inward.
