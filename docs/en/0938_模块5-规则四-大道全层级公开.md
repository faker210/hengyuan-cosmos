# Entry 938 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 4 | Full-Level Openness of the Great Dao

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 18, 08 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: the Dao may not be privately owned; private ownership is predation

The old cosmos's most insidious predation is not grabbing money or land, but "monopolizing the Dao." A sect locks core cultivation methods in its scripture pavilion, transmitting only to direct line; a forbidden zone hides the highest laws in a forbidden ground, and outsiders who peek die. Knowledge monopoly lets the weak not even see what "the road to strength looks like," so they can only work for the strong generation after generation. Heavenly-Dao Rule 4 must de-name this monopoly at the cosmic-law layer: the Great Dao, from Sense-Condensed Realm introductory breathing to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's ultimate laws, is open at every level, in every lineage, with no threshold.

The six top-level axioms run through it: the anti-predation axiom directly requires "Great Dao full-level openness, no private hoarding no monopoly"; the republican axiom of Myriad Races makes openness the equal admission ticket for Myriad Ranks 99%; the collective-ascension axiom makes the open Great Dao grow ever clearer through network-wide sharing; the causal self-discipline axiom requires openness itself not trap-clause causality-disturbing methods; the law-first axiom lets "openness"'s levels, versions, and errata be uniformly managed by the law network, never lost and never privately tampered.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: Four-Layer Openness Standard

Rule 4 stipulates all cultivation knowledge must satisfy:

1. **Full-level openness**: from Sense-Condensed Realm initiation through Origin Realm, Multiverse Realm, Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, every level's Dharma doors are open, with no hidden layer "only transmitted to a few prodigies";
2. **Full-lineage openness**: the five lineages' (human, alien beast, plant spirit, mechanical construct, consciousness-information) adapted methods are all open, with no wall by clan;
3. **Full-version openness**: a method's revision history, errata records, and failure cases are all public, not only the "success version";
4. **Zero-threshold openness**: open channels are free and barrier-free, with no wealth, bloodline, status, or contribution-point prerequisite to reading.

The 300 Origin Cultivation Systems (Document 10) have all been launched onto the Alaya Network public library per this standard.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis: Why the Great Dao Must Be Open

- **Every-road argument**: the Every-Road-Available Guarantee System (Document 08) points out evolutionary right is a basic right, and "not knowing where the road lies" is the greatest deprivation;
- **Multiplication argument**: one Dao cultivated by one person has finite use; one Dao cultivated by ten thousand people, with Multiplication-Fruit hundredfold deduction amplification superimposed, the Dao itself becomes more perfect under ten-thousand-fold verification — openness actually makes the Dao stronger;
- **Anti-monopoly argument**: once knowledge is privately hoarded, its holder sets himself up as "Dao lord," forming hidden rule over Myriad Ranks; this is the knowledge source of class solidification (Rule 19).

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Alaya Network public library sets a "Great-Dao open interface," which all beings may access by soul imprint, no application needed;
- The Anchor Council sets a "Dao-Version Committee" that uniformly erratas, archives, and tags extended settings "pending terminology-committee calibration";
- Holders of privately hoarded Dao must complete surrender registration within a time limit; those not surrendering after deadline have their privately hoarded Dao automatically "unlocked and opened" in the network;
- Openness is not coercion: cultivators may create secret methods, but once a secret method touches common law nodes, its core principles must be traceable and auditable.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: inadvertently retaining "oral master transmission" not open, make up submission within a time limit, no liability pursued;
- **Medium**: deliberately privately hoarding, walling and charging, controlling disciples with "untransmittable secrets," has law-anchor permissions frozen and enters the conversion program;
- **Heavy**: monopolizing some main Dao, using it to coerce Myriad Ranks, creating dependence that "without me the Dao cannot be walked," is downgraded per Entry 963's civilizational-weight assessment and stripped of high-level access.

Sanction logic: let the private hoarder go from "Dao lord" back to "Dao seeker," again standing before the open library studying with everyone.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision automatically compares the "existing Dao library" with the "known law-node map," discovering private hoarding and alerting as the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee checks and receives private-hoard registrations as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates monopoly cases as the third layer; Myriad Ranks' cultural-education autonomous groups supervise openness quality (whether hidden, whether mined) as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: one Dao verified by ten thousand, avoiding countless people repeating trial and error in the dark
- **Anti-predation**: knowledge monopoly is predation's highest form; Rule 4 directly tears down the wall; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type peerless academies pick people via "gifted slots," and most cannot even touch the door; Heng Yuan Zhou's public library is open to 99% with zero threshold
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the five lineages, Youth Supremes, and planet consciousness all have adapted open Dao texts, not favoring the human race alone
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: when new planes are incorporated, their native Dao texts enter the library simultaneously; cross-civilization Dao cross-verify, and the worldview may expand infinitely

## Conclusion

The Great Dao is not some sect's private property, but the cosmic public heritage for all Dao seekers. Rule 4 tears down the scripture pavilion's walls; only then do Myriad Ranks stand for the first time on the same starting line.
