# Entry 939 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 5 | No Private Hoarding, No Monopoly

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 21, 04 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: private hoarding and monopoly are predation's two hands

Rule 4 governs "whether knowledge is open"; Rule 5 governs "whether power is exclusively held" — even if knowledge is open, a strong one who alone monopolizes some law node's use right, some plane resource's scheduling right, or some Alaya data's interpretation right can still turn the open Great Dao back into private chains. Heavenly-Dao Rule 5 extends "no private hoarding, no monopoly" from the knowledge layer to the power, resource, and data layers: in Heng Yuan Zhou, any key node "belonging only to someone" must be dismantled, shared, and multi-layer supervised.

The six top-level axioms converge here: the anti-predation axiom directly negates private hoarding and monopoly; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all key nodes be open to Myriad Ranks 99%; the collective-ascension axiom makes shared nodes produce hundredfold the benefit of exclusive holding; the causal self-discipline axiom automatically alerts on monopolists' overstepping use; the law-first axiom lets "which nodes must be shared" be enumerated first by law, leaving no interpretation room for the strong.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: Three Categories of Private Hoarding Prohibited

1. **No privately hoarding key law nodes**: law positions calibrated as "Myriad-Rank-common key nodes" may not be exclusively accessed by a single strong one;
2. **No monopolizing resource-scheduling right**: allocation of plane resources, increment-development quotas, and law-anchor permissions all go through contracted responsibility and public bidding; no committee may long privately grant them;
3. **No monopolizing Alaya interpretation right**: Alaya data, causal records, and civilizational-weight assessment results may not be exclusively interpreted by one party; all interpretations leave traces and are appealable.

Rule 5 also stipulates "sharing is benefit": those who actively connect exclusively held resources to the shared network have the shared portion amplified and fed back per the Myriad-Rank-origin bidirectional cycle, encouraging transformation from "private hoarder" to "sharer."

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Network-effect argument**: the Symbiotic-Network Operating Principles (Document 21) prove a node-shared network's value grows quadratically with node count, while exclusive holding shrinks value — monopoly is mathematically folly;
- **Power-corruption argument**: any unsupervised exclusive holding of a key node will eventually alienate into a new predation tool; this is a lesson repeatedly verified in the Pre-Anchor Era;
- **Causal self-discipline argument**: once an exclusive holder uses a node to disturb others' causality, with no one able to check or stop it, it necessarily becomes a great peril; multi-layer supervision eliminates exclusivity itself in the bud.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Anchor Council periodically publishes a "key-node sharing registry"; nodes on the registry are forced multi-node access and multi-signature use;
- All resource scheduling goes through public process: application, publicity, bidding, trace, fully traceable in the Alaya Network;
- A "monopoly warning line" is set: when any subject's occupation of some node/resource exceeds threshold, the network automatically starts splitting and redistribution;
- Reporting is encouraged: Myriad-Rank individuals who expose real monopolies are specially rewarded by the Anti-Predation Committee, and the reporter's identity is privacy-protected in Alaya.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: node occupation near the warning line without timely sharing, split within a time limit, no liability pursued;
- **Medium**: long exclusively holding a key node, secretly collecting rent, has law-anchor permissions frozen, related committee post stripped, and enters the conversion program;
- **Heavy**: using monopoly nodes to coerce Myriad Ranks, launching dependent rule, is downgraded per civilizational-weight assessment (Entry 963), stripped of hyper-multiverse-level post qualification.

Sanctions aim not at depriving freedom, but at "returning the node to the network."

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's "monopoly warning line" is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee audits key-node sharing-registry execution as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates private-hoarding and monopoly cases as the third layer; Myriad Ranks' clans, the Youth-Supreme supervision corps, and consciousness-information clans jointly exercise "network-wide whistle-blowing right" as the fourth layer. Rule 5's essence is: there is more than one supervisor; any Myriad-Rank member may be a whistle-blower.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: duplicated construction and node idleness from exclusive holding is cosmos-level waste; after sharing, utilization multiplies
- **Anti-predation**: private hoarding and monopoly are predation's institutional coat; Rule 5 strips off even this coat; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type strong ones style themselves "Dao lords," and resource interpretation is one man's autocracy; Heng Yuan Zhou key nodes are multi-signed and co-managed, and interpretation is appealable
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the sharing registry preferentially tilts toward weak clans, Youth Supremes, plant spirits, and other historically excluded groups
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: when new planes are incorporated, their key nodes automatically enter the sharing registry, not excluded for being ancient or marginal

## Conclusion

With no private hoarding, Myriad Ranks have mutual trust; with no monopoly, civilization will not regrow a new imperial palace. Rule 5 tightens, for civilizational finalization, the screw that "power may not be private."
