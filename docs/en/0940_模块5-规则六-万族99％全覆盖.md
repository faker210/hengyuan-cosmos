# Entry 940 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 6 | Myriad Ranks 99% Full Coverage

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 08 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: coverage rate itself is a Heavenly-Dao metric

If the words "Myriad Ranks" remain only a slogan, they are pretty talk. Heavenly-Dao Rule 6 must turn it into a measurable, auditable, non-inflatable hard metric: the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's evolutionary system, guarantee system, and governance-participation rights must cover 99% of Myriad Ranks. This figure is not a propaganda figure, but a constant auto-verified quarterly at the Heavenly-Dao layer — below 99%, civilization finalization is judged failed, triggering a remedial project borne by all Myriad Ranks. This is Myriad-Rank republicanism's key leap from "wish" to "hard law."

The six top-level axioms here concretize into a set of numbers: the republican axiom of Myriad Races is this rule's body; the anti-predation axiom ensures coverage is not "charity coverage" but equal access; the collective-ascension axiom makes coverage itself the project lifting the whole; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the coverage process not disturb unenlightened clans' natural causality; the law-first axiom lets "99%" 's statistical caliber, clan list, and omission judgment be defined first by law, eliminating word games like "defining 99% as 90%."

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: Triple Meaning of 99% Coverage

1. **Clan coverage**: of all clans registered under the five lineages (carbon-based material, energy-condensed, mechanical-construct, consciousness-information, mixed-mutant), 99% must have accessed the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization;
2. **Individual coverage**: within accessed clans, 99% of individuals must enjoy full evolutionary right — including human males and females, Youth Supremes, alien beasts, plant spirits, planet consciousness, cosmic-origin bodies, and multiverse residual civilizations;
3. **Plane coverage**: within Heng Yuan Zhou's jurisdiction, 99% of material planes have completed basic anchor access.

The portion not reaching 99% must be listed on a "remediation list," with the Anchor Council and Symbiotic Executive Committee jointly storming it within a time limit.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Myriad-rank co-advancement argument**: the Myriad-Ranks Co-Advancement Basic Program (Document 05) points out a civilizational closed loop requires Myriad Ranks, not a strong-one bloc, to close; omitting any clan leaves a seam in the loop;
- **System-stability argument**: the cosmic causal network is a connected web; a node long left unconnected eventually becomes a blind spot, possibly exploited by predation remnants;
- **20%-activation-strategy argument**: the 20% Activation Strategy Plan (Document 23) proves connecting the unactivated 80% and activating to the key 20% position produces an overall energy jump far beyond continuing to pile on the strong.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Alaya Network maintains a "Myriad-Rank coverage dashboard," real-time showing the clan, individual, and plane three-layer coverage rates, public to all Myriad Ranks;
- For uncovered clans, the Symbiotic Executive Committee dispatches "observe-first-then-contact" missions (following Rules 14 and 15), not forcing assimilation;
- For clans temporarily unfit for access due to physiological/cultural/station reasons, a gradual access path is provided, not a single cut-off;
- Coverage metrics enter the Anchor Council's annual report; committees not meeting target must publicly explain and submit a remediation plan.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: coverage statistics lag, list registration missed, make up within a time limit;
- **Medium**: deliberately excluding some clan from statistics, inflating coverage, has the responsible person's access permissions frozen and enters the conversion program;
- **Heavy**: refusing access on grounds of "this clan is unworthy," or even cleansing unenlightened clans, is handled heavily under Rule 3 and Rule 17 (eradicate civilizational regression).

Rule 6's sanction focus is not punishing "slowness," but punishing "pretending to cover."

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's coverage dashboard is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee quarterly audits coverage authenticity as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates access litigation by "excluded clans" as the third layer; uncovered clans retain a direct petition channel to the Anchor Council as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the uncovered 80% is the cosmos's largest idle evolutionary resource; activating multiplies it
- **Anti-predation**: bringing marginal clans once treated as "prey" by predatory systems into the protected, empowered 99%; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems cover only the "destined protagonist" clan, others are background; Heng Yuan Zhou's 99% is a hard metric, missing one clan is unconstitutional
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: this rule is "full coverage" itself, with no exception among the five lineages
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: each newly incorporated multiverse residual civilization updates the coverage denominator synchronously, the metric always targeting "the present whole"

Landing in execution detail, 99% coverage is no slogan, but a clan-by-clan reconcilable list: the Alaya Network separately names the uncovered 1%, writing down which link it is stuck at — whether the civilizational stage is not yet reached, the clan's character is closed, or the mission's capability is insufficient. Each kind of jam has a corresponding unlock plan: those not at stage first enter observation; closed ones first send low-energy missions to build trust; capability-insufficient ones are reinforced by the Anchor Council. Coverage is not subduing, but recording every clan's "I still don't want to come" as a legal state, leaving the door forever open when it is willing. Under Axiom 6, coverage's meaning also changes: not force-pulling Myriad Ranks onto the same starting line, but letting every clan know it inherently possesses infinity; whether to come and when, this infinity is undiminished.

## Conclusion

99% is not courtesy; it is a military order. Heavenly-Dao Rule 6 writes "Myriad-Rank republicanism" as a quarterly reconciled numerical problem — whether civilization has truly finalized, look at this number.
