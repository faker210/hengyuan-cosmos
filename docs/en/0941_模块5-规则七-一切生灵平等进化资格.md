# Entry 941 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 7 | All Beings' Equal Evolutionary Qualification

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 08 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: qualification knows no high or low

Rule 6 governs "how many clans are covered"; Rule 7 governs "whether everyone is equal after being covered in." The old cosmos's default order is a rigid station hierarchy: the human race above alien beasts, high sects above scattered cultivators, Youth Supremes above ordinary youths, planet consciousness above individuals. Heavenly-Dao Rule 7 must shatter this entire station ladder — all beings, whether carbon-based, energy-based, mechanical-based, or consciousness-based; whether human, beast, plant, or planet; whether talent high or low, are born with completely equal evolutionary qualification. Qualification does not guarantee equal results, but guarantees the door is the same door and the road is the same road.

The six top-level axioms here land on the individual: the republican axiom of Myriad Races directly declares "all beings' equal evolutionary qualification"; the anti-predation axiom negates "bloodline determinism"; the collective-ascension axiom makes every individual's evolution flow into whole-system benefit; the causal self-discipline axiom requires equal qualification not be twisted by "well-meaning special arrangements" into new discrimination; the law-first axiom lets "equality" 's judgment standard be defined first by law, not swayed by public opinion.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: Three Criteria of Equal Qualification

1. **Same admission ticket**: regardless of clan, sex, talent, or birth, Sense-Condensed Realm initiation qualification is identical; bloodline privilege, clan priority, and sex restrictions are forbidden;
2. **Same runway**: from Sense-Condensed Realm to Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, all beings share the same realm ruler and open methods; double standards like "demon cultivators count separately" or "artifact spirits are not people" are forbidden;
3. **Same appeal right**: any being who believes his evolutionary qualification is violated may directly sue the Symbiotic Court, with no station, clan, or language threshold.

Equal qualification is not egalitarianism: contracted-responsibility more-work-more-pay remains, and talent differences are respected, but "talent differences" may not become "qualification differences."

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Self-growth argument**: the Self-Growing Evolution Law (Document 07) points out every being is a unique node in the cosmic causal web; his evolutionary qualification is a node's inherent property, not depending on external enfeoffment;
- **Anti-bloodline-determinism argument**: countless "loser counterattacks" in the Pre-Anchor Era prove birth and talent have extremely low predictive power; depriving qualification by birth is essentially self-castrating civilization's evolutionary potential;
- **Network-fairness argument**: a symbiotic network's health requires equal node-access cost; any discriminatory threshold accumulates friction in the network, ultimately slowing the whole.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Alaya Network automatically opens an "evolutionary-qualification account" for every being at birth, no application or review needed;
- Initiation resources (Sense-Condensed Realm methods, basic energy allocation) are automatically delivered per Rule 6's 99% coverage, no questions of birth;
- A "qualification-discrimination review group" is set up, specifically handling sect, clan, or regional deprivation of specific individuals' qualification;
- Youth-Supreme paths, D-route human-made Dao-rules soul rings, and other special channels are compensation mechanisms to "make up starting-point differences," not privilege channels.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: habitual judging by birth, verbal discrimination, forced into equal-ethics courses;
- **Medium**: publicly depriving some individual/clan's evolutionary qualification, setting thresholds and charging, has the relevant permissions frozen and enters the conversion program;
- **Heavy**: systematically enforcing bloodline rank systems, caste-like cultivation barriers, is handled specially under Entry 953's eradicate-class-solidification clause.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision automatically compares "qualification account" with "actual access" for consistency as the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee checks discriminatory sect regulations as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court accepts qualification litigation as the third layer; each clan and lineage's autonomous organization exercises self-correction over internal discrimination as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: geniuses blocked by birth are the greatest waste; equal qualification releases this batch of potential
- **Anti-predation**: bloodline privilege is predation's heredification; Rule 7 tears it off the birth certificate; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems grade into ranks by "bloodline/protagonist halo"; Heng Yuan Zhou qualification accounts are automatically opened for all, zero review
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: human males and females, alien beasts, plant spirits, planet consciousness, and consciousness-information clans have completely equal qualification
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated civilizations' beings automatically gain qualification accounts, no limit by "outsider" status

Equal qualification must also answer a sharp question: if some are born strivers and some born easygoing, does equality not become empty talk? Heavenly-Dao rules' answer is: equal qualification governs the "door," not "how fast one walks." Strivers, through effort, superimpose more and higher levels of infinity; easygoing ones, by Axiom 6, retain floor basic infinity; the two are completely equal before the door, and each gets what they deserve behind it. Qualification accounts set no performance threshold, and will not freeze admission qualification because some being has not broken through in years; nor set a talent ceiling, and will not assert some being has no path to the Origin Realm because he started slowly. Equality's enemy has never been difference, but writing difference into an insurmountable wall.

## Conclusion

Equal evolutionary qualification is the Myriad-Rank-republican axiom's smallest unit. It ensures: civilization's ceiling is not decided by some clan or some surname, but jointly pushed open by all beings.
