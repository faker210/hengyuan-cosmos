# Entry 942 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 8 | Strong Ones Do Not Ascend Alone

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Document 17 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: one person flying up equals leaving the whole civilization in place

The old cosmos's most chilling scene is the strong one surviving his tribulation, tearing open the void, ascending alone, leaving a realm's beings still struggling in the mire. Ascension is beautified as "personal perfection," yet no one asks: that step he stepped on, was it built by others? Heavenly-Dao Rule 8 judges this picture illegal: in Heng Yuan Zhou, strong ones do not ascend alone. The higher your realm, the how-high-you-can-go is deeply bound with how-deep-Myriad-Ranks-walk — if you want to fly, you must carry everyone up together.

The six top-level axioms erupt centrally here: the collective-ascension axiom is this rule's body, directly requiring "strong ones do not ascend alone, lifting Myriad Ranks forward together"; the republican axiom of Myriad Races decides the scope of "everyone"; the anti-predation axiom negates "using Myriad Ranks as fuel to climb alone to heaven"; the causal self-discipline axiom brings the strong one's ascension impulse into reporting and assessment; the law-first axiom lets "ascension conditions" be defined first by law, with no exceptions for individual strong-one achievement.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: Collective Conditions of Ascension

Rule 8 stipulates any strong one who wants to break through to plane sovereign or even Origin Realm, Multiverse Realm, Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm must simultaneously satisfy:

1. **Myriad-Ranks-progress condition**: his plane/civilization's Myriad-Ranks 99% coverage (Rule 6) has met standard, and the collective average realm is no lower than some baseline;
2. **Feedback record condition**: the strong one's past contributions of increment development, Dao-text openness, and discipleship records are network-verified as positive;
3. **No-solo-run condition**: during breakthrough he may not close his connection to the anchor network, nor take away an exclusive "ascension channel" for private use;
4. **Return-duty condition**: after ascension he must still perform Entry 919's Transcendent perpetual-construction duty, not leave forever.

Missing any of the four, breakthrough certification is not granted.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Causal-binding argument**: the strong one's every achievement arises within Myriad Ranks' causal web; severing the network and ascending alone equals pulling away civilization's load-bearing wall, and the plane left behind necessarily convulses;
- **Efficiency argument**: plane-sovereign strong-one four-dimensional assessment (Entry 911) has already proven, under the structure of force 40 / heart-nature 25 / contribution 20 / responsibility 15, strong ones only pursuing force are eventually backlashed by missing responsibility;
- **Civilizational-closed-loop argument**: what civilizational finalization requires is whole-system closure, not individual strong-one escape; one person ascending while civilization spins in place is loop failure.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Alaya Network generates an "ascension-condition dashboard" for each candidate strong one, publicly showing how far he is short of the four standards;
- Breakthrough certification is double-verified by the Symbiotic Court and Anchor Supervisory Committee jointly; missing any one is not approved;
- For high-position strong ones with deliberate "solo-run" tendency, the network automatically freezes their exclusive channel, guiding them into the "lift Myriad Ranks" route;
- A "lead-the-group ascension" technical path is provided: when a strong one breaks through, he may include fellow disciples and his civilization's synchronized advancement in the breakthrough project, and instead gains law amplification.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: only minding personal progress, neglecting feedback, dashboard long red, public warning;
- **Medium**: artificially suppressing Myriad-Ranks progress to satisfy personal ascension conditions (e.g., monopolizing Dao texts to slow others), permissions frozen, enters conversion program;
- **Heavy**: forcibly severing the network, opening a private ascension channel to escape, is network-wide pursued by the anchor network, stripped of all anchor benefits, handled per the causal self-discipline axiom.

Heavenly-Dao rules do not punish "flying fast," only "leaving people behind."

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's ascension-condition dashboard is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee re-reviews breakthrough applications as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates "solo-run" cases as the third layer; Myriad Ranks' clans have the right to comment on whether their own ascending candidate has fulfilled the lifting duty as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: experience and law insights carried away by solo ascension, if not returned, perish with the individual; this is civilizational-level waste
- **Anti-predation**: negating "using Myriad Ranks as stepping stones to climb alone to heaven"; predation's most powerful scene is legislatively forbidden; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type protagonists attain Dao alone, relatives bask in the halo; Heng Yuan Zhou strong ones who do not lift Myriad Ranks cannot even enter the breakthrough door
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: ascension conditions are bound to 99% coverage, forcing strong ones to direct resources toward the weakest clans
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the lead-the-group ascension path reserves a "clan synchronized leap" interface for the Multiverse Realm and Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm

## Conclusion

Ascension is not an escape pod, but civilization's elevator. Rule 8 declares: the elevator must carry everyone up to count as reaching the top.
