# Entry 943 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 9 | Lift Myriad Ranks Up Together to Evolve

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Document 25 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: being strong means being strong enough to make others strong too

Rule 8 forbids "ascending alone"; Rule 9 further gives a positive path — the strong one not only may not leave, but must actively walk in front, reach back, and lift Myriad Ranks up together to evolve. This is not a moral call, but a Heavenly-Dao-layer mandatory assignment: the higher your power level, the heavier the "lifting task" assigned; if you do not lift, the power itself is recovered in the causal loop. Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules are harder than the old cosmos's "strong-one self-discipline" precisely in that they write "taking disciples, supporting the weak, advancing civilization" from virtue into obligation.

The six top-level axioms run through it: the collective-ascension axiom is this rule's direct source; the republican axiom of Myriad Races defines "the lifted object" as all Myriad Ranks, not some sect or faction; the anti-predation axiom negates the Matthew cycle of "stronger the strong, weaker the weak"; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the lifting act itself carry no manipulation; the law-first axiom lets lifting tasks' measurement, assessment, and exemption be stipulated first by law.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: The Three-Part Lifting Task

1. **Dao-text feedback**: every major breakthrough, he must organize that realm's insights and pitfall records into an open Dao text, uploaded to the Alaya public library (connecting Rule 4);
2. **Discipleship quota**: the strong one must, by power level, take a certain number of juniors under his wing, especially prioritizing Youth Supremes, weak clans, plant spirits, and other starting-difficult groups;
3. **Civilizational engineering**: periodically participate in his plane's increment-development, infrastructure, and ecological-restoration projects, with contribution counted toward the plane-sovereign strong-one four-dimensional assessment's "responsibility 15."

The lifting task is not charity, but directly linked to the strong one's own breakthrough and law-anchor-permission renewal.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Feedback-cycle argument**: the Symbiotic Feedback Cycle Mechanism (Document 25) proves the energy flow the strong one feeds back to Myriad Ranks, amplified by the Myriad-Rank-origin bidirectional cycle and returned to the strong one, far exceeds what he gains by direct predation — lifting others is lifting oneself;
- **System-resilience argument**: a civilization with only a few strong ones collapses at disaster; whole-system Myriad-Ranks lifting is civilization's fundamental risk resistance;
- **Anti-Matthew argument**: the old cosmos's "strong stay strong" Matthew effect is precisely the source of class solidification; Rule 9 reverses the Matthew cycle into "strong ones grow stronger through lifting others, juniors grow stronger through being lifted and catch up."

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Alaya Network generates a "lifting task work order" for each strong one, dispatched by realm level, with progress real-time public;
- Discipleship quality is double-scored by disciples' growth records and juniors' comments, eliminating "name-only master-disciple";
- Lifting-task completion directly affects law-anchor-permission renewal and the plane-sovereign strong-one four-dimensional assessment score;
- Strong ones genuinely stuck in cultivation bottlenecks and temporarily unable to lift are allowed Quest-Sediment-Domain-study-style task exemptions, but must make it up after returning.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: work-order progress lags, network reminder and public notice;
- **Medium**: name-only discipleship, forged feedback records, law-anchor permissions frozen, recorded in Alaya's integrity archive;
- **Heavy**: under the name of "lifting" actually controlling — turning the lifted into private dependent forces, handled heavily under Rule 5 (no private hoarding no monopoly) and Rule 19 (eradicate class solidification).

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's work-order system is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee spot-checks master-disciple authenticity as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates "using lifting to control" cases as the third layer; the juniors being mentored and the Youth-Supreme supervision corps exercise reverse-commentary on the strong one's lifting-duty performance as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: a strong one's experience, if not fed back, goes to zero with his death; lifting makes experience compound
- **Anti-predation**: reversing the "strong ones sucking the weak" Matthew cycle into the symbiotic cycle of "strong ones lifting the weak"; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type strong ones treat followers as private soldiers and monopolize the halo; Heng Yuan Zhou strong ones who do not take disciples cannot even renew permissions
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: discipleship quotas are preferentially dispatched to weak clans', Youth Supremes', plant spirits', and planet consciousnesses' juniors
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cross-plane strong ones may take on newly incorporated civilizations' discipleship projects, extending the lifting radius to all of Heng Yuan Zhou

The Myriad-Ranks-lifting work order has one easily overlooked layer of meaning: it rewrites the strong-one-and-junior relationship from "gift" into "contract." In the old cosmos, a strong one taking a disciple was alms, grace, a favor that could be withdrawn anytime; Heavenly-Dao Rule 9 writes discipleship as a hard condition of the strong one's own breakthrough and permission renewal, so the disciple is no longer the strong one's appendage, but the strong one's partner in completing his own causality. This relationship reversal lets juniors owe no abject gratitude and need not grovel; the strong one also need not repeatedly flaunt "how many I have taken" as moral capital. Lifting becomes a two-way rush, not top-down alms.

## Conclusion

"Lift Myriad Ranks up together to evolve" is not emotionalism, but a Heavenly-Dao-layer work order. Rule 9 makes the strong one's every growth simultaneously a growth for Myriad Ranks.
