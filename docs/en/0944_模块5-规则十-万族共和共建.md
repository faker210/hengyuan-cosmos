# Entry 944 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 10 | Myriad-Rank Republican Co-Construction

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 03, 05 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: civilization is no one's private enterprise, but Myriad Ranks' construction site

Rule 9 speaks of individual strong ones lifting Myriad Ranks; Rule 10 pulls the lens to the whole civilization — the entire Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's governance, construction, and rule revision are not decided behind closed doors by a few Transcended Ones on the Anchor Council, but through Myriad-Rank republican co-construction. The human race, alien beasts, plant spirits, planet consciousness, and consciousness-information clans — the five lineages — must all have seats, votes, and veto power at the four levels of legislation, administration, judiciary, and supervision. This is Myriad-Rank republicanism's key step from "equal qualification" toward "shared governance."

The six top-level axioms here constitute the governance framework: the republican axiom of Myriad Races is this rule's body; the collective-ascension axiom decides co-construction's direction is whole-system ascension, not sharing spoils; the anti-predation axiom negates "the strong deciding for Myriad Ranks"; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the co-construction process respect each clan's causality, not forcibly assimilate; the law-first axiom lets "co-construction rules, seat allocation, voting procedures" be nailed first by the charter and Heavenly-Dao rules, not hijacked by temporary majorities.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: Three-Layer Co-Construction Structure

1. **Legislative co-construction**: Anchor Council seats are evenly allocated by Myriad Ranks' five lineages and planes; all charter amendments and new Heavenly-Dao rules must pass Myriad-Rank representatives' vote;
2. **Administrative co-construction**: the Symbiotic Executive Committee's department heads are recommended by clans and publicly elected, with no single lineage long monopolizing;
3. **Judicial and supervisory co-construction**: Symbiotic Court and Anchor Supervisory Committee jurors and investigators are randomly drawn from Myriad Ranks' clans, including Youth Supremes and planet-consciousness representatives.

Co-construction is not bickering over every matter: routine affairs are handled quickly per charter authorization; only major-direction issues trigger Myriad-Rank voting.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Civilizational-contract argument**: the civilizational contract signed when Myriad-Rank republicanism was established is essentially "sovereignty resides in Myriad Ranks"; co-construction is this sovereignty's daily exercise;
- **Pluralistic-wisdom argument**: the five lineages each have unique understandings of cosmic laws (e.g., consciousness-information clans excel at data, planet consciousness at ecology); co-construction is closer to truth than single-clan decision;
- **Anti-dictatorship argument**: history repeatedly proves handing civilization's direction to a single strong one/clan eventually alienates into predation; co-governance is the only sustainable stable structure.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Alaya Network provides Myriad-Rank representatives remote-attendance and anonymous/real-name dual-track voting interfaces;
- Council agendas, voting records, and amendment drafts are network-wide public, all traceable;
- A "any agenda materially affecting some clan entitles that clan to speak" principle is set: any topic substantively affecting some clan, that clan's representative has forced speech and delayed-vote rights;
- Newly incorporated civilizations with insufficient co-construction capability receive "lift-you-on-and-walk-a-stretch-along" council coaching from established civilizations, not having their rights exercised on their behalf.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: council procedure oversights, some clan's representatives marginalized, correct within a time limit;
- **Medium**: manipulating votes, diluting some clan's seats, legislating behind closed doors, relevant resolutions automatically void, responsible persons' post permissions frozen;
- **Heavy**: under the name of "efficiency" canceling Myriad-Rank voting, moving toward single-clan dictatorship, launches charter defense under the Anti-Predation Committee procedure (connecting Entry 901's emergency charter-defense mechanism).

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's agenda-public system is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee checks seat allocation and voting authenticity as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates "procedure-violating" cases as the third layer; Myriad Ranks' clans retain the original right to directly submit constitutional-amendment motions to the Anchor Council as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: avoiding single-clan decision-making's systematic blind spots; co-construction lets civilization detour less
- **Anti-predation**: negating "the strong act on heaven's behalf," eradicating dictatorial-predation soil from governance structure; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type civilizations rely on Gu Chensha's personal will that words settle everything; Heng Yuan Zhou major resolutions require Myriad-Rank representatives' vote, and a single clan may veto
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the five lineages, Youth Supremes, planet consciousness, and consciousness-information clans all have legislative seats
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated civilizations gain council seats upon access, seats automatically expand as civilization expands

Myriad-Rank republican co-construction in actual operation must also solve a technical puzzle: Myriad Ranks speak different languages, perceive time differently, and have differing council habits. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's solution is the Alaya Network's "cross-intent translation" interface — clan representatives need not first learn each other's language; the network directly translates causal intent and value demands into forms the other side can understand. The council hall is thus not some clan's cultural showroom, but Myriad-Rank cultures' convergence point. When a newly incorporated civilization's representative sits in council for the first time, what he feels is not being Sinicized or assimilated, but being seriously heard. Co-construction's quality ultimately depends on whether this hearing is real, long-term, and equal to every niche civilization.

## Conclusion

Myriad-Rank republican co-construction is civilizational finalization's political form. It ensures: no matter how laws evolve or how the worldview expands, the steering wheel always remains in Myriad Ranks' own hands.
