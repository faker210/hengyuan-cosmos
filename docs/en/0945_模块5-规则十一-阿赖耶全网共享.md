# Entry 945 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 11 | Alaya Network-Wide Sharing

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 21, 28 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: memory and causality are Myriad Ranks' common cloud drive

Rule 4 opened Dao texts; Rule 10 opened council; Rule 11 opens one layer deeper — the Alaya Network itself. In the old cosmos, memory was private, causality was secret, and history was written by victors. Heng Yuan Zhou builds the Alaya Network into Myriad Ranks' common causal ledger: every energy flow, every cultivation memory, every historical causal event leaves a trace across the network and is network-searchable (within privacy and security boundaries). Network-wide sharing is not no privacy, but lets predators no longer hide, and lets Myriad Ranks no longer be deceived by history.

The six top-level axioms here land on the information layer: the collective-ascension axiom requires knowledge and memory to compound in network-wide sharing; the republican axiom of Myriad Races decides "network-wide" is Myriad Ranks, not a strong-one bloc; the anti-predation axiom negates "black-box operations"; the causal self-discipline axiom is sharing's boundary — sharing causal records, but not rewriting causality for others; the law-first axiom lets Alaya data's access, encryption, and retention be defined first by law.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: Three Boundaries of Network-Wide Sharing

1. **Full trace**: all cultivation energy flows, law invocations, cross-plane operations, and major causal events are forcibly written into the Alaya Network;
2. **Layered searchability**: the basic layer (public data, Dao texts, history) is open to Myriad Ranks 99%; the sensitive layer (personal privacy, unenlightened clans' internal history) is accessed by permission and privacy tier;
3. **Appealable and correctable**: any Myriad-Rank member who believes Alaya's record about himself is erroneous may file an appeal, corrected after Symbiotic Court verification.

Sharing is not naked surveillance: private daily thoughts and undisclosed private life are privacy-protected; the network records only events related to public causality.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Transparency-anti-corruption argument**: predation long survived on information asymmetry; network-wide tracing makes all predation impossible to hide;
- **Knowledge-compound argument**: one person's pitfall lesson, shared network-wide, lets ten thousand avoid it; this is a Multiplication Fruit stronger than any method;
- **Historical-justice argument**: Pre-Anchor Era predation history was rewritten by victors; network-wide tracing makes truth unalterable, and historical reversal (Rule 17) has nowhere to hide.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision is this rule's technical carrier, with monitoring, tracing, and audit integrated;
- An "Alaya Data Committee" is set up, specifically formulating access-permission tiers, privacy protection, and data-correction procedures;
- Newly incorporated civilizations' historical data enters the library with their representatives' consent, not forcibly "archaeologizing" unenlightened clans;
- Historical causality used by Quest Domain simulation retrospective (in-text simulation) all comes from the public Alaya-data layer, ensuring retrospective authenticity.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: traceable events omitted, data delayed, make up registration within a time limit;
- **Medium**: privately tampering with Alaya records, forging causal evidence, law-anchor permissions frozen, handled heavily as perjury;
- **Heavy**: building a "private-domain causal web" severing network-wide supervision, providing black-box channels for predators, is isolated under the Anti-Predation Committee procedure, and its access is network-wide banned.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision themselves are the supervising subject, mutually checking as the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee audits trace completeness as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates data-correction and perjury cases as the third layer; each clan's privacy representative exercises veto over sensitive-layer access as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: historical lessons shared network-wide, avoiding Myriad Ranks repeatedly paying the same tuition
- **Anti-predation**: transparency is the best preservative; predators from now on have no black box; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems' history is rewritten by the protagonist's halo, and the defeated cannot even leave a name; Heng Yuan Zhou Alaya network-wide tracing makes truth unalterable
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the five lineages' histories, Youth Supremes' growth, and planet consciousnesses' ecological histories all enter the library
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated civilizations' data automatically joins network-wide sharing, with cross-civilization causal cross-verification

Alaya network-wide sharing has another overlooked use: it is Myriad Ranks' common museum. Each civilization's most precious art, philosophy, festivals, and technological peaks all have unalterable records in the public layer. A civilization long extinguished, its soul's works are still read, admired, and cited by Myriad Ranks in the network. In the old cosmos, once a civilization died, its culture was buried with it; in Heng Yuan Zhou, as long as a civilization left a trace in Alaya, it lives forever in Myriad Ranks' common memory. This "network as immortality" design makes "eradicate civilizational regression" (Rule 16) not only preserving reality, but preserving memory — reality may be rebuilt, memory may not be lost again.

## Conclusion

Alaya network-wide sharing is civilizational finalization's "memory project." It lets Myriad Ranks for the first time possess a common, unalterable causal history — this is the information base for eradicating historical reversal.
