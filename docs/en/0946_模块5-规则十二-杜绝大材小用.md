# Entry 946 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 12 | Eliminating Great-Material-for-Small-Use

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 10, 23 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: let everyone stand where he should

Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules must not only prevent "the strong bullying the weak," but also prevent another kind of waste — trapping a great talent in a small post. A genius who could deduce cosmic laws, if pressed by birth, opportunity, or prejudice into menial labor at the Sense-Condensed Realm, is civilization's greatest energy drain. Rule 12 writes "eliminate great-material-for-small-use" as a Heavenly-Dao duty: the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization has the responsibility to discover every buried material and place it where it can shine; equally, it does not allow small-material-for-great-use either — that would drag the whole engineering down. Symmetric in both directions is true matching people to their posts.

The six top-level axioms run through it: the collective-ascension axiom requires "eliminate great-material-for-small-use" to release all potential; the republican axiom of Myriad Races ensures the discovery process asks not about birth; the anti-predation axiom negates "geniuses confined by sects/the strong"; the causal self-discipline axiom requires post adjustment respect individual will and existing causality; the law-first axiom lets "talent" assessment, "post" matching, and "transfer" procedure be defined first by law.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: Three Iron Laws of Person-Post Matching

1. **Discovery**: the Alaya Network continuously profiles every being's talent inclination and adaptation to systems, automatically marking "buried high potential";
2. **Matching**: the 300 Origin Cultivation Systems (Document 10) give optimal adaptation per individual profile, avoiding energy waste from "wrong-system placement";
3. **Mobility**: those mismatched have the right to free, barrier-free post transfer, school transfer, and system switch; the original unit may not obstruct.

Eliminating great-material-for-small-use does not force geniuses to "be great": the individual has the right to choose an ordinary, peaceful life; the network only prompts and recommends, not forces.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **20%-activation argument**: the 20% Activation Strategy Plan (Document 23) points out placing the key 20% high-potential nodes correctly produces the largest overall energy jump;
- **Self-growth-utility argument**: the Self-Growing Evolution Law points out every being's talent is the cosmos's unique evolutionary increment; placing it wrong zeroes the increment;
- **Anti-confinement argument**: old-cosmos sects confining geniuses as private property and locking them in small posts is a form of predatory possession; Rule 12 abolishes it at the allocation level.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Alaya Network "person-post profiling system" continuously generates talent radars for Myriad-Rank individuals, viewable and correctable by the individual anytime;
- High-potential individuals receive "recommended post/system" notices, and may accept, decline, or apply for re-assessment;
- A "great-material-for-small-use appeal channel" is set: individuals believing themselves buried may apply for independent assessment and resource reallocation;
- For posts genuinely small-material-for-great-use endangering engineering, the Anchor Supervisory Committee coordinates smooth post adjustment, not sudden dismissal.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: post long mismatched, profile not updated, network automatically reminds;
- **Medium**: sects/units under the name of "lineage"/"contract" confining geniuses, obstructing mobility, have the relevant unit's permissions frozen;
- **Heavy**: deliberately pressing high-potential geniuses into dead posts, or forcibly pushing incompetents high, causing civilizational-engineering accidents, relevant decision-makers are pursued back to responsibility.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's person-post profiling system is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee spot-checks "confinement-style employment" as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates mobility-right-infringement cases as the third layer; the individual himself and each clan's talent committee exercise final appeal over person-post matching as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: this rule is "anti-energy waste" itself, eliminating great-material-for-small-use and small-material-for-great-use from both ends
- **Anti-predation**: abolishing the old rule of sects confining geniuses as private property; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type geniuses are "seen" via the protagonist's halo, other geniuses fend for themselves; Heng Yuan Zhou's profiling system automatically finds talent, asking not protagonist-or-not
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: human males and females, Youth Supremes, alien beasts, plant spirits, and consciousness-information clans each have a talent radar, no species limit
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated civilizations' high-potential individuals automatically enter the profiling system, with cross-civilization post scheduling

Eliminating great-material-for-small-use's most delicate operation is how to judge "truly great material" vs "self-styled great material." The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization relies neither on self-evaluation nor on a single talent scout, but on Alaya Network's long profiling plus multi-source feedback. If a Youth Supreme repeatedly hits walls in the deduction system yet is in his element in the life-cycle system, the network will actively suggest switching; if a self-important striver's data shows his talent is mediocre, the network also gently hints "ordinary and peaceful is also a legal choice." Judgment rests in no one's hands, but at the confluence of Myriad-Rank feedback and causal records. This both prevents great-material-for-small-use and small-material-for-great-use, and further prevents "overestimated geniuses" from wrongly occupying engineering critical points.

## Conclusion

Eliminating great-material-for-small-use turns "Myriad Ranks 99% potential" from a slogan into a post table. Civilizational finalization's efficiency depends on whether every material stands where it should.
