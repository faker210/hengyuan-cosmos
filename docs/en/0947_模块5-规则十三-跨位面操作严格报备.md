# Entry 947 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 13 | Strict Cross-Plane-Operation Reporting

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Document 28 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: before going to someone else's cosmos, first file a report

The causal self-discipline axiom's most easily broken opening is cross-plane. Old-cosmos strong ones cross planes with a thought, casually remake lower realms, and treat other realms as test fields, never questioned. Heavenly-Dao Rule 13 welds this opening shut: any strong one, any civilizational engineering, for any cross-plane operation — whether to develop, aid, research, or retreat — must first strictly report to the Anchor Supervisory Committee, stating purpose, means, and expected causal impact, and may execute only after approval. Reporting is not distrust of the strong, but preventing any strong one from privately writing history for two planes' beings.

The six top-level axioms here centrally constrain the strong one's outward conduct: the causal self-discipline axiom is this rule's direct source; the republican axiom of Myriad Races decides the interfered plane's beings are also "masters," and no outsider may decide for them; the anti-predation axiom negates "treating cross-planes as colonies"; the collective-ascension axiom requires cross-plane purpose be mutual benefit, not unilateral gain; the law-first axiom lets the "report—approve—trace" procedure be nailed first by law.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: Three Reporting Requirements

1. **Purpose report**: state the cross-plane operation's purpose — aid, development, research, evacuation, pursuing fugitive — classified and registered;
2. **Means report**: state the power level to be used, possible laws invoked, and estimated duration;
3. **Causal-impact report**: assess potential disturbance to the interfered plane, to this civilization, and to the cross-plane causal web, with a mitigation plan attached.

Situations like urgently pursuing predation fugitives allow "execute first, supplement report later," but must supplement the report in the shortest time and accept retroactive review.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Other-sovereignty argument**: the Cross-Plane Anchor Connection (Document 28) points out each plane is an independent causal subject; an outsider intervening without sovereignty is predation against that plane's beings;
- **Causal-chain argument**: cross-plane operations' causal impact far exceeds intra-plane; one rash intervention may stir civilizational regression in another realm, so pre-assessment is required;
- **Abuse-prevention argument**: behind the old cosmos's romantic narrative of "great gods touring lower realms" often hides hidden domination of weak planes; the reporting system puts this domination on the table.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Alaya Network sets a "cross-plane reporting interface"; all cross-plane anchor invocations must pass through this interface;
- Unreported cross-plane operations are automatically identified by the network and their plane-shuttling ability frozen until reporting is made up;
- Report approval is led by the Anchor Supervisory Committee, with related plane representatives and consciousness-information clan causal analysts jointly co-signing;
- Execution is fully traced; afterward a "causal-impact retrospective report" must be submitted, verifying whether the original assessment was accurate.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: incomplete report, rough assessment, correct within a time limit;
- **Medium**: unreported cross-plane operation, plane-shuttling ability frozen, enters conversion program;
- **Heavy**: under the name of cross-plane operation actually predating — lower-realm mining, colonization, harvesting — handled heavily under Rule 3 and Rule 18 (eradicate cyclical harvesting).

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's cross-plane reporting interface is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee's approval and post-event retrospective is the second layer; the interfered plane's representatives have the final say on "whether welcomed, whether intervention excessive" as the third layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates cross-plane predation cases as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: avoiding chain disasters from cross-plane misoperation, saving massive repair energy
- **Anti-predation**: legislatively banning the old habit of "treating cross-planes as a back garden"; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type strong ones cross planes with a thought, lower realms fend for themselves; Heng Yuan Zhou cross-plane first reports, and lower-realm representatives have veto
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: all beings in the interfered plane (including planet consciousness and unenlightened clans) are protected
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the cross-plane reporting system automatically applies to new planes as the worldview expands infinitely, with no exceptions

The cross-plane reporting system's flexibility in emergencies must also be made clear. Pursuing predation fugitives, intercepting cyclical-harvesting systems, and rescuing weak planes about to be devoured — these three situations allow "execute first, supplement report later." But flexibility is not a get-out-of-jail-free card: when supplementing the report afterward, a complete causal-impact retrospective must be submitted; if "emergency" is found to be an excuse, it is still handled as unreported afterward. The reporting system wants responsibility, not process; truly urgent rescue will not be delayed by process, and what must be guarded is using "emergency" as a master key. This flexible design gives the reporting system teeth, yet does not pedantically mishandle true disasters.

## Conclusion

Cross-plane reporting is the causal self-discipline axiom's first gate at cosmic scale. It ensures: no matter how long the strong one's foot, he may not walk into someone else's cosmos without knocking.
