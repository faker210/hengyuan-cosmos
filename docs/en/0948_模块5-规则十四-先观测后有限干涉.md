# Entry 948 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 14 | Observe-First-Then-Limited-Intervene

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Document 28 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: see clearly first, then touch gently

Rule 13 governs "whether cross-plane needs reporting"; Rule 14 governs "after reporting, how to act appropriately." Heavenly-Dao rules give an extremely restrained answer: observe first, then limited intervention. Any cross-plane mission, aid team, or research team, after entering an unfamiliar or semi-familiar plane, the first thing is not to remake, but to observe long-term, low-energy, imperceptibly — see clearly the plane's causal structure, civilizational stage, and beings' state, then decide whether to intervene and to what extent. "Limited" is the soul: if you can not act, do not; if small action suffices, do not act big; if remote suffices, do not come in person.

The six top-level axioms here become an aesthetic of restraint: the causal self-discipline axiom is this rule's direct source; the republican axiom of Myriad Races decides the observed are also subjects, not specimens; the anti-predation axiom negates "remaking by our template as soon as we enter"; the collective-ascension axiom requires intervention's purpose be to help them self-ascend, not replace their ascension; the law-first axiom lets "observation period length, intervention-intensity tiering, withdrawal conditions" be stipulated first by law.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: Tiering of Observation and Intervention

1. **Observation period**: newly contacted planes default to the lowest-energy observation period; the mission does not expose, contact, or disturb, only records causal structure, with duration set by civilizational stage;
2. **Limited-intervention tiers**:
   - Tier 1: only share knowledge (connecting Rule 15), do not enter;
   - Tier 2: provide technology/resource aid under the other side's explicit request;
   - Tier 3: emergency rescue (e.g., the plane about to be devoured by predation remnants), requires Anchor Supervisory Committee special approval;
3. **Withdrawal conditions**: once intervention purpose is achieved, or the interfered side explicitly says "please leave," the mission immediately withdraws, with no stationing or trusteeship.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Self-growth argument**: the Self-Growing Evolution Law points out each plane's civilization has its internal rhythm; excessive external intervention breaks its causal trunk, causing more insidious "stunted development" than disaster;
- **Veil-of-ignorance argument**: when the observer does not understand the plane's values, taboos, and ecology, any "well-meaning remake" may be an offense; observing first is basic respect for other sovereignty;
- **Anti-colony argument**: old-cosmos cross-plane colonization's first step is precisely "board first, pay later" forced intervention; Rule 14 snuffs out colonization possibility from the process.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- Cross-plane missions are equipped with "low-energy observation kits": consciousness remote perception primary, material presence secondary;
- Observation-period data is automatically archived in the Alaya Network; missions may not privately hoard or bring it into unreported uses;
- Intervention-tier upgrades must be approved level by level; Tier-2 missions are forbidden from unilaterally upgrading to Tier-3 action;
- The interfered plane's civilizational representatives have a one-vote veto over "whether to accept intervention, which tier to accept."

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: rushing to contact before the observation period ends, return to observation state within a time limit;
- **Medium**: unrequested forced aid, remaking under the name of "for your own good," cross-plane permissions frozen, enters conversion program;
- **Heavy**: under the name of limited intervention actually colonizing, turning other planes into vassals, handled heavily under Rule 10 (Myriad-Rank republican co-construction) and Rule 17 (eradicate historical reversal).

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's cross-plane action log is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee approves intervention-tier upgrades as the second layer; the interfered plane's civilizational representatives exercise veto as the third layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates "overstepping-intervention" cases as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: premature, excessive intervention's civilizational remaking is massive drain; observe-first-then-limited-intervene saves this energy
- **Anti-predation**: negating "savior-style remaking," eradicating cross-plane colonization from the process; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type strong ones descend into lower realms and reshape a region with "I am the Heavenly Dao"; Heng Yuan Zhou missions first stake out and observe, and leave if lower realms are unwelcome
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: all beings in the observed/interfered plane, including planet consciousness and unenlightened clans, have their rhythm respected
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: each newly contacted plane automatically enters the observation-period process, the template expanding with the worldview

Observe-first-then-limited-intervene's "limited" has another layer: the mission's own power must also self-restrain. Even if you are an Origin Realm strong one, entering an unenlightened plane, you should converge your power to a level compatible with that plane's ecology — the pressure an Origin Realm one releases casually is catastrophe to local beings. The observation period's low energy is not only "not disturbing," but "not letting our own strength itself become a violation." This self-restraint seems near masochistic to old-cosmos strong ones; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, it is basic manners for cross-plane presence — you enter someone else's home, so you must tone down your aura.

## Conclusion

Observe first, because we do not know; limited intervention, because we know our own limits. Rule 14 writes "know as you know" into cross-plane Heavenly-Dao discipline.
