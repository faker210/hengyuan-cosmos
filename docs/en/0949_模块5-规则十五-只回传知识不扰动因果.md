# Entry 949 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 15 | Knowledge-Return-Only Without Causality Disturbance

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Document 21 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: give knowledge, do not live their lives for them

The subtlest measure in cross-plane aid is "how much to give, how to give." Too much, and the other side henceforth lives on external aid, its own causal trunk growing crooked; too little, and it fails Myriad Ranks' common identity. Heavenly-Dao Rule 15 gives the standard answer: knowledge-return-only, no causality disturbance. We may send over calculated Dao texts, lessons learned from trial and error, and scientific methods, but do not choose the route for them, do not make decisions for them, do not take our values as the only correct ones, and do not intervene in their internal struggles. Knowledge is a gift; causality is their field — send seeds, do not farm for them.

The six top-level axioms here become aid ethics: the causal self-discipline axiom is this rule's direct source; the republican axiom of Myriad Races decides the aided are masters, not alms recipients; the anti-predation axiom negates "using knowledge as a control tool"; the collective-ascension axiom requires knowledge return ultimately help them self-ascend; the law-first axiom lets "what knowledge may be transmitted, what may not, and how to withdraw after transmission" be defined first by law.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: Three Red Lines of Knowledge Return

1. **Transmit methods only, not will**: return Dao texts, technology, and lessons, but do not specify "which road they must take"; the route is self-decided by their Myriad Ranks;
2. **Transmit public layer only, not privacy layer**: cross-plane return uses only Alaya public-layer data, carrying no other clan's privacy and not misappropriating other clans' causal archives;
3. **Withdraw after transmission**: after knowledge is delivered, the mission withdraws to observation position, no long-term staking out, no supervising "whether used correctly," allowing the other side to digest at its own rhythm.

Special red line: do not prematurely return to unenlightened planes "leap-style knowledge" that would destroy their existing civilizational structure (e.g., directly sending nuclear-weapon-level methods); it must match their civilizational stage.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Self-growth argument**: the Self-Growing Evolution Law points out the causal trunk must be grown by the civilization itself; an outside force choosing the route for it equals turning it into our extension, not an independent civilization;
- **Anti-knowledge-colony argument**: old-cosmos cross-plane predation often wears the coat of "sending advanced knowledge," actually tying the other side to its own track; Rule 15 returns knowledge to its gift nature;
- **Information-matching argument**: knowledge once far exceeding the aided side's civilizational stage causes civilizational rupture and value collapse, and is instead harm.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- Cross-plane knowledge return must pass a "knowledge-adaptation assessment": whether the knowledge matches the aided plane's civilizational stage;
- Return content goes through an Alaya public-layer encrypted channel, fully traced, with no mission's personal will smuggled in;
- The aided plane may at any time request "stop returning" or "view only a certain category," and the mission complies immediately;
- Return effects are not "use-assessed": whether to use it and how, depends entirely on the aided side's will.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: return content slightly ahead of the aided stage, adjust within a time limit;
- **Medium**: using knowledge as leverage to coerce the other side into taking sides, forcing adoption of our route, cross-plane permissions frozen, enters conversion program;
- **Heavy**: using returned knowledge to manufacture internal division, fostering agents, manipulating its politics, handled heavily under Rule 10 and Rule 17.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's cross-plane channel log is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee checks knowledge adaptability as the second layer; the aided plane's representatives have the right to terminate "whether to continue receiving" as the third layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates "knowledge manipulation" cases as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: avoiding both sides' energy drain from "sent uselessly / sent in the wrong direction"
- **Anti-predation**: legislatively cutting the colonial hidden line of "knowledge as control tool"; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type strong ones "preach" to establish authority, disciples obey absolutely; Heng Yuan Zhou withdraws after sending knowledge, and the other side uses it as it pleases
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the five lineages, unenlightened clans, and planet consciousness each have a "matched to its stage" knowledge-return package
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the knowledge-adaptation assessment template is automatically generated as new civilizations are incorporated

Knowledge-return-only without causality disturbance also especially forbids a hidden intervention: value judgment. The mission may not publicly assert "our civilization is superior to yours," nor smuggle in "your customs are backward" judgments when returning knowledge. Knowledge may be given neutrally; value judgments must be left to the aided side itself. Even if some plane's customs genuinely look problematic from the anchor perspective, they may only be slowly influenced through later equal exchange, never forcibly remade under the self-claim of "advanced." Behind this restraint is a belief: whether a civilization is good or bad, only the Myriad Ranks within it have the right to conclude; no matter how strong an outsider, he is only a guest, not an examiner.

## Conclusion

Knowledge-return-only without causality disturbance is the causal self-discipline axiom's gentlest yet hardest expression: I give you the seeds; you grow in your own shape.
