# Entry 950 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 16 | Eradicate Civilizational Regression

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Document 04 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: civilization may not regress from "having books" back to "knotted cords"

The Pre-Anchor Era's most despairing thing was not some individual's failure, but the whole civilization periodically regressing — just developed to where it could build law ships, a predatory war burned the books, killed off the people, and lost the technology; tens of thousands of years of achievement went to zero overnight, descendants crawling out of caves again. Heavenly-Dao Rule 16 must eradicate this "civilizational-level suicide" at the cosmic-law layer: once the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization reaches some development baseline, any attempt to pull it back to a lower stage is judged by Heavenly-Dao rules as an attack on all Myriad Ranks, not an "internal affair." This is the causal self-discipline axiom's projection on civilizational time axis.

The six top-level axioms here aim at the time dimension: the causal self-discipline axiom directly requires "eradicate civilizational regression"; the republican axiom of Myriad Races decides regression harms all Myriad Ranks' descendants; the anti-predation axiom points out regression's root is predation; the collective-ascension axiom requires civilization advance not retreat, whole-system lifting; the law-first axiom lets "regression baseline, regression judgment, regression response" be nailed first by law.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: Three Forms of Regression and Their Eradication

1. **Technological regression**: key methods, technology, and knowledge lost on a large scale immediately triggers "civilizational seed-preservation engineering" — Alaya public-library backups must immediately fully restore to the affected civilization;
2. **Structural regression**: civilizational governance regresses from republicanism to dictatorship, from openness to closure; the Anchor Council launches charter defense, not recognizing the post-regression pseudo-government;
3. **Demographic regression**: large-scale being death from predatory war and cyclical harvesting triggers Myriad-Rank relief and causal restoration.

Eradication is not "post-hoc fire-fighting," but "pre-event locking": all civilizational-development achievements have redundant backups in the Alaya Network, and regression attempts are alerted in their bud stage.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Historical-retrospective argument**: Document 04 proves predatory civilizations' endgame is necessarily regression; the symbiotic system is the only stably advancing structure, so eradicating regression = eradicating predatory restoration;
- **Irreversible-loss argument**: beings who died in civilizational regression and knowledge lost cannot be resurrected by any cultivation means — this is true irreversibility, so it must be prevented beforehand;
- **Descendants'-rights argument**: later Myriad Ranks have the right to stand on predecessors' shoulders; no one has the right to burn predecessors' books.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Alaya Network maintains a "development-baseline dashboard" for each civilization, monitoring its technology, structure, and population three indicators;
- Abnormal indicator decline triggers a "regression warning," and the Anchor Supervisory Committee dispatches personnel to observe first, then intervene;
- Key development achievements are redundantly backed up across multiple planes and media, preventing single-point destruction from wiping out all;
- Post-regression civilizational recovery takes the "knowledge return" path (Rule 15), with no external trusteeship, letting it rebuild itself.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: local regression from natural disaster, relief launched, no liability pursued;
- **Medium**: regression from man-made policy error, relevant decision-makers suspended, causal retrospective made up;
- **Heavy**: deliberately arson-burning books, launching predatory wars, fostering pseudo-governments to regress civilization, is isolated and tried under the Anti-Predation Committee procedure, with no leniency.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's development-baseline dashboard is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee accepts regression warnings as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates "deliberate regression" cases as the third layer; Myriad Ranks' descendants' representatives exercise supervision over "protecting predecessors' achievements" as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: civilizational regression is the cosmos's greatest repeated waste — tens of thousands of years of achievement going to zero; eradicating it saves immeasurable energy
- **Anti-predation**: regression's root is predation; eradicating regression = eradicating predatory restoration; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type civilizations after the protagonist's death often rapidly collapse, books gone and people dead; Heng Yuan Zhou development achievements are network-wide backed up and cannot regress
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the five lineages' civilizations' regression risks are monitored as one, with no relief abandonment because of weakness
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated civilizations automatically enter development-baseline monitoring, expanding with the worldview

Eradicating civilizational regression must in execution distinguish two kinds of "slow": one is natural slow determined by internal rhythm, which is healthy; the other is slow suppressed by external force and deliberately maintained by predation remnants, which is the regression precursor. The Alaya Network monitors not "progress not fast enough," but "whether abnormal decline appears" — lost technological achievements, collapsed governance structure, large-scale being death. Distinguishing "slow" from "regression" is to avoid misjudging easygoing civilizations and civilizations growing at their own rhythm as regression. Under Axiom 6, easygoing ones' slowness is legal; Heavenly-Dao rules only rescue "abnormal decline," and do not urge "content with the floor."

## Conclusion

Eradicating civilizational regression is civilizational finalization taking responsibility for "time." It ensures: from today on, every step Myriad Ranks take counts, and will not be returned to zero by one fire.
