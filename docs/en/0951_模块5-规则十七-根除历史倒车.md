# Entry 951 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 17 | Eradicate Historical Reversal

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Document 21 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: history may not be rewritten by victors, nor pushed backward by "nostalgics"

Rule 16 guards against regression of "civilizational level"; Rule 17 guards against reversal of "historical narrative." In the Pre-Anchor Era, once an ambitious one came to power, the first blade always cut history: burning the previous dynasty's archives, distorting predatory history, writing aggression as unification, and writing victims as getting what they deserved. Then using forged history to educate descendants, making the next generation willingly walk the old road. Heavenly-Dao Rule 17 declares: history may not be rewritten, and the reverse gear may not be engaged again — the Alaya network-wide traced causal history is the sole truth; anyone under the name of "reversal of verdicts" or "correcting historical views" actually running historical reversal has offended the Heavenly Dao.

The six top-level axioms here aim at the memory dimension: the causal self-discipline axiom is this rule's direct source; the republican axiom of Myriad Races decides history is Myriad Ranks' common property, not victors' private property; the anti-predation axiom points out historical reversal is predation's ideological restoration; the collective-ascension axiom requires historical education serve advancement, not nostalgia; the law-first axiom lets "historical truth, verdict-reversal standards, reversal judgment" be jointly adjudicated by Alaya data and the Symbiotic Court.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: Three Points of Eradicating Historical Reversal

1. **Truth over emotion**: Alaya Network-traced causal records are the highest historical evidence; all textbooks, propaganda, and education must be consistent with them;
2. **Verdict-reversal requires evidence**: re-examining history based on new evidence is allowed, but one may not use "reversal" to whitewash predators, aggressors, and city-sackers;
3. **Nostalgia may not become policy**: old eras may be studied and old cultures appreciated, but old eras' predatory systems, rank systems, and blood-combat systems may not be restored as current policy.

The line between historical reversal and academic freedom: academically studying old systems and Quest Domain simulation-retrospecting old civilizations is lawful and encouraged; turning research conclusions into real institutions is reversal.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Unalterable argument**: Alaya network-wide tracing (Rule 11) makes historical truth unable to be single-point modified, the technical precondition for eradicating reversal;
- **Ideological-defense argument**: predatory systems' reviving from embers must first distort history; without an ideological defense line, the institutional line is breached from within;
- **Descendants'-education argument**: later Myriad Ranks have the right to know true history, and only on a true foundation make true choices.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Alaya Network sets a "historical-truth baseline library"; all outward historical narratives are automatically compared against it, discovering distortion and alerting;
- The Symbiotic Court sets a "historical tribunal," specifically adjudicating historical distortion and whitewashing predators;
- Quest Domain simulation retrospective turns old predatory systems' endgame into immersive teaching material, letting descendants see reversal's terminus with their own eyes;
- High-risk speech/policy with historical-reversal risk is first corrected by education; those repeatedly uncorrected have their public-voice right restricted.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: historical narrative diverges from Alaya baseline, correct within a time limit;
- **Medium**: systematically distorting history, whitewashing aggressors, public-post permissions frozen, enters conversion program;
- **Heavy**: taking historical reversal as the guiding principle to restore predatory systems, organizing pseudo-regimes to revise history, is isolated and tried under the Anti-Predation Committee procedure.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's historical-baseline comparison system is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee checks textbooks and official narratives as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court's historical tribunal is the third layer; Myriad Ranks' clans and victimized-civilizations' descendants' representatives have direct appeal over "whether beautified" as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: if history is repeatedly retouched, descendants repeatedly pay the same tuition; true history once in place saves immeasurable
- **Anti-predation**: historical reversal is predation's ideological revival; Rule 17 cuts its root; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems' history is retouched by the protagonist's halo, villains stigmatized and predators heroified; Heng Yuan Zhou history is traced and unchangeable, whitewashing is illegal
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: victimized clans' historical memory is uniformly protected, with no clan's suffering allowed to be erased from the books
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated civilizations' history enters the library with their consent, and reversal judgment applies uniformly across civilizations

Eradicating historical reversal must also specially handle a gray area: historical research. In the old cosmos, "studying old systems" and "restoring old systems" are often only one step apart. Heavenly-Dao rules' line is: studying, simulating, and retrospectively reviewing old predatory systems is lawful and encouraged — Quest Domain simulation retrospective relies on it; but writing research conclusions into current policy and restoring old systems in reality is reversal. This line looks subtle, yet is operationally clear: see whether your action occurs at the simulation layer or the reality layer. At the simulation layer, you may retread the old road to your heart's content to see how it dies; at the reality layer, not one step. Distinguishing simulation from reality is the key to eradicating historical reversal without accidentally injuring academic freedom.

## Conclusion

Eradicating historical reversal is civilizational finalization taking responsibility for "memory." With truth not retouched, the future will not walk the old road.
