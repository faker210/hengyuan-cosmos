# Entry 952 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 18 | Eradicate Cyclical Harvesting

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 25, 04 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: souls may not be treated as crops

The old cosmos's deepest evil is cyclical harvesting. The strong, or the so-called "Heavenly Dao," lock beings in life-death reincarnation: this life you cultivate, suffer, and die; the soul is recycled, screened, and used as fertilizer for the next "heavenly-material-earth-treasure"; the harder you work, the more the harvester reaps. Reincarnation is packaged as "destiny" and "karmic retribution," actually a super farm crossing life and death. Heavenly-Dao Rule 18 must dismantle this farm: souls are not crops, life and death are not harvest cycles, and any harvesting mechanism using reincarnation as a tool touches the Heavenly Dao's great prohibition.

The six top-level axioms here reach the cosmos's deepest layer: the causal self-discipline axiom is this rule's direct source; the republican axiom of Myriad Races decides souls are equal and unharvestable; the anti-predation axiom directly targets cyclical harvesting as predation's ultimate form; the collective-ascension axiom requires evolution be a straight road, not a reincarnation mill; the law-first axiom lets "reincarnation mechanisms, harvest judgment, liberation procedure" be rewritten first by law.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: Three Points of Eradicating Cyclical Harvesting

1. **No treating reincarnation as cultivation resource**: no collecting, refining, or screening beings' souls as energy or material;
2. **No treating reincarnation as domination tool**: no designing "reincarnation-style rewards and punishments" to make beings fear, submit, and give up resistance;
3. **Liberate existing reincarnation**: for reincarnation systems left over from the Pre-Anchor Era and still held by some strong one/institution, the Anchor Council organizes takeover, dismantling, and purification, with locked souls choosing their own destination per their causality.

Heavenly-Dao rules do not negate life-death itself — life and death are natural laws; what they negate is "someone hiding behind life and death collecting rent."

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Feedback-cycle argument**: the Symbiotic Feedback Cycle Mechanism (Document 25) points out the causal debt cyclical harvesting manufactures is predatory systems' largest bad debt and must be thoroughly liquidated;
- **Soul-sovereignty argument**: each soul is an independent node in the causal web, and its reincarnation destination is its own freedom, no third party's business;
- **Efficiency argument**: the reincarnation mill makes beings spin in place with extremely low evolutionary efficiency; straight-road evolution lets each soul approach the origin a little more each time, the true road.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Alaya Network monitors all soul-flow nodes, identifying anomalous "harvested" loops;
- For suspected cyclical-harvesting systems, first observe and collect evidence, then the Anchor Supervisory Committee and Symbiotic Court jointly take over;
- Liberated souls enter a "self-destination-choice" procedure: enter the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, stay in the original plane, or enter the Quest Sediment Domain to settle, one of three;
- The liberation project is fully public, Myriad Ranks may attend, preventing "under the name of liberation actually harvesting again."

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: residual "reincarnation-style reward-punishment" thinking from old habits, enters ethics courses;
- **Medium**: privately collecting souls, refining soul-spirits, handled heavily under Rule 2 and Rule 3;
- **Heavy**: rebuilding cyclical-harvesting farms, using reincarnation as domination technique, is the highest-tier case under the Anti-Predation Committee procedure, network-wide banned and isolated for trial.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's soul-flow monitoring is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee takes over harvesting systems as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates soul-predation cases as the third layer; harvested souls' representatives and each clan's soul council exercise supervision over the liberation project as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: cyclical harvesting makes immeasurable souls idle in place, the cosmos's greatest energy waste
- **Anti-predation**: this rule directly targets predation's ultimate form — cross-life-death soul predation; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in Tang-Luo-type systems the "Heavenly Dao" itself is the greatest harvester; Heng Yuan Zhou takes over and dismantles harvesting systems, and souls choose freely
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the five lineages' souls, plant-spirit consciousnesses, and planet-consciousness remnant souls are uniformly protected
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cross-plane cyclical-harvesting systems are likewise within the removal scope, expanding with the worldview

Eradicating cyclical harvesting's trickiest part is souls already "used to" being harvested. To them, reincarnation is not oppression, but a familiar destination; suddenly being told "you could have chosen your own destination" instead frightens them. Heavenly-Dao rules do not forcibly liberate, but hand the choice completely to the soul itself: continue staying in the familiar reincarnation structure, enter the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, or enter the Quest Sediment Domain to settle, one of three; the network only provides information, does not decide for it. Liberation is not takeover, but returning the taken choice intact. The greatest respect for a soul is not "I'm doing this for your own good," but "you choose."

## Conclusion

Eradicating cyclical harvesting releases beings from the cross-life-death farm. Heavenly-Dao Rule 18 declares: souls are masters, not harvest.
