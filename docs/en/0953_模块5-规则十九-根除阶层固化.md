# Entry 953 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 19 | Eradicate Class Solidification

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Document 36 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: birth determines neither the terminus, nor may it lock the starting line

Rule 7 gave every being an equal admission ticket, but the old cosmos had a second lock — class solidification. Even if everyone may initiate, the strong's child is born in the sect's center, the weak's child at the mine's bottom; the former breaks through with casual cultivation, the latter exhausts himself only to touch Sense-Condensed Realm. Over generations, "equal qualification" is quietly hollowed out by "unequal starting points." Heavenly-Dao Rule 19 must smash this invisible lock: birth, bloodline, family, and sect affiliation may not become an evolutionary ceiling; classes must be mobile, and must move.

The six top-level axioms here aim at social structure: the republican axiom of Myriad Races is this rule's direct source; the anti-predation axiom points out class solidification is predation's heritification of gains; the collective-ascension axiom requires society's upper and lower channels clear and whole-system lifting; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the mobility process not manufacture new unfairness; the law-first axiom lets "solidification identification, mobility guarantee, anti-heredity" be stipulated first by law.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: Three Points of Eradicating Solidification

1. **Anti-heredity**: sect masters, law-anchor permissions, and high posts may not be hereditary, but all arise per the plane-sovereign strong-one four-dimensional assessment (Entry 911) and election;
2. **Guarantee mobility**: under contracted-responsibility more-work-more-pay (Entry 909), juniors may continuously rise by contribution, and the strong's descendants have no "inheritance priority";
3. **Subsidize starting points**: for individuals from weak birth, via the Youth-Supreme path, D-route human-made Dao-rules soul rings, and the 20% activation strategy (Entry 910), make up the starting-point gap, rather than letting the Matthew effect run.

Mobility is not "must climb upward": the individual has the right to choose ordinary life; Heavenly-Dao rules only lock "may not refuse to fall," and do not force "must rise."

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Social-structure argument**: the Social Structure and Class Mobility (Document 36) points out a healthy civilization's mobility is its vitality source; solidification is death;
- **Anti-heredity argument**: hereditarily passing the strong one's achievements to descendants equals privately sharing Myriad-Ranks' co-created gains with one family, essentially predation's continuation;
- **Efficiency argument**: solidification locks massive geniuses at the bottom, wasting civilization's most precious evolutionary resource; eradicating solidification releases potential.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Alaya Network monitors the "mobility rate" between strata; anomalously low mobility triggers a solidification warning;
- All high posts are publicly competed-for, with assessment records network-wide traced, eliminating predetermined appointments;
- Subsidized resources for weak-birth individuals are automatically delivered, no questions of birth, no favors owed;
- A "birth-discrimination review group" is set up, specifically handling sects, units, and regions' implicit exclusion of juniors.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: post competitions formalistic, predetermined-appointment tendency, rectify within a time limit;
- **Medium**: publicly pushing birth priority and bloodline hiring, relevant unit permissions frozen, enters conversion program;
- **Heavy**: rebuilding caste systems, writing class solidification into sect regulations, handled heavily under Rule 7 and Rule 10.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's mobility-rate dashboard is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee checks competition fairness as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates birth-discrimination cases as the third layer; each clan and the Youth-Supreme supervision corps exercise self-correction over their own solidification tendency as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: geniuses locked at the bottom are the greatest waste; eradicating solidification releases them
- **Anti-predation**: predation-gain heritification is legislatively banned; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems are "destined by heaven," the protagonist's clan fated to tower over all beings; Heng Yuan Zhou high posts are publicly competed for, and no birth, however noble, is hereditary
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the five lineages, Youth Supremes, plant spirits, and planet-consciousness representatives all enjoy mobility channels
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated civilizations' social structures automatically enter mobility-rate monitoring, preventing old solidification from being brought into Heng Yuan Zhou

Eradicating class solidification must also face a reality: a completely equal starting point is neither possible nor necessary. What Heavenly-Dao Rule 19 pursues is not "everyone identical," but "starting-point differences compensable, terminus gates open." A Youth Supreme from a humble clan gains subsidy via the D-route human-made Dao-rules soul rings; a mediocre-talented one finds his own rhythm via contracted responsibility; the easygoing retain their floor per Axiom 6 and are not eliminated by the times. Solidification's opposite is not egalitarianism, but mobility — a humble youth may rise, and a wealthy youth may also fall; both upward and downward channels simultaneously clear, and that is eradicating solidification.

## Conclusion

Eradicating class solidification lands "equal qualification" from paper to ground. Starting points may differ; the terminus must be open to all.
