# Entry 954 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 20 | The Law Comes First

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Document 20 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: establish the rules first, then tell the story

The law-first axiom is the most easily overlooked yet most decisive of the six top-level axioms. The old cosmos's laws were "post-hoc ratified": the plot needs the protagonist to suddenly power up, so a law is temporarily changed; the author needs the villain to suddenly grow strong, so a rule is temporarily loosened. This "laws follow the plot" way of setting ultimately necessarily retcons, ends unfinished, and collapses the worldview. Heavenly-Dao Rule 20 must reverse all this: the law comes first. Any new phenomenon, new plot, or new strong one must first have a legal basis before being allowed to happen in the cosmos; where no law is established, the matter may not be done. This is the law-first axiom's direct landing at the Heavenly-Dao layer.

The six top-level axioms here appear as meta-rules: the law-first axiom is this rule's body; the anti-predation axiom requires the law's content also be anti-predatory; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires Myriad-Rank participation in law-making; the collective-ascension axiom requires laws serve whole-system ascension; the causal self-discipline axiom requires law changes themselves also be legislated first — any post-hoc law change is void.

It must further point out the newly added Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing and layabouts retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated by the times; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose more and higher levels of infinity. This axiom rewrites predation's meaning at the root: old-cosmos resources and Dao were finite, so predation involution was forced; in Heng Yuan Zhou everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning. What this Heavenly-Dao rule establishes is precisely the law guardrail of this new order of "infinite origin, predation meaningless."

## I. Rule Content: Three Operations of Law-First

1. **Legislate first, act second**: new projects, new institutions, new strong-one posts must first complete law anchoring before operation begins; items unanchored have energy channels closed by the network;
2. **Interpret first, judge second**: any dispute is first law-interpreted by the Anchor Council, then adjudicated by the Symbiotic Court; "do it first, talk later" is forbidden;
3. **Calibrate first, expand second**: before the worldview adds planes, clans, or laws, first go through the Terminology Committee and anchor calibration (Document 27), then merge into the main network.

Law-first is not rigidity: laws may be amended, but amendment must follow the legislative procedure, not "temporarily change when something comes up."

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Anchor-structure argument**: the Anchor Structure and Hierarchy (Document 20) points out, of the cosmos's four-layer structure, the law network lies at the bottom, the base of material planes, the energy layer, and the consciousness domain; an unstable base means upper layers necessarily collapse;
- **Retcon-lesson argument**: countless old-cosmos worldviews collapsed because of "plot needs," their root cause all law-not-first; Heng Yuan Zhou wants a civilization enduring through ages, and must go the opposite way;
- **Predictability argument**: Myriad Ranks' evolution needs stable expectations — if laws change order daily, cultivators do not know what to believe, and civilization cannot plan long-term.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Alaya Network maintains a "current-law library"; all operations first consult the library: those with basis are released, those without return to the legislative procedure;
- Legislative procedure: Anchor Council proposal → Myriad-Rank representatives' vote → anchor calibration verification → promulgation and effect, fully public throughout;
- The Terminology Committee calibrates new terms and new concepts first, avoiding law ambiguity from "one word, many uses";
- Newly incorporated cross-plane civilizations' existing laws are first compared, calibrated, and connected with Heng Yuan Zhou's main laws before network merger.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: project already started but laws not fully anchored, make up anchoring within a time limit;
- **Medium**: under the name of "emergency"/"special" bypassing the legislative procedure to directly change laws, relevant acts are automatically void;
- **Heavy**: plot/public opinion/personal will kidnapping laws, post-hoc ratifying illegal operations, handled heavily under Rules 22 and 23 (setting self-consistency, plot does not kidnap).

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's "law-library verification" is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee checks "lawless operations" as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates "procedural-illegal" cases as the third layer; the Anchor Council itself, as the law-interpretation subject, is reverse-supervised by Myriad-Rank representatives as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: law-first avoids massive "post-hoc rework" idling; legislating right once saves ten thousand years
- **Anti-predation**: abolishing at the root the old logic of "the strong are the law"; before the law, strong and commoner are equal; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and finite-resource predation loses meaning at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems' laws change at Gu Chensha's whim, powering up on a word; Heng Yuan Zhou laws come first with Myriad-Rank vote, and even the strong cannot temporarily add a clause
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: law-making involves Myriad Ranks, and new laws apply equally to the five lineages
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the law library supports infinite expansion — new planes, clans, and projects enter via the legislative procedure without collapse

Law-first must also answer a follow-up: what if the law itself is wrong? The answer is laws may be amended, but amendment must follow the legislative procedure — Anchor Council proposal, Myriad-Rank representatives' vote, anchor calibration verification, promulgation and effect, fully public and traceable. "Wrong so change it" differs from "temporarily change when something comes up" by procedure: the former is Myriad Ranks jointly deciding to retire the old law, the latter is some person or some plot need making the law quietly yield. What law-first protects is not that some law is forever correct, but that "law changes always follow Myriad Ranks' procedure." This is true endurance through ages.

## Conclusion

The law comes first is civilizational finalization's "meta-rule of rules." It ensures: no matter how exciting the plot or vast the worldview, the base is forever stable.
