# Entry 955 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 21 | Never Retcon, Never End Unfinished

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Document 20 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: the cosmos may not temporarily change settings, and may not run away mid-writing

The old cosmos's cultivation civilizations are essentially all "stories that were written." Stories have stories' fates — once the author gets carried away, he retcons: earlier it says some method is the world's first, later the protagonist crushes it underfoot; earlier it says some strong one is invincible, later to make way for a new villain, the old setting quietly expires. Writing to the end it cannot be patched, and it ends unfinished: the protagonist suddenly says "I have awakened," the world suddenly "just is," and Myriad Ranks' causality is flung aside. Heavenly-Dao Rule 21 is civilizational finalization's final judgment on this matter: Heng Yuan Zhou this "great book" never retcons and never ends unfinished. Once a setting is anchored it is law-protected; once a worldview is opened it must be written to the closed loop.

The six top-level axioms converge here: the law-first axiom is this rule's body, directly requiring "never retcon, never end unfinished"; the anti-predation axiom ensures "no retconning" does not quietly launder old predation settings into new settings; the republican axiom of Myriad Races decides Myriad Ranks the readers have the right to see a complete closed-loop ending; the collective-ascension axiom requires the story's terminus be whole-system ascension, not an individual protagonist's ascension; the causal self-discipline axiom requires every setting change leave a trace for traceability; the law-first axiom's twin, Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law, gives "no retconning" its confidence — since everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power and predation loses meaning, the story need not manufacture conflict by temporarily inflating villains and temporarily suppressing protagonists, and naturally need not retcon.

## I. Rule Content: Three Iron Laws of No Retcon, No Unfinished End

1. **Anchored settings may not be unilaterally overturned**: any setting written into the law library (realms, methods, causality, clan relations) may not be voided for plot convenience; genuine adjustments go through the law-amendment procedure (Entry 931);
2. **Conflict must be explained, not covered**: when new and old settings appear in surface conflict, the Anchor Council must issue a legal interpretation explaining their unifying logic; "author's-amnesia" evasion is forbidden;
3. **The closed loop must be written to the end**: once civilizational evolution is opened, it must be deduced to the hyper-multiverse's only complete civilizational closed loop; "protagonist ascends and the story ends, Myriad Ranks' fate left blank" unfinished endings are forbidden.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Anchor-structure argument**: the Anchor Structure and Hierarchy (Document 20) points out the law network is the causal base; if settings on the base are casually rewritten, it equals tearing up and rewriting the causal ledger, which Myriad Ranks' causality cannot bear;
- **Readers'-rights argument**: Myriad Ranks are the civilizational story's co-authors and co-characters; retconning is deceiving readers, and ending unfinished is abandoning characters;
- **Infinite-origin argument**: under Axiom 6, conflict need not be manufactured by "villains suddenly growing strong," but may be supplied by "new law nodes awaiting lighting" — with conflict's source right, the motive for retconning is gone.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Alaya Network maintains a "setting version library"; all setting changes go through version-number management, old versions are not deleted but archived;
- New-old setting conflicts automatically trigger an "interpretation work order," giving a unifying explanation within a time limit;
- The civilizational-evolution roadmap is publicly deduced by the Anchor Council to the closed-loop node; "take one step, see one step" is not allowed;
- Quest Domain simulation-retrospective does special drills on "retcon-style setting collapse," letting cultivators intuitively see retconning's civilizational cost.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: setting expression inconsistent front and back, issue a unifying interpretation within a time limit;
- **Medium**: unilaterally overturning anchored settings for plot convenience, relevant plots are voided, responsible persons recorded in the integrity archive;
- **Heavy**: abandoning Myriad Ranks via "unfinished ending," prematurely terminating civilizational evolution, is betrayal of all Myriad Ranks, and the Anchor Council launches a forced continuation procedure.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's setting-version library is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee checks "retcon-style setting changes" as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates setting-tampering cases as the third layer; Myriad Ranks' clans, as "readers," have direct commentary and accountability over unfinished endings and retconning as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: retconning and unfinished endings let immeasurable causal accumulation go to zero with voiding, civilizational-level waste
- **Anti-predation**: no retconning also refuses to launder old predation settings into new justice; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, predation loses meaning
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems power up on a word and change settings on a whim, protagonist halo over everything; Heng Yuan Zhou settings are versioned, and even the strong cannot temporarily add scenes
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the closed-loop ending is Myriad Ranks' common ending, not one protagonist's private perfection
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the worldview may expand infinitely (Rule 24), but every expansion goes through version management, no collapse, no unfinished

Never retcon, never end unfinished has another layer of meaning for cultivators individually: it guarantees the cultivator's lifelong investment is not crossed out by the "author." In the old cosmos, once the protagonist switches realm systems, predecessors' arduous cultivation becomes a joke; in Heng Yuan Zhou, a cultivator's century of deduction, pits stepped in, and Dao texts left permanently enter the version library, becoming Myriad Ranks' permanent wealth. No retconning is ultimately respect for every cultivator who cultivates seriously — your effort will not be betrayed by the plot.

## Conclusion

Never retcon, never end unfinished, is civilizational finalization's promise to "completeness." Heng Yuan Zhou this great book must be written to the last line of Myriad Ranks' joint ascension.

In the end, the Rule 21 described in this entry — never retcon, never end unfinished — is no isolated case; it consistently points to the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's co-construction direction.
