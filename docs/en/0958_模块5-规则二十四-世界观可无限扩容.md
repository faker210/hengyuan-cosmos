# Entry 958 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 24 | The Worldview May Expand Infinitely

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 20, 28 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: the cosmos must grow, and may always keep growing

Once old-cosmos settings are written dead, they become a cage — the author draws the map to some boundary, and to add a new plane he must retcon. Heng Yuan Zhou goes the opposite way: the worldview is not a finished map, but a living chart that may always be added to. New planes, new clans, new laws, new realms, as long as they go through the law-anchoring procedure, may merge into the main network without bursting existing settings. Heavenly-Dao Rule 24 writes this "infinitely expandable" into the law itself: civilizational finalization is not sealing the cosmos shut, but building a cosmos that never stops growing and never grows collapse. This is the law-first axiom's promise to "the future."

The six top-level axioms here face the future: the law-first axiom is this rule's body, directly requiring "the worldview may expand infinitely without collapse"; the anti-predation axiom ensures expansion does not treat new worlds as colonies; the republican axiom of Myriad Races decides newly incorporated worlds' Myriad Ranks are equal to old Myriad Ranks; the collective-ascension axiom defines expansion as new space for whole-system ascension; the causal self-discipline axiom requires expansion follow the report-observe-knowledge-return process; Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law at the root lets expansion need no limit — since everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, civilization's "scale" should have no upper bound; the larger the cosmos, the wider the stage for infinite origin.

## I. Rule Content: Three Expansion Channels

1. **New-plane merger**: newly discovered material planes, after reporting (Rule 13), observing (Rule 14), and returning knowledge (Rule 15), merge into the main network;
2. **New-clan access**: new-lineage beings automatically gain equal evolutionary qualification (Rule 7) and 99% coverage (Rule 6);
3. **New-law anchoring**: new law nodes enter the law library via the legislative procedure (Rule 20); old laws are not overthrown, only connected.

Expansion is not stuffing new worlds into an old frame, but the old frame reserving interfaces.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Anchor-structure argument**: the Anchor Structure and Hierarchy (Document 20) points out the four-layer structure itself is an open network topology, with nodes infinitely addable;
- **Cross-plane-connection argument**: the Cross-Plane Anchor Connection (Document 28) proves new-plane access has a mature technical path;
- **Infinite-origin argument**: Axiom 6 makes "finite cosmos" self-limitation meaningless — since origin is infinite, the stage should be infinite.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Alaya Network maintains a "pending-access plane queue" and "new-clan access archives," with expansion fully traced;
- The Anchor Council holds legislative votes on new-law anchoring, with the Terminology Committee calibrating first;
- Expansion does not affect anchored old settings: new versions are superposed, not replaced;
- Quest Domain simulation-retrospective does "compatibility tests" on each expansion, confirming old civilizations are not impacted.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: expansion procedure incomplete, complete within a time limit;
- **Medium**: forcibly stuffing new worlds into the old frame causing setting conflict, returned for recombination;
- **Heavy**: under the name of "expansion" actually colonizing, treating new worlds as predation resource grounds, handled heavily under Rules 13 and 14.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's access archives are the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee checks expansion compatibility as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates expansion disputes as the third layer; newly incorporated worlds' representatives have direct say over "whether respectfully accessed" as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: infinite expandability lets civilization not stop because "the map is finished"
- **Anti-predation**: expansion follows report-and-observe; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and new worlds are not hunting grounds
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems' maps end wherever they end, new worlds mostly hard-added via protagonist halo; Heng Yuan Zhou expansion goes through the legislative procedure, never retconning
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: new clans automatically gain equal qualification, continuously expanding with expansion
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: this rule is the institutional embodiment of "hyper-multiverse full openness" — infinitely extending toward the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm

The worldview may expand infinitely, but does not mean stuffing things without restraint. Each expansion must pass compatibility testing and the legislative procedure, ensuring new planes, clans, and laws do not conflict with existing settings. "Infinite" means the interface is forever open, not content more-is-better. Expansion's direction is "finding new increment space for Myriad Ranks," not "piling settings for excitement." This restraint lets the worldview both grow infinitely and stay self-consistent — growing without falling apart is precisely the law-first axiom's hard constraint on expansion.

Infinite expandability must also solve "expand to where." Heng Yuan Zhou's answer is: forever opening the door to unlit law nodes, uncontacted planes, unallied civilizations. Expansion is not aimless piling, but extending along the clue of "what remains unsymbiotized." Under Axiom 6, this extension has no terminus — because infinite origin may support an infinitely large stage, and no stage is too large.

This "open yet procedural" expansion philosophy ultimately makes Heng Yuan Zhou a forever-youthful yet never-rash cosmos.

## Conclusion

The worldview may expand infinitely is civilizational finalization's promise to "the future." Heng Yuan Zhou sets no ceiling and no boundary, forever opening its door to new seas of stars.

In the end, the Rule 24 described in this entry — the worldview may expand infinitely — is no isolated case; it consistently points to the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's co-construction direction.
