# Entry 959 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 25 | Predatory Cultivation Prohibited

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 19 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: this road is henceforth closed

Rules 1 through 4 have already encircled predation from four directions — energy source, stock boundary, evolutionary path, and Great-Dao openness; Rule 25 completely closes the encirclement — predatory cultivation, as a whole cultivation paradigm, is comprehensively prohibited. No longer scattered prohibitions of "do this not, do that not," but a declaration: any cultivation system centered on devouring, refining, sacrificing, or preying on others as its core energy source or core ritual, no matter how ancient its name or long its lineage, is void ab initio in Heng Yuan Zhou. This is the anti-predation axiom's general declaration of war at the Heavenly-Dao layer.

The six top-level axioms erupt centrally here: the anti-predation axiom is this rule's body, directly prohibiting predatory cultivation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races decides the prey are no longer fuel; the collective-ascension axiom requires cultivation take the incremental co-construction road; the causal self-discipline axiom extends the prohibition to the thought and habit layer; the law-first axiom lets "predatory cultivation"'s judgment criteria be enumerated first by law; Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law gives this prohibition its deepest reason — since every being inherently possesses basic infinite power, preying on others is at the root a losing trade: the finite stock you snatch is far less than the infinite origin you already inherently have. Predation loses meaning, and prohibition follows naturally.

## I. Rule Content: Identification and Prohibition of Predatory Cultivation

Whatever meets any of the following features is predatory cultivation, uniformly prohibited:

1. Using devouring others' cultivation, soul-spirits, or bloodline as energy source;
2. Using kill counts or war-merit ranks as promotion ladders;
3. Using sacrificing beings or refining artifacts as breakthrough cost;
4. Using enslaving or penning others as long-term "cultivation batteries";
5. Using monopolizing the Great Dao and privately hoarding methods to manufacture dependence.

Prohibited systems cease immediately, and their cultivators transfer into the enlightened-predator conversion program (Rule 26).

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Efficiency-ceiling argument**: Document 04 has already proven predatory cultivation's efficiency ceiling — the higher the tier, the fiercer the backlash, a dead end;
- **Causal-debt argument**: the causal debt accumulated by predatory cultivation is reclaimed in reincarnation, the source of cyclical harvesting (Rule 18);
- **Infinite-origin argument**: under Axiom 6, everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power; finite stock snatched, relative to infinite origin, equals zero; predators cannot even call it a "good deal."

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Alaya Network does a "predatory-feature scan" on all cultivation methods, marking red on a hit;
- Cultivators already on the path, the network automatically diagnoses whether their method contains predatory components, giving a "symbiotic alternative";
- During the conversion period, the cultivator may not continue using the original predatory method, but his existing achievements are not simply confiscated, but repositioned in conversion;
- Quest Domain simulation provides immersive "predatory-cultivation endgame" experience, letting cultivators voluntarily lay it down.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: method contains residual predatory components without self-knowledge, replace within a time limit;
- **Medium**: knowingly continuing to use predatory methods, law-anchor permissions frozen, forced conversion;
- **Heavy**: promoting, organizing, or rebuilding predatory cultivation systems, is the highest-tier case under the Anti-Predation Committee procedure, network-wide banned and isolated for trial.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's method scan is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee checks predatory remnants as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates predatory-cultivation cases as the third layer; Myriad Ranks' clans exercise cleanup and conversion supervision over their own historical predatory lineages as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: predatory cultivation is lose-lose zero-sum idling; prohibiting it saves immeasurable energy
- **Anti-predation**: this rule is anti-predation itself — predatory cultivation as a paradigm is legislatively prohibited as a whole
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems' geniuses prey more the stronger, might-makes-right in their bones; Heng Yuan Zhou predatory cultivation is void ab initio, no matter how strong one is he may not walk this road
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the five lineages, Youth Supremes, plant spirits, and planet consciousness all may not use predatory cultivation
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated civilizations with predatory traditions are converted first, then networked

After predatory cultivation is prohibited, how are strong ones who already accumulated strength through predation dealt with? Heavenly-Dao rules' answer is not confiscation, but "conversionary repositioning": their combat experience and deduction ability may be retained and redirected to co-construction, but their predation-derived energy and causal debt must be retrogressed and returned as much as possible per Rule 2. This handling neither settles scores retroactively, nor lets predatory gains be laundered. Its message: lay down the knife, you are a builder; keep gripping the knife, you are a banned one. The door stays open, but whether to enter is up to you.

After predatory cultivation is prohibited, Quest Domain simulation-retrospective takes on a special function: it lets cultivators safely experience in simulation "what walking the predation road feels like," seeing with their own eyes how that road backlashes itself at the endgame. This immersive education, more than any sermon, makes people lay down the predation heart. The simulation is fake, but the backlash's feeling is real — someone who has seen the abyss in a safe environment will not jump into it again in reality.

## Conclusion

Predatory cultivation prohibited is civilizational finalization's general closure of "the old road." Henceforth, cultivation has only one road — develop the infinite origin you inherently possess.

In the end, the Rule 25 described in this entry — predatory cultivation prohibited — is no isolated case; it consistently points to the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's co-construction direction.
