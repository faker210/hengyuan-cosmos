# Entry 960 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 26 | Enlightened-Predator Conversion Mechanism

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 29 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: not the killing road, but the conversion road

After predatory cultivation is prohibited, the most realistic question arrives — what about the strong ones already on this road? Directly exterminating them is anti-predation via predation, violating the inner principle; letting them be is collusion. Heavenly-Dao Rule 26 gives a third road: the enlightened-predator conversion mechanism. So-called enlightened predators are old strong ones who acknowledge predatory systems have reached a dead end, are willing to lay down predatory arts, and turn toward the symbiotic road. Civilization does not kill them, but gives them a step down and a new road to learn. This is the key that distinguishes the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization from the old cosmos's "winner takes all, loser settles scores."

The six top-level axioms show warmth here: the anti-predation axiom requires prohibiting predation, but does not require exterminating the predators themselves; the republican axiom of Myriad Races decides once enlightened predators convert they are equal members; the collective-ascension axiom lets converts' experience become new civilization's wealth; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the conversion process respect their causality, no forced brainwashing; the law-first axiom lets "conversion procedure, assessment standards, permission restoration" be stipulated first by law; Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law gives conversion the most convincing "enrollment ad" — the finite stock you toiled to snatch is actually far less than the basic infinite power you inherently possess; laying down predation is truly what's good for you.

## I. Rule Content: Three Stages of the Conversion Mechanism

1. **Acknowledgment stage**: enlightened predators formally acknowledge predatory cultivation is a dead end, voluntarily stop predatory methods, and enter the conversion procedure;
2. **Learning stage**: systematically studying symbiotic ethics, incremental development, and Alaya Network operation in the Quest Sediment Domain, completing "symbiosis-degree" re-education;
3. **Return stage**: after passing the symbiosis-degree assessment (Document 29), gradually restoring law-anchor permissions, and their old combat and deduction experience is entered into the "lift Myriad Ranks" work order per Rule 9.

Conversion is not humiliation: no sin-label, no brand, no forced disclosure of the past, only asking about the future.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Causal-repairability argument**: the Symbiotic Feedback Cycle Mechanism points out causality, though not erasable, is repairable; conversion turns old predators from "causal debtors" into "feeders-back";
- **Human-capital argument**: enlightened predators are mostly great powers; their experience, if converted into symbiotic construction power, is enormously valuable; extermination is waste;
- **Infinite-origin argument**: Axiom 6 shows converts that rather than snatch finite stock again, activate the infinite origin they inherently possess — conversion's return is far higher than predation's.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Alaya Network builds a "conversion file" for converts, tracking their stopping old methods, learning progress, and symbiosis-degree changes;
- During the conversion period, their predatory permissions are frozen, but the floor evolutionary qualification corresponding to their basic infinite power is preserved (connecting Axiom 6);
- "Conversion mentors" are appointed: jointly mentored by successfully converted enlightened predators and senior symbionts;
- Conversion has no maximum duration, slowness allowed, but backsliding not; those who quit predatory cultivation midway are re-assessed.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: old habits recur during conversion, heart-demon warning, conversion period extended;
- **Medium**: pretending to convert while secretly restoring predation, all permissions frozen, handled heavily under Rule 25;
- **Heavy**: using "conversion" identity to infiltrate and sabotage the symbiotic network, isolated and tried under the Anti-Predation Committee procedure.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's conversion files are the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee checks conversion authenticity as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates "fake conversion, real predation" cases as the third layer; successfully converted ones form a "those-who-have-been-there council" exercising peer supervision over converts as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: turning great powers' experience from "continue predation" to "join co-construction" is the greatest activation of existing human capital
- **Anti-predation**: prohibiting predation while giving a conversion road; under Axiom 6 letting predators themselves see predation is not worth it
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems' losers die and old enemies must be killed; Heng Yuan Zhou enlightened predators may convert, return, and be trusted again
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the conversion channel is open to enlightened predators of all clans and all ranks
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cross-plane enlightened predators likewise use the conversion procedure

The enlightened-predator conversion mechanism also has a by-product: it makes "letting go" no longer shameful. In the old cosmos, a strong one admitting he was wrong was almost personal bankruptcy, so he could only walk one road to the dark. Heavenly-Dao rules write conversion as an honorable thing — a great power able to step down from the predation road understands the predation faction deeper than any upright gentleman, and this "those-who-have-been-there" identity is priceless in the anti-predation cause. Conversion is not demotion, but a post change: from a predation-faction general, to the symbiotic civilization's advisor who best understands the enemy.

The enlightened-predator conversion mechanism also sends a signal to all old-cosmos strong ones who "lay down the butcher's knife": the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization holds no old grudge, only asks about new deeds. A once-swaggering predation great power, as long as willing to convert, may find a new place in the new civilization — as mentor, as scout, as an eyewitness negative teaching example. This "no recrimination" embrace is itself the strongest deconstruction of predatory systems: it proves letting down takes more courage than fighting on.

## Conclusion

The enlightened-predator conversion mechanism is the anti-predation Heavenly-Dao rules' softest yet strongest link. No killing road, give people a road — that itself is anti-predation.
