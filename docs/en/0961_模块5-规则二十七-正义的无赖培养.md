# Entry 961 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 27 | Raising the Righteous Rogue

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 19, 29 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: to deal with rogues, someone must understand rogues better than rogues

Old-cosmos predators, speculators, and loophole-drillers never fear proper rules — they only fear meeting someone who understands loophole-drilling better than themselves. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization cannot rely solely on "upright gentlemen" to guard the rules; it must also cultivate a special kind of guardian: the righteous rogue. They know all predators' tricks, all speculators' paths, and all human nature's dark corners, but use this "rogue skill" to protect symbiosis, not to destroy it. Heavenly-Dao Rule 27 writes the cultivation of this special talent into a civilizational necessity.

The six top-level axioms appear visibly here: the causal self-discipline axiom needs "people who understand scheming to plug scheming's paths"; the republican axiom of Myriad Races decides the righteous rogue guards all Myriad Ranks' bottom line; the anti-predation axiom transforms the rogue's skill from "predation tool" to "anti-predation tool"; the collective-ascension axiom makes this special talent civilization's immune system; the law-first axiom lets the righteous rogue's permissions, boundaries, and supervision be stipulated first by law; Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law gives them a special positioning — they too are ordinary members inherently possessing basic infinite power, only choosing to use this infinity on "guarding the bottom line"; striver-style superimposed infinity manifests in them as "infinite familiarity with predatory tricks."

## I. Rule Content: Three Rules for the Righteous Rogue

1. **Rogue skill, righteous stance**: they must be deeply versed in the predation faction's every means, every psychology, every loophole, but use it only for scouting, early warning, and busting predatory acts;
2. **Restricted permissions**: their "rogue means" are used only within the Anti-Predation Committee's authorization, not for personal profit, not for purging dissenters;
3. **Strict isolation**: their identity and means records are traced on the Alaya Network, under the Anchor Supervisory Committee's independent supervision, preventing "using rogues against rogues" from alienating into new rogues.

The righteous rogue is not a person above the law, but a strictly constrained special post.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Evil-ten-feet argument**: upright gentlemen often cannot imagine how predators will drill loopholes; only those who understand scheming can plug scheming;
- **Immune-system argument**: civilization, like a body, needs "cells that recognize viruses"; the righteous rogue is the symbiotic civilization's immune cell;
- **Infinite-origin argument**: under Axiom 6, not everyone need be a saint; the floor infinity is enough to let someone who "understands rogues" peacefully guard the bottom line, not forced to truly become a rogue for survival.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- Select righteous rogues from converted enlightened predators and cultivators in Quest Domain simulation good at "taking shortcuts";
- Set up an "Anti-Predation Scout Bureau," composed of righteous rogues, specifically monitoring new predatory tricks;
- Their actions are fully traced and post-reviewed; any overreach using rogue means is handled per Rules 5 and 19;
- Set "rotation and cooldown periods," preventing guardians long working in gray zones from themselves being dyed gray.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: means overstep, cooldown period, ethics re-education;
- **Medium**: using rogue skill for profit or purging, permissions frozen, handled under Rule 5;
- **Heavy**: falling from righteous rogue into new rogue, rebuilding predation networks, handled heavily under Rules 25 and 26.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's full trace is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee independently audits the Anti-Predation Scout Bureau as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates guardian-overreach cases as the third layer; Myriad Ranks' clans have direct appeal over "whether guardians have become new oppressors" as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: letting those who understand scheming plug scheming is a hundred times more efficient than letting those who don't forcibly plug it
- **Anti-predation**: using predators' own language against predation; under Axiom 6 they themselves have floor infinity and need not truly prey
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems have no "immune system," and predators' scheming is unstoppable; Heng Yuan Zhou specifically sets righteous-rogue posts
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the righteous rogue guards Myriad Ranks' bottom line, regardless of clan
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: cross-plane new predatory tricks are likewise within their scouting scope

The righteous-rogue post's greatest risk is that those long working in gray zones themselves get dyed gray. Heavenly-Dao rules set three insurances for this: full trace, periodic cooldown, and peer supervision. During cooldown, the guardian leaves the scouting front line, returns to ordinary cultivation life, and re-feels what "normal" is like; peer supervision is composed of those who have successfully walked this post before, mutually checking for overstep. This design acknowledges a fact: long dealing with rogues, one may oneself become a rogue — acknowledging this and using institutions to prevent it is true responsibility.

The righteous-rogue post also has a social significance: it acknowledges "goodness" alone is insufficient to hold the line. A civilization entirely of upright gentlemen is helpless before true rogues. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization does not avoid this reality, but institutionalizes it — it needs upright gentlemen to legislate, and those who understand rogues to guard the bottom line. The two talents check each other: upright gentlemen fear rogues overstepping, rogues fear upright gentlemen being pedantic; in this tension, institutions truly come alive.

## Conclusion

Raising the righteous rogue is civilizational finalization's "immune-system engineering." Using the rogue's way to guard symbiosis, using strict supervision to prevent its fall.
