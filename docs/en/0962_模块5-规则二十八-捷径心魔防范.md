# Entry 962 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 28 | All-Beings' Origin Greed · Shortcut Heart-Demon Prevention

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 19, 07 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: the heart-demon is the deepest enemy

All predation's root is not external, but in all beings' origin greed and the shortcut heart-demon. Even if predatory cultivation is comprehensively prohibited, as long as someone still thinks "is there a faster road?" and "can I give less and get more?", he may at any moment be hijacked by the heart-demon, walking the old road sealed by the network. Heavenly-Dao Rule 28 sinks the defense line from "institution" down to "thought": preventing all beings' origin greed and the shortcut heart-demon is Heavenly-Dao rules' last post in every cultivator's heart. This is not suppressing desire, but letting people see the illusion behind desire.

The six top-level axioms land on heart-nature here: the causal self-discipline axiom constrains from the thought layer; the republican axiom of Myriad Races makes heart-demon prevention not a private matter, but civilization's common affair; the anti-predation axiom points out the heart-demon is predation's seed; the collective-ascension axiom lets heart-nature and realm grow in sync; the law-first axiom gives "heart-demon identification, intervention, and recovery" a legal basis; Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law is the heart-demon's most fundamental antidote — since everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, "taking shortcuts to snatch from others" is a thorough misunderstanding: you are already infinite, what are you hurrying for? The easygoing's floor infinity is precisely the ultimate comfort for "shortcut anxiety."

## I. Rule Content: Three Gates of Heart-Demon Prevention

1. **Identification**: Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision monitor cultivators' thought fluctuations; when "greedy shortcut" signals keep strengthening, a heart-demon prompt automatically triggers;
2. **Guidance**: after the prompt, the symbiotic network dispatches a heart-nature mentor, leading him to see the causal bill behind "shortcuts";
3. **Floor**: even if momentarily hijacked by the heart-demon and acting overstepping, he follows a recovery path, not being killed with one blow — but repeat offenders are handled heavily.

Heart-demon prevention is not monitoring private life, only watching the critical signal of "greedy shortcut → about to act."

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Self-growth argument**: the Self-Growing Evolution Law (Document 07) points out true growth may not skip grades; the shortcut-skipping heart-demon is essentially ignorance of causal law;
- **Causal-debt argument**: power bought by shortcuts has owed causality behind it; the heart-demon lets people only see the immediate sweetness, not the distant bill;
- **Infinite-origin argument**: Axiom 6 dissolves shortcut anxiety at the root — you are already infinite, taking it slow is also infinite, what are you hurrying for?

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Quest Sediment Domain sets up "shortcut heart-demon simulation": let cultivators walk a shortcut in simulation, seeing with their own eyes its endgame backlash;
- Symbiotic education lists "recognizing shortcut illusion" as a required course at every realm;
- Heart-nature mentor system: every cultivator may apply for a mentor, for regular talks and regular reviews;
- For "easygoing/layabout" cultivators, Axiom 6's floor infinity gives them no shortcut pressure, and the network actively confirms that "being content with the floor is lawful."

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: occasional greedy thought, self-reflects after the heart-demon prompt, no accountability;
- **Medium**: the heart-demon has already driven actual overstepping acts, transferred to the enlightened-predator conversion procedure;
- **Heavy**: the heart-demon has solidified into a predatory cultivation system and been promoted, handled heavily under Rule 25.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's thought monitoring is the first layer; heart-nature mentors and the symbiotic education system are the second layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee spot-checks whether "heart-demon intervention" is abused as thought control as the third layer; the cultivator himself has appeal over "being over-monitored" as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: shortcut-taking driven by the heart-demon eventually has to redo it through backlash, the most expensive waste
- **Anti-predation**: predation's seed is in the heart-demon; preventing the heart-demon prevents predation at the root; under Axiom 6 you are already infinite and need no shortcut
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems encourage "serendipity, adventures, grade-skipping," with heart-demons packaged as talent; Heng Yuan Zhou lists shortcut heart-demons as a required course
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: heart-demon prevention is regardless of clan; the five lineages, Youth Supremes, and plant spirits each have heart-nature courses
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated civilizations with "shortcut-worship" traditions first do heart-nature calibration before network merger

Shortcut heart-demon prevention must also face a psychological reality: easygoing ones also have heart-demons, just in the opposite direction. The easygoing one's heart-demon is not "wanting to take shortcuts," but "fearing being eliminated by the times" — seeing strivers superimpose infinity, he too becomes anxious. Axiom 6's floor infinity is precisely the antidote to this anxiety: Heavenly-Dao rules make clear that being content with the floor is lawful, and he will not be abandoned by the network for not striving. Preventing shortcut heart-demons must guard against both "too eager to be fast" and "forced to strive for fear of being left behind." The two heart-demons share a source — both are not believing one is already infinite. Seeing this, one is at peace.

Shortcut heart-demon prevention ultimately comes back to one sentence: you are already infinite, what are you hurrying for? This sentence is not chicken soup, but Axiom 6's daily practice. When a cultivator truly realizes he inherently possesses basic infinite power, that "if I don't take a shortcut now it'll be too late" anxiety loosens — you have infinite time and infinite possibilities, why snatch that one bite? The heart-demon's root is "thinking oneself not enough"; seeing "already enough," the heart-demon disperses of itself.

## Conclusion

All-beings' origin greed and shortcut heart-demon prevention is Heavenly-Dao rules' last post in people's hearts. Institutions guard the hands, heart-nature guards the heart.

In the end, the Rule 28 described in this entry — shortcut heart-demon prevention — is no isolated case; it consistently points to the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's co-construction direction.
