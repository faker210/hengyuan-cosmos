# Entry 963 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 29 | Civilizational-Weight Assessment

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Document 29 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: who has the say depends on contribution, not voice volume

Governing a cross-plane, cross-Myriad-Rank civilization must answer a question: in major decisions, which civilization's opinion carries more weight? The old cosmos's answer is "the strong decide" — whoever has the bigger fist has the higher weight. Heavenly-Dao Rule 29 gives a new answer: civilizational weight is determined by that civilization's actual contribution to Myriad-Rank symbiosis, not by its military might, long history, or loud voice. A civilization quietly doing incremental development and lifting weak clans may carry more weight than an old stronghold sitting on heavy armies. This is the republican axiom of Myriad Races' landing on "say's allocation."

The six top-level axioms allocate say here: the republican axiom of Myriad Races is this rule's body; the collective-ascension axiom decides weight should flow to civilizations that do more "lifting Myriad Ranks"; the anti-predation axiom negates "might equals weight"; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the assessment process be fair and appealable; the law-first axiom lets "assessment dimensions, calculation methods, rise-fall mechanisms" be stipulated first by law; Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law sets a "floor guarantee" for weight — even an easygoing civilization or one unwilling to superimpose infinity retains a basic weight and is not completely kicked off the decision table for "not striving enough."

## I. Rule Content: Civilizational-Weight Assessment Dimensions

Weight is calculated across four dimensions:

1. **Increment-development contribution** (highest weight): how many unlit law nodes this civilization has developed, how much public increment it has built;
2. **Lift-Myriad-Ranks contribution**: how many weak clans, Youth Supremes, and newly incorporated civilizations it has helped;
3. **Rule-abiding record**: whether it has long abided by Heavenly-Dao rules, any constitutional-violation record;
4. **Ecological contribution**: positive impact on plane ecology and causal-network health.

Weight is not lifetime: quarterly assessment, dynamic rise-fall; easygoing civilizations do not pursue high weight, but retain basic weight and a basic seat.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Symbiosis-degree argument**: the Symbiosis-Degree Assessment System (Document 29) proves a civilization's contribution to the network is quantifiable and comparable;
- **Anti-strong-equals-truth argument**: the old cosmos's "might equals say" is precisely predatory systems' political expression; switching weight to contribution removes the fuel from the bottom;
- **Floor-weight argument**: under Axiom 6, easygoing civilizations inherently possess basic infinite power and retain floor participation rights, not losing their seat for not striving.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Alaya Network automatically calculates a weight score for each civilization, with a dashboard open to all Myriad Ranks;
- In major decisions, weight affects the size of its voting right, but everyone has a basic seat;
- The entire weight rise-fall process is traced, and demoted civilizations may appeal;
- No civilization may long top the chart: a "weight rotation reminder" is set, preventing weight from solidifying into a new class (connecting Rule 19).

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: weight data lagging, update within a time limit;
- **Medium**: forging contribution, grinding weight, relevant weight cleared to zero, responsible persons enter the conversion program;
- **Heavy**: using weight as leverage, manipulating weight calculation, handled heavily under Rule 5 (no private hoarding, no monopoly).

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's weight dashboard is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee verifies weight authenticity as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates weight appeals as the third layer; lower-weight civilizations retain a direct channel to petition the Anchor Council as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: giving say to civilizations with truly large contributions avoids decision-making idling from "strong ones loud-voiced but low-contribution"
- **Anti-predation**: negating might equals weight; under Axiom 6 easygoing civilizations also have a floor seat and do not speak by fists
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems' say follows the protagonist halo; Heng Yuan Zhou's say follows symbiotic contribution
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the five lineages, easygoing and strivers, new and old civilizations are all in the assessment system
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated civilizations automatically gain basic weight, growing with contribution

Civilizational-weight assessment must also guard against a new bias: with higher weight, will it become new privilege again? Heavenly-Dao rules set two locks for this: first, weight is not directly equal to decision power, only affecting voting weight, and everyone has a basic seat; second, weight dynamically rises and falls with rotation reminders, no civilization allowed to long top the chart. Higher-weight civilizations' opinions matter more, but lower-weight civilizations' opinions always have a channel to be heard. This design makes weight an incentive, not a new class — it guides civilizations to do more contribution, not to scramble for seat order.

Civilizational-weight assessment also has a floor design: even if a civilization's weight drops to zero due to force majeure, its basic seat and basic say are still retained. Weight measures "contribution," not "qualification to exist." A civilization temporarily unable to contribute is not kicked out of the Myriad-Rank assembly for low weight — it simply has smaller voting weight, but the door stays open, and weight naturally rebounds after it recovers. This dual track of "contribution measurable, qualification not revoked" is true republicanism.

This is the whole wisdom of weight design: incentivize upward, do not revoke existence.

## Conclusion

Civilizational-weight assessment changes "who decides" from fists to contribution. Weight tilts toward those who lift Myriad Ranks, and civilization runs in the direction of lifting Myriad Ranks.

In the end, the Rule 29 described in this entry — civilizational-weight assessment — is no isolated case; it consistently points to the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's co-construction direction.
