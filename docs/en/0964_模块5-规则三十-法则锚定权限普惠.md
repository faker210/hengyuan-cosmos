# Entry 964 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 30 | Law-Anchor Permission Universalization

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Document 20 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: even law-access rights must be everyone's share

The last few Heavenly-Dao rules land on the hardest technical layer — law-anchor permissions. In the old cosmos, those who could directly converse with law and invoke law's power were always a few prodigies, a few sects, a few bloodlines. Most beings spent a lifetime only "borrowing force," never even touching law's door. Heavenly-Dao Rule 30 must universalize even this door to Myriad Ranks: law-anchor permission is not the strong's exclusive, but the basic qualification every being of Myriad Ranks possesses. How deep a law you may anchor may differ by effort; but "whether you may anchor" is something everyone can.

The six top-level axioms land on the technical layer here: the republican axiom of Myriad Races is this rule's body, directly requiring law-anchor permission universalization; the anti-predation axiom negates "law-access monopoly"; the collective-ascension axiom makes universalized permission the technical precondition for Myriad Ranks' joint ascension; the causal self-discipline axiom requires open permissions still under multi-layer supervision; the law-first axiom lets "permission tiers, application, renewal" be stipulated first by law; Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law gives universalization its deepest confidence — since every being inherently possesses basic infinite power, he naturally qualifies to access the law network to activate this infinity; easygoing ones need not anchor deep layers, but the floor access right is forever theirs; strivers and Youth Supremes may by effort anchor deeper layers and more laws.

## I. Rule Content: Three Points of Permission Universalization

1. **Basic permission for all**: every being is born with basic law-anchor permission, no application, no review;
2. **Deeper permission by effort**: deeper, stronger law-anchor permissions are unlocked step by step by realm, contribution, and heart-nature, not by bloodline, not by grace;
3. **Permissions may not be privately granted or monopolized**: no strong one may "transfer" his own anchor permission to specific disciples to manufacture dependence, nor block some law's access.

Universalization is not equalization — strivers may anchor more, easygoing ones may rest on the basics.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Anchor-structure argument**: the Anchor Structure and Hierarchy (Document 20) points out the law network opens interfaces to all nodes; monopolizing access is originally man-made tolls;
- **Infinite-origin argument**: under Axiom 6, everyone inherently possesses infinite origin; accessing the law network is only "activating" what one already has, not "receiving" external grace;
- **Anti-dependence argument**: the old cosmos's dependence structure of "only joining a sect earns law recognition" essentially takes access rights hostage; universalization removes the hostage.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- The Alaya Network automatically opens a "law-anchor permission account" for every being, with basic permission on by default;
- Deeper permissions are unlocked step by step via the plane-sovereign strong-one four-dimensional assessment (Entry 911) and contracted-responsibility contribution;
- Permission use is fully under Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision, preventing "universalized but abused";
- For beings of newly incorporated civilizations, their basic access right opens automatically with network entry.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: permission-upgrade process not smooth, investigate within a time limit;
- **Medium**: setting up tolls, privately granting permissions to manufacture dependence, relevant persons' permissions frozen, enters conversion program;
- **Heavy**: monopolizing some law's access, using "cutting off your access" to coerce Myriad Ranks, handled heavily under Rules 5 and 19.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's permission-account system is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee checks access monopoly as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates permission-infringement cases as the third layer; Myriad Ranks' clans have direct appeal over "whether their own access is tolled" as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: opening law-access rights, Myriad Ranks simultaneously activate infinite origin, whole-system energy leaps
- **Anti-predation**: dismantling "access monopoly," predation's technical base; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently qualifies to access
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems' law recognition follows the protagonist halo, most never touch it in a lifetime; Heng Yuan Zhou basic permission opens at birth
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the five lineages, easygoing and strivers, Youth Supremes, and planet consciousness each have permission accounts
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: new planes incorporated, their beings automatically gain basic access rights

Law-anchor permission universalization must also answer: everyone has basic permission, will it be chaotic? Heavenly-Dao rules' answer is that basic permission's design is itself safe — it only lets beings access law nodes matching their realm, with deeper nodes unlocked by effort. Universalization does not equal danger, because access rights are tiered: you may touch the door, but how deep behind depends on how far you walk. This "universalization plus tiering" design both breaks the old cosmos's monopoly and avoids "everyone invoking laws" chaos. Permission universalization is not permission flood.

Law-anchor permission universalization must also emphasize: universalization does not mean automatically growing strong. Basic permission only lets you "be able" to access; after access, how deep you anchor and how well you use it still depend on your effort, heart-nature, and contribution. What universalization dismantles is the wall of "not letting you through the door," not the ladder of "reaching the summit upon entering." Easygoing ones may rest on the basics; strivers climb by effort — permission universalization gives a fair starting point, not an equalized terminus.

## Conclusion

Law-anchor permission universalization turns "conversing with law" from a few prodigies' privilege into a daily thing accessible to all Myriad Ranks.

In the end, the Rule 30 described in this entry — law-anchor permission universalization — is no isolated case; it consistently points to the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's co-construction direction.
