# Entry 965 | Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules · Rule 31 | Myriad-Rank Origin Two-Way Cycle

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Document 25 | Series: Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules

## Introduction: from taking to feedback, the closed loop completes

Of the thirty-one Heavenly-Dao rules, the last must answer: what ultimately keeps this whole order turning? The answer is the Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle. The old cosmos's energy was one-way — from the weak to the strong, from Myriad Ranks to predators, one-way extraction until exhaustion. Heng Yuan Zhou must change this one-way pipe into a two-way cycle: the strong ones' developed increment feeds back to Myriad Ranks; Myriad Ranks' grown origin flows back as the strong ones' new fuel; you lift me and I lift you, and the whole cosmos's origin turns more vigorous in the cycle. This is the closed-loop buckle of the Complete Collection of Anti-Predation Heavenly-Dao Rules.

The six top-level axioms close into a circle here: the collective-ascension axiom is this rule's body, requiring origin flow two-way among Myriad Ranks; the republican axiom of Myriad Races decides the cycle is impartial; the anti-predation axiom negates one-way extraction; the causal self-discipline axiom makes the cycle transparent and auditable; the law-first axiom lets "cycle paths, metering, distribution" be stipulated first by law; Axiom 6 · Universal Equal Infinite Law makes the cycle's starting point incomparably fair — since everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, the cycle is not "snatch from nothing to something," but "access everyone's inherent infinity into the network and amplify each other": the easygoing's floor infinity accesses the network, the striver's superimposed infinity accesses the network, the two in the cycle fulfill each other, no one lacks, no one is drained.

## I. Rule Content: Three Channels of the Two-Way Cycle

1. **Strong → Myriad Ranks**: the strong ones' developed increment, refined Dao texts, and accumulated experience feed back to all Myriad Ranks via the Alaya Network;
2. **Myriad Ranks → Strong**: the origin gains from Myriad Ranks' joint growth flow back to builders via the two-way cycle by contribution;
3. **Network self-cycle**: network-effect gains generated by the cycle process itself are reinvested in the unactivated 80% (20% activation strategy).

The cycle is not charity, but making every expenditure return to civilization, principal plus interest, on a larger scale.

## II. Jurisprudential Basis

- **Feedback-cycle argument**: the Symbiotic Feedback Cycle Mechanism (Document 25) proves two-way cycle gains are amplified — one expenditure, amplified by the Myriad-Rank network into a hundredfold return;
- **Self-growth argument**: the Self-Growing Evolution Law points out the cycle makes the cosmos's origin turn more, not less;
- **Infinite-origin argument**: under Axiom 6, the cycle's raw material is everyone's inherent infinite origin; the cycle is not passing finite things around, but activating infinite things in each other.

## III. Execution Mechanism

- Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision are the cycle's "pipe," with all origin flows traced and auditable;
- Contracted-responsibility more-work-more-pay (Entry 909) ensures feedback is distributed by contribution, neither egalitarian nor strong-takes-all;
- A "cycle health dashboard" monitors whether channels are clear, immediately alerting on one-way tendencies;
- The easygoing's floor infinity is included in the cycle but not forced to "strive" — access is enough, no need to superimpose.

## IV. Consequences of Violation

- **Light**: some channel not cycling smoothly, clear within a time limit;
- **Medium**: secretly switching to one-way extraction, taking without giving, relevant persons' permissions frozen, handled heavily under Rules 2 and 5;
- **Heavy**: destroying the two-way cycle, rebuilding one-way predation pipes, is the highest-tier case under the Anti-Predation Committee procedure.

## V. Supervising Subjects

Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's cycle dashboard is the first layer; the Anchor Supervisory Committee audits flow direction as the second layer; the Symbiotic Court adjudicates "one-wayization" cases as the third layer; Myriad Ranks' clans have direct account-checking over "whether their origin is fed back" as the fourth layer.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: one-way extraction necessarily exhausts; two-way cycle makes origin more used and more
- **Anti-predation**: changing predation's "one-way pipe" into a "two-way cycle"; under Axiom 6 everyone inherently possesses infinity, and the cycle is only mutual activation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems' origin flows one-way to the protagonist halo; Heng Yuan Zhou's origin two-way cycles, with Myriad Ranks sharing gains
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the five lineages, easygoing and strivers, Youth Supremes, and planet consciousness are all in the cycle network
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated civilizations automatically access the cycle, expanding with the worldview

Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle must also resolve a doubt: will the cycle make the strong weak? The answer is precisely the opposite. Old-cosmos strong ones maintained strength by one-way extraction; once the extraction object exhausted, the strong themselves collapsed. In the two-way cycle, the strong's power comes from the network's continuous feedback — he lifts Myriad Ranks, Myriad Ranks feed back to him, the stronger he is the more he can lift, the more he lifts the stronger. This cyclical strength is far more stable than extractive strength: extractive is borrowing tomorrow's money to spend today; cyclical is planting today to harvest tomorrow. What the thirty-one Heavenly-Dao rules ultimately want is this more-vigorous, no-one-can-drain strength.

Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle also deeply meshes with the 20% activation strategy: network-effect gains from the cycle first flow back to activate the 80% not yet in the fast channel. This makes the cycle not a "strong get stronger, weak get weaker" Matthew accelerator, but a "first-rich-help-late-rich" universalization pipe. The more the cycle turns, the more activated; the more activated, the faster the cycle. This is a self-reinforcing positive feedback, and also Axiom 6's institutional implementation.

## Conclusion

Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle is the last buckle of the thirty-one Heavenly-Dao rules. Henceforth, origin is no longer drained away by anyone, but turns among Myriad Ranks into a fire that burns more vigorously.

In the end, the Rule 31 described in this entry — Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle — is no isolated case; it consistently points to the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's co-construction direction.
