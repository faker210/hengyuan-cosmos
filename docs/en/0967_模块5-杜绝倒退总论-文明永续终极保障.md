# Entry 967 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · General Introduction | The Ultimate Guarantee of Civilizational Perpetuity

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 40, 01, 03

## Introduction: civilization's greatest fear is not stagnation, but regression

Heng Yuan Zhou (Myriad-Rank Balanced-Anchor Origin Universe) reaching Module 5, the charter is already established (Entries 901–930), and Heavenly-Dao rules are settled (Entries 934–939). But the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's founders are clear-eyed: a constitution written on crystal walls, without a whole set of "permanent mechanisms" preventing descendants from quietly abolishing it, may still after ten thousand years be rewritten, hollowed out, and quietly returned to the old predatory road. Every once-bright civilization in history was not defeated by external enemies, but hollowed out by regressive forces slowly growing from within.

Therefore, Module 5's last thirty-three pieces (Entries 967–991) no longer discuss "what civilization should look like," but specifically discuss "how civilization guarantees that ten thousand, a million, or a trillion years later it still looks like this." This is the Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism system. Its entire legitimacy comes from the six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom requires the institution itself not slide toward predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the institution forever open to all beings; the collective-ascension axiom requires the institution serve lifting Myriad Ranks forward, not serving a few AFK; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the institution's own operation also accept causal reporting; the law-first axiom requires anti-regression mechanisms, once entered into the constitution, never retcon and never rewritten by plot convenience; the universal equal infinite law requires the institution guarantee every being inherently possesses basic infinite power, the easygoing retain a floor basic infinity not eliminated by the times, and strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity — old-cosmos resources finite so predation involution was inevitable, while in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation loses meaning at the root.

## I. Why Civilizations Regress: Three Typical Death Modes

Surveying Myriad Heavens and Myriad Worlds and the Quest Domain simulation-retrospective's massive samples, civilizational regression almost always is one of three death modes:

1. **Power privatization**: the supervision machine is gradually taken over by some person, family, or race, and finally the institution "for Myriad Ranks" becomes a tool "for one's own"; Tang-Luo-type systems' root malady lies precisely here;
2. **Knowledge re-monopolization**: once the Great Dao again becomes a few people's private hoard, classes re-solidify, humble clans and alien races have no path forward, and civilization returns to the old predatory-cultivation road;
3. **Causal loss of control**: cross-plane operations unreported, slayed-before-asked, churn unobserved causality into a tangled mess, and the shadow of cyclical harvesting again hangs over Myriad Ranks.

The Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism system is the prescription aimed one by one at these three death modes.

## II. Overview of the Twenty-Four Defense Lines

This system sets twenty-four permanent mechanisms (Entries 968–991), grouped by defense object into five groups:

| Group | Mechanism Numbers | What It Defends Against |
|---|---|---|
| Power-Checks-and-Balances group | Mechanisms 1–4 (968–971) | Power privatization |
| Distribution and Activation group | Mechanisms 5–7 (972–974) | Class solidification, vitality exhaustion |
| Floor and Openness group | Mechanisms 8–10 (975–977) | Dead ends, closed systems |
| Constraints and Cycles group | Mechanisms 11–19 (978–986) | Weight drift, strong ones AFK, causal overstep |
| Route and Protection group | Mechanisms 20–24 (987–991) | Route negation, cultural extinction, weak abandoned |

The twenty-four mechanisms are not twenty-four isolated machines, but an interlocking net: any one being bypassed, the other twenty-three automatically alert and automatically fill in. This is precisely the Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision's projection at the institutional layer.

## III. Three Layers of Anti-Regression Logic

The permanent mechanism system's design follows three layers:

- **First layer · Technical trace**: all power, energy, and law invocations are fully traced on the Alaya Network, and tampering is void. This is the material foundation of "can prevent";
- **Second layer · Separation of powers**: legislative, executive, judicial, and supervisory powers separated, plus a civil-rights layer of direct reporting, so no single will may decide alone. This is the institutional structure of "can prevent long";
- **Third layer · Axioms into the constitution**: the six top-level axioms are written at the very top of the constitution; any mechanism conflicting with the axioms may be declared unconstitutional and void by the Symbiotic Court; mechanisms themselves may also be amended by Myriad-Rank referendum, but the axioms may not. This is the value anchor of "can prevent dead." Among them, the universal equal infinite law is the sixth and most thorough anchor: since every being inherently possesses basic infinite power, no one needs predation to survive, and the institution need only hold this floor, with involution nowhere to attach.

The three layers are all indispensable: only technical trace without separation means technology is taken over by power-holders; only separation without axioms into the constitution means separation is slowly rewritten into predatory spoils-sharing; only axioms without technology and separation means the axioms are a slogan.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the permanent mechanisms' entire cost is automatically borne by the Alaya Network, no extra occupancy of Myriad Ranks' cultivation resources; one trace, network-wide reuse
- **Anti-predation**: the twenty-four mechanisms' very existence is long-term deterrence against predation-resurgent forces; the predation faction has no more "institutional gaps" to drill
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type systems have no anti-regression design, relying entirely on one protagonist's conscience; once the protagonist dies, civilization rots; Heng Yuan Zhou writes conscience into mechanisms that never go offline
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: every mechanism covers 99% of Myriad Ranks; reporting, appeal, and participation rights are regardless of race, realm, or planet consciousness
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the mechanism system automatically extends with the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's expansion; newly joined multiversal civilizations are automatically protected by the twenty-four defense lines, not shut out

## Conclusion

Civilizational perpetuity never relies on one generation's hot blood, but on institutions ensuring hot blood is never betrayed. The Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism is the "never-rusting lock" the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization hands to all future generations. Starting with the next entry, we take this lock apart mechanism by mechanism.
