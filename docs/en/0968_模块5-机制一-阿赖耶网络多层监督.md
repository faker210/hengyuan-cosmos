# Entry 968 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 1 | Alaya Network Multi-Layer Supervision

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 21, 49, 20

## Introduction: let every exercise of public power be seen network-wide

Of all anti-regression mechanisms, Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision is the first and hardest. Entry 908 already established its jurisprudential status; this mechanism specifically answers a sharper question: why does handing all tracing to one network guarantee civilization a million years later is not quietly dragged back to the predatory era? The answer returns to the six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom requires predation have nowhere to hide; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires every Myriad-Rank member may check; the collective-ascension axiom requires the strong's power be constrained network-wide; the causal self-discipline axiom requires cross-plane operations be fully traceable; the law-first axiom requires the network's algorithms themselves also accept legal review and never retcon; the universal equal infinite law requires the network guarantee every being's inherent basic infinite power not be revoked, the easygoing floor-not-eliminated, strivers able to superimpose higher infinity — since everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, predation loses meaning at the root, and supervision is only the last gate holding this meaning.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 1 stipulates: within the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, every exercise of public power, every public-energy invocation, every law invocation, and every cross-plane operation must be fully traced, fully checkable, and fully traceable on the Alaya Network. Traces may not be deleted, altered, or shielded; any node attempting to act alone off the network has its acts void ab initio. This is not "asking everyone to self-discipline," but "at the system level, operations without traces are not allowed to exist."

## II. Operation

Multi-layer supervision runs as five interlocking layers:

1. **Network-layer automatic monitoring**: once energy flows, law invocations, and plane-shuttling show anomalous patterns, the Alaya Network immediately alerts, no human report needed;
2. **Legislative-layer review**: the Anchor Council periodically re-reviews traces, checking whether execution and adjudication comply with the charter;
3. **Executive-layer audit**: the Symbiotic Executive Committee internally has upper-lower cross-audit;
4. **Judicial-layer adjudication**: the Symbiotic Court accepts charter-level disputes based on original traces, traces being ironclad evidence;
5. **People-layer direct report**: any individual, clan, or planet consciousness of Myriad Ranks may directly inspect, directly report anomalies, directly request adjudication.

The five layers are not subordinate to one another, but mutually back up; any layer failing, the other four automatically fill in.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

The first step of historical civilizational regression is often "the supervisor is unsupervised." This mechanism cuts that road at the root:

- **Alteration void**: traces are jointly witnessed by massive nodes distributed across the four layers of material plane, energy layer, consciousness domain, and law network; single-point alteration equals lying to the whole network, exposed on the spot;
- **Higher, stricter**: higher-realm and more-powerful beings have finer trace granularity and higher monitoring tier; the strong enjoy no "inspection-exemption right";
- **The network is also supervised**: the network's algorithms are periodically audited by a technical-supervision council composed of Myriad-Rank clan representatives, preventing "the supervisor itself" from becoming a new monopoly point.

As long as this network is online, public-power privatization has nowhere to hide; as long as nowhere to hide, regression cannot take its first step.

## IV. Supervising Subject

This mechanism's supervising subject is a "subject without a subject": the network layer self-runs, the legislative, executive, and judicial three horizontally cross-supervise, and the final, highest supervising subject is all Myriad Ranks — every being holds the inspect button and the report button. Any attempt to monopolize the supervision right itself triggers a network-layer alert.

## V. A Concrete Scenario: How Tracing Stops a "Private Ascension"

Imagine a Dao Integration Realm strong one, tired of public construction, planning to cut off his connection to the Alaya Network and hide alone in an unregistered small universe for seclusion. In the old cosmos, this is merely the strong one's freedom; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, the moment he tries to cut the anchor, the network layer alerts — the cutting act itself synchronously leaves traces across the four-layer nodes, and the legislative, executive, judicial, and people layers simultaneously receive the prompt. If he continues forced separation, Mechanism 4's audit immediately files a case; Mechanism 3's Symbiotic Court axiom court determines this violates the collective-ascension and universal-equal-infinite laws, and his separated extra infinity-use is tier-reclaimed, leaving only basic infinite power equal to all beings. From alert to adjudication takes no more than several standard shichen; no one needs to "happen to pass by" to stop him — this is multi-layer supervision's actual power, not relying on upright officials or protagonists.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: one trace, network-wide reuse, avoiding repeated investigation and evidence collection, audit cost approaching zero
- **Anti-predation**: anomalous energy flows automatically alert, predatory cultivation almost impossible to hide at the network layer
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems rely on the protagonist's conscience and sect pressure; once the protagonist retreats, supervision collapses; Heng Yuan Zhou's supervision does not depend on any upright official or any protagonist
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: inspect and report rights cover human men and women, Youth Supremes, alien beasts, plant spirits, planet consciousness, even cosmic-origin bodies
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated multiversal life automatically gains tracing and inspection capability, supervision extends with the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's expansion

## Conclusion

Alaya Network multi-layer supervision is the anti-regression system's foundation. As long as the foundation does not collapse, the twenty-three mechanisms above it have meaning; once the foundation is online, civilization need never fear "another strong one appropriating public affairs as private property."
