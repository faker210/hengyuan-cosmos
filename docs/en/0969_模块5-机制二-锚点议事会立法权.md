# Entry 969 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 2 | Anchor Council Legislative Power

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 49, 40, 03

## Introduction: who writes the rules decides where civilization goes

Mechanism 1 relies on tracing to prevent "reckless acts," but the rules themselves must be written, amended, and interpreted by someone. The Anchor Council's legislative power is the anti-regression system's "rules of rules." Its entire meaning is: civilization's direction may not be decided by one strong one, one clan, or one era's occasional whim, but must be decided by a Myriad-Rank-elected council per the six top-level axioms. The anti-predation axiom constrains legislation from rewarding predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires council seats allocated by clan contribution, not by might; the collective-ascension axiom requires legislation forever serve lifting Myriad Ranks forward; the causal self-discipline axiom requires legislation itself be retroactively reviewable; the law-first axiom requires legislation not conflict with the axioms, with conflicts void ab initio; the universal equal infinite law requires legislation guarantee every being's inherent basic infinite power, the easygoing retained a floor and not eliminated by the times, and strivers and Youth Supremes able to superimpose higher-level infinity — old-cosmos finite resources forced predatory involution, while in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and legislation need only hold this equality, with predation nowhere to speak of.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 2 stipulates: all law details, mechanism amendments, resource-allocation charters, and cross-plane operating norms of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization have their sole legislative body in the Anchor Council. Council seats are allocated by Myriad Ranks' five lineages (carbon-based matter, energy-condensed, mechanical-constructed, consciousness-information, mixed-mutant) and each clan's civilizational weight, with the strong not extra-seated by high realm. Council legislation must go through three readings: proposal, public Myriad-Rank commentary, and vote, with the whole process traced on the Alaya Network.

## II. Operation

The legislative process is a four-step closed loop:

1. **Proposal**: any Myriad-Rank individual, clan, or planet consciousness may propose, with a very low threshold, accepted with reasons attached;
2. **Public commentary**: the proposal is publicized on the Alaya Network, with a Myriad-Rank commentary period of no less than one standard epoch, opinions fully visible;
3. **Vote**: the Council votes by seat weight, with major amendments (touching the five axioms) requiring a popular referendum;
4. **Constitutionality check**: after passing, it is automatically sent to the Symbiotic Court for law-first axiom verification, with conflicts automatically returned.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

Civilizational regression often appears in the form of "law amendment": today amend a clause exempting the strong, tomorrow change a clause re-grading knowledge. This mechanism's defense points:

- **Seats not by fists**: however high a strong one's realm, in the Council he has only one vote's corresponding weight, eliminating "might equals legislative power";
- **Commentary period not compressible**: major amendments must pass a sufficiently long Myriad-Rank commentary, preventing short-term public-opinion coercion of regressive clauses;
- **Axioms not amendable**: the Council may amend details, but forever has no power to amend the six top-level axioms themselves — above the axioms there is no legislator, and the universal equal infinite law's promise of "everyone inherently possesses basic infinity" is especially unshakeable.

As long as legislative power is firmly held by the Myriad-Rank representative body, rules cannot be quietly changed into predation's tools.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — What Happens Trying to Amend a "Strong-One Exemption" Law

Some clan proposes an amendment in the Council: those of Origin Realm and above may be exempt from reporting cross-plane operations, on the grounds that "the strong will know how to handle it." This proposal enters the public commentary period per procedure. During commentary, the Alaya Network automatically compares it against the six axioms — it violates the causal self-discipline axiom's "strict reporting," and also violates the equal treatment required by the universal equal infinite law. The Myriad-Rank people layer during commentary files large numbers of opposing opinions: exempting reporting equals opening a back door for the strong to roam cross-plane at will. At vote, even if the Council narrowly passes, automatic constitutionality check returns it; if it forces through, the Symbiotic Court's axiom court directly declares it unconstitutional and void. A bill seemingly "convenient for the strong" hits brick walls at four gates — proposal, commentary, vote, check. This is the actual effect of locking legislative power in Myriad Ranks' hands and in the axioms' hands.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

The Anchor Council itself is under triple supervision: Alaya Network full tracing (Mechanism 1); the Symbiotic Court's post-hoc constitutionality check; the Myriad-Rank people layer may directly express will during commentary and referendum periods. Council seats rotate periodically, representatives are responsible to the clans they represent, and may be recalled at any time.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: low-threshold proposal + network commentary lets Myriad-Rank wisdom directly enter legislation, avoiding closed-door resource misallocation
- **Anti-predation**: the legislative procedure structurally blocks "the strong making private laws"; the predation faction can never whitewash itself through amendment
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' rules change at the protagonist's whim, with whatever law the plot needs; Heng Yuan Zhou's rules go through three readings + axiom check, and plot may never kidnap law
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the five lineages, planet consciousness, and cosmic-origin bodies all have proposal and commentary rights, not biased toward humans just because humans are strong
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly joined multiversal civilizations automatically gain Council observer seats and commentary rights, gradually converting to formal seats with the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's expansion

## Conclusion

The Anchor Council's legislative power is the anti-regression system's steering-wheel lock. With the steering wheel locked in Myriad Ranks' hands, civilization will never be driven back to the old predatory road by some strong one.
