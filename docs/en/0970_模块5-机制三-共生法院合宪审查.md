# Entry 970 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 3 | Symbiotic Court Constitutional Review

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 48, 40, 03

## Introduction: written rules still need someone with a ruler to measure

Mechanism 2 ensures rules are written by a Myriad-Rank-elected council, but written rules may still drift or be executed crookedly. Who measures? This is the Symbiotic Court's constitutional-review power. It is the law-first axiom's direct institutional embodiment: setting self-consistency above all convenience; any rule, judgment, or strong one's temporary disposition, as long as it conflicts with the six top-level axioms, is uniformly void.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 3 stipulates: the Symbiotic Court has constitutional-review power over Anchor Council legislation, Symbiotic Executive Committee administration, plane-sovereign strong-one dispositions, and cross-plane operation teams' conduct. Any Myriad-Rank individual, clan, or planet consciousness may file a constitutional-review request; once accepted, the court first freezes the relevant act, then does axiom comparison, and publicizes the judgment network-wide. The ruler in the court's hand, never worn, is the six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom, the republican axiom of Myriad Races, the collective-ascension axiom, the causal self-discipline axiom, the law-first axiom, and the universal equal infinite law — any rule that dares revoke some being's inherent basic infinite power or eliminate the easygoing is uniformly voided by the axiom court, because before infinite origin, predation has lost its reason to exist from the start.

## II. Operation

Review runs in three parallel chambers:

1. **Axiom chamber**: specifically adjudicates whether rules violate the six top-level axioms, responsible only to law, not to any institution;
2. **Causal chamber**: specifically adjudicates whether cross-plane operations violate causal self-discipline, comparing Alaya Network traces;
3. **Symbiosis chamber**: specifically adjudicates whether distribution, assessment, and grant of authority comply with Myriad-Rank republicanism and a path for all.

The three chambers' judgments are independent, with major disputes by a joint panel of three chambers for final judgment. Once a judgment is made, relevant rules automatically become void or are rectified, no need to wait for the legislature's ratification.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

The most hidden regression in history is "legally regressing" — every step follows old rules, but the old rules themselves were long ago hollowed out. This mechanism's defense points:

- **Axioms above rules**: even if a rule has gone through the full legislative procedure, as long as it conflicts with the six axioms, the Symbiotic Court has the power to declare it void. The Council cannot use "legal procedure" to hollow out the axioms;
- **Freeze first**: suspicious acts are frozen immediately upon filing, preventing regressive forces from "cooking raw rice into cooked" before reversal;
- **Responsible only to law**: judges are not subordinate to any executive institution; their authority comes from the law-first axiom itself, and no one may remove or replace them.

With this ruler, any "regression in legal clothing" cannot pass.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

The Symbiotic Court itself is under Mechanism 1's trace supervision and Mechanism 4's audit supervision; its judgments are public and checkable, and the Myriad-Rank people layer may file retrials for obvious perversion of law; the three chambers are mutually independent and mutually error-correcting. The court's ultimate supervisor is not some institution, but the six axioms themselves — if a court judgment conflicts with the axioms, any Myriad-Rank being may per the axioms directly petition for a network-wide referendum.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — Why a "Legal" Distribution Law Is Judged Void

Some construction unit passes a detail in the Council: the unit's output of extra infinite power is distributed by realm, with those at Dao Integration Realm and above taking 70% exclusively. The detail has gone through the full legislative procedure, about to take effect. At this point, a Sense-Condensed Realm cultivator files a constitutional-review request. The Symbiotic Court's axiom chamber compares the six axioms: the detail distributes by realm, violating the republican axiom of Myriad Races' equal evolutionary qualification, and also violating the universal equal infinite law — although extra infinity is superimposed by contribution, cutting the cake "70%" by realm is essentially converting realm into distribution privilege. The court first freezes the detail, then declares it unconstitutional and void, returning it to the Council to redo distribution ratios by contribution, not by realm. The whole process requires this Sense-Condensed Realm cultivator to have no background or combat power — he only needs to press the appeal button. Axioms above rules, rules above procedure, procedure above individuals; this ruler measures precisely such moments.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: constitutional check is automated and preposed, blocking possibly drifting rules before they take effect, avoiding the enormous loss of post-hoc overturn and redo
- **Anti-predation**: however beautifully packaged predatory rules are, the axiom chamber sees through them at a glance
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: in Tang-Luo systems "the protagonist is right means just," judgments reverse with the plot; Heng Yuan Zhou's judgments compare only axioms, and plot may never reverse a judgment
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: constitutional-review request rights cover all beings, weak and strong equal before the court
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated multiversal civilizations' disputes are likewise under the three chambers' jurisdiction; the larger the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, the more uniform the court's axiom ruler

## VI. Constitutional Review's Ultimate Confidence: Axiom 6 Lets the Weak Truly Afford to Be Heard

The Symbiotic Court's ruler is sharp not only because the ruler itself is iron, but because the one holding it no longer needs any background. However perfect the old cosmos's judiciary, the weak suing the strong still must compete on family, connections, and what kind of advocate they can afford; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, all this is replaced at the root by the universal equal infinite law. Axiom 6 writes it plainly: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power, the easygoing retain a floor and are not eliminated, and strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity. This means when any Sense-Condensed Realm cultivator files constitutional review, he stands in court relying not on combat power, not on clan, not on realm, but on the basic infinity he was born with — this infinity, before the axioms, is completely equal to a Dao Integration strong one's superimposed infinity. The Alaya Network traces his appeal, the court's comparison, and the judgment's publication all checkably; the law-first axiom guarantees this process never retcons and cannot be bypassed by "this time is special." The weak daring to sue, able to sue, and winning — only then is constitutional review not a scrap of paper. The reason Heng Yuan Zhou dares hand this ruler to the weakest being is precisely that Axiom 6 has welded "everyone inherently possesses infinity" onto the constitution's base — the ruler measures not whose voice is loud, but whose reasoning matches the axioms.

## Conclusion

The Symbiotic Court's constitutional review is the anti-regression system's "final ruler." The ruler is there, and the axioms are there; the ruler does not wear, and civilization forever has the ability to correct its course.
