# Entry 971 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 4 | Anchor Supervisory Committee Audit

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 49, 27, 20

## Introduction: beyond the three powers, one more independent eye

Mechanism 2 governs "legislation," Mechanism 3 "adjudication," Mechanism 1 "tracing." But in daily operation, how resources are spent, how assessment is done, and to whom authority is granted still need an independent, full-time, non-decision-making eye watching — this is the Anchor Supervisory Committee. It is the anti-regression system's fourth defense line, specifically watching "the executive layer's chronic rot." The six top-level axioms run through it equally: the anti-predation axiom requires audits plug dark channels of private resource swallowing; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires audits treat Myriad Ranks equally; the collective-ascension axiom requires audits ensure the strong's contribution matches their authority; the causal self-discipline axiom requires audits cover all cross-plane accounts; the law-first axiom requires audit standards themselves never retcon; the universal equal infinite law requires audits verify public guarantees truly let every being receive back that basic infinite power, with the easygoing's floor not skimmed and strivers' superimposition not crowding others' floor — old-cosmos finite resources meant audits only prevented graft, while in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and audits must further confirm this infinity truly reaches everyone's hands.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 4 stipulates: the Anchor Supervisory Committee is independent of legislative, executive, and judicial branches, specifically conducting regular audits and special audits over all public-power operations. Audit objects include energy budgets, law-invocation quotas, civilizational-weight scores, grants of authority, cross-plane operation accounts, and dual-track time-domain allocation. Audit results are publicized network-wide, with problem ledgers item-by-item closed out.

## II. Operation

Audit runs at three levels:

1. **Routine annual audit**: every standard epoch does a full reconciliation of all public-power institutions, comparing Alaya traces against actual expenditures;
2. **Special audit**: when anomaly alerts (Mechanism 1), people-layer reports, or weight mutations occur, immediately file a case and dig deep;
3. **Flight inspection**: no advance notice, directly pulling node raw data, preventing "reconciliation-style back-accounting."

Audit teams are mixed by Myriad-Rank lineage, with aliens auditing their own clan and cross-region auditing their own region, eliminating "auditing oneself."

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

Civilizational regression's typical executive-layer symptom is "accounts slowly going murky": budgets quietly tilting to the strong, assessment scores inflated for acquaintances, authority granted to insufficient contributors. This mechanism's defense points:

- **Reconcile to raw**: audit takes Alaya Network raw traces as authoritative, does not credit any post-hoc report; back-accounting and smoothing accounts are all void;
- **Mixed teams**: auditors and audited are forcibly crossed in clan, lineage, and jurisdiction, cutting "acquaintance closed loops";
- **Ledger closure**: found problems are itemized, rectified item by item, publicized item by item; rectification not in place means no closure, eliminating "investigate then turn the page."

As long as accounts are forever transparent and someone independent forever checks, the executive layer has no room to slowly rot.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

The Anchor Supervisory Committee itself is under Mechanism 1's trace supervision and Mechanism 3's constitutional review; its audit standards are established by Anchor Council legislation, not self-interpreted; audit staff rotate, recuse, and cross, and the people layer may directly report ineffective audits. The Supervisory Committee decides nothing, allocates nothing, only audits and reports, structurally avoiding itself becoming a new power center.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — A "Looking-Normal" Energy Budget

Some cross-plane engineering team's annual energy budget has 10% spent under "loss," with the accounts written watertight. Routine annual audit finds no problem, but an unannounced flight inspection has the audit team directly pull Alaya Network raw node data, comparing and finding this 10% "loss" did not convert into engineering output but flowed to team core members' private cultivation spaces. The problem is immediately ledgered: relevant members' contracted qualification suspended, flowed-out energy recovered per raw traces, accounts re-smoothed. The whole process does not credit any explanation the team post-hoc submits — because audit recognizes only raw traces. This kind of "accounts slowly going murky" could hide for decades in the old cosmos; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, one flight inspection exposes it.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: independent audit spends every cent of public energy in the open, eliminating energy idling from leaks
- **Anti-predation**: once dark resource channels are found by audit reconciliation, predatory extraction is immediately exposed
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' resources flow to one protagonist, no one dares check; Heng Yuan Zhou's resource flows are public network-wide and independently reconciled
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: audit treats humans and aliens, strong and mortals, planet consciousness and cosmic life equally, with no privileged exemptions
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated multiversal civilizations' accounts automatically enter audit scope; however much the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm expands, the audit network covers that much

## VI. The Audit's End: Letting "Chronic Rot" Be Snuffed the Moment It Peeks

The Anchor Supervisory Committee's eye truly guards not against giant graft, but that kind of "chronic rot" that shows no problem for decades and once checked is full of holes. Old-cosmos audits failed often not for lack of institutions, but because institutions only checked large sums and not petty bits — each year shifting a bit of budget, each assessment inflating acquaintances by half a point, each authority granted to the slightly-less-qualified one; each item individually looks "compliant," and ten years later the whole system is hollowed out. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's audit precisely targets this chronic illness: it takes Alaya Network raw traces as authoritative, crediting no post-hoc reports; it is mixed by Myriad-Rank lineage, with aliens auditing their own clan and cross-region auditing their own region, cutting acquaintance closed loops; it ledger found problems item by item and closes them item by item, never turning the page before rectification is in place. Layer on the universal equal infinite law's bottom line — audit must verify public guarantees truly let every being receive back basic infinite power, with the easygoing's floor not skimmed under any "loss" name — and audit is no longer just graft-prevention, but guarding Axiom 6 itself. The law-first axiom requires audit standards never retcon, and Heng Yuan Zhou's account system is forever transparent to Myriad Ranks. As long as this eye does not sleep, the executive layer has no room to "slowly rot."

## Conclusion

The Anchor Supervisory Committee's audit is the anti-regression system's "eye that does not sleep." Legislation prevents wild writing, adjudication prevents wild judging, and this eye prevents wild spending, wild grants, and wild tilting — the three powers plus this eye, and the executive layer cannot rot.
