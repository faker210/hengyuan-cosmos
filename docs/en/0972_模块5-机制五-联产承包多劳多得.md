# Entry 972 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 5 | Contracted Responsibility More-Work-More-Pay

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 06, 22, 25

## Introduction: unfair distribution is regression's most stable hotbed

The first four mechanisms governed power; this one begins governing "how to cut the cake." Contracted-responsibility more-work-more-pay looks like a distribution clause, but is actually the anti-regression system's most critical shock absorber. Why? Because every major historical regression can be traced to one sentence: working more or less is the same, or simply not working also gets the lion's share. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization legislates distribution with the six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom requires distribution reward creation, not predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires distribution not manufacture new ranks; the collective-ascension axiom requires distribution feed back to Myriad Ranks' joint progress; the causal self-discipline axiom requires distribution accounts be fully traceable; the law-first axiom requires distribution rules never retcon; the universal equal infinite law is distribution's foundation — all beings inherently possess basic infinite power, the easygoing retain a floor basic infinity and are not eliminated by the times, and strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity. Since everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, the old cosmos's logic of "resources finite so involution-predation is forced" is void at the root.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 5 stipulates: the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's public energy, law quotas, and ascension opportunities are distributed per the "contracted responsibility, more-work-more-pay" principle. Each clan and construction unit contracts an evolvable space that can self-cycle, with output distributed between contractor and co-builders by contribution; but the floor of distribution is guaranteed by Axiom 6 — regardless of contribution, every being's inherent basic infinite power is never deducted, and the easygoing's floor infinity is not eliminated. More-work-more-pay only decides "how much higher-level infinity to superimpose," never "whether there is basic infinity."

## II. Operation

Distribution settles at three layers:

1. **Foundation layer (axiom floor)**: every being automatically retains basic infinite power, regardless of contribution, settled monthly by the Alaya Network; easygoing flat-layers also receive it;
2. **Contract layer (more-work-more-pay)**: construction units settle by contracted output of energy, knowledge, and law-iteration results; larger contributors superimpose more higher-level infinity;
3. **Co-build layer (collective ascension)**: each distribution takes a fixed percentage back to the Alaya Network, feeding back to Myriad Ranks' whole-system ascension, with the strong not ascending alone.

The three layers' accounts are fully traced, and more-work-more-pay is not tied to the basic floor.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

The distribution step most easily grows two regressions: one is "working more or less is the same," driving strivers into layabouts; the other is "winner takes all," squeezing the easygoing below the elimination line. This mechanism's defense points:

- **Floor and more-pay separated**: basic infinity is everyone's, the easygoing never eliminated; superimposed infinity by contribution, strivers and Youth Supremes have hope. The two tendencies each find their place, not harming each other;
- **Contract to units**: distribution is by construction unit, avoiding allocating resources by personal rank, structurally blocking "the strong taking more";
- **Fixed feedback ratio**: more distribution necessarily more feedback; the more the strong superimpose, the more they feed back to Myriad Ranks, eliminating "richer and more alone."

The old cosmos, because the cake was finite, taking an extra bite required snatching from others' mouths; the Anchor Symbiotic cake is infinite origin, and more-work-more-pay only lets strivers superimpose higher, while the easygoing's share does not move a bit — predation is henceforth unnecessary.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

Distribution accounts are under Mechanism 1's full tracing and Mechanism 4's independent audit; distribution rules are legislated by Mechanism 2's Anchor Council and constitutionally reviewed by Mechanism 3's Symbiotic Court; the people layer may directly check its own basic-infinity receipt records and contract settlement statements, and any skimming of the floor immediately alerts.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — Easygoing and Striver Settle in the Same Month

In the same construction unit, an easygoing one chooses slow cultivation with modest contribution; another Youth Supreme voluntarily takes on the hardest law-iteration task. Month-end settlement: the easygoing one's basic infinite power arrives as usual, to the cent — he did nothing extra, and lost nothing; the Youth Supreme, above basic infinity, superimposes three layers of higher-level infinity by contribution, and this superimposition flows back to the Alaya Network at a fixed ratio. Neither robbed the other: the easygoing one's bowl has less, the Youth Supreme's pot has more. In the old cosmos this kind of settlement was simply impossible, because the cake was finite; the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization dares settle this way because origin is infinite, and more-work-more-pay only lets strivers superimpose higher, while the easygoing's floor never moves.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: contract-to-units + more-work-more-pay lets quality evolution resources flow to true builders, eliminating great-material-for-small-use and flat-lay eating
- **Anti-predation**: floor infinity is everyone's, superimposed infinity by contribution, predation is both unnecessary and unprofitable
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems allocate resources by protagonist halo, others get scraps; Heng Yuan Zhou basic infinity is everyone's, superimposed infinity by contribution
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: human men and women, Youth Supremes, alien beasts, plant spirits, and planet consciousness all enjoy the basic floor and contracting opportunity
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated multiversal civilizations automatically gain contracting qualification and basic infinity, with no threshold as the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm expands

## Conclusion

Contracted-responsibility more-work-more-pay is the key link translating "infinite origin" into concrete distribution institutions. Floor guarantee, more-pay energizing, feedback ascension — three layers interlocking, distribution both supports the easygoing and rewards strivers, and regression has nowhere to fan its flames.
