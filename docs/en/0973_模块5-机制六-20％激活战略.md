# Entry 973 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 6 | 20% Activation Strategy

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 23, 75, 29

## Introduction: a pool of stagnant water, however fair, will still reek

Mechanism 5 solves "how to distribute"; Mechanism 6 solves "how to stay alive." Civilization has another regression called "vitality exhaustion": everyone has a floor, so everyone flat-lays, and Myriad Ranks gradually lose the drive forward. The 20% activation strategy is the regular injection of stirring force into this living pool. Its positioning under the six top-level axioms is especially clear: the anti-predation axiom requires activation by ignition, not by driving; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires activation slots open to all beings; the collective-ascension axiom requires activated energy feed back to the whole network; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the activation process not manufacture anxious involution; the law-first axiom requires activation ratios and procedures never retcon; the universal equal infinite law draws the bottom line — activation is "inviting strivers to superimpose higher infinity," never "eliminating the easygoing." The easygoing's basic infinity is steadily retained; the activation strategy is only responsible for making those willing to rush rush more steadily.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 6 stipulates: every standard epoch, the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization screens about 20% of Myriad Ranks as "activation objects," giving them accelerated resources, mentor matching, and dual-track time-domain priority to break through to higher realms and greater contribution. Activation objects are selected not by birth, clan, or old realm, but solely by present willingness and symbiosis-degree potential. The key: the 80% not activated suffer no derogation, and basic infinite power does not move a bit.

## II. Operation

Activation runs as a four-step cycle:

1. **Voluntary enrollment**: every being chooses whether to enroll; the easygoing not enrolling is not backward;
2. **Potential assessment**: Alaya Network + multi-layer supervision give a potential curve per the symbiosis-degree assessment system, open and transparent;
3. **Resource superimposition**: selected ones gain Origin Acceleration Domain time, Multiplication Fruit quotas, and mentor slots, superimposing higher-level infinity;
4. **Feedback recovery**: after activation objects break through, they must return results and knowledge network-wide, feeding back to the next round's 20%.

Activation objects rotate each round, no lifetime elite.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

The vitality-exhaustion type's regression marker is "everyone stopped moving." This mechanism's defense points:

- **Voluntary, not elimination**: activation is invitation, not screening-style elimination; easygoing flat-layers are never abandoned by the times, and Axiom 6's floor is a hard constraint;
- **Rotation, not solidification**: activation objects rotate each round, preventing the "activation list" from becoming a new privileged class;
- **Feedback closed loop**: activated ones must return results, avoiding "the activated strong ascending alone."

The old cosmos's "activation" was essentially elimination: stepping on the remaining 80% as a foothold; the Anchor Symbiotic activation is ignition: those lit burn brighter, and the fire still warms all Myriad Ranks.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

Activation lists and potential assessments are fully traced (Mechanism 1); activation-resource disbursement is audited by Mechanism 4; if "activation-list solidification," "activation-resource private deal-making," or "unactivated being de facto discriminated" appears, the people layer may directly report, and the Symbiotic Court accepts. Activation ratios and procedures are fixed by Anchor Council legislation, not casually raised for momentary results.

## VI. Activation Is Ignition, Not Elimination

It must be emphasized: the activation strategy's measure is that it ignites those willing to burn, not blows out those not willing. The old cosmos's selection was essentially elimination — select 20%, eliminate 80%. The Anchor Symbiotic activation is essentially invitation — select 20% to accelerate, leaving 80% also living well. This one-character difference is backed by Axiom 6's infinite confidence: because origin is infinite, activating one need not cost eliminating another. Those lit burn bright; those unlit have their own warmth. A civilization's vitality should not be built on making most people feel left out.

Some cultivator misses the 20% activation list for two consecutive rounds. In the old cosmos, this means he is "eliminated," and surrounding glances make it impossible to stay. But in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, he opens his account: basic infinite power arrives monthly, the six gates of "a path for all" are all open, and he may fully slow-cultivate at his own pace in the Quest Sediment Domain. Next round, if he wishes to enroll, the network re-assesses based on his state this round; if he does not wish to enroll, no one presses him. The activation list is not the Book of Life and Death, but an invitation ticket. And those activated, after breaking through, must return results — this slow cultivator next round freely learns the new road the activated ones blazed. Between stillness and motion, no one is eliminated, no one is wasted.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: precisely placing accelerated resources on the 20% most willing to rush right now is far more efficient than spreading pepper evenly
- **Anti-predation**: activation by voluntary enrollment and potential assessment, not by stepping on others; basic infinity is everyone's, and activation manufactures no predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems activate only the protagonist, others as backdrop; Heng Yuan Zhou activates 20% each round with rotation, everyone has a chance
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: activation slots are fully open to the five lineages, planet consciousness, and Youth Supremes, with no clan threshold
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated multiversal civilizations automatically enter the activation pool; the larger the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, the more ignition seeds each round

## Conclusion

The 20% activation strategy is civilization's heartbeat. Floor infinity keeps civilization from starving; the activation strategy keeps it from suffocating — one still, one moving, Myriad Ranks neither eliminated nor flat-laying into stagnant water. This is the activation strategy's whole meaning as civilization's heartbeat.
