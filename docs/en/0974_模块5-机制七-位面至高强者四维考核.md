# Entry 974 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 7 | Plane-Sovereign Strong-One Four-Dimensional Assessment

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 17, 29, 26

## Introduction: the greater the power, the harder the exam

Mechanism 6 lets strivers rush upward, but rushing to the plane-sovereign strong-one tier, power has grown strong enough to single-handedly overturn a civilization. For such beings, tracing alone is not enough; there must be a periodically re-taken exam. The plane-sovereign strong-one four-dimensional assessment is the anti-regression system's lock that "puts shackles on the strong." Its logic under the six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom requires the strong's power not be used for predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the strong be responsible to Myriad Ranks; the collective-ascension axiom requires the strong lift Myriad Ranks rather than ascend alone; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the strong's cross-plane power be reported; the law-first axiom requires assessment standards never retcon; the universal equal infinite law is the assessment's touchstone — if a sovereign strong one harms any being's basic infinite power, no matter how great his power or high his heart-nature, the assessment directly fails. Old-cosmos strong ones spoke by might, might equals truth; Anchor Symbiotic strong ones speak by exam paper, and if the paper fails, however strong they must hand over power.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 7 stipulates: all beings reaching Origin Realm and becoming plane-sovereign strong ones must accept a four-dimensional periodic assessment, with weights power 40, heart-nature 25, contribution 20, responsibility 15. Those failing the assessment have their authority tier-reclaimed and law-invocation quotas downgraded, until they re-take and pass. The assessment is once per standard epoch, exempt from no seniority, fame, or past merit.

## II. Operation

The four dimensions correspond respectively:

1. **Power dimension (40)**: whether the power itself is pure, controllable, and hides predatory remnants;
2. **Heart-nature dimension (25)**: whether it withstands the shortcut heart-demon test and still puts Myriad Ranks first;
3. **Contribution dimension (20)**: what it has actually done for the Alaya Network and Myriad Ranks' ascension;
4. **Responsibility dimension (15)**: whether it fulfills the duty of lifting Myriad Ranks forward and holds causal reporting.

Any dimension falling below the passing line means overall failure, with no "covering flaws with power."

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

Strong-one-out-of-control regression is the most common across Myriad Heavens and Myriad Worlds: a strong one who crushes the cosmos ends up living as the sky above Myriad Ranks. This mechanism's defense points:

- **Power is only one passing line**: power takes 40 but is not all; heart-nature, contribution, and responsibility together take 60, eliminating "can fight means a good strong one";
- **Periodic re-exam, not lifetime**: sovereign strong ones are not once-in-office forever done, but must re-submit exam papers every epoch;
- **Axiom 6 one-vote veto**: as long as it harms any being's inherent basic infinite power, no matter how high the other three dimensions, it directly fails.

The old cosmos exalts the strong onto divine altars, and the strong live more and more like gods; the Anchor Symbiotic puts the strong in the exam room, and the strong live more and more like public servants.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

The assessment is chaired by the Anchor Supervisory Committee (Mechanism 4), with exam questions fixed by Anchor Council legislation, the assessment process and scores fully traced network-wide (Mechanism 1), and the people layer may file retrials for obvious leniency. Sovereign strong ones do not serve as examiners for each other, eliminating same-school mutual protection.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — Why a Full-Mark-Power Strong One Failed

An Origin Realm strong one scored full 40 in the power dimension, with combat power enough to crush a region. But across the four-dimensional assessment, his heart-nature dimension scored only 10 — network traces show he over the past epoch multiple times used power to interfere with subordinate clans' internal affairs without reporting; the responsibility dimension scored only 5, because he has for two consecutive epochs held no public post. However strong the power, two dimensions fell below the passing line, and overall failure. His law-invocation quota is downgraded, his authority tier-reclaimed, until he retakes heart-nature and responsibility. In the old cosmos, such a strong one would be revered as sky, worshipped as emperor; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, he is merely a candidate who did not do well on the exam — the paper writes plainly: being able to fight does not mean deserving the seat.

## VI. Putting the Strong Back in the Exam Room, Civilization Need Not Fear the Strong

The four-dimensional assessment ultimately guards the iron law that "power must match responsibility." Once old-cosmos strong ones reach the summit, no one examines them again — they themselves become the standard. This situation sooner or later has problems: a highest being no one manages sooner or later lives himself as the sky above Myriad Ranks. The four-dimensional assessment snuffs this possibility at the root: the higher one is, the harder the paper, the more frequent the exam, and the more public the exam room. The strong are not sky, but candidates; civilization does not fear the strong being strong, but fears no one manages the strong after the exam. Keeping sovereign strong ones forever in the exam room, Myriad Ranks need never fear waking one day and finding the sky above has changed color.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: directing the strong's power toward contribution and responsibility, avoiding sovereign strong ones AFK retirement and great-material-for-small-use
- **Anti-predation**: the power dimension specifically checks predatory remnants, the heart-nature dimension specifically guards shortcut heart-demons, and predation-type strong ones cannot pass
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type protagonists the stronger the less managed, finally living as despotic heavenly emperors; Heng Yuan Zhou the stronger the stricter the paper, power and responsibility forever equal
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: assessment standards treat human sovereigns, alien sovereigns, planet-consciousness sovereigns, and cosmic-origin bodies equally
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated multiversal planes' sovereign strong ones automatically enter the assessment; the larger the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, the more uniform the paper

## Conclusion

The plane-sovereign strong-one four-dimensional assessment turns "the strong must be good" from an expectation into an executable paper. The strong are no longer sky, but candidates — this mechanism holds, and civilization need never fear being backlashed by the power it raised.
