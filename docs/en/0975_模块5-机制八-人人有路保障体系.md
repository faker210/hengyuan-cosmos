# Entry 975 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 8 | A-Path-for-All Guarantee System

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 08, 43, 37

## Introduction: the last floor is that no one is locked out

Mechanisms 5 through 7 governed distribution, vitality, and the strong respectively. But there is another regression called "dead-end": some clan, some planet consciousness, some individual with the worst inborn aptitude, slowly finds himself in civilization with simply no door upward. The A-Path-for-All Guarantee System is the anti-regression system's floor project. It is the six top-level axioms' most direct landing: the anti-predation axiom requires roads be built, not snatched; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires 99% full coverage, not one door missing; the collective-ascension axiom requires stragglers be pulled up, not left behind; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the guarantee itself leave no causal hidden wound; the law-first axiom requires the "a path for all" promise never retcon; the universal equal infinite law pushes this to the extreme — since every being inherently possesses basic infinite power, "no road" theoretically should not exist, and the guarantee system only cashes this inherent infinity into visible doors.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 8 stipulates: the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization guarantees all beings — human men and women, Youth Supremes, alien beasts, plant spirits, planet consciousness, cosmic-origin bodies, and multiverse residual civilizations — a complete path from Sense-Condensed Realm to higher realms, including education, medicine, housing, resources, mentors, and trial-and-error opportunities in six aspects. Any being, due to inborn aptitude, birth, clan, or planetary environment, feeling "nowhere to go," may apply with one click, and the guarantee system must within a time limit open a personalized path for him.

## II. Operation

Guarantee runs as a six-gate closed loop:

1. **Education gate**: the symbiotic education system matches every being with an entry method and basic perception training;
2. **Medical gate**: the symbiotic medical system repairs cultivation hidden wounds, not abandoning treatment due to birth differences;
3. **Housing gate**: safe cultivation space is available to all;
4. **Resource gate**: basic cultivation energy is disbursed per Axiom 6's floor, decoupled from contribution;
5. **Mentor gate**: every being may apply for an Alaya-Network-matched guide;
6. **Trial-and-error gate**: early breakthrough failures are not punished and not sentenced to death, allowing repeated road-testing.

All six gates open counts as "has a road."

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

The dead-end type's regression marker is "humble clans no longer produce talents," and over time civilization loses new blood. This mechanism's defense points:

- **One-click application**: the road is not waiting for the strong's charity, but institutionalized floor; the weak may also actively speak up;
- **All six gates open counts**: giving education without medicine, or resources without a mentor, does not count as "has a road," eliminating half-baked guarantees;
- **Meshed with Axiom 6**: basic infinite power is inherent to all, and the guarantee system only cashes it into doors; the easygoing flat-laying also has a door to walk, and strivers and Youth Supremes have doors to rush through.

The old cosmos's "road" was snatched, with those who snatched it mocking those who did not; the Anchor Symbiotic "road" is built to the door, and knocking gets an answer.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

The six gates' receipt status is audited by Mechanism 4; any gate long blocked or application shunted, the people layer may directly report; guarantee standards are legislated by the Anchor Council, and Mechanism 3's Symbiotic Court accepts constitutional review of "refused roads." The guarantee system's ultimate supervisor is every being himself — whether the door is underfoot, one step tells.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — A Small Clan That "Cannot See a Future"

Some small clan living on a barren energy belt, with the whole clan naturally slow-perceiving; in the old cosmos such a clan would sooner or later be swallowed by surrounding strong ones. In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, the clan leader applies with one click for a "path assessment." Education gate: the network customizes an entry method suited to slow perception; medical gate: repairs generations of accumulated perception hidden wounds; resource gate: basic cultivation energy arrives per Axiom 6's floor; mentor gate: matches an old cultivator also from slow-perception stock; housing and trial-and-error gates open simultaneously. Three epochs later, this small clan produces its first Law-Origin Realm cultivator — not a sudden talent leap, but the road finally built to their feet. The old cosmos asks "why has this clan not died"; the Anchor Symbiotic asks "why did no one before ask what they needed."

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: placing guarantees on those most in need, avoiding quality seedlings dying from resource misallocation
- **Anti-predation**: roads built to the door, beings need not prey to open doors, and predatory cultivation loses its soil
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems only the protagonist has a road, others as fertilizer; Heng Yuan Zhou everyone has a road, 99% coverage
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: human men and women, Youth Supremes, alien beasts, plant spirits, planet consciousness, and multiverse residual civilizations all have a road
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated multiversal civilizations automatically gain six-gate guarantees; the larger the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, the denser the doors

## Conclusion

The A-Path-for-All Guarantee System is the anti-regression system's last floor brick. The first five mechanisms govern the strong, distribution, and vitality; this one governs the weakest — as long as the weakest also has a road, civilization will never regress to the old cosmos of "strong takes all, weak wait to die." Roads built to the door, knocking gets an answer; this is what "a path for all" four characters ultimately look like. In the end, a path for all is not a slogan, but a button every being may press with his own hand.

Roads built to the door, knocking gets an answer; this mechanism is the floor brick, and civilization's most simple promise to every being.
