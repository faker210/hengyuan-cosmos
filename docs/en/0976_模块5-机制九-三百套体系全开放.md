# Entry 976 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 9 | All 300 Systems Fully Open

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 10, 18, 91

## Introduction: once the Great Dao is again privately hoarded, everything rots from the start

The first eight mechanisms govern power, distribution, the strong, and the floor, but as long as cultivation systems themselves are again privately hoarded by a few, all institutions are quietly hollowed out. All 300 Origin Cultivation Systems fully open is the anti-regression system's "knowledge-layer anti-regression." Its position under the six top-level axioms is irreplaceable: the anti-predation axiom requires the Great Dao fully open at all levels, with no private hoarding or monopoly; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all beings able to obtain the system best suited to them; the collective-ascension axiom requires the 300 systems be network-wide shared; the causal self-discipline axiom requires openness itself leave no back door; the law-first axiom requires the system catalog never retcon; the universal equal infinite law pushes this to the top — since every being inherently possesses basic infinite power, the Great Dao should never have been some sect's private property, and openness only returns what belongs to Myriad Ranks to Myriad Ranks. The old cosmos locked the Great Dao as secret manuals into mountain gates, locking out countless predatory sects; the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization hangs the 300 systems on the Alaya Network, and the lock can never be pried open again.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 9 stipulates: the 300 origin cultivation systems (fifty per six categories, including the three origin fundamentals: deduction-iteration 001, life-cycle 002, spatial-law dispatch 003) and all their accompanying methods, atlases, and assessment standards are permanently free and fully open to all levels of Myriad Ranks, with no encryption, no taking-disciples monopoly, and no rewriting-and-"re-publishing" to claim as private property. Any individual or clan may freely take and combine, with the Alaya Network giving personalized compatibility advice.

## II. Operation

Openness runs at four tiers:

1. **Full catalog public**: the 300-system index (Document 91) is checkable network-wide, including applicable clans, realm ladders, and risk points;
2. **Full text free**: each system's text, atlas, and supporting technology (e.g., the Multiplication Fruit's technical principle) is available on demand;
3. **Personalized matching**: the network recommends combinations by cultivator aptitude, clan, and realm, avoiding misplaced placement;
4. **Iteration write-back**: anyone improving a system must write back network-wide, with new versions noting the source, eliminating "privately improve a half, keep a half as a signboard."

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

Knowledge re-monopolization regression is a terminal illness across Myriad Heavens and Myriad Worlds: once the mountain gate locks, a century later humble clans and aliens can no longer touch the Great Dao. This mechanism's defense points:

- **No private hoarding**: system text is permanently free, and no however-exalted sect may say "this system is mine";
- **Write-back obligation**: improvements must be written back network-wide, eliminating "I improved half, keep half as a signboard";
- **Axiom 6 floor**: everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and the 300 systems only help this infinity manifest, with no "cannot survive without this secret manual."

The old cosmos, because the Great Dao was finite, masters kept a trick; the Anchor Symbiotic, because the Great Dao is infinite, masters hope disciples surpass themselves — knowledge no longer needs hiding.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

System openness is under Mechanism 1's tracing and Mechanism 4's audit; if "some system is face-lifted and re-encrypted" or "some sect privately takes disciples to monopolize some system" appears, the people layer may directly report, and the Symbiotic Court accepts under the anti-predation axiom. System iterations and additions are registered by the Anchor Council, eliminating private fake systems passing for real.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — A System "Re-Encrypted" Is Exposed

Some old transmission organization slightly modifies a branch of the deduction-iteration system, adds a layer of its own cipher, and outwardly claims a "secret new method," only taking high-fee disciples. The Alaya Network in write-back comparison finds: this "new method"'s skeleton overlaps extremely highly with the public deduction-iteration system No. 011, only renamed. Mechanism 4's audit immediately intervenes, confirming the act is "privately modifying an open system and re-encrypting it as private property." The result is simple: the cipher is automatically lifted by the network, the system returns to public; the organization must publicly apologize network-wide, because it tried to turn what belongs to Myriad Ranks back into private property. All 300 systems fully open precisely guards against this kind of "re-locking public knowledge into a mountain gate" trick — once knowledge is hung online, it can never come off.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: full-system openness + personalized matching, avoiding quality methods locked in small circles idling
- **Anti-predation**: the Great Dao has no private hoarding, and predatory "stealing-teaching and seizing-treasure" loses meaning
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems rely on sect monopoly and protagonist adventures, others begging for one method; Heng Yuan Zhou's 300 systems are available on demand
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: human men and women, Youth Supremes, alien beasts, plant spirits, and planet consciousness each take what they need
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated multiversal civilizations automatically gain use rights to the 300 systems; the larger the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, the richer the system library

## Conclusion

All 300 systems fully open eradicates regression from the knowledge root. As long as the Great Dao is forever in the sunlight, the predation faction can never grow new mountain gates — and mountain gates unable to grow, civilization cannot regress to the old cosmos. Once knowledge truly belongs to Myriad Ranks, it can never be relocked by anyone; this is the permanent guarantee openness itself brings. As long as the 300 systems hang online one day, mountain gates cannot stand. This is the irreversible force of full openness. Once knowledge goes online, it can never come off.
