# Entry 977 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 10 | Dual-Track Time-Domain Use Right

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 920, 80, 82

## Introduction: time is the last resource that may not be privately divided

Power, knowledge, and energy are all governed; one thing remains most easily monopolized by the strong — time. The Origin Acceleration Domain lets civilization iterate at extreme speed, and the Quest Sediment Domain lets individuals slowly experience epics; if the use rights of these two time domains are monopolized by a few, the strong can use "extra time" to leave Myriad Ranks countless realms behind. Dual-track time-domain use right is the anti-regression system's "time allocation" lock. In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, time domains are not the strong's private property, but Myriad Ranks' shared cultivation rhythm. Its logic under the six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom requires time resources not be preyed upon; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires time-domain use rights open to Myriad Ranks; the collective-ascension axiom requires acceleration-domain results feed back to the whole network; the causal self-discipline axiom requires time operations be strictly reported; the law-first axiom requires dual-track rules never retcon; the universal equal infinite law reminds — time domains are not "extra lives," but help beings use their inherent basic infinite power more gracefully; strivers use the acceleration domain to superimpose higher infinity, and the easygoing take it slow in the sediment domain, with neither delayed.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 10 stipulates: use rights to the Origin Acceleration Domain and Quest Sediment Domain are allocated to Myriad Ranks by public rules. The acceleration domain is for civilization's extreme iteration and key individual breakthroughs; the sediment domain is for individuals' slow experience and epic sediment. Use rights are disbursed via an apply-approve-trace process, with no individual allowed to long monopolize the acceleration domain or turn the sediment domain into his private backyard.

## II. Operation

The dual track splits by two uses:

1. **Origin Acceleration Domain**: for the 20% activation objects, key civilizational projects, and emergency boundary-breaking; time flow doubles but the process is fully traced;
2. **Quest Sediment Domain**: for individuals' slow experience, Quest Domain simulation-retrospective, and epic narrative; time flow is unhurried, no rushing.

The two domains' use rights rotate periodically, and the sediment domain is open to the easygoing — those wanting to slow down have the right to slow, those wanting to rush have the right to rush.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

Time-monopoly regression is advanced civilizations' most insidious regression: the strong cultivate with a hundredfold time to a level Myriad Ranks cannot match, then turn around and announce "this is talent." This mechanism's defense points:

- **Rotation, not monopoly**: acceleration-domain use right rotates periodically, eliminating "some strong one soaking in the acceleration domain year after year";
- **Fast and slow both have rights**: those wanting fast have the acceleration domain, those wanting slow have the sediment domain, and the easygoing are not rushed into elimination by the times;
- **Full trace**: how much time domain who used and for what, the Alaya Network records stroke by stroke.

Old-cosmos strong ones relied on "seclusion for a century" to leave others behind, with time becoming class's accomplice; the Anchor Symbiotic rotates and publicizes time domains, and time can no longer become private property.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

Time-domain allocation is audited by Mechanism 4 and traced by Mechanism 1; abusing time domains or falsely reporting acceleration purposes, the people layer may directly report; time-domain rules are legislated by the Anchor Council, and Mechanism 3's Symbiotic Court accepts time-domain disputes.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — A Strong One "Year-After-Year Seclusion" Is Rotated Out

Some Boundary-Breaking Realm strong one, for eight consecutive epochs, uses his Origin Acceleration Domain quota to the full, and the outside world almost never sees him. Mechanism 4 in annual audit finds: his acceleration-domain usage duration far exceeds normal, and has never rotated. Mechanism triggers: his acceleration-domain quota is temporarily downgraded, with part forcibly yielded to the next batch of 20% activation objects; simultaneously notifying him the acceleration domain is not private property, and he may not soak in it year after year alone. He is invited to the Quest Sediment Domain to mentor Youth Supremes for two epochs — both returning time and making contribution. The old cosmos treats "seclusion for a century" as the strong one's privilege; the Anchor Symbiotic rotates and publicizes time domains, and whoever wants to use it long must first prove he is not treating time as private property.

## VI: Two Kinds of Life, Fast and Slow, Both Respected in the Time Domain

The dual-track time domain's deepest kindness is that it acknowledges people have two ways to live. Some want fast — Youth Supremes must seize the best years to break through realms; some want slow — the easygoing must in the sediment domain experience cultivation at their own pace. The old cosmos has only one view of time: the faster the stronger, and slow means backward. So everyone is driven by an invisible whip to run, and those running slow are eliminated by the times. The Anchor Symbiotic makes both fast and slow lawful: if you want fast, the acceleration domain speeds you up; if you want slow, the sediment domain waits for you. Fast and slow are not high and low, only choice. This respect for "the right to slow down" is precisely the time dimension of Axiom 6's "the easygoing retain a floor and are not eliminated by the times."

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the acceleration domain is only placed on projects and individuals most in need of speed right now, eliminating time-resource idling
- **Anti-predation**: time domains not monopolized, the strong cannot form an insurmountable generational gap via "extra time"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo protagonists gain countless "seclusion times" via adventures, others only wait; Heng Yuan Zhou time domains rotate and anyone may apply
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: fast and slow time rights cover human men and women, Youth Supremes, alien beasts, and planet consciousness
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated multiversal civilizations automatically gain dual-track time-domain application rights; the larger the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, the larger the domains

## Conclusion

Dual-track time-domain use right returns time from the strong's private property to Myriad Ranks' public resource. Once time is public, "talent gaps" can no longer be surreptitiously swapped into "time gaps."
