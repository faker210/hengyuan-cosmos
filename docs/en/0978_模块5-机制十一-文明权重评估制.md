# Entry 978 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 11 | Civilizational-Weight Assessment System

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 29, 50, 26

## Introduction: who decides needs an algorithm not based on fists

Council seats, resource allocation, and activation slots — none may be divided by voice volume, but need an objective "weight." The civilizational-weight assessment system is the institution computing this ruler for each Myriad-Rank clan and construction unit. Its positioning under the six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom requires weight reward only co-construction, not predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires weight earnable by all, regardless of clan birth; the collective-ascension axiom requires weight guide whole-system ascension; the causal self-discipline axiom requires weight scores be retroactively reviewable and appealable; the law-first axiom requires the algorithm never retcon; the universal equal infinite law is weight's floor — no matter how high or low a clan's weight, the basic infinite power of every being within it does not move a bit; weight only decides "how much say this clan has in public affairs," never decides "whether this clan's people may live."

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 11 stipulates: the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, per the symbiosis-degree assessment system (Document 29), periodically assesses civilizational weight for each clan, construction unit, and public institution. Weight is used for council seat allocation, public-resource ratio, and activation-slot tilting. Weight is tied only to "actual contribution to Myriad-Rank symbiosis," decoupled from realm height, population size, and combat power.

## II. Operation

Weight is synthesized from four items:

1. **Co-construction contribution**: output energy, knowledge, and law-iteration results;
2. **Symbiosis degree**: actual record of collaborating, sharing, and feeding back with other clans;
3. **Floor guarding**: whether this clan has guarded every individual's basic infinite power;
4. **Causal self-discipline**: whether cross-plane operations comply.

The four items are weighted, quarterly updated, publicized network-wide, and appealable for recalculation.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

Weight-drift regression is the most hidden kind in civilizational governance: the scoring algorithm is slowly tampered with by the strong, and finally weight all tips to the few fighting clans. This mechanism's defense points:

- **Decoupled from combat power**: high combat power does not equal high weight, eliminating "can fight means decides";
- **Appealable recalculation**: after weight is publicized, any clan may appeal; recalculation uses raw traces, eliminating black boxes;
- **Axiom 6 floor**: however low the weight, individuals' basic infinite power within the clan is unaffected, and weight does not decide life and death.

The old cosmos ranked by fist size, with fighters making the rules; the Anchor Symbiotic computes weight by co-construction contribution, with builders speaking more — and clans unable to build still retain their own infinity and dignity.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — Why a Fighting Clan's Weight Did Not Rise

Some carbon-based strong clan, with formidable combat power, assumes its council seat should rise with the tide. Quarterly weight is announced, but their total score is only mid-tier: co-construction contribution average, symbiosis degree low — over the past epoch, their collaboration record with neighbor clans is sparse, and they refused several sharing requests. Someone in the clan disagrees and files an appeal. Recalculation uses Alaya Network raw traces: collaboration count, sharing records, cross-clan feedback, laid out stroke by stroke. The strong clan finally understands that in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, a big fist does not equal high weight. They then adjust strategy, voluntarily opening their clan's technology and increasing collaboration, and next quarter weight steadily rises — this ruler measures not how strong you are, but how much you have done for Myriad Ranks.

## V. Supervising Subjects

The weight algorithm is fixed by Anchor Council legislation and constitutionally reviewed by Mechanism 3's Symbiotic Court; scoring data comes from Mechanism 1's traces and Mechanism 4's audit; the people layer and each clan have direct appeal over scores. The weight algorithm itself is periodically publicized transparently, eliminating "black-box weight."

## VI: Letting Contribution Speak Lasts Longer Than Letting Fists Speak

The weight system's deep meaning is changing the "who decides" standard from power to contribution. The old cosmos ranked by fists, and the result is those best at fighting scrambling to speak, with civilization's direction led by force. The Anchor Symbiotic computing weight by co-construction contribution is publicly acknowledging: those truly qualified to decide civilization's direction are those who lay bricks and tiles for Myriad Ranks, not those who only swing fists. This ruler may at first make old clans uncomfortable — they are used to trading strength for say; but after several generations, Myriad Ranks will find that a civilization governed by contribution walks more steadily and farther than one governed by fists. Because contribution points to building, while fists point only to contention.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: weight directs resources to true co-builders, avoiding high-combat low-contribution ones long occupying seats
- **Anti-predation**: combat power does not trade for weight, and predatory expansion gains nothing in governance
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems whoever has the bigger fist decides; Heng Yuan Zhou whoever contributes more has higher weight, appealable and recalculable
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the five lineages, planet consciousness, and cosmic-origin bodies all participate in weight assessment, with no exceptions
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated multiversal civilizations automatically gain a weight starting point; the larger the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, the more diverse the weight system

## Conclusion

The civilizational-weight assessment system is the algorithm converting "who decides" from force to contribution. Algorithm public, appealable, decoupled from combat power — once this ruler is accurate, the council will not slowly become a strong-ones' club. The ruler measures contribution, not fists, and civilization's direction is forever led by builders.
