# Entry 979 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 12 | Transcendent One's Perpetual-Motion Construction Duty

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 919, 904, 03

## Introduction: true transcendence is not hiding away for retirement

The most common regression across Myriad Heavens and Myriad Worlds is that once the strong transcend, they "AFK": finding a cosmic corner to seclude, leaving Myriad Ranks to struggle on their own. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization rejects this kind of transcendence by definition. The transcendent one's perpetual-motion construction duty is the anti-regression system's lock that "prevents the strong from running away." Its logic under the six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom requires transcendence not by fleeing; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the strong have a beginning and an end with Myriad Ranks; the collective-ascension axiom explicitly says "the strong do not ascend alone, but lift Myriad Ranks along"; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the transcendent one's every step still be traceable; the law-first axiom requires the transcendence definition never retcon; the universal equal infinite law draws the transcendent one's responsibility — you have superimposed infinitely more layers of infinity than Myriad Ranks, so you have a duty to let this extra infinity continue flowing back to Myriad Ranks, rather than tucking away your infinity and roaming free. The old cosmos treats transcendence as retirement; the Anchor Symbiotic treats transcendence as a perpetual-motion post.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 12 stipulates: all beings reaching the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm and becoming transcendent ones automatically bear the perpetual-motion construction duty — no AFK seclusion, no running off to hermitage, no extracting their infinity from the Alaya Network. Transcendent ones must continuously bear construction duties: as mentors, as engineering heads, as law guardians, or as Youth-Supreme nurturers; specific posts are negotiated by the Council and the individual, not forced into a single trade.

## II. Operation

Perpetual-motion construction runs by post rotation:

1. **Mentor post**: in the Quest Sediment Domain mentoring Youth Supremes and new clans;
2. **Engineering post**: leading hyper-multiverse-level methods, materials, and rules' infinite iteration;
3. **Law post**: maintaining the law network, calibrating setting self-consistency;
4. **Guardian post**: guarding borders, defending against predation faction and Dark Substitution Body infiltration.

Posts rotate every epoch; transcendent ones may not long monopolize some post, nor refuse a post.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

Strong-one-AFK regression is civilization's highest-grade illness: the strongest batch runs off, and the remaining Myriad Ranks slowly lose direction. This mechanism's defense points:

- **Transcendence means taking a post**: transcendence is not an endpoint, but a higher-intensity construction starting point;
- **Post rotation**: eliminating some transcendent one turning some post into private domain;
- **Echoing Axiom 6**: the higher the transcendent one superimposes, the more he feeds back; the easygoing mortals' basic infinity partly comes from this feedback.

Old-cosmos transcendent ones "leap beyond the Three Realms, outside the Five Elements," and leave once and for all; Anchor Symbiotic transcendent ones "leap beyond the Three Realms, and come back to mend roads."

## IV. Supervising Subjects

Transcendent ones' duty performance is under Mechanism 7's four-dimensional assessment, Mechanism 1's tracing, and Mechanism 4's audit; those refusing posts, AFKing, or running off have their extra infinity-use tier-reclaimed per the collective-ascension axiom, retaining only basic infinity equal to all beings. Post allocation is legislated by the Council, and disputes adjudicated by the Symbiotic Court.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — A Transcendent One Wants "a Small Universe to Rest"

A newly Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm transcendent one petitions the Council: he wants to apply for an undeveloped small universe for secluded quiet rest, no longer holding a public post. The Council replies per the perpetual-motion construction duty: you may change posts and may rest, but may not AFK-seclude nor extract from the network. He is finally assigned to the Quest Sediment Domain mentor post, for one epoch — no fighting and killing, just mentoring Youth Supremes. He is at first a little disappointed, but one epoch later he finds: the three Youth Supremes he mentored later all became construction backbones, and he himself in mentoring has a deeper layer of understanding of law. The old cosmos treats transcendence as retirement, the more transcendent the lonelier; the Anchor Symbiotic treats transcendence as a perpetual-motion post, the more transcendent the more to do, and the more needed by Myriad Ranks.

## VI: Deleting the Concept of "Retirement" from Civilization

The transcendent one's perpetual-motion construction duty is essentially redefining the endpoint. The old cosmos sets cultivation's endpoint as "can stop working" — so everyone's ultimate dream is finding a place to flat-lay. This setting looks beautiful, but actually bets civilization's future on the impossible premise that "the strong all want to lie down." The Anchor Symbiotic changes this endpoint to "always something to do, the more done the more interesting." Transcendent ones are no longer retired elders, but civilization's most senior builders. Deleting the concept of "retirement," civilization forever has the strongest batch on the job. This is not hard labor, but gives the transcendent one a more fulfilling position than roaming alone — being needed by Myriad Ranks is itself a higher infinity.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: directing transcendent ones' massive energy toward perpetual-motion construction, eliminating great-material-for-small-use AFK retirement
- **Anti-predation**: transcendent ones may not extract from the network to roam alone, their power always serving Myriad Ranks
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type heavenly emperors after cultivation flat-lay, with Myriad Ranks as backdrop; Heng Yuan Zhou transcendent ones perpetually construct, the more transcendent the more to work
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: transcendent ones' mentor and guardian posts directly face all Myriad-Rank beings
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: transcendent ones' engineering posts iterate infinitely with the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's expansion, with no retirement line

## Conclusion

The transcendent one's perpetual-motion construction duty rewrites "transcendence" from flight into responsibility. As long as the strongest forever construct, civilization never loses its leader — and regression loses its most fatal exit of "the strong run off."
