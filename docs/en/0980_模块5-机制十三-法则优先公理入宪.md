# Entry 980 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 13 | Law-First Axiom Enshrined in the Constitution

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 906, 97, 09

## Introduction: the world's greatest fear is not enemies, but the author himself changing settings

Civilizational regression has a most special, most fatal form: the laws themselves being quietly changed. Today it is set that the strong may not revive, tomorrow the protagonist dies and the setting is changed to revive him; today it is stipulated the Great Dao is finite, tomorrow for the plot the protagonist is infinite. This kind of "retcon and unfinished ending" looks like merely a plot hole, but at the civilizational layer it means laws are no longer credible and Myriad Ranks lose faith in institutions. The law-first axiom enshrined in the constitution is the anti-regression system's lock that "protects law itself." It ranks fifth among the six top-level axioms, enshrined with the other five: the anti-predation axiom, the republican axiom of Myriad Races, the collective-ascension axiom, the causal self-discipline axiom, and the universal equal infinite law, all built on "law stable and credible." The law-first axiom requires: law comes first, never retconning, never ending unfinished; setting self-consistency above plot convenience, plot may not kidnap settings; the worldview may expand infinitely without collapse.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 13 stipulates: the six top-level axioms, along with all enacted laws, mechanisms, and standards, once enshrined in the constitution have supreme effect; any amendment must go through Mechanism 2's legislative procedure, and may not violate the six axioms themselves. Plot, narrative, personal cultivation insights, and temporary dispositions may in no case override law; finding law conflicts with practice, one may only amend the law itself, not bypass with "this time is special."

## II. Operation

Law-first runs by three gates:

1. **Project gate**: when any new mechanism, method, or civilizational project is initiated, first do a setting-self-consistency assessment; those conflicting with enacted laws are not initiated;
2. **Operation gate**: finding conflict during operation, immediately freeze relevant acts, go back and amend law, forbidding "do it first, talk later";
3. **Audit gate**: Mechanism 4 periodically does setting-contradiction self-check (Document 97), cleaning retcon/unfinished-ending risk points.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

Law-changed regression is the root across Myriad Heavens and Myriad Worlds: once the author changes settings, the rules Myriad Ranks guarded before all become jokes. This mechanism's defense points:

- **Plot may not kidnap settings**: however moving the plot or urgent the breakthrough, axioms and enacted laws may not be changed;
- **Conflict only amends law**: finding law conflicts with reality, what is amended is law, not quietly bypassed;
- **Infinite expansion without collapse**: the larger the worldview the more self-consistency required, and every Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm expansion passes the project gate.

Old-cosmos strong ones often "break settings" to look awesome; the Anchor Symbiotic strong ones are precisely the opposite — truly capable ones get things done within law.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

Law self-consistency is specifically adjudicated by Mechanism 3's Symbiotic Court axiom chamber and periodically audited by Mechanism 4; the people layer may directly report retcon/unfinished-ending on discovery; law amendments pass Anchor Council three readings, and the six axioms themselves are forever unamendable. The law network's operating state is transparent to Myriad Ranks, with all legislation and amendments fully traced on the Alaya Network, traceable, comparable, and recalculable.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — Why "This Time Is Special" Is Rejected

Some emergency boundary-breaking, with some strong one proposing: to rescue a besieged clan, temporarily bypass cross-plane reporting and directly draw origin from the nearest plane. The proposer emphasizes "this time is special, saving people matters." Mechanism 3's axiom chamber replies: saving people may be done, but the process may not be bypassed — the emergency channel also exists, but it too has its own laws: report first, assess first, take minimal disturbance. "Saving people" that bypasses law saves this once, but harms law's forever credibility. The breakthrough finally went through the emergency channel's formal procedure, the besieged clan was saved, and law did not get a hole. However urgent the plot, law does not yield — this is what "plot may not kidnap settings" looks like in reality.

## VI: Never Retcon Is Civilization's Promise to Myriad Ranks

Law-first ultimately guards Myriad Ranks' trust in institutions. When a being stakes a lifetime on cultivation, what he needs most is not talent, but certainty — certainty that the rules he guards will still be here tomorrow, certainty that the road he walks will not be voided by some temporary decision. Old-cosmos strong ones fear nothing but the author changing settings; Anchor Symbiotic Myriad Ranks fear nothing, because law has ahead of them promised "I do not change." Once this promise is written into the constitution, it becomes civilization's heaviest yet most reliable foundation. Myriad Ranks may dream, cultivate, and send children into the symbiotic education system with peace of mind, because the law-earth underfoot, ten thousand years later, is still there.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: law stable and credible, Myriad Ranks need not repeatedly "re-understand the world," cognitive cost approaching zero
- **Anti-predation**: the predation faction wants to roam free by "breaking law"; law-first directly blocks this road
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type protagonists cheat via the author changing settings; Heng Yuan Zhou's all power is generated within stable law, never retconning
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: stable law benefits all beings, with no "exception clauses" based on individual strength
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the law network extends with the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's expansion, self-consistency assessment synchronously covering

## Conclusion

The law-first axiom enshrined in the constitution is the anti-regression system's lock that "protects the sky itself." The sky does not change lightly, and Myriad Ranks can trust the sky; once the sky is stable, civilization may expand infinitely with peace of mind.
