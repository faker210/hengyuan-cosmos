# Entry 981 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 14 | Cross-Plane Operation Reporting System

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 81, 915, 28

## Introduction: before walking out the door, say hello first

Mechanism 13 holds the law; Mechanism 14 begins governing the "cross-plane" domain most prone to loss of control. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization connects to countless planes; once a strong one crosses planes to act privately, he may churn an entire unobserved civilization into chaos. The cross-plane operation reporting system is the anti-regression system's lock that "reports before going out." Its position under the six top-level axioms is directly stipulated by the causal self-discipline axiom: cross-plane operations strictly reported, observe first then limitedly intervene, only returning knowledge and not disturbing causality. The other five axioms run through it equally: the anti-predation axiom requires cross-plane no predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires respecting each plane's beings; the collective-ascension axiom requires cross-plane results feed back to the whole network; the law-first axiom requires the reporting process never retcon; the universal equal infinite law reminds — every plane's beings inherently possess basic infinite power, and rash intervention is touching others' roots.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 14 stipulates: all cross-plane shuttling, cross-plane energy invocation, and cross-plane law deployment must, before operation, report to the Anchor Council and Symbiotic Court, stating purpose, path, expected impact scope, and return method. Crossing planes without reporting has its acts void ab initio, and is held accountable under the causal self-discipline axiom.

## II. Operation

Reporting runs by four steps:

1. **Application**: the operation team submits a cross-plane plan, with observation data attached;
2. **Review**: the Symbiotic Court's causal chamber reviews whether it complies with observe-first-then-limited-intervene and only-return-knowledge;
3. **Approval and release**: upon approval, a temporary cross-plane anchor-connection credential is issued;
4. **Post-operation return**: after operation, return knowledge and causal-impact assessment.

The whole process is traced on the Alaya Network.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

Cross-plane out-of-control regression is the disaster advanced civilizations most easily "accidentally" cause: one footstep in, churning an indigenous civilization into historical regression. This mechanism's defense points:

- **Approve first, act second**: no approval no action, eliminating "slay before asking";
- **Credential binding**: cross-plane anchor connections open by temporary credential, with uncredentialed shuttling automatically blocked by the law network;
- **Post-operation return**: those going out must come back to report, with causal impacts filed.

Old-cosmos cross-plane strong ones come and go freely, trampling countless planes without knowing; Anchor Symbiotic cross-plane operations, every step is under the causal chamber's nose.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

Reporting and execution are traced by Mechanism 1 and audited by Mechanism 4; unreported cross-planers, the Alaya Network automatically alerts, and the Symbiotic Court's causal chamber holds accountable; cross-plane rules are legislated by the Council, and Mechanism 13's law-first guarantees the reporting process not bypassed by plot convenience.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — A "Act-Before-Approval" Is Blocked by the Law Network

Some exploration team hears a new plane has discovered anomalous energy, eager to arrive before other civilizations, and initiates plane-shuttling without submitting a report. The anchor connection just opens, and the law network automatically blocks it — because the cross-plane credential is not bound to a report. The team is asked back to the approval window, supplementing an observation plan, impact assessment, and return method. Only after the formal credential comes through do they truly set off. Someone on the team feels "it's just a few days slower," but the causal chamber explains: these few days buy that plane's beings never face a history where someone came, yet has already been disturbed and can never go back. Old-cosmos explorers pride themselves on "first-come-first-served"; Anchor Symbiotic explorers take "approve first, act second" as their rule — without the permit in hand, not one step may be taken.

## VI: A Permit Protects Two Planes

The reporting system looks like it only bothers explorers, but actually protects two planes simultaneously. For the explored plane, it blocks uninvited disturbance; for the Anchor Symbiotic itself, it guarantees cross-plane actions are always clean, leaving no causality to backfire later. A permit looks like a process on the surface, but behind it is two civilizations' mutual respect. The old cosmos treats the channel between planes as an unowned highway, whoever is fastest takes it first, and hitting someone is their bad luck; the Anchor Symbiotic turns this channel into a bridge with a toll booth — cross the bridge you get a permit first, and the permit looks at impact, and no one may slip through. These few slower steps buy countless planes never facing the history of "someone came, yet it can never go back."

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: reporting avoids repeated exploration and blind shuttling, using cross-plane energy where the blade cuts
- **Anti-predation**: cross-plane may not prey on indigenous civilizations, and predatory crossing is void ab initio
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type protagonists roam planes at will, churning wherever they go; Heng Yuan Zhou cross-plane first reports and observes
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: cross-plane operations respect each plane's beings with inherent basic infinity, not treating them as fertilizer
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the reporting system extends with the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's expansion, with new planes protected upon access

## VII: The Reporting System's End: Making Far Travel a Gentle Restraint

The reporting system looks like it puts layer upon layer of shackles on explorers, but actually turns cross-plane far travel from "conquest" into "visiting." Old-cosmos strong ones cross a plane thinking "this is mine now"; Anchor Symbiotic explorers cross a plane thinking "I must first figure out whose home this is." This shift in mindset, more than any process, approaches civilizational maturity. The universal equal infinite law tells everyone about to go out: the beings beyond the door, like you, inherently possess basic infinite power; their planet is not an uninhabited zone, and their history is not a blank page. The reporting system translates this respect into an executable permit — you may go far, but the distance is not yours; you may explore, but exploration does not equal possession. When cross-plane strong ones have all learned to knock, the cosmos, from a might-makes-right hunting ground, becomes a garden where Myriad Ranks visit each other. The law-first axiom guarantees this permit never retcons, and the Alaya Network guarantees every far travel is clean and checkable. Small as the permit is, it measures the whole civilization's breeding.

## Conclusion

The cross-plane operation reporting system translates "causal self-discipline" into a permit. Permit in hand, causality is not chaotic; permit not in hand, not one step — this institutional permit blocks cross-plane regression's most dangerous exit. With the permit here, cross-plane causality is forever clean, and however far Myriad Ranks walk, they leave no mess behind.
