# Entry 982 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 15 | Observe First Then Limitedly Intervene

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 83, 916, 84

## Introduction: see clearly, then act; when acting, hold back

The reporting system governs "whether to go out"; observe-first-then-limitedly-intervene governs "what to do after entering." This is the causal self-discipline axiom's finest nerve. Its logic under the six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom requires intervention not be predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires respecting the intervened plane's autonomy; the collective-ascension axiom requires intervention's purpose be to help, not replace; the causal self-discipline axiom itself is this mechanism; the law-first axiom requires the observation process never retcon; the universal equal infinite law draws the bottom line — every plane's beings inherently possess basic infinite power, and the road they walk out themselves, however slow, is their infinity. The observer's task is to help, not replace.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 15 stipulates: once a cross-plane operation enters the observed plane, it must first do sufficient observation — understanding its history, law, being structure, and civilizational stage — and may not interfere in any way before observation is complete. After observation, intervention must also be "limited": only when the observed civilization faces an extinction-level crisis and cannot handle it itself, limitedly intervene, with the intervention means following the minimal-disturbance principle.

## II. Operation

Per the Archaeological Protocol Outline (Document 84), in three stages:

1. **Pure observation period**: only using copy-mirror technology for remote archaeology and isolated-space low-pollution observation, no contact with indigenous civilization;
2. **Limited-contact period**: only when the other side actively contacts, exchange as equals, not revealing crushing power;
3. **Limited-intervention period**: only intervene in extinction crises, withdraw immediately after intervention, no long-term trusteeship.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

Intervention-out-of-control regression is "good intentions gone wrong" in the typical: an advanced civilization steps into a backward plane, meaning to help, but churns their history into regression. This mechanism's defense points:

- **No observation no action**: acting without seeing clearly is itself unconstitutional;
- **Small over large**: when intervention is necessary, choose the minimal-disturbance option, eliminating "by the way remodeling their civilization";
- **Intervene then withdraw**: withdraw once the crisis is resolved, no long occupation, no deciding for them.

Old-cosmos cross-plane strong ones under the name of "I'm doing this for your own good" remodel natives, then leave, leaving wreckage; Anchor Symbiotic observers would rather be slow, not touch wildly — because every plane's infinity belongs to that plane itself.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

Observation and intervention's whole process is traced by Mechanism 1 and supervised by the causal chamber; whether the intervention means is "limited" is post-adjudicated by the Symbiotic Court's causal chamber; over-scope interveners are held accountable under the causal self-discipline axiom. Observation data is open to Myriad Ranks' research layer, but strictly confidential to the observed plane.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — After Seeing Clearly, Choosing "To Do Nothing"

An observation group enters an indigenous plane still at the cold-weapons stage. Per old-cosmos habit, observers cannot help leaving a few treasures and a few "prophecies," considering themselves "helping" the natives. This mechanism requires observing first: the group uses copy-mirror remote archaeology for three standard epochs, mapping this plane's historical trajectory — they are in an endogenous, healthy civilizational ascent, with no extinction crisis. The final conclusion is "do nothing." The observation group only files and returns this plane's complete knowledge, withdrawing the body as is. The natives continue their own road, perhaps slow, but that is their infinity. Someone in the group regrets "came for nothing," but the causal chamber says: sometimes the best intervention is restraining from intervening.

## VI: Restraint Is a Higher Ability Than Intervention

Why is "observe first" written so heavily? Because cross-plane civilization's greatest arrogance is thinking that seeing means one should act. An advanced civilization standing before a backward plane, the easiest mistake is not malice, but good-intentioned arrogance — it thinks its experience must be useful to the natives. But every plane's history, law, and being structure is unique, and an outsider's "good advice" may be precisely that plane's greatest disaster. Observe first is essentially acknowledging: I am not yet qualified to decide for you. This humility is harder than any crushing power. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization writes "see first, act, act lightly, leave once done" into an iron law, precisely to solidify this humility into instinct. A civilization willing to keep restraint with unfamiliar civilizations is truly qualified to walk out of its own plane.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: observe first then act, avoiding the enormous causal loss from blind intervention
- **Anti-predation**: intervention is not by-the-way predation, with limited intervention and predatory conquest a clear line apart
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo protagonists push across planes, ruling wherever they go; Heng Yuan Zhou observes first, limitedly intervenes, and leaves once done
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: observed planes' indigenous beings are fully treated as "masters with inherent infinity"
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the observation protocol covers every newly incorporated plane as the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm expands

## VII: The Observer's Highest Realm, Becoming a Good Guest

Observation-first-then-limited-intervene taken to the extreme, the observer becomes the cosmos's most welcome guest. He is quiet entering the door, does not point fingers when sitting down, helps within his means, and tidies the room when taking leave. The universal equal infinite law gives this guest its deepest confidence: he knows every one of the host's possessions, every road, every piece of history is connected to the host's inherent basic infinite power, and breaking any one is touching their root. So he would rather observe a few more standard epochs than rush to show off; he would rather watch the host walk his own detours than rush to choose a "more correct" road for him. This restraint is not coldness, but a deep feeling treating unfamiliar beings as equal subjects. The Alaya Network traces his observation throughout, and the law-first axiom writes "minimal disturbance" into an un-bypassable iron law. A civilization that can train its strong ones into such guests, however many planes it walks, leaves behind only gratitude, not ruins.

## Conclusion

Observe-first-then-limitedly-intervene writes "respect" into every step of cross-plane operation. See clearly, act lightly, withdraw fast — with these three done, cross-plane civilization will never be regressed by the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization itself. See clearly then act, act lightly then last; this is the most decent attitude toward unfamiliar planes, and the mark of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's maturity.
