# Entry 983 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 16 | Knowledge-Return-Only Principle

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 87, 917, 85

## Introduction: what is brought back may only be knowledge, not others' causality

After observing first and limitedly intervening, the last step of cross-plane operation is "returning." What to bring back? The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's answer is extremely strict: only knowledge, no causality. The knowledge-return-only principle is the anti-regression system's lock that "cross-plane leaves no mess." Its logic under the six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom requires going out once not be moving things; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires not taking others' beings and civilizational fruits; the collective-ascension axiom requires the knowledge brought back feed back to the whole network; the causal self-discipline axiom requires not disturbing the observed plane's causality; the law-first axiom requires the principle never retcon; the universal equal infinite law is the bottom line — every plane's beings' inherent basic infinity belongs to themselves, and not one hair may be taken.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 16 stipulates: after cross-plane operation ends, what is returned to the Alaya Network may only be knowledge — observation records, law samples, technical atlases, civilizational experience. No observed plane's beings, energy, treasures, or law origin may be taken; no permanent anchors, transformation traces, or domination structures may be left in the observed plane.

## II. Operation

Return closes in three steps:

1. **Knowledge extraction**: using copy-mirror technology to remotely extract knowledge samples, no contact with the body;
2. **Causal cleanup**: before withdrawal, clean all temporary anchors, temporary credentials, and temporary channels;
3. **Return and archive**: knowledge is archived in the Alaya Network for whole-network learning, with the observed plane unaffected.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

Cross-plane pollution regression is "what is brought back is not knowledge, but trouble": taking natives, taking treasures, leaving back doors, over time churning both planes into chaos. This mechanism's defense points:

- **Only knowledge**: knowledge is copyable and non-depleting, and taking it harms no one;
- **No taking beings**: natives are not spoils, and cross-plane taking disciples as "fertilizer" is not allowed;
- **No traces**: withdrawal is clean, eliminating "long-term back-door" colonization.

Old-cosmos cross-plane strong ones emptied natives, took treasures, left puppets, treating the observed plane as a colony; Anchor Symbiotic observers do only one thing — learn back the knowledge seen, and leave the world seen as is.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

Return content and withdrawal traces are traced by Mechanism 1 and reviewed by the causal chamber; if taking beings or leaving back doors is found, accountability per causal self-discipline and the anti-predation axiom; knowledge after return is shared by the Alaya Network whole-network, with private hoarding forbidden.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — A "By-The-Way Taken" Spoils Is Returned

Some cross-plane exploration team, on a plane newly destroyed, discovers a few natural law crystals — the spoils old-cosmos strong ones most crave. By convention, they wanted to put them in storage treasures to bring back to the Alaya Network for "research." At return check, Mechanism 1's trace is pulled up: although the crystals are unowned, they belong to that destroyed plane's origin remnant. Per the knowledge-return-only principle, objects may not be taken. The team finally uses copy-mirror to fully extract and archive the crystals' structure, frequency, and law atlas, with the crystal bodies sealed in place. What is brought back is knowledge; what cannot be brought back is things — because copying knowledge harms no one, but moving things harms that plane's last dignity. Someone on the team feels "no witnesses, why not take it," but the rule is the rule: even if that plane has no one left, everything it left is not spoils.

## VI: Only Knowledge, Because Knowledge Is the Thing That Truly Belongs to All

Why may it specifically be knowledge that is taken? Because knowledge is the only thing that "when shared out, one's own becomes more." Take a crystal, and the crystal becomes one less; take a piece of knowledge, and the knowledge stays in place while another copy is in hand, with neither side losing. Cross-plane operation's purpose is not to prey on that plane's wealth, but to gather scattered wisdom in the cosmos into the Alaya Network, benefiting Myriad Ranks together. Only knowledge equals only taking the seed inside the fruit that does not harm the tree — the tree stays in place continuing to bear fruit, and the seed is planted in Myriad Ranks' garden, growing into a new tree of knowledge. This restraint of "only taking the seed, not destroying the tree" is the precondition for cross-plane civilization to be accepted by the cosmos.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: knowledge copying is zero-loss, one observation whole-network learning, avoiding repeated expeditions
- **Anti-predation**: no taking beings and origin, and cross-plane predation is banned at the root
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo protagonists cross planes taking people, treasures, and harems; Heng Yuan Zhou only brings knowledge, not causality
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: observed planes' beings are fully respected, their infinity belonging to themselves
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: knowledge returns network-wide, and newly incorporated multiversal civilizations automatically share this experience

## VII: Only Knowledge, Leaving the Cosmos a Library That Never Dries

The knowledge-return-only principle taken to the extreme, the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization becomes a never-drying flowing library in the cosmos. It passes through every plane, not moving a single book, but copies back the wisdom in that book; it takes no seed, but leaves that seed's atlas on the whole network. Billions of years later, those passed-by planes may have long risen and fallen, but the knowledge they contributed still shines in the Alaya Network, nourishing later Myriad Ranks. This is the fundamental difference between knowledge and spoils: once a spoil is moved, the original owner loses it forever; once knowledge is shared, the original owner never loses it, but gains a kindred spirit who understands it. What the universal equal infinite law protects is precisely this bottom line of "not taking any being's things" — even if that plane has no one left, its origin remnant is no one's spoils. The law-first axiom welds "only knowledge" into an iron law, making every far travel's ending clean. However far civilization walks, the cosmos's bookshelf gains books, and not one thing on the ground is missing.

## Conclusion

The knowledge-return-only principle is cross-plane operation's closing gate. Go out to see, limitedly help, only bring knowledge, return as is — with this set of motions complete, cross-plane never becomes a new predation ground. What is brought back is knowledge, what is left is the world as is; this is cross-plane's cleanest ending.
