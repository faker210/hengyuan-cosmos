# Entry 984 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 17 | Myriad-Rank Origin Two-Way Cycle

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 15, 913, 25

## Introduction: origin is not a reservoir, but flowing water

The first sixteen mechanisms, one by one, close regression's doors. This last cycle mechanism is the blood vessel that truly makes civilization "live." Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle, as the name says: origin power flows from the Alaya Network to Myriad Ranks, and Myriad Ranks' results flow back to the network, forming a never-drying two-way reciprocation. Its position under the six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom requires the cycle be incremental development, not stock consumption; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires the cycle be open to all beings; the collective-ascension axiom requires the cycle turn stronger; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the cycle itself be traceable; the law-first axiom requires cycle law never retcon; the universal equal infinite law is the cycle's source — every being inherently possesses basic infinite power, and precisely this "everyone has origin" makes the two-way cycle possible, not one-way transfusion of a few supporting the many.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 17 stipulates: the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's origin power cycles two-way per "network → Myriad Ranks → network." The network per Axiom 6 disburses basic infinite power to every being; beings' energy, knowledge, and law iterations produced in co-construction flow back to the network per the contracted-responsibility ratio; the network then transforms the returned origin into higher-level infinity, superimposing it on strivers and Youth Supremes, while continuing to floor the easygoing.

## II. Operation

The cycle flows by three stations:

1. **Disbursement station**: the network disburses basic infinite power to every being, decoupled from contribution;
2. **Transformation station**: beings cultivate, co-build, and produce in the 300 systems, with origin manifesting into results;
3. **Return station**: results flow back to the network at a fixed ratio, refined via the Symbiotic Feedback Cycle Mechanism (Document 25).

The three stations repeat, and the more it turns the thicker origin becomes.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

Cycle-break regression is civilization's chronic illness of losing vitality: origin only out not in, or only in not out; once the cycle stops, civilization stiffens to death. This mechanism's defense points:

- **Disbursement to all**: basic infinite power first reaches every being's hands, and the cycle does not depend on a few strong ones;
- **Return has ratio**: output must return, eliminating strong ones drawing origin into private vaults;
- **Two-way, not one-way**: not one-way transfusion of "the strong support the weak," but Myriad Ranks mutually being sources.

The old cosmos's origin is a reservoir, with a few opening the floodgates and the many lining up for water; the Anchor Symbiotic origin is flowing water, with Myriad Ranks mutually upstream and downstream, and the water forever flowing.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

Cycle accounts are audited by Mechanism 4 and traced by Mechanism 1; disbursement and return ratios are legislated by the Council; if "return privately swallowed" or "disbursement skimmed" appears, the people layer may directly report, and the Symbiotic Court accepts under anti-predation and Axiom 6.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — How an Easygoing One's Origin "Flows"

A human youth chooses easygoing slow cultivation, monthly receiving basic infinite power from the network, slowly refining it himself, with modest output. He worries he "only receives and produces nothing," whether he has become the cycle's loophole. The Alaya Network computes an account for him: his stable energy fluctuations during refinement are themselves microscopic nourishment for the law network; the cultivation feelings he occasionally shares in public forums are read and used by three Youth Supremes; his existence keeps the dual-track time domain's sediment domain from idling. His "output," though small, truly flows back. And the basic infinity he receives comes from flowing water jointly gathered by beings like him and like strivers. The cycle does not require everyone be a dam; it allows everyone be a drop — as long as the water flows, the river does not run dry.

## VI: Flowing Water Does Not Rot; the Closed Loop's Secret Is Forever Flowing

Why the two-way cycle is civilization's cardiovascular system is because it eliminates "stagnant water." Once origin only spins in a few hands, it reeks, clots, and becomes a predation hotbed. Let it flow — from the network to every being, then from Myriad Ranks back to the network — and origin is forever fresh, forever appreciating. The easygoing flow slowly, strivers flow fast, but all are in the same river. This river relies on no one single supply, but on Myriad Ranks mutually upstream and downstream. The old cosmos's origin is a reservoir, the more used the shallower; the Anchor Symbiotic origin is flowing water, the more flowing the wider. A civilization that lets origin forever flow naturally will never be caught by the old-cosmos nightmare of "resources exhausted."

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: the two-way cycle makes origin appreciate in circulation, eliminating "left unused" energy idling
- **Anti-predation**: origin comes from everyone inherent, and preying on others' origin is utterly unnecessary in the cycle
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' origin concentrates on the protagonist, others relying on his charity; Heng Yuan Zhou's origin is mutually generated by Myriad Ranks, everyone a source
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the five lineages, planet consciousness, and cosmic-origin bodies all access the two-way cycle
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated multiversal civilizations automatically access the cycle; the larger the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, the denser the flowing-water network

## Conclusion

Myriad-Rank origin two-way cycle is the anti-regression system's cardiovascular system. Once the cycle turns, civilization lives; once the cycle stops, however tight the institutions stiffen to death. Everyone inherently possessing infinity, Myriad Ranks mutually generating and returning — this is the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's secret of forever freshness. Once the water flows, civilization never ages, never dries up, and is never caught by resource exhaustion.
