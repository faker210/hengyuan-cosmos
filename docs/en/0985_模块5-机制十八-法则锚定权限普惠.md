# Entry 985 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 18 | Law-Anchor Permission Universalization

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 914, 24, 69

## Introduction: the right to touch law may not only be issued to the strong

Above Origin Realm, cultivators begin directly "anchoring" law — fixing cosmic laws for their own use. This was originally the Myriad Heavens and Myriad Worlds' strong ones' most secret privilege, and also the door most prone to class solidification: those who can anchor are gods, those who cannot are mortals. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, at the root, opens this door to Myriad Ranks. Law-anchor permission universalization is the anti-regression system's lock that "permissions are not locked down." Its logic under the six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom requires law anchoring have no private hoarding; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires everyone qualify to learn anchoring; the collective-ascension axiom requires anchoring results feed back to the whole network; the causal self-discipline axiom requires anchoring operations be traced; the law-first axiom requires anchoring not damage the law network; the universal equal infinite law pushes this to the end — since everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, anchoring law is only docking this inherent infinity with cosmic law, and should by right be universal, not a few strong ones' patent.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 18 stipulates: law-anchor permission, regardless of realm or clan, all who complete corresponding method study and pass the anchoring-safety assessment may, under Alaya Network authorization, anchor designated laws. Anchoring results are not privatized, and anchored law samples are synchronously archived to the network for Myriad Ranks to learn. High-risk laws (e.g., causality, time-space) are tier-authorized, with no "strong ones auto-unlock."

## II. Operation

Permissions open at three tiers:

1. **Basic anchoring**: basic law anchoring learnable from Sense-Condensed to Transformation Realm, universally open;
2. **Advanced anchoring**: domain-law anchoring learnable from Law-Origin to Dao-Integration Realm, open after assessment;
3. **High-dimensional anchoring**: causality and time-space anchoring above Boundary-Breaking Realm, tier authorization + full trace.

All three tiers are open to all Myriad-Rank clans, with no clan threshold.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

Permission-solidification regression is advanced civilizations' most lasting illness: the right to anchor law is held by generations of strong ones, and mortals have no path forward. This mechanism's defense points:

- **Assessment, not birth**: gaining permission relies on study and assessment, not on taking a master or bloodline;
- **Sample archiving**: anchoring results belong to the whole network, eliminating "anchor one set, hide another";
- **Axiom 6 floor**: even if no law is anchored, the being's basic infinite power remains, and anchoring is superimposition, not survival.

The old cosmos treats anchoring law as divine right, with mortals only looking up; the Anchor Symbiotic treats anchoring law as a course, and whoever wants to learn may enroll.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

Anchoring operations are traced by Mechanism 1 and audited by Mechanism 4; high-dimensional anchoring is supervised by the causal chamber; permission rules are legislated by the Council; if "some law's anchoring right is monopolized by some family" appears, the people layer may directly report, and the Symbiotic Court accepts under anti-monopoly.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — A Mortal Also Gets the Anchoring Syllabus

A human youth at Sense-Condensed Realm originally thought "anchoring law" was something only Boundary-Breaking-Realm-above strong ones deserved to think about. He checks the symbiotic education system and finds the basic anchoring syllabus is fully open to him, enrollable as long as he passes a safety assessment. He spends three years completing basic anchoring, successfully anchoring a gentlest perception law — not strong, not flashy, but this is the first time he "touches the sky." He archives the anchored sample to the network for later people's reference. In the old cosmos, this anchoring is a sect-secret divine right; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, it is just an ordinary course also open to mortals. The youth does not feel he has become a god; he only knows: "touching the sky" was never a prodigy's privilege.

## VI: Returning the Right to "Touch the Sky" to Mortals, Class Hardens No More

Why law-anchor permission universalization is key is because it moves class solidification's root. In the old cosmos, strong ones are forever strong not only because their power is great, but because they monopolize the method of "touching the sky" — however hard mortals strive, they cannot touch law's edge, and generation after generation are crushed under the mountain. The Anchor Symbiotic opening anchoring courses to mortals equals taking the key to "touching the sky" off the divine altar, hanging it on a classroom wall accessible to all. A mortal, as long as willing to learn and take exams, may for the first time converse with law. Once "touching the sky" is no longer a few people's privilege, class's ceiling is pierced. What class solidification most fears is never the wealth gap, but the upward channel being locked; this mechanism is precisely reopening the locked door.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: anchoring samples archived network-wide, avoiding repeated groping for the same law
- **Anti-predation**: anchoring permission universalized, and predatory "seizing law" is utterly meaningless
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' law strong ones monopolize; Heng Yuan Zhou's law anchoring is universalized by assessment
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: human men and women, Youth Supremes, alien beasts, plant spirits, and planet consciousness may all learn
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated multiversal civilizations automatically gain anchoring courses; the larger the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, the fuller the law courses

## VII: When Mortals May Also Anchor Law, the Gods Step Down from the Altar

Law-anchor permission universalization's ultimate meaning is liberating the identity of "god" from bloodline. Old-cosmos gods are born, carried in blood, locked in sect secrets; mortals look up to gods like looking up at a mountain they can never climb. After the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization turns anchoring into a course open to all beings, "god" is no longer an identity, but a choice — you are willing to spend a few years completing basic anchoring and pass the safety assessment, and you may converse with law. The universal equal infinite law gives this liberation its hardest base: since every being inherently possesses basic infinite power, anchoring law is only docking this inherent infinity with cosmic law, and should by right belong to everyone, not a few strong ones' private property. Anchoring samples are archived in the Alaya Network, and any mortal's beaten path becomes later people's steps; the law-first axiom guarantees this course is never monopolized by some family. When a mortal for the first time anchors law, what he touches is not the sky, but the infinity he inherently possesses. The moment the god steps down from the altar is the moment Myriad Ranks truly stand up.

## Conclusion

Law-anchor permission universalization moves "the right to touch the sky" from the divine altar to the classroom. The sky is no longer a few people's private property — as long as mortals may also learn anchoring, class hardens no more. The right to touch the sky descends to the classroom, and class's ceiling is forever pierced, hard to close again.
