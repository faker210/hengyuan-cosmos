# Entry 986 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 19 | D-Route Legalization

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 921, 98, 51

## Introduction: a man-made road, as long as walked right, should be walked upright

In cultivation history there is a road long stigmatized — the D route, humanity using itself as the furnace to artificially refine Dao-rules soul rings. In the old system, this road was denounced by orthodox sects as "heretical," and those walking it were stigmatized and hunted, forced to hide in the shadows, finally often driven into true predation factions. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization does the opposite: writes the D route upright into the charter, legalized, standardized, and sunlit. D-route legalization is the anti-regression system's lock that "does not force new roads into heresy." Its logic under the six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom requires legalization root out predation; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires all cultivation roads open to all; the collective-ascension axiom requires the D route's results feed back to the whole network; the causal self-discipline axiom requires D-route refinement fully traced; the law-first axiom requires the D-route definition never retcon; the universal equal infinite law is its confidence — since everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, refining Dao-rules soul rings by oneself is only guiding this infinity toward Dao-rules, natural by right, why sneak around.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 19 stipulates: the D route · human-made Dao-rules soul rings, as one of the formal cultivation routes, is charter-protected alongside the 300 origin cultivation systems. D-route cultivators' refinement process must be fully traced on the Alaya Network, and soul-ring sources must be lawful — no hunting beings to seize souls, no preying on origin. Lawfully refined Dao-rules soul rings have equal status with natural soul rings, no discrimination, no stigma.

## II. Operation

The D route is standardized by three steps:

1. **Learning the method**: D-route methods are free, with accompanying safe-refinement courses;
2. **Traced refinement**: the refinement process is fully traced, material sources traceable, eliminating hunting-to-seize-souls;
3. **Result archiving**: refined human-made Dao-rules soul rings are archived to the network for Myriad Ranks' research and reference.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

New-route stigma regression drives living people into enemies: once orthodoxy stigmatizes, D-route cultivators can only hide, and hiding long enough they truly become predation factions. This mechanism's defense points:

- **Legalization, not stigma**: walking the D route is no longer "heretical," but a formal route;
- **Trace, not dark-refine**: refinement is fully traced, separating true heresy (hunting to seize souls) from the lawful D route;
- **Equality, not discrimination**: human-made Dao-rules soul rings have equal rights with natural ones.

The old cosmos forces man-made routes into heresy, finally breeding a true horde of villains; the Anchor Symbiotic legalizes man-made routes, and instead recruits them into co-construction.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

D-route refinement is traced by Mechanism 1 and the causal chamber reviews material sources; lawful D-route cultivators are protected by Myriad-Rank republicanism, and may directly appeal against stigmatization and hunting; D-route standards are legislated by the Council, connecting with the Lin Hao route (Mechanism 20).

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — A Formerly Hunted D-Route Cultivator Takes the Upright Road

A young cultivator, of humble birth, cannot afford natural soul rings, and was once forced into the gray path of hunting to seize souls. In the old cosmos, he either goes black to the end or is hunted by the orthodox. In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, he enrolls in the D-route safe-refinement course: the network traces throughout, and using lawfully synthesized Dao-rules materials, he personally refines his first human-made Dao-rules soul ring — harming no being, seizing no origin. This soul ring has equal rights with natural ones, and he finally need no longer hide in the shadows. He later becomes a teaching assistant in the D-route safe-refinement course, specifically admonishing later people not to take the hunting old road. Legalizing the D route is not condoning heresy, but recruiting countless who might have been forced by the old system into heresy into the sunlight.

## VI: Sunlighting the Gray Zone Is the Most Cost-Effective Anti-Regression

D-route legalization hides a profound governance wisdom: rather than forcing a road people walk into a dark passage, pave it into a sunny road. The old cosmos's stigmatization of new routes, ostensibly maintaining orthodoxy, actually pushes countless who could have been guided into true darkness. The Anchor Symbiotic does the opposite — as long as this road harms no being and seizes no origin, give it a name, a course, and a trace. Once people walk in sunlight, they need no longer do evil to survive. D-route legalization, on the surface giving a route a name, actually plugs the huge regression source of "forced by the old system into villain." Turning the gray zone into a sunny zone, civilization loses a batch of potential enemies and gains a batch of co-builders.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: bringing the D route onto the upright road avoids its idling in shadows and alienating under coercion
- **Anti-predation**: D-route legalization is precisely to root out the true predation of "hunting beings to seize souls"
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' soul rings are obtained by hunting, naturally predatory; Heng Yuan Zhou's D route lawfully refines and harms no being
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the D route is a human-special route, but is equally open to all willing to walk it, with no discrimination
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the D route's technical results open to Myriad Ranks for research as the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm expands

## Conclusion

D-route legalization is the anti-regression system's step of "turning the gray zone into a sunny zone." Legislating new roads upright, and those walking new roads are not forced into the old cosmos's villains — this step looks gentle, but actually plugs a major regression source.
