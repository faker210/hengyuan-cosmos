# Entry 987 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 20 | Lin Hao-Route Constitutional Status

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 922, 95, 01

## Introduction: once a road is walked through, it must be written into the constitution

The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization was not designed out of thin air; it has a pathfinder — Lin Hao. Lin Hao, with a mortal's bones, in a predation-ridden old cosmos blazed a ringless Balanced-Lord symbiotic road: not by predation, not by devouring, not by conquering all directions, but using himself as the seed and Myriad Ranks as the root, step by step in Heng Yuan Zhou walking to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm. Such a road, if left only in legend, may by later generations be rewritten, reinterpreted, even perverted into "actually Lin Hao also fought his way up." Writing the Lin Hao route into the constitution is the anti-regression system's lock that "finalizes the pathfinder's road." Its logic under the six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom requires the Lin Hao route's anti-predation essence never spoil; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires this road open to mortals never close backward; the collective-ascension axiom requires the Lin Hao route as the sample for lifting Myriad Ranks; the causal self-discipline axiom requires records of the Lin Hao route be traceable; the law-first axiom requires the route definition never retcon; the universal equal infinite law is the Lin Hao route's core — a mortal, precisely by inherent basic infinite power and Myriad-Rank co-construction, walked to the hyper-multiverse realm, which precisely proves: everyone has this road.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 20 stipulates: the Lin Hao route (ringless Balanced Lord · self-as-seed · Myriad-Rank symbiosis), as one of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's founding routes, enjoys constitutional status. Its core definition — no predation, no devouring, self-as-seed, lifting Myriad Ranks forward — may not be rewritten by later generations, nor distorted into "another predatory strong-one narrative." The Lin Hao route's cultivation methods are open, for Myriad Ranks' reference.

## II. Operation

Route finalization runs by three steps:

1. **Definition into the constitution**: the Lin Hao route's core definition is written into the charter, protected alongside the six axioms;
2. **Methods open**: Lin Hao route cultivation insights, breakthrough records, and retrospective cases are archived in the Alaya Network;
3. **Transmission mechanism**: the Lin Hao route's contemporary inheritors are not hereditary and not monopolized, but publicly selected by the symbiotic education system.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

Pathfinder-perversion regression is the most insidious: perverting the founder's spirit into a predatory footnote, and later generations under the name of "tradition" actually practice predation. This mechanism's defense points:

- **Definition unchangeable**: the Lin Hao route's anti-predation essence is in the constitution, and perversion is unconstitutional;
- **Methods open**: the route is not privatized, eliminating "only Lin Hao's inheritors understand";
- **Inheritors not hereditary**: no family transmission, and the route belongs to Myriad Ranks.

The old cosmos transmits founders more and more divine, finally into a monopoly signboard; the Anchor Symbiotic writes the Lin Hao route as public property, and later people walk the road, not worship idols.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

The Lin Hao route's interpretation is specifically adjudicated by Mechanism 3's Symbiotic Court axiom chamber; those perverting the route are held accountable under the anti-predation axiom; route transmission is supervised by the Council, eliminating "Lin Hao descendants"-style new privileged class. The Lin Hao route connects with the D route (Mechanism 19), jointly forming the lawful path library for mortal counter-ascension.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — Someone Wants to Frame Lin Hao as "Another Kind of Strong One"

Some newly incorporated multiversal-civilization researcher, introducing the Lin Hao route, interprets it as "Lin Hao was heaven-blessed, proving the Dao by force, finally crushing Myriad Ranks by personal invincibility." This interpretation just goes public, and the Symbiotic Court's axiom chamber intervenes: this is a perversion of the Lin Hao route. The Lin Hao route's core is not "personal invincibility," but "self-as-seed, no predation, lifting Myriad Ranks forward." The court requires the researcher to publicly correct, and re-archive the Lin Hao route's complete definition. The old cosmos loves deifying founders, then turning deification into predation's footnote; the Anchor Symbiotic does not allow this — Lin Hao's road is a road mortals may walk, not an idol to bow to, still less the predation faction's whitewash signboard.

## VI: Writing Founders as Passersby, Civilization Has Later People

The Lin Hao-route constitutional status's deepest intention is to turn "legend" back into "road." The old cosmos deifies founders beyond reach, and later people only kneel, not daring to say "I can walk it too." Deifying the patriarch is essentially blocking the backward road — turning the road into an idol. The Anchor Symbiotic opens Lin Hao's methods, archives his insights, and institutionalizes selection of his inheritors, precisely to step this idol back into a road. The more Lin Hao steps down from the altar, the more mortals know: this road I can also walk. The founder's greatness lies not in making people look up, but in making people dare follow. Writing the Lin Hao route into the constitution is ultimately written to countless ordinary "Lin Hao seeds" — your road has been recognized.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: making Lin Hao's lifetime-blazed experience public, later people need not re-trial-and-error
- **Anti-predation**: the Lin Hao route's anti-predation essence is in the constitution, and the predation faction cannot whitewash under the founder's name
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo protagonists' roads are unreplicable, becoming unclimbable gods; Lin Hao's road is open and learnable, mortals may walk it
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the Lin Hao route is open to human mortal bones, and also to all beings willing to walk the symbiotic road
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the Lin Hao route, as a symbiotic model, propagates to new civilizations as the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm expands

## Conclusion

The Lin Hao-route constitutional status casts the pathfinder's footprints into a never-worn road. Footprints cast into the constitution, later mortals forever know: this road has been walked, is walkable, walked upright — and civilization forever cannot regress to the old age where mortals are only fertilizer.
