# Entry 988 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 21 | Youth-Supreme Cultivation System

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 923, 37, 14

## Introduction: prodigies must not be forced into prodigy predators

Every era produces a handful of heaven-blessed youths — Youth Supremes. In the old cosmos, this batch was the tool fought over, exploited, and driven to death: snatched as disciples, forced as assassins, finally growing into the fiercest predators. The Youth-Supreme Cultivation System is the anti-regression system's lock that "treats prodigies well." In the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, prodigies are no one's private property, but Myriad Ranks' common ignition seed. Its logic under the six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom requires Youth Supremes not be fought over or preyed upon; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires Youth Supremes be merely one of Myriad Ranks, with no privilege and no exploitation; the collective-ascension axiom requires Youth Supremes' talent feed back to Myriad Ranks; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the cultivation process not manufacture causal distortion; the law-first axiom requires cultivation standards never retcon; the universal equal infinite law is the Youth Supreme's double definition — his talent is high, and he may superimpose more higher-level infinity; but his basic infinite power is exactly the same as the easygoing classmate beside him, and the extra infinity is for building, not for crushing.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 21 stipulates: once a Youth Supreme is identified, he automatically enters the "fast-track + quest" dual-route cultivation system. The fast-track route lets him quickly solidify foundations; the quest route lets him slowly temper heart-nature. The whole cultivation is mentored by Alaya Network matching, and may not be fought over, absorbed, or exploited as a predation tool by any faction. Youth Supremes enjoy no privilege — their basic infinity equals others', and the superimposed part is by contribution.

## II. Operation

Dual routes run in parallel:

1. **Fast track**: rapidly break through realms in the Origin Acceleration Domain, with full basic methods and safety assessments;
2. **Quest track**: in the Quest Sediment Domain experience sufficient hardships and trials, polishing heart-nature, preventing talent backlash.

Both tracks are indispensable: only fast without quest, the prodigy grows into a tyrant; only quest without fast, the prodigy is buried.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

Prodigy-predation regression is civilization's most painful: the best seedling is squeezed by the old system into the fiercest predator. This mechanism's defense points:

- **No fighting over**: Youth Supremes are not "disciple-snatched" by any faction, and cultivation rights belong to the symbiotic education system;
- **Both routes must be walked**: heart-nature track and realm track equally weighted, eliminating "only driving realm without tempering heart-nature";
- **No privilege**: the extra infinity talent superimposes is by contribution, not automatically converted to privilege.

The old cosmos snatches Youth Supremes as weapons, and finally the weapon bites back; the Anchor Symbiotic puts Youth Supremes on dual routes, and finally he grows into a builder lifting Myriad Ranks.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

Youth-Supreme cultivation is the symbiotic education system's responsibility, traced by Mechanism 1 and audited by Mechanism 4; if Youth Supremes are fought over, absorbed, or exploited, accountability under anti-predation and Myriad-Rank republicanism; upon adulthood, Youth Supremes must accept Mechanism 7's four-dimensional assessment, same standard as other strong ones.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — A Youth Three Factions Want to Fight Over Is Protected

A once-in-myriad Youth Supreme just identified, three factions almost simultaneously show up: one wants him as a closed-door disciple, one wants him as a vanguard in war engineering, one wants to cultivate him as a chess piece. Per the cultivation system, these three are all stopped: Youth Supremes are not fought over by any faction, and cultivation rights belong to the symbiotic education system. He is sent into the fast track to quickly solidify realms, while arranged on the quest track to experience sufficient hardships — not to temper his heart-nature to make him stronger, but to prevent his talent from backlashing into arrogance. Upon adulthood, he chooses to return to the sediment domain as an ordinary mentor, rather than fight for the strongest position. The old cosmos snatches Youth Supremes as weapons, and the weapon bites back; the Anchor Symbiotic raises Youth Supremes into people, and he grows into a builder lifting Myriad Ranks.

## VI: Treating Prodigies Well Is Civilization's Investment in Its Own Future

Why the Youth-Supreme Cultivation System matters is because Youth Supremes are civilization's tomorrow. The old cosmos treats prodigies as tools, used and discarded; the Anchor Symbiotic treats prodigies as the future, carefully raising them. This difference manifests decades later: the tool-ized prodigy grows into a predator, hollowing out civilization; the carefully raised prodigy grows into a builder, lifting civilization forward. The extra infinity a Youth Supreme superimposes, if directed to co-construction, is civilization's fastest rocket; if directed to predation, is civilization's fiercest wildfire. What the cultivation system must do, between rocket and wildfire, is calibrate the direction. How a civilization treats its own prodigies is how it treats its own tomorrow — this mechanism's answer is more precious than any talent.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: using heaven-blessed seedlings on the upright road, avoiding prodigies wasted by the old system into predators
- **Anti-predation**: Youth Supremes are not fought over and exploited, and talent does not trade for predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo-type prodigies are fought over and slaughter each other; Heng Yuan Zhou Youth Supremes are dual-route cultivated with no privilege
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: Youth Supremes are regardless of clan, with human youths, alien youths, and alien-beast cubs equal
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated multiversal civilizations' Youth Supremes automatically enter the cultivation system

## Conclusion

The Youth-Supreme Cultivation System directs "prodigy," the sharpest resource, to co-construction. A well-forged blade must aim at one's own — the Youth Supreme is both civilization's tip and civilization's root; hold this mechanism, and the prodigy never becomes civilization's gravedigger.
