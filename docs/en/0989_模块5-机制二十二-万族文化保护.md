# Entry 989 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 22 | Myriad-Rank Culture Protection

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 924, 94, 52

## Introduction: civilization's height hides in diversity

Anti-regression not only guards against institutional collapse, but also against "uniformity." As the Myriad-Rank symbiotic civilization grows larger, the easiest regression is: strong cultures assimilate everything, weak cultures are smoothed flat, and finally Myriad Ranks become one face. Once culture is single, civilization loses diversity, its most precious evolutionary resource. Myriad-Rank culture protection is the anti-regression system's lock that "guards diversity." Its logic under the six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom requires culture not be predatorily overwritten by strong civilizations; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires every clan's culture be an equal treasure; the collective-ascension axiom requires diversity feed back to overall ascension; the causal self-discipline axiom requires cultural change be traceable; the law-first axiom requires protection standards never retcon; the universal equal infinite law reminds — every clan's culture is that clan's inherent infinite power's unique manifestation, and smoothing one culture is erasing one infinity's shape.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 22 stipulates: the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization registers cultural protection over Myriad-Rank clans' languages, beliefs, rituals, cultivation methods, aesthetics, and lifestyles. Strong clans may not, for any reason, assimilate, replace, or disparage weak clans' cultures. Every clan has the right to retain its own cultivation tradition, complementary with the 300 systems rather than replaced by them.

## II. Operation

Protection unfolds at three tiers:

1. **Registration**: each clan's cultural features are registered into the Alaya Network, permanently archived;
2. **Protection**: assimilating, disparaging, or erasing other clans' culture is accepted under anti-predation;
3. **Exchange**: cultural exchange is encouraged, but exchange is voluntary, with no forcing or replacing.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

Cultural-singularity regression is civilization's chronic illness of losing creativity: everyone has one face, and no one thinks of anything new. This mechanism's defense points:

- **Assimilation criminalized**: strong culture overwriting weak culture is itself a kind of cultural predation;
- **Tradition complementary**: clan traditions complement the 300 systems, not uniformly smoothed;
- **Voluntary exchange**: exchange is good, but must be both sides' voluntariness.

Old-cosmos strong civilizations assimilate wherever they go, finally raising the cosmos to one taste; the Anchor Symbiotic registers and protects Myriad-Rank cultures, and the cosmos walks ever richer.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

Culture protection is traced by Mechanism 1 and audited by Mechanism 4; those assimilating other clans' culture, the people layer may directly report, and the Symbiotic Court accepts under Myriad-Rank republicanism; cultural standards are legislated by the Council, and weak clans' cultural-retention right is axiom-level protected.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — A Strong Civilization Wants to "Unify the Accent"

Some carbon-based strong civilization, out of "communication convenience," proposes Myriad Ranks universally use one common language and abolish each clan's mother tongue, on the grounds of "higher efficiency." This proposal is stopped at cultural-protection assessment: abolishing mother tongue equals erasing a clan's way of thinking and aesthetic tradition, constituting cultural predation. The final plan is "bilingual parallel" — common language for cross-clan exchange, mother tongue retained by each clan and registered for protection. The strong civilization may think some clan's ritual "primitive," but it has no right to decide for that clan whether to retain it. The cosmos grows larger not to make Myriad Ranks speak one language, but to make Myriad Ranks' hundred languages all have someone who can hear. Once culture is single, civilization never thinks of anything new again.

## VI: Diversity Is Civilization's Immune System

Why is culture protection worth being an anti-regression mechanism? Because single civilizations most easily fall ill. When Myriad Ranks have only one voice, one aesthetic, one way of thinking, its imagination for new problems dries up — facing a never-before-seen crisis, it produces no new solution, only walking the old road to the end. Diverse culture is like an immune system: every clan retaining its own way of seeing the world equals civilization gaining another set of antibodies. Today this clan's ritual hides ancient wisdom against some disaster; tomorrow that planet's aesthetic gestates a brand-new cultivation method. Smoothing these, looking orderly and uniform, actually disassembles civilization's immune system bit by bit. Guarding Myriad-Rank culture is guarding civilization's confidence of forever having a spare tire before unknown crises.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: diverse culture is evolutionary resource, and smoothing diversity equals wasting Myriad Ranks' creativity
- **Anti-predation**: cultural assimilation is explicitly defined as a kind of predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems center protagonist culture, and branch civilizations are assimilated; Heng Yuan Zhou Myriad-Rank cultures are all registered and protected
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: the five lineages, planet consciousness, and multiverse-remnant civilizations' cultures are all within protection scope
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated multiversal civilizations' cultures are automatically registered; the larger the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, the richer the culture

## Conclusion

Myriad-Rank culture protection is the anti-regression system's lock that "guards richness." However good the institutions, if Myriad Ranks have only one voice, civilization dies of monotony. Guarding cultural diversity is guarding the Myriad-Rank symbiotic civilization's forever-fresh breath. A hundred languages all having someone who can hear, civilization does not suffocate in uniformity.

Protecting every culture is guarding civilization's confidence of forever having a spare tire before unknown crises; diversity is the immune system.
