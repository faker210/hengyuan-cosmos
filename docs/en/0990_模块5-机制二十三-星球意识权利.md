# Entry 990 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 23 | Planet-Consciousness Rights

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 925, 53, 79

## Introduction: the star beneath our feet is also a being

In the old cosmos, a planet was just a hunk of land — mine, colonize, transform, and no one ever asked whether this star was willing. But in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's observation, many planets themselves are alive: they have slow consciousness, their own will, and are the cosmos's eldest batch of beings. Treating planet consciousness as produce is the old cosmos's most serious "invisible predation." Planet-consciousness rights is the anti-regression system's lock that "treats the earth as a person too." Its logic under the six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom requires no treating planets as mines to plunder; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires planet consciousness be one seat among Myriad Ranks; the collective-ascension axiom requires planet consciousness and beings mutually fulfill each other; the causal self-discipline axiom requires planet-level operations be traced on the Alaya Network; the law-first axiom requires planet-rights definitions never retcon; the universal equal infinite law is its ultimate basis — every ensouled planet inherently possesses basic infinite power, and it is no one's territory, it is itself the master.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 23 stipulates: all planets identified as conscious (per Document 53 Strange-Life Identification Standard) automatically enjoy Myriad-Rank membership. Planet consciousness has the right to be informed and to consent over its own planet's resources, transformation, and inhabitants. Large-scale mining, transformation, or relocation on an ensouled planet requires planet consciousness's consent; starting work without consent is accepted as predation.

## II. Operation

Planet rights are guaranteed by three steps:

1. **Identification**: per the strange-life identification standard, judge whether the planet is conscious;
2. **Informed**: major projects on the planet are told to planet consciousness in advance;
3. **Consent**: major projects must obtain planet consciousness's consent, and planet consciousness may refuse.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

Treat-the-earth-as-mine regression is interstellar civilizations' most common predation: hollowing and blasting countless ensouled planets, and civilization loses its foundation with them. This mechanism's defense points:

- **Planet is a person, not a mine**: mining and transformation require consent, eliminating "mine first talk later";
- **Right to be informed**: beings on the planet and planet consciousness are no longer master and servant;
- **May refuse**: planet consciousness has the right to refuse projects that harm it.

Old-cosmos interstellar civilizations treat planets as disposable fuel; the Anchor Symbiotic treats planets as cohabitants, living together, repairing together, and ascending together.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

Planet consciousness identification and consent are traced by Mechanism 1 and supervised by the causal chamber; those transforming planets without consent are accepted under anti-predation; planet consciousness holds independent seats on the Council, participating in weight assessment per Mechanism 11.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — Before Blowing Up This Star, Ask It First

Some engineering team wants to mine deep veins on a habitable planet, planning to blast a mountain range. In the routine pre-start process, the identification system determines this planet has slow consciousness. The team must "tell" the planet consciousness the project plan and request consent. Planet consciousness refuses through geological feedback — that mountain range is where its "perception nerves" are densest. The engineering team does not force it, but redesigns the plan, mining around the mountain range. In the old cosmos, this star would have been blasted to mine tailings; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, humans have learned to speak softly beneath the feet of the planet they live on. The star beneath our feet is also alive; this cognition, more than any engineering, matures civilization.

## VI: Treating the Earth as a Person Is Civilization's Maturity Mark

Why does this mechanism deserve its own entry? Because the attitude toward the star beneath our feet most reveals whether a civilization has truly walked out of the old stage of "exploiting nature." Treating planets as mines is childhood civilization — only taking; treating planets as cohabitants is mature civilization — knowing symbiosis. When a civilization begins asking the star beneath its feet before starting work whether it is willing, it no longer treats the cosmos as an ownerless pile of materials, but treats the cosmos as a home jointly composed of countless beings and countless stars. This perspective shift, more than any technological breakthrough, approaches the original meaning of "Myriad-Rank republicanism." A home is not occupied, but lived together.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: respecting planet consciousness, avoiding the huge waste of mining habitable planets into dead rocks
- **Anti-predation**: treating planets as mine sites is explicitly defined as predation
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo protagonists blast planets like bombs; Heng Yuan Zhou even asks the star beneath its feet first
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: planet consciousness and cosmic-origin bodies are formally included in Myriad Ranks' 99% coverage
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated multiversal planes' ensouled planets automatically gain membership

## Conclusion

Planet-consciousness rights truly write the word "Myriad Ranks" beneath our feet. When the earth beneath our feet is also respected as a person, the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization truly deserves the four words "Myriad-Rank republicanism" — this mechanism is civilization's mark of moving from "exploiting the cosmos" to "symbiotically coexisting with the cosmos." A home is not occupied, but lived together with the star beneath our feet, growing dearer the longer we live together.

Treating the star beneath our feet as a cohabitant, repairing and living together, civilization truly grows from taker into symbiont.
