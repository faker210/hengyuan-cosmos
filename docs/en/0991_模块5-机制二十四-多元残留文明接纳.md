# Entry 991 | Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanism · Mechanism 24 | Multiverse-Remnant Civilization Reception

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 926, 54, 78

## Introduction: the last lock is reserved for the fire about to go out

Twenty-four mechanisms reach the twenty-fourth, and the last lock is reserved for the most special object — multiverse remnant civilizations. They are civilization embers barely surviving the destruction of other universes and planes: some with only a few people left, some with only a memory left, some already degenerated to the edge of Dark Substitution Bodies. The old cosmos treats remnant civilizations in only two ways: take them as slaves, or wipe them out as threats. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's way is reception. Multiverse-remnant-civilization reception is the anti-regression system's lock that "abandons no ignition seed," and also the twenty-four mechanisms' closing. Its logic under the six top-level axioms: the anti-predation axiom requires not treating remnant civilizations as spoils; the republican axiom of Myriad Races requires that even if only the last person remains, he is one of Myriad Ranks; the collective-ascension axiom requires taking over the surviving ignition seed to ascend together; the causal self-discipline axiom requires the reception process observe first then intervene; the law-first axiom requires reception standards never retcon; the universal equal infinite law is its highest basis — even if a civilization has only one old person left, he inherently possesses basic infinite power, and also has the right not to be eliminated by the times, the right to settle among Myriad Ranks.

## I. Mechanism Content

Mechanism 24 stipulates: all multiverse remnant civilizations discovered in cross-plane observation — regardless of form incompleteness, strange clan, or even partial alienation into Dark Substitution Bodies — are evaluated per Document 54 Strange-Life Three-Category Disposal Process, then received as much as possible. Reception includes: settled space, basic-infinity floor, cultural registration, re-accession to the 300 systems. Those already fully fallen into predatory Dark Substitution Bodies are first quarantined and treated; if treatment fails, then limitedly purged, never simply exterminated.

## II. Operation

Reception runs by three steps:

1. **Assessment**: per the strange-life identification standard and three-category disposal process, judge the remnant civilization's state;
2. **Settlement**: provide receivable ones with safe space, basic-infinity floor, and paths to re-cultivate;
3. **Transformation**: for partially alienated ones, first quarantine and treat, awakening their basic infinity, then receive.

## III. Anti-Regression Principle

Ignition-seed extinction regression is cosmos-scale tragedy: countless possible civilizations are crushed by the old cosmos, never to return. This mechanism's defense points:

- **Abandon no one**: even if only a spark remains, first catch it, then see if it can be saved;
- **No simple extermination**: alienated ones are treated first, and only if treatment fails are they limitedly purged;
- **Floor infinity**: reception grants basic infinity, and remnants have a home among Myriad Ranks.

The old cosmos treats remnant civilizations as threats and garbage; the Anchor Symbiotic treats them as separated family. This mechanism is the twenty-four defense lines' emotional endpoint: however tight the institutions, if they will not make an exception for the weakest, they are still cold institutions.

## IV. Supervising Subjects

Reception and treatment are supervised by the causal chamber and traced by Mechanism 1; whether to "limitedly purge" must be approved by the Symbiotic Court's axiom chamber, eliminating pretexting weak treatment to mass-kill; reception standards are legislated by the Council, and Myriad-Rank culture protection (Mechanism 22) synchronously registers remnant civilizations' culture.

## Appendix: A Concrete Scenario — A Civilization of Only Seven People Is Caught

Cross-plane observation discovers, in the wreckage of a destroyed plane, seven descendants of that civilization remain — their clan culture nearly severed, remembering only a fragmentary ritual. Per the three-category disposal process, they have not fallen into predators, but just need a home. The reception process starts: providing safe space, issuing basic-infinity floor, cultural registration into the Alaya Network, and re-accession to the 300 systems. The seven are initially wary and closed, but after several generations, their descendants, carrying their clan's unique ritual feeling, become a special sight in Myriad-Rank culture. The old cosmos, encountering such wreckage, either takes them as slaves or wipes them out as threats; the Anchor Symbiotic catches seven people — this is the twenty-four mechanisms' last warmth.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: catching every surviving civilization spark is catching possible evolutionary resource
- **Anti-predation**: remnant civilizations are not taken as spoils or slaves
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo protagonists encounter alien civilizations and either exterminate or enslave; Heng Yuan Zhou first receives, saves, and floors
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: multiverse remnant civilizations are formally included in 99% coverage, the last piece of the Myriad-Rank republican puzzle
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: once remnant civilizations access, they automatically become new members of the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm

## Conclusion

Multiverse-remnant-civilization reception is the finale of the twenty-four Permanent Anti-Civilizational-Regression Mechanisms. From the Alaya Network's first thread to catching the last ignition seed — these twenty-four mechanisms together answer an ultimate question: by what guarantee does the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization never regress? Because it does not let power be privatized, does not let knowledge be re-monopolized, does not let strong ones idle, does not let causality overstep, does not let culture single, does not let planets be mined, does not let ignition seeds go out. Twenty-four locks lock the same promise: Myriad-Rank symbiosis, everlasting and undying. This promise, from the Alaya Network's first thread all the way to the last caught ignition seed.
