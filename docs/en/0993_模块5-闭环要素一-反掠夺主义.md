# Entry 993 | Hyper-Multiverse's Only Complete Civilizational Closed Loop · Closed-Loop Element 1 — Anti-Predation

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 04, 902, 935

## Introduction: a closed loop's first push must be clean

What a closed loop uses as momentum determines what it ultimately becomes. The old-cosmos closed loop (actually open loop) uses predation as momentum: taking others', growing its own. This momentum looks efficient short-term, but long-term must collapse — because the taken eventually rebel, the taken energy eventually dries, and finally the whole system exhausts itself in life-and-death. The Anchor Symbiotic closed loop's first loop is replacing the momentum at the root: anti-predation. It is the six top-level axioms' first cornerstone, together with the republican axiom of Myriad Races, the collective-ascension axiom, the causal self-discipline axiom, the law-first axiom, and the universal equal infinite law holding up the whole closed loop. Anti-predation requires developing increment, not consuming stock, not taking slaughter and predation as the evolutionary path, with the Great Dao open at all tiers, no private hoarding, no monopoly. This is not moral posturing, but closed-loop physics — a system using predation as momentum can never close the loop.

## I: Why Predation as Momentum Must Fail to Close

A predatory system has three fatal wounds:

1. **Stock finite**: take a piece, lose a piece, until nothing remains to take;
2. **People mutually suspect**: the taken hate, the taker guards, and system infighting is endless;
3. **Unsustainable**: the strong grow stronger, the weak die, participants fewer and fewer, and the loop spins thinner and thinner.

This is precisely the old cosmos's death loop of "resources finite → predatory involution → civilizational decay." It is not a closed loop, but a spiral spinning narrower and narrower.

## II: How the Anchor Symbiotic Replaces Momentum with Increment

The anti-predation closed loop's way:

- **Develop increment**: origin power is infinite (universal equal infinite law), everyone inherently possesses basic infinity, and developing increment does not fight over stock;
- **Great Dao open**: the 300 systems are fully open, knowledge archived in the Alaya Network, with no knowledge "hidden as bargaining chips";
- **No hoarding, no monopoly**: energy, knowledge, law, and time domains are all openly allocated.

After the momentum is replaced with increment, the system no longer needs "someone losing" to turn — everyone is creating increment, and the loop spins larger and larger.

## III: Closed-Loop Verification: Whom Does the Anti-Predation Loop Lock

Anti-predation locks the starting points of the other four loops:

- It makes Myriad-Rank republicanism possible (if not taking people, people are willing to be in the loop);
- It gives collective ascension something to return (if not privately swallowed, it can return);
- It makes causal self-discipline not need firefighting every day (if not taking people, there is far less causal mess);
- It makes the law-first axiom meaningful (if law does not reward predation, law is credible).

## IV: Comparison with Myriad-Heavens IPs

Tang-Luo-type systems, Shrouding-the-Sky-type systems, Dragon-Ball-type systems, the protagonist's momentum is predatory — kill the strong, seize treasures, devour origin. Short-term satisfying, long-term, in these systems Myriad Ranks are only the protagonist's fertilizer, and the loop has not closed at all. The Anchor Symbiotic closed loop's first push is clean, so it deserves to be called a closed loop.

## V: Anti-Predation Is Not Moral Posturing, But Physical Necessity

Some will ask: anti-predation sounds noble, but what if one day resources really tighten? The answer is in Axiom 6: origin is infinite, and there is no "really tighten" day. Every being inherently possesses basic infinite power, and the easygoing's floor and the striver's superimposition both come from this infinite origin. Why the old cosmos had to prey is because it assumed origin finite — a finite cake must be fought over. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization at the root replaces this assumption: origin is infinite, so predation is neither moral, nor necessary, nor cost-effective. Think this through, and anti-predation is no longer a slogan of "I should be kind," but the physical conclusion that "predation is useless." Even if a predator temporarily takes something, the finite thing he took, compared with the increment he could have created by infinite origin, is pitifully small. Predation is economically a losing deal. This is the anti-predation closed loop's true hardness of momentum.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: incremental development uses energy on creation, not on scrambling
- **Anti-predation**: this loop itself is anti-predation institutionalized
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo protagonists upgrade predatorily; Heng Yuan Zhou upgrades incrementally
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: increment is open to all beings, with no predation threshold
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: as the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm expands, incremental development's space is infinite

## VI: The Anti-Predation Closed Loop Gives Ambition a Better Place

Finally a word to the ambitious: anti-predation does not eliminate ambition, but gives ambition a new track. The old cosmos's ambition has only one road — take, suppress, beat others. This road walked to the end, the winner is also lonely, because he steps on countless people. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization gives ambition a new road: you want to become stronger? Go deduce and iterate, go refine new methods, go develop infinite origin in an unprecedented direction. What you beat is no longer others, but yesterday's self; your strength no longer rests on others' loss, but on the increment you add to Myriad Ranks. Ambition is not suppressed, but liberated into a direction that harms no one. This is the anti-predation closed loop's most powerful place — it turns the strong ones' longing into civilization's momentum, not civilization's wound.

## Conclusion

Anti-predation is the hyper-multiverse's only complete civilizational closed loop's first push. Clean momentum, and the loop turns long; unclean momentum, the faster it turns the faster it dies.
