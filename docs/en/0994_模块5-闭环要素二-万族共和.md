# Entry 994 | Hyper-Multiverse's Only Complete Civilizational Closed Loop · Closed-Loop Element 2 — Myriad-Rank Republicanism

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 05, 903, 50

## Introduction: on the loop there may be no outsiders

Anti-predation replaced the momentum; the next question is: who is on the loop? The old cosmos's closed loop (actually open loop) has only a few on it — the protagonist, his followers, his clan; the rest of Myriad Ranks are fertilizer outside. This "few people's closed loop" must collapse, because those outside eventually smash the loop. Myriad-Rank republicanism is the six top-level axioms' second cornerstone alongside anti-predation, expanding the closed loop's participants to the maximum: all beings have equal evolution qualifications, with 99% Myriad-Rank full coverage. Human men and women, Youth Supremes, alien beasts, plant spirits, planet consciousness, cosmic-origin bodies, and multiverse remnant civilizations all have a road.

## I: Why "a Few People's Closed Loop" Must Collapse

A local closed loop has three fatal points:

1. **Too few participants**: the loop spins smaller and smaller, with insufficient energy;
2. **Too many outsiders**: those excluded from the loop are potential destroyers;
3. **Innovation dries up**: a single group thinks of nothing new, and the loop grows older spinning.

The old cosmos's sects, empires, and heavenly courts are all this few-people closed loop — finally overthrown by outsiders, or rotting from within.

## II: How Myriad-Rank Republicanism Expands the Loop to the Maximum

The Anchor Symbiotic closed loop's way:

- **99% full coverage**: regardless of clan, realm, birth, or planet, all are on the loop, and all loop-entry records are archived in the Alaya Network;
- **Everyone has a road**: the Entry 975 guarantee system builds the road to every door;
- **Equal evolution qualification**: no one is excluded for being alien, young, or planet consciousness.

No outsiders on the loop, and the loop may bear infinite expansion.

## III: Axiom 6 Is Myriad-Rank Republicanism's Confidence

The old cosmos did not dare truly preach Myriad-Rank republicanism, because resources finite — true equality means not enough to share. The Anchor Symbiotic dares, because of the universal equal infinite law: every being inherently possesses basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a floor and are not eliminated, and strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher infinity. Since origin is infinite, Myriad-Rank republicanism is no longer "dividing the cake," but "baking the cake together" — equality thus has a physical basis.

## IV: Closed-Loop Verification: Whom Does Myriad-Rank Republicanism Lock

- It gives anti-predation an object (not taking whom? not taking every Myriad-Rank on the loop);
- It gives collective ascension a subject (lifting whom? lifting every one on the loop);
- It gives causal self-discipline a boundary (interfering in whose causality? respecting every one's autonomy on the loop);
- It gives the law-first axiom universal meaning (law equally applies to every one on the loop).

## V: Myriad-Rank Republicanism's End: A Circle with No Missing Corner

When the closed loop is drawn to the Myriad-Rank-republicanism element, what is most worth emphasizing is "no missing corner." Any old-cosmos "Great Unity" ideal finally secretly keeps a missing corner: this clan doesn't count, that life doesn't count, people at this stage don't count. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's Myriad-Rank republicanism fills the missing corners one by one: human men and women fill in the Youth-Supreme corner, Youth Supremes fill in the alien-beast and plant-spirit corner, then the planet-consciousness and cosmic-origin-body corner, finally even the multiverse-remnant-civilization corner. 99% full coverage does not mean "more or less counts," but "as long as it is a being, don't think it can be excluded." Why must it be filled to 99%? Because a missing corner is the loop's crack — where the crack is, the loop cannot close. Even if only one corner's beings are not included, that corner eventually becomes the hammer that smashes the loop. Only inviting the last corner's people into the loop does the loop truly close and truly be unsmashable.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: all Myriad-Rank wisdom enters the closed loop, not wasted by birth
- **Anti-predation**: no outsiders on the loop, and predation has structurally no object
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' Myriad Ranks are background; Heng Yuan Zhou's Myriad Ranks are the loop's subject
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: this loop itself is Myriad-Rank full coverage institutionalized
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: newly incorporated multiversal civilizations automatically enter the loop

## VI: Myriad-Rank Republicanism Is Not Equalitarianism, But Equal Qualification

A common misunderstanding must also be clarified: Myriad-Rank republicanism is not "everyone equally strong." The easygoing and strivers are of course different in power; Youth Supremes and ordinary mortals are of course different in realm. What Myriad-Rank republicanism guarantees is not outcome equality, but qualification equality — everyone has the same chance to enter the loop, with no one blocked at the door by birth; everyone's inherent basic infinite power is equally protected, with no one disqualified for "not being strong enough." On this equal-qualification floor, you may keep the floor easygoing, or you may strive to superimpose higher infinity. The loop does not require everyone to walk at the same speed; it only requires no one be shut out. This is the true meaning of 99% full coverage.

## VII: No Outsiders, No Enemies

Myriad-Rank republicanism has another often-overlooked benefit: it structurally eliminates the concept of "enemy." The old cosmos's civilizations always need an enemy outside themselves — either the next-door clan, or the otherworld fiend — to cohere internally. Myriad-Rank republicanism expands the loop to 99%, which is to tell Myriad Ranks: no one is outside the loop, so no one is born an enemy. The tiny remaining outside-the-loop existence is not destroyed as an enemy, but received as multiverse remnant civilization (Mechanism 24). When a civilization no longer needs an enemy to cohere itself, it escapes the old mode of "maintaining internal unity by external predation." Myriad-Rank symbiosis, essentially, draws the boundary of "us" and "them" to the cosmos's edge.

## Conclusion

Myriad-Rank republicanism is the closed loop's participant boundary. No outsiders on the loop, and the loop is unsmashable — this is the closed loop's everlasting demographic foundation.
