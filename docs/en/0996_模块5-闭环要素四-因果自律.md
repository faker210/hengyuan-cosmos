# Entry 996 | Hyper-Multiverse's Only Complete Civilizational Closed Loop · Closed-Loop Element 4 — Causal Self-Discipline

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 905, 83, 89

## Introduction: the loop must not grow dirtier the more it turns

The first three loops let the closed loop turn, with participants complete and results returning. But the closed loop has another hidden risk: while turning, will it itself grow dirty? Cross-plane shuttling, time regression, causal interference — every operation may leave trouble. If trouble accumulates more and more, however beautiful the loop is, it is a dirty loop. The causal self-discipline axiom is the six top-level axioms' fourth cornerstone; it is the closed loop's "self-cleaning system": cross-plane operations strictly reported, observe first then limitedly intervene, only return knowledge and not disturb causality; civilizational regression, historical reverse, cyclical harvesting, and class solidification are thoroughly eradicated.

## I: Why a Closed Loop Needs Self-Cleaning

Causally polluted closed loops are typical of growing dirtier the more they turn:

1. **Cross-plane wandering**: trampling indigenous civilizations, leaving historical reverse;
2. **Time regression recklessly**: changing the past to save the future, creating time paradoxes;
3. **Causality unclear**: operations untraced, and no one knows who caused the trouble.

A dirty loop turning long clogs itself to death.

## II: How Causal Self-Discipline Self-Cleans

The Anchor Symbiotic closed loop's way:

- **Reporting system**: Entry 981, cross-plane approve first then act;
- **Observe first then limitedly intervene**: Entry 982, act only after seeing clearly, and act lightly;
- **Knowledge-return-only**: Entry 983, only bring knowledge, not causality;
- **Full trace**: all operations traceable on the Alaya Network.

Trouble as soon as it surfaces can be located and cleaned, and the loop always stays clean.

## III: Axiom 6 Gives Causal Self-Discipline a Gentle Undertone

The old cosmos talks causality often as "karmic retribution"-style fear — afraid of retribution so not daring to act wildly. The Anchor Symbiotic talks causal self-discipline with a different undertone: because the universal equal infinite law stipulates every plane's beings inherently possess basic infinite power, and rash intervention is touching others' roots; self-discipline is not fear of retribution, but respect. This undertone makes causal self-discipline not cold, but warm.

## IV: Closed-Loop Verification: Whom Does Causal Self-Discipline Lock

- It gives anti-predation a cross-plane extension (not only not taking our own, but also not taking outsiders);
- It gives Myriad-Rank republicanism a cross-plane boundary (respecting every plane's Myriad Ranks);
- It gives collective ascension no side effects (ascension does not trample others);
- It gives the law-first axiom a causal basis (time-paradox prevention itself is law self-consistency).

## V: Causal Self-Discipline's End: A Civilization That Manufactures No New Wounds

Causal self-discipline taken to the extreme is a brand-new civilizational character: it no longer prides itself on "changing history," but on "not causing trouble" as virtue. Old-cosmos strong ones take rewriting the past and shuttling time as achievements, not knowing every rewrite plants new trouble in the future. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization reverses this drive — its strong ones' greatest feat is making the planes they passed "seem as if nothing happened": observe then leave, help then withdraw, only leaving knowledge, returning causality as is. This restraint, more than pushing through everything, is harder and more advanced. It means however far this civilization walks in the cosmos, behind it leaves no string of churned planes. A thousand years later looking back, the vast majority of the Myriad Heavens and Myriad Worlds' planes still look as they originally did — only occasionally remembering that a group quietly came and quietly left. This is causal self-discipline's greatest tenderness to the cosmos.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: reporting + trace avoids the cleanup cost of repeated causal trouble
- **Anti-predation**: cross-plane no predation, and causal self-discipline is anti-predation's cross-plane version
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo protagonists wander planes and recklessly change time; Heng Yuan Zhou cross-plane reports first
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: every plane's beings with inherent infinity are protected by causal self-discipline
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the causal protocol covers new planes as the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm expands

## VI: Causal Self-Discipline Is Not Coldness, But Reserve to the End

It must also be made clear: causal self-discipline sounds like it restricts strong ones everywhere, seemingly cold. But its undertone is precisely the warmest — because it defaults that every plane, every group of natives, every being deserves to be treated as the master, not toyed with by passersby. Old-cosmos strong ones treat cross-plane as "my map," coming and changing as they wish; Anchor Symbiotic strong ones treat cross-plane as "others' home," knocking before entering, greeting before sitting, and taking out the trash when leaving. This respect for unfamiliar beings ultimately protects the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization itself in return — because when one day others come to our plane, they will also treat us by the same rules. Causal self-discipline is not an ascetic practice that only restrains oneself, but a gentleman's non-harm agreement signed with the entire cosmos.

## VII: Causal Self-Discipline Is the Clean Account Civilization Gives the Future

Causal self-discipline has another long-term meaning: it keeps the account clean for future Myriad Ranks. Old-cosmos strong ones recklessly change the past and shuttle time, leaving piles of causal messes no one dares touch, and later generations live in their predecessors' wreckage. The Anchor Symbiotic's cross-plane operations are fully traced, observe first, and only return knowledge, which equals every step giving future Myriad Ranks a clean account. Future generations opening this account will clearly know where predecessors walked, what they did, what they left — no dark history, no time paradox, no old pits needing countless lives to fill. A civilization responsible for the future should not make later generations clean up its mess. Causal self-discipline is today's Myriad Ranks' most decent accounting to tomorrow's Myriad Ranks.

## Conclusion

Causal self-discipline is the closed loop's self-cleaning system. The loop turning long cannot rely only on momentum and return, but also on constantly wiping off the dirt it leaves — wiped off, and the loop is clean and lasting.
