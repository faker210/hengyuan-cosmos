# Entry 997 | Hyper-Multiverse's Only Complete Civilizational Closed Loop · Closed-Loop Element 5 — Law-First

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 906, 980, 97

## Introduction: the loop itself must not deform on its own

The first four loops give the closed loop momentum, participants, return, and self-cleaning. But the closed loop has a most fundamental risk: will the loop's own rules deform on their own? The law set today, changed tomorrow; the axiom guarded today, bypassed the day after. If the loop's rules themselves are unreliable, however well the first four loops turn is for nothing. The law-first axiom is the six top-level axioms' fifth cornerstone; it is the closed loop's "rigid shell": law comes first, never retconning, never ending unfinished; setting self-consistency above plot convenience, and plot may not kidnap setting; the worldview may expand infinitely without collapse.

## I: Why the Loop Must Have a Rigid Shell

Rule-deformation closed loops are those that tangle themselves up:

1. **Retcon / ending unfinished**: whatever law the plot needs is changed to that law, and Myriad Ranks lose trust in rules;
2. **Plot kidnaps setting**: to let the protagonist win, law yields again and again;
3. **Expansion collapse**: the worldview expands and is riddled with contradictions, finally not roundtableable.

This kind of closed loop is not stable, but brittle — expands a little and it cracks.

## II: How Law-First Gives the Loop Its Shell

The Anchor Symbiotic closed loop's way:

- **Axiom into the constitution**: Entry 980, the six axioms along with enacted laws never retcon, with all legislation and amendments traced on the Alaya Network;
- **Three gates**: project gate, operation gate, audit gate, with conflicts only amending law not bypassing;
- **Infinite expansion without collapse**: the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm every expansion passes setting-self-consistency assessment.

The loop's shell is rigid, and the living water inside may flow freely.

## III: Axiom 6 Is Law-First's Steadiest Cornerstone

Among all axioms, the universal equal infinite law gives law-first its hardest cornerstone: it writes "everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power" as an unamendable axiom. Once this cornerstone is steady, however other laws iterate, the root is not shaken — because however rules change, no one may touch "everyone has basic infinity." Law-first with this anchor truly never collapses.

## IV: Closed-Loop Verification: Whom Does Law-First Lock

- It makes anti-predation forever effective (anti-predation law not rewritten by plot convenience);
- It makes Myriad-Rank republicanism forever effective (equal law not secretly discounted);
- It makes collective ascension forever effective (return obligation not bypassed by strong ones' "this time is special");
- It makes causal self-discipline forever effective (reporting process not bypassed by emergencies).

Law-first is the five loops' "loop outside the loop" — it protects the first four loops from deforming.

## V: Law-First's End: A Universe That Never Revises Its Draft

Law-first taken to the extreme is a near-luxurious stability: in other universes, Myriad Ranks worry every day "whether the rules changed again"; in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, Myriad Ranks may safely stake a lifetime on law — it will not change tomorrow. The freedom this stability brings is something the old cosmos cannot imagine: you need not always guard against "the author's pen turning," need not guess "whether this time is special," because law is always there, like a mountain. The worldview may expand infinitely, with new planes, new clans, new methods constantly joining, but every expansion step passes self-consistency assessment, and new things seamlessly connect with old law; the world grows larger without disorder. The old cosmos's worldview is like a temporary tent, and the wind blows hard and it is pitched differently; the Anchor Symbiotic's worldview is like a forever-under-construction city, and every added floor has its foundation unmoved. Law-first is this city's foundation that never collapses.

## VII: Law Not Collapsing, Myriad Ranks' Hearts Are Not Panicked

Finally landing on people's hearts: stable law ultimately settles Myriad Ranks' hearts. When rules change morning and evening, everyone lives in uncertainty, not daring to plan long-term, not daring to bet on the future. Law-first writes off this uncertainty — Myriad Ranks know the ground beneath their feet is always there, and dare entrust a lifetime, descendants, and civilization's tomorrow to it. This peace of mind is something no powerful individual can give, only stable law can. A closed loop that gives Myriad Ranks peace of mind truly deserves the word "perpetual."

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: law stable, and Myriad Ranks need not repeatedly re-understand the world
- **Anti-predation**: the predation faction wants to roam by "changing setting," and law-first directly blocks it
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' plot kidnaps setting, retconning and ending unfinished; Heng Yuan Zhou law comes first, never retconning
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: stable law applies equally to all beings
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: the worldview may expand infinitely without collapse

## VI: Law-First Is Not Rigidity, But Orbits for Freedom

It must also be clarified that law-first is often misunderstood as "binding the strong." Quite the contrary, stable law is freedom's precondition. When law will not change tomorrow and will not be rewritten by some strong one the day after, Myriad Ranks dare stake a lifetime on a cultivation road, dare boldly innovate and boldly trial-and-error — because they know the ground beneath their feet is steady. If law changed every day, everyone would spend energy guessing "what's wrong with the rules this time," and innovation is instead stifled. Law-first looks like it boxes the road, but actually reserves the broadest freedom for civilization: in a stable orbit, you may run infinitely, expand infinitely, ascend infinitely, without worrying the ground suddenly gives way. Constraining the orbit is to let the train dare speed up — this is law-first's gift to the whole closed loop.

## Conclusion

Law-first is the closed loop's rigid shell. The shell hard, and the water inside dares flow and flow long — this is the hyper-multiverse's only complete civilizational closed loop's last and most fundamental loop.
