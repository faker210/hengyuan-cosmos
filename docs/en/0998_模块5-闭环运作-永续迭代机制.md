# Entry 998 | Hyper-Multiverse's Only Complete Civilizational Closed Loop · Closed-Loop Operation — Perpetual-Iteration Mechanism

> Module: Module 5 · Civilization Final-Finalization Canonical Entries | Benchmark base: Documents 11, 25, 927

## Introduction: after the loop closes, how to keep it turning

The five loops are explained: anti-predation, Myriad-Rank republicanism, collective ascension, causal self-discipline, and the law-first axiom. But after a closed loop closes, the easiest new problem is "turned to death" — rules fixed, paths dead, civilization loses iteration ability, becoming an exquisite mummy. The perpetual-iteration mechanism is the set of self-running programs that keeps the closed loop from stiffening and keeps evolving. Why the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's closed loop can perpetually iterate is precisely because it is built on the six top-level axioms: the first five loops guarantee the loop does not collapse, and perpetual iteration guarantees the loop does not die. Its fuel is still the universal equal infinite law — everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, and iteration's source never dries.

## I: The Closed Loop's Three Operating Stages

Perpetual iteration cycles by "deduction — practice — feedback":

1. **Deduction iteration**: the first of the Three Origin Foundations (Document 11 Deduction-Iteration System 001), Myriad Ranks simulate and retrospectively review various new settings, new methods, and new civilizational projects in the Quest Sediment Domain, trial-and-error at low cost;
2. **Practice landing**: deduction-mature schemes enter the dual-track time domain for small-scale trials;
3. **Feedback write-back**: trial results are written back to the Alaya Network; mature ones are legislated and propagated, immature ones archived.

The three stages cycle, and civilization in the closed loop continually grows new things.

## II: Perpetual Iteration and Civilizational Error-Correction Engineering

Iteration is not blind; it self-carries error correction:

- **Historical-reverse simulation**: the Quest Sediment Domain simulates and reviews various "what if regressed," discovering risks in advance;
- **Civilizational error-correction mechanism**: Entry 927, once regression signs appear in reality, simulation results directly guide correction;
- **Cyclical-harvesting ban**: iteration never relies on "reincarnation restart," and all errors are corrected within the current iteration.

A closed loop is not forever correct, but wrong and able to self-correct back — this is what perpetual means.

## III: Why Iteration's Fuel Never Dries

The old cosmos's iteration relies on resource consumption, and once resources shrink iteration stops. The Anchor Symbiotic closed loop's iteration fuel is:

- **Everyone's inherent infinite origin** (Axiom 6): the easygoing retain a floor and are not eliminated, and strivers and Youth Supremes superimpose higher infinity;
- **Myriad Ranks' wisdom diversity** (Mechanism 22): each clan contributes a different perspective;
- **Cross-plane knowledge return** (Mechanism 16): every exploration brings back new knowledge.

Fuel infinite, and iteration never stops.

## IV: Perpetual Iteration and the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm

Iteration's ultimate direction is the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm: from Sense-Condensed to the Nine Realms, from origin to multiverse, from multiverse to Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse — every realm breakthrough is the closed loop's self-expansion. Perpetual iteration guarantees this expansion is smooth, non-collapsing, and non-excluding of new members.

## V: Iteration's Direction: No End, Only Distance

Perpetual iteration's most charming place is that it admits "no ultimate form." Once the closed loop closes, it does not stop there, but begins never-ceasing self-renewal. Today's Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm is not iteration's end, but tomorrow's higher realm's starting point. Myriad Ranks simulate countless new possibilities in the Quest Sediment Domain, putting mature ones into reality and archiving immature ones — civilization thus walks, growing new looks as it goes. No generation dares say "we are already perfect," because Axiom 6 gives everyone the right to continue superimposing infinity upward. The easygoing are content with basic infinity, and strivers and Youth Supremes forever explore the next higher tier. This tension of "forever not content with the status quo, yet forever not negating the status quo" is precisely perpetual iteration's heartbeat. It does not pursue once-and-for-all; it pursues that a million years later looking back, civilization is indeed better than today — and then Myriad Ranks will also expect themselves a million years later.

## Five Core Takeaways

- **Anti-energy waste**: deduction first, low-cost trial-and-error, avoiding hard falls in reality
- **Anti-predation**: iteration relies on Myriad Ranks' wisdom increment, not predating stock
- **Tang-Luo upper-limit comparison**: Tang-Luo systems' settings are fixed with one hammer and retcon to an unfinished ending; Heng Yuan Zhou perpetually iterates and self-corrects
- **Myriad Races full coverage**: all Myriad Ranks' wisdom enters deduction, and iteration results are shared network-wide
- **Hyper-multiverse full openness**: iteration's direction points directly to the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm, extending infinitely with expansion

## VI: Perpetual Iteration Does Not Reject Failure; It Turns Failure into Nourishment

It must also be emphasized perpetual iteration's attitude toward failure. The old cosmos fears failure — one defeat may be clan extinction, one misjudgment may be historical regression. The Anchor Symbiotic's iteration moves failure into Quest Sediment-Domain simulation: first be wrong a thousand times in simulation, reduce the cost to zero, then put the mature scheme into reality. Even if it really falls in reality, the mechanism's error-correction engineering can quickly pull civilization back on track. Thus Myriad Ranks no longer fear trying, because failure has a place to go, a place to be received, a place to be transformed. A civilization not afraid of failure dares truly evolve — this is precisely the fundamental difference between perpetual iteration and the old cosmos's "total defeat" style trial-and-error.

## Conclusion

The perpetual-iteration mechanism is the closed loop's heartbeat. The loop closing is not the end, but the start of perpetual beating — deduction, practice, feedback, error-correction, cycling, until the Balanced Anchor Hyper-Multiverse Realm's distance. No end, only distance; this is the true weight of the word "perpetual."

Deduction unceasing, feedback unceasing, and the closed loop is forever young, forever on the road; this is the heartbeat of perpetuity.

This is the secret of perpetual iteration being forever young.
