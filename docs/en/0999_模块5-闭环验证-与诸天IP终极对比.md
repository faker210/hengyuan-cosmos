## II: Three Key Gaps

Gap one: **is the momentum predatory or incremental**. Myriad-Heavens IP protagonists almost all upgrade by predation — killing the strong, seizing soul rings, devouring origin. Short-term satisfying, long-term Myriad Ranks are fertilizer. Heng Yuan Zhou takes incremental development as momentum, and because of the universal equal infinite law, everyone inherently possesses basic infinite power, so predation fundamentally loses meaning.

Gap two: **does the closed loop depend on the protagonist**. Myriad-Heavens IP "closed loops" are essentially one-protagonist closed loops — the protagonist is, civilization is; once he leaves, civilization rots. Protagonist-uniqueness dependence (Tang-Luo very high, Ye Fan medium, Heng Yuan Zhou very low) determines whether the closed loop can last. Heng Yuan Zhou writes the closed loop into institutions, depending on no protagonist.

Gap three: **are resources finite or origin infinite**. Myriad-Heavens IPs default resources finite, so involutionary predation is necessary; Heng Yuan Zhou by Axiom 6 has infinite origin, and predatory involution loses its physical basis — this is the most fundamental gap.
