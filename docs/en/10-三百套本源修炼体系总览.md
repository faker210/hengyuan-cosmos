## I. Background and Significance of System Opening

In the long epochs dominated by predatory cultivation systems, cultivation methods were monopolized by a few powerful beings and sects, and the beings at the bottom had almost no legitimate evolutionary paths. This monopoly not only caused class solidification, but also led to extremely low overall evolutionary efficiency of the universe — the potential of the vast majority of lives was wasted, while the evolution of the few predators came at the cost of consuming the cosmic Origin.

After the establishment of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, the first fundamental measure was to open the Three Hundred Cosmic Origin Cultivation Systems. These Three Hundred Systems are not artificially fabricated techniques, but orthodox cultivation paths directly distilled from the cosmic Origin laws and verified through anchors. They cover all evolutionary dimensions from matter to consciousness, from individual to group, from cultivation to application, ensuring that "A Path for Everyone" is no longer an empty phrase.

The core significance of system opening lies in returning the right to choose evolution to all beings. Every life can choose the cultivation system most suited to itself according to its own traits, interests, and environment, rather than being forced to accept the only predatory path.

## II. Classification Architecture of the Three Hundred Systems

The Three Hundred Origin Cultivation Systems are divided into six major categories according to evolutionary dimensions, with fifty systems in each category, forming a complete evolutionary spectrum:

| Category | Quantity | Core Direction | Representative System |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| Origin Deduction Category | 50 | Intelligence-type evolution centered on deduction, calculation, and iteration | Deduction and Iteration System (System No. 1) |
| Life Circulation Category | 50 | Centered on life energy, metabolism, and genetic evolution | Life Circulation System (System No. 2) |
| Space Law Category | 50 | Centered on space, dimensions, and law dispatch | Space Law Dispatch System (System No. 3) |
| Consciousness Ascension Category | 50 | Centered on spirit, consciousness, and soul evolution | Consciousness Anchoring System |
| Symbiotic Network Category | 50 | Centered on group collaboration, network resonance, and collective evolution | Symbiotic Resonance System |
| Application Engineering Category | 50 | Centered on the technological and engineering application of cultivation achievements | Law Engineering System |

The first three systems — Deduction and Iteration, Life Circulation, and Space Law Dispatch — are the foundation of all systems, called the "Three Foundations of Origin". Cultivators of any other system are advised to first practice at least one of the Three Foundations of Origin to establish a solid evolutionary foundation.

## III. Summary of the First Three Foundational Systems

### 3.1 Deduction and Iteration System (System No. 1)

The Deduction and Iteration System takes "hundredfold cultivation deduction amplification" as its core feature. Through the technological application of the Multiplying Fruit from One Piece, it enhances cultivators' deduction ability a hundredfold. This system emphasizes optimizing cultivation paths through continuous deduction, verification, and iteration, suitable for intelligence-type and research-type lives. Its core realms include five stages: Initial Insight, Deduction, Iteration, Integration, and Origin.

### 3.2 Life Circulation System (System No. 2)

The Life Circulation System takes the benign circulation of life energy as its core, emphasizing that the energy exchange between the cultivator and the environment must be circular rather than predatory. This system not only enhances individual life force, but also improves the ecological environment in which the cultivator lives. Core realms include five stages: Life Perception, Circulation, Generation, Transformation, and Origin Birth.

### 3.3 Space Law Dispatch System (System No. 3)

The Space Law Dispatch System takes the understanding and dispatch of space laws as its core, and is the cultivation foundation for important applications such as cross-plane interaction, Isolation Space construction, and Replication Mirror technology. This system emphasizes precise manipulation of space laws, rather than simple spatial tearing. Core realms include five stages: Space Recognition, Space Control, Space Transformation, Space Law, and Space Origin.

## IV. System Selection and Concurrent Practice Mechanism

The Three Hundred Systems are not mutually exclusive; cultivators can choose a major system and auxiliary systems according to their own circumstances. The symbiotic civilization has established a "System Compatibility Evaluation Mechanism," providing personalized system combination suggestions to every cultivator through the anchor network. Generally speaking:

- The compatibility among the Three Foundations of Origin is the highest, and concurrent practice is recommended
- Systems within the same major category have good compatibility
- Concurrent practice across major categories requires higher foundations and anchor support
- It is not recommended to major in more than three systems simultaneously, to avoid scattered effort

## V. Safeguard Measures for System Opening

To ensure the Three Hundred Systems truly benefit all beings, the symbiotic civilization has adopted the following safeguard measures:

1. **Free opening**: the basic methods of all systems are open free of charge to all beings, without thresholds
2. **Mentor network**: establish a cross-plane mentor network, where advanced cultivators have the obligation to guide junior cultivators
3. **Cultivation resource allocation**: provide necessary resource support to cultivators through the Output-Linked Contract System
4. **Continuous system optimization**: the anchor network continuously collects cultivation feedback and optimizes the cultivation paths of each system
5. **Anti-monopoly provisions**: the law prohibits any organization or individual from monopolizing the authorization to transmit cultivation systems

## Conclusion

The opening of the Three Hundred Origin Cultivation Systems is one of the most revolutionary measures of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. It fundamentally broke the monopoly of cultivation and provided tangible path guarantees for Myriad Races Advancing Together. Subsequent documents will elaborate on each system in more detail.
