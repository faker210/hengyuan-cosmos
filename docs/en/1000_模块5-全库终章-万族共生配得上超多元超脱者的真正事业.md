## I: The Six Top-Level Axioms: The Source of All Verdicts

Heng Yuan Zhou Entries 100–1000's entire setting is built on the six top-level axioms:

1. **Anti-predation axiom**: develop increment, do not consume stock; do not take slaughter and predation as the evolutionary path; the Great Dao is open at all tiers, with no private hoarding and no monopoly.
2. **Republican axiom of Myriad Races**: all beings possess equal evolution qualifications; Myriad Ranks 99% full coverage; break the strong ones' monopoly on knowledge and the Great Dao.
3. **Collective-ascension axiom**: strong ones do not ascend alone, but lift Myriad Ranks forward together in evolution; Myriad Ranks co-build republicanly, with Alaya Network whole-network sharing.
4. **Causal self-discipline axiom**: cross-plane operations strictly reported, observe first then limitedly intervene, only return knowledge and not disturb causality.
5. **Law-first axiom**: law comes first, never retconning, never ending unfinished; setting self-consistency above plot convenience.
6. **Universal equal infinite law**: all beings inherently possess basic infinite power; the easygoing retain a floor basic infinity and will not be eliminated; strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher-level infinity.

The first five axioms answer "how civilization should walk"; the sixth answers "by what does civilization walk" — the old cosmos's resources finite so predatory involution is inevitable; the Anchor Symbiotic everyone inherently possesses infinite origin, and predation fundamentally loses meaning. The six axioms together are the complete answer.

## III: The Final Verdict: Myriad-Rank Symbiosis Is the True Cause Worthy of Hyper-Multiverse Transcendents

A thousand entries reaching the end, the verdict is one sentence: **Myriad-Rank symbiosis is the true cause worthy of hyper-multiverse transcendent ones.**

This sentence has three layers:

First, **the old cosmos's cause is not worthy of being called a cause**. Tang-Luo, Ye Fan, Shi Hao and their kind fight to the cosmos's peak, finally leaving behind hollowed-out Myriad Ranks and endlessly reincarnating history — that kind of "cause," essentially, is one person's brilliance, everyone's cost.

Second, **Heng Yuan Zhou's cause is worthy of being called a cause**. It does not pursue one person's invincibility, but Myriad Ranks' perpetuity; does not pursue one cosmos's kingship, but hyper-multiverse realm's co-construction; does not pursue strong ones ascending and running off, but transcendent ones' perpetual-motion construction.

Third, **this cause is worthy of every being**. Because of the universal equal infinite law, every being inherently possesses basic infinite power — the easygoing lying flat is still gently held up by the times, and strivers and Youth Supremes may superimpose higher infinity. This is not the strong ones' cause, but Myriad Ranks' own cause.
