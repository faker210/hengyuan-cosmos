# Chapter 105 | Sense-Origin Realm: Breakthrough Risks, Costs, and Alaya Network Supervision

> Module: Module One · Deep Realm Refinement | Grounding documents: Docs 14, 19, 04, 97, 17, 90

## Introduction: Ascension Has a Price, and the Price Must Be a Causal One

No cultivation system can promise a free lunch, and Heng Yuan Zhou is no exception. The Sense-Origin Realm is the starting point of a cultivator's life, yet it is precisely where the first divergence happens: some people's first breakthrough brings clarity, while some people's first breakthrough seeds madness. The predatory faction treats this divergence as "natural elimination"—the weak are supposed to die; the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization treats it as a systemic problem: risk must be managed, costs must be transparent, and supervision must be layered.

This article unfolds the four core risks of Sense-Origin cultivation, the honest cost of breakthrough, and the five-layer supervision mechanism of the Alaya Network.

The Six Top Axioms provide the supervisory foundation: the Axiom of Anti-Predation—risk control must not become a new predation, that is, exploiting the weak's fear to harvest them; the Axiom of Myriad Races Republic—supervision applies equally to all races, no "noble" race exempt from risk; the Axiom of Collective Dimensional Ascension—an individual's breakdown is a loss to the whole, so early warning is duty, not kindness; the Axiom of Causal Self-Discipline—the cost of breakthrough must be the cultivator's own causal fruit, must not be transferred to surface beings; the Axiom of Law Priority—the supervisory framework of the Alaya Network is stable and self-consistent, never retconned for plot convenience, and can expand infinitely without collapsing. The Law of Universal Infinite Equality for All Beings establishes that every being inherently carries Fundamental Infinite Power; the laid-back retain a guaranteed baseline infinity and will not be eliminated by the times, while grinders and peerless youths can stack higher tiers of infinity. Infinite origin is carried by all; the weak falling in breakthrough is not "just," but the failure of the supervisory system.

## I. Four Core Risks of Sense-Origin Cultivation

1. Perception chaos risk: energy surges are misjudged, the cultivator mistakes external noise for inner voice, opening the door to illusion—this is the earliest risk at the Sense-Origin Realm.
2. Foundation-dry risk: absorption outpaces digestion, the foundation layer is stuffed with unrefined energy, and the Congealing-Origin Realm ahead becomes a powder keg.
3. Ego-fanaticism risk: a first breakthrough gives an inflated sense of "chosen one," the cultivator confuses himself with the path, and the Shortcut Inner Demon of Universal Origin Greed is born.
4. Causal leakage risk: premature borrowing of future potential, overdrawing later-stage growth for early-stage speed—this is the risk the old system most liked to encourage.

These four risks are not uncommon accidents; they are the standard profile of Sense-Origin Realm breakthroughs. Pretending they do not exist is the first evil of the predatory system.

## II. The Honest Cost of Breakthrough

Heng Yuan Zhou's attitude toward breakthrough cost is "truth first":

- Physical cost: the consumption and wear of the body, openly listed in the Output-Linked Contract System's ledger.
- Time cost: the true duration of absorption, no "hundredfold speed" lies.
- Causal cost: the karmic debt a breakthrough may incur, the Alaya Network records and traces it.
- Opportunity cost: a detour is still a detour, and the extended-setting suggestion list (Doc 98) explicitly rejects "pretending a detour is a shortcut."

The predatory faction sells breakthroughs as free gifts—until the day the bill comes. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization lets every cultivator see the bill before paying: this is "Axiom of Causal Self-Discipline" at the entry level.

## III. The Five-Layer Supervision Mechanism of the Alaya Network

The Alaya Network (Doc 19's ethical anchor, Doc 97's supervision overview) does not replace cultivation, but stands beside it:

- Layer one · Perception monitoring: energy fluctuation anomalies are sensed, early warnings issued before chaos.
- Layer two · Foundation audit: absorption quality is scored, and "overfilled but not digested" foundations get a red flag.
- Layer three · Ego-early-warning: the "chosen-one" delusion pattern is recognized, and the peerless youth dual-route (Doc 102) pulls them back in time.
- Layer four · Causal ledger: every breakthrough's cost and debt is recorded, traceable at any time.
- Layer five · Rescue and retreat: a cultivator on the edge of breakdown can retreat to the safe zone; retreat is not failure, but a rational choice.

These five layers are not a prison, but a guardrail—they stand the same whether the cultivator is speeding fast or crawling slow.

## IV. Six Cultivation Ethics Landing Points

Combined with the cultivation ethics and boundaries of Doc 19, Sense-Origin cultivation ethics land as six points:

1. No predatory absorption—energy only from the world cycle, never from the lives of others.
2. No concealment of cost—the bill is shown before the price.
3. No forced breakthrough—retreat is an inalienable right.
4. No exploitation of fear—risk control may not harvest the fearful.
5. No "chosen one" monopoly—the path is open to everyone.
6. No abandonment of the fallen—a breakdown is a systemic alarm, not a verdict of worthlessness.

## V. Comparison: Two Attitudes Toward the Weak Breaking Down

- Predatory attitude: "the weak deserve to die," risk is a filter, and supervision is only for the strong's convenience.
- Symbiotic attitude: "the weak falling is our failure," risk is a problem to solve, and supervision is for everyone's safety.
- The predatory faction's safety net protects only the strong's seat; the symbiotic faction's guardrail protects everyone's way home.

This is the root difference between "survival of the fittest" and "not one left behind."

## Conclusion

The Sense-Origin Realm is where a cultivator first touches risk. In Heng Yuan Zhou, touching risk does not mean "you either become god or become monster," but "we see you, we warn you, and you can always choose to step back." A cultivation system's goodness is not measured by how high its ceiling climbs, but by how it treats the first step—because that is where every cultivator started.
