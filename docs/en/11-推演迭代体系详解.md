## I. System Overview

The Deduction and Iteration System is System No. 1 among the Three Hundred Origin Cultivation Systems, ranking first among the "Three Foundations of Origin", and is the foundation and forerunner of all cultivation systems. This system takes "deduction" as its core method and "iteration" as its core process, achieving the dual evolution of the cultivator's own abilities and cultivation path through continuous deduction, verification, correction, and optimization. The greatest feature of the Deduction and Iteration System is the "hundredfold cultivation deduction amplification" provided by the technological application of the Multiplying Fruit, which elevates the cultivator's deduction ability to a hundred times the normal level, greatly accelerating the cultivation process and theoretical innovation.

The Deduction and Iteration System is suitable for intelligence-type, research-type, and creative-type lives. For cultivators skilled in logical thinking, theoretical analysis, and innovative exploration, this system can maximize their talent advantages. At the same time, the Deduction and Iteration System is also the "meta-system" of all other systems — cultivators of any system can optimize their own cultivation methods and accelerate breakthroughs by practicing the Deduction and Iteration System.

## II. Core Principles of the System

The core principle of the Deduction and Iteration System can be summarized as the "Deduction — Verification — Iteration" three-ring cycle:

### 2.1 The Deduction Ring

Deduction refers to the cultivator's advance simulation and calculation of cultivation paths, energy operation, and law changes in the consciousness domain. The essence of deduction is to preview various possibilities of cultivation through mental computation, without consuming actual energy and resources. The quality of deduction depends on three factors: the cultivator's deduction ability, knowledge reserves, and depth of law understanding.

With the support of Multiplying Fruit technology, the cultivator's deduction speed and precision receive a hundredfold amplification. This means that a deduction calculation that originally required a hundred years can be completed in one year under hundredfold amplification; energy operation that could originally only be roughly simulated can now be precise to the trajectory of every energy particle.

### 2.2 The Verification Ring

Verification refers to conducting small-scale experiments and tests of the deduction results in reality. After all, deduction is a simulation in consciousness, and deviations may exist between it and reality. The function of the verification ring is to test the correctness and feasibility of deduction results through controllable experiments, discovering problems not foreseen in the deduction.

The verification ring follows the "minimum risk principle": all verification experiments must be conducted in Isolation Space, using the minimum amount of energy, ensuring that even if the experiment fails, it will not harm the cultivator or the surrounding environment. The verification results are recorded in the form of data and fed back to the deduction ring for correction.

### 2.3 The Iteration Ring

Iteration refers to correcting and optimizing the deduction model based on verification results, then entering the next round of deduction. Each iteration deepens the cultivator's understanding of laws, refines their energy control, and optimizes their cultivation path selection. Iteration is not simple repetition, but a spiraling upward evolutionary process.

The frequency of the three-ring cycle depends on the cultivator's realm and the amplification intensity of the Multiplying Fruit. In the initial stage, completing one full cycle may take several days; in the advanced stage, cultivators can complete thousands of iterations in an instant, achieving "epiphany-style" breakthroughs.

## III. Realm Division and Characteristics of Each Stage

The Deduction and Iteration System is divided into five core realms, each with clear characteristics and assessment standards:

| Realm | Name | Core Characteristics | Deduction Ability | Signature Ability |
|------|------|----------|----------|------------|
| First Realm | Initial Insight | Preliminarily establish deduction consciousness, able to perform simple cultivation path deduction | 1-10 times normal deduction speed | Basic Deduction Technique: can preview single-step cultivation in consciousness |
| Second Realm | Deduction | Deduction ability greatly improved, able to perform complex multivariate deduction | 10-50 times normal deduction speed | Multidimensional Deduction: can simultaneously deduce multiple cultivation paths and compare them |
| Third Realm | Iteration | Master the complete deduction-verification-iteration cycle, able to autonomously optimize cultivation paths | 50-100 times normal deduction speed | Self-Optimizing Iteration: cultivation paths automatically optimize, bottleneck breakthrough speed greatly improved |
| Fourth Realm | Integration | Deduction ability penetrates all aspects of cultivation, achieving seamless integration of deduction and combat | 100-500 times normal deduction speed | Combat Deduction: in battle, deduce all possible actions of the opponent in real time and formulate the optimal response |
| Fifth Realm | Origin | Deduction touches the cosmic Origin laws, able to deduce the operation and changes of laws themselves | 500 times and above, approaching infinite deduction | Law Deduction: can deduce new law application methods, even participate in fine-tuning and optimization of laws |

The breakthrough of each realm requires passing a "deduction assessment": under the supervision of the anchor network, complete deduction tasks of specified difficulty; only after passing the assessment can the realm elevation be confirmed.

## IV. Application of Multiplying Fruit Technology in the System

The technological application of the Multiplying Fruit is the core technological support enabling the hundredfold deduction amplification of the Deduction and Iteration System. Its applications in the system include:

### 4.1 Consciousness Acceleration

Multiplying Fruit technology can accelerate the computation speed of the cultivator's consciousness domain, creating a difference between the flow of time in consciousness and time in reality. Under hundredfold amplification, the cultivator performs a hundred seconds of deduction in consciousness while only one second passes in reality. This consciousness acceleration does not consume extra life energy, but is a purely consciousness-level acceleration achieved through law resonance.

### 4.2 Parallel Deduction

Multiplying Fruit technology supports cultivators in simultaneously conducting multiple independent deduction threads. Beginner cultivators can simultaneously deduce 3-5 paths; advanced cultivators can simultaneously deduce hundreds of paths, conducting comprehensive comparison and screening of various possibilities.

### 4.3 Deduction Precision Enhancement

Multiplying Fruit technology not only enhances the speed of deduction, but also the precision. Under hundredfold amplification, cultivators can perceive more subtle energy changes and law fluctuations, making deduction results closer to reality.

### 4.4 Deduction Memory Consolidation

Multiplying Fruit technology can consolidate the insights and understandings obtained during deduction into long-term memory, preventing the forgetting of deduction results. Cultivators can retrieve past deduction records at any time for review and re-analysis.

## V. Cultivation Methods and Daily Practice of the System

The cultivation of the Deduction and Iteration System is not done behind closed doors, but requires close integration with practice. Daily cultivation methods include:

1. **Daily deduction training**: conduct deduction training at a fixed time each day, gradually transitioning from simple energy operation deduction to complex law change deduction
2. **Deduction log recording**: record the process, results, and verification of each deduction, forming a personal deduction database
3. **Cross-system deduction application**: apply deduction ability to the cultivation of other cultivation systems, optimizing their cultivation paths
4. **Deduction exchange and discussion**: exchange deduction insights with other cultivators of the Deduction and Iteration System, mutually testing deduction results
5. **Deduction competition participation**: participate in deduction competitions organized by the civilization, improving deduction ability through competition

## VI. Advantages and Limitations of the System

### 6.1 Advantages

- Extremely high cultivation efficiency: the hundredfold deduction amplification makes cultivation speed far exceed conventional systems
- Strong bottleneck breakthrough ability: the optimal path to break through bottlenecks can be found through deduction
- Strong universality: deduction ability can be applied to the cultivation of any other system
- High safety: most cultivation is completed in consciousness, reducing the risk of cultivation deviation
- Strong innovation ability: deduction is the core method of theoretical innovation; cultivators of this system are often the creators of new techniques and technologies

### 6.2 Limitations

- High requirements for intelligence and mental disposition: not suitable for cultivators lacking logical thinking ability or with impetuous dispositions
- Weak early combat ability: in the initial stage, cultivation is mainly in consciousness, and actual combat ability improves slowly
- Excessive reliance on deduction may neglect actual combat: some cultivators may become obsessed with deduction and neglect actual verification
- Dependence on Multiplying Fruit technology: the hundredfold amplification requires the support of Multiplying Fruit technology; the effect is limited in areas with insufficient technological coverage

## VII. Compatibility with Other Systems

As the first of the "Three Foundations of Origin", the Deduction and Iteration System has good compatibility with all other cultivation systems. In particular:

- Concurrent practice with the Life Circulation System: can deduce the optimal life energy circulation path, accelerating the cultivation of the Life Circulation System
- Concurrent practice with the Space Law Dispatch System: can deduce various application methods of space laws, improving the precision of space control
- Concurrent practice with consciousness ascension systems: deduction itself is training of consciousness, which can accelerate consciousness ascension
- Concurrent practice with application engineering systems: deduction is the core method of law engineering design, greatly improving engineering design efficiency

## Conclusion

The Deduction and Iteration System is the cornerstone and forerunner of the cultivation systems of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. With the hundredfold deduction amplification as its sharp weapon and the deduction-verification-iteration cycle as its method, it provides cultivators with an efficient, safe, and universal evolutionary path. The ultimate goal of the Deduction and Iteration System is to make every cultivator the "chief scientist" of their own evolution, finding the evolutionary path most suited to themselves through continuous deduction and iteration, and contributing to the law innovation and technological progress of the entire civilization.
