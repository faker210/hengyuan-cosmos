## I. System Overview

The Space Law Dispatch System is System No. 3 among the Three Hundred Origin Cultivation Systems, ranking third among the "Three Foundations of Origin", and is the foundation of all cultivation systems and technological applications involving space, dimensions, and plane operations. This system takes "the understanding and dispatch of space laws" as its core, emphasizing precise cognition and orderly manipulation of space laws, rather than simple and crude spatial tearing and destruction. The Space Law Dispatch System is the cultivation foundation for important applications such as cross-plane interaction, Isolation Space construction, Replication Mirror technology, and anchor maintenance, playing an irreplaceable role in the infrastructure construction of the symbiotic civilization.

The Space Law Dispatch System is suitable for cultivators with strong spatial perception, meticulous logical thinking, and a focus on precision and order. Cultivators of this system are the most important technical talent group in the symbiotic civilization, undertaking key tasks such as anchor construction, plane channel maintenance, Isolation Space management, and archaeological space construction.

## II. Core Principles of the System

The core principle of the Space Law Dispatch System is "space is law, dispatch is order", which can be understood from the following three levels:

### 2.1 The Legal Essence of Space

In the cosmic view of Heng Yuan Zhou, space is not an empty container, but an ordered structure composed of a series of law rules. The attributes of space — dimensions, distance, direction, curvature, stability, etc. — are all determined by the underlying space law rules. Understanding the essence of space means understanding the operating mechanisms of these law rules.

Space laws can be divided into three levels: foundational laws (determining the basic attributes of space, such as three-dimensional structure, distance definition, etc.), transformational laws (determining the variability of space, such as spatial folding, curvature changes, etc.), and Origin laws (determining the creation and destruction of space, such as the formation and collapse of planes). The cultivation of the Space Law Dispatch System starts from understanding foundational laws, gradually deepening into transformational and Origin laws.

### 2.2 The Core Method of Dispatch

"Dispatch" is the core methodology of the Space Law Dispatch System. Unlike the "spatial tearing" of predatory cultivation, dispatch does not use violence to destroy spatial structures, but achieves orderly adjustment and manipulation of space through understanding and following space laws.

The core methods of dispatch include:

- **Law Perception**: perceive the operating state of law rules in space, identifying spatial structures and anomalies
- **Law Guidance**: guide space laws to operate in the expected direction through one's own energy and consciousness
- **Law Resonance**: resonate with space laws, amplifying the effect and range of dispatch
- **Law Solidification**: solidify the results of dispatch into stable spatial structures, such as Isolation Space, plane channels, etc.

The key to dispatch is "following the momentum". The cultivator is not the master of space, but the "dispatcher" of space laws — achieving orderly manipulation of space through understanding and following the laws.

### 2.3 Ethical Principles of Space Operations

The Space Law Dispatch System establishes strict ethical principles for space operations:

- **Principle of Minimal Intervention**: operations on space should be as small as possible, avoiding unnecessary destruction of spatial structures
- **Principle of Recoverability**: all temporary space operations must restore the space to its original state after completion, leaving no permanent damage
- **Principle of Isolation**: space operations that may produce risks must be conducted in Isolation Space, preventing the spread of risks
- **Principle of Approval**: advanced space operations (such as plane traversal, spatial folding, etc.) must be approved by the anchor management institutions

## III. Realm Division and Characteristics of Each Stage

The Space Law Dispatch System is divided into five core realms:

| Realm | Name | Core Characteristics | Spatial Manipulation Range | Signature Ability |
|------|------|----------|--------------|------------|
| First Realm | Space Recognition | Preliminarily perceive the existence of space laws, able to identify the basic structure of space | Perception range: one hundred meters | Spatial Perception: can perceive the law state and anomalies of surrounding space |
| Second Realm | Space Control | Able to perform basic manipulation of space, such as short-distance spatial displacement | Manipulation range: ten meters | Short-Range Teleportation: can perform spatial displacement within ten meters |
| Third Realm | Space Transformation | Able to change local attributes of space, such as spatial folding, curvature adjustment | Manipulation range: one hundred meters | Spatial Folding: can fold a distance of one hundred meters into a single step |
| Fourth Realm | Space Law | Able to construct stable spatial structures, such as Isolation Space, storage space | Manipulation range: kilometers | Isolation Space Construction: can create stable independent spaces for cultivation or experiments |
| Fifth Realm | Space Origin | Touches the Origin laws of space, able to perform plane traversal and plane channel construction | Manipulation range: cross-plane | Plane Traversal: can perform stable cross-plane traversal with anchor support |

The breakthrough of each realm requires passing the "Space Law Assessment": assessing the cultivator's depth of understanding of space laws, the precision and stability of spatial manipulation, and compliance with space operation ethics; only when all three standards are met can the realm elevation be confirmed.

## IV. Core Cultivation Methods of the System

### 4.1 Law Perception Cultivation Methods

Law perception is the foundation of the Space Law Dispatch System, including:

- **Space Listening Method**: in a quiet space, close the eyes and concentrate, perceiving the subtle operation of space laws, like "listening" to the breathing of space
- **Anomaly Identification Method**: conduct perception training in areas containing spatial anomalies (such as spatial cracks, curvature anomalies), improving the ability to identify spatial anomalies
- **Multidimensional Perception Method**: gradually train to perceive spatial structures beyond three dimensions, laying the foundation for understanding high-dimensional space laws
- **Law Map Method**: draw the perceived spatial law structures into a "law map" in consciousness, improving overall cognition of spatial structures

### 4.2 Spatial Manipulation Cultivation Methods

Spatial manipulation is the core skill of the system, including:

- **Micro-Manipulation Training Method**: start training from the smallest range of spatial manipulation, such as folding one centimeter of space, gradually improving the precision and range of manipulation
- **Displacement Cultivation Method**: start from short-distance spatial displacement, gradually increasing the distance and complexity, ultimately achieving continuous teleportation
- **Folding Cultivation Method**: train the ability of spatial folding, from simple linear folding to complex multidimensional folding
- **Curvature Cultivation Method**: train the ability to adjust spatial curvature; curvature manipulation is the foundation of advanced space technology

### 4.3 Spatial Construction Cultivation Methods

Spatial construction is the advanced skill of the system, including:

- **Storage Space Construction Method**: learn to construct small stable storage spaces, mastering spatial boundary solidification technology
- **Isolation Space Construction Method**: learn to construct independent spaces with isolation effects, mastering spatial barriers and energy isolation technology
- **Cultivation Space Construction Method**: learn to construct special spaces suitable for cultivation, such as cultivation rooms with different time flow rates, cultivation fields with adjustable energy concentration, etc.
- **Plane Channel Construction Method**: with anchor support, learn to construct stable channels connecting two planes

## V. Applications of the System in the Symbiotic Civilization

The Space Law Dispatch System has extensive and important applications in the symbiotic civilization:

### 5.1 Infrastructure Construction

- **Anchor construction and maintenance**: advanced cultivators of the Space Law Dispatch System are the core force of anchor construction, responsible for the design and construction of anchor spatial structures
- **Plane channel maintenance**: maintain the spatial channels connecting various planes, ensuring the stability and safety of the channels
- **Public cultivation space construction**: construct special spatial facilities such as public cultivation rooms, law comprehension rooms, etc.

### 5.2 Archaeology and Research

- **Replication Mirror remote archaeology**: Space Law Dispatch cultivators participate in the spatial construction of Replication Mirror technology, providing isolated remote spaces for archaeology
- **Isolation Space experiments**: provide Isolation Space for dangerous law experiments and energy experiments, preventing the spread of risks
- **Ancient space research**: research the spatial structures and spatial technologies left by ancient civilizations

### 5.3 Livelihood Applications

- **Spatial storage technology**: provide spatial storage appliances to ordinary people, facilitating daily life
- **Spatial medicine**: use spatial technology for precise medical operations, such as non-invasive surgery, removal of internal lesions, etc.
- **Spatial transportation**: short-distance transportation systems based on spatial folding technology, greatly improving travel efficiency

### 5.4 Safety and Defense

- **Spatial barriers**: construct spatial barriers for the protection of important facilities
- **Spatial lockdown**: in emergencies, carry out spatial lockdown of specific areas to prevent the spread of danger
- **Spatial rescue**: use spatial displacement ability for emergency rescue, such as quickly transferring personnel from disaster sites

## VI. Advantages and Limitations of the System

### 6.1 Advantages

- Extremely high practical value; an indispensable core system for the infrastructure construction of the civilization
- Spatial manipulation ability has enormous advantages in combat, exploration, rescue, and other scenarios
- High social status for cultivators; rich employment and development opportunities
- Close integration with technological applications; cultivation results can be directly transformed into technological products
- After spatial perception improves, it also assists the cultivation of other systems

### 6.2 Limitations

- Certain requirements for spatial perception talent; not all races are suitable for practicing it
- Combat ability improves little in the early stage; main abilities lean toward assistance and technology
- Advanced spatial operations carry higher risks; operational errors may lead to serious consequences such as spatial cracks
- The breakthrough of high realms requires anchor support; difficult to practice in areas with insufficient anchor coverage
- The cultivation process is relatively tedious, requiring a large amount of precise manipulation training

## VII. Compatibility with Other Systems

The Space Law Dispatch System has good compatibility with other systems, especially:

- Concurrent practice with the Deduction and Iteration System: can deduce various application methods of space laws, improving the precision and innovativeness of spatial manipulation
- Concurrent practice with the Life Circulation System: can construct dedicated life energy circulation spaces, improving the efficiency of life circulation cultivation
- Concurrent practice with consciousness ascension systems: understanding space laws helps consciousness perceive and adapt to high-dimensional space
- Concurrent practice with application engineering systems: space law technology is an important branch of law engineering; concurrent practice can greatly improve engineering ability

## Conclusion

The Space Law Dispatch System is the "spatial cornerstone" of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. Based on profound understanding of space laws and with orderly dispatch as its method, it provides indispensable spatial technology support for the civilization's cross-plane interaction, infrastructure construction, scientific research, and livelihood applications. The ultimate goal of the Space Law Dispatch System is to make cultivators the "guardians and builders" of cosmic space — on the basis of understanding space laws, constructing a more orderly, more connected, and safer cosmic spatial network.
