## I. Background and Goals of the 20% Activation Strategy

The 20% activation strategy is a core strategy implemented by the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization in the early stage of its establishment. Its core content is: in the early stage of the symbiotic system, concentrate resources to activate 20% of all symbiotic lives, enabling them to take the lead in reaching the Plane Supreme Expert realm, thereby providing the entire symbiotic civilization with sufficient high-end combat power, technical support, and governance capability.

The proposal of this strategy is based on a profound judgment of the severe situation in the early stage of the symbiotic civilization's establishment:

1. **The remaining forces of predationism are still strong**: after the establishment of the symbiotic civilization, the residual forces of the predatory cultivation system were not completely eliminated; they still occupy large amounts of resources and territories across the cosmos and may counterattack at any time. The symbiotic civilization needs sufficient high-end combat power to defend the nascent symbiotic order
2. **Anchor construction requires high-end power**: the establishment, activation, and maintenance of anchors all require energy input and law manipulation capabilities at the level of Plane Supreme Experts. Without a sufficient number of high-end experts, the construction of the anchor network will be extremely difficult
3. **The Three Hundred Systems need verification and optimization**: although the Three Hundred Origin Cultivation Systems were extracted from laws, they still require a large number of high-end cultivators for verification, feedback, and optimization in practical application. The 20% activation ratio can provide a sufficient sample size
4. **The common progress of the Myriad Races needs leading examples**: the successful cases of high-end experts can provide confidence and demonstration for the bottom masses, inspiring the cultivation enthusiasm of all symbiotic lives, forming a virtuous cycle of "the rich first help the rich later"

The ultimate goal of the 20% activation strategy is not to make this 20% a new privileged class, but to pave a viable evolutionary path for all symbiotic lives through their pioneering breakthroughs, ultimately realizing the ultimate goal of "A Path for Everyone, Common Progress of the Myriad Races".

## II. Scientific Basis of the 20% Activation Ratio

Choosing 20% rather than a higher or lower ratio is the optimal solution obtained through rigorous law deduction and data analysis. The scientific nature of this ratio is reflected in the following aspects:

### 2.1 Network Effect Critical Point

According to the topological analysis of the symbiotic network, when the proportion of high-end nodes in a network reaches 20%, the overall connectivity and information transmission efficiency of the network undergo a qualitative leap — this is the so-called "network effect critical point". Below 20%, high-end nodes are too dispersed to form an effective collaborative network; above 20%, the marginal benefit begins to diminish, and the output ratio of additional invested resources declines significantly.

### 2.2 Resource Carrying Capacity Upper Limit

In the early stage of the symbiotic civilization's establishment, the total amount of public energy pools and cultivation resources is limited. According to calculations, on the premise of guaranteeing the basic guarantee field allocations of all symbiotic lives, the remaining resources can at most support 20% of lives in reaching the Plane Supreme Expert realm. If a higher activation ratio is forcibly pursued, the basic guarantee resources will have to be squeezed, violating the basic principle of "Everyone Has a Field".

### 2.3 Optimal Governance Structure Ratio

From the perspective of social governance, a 20% high-end expert ratio can form a stable governance structure — these 20% experts can form anchor management institutions, mentor teams, law enforcement teams, etc. at all levels, providing services and guarantees for the remaining 80% of symbiotic lives. If the high-end expert ratio is too low, governance capacity is insufficient; if too high, there will be governance redundancy of "too many officials, too few soldiers".

### 2.4 Cosmic Verification of the Pareto Principle

The Pareto principle (80/20 rule) in the real world has also been verified in the cultivation field of Heng Yuan Zhou: approximately 20% of cultivators can produce 80% of high-quality cultivation feedback and technological innovations. Concentrating resources on these 20% high-potential individuals can maximize resource utilization efficiency.

## III. Implementation Phases of the 20% Activation Strategy

The 20% activation strategy is not achieved overnight, but is advanced in three phases:

| Phase | Name | Time Span | Activation Target | Core Tasks |
|------|------|----------|----------|----------|
| Phase 1 | Seed Phase | First 10 standard cycles | Activate 1% core seeds | Select the most promising individuals, concentrate resources to cultivate the first batch of Plane Supreme Experts |
| Phase 2 | Expansion Phase | 10-30 standard cycles | Activate to 10% | The first batch of experts serve as mentors, expanding the activation scope through master-disciple inheritance and system optimization |
| Phase 3 | Popularization Phase | 30-50 standard cycles | Activate to 20% | The anchor network is basically completed, the symbiotic network runs comprehensively, and activation efficiency greatly improves |

### 3.1 Seed Phase (0-10 cycles)

The core task of the seed phase is "from 0 to 1" — with almost no high-end power, select the 1% most promising individuals and, through extraordinary concentrated resource investment, enable them to break through to the Plane Supreme Expert realm first.

The selection criteria of the seed phase are extremely strict, mainly examining the following indicators:

- Origin affinity: the individual's natural affinity with the cosmic Origin laws
- Will steadfastness: the individual's will quality when facing difficulties and temptations
- Symbiosis identification: the individual's identification with the symbiotic philosophy and willingness to contribute
- Learning ability: the individual's ability to absorb and apply new systems and new knowledge

The cultivation of the seed phase adopts the "one-on-one" model; each seed candidate is personally guided by the founding core members of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, and receives resource allocations far exceeding the norm.

### 3.2 Expansion Phase (10-30 cycles)

The core task of the expansion phase is "from 1 to 10" — the first batch of seed experts are in place and begin to serve as mentors, expanding the activation scope from 1% to 10% through master-disciple inheritance, system optimization, anchor construction, and other means.

Key measures of the expansion phase include:

1. **Mentor network establishment**: each seed expert is responsible for guiding 10-20 advanced cultivators, forming a large-scale master-disciple inheritance system
2. **Cultivation system optimization**: based on the cultivation feedback of the seed phase, conduct the first round of large-scale optimization of the Three Hundred Systems, improving the universality and efficiency of the systems
3. **Anchor network construction**: seed experts lead teams to establish anchors across the cosmos, gradually expanding the coverage of the symbiotic network
4. **Resource production capacity improvement**: through technological innovation and law engineering, improve the production capacity of the public energy pool, providing resource guarantees for larger-scale activation

### 3.3 Popularization Phase (30-50 cycles)

The core task of the popularization phase is "from 10 to 20" — the anchor network is basically completed, the symbiotic network runs comprehensively, activation efficiency greatly improves, and finally the 20% activation target is realized.

The characteristic of the popularization phase is "systematized activation" — no longer relying on the precept and example of individual mentors, but realizing large-scale, high-efficiency activation through complete cultivation systems, standardized training processes, and intelligent personalized guidance.

## IV. Strategic Transformation after 20% Activation

When the 20% activation target is achieved, the strategic focus will shift from "concentrated activation" to "comprehensive popularization". The core of this transformation is:

1. **Resource tilt adjustment**: gradually shift the resources originally concentrated on the high-end cultivation of the 20% to the popularization and improvement of the remaining 80%
2. **High-end power transformation**: the 20% Plane Supreme Experts transform from "those being cultivated" to "servers", undertaking core tasks such as anchor maintenance, mentor guidance, and technological R&D
3. **Dynamic adjustment of activation standards**: as the overall cultivation level improves, the standards for Plane Supreme Experts will also be dynamically raised, ensuring the gold content of high-end power
4. **Advancing toward the interstellar symbiosis stage**: the achievement of the 20% activation target marks that the symbiotic civilization possesses the capability of cross-plane exploration and interstellar symbiosis, and the four-stage dimensional ascension path enters a new stage

## Conclusion

The 20% activation strategy is an inevitable choice of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization at a specific historical stage. It is neither a revival of "elitism" nor a compromise of "egalitarianism", but the optimal strategic arrangement based on cosmic laws and actual conditions. Through the pioneering activation of 20%, the symbiotic civilization will gain sufficient power and experience, ultimately leading all symbiotic lives to realize the great goal of the common progress of the Myriad Races.
