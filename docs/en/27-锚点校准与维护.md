## I. Why Anchors Need Calibration and Maintenance

Anchors are the core infrastructure of the symbiotic network. Just like any infrastructure, anchors inevitably experience parameter drift, performance degradation, and even faults during long-term operation. The reasons include:

1. **Law fluctuation**: the laws of Heng Yuan Zhou are not absolutely static; they undergo subtle fluctuations with the operation of the cosmos, causing anchor parameters to drift
2. **Energy erosion**: long-term bearing of massive energy flow can cause subtle erosion and wear of the anchor structure
3. **External interference**: plane invasions, law anomalies, and other external factors can cause anchor damage
4. **Aging and degradation**: with the extension of operating time, the performance of the anchor's internal components gradually degrades

Therefore, anchors need regular calibration and maintenance to maintain their stable operation. The purpose of calibration is to keep anchor parameters within the optimal range; the purpose of maintenance is to discover and eliminate hidden faults in time, extending the service life of anchors.

## II. The Calibration System of Anchors

The calibration system of anchors consists of three parts: the Law Core, the Calibration Ring, and the calibration network:

| Component | Function | Operating Principle | Calibration Cycle |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| Law Core | Anchor's central computing and law control unit | Continuously monitor anchor operation parameters, issue calibration instructions | — |
| Calibration Ring | Anchor's self-calibration executive unit | According to Law Core instructions, output law energy to correct anchor parameters | Standard cycle ×1 |
| Calibration Network | Cross-anchor collaborative calibration system | Between adjacent anchors, mutual calibration to eliminate systematic errors | Standard cycle ×12 |

### 2.1 Law Core

The Law Core is the "brain" of the anchor, responsible for monitoring the anchor's operation status and issuing calibration instructions. It operates on the following principles:

1. **Real-time monitoring**: continuously collect the anchor's energy flow, law state, structural parameters and other data, establishing the anchor's dynamic model
2. **Deviation detection**: compare the real-time data with the standard model, detecting parameter deviations in time
3. **Instruction generation**: for deviations of different types and degrees, generate corresponding calibration instructions
4. **Self-learning**: accumulate calibration experience through long-term operation, continuously optimizing the calibration strategy

### 2.2 Calibration Ring

The Calibration Ring is the executive unit that specifically performs calibration, surrounding the anchor structure, responsible for converting calibration instructions into actual law energy output. It operates on the following principles:

1. **Targeted output**: according to the type of deviation, output law energy of corresponding properties to correct the corresponding parameters
2. **Graded calibration**: for minor deviations, use low-intensity continuous calibration; for severe deviations, use high-intensity concentrated calibration
3. **Energy conservation**: the calibration process itself also consumes energy; the Calibration Ring must ensure the energy efficiency of calibration while achieving the calibration goal
4. **Safe cutoff**: once abnormal conditions are found during calibration, immediately stop calibration and report to the Law Core

### 2.3 Calibration Network

Single-anchor calibration can only correct the anchor's own parameter drift, but cannot eliminate systematic errors. Therefore, the symbiotic network has established a cross-anchor collaborative calibration network: through mutual calibration between adjacent anchors, systematic errors are discovered and eliminated, ensuring that the entire anchor network operates with unified standards.

The calibration network mainly performs the following functions:

1. **Baseline alignment**: through cross-calibration between adjacent anchors, unify the law standards of the entire network
2. **Error tracing**: when discovering systematic error drift, trace the source of the error through the network topology
3. **Overall optimization**: globally optimize the parameter configuration of the entire anchor network

## III. The Maintenance System of Anchors

Maintenance is a systematic project covering the entire life cycle of anchors, including regular inspection, preventive maintenance, fault handling, and upgrade iteration:

| Maintenance Type | Content | Cycle | Executing Subject |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| Daily Inspection | Check the basic operation status of anchors, discover abnormal signs | Every standard cycle | Anchor management institution |
| Preventive Maintenance | Replace aging components, clean energy deposits, optimize structure | Every 10 standard cycles | Professional maintenance team |
| Annual Overhaul | Full-scale inspection, performance testing, deep calibration | Every 100 standard cycles | Anchor repair institution |
| Emergency Maintenance | Handle sudden faults, restore anchor function | On demand | Emergency repair team |

### 3.1 Daily Inspection

Daily inspection is the most basic maintenance work, mainly checking the anchor's energy flow status, law stability, structural integrity, and network connectivity. Inspection personnel (or automated inspection systems) compare the inspection results with the standard model to discover abnormal signs in time.

### 3.2 Preventive Maintenance

Preventive maintenance is carried out according to a fixed cycle, mainly including:

1. **Component replacement**: replace components that are approaching their service life, preventing faults
2. **Energy deposit cleaning**: clean up energy residues and impurities accumulated in the anchor structure
3. **Structural optimization**: adjust the anchor structure according to the operation data, improving its operating efficiency
4. **Auxiliary system inspection**: check the anchor's protection ring, energy pool, information core and other auxiliary systems

### 3.3 Annual Overhaul

Annual overhaul is the largest-scale maintenance work, carried out every 100 standard cycles. The content includes full-scale inspection, performance testing, and deep calibration. During the annual overhaul, the anchor usually needs to be temporarily shut down or switched to backup mode; the symbiotic network will arrange replacement energy supply in advance to ensure that the symbiotic services of the region are not interrupted.

### 3.4 Emergency Maintenance

When an anchor suddenly fails, the emergency repair team immediately activates the emergency plan. The emergency maintenance process includes: fault diagnosis, isolation measures, on-site repair, functional verification, and recovery to normal. During the repair period, the anchor network automatically switches the region's symbiotic services to backup anchors.

## IV. Fault Handling Mechanism of Anchors

Fault handling is an important part of anchor maintenance. According to the severity of the fault, the anchor fault handling mechanism is divided into three levels:

| Fault Level | Fault Type | Handling Measures | Recovery Time |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| Level 1 | Minor fault (such as parameter slight drift) | Automatic calibration, no manual intervention needed | Within 1 standard cycle |
| Level 2 | Moderate fault (such as component damage) | Remote repair or on-site maintenance, may require temporary suspension of partial functions | Within 10 standard cycles |
| Level 3 | Severe fault (such as Law Core damage) | Full shutdown, emergency replacement, large-scale backup dispatch | Within 100 standard cycles |

### 4.1 Fault Diagnosis

Fault diagnosis is the first step of fault handling. The symbiotic network performs fault diagnosis through the following means:

1. **Fault code analysis**: the anchor's own monitoring system generates fault codes for different abnormal states, enabling rapid preliminary positioning
2. **Data analysis**: analyze the anchor's historical operation data, looking for the cause of the fault
3. **Simulation deduction**: perform simulation deduction of the fault scene to verify the fault cause

### 4.2 Fault Isolation

Before repairing a fault, the faulty anchor must be isolated from the network to prevent the fault from spreading. Isolation measures include: cutting off the energy conduction paths of the anchor, switching the symbiotic services of the region to backup anchors, and restricting access to the faulty anchor.

### 4.3 Recovery and Verification

After the fault is repaired, the anchor must undergo strict functional verification before returning to the network. The verification content includes: energy flow test, law stability test, network connectivity test, and stress test. Only after all verification indicators pass can the anchor resume normal operation.

## V. The Talent System for Anchor Calibration and Maintenance

Anchor calibration and maintenance require specialized technical talents. The symbiotic civilization has established a complete talent cultivation system:

1. **Anchor Repair Specialist**: responsible for anchor structure repair and component replacement, requiring proficiency in anchor structure and materials
2. **Law Calibration Specialist**: responsible for anchor law parameter calibration, requiring deep understanding of laws and calibration technology
3. **Network Operation Specialist**: responsible for anchor network operation monitoring and dispatch, requiring mastery of network operation knowledge
4. **Fault Diagnosis Specialist**: responsible for fault diagnosis and handling, requiring rich experience and keen judgment

These specialists undergo systematic training in cultivation institutions, and must pass certification before they can take up their posts. At the same time, the symbiotic civilization encourages all lives to participate in anchor maintenance in a simple way — for example, every cultivator can act as an "information observer" of the anchor network, reporting discovered abnormalities to the management institution in time.

## Conclusion

Anchor calibration and maintenance are the guarantee of the long-term stable operation of the symbiotic network. Through the perfect calibration system, comprehensive maintenance system, sound fault handling mechanism, and professional talent system, the symbiotic network can maintain the stable operation of anchors for a long time, providing a solid foundation for the development of the symbiotic civilization. Anchors are the cornerstone of the symbiotic civilization; protecting anchors is protecting the foundation of the common progress of the Myriad Races.
