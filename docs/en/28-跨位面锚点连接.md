## I. Why Cross-Plane Connections Are Needed

Heng Yuan Zhou is not a single plane, but a multi-plane universe composed of countless planes. Each plane has its own complete operation system — its own laws, resources, energy systems, and life forms. These planes are independent of each other, yet interconnected.

The predatory system era's civilization confined itself to a single plane, carrying out endless internal plunder; while the strong could barely glimpse the existence of other planes, they could not truly connect with them. The Anchor Symbiotic Civilization fundamentally changed this situation: through anchor connections, different planes were linked into a unified symbiotic network, enabling energy dispatch, resource sharing, experience exchange, and cultural integration between planes.

The significance of cross-plane anchor connections mainly lies in the following aspects:

1. **Resource complementarity**: different planes have different resource distributions; some planes are rich in energy but poor in materials, others vice versa. Cross-plane connections enable the flow of resources between planes, realizing complementary advantages
2. **Risk diversification**: a single plane faces various cosmic risks (such as law anomalies, plane disasters); after cross-plane connections, the symbiotic network can dispatch resources from other planes to support the affected plane
3. **Civilization exchange**: different planes have different civilizations and cultures; cross-plane connections enable civilization exchange and integration, promoting the common progress of the Myriad Races
4. **Common development**: cross-plane connections enable the sharing of cultivation systems and technology, accelerating the development of backward planes

## II. The Technical Principles of Cross-Plane Anchor Connections

Cross-plane anchor connections are the most complex technology in the anchor network. Its core difficulty lies in: the boundaries between planes are not ordinary space, but a special law barrier — the "plane boundary". To connect anchors across planes, one must first break through or pierce this barrier.

The technical system of cross-plane anchor connections mainly includes:

| Technical Component | Function | Core Principle |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Plane Boundary Detection | Locate the exact position and structure of the plane boundary | Detect the law gradient of the plane boundary through law waves |
| Connection Node Establishment | Establish stable connection points on the plane boundary | Create stable "law windows" on the boundary using law resonance technology |
| Energy Channel Opening | Open cross-plane energy channels between connection nodes | Through the law window, open a stable energy passage between the two planes |
| Anchor Binding | Bind the two planes' anchors to the connection nodes | Bind the Plane Core Anchors of both planes to the connection nodes, forming a unified system |
| Stability Maintenance | Maintain the long-term stability of the connection | Regular calibration and maintenance to prevent connection degradation |

### 2.1 Plane Boundary Detection

The plane boundary is invisible to ordinary perception, but through law waves it can be detected. The detection technology emits specific law waves; when they encounter the plane boundary, they produce a unique reflection pattern; through analyzing the reflection pattern, the exact position, thickness, and structure of the plane boundary can be determined.

### 2.2 Connection Node Establishment

After the plane boundary is detected, the next step is to establish connection nodes on it. The connection node is like a "bridge pier" erected on the plane boundary — it is a stable law structure that provides a fixed support point for cross-plane connections.

The establishment of connection nodes uses law resonance technology: specific law frequencies are used to resonate with the plane boundary, creating stable "law windows". Each connection node can accommodate multiple law windows, providing multiple parallel channels for cross-plane connections.

### 2.3 Energy Channel Opening

After the connection nodes are established, the next step is to open energy channels. Energy channels are the actual paths for energy flow; through the law windows, stable energy passages are opened between the two planes.

The opening of energy channels requires the joint action of both planes' anchors. During the opening process, the law outputs of both sides must be precisely synchronized, otherwise the energy channel may collapse. Once the energy channel is opened, energy can flow freely between the two planes.

### 2.4 Anchor Binding

After the energy channels are opened, the Plane Core Anchors of both planes are bound to the connection nodes, forming a unified anchor system. After binding, the two planes' anchor networks become one interconnected whole; the Plane Core Anchors of both sides can mutually calibrate and coordinate dispatch.

### 2.5 Stability Maintenance

Cross-plane connections face greater stability challenges than intra-plane connections, because the plane boundary itself fluctuates. Therefore, connection stability maintenance is particularly important, mainly including:

1. **Regular calibration**: regularly calibrate the law parameters of the connection nodes to prevent drift
2. **Energy flow monitoring**: monitor the energy flow state of the channels in real time to prevent overload
3. **Boundary fluctuation compensation**: when the plane boundary fluctuates, automatically compensate to maintain connection stability

## III. The Management System of Cross-Plane Connections

Cross-plane connections are major projects involving the interests of both planes. Their management follows strict procedures and rules:

### 3.1 Connection Approval System

Cross-plane connections are not freely established but must undergo strict approval. The approval process includes:

1. **Application submission**: the plane proposing the connection submits an application, stating the purpose, plan, and expected benefits of the connection
2. **Feasibility assessment**: professional institutions assess the feasibility of the connection, including the stability of the plane boundary, resource conditions, and potential risks
3. **Impact assessment**: assess the impact of the connection on both planes, ensuring that the connection will not harm the ecological and social order of either plane
4. **Approval decision**: the Cosmic Origin Anchor reviews and decides on the application; major cross-plane connections also require democratic deliberation

### 3.2 Connection Operation Rules

1. **Energy dispatch rules**: energy dispatch between planes follows the principle of fairness and mutual benefit, and must not damage the interests of either side
2. **Information exchange rules**: information exchange between planes follows the principles of openness and transparency, with due attention to the protection of core secrets
3. **Cultural exchange rules**: cultural exchanges between planes respect each other's cultural traditions and must not forcibly interfere with each other's internal affairs
4. **Dispute resolution rules**: disputes between planes are resolved through the arbitration institutions of the symbiotic civilization, not through force

### 3.3 Connection Withdrawal Mechanism

When a plane no longer needs to maintain a cross-plane connection, it can apply for withdrawal. The withdrawal process includes: gradual reduction of dispatch scale, transfer of affected symbiotic services, unbinding of anchors, and closure of channels. The withdrawal mechanism ensures that the adjustment of cross-plane connections proceeds orderly and safely.

## IV. The Development Stages of Cross-Plane Connections

The cross-plane connections of the symbiotic network are not established all at once, but develop step by step. According to the current plan of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, the development of cross-plane connections is divided into the following stages:

| Stage | Name | Connection Scope | Core Tasks |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| Stage 1 | Local Connection Stage | Connect 2-3 planes adjacent to the original plane | Verify the technical feasibility of cross-plane connections, accumulate experience |
| Stage 2 | Regional Connection Stage | Connect all planes in the original star domain | Initially form a regional cross-plane network |
| Stage 3 | Cross-Domain Connection Stage | Connect star domains within the original plane | Form a complete cross-plane network of the original plane |
| Stage 4 | Inter-Plane Network Stage | Connect the networks of different planes | Form a cosmos-wide unified symbiotic network |

### 4.1 Local Connection Stage

The local connection stage is the pilot stage of cross-plane connections. The symbiotic civilization selects 2-3 planes adjacent to the original plane to establish the first batch of cross-plane connections. The purpose of this stage is to verify the technical feasibility of cross-plane connections and accumulate practical experience, providing a reference for later large-scale connections.

### 4.2 Regional Connection Stage

Based on the successful experience of the local connection stage, the symbiotic network expands the scope of connections, connecting all planes in the original star domain. At this stage, the regional cross-plane network initially takes shape, and cross-plane dispatch becomes routine.

### 4.3 Cross-Domain Connection Stage

After the regional connection network matures, the symbiotic network further expands the connection scope, connecting the star domains of the original plane. At this stage, the cross-plane connection network of the original plane is fully formed.

### 4.4 Inter-Plane Network Stage

The final stage of cross-plane connections: the connection networks of different planes are linked together, forming a cosmos-wide unified symbiotic network. At this stage, the entire Heng Yuan Zhou becomes a unified symbiosis whole; every plane, every star domain, every life is in the same symbiotic network.

## Conclusion

Cross-plane anchor connections are the great project through which the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization extends the symbiotic network from a single plane to the entire cosmos. Through technological breakthroughs, system construction, and staged development, cross-plane connections will eventually link all planes of Heng Yuan Zhou into a unified symbiotic whole, realizing the grand vision of the common progress of the Myriad Races across planes. Cross-plane connections are not only a technical feat but also the materialization of the symbiotic ideal — all lives in the cosmos are interconnected and prosper together.
