## I. Basic Concepts of Social Structure and the Pursuits of the Symbiotic Civilization

Social structure refers to the organizational patterns and relational models among different groups within a society, encompassing multiple dimensions such as class division, role allocation, power distribution, and resource configuration. In any form of civilization, the social structure profoundly affects individual development opportunities, the operational efficiency of society, and the long-term stability of civilization.

In the era of the Predatory Cultivation System, the social structure exhibited extreme rigidity and inequality: an insurmountable chasm stood between the strong and the weak, birth determined destiny, and the beings at the bottom had almost no possibility of upward mobility. This rigid social structure not only caused endless suffering but also led to the loss of social vitality and the stagnation of civilization — the potential of the vast majority of individuals was wasted, while a minority of vested interests, in order to preserve their privileges, did not hesitate to suppress innovation and progress.

After the establishment of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, building a social structure of "openness, mobility, fairness, and vitality" became one of its core objectives. The symbiotic civilization firmly believes that a healthy social structure should allow every individual to change their destiny through their own efforts, should channel social resources toward the most capable and most contributing individuals, and should maintain unimpeded channels of mobility between different classes. Only in this way can the enthusiasm and creativity of all symbiotic beings be fully stimulated and the great goal of the joint advancement of all races be realized.

The core pursuits of the symbiotic civilization's social structure can be summarized in four phrases:

1. **Openness and Inclusiveness**: Society is open to all individuals, without barriers to entry based on race, birth, gender, or other factors
2. **Unimpeded Mobility**: Unimpeded channels of mobility are maintained between different classes, and individuals can achieve upward mobility through effort
3. **Fairness and Justice**: Resource allocation and opportunity provision follow principles of fairness, and every individual has equal development opportunities
4. **Full Vitality**: The social structure can stimulate the enthusiasm and creativity of individuals and maintain abundant development vitality

## II. Class Division in the Symbiotic Civilization

The class division of the symbiotic civilization does not use birth, race, or wealth as criteria, but takes "Symbiosis Rating" and "Cultivation Realm" as core references, forming a dynamic and open class system:

| Class | Name | Proportion | Core Characteristics | Primary Responsibilities |
|------|------|------|----------|----------|
| First class | Symbiotic Leaders | approx. 1% | Supreme Powers of the Plane, S-grade Symbiosis Rating, core leaders of the civilization | Strategic decision-making, law maintenance, cross-plane governance |
| Second class | Symbiotic Backbone | approx. 5% | Advanced cultivators, A-grade Symbiosis Rating, backbone force in all fields | Technological research, organizational management, talent cultivation |
| Third class | Symbiotic Practitioners | approx. 20% | Intermediate cultivators, B-grade Symbiosis Rating, professionals in all industries | Professional services, technological application, grassroots management |
| Fourth class | Symbiotic Masses | approx. 60% | Primary cultivators, C-grade Symbiosis Rating, foundational force of the civilization | Basic cultivation, production services, community participation |
| Fifth class | Symbiotic Novices | approx. 14% | Those newly initiated or awaiting advancement, D-grade Symbiosis Rating, in need of help and guidance | Learning and growth, receiving guidance, gradual integration |

It must be especially emphasized that this class division is not fixed and immutable, but dynamic and mobile. Every individual can raise their Symbiosis Rating and Cultivation Realm through their own efforts, achieving upward class mobility; conversely, if an individual makes no progress or even violates symbiotic ethics, downward class mobility may also occur.

Furthermore, class division does not imply privilege or discrimination. Individuals in every class enjoy equal basic rights and personal dignity; class differences are mainly reflected in the division of responsibilities and resource allocation, not in the superiority or inferiority of persons.

## III. Mechanisms and Channels of Class Mobility

The symbiotic civilization has established multi-channel, multi-level class mobility mechanisms, ensuring that every individual has the opportunity to achieve upward mobility through their own efforts:

### 3.1 Cultivation Promotion Channel

Cultivation promotion is the most important channel of class mobility. Through the opening of the Three Hundred Sets of Origin Cultivation Systems, the symbiotic civilization provides every individual with a clear cultivation promotion path:

- **Realm Promotion**: Cultivators raise their realm through cultivation, from primary to intermediate, advanced, and supreme; each realm breakthrough may bring about upward class mobility
- **System Breakthrough**: Cultivators who achieve breakthrough results in a certain cultivation system can obtain an increase in Symbiosis Rating and upward class mobility
- **Technological Innovation**: Cultivators who achieve innovative results in cultivation technology, engineering technology, and other fields can receive honors and resource rewards, driving upward class mobility

The core principle of the cultivation promotion channel is "a path for everyone" — regardless of birth, as long as one cultivates diligently, there is an opportunity to achieve upward class mobility.

### 3.2 Contribution Promotion Channel

In addition to cultivation realm, contributions to the symbiotic civilization are also an important basis for class mobility:

- **Public Service Contributions**: Participating in public services such as anchor maintenance, mentor guidance, and emergency rescue, accumulating contribution points
- **Technological Research Contributions**: Making research contributions in fields such as cultivation technology and engineering technology
- **Cultural Dissemination Contributions**: Disseminating symbiotic concepts, creating cultural works, and promoting civilizational exchange
- **Governance Participation Contributions**: Participating in social governance, proposing policy recommendations, and promoting institutional optimization

The core principle of the contribution promotion channel is "more work, more reward" — the greater one's contribution to the symbiotic civilization, the greater the returns and class promotion one receives.

### 3.3 Education Promotion Channel

Education is an important ladder for class mobility. The symbiotic civilization has established a comprehensive education promotion system:

- **Basic Education**: Providing free basic cultivation education to every individual, ensuring fairness of starting points
- **Advanced Education**: Selecting outstanding students through assessment into advanced cultivation academies to receive higher-level training
- **Elite Education**: Selecting the most promising individuals into elite cultivation programs to receive top-tier resource support
- **Lifelong Education**: Providing continuous learning and improvement opportunities to individuals of all ages

The core principle of the education promotion channel is "equal opportunity" — every individual has an equal opportunity to achieve upward class mobility through education.

### 3.4 Special Promotion Channel

For individuals who make outstanding contributions or demonstrate special talents in special fields, the symbiotic civilization has established special promotion channels:

- **Exceptional Promotion for Special Talents**: Individuals with special talents or special contributions in a certain field may be promoted across grades
- **Meritorious Promotion in Emergencies**: Individuals who render meritorious service in responding to emergencies (such as disasters, invasions, crises, etc.) may receive rapid promotion
- **Promotion for Cross-Plane Exploration Contributions**: Individuals who make outstanding contributions in cross-plane exploration and archaeology may receive promotion

## IV. Institutional Guarantees for Preventing Class Rigidity

Unimpeded class mobility requires institutional guarantees. The symbiotic civilization has established the following institutions to prevent class rigidity:

### 4.1 Anti-Monopoly Institution

The Symbiotic Constitution and the Anti-Monopoly Law explicitly prohibit any organization or individual from monopolizing cultivation resources, cultivation systems, educational opportunities, and the like. The anti-monopoly institution prevents vested interest groups from consolidating their privileges through monopolizing resources, ensuring the openness and mobility of resources.

### 4.2 Inheritance Tax and Gift Tax Institution

In order to prevent wealth and resources from causing class rigidity through intergenerational transmission, the symbiotic civilization implements a progressive inheritance tax and gift tax system. High-value inheritances and gifts are required to pay a relatively high proportion of tax, which goes into the public resource pool for the common benefit of all symbiotic beings.

### 4.3 Education Fairness Institution

The symbiotic civilization ensures educational fairness through the Education Guarantee Law:

- Basic education is free and open to all citizens
- Selection for advanced and elite education is based on ability and potential as criteria, not on birth and wealth
- Additional educational support and compensation are provided to disadvantaged groups
- Any form of educational discrimination and educational privilege is prohibited

### 4.4 Class Mobility Monitoring Institution

The symbiotic network monitors in real time the mobility of social classes, with core monitoring indicators including:

- **Intergenerational Mobility Rate**: the degree of difference between the class of the offspring generation and that of the parental generation
- **Upward Mobility Rate**: the proportion of individuals achieving upward class mobility in each cycle
- **Downward Mobility Rate**: the proportion of individuals experiencing downward class mobility in each cycle
- **Class Rigidity Index**: the degree of "heredity" within each class

When monitoring indicators show that class mobility is impeded or a trend of rigidity appears, governance institutions promptly make policy adjustments to ensure unimpeded class mobility.

## V. Differences in Social Structure Among the Five Civilization Typologies

The five symbiotic civilization typologies each have their own characteristics in social structure:

| Typology | Social Structure Characteristics | Class Mobility Characteristics |
|------|--------------|--------------|
| East Asian Intensive Type | Collective-oriented, clear hierarchy, based on families and sects | Mobility channels mainly through sect promotion, medium mobility speed |
| Nordic Welfare Type | Flat, highly equal, weak classes | Small class gaps, low mobility pressure, slower mobility speed |
| North American Pluralistic Type | Market-oriented, pluralistic, intense competition | Mobility channels mainly through market competition, fast mobility but large gaps |
| Continental European Social Type | Corporatism, labor-management co-determination, deliberative democracy | Mobility channels mainly through professional qualifications and collective bargaining, stable mobility |
| Developing Catch-up Type | Strong government, fast mobility, gradient development | Fast mobility speed, many advancement opportunities, abundant social vitality |

Although the social structures of the various typologies differ, they all follow the basic principles of the Symbiotic Constitution and are all committed to building open, mobile, fair, and vibrant social structures.

## Conclusion

Social structure and class mobility are one of the core topics in the institutional construction of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. Through dynamic class division centered on Symbiosis Rating and Cultivation Realm, multi-channel class mobility mechanisms, and institutional guarantees against class rigidity, the symbiotic civilization has built an open, mobile, fair, and vibrant social structure. In such a social structure, every individual can change their destiny through their own efforts, social resources can flow to the most capable and most contributing individuals, and the entire civilization maintains abundant development vitality. This is precisely the important social foundation for the symbiotic civilization to achieve the joint advancement of all races.
