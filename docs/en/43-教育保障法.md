## I. Legislative Purpose and Basic Principles

The *Education Guarantee Law* is one of the five supporting laws of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. It aims to guarantee the right to education of all lives within the Hengyuan Universe, establish an education system with "symbiotic evolution" as its core concept, and provide knowledge and talent support for the joint advancement of all races.

This Law establishes the following basic principles:

| Principle | Connotation |
|------|------|
| Educational Equity | All lives, regardless of race, cultivation, or birth, enjoy equal educational opportunities |
| Teaching According to Aptitude | Respects the talent differences and evolutionary path choices of every life, providing personalized education plans |
| Unity of Knowledge and Action | Combines theoretical knowledge with practical cultivation; opposes empty talk education divorced from reality |
| Openness and Sharing | Educational resources are open to the whole society; the basic methods of the Three Hundred Sets of Origin Cultivation Systems are taught free of charge |
| Lifelong Learning | Education runs through the entire process of life, supporting continuous learning and capacity improvement at all stages |
| Cultural Pluralism | Respects the cultural traditions of all races, encourages pluralistic cultural exchange and mutual learning |

## II. Legal Guarantees of the Right to Education

This Law explicitly stipulates that the right to education is a basic right conferred upon every life by the Symbiotic Constitution, and that the state and civilization have the obligation to guarantee the realization of this right:

1. **Free Basic Education**: All lives have the right to receive free basic education, including general knowledge, basic cultivation methods, symbiotic civilization concepts, etc. The schooling period is six to twelve years, adjusted according to the developmental cycle of each race.
2. **Free Cultivation Initiation**: The basic methods of the Three Hundred Sets of Origin Cultivation Systems are open to all lives free of charge; no organization or individual may appropriate basic cultivation methods as private property and charge for teaching them.
3. **Equal Opportunities for Advancement**: Educational institutions at all levels shall not set discriminatory conditions based on race, cultivation, or birth in admissions; admission criteria are open and transparent.
4. **Special Education Guarantees**: Provide adapted special education resources and distance education support for groups such as disabled lives, non-human lives, and lives in remote planes.
5. **Education Assistance Institution**: Provide grants, scholarships, and interest-free loans to financially disadvantaged learners, ensuring that no one loses educational opportunities due to economic reasons.

## III. Architecture of the Education System

The education system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization is divided into four levels, organically linked between levels:

| Level | Name | Objective | Schooling Period |
|------|------|------|------|
| First level | Initiation Education | Cultivate basic cognitive abilities and symbiotic awareness; teach basic cultivation methods | 3-6 years |
| Second level | Basic Education | Systematic learning of general knowledge and cultivation theory; discover individual talent directions | 6-9 years |
| Third level | Professional Education | In-depth learning and practice in the selected cultivation system or professional field | 3-10 years |
| Fourth level | Advanced Research | Frontier exploration and innovative research for advanced cultivators and researchers | Unlimited |

In addition, a "Mentor Network Institution" is established: cultivators who reach a certain cultivation realm have the obligation to guide junior cultivators within their capacity; guidance contributions are counted into the contribution amount of the Joint-Output Contracting Mechanism and receive corresponding resource allocation.

## IV. Educational Institutions and Teachers

- **Public Educational Institutions**: Established by the state or the civilizational community, undertaking free basic education and public education services, with funding guaranteed by public accumulation.
- **Private Educational Institutions**: Established by social forces in accordance with the law; may charge reasonable fees, but must accept supervision by the competent education authorities and shall not engage in false publicity or predatory fee-charging.
- **Cultivation Sects**: Traditional cultivation organizations may, after registration, operate as professional educational institutions; they must comply with the education law and shall not close off and monopolize cultivation resources.
- **Teachers' Rights and Obligations**: Teachers enjoy rights such as academic freedom, reasonable remuneration, and vocational training, while bearing obligations such as educating students, setting an example, and protecting student privacy.

## V. Educational Content and Curriculum Standards

Educational content must meet the following requirements:

1. Include the core concepts of the symbiotic civilization and anti-predation education, cultivating learners' symbiotic awareness.
2. Include the basic theories and initiation methods of the Three Hundred Sets of Origin Cultivation Systems.
3. Include general scientific knowledge such as cosmic laws, plane knowledge, and life sciences.
4. Include the history and culture of all races, promoting mutual understanding and respect among races.
5. Include practical cultivation and social service components, cultivating the ability to unite knowledge and action.
6. Prohibit the dissemination of predatory cultivation concepts and discriminatory ideas.

## VI. Legal Liability

Acts violating the provisions of this Law shall bear the following liabilities depending on the severity of the circumstances:

- Those who refuse or obstruct school-age lives from receiving compulsory education shall be ordered to rectify and penalized.
- Educational institutions that charge fees in violation of regulations or make false publicity shall be ordered to refund the fees and fined; in serious circumstances, their school-running licenses shall be revoked.
- Teachers who physically punish or discriminate against students shall be disciplined; in serious circumstances, their teaching qualifications shall be revoked and legal liability pursued.
- Those who appropriate public cultivation resources as private property or monopolize fee-charging shall have their illegal gains confiscated and be fined.

## Conclusion

The *Education Guarantee Law* is the educational practice of the "a path for everyone" philosophy of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. By guaranteeing the right to education, building a tiered education system, and opening cultivation resources, it ensures that every life can obtain an evolutionary path suited to itself, continuously supplying knowledge and talent for the joint advancement of all races.
