## I. Legislative Purpose and Basic Principles

The *Medical Security Law* is one of the five supporting laws of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. It aims to guarantee the right to health and medical care of all lives within the Hengyuan Universe, establish a symbiotic medical system covering all races, and ensure that every life can receive timely and effective treatment when injured or ill.

This Law establishes the following basic principles:

| Principle | Connotation |
|------|------|
| Health Priority | Life and health are the foundation of all evolution; the state and civilization place the protection of life and health in a priority position |
| Universal Coverage | Medical security covers all lives, regardless of race, cultivation, birth, or economic status |
| Hierarchical Diagnosis and Treatment | Establish a three-tier diagnosis and treatment system of grassroots medicine, regional medicine, and advanced medicine, rationally allocating medical resources |
| Combination of Prevention and Treatment | Prevention first, combining prevention and treatment; emphasize the prevention and rehabilitation of cultivation injuries |
| Non-Human Adaptation | Provide adapted medical plans for the physiological characteristics of different races; prohibit one-size-fits-all application of human standards |
| Public Welfare Attribute | Basic medical services have a public welfare attribute; the right to treatment of lives shall not be deprived for profit-making purposes |

## II. Legal Guarantees of the Right to Life and Health

This Law explicitly stipulates that the right to life and health is a basic right conferred upon every life by the Symbiotic Constitution, specifically including:

1. **Right to Basic Medical Care**: Every life has the right to receive free basic medical services, including diagnosis and treatment of common diseases, basic medicines, routine physical examinations, etc.
2. **Right to Emergency Treatment**: Every life has the right to receive emergency treatment in case of sudden injury or illness; medical institutions shall not refuse emergency treatment for any reason.
3. **Right to Treatment of Cultivation Injuries**: Cultivators who suffer energy disorders, meridian damage, deviation, and other injuries during the cultivation process have the right to receive professional treatment.
4. **Right to Informed Consent**: Patients have the right to understand their own condition, treatment plan, risks, and prognosis, and have the right to choose or refuse treatment plans.
5. **Right to Privacy Protection**: Patients' medical information and body data are protected by law; without patient consent, they shall not be disclosed or used for other purposes.
6. **Right to Rehabilitation Guarantee**: Injured and ill patients have the right to receive rehabilitation treatment and assistive devices, helping them recover their labor and living abilities.

## III. Architecture of the Symbiotic Medical System

The medical system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization is divided into three levels, with referral and collaboration mechanisms established between levels:

| Level | Name | Functions | Coverage |
|------|------|------|----------|
| First level | Grassroots Medical Station | Diagnosis and treatment of common diseases, preventive health care, basic physical examinations, emergency transport | Communities/villages/small planes |
| Second level | Regional Medical Center | Specialist diagnosis and treatment, surgical treatment, preliminary handling of cultivation injuries, rehabilitation | Cities/regions/medium planes |
| Third level | Advanced Medical General Hospital | Difficult and severe cases, advanced cultivation injuries, consciousness domain diseases, cross-race difficult diseases | Civilizational core/large planes |

In addition, a "Cultivation Injury Specialty" is established: specifically handling all types of injuries during cultivation, including energy overload, meridian rupture, heart-demon invasion, soul trauma, etc., equipped with medical personnel proficient in cultivation laws.

## IV. Medical Security Fund

The Medical Security Fund is the economic foundation for the operation of the symbiotic medical system, with sources including:

- Public accumulation appropriations (main source)
- Medical security withholdings in the Joint-Output Contracting Mechanism
- Social donations
- Fund investment returns

The fund's payment scope includes: basic medical service costs, emergency treatment costs, cultivation injury treatment costs, basic medicine costs, rehabilitation costs, etc. Patients only need to pay a small amount of out-of-pocket expenses, with reductions and exemptions for those in financial difficulty.

## V. Medical Institutions and Medical Personnel

- **Public Medical Institutions**: Established by the state or the civilizational community, undertaking basic medical services and public health functions, with funding guaranteed by the Medical Security Fund.
- **Private Medical Institutions**: Established by social forces in accordance with the law; may provide high-end medical services, but must accept supervision by the competent medical authorities and shall not refuse emergency patients.
- **Non-Human Medical Institutions**: Specialist medical institutions established for the physiological needs of special races, equipped with non-human medical personnel.
- **Rights and Obligations of Medical Personnel**: Medical personnel enjoy rights such as practice protection, reasonable remuneration, and vocational training, while bearing obligations such as saving the dying and healing the injured, respecting patients, and maintaining medical confidentiality.

## VI. Public Health and Prevention

The state establishes a public health system, performing the following functions:

1. Monitoring and prevention of infectious diseases, establishing a cross-plane epidemic early warning mechanism.
2. Nationwide health examinations and health record management.
3. Cultivation safety publicity and guidance on the prevention of cultivation injuries.
4. Environmental health and food and drug safety supervision.
5. Emergency response to public health emergencies.

## VII. Legal Liability

Acts violating the provisions of this Law shall bear the following liabilities depending on the severity of the circumstances:

- Medical institutions that refuse emergency patients shall be ordered to rectify and fined; if serious consequences result, their practice licenses shall be revoked.
- Medical personnel whose gross negligence causes harm to patients shall bear compensation liability; in serious circumstances, their practice qualifications shall be revoked.
- Medical institutions that charge fees in violation of regulations or provide excessive medical treatment shall be ordered to refund the fees and fined.
- Those who disclose patients' medical privacy shall bear compensation for mental damage; in serious circumstances, criminal liability shall be pursued.
- Those who produce and sell counterfeit or inferior medicines shall have their illegal gains confiscated and be fined; if constituting a crime, criminal liability shall be pursued.

## Conclusion

The *Medical Security Law* is the embodiment of the "a path for everyone" philosophy of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization in the health domain. By establishing a universally covered medical security system, setting up a cultivation injury specialty, and guaranteeing the right to emergency treatment, it ensures that every life can receive timely treatment when injured or ill, laying a solid health foundation for the joint advancement of all races.
