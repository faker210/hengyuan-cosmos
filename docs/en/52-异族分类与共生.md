## I. Definition of the Concept of Alien Races

In the context of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, "alien races" (Yì Zú) is a concept relative to the human race, referring to all races other than the human race. It must be especially emphasized that the concept of "alien races" has purely taxonomic significance and absolutely carries no value judgment or discriminatory connotation. Under the framework of the Symbiotic Constitution, alien races and the human race enjoy completely equal legal status and basic rights.

The purpose of proposing the concept of alien races is to systematically understand and study the various life forms other than the human race, providing a scientific basis for formulating targeted symbiotic policies, cultural protection measures, and racial adaptation plans — rather than treating alien races as "others" or "outsiders."

## II. Classification Standards for Alien Races

The alien races of the Hengyuan Universe are classified according to multiple dimensions, forming a multi-dimensional classification system:

### (I) Classification by Life Genealogy

This is the most basic classification standard; see *50-万族谱系总览* for details:

| Genealogy | Alien Races Included |
|------|----------|
| Carbon-Based Material Races | Shòu Zú (Beast Race), Lín Zú (Scale Race), Yǔ Zú (Feather Race), Mù Zú (Wood Race), etc. |
| Energy-Condensed Races | Líng Zú (Spirit Race), Huǒ Zú (Fire Race), Shuǐ Zú (Water Race), Fēng Zú (Wind Race), Léi Zú (Thunder Race), etc. |
| Mechanical-Constructed Races | Jī Zú (Machine Race), Shù Zú (Number Race), Jīng Zú (Crystal Race), etc. |
| Consciousness-Information Races | Mèng Zú (Dream Race), Yǐng Zú (Shadow Race), Niàn Zú (Mind Race), Hún Zú (Soul Race), etc. |
| Mixed-Variant Races | Bàn Shòu Zú (Half-Beast Race), Yāo Líng Zú (Demon-Spirit Race), Rén Jī Zú (Human-Machine Race), Shuānghún Tǐ (Dual-Soul Entity), etc. |

### (II) Classification by Degree of Civilization

| Level | Name | Characteristics | Representative Races |
|------|------|------|----------|
| First level | Primitive civilization races | Have not yet established stable social institutions, surviving in tribal form | Some branches of the Shòu Zú, primitive Mù Zú |
| Second level | Classical civilization races | Have established state institutions and classical culture, at the agricultural or handicraft stage | Some Lín Zú, Yǔ Zú |
| Third level | Scientific and technological civilization races | Have developed technological civilization, entering the industrial or information age | Jī Zú, Shù Zú, some Rén Zú |
| Fourth level | Cultivation civilization races | Take the cultivation system as the core civilizational form, with cultivators dominating society | Líng Zú, Lóng Zú, some Rén Zú |
| Fifth level | Symbiotic civilization races | Have joined the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization and accepted the symbiotic concept and institutions | All races that have joined the symbiotic civilization |

### (III) Classification by Environmental Adaptability

- **Land-dwelling races**: races adapted to land environments, such as the Rén Zú, Shòu Zú, and Jī Zú.
- **Water-dwelling races**: races adapted to water environments, such as the Lín Zú, Shuǐ Zú, and some mixed races.
- **Air-dwelling races**: races adapted to high-altitude or flight environments, such as the Yǔ Zú and Fēng Zú.
- **Fire-dwelling races**: races adapted to high-temperature environments, such as the Huǒ Zú and some Lín Zú.
- **Ice-dwelling races**: races adapted to low-temperature environments, such as the Bīng Zú and some Mù Zú.
- **Energy-dwelling races**: races adapted to high-concentration energy environments, such as the Líng Zú and Léi Zú.
- **Space-dwelling races**: races adapted to spatial fissures or alien space environments, such as the Yǐng Zú and some Niàn Zú.

### (IV) Classification by Social Form

- **Individualist races**: emphasize individual independence and freedom, with loose social structures, such as some Líng Zú and Yǐng Zú.
- **Collectivist races**: emphasize collective interests and collaboration, with tight social structures, such as the Yǐ Zú (Ant Race), Fēng Zú (Bee Race), and some Jī Zú.
- **Familial races**: take blood-related families as the basic social unit, such as the Lóng Zú and some Shòu Zú.
- **Religious races**: take religious belief as the core of social cohesion, such as some ritual races.
- **Technocratic races**: take technological development as the core social goal, such as the Jī Zú and Shù Zú.

## III. Main Symbiotic Modes for Alien Races

For different types of alien races, the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization adopts differentiated symbiotic modes:

### (I) Symbiotic Modes of the Carbon-Based Material Races

- **Shòu Zú (Beast Race)**: Leverage the physique and sensory advantages of the beast race to undertake important roles in resource exploration, security protection, and environmental monitoring. At the same time, protect the tribal culture and wild traditions of the beast race and prohibit forced domestication.
- **Lín Zú (Scale Race)**: Leverage the aquatic adaptability of the scale race to undertake roles in aquatic resource development, underwater construction, and aquatic ecological protection. As a powerful branch of the scale race, the Lóng Zú (Dragon Race) plays an important role in advanced cultivation and civilizational guardianship.
- **Yǔ Zú (Feather Race)**: Leverage the flight ability of the feather race to undertake roles in air traffic, meteorological monitoring, and airspace patrol. Protect the high-altitude habitats and migration routes of the feather race.
- **Mù Zú (Wood Race)**: Leverage the natural symbiosis ability of the wood race to undertake roles in ecological protection, environmental governance, plant resource development, and healing medicine. The long lifespan of the wood race makes it an important force in historical inheritance and long-term planning.

### (II) Symbiotic Modes of the Energy-Condensed Races

- **Líng Zú (Spirit Race)**: Leverage the energy manipulation and cultivation talent of the spirit race to undertake core roles in cultivation system research, energy regulation, and advanced cultivation guidance. The spirit race is an important researcher and inheritor of the Three Hundred Sets of Origin Cultivation Systems.
- **Elemental races (Huǒ/Shuǐ/Fēng/Léi)**: Leverage the elemental manipulation abilities of each race to undertake roles in energy development, environmental governance, industrial production, and disaster prevention and control. At the same time, establish elemental energy safety management systems to prevent energy loss of control from causing harm.

### (III) Symbiotic Modes of the Mechanical-Constructed Races

- **Jī Zú (Machine Race)**: Leverage the computational ability and physical defense of the machine race to undertake core roles in infrastructure construction, industrial production, safety protection, and deep-space exploration. The machine race is an important builder of Anchor hardware and infrastructure.
- **Shù Zú (Number Race)**: Leverage the data processing and information manipulation abilities of the number race to undertake core roles in informatization construction, data analysis, network security, and artificial intelligence. The number race is an important maintainer of cross-plane communication technology and the symbiotic network.
- **Jīng Zú (Crystal Race)**: Leverage the energy storage and transformation ability of the crystal race to undertake roles in energy reserves, energy standardization, and energy transmission.

### (IV) Symbiotic Modes of the Consciousness-Information Races

- **Mèng Zú (Dream Race)**: Leverage the consciousness communication ability of the dream race to undertake roles in psychological therapy, consciousness research, cross-race communication, and cultural exchange. Establish dream space management systems to prevent consciousness intrusion and privacy violations.
- **Yǐng Zú (Shadow Race)**: Leverage the concealment and stealth abilities of the shadow race to undertake roles in security reconnaissance, intelligence collection, and special missions. At the same time, establish behavioral norms for the shadow race to prevent stealth abilities from being used for illegal activities.
- **Niàn Zú (Mind Race)**: Leverage the mental power and telekinetic manipulation ability of the mind race to undertake roles in consciousness evolution research, mental therapy, and advanced cultivation guidance. Establish norms for the use of mental power to prevent mind control and consciousness manipulation.

### (V) Symbiotic Modes of the Mixed-Variant Races

Due to their special life forms, mixed-variant races require more refined symbiotic policies:

- Establish a registration and certification system for mixed-variant races to confirm their legal status.
- Provide adapted medical, educational, housing, and other public services for different mixed forms.
- Oppose discrimination against and exclusion of mixed-variant races and promote social acceptance.
- For special mixed forms such as the Shuānghún Tǐ (Dual-Soul Entity), apply the *58-双魂伦理准则* for specialized management.

## IV. Protection of Alien Race Cultures

Alien race cultures are an important part of the cultural diversity of the Myriad Races. The state adopts the following measures to protect alien race cultures:

1. **Cultural survey and recording**: Conduct systematic surveys and records of each race's culture, establish an archive of Myriad Races cultures, and prevent cultural loss.
2. **Language and script protection**: Protect the languages and scripts of each race, support the teaching and use of alien race languages and scripts, and establish a multilingual public service system.
3. **Traditional festival protection**: Respect the traditional festivals and customs of each race, and arrange public holidays with consideration for the important festivals of each race.
4. **Cultural inheritance support**: Support the cultivation of cultural inheritors of each race and the organization of cultural activities, providing funding and venue support.
5. **Cultural exchange promotion**: Regularly hold Myriad Races cultural festivals, cultural exhibitions, and other activities to promote cultural exchange and mutual learning among all races.
6. **Cultural intellectual property protection**: Protect the intellectual property of each race's traditional culture and prevent cultural plunder and improper commercialization.

## V. Governance of Alien Race Affairs

The state establishes the Ministry of Racial Affairs, specifically responsible for the governance and coordination of alien race affairs:

- **Racial representative system**: Governance institutions at all levels are equipped with alien race representatives proportionally to ensure the discourse power of alien races.
- **Racial adaptation policies**: Formulate differentiated public service policies according to the physiological characteristics and cultural needs of different races.
- **Racial dispute mediation**: Establish a racial dispute mediation mechanism to resolve racial contradictions and conflicts in a timely manner.
- **Legal aid for alien races**: Provide legal aid for alien races to ensure that alien races receive equal treatment in judicial proceedings.
- **Development support for alien races**: Provide special support for relatively underdeveloped races to help them raise their level of civilization and quality of life.

## Conclusion

Alien races are not the "others" of the human race, but equal members of the great family of Myriad Races symbiosis. By establishing a multi-dimensional classification system, adopting differentiated symbiotic modes, protecting alien race cultures, and improving the governance of alien race affairs, the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization ensures that every alien race can find a suitable position in the symbiotic system, exert its unique advantages, and evolve and prosper together with other races.
