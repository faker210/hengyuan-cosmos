## I. Overall Principles of the Disposition Process

The disposition of anomalous life is an important part of life governance in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. According to the risk rating results of *53-奇特生命识别标准*, the disposition of anomalous life is divided into three types of standardized processes: friendly symbiosis type, observation and isolation type, and dissolution and transformation type. The three types of processes correspond respectively to the low-risk, medium-risk, and high-risk levels, forming a complete disposition system from acceptance to control and then to elimination.

The disposition process follows the following overall principles:

| Principle | Connotation |
|------|------|
| Principle of proportionality | The intensity of disposition measures should correspond to the risk rating of the anomalous life; excessive disposition is prohibited |
| Principle of due process | Disposition must be carried out according to legal procedures, safeguarding the basic rights of the anomalous life and the right to know of relevant parties |
| Principle of minimal harm | On the premise of achieving the disposition goal, minimize harm to the anomalous life and the surrounding environment as much as possible |
| Principle of reversibility | Prioritize reversible disposition measures, leaving room for subsequent re-evaluation and adjustment |
| Principle of full recording | The entire disposition process is recorded, forming a complete disposition archive for review and traceability |

## II. Type One: Friendly Symbiosis Disposition Process

Friendly symbiosis disposition applies to anomalous life identified and assessed as First Level (low risk), i.e., life forms with relatively high consciousness levels, no aggressiveness, controllable environmental impact, and high symbiotic potential.

### (I) Disposition Goal

Formally incorporate the anomalous life into the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization system, grant it legal status and basic rights equal to other life, help it integrate into the symbiotic society, and achieve joint evolution.

### (II) Disposition Steps

| Step | Name | Specific Content | Responsible Institution |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| Step 1 | Communication establishment | Establish an effective communication channel with the anomalous life to understand its origin, needs, and wishes | Life Administration Bureau + Communication Expert Group |
| Step 2 | Concept transmission | Introduce the core concepts, legal system, and social norms of the symbiotic civilization to the anomalous life | Ministry of Racial Affairs + Educational Institutions |
| Step 3 | Identity registration | Handle identity registration for the anomalous life, issue a symbiotic citizen identity certificate, and confirm its legal status | Life Administration Bureau + Household Registration Administration |
| Step 4 | Rights conferral | Formally confer upon the anomalous life all basic rights stipulated by the Symbiotic Constitution, including the right to choose evolution, the right to access resources, etc. | Symbiotic Executive Committee |
| Step 5 | Integration assistance | Provide the anomalous life with language training, cultural adaptation, cultivation guidance, and other integration assistance services | Educational Institutions + Mentor Network |
| Step 6 | Settlement and residence | Provide adapted living space and livelihood guarantees according to the physiological characteristics and living needs of the anomalous life | Housing Administration + Ministry of Racial Affairs |
| Step 7 | Follow-up visits | Include in routine social management, conduct regular follow-up visits to understand its integration situation, and promptly resolve problems encountered | Grassroots Communities + Life Administration Bureau |

### (III) Special Arrangements

- For anomalous life with special abilities or knowledge, it may be invited to participate in research and construction in relevant fields, leveraging its unique value.
- For anomalous life from other planes or universes, assistance may be provided to establish contact with its original habitat, or to help it return to its original habitat (if it so wishes).
- For anomalous life appearing in groups, dedicated symbiotic communities may be established to help them integrate collectively.

### (IV) Exit Mechanism

If, during the integration process, it is discovered that the actual risk of the anomalous life is higher than the initial assessment, or its behavior threatens the symbiotic order, a re-evaluation process may be initiated, and the disposition type adjusted according to the re-evaluation results.

## III. Type Two: Observation and Isolation Disposition Process

Observation and isolation disposition applies to anomalous life identified and assessed as Second Level (medium risk), i.e., life forms with average consciousness levels, some aggressiveness or environmental impact, requiring guidance and monitoring.

### (I) Disposition Goal

Under the premise of ensuring safety, conduct continuous observation and guidance of the anomalous life, gradually reduce its risk rating, and ultimately achieve friendly symbiosis; for those whose risk cannot be reduced, maintain long-term isolation and control.

### (II) Disposition Steps

| Step | Name | Specific Content | Responsible Institution |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| Step 1 | Isolation and placement | Transfer the anomalous life to a dedicated isolation and observation facility, equipped with energy shielding, spatial stabilization, safety protection, and other devices | Life Administration Bureau + On-Site Disposition Team |
| Step 2 | Continuous observation | Conduct 24-hour continuous observation of the anomalous life, recording data such as its behavioral patterns, energy changes, and consciousness activity | Identification Expert Group + Monitoring Team |
| Step 3 | Communication attempts | On the basis of observation, attempt to establish communication with the anomalous life to understand its behavioral motives and needs | Communication Expert Group |
| Step 4 | Behavioral guidance | Through a combination of positive incentives and negative constraints, guide the anomalous life to form behavioral patterns conforming to symbiotic norms | Behavioral Experts + Cultivators |
| Step 5 | Risk re-assessment | Conduct a risk re-assessment every three months, adjusting the risk rating according to observation and guidance results | Identification Expert Group |
| Step 6 | Graded opening | According to the degree of risk reduction, gradually expand the anomalous life's activity range and contact permissions, from complete isolation to limited opening | Life Administration Bureau |
| Step 7 | Type conversion | If the risk rating drops to First Level, convert to friendly symbiosis disposition; if it rises to Third Level, convert to dissolution and transformation disposition | Life Administration Bureau + Anomalous Life Tribunal |

### (III) Isolation Facility Standards

- **Spatial standards**: The size of the isolation space is determined according to the body size and activity needs of the anomalous life, with a minimum not less than ten times its body size.
- **Energy standards**: Equip with energy shielding and energy regulation devices to prevent the anomalous life's energy from leaking or being disturbed by external energy.
- **Safety standards**: Equip with multiple safety protection devices, including physical barriers, energy barriers, spatial locking, etc., to ensure the anomalous life cannot break through the isolation.
- **Monitoring standards**: Equip with comprehensive monitoring equipment to monitor the anomalous life's vital signs, energy fluctuations, and behavioral activities in real time.
- **Environmental standards**: Provide suitable environmental conditions according to the physiological needs of the anomalous life, including temperature, humidity, energy concentration, etc.

### (IV) Long-Term Isolation Management

For anomalous life that cannot reduce its risk rating even after long-term observation and guidance, long-term isolation management is implemented:

- Anomalous life under long-term isolation enjoys basic life security rights, including a suitable living environment, necessary energy supply, medical treatment, etc.
- Long-term isolation facilities should be regularly inspected and maintained to ensure the safety and suitability of the isolation.
- A comprehensive assessment of long-term isolated anomalous life is conducted annually to confirm whether there is a possibility of adjusting the disposition type.
- If long-term isolated anomalous life expresses a wish for voluntary dissolution or transformation, its wish should be respected and corresponding assistance provided.

## IV. Type Three: Dissolution and Transformation Disposition Process

Dissolution and transformation disposition applies to anomalous life identified and assessed as Third Level (high risk), i.e., life forms with low or malicious consciousness levels, strong aggressiveness, severe environmental damage, and low symbiotic potential. Anomalous life at the Fourth Level (extremely high risk) is subject to the emergency dissolution procedure.

### (I) Disposition Goal

Through dissolution or transformation, eliminate the threat of anomalous life to the symbiotic order, while converting its energy and material into beneficial resources as much as possible, achieving "turning harm into benefit."

### (II) Disposition Methods

Dissolution and transformation disposition is divided into two methods, selected according to the specific circumstances of the anomalous life:

| Method | Name | Applicable Situation | Specific Method |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| Method 1 | Energy dissolution | Anomalous life that cannot be transformed or for which transformation risk is too high | Use dedicated dissolution devices or spells to decompose the anomalous life's energy and material into fundamental particles, returning them to the Origin of the universe |
| Method 2 | Benign transformation | Anomalous life that can be transformed and for which post-transformation risk is controllable | Through energy reconstruction, consciousness purification, form reshaping, and other means, transform the anomalous life into harmless or beneficial life forms or energy resources |

### (III) Disposition Steps

| Step | Name | Specific Content | Responsible Institution |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| Step 1 | Disposition approval | The dissolution and transformation of high-risk anomalous life must undergo judicial review and approval by the Anomalous Life Tribunal; extremely high risk may be disposed first and approved retroactively | Anomalous Life Tribunal |
| Step 2 | Plan formulation | Formulate a detailed dissolution and transformation plan, including disposition methods, technical means, safety measures, emergency plans, etc. | Life Administration Bureau + Technical Expert Group |
| Step 3 | Safety isolation | Transfer the anomalous life to a dedicated dissolution and transformation facility, equipped with the highest level of safety protection and energy containment devices | On-Site Disposition Team |
| Step 4 | Dissolution and transformation | Implement the dissolution and transformation according to the plan, with the entire process monitored; upon any anomaly, immediately activate the emergency plan | Technical Expert Group + Cultivators |
| Step 5 | Product handling | Test and handle the energy and material produced by the dissolution and transformation; usable ones are incorporated into resource reserves, harmful ones undergo safe disposal | Resource Administration |
| Step 6 | Environmental restoration | Restore the environment affected during the disposition process, restoring ecological and energy balance | Ministry of Ecology and Environment |
| Step 7 | Archive filing | Organize and archive the records, data, and reports of the entire disposition process, forming a complete disposition archive | Life Administration Bureau |

### (IV) Emergency Dissolution Procedure

For Fourth Level (extremely high risk) anomalous life, i.e., anomalous life with cosmic-level destructive power that cannot communicate, is uncontrollable, and directly threatens civilizational survival, the emergency dissolution procedure applies:

1. The highest-ranking person on site may directly order the initiation of emergency dissolution without prior approval, but must file a report with the Anomalous Life Tribunal for the record within 24 hours after the disposition.
2. Emergency dissolution may use the civilization's highest-level force and energy resources, including the joint action of Plane Supremes.
3. The primary goal of emergency dissolution is to eliminate the threat and protect the safety of life and civilization, not to pursue "turning harm into benefit."
4. After emergency dissolution, a comprehensive environmental assessment and restoration should be conducted to prevent secondary disasters.

### (V) Ethical Constraints

Dissolution and transformation disposition involves the termination of life and must be subject to strict ethical constraints:

- Dissolution and transformation must be the last resort, adopted only when neither friendly symbiosis nor observation and isolation is feasible.
- For anomalous life with consciousness and emotions, dissolution should adopt a painless and rapid method as much as possible to reduce its suffering.
- It is prohibited to engage in plunder in the name of dissolution and transformation; the energy and material of anomalous life must not be appropriated as private property.
- Decisions on dissolution and transformation must undergo strict judicial review to prevent abuse of power.
- Conduct an annual summary analysis of dissolution and transformation cases, evaluate the necessity and rationality of the dispositions, and continuously improve the identification and disposition standards.

## Conclusion

The three-type disposition process for anomalous life is the standardized operational system of life governance in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. From the open acceptance of friendly symbiosis, to the cautious control of observation and isolation, to the last resort of dissolution and transformation, the three types of processes form a complete risk response chain. They embody the symbiotic civilization's respect for and inclusion of life while upholding the bottom line of protecting civilizational security, achieving the governance balance of "open without losing ground, inclusive without indulgence."
