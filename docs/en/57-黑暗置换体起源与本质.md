## I. Definition of Dark Replacement Entities

In the Wanling Hengmao Origin Universe (Hengyuan Universe), a "Dark Replacement Entity" refers to a special life form formed after a living being is eroded by Abyssal Energy or other dark energy, wherein the original soul is partially or completely replaced by dark energy, resulting in the coexistence of the original soul and a dark soul. A Dark Replacement Entity is not simply a "living being infected by dark energy", but one that has undergone soul-level replacement and reconstruction. Its essence is that a single body contains two mutually conflicting yet mutually dependent souls — the original host soul and the dark replacement soul.

Dark Replacement Entities are among the most complex life ethics and safety governance problems faced by the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. They differ both from anomalous life (anomalous life is entirely new unknown life, while Dark Replacement Entities are the products of known life being modified) and from ordinary energy-infected beings (ordinary infected beings have only their bodies eroded, while the souls of Dark Replacement Entities have already been replaced). Their particularity lies in involving the rights conflict of two souls, ethical dilemmas, and safety risks, requiring dedicated identification, ethics, and disposition rules.

## II. Origin of Dark Replacement Entities

The generation of Dark Replacement Entities requires specific conditions and processes. Their origin can be traced to the following aspects:

### (I) The Source of Abyssal Energy

Abyssal Energy is the core factor in the generation of Dark Replacement Entities. Abyssal Energy comes from the Cosmic Abyss — the darkest region at the lowest level of the Hengyuan Universe, where the primitive dark energy rejected at the birth of the universe gathers. Abyssal Energy has the following characteristics:

| Characteristic | Description |
|------|------|
| Erosiveness | Abyssal Energy can erode the body and soul of living beings, gradually transforming them into dark attributes |
| Temptation | Abyssal Energy can amplify the negative emotions and desires of living beings, inducing them to actively accept dark energy |
| Replacement | After reaching a certain concentration, Abyssal Energy can replace the soul of a living being, forming a dark replacement soul |
| Infectivity | Dark Replacement Entities can transmit Abyssal Energy to other living beings through contact, attacks, consciousness intrusion, and other means |
| Evolvability | Abyssal Energy can evolve along with the evolution of the host, producing more powerful dark abilities |

### (II) Generation Conditions

The generation of Dark Replacement Entities requires the simultaneous satisfaction of the following conditions:

1. **Abyssal Energy exposure**: The living being is exposed to high-concentration Abyssal Energy, or has long-term contact with low-concentration Abyssal Energy.
2. **Soul vulnerability period**: The living being's soul is in a vulnerable state, such as during cultivation breakthroughs, severe injuries, extreme emotional fluctuations, mental breakdowns, etc.
3. **Negative emotion catalysis**: The living being harbors strong negative emotions, such as resentment, fear, greed, despair, etc.; these emotions are the best catalysts for Abyssal Energy.
4. **Active or passive acceptance**: The living being actively accepts dark power under the temptation of Abyssal Energy, or is passively eroded when powerless to resist.
5. **Soul replacement completion**: Abyssal Energy takes root in the soul and completes the replacement, forming an independent dark replacement soul.

### (III) Generation Pathways

Dark Replacement Entities are mainly generated through the following pathways:

- **Abyssal region contact**: The living being enters the Cosmic Abyss or an Abyssal Energy leakage zone and is eroded by high-concentration Abyssal Energy.
- **Dark cultivator conversion**: The cultivator actively practices dark arts, absorbing Abyssal Energy over a long period, and is ultimately replaced by a dark soul.
- **Dark Replacement Entity infection**: Being attacked by an existing Dark Replacement Entity or subjected to consciousness intrusion, with Abyssal Energy invading through wounds or consciousness channels.
- **Ruins and relic curses**: Contacting ancient ruins or cursed items contaminated by Abyssal Energy, being eroded by the dark energy sealed within.
- **Experiment accidents**: Accidents occur in experiments involving Abyssal Energy, and experiment personnel are eroded by out-of-control Abyssal Energy.
- **Plane fissure intrusion**: Dark beings intruding from the Abyssal Plane through plane fissures directly replace the souls of local living beings.

## III. Essence of Dark Replacement Entities

The essence of a Dark Replacement Entity is a "dual-soul coexistence entity", with the following core characteristics:

### (I) Dual-Soul Structure

There are two independent souls in the body of a Dark Replacement Entity:

| Soul | Name | Characteristics |
|------|------|------|
| Original soul | The soul originally belonging to the host | Retains the original host's memories, personality, consciousness, and emotions, but its power is suppressed by the dark soul |
| Replacement soul | The dark soul formed by the condensation of Abyssal Energy | Has independent consciousness, memories, and personality, with powerful strength, inclined toward darkness and predation |

The two souls share the same body and contend for control of the body. In most cases, the dark replacement soul occupies the dominant position, and the original soul is suppressed deep in consciousness; but under specific conditions (such as intense emotional stimulation, purification by holy light energy, or the eruption of the original host's will), the original soul can temporarily regain control of the body.

### (II) Soul Conflict and Interdependence

There is a complex conflict and interdependence between the two souls:

- **Conflict**: The two souls contend for control of the body; the original soul attempts to expel the dark soul to regain freedom, while the dark soul attempts to completely annihilate the original soul and fully control the body.
- **Interdependence**: The two souls share the same body and life origin; the demise of one may cause the demise of the other or the collapse of the body. The dark soul needs the original soul as an "anchor" to maintain its existence in the material world, while the original soul needs the power of the dark soul to maintain the operation of the body (in cases of deep replacement).
- **Fusion possibility**: In extremely rare cases, the two souls can reach reconciliation and achieve soul fusion, forming a new soul that possesses both the original host's memories and dark power.

### (III) Ability Characteristics

Dark Replacement Entities usually possess the following ability characteristics:

1. **Dark energy manipulation**: Can manipulate Abyssal Energy and dark-attribute energy, casting dark spells and attacks.
2. **Body enhancement**: The body is enhanced by Abyssal Energy, with strength, speed, and defensive power far exceeding those of ordinary living beings.
3. **Regeneration ability**: Possesses powerful regeneration ability, with ordinary injuries recovering quickly.
4. **Infection ability**: Can transmit Abyssal Energy to other living beings through attacks or contact, creating new Dark Replacement Entities.
5. **Enhanced perception**: Possesses extremely strong perception of dark energy, negative emotions, and life aura.
6. **Form transformation**: Can transform into a dark form or monster form, further enhancing combat power.
7. **Soul attack**: Can directly attack the souls of other living beings, causing soul trauma or soul contamination.

### (IV) Behavioral Tendencies

The behavioral tendencies of a Dark Replacement Entity depend on which soul occupies the dominant position:

- **When the dark soul dominates**: Behavior tends toward destruction, predation, killing, and the spread of dark energy, with strong aggressiveness and antisocial tendencies.
- **When the original soul dominates**: Behavior tends toward the original host's original personality and values, but is influenced by dark energy, with emotions more easily fluctuating and more easily provoked.
- **When the two souls conflict**: Behavior manifests as chaos and contradiction, sometimes lucid and sometimes out of control, posing danger to both itself and others.

## IV. Development Stages of Dark Replacement Entities

From erosion to complete replacement, a Dark Replacement Entity usually undergoes the following development stages:

| Stage | Name | Characteristics | Reversibility |
|------|------|------|--------|
| First stage | Energy erosion period | Abyssal Energy begins to erode the body, with physical discomfort and emotional fluctuations; the soul has not yet been replaced | Fully reversible |
| Second stage | Soul contamination period | Abyssal Energy invades the soul; the original soul is contaminated, with personality changes and dark impulses | Mostly reversible |
| Third stage | Dual-soul formation period | The dark replacement soul forms, the dual-soul coexistence structure is established, and the original soul begins to be suppressed | Partially reversible |
| Fourth stage | Dark dominance period | The dark soul completely occupies the dominant position, the original soul is deeply suppressed, and the body is basically transformed by dark energy | Difficult to reverse |
| Fifth stage | Complete replacement period | The original soul is completely annihilated or assimilated, the dark soul fully controls the body, becoming a purely dark existence | Irreversible |

From the first to the third stage, Dark Replacement Entities still have the possibility of recovery; after entering the fourth stage, recovery is extremely difficult; in the fifth stage, the original host no longer exists and can only be disposed of as a purely dark existence.

## V. The Relationship Between Dark Replacement Entities and the Symbiotic Civilization

Dark Replacement Entities are a major challenge faced by the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. Their relationship with the symbiotic civilization is reflected in the following aspects:

1. **Conceptual conflict**: The dark soul of a Dark Replacement Entity tends toward predation and destruction, fundamentally opposing the symbiotic civilization's concept of "anti-predation, advocating symbiosis".
2. **Security threat**: Dark Replacement Entities possess powerful combat ability and infection ability, posing a direct threat to the security of the symbiotic civilization.
3. **Ethical dilemma**: The original soul within a Dark Replacement Entity is a legitimate member of the symbiotic civilization, and its basic rights should be protected; however, the danger of the dark soul requires control measures. How to balance the two is a profound ethical dilemma.
4. **Governance challenge**: The identification, isolation, treatment, and disposition of Dark Replacement Entities require dedicated institutions and resources, placing high demands on the governance capability of the symbiotic civilization.
5. **Transformation possibility**: While the original soul still exists, through purification, fusion, separation, and other means, some Dark Replacement Entities can be saved and reintegrated into the symbiotic system.

## Conclusion

Dark Replacement Entities are special existences produced by the interweaving of Abyssal Energy and the souls of living beings. Their essence is a contradictory unity of dual-soul coexistence. They are both a security threat to the symbiotic civilization and a test of its ethical depth and governance capability. Understanding the origin and essence of Dark Replacement Entities is the premise and foundation for formulating the Dual-Soul Ethics Code (see *58-双魂伦理准则*) and the four-branch disposition rules (see *59-黑暗置换体四类分支处置*).
