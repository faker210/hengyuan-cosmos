## I. Overall Framework of Branch Disposition

The disposition of Dark Replacement Entities is the most complex field of life governance in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. Unlike the three-type disposition processes for anomalous life and anomalous energy, Dark Replacement Entities, due to their special dual-soul coexistence structure (see *57-黑暗置换体起源与本质*), require more refined branch disposition plans. According to the ethical principles established in *58-双魂伦理准则*, combined with the development stage of the Dark Replacement Entity, the state of the original host soul, the characteristics of the dark soul, and the risk rating, the disposition of Dark Replacement Entities is divided into four branches: fusion symbiosis type, separation and resettlement type, sealing and monitoring type, and dissolution type.

The selection of the four branch dispositions follows the following decision logic:

| Decision Factor | Consideration Points |
|----------|---------|
| Development stage | The first to third stages prioritize rescue; the fourth stage is cautiously assessed; in the fifth stage the original host has perished |
| State of the original host soul | Whether the original host soul still exists, whether it can express its will, whether there is a possibility of recovery |
| Characteristics of the dark soul | Whether the dark soul has communication potential, whether there is room for reform, the degree of destructiveness |
| Risk rating | The degree of threat to public safety and the rights and interests of others |
| Ethical review conclusion | The review opinion and adjudication of the Dual-Soul Ethics Review Committee |
| Will of the original host | The choice of the original host soul when it can express its will |

## II. First Type: Fusion Symbiosis Disposition

Fusion symbiosis disposition is the most positive and constructive disposition among the four branches, applicable to Dark Replacement Entities in which the original host soul and the dark soul have the possibility of reaching reconciliation and achieving soul fusion.

### (I) Applicable Conditions

Fusion symbiosis disposition must simultaneously satisfy the following conditions:

1. The Dark Replacement Entity is in the third stage (dual-soul formation period) or early fourth stage (early dark dominance period); the original host soul still exists and has a certain degree of autonomous consciousness.
2. The original host soul clearly expresses the will to attempt fusion with the dark soul, and this will is determined to be genuine and autonomous.
3. The dark soul has a certain communication ability, displays non-purely destructive characteristics, and has the possibility of accepting fusion.
4. The risk rating is assessed as Second level (medium risk) or below, and the fusion process will not pose an uncontrollable threat to public safety.
5. Approved by the review of the Dual-Soul Ethics Review Committee.

### (II) Disposition Goal

Through guiding the original host soul and the dark soul to reach reconciliation, achieve the organic fusion of the two souls, forming a new soul that possesses both the original host's memories, personality, and dark power. The fused new soul retains the original host's citizenship and legitimate rights, while obtaining the ability to manipulate dark energy, becoming a special member of the symbiotic civilization.

### (III) Disposition Steps

| Step | Name | Specific Content | Responsible Institution |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| Step one | Ethical review | The Dual-Soul Ethics Review Committee conducts a comprehensive review of the fusion plan, confirming compliance with ethical principles | Dual-Soul Ethics Review Committee |
| Step two | Communication guidance | In a controlled environment, professional soul guides assist the original host soul and the dark soul in communicating, promoting mutual understanding | Soul Treatment Center + cultivators |
| Step three | Reconciliation negotiation | Guide the two souls to reach a reconciliation agreement on the fused identity, memories, abilities, behavioral standards, etc. | Soul Treatment Center + legal advisors |
| Step four | Fusion preparation | Conduct pre-fusion preparations such as body conditioning, energy balancing, and psychological building | Medical team + cultivation instructors |
| Step five | Soul fusion | With the joint assistance of advanced cultivators and soul therapists, implement the soul fusion surgery/ritual | Advanced cultivators + soul therapists |
| Step six | Post-fusion stabilization | Conduct stabilization treatment for the fused new soul, monitoring soul integrity and body adaptability | Medical team + monitoring team |
| Step seven | Identity confirmation | Confirm the identity of the fused new soul, retain the original host's citizenship, and update identity archives | Household registration institution + Life Administration Bureau |
| Step eight | Integration assistance | Provide auxiliary services such as psychological counseling, cultivation guidance, and social integration for the fused individual | Myriad Races Affairs Department + community |
| Step nine | Long-term tracking | Conduct long-term tracking and monitoring of the fused individual for no less than five years, assessing fusion stability and social adaptability | Life Administration Bureau + community |

### (IV) Post-Fusion Management

Although the fused individual retains the original host's citizenship, it must accept special management:

- **Energy control**: The fused individual's use of dark energy is strictly controlled; periodic energy inspections are required, and the use of dark energy in public places is prohibited.
- **Behavioral supervision**: The fused individual must periodically report its behavioral status to the Life Administration Bureau and accept community supervision and assistance.
- **Cultivation restrictions**: The fused individual may not cultivate techniques that may aggravate dark attributes; cultivation plans must be approved.
- **Emergency plan**: Establish an emergency plan for dark energy loss of control in fused individuals; once signs of loss of control appear, immediately activate control.
- **Exit mechanism**: If severe instability or persistent dangerous behavior appears after fusion, re-assessment may be conducted and the disposition type may be converted.

### (V) Handling of Fusion Failure

Soul fusion carries a risk of failure; the handling plans for fusion failure:

- **Fusion interruption**: If severe instability occurs during the fusion process, immediately interrupt the fusion and restore the two souls to their pre-fusion state.
- **Incomplete fusion**: If soul incompleteness or personality splitting appears after fusion, transfer to separation and resettlement disposition for follow-up handling.
- **Fusion loss of control**: If the new soul after fusion displays uncontrollable destructiveness, immediately transfer to sealing and monitoring or dissolution disposition.

## III. Second Type: Separation and Resettlement Disposition

Separation and resettlement disposition applies to Dark Replacement Entities in which the original host soul and the dark soul cannot fuse but can be separated; through soul separation surgery, the two souls are separated and resettled respectively.

### (I) Applicable Conditions

1. The Dark Replacement Entity is in the third or fourth stage; the original host soul still exists and has the possibility of recovery.
2. The fusion attempt of the two souls has failed or does not have the conditions for fusion.
3. Technical assessment deems soul separation feasible, and the original host soul can survive independently after separation.
4. The original host soul clearly expresses the will to be separated.
5. Approved by the review of the Dual-Soul Ethics Review Committee.

### (II) Disposition Goal

Through soul separation surgery, separate the original host soul from the dark soul; the original host soul returns to the body to resume normal life, while the dark soul is placed in a dedicated container or facility for control and research.

### (III) Disposition Steps

| Step | Name | Specific Content | Responsible Institution |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| Step one | Ethical review | The Dual-Soul Ethics Review Committee reviews the separation plan, confirming the necessity and feasibility of separation | Dual-Soul Ethics Review Committee |
| Step two | Separation preparation | Conduct pre-separation preparations such as body assessment, soul state inspection, and energy balance adjustment | Medical team + soul therapists |
| Step three | Soul separation | With the assistance of advanced cultivators and soul therapists, implement the soul separation surgery to separate the two souls from the body | Advanced cultivators + soul therapists |
| Step four | Original host repositioning | Re-implant the original host soul into the body, conducting soul stabilization and body rehabilitation treatment | Medical team |
| Step five | Dark soul placement | Place the separated dark soul in a dedicated soul containment container and store it in a soul sealing facility | Life Administration Bureau + technical team |
| Step six | Original host recovery | Conduct comprehensive body rehabilitation, psychological treatment, and cultivation recovery for the original host, helping it return to normal life | Medical team + psychological counselors |
| Step seven | Identity restoration | Confirm the complete restoration of the original host's identity and rights, and update identity archives | Household registration institution |
| Step eight | Dark soul management | Conduct continuous monitoring and research on the separated dark soul, exploring the possibility of its reform or dissolution | Life Administration Bureau + scientific research institution |

### (IV) Placement of the Separated Dark Soul

The separated dark soul is placed in the following manner:

- **Soul container**: The dark soul is placed in a dedicated soul containment container equipped with devices such as energy shielding, consciousness suppression, and safety locking.
- **Sealing facility**: The container is stored in a dedicated soul sealing facility that implements the highest level of safety management.
- **Continuous monitoring**: Conduct 24-hour continuous monitoring of the dark soul's consciousness activity and energy fluctuations.
- **Research and utilization**: Under strict ethical review, scientific research may be conducted on the dark soul, exploring the essence of Abyssal Energy and purification methods.
- **Final disposition**: According to research results and risk assessment, make a final disposition decision for the dark soul — reform into a non-destructive existence, long-term sealing, or purification and dissolution.

### (V) Separation Risks and Responses

Soul separation surgery carries the following risks, for which response plans must be formulated:

| Risk | Response Plan |
|------|---------|
| The original host soul is damaged during separation | Conduct soul strengthening before separation; advanced cultivators provide full protection during separation |
| The original host's body cannot adapt after separation | Conduct comprehensive body rehabilitation and soul stabilization treatment after separation |
| The dark soul escapes during separation | Separation is conducted in the highest-level safety facility, equipped with multiple containment devices |
| Incomplete separation, residual dark energy | Conduct deep purification treatment after separation to remove residual dark energy |
| Psychological trauma in the original host soul after separation | Provide long-term psychological counseling and mental treatment |

## IV. Third Type: Sealing and Monitoring Disposition

Sealing and monitoring disposition applies to Dark Replacement Entities that cannot fuse or be separated, but whose original host soul still exists and which do not have conditions for immediate dissolution; through sealing means, their danger is controlled, with long-term monitoring and continuous assessment.

### (I) Applicable Conditions

1. The Dark Replacement Entity is in the fourth stage (dark dominance period); the original host soul is deeply suppressed but has not yet perished.
2. Both fusion and separation lack technical feasibility or their risks are too high.
3. The risk rating of the Dark Replacement Entity is Third level (high risk), but has not yet reached the degree requiring immediate dissolution.
4. The original host soul still has faint hope of recovery, requiring time and conditions to await a turning point.
5. The Dual-Soul Ethics Review Committee reviews and approves the sealing plan.

### (II) Disposition Goal

Through sealing means, suppress the power and activity of the dark soul, control the danger of the Dark Replacement Entity, while protecting the original host soul from complete annihilation, awaiting in long-term monitoring the maturity of opportunities for recovery or disposition.

### (III) Sealing Methods

According to the power level and characteristics of the Dark Replacement Entity, choose the following sealing methods:

| Sealing Method | Principle | Applicable Targets |
|----------|------|---------|
| Energy sealing | Use sealing arrays or sealing devices to suppress the flow and output of dark energy | Dark Replacement Entities of low to medium power level |
| Spatial sealing | Seal the Dark Replacement Entity in an independent isolated space, cutting off contact with the outside | Dark Replacement Entities of high power level or with infectivity |
| Consciousness sealing | Suppress the consciousness activity of the dark soul, placing it in a dormant or semi-dormant state | Dark Replacement Entities with active consciousness and soul attack ability |
| Multiple sealing | Simultaneously use multiple sealing methods to form multiple layers of protection | Dark Replacement Entities of extremely high power level or with multiple dangerous characteristics |
| Anchor sealing | Use the origin attributes of Anchor energy for sealing, the most powerful sealing method | Dark Replacement Entities dangerous at the cosmic level |

### (IV) Disposition Steps

| Step | Name | Specific Content | Responsible Institution |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| Step one | Ethical review | The Dual-Soul Ethics Review Committee reviews the sealing plan, confirming the necessity and appropriateness of sealing | Dual-Soul Ethics Review Committee |
| Step two | Sealing preparation | Prepare the sealing array/device/space, allocating the energy and personnel required for sealing | Technical team + advanced cultivators |
| Step three | Subjugation and control | With the cooperation of the security team, subjugate the Dark Replacement Entity, ensuring no accidents occur during the sealing process | Safety Security Department + advanced cultivators |
| Step four | Implementing the sealing | Implement the sealing according to the sealing plan, suppressing the power and activity of the dark soul | Advanced cultivators + sealers |
| Step five | Sealing verification | Verify the effectiveness and stability of the sealing, confirming that the danger of the Dark Replacement Entity has been controlled | Technical team + monitoring team |
| Step six | Transfer and placement | Transfer the sealed Dark Replacement Entity to a dedicated sealing and monitoring facility | Safety Security Department + Life Administration Bureau |
| Step seven | Continuous monitoring | Conduct 24-hour continuous monitoring of the sealing state, periodically checking the stability of the sealing | Monitoring team |
| Step eight | Periodic re-assessment | Conduct a comprehensive re-assessment every six months, evaluating the state of the original host soul, the characteristics of the dark soul, and the stability of the sealing, determining whether there is a possibility of adjusting the disposition type | Identification expert group + Ethics Review Committee |
| Step nine | Sealing maintenance | Periodically maintain and reinforce the sealing to prevent weakening or damage | Sealers + technical team |

### (V) Sealing and Monitoring Facility Standards

- **Site selection**: Sealing and monitoring facilities are sited in remote areas with stable geology, stable energy environment, and sparse population, or in independent isolated planes.
- **Construction**: Facilities adopt multiple protective structures, including three layers of protection: physical barriers, energy barriers, and spatial locking.
- **Equipment**: Equipped with high-precision sealing monitoring devices, automatic reinforcement systems, emergency response systems, and backup energy systems.
- **Personnel**: Implement a 24-hour duty system, equipped with sealers, monitors, security guards, and emergency response personnel.
- **Emergency**: Formulate various emergency plans for sealing damage, dark soul escape, original host soul demise, etc., with regular drills.

### (VI) Lifting and Conversion of Sealing

Under the following circumstances, the sealing may be lifted or the disposition type converted:

- **Original host recovery**: If the original host soul gradually recovers during the sealing period and possesses the conditions for separation or fusion, the sealing may be lifted and converted to separation and resettlement or fusion symbiosis type.
- **Dark soul reform**: If the dark soul displays reform potential during the sealing period, the sealing may be lifted under controlled conditions for reform attempts.
- **Sealing failure**: If the sealing suffers irreparable damage or weakening, and the danger of the Dark Replacement Entity is uncontrollable, it may be converted to dissolution type.
- **Original host demise**: If the original host soul completely perishes during the sealing period, and the Dark Replacement Entity becomes a purely dark existence, it may be converted to dissolution type or continue sealing according to its danger.

## V. Fourth Type: Dissolution Disposition

Dissolution disposition is the most severe disposition among the four branches, applicable to Dark Replacement Entities that cannot be rescued and are extremely dangerous; through annihilating the dark soul (and when necessary, annihilating the entire body), the threat is eliminated.

### (I) Applicable Conditions

Dissolution disposition applies to the following situations:

1. The Dark Replacement Entity is in the fifth stage (complete replacement period); the original host soul has completely perished, the body is fully controlled by the dark soul, becoming a purely dark existence.
2. The Dark Replacement Entity is in the fourth stage; the original host soul still exists but cannot recover, fusion, separation, and sealing are all infeasible, and its danger continues to escalate.
3. The risk rating of the Dark Replacement Entity is Fourth level (extremely high risk), with cosmic-level destructive power, directly threatening civilizational survival, and other disposition methods cannot control its threat.
4. In sealing and monitoring disposition, the sealing has failed and cannot be re-applied, and the danger of the Dark Replacement Entity is uncontrollable.
5. The Dual-Soul Ethics Review Committee reviews and approves the dissolution plan (extremely high risk emergencies may be disposed first and approved retroactively).

### (II) Dissolution Principles

Dissolution disposition involves the annihilation of life and must strictly follow the following principles:

- **Last resort principle**: Dissolution must be the last resort when all other disposition methods are infeasible.
- **Proportionality principle**: The intensity of dissolution should correspond to the degree of threat; excessive use of force is prohibited.
- **Minimal suffering principle**: Dissolution should adopt painless, rapid methods as much as possible, reducing the suffering of the dark soul (and the original host soul, if still alive).
- **Respect for the original host principle**: If the original host soul still exists, the original host soul should be kept lucid and dignified as much as possible before dissolution; when necessary, allow the original host to bid farewell to relatives and friends.
- **Full recording principle**: The dissolution process is fully recorded, forming a complete archive for review and traceability.
- **Post-hoc review principle**: After dissolution is completed, conduct a post-hoc review, assessing the necessity and appropriateness of the dissolution, and summarizing lessons learned.

### (III) Dissolution Methods

According to the power level and characteristics of the Dark Replacement Entity, choose the following dissolution methods:

| Dissolution Method | Principle | Applicable Targets |
|----------|------|---------|
| Holy Light Purification Dissolution | Use high-intensity Holy Light Energy to purify the dark soul, causing it to dissipate in the light | Dark Replacement Entities of low to medium power level |
| Soul Annihilation Art | Directly attack and annihilate the soul core of the dark soul, causing its complete demise | Dark Replacement Entities of high power level |
| Spatial Exile Dissolution | Exile the Dark Replacement Entity to void space or the end of time, causing it to dissolve in spacetime decay | Dark Replacement Entities with strong physical defense |
| Anchor Origin Dissolution | Invoke the origin energy of the Anchor, decomposing the Dark Replacement Entity and returning it to the origin of the universe | Dark Replacement Entities dangerous at the cosmic level |
| Multi-Plane Joint Dissolution by Powers | Multiple Supreme Powers of the Plane join forces, using absolute power to suppress and annihilate the Dark Replacement Entity | Dark Replacement Entities of extremely high power level |
| Induced Self-Detonation Dissolution | Under strict control, induce the dark energy of the Dark Replacement Entity to self-detonate, dissolving it in the energy explosion | Dark Replacement Entities with unstable energy |

### (IV) Disposition Steps

| Step | Name | Specific Content | Responsible Institution |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| Step one | Ethical review/emergency approval | In normal circumstances, reviewed and approved by the Dual-Soul Ethics Review Committee; in extremely high risk emergencies, ordered by the highest-ranking person in charge on site, with retroactive approval | Dual-Soul Ethics Review Committee / highest-ranking person in charge on site |
| Step two | Plan formulation | Formulate a detailed dissolution plan, including dissolution methods, force allocation, safety measures, emergency plans, and environmental impact assessment | Combat command headquarters + technical expert group |
| Step three | Force assembly | Assemble the cultivators, technical personnel, equipment, and energy resources required for dissolution | Safety Security Department + advanced cultivators |
| Step four | Area lockdown | Lock down the dissolution area, evacuate surrounding personnel, and set up safety cordons | Safety Security Department + local government |
| Step five | Subjugation and suppression | First use suppressive force to control the actions of the Dark Replacement Entity, preventing its escape or greater destruction | Advanced cultivators + security forces |
| Step six | Implementing the dissolution | Implement the dissolution according to the dissolution plan, with whole-process monitoring; upon any anomaly, immediately activate the emergency plan | Advanced cultivators + technical team |
| Step seven | Confirming demise | Confirm that the dark soul (and body) has completely perished, inspecting for energy residue or soul fragments | Monitoring team + appraisal expert group |
| Step eight | Environmental restoration | Conduct environmental restoration of the dissolution area, removing residual dark energy and restoring ecological balance | Department of Ecological Environment |
| Step nine | Aftermath handling | Notify the relatives and friends of the original host (if the original host has perished), handle the original host's estate, and conduct psychological counseling | Civil affairs department + psychological counselors |
| Step ten | Archive filing and post-hoc review | Archive the full dissolution process records and conduct a post-hoc review, assessing necessity and appropriateness | Life Administration Bureau + Ethics Review Committee |

### (V) Emergency Dissolution Procedure

For Fourth level (extremely high risk) Dark Replacement Entities that directly threaten civilizational survival, the emergency dissolution procedure applies:

1. The highest-ranking person in charge on site may directly order the initiation of emergency dissolution without prior ethical review, but must report to the Dual-Soul Ethics Review Committee for filing within 24 hours after the dissolution.
2. Emergency dissolution may mobilize the civilization's highest level of force and energy resources, including the centralized invocation of Anchor energy and the joint action of multiple Supreme Powers of the Plane.
3. The priority goal of emergency dissolution is to eliminate the threat and protect life and civilizational safety, not to pursue the "elegance" or "painlessness" of the dissolution method (but collateral harm should still be minimized as much as possible).
4. After emergency dissolution, a comprehensive environmental assessment and restoration should be conducted to prevent dark energy residue and secondary disasters.
5. Emergency dissolution cases must undergo post-hoc review and in-depth analysis, summarizing lessons learned and improving the early warning and disposition mechanisms.

## VI. Conversion Mechanism of the Four Branch Dispositions

The disposition of Dark Replacement Entities is not fixed; according to changes in the situation during the disposition process, conversion among the four branches is possible:

| Conversion Direction | Conversion Conditions | Conversion Procedure |
|----------|---------|---------|
| Fusion → Separation | Fusion attempt fails or severe instability appears after fusion | Re-assessment; after ethical review approval, convert to separation |
| Fusion → Sealing | Fusion fails and separation is infeasible; risk control is needed | Re-assessment; after ethical review approval, convert to sealing |
| Fusion → Dissolution | Fusion loses control; the new soul has uncontrollable destructiveness | Emergency assessment; immediately convert to dissolution |
| Separation → Sealing | Separation fails or the original host cannot recover after separation; risk control is needed | Re-assessment; after ethical review approval, convert to sealing |
| Separation → Dissolution | The dark soul escapes during separation and is uncontrollable | Emergency assessment; immediately convert to dissolution |
| Sealing → Fusion | During the sealing period, the original host soul recovers and possesses fusion conditions | Discovered in periodic re-assessment; after ethical review approval, convert to fusion |
| Sealing → Separation | During the sealing period, the original host soul recovers and possesses separation conditions | Discovered in periodic re-assessment; after ethical review approval, convert to separation |
| Sealing → Dissolution | The sealing fails and cannot be re-applied; the danger is uncontrollable | Emergency assessment; immediately convert to dissolution |
| Dissolution → Others | Dissolution is the last resort; once implemented, it is irreversible | Not applicable |

## Conclusion

The four-branch disposition of Dark Replacement Entities is the culmination of life governance in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. From the active rescue of fusion symbiosis, to the precise cutting of separation and resettlement, to the patient waiting of sealing and monitoring, to the last resort of dissolution, the four branches form a complete disposition spectrum from the gentlest to the most severe, covering all possible situations of Dark Replacement Entities. This disposition system both upholds the ethical bottom line of "making every effort to rescue every soul" and maintains the safety bottom line of "resolutely eliminating uncontrollable threats", embodying the exquisite balance of the symbiotic civilization between life ethics and safety governance.
