## I. The Origin of the Multiplication Fruit and Its Technological Positioning

The Multiplication Fruit originates from the Devil Fruit system of the One Piece world; its origin ability is to multiply a specific attribute of the user by a factor. In the starting phase of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, the Multiplication Fruit was selected as the core starting buff, but its application method is not to obtain superpowers by eating it directly, but to convert the fruit's multiplication mechanism into a mass-producible, shareable technological system through the path of "law extraction — mechanism analysis — engineering reconstruction".

This technological path follows the principle of "reality as foundation, law-driven upgrading": the essence of the Multiplication Fruit is an exponential amplification mechanism of information processing efficiency, whose underlying logic shares common ground with parallel computing and quantum acceleration in reality. The scientific research team of the symbiotic civilization, using the Deduction Iteration System as the theoretical framework, locks the fruit's multiplication ability onto the specific dimension of "cultivation deduction", achieving the stable output of a hundredfold cultivation deduction amplification.

## II. The Working Principle of the Multiplication Fruit Deduction Engine

The Multiplication Fruit Deduction Engine (hereinafter "the Deduction Engine") is the core product of the engineering application of the Multiplication Fruit. Its working principle is divided into three stages:

| Stage | Name | Functional Description | Technical Implementation |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| First Stage | Law Resonance | The engine core establishes a resonance channel with the Deduction Iteration Laws, activating the multiplication mechanism | Law anchoring array based on spatial legal principle dispatch |
| Second Stage | Parallel Deduction | Splits a single deduction task into a hundred parallel deduction paths, executed simultaneously | Quantum-like parallel computing architecture, with the Multiplication Fruit mechanism providing computational power amplification |
| Third Stage | Result Fusion | Conducts cross-validation and optimal-solution fusion of the results of the hundred deduction paths | Anchor network collaborative algorithms, symbiotic feedback loop optimization |

The "hundredfold" of the Deduction Engine is not a simple multiplication of speed by one hundred, but refers to the number of deduction paths that can be completed in the same time reaching one hundred times that of conventional methods. This means a cultivator can simultaneously verify a hundred possible cultivation paths in a single cultivation session, greatly shortening the iteration cycle from theory to practice.

## III. The Engineering Architecture of the Deduction Engine

In engineering, the Deduction Engine adopts a three-tier architecture of "core — array — network":

1. **Multiplication Core**: a miniature law resonator with the origin law of the Multiplication Fruit as its core, approximately the size of one standard energy unit, serving as the source of the engine's multiplication mechanism.
2. **Deduction Array**: a parallel computing array composed of a large number of law processing units, each capable of independently executing one deduction path; the scale of the array determines the parallel upper limit.
3. **Anchor Network Interface**: connects to the symbiotic feedback system through the Anchor network, obtaining the entire civilization's cultivation data as deduction material, while returning optimization results to the network.

The deployment forms of the Deduction Engine include:

- **Personal terminal type**: portable deduction assistance equipment for individual cultivators, with a parallelism of approximately the hundredfold baseline value.
- **Research center type**: large-scale deduction clusters for scientific research institutions, with parallelism that can be stacked to over a thousandfold.
- **Anchor integrated type**: deduction modules embedded in Anchor hardware, providing global deduction capability for the Anchor network.

## IV. Application Scenarios and Boundaries

The main application scenarios of the Deduction Engine include:

- **Cultivation path optimization**: real-time deduction of optimal cultivation plans for cultivators, reducing the risk of Deviation.
- **Law mechanism research**: accelerating the understanding and verification of unknown laws, promoting technological breakthroughs in the Law Engineering Layer.
- **Acceleration of technological research and development**: completing a large amount of theoretical deduction before isolated-space experiments, reducing the number of experiments.
- **Crisis contingency deduction**: conducting advance deduction of high-risk operations such as cross-plane interaction and time regression.

The usage boundaries of the Deduction Engine are strictly defined by technology ethics and safety boundaries:

- It must not be used to deduce predatory cultivation paths or plans that harm others.
- Deduction results must be verified against reality before execution; deduction conclusions must not be used directly as a basis for action.
- The parallelism of personal terminal engines has an upper limit, preventing consciousness damage caused by individual deduction overload.

## Conclusion

The engineering application of the Multiplication Fruit is the core implementation of the "starting buff" of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. It transforms a superpower from a fictional world into an engineering-capable, popularizable deduction acceleration technology, perfectly embodying the technological philosophy of "reality as foundation, law-driven upgrading". The popularization of the Deduction Engine makes the hundredfold cultivation deduction amplification no longer a privilege of the few, but an evolutionary infrastructure shared by the entire civilization.
