## I. The Positioning and Origin of Replication Mirror Technology

Replication Mirror technology is one of the two core technologies of the archaeological program of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization (the other being Isolated Space Construction Technology). Its core function is to perform high-precision mirror replication of a target plane or target area, allowing archaeological and research work to be conducted on the replica, thereby avoiding interference with and pollution of the original plane.

The origin of Replication Mirror technology can be traced back to the research on the "Space Transformation" (Huàkōng) realm of the spatial legal principle dispatch system. While exploring the mechanism of space-matter transformation, the scientific research team discovered that by performing a complete law scan and reconstruction of the spatial structure of a target area, a mirror replica highly consistent with the original area could be generated in another space. This discovery, after extensive optimization and iteration with the Multiplication Fruit Deduction Engine, ultimately formed a Replication Mirror system that could be implemented through engineering.

## II. The Working Principle of the Replication Mirror

The working process of the Replication Mirror is divided into four stages, each with strict technical parameters and safety thresholds:

| Stage | Name | Functional Description | Key Parameters |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| First Stage | Law Scanning | Perform a full-dimension law scan of the target area, obtaining all information including spatial structure, material distribution, energy fields, and consciousness residue | Scanning precision no lower than 0.001 law units, scanning coverage ≥99.99% |
| Second Stage | Information Encoding | Encode the massive information obtained from scanning into transmittable law data packets, removing redundancy and verifying integrity | Encoding compression ratio approximately 10^6:1, verification using the Anchor hash algorithm |
| Third Stage | Mirror Reconstruction | Reconstruct the mirror replica of the target area in an isolated space based on the law data packets | Reconstruction fidelity ≥99.95%, reconstruction deviation must remain within controllable limits |
| Fourth Stage | Synchronization Calibration | Perform continuous synchronization calibration between the mirror replica and the original area, ensuring changes during research are traceable | Synchronization delay ≤0.1 seconds, calibration frequency adjustable according to research needs |

The "replication" of the Replication Mirror is not simple material cloning, but law-level reconstruction based on spatial legal principles. The mirror replica is highly consistent with the original area at the levels of physical structure, energy distribution, and even consciousness residue, but it is essentially an independent spatial instance, and its internal changes do not reversely affect the original area.

## III. The Engineering Architecture of the Replication Mirror System

In engineering, the Replication Mirror system consists of three major subsystems:

1. **Scanning subsystem**: a law scanning array deployed near the target plane, driven by the Space Perception Module, capable of scanning targets of different scales from planetary level to regional level. The scanning array adopts a non-intrusive design, only passively receiving law information and not emitting any energy to the target area.
2. **Transmission subsystem**: a law data transmission channel based on cross-plane communication technology, transmitting the encoded scanning data to the research station where the isolated space is located. The transmission process uses end-to-end encryption to prevent law data from being tampered with or stolen during transmission.
3. **Reconstruction subsystem**: a mirror reconstruction array deployed within the isolated space, driven by the Space Transformation Module, reconstructing the target area in the isolated space based on the law data packets. The scale of the reconstruction array determines the maximum area scale that can be replicated.

The deployment levels of the Replication Mirror system are divided into three levels:

- **Planetary level**: can replicate a mirror of a complete planet, used for civilization-level archaeological research.
- **Regional level**: can replicate regions of continental or national scale, used for key area research.
- **Site level**: can replicate specific ruins or scenes, used for fine archaeological excavation.

## IV. Ethical and Safety Constraints of Replication Mirror Technology

Although Replication Mirror technology is powerful, its use is subject to strict ethical and safety constraints:

- **Knowledge-return-only principle**: the outputs of archaeological research conducted on the mirror replica may only be returned to the main plane in the form of knowledge; no matter, energy, or life form from the mirror replica may be brought back to the original plane or the main plane.
- **Non-intrusion principle**: the scanning process must not cause any interference with the normal operation of the target area; the scanning array must maintain a passive observation state.
- **Mirror destruction principle**: after research is completed, the mirror replica must be safely destroyed according to standard procedures, and must not be retained long-term or diverted to other uses.
- **Consciousness residue handling**: if the mirror replica contains consciousness residue, it must be assessed and handled according to the Disposition Process for the Three Types of Anomalous Life, and consciousness residue must not be treated as inanimate data.

## Conclusion

The technical principle of the Replication Mirror is the technological embodiment of the principle of "observe first, interfere in a limited manner afterward". It allows researchers of the Hengyuan Universe to conduct in-depth archaeology and research on targets without touching the original plane, fundamentally solving the pollution and interference problems in cross-plane archaeology. The combination of the Replication Mirror and isolated spaces constitutes the technical cornerstone of the low-pollution research program of the symbiotic civilization.
