## I. The Definition and Functions of Isolated Spaces

An isolated space is an independent spatial instance constructed outside the main plane through spatial legal principle technology, possessing a high degree of law isolation. It is one of the most important safety infrastructure elements in the technological system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, providing safe operating environments for scenarios such as dangerous law experiments, Replication Mirror reconstruction, Anomalous Energy containment, and cross-plane interaction buffering.

The core functions of isolated spaces can be summarized as "three isolations":

- **Law isolation**: the laws operating inside an isolated space do not permeate into the main plane, and changes in the laws of the main plane do not interfere with the interior of the isolated space.
- **Material isolation**: there is no natural material exchange channel between an isolated space and the main plane; all material entering or leaving must pass through controlled spatial interfaces.
- **Consciousness isolation**: consciousness activity inside an isolated space cannot be directly perceived by the consciousness domain of the main plane, preventing consciousness pollution and information leakage.

## II. The Construction Principle of Isolated Spaces

The construction of isolated spaces is based on the "Law Space" (Fǎkōng) realm technology of the spatial legal principle dispatch system, whose core is to create a closed region with custom laws at the level of spatial laws. The construction process is divided into three key steps:

| Step | Name | Technical Description | Energy Requirement |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| Step one | Spatial Anchoring | Establish a spatial legal principle Anchor at the target location, determining the spatial coordinates and boundaries of the isolated space | Medium energy, requiring continuous supply |
| Step two | Law Barrier | Construct multi-layer law barriers at the boundaries, achieving law isolation between the main plane and the isolated space | High energy; the number of barrier layers determines the isolation degree |
| Step three | Internal Initialization | Initialize the custom law environment inside the isolated space, including physical constants, energy fields, time flow rate, etc. | Extremely high energy, required only during the initialization stage |

The law barrier is the technological core of isolated spaces. Standard isolated spaces adopt a seven-layer barrier structure, from the inside out: material blocking layer, energy attenuation layer, law filtering layer, consciousness shielding layer, time buffering layer, spatial locking layer, and Anchor fixing layer. Each layer is specially designed against different types of penetration threats; the seven layers combined achieve a comprehensive isolation degree of over 99.97%.

## III. Classification and Parameters of Isolated Spaces

Isolated spaces are divided into multiple levels according to use and scale:

| Level | Name | Internal Spatial Scale | Isolation Degree | Typical Uses |
|------|------|-------------|--------|----------|
| S Level | Planetary Isolated Space | Can accommodate a complete planet | 99.99% | Civilization-level archaeology, high-risk law experiments |
| A Level | Regional Isolated Space | Continental scale | 99.97% | Replication Mirror regional reconstruction, large-scale experiments |
| B Level | Laboratory Isolated Space | City scale | 99.95% | Routine law experiments, Anomalous Energy research |
| C Level | Chamber Isolated Space | Building scale | 99.90% | Personal cultivation rooms, small experiments, medical isolation |
| D Level | Portable Isolated Space | Room scale | 99.50% | Temporary operations, field experiments, emergency isolation |

The key operating parameters of isolated spaces include:

- **Time flow ratio**: the ratio of time inside the isolated space to time in the main plane, adjustable between 1:100 and 100:1, used to accelerate experiments or delay dangerous reactions.
- **Energy self-sufficiency rate**: the degree of self-sufficiency of the internal energy cycle of the isolated space; advanced isolated spaces can achieve over 90% energy self-sufficiency.
- **Maximum load capacity**: the upper limit of matter and energy that an isolated space can accommodate, constrained by the strength of spatial anchoring.
- **Safe destruction protocol**: the standard destruction process when an isolated space is decommissioned, ensuring no spatial residue remains.

## IV. Operation and Maintenance of Isolated Spaces

The operation of isolated spaces requires continuous energy supply and professional maintenance:

1. **Energy supply**: the law barrier of an isolated space requires continuous energy to maintain, usually provided by the energy distribution system of Anchor hardware. Advanced isolated spaces are equipped with energy circulation systems that can recover energy generated by internal experiments to reduce external supply requirements.
2. **Status monitoring**: isolated spaces are equipped with comprehensive status monitoring systems, real-time monitoring of barrier integrity, internal law stability, energy balance, and other parameters. Any anomaly triggers automatic alarms and emergency protocols.
3. **Entry and exit control**: all matter, energy, and life forms entering or leaving an isolated space must pass through controlled spatial interfaces, which are equipped with law scanning and disinfection devices to prevent pollution from spreading.
4. **Decommissioning and destruction**: when an isolated space reaches its service life or after experiments are completed, it must be safely destroyed according to standard protocols, including steps such as internal content disposal, gradual barrier removal, spatial Anchor recovery, and residue scanning confirmation.

## Conclusion

Isolated Space Construction Technology is the material cornerstone of the safe research system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. It enables high-risk law experiments, cross-plane archaeology, and Anomalous Energy research to be conducted in controlled environments, guaranteeing the implementation of the "low pollution" principle at the technical level. The combination of isolated spaces and the Replication Mirror jointly constitutes the technological paradigm of the symbiotic civilization for exploring the unknown without destroying the known.
