## I. The Necessity of Energy Standardization

Before the establishment of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, there were thousands of energy measurement systems in the Hengyuan Universe. Different planes, different races, and different cultivation schools each defined their own energy units and measurement methods, causing severe conversion obstacles in energy trading, technological exchange, and cultivation guidance. More seriously, inconsistent measurement standards made energy abuse and energy pollution difficult to accurately identify and hold accountable.

The establishment of the energy standardization and measurement system is one of the core infrastructure projects in the technology empowerment stage of the symbiotic civilization. Guided by the principle of "reality as foundation, law-driven upgrading", it introduces law-level energy measurement benchmarks on the basis of the real-world International System of Units, constructing a full-scale energy measurement system covering everything from microscopic particles to cosmic laws.

## II. The Energy Measurement Unit System

The energy measurement unit of the Hengyuan Universe takes the "Anchor Standard Energy Unit" (abbreviated as "Anchor Energy", symbol Ae) as its base unit. The definition of Anchor Energy is based on the benchmark energy quantity conducted by a standard Anchor in the Anchor network per unit time, possessing stability across planes and across laws.

| Unit Name | Symbol | Conversion Relationship | Typical Application Scenarios |
|----------|------|----------|-------------|
| Micro Anchor Energy | μAe | 10^-6 Ae | Microscopic particle energy, cell-level cultivation |
| Milli Anchor Energy | mAe | 10^-3 Ae | Daily personal cultivation consumption, small equipment |
| Anchor Energy | Ae | 1 Ae | Standard measurement unit, civilian equipment |
| Kilo Anchor Energy | kAe | 10^3 Ae | City-level energy supply, medium experiments |
| Mega Anchor Energy | MAe | 10^6 Ae | Anchor hardware operation, large-scale projects |
| Giga Anchor Energy | GAe | 10^9 Ae | Plane-level energy dispatch, ascension operations |

In addition to the base units, the energy standardization system also defines a series of derived units:

- **Energy density**: the energy stock within a unit space, with the unit Ae/m³.
- **Energy power**: the energy flow per unit time, with the unit Ae/s (defined as "Anchor Watt", Aw).
- **Energy purity**: the proportion of origin laws within energy, expressed as a percentage; the purity of standard cultivation energy shall not be lower than 95%.
- **Energy symbiosis rating**: the proportion of benign circulation within the energy cycle, a core indicator for assessing the health of an energy system.

## III. Energy Grading and Classification

The energy standardization system divides energy into five grades according to its source and nature:

| Grade | Name | Characteristics | Usage Restrictions |
|------|------|------|----------|
| Grade One | Origin Energy | Pure energy directly from the law network, purity ≥99.99% | Limited to Anchor cores and ascension operations |
| Grade Two | Law Energy | Energy conducted and purified through the Anchor network, purity ≥95% | Advanced cultivation, law engineering experiments |
| Grade Three | Standard Energy | Universal energy processed through standardization, purity ≥85% | Civilian equipment, daily cultivation, industrial production |
| Grade Four | Cyclic Energy | Energy recovered from life cycles and symbiotic feedback, purity ≥70% | Agriculture, environmental restoration, low-power equipment |
| Grade Five | Raw Energy | Untreated natural energy, unstable purity | Must be purified before use |

Energy classification is further divided according to the law attributes of energy, including thirty-one categories such as spatial-attribute energy, life-attribute energy, consciousness-attribute energy, and deduction-attribute energy. Each category of energy has dedicated measurement calibration methods and storage specifications.

## IV. The Measurement Benchmark and Calibration System

The accuracy of energy measurement relies on a complete benchmark and calibration system:

1. **Primary benchmark**: the "Origin Energy Benchmark Device" deployed at the main Anchor of the Hengyuan Universe, which uses the origin energy of the law network as its reference and is the highest benchmark for energy measurement in the entire universe.
2. **Secondary benchmark**: standard energy generators deployed at the Anchors of each major plane, periodically compared and calibrated against the primary benchmark.
3. **Working benchmark**: standard energy sources used by research institutions and measurement stations, calibrated against the secondary benchmark once a year.
4. **On-site measurement**: various energy measurement devices, regularly submitted for inspection according to usage frequency.

The calibration system adopts the "Anchor hash verification method", which remotely verifies the readings of measurement devices through the Anchor network, ensuring that measurement results remain comparable even in cross-plane environments.

## V. The Social Significance of Energy Standardization

The establishment of the energy standardization system has brought far-reaching social impacts:

- It enables energy to be accurately measured and traded like currency, providing a technical foundation for energy distribution in the Joint-Output Contracting Mechanism.
- It enables energy pollution and abuse to be quantified and held accountable, providing technical means for the enforcement of the Environmental Protection Law and the Anti-Monopoly Law.
- It enables cultivators of different planes and different races to communicate and receive guidance using unified energy parameters, greatly lowering the threshold of cultivation.
- It provides unified interface specifications for the standardized production and cross-plane deployment of Anchor hardware.

## Conclusion

Energy standardization and measurement are the "weights and measures" of the technological system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. Without unified measurement standards, the large-scale implementation and popularization of law engineering would be impossible to discuss. The establishment of this system marks the transition of the Hengyuan Universe's energy utilization from the extensive stage of "each acting on its own" to the refined stage of "unified norms", laying the material measurement foundation for subsequent consciousness evolution and interstellar symbiosis.
