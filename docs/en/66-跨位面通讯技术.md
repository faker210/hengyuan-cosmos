## I. The Technical Challenges of Cross-Plane Communication

In a traditional single plane, communication technology uses electromagnetic waves as its main carrier; its transmission speed is limited by the speed of light, and its transmission distance is constrained by signal attenuation. In the multi-plane structure of the Hengyuan Universe, however, there is no direct physical spatial connection between planes. Electromagnetic waves cannot penetrate the plane barriers, and traditional communication technology fails completely.

Cross-plane communication technology is precisely the law-engineering-level technology developed to solve this problem. Based on the spatial legal principle dispatch system and the Anchor network, it establishes stable information transmission channels between different planes through law encoding and spatial folding. This technology is the key infrastructure for the symbiotic civilization to achieve the "joint advancement of all races" — without cross-plane communication, life scattered across countless planes could not form a unified symbiotic network.

## II. The Basic Principles of Cross-Plane Communication

The core principle of cross-plane communication can be summarized as a four-step process: "law encoding — spatial tunneling — Anchor relay — law decoding":

| Step | Name | Technical Description | Key Parameters |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| First Step | Law Encoding | Encode the information to be transmitted into law fluctuations of specific frequencies, rather than electromagnetic waves | Encoding efficiency approximately 10^12 bit/law period |
| Second Step | Spatial Tunneling | Use spatial legal principle technology to open a temporary microscopic spatial channel through the plane barrier, through which law fluctuations tunnel to the target plane | Channel diameter approximately 10^-15 meters, extremely short duration |
| Third Step | Anchor Relay | Law fluctuations are relayed, amplified, and routed through the Anchor network, ensuring the stability of long-distance transmission | Relay delay approximately 0.001 seconds/hop |
| Fourth Step | Law Decoding | The receiving end restores the law fluctuations to the original information, verifies integrity, and delivers it to the user | Decoding error rate below 10^-12 |

Unlike traditional communication, the information carrier of cross-plane communication is law fluctuations rather than electromagnetic waves. Law fluctuations are not limited by the speed of light and can achieve near-real-time transmission between planes; their theoretical delay mainly comes from the processing time of Anchor relays.

## III. The Architecture of the Cross-Plane Communication Network

The cross-plane communication network adopts a three-tier architecture of "Anchor backbone — plane access — terminal devices":

1. **Anchor backbone network**: composed of the main Anchors deployed in each major plane, it is the core backbone of cross-plane communication. The main Anchors are connected through high-bandwidth law channels, with a single channel bandwidth of up to 10^15 bit/s. The backbone network adopts a multi-path redundancy design; a failure of a single channel does not affect overall connectivity.
2. **Plane access network**: within each plane, secondary Anchors and spatial legal principle base stations form the access network of that plane. The access network is responsible for connecting terminal device signals to the Anchor backbone network and distributing backbone information to terminals.
3. **Terminal devices**: cross-plane communication terminals used by cultivators and institutions, including personal communicators, fixed communication stations, embedded communication modules, etc. Terminal devices have built-in micro law codecs and can communicate directly with the access network.

The routing algorithm of the communication network is based on the symbiotic feedback system of the Anchor network, capable of perceiving the load and stability of each channel in real time and dynamically selecting the optimal transmission path.

## IV. Communication Grades and Service Types

Cross-plane communication is divided into multiple service grades according to bandwidth and priority:

| Grade | Name | Bandwidth Range | Typical Applications | Priority |
|------|------|----------|----------|--------|
| Special Grade | Anchor-Dedicated Channel | ≥10^15 bit/s | Anchor synchronization, ascension operations | Highest |
| First Grade | Civilization-Level Communication | 10^12—10^15 bit/s | Inter-plane government communication, emergency broadcasts | High |
| Second Grade | Institution-Level Communication | 10^9—10^12 bit/s | Research institutions, large enterprises, education networks | Medium |
| Third Grade | Commercial Communication | 10^6—10^9 bit/s | Commercial communication, media transmission | Ordinary |
| Fourth Grade | Civilian Communication | ≤10^6 bit/s | Personal communication, daily information | Basic |

All grades of communication services follow the principle of "basic services free, value-added services charged". Basic civilian-level communication services are provided free of charge by the symbiotic civilization, ensuring the implementation of "a path for everyone" in the field of communication; high-bandwidth, high-priority value-added services charge reasonable fees according to energy consumption.

## V. Communication Security and Ethics

The security and ethics of cross-plane communication are subject to strict regulations:

- **Encryption standards**: all cross-plane communication must adopt Anchor-level end-to-end encryption; encryption keys are generated by the true random number generators of the Anchor network and are theoretically unbreakable.
- **Privacy protection**: communication content is protected by both the Symbiotic Constitution and the Consumer Protection Law; no organization or individual may eavesdrop on or intercept communication information without authorization.
- **Information pollution prevention and control**: the communication network is equipped with a law-level information filtering system, preventing dangerous information such as predatory cultivation arts and harmful consciousness patterns from spreading through the communication network.
- **Emergency broadcast authority**: in a civilization-level emergency, symbiotic governance institutions have the authority to issue emergency broadcasts to all planes through special-grade channels; this authority is subject to strict legal supervision.

## Conclusion

Cross-plane communication technology is the "nervous system" of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. It connects life scattered across countless planes into a unified information community, making the feedback and coordination of the symbiotic network possible. With the continuous improvement of communication bandwidth and the continuous reduction of latency, the Hengyuan Universe is evolving toward "real-time shared sensing of all races", laying the communication foundation for collective consciousness symbiosis in the stage of consciousness evolution.
