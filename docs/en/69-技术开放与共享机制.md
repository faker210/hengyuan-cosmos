## I. The Core Concept of Technology Openness

The technology openness and sharing mechanism of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization is the concrete embodiment of the basic program of "more work, more reward; self-growth and self-development; a path for everyone; joint advancement of all races" in the field of technology. Its core concept is: technological knowledge, as a product of the collective evolution of the entire civilization, should be shared by the entire civilization; technological monopoly and knowledge barriers are remnants of the predatory system in the field of technology, and must be eliminated through institutional design.

Technology openness does not mean denying the contributions of technology developers. On the contrary, the symbiotic civilization has established a technology incentive mechanism with "measurable contributions and traceable benefits", ensuring that the labor of developers receives reasonable returns while technological achievements benefit all life forms at the lowest threshold. The essence of this mechanism is to transform technology from a "commodity monopolized by the few" into "infrastructure shared by the entire civilization".

## II. The Tiered Technology Openness System

Technology openness is implemented in tiers according to risk grade and maturity:

| Openness Tier | Applicable Technologies | Openness Method | Access Conditions |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| Fully Open | Mature R0 and R1-grade technologies | All technical documents and design drawings are publicly available free of charge | No threshold; all life forms can access them |
| Licensed Open | Mature R2-grade technologies | Core principles are public; engineering implementation requires a license application | Must pass basic safety training and commit to complying with usage norms |
| Restricted Open | R3-grade technologies | Open only to authorized research institutions, requiring signed safety agreements | Must possess an S-Level isolated space and ethics review qualifications |
| Prohibited Open | R4-grade forbidden technologies | Not open | Fully prohibited |

For fully open technologies, the symbiotic civilization has established an "Open-Source Law Library" — a technological knowledge base similar to open-source software communities in the real world. All developers can submit their technological achievements to the Open-Source Law Library, which opens them to the entire civilization after review by the Technology Committee. Technologies in the Open-Source Law Library adopt the "Symbiotic Sharing License", allowing free use, modification, and redistribution, but derivative works must likewise be shared in an open-source manner.

## III. The Infrastructure for Technology Popularization

To ensure that technology openness does not remain a formality, the symbiotic civilization has built a complete infrastructure for technology popularization:

1. **Technology education system**: the Symbiotic Education System lists basic law engineering knowledge as general education content, ensuring that every life form possesses the ability to understand and apply basic technologies. Advanced technology education is open free of charge to all willing learners.
2. **Technology dissemination network**: relying on the cross-plane communication network, a universe-wide technology dissemination platform is established, including online courses, technology forums, and virtual laboratories, enabling learners in remote planes to obtain the latest technological knowledge.
3. **Public technology facilities**: community Anchors are equipped with public technology laboratories and manufacturing equipment, which any life form can reserve for use, transforming technological knowledge into actual products.
4. **Technology mentor system**: senior technical personnel are obliged to periodically serve as technology mentors, guiding junior learners; this obligation is counted in their symbiotic contribution rating.

## IV. The Measurement and Incentive of Technology Contributions

The sustainability of the technology openness mechanism depends on a reasonable contribution measurement and incentive system:

- **Contribution measurement**: after the technological achievements submitted by developers are collected by the Open-Source Law Library, a "technology contribution rating" is calculated based on their technological value, application scope, and derivative contributions. The contribution rating is an important basis for resource allocation in the Joint-Output Contracting Mechanism.
- **Priority usage rights**: technology developers enjoy priority usage rights of their technological achievements and priority access to advanced services within a certain period, but must not monopolize the licensing rights of the technology.
- **Honor system**: technology developers with major contributions to the entire civilization are awarded honors such as the "Symbiotic Technology Medal", and their achievements are recorded in the civilization's technology history.
- **Ongoing benefits**: for advanced technology applications requiring large amounts of energy, developers may obtain a reasonable proportion of the application proceeds, but the sharing ratio is subject to the upper limits of the Anti-Monopoly Law.

## V. Anti-Monopoly and Technological Fairness

The implementation of the technology openness mechanism is strictly guaranteed by the Anti-Monopoly Law:

- It is prohibited for any organization or individual to monopolize the licensing rights of basic technologies through patent barriers, technological secrecy, or other means.
- "Technology lock-in" behavior is prohibited, i.e., forcing users to depend on specific technology suppliers by deliberately creating incompatible standards.
- Mandatory openness is implemented for key infrastructure technologies (such as Anchor hardware, energy distribution, and cross-plane communication), and their core standards must be jointly formulated by the entire civilization.
- A technology fairness assessment mechanism is established to periodically assess the balance of technology popularization and provide targeted support to planes and groups lagging in technology.

## VI. The Balance Between Technology Openness and Security

A delicate balance must be struck between technology openness and technology security. The approach of the symbiotic civilization is:

- Basic principles and general knowledge are fully open, allowing more people to understand the essence of technology and thereby better identify technological risks.
- Specific parameters and implementation details of high-risk operations are open in a restricted manner, provided only to institutions meeting safety conditions.
- Knowledge of forbidden technologies is strictly confidential, but the ethical analysis of "why they are forbidden" is open and transparent, allowing the entire civilization to understand the meaning of the boundaries' existence.
- A "responsible technology dissemination" code is established, whereby technology disseminators have the obligation to simultaneously disseminate safe usage methods and ethical boundaries.

## Conclusion

The technology openness and sharing mechanism is one of the key features distinguishing the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization from predatory civilizations. In the predatory system, technology is a tool for the strong to suppress the weak; in the symbiotic civilization, technology is the ladder for the joint evolution of all races. Through tiered openness, popularization infrastructure, reasonable incentives, and anti-monopoly guarantees, the symbiotic civilization is building a technological ecosystem of "free flow of knowledge, universal enhancement of capabilities, and fair rewards for contributions", providing a steady stream of innovation momentum for the ascension of the civilization as a whole.
