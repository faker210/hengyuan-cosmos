## I. The Basic Characteristics of the Pre-Anchor Era

The Pre-Anchor Era is the longest and darkest era in the history of the Hengyuan Universe. In this era, no unified Anchor mechanism existed in the universe; the origin laws were in a state of natural dispersion, and the distribution of energy and resources entirely followed the law of the jungle — "the strong take all". The Predatory Cultivation System was the absolute dominant force of this era; cultivation methods were monopolized by a few powerhouses and sects, and the lower life forms had almost no formal evolutionary path.

The core characteristics of the Pre-Anchor Era can be summarized as the "Five Absences":

- **No Anchors**: no stable law anchors, disordered energy flow, fragile plane barriers.
- **No Order**: no unified civilization institutions, constant wars between planes, frequent genocide.
- **No Pathways**: no cross-plane communication and transportation technologies; planes were basically isolated from one another.
- **No Safeguards**: no public safeguard systems such as education, medical care, and housing; the survival of life depended entirely on individual strength.
- **No Hope**: the evolutionary channels of lower life forms were completely blocked, and class stratification reached its extreme.

## II. The Operating Mechanism of the Predatory Cultivation System

The cultivation system of the Pre-Anchor Era operated on predation as its core mechanism. Its basic logic was: the origin energy of the universe is finite, and the evolution of an individual necessarily comes at the cost of consuming the energy of others. The strong achieve their own ascension by devouring the energy, fortune, and even the origin of the weak, while the weak are reduced to "cultivation resources" for the strong.

The typical forms of the predatory system include:

| Predation Type | Method of Operation | Harm to the Universe |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Energy Predation | Directly extracting the cultivation energy of other life forms | Causes individual death or complete loss of cultivation |
| Fortune Predation | Seizing the fortune and opportunities of others through arts or rituals | Destroys causal balance, triggers plane-level calamities |
| Origin Predation | Devouring fragments of the origin laws of other life forms | Causes irreversible loss of evolutionary potential |
| Plane Predation | Powerhouses conquering entire planes, using plane life as cultivation resources | Leads to civilization extinction and plane desolation |
| Law Predation | Forcibly seizing control of a plane's origin laws | Damages the law network, triggers spatial collapse |

In the short term, the predatory system may produce a few supreme powerhouses, but in the long run, it continuously consumes the evolutionary potential of the universe, accelerating the overall entropy increase of the universe and causing frequent civilization ruptures.

## III. Civilization Ruptures and Cyclical Destruction

The most notable historical phenomenon of the Pre-Anchor Era is the "civilization rupture". Due to the inherent contradictions of the predatory system, every once-glorious civilization ultimately moved toward its own self-destruction:

1. **Rise Period**: a race or sect rises rapidly through predation, establishing a powerful civilization.
2. **Peak Period**: the civilization reaches the pinnacle of power, suppressing resistance within and expanding predation outward.
3. **Internal Strife Period**: the powerhouses within the civilization engage in internal strife over limited resources, depleting their strength.
4. **Collapse Period**: internal strife greatly weakens the civilization, allowing external predators to strike while it is vulnerable; the civilization goes extinct.
5. **Rupture Period**: after the civilization's extinction, most of its knowledge and technology is lost, and subsequent civilizations must start from zero.

This cycle of "rise—peak—internal strife—collapse—rupture" repeated countless times in the Pre-Anchor Era. Each cycle consumed a vast amount of the cosmic origin, leaving the overall evolutionary level of the universe stagnant or even regressive for a long time.

## IV. The Omens of the End of the Pre-Anchor Era

In the late Pre-Anchor Era, a series of omens heralding change appeared in the universe:

- **Intensifying law disorder**: long-term predation severely damaged the law network, spatial collapse events became frequent, and plane barriers thinned.
- **Exhaustion of life potential**: the origin energy available for predation grew scarcer, and resource struggles among powerhouses became white-hot.
- **The germination of anti-predation thought**: some visionary thinkers began to reflect on the fundamental defects of the predatory system and put forward preliminary conceptions of symbiotic evolution.
- **Increased contact between different planes**: the thinning of plane barriers increased contact between different planes, making cross-plane exchange of thought possible.
- **The appearance of first-generation Anchor candidates**: in certain corners of the universe, special life forms capable of natural resonance with the law network appeared — the precursors of the future first-generation Anchor awakeners.

These omens indicated that the predatory system had reached its end, and the universe was calling for an entirely new form of civilization.

## V. The Historical Legacy of the Pre-Anchor Era

Although the Pre-Anchor Era was dark, it was not without value. It left an important historical legacy for the subsequent Anchor Symbiotic Civilization:

- **The legacy of lessons**: the repeated failures of the predatory system provided the most profound negative example for the symbiotic civilization, proving that the path of predation leads nowhere.
- **The legacy of knowledge**: although a vast amount of knowledge was lost in civilization ruptures, some cultivation methods and law comprehension survived through various means, becoming source material for the Three Hundred Sets of Origin Cultivation Systems.
- **The legacy of life**: the various life forms that survived the long dark era constituted the racial foundation of the future symbiosis of all races.
- **The legacy of will**: the enduring longing of lower life forms for fairness and hope was the most fundamental spiritual driving force of Anchor awakening.

## Conclusion

The Pre-Anchor Era is the "dark eve" of the history of the Hengyuan Universe. Its length and darkness precisely set off the preciousness and inevitability of the birth of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. Understanding the Pre-Anchor Era is understanding the historical root of the symbiotic civilization's fundamental stance of "opposing predation and advocating symbiosis" — this is not abstract moral preaching, but a lesson of blood and tears paid for by the destruction of countless civilizations.
