## I. The Strategic Positioning of the 20% Activation Strategy

The 20% Activation Strategy is the core evolutionary strategy implemented by the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization after the comprehensive opening of the Three Hundred Sets of Systems. Its goal is to activate 20% of the life forms of the entire universe as cultivators at the level of Supreme Powers of the Plane within a set time, thereby accumulating sufficient energy and experience for the civilization's first ascension.

The proposal of this strategy is based on a core judgment: the overall ascension of the universe requires a sufficient number of high-level cultivators as "ascension carriers". If the proportion of high-level cultivators is too low, the ascension energy will be insufficient and the ascension process cannot be sustained; if one pursues 100% simultaneous activation, resources would be dispersed, the cycle too long, and forcing the activation of a large number of life forms with weak foundations before they are ready would instead create safety risks. 20% is the optimal proportion determined after extensive deduction by the Multiplication Fruit Deduction Engine — sufficient to support ascension while balancing fairness and safety.

## II. The Implementation Principles of the Activation Strategy

The implementation of the 20% Activation Strategy follows the following principles:

| Principle | Connotation |
|------|------|
| Principle of Voluntariness | Activation is entirely based on individual willingness; no life form may be forced to participate in high-level activation |
| Principle of Fairness | Activation opportunities are equally open to all life forms, with open and transparent selection criteria |
| Principle of Safety | The activation process must be conducted under strict safety monitoring, ensuring no irreversible harm is caused |
| Principle of Giving Back | Activated powerhouses have the obligation to give back to the civilization and guide more life forms in cultivation |
| Principle of Dynamism | 20% is a stage goal; the activation ratio will be gradually raised in the future |

## III. The Implementation Stages of the Activation Strategy

The 20% Activation Strategy is implemented in three stages:

### 3.1 The Selection and Preparation Stage (5 years)

- An "Activation Candidate Assessment System" was established, comprehensively assessing candidates across dimensions such as cultivation willingness, foundational cultivation, psychological quality, and health status.
- Approximately 5% of life forms were selected in the first batch as the "Vanguard Activation Group", prioritizing individuals with an existing cultivation foundation and stable psychological quality.
- Concentrated training was provided to candidates, including advanced cultivation methods, safety knowledge, and ethics norms.
- Activation simulation drills were conducted in isolated spaces, ensuring candidates were familiar with the activation process.

### 3.2 The Batch Activation Stage (20 years)

- Activation was carried out in batches in the order of "vanguard group → main-force group → expansion group".
- The Vanguard Group (5%) completed activation first, verifying the safety and effectiveness of the activation process.
- The Main-Force Group (10%) underwent activation based on the experience of the Vanguard Group, on a larger scale.
- The Expansion Group (5%) completed activation last, focusing on individuals who previously had weaker foundations but greater potential.
- Each batch of activation was separated by an interval of 2–3 years, used to summarize experience, optimize processes, and digest risks.

### 3.3 The Consolidation and Giving-Back Stage (ongoing)

- Activated Supreme Powers of the Plane received continuous health monitoring and cultivation guidance.
- A "Powerhouse Mentor System" was established: every activated powerhouse has the obligation to guide a certain number of junior cultivators.
- The experience and data accumulated during the activation process were fed back into the cultivation system optimization process.
- Preparation for the next round of activation was initiated, gradually advancing the activation ratio from 20% toward a higher level.

## IV. The Technical Implementation of Activation

The activation of a Supreme Power of the Plane is a complex technical process involving the coordination of multiple technical systems:

1. **Energy supply**: the activation process requires a large amount of high-purity origin energy, supplied directly by the Plane Main Anchor to ensure stable energy.
2. **Law guidance**: high-level cultivators of the spatial legal principle dispatch system conduct law guidance, helping activators establish a deep connection with the origin laws.
3. **Deduction assistance**: the Multiplication Fruit Deduction Engine deduces the optimal activation path for activators in real time, reducing the risk of Deviation.
4. **Consciousness guardianship**: cultivators of consciousness ascension systems form a consciousness guardianship team, protecting the stability of the activator's consciousness during the activation process.
5. **Medical safeguards**: a cultivation medical team remains on standby throughout, responding to physical reactions that may occur during activation.

The core mechanism of activation is: through directional energy infusion and law guidance of the Anchor network, help cultivators break through their own evolutionary bottlenecks, achieving the leap from ordinary cultivator to Supreme Power of the Plane. This process is not "instilling power" but "activating potential" — the activator must possess the corresponding foundation and willingness; external forces serve only as catalysis and guidance.

## V. The Social Impact of the Activation Strategy

The implementation of the 20% Activation Strategy had a profound impact on the society of the Hengyuan Universe:

- **Change in the balance of power**: the emergence of a large number of Supreme Powers of the Plane completely changed the balance of power in the universe, and the remaining predatory forces lost their last advantage in strength.
- **Accelerated technological development**: the activated powerhouses greatly improved their capabilities in law comprehension and technological research, driving rapid breakthroughs in law engineering layer technologies.
- **Enhanced social mobility**: activation selection provided lower life forms with a clear upward channel, extending "a path for everyone" from basic cultivation to high-level evolution.
- **Enhanced civilizational cohesion**: the shared experience of participating in the activation strategy strengthened the sense of identity and solidarity among all ethnic groups.
- **Maturation of ascension conditions**: the achievement of the 20% activation goal provided the necessary energy foundation and experience accumulation for the first ascension.

## VI. Ethical Challenges in the Activation Strategy

The 20% Activation Strategy also faced ethical challenges during implementation:

- **The psychological gap of the unactivated**: how to ensure that the 80% of life forms not activated do not feel abandoned is an important topic of social governance. Countermeasures include: clarifying that 20% is a stage goal, strengthening basic cultivation safeguards, and establishing a queued activation mechanism.
- **Power constraints on the powerful**: the emergence of a large number of powerhouses requires corresponding power constraint mechanisms to prevent abuse of power. The Anti-Monopoly Law and cultivation ethics norms were further strengthened during this period.
- **Fair distribution of activation resources**: the allocation of activation quotas among different races and regions must be fair and transparent, preventing a Matthew effect of "the strong get stronger".

## Conclusion

The implementation of the 20% Activation Strategy is a key step for the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization from "popularization of cultivation" to "elite ascension". It is not meant to create a new privileged class, but to open the way for the ascension of the entire civilization through phased, focused breakthroughs. The activated 20% are not the "masters" of the civilization but its "vanguard" — their mission is to use the power they have obtained to pave the way forward for the remaining 80% of life forms. When the 20% activation goal is achieved, the conditions for the first ascension will have matured.
