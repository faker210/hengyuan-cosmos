## I. The Historical Significance of the First Ascension

The first ascension is the most milestone-like event in the history of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. It marked the Hengyuan Universe's formal transition from the "technological empowerment" stage into the "consciousness evolution" stage, verifying the core hypothesis that the symbiotic evolutionary path can ultimately achieve the ascension of the civilization as a whole. Before this, ascension was only a theoretical goal and a conclusion of deduction; after this, ascension became a reality verified by practice.

The first ascension was not a one-time ascension of the entire universe, but an "ascension experiment" piloting a single major plane. Choosing a single plane to ascend first could both control risks and accumulate experience for the subsequent ascension of the entire universe. After ascending, this pilot plane became the "ascension demonstration plane" of the Hengyuan Universe, and its experience was systematically summarized and promoted to other planes.

## II. The Preparatory Work Before the Ascension

The preparatory work for the first ascension lasted several decades and involved all aspects of the civilization:

| Preparation Area | Core Work | Completion Standard |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Energy preparation | Anchor network energy reserves reached the ascension threshold | Main Anchor energy reserves ≥ 100 Giga-Anchor Energy |
| Personnel preparation | The 20% Activation Strategy completed, Supreme Powers of the Plane in position | Activation ratio reached 20%, powerhouse team assembled |
| Technical preparation | Ascension device development completed, verified in isolated space | Ascension device passed R3-level safety certification |
| Law preparation | The law network of the target plane calibrated and reinforced | Law stability ≥ 99.9% |
| Social preparation | Ascension science popularization completed, public psychological preparation sufficient | Ascension awareness popularization rate ≥ 95% |
| Emergency plan | Emergency plans for ascension failure completed | Three-level emergency plans verified through drills |

The most critical part of the ascension preparation was the development of the "ascension device". This device, based on the highest realm (Source Void) of the spatial legal principle dispatch system and the core technologies of the consciousness ascension systems, could, with the support of the Anchor network, synchronously elevate the consciousness of all life in the plane and the law structure to a higher dimension.

## III. The Key Nodes of the Ascension Process

The first ascension process lasted about forty-nine days (main-plane standard time) and was divided into five key nodes:

### 3.1 The Initiation Node (Day 1)

- The first-generation Anchor personally initiated the ascension procedure at the Universal Main Anchor.
- The ascension device established law resonance with the main anchor of the target plane.
- The entire plane entered the ascension preparation state, and routine cultivation activities were suspended.

### 3.2 The Energy Infusion Node (Days 7–14)

- The Anchor network directionally infused high-purity origin energy into the target plane.
- The plane's energy density gradually rose to the ascension threshold.
- The "Ascension Guidance Corps", composed of Supreme Powers of the Plane, began guiding the flow of energy.

### 3.3 The Law Reconstruction Node (Days 15–28)

- The ascension device began reconstructing the law network structure of the target plane.
- Core laws such as space, time, and consciousness gradually transformed toward higher-dimensional forms.
- Isolated space technology was used to buffer fluctuations during the law reconstruction process.

### 3.4 The Consciousness Sublimation Node (Days 29–40)

- The collective consciousness of the plane's life forms began migrating toward the consciousness domain.
- Deeper symbiotic connections were established between individual consciousnesses.
- The Multiplication Fruit Deduction Engine monitored the consciousness sublimation process in real time, ensuring stability.

### 3.5 The Stabilization and Consolidation Node (Days 41–49)

- The law structure and consciousness forms after ascension gradually stabilized.
- The Anchor network established new connection protocols with the ascended plane.
- The ascension was declared successful, entering the subsequent adaptation and consolidation period.

## IV. The Changes After the Ascension

After the success of the first ascension, a series of fundamental changes occurred in the target plane:

| Dimension | Before the Ascension | After the Ascension |
|------|--------|--------|
| Spatial structure | Predominantly three-dimensional material space | Higher-dimensional spatial structure, more natural spatial manipulation |
| Consciousness level | Individual consciousness dominant, collective consciousness weak | Individual and collective consciousness in symbiosis, telepathy universalized |
| Energy utilization | Reliance on external energy supply | Life can draw energy directly from the law network |
| Lifespan limit | Limited by the material body | Lifespan greatly extended after consciousness transformation |
| Communication method | Reliance on technological devices | Direct communication through the consciousness domain became possible |
| Cultivation method | Gradual cultivation | Direct fusion of consciousness and laws, qualitative leap in cultivation efficiency |

The ascended plane did not depart from the material world; rather, the relationship between matter and consciousness underwent a fundamental transformation — consciousness is no longer an appendage of matter, but a form of existence that coexists with matter in equality and symbiosis.

## V. The Summary and Promotion of the Ascension Experience

After the success of the first ascension, the symbiotic civilization immediately launched the work of summarizing and promoting the experience:

- An "Ascension Experience Committee" was established to systematically organize all data and experience from the ascension process.
- The ascension procedure was standardized, forming a replicable "Ascension Operation Manual".
- In-depth analysis was conducted on the problems and risks that emerged during the ascension, perfecting the emergency plans.
- Preparation for the ascension of the second batch of planes was initiated, gradually expanding the scope of ascension.
- The ascension experience was fed back into the optimization of the cultivation systems, improving the cultivation efficiency of planes not yet ascended.

The success of the first ascension also directly promoted the founding of the Myriad Races Republic — the experience of the ascension demonstration plane proved that only with the unity of all races and unified institutions could the ascension process be advanced efficiently.

## Conclusion

The First Ascension Milestone is the "coming-of-age ceremony" of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. It proved that the symbiotic evolutionary path is not only valid in theory but also feasible in practice. When the news of the first plane's successful ascension spread across the universe through the Anchor network, countless life forms saw their own future direction — ascension was no longer an unreachable myth, but a goal achievable through joint effort. This milestone laid a solid foundation of confidence for the founding of the Myriad Races Republic and the opening of the Cross-Plane Exploration Era.
