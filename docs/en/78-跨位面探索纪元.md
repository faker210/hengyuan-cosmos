## I. The Conditions for the Opening of the Cross-Plane Exploration Era

The Cross-Plane Exploration Era is a new era opened after the founding of the Myriad Races Republic, corresponding to the stage of transition from "consciousness evolution" to "interstellar symbiosis" in the four-stage ascension path. The opening of this era required the simultaneous maturation of three conditions:

- **Technological condition**: spatial legal principle technology reached the "Source Void" realm, making the stable opening of cross-plane passages possible; the Replication Mirror and isolated space technologies matured, providing technical safeguards for low-pollution exploration.
- **Institutional condition**: the Myriad Races Republic was founded, and unified cross-plane exploration policies and the archaeological protocol were completed.
- **Motivational condition**: the ascension work of known planes advanced in an orderly manner, and the civilization had both the capability and the willingness to expand toward unknown planes.

When these three conditions matured simultaneously, the Myriad Races Republic officially announced the launch of the "Grand Exploration Plan", and the Cross-Plane Exploration Era thus began.

## II. The Basic Principles of Cross-Plane Exploration

Cross-plane exploration strictly follows the following basic principles, which are written into the "Basic Law of Cross-Plane Exploration":

| Principle | Connotation |
|------|------|
| Observe first, then interfere within limits | Unknown planes are first subjected to passive observation; only after full understanding are limited contact and interference carried out |
| Low-pollution exploration | Adopts the archaeological scheme of Replication Mirror + isolated space, transmitting only knowledge back, bringing no matter or life in or out |
| Protection of indigenous civilizations | For planes with indigenous civilizations, respect their right to independent development; must not forcibly impose the symbiotic system |
| Resource sharing | The knowledge and resources discovered through exploration belong to the entire civilization; no organization may monopolize exploration results |
| Safety first | Safety is always given top priority during exploration; exploration of high-risk planes must undergo strict risk assessment |

## III. The Technical Means of Exploration

Cross-plane exploration comprehensively employed multiple core technologies of the symbiotic civilization:

1. **Plane scanning technology**: remote plane scanning based on spatial legal principle perception modules can obtain basic information about a target plane without contacting it (spatial structure, energy level, signs of life, etc.).
2. **Replication Mirror archaeological scheme**: performs high-precision mirror replication of the target plane, conducting in-depth research on the mirror replica in isolated space, transmitting only knowledge back.
3. **Cross-plane communication probes**: miniature law communication devices that can be sent into the target plane for close observation, with data transmitted back in real time through the cross-plane communication network.
4. **Consciousness domain perception**: high-level cultivators remotely perceive the target plane through the consciousness domain, obtaining consciousness-level information that scanning technology cannot capture.
5. **Remote Anchor deployment**: for planes with a higher safety rating, temporary Anchors can be remotely deployed to establish stable connection channels.

## IV. The Organization and Implementation of Exploration

Cross-plane exploration is organized and implemented in a unified manner by the "Cross-Plane Exploration Bureau" of the Myriad Races Republic, adopting a "graded exploration" model:

| Exploration Grade | Target Type | Exploration Method | Personnel Configuration |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| First-Level Exploration | Lifeless barren planes | Remote scanning + Replication Mirror research | Technical teams primarily |
| Second-Level Exploration | Planes with low-level life | Replication Mirror archaeology + limited on-site investigation | Technical teams + biologists |
| Third-Level Exploration | Planes with indigenous civilizations | Observe first, then contact, strictly following the contact protocol | Full team + diplomats + ethics observers |
| Fourth-Level Exploration | High-risk/unknown planes | Only simulated research in isolated space, on-site contact prohibited | Highest-level security team |

Every exploration project must go through the complete process of "exploration application — risk assessment — ethics review — approval and implementation — process monitoring — benefit sharing".

## V. The Major Discoveries of the Exploration Era

After the opening of the Cross-Plane Exploration Era, the symbiotic civilization achieved a series of major discoveries:

- **Discovery of new races**: a large number of previously unknown intelligent races were discovered in unknown planes, greatly expanding the genealogy of the myriad races. These new races gradually joined the Myriad Races Republic after voluntary choice.
- **Discovery of new laws**: the law structures of different planes differ, and many new law variants were discovered during exploration, enriching the theoretical foundation of the Three Hundred Sets of Cultivation Systems.
- **Ruins of ancient civilizations**: the ruins of multiple extinct civilizations from the Pre-Anchor Era were discovered, and a large amount of precious historical knowledge was obtained through Replication Mirror archaeology.
- **Symbiotic civilizations of other planes**: on certain planes, indigenous civilizations that independently developed the concept of symbiosis were discovered, forming a resonance of thought across space with the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization.
- **Exploration of the universe's boundary**: exploration gradually touched the known boundary of the Hengyuan Universe, continuously deepening the understanding of the universe's overall structure.

## VI. Challenges and Reflections in Exploration

Cross-plane exploration was not smooth sailing; the process also encountered challenges:

- **Cultural conflicts**: cultural misunderstandings and conflicts arose in contact with indigenous civilizations, prompting continuous improvement of the contact protocol.
- **Exploration ethics controversies**: there were ongoing ethical debates on "whether indigenous civilizations should be proactively contacted", ultimately forming the consensus of "not proactive, not avoidant, respect choices".
- **Safety incidents**: a barrier breach of isolated space occurred during exploration of a high-risk plane; although it did not cause serious consequences, it drove further improvement of safety standards.
- **Resource allocation**: large-scale exploration consumes substantial resources, and the balance between exploration investment and investment in local livelihood became the focus of political debate.

## Conclusion

The Cross-Plane Exploration Era is the "Age of Great Voyages" of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. It extended the civilization's horizons from known planes to the entire universe, deepening the understanding of itself while discovering new worlds. The low-pollution exploration scheme of Replication Mirror + isolated space makes the symbiotic civilization's exploration fundamentally different from all predatory colonial expansion in history — what we bring is not conquest and predation, but respect and knowledge; what we bring back is not resources and slaves, but understanding and wisdom. When enough planes have been explored and the Anchor network is dense enough, the gate of the Interstellar Symbiosis Era will slowly open.
