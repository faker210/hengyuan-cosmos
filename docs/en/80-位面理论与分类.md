## I. The Basic Definition of Planes

Within the framework of the Wanling Hengmao Origin Universe (Hengyuan Universe), a "plane" refers to a region of existence defined by a relatively stable set of laws, possessing an independent spatiotemporal continuity. A plane is not simply "another world", but a concretized projection of the law network within different parameter intervals. Every plane possesses its unique physical constants, energy density, time flow rate, and consciousness activity level, and these parameters together constitute the plane's "law fingerprint".

The existence of planes originates from the non-uniformity of the law network of the Hengyuan Universe. During the operation of the origin laws of the universe, due to differences in energy distribution, consciousness disturbance, and historical evolution, countless law regions with varying parameters naturally formed. These regions are separated by "law barriers", whose strength depends on the degree of difference in law parameters between adjacent planes — the greater the difference, the thicker the barrier, and the higher the difficulty of crossing.

The Anchor mechanism holds a central position in plane theory. An Anchor is essentially a stable node in the law network; it can not only maintain the law stability of its own plane, but also serve as the anchoring benchmark for cross-plane connections. All interactive behaviors such as cross-plane travel, communication, and archaeology must use the Anchor as the coordinate origin for positioning and calibration.

## II. The Plane Classification System

The planes within the Hengyuan Universe are divided into the following five major categories according to law parameters, energy levels, and the degree of civilizational development:

| Category | Law Characteristics | Energy Density | Typical Civilizational Form | Travel Difficulty |
|------|----------|----------|--------------|----------|
| Material-Dominant Plane | Physical laws dominant, energy latent | Low | Technological civilization, beginner cultivation civilization | Low |
| Energy-Manifest Plane | Energy laws semi-manifest, cultivation systems mature | Medium | Mid-to-high cultivation civilization, magical civilization | Medium |
| Consciousness-Dominant Plane | Consciousness laws manifest, matter shapeable by will | High | Spirit-body civilization, consciousness-aggregate civilization | High |
| Law-Fractured Plane | Laws unstable, numerous law rifts | Extremely high and chaotic | Untamed planes, ruins planes | Extremely high |
| Origin-Adjacent Plane | Close to the origin laws of the universe, parameters approaching limits | Approaching the origin | Supreme civilization, plane guardians | Theoretical limit |

In addition to the above five major categories, there are also some special plane types, including: Mirror Planes (parallel existences highly symmetric in law parameters with a certain plane), Interstitial Planes (transitional regions located between the law barriers of two main planes), Dormant Planes (ancient planes with extremely low law activity, nearly stagnant), and Nascent Planes (young planes newly formed in the law network, with parameters not yet stabilized).

## III. Plane Coordinates and the Principle of Positioning

The prerequisite of cross-plane interaction is precise plane positioning. The Hengyuan Universe has established a "four-dimensional plane coordinate system" based on the Anchor, with each plane uniquely determined by four parameters:

1. **Law fingerprint code**: a unique identifier generated by hashing the plane's core law parameters, 128 bits in length.
2. **Anchor association degree**: the connection strength between the plane and the known Anchor network, ranging from 0 to 1.
3. **Time flow ratio**: the ratio of the plane's time flow to the standard time of the Hengyuan Universe, commonly ranging from 0.1 to 10.
4. **Number of spatial dimensions**: the number of perceivable spatial dimensions of the plane, usually 3 to 11 dimensions.

The methods of obtaining plane coordinates include: automatic detection by the Anchor network, backtracking of cross-plane communication signals, coordinate remnants in archaeological ruins, and manual records by plane travelers. All confirmed plane coordinates are entered into the "Hengyuan Universe Plane Coordinate Master Database", uniformly maintained by the Plane Administration under the Anchor Assembly.

## IV. Natural Interactions Between Planes

Even without active travel, natural interaction phenomena exist between planes. The most common is "law seepage" — due to locally weak barriers between adjacent planes, a small number of law parameters seep into each other, causing local anomalous phenomena. For example, the occasional "supernatural events" in material-dominant planes are often caused by law seepage from a neighboring energy-manifest plane.

Another natural interaction is "plane resonance". When the law fingerprint codes of two planes have a high degree of similarity, periodic law resonance occurs between them, manifesting as similar historical events, civilizational trajectories, and even personal destinies appearing in the two planes during specific time periods. Plane resonance is an important research subject of plane archaeology; by analyzing resonance patterns, the civilizational state of unknown planes can be inferred.

## Conclusion

Plane theory is the theoretical cornerstone of all practices of cross-plane interaction. Accurately understanding the essence, classification, and positioning principles of planes is the prerequisite for formulating travel rules, ethical guidelines, and prevention and control mechanisms. The plane exploration of the Hengyuan Universe always follows the principle of "cognition first, interaction with caution", ensuring that while expanding the boundaries of civilization, no irreversible interference is caused to the autonomous evolution of any plane.
