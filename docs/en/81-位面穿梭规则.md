## I. The Legal Positioning of Travel Behavior

Plane travel refers to the behavior of an individual or group of living beings crossing a law barrier from one plane into another through technical means or cultivation ability. Within the legal framework of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, plane travel is classified as a "high-risk special behavior" and must be subject to strict permit management and procedural supervision. Article 47 of the Symbiotic Constitution clearly stipulates: "No organization or individual may conduct cross-plane travel without authorization, nor use plane travel to engage in activities of predation, interference, or pollution against the civilization of other planes."

The fundamental reason why plane travel requires strict control is that the travel behavior itself may cause multiple impacts on the target plane: law disturbance, energy injection, consciousness pollution, microbial invasion, and cultural interference. These impacts may, at the lightest, trigger local anomalies in the target plane, and at the worst, alter its civilizational evolution trajectory, or even lead to the collapse of its laws. Therefore, the core goal of the travel rules is "to control the external impact of travel behavior within an acceptable threshold."

## II. The Hierarchical System of Travel Permits

The Hengyuan Universe implements a hierarchical system of plane travel permits, dividing permits into four levels according to the purpose of travel, the type of target plane, and the qualifications of the traveler:

| Permit Level | Applicable Scenario | Approving Body | Validity | Additional Conditions |
|----------|----------|----------|--------|----------|
| Level A (Archaeological Observation) | Purely observational archaeology, no physical contact of any kind | Plane Administration | Single mission | Must adopt the Replication Mirror + isolated space scheme |
| Level B (Academic Exchange) | Limited knowledge exchange with the civilization of the target plane | Plane Administration + Ministry of Culture | Up to one year | A mutual non-interference agreement must be signed |
| Level C (Resource Collaboration) | Resource and technology collaboration on the premise of equality and mutual benefit | Special approval of the Anchor Assembly | Up to three years | Must obtain the consent of the civilization of the target plane |
| Level D (Emergency Intervention) | Humanitarian intervention when the target plane faces an extinction-level crisis | Emergency resolution of the Anchor Assembly | Duration of the crisis | A complete intervention report must be submitted afterward |

Travel permits at any level prohibit the following behaviors: plundering the origin energy of the target plane, forcibly spreading one's own plane's ideology, conducting genetic or consciousness modification without consent, establishing permanent military bases in the target plane, and any form of colonial rule.

## III. Travel Technical Requirements and Energy Thresholds

Plane travel must satisfy strict technical conditions. First, the traveler must possess cultivation above the third level (the Huàkōng Realm) of the "spatial legal principle dispatch system", or ride a plane travel vehicle that meets the standards. Second, both the starting point and the destination of travel must have an Anchor for coordinate calibration; blind travel without Anchor positioning is strictly prohibited.

The energy consumption of the travel process is proportional to the strength of the law barrier of the target plane. The Hengyuan Universe has established a standardized "travel energy threshold table". The minimum energy required for travel to different types of planes is as follows:

- Material-Dominant Plane: 1,000 standard energy units
- Energy-Manifest Plane: 5,000 standard energy units
- Consciousness-Dominant Plane: 20,000 standard energy units
- Law-Fractured Plane: 50,000 standard energy units (with a success rate below 30%)
- Origin-Adjacent Plane: theoretical value above 100,000; no successful record to date

Travel energy must be carried by the traveler or the vehicle itself; extracting energy from the target plane for the travel process is prohibited. This provision is designed to prevent travel behavior from causing energy predation on the target plane.

## IV. Pollution Prevention and Control in the Travel Process

One of the greatest risks of plane travel is "cross-plane pollution", including biological pollution (carrying microorganisms from one's own plane into the target plane), energy pollution (interference of one's own plane's energy residue with the laws of the target plane), and consciousness pollution (the influence of the traveler's consciousness fluctuations on the consciousness domain of the target plane).

To prevent and control pollution, all travelers must complete the "three-level purification procedure" before departure:

1. **Biological purification**: conducting 72 hours of microorganism screening and elimination in an isolated space to ensure that no pathogens from one's own plane are carried.
2. **Energy purification**: adjusting the traveler's energy frequency to a range compatible with the target plane through the Anchor calibration device, reducing energy repulsion reactions.
3. **Consciousness purification**: undergoing consciousness shielding training to ensure that no thought fluctuations that might interfere with the consciousness domain of the target plane are actively released during travel.

In addition, the traveler's range of activity in the target plane is strictly limited to the "area designated in the travel permit", and unauthorized dispersal is prohibited. After returning to one's own plane, the traveler must undergo quarantine observation again, for a period no less than 30 days converted according to the time flow rate of the target plane.

## Conclusion

The plane travel rules are the first line of safety defense for cross-plane interaction. Through the triple mechanisms of permit classification, technical thresholds, and pollution prevention and control, they ensure that travel behavior always operates on the track of "low impact, traceability, and accountability". The ultimate purpose of the rules is not to restrict exchanges between civilizations, but to make exchanges occur in the way of symbiosis, rather than in the way of predation.
