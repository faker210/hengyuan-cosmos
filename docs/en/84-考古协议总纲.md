## I. The Positioning and Goals of the Archaeology Protocol

Cross-plane archaeology and time-regression archaeology are important means by which the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization acquires historical knowledge and understands the evolution of the universe. However, archaeological behavior itself carries a high degree of risk — directly entering the target plane or target period for archaeological excavation may cause physical damage, law disturbance, and cultural pollution to the target object. To regulate archaeological behavior and reduce archaeological risk, the Hengyuan Universe has formulated the unified "Archaeology Protocol Outline" as the fundamental operational specification for all cross-plane and time-regression archaeological activities.

The core goals of the archaeology protocol can be summarized as "three maximums": maximizing the acquisition of complete historical knowledge, minimizing interference and damage to the target object, and maximizing the safety of archaeological personnel. There is an inherent tension among these three goals — the completeness of knowledge acquisition often requires deeper contact, while the low-interference principle limits the depth of contact. All the institutional design of the archaeology protocol is aimed at finding the optimal balance among the three.

The archaeology protocol applies to the following scenarios: archaeological investigation of civilizational relics in unknown planes, historical restoration of extinct civilizations through time regression, deep archaeology at the law level of ancient relics in one's own plane, and rescue excavation of ancient remains in law-fractured planes.

## II. The Three Elements of the Archaeological Scheme

The standard archaeological scheme of the Hengyuan Universe consists of three core elements, known as the "three elements of archaeology":

### 2.1 Replication Mirror

The Replication Mirror is a high-precision replication device based on the laws of spatial legal principles, capable of performing a complete 1:1 replication of the material, energy, and information structures within the target area without touching the original object. The working principle of the Replication Mirror is to scan all particle states and energy field distributions of the target area through spatial law scanning, and then reconstruct a completely identical copy in an isolated space. The replication process does not change any state of the original object, and therefore causes no physical interference to the archaeological site.

The replication precision of the Replication Mirror reaches the sub-atomic level, capable of completely restoring the material structures, energy residues, consciousness imprints, and even fragments of causal chains within the target area. The replication range can be adjusted according to archaeological needs; the smallest replication unit is a single artifact, and the largest can cover an entire relic group. The replication time depends on the size of the replication range; replication of a single artifact is usually completed within minutes, while replication of a large relic group may take several days.

### 2.2 Isolated Space

An isolated space is an independent law region constructed through spatial legal principle dispatch technology, used to house the archaeological copies generated by the Replication Mirror and serve as the working place for archaeological personnel. The isolated space is separated from the target plane or target period by a complete law barrier, ensuring that archaeological operations cause no reverse impact on the original target.

The law parameters inside the isolated space can be customized according to archaeological needs, including: simulating the environmental parameters of the target plane to ensure the stability of artifact copies, setting up special analytical law fields to assist archaeological research, and establishing strict pollution prevention and control fields to prevent the leakage of archaeological personnel's energy and consciousness. The size of the isolated space can be expanded as needed, with the maximum capable of accommodating a city-level relic copy.

### 2.3 Knowledge-Only Retrieval

"Knowledge-only retrieval" is one of the core principles of the archaeology protocol, meaning that during the archaeological process, only abstract knowledge and information may be obtained from the target object; no material, energy, or living beings may be retrieved. After archaeological personnel study the copy in the isolated space, they may only bring back research conclusions, data records, and analysis reports to their own plane in the form of pure information; the copy itself and any material samples extracted from it must be destroyed within the isolated space.

The fundamental purpose of this principle is to prevent cross-plane pollution and timeline disturbance. Even a speck of dust extracted from the copy may carry the law information of the target plane; once it enters the law network of one's own plane, it may trigger unpredictable chain reactions. Therefore, "knowledge-only retrieval" is not a negotiable operational suggestion, but a rigid principle with legal coercive force.

## III. The Standard Procedure of Archaeological Operations

A complete cross-plane or time-regression archaeological operation follows the following standard procedure:

| Stage | Operation Content | Responsible Party | Duration Reference |
|------|----------|--------|----------|
| Stage 1: Project Approval | Submit the archaeological application, clarify the archaeological goal, scope, and scheme, obtain a Level A travel permit or regression permit | Archaeological team → Plane Administration / Time Law Committee | 7–30 days |
| Stage 2: Preliminary Observation | Conduct pure observation of the target area for no less than 72 hours in accordance with the "observe first, then intervene within limits" guideline | Archaeological team | 72+ hours |
| Stage 3: Replication Mirror Deployment | Deploy the Replication Mirror at a safe location near the target area, set the replication range and precision parameters | Technical team | Hours to days |
| Stage 4: Copy Generation | Activate the Replication Mirror to completely copy the target area into the pre-built isolated space | Technical team | Minutes to days |
| Stage 5: Isolated Space Archaeology | Archaeological personnel enter the isolated space to conduct systematic excavation, analysis, and research on the copy | Archaeological team | Days to years |
| Stage 6: Knowledge Organization | Organize archaeological results, form research reports and knowledge databases, ensuring that all results are in pure information form | Archaeological team | Days to months |
| Stage 7: Withdrawal and Destruction | Archaeological personnel withdraw from the isolated space, destroy all copy material and energy residues, and close the isolated space | Technical team + supervision team | Hours |
| Stage 8: Post-Hoc Audit | Submit the complete archaeological report and accept the post-hoc audit of the Time Law Audit Committee or the Plane Administration | Audit committee | Within 30 days |

## IV. Qualification and Ethical Requirements for Archaeological Personnel

Personnel engaged in cross-plane and time-regression archaeology must possess the following qualifications: cultivation above the third level of the spatial legal principle dispatch system, certification of qualification by the Archaeology Ethics Committee, completion of no less than 100 hours of simulated archaeological training, and no record of violating any symbiotic law.

Archaeological personnel must comply with the following ethical requirements: respect the culture and beliefs of the target civilization, and refrain from cultural discrimination or value judgments during research; protect the integrity of the archaeological site, and refrain from destroying relic structures for the sake of obtaining artifacts; truthfully record and report archaeological findings, and refrain from tampering with or concealing archaeological data; and comply with the "knowledge-only retrieval" principle, and refrain from privately carrying any material samples out of the isolated space.

## Conclusion

The Archaeology Protocol Outline is the institutional bridge established by the Hengyuan Universe between knowledge exploration and civilization protection. With the three elements of the Replication Mirror, isolated space, and knowledge-only retrieval as technical support, standardized procedures as the operational framework, and personnel qualifications and ethical requirements as the humanistic guarantee, it constructs an archaeological paradigm of "low pollution, high efficiency, and traceability". This paradigm ensures that the Hengyuan Universe, while pursuing historical truth, always maintains the highest respect for other civilizations and eras.
