## I. The Definition and Functions of Isolated Spaces

An isolated space is an independent enclosed region constructed in the law network through spatial legal principle dispatch technology, and is the core working place of the off-site archaeology scheme of the Replication Mirror. The isolated space is completely separated from the external universe by a complete law barrier, with an independent law parameter system and energy circulation system inside. Its core function is to provide a stable, controllable, and completely isolated research environment for the archaeological copy, while ensuring that all pollution generated during the archaeological process is strictly confined within the isolated space and does not leak to one's own plane or the target plane.

The construction of an isolated space is based on the law operation capability of the fourth level (the Fǎkōng Realm) of the spatial legal principle dispatch system. The builder needs to select a "law blank zone" in the law network — an area where existing law parameters are relatively uniform and there is no plane structure — and then create a spherical or cubic enclosed space in that area through spatial law operations, setting independent law parameters for it. The size of the isolated space can be adjusted according to needs; the volume of isolated spaces for routine archaeology is usually between 1,000 and 100,000 cubic meters.

An isolated space is not a one-time consumable, but reusable infrastructure. The Hengyuan Universe has established standardized "isolated space clusters" near major Anchors for archaeological teams to rent as needed. After each archaeological mission, the isolated space undergoes thorough purification and reset, and can then be put into the next use.

## II. The Overall Framework of the Low-Pollution Procedure

The low-pollution procedure of isolated spaces is a pollution prevention and control system that runs through the entire archaeological process, whose overall framework is divided into three stages: "pre-operation prevention and control, mid-operation monitoring, and post-operation purification", each with clear operational specifications and quality standards. The goal of the low-pollution procedure is to reduce the pollution impact of archaeological operations on the external environment (including both one's own plane and the target plane) to the "law-negligible level" — that is, the pollution amount is lower than the natural fluctuation amplitude of the law network, and cannot be perceived by the law monitoring systems of any plane.

### 2.1 The Pre-Operation Prevention and Control Stage

Pre-operation prevention and control is conducted before the formal start of the archaeological mission, mainly including the following work:

1. **Isolated space selection and customization**: according to the law characteristics of the archaeological target, select or customize an isolated space with corresponding law parameters. The law parameters inside the isolated space should be as close as possible to the environmental parameters of the target plane to ensure the stability of the copy, while maintaining sufficient difference from the law parameters of one's own plane to prevent adverse reactions between copy matter and one's own plane's laws.

2. **Barrier strength detection**: conduct a comprehensive detection of the law barrier of the isolated space to ensure that the integrity and strength of the barrier reach the standard. Barrier strength is measured by the "law permeability coefficient"; the standard requires the permeability coefficient to be lower than 10 to the negative 15th power — that is, the amount of law information passing through the barrier per second does not exceed 10 to the negative 15th power bits. Isolated spaces that fail detection must not be put into use.

3. **Personnel purification**: all archaeological personnel entering the isolated space must complete the "three-level purification procedure" (biological purification, energy purification, consciousness purification) before entry; see Document 81, "Plane Travel Rules". After purification, personnel must wear standard "law isolation suits", which can further block the leakage of energy and consciousness fluctuations from within the personnel's bodies.

4. **Equipment purification**: all equipment and tools brought into the isolated space must also undergo purification treatment, ensuring that the surfaces and interiors of the equipment carry no microorganisms, energy residues, or information fragments from one's own plane. Equipment purification adopts the "law scanning elimination method", stripping all foreign residues on the equipment through high-frequency law scanning.

### 2.2 The Mid-Operation Monitoring Stage

During archaeological operations, the focus of the low-pollution procedure is real-time monitoring and immediate response. A comprehensive pollution monitoring system is deployed inside the isolated space, including:

| Monitoring Type | Monitoring Content | Alarm Threshold | Response Measure |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| Biological pollution monitoring | Whether microorganisms not from the copy appear in the isolated space | 1 foreign microorganism detected | Immediately initiate local sterilization, review personnel purification records |
| Energy pollution monitoring | Whether abnormal fluctuations occur in the energy field of the isolated space | Fluctuation amplitude exceeds 0.01% of the baseline value | Suspend archaeological operations, locate the source of the fluctuation and eliminate it |
| Consciousness pollution monitoring | Whether foreign consciousness signals appear in the consciousness domain of the isolated space | Signal intensity exceeds 10 to the negative 8th power consciousness units | Initiate the consciousness shielding field, review personnel consciousness states |
| Barrier integrity monitoring | Whether fissures or weak points appear in the law barrier | Permeability coefficient exceeds 10 to the negative 12th power | Immediately initiate the barrier repair procedure, emergency evacuation if necessary |

The monitoring system is attended by an independent "pollution supervisor", who does not participate in specific archaeological operations and is exclusively responsible for pollution monitoring and emergency response. Once pollution exceeding the standard is detected, the supervisor has the right to immediately suspend all archaeological operations and initiate the corresponding emergency procedures.

### 2.3 The Post-Operation Purification Stage

After the archaeological mission ends, the isolated space must undergo thorough purification and reset, ensuring that no copy matter, energy residues, or information fragments remain in the isolated space. Post-operation purification is divided into four steps:

1. **Copy destruction**: completely destroy all copy matter in the isolated space through the "law annihilation procedure". The law annihilation procedure completely dismantles the particle structures of the copy matter into base energy, and then guides the energy into the energy circulation system of the isolated space for neutralization. The destruction process must ensure that the material annihilation rate reaches 100%, with no particle residue permitted.

2. **Energy neutralization**: conduct a comprehensive neutralization of the energy field in the isolated space, eliminating all abnormal energy fluctuations generated during the archaeological process, restoring the energy field to the baseline state of the isolated space. Energy neutralization adopts the "reverse wave cancellation method", achieving precise cancellation by generating energy waves with phases opposite to the abnormal fluctuations.

3. **Information clearance**: conduct deep clearance of the consciousness domain and information layer of the isolated space, eliminating all information residues generated during the archaeological process. Information clearance adopts the "law overwriting method", covering the original information residues with random noise information, and then clearing the noise information, ensuring that the cleared information cannot be recovered by any technical means.

4. **Space reset**: after completing the above three steps, conduct an overall law reset of the isolated space, restoring the internal law parameters to the factory standard state. After the reset, the isolated space undergoes a new round of barrier detection and pollution detection; only after passing the inspection can it be returned to storage for standby.

## III. The Classification and Handling of Pollution Accidents

Despite the strict low-pollution procedure, the possibility of pollution accidents cannot be completely ruled out. The Hengyuan Universe divides isolated space pollution accidents into three levels:

- **Level 1 accident (minor pollution)**: a small amount of pollutant matter or energy is confined within the isolated space and does not breach the law barrier. Handling method: conduct local purification within the isolated space, no impact on the external environment, and submit an accident report afterward.

- **Level 2 accident (barrier breach)**: pollution breaches the law barrier, but the amount is small and detected in time, and has not spread to the law network of one's own plane. Handling method: immediately initiate the barrier repair and external purification procedures; the pollution supervisor must submit a detailed accident report to the Plane Administration, and relevant personnel undergo investigation.

- **Level 3 accident (severe diffusion)**: pollution spreads massively into the law network of one's own plane, potentially affecting the law stability or ecological environment of one's own plane. Handling method: immediately initiate the highest-level emergency response, with the Anchor Assembly directly commanding pollution clearance and causal restoration work; the responsible parties will face legal sanctions.

## Conclusion

The low-pollution procedure of isolated spaces is a complete pollution prevention and control system of "prevention first, monitoring as support, purification as the bottom line". Through strict pre-operation prevention and control, real-time mid-operation monitoring, and thorough post-operation purification, it minimizes the pollution risk of archaeological operations. This procedure embodies the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization's sense of responsibility toward the cosmic environment — even in the process of pursuing knowledge, it never pays the price of polluting other spaces.
