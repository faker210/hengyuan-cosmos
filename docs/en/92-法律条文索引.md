## I. Index Description

This index contains the major currently effective legal documents and their core provisions of the Anker Symbiosis Civilization, organized by legal hierarchy and department. The scope of the index covers the "Symbiosis Constitution", "Symbiosis Labor Law", "Consumer Protection Law", as well as five supporting laws on education, medical care, housing, environment, and antitrust. Each index entry lists the legal name, provision number, and a summary of the core content of the provision. This index is a reference tool; for the complete text of legal provisions and judicial interpretations, please refer to the official text of each law.

## III. Symbiosis Labor Law

The "Symbiosis Labor Law" is the basic law regulating labor relations and protecting workers' rights and interests, based on Article 42 of the Symbiosis Constitution as the legislative basis.

| Provision Number | Core Content |
|------------------|--------------|
| Article 1 | Legislative purpose: regulate labor relations, protect the legitimate rights and interests of workers, and build harmonious and symbiotic labor relations |
| Article 2 | Scope of application: all labor relations within Hengyuanzhou, including material labor, cultivation labor, and creative labor |
| Article 3 | Establishes the principle of equal employment for workers; prohibits employment discrimination based on race, gender, cultivation level, or origin |
| Article 4 | Establishes the principle of free choice of occupation for workers; prohibits forced labor and slavery |
| Article 5 | Stipulates the labor contract system; a written labor contract shall be concluded when establishing a labor relationship |
| Article 6 | Stipulates the necessary clauses of a labor contract: contract term, work content, labor remuneration, working hours, labor protection, social insurance |
| Article 7 | Establishes the eight-hour work system and the working hour system of no more than forty-four hours per week |
| Article 8 | Stipulates overtime restrictions: daily overtime shall not exceed one hour, special circumstances shall not exceed three hours, monthly overtime shall not exceed thirty-six hours |
| Article 9 | Stipulates overtime wage standards: 1.5 times wages for working day overtime, 2 times wages for rest day overtime, 3 times wages for statutory holiday overtime |
| Article 10 | Establishes the minimum wage guarantee system; minimum wage standards are formulated by each star domain according to local conditions |
| Article 11 | Stipulates the wage payment system: wages shall be paid monthly to the workers themselves in monetary form, and shall not be deducted or defaulted without reason |
| Article 12 | Establishes the distribution principle of more work more gain; implements a distribution system with distribution according to work as the main body and multiple distribution methods coexisting |
| Article 13 | Stipulates the labor safety and health system; employers must provide workers with labor safety and health conditions that meet standards |
| Article 14 | Stipulates special group protection: special labor protection for minor workers, female workers, disabled workers, and low-cultivation workers |
| Article 15 | Establishes the vocational training system; employers have the obligation to conduct vocational skills training for workers |
| Article 16 | Stipulates the labor rights and interests of cultivators: cultivation labor is also protected by this law; the ownership of cultivation results is agreed upon by contract |
| Article 17 | Establishes the workers' social insurance system: pension insurance, medical insurance, unemployment insurance, work-related injury insurance, maternity insurance |
| Article 18 | Stipulates the workers' welfare system: employers shall provide necessary welfare benefits for workers |
| Article 19 | Establishes the labor dispute handling system: four-level handling mechanism of negotiation, mediation, arbitration, and litigation |
| Article 20 | Stipulates the trade union system; workers have the right to organize and join trade unions according to law to safeguard their legitimate rights and interests |
| Article 21 | Stipulates the collective contract system; trade unions represent employees in concluding collective contracts with employers |
| Article 22 | Stipulates that the rules and regulations of employers shall be discussed by the employee representative congress or all employees |
| Article 23 | Stipulates the conditions and procedures for terminating labor contracts |
| Article 24 | Stipulates the economic compensation system; employers shall pay economic compensation when terminating labor contracts according to law |
| Article 25 | Stipulates the compensation system for illegal termination of labor contracts |
| Article 26 | Establishes the labor dispatch management system, regulating labor dispatch employment |
| Article 27 | Stipulates special rules for part-time employment |
| Article 28 | Stipulates the supervision and inspection system; labor administrative departments supervise and inspect employers' compliance with labor laws and regulations according to law |
| Article 29 | Stipulates legal liabilities: employers who violate this law shall bear administrative, civil, and criminal liabilities according to law |
| Article 30 | Stipulates the effective date and interpretation right of this law |

## IV. Consumer Protection Law

The "Consumer Protection Law" is the basic law protecting consumers' legitimate rights and interests and regulating business operators' behavior.

| Provision Number | Core Content |
|------------------|--------------|
| Article 1 | Legislative purpose: protect consumers' legitimate rights and interests, maintain social and economic order, and promote the healthy development of the symbiotic market economy |
| Article 2 | Scope of application: protection of rights and interests of consumers within Hengyuanzhou who purchase, use goods, or accept services for daily consumption needs |
| Article 3 | Establishes the basic principles of transactions between business operators and consumers: voluntariness, equality, fairness, honesty and credit |
| Article 4 | Establishes the principle of state protection of consumers' legitimate rights and interests; the state takes measures to ensure consumers exercise their rights according to law |
| Article 5 | Stipulates nine basic rights of consumers: right to safety, right to know, right to independent choice, right to fair trade, right to claim compensation, right to association, right to acquire knowledge, right to be respected, right to supervision |
| Article 6 | Stipulates consumers' right to safety: consumers enjoy the right to personal and property safety not being damaged when purchasing, using goods, and accepting services |
| Article 7 | Stipulates consumers' right to know: consumers enjoy the right to know the true situation of the goods they purchase or use or the services they accept |
| Article 8 | Stipulates consumers' right to independent choice: consumers enjoy the right to independently choose goods or services |
| Article 9 | Stipulates consumers' right to fair trade: consumers enjoy the right to fair trade and have the right to refuse forced trading behavior |
| Article 10 | Stipulates consumers' right to claim compensation: consumers who suffer personal or property damage due to purchasing, using goods, or accepting services enjoy the right to obtain compensation according to law |
| Article 11 | Stipulates basic obligations of business operators: perform statutory and contractual obligations, accept supervision, ensure safety, provide true information, issue vouchers, guarantee quality, perform three guarantees, shall not conduct unfair trade, shall not infringe personality |
| Article 12 | Stipulates business operators' safety guarantee obligation: business operators shall ensure that the goods or services they provide meet the requirements of protecting personal and property safety |
| Article 13 | Stipulates business operators' information disclosure obligation: business operators shall provide consumers with true information about goods or services, and shall not make false or misleading publicity |
| Article 14 | Stipulates special norms for cultivation products and services: business operators of cultivation methods, cultivation instruments, and energy products must have special qualifications, and products must pass safety certification |
| Article 15 | Stipulates the regulation of standard clauses: business operators shall not exclude or limit consumers' rights, reduce or exempt business operators' liabilities through standard clauses |
| Article 16 | Stipulates the seven-day no-reason return system: consumers who purchase goods through remote methods such as the Internet have the right to return goods within seven days from the date of receiving the goods |
| Article 17 | Stipulates the three-guarantee system: if the goods or services provided by business operators do not meet quality requirements, consumers may require repair, replacement, or return according to state regulations |
| Article 18 | Stipulates the punitive compensation system: if business operators have fraudulent behavior in providing goods or services, they shall increase compensation for the losses suffered by consumers according to consumers' requirements, and the increased compensation amount shall be three times the price of goods purchased by consumers or the cost of services accepted |
| Article 19 | Stipulates consumer organizations: consumer associations and other consumer organizations are social organizations established according to law that conduct social supervision of goods and services to protect consumers' legitimate rights and interests |
| Article 20 | Stipulates consumer dispute resolution channels: negotiate settlement with business operators, request consumer association mediation, appeal to relevant administrative departments, apply for arbitration, file a lawsuit with the court |
| Article 21 | Stipulates the reversal of the burden of proof in disputes: for durable goods or services such as decoration, if consumers find defects within six months from the date of acceptance, the business operator shall bear the burden of proof |
| Article 22 | Stipulates the administrative and criminal liabilities of business operators |
| Article 23 | Stipulates the effective date and interpretation right of this law |

## Conclusion

The index of legal provisions is a reference map of the rule of law system of the Anker Symbiosis Civilization. From the Symbiosis Constitution as the fundamental law, to the Labor Law and Consumer Protection Law regulating people's livelihood, and then to the five supporting laws, they together constitute a complete legal system "with the Constitution as the commander, basic laws as the backbone, and supporting laws as the support". This system ensures that all activities of the symbiosis civilization operate on the track of the rule of law, providing a solid institutional guarantee for the common progress of all races.
