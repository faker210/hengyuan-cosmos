## I. Notes on the Register

This register records the known major intelligent races within the framework of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization (the Republic of Ten Thousand Races), arranged by classification according to life form and evolutionary path. For each race, the register lists its name, life form, core traits, cultural characteristics, and its positioning within the symbiotic civilization. This register is a dynamically updated document; as cross-plane exploration deepens, newly discovered races will continue to be added. The fundamental principle of the Republic of Ten Thousand Races is: all intelligent races — regardless of their form or level of cultivation — are equal before the law and enjoy all the rights conferred by the symbiotic civilization.

## IV. Guarantee of Racial Rights

Under the system of the Republic of Ten Thousand Races, all intelligent races enjoy equal legal rights. Specific safeguard measures include:

1. **Equality of political rights**: Each race holds representative seats in the Anchor Assembly allocated by population proportion; races with smaller populations are guaranteed at least one guaranteed seat.
2. **Guarantee of cultural rights**: Each race has the right to preserve and develop its own language, script, religion, customs, and cultural traditions; the symbiotic civilization has established a special fund to protect the cultures of endangered races.
3. **Equality of educational rights**: Members of every race enjoy equal rights to education; the education system fully considers the physiological and cognitive characteristics of different races and provides differentiated educational programs.
4. **Equality of cultivation rights**: The Three Hundred Origin Cultivation Systems are opened equally to all races; the allocation of cultivation resources fully considers the differences in physique and energy needs of different races.
5. **Guarantee of special needs**: For races with special physiological needs (such as the energy-environment needs of the elemental races and the light needs of the plant races), the symbiotic civilization provides targeted safeguards in public facilities and living environments.

## Conclusion

The Register of All Races is the concrete embodiment of the "advancement of all races together" ideal of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. From humanoid forms to consciousness forms, from material life to digital life, every race contributes irreplaceable strength to the symbiotic civilization through its unique way of existence. The Republic of Ten Thousand Races does not assimilate all races into the human race, but achieves common evolution on the basis of respecting diversity — this is precisely the fundamental reason why the symbiotic civilization surpasses predatory civilizations.
