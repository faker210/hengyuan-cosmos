## I. Notes

This document lists the directions and suggestions available for expansion creation in the setting system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization, aiming to provide inspiration and guidance for setting creators and help them make valuable setting expansions within the existing framework. This list is classified by module; each expansion direction includes a description of the direction, the expansion value, the connection with existing settings, and creation suggestions. It should be emphasized that this list is only advisory; creators may give full play within the premise of maintaining setting consistency, and may also develop entirely new directions not covered by this list. All expanded settings must pass the check of Document 97 "Setting Contradiction Self-Check List" before being formally incorporated.

## X. Creation Principles of Expanded Settings

When creating expanded settings, the following principles should be followed:

1. **Consistency first**: All expanded settings must remain logically consistent with existing settings and pass the check of the Setting Contradiction Self-Check List.
2. **Reality as the Foundation**: Expanded settings should be reasonably deduced based on objective laws, avoiding pure fantasy and the collapse of settings.
3. **Symbiotic stance**: Expanded settings should always uphold the core stance of opposing predation and advocating symbiosis, and must not defend predatory behavior.
4. **Openness**: Expanded settings should maintain a certain degree of openness, leaving room for further expansion in the future.
5. **Practicality**: Expanded settings should have practical creative value, providing useful material for story creation, game design, and theoretical discussion.

## Conclusion

The Expanded Settings Suggestion List is the "open door" of the setting system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. It shows that this setting system is not a closed, completed product, but a living system that continuously grows and enriches. The thirty-six expansion directions are only a starting point; the true creativity of settings comes from the imagination and enthusiasm of every participant. As long as the basic principle of setting consistency is followed, every valuable expansion will become an indispensable component of the grand narrative of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization.
